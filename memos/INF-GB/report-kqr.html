If regional instability spreads, Informa's consolidated earnings growth will decelerate as high-margin sponsorship sales are cancelled.
11.
Suppressed travel demand: Geopolitical conflicts directly impact international traveler sentiment, presenting a major threat to premium hub events. Deutsche Bank Research observed on 16 October 2026 that RELX's Arabian Travel Market in Dubai faced high exposure to tensions. If international audiences decline, RELX will experience persistent divisional headwinds that will impair consolidated exhibitions profit growth.
12.
Restricted capacity expansion: The long-term development of exhibition infrastructure in the Middle East depends entirely on regional security stability. Deutsche Bank Research highlighted in May 2025 that Informa relies heavily on state-backed venue expansion to 8 million square meters by 2030. If political instability halts these projects, Informa cannot execute its regional cloning to sustain robust long-term growth targets.

Counterviews and alternative scenarios

13.
Defensive publisher ballast: Academic publishing assets provide highly recurring and stable subscription revenues that insulate diversified portfolios from macro shocks. Bernstein Research pointed out on 6 October 2026 that Taylor & Francis remains a reliable cash cow in downturns. If a severe global recession occurs, retaining this business would protect consolidated Informa's earnings while pure-play live event peers suffer.
14.
Other side of the story: Keeping a diversified portfolio provides a financial cushion, but it fails to resolve the deep complexity discount that global investors apply. Morgan Stanley argued on 7 October 2026 that separating Taylor & Francis and integrating Clarion represents the most effective path to clarify the corporate equity story. If the separation is cancelled, Informa will continue trading at depressed multiples compared to pure-play professional events peers.
