This persistent imbalance leaves the group highly vulnerable to sterling appreciation, which diminishes the value of inflows.

Integration with Failure Scenarios and Verdict

9.
Aggravating failure scenarios: Oxford Nanopore Technologies' foreign exchange risks directly aggravate several pre-mortem failure scenarios, particularly the core consumable pull-through bottleneck. Slower consumables adoption combined with negative currency translation would compound revenue shortfalls and expand cash consumption. Furthermore, currency-driven margin compression would force management to tinker with core operating expenses, risking long-term technical competitiveness.
10.
Overall risk conclusion: Oxford Nanopore Technologies faces a medium currency risk where persistent sterling strength and transaction mismatches pose a continuous threat to operating margins. While the transition to formal hedge accounting in FY26 provides short-term transaction insulation, prolonged sterling appreciation would delay EBITDA breakeven. Ultimately, translating placements into recurring consumables remains the primary operational determinant of earnings, but unfavorable currency paths will act as a major headwind.
