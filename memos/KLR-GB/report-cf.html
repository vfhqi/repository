EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 24 findings

Keller Group plc has undergone material operational transformation since CEO James Wroath's orderly appointment in August 2025, sustaining record underlying operating margins of 7.3% in H1 2026 versus historical averages of 4-5%, driven by portfolio rationalization, North American infrastructure momentum, and disciplined bidding governance that have structurally repositioned the group from commodity services to complex, highmargin design-and-build mega-projects.

The balance sheet exhibits exceptional financial strength with covenant leverage at just 0.1x versus target 0.5-1.5x, enabling aggressive capital returns including a £100m share buyback program launched in early 2026. However, significant insider selling by EME President Peter Wyton, who disposed of 60,000 shares across three transactions in August-September 2026 at prices ranging from £29.66 to £30.85, raises material questions about regional alignment and potential margin pressures in the European and Middle Eastern operations.
