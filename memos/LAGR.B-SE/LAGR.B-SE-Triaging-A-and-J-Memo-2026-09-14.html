[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Lagercrantz is a 'Compounder': a proven, decentralised acquirer of product-owning niche businesses whose out-year consensus is built to be low, because the sell-side models only the deals already announced, and whose earnings have therefore been upgraded year after year for fifteen years. At 221.0 SEK the shares pay about 15 percent a year for that machine with the multiple held, against the framework's 20 percent bar, so the case is well evidenced and not, at this price, compelling. It becomes compelling below about 195 to 207 SEK, or on evidence at the next two reports that the Q1 FY2027 margin miss was timing.

  • The change inside the company is a step-up in pace and targets, not a new direction.
    • In February 2026 the board raised the medium-term EBITA margin target to 20 percent from over 15 percent, the return-on-working-capital target to over 60 percent from 45 percent, the acquisition pace to 8 to 12 companies a year from 5 to 8, and the proprietary-products ambition to 85 percent of sales from 75 percent
    • the SEK 2 billion EBT ambition was reaffirmed in July 2026, halfway reached at SEK 1.5 billion in FY2026.
    • The engine has since run above its raised target: 14 acquisitions since April 2025 adding about SEK 1,500m of annual sales (about 14 percent of FY2026 revenue), six of them in Q1 FY2027, at about 7x EV/EBITA for Mastsystem against a cohort median of 6.8x.
    • A UK acquisition hub with a local M&A manager and a new chief financial officer from 1 April 2026 extend the machine beyond the Nordics
      • the chief executive since 2006 is unchanged and no successor is disclosed.
  • The external forces net to a mild tailwind, and none of them is new.
    • Grid electrification (about 17 percent of revenue, 10 percent organic growth in Q1 FY2027) and European defence demand are strengthening tailwinds
    • Nordic and UK construction (about 13 percent of sales), raw-material and freight inflation, krona strength on wholly unhedged translation exposure and floating-rate debt of about SEK 4.5 billion are headwinds, and cost the group 30 basis points of margin in the latest quarter.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are improving inside the framework's target band, with a wobble in the latest quarter.
    • EPS rose 18 percent in FY2026 and 19.5 percent in Q1 FY2027
    • revenue rose 17.5 percent in Q1 FY2027 (organic 6 percent, acquired 12 percent) and organic order intake ran 10 percent above invoiced sales.
    • The marks against: the EBITA margin fell to 17.1 percent in Q1 FY2027 from 17.5 percent a year earlier, on Control at 14 percent (price adjustments lagging raw-material inflation) and International at 14.6 percent (summer seasonality in newly bought units), so EBITA of SEK 498m missed consensus by 5 percent
      • cash conversion has fallen for two years, to 74 percent of net income on FactSet's definition in FY2026.
    • The sell-side's response was a pause, not a cut: consensus FY2027 EPS moved 0.9 percent lower over three months, three houses upgraded to Buy, and one house titled its note "the beat-and-raise run has ended".
  • The change has travelled all six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, and the last two show the pause.
    • Resources (the five-division structure, the UK hub, the new chief financial officer), choices (the raised targets), actions (14 deals; price rises under way), key operating metrics (organic growth 6 percent, order intake 10 percent above sales), financial statements (revenue +17.5 percent, EBITA +15 percent, margin down 30 basis points) and sell-side estimates (out-year EPS up 7 to 24 percent over twelve months, flat over three).
  • Earnings growth of about 14 percent a year is the return's engine, and its parts reconcile.
    • Revenue growth of about 13.5 percent a year (organic 4 to 5 percent, acquired about 10 percent, currency a small drag), the EBITA margin from 18.1 percent in FY2026 to about 19 percent by FY2029 (about 1.5 percent a year of profit growth), a below-EBITA drag of about 1 percent a year and a flat share count multiply to 14.1 percent a year
    • our EPS path of 5.82 SEK (FY2026, reported basic) to 8.62 SEK (FY2029) is 14.0 percent a year and lands on the consensus median for FY2029.
  • What it costs is a fair multiple for the cohort and the lower quartile of the stock's own range. 27.0x the 24-month-forward consensus EPS of 8.17 SEK at 221.0 SEK: the 24th percentile of the stock's own five-year range (median 29.8x), the 53rd percentile of its ten-year history, level with Addtech (27.3x), above Lifco (25.7x), Diploma (25.5x) and Halma (24.6x), and the second-highest multiple of the 43-stock sector.
    • What is priced: the announced deals (in consensus FY2027 revenue of +13.7 percent), the 15 percent growth model (in the multiple) and the Q1 miss (in the 17 percent fall from the 52-week high).
      • What is not priced: the 20 percent margin target (consensus margin flat) and the un-announced deals of FY2028 and FY2029 (consensus revenue growth of 6.5 and 2.9 percent).
  • The 'Modal return' with the multiple held is about 15 percent a year at both horizons, a C against the 20 percent bar, and the re-rating is the swing.
    • Earnings growth of 14 percent a year plus a dividend of about 1.3 percent a year and no re-rating gives 14.8 percent a year over eighteen months and 15.0 percent a year over three years.
    • A return to the five-year median multiple of about 30x on a cleared margin and a bid style gives 20.0 percent a year over three years
      • we put that branch at about one chance in four.
      • A fall to the five-year 10th percentile of 24x on a second margin miss gives 8.8 percent a year
      • also about one chance in four.
      • The held branch is about half.
    • A plausibly worse drop-through from EBITA to EPS (rates 100 basis points higher on all-floating debt, amortisation outgrowing EBITA, one year of a 5 percent stronger krona) cuts EPS growth to 11.8 percent a year and the return to 12.9 percent a year, a D.
  • The chain holds from the inputs to the outputs and breaks at the price.
    • The one link inside the company not yet in the numbers is the timing of price pass-through in Control, which management asserts and does not quantify and which the Q3 FY2027 report (about February 2027) tests
    • the one link outside the company is the multiple, which is the serial-acquirer basket's multiple in a basket that de-rated 13 percent in the twelve months to June 2026 and is not ours to earn.

  • The case therefore is a top-decile operator in a machine that works, at a fair price in a style the market is not paying for, one quarter away from the evidence that would settle whether the margin wobble was noise

    • a watch case with a named entry price, not a park and not a buy at 221.0 SEK.

  • Rating = C, because the chain holds from the inputs to the outputs but the return it produces at this price is about 15 percent a year, not 20: the change forces are a step-up in targets and pace inside a proven machine (E 1 at C), the outputs are improving inside the target band with a wobble in the latest quarter (E 2 at B), and the 'Modal return' with the multiple held is a C (E 3 at C)

    • the parent sits at C rather than the children's midpoint because materiality is measured against the 20 percent bar and the modal path does not reach it.
    • Not B, because a B needs either the multiple at 30x or growth at 18 percent a year, and we take neither as modal
    • not D, because 14 percent EPS growth with fifteen years behind it and the whole chain propagated is a material, well-evidenced expectation.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The strongest case against is the risks report's own verdict that the company is a false friend: record margins are masking organic decay (organic growth of 3 percent in FY2026 against a five-year average of 5 percent), so the margin was bought (proprietary-product acquisitions at 16 to 17 percent margins, Mastsystem at 40 percent) rather than earned
    • growth is bought at rising private multiples (4 to 6x historically, now 8 to 10x or higher by a former Vice President's account)
    • the beat-and-raise run ended the quarter after the targets were raised, which is what happens when targets are set at the top
    • cash conversion runs below 80 percent
    • a return to peer-median multiples is a fall of about 35 percent (DNB Carnegie's severe case 165 SEK, Redeye's bear case 175 SEK)
    • and the chief executive of twenty years has no disclosed successor.
  • What we concede: the return at this price is 15 percent a year, not 20
    • the margin recovery is asserted by management and not yet in the numbers
    • the paid multiple is the driver that would break the model and it is drifting up around the company
    • the multiple is industry-driven and the industry is de-rating
    • the succession is the one foundation we cannot test.
  • Why we still hold the view: organic growth of 6 percent in two consecutive quarters (Q4 FY2026 and Q1 FY2027) and order intake 10 percent above sales refute organic decay on the latest evidence
    • the miss was confined to Control (cost timing) and International (seasonality of Epoke and Friggeråkers) while Electrify (20.1 percent) and Niche Products (20.8 percent), the divisions that carry the proprietary thesis, ran above the 20 percent target and revenue beat
    • the company paid about 7x for a 40 percent-margin business in Mastsystem and the cohort's median paid multiple over 2023 to 2025 is 6.8x, so the company's own prices have not moved with the market's
    • and the multiple is already at the 24th percentile of its own five-year range, so the 35 percent downside case needs a level the stock has not seen since 2020 (the five-year low is 20.2x).
  • The false-friend reading was made on FY2026 data, before the two 6 percent quarters
    • on the current base it is a risk to watch, not the modal case, and the Q3 FY2027 report tests both readings on the same evidence.
