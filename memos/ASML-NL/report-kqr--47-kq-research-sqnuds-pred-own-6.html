This tax-exempt status means no cash tax liability was incurred on the massive paper gain, protecting ASML's net margins from the 25.8% statutory rate.

Consequently, the paper gain flowed directly to the bottom line with zero tax leakage, maximizing the immediate benefit to reported earnings.
