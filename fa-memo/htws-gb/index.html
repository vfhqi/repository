The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Tenancy additions: 4,000 (FY2026, guidance about 4,000; nine months 3,435), 3,100 (FY2027, guidance "more than 3,000"), 2,400, 2,200, 2,100 (management's informal "about 6% tenancy growth a year", supported by a colocation floor from the 2026 to 2027 new-site cohort). Year-end tenancies 35,944, 39,044, 41,444, 43,644, 45,744.
  • Average tenancies (what drives revenue, not additions): +11.8%, +9.4%, +7.3%, +5.7%, +5.1%. 2026 additions were front-loaded, so FY2027's carry is smaller than a simple midpoint suggests.
  • New-site share of additions: about 29% (FY2026; Q3 2026 was 45%), 35%, 30%, 27%, 25%; capex about 130k to 135k USD per new site and about 12k per colocation (Deutsche Bank about 138k and 12k). Sites about 15,915, 17,000, 17,720, 18,315, 18,840; tenancy ratio 2.26x, 2.30x, 2.34x, 2.38x, 2.43x.
  • Price: escalators of about 2% to 3% gross, about +0.5% a year net of currency translation and mix in USD revenue per average tenancy (range -1% to +1%).
  • Fuel pass-through (zero margin): a revenue premium of about 30 (FY2026), 27 (FY2027: about 18 in H1, 9 in H2), then about 12 a year, assuming oil stays below about 100 USD after its fall on 5 November 2026. This is why H2 2027 revenue growth is only 5.3% while EBITDA grows about 11%, and why the margin rises sharply in H2 2027.
  • DRC renewals (Airtel Africa and Orange, initial terms ending 2028 to 2029): base case concession of about 1% of group revenue (-5 FY2028, -12 FY2029, -13 FY2030), all lost from EBITDA.
  • EBITDA 535, 606, 657, 700, 745: Q4 2026 exit run-rate about 571; each 500 additions about 5 USDm in-year and more than 10 annualised; net price and efficiency about +7 to +12 a year; overheads growing about 6% to 7% a year.
  • D&A (excluding acquired amortisation of about 23 to 26 a year): the 2026 build (290 capex, about 1,170 new sites with new ground leases) lifts FY2027 by about 10%.
  • Net financial items: about 185 a year excluding currency revaluations of intercompany loans and one-offs (H1 2026: about 101 net before a 21.8 currency loss); net debt including leases held at about 1,850 to 1,950 as buybacks absorb free cash flow; the convertible principal (180, March 2027) refinanced from the 250 term loan; the 7.5% 2029 notes refinanced at about the same cost.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
