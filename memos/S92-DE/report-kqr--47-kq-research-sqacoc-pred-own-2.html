Section 4 – Contractual Indexation and Price Adjustments

Section summary

Service Agreement Indexation

1.
Structured adjustments: SMA Solar utilizes a formal pricing indexation framework specifically for its long-term service agreements and commercial warranty products. The company disclosed in April 2026 that remuneration for services under the FLEX Warranty is adjusted using the official Producer Price Index of Industrial Products. This index is managed by the German Federal Office of Statistics to capture broader macroeconomic price shifts. This indexing mechanism helps SMA stabilize long-term service revenues against German industrial inflation.
2.
Specific thresholds: SMA Solar implements strict operational boundaries before any price adjustments can be activated on commercial warranty contracts. The company's contract terms in December 2025 specified that a price adjustment does not occur unless the relevant index has changed by at least 2.5% from the contract's commencement date. This threshold prevents minor index fluctuations from triggering administratively costly contract revisions. Consequently, these terms ensure that only significant price changes translate into adjusted customer invoices.
3.
Temporal lags: SMA Solar enforces a substantial time delay before implementing revised pricing for its commercial business customers. According to company documents published in May 2026, each subsequent price adjustment cannot occur until at least 12 months after the last adjustment. Furthermore, the contracts explicitly prevent customers or SMA from requesting any retroactive pricing adjustments. This rigid timing means that operational cost changes are delayed before affecting the company's service margins.
4.
Legal protections: SMA Solar maintains strong contractual rights to protect its cash flows when commercial customers delay payments under warranty contracts. The company's legal terms in May 2026 stated that SMA can refuse to provide warranty services if a customer fails to pay within a 14-day deadline. Furthermore, the company reserves the right to terminate the extended warranty entirely after this period. These protective clauses minimize the risk of rising customer defaults during periods of high economic volatility.
