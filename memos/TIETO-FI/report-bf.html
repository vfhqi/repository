EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Tietoevry completed a transformative structural pivot in 2025, divesting its capital-intensive legacy infrastructure business Tietoevry Tech Services to Agilitas Private Equity for EUR 300 million, repositioning itself as a pure-play vertical software and consulting specialist focused exclusively on high-margin, regulated software markets across Nordic banking, healthcare, and industrial sectors.

The divestment fundamentally altered the group's financial profile, eliminating asset-heavy operations and redirecting EUR 147 million in net cash proceeds toward debt reduction, while enabling management to concentrate capital allocation on proprietary software development and shareholder returns through a EUR 150 million buyback program.

Strategic Portfolio and Vertical Software Strengths

1.
Focused vertical specialization: Tietoevry operates four core business units–Banktech, Caretech, Indtech, and Tech Consulting–aligned to highly regulated, mission-critical sectors with high barriers to entry and structural growth drivers.
2.
Superior software profitability dominance: The three vertical software units (Banktech, Caretech, Indtech) combined generate approximately 70% of adjusted EBITA despite representing 60% of revenue, demonstrating structural margin superiority.
3.
Segment profit contribution mix: Banktech delivers 34% of EBITA on 31% revenue share, Caretech contributes 21% EBITA on 13% revenue, while Indtech generates 13% EBITA on 14% revenue.
4.
Exceptional revenue resilience: The software business units maintain 74% to 80% recurring revenue shares, with Indtech achieving the highest at 80%, Banktech at 78%, and Caretech at 74%.
5.
Accelerating SaaS transition: Standardized SaaS comprises 61% of Indtech software revenue and 55% of Banktech, while Caretech has only 3% SaaS penetration, representing a massive future conversion opportunity.

Financial Performance and Capital Allocation

6.
Bifurcated financial profile: The peer comparison reveals Tietoevry operates at EUR 1.85 billion revenue scale with a negative L3Y revenue CAGR of -1.42% and 13.60% EBITDA margin, lagging pure-play software peers.
7.
Asset-light capital discipline: Post-divestment CapEx intensity stands at 3%, significantly below the inflated 29.20% at peer cBrain, which was distorted by headquarters acquisition.
8.
Aggressive cost restructuring execution: A comprehensive EUR 130 million run-rate savings program progressed ahead of schedule, achieving EUR 105 million in savings by Q1 2026 to expand margins.
9.
Shareholder-first capital allocation: Management replaced the historical 100% payout with a flexible 60-80% dividend policy, proposed EUR 0.88 per share for 2025, and launched a EUR 150 million buyback funded by divestment proceeds.
10.
Dramatic deleveraging success: Interest-bearing net debt declined from EUR 708 million to EUR 377 million by Q1 2026, bringing leverage to 1.30x and well below the 2.0x long-term target.

Customer Lock-In and Value Chain Position

11.
Prohibitive customer switching barriers: Multi-year software contracts extending up to eleven years, deeply embedded database integration, and massive migration project risks lock customers into core platforms with minimal churn risk.
12.
Exceptionally low banking churn: Former VP interviews indicate annual customer churn in credit and banking software is below 5%, with actual displacement closer to 2% in core platforms.
13.
Dominant high-margin positioning: Tietoevry occupies the proprietary vertical software stage, capturing 65-70% gross margins and 20-28% EBIT margins, while avoiding commoditized infrastructure services yielding only 5-10% EBIT.
14.
Software pricing power resilience: The mission-critical nature of vertical software allows Tietoevry to regularly push through price increases, protected by high integration into client workflows and replacement costs.
15.
Consulting margin vulnerability: Technology Consulting has low pricing power due to intense labor competition in the Nordics, remaining vulnerable to salary inflation unless offset by offshore delivery centers.

Competitive Dynamics and Market Share Threats

16.
Aggressive regional banking challenger: Netcompany Banking Services emerges as the most aggressive Nordic banking platform challenger, directly threatening Tietoevry's historically secure Norwegian market dominance.
17.
Major Norwegian displacement loss: Netcompany Banking Services was selected to replace Tietoevry's core banking platform at OBOS-banken, with the contract expiring December 31, 2026.
18.
Norwegian market share volatility: Tietoevry maintains a 22% Nordic banking software market share and dominates Norway historically, but captured major share from SDC through Eika Alliance and Lokalbank consortium wins.
19.
Entrenched public sector duopoly: Tietoevry Care maintains a secure 50% market share in Swedish and Norwegian municipal social care and sustains a resilient duopoly with CGI in Finnish healthcare.

Operator Quality and Leadership Transition

20.
Strategic direction renewal: CEO Endre Rangnes was appointed in July 2025, bringing sharp focus on commercial execution, portfolio simplification including Tech Services divestment, and rebranding to Tieto.
21.
Operational urgency restoration: New leadership is executing massive cost base resets, achieving EUR 105 million in savings ahead of schedule, while reducing Tech Consulting headcount by up to 500 employees.
22.
Long-term growth inflection challenge: Over the last 12 years, continuing operations delivered flat organic growth near 0%, with the 2027-2028 framework targeting over 5% CAGR despite 3% legacy contract headwinds.
23.
Ambitious profitability targets: Tieto aims to expand adjusted EBITA margin to over 16% by 2028, requiring Caretech margins exceeding 28% and Banktech over 17%, driven by the asset-light transformation.
