EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 42 findings

BFF Bank is executing a strategic transition toward transaction services while aggressively de-risking its capitalintensive core Italian public sector factoring portfolio, with non-factoring activities generating 64% of net revenues in first half 2026 compared to 58% in first half 2025. The group operates across nine European countries, with France representing the fastest-growing market where new business volumes increased 155% year-on-year to EUR 275 million.

Factoring revenue is driven by upfront maturity commissions and accrued late payment interest under European late payment laws, with regulated interest spreads accruing at 8% over the European Central Bank reference rate on overdue public invoices. However, the bank reduced total net exposure to negative court rulings from EUR 240 million to EUR 100 million via retrocessions and suspended the 2025 dividend distribution to solidify the balance sheet.
