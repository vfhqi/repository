Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Market participants closely monitored the organic growth trajectory of ID Logistics as new contract ramp-ups continued.
  2. Sell-side expectations remained divided over the balance between capital-intensive e-commerce contract launches and free cash flow generation.
  3. Peer comparisons highlighted the resilience of the asset-light business model compared to asset-heavy cold storage and maritime transportation networks.
1.
Broad exposure: ID Logistics operates an asset-light contract logistics business primarily focused on highly technical customer-specific warehouse logistics. The customer portfolio is well-balanced across retail, ecommerce, and fast-moving consumer goods segments. An asset-light approach has allowed the group to expand into 19 countries across Europe, the Americas, Asia, and Africa as of early 2026.
2.
Small footprint: ID Logistics held a 0.5% share of the highly fragmented global outsourced warehousing market in 2026. Direct competitor DHL Supply Chain led the outsourced market with a 2.4% share, followed by GXO Logistics at 1.6% and Kuehne+Nagel at 0.7% in the same period. Global contract logistics remained highly underpenetrated with 68% of the total warehousing market managed in-house during 2026.
