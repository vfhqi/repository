  2. Unit discount rates of up to 28 percent mitigate competitive risks and protect OTE's domestic EBITDA margins against new infrastructure builders.

  1. The non-binding joint venture agreement between Vodafone Greece and PPC targets a fifty-fifty wholesale fiber platform covering over 1.6 million homes initially.
  2. Ongoing regulatory review by the Hellenic Competition Commission represents a critical milestone before the targeted transaction closing in the first half of 2027.

Aggressive pricing disruption: Retail tariffs in the Greek broadband sector could collapse if competitors pass wholesale savings directly to consumers.
