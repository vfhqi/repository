Section 2 – Financial Targets and Management Steer

Section summary
  1. Management has shifted focus from turnaround to growth, but high confidence in the 12% EBITA margin target is tempered by acknowledged supply-side restrictions.

Formal Financial Framework and Targets Hexatronic maintains a long-term financial target of a 12

percent adjusted EBITA margin, supported by segment-specific targets of 10 percent for Fiber Solutions over a cycle and 15 to 17 percent for Data Center. Management noted in July 2026 that Data Center profitability has remained within the 15-17% range for four consecutive quarters, validating its structural role in the Group's earnings. The Group's capital allocation priorities emphasize organic growth and a "structured search" for acquisitions, particularly in service-oriented businesses to increase resilience. Adjusted leverage was reported at 1.3x EBITDA as of September 30, 2026, down from 1.7x in the previous quarter, providing flexibility for M&A.

Informal Steer on Q4 2026 and 2027 Management explicitly warned in the Q3 2026 report that "

some segments are beginning to see growth restrictions," specifically naming glass fibre and certain connector types as limiting factors. In terms of quarterly cadence, CEO Rikard Fröberg noted that North American Fiber Solutions is "shifting back towards growth" with 13% organic sales growth in Q3. However, Redeye research on October 23, 2026, highlighted that management guides for a normalization in Q4 2026 for the Data Center segment following exceptional seasonal effects.

Section 5 – Industry Demand and Supply (Read-Across)

Section summary
  1. Global demand for high-density fiber is entering a structural super-cycle, but supply-side preform bottlenecks remain the primary constraint for non-integrated players.

The AI-Driven Fiber Super-Cycle The global optical fiber market is recovering from a 2023-2025

inventory correction into an AI-led super cycle with increased fiber intensity across all architectures. Each successive generation of AI infrastructure requires materially greater fiber density, with recent GPU clusters requiring up to 64x higher fiber count per rack. 59 initiates Corning with a "Buy" at $134, noting that Corning sits at the center of an AI-driven fiber shortage.

Supply Bottlenecks and Pricing Preforms are the primary bottleneck in the supply chain, with global

utilization above 90 percent in the U.S. and APAC regions. Fiber prices are reportedly up 3.6x in Europe and 1.6x in the U.S. since July 2025, with hyperscalers prioritizing supply over price. Contrary evidence: CRU Group expects approximately 230 mm fiber-km of new Chinese preform capacity by end- 2027, which could normalize prices sooner than Western suppliers anticipate.

Data Center vs. Telecom Demand Data center optical cable demand is projected to grow at a 20

percent CAGR over 2025-2031, significantly outpacing the 7% growth for the overall market. U.S. hyperscale optical module market size is projected to increase from $18 bn in 2026 to over $35 bn by 2030. Clearfield management notes that AT&T has consumed much of the U.S.-made cable supply, delaying smaller regional broadband builds.

Other side of the story The industry's optimism assumes that hyperscaler capex remains

1.
unconstrained by power availability or cooling limits. If supply shortages for heavy electrical equipment like transformers–where lead times are currently 2.5 to 4 years–delay data center commissioning, the demand for Hexatronic's passive fiber products will be pushed out into 2028 or later. This mismatch between fiber availability and power infrastructure could create a period of "dead air" for connectivity suppliers in late 2027.

Section 7 – If the Answer Differs from the Common View

Section summary
  1. A deviation from the expected AI upcycle would likely be driven by power-related delays in data center construction rather than a lack of fiber demand. If the fiber super-cycle is resolved differently from the current 20% CAGR expectation, Hexatronic's earnings would face significant volatility. The primary threat is not a lack of demand, but structural power shortages in major data center hubs like Northern Virginia or Northern Europe, where grid connection delays could stretch
1.
into 2028-2029. If hyperscalers like Meta or Microsoft are forced to defer projects from 2027 to 2028 due to a lack of power, Hexatronic’s record Q3 2026 order intake could become a "high-water mark" rather than a new baseline. Peers like Prysmian are already reallocating capacity, but a looming oversupply of fiber–if Chinese preform capacity ramps faster than 230 mm fkm by late 2027–could crash global pricing. While current consensus ignores this risk, unusual cases like the 2023-2025 inventory correction show that the fiber market can shift from shortage to glut rapidly if demand signals are misinterpreted. Regulators in Europe or the U.S. could also intervene with new anti-dumping duties on Chinese fiber, which would further tighten supply and potentially drive Hexatronic's input costs higher than price increases can cover.
