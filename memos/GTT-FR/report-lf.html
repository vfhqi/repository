EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 19 findings

GTT successfully transitioned to external successor François Michel in January 2026, bringing deep industrial expertise from John Cockerill and Saint-Gobain to restore separated governance structures. Legacy CEO Philippe Berterottière delivered exceptional 15-year tenure, exceeding typical oil service leadership longevity while compounding shareholder value through strict capital discipline. Berterottière's aerospace commercial pedigree at Airbus, Matra, and Arianespace established repeatable sales-focused patterns successfully imported to GTT's licensing model. Michel exhibits a pragmatic, service-oriented leadership style that prioritizes digital integration and cost control, contrasting with Berterottière's classic philosophical approach. The abrupt eight- month resignation of interim CEO Jean-Baptiste Choimet created temporary operational disruption, forcing Berterottière to resume executive duties during an 11-month search process.
