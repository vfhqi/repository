Section summary
  1. the divisional integration utilizes low-margin cash-generative construction to provide free working capital to high-margin property development
  2. Skanska's operations span high-performing US civil infrastructure and European residential housing, balanced against depressed commercial offices
  3. contractual revenue structures de-risk construction cost inflation while segment and IFRS reporting methods diverge on property transaction recognition

Working capital funding: Free working capital from Construction provides internal funding to the capital- heavy development business streams.

Plausible problems to monitor

The financial analysis of Skanska reveals several critical plausible problem to monitor that merit close attention from stakeholders evaluating the company's near-term operational trajectory and medium-term capital efficiency. The most prominent concern centers on deeply depressed returns on capital, with group ROE reaching only 10.2% in 2025–substantially below the long-term target of 18%–and Commercial Property Development ROCE collapsing to just 1.0% versus the 10.0% segment target.

This return underperformance is anchored against both internal benchmarks and peer standards, where Skanska trades at a lower price-to-book multiple than international peers due to cyclically depressed property returns. The concern is compounded by capital employed in Commercial Development and Investment Properties rising from SEK 26bn to SEK 79bn by mid-2025, representing a massive expansion in trapped, low-returning capital that drags down overall group profitability
