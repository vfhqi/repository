Section 2 – Nolato's Cost Structure and Historical Operating Leverage

Section summary
  1. Nolato exhibits high operating leverage because of a substantial fixed-cost base comprising personnel, depreciation, and facility overheads.
  2. Historical margin fluctuations demonstrate that underutilized capacity severely depresses profitability during down-cycles, while cost alignment and price adjustments support swift margin recovery.

Cost Structure Breakdown

1.
Vulnerable raw material costs: Nolato and ABG Sundal Collier estimated that raw materials represented 36% of sales in 2025. Management indicated that roughly 60% of these inputs are directly linked to volatile oil prices. This correlation introduces temporary gross margin pressure during crude market upswings due to pricing pass-through lag times of three to six months. This lag directly dampens near-term operating leverage.
2.
High fixed personnel expenses: Nolato reported that adapting total costs and managing staff levels in response to severe volume drops represents a primary management focus. The group employed 5,732 workers in 2023, which was lower than the 6,627 workers in 2022. This reduction shows that personnel costs, especially retaining validated cleanroom staff, require extensive management effort to reduce when demand fluctuates. Such personnel rigidities limit how quickly fixed costs can be eliminated during cyclical downturns.
3.
Similar cost structures: Similar cost structures exist across the broader polymer contract manufacturing sector. Analyst reports on SKF show that raw materials and labor together account for approximately 72% of its total operating cost base. Within that peer group, raw materials make up 34% of the expenses and labor represents 38%. This comparable distribution demonstrates that labor and materials dominate polymer-related manufacturing profiles. Consequently, managing these volatile lines is crucial for maintaining stable industry margins.

High fixed overheads and low underutilization absorption drove this underperformance.
