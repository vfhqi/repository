Section 1 – Business Model + Organisation 7 findings

Section summary
  1. Posti operates a hybrid postal and logistics model across six countries, driving transformation via its Astra program and integrated operations unit to offset structural mail declines.

Operational structure and business unit overview

1.
Unit-level operations: Posti Group Corp operates as a parcel, freight, postal, and contract logistics provider across Finland, Sweden, and the Baltics. Divisional structure: Operations are organized into three primary reportable segments: eCommerce and Delivery Services, Fulfillment and Logistics Services, and Postal Services. eCommerce and Delivery Services: The unit delivers parcel services across Finland and the Baltics, alongside groupage freight and value-added logistics in Finland. Fulfillment and Logistics Services: The segment provides contract logistics and in-house logistics operating across 620,000 square meters of warehouse space in Finland and Sweden. Postal Services unit: The division delivers corporate and consumer letters, direct marketing, publications, and digital messaging nationwide in Finland. Geographic footprint: Posti operates across six countries, generating 90.2% of net sales in Finland, 4.6% in Sweden, and 2.5% in the Baltics
