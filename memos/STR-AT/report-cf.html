Appendix

Outliers worth noting

The group achieved a record EBIT margin of 6.7% in fiscal year 2025, representing an all-time high that places STRABAG in the top decile of European construction companies. This profitability level significantly exceeds the 3.5% margin target expected for peer Skanska's construction unit, suggesting either exceptional operational execution or transient favorable conditions that merit closer scrutiny regarding sustainability [Cross-ref: Section 4, Section 5].

STRABAG demonstrates a highly unusual cash conversion rate of approximately 150% over the 2022-2025 period, with cumulative operating cash flow of EUR 5.8 billion substantially exceeding EBIT of EUR 3.9 billion. This represents an anomalous conversion pattern rarely observed in the construction sector, driven by public infrastructure advance payments and efficient working capital management that creates a structural cash generation advantage over peers [Cross-ref: Section 5, Section 7].

The group operates an exceptionally deep vertical integration model, producing 86% of required asphalt in-house through a network of more than 550 proprietary materials production facilities. This self-supply ratio places STRABAG in the extreme tail of the distribution for materials vertical integration within the European construction sector, creating formidable competitive moats through cost hedging and supply security that are virtually impossible for smaller competitors to replicate [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 9].

Management exhibits an extreme conservative sandbagger guidance archetype, with actual output volume consistently beating original targets by an average of over 5% since 2022, and the 2025 actual EBIT margin of 6.7% significantly exceeding the normalized forecast of 4.5%. This pattern of systematic under-promising represents an outlier behavior among European construction companies, where peers typically guide closer to actual outcomes, raising questions about whether this conservatism reflects genuine uncertainty or a deliberate strategy to manage market expectations [Cross-ref: Section 10, Section 11].
