Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Hexpol entered the third quarter of 2026 backed by a recovery in organic growth and a strategic consolidation of its thermoplastic elastomers platform.
  2. Market expectations before the third quarter focused on the sustainability of Hexpol's positive organic growth trajectory.
  3. Investors closely monitored potential margins and the timing of a permanent chief executive officer appointment.
  4. Strategic compounding advantages in North America set Hexpol apart from its lower-margin capital goods competitors.
1.
Global leadership: Hexpol operates as a world-leading polymers group specializing in advanced polymer compounds, gaskets, and wheels. The group serves diverse end markets including the automotive, construction, and engineering industries. Operations span across fourteen countries with approximately 5,000 employees globally.
2.
Structured organization: Hexpol is divided into three distinct business areas comprising Rubber Compounding, Thermoplastic Compounding, and Engineered Products. Decentralized management structures allow local business units to remain flexible. Rubber Compounding remains the largest segment in terms of total sales.
3.
Volatile sales: Hexpol experienced fluctuating annual revenues over the past three fiscal years. Group net sales decreased to SEK 19,324 mm in 2025 compared to SEK 20,437 mm in 2024. Organic sales fell 4 percent during 2025. Revenues reached SEK 22,046 mm during the 2023 fiscal year.
4.
Pressure on profitability: Hexpol recorded declining profit margins during the preceding three-year period. Operating profit amounted to SEK 2,791 mm in 2025 representing an operating margin of 14.4 percent. Operating margin reached 15.5 percent during the 2024 fiscal year. Adjusted EBIT reached SEK 3,659 mm in 2023.
5.
Peer comparison: AirBoss of America represents the primary North American competitor in custom rubber compounding. Consolidated sales for AirBoss reached USD 410,203 k in 2025 compared to USD 387,024 k in 2024. Compounding sales contracted in 2025 due to weaker volumes.
6.
Margin gap: Peer compounding margins remain well below the levels historically achieved by Hexpol. AirBoss of America generated historically lower margins compared to Hexpol's EBITDA margins. Hexpol historical EBITDA margins averaged between 18 percent and 19 percent.
7.
Resilient peer: Trelleborg represents another major polymer peer demonstrating stable fundamentals. Trelleborg achieved annual sales of SEK 34 billion in 2025 which remained flat compared to 2024. Organic growth reached 1 percent for Trelleborg in 2025.
8.
Financial strength: Hexpol maintained a solid balance sheet as it prepared for accelerated acquisitions. Net debt to EBITDA ratio reached 0.95 in 2025. Leverage stood at a lower ratio of 0.59 during 2024. Net debt reached SEK 3,184 mm at the end of 2025.
9.
Underperforming returns: Hexpol shares lagged the broader Swedish market over the twelve months prior to September 2026. Absolute share price return reached 13.71 percent. Relative return fell by 2.16 percent against the OMX Stockholm Index.
10.
Target adjustments: Broker opinions on Hexpol progressed upward during the six months leading to the Q3 2026 earnings. Analysts from DNB Carnegie raised their target price to SEK 88 from SEK 82 in July 2026. Hold rating was reiterated despite the positive estimate adjustments.
11.
Positive revisions: Broker expectations strengthened further in September 2026 following improved organic trends. DNB Carnegie raised the target price to SEK 95 from SEK 88 on September 25, 2026. Organic sales growth reached 8 percent during the second quarter of 2026.
12.
Other side of the story: Strategic execution risks remain high if the automotive sector experiences further demand contraction. Hexpol has high exposure to light vehicles which accounts for approximately 38 percent of total volumes. Weaker consumer sentiment could pressure Hexpol's organic growth trajectory.

Section 3 – Key moving parts and changes

Section summary
  1. Hexpol's Q3 2026 results demonstrated explosive organic growth that substantially beat market expectations.
  2. Adjusted EBIT margins compressed due to growth-enabling overhead, leading to a bottom-line miss.
  3. A strong balance sheet and robust cash conversion support the group's accelerated compounding acquisition strategy.
1.
Strategic leadership transition: Profound leadership shifts occurred as Henrik Elmin assumed the role of Chief Executive Officer on August 1, 2026. The Board of Directors and Henrik Elmin agreed on his permanent continuation on October 23, 2026. Outgoing leader Klas Dahlberg departed following the establishment of the company's long-term strategy. Elmin's appointment is expected to accelerate strategic execution across decentralized units.
2.
Seamless executive coordination: Chief Financial Officer Peter Rosén temporarily managed operations as acting Chief Executive Officer from late May until August 2026. Peter Rosén resumed his permanent duties as Chief Financial Officer once Henrik Elmin took office. The smooth handover minimized disruption and preserved executive continuity during critical negotiations.
3.
Expanding thermoplastic capabilities: Hexpol expanded its North American thermoplastic compounding presence by acquiring eighty percent of Piedmont Resin Supply LLC on October 31, 2024. The transaction was valued at eighty-six million dollars on a cash and debt-free basis. Piedmont Resin Supply LLC introduced advanced technical nylon compounding capabilities to the group. Founders Matt Griffith and Paul Daniel retained a twenty percent ownership stake.
4.
Profitable cable consolidation: Hexpol consolidated its thermoplastic platform by purchasing eighty percent of the Turkish compounder Kabkom Kimya Sanayi on April 1, 2025. The purchase price of fifty-four million euros was funded through cash and credit lines. Kabkom Kimya Sanayi added specialized thermoplastic and thermoset cable compounds to Hexpol's global offering. The acquisition delivered profitability above the group average.
5.
Accelerated European growth: Hexpol completed the acquisition of one hundred percent of Italy's Vipa Group on October 1, 2026. The purchase price reached one hundred and forty-three point five million euros. Vipa Group brought a leading position in European thermoplastic compounds for the wire and cable sector. The transaction significantly strengthened Hexpol's position in electrification markets.
6.
Strategic Turkish expansion: Hexpol signed an agreement on October 21, 2026, to acquire eighty percent of Turkish elastomer compounder Symplast A.S. The acquisition price of fifty-four million euros is funded through existing bank facilities. Symplast operates manufacturing sites in Turkey and Georgia with annual sales of thirty-four million euros. Closing is expected by the first quarter of 2027.
7.
Compressed operating margins: Hexpol reported an adjusted EBIT margin of thirteen point six percent in the third quarter of 2026. The margin fell below the thirteen point eight percent achieved in the preceding half-year. Increased overhead and integration costs linked to organic growth initiatives drove the margin compression. Analysts reduced full-year profit assumptions while anticipating gradual margin recovery over time.
8.
Potential capital allocation: DNB Carnegie analysts highlighted Hexpol's solid cash generation and low net debt to EBITDA ratio. The broker suggested the board should consider initiating share buybacks at the expense of high dividends. Hexpol's dividend policy targets a payout of forty to sixty percent of profit after tax. Future capital allocation shifts could provide positive triggers for share re-rating.
9.
Accelerating organic demand: Hexpol achieved double-digit organic growth of twelve percent during the third quarter of 2026. The rubber compounding segment saw volume growth across North America and Europe. Thermoplastic compounding organic sales rose nineteen percent on higher volumes and raw material price hikes. Captive volume takeovers in North America further supported positive organic growth trends.
10.
Ambitious corporate goals: Hexpol established clear financial goals running through 2030 to drive shareholder value. The goals include an earnings per share CAGR exceeding ten percent. Management aims to maintain an EBIT margin of fourteen to sixteen percent. The company seeks to sustain revenue growth above ten percent.
11.
Growing structural competition: DNB Carnegie analysts noted long-term threats from original equipment manufacturers in- shoring production. Independent compounders face margin pressure as tier-one suppliers expand their footprint. Hexpol has managed price variations successfully despite these down-market integration trends. Excess manufacturing capacity allows the group to offer highly competitive pricing to clients.
12.
External logistics headwinds: Hexpol confronted supply chain imbalances caused by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz during 2026. The closure triggered volatility in raw material and oil derivative pricing. Localized production facilities in North America and Europe shielded Hexpol from extensive transport delays. Active pricing adjustments successfully neutralized raw material cost imbalances sequentially.
13.
Shifting customer mix: Hexpol's light vehicle automotive exposure historically accounted for approximately thirty-eight percent of group volumes. European automotive markets remain challenging as Asian manufacturers expand their regional footprint. Management seeks to lower this cyclical exposure by growing into adjacent areas. Rapid expansion in the wire and cable segment helps diversify the company's customer base.
14.
Volatile macroeconomic backdrop: DNB Carnegie analysts expressed concern about high interest rates and weak consumer sentiment in major regions. Looming tariff uncertainties in North America threaten to suppress general industrial demand. Hexpol's flexible cost structure and localized footprint mitigate direct trade policy impacts. High manufacturing flexibility enables Hexpol to preserve resilient margins.
15.
Optimized business structure: Hexpol implemented organizational changes to align its corporate layout with growth ambitions. The group moved its Izmir and Vilafranca operations into the Thermoplastic Compounding business area in July 2026. The reorganization consolidated Hexpol's wire and cable activities following the Vipa Group transaction. Increased operational focus is expected to drive higher organic sales growth margins.
16.
Other side of the story: Aggressive acquisition pacing and rapid segment restructuring could backfire if integration bottlenecks occur in Turkey or Italy. Deepening industrial recessions or prolonged European automotive downturns might offset the organic growth momentum. Rising overhead expenses and digital investments could persistently pressure adjusted operating margins. Elmin's expansion plan might ultimately disappoint investors expecting rapid earnings expansion.
