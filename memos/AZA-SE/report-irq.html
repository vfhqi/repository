What the call should settle, and why

The case in one line: a 40%-return-on-equity franchise whose asset engine is at its strongest since 2021 but whose earnings conversion has reset lower, so that 2027 is a squeeze year (revenue +8%, costs +13%) before a rate lever, a loosening leverage ratio and Denmark turn the skew positive from the spring of 2027. Six judgements decide that.

  • Judgement 1, how much of the yield compression is structural. Brokerage yield fell to 10.3 basis points (from 11.0) as the Private Banking and Pro share of trading rose to 30% (from 26%); the fund margin to 22.2 (from 22.9); income per customer -6% in a quarter to SEK 2,170. Management called it temporary market sentiment. If it is cyclical and the revenue margin holds near 43.3 basis points, FY2027 EPS is about SEK 21.2 (+4.8%); if the drift runs at the 2023 to 2025 pace of 2 basis points a year, about SEK 19.4 (-4.2%).
  • Judgement 2, trading velocity. Brokerage plus currency is SEK 2,160m in the 2027 base, 39% of revenue; transactions fell 8% quarter on quarter in the third quarter and Deutsche Bank's tracker had September trades -10% year on year. A return of first-half 2026 velocity is +SEK 290m of revenue, +SEK 1.5 of EPS (+7.5%); a 2022-type year is EPS about SEK 16.2 (-20%) and a share price near SEK 300.
  • Judgement 3, net interest income after the November hike. The base carries one 25 basis point hike at 60% to 70% pass-through (about SEK 160m a year); Avanza's own cumulative deposit beta in the 2022 to 2023 hikes was 36%; deposits fell to 8.9% of savings capital. A lower pass-through, or a second hike by February 2027, is +SEK 100m (+2.7% of EPS) or more; a full point of cuts later is -SEK 420m (-11%).
  • Judgement 4, the 2027 cost guide on 22-Jan-2027. Our central case is 7% to 8% for Sweden (8.5% the negative trigger) plus a Danish bill stepping from SEK 40m to about SEK 130m; consensus costs for 2027 are about SEK 1,748m against our SEK 1,780m. Each point of Swedish cost growth is SEK 16m (SEK 0.09 of EPS, 0.4%); the signal value is larger than the money, because a guide above 8.5% would support the platform-capacity reading.
  • Judgement 5, price at the affluent tier. SAVR and Montrose undercut on currency fees; Nordnet ran a commission-free campaign in Sweden from September 2026; Interactive Brokers launched Swedish ISK accounts. If the affluent tier's half-price currency conversion is the price of keeping contestable customers, the drift is not bounded and the memo's down-trigger (a first-quarter 2027 brokerage yield below 10 basis points) is hit; a move on the 25 basis point standard currency fee is SEK 100m to 150m a year.
  • Judgement 6, capital. The leverage ratio rose from 4.2% (March) to 4.8% (September) against a 3.5% requirement; new mortgages for one customer tier are paused; a 10% buyback authorisation is renewed but unused; payout 75%. A first buyback or a payout above 78% is a 2028 option the estimates do not carry; each turn of the two-year-forward multiple is SEK 23.3 of share price (6.5%).

Range A: scope for guidance and sell-side upgrades or cuts over the next four quarters (the fourth quarter of 2026 to the third quarter of 2027)

- Tell: "on track for the guide, no unusual items" = our base, and the fifth year of guides met or beaten; "some catch-up in the fourth quarter" = a cost miss on top of the revenue gap; "below the guide" = the record of beats holds and the margin gap to consensus narrows.

A2 ★ How the 2027 cost guide is built. "When you give the 2027 cost guidance on 22 January, will it again be a Swedish growth rate plus a separate Danish amount, and is it right to read the 8% average to 2030 as stepping down from 9% in 2026 toward about 5% in 2030, so that 2027 sits nearer 8%?"

A3 ★ Brokerage yield and mix. "Gross brokerage income fell to 10.3 basis points of turnover from 11.0, with Private Banking and Pro customers' share of trading up to 30% from 26%. - Tell: "all mix, yield per customer group unchanged" = the memo's structural-mix base, bounded; "lower prices at some tiers" = the price-taking reading, not bounded; "volatility and trade sizes, it reverses" = the cyclical branch, +4.8% of FY2027 EPS.

A4 ★ Income per customer. "Annualised income per customer fell 6% in the quarter to SEK 2,170. When you call the yield decline temporary market sentiment, what would you point us to in the monthly statistics that would show it reversing?"

A5 ★ Deposits after the November rate rise. "After the Riksbank's rise to 2.00% in November, how much of it have you passed on to savings account customers, and how should we think about the pass-through compared with the 2022 to 2023 rises?"

Range B: the key risks, optionality and drivers of the case

B1. Pricing and competition

B1.1 ★ The Nordnet campaign and Interactive Brokers. "Nordnet's anniversary campaign offers new clients commission-free Nordic trading up to SEK 10 million of turnover until June 2027, and Interactive Brokers has launched Swedish ISK accounts. Have you seen any effect on net new customers or outflows in October and November?"
- Why: customer growth is 8% against Nordnet's 12%; the 16-Oct-2026 earnings summary's first reading, that the duopoly had started using price, was dropped on 29-Oct-2026 because Nordnet's margin rose; this checks it. September had 19,200 net new customers against the memo's confirm level of 15,000 a month.
- Tell: "no measurable effect" = the duopoly is margin-first; "some customers trying both" = the growth gap widens; any response on price = Judgement 5 in the bear direction.

B1.2 ★ Currency fees and the affluent tier. Or save for management. "SAVR and Montrose compete on currency fees. Do you see the half-price currency rate for Private Banking and Pro customers as something you chose, or as the price of keeping those customers, and would you consider moving the 25 basis point standard currency fee?"
- Why: a move on the standard fee is SEK 100m to 150m a year (2.5% to 4% of FY2027 EPS); the affluent customers are 30% of trading. Management has itself said its brokerage pricing may not align with its simplicity goals.
- Tell: "a design choice to win wallet share" = the memo's bounded mix; "we watch competitors closely" = the price-taking reading gains; "no change planned" = the step-change stays a tail risk.

B1.3 Nordnet in Germany and in Denmark. "Nordnet launches in Germany in November and is the incumbent in Denmark. What have you learned from its experience of pricing across four markets that shapes how you will price in Denmark?"

- Tell: "we will compete on product, not price" = bounded; "we will be cheaper" = the Danish price war is live.

B2. Costs, the platform and cloud

B2.2 Cloud migration. "The cloud move is about SEK 300 million to 2030, expensed. How is it phased by year, and does it sit inside the 8% framework or on top?"

B7. Regulation and tax

B7.1 ★ Swedish tax after the election. "The post-election debate has raised a bank tax, a redesign of the ISK and the deductibility of endowment insurance. Which of those do you see as live, and could the risk tax on credit institutions ever apply to Avanza, given its threshold?"
