Peak margin structural outlier: Keller's operating margin of 7.1% in 2025 represents a peak not seen since pre-GFC levels, yet Deutsche Bank highlights that margins have historically been volatile over the long-term, creating severe inconsistency with sustaining the core YES bullish thesis [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 6, Section 13]
2.
Extreme order book visibility gap: Keller's order book provides only six months of cover, creating pressure to constantly replenish bidding, which is diagnostic because it fails to protect against sudden funding freezes and represents the shortest backlog duration relative to construction peers with multi-year visibility [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 5, Section 6, Section 13]
3.
Translation exposure concentration outlier: Nearly 95% of revenues are generated in non-sterling currencies, dominated by the US dollar representing 59% of revenue, while peer comparisons reveal Morgan Sindall carries minimal translation risk and Costain reports zero translation risk since all corporate revenues originate directly within the United Kingdom, making Keller's unhedged translation profile an extreme outlier among UK-listed construction peers [Cross-ref: Section 15, Section 17, Section 21, Section 23]
