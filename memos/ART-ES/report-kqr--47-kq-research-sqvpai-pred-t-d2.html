Arteche (ART-ES). Question: Arteche Lantegi Elkartea, S.A. (ART-ES; Spanish main market since 2-Feb-2026), Spanish maker of electricity-grid equipment in three segments: Measurement and Monitoring (high- and medium-voltage instrument transformers, sensors and optical current transformers; about 72% of FY2025 revenue), Transmission and Distribution Grid Automation (auxiliary and protection relays and substation automation, including SEG Electronics, acquired in June 2026; about 17%) and Network Reliability (power quality, capacitor banks, reclosers and harmonic filters; about 11%), with plants in Spain, Mexico, Brazil, China, Turkey, Indonesia and Germany.

1.
Key question: how concentrated or diffuse are the drivers that separate the next 18 months to four years' financials from the last four to six years, and how wide and how skewed is the range of plausible outcomes over the next 18 months? The drivers and forecast differences to test: (1) Which few drivers will most separate Arteche's FY2026 to FY2030 financials from FY2019 to FY2025: the capacity ramp and backlog conversion (backlog \u20ac417.2m at 30-Jun-2026 against FY2025 revenue of \u20ac508.4m); the price contribution; the direct margin; the structural-cost ratio; depreciation from capital spending of about \u20ac40m in FY2026 (management, H1 2026 call) against about \u20ac25m in FY2025; the tax rate (26.3% in FY2024, 14.2% in FY2025 and about 16% in H1 2026, against Bizkaia's 24% nominal rate); net financial items (FactSet net interest of \u20ac9.1m, \u20ac9.0m and \u20ac9.4m in FY2023 to FY2025); minority interests (Hitachi Energy's 49% of AGIT; Elewit's 45.85% of Arin); working capital (FactSet net working capital 17.8% of last-twelve-month revenue at end-2025 and 20.9% at 30-Jun-2026) and reverse factoring (\u20ac25.2m to \u20ac33.8m in H1 2026)? (2) How volatile have Arteche's results been from half to half (second-half against first-half margins since 2023; Latin American recloser frame-contract timing; currency translation, with about 49% of revenue outside the euro and North American revenue up 5.9% against North American orders up 22.6% in H1 2026)? (3) What is the historical dispersion of 12-to-18-month outcomes against forecasts for European grid-equipment makers of similar size, and how often did forecasts that rested on one or two drivers (price, a capacity ramp, a single acquisition) hold? (4) Which events widened or narrowed the range of outcomes in past cycles (input-cost spikes, destocking, tariff changes, plant ramp problems, project deferrals), and how quickly did each act on margins? (5) What does peer evidence say about the sensitivity of a business with Arteche's cost structure (purchases about 50% of revenue, direct personnel about 8.5%, structural costs about 22% to 24%) to changes in volume and price?.
