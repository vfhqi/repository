Extreme asset ownership: STEF maintains a highly asset-heavy model, owning its real estate assets and a fleet of nearly 4,000 vehicles.
2.
Structural peer contrast: Asset-light logistics giants like DSV minimize capital lockup by extensively subcontracting their transport and warehousing requirements.
3.
Massive property division: STEF operates its own dedicated property development division called ImmoSTEF to build and maintain warehouses.
4.
Unique labor internalization: STEF is executing a complete internalization of production staff in Italy, targeting full insourcing by 2028.
5.
Cooperative model shift: This strategy represents a major departure from the standard Italian practice of subcontracting logistics labor to independent cooperatives.
6.
Internal control shield: Insourcing aims to permanently eliminate subcontractor-related tax compliance and VAT risks that triggered historical audit penalties.
7.
Exceptional capital lockup: STEF recorded unprecedented capital investments of €371.50m in 2025, representing an exceptionally capital-intensive profile for its peer group.
8.
Diluted cash generation: High capital expenditure drove group free cash flow to negative €66.70m, contrasting with peers.
9.
Peer cash contrast: Kuehne+Nagel maintains capital expenditure at 1% of sales, generating strong positive free cash flows organically.
