[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. Tecan is a self-help margin recovery under new hands, a 'Major Corporate Change' in a laboratory-automation business that lost a quarter of its revenue and 440 basis points of adjusted EBITDA margin between 2022 and 2025. The change is real, funded and already producing savings. At 211.6 CHF the shares have paid for it in advance, and for a demand recovery the Q2 2026 order book did not show, so the 'Modal return' at a fair multiple is about minus 2 percent a year over eighteen months and about 3 percent a year over three years against a 20 percent bar.

  • The change is internal and broad, and it passes the framework's screen twice over.
    • A chief executive from Thermo Fisher since 1 August 2025, a finance director since 1 June 2026, a new chairman and three new directors since April 2026, and a resourced three-year programme, 'Rewired', with dated targets (CHF 1bn of sales and a 20 percent adjusted EBITDA margin by 2028).
    • The programme has booked CHF 18.0m of structural savings in H1 2026, closed the Boston design site, sold the California machining unit, exited the Genomics activities and launched a CHF 120m buyback, all inside about twelve months.
    • The false-positive checks are clean: no caretaker chief executive, no 'PowerPoint transformation', no crown jewels sold, no dangerous leverage.
  • The external forces net to neutral, so the case cannot lean on demand.
    • Biopharma capital demand grew high-single-digit in local currencies in H1 2026 and China returned to growth, while academic and government demand fell double-digit, the largest customer (about 17 percent of group sales) is guided slightly down for FY2026, the franc costs about 60 basis points of margin a year at budget rates, and competition is tightening at both ends of the price range.
    • The company's own cycle has turned: order entry grew 8.6 percent in H2 2025 and 6.7 percent in Q1 2026 in local currencies, then went flat in Q2 2026.
      • H1 2026 sales grew 3.4 percent in local currencies with a book-to-bill of 1.04x.
  • The change has reached the key operating metrics and not yet the reported financial statements.
    • The adjusted EBITDA margin rose 180 basis points underlying in H1 2026 to 15.1 percent, guided to the upper end of 15.5 to 16.5 percent for FY2026.
    • Against that, the gross margin fell 230 basis points to 33.9 percent, operating cash flow fell 71.7 percent to CHF 17.0m, and adjusted EPS fell 1.5 percent.
    • The sell-side's estimates have only just begun to move: FY2027 and FY2028 consensus EPS rose 5 to 7 percent over six months to 2026-09-18 after falling 9 to 14 percent over twelve.
  • What we think it earns.
    • Adjusted EPS on the brokers' basis from CHF 6.40 in FY2026 to CHF 9.40 in FY2029 on our path, 13.7 percent a year, against consensus of CHF 10.57 for FY2029
    • we sit 6 to 11 percent below consensus because Q2 orders were flat and two thirds of the EBITDA bridge is cost-out.
    • Of the 13.7 points a year, 5.6 come from cost-led margin, 3.2 from the ratio below EBITDA, 0.8 from buybacks and only 3.4 from revenue, which is the least coveted mix the framework describes.
    • A plausibly worse drop-through (a 22 percent tax rate, CHF 4m a year more depreciation, no further buyback) takes FY2029 EPS to about CHF 8.68, 8 to 9 percent below our path.
  • What it costs. 24.2 times consensus 24-month-forward EPS of 8.7416 CHF on 2026-09-21, the 98th percentile of the stock's own two-year range, a third above the dashboard peers' median of 17.65x, and above four of five brokers' targets (mean 188.60 CHF, median 179.00 CHF).
    • The shares rose 88.5 percent in six months.
    • The multiple moved from a two-year median of 19.0x to 24x with the life-science tools sector's re-rating, not with Tecan's own outputs, so it is less ours to earn and it can fall with a peer print.
  • The return does not clear the bar on any path we can defend.
    • At a fair 21x on our path, minus 3.5 percent in total over eighteen months and plus 9.0 percent over three years, about minus 2.3 percent and plus 2.9 percent a year.
    • At today's 24.2x held, about 7 percent a year at either horizon.
    • Only the consensus path with the multiple held reaches about 17 percent a year, a C, and that outcome needs consensus right on both revenue and margin and the multiple holding at the top of its two-year range, about 25 percent together.
    • The same path clears the framework's bar below about 155 CHF on the eighteen-month test and about 135 CHF on the three-year test
      • we use about 150 CHF as the round level.
  • The pattern is 'Major Corporate Change', crisp on its input side and early on its output side.
    • The mechanism is the cost base under new hands inside a business whose regulated installed base and 64.4 percent recurring share of Life Sciences sales hold revenue steady while CHF 25m to 35m of cost comes out.
    • The price has moved as if for a 'Trough on Trough' at a re-priced multiple, which is the case's problem rather than its pattern.
  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.
    • It holds from the people to the choices to the actions to the key operating metrics.
    • It breaks at the reported financial statements, where gross margin, cash and EPS still fell in H1 2026, and it breaks at the price, which assumes the FY2028 consensus (CHF 982m of revenue, a 19.2 percent margin, EPS CHF 9.10) at the stock's five-year median multiple.

  • The case is therefore a good change at the wrong price.

    • The change is material
    • the return at this price is not, so we park and wait for the price rather than for the event.

  • Rating = D, because the 'Investment Thesis' measures materiality through to the return, and the return leg binds: the change forces are a B, the outputs a C and the 'Modal return' an F, and a material change that the price has already paid for twice over is not a material thesis.

    • Not C, because a C would need the modal return within sight of the framework's 13 to 17 percent band, and even at a held 24.2x multiple our path gives about 7 percent a year.
    • Not F, because the change forces are real, funded and already producing savings, and below about 150 CHF the same findings would rate the Building Block a B.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The strongest case against our D is the bull case at its strongest: flat Q2 2026 orders were phasing (delayed Cavro shipments, biopharma budget timing, an easy Q1 comparison at the largest customer), H2 2026 and 2027 orders re-accelerate to mid single digits, 'Rewired' captures the full CHF 60m EBITDA gap to the 2028 target, consensus is forced up twice, the multiple holds at 24 to 26x and the shares deliver the 40 percent-plus return the bull case names.

  • The evidence for it: order entry has been positive in local currencies for three consecutive periods with a book-to-bill above one

    • the H1 2026 margin beat consensus by 80 basis points on savings that are itemised and structural
    • management guides to the upper end and has cleared the FDA at Penang
    • UBS has FY2028 EPS at CHF 11.11 and a 222 CHF target
    • the tariff drag has halved and a refund of about CHF 6m is expected in H2 2026
    • Waters, Agilent and Sartorius have all beaten and raised.
  • What we concede: the cost-out is credible and half of the FY2026 bridge is banked
    • the balance sheet is net cash, the operator is credible and the recurring base is real
    • and on the consensus path with the multiple held the eighteen-month return is about 17 percent a year, a C and within reach of the bar.
  • Why we hold our view: the outcome that clears the bar today needs consensus right on both revenue and margin (possible, about 35 percent) and the multiple held given that (about 70 percent), about 25 percent together, which is unlikely.
    • The last print points the other way on the demand leg: Q2 orders flat, the largest customer guided down, academia falling double-digit, United States organic growth about 1 percent.
    • The shares stand above four of five brokers' targets, so the upgrades the bull needs must come from a sell-side that sees 10 to 20 percent downside.
  • Where the case against would win: a Q3 2026 trading update with order growth of 5 percent or more in local currencies, followed by FY2027 margin guidance of 17.5 percent or more.
    • That is the cleared branch, and on it the fair anchor moves to 24x and the case clears the bar up to about 175 CHF on our path.
    • The framework's answer to a case that is compelling only once the event has happened is to wait for the price, not the event.
