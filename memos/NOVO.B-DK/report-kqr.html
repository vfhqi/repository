The inputs that move the 2027-2029 EPS bridge most (US net price by channel, the pace of international generic erosion, and the depreciation path) are contested across sources, and the evidence contains no explicit 2027-2029 broker or consensus EPS figures. Overall confidence in what the evidence shows about future EPS is medium, owing to high commercial pricing volatility. US net price is the largest swing factor. Novo Nordisk said Ozempic gross-to-net pricing is eroding by 10% to 15% annually. The Medicare Bridge raises prescription volume but dilutes the blended net price per prescription. Jefferies says Eli Lilly captured about 70% of senior initiations. North Carolina and California, about 50% of 2025 Medicaid Wegovy volume, stopped offering obesity coverage. The Wegovy pill reached about 3.6 million prescriptions and DKK 4,178 million of US sales in Q3 2026. That makes it the main volume offset.

International growth is real but exposed to generic erosion. The pill has launched in eight markets, with more than 20 planned by mid-2027. International Operations grew 10% in Danish kroner and 8% at constant exchange rates in Q3 2026. Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Answering the key question is essential because these specific operational and financial inputs directly determine Novo Nordisk's long-term valuation, competitive positioning, and earnings per share trajectory through the upcoming semaglutide patent cliff.

Defining the Key Question

1.
Core inquiry: "Novo Nordisk A/S, NOVO.B-DK (Nasdaq Copenhagen), a Danish global healthcare company with two segments: Obesity and Diabetes Care (semaglutide medicines Wegovy injection, Wegovy pill, Ozempic and Rybelsus, plus insulins and pipeline assets such as CagriSema and zenagamtide) and Rare Disease (haemophilia, growth hormone and other rare blood and endocrine disorders). Key question: what does the evidence say about the specific inputs that drive this company's earnings per share over the next 18 months to four years, namely: United States net sales by channel (commercial, Medicare including the GLP-1 Bridge, Medicaid, cash and self-pay) for the Wegovy injection, the Wegovy pill and Ozempic, as prescriptions and net price per prescription; International Operations growth from the pill rollout to more than 20 markets by mid-2027 (company, November 2026) against generic erosion in Canada, Brazil, India and China and insulin decline; gross margin of the pill against the injection, against the company's target of about 80% through 2030 (Capital Markets Day, September 2026); the depreciation and amortisation path after the capacity build and the risk of further impairments; research and selling cost ratios; net financial items, including interest and currency hedging (the company reported a net currency hedging gain of DKK 6,007 million in 2025 and a deferred hedging gain of DKK 4,339 million at end-2025, with about 12 months of dollar cash flows hedged); the tax rate (company guidance 21% to 23% for 2026); the share count under the DKK 15 billion 2026 buyback and future buyback capacity as capex falls; which items the adjusted measures will exclude (impairments, 340B provision changes, restructuring) and whether the 2025 adjusted base includes the 2025 restructuring charge; currency sensitivity (company: a 5% dollar move changes adjusted operating profit by about DKK 5.1 billion)?"

Valuation Trajectory and Competitive Risks

2.

The broker projected that the company's valuation of 11x 2027 estimated price-to-earnings is constrained by the looming semaglutide loss of exclusivity in 2031-2032. This uncertainty means that defining net sales by channel is critical to projecting whether the mid-term earnings trajectory can sustain historic valuation premia.
3.
Downward pressure: Deutsche Bank reported on November 23, 2026, that semaglutide products are expected to face intense pricing competition like complex generics rather than standard biosimilars. The broker noted that commercial channels are experiencing significant pricing erosion of 10% to 15% annually, which directly impacts gross-to-net margins. If these price declines accelerate, they could severely compress the gross profit and limit future capacity for capital returns through dividends.
4.
Disciplined allocation: Novo Nordisk stated during its September 2026 Capital Markets Day that it aims to maintain a broadly stable operating margin through 2030 by offsetting pricing headwinds with yield and productivity improvements. The company's corporate presentation showed that capital expenditures are expected to decline to around DKK 50 billion in 2026 from DKK 60 billion in 2025. This decline in capital intensity is designed to expand free cash flow and maintain an attractive dividend per share over the next four years.

Section 2 – United States Sales Dynamics and Channel Pricing

Section summary
  1. United States commercial channels face steep annual net price erosion of ten to fifteen percent for Ozempic and up to thirty-five percent for Wegovy.
  2. The rapid expansion of the discounted Medicare Bridge program drives significant prescription volume growth but dilutes the blended net price per prescription.
  3. Peer disclosures and expert insights suggest that growing price competition and pharmacy benefit manager negotiations will compress long-term operating margins.

Main Commercial and Ozempic Pricing

1.
Accelerated expansion: Novo Nordisk reported on November 4, 2026, that the Wegovy franchise maintains its United States leadership in new patient starts with more than 50% market share. This analysis evaluates 14 relevant sources, 3 companies, and 2 comparable past cases. The company disclosed that total weekly prescriptions for Wegovy injectable reached 4.1 million, with 35% filled through self-pay channels. This volume growth is crucial because it offsets intensifying pricing competition and channel shifts that threaten longterm average net prices.
2.
Steady compression: Novo Nordisk stated during its third-quarter 2026 earnings call on November 4, 2026, that Ozempic gross-to-net pricing is experiencing annual erosion of 10% to 15%. Payer pressure on net prices in the type 2 diabetes category remains a persistent headwind that management expects to continue. This pressure means that Ozempic net sales will increasingly depend on volume expansion to mitigate the ongoing price decline in commercial segments.
3.
Pronounced erosion: Morgan Stanley reported on November 9, 2026, that Wegovy subcutaneous net prices in the United States are projected to decline by 20% in 2027. The broker attributed this step-down to a 15% year-overyear price cut in commercial channels combined with a negative shift toward lower-priced Medicare and cash channels. This pricing pressure is expected to restrict absolute revenue growth for the injectable franchise.
4.
Strong launch: Novo Nordisk reported on November 4, 2026, that its Wegovy pill achieved around 3.6 million prescriptions in the third quarter of 2026 since its January launch. The tablet contributed DKK 4,178 million in United States sales during the quarter, supported by commercial formulary access that is on par with the injectable formulation. This suggests that the oral segment represents a highly effective volume-driven growth lever for the franchise.

Medicare Bridge and Medicaid Channels

5.
Rapid adoption: Novo Nordisk disclosed on November 4, 2026, that the Medicare Part D Bridge program, launched on July 1, 2026, has driven encouraging early uptake. Eligible seniors can access selected obesity medications for a co-pay of USD 50 per month, expanding the covered patient base. This channel shift increases overall prescription volume but dilutes the blended average net price per prescription.
6.
Competitor dominance: Jefferies reported on November 20, 2026, that the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge program has heavily favored Eli Lilly, which captured approximately 70% of senior initiations. The broker estimated that more than 1.1 million beneficiaries had signed up for the program by October 7, 2026, driving a substantial revenue opportunity for Eli Lilly. This competitive dynamic indicates that Novo Nordisk faces stiff headwinds in capturing senior market share compared to its primary rival.
7.
Restrictive access: DNB Carnegie reported on February 4, 2026, that Medicaid volumes for Wegovy are expected to decline due to coverage reductions in key states. The broker highlighted that North Carolina and California, which accounted for approximately 50% of 2025 Medicaid Wegovy volume, decided to stop offering obesity coverage. These state-level budget decisions are expected to reduce Medicaid segment contribution to total sales.
8.
Operational precedent: Morgan Stanley reported on October 30, 2026, that Sanofi experienced a significant third- quarter sales beat that was driven by positive gross-to-net adjustments across its US portfolio, including Dupixent and Lantus. This historical case demonstrates how quarterly earnings can be distorted by one-off rebate and discount estimate changes. This precedent highlights the unusual volatility of gross-to-net accounting in United States channels.

Formulary Battles and PBM Dynamics

9.
Intense negotiations: Expert call transcripts with former managed care executives on November 2, 2026, indicate that pharmacy benefit managers are aggressively using formulary exclusions to force steep price cuts. The experts noted that average GLP-1 costs of USD 800 to USD 900 per month are unsustainable for employers, who require net costs to fall below USD 250 by 2028. This trend indicates that Novo Nordisk will face increasing pressure to offer deep discounts to maintain commercial formulary placement.
10.
Accounting precision: Rothschild and Co Redburn reported on November 4, 2026, that Sanofi has deployed artificial intelligence tools to more accurately record gross-to-net rebate provisions and prevent unexpected quarterly adjustments. This operational shift followed years of volatile gross-to-net pricing dynamics on major brands like Dupixent. This precedent suggests that adopting advanced accounting systems is critical to stabilize highly complex US drug revenues amidst rapid channel shifts.
11.
Dynamic access: Morgan Stanley reported on November 20, 2026, that CVS Caremark added other weight-loss medications to its commercial template formularies effective October 1, 2026, after previously favoring Wegovy. This decision reversed a prior preferred status that disrupted Zepbound volumes in the commercial segment. This shift shows that payer coverage decisions remain highly fluid, which introduces substantial quarterly sales volatility for both competitors.

Cash Pay and Direct Channels

12.
Price adjustments: Novo Nordisk reported on November 4, 2026, that its self-pay prices for the Wegovy pill range from USD 149 to USD 299 per month depending on the dose. The company also plans to lower the United States list price of Wegovy and Ozempic to USD 675 effective January 1, 2027. This aggressive pricing strategy is designed to attract uninsured and cash-pay patients away from compounded alternatives.
13.
Direct competition: Eli Lilly reported on October 29, 2026, that US Zepbound sales increased 48% to USD 5.3 billion, partially offset by lower realized prices from cash-pay reductions. The company has cut self-pay pricing for Zepbound vials to expand access through its LillyDirect platform. This peer action shows that price competition is intensifying in the consumer-driven cash segment.
14.
Contrary evidence: DNB Carnegie reported on October 14, 2026, that Novo Nordisk's massive global commercial scale and pipeline differentiation will protect its market share. The broker argued that supply capacity expansions will allow Novo to satisfy massive underlying global demand and mitigate near-term pricing headwinds in the United States. This suggests that US commercial price erosion may have a smaller impact on group earnings than bearish sell-side models assume.
15.
Other side of the story: Deutsche Bank reported on November 23, 2026, that semaglutide products will compete more like complex chemical generics rather than standard biosimilars, leading to severe and rapid price erosion. The broker estimated that weekly total prescriptions for injectable Wegovy grew 18% while Ozempic fell 3%, proving that volume growth is increasingly concentrated in lower-margin segments. Therefore, the rapid shift toward discounted Medicare Bridge and cash-pay channels, combined with intensifying commercial rebate pressure, is highly likely to compress the long-term margin profile of Novo Nordisk's flagship franchise.

Section 3 – International Operations Growth and Generic Erosion

Section summary
  1. Novo Nordisk's International Operations growth is driven by the rollout of the oral Wegovy pill to over 20 markets by mid-2027.
  2. Generic erosion of semaglutide in early loss-of-exclusivity markets like Canada, Brazil, and India creates immediate pricing headwinds.
  3. Declining older insulin volumes will compress diabetes franchise margins unless next-generation weekly formulations are launched successfully.

Oral Wegovy Pill Rollout

1.
Rapid international expansion: Novo Nordisk announced in its Q3 2026 interim report on November 4, 2026, that it has successfully launched the oral Wegovy semaglutide pill in eight global markets. The company plans to roll out this oral weight-loss treatment to more than 20 countries by mid-2027 to capture expanding international market share. This aggressive geographical expansion is designed to drive volume growth and support the company's long-term earnings per share trajectory.
2.
Strong early momentum: Novo Nordisk reported on November 4, 2026, that early launches of the oral Wegovy pill in the United Kingdom and Germany achieved 90% and 80% shares of new-to-brand prescriptions, respectively. The company expanded its geographic reach in the third quarter of 2026 by launching the pill in Mexico, France, Spain, and Poland. These high market shares indicate that oral obesity medications are expanding the total addressable patient pool internationally.
3.
Favorable competitive comparison: Rothschild and Co Redburn reported on November 5, 2026, that the Wegovy pill is significantly outperforming Eli Lilly's oral competitor Foundayo due to superior efficacy and easier concomitant dosing with statins. While Eli Lilly reported sales misses for Foundayo in 2026, Novo Nordisk has achieved rapid international volume growth. This commercial lead suggests that the oral formulation will remain a primary growth engine for Novo Nordisk's global obesity franchise.
4.
Expanding regional sales: Novo Nordisk reported on November 4, 2026, that its International Operations segment delivered 10% sales growth in Danish kroner and 8% growth at constant exchange rates during the third quarter of 2026. Growth was heavily concentrated in the Europe and Canada region, which expanded by 18% at constant exchange rates due to strong Wegovy uptake. This robust regional performance demonstrates that international expansion can help offset slowing demand in other geographic areas.

Generic Erosion in Key Markets

5.
Accelerating generic competition: Apotex Health reported on November 12, 2026, that its generic Apo- Semaglutide injection reached a 21% share of the total Canadian semaglutide market since its first-quarter launch. Sandoz also secured Health Canada marketing authorization for its generic semaglutide version of Ozempic on September 18, 2026, to expand patient access to affordable GLP-1 therapies. This influx of generic alternatives is eroding Novo Nordisk's realized prices and compressing net margins in Canada.
6.
Rapid generic erosion: Sandoz announced on November 9, 2026, the commercial launch of its generic semaglutide injection Owozy in Brazil, which represents a USD 2.7 billion GLP-1 market. Dr. Reddy's Laboratories also secured Brazilian regulatory approval for its generic semaglutide injection on November 5, 2026, to address the country's significant diabetes burden. These launches mean Novo Nordisk faces immediate volume and price competition in Latin America's largest healthcare market.
7.
Intense price pressure: Eris Lifesciences reported in its 2026 annual report that it established a leading volume position in India following the semaglutide loss of exclusivity in March 2026. Torrent Pharmaceuticals also maintained a dominant 90% share of India's generic oral semaglutide market in the second quarter of fiscal 2027. This domestic generic scale-up will severely limit Novo Nordisk's growth potential and pricing power within the Indian subcontinent.
8.
Impending patent expiration: Hangzhou Jiuyuan Genetic Biopharmaceutical reported on September 23, 2026, that its semaglutide biosimilar is advancing rapidly through clinical trials ahead of China's expected patent cliff. Novo Nordisk is attempting to mitigate this erosion by submitting its oral Wegovy pill for Chinese regulatory approval in the second half of 2026. Capturing the Chinese market is critical because regional sales fell 5% in the third quarter of 2026 due to pricing pressures.
9.
Contrary evidence: Morgan Stanley reported on November 9, 2026, that the negative impact of generic erosion in Canada was temporarily mitigated by device and active ingredient supply bottlenecks at rival firms. These industry-wide supply constraints allowed Novo Nordisk to maintain stable sales volumes in Canada despite the formal loss of patent exclusivity. This indicates that generic competition may erode branded sales at a slower rate than bears project.

Older Insulin Secular Decline

10.
Secular volume contraction: Novo Nordisk reported in its Q3 2026 interim report on November 4, 2026, that its human insulin sales volumes continue to decline as patients transition to more advanced therapies. This decline is highlighted by a 7% year-over-year drop in vulnerable patients treated with Novo products due to lower public tender volumes. The transition away from older insulins is expected to compress margins unless next-generation insulin products can be launched quickly.
11.
Strategic product substitution: Novo Nordisk announced on November 6, 2026, that it plans to launch its next- generation Awiqli and Kyinsu insulins in more than 40 markets by the end of 2027. This proactive upgrade strategy is designed to stabilize the declining diabetes franchise by replacing low-margin older insulins with high-value weekly formulations. Successfully executing these launches is critical to maintaining Novo Nordisk's global volume leadership across the diabetes care market.

Loss of Exclusivity Precedent

12.
Historical erosion precedent: Novartis serves as a critical historical precedent showing that innovator companies can lose up to 96% of their market share within a year of generic entry. IIFL Research reported on July 16, 2026, that Novartis lost nearly its entire US market share for sacubitril-valsartan following the entry of low-cost generic competitors. This precedent suggests that Novo Nordisk's established semaglutide franchise faces rapid decay curves in international markets once patent protections expire.
13.
Ambitious revenue replacement: Morgan Stanley reported on November 19, 2026, that Novartis is targeting a 5% top-line compound annual growth rate through 2030 despite looming patent cliffs on key drugs. Novartis intends to offset these losses by accelerating new product launches and executing strategic, bolt-on acquisitions to bolster its clinical pipeline. This strategic path indicates that Novo Nordisk must continuously launch innovative pipeline assets to sustain long-term earnings growth after semaglutide genericization.
14.
Other side of the story: Deutsche Bank reported on November 16, 2026, that Novo Nordisk's international growth will be severely pressured by compound patent expiries in major regions like China. The rapid erosion of realized prices in early loss-of-exclusivity markets will create an immediate DKK 3 billion headwind that oral rollouts cannot fully absorb. Therefore, the combination of international generic erosion and declining insulin sales is highly likely to constrain growth over the next four years.

Unit Economics of Semaglutide Formulations

1.
Significant cost disparity: Morgan Stanley reported on October 13, 2026, that the oral Wegovy tablet carries a highly demanding active ingredient profile. At the twenty-five milligram daily maintenance dose, the oral Wegovy pill requires seventy-three times more active pharmaceutical ingredient per patient than the weekly subcutaneous injection. This massive raw material requirement severely limits the global scalability of the oral formulation to select geographic regions. Consequently, the broker expects the oral product to face substantial margin pressures.
2.
Elevated manufacturing costs: Incred Capital reported on September 28, 2026, that pure semaglutide active ingredient costs approximately USD 39,867 per kilogram. This high raw material cost translates to an annual production cost of USD 186 to USD 380 per patient for the oral Wegovy tablet. By comparison, the standard subcutaneous injectable version costs merely USD 28 to USD 140 per patient annually. These figures demonstrate why expanding oral production dilutes the corporate gross margin relative to historical levels.
3.
Substantial scalability barriers: Former product executives at Novo Nordisk noted that peptide-based oral formulations face significant geographic scalability constraints due to capacity bottlenecks. Novo Nordisk announced that the oral Wegovy formulation requires substantially more active ingredient per patient due to lower bioavailability. This means the company must focus its initial available supply capacity on high-value regions. These operational constraints will restrict the geographic reach of the oral semaglutide rollout.
