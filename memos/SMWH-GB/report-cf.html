EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. WH Smith has inflected structurally toward pure-play travel, abandoning its legacy high street and sub-scale European footprints.
  2. Profitability recovery relies on North American cost remediation, UK airport one-stop expansion, and global franchise shift.
  3. Earnings remain under severe pressure with successive profit warnings culminating in a disappointing £75m PBT outlook.
  4. Sell-side sentiment is heavily cautious due to low turnaround visibility and historical accounting failures.
  5. Balance sheet deleveraging holds priority over shareholder returns, with dividend reinstatement unlikely for 18 months.
