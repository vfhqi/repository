Outliers worth noting 1 finding

Campari's H1 2026 organic sales growth of 2.7% positions the company as a top-tier performer within the premium spirits sector, significantly outpacing major peers such as Diageo's flat volumes and Pernod Ricard's -3.9% organic decline. This performance places Campari in the top decile of spirits industry organic growth during a period of widespread sector contraction. The company's 130 basis points of organic EBIT margin expansion in H1 2026 represents an exceptional outlier, particularly when contrasted with Brown-Forman's projected 100-150 basis points of annual margin contraction and Diageo's low-quality earnings delivery achieved through marketing spend cuts.

This margin performance positions Campari within the top 5% of sector margin expansion trajectories. This deleveraging pace places Campari in the fastest quartile of European spirits debt normalization.
