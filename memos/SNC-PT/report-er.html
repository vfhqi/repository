Section 16 – Conclusion

Sonaecom's consolidated net income surged by 71.2% year-on-year to reach €37.40 million in 1H26, compared to €21.80 million in 1H25, heavily underpinned by equity-accounted earnings from its telecom operations. This holding company has successfully completed its transition from a legacy operating telecom entity into a pureplay investment vehicle with three distinct pillars: a 37.37% strategic stake in Portuguese operator NOS, the Bright Pixel venture capital portfolio managing over 50 active technology startups, and the Público digital media franchise.

Sonaecom's overarching corporate ambition remains anchored in selective capital allocation rather than rapid organic expansion, with its net cash position expanding to €246.6 million in 1H26 from €205.8 million at the end of FY25.

The primary investment thesis for Sonaecom over an 18-month to 3-year holding period rests on the combination of structurally predictable upstream cash flows from NOS dividends and asymmetric upside optionality embedded in Bright Pixel's global venture portfolio. Going into the mid-2026 reporting cycle, the primary focus for Sonaecom's covering analysts was the dividend outlook of its core 37.37% owned asset, NOS, which serves as Sonaecom's principal upstream cash generator, with NOS's board subsequently proposing and paying a €0.35 per share ordinary dividend alongside a surprise €0.10 per share extraordinary dividend, which ultimately secured €86.6 million in cash proceeds for Sonaecom.

This extraordinary capital return validates the core thesis that NOS's declining capital expenditure trajectory following the completion of 5G network rollouts and fiber-to-the-home coverage will sustain robust free cash flow generation.
