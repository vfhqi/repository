Note for the Financial Analyst, no input changed: the gap between the second set's cross-check free cash flow (SEK 2,900m, 3,500m, 4,020m, 4,410m, 4,880m) and the model's (2,805m, 3,296m, 3,788m, 4,134m, 4,548m) is a definitional one. The model absorbs the working-capital balance change on total revenue, including the SEK 500 to 650m a year that arrives with acquired companies and is already paid for inside the acquisitions line, while the cross-check absorbs only the organic change and separately deducts about SEK 350m a year of other operating items the model does not carry; the net is the model understating free cash flow by about SEK 100 to 330m a year (3 to 7%) and overstating year-end net debt by a cumulative SEK 1 to 2bn by 2030. No input is wrong on its own definition, earnings per share are unaffected, and the model's year-end 2026 net debt (SEK 9.7bn on the original inputs) is close to the company's SEK 9.6bn at 30 September, so I leave the working-capital percentage unchanged rather than distort the balance-sheet ratio to correct a cash-flow classification.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

Revenue growth is built as organic growth plus acquired growth plus currency. Organic: H2 2026 +4.5% (Q3 +4% against a -1% base, Q4 +4.5 to 5% against a 0% base), 2027 +3.5% (Q1 +4% against 0%, then +3% as the comparisons harden from Q2 2027), 2028 to 2030 +3%, the decade norm; price is about 2 points of each year's organic growth.
