Pausing share buybacks means Teleperformance cannot rely on share count reduction to boost EPS near-term.

Section 4 – Contract Repricing, Single-Client Step Losses, and Macro Pressures

Section summary
  1. Operational repricing and annual contract renewals are accelerating deflationary pressures across the customer experience sector.
  2. Single-client step losses represent severe structural threats that can quickly wipe out operating leverage for service providers.
  3. Persistent wage inflation in key offshore hubs combined with volatile exchange rates squeeze margins for highly leveraged operators.
  4. Customer experience providers demonstrate variable guidance track records as macroeconomic headwinds and localized wage inflation compress recurring operating margins.
  5. Outcome-based commercial transitions decouple revenues from headcount through risk-sharing gainshare models and transaction-based pricing structures.
  6. Structural resets of mid-term targets are accelerating across the outsourcing landscape as generative artificial intelligence cannibalizes legacy headcount-based agreements.

Operational renewal and pricing realities

1.
Intense pricing: Customer experience providers are facing severe contract negotiations as clients leverage automated tools to drive down transaction costs. Enterprises are actively involving procurement teams earlier in negotiations to use AI displacement as leverage in renewals. This strategic procurement shift forces vendors to transition away from traditional labor-based pricing to outcome-based terms. Consequently, these pricing pressures threaten to permanently squeeze traditional service margins over the next eighteen months.
2.
Shorter contract: The duration of customer agreements in the outsourcing sector is increasingly moving toward annual renewals rather than multi-year commitments. Many customer contracts can now be terminated with or without cause by providing short notice and without incurring termination-related penalties. Former Vice President at Teleperformance confirmed that pricing has come under extreme pressure post-pandemic as clients seek immediate savings. This structural shift toward shorter terms increases revenue volatility and limits long-term organic growth visibility.
3.
Aggressive buyer: Enterprise procurement teams are adopting standardized frameworks to benchmark vendor performance and demand price concessions during contract renewals. Procurement strategies now include establishing strict pricing, usage, and exit protections during initial pilot programs rather than waiting for full production. This proactive stance limits the ability of outsourcers to charge premiums as AI agents scale. These aggressive negotiations will likely accelerate top-line deflation across the customer care landscape.
4.
Contrary evidence: The findings show that Teleperformance's underlying customer experience demand remains highly resilient despite broader contract renewal pressures. Bernstein Research reported on November 9, 2026, that the group like-for-like organic growth rate excluding the Trust and Safety drag was actually positive 2.0%. This positive core growth suggests that core customer experience demand persists even as specific transactional segments face severe deflationary headwinds.
