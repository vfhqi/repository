Scale-to-strategy transition – expanded European reach: Management framed the group's evolution as a shift from a multi-local holding company to an integrated international business model. By securing majority control of ProSiebenSat.1 and expanding its footprint to six countries, MFE highlighted a combined reach of 190 million out of 200 million people within its multi-country media portfolio. Centralized coordination model – capturing international budgets: Management heavily emphasized MFE Advertising's role in centrally coordinating cross-country campaign collection for multinational headquarters. Under this centralized go-to-market strategy, MFE targets €110 million in ad spend from international brands that lack local corporate HQs in target territories.
