Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Currency fluctuations significantly impact Elekta's financial trajectory due to structural mismatches between its Swedish krona reporting, US dollar revenues, and British pound costs.
  2. Understanding these currency transaction and translation effects is crucial for forecasting Elekta's earnings per share and gross margins over the next three fiscal years.

2.
Negative movement: Elekta faces substantial operational risks because its reporting currency is the Swedish krona while its primary revenues are in foreign currencies. When the Swedish krona strengthens against the US dollar and the euro, reported revenues and earnings automatically contract. Such translation headwinds directly threaten earnings per share expansion over the next three fiscal years because management cannot easily offset currency translation through pricing alone.
3.
Severe pressure: Elekta suffers from a structural currency mismatch because its revenues are heavily weighted in US dollars while its manufacturing cost base is heavily denominated in British pounds and euros. A weakening US dollar against these cost currencies causes immediate transactional gross margin compression. According to company disclosures on April 30, 2026, a general one percent weakening of the Swedish krona would impact operating profit by approximately SEK 18 M for the US dollar and minus SEK 27 M for the British pound.
4.
Delayed impact: Elekta manages its transaction exposures by utilizing derivative contracts to hedge forecasted net currency flows up to 24 months in advance. Hedging policies smooth short-term fluctuations, but they can also delay the realization of favorable exchange rate shifts or create unexpected hedging losses. Realized cash-flow hedges contributed a positive SEK 135 M during fiscal 2025/26, yet future quarters remain highly vulnerable as older, high-rate hedges roll off.

Other side of the story: Elekta continues to face major execution risks that could prevent it from achieving its medium-term targets of mid-single-digit sales growth and an adjusted EBIT margin of 14 to 16 percent.
