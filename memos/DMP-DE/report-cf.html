EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

1.
Net materiality signal on internal/external change: Dermapharm faces moderate internal transformation as management pivots Arkopharma from direct-to-consumer TV advertising to a pharmacist-centric B2B2C sales model while deliberately contracting low-margin parallel imports by 21.12% YoY to optimize profitability, though external regulatory and macro forces remain largely stable with German pharmaceutical market volumes growing 5.70% and only modest US tariff drags at Euromed offset by European expansion.
2.
Forward EPS driver identification and ranking: The forward earnings trajectory depends on six core drivers ranked by impact and plausibility: (1) axicorp parallel import margin optimization delivering +100 bps group EBITDA margin with HIGH plausibility as unit contribution margins already doubled from EUR 13 to EUR 27, (2) Mucos and F. Trenka M&A integration adding EUR 25-30m in Mucos sales with HIGH plausibility given existing logistics synergies via Montavit, (3) Branded Pharmaceuticals organic growth sustaining 5.70% revenue CAGR with HIGH plausibility backed by robust 40%+ segment margins, (4) Euromed non-US geographic expansion offsetting low seven-figure tariff drag with HIGH plausibility as European and Asian B2B demand accelerates, (5) Arkopharma B2B2C pharmacist pivot targeting low-single-digit EBITDA lift with MEDIUM plausibility pending multi-quarter operational verification despite 27% pilot sell-out gains, and (6) BioNTech vaccine contract expiry representing a visible EUR 10-15m annual sales cliff by mid-2027 with HIGH plausibility as the fixed-capacity reserve agreement expires.
3.
Recent earnings delivery summary: Q1 2026 results delivered a solid beat with revenues rising 1.12% YoY to EUR 305.80m (consensus +1.93%) and adjusted EBITDA expanding 7.50% YoY to EUR 87.40m (consensus +4.05%), driven by 11.72% organic growth in Branded Pharmaceuticals offsetting the planned 21.12% contraction in Parallel Imports, while reported EPS surged 35.48% YoY to EUR 0.84 as operating cash flow improved to EUR 59.80m from just EUR 4.20m in Q1 2025.

FY 2025 full-year trajectory: Full-year 2025 performance showed muted revenue decline of 1.34% to EUR 1,165m missing consensus by 1.52% due to deliberate parallel import product delistings, yet adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 120 bps to 27.90% as the group successfully compensated for phasing-out BioNTech vaccine revenues through 6.60% organic branded growth and margin-accretive portfolio pruning.
5.
Historical earnings trajectory characterization: The L3Y performance record demonstrates consistent adjusted EBITDA margin delivery near 27-28% with clear seasonal strength in Q1 and Q4 driven by Allergopharma contributions, while quarterly inflections reveal management successfully navigating the transition from high-margin vaccine manufacturing toward sustainable organic branded expansion despite Arkopharma turnaround delays.
6.
Management guidance credibility archetype: Dermapharm management exhibits a clear honest-tracker archetype rather than sandbagger or over-promiser, consistently delivering actual results within or slightly above conservative guidance corridors as evidenced by FY2025 adjusted EBITDA of EUR 324.80m landing within the EUR 322-332m guided range, FY2024 EBITDA beating the midpoint by 1.81%, and FY2023 EBITDA exceeding guidance by 1.70%, establishing a reliable execution track record.

Forward FY2026 guidance assessment: Management targets FY2026 consolidated revenue of EUR 1,182- 1,218m and adjusted EBITDA of EUR 331-341m, representing mid-single-digit topline growth and continued margin expansion anchored to Mucos/Trenka M&A contributions, ongoing axicorp optimization gains, and stable core branded momentum, with sensitivity analysis showing 50 bps EBIT margin shifts drive 3.28% EPS impacts while +1 pp revenue growth translates to +2.92% EPS movements.
8.
Consensus sell-side positioning: The active analyst pool remains cautiously constructive as lead Jefferies analyst Fabian Piasta maintains a Hold rating while upgrading the price target from EUR 34 to EUR 44 post Q1 2026, citing improved gross margin expectations yet expressing concern that the 14x EV/EBITDA multiple appears rich versus the 12x historical baseline and that the Arkopharma B2B2C turnaround remains operationally unproven over multiple quarters.
9.

  2. competitive intensity is low in selected therapeutic niche markets where high entry barriers discourage larger players.
  3. tech disruption is minimal but emerging in phytotherapy and cannabis markets with shifting distribution channels.
  4. regulatory pressure remains a key driver, with mandatory manufacturer rebates and price moratoria shaping German pricing.
  5. raw material supply relies on backward integration, with Euromed securing herbal extracts and controlled organic sourcing.
  6. inventory cycle adjustments are bottoming out, as French pharmacy de-stocking at Arkopharma transitions to recovery.
  7. macroeconomic headwinds are manageable, with US tariff volatility on Euromed offset by European growth.
