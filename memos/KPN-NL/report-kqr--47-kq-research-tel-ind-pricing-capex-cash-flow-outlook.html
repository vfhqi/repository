Deutsche Telekom AG: Operational Performance and Financial Outlook Price Increases & Inflation Contracts: In Germany, Deutsche Telekom (DT) is leveraging its position as the quality and market leader to implement front- and back-book price increases, which successfully accelerated broadband revenue growth in Q3 2026. Management emphasizes that tariff adjustments must be executed carefully with underlying value to avoid creating difficult year-on-year comparables.
2.
Competition & Promotional Intensity: Competition in the German mobile space spiked following the 2024 mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) change. DT has responded through heavy investment and raising prices to target a 10% return rather than slashing prices. DT's CEO noted that competitors newly enabling low-cost MVNOs on their networks have underbid carriers and restricted industry-wide price improvements Competition & Promotional Intensity (cont.):. Meanwhile, German broadband net additions recovered in Q3 2026 to +11k as the commercial impact of previous price rises waned and competition eased. Fibre & Mobile Capex Outlook: DT's cash capex before spectrum was €11.82 bn for 9M 2026, slightly down from €12.21 bn in 9M 2025 due to build phasing, with a catch-up expected in Q4 2026. The German fiber rollout is highly active, with FTTH deployment expected to exceed 2.50 mm homes passed in 2026, while German mobile capex is maintained at a 21% capex-to-sales ratio.
