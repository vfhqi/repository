Section 2 – Rightmove Operating Metrics and Product Uptake

  2. Upgrades to premium tiers and rapid valuation software adoption are successfully decoupling ARPA from transaction volumes.

1.

2.
Unprecedented partner retention: Rightmove recorded its highest first-half estate agency retention rate in over ten years at 96%, proving that agents continue prioritising market leaders.
3.
Accelerating ARPA monetization: Total core average revenue per advertiser increased 7.3% to £1,726, comfortably beating consensus expectations of £1,715.
4.
