Business Model and Operating Segments

2.
Compounding decentralized structure: Judges Scientific operates a highly disciplined buy-and-build capital allocation model focused on acquiring small to medium-sized niche scientific instrument manufacturers. The group maintains 25 autonomous operating companies, giving individual management teams control over their operational destinies while enforcing robust central financial controls. This strategy has delivered a total revenue CAGR of 19% and an EBIT CAGR of 23% from admission in 2005 through 2025. This buy-and-build focus relies on acquiring businesses with sustainable margins that generate strong cash flow to pay down acquisition debt.
3.
Segmented financial architecture: The group's activities are split into two core reporting segments, Materials Sciences and Vacuum. In fiscal year 2025, Materials Sciences generated £71.9m in revenue and £15.5m in adjusted operating profit, whereas the Vacuum segment generated £73.9m in revenue and £18.8m in adjusted operating profit. Materials Sciences includes major business units like Geotek coring, GDS, and Armfield, which are exposed to geophysics, civil engineering, and education. The Vacuum segment comprises niche physical-science manufacturers including Oxford Cryosystems, Scientifica, and UHV Design, which serve advanced microscopy, cryogenics, and semiconductor thin-film deposition.

This domestic US funding freeze, combined with extensive Chinese import tax-exemption delays, caused an 11% year-on-year drop in order intake during the first half of 2026. Given the high operational leverage inherent to high-tech manufacturing, these volume declines resulted in adjusted operating margins compressing from 20.9% in 2024 to 19.2% in 2025.
6.
Foreign exchange and sterling leverage: With approximately 50% of group sales denominated in US dollars and 33% in euros against a cost base concentrated in British sterling, currency fluctuations exert powerful direct leverage over reported EPS. Although historically a weaker sterling has enhanced the competitiveness and translation margins of UK exporters, recent sterling strengthening has created transaction and translation headwinds. This exchange rate risk is magnified because the group entered the second half of fiscal year 2026 with an organic order book of 17.3 weeks. Unfavorable GBP/USD movements during Q4 2026 shipments could erode final margins even if underlying volumes recover.
