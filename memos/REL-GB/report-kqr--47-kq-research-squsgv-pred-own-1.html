Isolating central overhead ensures that divisional operating performance is not distorted by corporate activities managed at the group level.
3.
Reconciling total profit: The sum of the divisional profits and the print segment net of unallocated costs equals group adjusted operating profit exactly. Adding the core divisions' £3,165m (calculated) to the unallocated and print segment's £177m yields the reported group adjusted operating profit of £3,342m. Achieving exact reconciliation gives investors high confidence in the integrity of RELX's financial reporting and segment disclosures.

Excluding these non-cash amortization expenses allows management to present core operational earnings trends more clearly.

This methodology allows investors to assess the true organic momentum of the group's digital assets.
