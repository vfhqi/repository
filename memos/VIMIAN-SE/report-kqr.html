Shared rebate integration: Buying groups like CVS Group's MiVetClub and VetShare negotiate volume rebates directly with pharmaceutical and consumable manufacturers on behalf of their members. Managing these administrative pipelines supports clinic-level margins and cash flow for both member practices and the central corporate platform.

Contrary evidence: VetFamily's member clinic base is not fully shielded from corporate consolidation as major veterinary consolidators actively acquire independent practices. This ongoing acquisition trend directly shrinks VetFamily's addressable independent market because consolidated corporate chains migrate acquired clinics to their own centralized procurement networks. Thus, aggressive clinic acquisitions threaten member-based platform revenues because they remove independent buyers from VetFamily's purchasing pool.
