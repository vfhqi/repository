Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Understanding Elekta's gross margin movement is critical for investors because margin expansion directly drives the recovery of its adjusted earnings per share and determines whether the company can achieve its mid-term targets.
  2. Elekta adjusted gross margin rebounded from 37.0 percent to 42.6 percent over the last eight quarters.
  3. This recovery was driven by premium pricing on the Evo, structural COGS savings, and US tariff refunds.
  4. Structural margin improvements are partially offset by persistent foreign exchange headwinds and geographic mix shifts.

Defining the Key Question

1.
Key Question: "Elekta AB (EKTA.B-SE), the Swedish pure-play radiotherapy company: linear accelerators (Evo, Versa HD, Harmony, Unity MR-Linac), Leksell Gamma Knife neuro-radiosurgery, brachytherapy, oncology software (Elekta ONE, MOSAIQ) and service on an installed base of about 7,500 systems; reported as Solutions and Service and, from Q1 FY2026/27, in five regions (Americas; Europe; China; Asia Pacific and Japan; Turkey, India, Middle East and Africa); fiscal year ends 30 April. What explains the movement in Elekta AB's (EKTA.B-SE) adjusted gross margin over the last eight quarters (37.0 percent in Q1 FY2025/26 to 42.6 percent in Q1 FY2026/27), quantified by price, product and service mix, cost-of-goods savings, tariffs and tariff refunds, currency transaction effects and order-book quality after the SEK 2.2bn order cancellation; what do the company and individual brokers expect for each component in Q2 to Q4 FY2026/27 and in FY2027/28 as solutions volumes recover; and how have Siemens Healthineers' Precision Therapy and Varian gross margins behaved through comparable mix and input-cost shifts?"
2.
Significant profitability: Elekta adjusted gross margin recovery directly impacts its ability to achieve mid-term earnings targets. According to the Elekta first quarter interim report published on August 26, 2026, the gross margin expanded to 42.6 percent from 37.0 percent in the previous year. This gross margin expansion was the primary driver behind the EBIT margin rise to 11.2 percent from 6.5 percent, which significantly lowers execution risks for investors.
3.
Volatile performance: Elekta historical margin volatility has created skepticism among financial analysts regarding its long-term financial targets. A broker report by DNB Carnegie on November 10, 2026, notes that Elekta has historically struggled to sustain its gross margins, which hovered around 37 to 38 percent over the past five years. Resolving the durability of this margin upturn determines whether adjusted earnings per share can grow by double-digit percentages.

Section 2 – Drivers of Elekta Gross Margin over the Last Eight Quarters

Chronological Margin Progression

1.
Sustained recovery: Elekta adjusted gross margin has progressed significantly over the last eight quarters from its lowest depths. In Q1 FY2025/26, the adjusted gross margin stood at a low 37.0 percent due to combined headwinds. However, the margin improved to 37.9 percent in Q2 FY2025/26 as product launches and pricing began to contribute. This sequence marked the beginning of a gradual turnaround.
2.
Steady improvement: Elekta gross margin continued its upward trajectory into the second half of fiscal year 2025/26. The adjusted gross margin reached 38.3 percent in Q3. It then expanded to 39.9 percent in Q4 FY2025/26. These improvements were achieved despite severe headwinds from foreign exchange and tariffs.
3.
Favorable peak: Elekta recorded a substantial margin expansion in the first quarter of fiscal year 2026/27. The adjusted gross margin reached 42.6 percent, representing a 560 basis points increase year-over-year. This performance was heavily supported by a one-time US tariff refund of SEK 53 mm. It represents the highest adjusted gross margin recorded by the company in several years.
4.
Contrary evidence: Elekta underlying margin expansion is smaller than the reported figures suggest because of transient benefits. The adjusted gross margin of 42.6 percent in Q1 FY2026/27 includes a 150 basis points benefit from US tariff refunds. Excluding this non-recurring refund, the underlying gross margin was 41.1 percent. While this is still a strong result, it indicates that operational improvements were partially inflated by regulatory compensation.

Price and Mix Dynamics

5.
High-margin mix: Elekta benefited significantly from a structural shift in its product and service mix. In Q1 FY2026/27, solutions sales declined by 9 percent while service sales grew by 5 percent. Software systems like Elekta ONE and MOSAIQ carry gross margins near 80 percent. This higher proportion of recurring software and services drew the group average upward during low-volume quarters.
6.
Premium pricing: Elekta leveraged new product introductions to enforce strict pricing discipline across mature markets. The rollout of the Elekta Evo linear accelerator has been executed at a 30 percent price premium. This premium pricing is crucial to offset the cost inflation of raw materials. Selling these advanced systems enables Elekta to protect profitability even when total installation volumes are temporarily subdued.
7.
Lucrative upgrades: Elekta gross margin was also supported by strong momentum in specialty products and software upgrades. Sales of Leksell Gamma Knife neuro-radiosurgery and brachytherapy solutions historically support elevated profitability. Furthermore, software upgrades like Iris carry gross margins of 80 percent and above. Expanding this installed base creates a highly profitable stream of post-sales upgrades.

Operational Self-Help and COGS

8.
Tangible savings: Elekta operational model restructuring has begun to deliver structural cost-of-goods-sold reductions. The savings program initiated in Q2 FY2025/26 aimed to cut SEK 500 mm in annualized costs. Management allocated 30 percent of these savings directly to COGS. This structural delayering successfully lowered the overall cost of product sales by Q1 FY2026/27.
9.
Continuous engineering: Elekta is pursuing additional margin expansion through its continuous cost-of-goods- sold reduction program. This initiative focuses on reducing variable product costs through components cost engineering. Redesigning assembly processes helps to lower the bill of materials. Sourcing optimization remains essential to rebuild structural profitability to pre-pandemic levels.
10.
Historical precedent: Elekta has historically used COGS reduction programs to recover from margin squeezes caused by supply chain disruptions. During fiscal year 2019/20, the company intensified engineering efforts to redesign components and qualify alternative suppliers. This past program successfully supported gross margin improvements after resource focus shifted away from Unity development. It demonstrates that engineering-led cost reductions can drive sustainable profitability gains when execution is disciplined.

External Factors and Backlog Quality

11.
Severe headwinds: Elekta encountered significant external margins pressure from geopolitical tariffs and currency translation effects over the past year. In Q3 FY2025/26, FX and tariffs dragged adjusted gross margin down by 130 and 100 basis points respectively. These negative transactional impacts diluted the pricing improvements achieved on new hardware shipments. Stronger domestic currencies relative to the US dollar continue to squeeze export profitability.
12.
Backlog improvement: Elekta proactive order backlog cancellations have successfully enhanced the underlying quality of its future revenue streams. The company cancelled SEK 2.2 bn of non-accretive orders in Q2 FY2025/26 to safeguard future margins. Stricter acceptance criteria on prepayments and delivery timelines were applied during this process. Clearing these low-profitability commitments improves overall backlog predictability and protects the future solutions margin.
13.
Competitive gap: Elekta gross margin recovery occurs in a highly consolidated market where its primary peer has demonstrated stronger top-line momentum. The company's larger competitor Varian has grown its revenues by double digits annually over recent years. This competitive scale allows peers to absorb input-cost inflation more effectively than Elekta. Sustaining margin expansion is crucial for Elekta because it remains significantly outgrown by its main rival.
14.
Other side of the story: Elekta adjusted gross margin expansion may be difficult to sustain once solutions shipment volumes recover in emerging markets. Emerging market shipments typically carry lower average selling prices and thinner service margins than those in mature Western markets. If volume growth in China and other developing regions outpaces Western Europe and the Americas, the unfavorable geographic mix could depress the consolidated gross margin. This geographic dilution could offset the benefits of both premium pricing on the Evo and the structural COGS savings.

Section 3 – Forward Outlook and Broker Expectations for FY2026/27 and FY2027/28

Section summary
  1. Elekta targets an adjusted EBIT margin of 12.5 to 13.5 percent for FY2026/27, supported by a gradual return of gross margins to pre-pandemic levels.
  2. Individual brokers expect sequentially lower gross margins in Q2 and Q3 due to hardware installation mix before recovering in FY2027/28.

Management Guidance and Margin Targets

1.
Ambitious targets: Elekta management reiterated its FY2026/27 outlook for 2 to 4 percent net sales growth in constant currency and an adjusted EBIT margin of 12.5 to 13.5 percent. Achieving these targets is supported by price improvements, continued services growth, and disciplined cost management. Sustained profitability improvements will support earnings growth in coming quarters, lowering overall delivery risks for investors.

Stepwise expansion: Elekta seeks stepwise gross and EBIT margin expansion towards its medium-term targets of moving gross margins back to pre-pandemic levels of above 40 percent. Operational self-help: Elekta expects its operational restructuring to deliver over SEK 500 mm in annualized run- rate cost savings. About 30 percent of these savings will benefit the adjusted gross margin directly through COGS. These structural savings protect the bottom line against ongoing price erosion and input cost inflation.

Section 4 – Peer Comparison: Siemens Healthineers Precision Therapy and Varian

  2. Varian continues to outgrow Elekta in key markets but faces comparable margin pressure from unfavorable product mix shifts.

1.
Severe contraction: Siemens Healthineers Precision Therapy adjusted EBIT margin fell to 13.8 percent in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 from 17.1 percent in the prior year. This 330 basis points drop occurred because of a 100 basis points inflation hit and a 100 basis points currency headwind. These cost pressures severely diluted the segment's underlying operating conversion despite solid revenue growth. Management expects these supply chain challenges to persist into early fiscal year 2027.
2.
Comparable pressure: Varian standalone adjusted EBIT margin decreased to 16.2 percent in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 from 19.7 percent in the prior-year quarter. According to Deutsche Bank, this drop was driven by an unfavorable business mix and a lack of high-margin software comps. While Elekta's Q1 margin was boosted by software, Varian struggled with a transition away from premium mix elements in mature markets. These shifts demonstrate that both leading radiotherapy vendors are vulnerable to supply-chain and currency disruptions.

Other side of the story: Elekta margins could easily stagnate if the geographic sales mix continues to tilt toward price-sensitive developing countries.
