EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Business model and strategic positioning

1.
Business structure resilience: Elecnor operates through three independent divisions comprising Services (Euros 2,419.4 million turnover in 2025), Projects (Euros 1,955.7 million), and Concessions via the Celeo joint venture, balancing recurring essential services with high-value global project execution.
2.
Strategic capital rotation: the company completed the divestment of its Enerfin wind business in 2024, generating an extraordinary net profit of Euros 823.3 million and redirecting proceeds toward high-margin transmission assets and digital infrastructure investments.
3.
Geographic concentration risk: Europe represents 52.2% of consolidated sales, with Spain as the core anchor, while the Americas contribute 35.9% and Oceania adds 9.2%, exposing the group to regional economic cycles.
4.
Celeo partnership structure: the Concessions segment is managed through a 51%-49% joint venture with Dutch pension fund APG, operating Euros 6,000 million in transmission assets across 7,949 km of electricity lines.

Financial profile and capital strength

5.
Revenue and EBITDA trajectory: consolidated revenue reached Euros 4,387.3 million in 2025 (up 15.15% year-on-year), while recurring EBITDA surged 71.45% to Euros 267.8 million, reflecting substantive operational recovery.
6.
Exceptional balance sheet position: the group maintains a positive net recourse cash position of Euros 199.0 million with leverage at negative 0.80x, sitting well below the 3x covenant threshold and providing substantial strategic flexibility.
7.
Structural margin deficit: the consolidated EBITDA margin of approximately 6.10% lags Vinci's Cobra IS division by 390 basis points, reflecting the group's high exposure to low-margin maintenance services and execution-intensive EPC contracts.
8.
Extraordinary shareholder distribution: total dividend payments reached a historic peak of Euros 805.0 million from 2024 earnings, comprising a Euros 540.0 million interim payment and Euros 265.0 million final distribution funded by the Enerfin divestment.

Competitive positioning and operational risks

9.
Segment profitability challenges: Services achieved an EBITDA margin of 6.0% in 2025 despite 37.4% absolute EBITDA growth, while Projects yielded 6.8% but was impacted by provisions for railway project claims.
10.
Value chain margin compression: the company operates primarily in downstream construction and services where Projects division margins of 6.80% trail Cobra IS by 320 basis points, limiting overall profitability despite scale.
11.
Moat assessment and competitive barriers: the group secures high customer retention through long-term framework agreements with major utilities, yet independent analysis assigns a quantitative economic moat rating of none due to limited structural advantages.
12.
Domestic market dominance: Elecnor has defended its leading position in Spanish electricity distribution maintenance, serving all major domestic utilities and achieving 12.50% Services revenue growth in 2025.

Strategic execution and capital allocation discipline

13.
2025-2027 strategic commitments: the board established clear targets including cumulative dividend payments exceeding Euros 220 million and sustaining annual revenues near Euros 4,000 million under the new Strategic Plan.
14.
Capital redeployment strategy: management commits to reinvesting Euros 400 million over three years into high-margin electricity transmission lines and energy storage assets following the Enerfin sale proceeds.
15.
Adjacent sector diversification: the 100% acquisition of Jet Aircraft Services in 2025 marks strategic entry into high-growth aeronautical maintenance and aircraft recycling, expanding services into technically complex adjacent markets.
16.
Foundation quality verdict: confidence in advantaged business strength assessed as MEDIUM, with the single largest risk being low services profitability and execution-stage EPC contract margin erosion under inflationary pressures, with early warning signals including sudden client payment term extensions.
