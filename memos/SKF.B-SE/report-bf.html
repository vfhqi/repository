EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Strategic restructuring and portfolio transformation

1.
Transformational separation: SKF is executing a tax-free spin-off of its low-margin Automotive business (SKF Vertevo) via Lex Asea distribution, scheduled for Nasdaq Stockholm listing in November 2026, creating a highly focused industrial pure-play targeting >17% adjusted operating margins mid-term.
2.
Significant dis-synergy headwinds: The demerger triggers SEK 1.5 billion in estimated dis-synergies and stranded costs, requiring a front-loaded SEK 2 billion rightsizing program targeting net headcount reduction of 1,200 positions through 2027.
3.
Portfolio pruning execution: Management successfully exited non-core aerospace assets including the Elgin PED business for $75 million and the SEK 2.3 billion Hanover divestment, while securing bolt-on acquisitions like John Sample Group adding SEK 550 million in lubrication sales.
4.
Robotics growth opportunity: SKF established a 60% majority-owned humanoid robotics joint venture in China during July 2026, estimated by Deutsche Bank to add SEK 14-17 per share in valuation upside.

Financial performance and working capital constraints

5.
Cyclical growth deceleration: Group organic sales growth remained soft at 1.4% in second-quarter 2026, with Bearing Solutions flat at 0.2% while Specialized Industrial Solutions delivered 8.3% organic growth.
6.
Elevated restructuring drag: Items affecting comparability reached SEK 1,004 million in second-quarter 2026 due to footprint optimization and spin-off costs, with total restructuring cash outflows guided to SEK 2.5-3.0 billion.
7.
Working capital build pressures: Net working capital reached 36.4% of twelve-month rolling sales in June 2026 driven by separation-related safety stocks and high accounts receivables, well above the 29% mid-term and sub-27% long-term targets.
8.
Constrained capital returns: Adjusted return on capital employed was 14.5% in second-quarter 2026, below the stated 20% ROCE target and historical 16% benchmark, as elevated capex intensity peaks at 5% of sales mid-term.
9.
Resilient balance sheet: Net debt to adjusted EBITDA stood at 0.9x in second-quarter 2026, though gearing reached 30.6% when including SEK 6,280 million in post-employment benefit provisions.

Industry structure and competitive dynamics

10.
Consolidated global oligopoly: The top six bearing manufacturers control approximately 55% of the global rolling element market, with SKF commanding a dominant 45% European market share and 15-20% globally.

Structural consolidation wave: Japanese rivals NSK and NTN signed a memorandum of understanding in May 2026 to establish a joint holding company by October 2027 with combined sales of JPY 2.0 trillion, addressing automation scale and low-cost Chinese competition.
