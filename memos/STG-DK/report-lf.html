Margin collapse despite top-line growth: Net sales rose from DKK 8,233 mm in 2021 to DKK 9,036 mm in 2025, a 9.75% (calculated) increase. Adjusted EBIT margin still fell from 22.03% to 12.64%, a compression of 9.39 percentage points. This is an unusual split between growth and profitability for a company running consolidation and efficiency programmes. This report does not benchmark it against a sector percentile, so its rank among peers is not verified. [Cross-ref: Section 2]
2.
Insider signals pointing in opposite directions: Employee-elected directors Karsten Dam Larsen and Thomas Thomsen made the largest open-market purchases, adding 5,000 and 4,000 shares respectively. CEO Niels Frederiksen and CFO Marianne Rørslev Bock made no open-market purchases or sales with their own money over the last year. Alongside this, participants received only 15% of the 2022 long-term incentive grant in April 2025, and the remaining 85% lapsed. The lowest-ranking board members showed the most confidence, while the senior executives who set the targets did not buy shares and saw most of their incentives lapse. [Cross-ref: Section 13] [Cross-ref: Section 8]
3.
A supply-chain career steering a commercial pivot: Before becoming CEO, Frederiksen's most recent role was Executive Vice President of the global supply chain. He carried no direct responsibility for sales, marketing or commercial brand growth before 2015. Even so, the company is executing Focus2030, which prioritises modern oral nicotine pouch brands like XQS. That transition requires a commercial mindset that differs from his historical cost-cutting focus. A career built on cost centres is now being tested on category creation.
