[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. ING Groep is a 'Major Corporate Change' case riding a dated external tailwind, bought late. The earnings leg is probable for two years, because a contractual repricing of a 524 billion EUR hedge book and a set of internal changes that have all arrived early lift earnings per share about 16.5 percent a year to FY2028. At 31.035 EUR the shares already pay for that leg and pay nothing for the fade after it, so the modal return is 5.3 percent a year over eighteen months and 9.6 percent a year over three years against the framework's 20 percent bar; the case clears the bar only below about 25 EUR.

  • The largest change force is external, mechanical and dated.
    • The euro retail replicating portfolio of 524 billion EUR, 6.7 times the market capitalisation, reprices from a 1.85 percent yield in 2026 to 2.23 percent in 2027 and 2.41 percent in 2028 as legacy swaps mature into higher market swap rates.
    • It has already lifted the liability margin from 1.04 percent to 1.07 percent in one quarter and commercial net interest income 10.7 percent year on year to 4,174 million EUR in Q2 2026.
    • The step-up is contractual in the maturing swaps, not a forecast
      • That is why the earnings leg is probable.
      • It plateaus in 2028
      • That is why the return leg is not.
  • The internal change is real and multi-dimensional, but it is optimisation rather than transformation.
    • Six of the twelve internal forces are at B or better and none of the screen's red flags is present.
    • The capital reallocation from Wholesale to Retail reached 56 percent of risk-weighted assets eighteen months early
      • Fees grew 14 percent and reach the 5 billion EUR target a year early
      • About 1,250 operations staff are being cut in 2026
      • More than 7 billion EUR was returned in 2025 and a 1.5 billion EUR buyback runs in Q3 2026.
    • The chief executive has been in place since July 2020 and the strategy since mid-2024.
      • The leadership research's own verdict is incremental optimisation
      • We agree.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are improving on every line, and the change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • Revenue growth has stepped from about 0 to 2 percent a year over FY2023 to FY2025 to 7 to 10 percent
    • The pre-tax margin rises about 8 points over three years on consensus
    • The share count shrinks 3 to 4 percent a year.
    • Guidance was raised on five metrics at the Q2 2026 results, after a raise at Q1 2026.
      • FY2027 consensus earnings per share are up 9.6 percent in twelve months with no monthly cut on the way.
    • The improvement is dominated by one line, the liability margin, which is a rate line the whole sell-side models.
      • The next upgrades are therefore likely but small.
  • What we think it earns: earnings per share of 2.12 EUR in FY2025 rising to about 3.35 EUR in FY2028 on our path, 16.5 percent a year, inside the framework's 12 to 20 percent band, with consensus at 3.39 EUR.
    • After FY2028 the path slows to 8 to 9 percent a year as the hedge roll completes: 3.65 EUR in FY2029, 3.95 EUR in FY2030, 4.25 EUR in FY2031.
  • What it costs: 31.035 EUR, or 9.4 to 9.6 times 24-month-forward earnings, the 92nd percentile of the stock's own five-year range and the 78th of its ten-year range, after a 54.6 percent twelve-month return.
    • The whole euro-area cohort sits at a similar extreme, so the multiple is industry-driven and the re-rating is not ours to earn.
  • The return does not clear the bar, because the re-rating leg is negative.
    • Our modal multiple at both horizons is 8.6x, a blend of a 40 percent branch in which the current 9.4x holds and a 60 percent branch in which it reverts to the two-year median of 8.1x.
    • Earnings growth contributes 14.8 percent over eighteen months and 28.7 percent over three years
      • The cash return 4.6 and 13.7 percent
      • The re-rating -10.0 percent at both horizons.
      • The product is 5.3 percent a year and 9.6 percent a year, an F on the framework's anchors.
    • If the multiple holds the return is 11.5 and 12.5 percent a year, a D.
      • It reaches a B only in the ten-year "mania" band of 10.5x.
  • The pattern it fits: 'Major Corporate Change' at B, not crisply, because about half the earnings upgrade comes from a rate line the pattern does not describe
    • The secondary fit is 'Compounder' at C.

  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.

    • The chain holds from the forces through the outputs to the estimates: two links of three.
    • It breaks at the third, the return, because the change is already in the price.

  • Rating = C, because the chain holds for two of its three links and breaks at the third: the input change forces (B) are material and have reached the sell-side's estimates, the financial outputs (B) are improving on every line with earnings growth inside the band, but the 'Modal return' (F) does not reach the bar because the change is in the price.

    • The rating is a judgement about the whole: a material, visible change bought at the 92nd percentile of its own valuation range is a C thesis for a capital allocator, however good the company's quarter.
    • Not B, because the Building Block's own question includes the 20 percent-plus return and ours is 5 to 10 percent a year
    • Not D, because the thesis is not bland or unevidenced, the sell-side is still upgrading, and a 20 percent de-rating would make it a B case on the same facts.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The hypothesis, at its strongest: ING is a peak-cycle beneficiary whose liability margin falls back toward 100 basis points as the ECB returns to 2 percent in 2027 while German deposit competition forces rate matching, so FY2028 earnings land nearer 3.0 EUR than 3.35 EUR and the multiple de-rates at the same time.
      • This is the risks research's FALSE FRIEND profile, the one with the lowest inconsistency count of its three profiles.
      • The replicating portfolio is 6.7 times market capitalisation against 2 to 3 times at peers
        • A parallel downward rate shock costs 1,075 million EUR of net interest income
        • The reinvestment profile was shortened in late 2023 to 50 percent maturing within twelve months, so the book reprices faster than peers' in both directions.
    • What we concede: the exposure is real and uniquely large, and the 2023 duration shortening makes the book more pro-cyclical than peers'.
    • What we hold: the earnings leg is probable for two years.
      • The swap roll through 2028 is contractual and is sized by three houses at 1.85 to 2.41 percent.
      • Q2 2026 showed Germany growing deposits 5 percent at a 3.75 percent campaign rate against a 4 percent teaser from JP Morgan Chase, with the liability margin rising 3 basis points.
      • The ECB is raising, not cutting, into the end of 2026
        • The market prices a terminal rate of 2.75 to 3 percent.
    • What we do not hold: that the multiple survives the fade.
      • That is why the return, not the thesis, carries the F, and why we park rather than progress.

Revenue and demand cycle: where is the company's own cycle, and is it turning? (KQ 6, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = B
  • Rating = B, because the company's own cycle, which for ING is the rate and hedge cycle, is in a second, dated leg: the extraordinary first-phase uplift is over, but the replicating book reprices for two more years and the ECB is raising, not cutting, in the near term.
  • Replicating yield: reinvestment yields step from 1.85 percent in 2026 to 2.23 percent in 2027 and 2.41 percent in 2028, while prevailing euro swap rates sit well above those levels.
  • Liability margin: rose to 1.07 percent in Q2 2026 from 1.04 percent
    • Management expects the upper-middle of the 100 to 110 basis point range, and the finance director expects more than 110 basis points in 2027.
  • Policy rate: Eurozone inflation was 3.3 percent in August 2026
    • The ECB was expected to raise the deposit rate 25 basis points to 2.50 percent in September 2026
    • Terminal pricing is 2.75 to 3 percent
    • Morgan Stanley's baseline then has the rate declining to 2.0 percent by 2027.
  • First phase over: industry analysts say the extraordinary net interest income uplift of the first phase of the rate cycle is definitively over for European banks.
  • Duration anomaly: ING's pro-cyclical shortening of the reinvestment profile in late 2023, to 50 percent maturing within twelve months, is an unexplained anomaly against KBC and ABN AMRO.
  • Direction: tailwind, with about two years' visibility and a dated fade.
  • Not A, because the 2023 duration decision means the book repriced down faster than peers' when rates were cut and reprices up faster only if swap rates hold.
    • Not C, because the step-up is contractual in the maturing swaps, not a forecast.
