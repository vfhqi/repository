EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Key Question asks how likely Munters Group AB (MTRS-SE) is to raise, meet, or reduce company guidance at earnings events over the next 18 months, and how sell-side consensus forecasts and revision breadth will move over the same period. This question is critical because Munters' earnings per share trajectory hinges on converting its record order backlog into revenue amidst US manufacturing ramp-up bottlenecks. The evidence gathered across multiple research dimensions allows the Key Question to be answered partially, with high confidence on demand visibility but material uncertainty around near-term execution timing and margin recovery.

Munters' Data Center Technologies segment must deliver between SEK 3,130m and SEK 3,720m in Q4 2026 to meet its full-year guidance of 30% to 40% sales growth, representing an unprecedented sequential jump of 75% to 108% from Q3 2026 segment sales of approximately SEK 1,794m. Jefferies models a more cautious 40% sequential increase to SEK 2.5bn for Q4 DCT sales, leading to full-year sales of SEK 7.0bn, which falls short of the company's explicit SEK 7,678m minimum target for 30% growth, suggesting a material guidance miss is highly possible unless factory execution significantly exceeds current sell-side expectations.

This execution gap represents the single largest near-term risk to consensus earnings estimates and the company's credibility with investors.
