Heavier burdens: The Polish government has a strong fiscal incentive to implement these tax hikes to fund proposed personal income tax cuts. The Ministry of Finance expects the overall tax package to be budget-neutral because the higher corporate taxes directly offset individual relief. This shifting tax structure means that state-controlled energy groups like PGE are essentially funding broader national welfare programs. If these changes become law, PGE's cash available for capital investments will shrink significantly.

Growing fiscal-pressures: Proposed tax rules also contain significant ambiguities that create persistent legal uncertainty for Polish utilities.
