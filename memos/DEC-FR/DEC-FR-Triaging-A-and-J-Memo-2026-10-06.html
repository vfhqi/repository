[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. JCDecaux is a proven under-promiser converting a physical advertising network into a digitally traded one, and the change has already reached every layer of the numbers. At EUR 24.40 the shares pay for it: our modal earnings path sits above consensus, but with the multiple at the stock's own five-year median the modal return is about 11 percent a year against the 20 percent bar. It is a compelling business and not a compelling investment at this price.

  • The change is mostly external, and it is a tailwind.
    • Advertising budgets are shifting to digital out-of-home, and the exit of the most aggressive European bidder has made municipal tenders more rational.
    • Digital out-of-home grew 14.5 percent organically in H1 2026 against 5.7 percent for the group, taking digital to 42.8 percent of revenue from 36.8 percent a year earlier.
    • Programmatic revenue grew 30.9 percent organically to EUR 102.8m in H1 2026 and carries a 25 to 30 percent CPM premium over direct digital bookings.
    • Clear Channel's acquisition by Mubadala at 12.3x EBITDA removed the loss-tolerant bidder from European tenders
      • JCDecaux won 82 percent of the street furniture tenders it contested in 2025.
  • The change inside the company is evolution, done well, rather than a break with the past.
    • There is no chief executive change, no raised mid-term guidance and no step-change in capital deployment
    • The adtech platform, the portfolio recycling, retail media and the resumed dividend are what this management has done for a decade.
    • VIOOH was EBIT-positive and cash-positive for the first time in H1 2026
      • APG|SGA was sold down at above 13x EBITDA in April 2026
      • The dividend was resumed at EUR 0.55 for FY2024 and raised 18.2 percent to EUR 0.65 for FY2025.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have moved, all four of them, in the same half.
    • This is the part of the chain that is proven rather than argued.
    • Organic growth rose to +5.7 percent in H1 2026 from +1.8 percent in FY2025
      • The operating margin rate rose 190 bps to 18.4 percent, with fixed operating costs up 0.9 percent and rents and fees up 3.4 percent against revenue up 5.7 percent.
    • Free cash flow was positive at EUR 26.2m in a seasonally negative half, against minus EUR 64.9m a year earlier
      • Net financial debt fell to EUR 628.8m at 30 June 2026 from EUR 912.9m.
    • Consensus EPS rose 9 to 10 percent over the three months to 2026-10-02 on every forecast year, and the buy share rose 13 points to 67 percent.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, which is why the price has moved.
    • From the co-CEOs' choices (adtech, portfolio recycling, bidding discipline) to actions (VIOOH, renegotiated Chinese leases, pruned billboards) to operating metrics (digital 42.8 percent, programmatic +30.9 percent) to the financial statements (margin +190 bps, cash flow positive) to the sell-side's estimates.
    • The shares rose 67.6 percent in the twelve months to 2026-10-05.
  • Our modal earnings path is above consensus, by a few percent, not by a step.
    • FY2026 underlying EPS of about EUR 1.40 (before the EUR 47.5m APG|SGA gain), EUR 1.64 in FY2027 and EUR 1.78 in FY2028, then 6 to 7 percent a year
    • Consensus on the brokers' basis is EUR 1.59 for FY2027 and EUR 1.72 for FY2028.
    • The gap is the operating margin rate, which we expect to rise 40 to 80 bps a year to 22.6 percent by FY2028 because a growing share of the rent line is variable and programmatic converts up to 75 percent of incremental revenue into operating profit.
  • What it costs is where the chain breaks.
    • At EUR 24.40 the shares trade at 14.5x consensus 24-month-forward EPS of EUR 1.6821: the stock's five-year median, the 90th percentile of the last two years, the third-highest multiple in a comparison set whose median is 9.1x.
    • The multiple is company-driven and was earned by the margin delivery that is now in the numbers.
    • Holding 14.5x on our path, the value including cash returns is EUR 28.69 at the eighteen-month horizon and EUR 32.76 at three years: 11.4 percent and 10.3 percent a year, a D on the framework's anchors.
    • The case clears the 20 percent bar below about EUR 21.8 on the eighteen-month horizon, or if the market re-rates toward the ten-year median of 17.8x, which we judge possible at about a quarter's odds and not modal.
  • The pattern is 'Compounder' at C.
    • The proven-upgrader half is crisp (two missed guidances since the 2001 IPO; beats of 70 to 430 bps over guidance across the last five periods)
    • The mis-modelled half is a plausible 3 to 4 percent on consensus EPS, not a step
    • And the pattern's payoff has largely been collected by the rally.

  • The case therefore is a good business at the wrong price.

    • The forces and the outputs are material and still propagating
    • The return at this price is not.
    • We would rather own the bet on this management at about EUR 21.8 than at EUR 24.40.

  • Rating = C, because the materiality test has three links and the case passes two: the change forces are sufficiently strong (B) and the financial outputs are sufficiently improving (B), but at EUR 24.40 the modal total shareholder return is about 11 percent a year (D), below the 20 percent bar

    • The three Elements combine as a chain, and the third link, the price, sets the level.
  • Not B: a B needs the return leg at C or better, which at this price needs a re-rating we do not expect in the modal case.

  • Not D: the forces and the outputs are both at B and still propagating

    • This is a good case at the wrong price, not a weak case.

    • The strongest case against is the bull's, and it runs as follows.

      • With the EUR 1,996.1m of lease liabilities at end-2025 added it is EUR 7,864m, 9.0x.

    • Why we still hold our view.

      • At about a quarter's odds it does not make the modal case.

      • The comparison set is being de-rated, not re-rated.

    • Where the bull would win.

      • We would rather own that bet at EUR 21.8 than at EUR 24.40.

External forces: are there sufficiently strong external forces acting on the company, new or intensifying, and in which direction? (RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = B
  • Rating = B, because the two strongest external forces, the structural shift of advertising budgets to digital out-of-home and the easing of competitive intensity after Clear Channel's European exit, are tailwinds with two-plus years of visibility, while the headwinds (China transit, French macro, category bans) are real but smaller.
  • Digital demand: digital out-of-home grew 14.5 percent organically in H1 2026 against 5.7 percent for the group, taking digital to 42.8 percent of revenue from 36.8 percent a year earlier (30 July 2026).
  • Competition: Clear Channel's acquisition by Mubadala at 12.3x EBITDA removed an aggressive bidder from municipal tenders.
  • Headwinds: Mainland China trading was soft at low-single-digit organic growth with "no major cyclical inflection visible"
    • France's organic revenue fell 1.9 percent in H1 2026.
Demand: is end-market demand changing, structurally or cyclically (a swing with two-plus years of visibility)? (KQ 1, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = B
  • Rating = B, because end-market demand is changing structurally in the company's favour: worldwide out-of-home is forecast to grow at 5.2 percent a year from 2025 to 2030 and digital out-of-home at 8.8 percent, against broadcast television at 2.1 percent and publishing at minus 1.0 percent (the company's presentation, 30 July 2026).
  • Category demand: in H1 2026 Food and Beverage grew 13 percent, Retail 9 percent and Finance 15 percent
    • Fashion, Personal Care and Luxury, 17 percent of sales, were stable after minus 5 percent in 2025 and minus 2 percent in H1 2026.
  • New advertisers: AI-related campaigns generated 30 percent of outdoor advertising revenue in San Francisco (the AGM presentation, 13 May 2026).
  • Not A: this is a mid-single-digit market, not a demand explosion
    • The 'Demand Inflection' test of a sector-level surge is not met.
