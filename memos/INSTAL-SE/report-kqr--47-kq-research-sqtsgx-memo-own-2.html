If Instalco's decentralised subsidiaries fail to match this discipline, its valuation discount relative to Bravida will widen. 3.
Customer selectiveness risks: A failure to maintain selective tendering could lead to severe margin dilution if subsidiary managers chase low-margin volumes in a weak building market. CFO comments in 2025 emphasized a strict strategic focus of prioritizing margin over volume. If this occurs, Instalco's profitability will diverge negatively from current consensus.

Restructuring execution delays: Restructuring program delays could prevent the Swedish segment from achieving the operational leverage assumed by bullish brokers. DNB Carnegie notes that costsaving measures were undertaken to address a more challenging market. If these turnaround initiatives are delayed by local management resistance, the group EBITA margin will remain depressed below 7.0%. Such operational friction would prevent the expected earnings step-up from materializing in FY2027.

Fabri Integration and Minority Leakage

5.
Unexpected minority interest drag: The structural consolidation of the German platform Fabri AG could result in a much larger earnings drag than anticipated by optimistic models. DNB Carnegie models a substantial minority interest of up to SEK 650 million following the 51% acquisition. If Fabri AG's earnings exceed expectations, the required payouts for the remaining noncontrolling interest shares will escalate. This outcome would increase cash leakage and depress the net profit attributable to parent shareholders.
6.
Delayed integration timeline: Should international expansion face severe execution issues, the expected margin accretion from the German market will fail to materialize. Instalco consolidated Fabri AG with a one-month reporting lag starting in August 2026. However, integrating 23 separate companies under local leadership presents severe operational and cultural challenges. Any execution misstep in Germany would severely derail the group's expected organic growth recovery.

Macroeconomic and Debt Headwinds

7.
Interest rate volatility: Divergent central bank policies represent a critical swing factor for Instalco's capital structure and interest expenses over the next two years. DNB Carnegie highlights that the Swedish Riksbank cut benchmark rates to 1.75% in late 2025. If inflation surges and forces lenders to raise benchmark rates once again, Instalco's financing costs will spike. This rate reversal would result in elevated interest expenses continuing to restrict EPS growth.
8.
Debt reduction failures: A failure to reduce outstanding debt levels will keep interest rate sensitivities elevated and depress net profit. DNB Carnegie reported that Instalco's high net debt of SEK 3.8 billion was a key focus. If cash flow conversion fails to recover from 74%, the group cannot organically reduce its leverage below targets. This leverage drag would keep borrowing costs elevated and prevent bottom-line expansion.
9.
Lenders power and covenants: Instalco's banking consortium possesses significant power to alter strategic outcomes if leverage ratios exceed strict covenant thresholds. Instalco refinanced its SEK 3.4 billion credit facility with Danske Bank, SEB, and Svensk Exportkredit. If seasonal working capital spikes push leverage above the 2.5x target, lenders could demand higher borrowing margins. This credit tightening would restrict M&A and severely limit strategic flexibility.
