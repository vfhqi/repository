The earnings print under review covers the second quarter and first half of FY2026, officially published on August 13, 2026, where group revenue reached €1,512.9m in H1 2026, expanding 24.7% year-over-year and matching published sell-side consensus estimates. Consolidated adjusted EBITDA declined 5.9% year-over-year to €242.2m in H1 2026, with the profit contraction directly driven by an ongoing commercial dispute with a mobile network operator that created a €22.0m headwind in H1 2026 and a €1.4m inventory catch-up charge in Q2 2026. IPTV subscribers reached 1.832m at the end of Q2 2026, registering 35k net additions in H1 2026 despite the migration of 200k legacy Telefónica O2TV users, while IPTV segment adjusted EBITDA surged 36.5% year-over-year in H1 2026 to €18.5m, demonstrating structural operating leverage from targeted TV advertising. - Earnings Calls Sell-side expectations heading into earnings were severely anchored by an unquantified €50m annual MNO contract headwind, creating a high bar for waipu.tv growth and mobile ARPU stability to defend current dividend payouts.

CFO Ingo Arnold stressed to investors that adjusting for the negative MNO agreement headwind, underlying group gross profit expanded 3.0% year-over-year and underlying EBITDA rose 3.0% year-over-year in H1 2026, arguing this underlying expansion proves the core service provider model remains fundamentally healthy. 14 - Earnings Calls Management showcased the transition from strategy to execution in its AI-first initiative, where the Finn AI voicebot now autonomously handles over 10% of first-line customer service inquiries, driving structural operating cost reductions as customer density grows.

Management forcefully re-committed to its temporary dividend floor, guaranteeing a minimum payout of €2 per share or 80% adjusted FCF for FY2026 through FY2028, emphasizing that the €2 floor anchors dividend yield in the upper third of full-year FCF guidance, providing payout certainty while the MNO dispute is resolved. Management reiterated its rebased 2028 financial ambition of achieving adjusted EBITDA of at least €620 million and adjusted FCF of at least €340 million, with CEO Robin Harries emphasizing that this financial trajectory implies an expected 2028 dividend payout of at least €2.30 per share.

Deutsche Bank analyst Lars vom-Cleff underscored that waipu.tv adjusted EBITDA expanded 27% year-over-year in Q2 2026 to €10.0m despite a €1.4m inventory catch-up charge, with Bernstein analyst Ulrich Rathe previously emphasizing that waipu.tv's software-based infrastructure delivers high fixed-cost operating leverage as subscriber density rises, confirming that IPTV profit growth represents the primary internal offset to mobile contract erosion.

Robin Harries assumed the position of Chief Executive Officer on June 1, 2025, replacing long-standing CEO Christoph Vilanek after a 16-year tenure, bringing relevant sector experience from his prior roles as CFO at Trivago and CSO at 1&1 Telecommunication SE. Management formally supplemented its 80% adjusted FCF payout policy by introducing a temporary minimum dividend floor of €2 per share for FY2026 through FY2028, with the €2 floor protecting equity investors against earnings volatility and enhancing payout certainty. The statutory abolition of the German cable TV privilege on July 1, 2024, permanently unbundled housing rental contracts from mandatory cable television fees across approximately 12.5 million households, where roughly 4 million German households remain unlicensed viewers, with management observing that cable operators face severe technical constraints in physically disconnecting non-paying tenants, creating a multi-year migration pool that provides a multi-year tailwind sustaining organic customer acquisition for independent IPTV platforms.

The most critical deviation versus consensus centered on adjusted EBITDA contraction of 5.9% year-over-year driven by the €22m MNO wholesale contract headwind, while waipu.tv's 36.5% year-over-year EBITDA expansion to €18.5m materially exceeded sell-side operating leverage expectations. Management confirmed that the FY2026 dividend will strictly adhere to the €2 per share minimum dividend floor or 80% adjusted FCF payout, providing an 8.5% dividend yield anchor, reinforcing capital allocation priorities toward shareholder distribution certainty despite mobile wholesale margin compression.
