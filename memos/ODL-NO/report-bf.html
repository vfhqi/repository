EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section 1 – Business Model + Organisation

Section summary
  1. Odfjell Drilling operates a highly predictable, premium harsh-environment semi-submersible fleet with structurally high financial utilization and long-term contract coverage.

Operational Fleet and Rig-Level Activities

1.
Premium floater fleet: Odfjell Drilling owns and operates a modern, high-specification fleet of five harsh- environment semi-submersible drilling rigs specifically designed for colder, deepwater, and ultra-deepwater environments.
2.
Deepsea Aberdeen: The unit operated exclusively for Equinor on the Norwegian Continental Shelf (NCS) during 2025, completing its 10-year Special Periodic Survey (SPS) in Q2 2025.
3.
Deepsea Atlantic: The rig operated for Equinor in the North Sea in 2025 and is contracted to transit to the UK sector in Q1 2026 to begin its Rosebank campaign.
4.
Deepsea Nordkapp: The unit worked continuously for Aker BP under an Alliance agreement on the NCS throughout 2025, securing an extension to the end of 2028.
5.
Deepsea Stavanger: This rig commenced a five-year contract with Aker BP in April 2025 for the Yggdrasil development after completing its SPS in Q1/Q2 2025.
6.
Deepsea Bergen: Formerly known as Deepsea Bollsta, this high-specification unit was acquired by Odfjell Drilling in December 2025 for USD 480 million and is contracted with Equinor until Q1 2028.

  2. market concentration has tightened significantly among top floaters, while disciplined pricing conduct replaces market-share chasing across major public contractors
  3. value chain economics favor high-specification harsh-environment floaters, establishing firm dayrate floors near $450k/day and high EBITDA margin potential

3.
Accelerating floater consolidation: Mega-mergers like Transocean's $5.8 billion acquisition of Valaris have rationalized global floating capacity.
4.

7.

Emerging geographical frontiers: High-specification harsh semi-submersibles are increasingly deployed into new deepwater basins like Namibia and South Africa.

This exceptional profitability stems from Odfjell's structural concentration in high-day-rate harsh-environment Norwegian floaters, which command premium pricing power unavailable to benign-water competitors.
