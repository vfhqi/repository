EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

RENK Group AG is executing a profound structural transformation toward high-margin defense systems, with leadership stabilized under CEO Dr. Alexander Sagel's five-year contract extension through 2032, organic revenue targets raised to EUR 2.8-3.2 billion by 2030, and adjusted EBIT margin targets exceeding 20%. The investment case is characterized by highly institutional ownership with no active activist presence, supported by a landmark EUR 1.05 billion unsecured refinancing package that eliminates private equity collateral constraints and saves EUR 7 million annually in financing costs.

MATERIALITY OF CHANGE

The company's strategic pivot toward defense applications has reached material scale, with defense revenue mix rising to 74% in 2025 and targeted to reach 90% by 2030, while management raised 2030 organic revenue ambitions from EUR 2.2-2.8 billion to EUR 2.8-3.2 billion and introduced new mid-term adjusted EBIT margin targets above 20%. The July 2026 binding agreement to acquire UK-based David Brown Defence represents a transformational entry into Five Eyes naval markets, securing access to long-term UK, Canadian, and Australian programs with over GBP 700 million in fixed contracts and high-probability pipeline. Leadership governance has shifted materially with Dr. Klaus Richter replacing Triton's Claus von Hermann as Supervisory Board Chairman in June 2026, viewed by Bernstein Research as constructive for reducing perceived private equity sponsor influence and rebuilding investor trust. MOST RECENT EARNINGS DELIVERY

Q1 2026 delivery demonstrated strong operating leverage with record order intake of EUR 582 million marking the highest opening quarter volume in company history, consolidated revenue rising moderately by 4% year-on-year to EUR 283.6 million, group adjusted EBIT climbing 10.4% to EUR 42.4 million, and consolidated adjusted EBIT margin rising 90 basis points to 15.0%. Vehicle Mobility Solutions drove group performance with revenue growth of 11.2% to EUR 191.5 million and adjusted EBIT margins up 170 basis points to 18.3% via capacity scaling in Augsburg.

Marine & Industry faced supply chain logistics slippage with revenue declining 10.8% to EUR 65.2 million and adjusted EBIT margin falling 350 basis points to 6.7% due to supplier quality bottlenecks from an external housing vendor. Slide Bearings continued to face industrial headwinds with revenue falling 1.3% to EUR 30.2 million and adjusted EBIT margins dropping 400 basis points to 13.3% due to US tariffs and unfavorable product mix.

Preliminary Q2 2026 indicators point to order intake surpassing the previously expected EUR 400-500 million range, heavily buttressed by international naval program commitments including the Italian FREMM frigate.

FORWARD GUIDANCE CREDIBILITY AND MANAGEMENT ARCHETYPE

Management reconfirmed FY26 guidance with revenue expected to exceed EUR 1.5 billion and adjusted EBIT between EUR 255-285 million, explicitly targeting the upper half of the range, with over 90% of planned FY26 revenue contractually secured by fixed order backlog. Historical guidance accuracy demonstrates exceptional credibility with consolidated revenue and adjusted EBIT targets fully achieved across both FY24 and FY25, consistently landing at the upper limit of narrowed guidance ranges, following a pattern of conservative initial planning before narrowing upward.

Management has established strong short-term guidance credibility by successfully hitting the upper end of adjusted EBIT targets in both FY24 and FY25, though revenue scaling from EUR 925.5 million in FY23 to EUR 1,366.2 million in FY25 was heavily buttressed by the post-2022 defense rearmament boom. The management archetype is characterized as operationally focused with strong tactical remediation capabilities, evidenced by the 15% improvement in assembly productivity through modular final assembly concept introduction in Augsburg, though tactical execution remains vulnerable to third-party slippage as seen in the EUR 10 million Q1 2026 Marine & Industry revenue deferral.

SELL-SIDE POSITIONING

Buy and Outperform ratings represent 100% of the active sell-side pool covering RENK with zero Sell recommendations, though SOTP price targets range narrowly from EUR 60 to EUR 73 showing tight consensus dispersion. Target price reductions are driven by sector-wide multiple compression rather than deterioration of underlying earnings, with the bullish narrative centered on a record EUR 6.9 billion backlog covering over 90% of 2026 revenue providing unmatched visibility, and the EUR 2.0 billion FY28 revenue target almost entirely de-risked by current contract holdings.

Bearish arguments highlight weak industrial demand continuing to drag on civil slide bearings triggering 10-14% revenue forecast cuts, phasing risks from German procurement throughput delays of 12-24 months, and long-term risks of prime OEMs dual-sourcing transmissions eroding RENK's nearmonopoly pricing power. Recent rating migrations show Jefferies cutting its price target from EUR 78 to EUR 60 while maintaining Buy, Redburn reducing its SOTP-based target from EUR 74 to EUR 63 on Buy, Deutsche Bank marginally raising to EUR 73 on Buy, and Bernstein initiating coverage with Outperform at EUR 62.60 in July 2026.

FUNDAMENTAL PEER POSITIONING

RENK's Q1 2026 revenue expansion of 4% year-on-year sits below Rheinmetall's 69% Q2 pre-release rate but above BAE's 9% H1 clip, while group adjusted EBIT margin of 15% outpaces BAE's 10.8% and Hensoldt's 6.0% H1 actuals. The company delivers top-tier margins reaching 17.5% adjusted operating margin by FY28 outperforming Hensoldt's 12.2% target, with revenue CAGR of 16.7% through 2028 matching Hensoldt's 16.4% but trailing Rheinmetall's 36.2% trajectory, yet trades at an underappreciated valuation discount of 12.5x FY28 EV/EBIT compared to subsystem specialist Hensoldt at 20.2x.

RENK ranks third overall within its specialized peer set for fundamental momentum, trailing Rheinmetall and Leonardo. Working capital positioning shows net working capital at 27.6% of sales, below MilDef's 33.6% and Hensoldt's peer averages, as the company actively pursues measures to reduce from 25.1% of sales toward 20%.

Outliers worth noting

RENK Group AG exhibits several statistical and operational outliers across its financial profile, divisional performance, and peer positioning that warrant explicit recognition and monitoring. These anomalies are not necessarily adverse but represent distinct deviations from sector norms or internal consistency, requiring careful interpretation in the context of the company's broader investment narrative. The most pronounced outlier is RENK's margin-to-valuation disconnect relative to European defense peers.

The company delivers best-in-class adjusted operating margins of 17.5% by FY28, outperforming specialized peer Hensoldt's 12.2% target by over 530 basis points, yet trades at a steep valuation discount of 12.5x FY28 EV/EBIT compared to Hensoldt's 20.2x multiple. While RENK's 16.7% revenue CAGR through 2028 closely matches Hensoldt's 16.4% trajectory, the market applies a dramatic valuation penalty that appears disconnected from fundamental margin superiority and comparable growth momentum.

This disparity is addressed in Section 7 – Peer-Relative Earnings Momentum but remains statistically extreme and inadequately explained by standard liquidity, scale, or diversification premiums typically observed in defense subsystem suppliers. Slide Bearings experienced an even more severe 400 basis point margin compression to 13.3%, driven by unfavorable product mix and US tariff impacts.
