The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

Notes the Financial Analyst must read before entering Part 1: - EBITDA basis: the company's "underlying" EBITDA, which is the basis the brokers use (FY2025: 9,572 on the brokers' basis, against FactSet operating EBITDA of 9,200). Implied EBITDA: 12,350 (FY2026), 7,420, 6,320, 7,130, 8,500. - Depreciation and amortisation is total D&A, including the amortisation of acquired intangibles. For Maersk neither the company's underlying EBIT nor the brokers' adjusted EPS adds that amortisation back, so leaving it in keeps our figures on the consensus basis. The amount is not separately disclosed in our files (we estimate about 0.2bn a year); if the model requires D&A excluding it, reduce each year by about 200 and our EPS cross-check rises by about 14 a share. D&A includes the vessel useful-life benefit (about 700 a year lower than under the old 20-year policy). - Net financial items carries three things, because the form has no separate lines: (1) the share of profit of joint ventures and associates, which Maersk reports inside EBIT (+400, +370, +400, +430, +460); (2) net interest and other financial items, including lease interest of about 700 a year (+50, -150, -350, -500, -600); (3) minority interests (-170, -120, -100, -110, -120). The model's "pre-tax profit" is therefore after minorities, and "pre-tax profit less tax" equals net profit to shareholders. - Tax rate: the rates are derived from absolute tax charges of -1,000, -450, -400, -420 and -480 divided by the model's pre-tax profit (6,380; 1,070; -280; 200; 1,240). Maersk's Ocean profit is under tonnage tax and Ocean losses give no relief, while Terminals and Logistics & Services profits are taxed normally, so the charge is close to fixed at low profit (FY2019: 377 of tax on 657 of pre-tax profit). The FY2028 rate is negative only because pre-tax profit is negative; with the convention tax = -rate x pre-tax profit, it produces a charge of 400. If the model floors or caps the rate, override it with the absolute charges above. - Dividend per share is in USD, for the fiscal year's earnings (paid the following March), at 40% of underlying net profit: FY2026 about DKK 1,000. The bridge from the company's own reported figures to the basis consensus uses

  • EBITDA and EBIT: the company reports "underlying" EBITDA and EBIT, which exclude restructuring and integration costs, gains and losses on disposals and impairments. The brokers' consensus is on this underlying basis (FY2025 brokers' basis EBITDA 9,572 against FactSet operating EBITDA 9,200). Underlying EBIT includes the share of profit of joint ventures and associates (about 0.4bn a year) and the vessel useful-life benefit (about 0.7bn a year); neither the company nor the brokers adjust for the latter, so every FY2026-FY2028 figure, ours included, contains it.
  • EPS: brokers' adjusted EPS is close to underlying net profit to shareholders over shares outstanding. FY2025: brokers' basis 181.63, reported basic 178.99, FactSet adjusted diluted 148.17. Our cross-check EPS is underlying net profit after minorities over average shares outstanding (14.55m in FY2026, 14.34m thereafter), including the vessel-life benefit and excluding restructuring and any impairment. Consensus net income divided by consensus EPS gives inconsistent share counts (different broker sets); do not derive shares from consensus.
  • Free cash flow: the company's definition is operating cash flow less gross capex, less lease repayments, less lease interest (Q2 2026: 549). FactSet's definition is operating cash flow less capex, before any lease payment (consensus FY2026 4,754). Our cross-check is on the company's definition; on FactSet's definition our figures would be about 6,600, 2,500, 1,600, 2,000 and 3,600.
  • Dividends: declared in DKK; FactSet's DPS is dated by period and converted to USD (FY2025: 72.58, which is DKK 480).

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
