Executive Summary

Section summary
  1. The evidence shows that D'Ieteren Group is moderately likely to meet its fiscal year 2026 guidance and consensus EPS targets over the next eighteen months, driven primarily by Belron's structural margin expansion offsetting severe cyclical headwinds in automotive retail, though the trajectory of sell-side revisions remains constrained by persistent automotive segment weakness and uncertain CEO transition dynamics that cannot be fully resolved until the March 2027 earnings release.

The group's earnings trajectory depends critically on whether Belron's compounding growth in vehicle glass repair and advanced driver-assistance systems calibration services can sustainably offset the sharp deterioration in D'Ieteren Automotive's profitability and cyclical softness across industrial parts distribution.

Historical guidance performance demonstrates a consistent pattern of conservative initial targets followed by upward revisions or beats across fiscal years 2022 through 2025, which establishes a credible baseline expectation that management will deliver on its low-to-mid-single-digit growth guidance for fiscal year 2026 despite the automotive retail slump.

However, the severity of the first-half 2026 automotive profit collapse, which contracted by 66.6 percent to EUR 36.4 million, represents a structural headwind that significantly constrains near-term consensus upgrade potential and raises fundamental questions about the sustainability of the division's medium-term margin targets.
