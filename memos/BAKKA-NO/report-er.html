Section 2 – Sell-side and market focus prior to these recent earnings

Section summary
  1. Sell-side expectations were highly sensitive to volatile biological execution in Scotland and the timing of Faroese post-smolt volume benefits. Scottish operational recovery – high execution risk: Prior to the earnings release, the sell-side focus was heavily anchored on the long-term margin recovery potential of Farming Scotland, which remained a key source of estimate risk. Biological headwinds – Pasteurella drag: The market was highly focused on the aftermath of the Pasteurella outbreak at the Portree site in Scotland, which was harvested out in October. Divergent biological performance – Faroese stability: Analysts highlighted a clear operational contrast, with the Faroese operations exhibiting strong biological performance and record-high biomass. Earnings volatility – high sensitivity to volume: The sell-side remained cautious regarding historical estimate misses, leading some analysts to build a larger margin of safety into their financial models. Peer performance comparison – structural efficiency gap: Going into the earnings, the market scrutinized Bakkafrost's low return on capital employed relative to peers such as Mowi and SalMar.

Large post-smolt strategy – scale benefits: Investors were looking for evidence that the massive investment program in freshwater hatcheries would successfully transition marine biomass to larger smolt. Faroese tax changes – regulatory overhang: The market closely monitored potential structural changes or increased taxation regarding conventional salmon farming in the Faroe Islands. Scottish mortality reduction – de-risking scorecard: Investors evaluated whether the 2024 de-risking strategy in Scotland would yield sustained biological stability into 2025 and 2026.

Reference pricing dynamics – large fish premium: Analysts focused on Bakkafrost's ability to capture premium pricing, which was heavily dependent on the proportion of harvest-ready fish above 4kg. Smolt release targets – freshwater capacity ramp-up: The sell-side tracked the operational progress of the Applecross hatchery in Scotland as the foundation for future harvest growth.
