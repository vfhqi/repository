  2. Akelius cemented control through a sweeping board overhaul at the July 2025 EGM, electing aligned trustees to direct strategy.

Forced CEO departure: Joacim Sjöberg was ousted as CEO in August 2025 after major shareholder pressure from Akelius.
2.

3.
CFO transition: Jens Andersson left as CFO in late 2025, following the rapid change in executive leadership.
4.
Acting CFO promotion: Christoffer Strömbäck became acting CFO in 2025, combining financial reporting with transactions management.
5.
Permanent CFO appointment: Christoffer Strömbäck was confirmed as permanent CFO by July 2026, securing his executive position.

Regional management departure: Ola Orsmark will leave Region Öresund by October 2026, highlighting ongoing second-level management turnover.

Board Overhaul and Governance Realignment

Board purge: Roger Akelius forced the resignations of Chairman Louise Richnau and Stefan Ränk after hiking his stake to 25%.
8.
Chairman election: Ralf Spann, CEO of Akelius Residential Property, was elected Chairman on July 18, 2025, ensuring tight owner alignment.
9.
Board refreshment: Stefan Wallander and Knut Rost joined the board in July 2025, adding institutional real estate and banking depth.

Other side of the story: aggressive executive turnover may fracture corporate culture; a loss of regional leasing talent could prevent vacancy reduction targets.

Section summary
  1. Major shareholder Akelius enforced a board restructuring and dividend suspension, pivoting capital allocation strictly toward asset sales and share buybacks.
  2. Castellum's "Back to Basics" program targets a minimum 10% return on equity by aggressively cutting costs and prioritizing active leasing. Net read: Heavy asset recycling, including the dilutive SEK 13.32 billion Skåne portfolio disposal, sacrifices long- term commercial scale to fund short-term buybacks.

2.
Restructuring demands: The activist demanded the immediate departure of former CEO Joacim Sjöberg and pushed for a three-way company split.
3.
Board capture: Akelius successfully elected Ralf Spann as Board Chairman during the July 2025 EGM to direct the strategic transition.
4.
Capital reallocation demand: The campaign successfully pressured the suspension of cash dividends in favor of active share buyback programs over distributions.
5.

  3. Green designations and sustainability ratings attract premium capital flows in a selective transaction market
  4. Stricter ESG and EU Taxonomy compliance threaten older, non-compliant assets with regulatory obsolescence
  5. Significant project developments like Infinity stabilize cash flows via large pre-lets to anchor tenants
  6. The Nordic property cycle faces asset valuation writedowns as investment yields expand outward
  7. Persistent STIBOR 3m volatility inflates interest expenses given floating debt exposures

Outliers worth noting

Castellum strategically terminated its S&P credit rating while maintaining Moody's Baa2 stable rating, creating a single-agency dependency that is highly unusual among Nordic investment-grade real estate companies where dual-rating coverage remains the market standard for institutional bond issuers. This creates credit market opacity and potential pricing disadvantages during refinancing cycles compared to peers maintaining comprehensive rating coverage.
