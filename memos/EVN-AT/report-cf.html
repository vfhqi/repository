EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 38 findings

EVN AG presents a stable investment thesis anchored by refreshed executive leadership steering a EUR 1 billion annual capital program through 2030, operating within a framework of 90% collective bargaining coverage and exceptional workforce retention averaging 15 years of service. The company's governance structure is constitutionally protected by the Province of Lower Austria's 51% majority stake combined with Wiener Stadtwerke's 28.4% syndicated position, completely insulating the utility from hostile activist campaigns and change-of-control events. The March 2026 divestment of the international environmental project business for EUR 100 million unlocked a EUR 206 million positive cash effect while eliminating volatile exposures, though the successful transaction simultaneously strengthened the balance sheet and simplified the corporate structure.
