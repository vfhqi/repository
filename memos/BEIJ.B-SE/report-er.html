Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side expectations ahead of the third quarter focused heavily on an organic growth inflection driven by hot European summer weather.
  2. North American platform expansion and the integration of Enertrak emerged as critical drivers to offset residential HVAC market softness.

1.
Strong historical foundation: Beijer Ref was founded in 1866 by Gottfried Beijer and Lorens Beijer as a Swedish trading company. Headquartered in Malmö, Sweden, the company has grown into a global leader in temperature control. The group maintains a highly decentralized operational structure with more than 170 subsidiaries. Beijer Ref's extensive network now spans 45 countries across five continents.
2.
Specialized business model: Beijer Ref operates primarily as a specialized wholesaler of refrigeration and air conditioning products. The business focuses on commercial and industrial refrigeration alongside comfort cooling solutions. Wholesaling activities are complemented by proprietary manufacturing of green cooling systems. The group distributes products from leading international manufacturers to professional installers.

Diverse segment mix: Beijer Ref divides its operations into air conditioning and heating, commercial refrigeration, and own manufacturing. Comfort cooling and heating represented 62% of net sales in fiscal year 2025. Commercial and industrial refrigeration accounted for 31% of the group's sales. Original equipment manufacturing solutions using natural refrigerants contributed the remaining 8% of sales.
4.
Robust revenue expansion: Beijer Ref generated SEK 37,067 million in net sales during 2025. Net sales represented a 4% increase compared to SEK 35,662 million in 2024. Sales in 2024 grew by approximately 11% compared to SEK 32,150 million in 2023. Organic sales growth reached 3% in 2025, up from 1.9% in 2024.

Expanding operating profitability: Beijer Ref reported EBITA of SEK 3,962 million in fiscal year 2025. Operating profitability grew 5% compared to SEK 3,776 million in 2024. Profitability in 2024 stood 11% higher than SEK 3,398 million in 2023. The underlying EBITA margin expanded slightly to 10.7% in fiscal year 2025 from 10.6% in 2024.

Disparate peer scale: Beijer Ref competes globally against much larger, specialized building and plumbing distributors. Ferguson reported net sales of $31.3 billion in calendar year 2025. Watsco continues to lead the highly fragmented North American market as its largest independent HVAC-R distributor. Lennox remains a dominant integrated manufacturer and distributor in the American residential market.
7.
Strong cash generation: Beijer Ref generated record-high full-year operating cash flow of SEK 4,400 million in 2025. Cash conversion and working capital optimization helped the group outpace SEK 3,464 million in 2024. Free cash flow yield was projected by analysts to reach 2.7% in 2026 and 3.9% in 2027. Strong cash generation provides the group with ample capital to fund its roll-up strategy.

Outstanding share performance: Beijer Ref delivered a total share return of 2.3% over the 12 months preceding October 2026. Share performance over the 3 months and 1 month preceding October 2026 was 9.6% and 3.6% respectively. Beijer Ref's stock outpaced major peers who suffered from broader market derating. Watsco posted a negative 12-month total share return of 10.3%, while Lennox registered a negative 12-month return of 24.8% in the same period.

Fluctuating price targets: Jefferies maintained a price target of SEK 200 on Beijer Ref in January 2026. Steady expectations reflected confidence in improving European organic growth trends.
11.
Reaccelerating M&A engine: Beijer Ref deployed capital into numerous strategic acquisitions throughout 2025 and 2026. The group integrated seven acquisitions in 2025, supporting regional scale. Bolt-on activity continued in 2026 with key additions such as AM Distributors in South Florida and Enertrak in Canada. Platform expansion remains a critical driver of the company's long-term earnings compounding model.

Promising sustainability tailwinds: Beijer Ref's long-term growth is supported by structural demand for environmentally friendly HVAC-R solutions. Tightening European F-gas phase-out regulations are driving replacement cycles toward sustainable solutions. Propelled by these rules, proprietary OEM sales based on natural refrigerants represent a high-margin growth vertical.
