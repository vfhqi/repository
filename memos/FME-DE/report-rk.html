The most significant plausible problem to monitor emerges from the persistent deterioration in US same-market treatment growth, which worsened from negative 0.4% in Q1 2026 to negative 0.9% in Q2 2026, representing a company-specific operational referral conversion bottleneck rather than general market dialysis demand issues. The severity of this signal is amplified by management's complete inability to deliver the promised second-half recovery, indicating that the referral capture problems are systemic rather than transient. This pattern suggests that management may be systematically categorizing normal operating expenses as special items to artificially inflate adjusted performance metrics, which directly contradicts the narrative of successful operational restructuring.
