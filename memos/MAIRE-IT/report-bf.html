EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

MAIRE operates a dual-engine business model combining high-volume, low-margin EPC construction with highmargin, asset-light technology licensing, creating a complementary risk-return profile that distinguishes it from traditional engineering contractors. The integrated model combines low-margin construction with high-margin technology licensing, boosting the overall competitive moat, while highly profitable technology licenses provide a resilient and capital-light stream with exceptionally high profit margins.

& Securities The company's Sustainable Technology Solutions segment, led by Stamicarbon and Nextchem, delivers EBITDA margins of 23-26% through proprietary process patents covering over 2,600 equivalents, while the Integrated E&C Solutions segment executes multi-billion-dollar lump-sum projects at 5.6-5.8% margins. The technology licensing business achieves EBITDA margins of 24.70% in FY 2025, contrasted with typical execution margins of 5.60% to 5.80% for the Integrated E&C segment. & Securities Leadership under CEO Alessandro Bernini has driven exceptional value creation, with the share price increasing approximately 165% since May 2022, while simultaneously achieving unprecedented backlog growth to €15.7 billion and upgrading strategic targets for three consecutive years.

CEO Alessandro Bernini has successfully steered the group since May 2022, with the company's share price tripling in the two years following his appointment.

The key unresolved question centers on whether this return profile represents a permanent structural shift or a cyclical peak driven by the unprecedented $8.7 billion ADNOC Hail & Ghasha contract that distorts near-term execution economics.
