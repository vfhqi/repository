Executive Overview and Period under Review

First-Half 2026 (1H26) Financial Performance

Revenue delivery – consensus outperformance: Forvia printed 1H26 sales of €10,509m, which fell 4.3% on a reported basis but exceeded the Visible Alpha consensus of €10,299m by 2%. Organic volume trajectory – modest market outperformance: The group's 1.9% organic sales decline outpaced the global light vehicle production contraction of 1.0% by 90 basis points, supported by a supportive geographical mix. Operating income – margin expansion beat: Operating income reached €632m, representing an operating margin of 6.0% which beat the Visible Alpha consensus of 5.8% by 20 basis points.

Cash flow dynamics – investment timing: The major cash flow beat was driven by capital expenditure and capitalized R&D falling to 4.8% of sales, though management expects this to catch up in the second half of 2026 to reach the full-year target of 6.0% to 6.5%. Full-Year 2025 (FY25) Financial Performance
