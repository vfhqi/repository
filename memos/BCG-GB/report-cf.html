Outliers worth noting

Baltic Classifieds Group exhibits several exceptional operational and financial characteristics that place it at the extreme ends of relevant distribution curves when benchmarked against European classified marketplace peers and broader sectoral norms. The most striking outlier is the group's 78.0% EBITDA margin in FY26, which positions it substantially above the European classifieds peer median of approximately 60.1% and materially ahead of premium operators like Rightmove at 70.0% and Scout24 at 60.1%.

This margin structure reflects both the group's near-monopoly traffic positions across core Baltic verticals and the extremely low variable cost structure inherent in its prepaid listing model, where negative working capital dynamics and minimal sales and marketing expenses create an operating leverage profile that sits in the top 5% of global online marketplace businesses. The underlying EBITDA margin of 78.0% far exceeds Scout24's organic EBITDA margins of 62.5% and Rightmove's underlying margin of 70%.

A second material outlier emerges in the group's 99.0% cash conversion rate, which again places Baltic Classifieds at the upper bound of European classified operators and reflects the structural advantages of its fully prepaid business model. Adjusted cash conversion was maintained at 99% in FY26, matching Hemnet's top-tier operational cash performance. This exceptional cash generation quality is further amplified by the group's negative working capital position, where customers prepay for listing packages and the business carries virtually zero debtor risk.

The combination of industry-leading margins and near-perfect cash conversion creates an unmatched free cash flow yield profile that supports aggressive debt-funded capital returns while maintaining conservative leverage ratios well below covenant thresholds.
