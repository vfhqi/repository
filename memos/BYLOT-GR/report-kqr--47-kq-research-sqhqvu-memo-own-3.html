Section 2 – Valuation and Leverage Trajectories of Evoke and Entain

Section summary
  1. Historical market trends indicate that levered online-gambling consolidators like Evoke and Entain experience significant valuation multiple compression when leverage exceeds target thresholds during regulatory and tax shocks.
  2. Valuation multiples for highly geared operators recover only after sustained operational mitigation, deleveraging milestones, and clear regulatory resolutions are established.

Evoke Valuation and Leverage

1.
Depressed multiples: Standalone 888 Holdings traded at 5.1x EV/EBITDA and a 9.8% free cash flow yield in 2021 before its transformational acquisition of William Hill was finalized. This conservative initial valuation reflected an ongoing market discount associated with self-imposed safer gambling measures in the United Kingdom. The pre-deal valuation highlights that highly focused online operators often carry moderate multiples even before taking on significant debt. Investors used this baseline to measure subsequent postacquisition multiple compression once leverage increased.
2.
Elevated leverage: Evoke experienced severe balance sheet pressure as its leverage target of under 3.5x net debt to EBITDA was delayed to FY27 during the William Hill integration. Deutsche Bank Research reported in April 2025 that the company's shares traded at an inexpensive 5.3x FY25 EV/EBITDA due to soft trading and integration delays. In late 2024, the broker had already highlighted that the low multiple was compelling despite a cautious outlook on the earnings trajectory. This persistent discount demonstrates how high leverage suppresses equity multiples over extended periods.
3.
Compressed multiples: Evoke initiated a strategic review immediately after the November 2025 UK Budget because high leverage and rising duties severely compromised its deleveraging path. Bernstein Research reported in December 2025 that the combination of existing debt and the tax shock rendered standalone equity stories increasingly untenable for highly levered mid-tier operators. The broker noted that thin-margin businesses cannot simultaneously fund customer acquisition and absorb higher remote gaming duties. Consequently, these regulatory pressures are highly likely to trigger forced asset sales and consolidations.

Entain Post-Acquisition and Duty Cycles

4.
Moderate gearing: Entain (then GVC Holdings) experienced a leverage increase to 3.0x net debt to EBITDA in FY19 following its £1.4 billion debt-funded acquisition of Ladbrokes Coral. This leverage spike occurred alongside regulatory downgrades from the UK Triennial Review which capped maximum Fixed Odds Betting Terminal stakes at £2. In response, Entain's valuation multiple adjusted to a forward EV/EBITDA of 7.5x and a price-to-earnings multiple of 10.0x by early 2019. This transition highlights how regulatory changes can offset expected synergy-driven multiple expansions during integration phases.
5.
Resilient multiples: Entain maintained its long-term cash generation targets in late 2026 despite facing persistent UK tax headwinds across both retail and online divisions. Morgan Stanley reported in October 2026 that the operator's valuation remained compelling at 7.8x forward EBITDA as it navigated the Machine Gaming Duty increase to 25%. The broker estimated that the unmitigated impact of the duty rise would be manageable at approximately £25 million. This regulatory clarity represents a key clearing event that supports future valuation multiple expansion.

Section 3 – Peers and Consolidation Multiples: Flutter, Lottomatica, and Others

Section summary
  1. Equity markets value levered gambling consolidators at significant discounts during localized tax shocks, but multiples recover once regulatory resolution is achieved.
  2. Large transaction multiples like Evoke's acquisition at 6.3x EBITDA highlight that scale consolidation is completed at highly attractive valuations.
  3. Defensive lottery-led peers trade at stable mid-single-digit EBITDA multiples, demonstrating superior resilience and robust capital return profiles compared to high-beta sports betting operators.
  4. Investment analysts apply distinct sum-of-the-parts frameworks to Ballys Intralot S.A. by valuing its stable B2B lottery segment at a premium or discount relative to the higher-beta B2C iGaming division.
  5. Our confidence in this broker valuation consensus is medium because while the primary coverage from Jefferies and Deutsche Bank provides detailed segment models, research did not surface valuations from Barclays or Optima Bank.
