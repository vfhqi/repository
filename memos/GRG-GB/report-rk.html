Section 10 – Pre-Mortem: Executive Summary

While headline metrics such as first-half profits of seventy-six million pounds and robust market share gains superficially support continued expansion, the deeper structural evidence reveals that Greggs' apparent operational success disguises fundamental volume weakness that threatens the long-term viability of its capital-intensive vertically integrated model.
