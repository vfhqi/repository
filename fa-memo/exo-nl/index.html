Revenue growth is against the same period a year earlier. This company uses the operating profit form: its financials block shows no EBITDA in its last four reporting periods. Operating profit is after depreciation and amortisation, except the amortisation of acquired intangibles, as adjusted earnings are. For banks and insurers, interest is part of the business, so operating profit is normally close to pre-tax profit.

Basis of these inputs (read before using them). EXOR is an IFRS 10 investment entity. All inputs are on the net cash earnings basis that the brokers' adjusted EPS uses, and exclude fair-value changes on investments. Revenue is dividend income from the holdings (including the part of the Philips dividend taken in shares) plus interest and other income on cash, consistent with FactSet's revenue line since FY2024 (FY2025 base: €790m). Operating profit is revenue less head-office costs (about €50m a year; EXOR has no material depreciation). Net financial items are interest on gross debt (interest income on cash sits in revenue, as in FactSet's lines). The FactSet history lines for EBIT and net income include fair-value changes (FY2025 EBIT minus €3,595m; H1 2026 minus €1,114m) and, before FY2024, consolidated industrial revenue; the model's implied margin history is therefore not comparable with these inputs. The dividend per share is the dividend declared for the fiscal year and paid the following spring (FactSet dates it by payment period). The share-count change is treasury shares transferred to employees under incentive plans (no new shares are issued; plans are serviced from treasury).

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Ferrari: adjusted EPS growth of about 10% in 2026 and 9% to 10% a year after (Ferrari guidance FY2026 revenue about €7.65bn, EBIT margin 29.8%; 2027 mix dilution from the 296 Challenge Stradale and Amalfi per Jefferies, 22 November 2026); 40% payout; dividend per share paid about €3.95 (2027) to €5.16 (2030).
  • Philips: dividend per share about €0.88 to €1.00 (stable to increasing policy); EXOR's stake rises from about 18.9% towards 22% through scrip and about €600m of purchases in 2027; about half of each dividend taken in shares (non-cash revenue of about €80m to €105m a year).
  • CNH: recovery from the agricultural trough from 2027 (FY2026 EPS guidance $0.44 to $0.48; dealer destocking ending; mid-cycle EPS about $1.00 per Morgan Stanley); payout about 25%; EUR/USD about 1.16.
  • Stellantis: no dividend paid in 2026 or 2027 (Q3 2026 industrial cash outflow of about €4bn; US inventory 99 days); a token €0.10 a share in 2028 on FY2027 results; about €0.25 and €0.33 in 2029 and 2030. This is the largest single source of forecast error from 2028.

How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
