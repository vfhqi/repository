EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus EPS

1.
Consensus sits above the guided midpoint: Sell-side consensus set a baseline FY2027 adjusted EPS of €2.37 before the full-year release. Management guided €2.20–€2.40, so the €2.30 midpoint sits about 3% below consensus. After the results, estimates broadly match guidance, leaving little room for earnings-driven upside. Post-results estimate cuts were systematic: Brokers cut FY2027 adjusted EPS to €2.31 (Bernstein), €2.33 (Deutsche Bank), €2.32 (Jefferies), €2.28 (Morgan Stanley) and €2.28 (Redburn).
