EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Yubico stands at a critical strategic inflection point, having executed a 10% workforce reduction and completed a comprehensive C-suite transition while simultaneously pivoting from perpetual hardware sales to subscriptionbased revenue models. The company delivered a decisive Q2 2026 turnaround, with organic sales growth recovering to 11.7% and operating margins expanding to 14.4%, yet the near-term trajectory remains constrained by upfront subscription COGS front-loading, unhedged USD/SEK currency exposures, and prolonged enterprise purchasing cycles.

The global restructuring executed in Q1 2026 reduced headcount by approximately 10% while net sales declined 8.8% in H1 2026 to SEK 1,023.2 million, before organic sales grew 11.7% in Q2 2026 to SEK 543.9 million.

The materiality of change centers on three simultaneous transformations that compound both opportunity and execution risk. Leadership transitions included Jerrod Chong assuming the CEO role in December 2025, finalized as permanent CEO on May 4, 2026, while Yubico is shifting from perpetual hardware sales to subscription-based YubiKey as a Service models. The restructuring completed in Q1 2026 reduced the global workforce by approximately 10%, impacting 45 employees, with management expecting the workforce reduction to deliver SEK 95 million in annualized cost savings. These organizational changes unfold against an increasingly favorable regulatory backdrop, where global regulatory frameworks are shifting from high-level guidance to strict enforcement of phishing-resistant authentication, with the EU NIS2 Directive mandating strong multi-factor authentication across 15 critical sectors starting October 2024.

The five to eight key forward EPS drivers exhibit differentiated plausibility and dependency profiles, with subscription ARR growth and sovereign regulatory mandates commanding the highest confidence while agentic AI authorization remains nascent. First, subscription ARR expansion represents the primary revenue visibility engine, with Annual Recurring Revenue expanding to SEK 411.8 million in Q2 2026, up 4.4% year-over-year.

Subscription-based orders expanded to reach 28% of total bookings in FY25, with YubiKey as a Service bookings accounting for 24% of total bookings in Q2 2026. Second, regulatory compliance mandates drive immediate procurement urgency, as NIS2 enforces penalties up to EUR 10 million or 2% of annual global revenue for noncompliance, while the Central Bank of UAE directed financial institutions to eliminate SMS and email passwords by March 2026.

Third, post-quantum cryptography product replacement cycles accelerate hardware refresh demands, with US Department of Defense guidance uniquely authorizing YubiKey 5 FIPS to support both DoD PKI and FIDO2. Fourth, agentic AI authorization workflows present medium-term upside, as Yubico launched a partnership with OpenAI in April 2026, with OpenAI mandating hardware-backed passkeys for its Trusted Access for Cyber program on June 1, 2026.

Fifth, headcount cost synergies provide near-term margin protection, with annualized opex savings of SEK 95 million realized beginning in Q2 2026.

Recent earnings delivery demonstrates marked sequential acceleration following a weak Q1 2026, with Q2 2026 results significantly exceeding sell-side expectations across revenue, profitability, and bookings metrics. Net sales reached SEK 543.9 million in Q2 2026, beating consensus of SEK 478.0 million. Geographic performance reveals differentiated momentum, with EMEA sales representing 27.6% of net sales in Q2 2026, expanding from 19.3% in Q2 2025, driven by a robust partner network and significant defense contract activity.

Forward guidance credibility warrants cautious assessment, as management maintains ambitious long-term targets of 25% annual sales growth and 20% EBIT margins despite persistent underperformance against these benchmarks.
