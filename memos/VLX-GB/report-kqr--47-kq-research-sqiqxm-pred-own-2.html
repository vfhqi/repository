Section 3 – Rack Programmes, Cable Generations, and Technical Specifications

Section summary
  1. Volex provides high-speed copper interconnects across multiple cable generations up to 1.6T speeds to support major AI and hyperscaler architectures.
  2. The company's advanced analog active copper cables extend copper reach with significantly lower power consumption compared to digital active electrical cables.
  3. While major competitors showcase advanced co-packaged copper designs, historical qualification delays reveal that next-generation speeds present integration hurdles.

Pluggable Cable Platforms and

1.
Speeds High-speed platforms: Volex designs and manufactures pluggable copper interconnects supporting Ethernet, InfiniBand, PCIe, and SAS standards at speeds up to 800 Gbps, with 1.6T systems currently under active development. The high-speed twin-ax assemblies undergo 100% signal integrity testing before factory shipment to guarantee optimal performance under intense workloads. This strict testing regime underpins Volex's ability to maintain market-leading high-speed signal integrity as port speeds accelerate.
2.
Advanced passive options: Volex's 1.6T OSFP1600 passive Direct Attach Copper cable features 8 transmitting and 8 receiving lanes operating at 224 Gbps PAM4 per lane. This product meets the IEEE 802.3df and 802.3dj standards with a substantial signal integrity margin, delivering high-density routing with near-zero power consumption. The zero-power profile is highly competitive compared to optical transceivers, helping delay expensive optical transitions in next-generation datacenters.
3.
Analog active cables: Volex has historically collaborated with Spectra7 to deliver next-generation 800 Gbps Active Copper Cables using Spectra7's GC1122 GaugeChanger analog chips. These active analog copper assemblies successfully compensate for high-frequency signal loss over four meters of thin twin-ax copper cable. This partnership enables Volex to offer highly cost-effective alternative designs to expensive, power-intensive DSP-based retimed active cables.
4.
Efficient power profiles: Morgan Stanley reports that active copper cables using analog re-drivers consume only 1W to 3W per end, which is significantly lower than active electrical cables. By comparison, digital active electrical cables utilizing active retimers consume approximately 7W to 10W per end, which increases datacenter cooling burdens. This power gap is highly critical inside dense AI server racks because elevated power consumption accelerates thermal constraints and limits compute density.
5.
Robust active options: Jefferies reports that Credo is the dominant provider of active electrical cables with an estimated 70% to 80% global market share. Credo's manufacturing partner BizLink produces these assemblies in Malaysia, integrating DSP-based retimers to stretch copper reach up to seven meters. These retimer chips provide full adaptive equalization and clock data recovery to ensure uncompromised digital signal integrity over longer inter-rack distances.
