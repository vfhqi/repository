EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

CHANGE MATERIALITY

Asmodee Group AB has undergone a fundamental transformation following its spin-off from Embracer Group in early 2025, establishing a focused, execution-oriented leadership team to drive standalone strategy while preserving deep operational continuity through internal executive promotions. The company appointed Thomas Koegler as Chief Executive Officer in August 2024 and elevated Andrea Gasparini to Chief Financial Officer, creating an integrated management structure that operates within a highly concentrated competitive landscape where Asmodee ranks as the second-largest tabletop player globally behind Hasbro, with the top four players controlling nearly 40% of the market and a moated specialty distribution network reaching over 7,000 hobby stores.

The strategic recalibration has repositioned Asmodee as a high-margin tabletop specialist with robust organic growth acceleration, though the business remains structurally dependent on volatile trading card game cycles and partner-published franchises that introduce significant quarterly revenue volatility.

TOP FORWARD DRIVERS (5-8 CRITICAL FACTORS)

The forward earnings trajectory is anchored by the upcoming 30th Pokémon anniversary celebrations expected to drive exceptional trading card sales during FY27, representing the largest single top-line catalyst with an estimated 10 percentage point sales impact. The ATM Gaming acquisition delivers an estimated 350 basis points of sales contribution alongside an outstanding 50% EBITDA margin profile, providing material margin mix improvement and accelerating social board game segment expansion.

US retailer inventory normalization supports an anticipated 5% increase in board game sales as distributor stock levels return to healthy positions following prior-year excess builds, restoring positive sell-in dynamics across North American channels. Structural interest expense reductions from debt refinancing secure EUR 5 million in annual savings through a 4.25% fixed coupon that lowers financing costs by 150 basis points, directly enhancing free cash flow generation and debt coverage ratios.

Crowdfunding platform expansion provides unprecedented demand visibility and working capital benefits, with campaigns like Zombicide Dead Men Tales raising over USD 4 million on Gamefound while reducing inventory write-down risks through upfront consumer commitments. Transmedia brand extensions through partnerships with platforms like Netflix drive massive physical sales flywheels, creating complementary revenue streams that reinforce core tabletop demand.

The robust Magic: The Gathering release pipeline with seven card set releases scheduled for 2026 underpins sustained partner-published momentum throughout the fiscal year.

RECENT DELIVERY PERFORMANCE

Asmodee delivered exceptional 25.9% organic growth in FY26 that substantially beat the mid-single-digit target, though standalone adjusted EBITDA margin missed the 18% target at 17.0% due to partner-published product mix dilution, while year-end leverage reached 1.5x adjusted EBITDA and surpassed long-term targets below 2.0x. The first quarter of FY27 saw net sales surpass consensus by 6% with strong operational execution post-spin, supported by robust free cash flow that reached 71% conversion over three years and enabled proposed dividends of EUR 0.17 per share.

Quarterly organic sales grew 20.2% year on year driven by board games and trading card games, with partner-published games net sales increasing by 27.0% in Q1 FY27 as retailer inventory levels in the US normalized and drove better sell-in growth. The beat magnitude against consensus expectations demonstrates accelerating operational momentum, though Star Wars Unlimited set postponement delayed EUR 10 million in revenue into Q2, illustrating material quarterly pipeline volatility from product timing effects.

FORWARD CREDIBILITY ASSESSMENT

Management targets average mid-single-digit annual organic sales growth over the three-to-five-year period with adjusted EBITDA margins exceeding 18% in the medium term, capital expenditure-to-sales of 2% to 3%, and net debt below 2.0x EBITDA over the long term. Historical performance consistently exceeded mid-term targets as organic growth reached 25.9% in the fiscal year far outperforming the mid-single-digit target, adjusted EBITDA margin reached 17.0% expanding towards the 18% goal, capital expenditure was only 1.4% of net sales beating the guided 2-3% range, and year-end leverage reached 1.5x adjusted EBITDA after M&A meeting long-term targets ahead of schedule.

DNB Carnegie notes management could reach the EBITDA margin target above 18% this year, reflecting high analyst confidence in the medium-term financial trajectory. However, directly owned titles generate on average double the operating margin of distributed partner games, while Pokémon represents approximately 20% of net sales and Magic: The Gathering represents approximately 15% of net sales without long-term guarantees, introducing material execution risk if primary franchise relationships deteriorate and severely damage standalone profitability trajectories . SELL-SIDE POSITIONING AND SENTIMENT

Four core brokerage houses actively cover Asmodee standalone representing a robust European mid-cap coverage pool, with Bloomberg consensus as of August 2026 exhibiting 57% Buy ratings, 39% Hold, and 4% Sell recommendations, while the consensus price target stands at SEK 181 with wide absolute dispersion ranging from SEK 135 to SEK 187. Bernstein analyst Marie-Line Fort rates Asmodee Outperform and raised her target to a street-high SEK 173.90 in July 2026, arguing that robust organic growth driving operating leverage confirms Asmodee as a rare consumer growth stock.

DNB Carnegie analyst Martin Arnell maintains a Buy rating and increased his target price to SEK 187 in August 2026, highlighting six consecutive quarters of strong organic growth and emerging cash distribution potential. The dominant bull narrative positions Asmodee as uniquely positioned as a high-growth and defensive market leader in tabletop gaming globally, leveraging unrivaled global distribution networks spanning more than 100 countries internationally, with the 30th Pokémon anniversary expected to drive exceptional trading card sales during FY27.

The dominant bear narrative emphasizes intense concentration risks as two third-party IPs represent 35% of total sales, with Asmodee holding no longterm exclusive contracts for its primary third-party distributed franchises, while peak inventory build-ups limit firsthalf free cash flow generation. Severe consensus groupthink is building around the Pokémon 30th anniversary tailwind, creating material downside risk if card sales disappoint as the pool faces abrupt and synchronized target price downgrades .

PEER-RELATIVE POSITIONING

Asmodee occupies the top-quartile of fundamental momentum across peer groups driven by partner trading card outperformance, delivering 20.2% organic revenue growth in Q1 that places it in the top decile of the global tabletop sector and substantially exceeds Mattel's constant-currency target of 3% to 6% and Hasbro's low-singledigit guidance. Scalable distribution networks protect standalone operating leverage relative to margin compression at traditional toy manufacturers, with Asmodee adjusted EBITDA margin reaching 14.7% in Q1 FY27 versus the toy sector median of 11.4%, though this trails Games Workshop's industry-leading 39.1% operating profit margins that demonstrate unmatched premium wargaming economics.

Asmodee free cash flow conversion averaged 70% over three years versus capital-intensive toy manufacturer peers, reflecting a highly capital-light operational footprint with capital expenditures remaining between 1.4% and 3.0% of net sales beating the industry average. Critical plausible problem to monitor include Pokémon and Magic representing 35% of total sales without long-term exclusive contract guarantees versus peers, net working capital rises creating severe seasonal cash flow restriction before holiday product releases, and own-studio board game growth sluggish at 1.8% year on year trailing peer Games Workshop's double-digit wargaming growth.

Operational outliers include Pokémon representing approximately 20% of sales through a completely contract-less 20-year old relationship unmatched by peers' typical agreements, Asmodee averaging an exceptional 71% cash conversion over three years while capital-intensive peers typically convert below 40%, and its top two third-party franchises representing approximately 35% of total sales as an extreme outlier versus diversified peers. - Earnings Calls CRITICAL RISK CONCENTRATION

Uncontracted IP and high concentration represent the single most material structural vulnerability if relationships abruptly dissolve as this would collapse Asmodee's cash model and leave its distribution infrastructure stranded , creating catastrophic downside to the standalone investment thesis and invalidating the current sell-side consensus price targets.
