Section 8 – If the Answer Differs from the Common View

Section summary
  1. The evidence gathered in this report supports a consensus path of recovering earnings, but that path rests on a narrow set of assumptions and has thin sell-side verification. If the Key Question is answered differently from the broker view, the main exposure is to EPS in 2027 and 2028, because the consensus ramp is steepest in those years. The EPS downside is likely to come less from one dramatic failure than from several modest shortfalls arriving together: slower equipment conversion, a delayed services ramp, and costs that have already been added. The confidence behind this assessment is medium to low, because the report found no broker research beyond a single UBS data set. The common view, as far as it can be reconstructed, is a sharp rebound from a depressed base. EPS fell from CHF 1.73 in 2024 to CHF 0.72 in 2025 due to low capacity utilization. UBS estimates from 29 September 2026 expect EPS to rebound to CHF 1.54 in 2026. Those estimates then project acceleration to CHF 1.88 in 2027 and CHF 2.40 by 2028.
