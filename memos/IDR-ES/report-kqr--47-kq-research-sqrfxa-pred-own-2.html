Section summary

Payments

Administrative and Milestone Flow

1.
Favorable structure: Indra manages the pre-financing advances through specialized accounts established for each individual program. Chief Financial Officer Miguel Forteza explained on October 30, 2025, that the Ministry of Industry deposits the funds into restricted, independent cash accounts. These restricted balances are only released to active cash as work progresses, which governs the actual cash flow generation of the defense segment. This structured release mechanism directly protects the Treasury from early spending without delivery.
2.
Rigid milestones: The release of funds from restricted accounts is tightly coupled with technical milestones verified by the state. According to Indra's Q3 2025 earnings transcript, the cash is only made available once the Spanish Ministry of Defence authorizes certified milestones. This administrative flow ensures that the group's recorded working capital aligns with real physical progress rather than arbitrary timelines. Consequently, milestone delays directly postpone cash release and could negatively affect shortterm earnings per share.
3.
Systemic delays: Slowing execution speeds present a notable risk to the timely conversion of pre-financing into actual revenue. Bernstein Research highlighted on October 31, 2025, that delays at TESS Defence for land combat vehicles create ongoing execution risks. Repayment and Offsetting Mechanics

4.
Automatic matching: The actual repayment of pre-financing does not involve outward cash payments from Indra to the Treasury. Indra's Q4 2025 annual report states that repayment is secured by contracts and executed through an automatic offsetting of flows. When works are completed and accepted, the Ministry of Defence payments are automatically matched against the outstanding Ministry of Industry loans. This structural design is critical because it prevents actual cash outflows for loan amortization.
5.
Acceptance- based billing: The administrative flow concludes when the defense contractor delivers the completed systems to the Armed Forces. According to a news report from September 13, 2026, defense companies invoice their products only upon physical delivery. Once the invoice is generated, the Ministry of Defence initiates the payment, which is used to settle the pre-financing liability with the Ministry of Industry. This deliverydependent timeline means any production holdup delays the entire repayment and liability clearance process.
6.
Secured alignment: The alignment between project milestones and loan amortization schedules creates high financial stability. Indra's financial reports on April 27, 2026, confirmed that the repayment schedule matches project collections. This matching principle guarantees that Indra is never forced to repay prefinancing prior to receiving customer cash or billing approval. Consequently, the group avoids liquidity strain even if the overall modernization programs extend over multiple years.
