Comprehensive bundles obfuscate the explicit pricing of individual services like pay-TV, which makes consumer comparison shopping significantly more difficult. Consequently, high fixed-broadband and mobile convergence represents the primary defense mechanism for preserving retail telecom revenues after content changes.
