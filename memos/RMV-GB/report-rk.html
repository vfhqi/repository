Outliers worth noting

Rightmove's bear case analysis reveals three extreme statistical outliers that expose fundamental contradictions between the company's dominant consumer engagement metrics and its structural inability to convert market power into proportional revenue capture. These anomalies highlight unexplained disconnects that warrant deep investor scrutiny, as they represent the most statistically improbable data configurations across the entire bear case evidence base.
