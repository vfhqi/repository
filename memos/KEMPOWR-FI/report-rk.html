Section 10 – Pre-Mortem: Executive Summary

This profile describes a scenario where the company appears to hit superficial growth milestones–particularly through expanding order backlogs and sustained revenue generation–yet systematically fails to convert these achievements into positive operational cash flows or sustainable earnings power.

The Analysis of Competing Hypotheses matrix evaluated three distinct failure profiles: YES (bull case recovery), NO (outright business failure), and FALSE FRIEND (misleading operational indicators). The YES profile was rejected due to five hard inconsistencies with actual cash flow trends and margin trajectories, while the NO profile carried three hard inconsistencies given ongoing sequential gross margin improvements and solid backlog expansion. The FALSE FRIEND hypothesis alone reconciles all observed evidence without material contradiction.

The disconfirming evidence count stands at zero for the FALSE FRIEND profile when evaluated against fifteen distinct evidence items spanning operational cash generation, working capital dynamics, competitive positioning, margin trajectory, and management actions. Critical diagnostic evidence includes the EUR negative 11.7 million operating cash flow in Q2 2026, receivables building to EUR 60.5 million as sales shifted toward distribution partners with extended payment terms, and the 18% year-on-year backlog expansion to EUR 138 million representing a classical false-friend growth milestone.

These data points demonstrate how apparently strong volume indicators mask fundamental cash conversion deterioration. The joint failure probability across the pre-mortem's ten-plus granular failure narratives is elevated due to substantial correlation among root causes. Multiple failure modes–including grid capacity delays triggering project rollouts postponements, CPO consolidation freezing capital budgets, intense competitive dominance eroding contract wins, and persistent legacy warranty drags depressing operating earnings–can trigger simultaneously.

When major European charge point operators prioritize utilization metrics over coverage expansion, Kempower faces compounding revenue deferrals, margin compression from competitive bidding, and extended receivables terms as desperate measures to retain strategic partnerships.

The critical false-friend signal to monitor is the divergence between reported order backlog growth and operational cash flow generation.
