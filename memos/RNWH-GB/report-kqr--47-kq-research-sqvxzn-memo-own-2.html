Section summary

Employment-Linked Deferred Consideration Treatment

1.
Adjusted profit definition: The group defines its alternative performance measures to highlight ongoing trading performance. Renew Holdings plc's annual report of 17 December 2025 states that adjusted operating profit is calculated by excluding exceptional items and intangible amortisation. This adjusted metric helps investors evaluate underlying profitability without transactional distortions. Therefore, these exclusions directly influence the reported adjusted operating margins.
2.
Emerald transaction accounting: The acquisition of Emerald Power Limited included structured deferred terms. Renew Holdings plc's announcement on 25 November 2025 details an additional consideration of up to £4.5m payable across 2026 and 2027. Because these payments require the vendors to remain employed, standard IFRS guidelines prevent them from being treated as purchase consideration. This means the payments are recognized as remuneration expense over the retention period.
3.
Adjusted operating profit exclusion: Remuneration expenses from acquisitions are adjusted out of operational metrics. Renew Holdings plc's interim report on 22 May 2026 confirms that deferred remuneration for Excalon and Emerald Power is included in exceptional items. Consequently, these employment-linked costs do not depress the Group's reported adjusted operating profit. This accounting treatment preserves the comparability of the engineering services segment margins.
