The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

The split is not shown where a two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple or price is missing, zero or negative (losses ahead): a multiple's change has no meaning there.

The two horizons are the model's fixed columns after the last reported period, so they end on the dates shown, not 18 months and three years from today.

Notes to Part 1 (read before using the inputs): - EBITDA margin is the company's Adjusted EBITDA, which is after the triple-net rent paid to GLPI. This is the brokers' basis: FY2025 actual $504m, 19.0%; FY2026 consensus $484m, 15.3%. The underlying EBITDA after rent is $483m, $459m, $486m, $548m and $588m. Rent inside it is -$248m, -$302m, -$346m, -$351m and -$357m. - Depreciation and amortisation is the total, including acquired-intangible amortisation. This departs from the form's convention, for two reasons. First, the brokers' EPS for Bally's includes it: consensus EBITDA $484m less D&A $379m reconciles to consensus EBIT of $105m to $115m. Second, the split is not disclosed reliably. On the form's convention, the excluded amortisation would be roughly $150m to $250m a year (the Intralot and Gamesys intangibles), and EPS would be about $2.5 to $4 a share better than consensus's basis. - Net financial items are reported net interest. They include about $85m a year (FY2026 to FY2028), falling to about $65m (FY2029) and $45m (FY2030), of non-cash accretion of the fair-value discount on the 2029 and 2031 notes. They exclude FX, Star fair-value moves and debt-extinguishment items. They assume a debt-funded bridge: a covenant reset by 31 March 2027 plus about $450m of new secured private credit at about 13%, with further debt-funded deficits of about $330m a year at about 13% from FY2028. - Tax rate is the tax charge over pre-tax profit, on FactSet's sign convention. A negative rate means a tax charge despite a pre-tax loss: $25m, $35m, $40m, $45m and $50m. This is cash tax in the UK, Gibraltar, Greece and Turkey, with no shield from US losses (full valuation allowance). - Share count: +12.1m in FY2026 takes FactSet's FY2025 count of 48.5m to the EPS denominator of about 60.6m. That is 11.62m penny warrants, counted in basic shares because they are exercisable for almost nothing, plus about 0.5m of equity awards. After that, +0.5m a year from awards. If the model starts from a different opening count, the target weighted shares are 60.6m, 61.1m, 61.6m, 62.1m and 62.6m. - The model has no minorities line. Bally's Intralot's minorities (about 42%) currently absorb losses: +$20m (FY2026), +$20m (FY2027), +$12m, +$5m, then 0. The model's EPS will therefore be about $0.3 a share more negative than my cross-check in FY2026 and FY2027. - Perimeter: excludes the pending Evoke acquisition (overlay in Part 4).
