Roko (ROKO.B-SE): what the Q3 2026 results do to the existing case

These results strengthen the thesis modestly and weaken the case as a return by somewhat more: the business beat on every line, but stripped of one subsidiary the organic print is the 3% the case already assumed, and at SEK 2,005 (9 November 2026) the price has taken the beat, so the net is mildly negative and the park stands.

1. What changed

  • Sales and organic growth. Net sales for the quarter to 30 September 2026 were MSEK 2,180, up 44%, against a Bloomberg consensus of MSEK 1,988. Organic growth was 9% in local currency against a consensus of 4% and 2% a year earlier. The case's modal assumption was 3 to 4%.
  • One unit did most of it. ETB Technologies contributed about 6 of the 9 points, on memory shortages driven by artificial intelligence hardware demand and on customer pre-buying. The rest of the group grew about 3%.
  • Profit. Adjusted EBITA was MSEK 433, up 57%, against a consensus of MSEK 363. The margin was 19.9% against 18.1% a year earlier and a consensus of 18.3%. Reported EPS was SEK 15.49 against SEK 10.00 a year earlier and a consensus of SEK 12.70.
  • Leverage did not move. Net debt to adjusted EBITDA on the company's own definition was 2.6x at late September 2026 (2.1x a year earlier), with financial net debt including options at MSEK 4,842; the interest-bearing ratio alone was 1.1x. DNB Carnegie had expected 2.8x going in. The case had 2.6x at 30 June 2026, an alarm above 2.7x and an early-progress condition of 2.5x or below.
  • Cash and deals. Cash flow from operations was MSEK 337 against MSEK 210, the prior year held back by MSEK 41 of listing costs. Five acquisitions were consolidated in the nine months, adding MSEK 1,543 of annual sales; none is recorded after CyTech in August 2026.
  • The sell-side. DNB Carnegie is the only house to have published. It raised adjusted EPS by 9% for 2026e to SEK 85.00, by 3% for 2027e to SEK 95.50 and by 2% for 2028e to SEK 103.40, lifted its target to SEK 2,150 from SEK 2,090 and kept Hold at about 18x 2027e EV/EBITA, with the shares at SEK 2,145 on its note of 26 October 2026. The upgrade fades by year: the broker treats most of the beat as transient. The case's early-progress condition needs the FY2028 consensus up 10% or more.
  • The price. SEK 2,005 on 9 November 2026 is 11% above the case's park price of SEK 1,808, 18% above its progress level of about SEK 1,700, and about 7% below where the shares stood at the broker's note.

2. The challenge

Reading one: nothing changed underneath, and the print has blinded the case's main instrument. This is the reading we think probable.

  • Without ETB the group grew 3%, which is the case's modal number. The quarter is therefore not evidence that the organic stall cleared faster than the case assumed; it is the same portfolio plus a windfall.
  • The windfall is large for one unit. On our arithmetic, 6 points on the prior-year base of MSEK 1,518 is about MSEK 91 of extra sales in one quarter. If ETB is no larger than the 9% of group sales the review cites for the largest unit, it grew by about two-thirds or more.
  • Part of the profit beat is probably the same windfall. Sales beat consensus by about 10% and EBITA by about 19%, and the shortage was managed through pricing. Price-led gains carry the highest margins and reverse fastest.
  • What the case does not hold is what this does to its own monitor. Its alarm (organic growth below 2% for two quarters) and its upgrade trigger (above 5% for two quarters) were written for a diversified print. Q4 2026 laps 0%, so the headline is likely to clear 5% again with the underlying rate unchanged: a false positive on the case's own trigger. From Q3 2027 the group laps the windfall. If ETB merely returns to its earlier level, about 6 points come off, and the group could print near zero or below with every other unit healthy: an alarm the case cannot tell from a real one.
  • What would have to be true: ETB's demand was pre-buying that unwinds within twelve months, which is what DNB Carnegie expects and what the company's own description implies.

Reading two: the constraint lifts by a door the case did not model. Plausible, not probable.

  • The case holds that leverage rations the next leg, and that the cleared branch arrives through announced deals lifting the FY2028 consensus. The alternative is that windfall profit and cash do the deleveraging first.
  • The evidence for it: leverage held at 2.6x through the CyTech purchase when the broker expected 2.8x; operating cash flow rose 60%; return on capital employed was 15.5% for the nine months against 14.5%. The review calls artificial intelligence infrastructure the primary structural demand driver for IT refurbishment, so the shortage may last longer than a pre-buying story implies.
  • If it lasts through the first half of 2027, year-end leverage could approach DNB Carnegie's test of 2.3x, acquisition capacity would return sooner than the case's "at or below 2.5x within a year", and the deal-driven upgrades the case waits for would follow earlier.
  • What would have to be true: ETB's profit persists for at least two more quarters, and it reduces leverage on the company's definition. The second is not assured. The case's own arithmetic is that option liabilities rise about SEKm 191 for every SEKm 100 of subsidiary EBITA, and those liabilities sit inside financial net debt. A windfall in a part-owned unit raises the numerator with the denominator. Whether ETB has a minority holder is not in our evidence. And the best quarter in the record left the ratio where it stood in June.

A third point, a fact more than a reading. The case treats forty-plus units as a cost to simplicity and a protection for earnings, with no unit above about 10% of sales. One unit moving group organic growth by 6 points says earnings are lumpier than the perimeter implies, in both directions.

The obvious reading that fails: that the stall has cleared and Roko now outgrows its peers. Without ETB it grew 3%. Indutrade's organic order intake rose 6% in the same quarter. Lifco slowed to 1.6%, so the gap to peers narrowed partly because a peer weakened. UK construction remained weak through the nine months.

4. What would settle it

  • The second review, four working days on. It will carry any further broker estimates and targets and where the FY2028 consensus settles, which tests the case's 10% condition and shows whether other houses fade the beat as DNB Carnegie did. That broker published on 26 October 2026 and no other Nordic house has, so it is possible it adds little. It will not carry ETB's size, margin or ownership, the group's growth without ETB, or year-end leverage.
  • The year-end report, on the pattern of prior years in early February 2027; we hold no confirmed date. It carries year-end leverage against 2.3x (the broker's test), 2.5x (the case's condition) and 2.7x (the case's alarm). Its organic print laps 0% and is likely to flatter; it is useful only if ETB is again split out.
  • The Q1 2027 report, probably April 2027: the second read without ETB. The review's test for the bullish reading is 6 to 7% without ETB across those two quarters. The case needs only 3 to 4%.
  • The Q3 2027 report, October 2027: the lap, and the first print that separates the two readings by itself.
  • Deal announcements, as they occur. The next stated multiple, against the case's alarm of 9x EBITA and its alarm of two consecutive quarters without a platform deal.
  • What nothing commits the company to answer. It gave ETB's contribution this quarter. It has not committed to give it again, and it does not disclose subsidiary margins or the split of price and volume. If it stops, the question of what the portfolio grows at without ETB has no answer from the company, and the case's organic monitor stays blind until the comparisons wash out at the end of 2027.
