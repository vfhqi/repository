Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Convatec Group PLC manufactures and distributes single-use, non-discretionary medical consumables across four chronic-care divisions: Advanced Wound Care, Ostomy Care, Continence Care and Infusion Care
2.
Divisional mix. Advanced Wound Care is 31-32% of FY25 group revenue, Ostomy Care 28%, Continence Care 22% and Infusion Care 18-19%, per the Business Foundations digest's divisional split
3.
Scale. The group sells over 900 million consumable products annually and employs over 10,000 people, with FY25 reported revenue of $2,439m, a 6.5% reported increase
4.
Margin and cash character. FY25 adjusted operating margin was 22.3%, up 110 basis points reported; adjusted diluted EPS grew 16.0% to 17.6 cents; operating cash conversion was 99.4% in FY25 (105.6% in FY24)
5.
Balance sheet. Net debt to adjusted EBITDA leverage ended FY25 at 2.0x, matching the company's own target, after a $300m share buyback and 13% dividend growth in the year
6.
Geography. North America contributed 56% of FY25 group revenue, Europe 30%, and the Rest of World 14%, per the Business Foundations digest
7.
Reporting-currency mismatch. Convatec reports in US Dollars but lists and trades in Pounds Sterling on the London Stock Exchange, a structural translation exposure the Risks report treats as a distinct FX vulnerability
8.
Investment style implication. The business behaves as a self-help turnaround compounder: recurring, over 90%-non-discretionary chronic-care consumable revenue overlaid on a multi-year margin-expansion and product-launch programme (the "Accelerate" strategy)
