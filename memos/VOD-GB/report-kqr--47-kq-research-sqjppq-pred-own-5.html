This pressure inflated local borrowing benchmarks and increased the interest rates passed on to Safaricom. If regional fiscal conditions worsen, local currency debt will demand much higher coupons than originally anticipated.

Tightening regulatory pressure: Regulatory decisions in Europe could restrict operating cash flows and prevent timely debt reduction. Morgan Stanley reported that a European Court of Justice ruling will reduce Vodafone's German fixed pricing. This adverse judgment pressures German Adjusted EBITDAaL, which represents half of Group operating cash flow. Declining operating cash flows will increase reliance on refinancing maturing bonds during unfavorable periods.
12.
Other side of the story: While severe rating and macroeconomic headwinds could escalate borrowing costs, Vodafone's highly defensive debt structure minimizes short-term damage. Morgan Stanley reported on October 8, 2026, that the Group maintains more than ninety-five percent of its outstanding debt in fixed-rate obligations. This extensive hedging shields intermediate cash flows, ensuring that intermediate rate spikes do not dilute earnings. Consequently, even if consensus assumptions prove wrong, Vodafone's refinancing risk remains exceptionally managed and unlikely to trigger severe near-term EPS degradation.
