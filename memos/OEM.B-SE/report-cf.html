  3. European technical trading remains highly fragmented, allowing OEM to consistently gain market share
  4. Competitor consolidation is increasing pressure on sub-scale distributors, favoring larger platform players
  5. Secular investments in automation and electrification accelerate organic demand for high-spec components
  6. Direct-to-customer selling models represent a structural risk if suppliers bypass distributors
  7. European environmental regulations drive structural demand for sustainable and certified products
  8. Supply-chain disclosure rules escalate administrative compliance burdens for industrial operators
  9. Low supplier concentration mitigates individual counterparty risk across the procurement network
  10. Sourcing dependencies on Asian manufacturers expose OEM to supply chain shocks
  11. Europe's manufacturing downturn has bottomed out, setting the stage for industrial production recovery
  12. Post-pandemic inventory normalisation unwinds the excess backlogs built during prior supply crises
