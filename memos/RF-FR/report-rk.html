Third, fee-rate step-downs arising from product mix shifts toward private credit strategies represent a silent margin compression threat that current investor models fail to stress-test adequately.

The key false-friend warning signs operate through four diagnostic channels that require continuous monitoring. The critical leading indicator tracks flat or declining portfolio value per share diverging from rising total third-party fee-paying assets under management, signaling that asset accumulation masks balance sheet degradation.

A second false friend pattern emerges when aggressive share buybacks funded through syndicated credit facilities artificially support reported NAV per share metrics while consolidated net financial debt increases toward €1,500 million and cash buffers erode, carrying medium plausibility if realization proceeds remain delayed. Currency translation dynamics materially amplify the false friend risk profile by creating paper valuation volatility that obscures underlying operational trends.

Exchange rate fluctuations drove a 2.5% negative currency effect on balance sheet values in 2025, with non-euro exposures of €2,308 million dominated by USD holdings of €1,655 million ensuring that consensus EUR/USD appreciation toward 1.20 over the next 12 to 24 months will systematically depress reported NAV even if local currency asset performance remains robust. This translation headwind compounds the high-plausibility pre-mortem failure modes by restricting balance sheet rotation flexibility precisely when capital return velocity matters most for validating intrinsic asset worth.

The synthesis verdict establishes that Eurazeo's investment thesis faces elevated execution risk not from catastrophic business model failure but from a gradual erosion pattern where strong third-party franchise building successfully masks deteriorating balance sheet asset quality, rising operational leverage, and structurally slower capital recycling.

The winning FALSE FRIEND profile carries high strategic importance because traditional bear case monitoring focused on outright NAV collapse or fee revenue implosion will systematically miss the early warning signals of this more nuanced degradation path until irreversible valuation damage has occurred across legacy holdings and shareholder capital return capacity has been permanently impaired.
