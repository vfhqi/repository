EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 30 findings

Systemair AB appointed Robert Larsson as CEO in January 2026, succeeding decade-tenured Roland Kasper in a board-initiated transition designed to accelerate margin expansion and reactivate strategic M&A execution. Larsson brings a 31-year industrial operator arc spanning ABB and AFRY, where he managed SEK 10 billion in revenue and oversaw 7,000 employees as Head of AFRY's Infrastructure Division. The outgoing CEO delivered sustained operational excellence, doubling group revenue to SEK 12.5 billion and expanding operating margins from 5.6% to 9.4% over his ten-year tenure.

Governance is anchored by founder Gerald Engström's 43.5% ownership stake, while insider conviction reached SEK 108.8 million in open-market purchases during the leadership handoff. Sell-side analysts view the commercial engine upgrade as a key re-rating catalyst, though expert networks flag channel distribution friction and acute price competition in weak European construction markets. The single largest credibility risk centers on whether Larsson's consulting-centric restructuring playbook can successfully drive organic top-line compounding in a decentralized manufacturing culture without triggering commercial disruption or talent attrition.
