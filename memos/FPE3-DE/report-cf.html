Appendix

Plausible problems to monitor

Fuchs SE has exhibited persistent revenue misses against initial targets across the L3Y cycle, falling short in FY23, FY24, and FY25 primarily due to weak European industrial manufacturing activity, whereas specialty chemicals peers such as Croda maintained more stable top-line delivery despite facing comparable macroeconomic headwinds. This pattern suggests structural forecasting weakness in demand modeling relative to peer baseline planning disciplines (Section 11).

Raw material cost inflation drove a dramatic EUR 130 million inventory expansion in H1 2026, representing a 23% increase, which severely depressed free cash flow to EUR 61 million before acquisitions, representing a substantially weaker cash conversion rate than the group's own FY25 target of maintaining at least 0.8x conversion and falling meaningfully below the sector median cash efficiency observed among independent lubricant specialists (Section 3, Section 4).

The EMEA region recorded an 85 basis point EBIT margin contraction in H1 2026, burdened by OPET Turkey consolidation adjustments and a fire at the Red Sea joint venture facility, making it the only declining regional segment while the Americas surged by 779 basis points and Asia-Pacific expanded by 214 basis points, indicating disproportionate regional execution risk versus peer regional portfolios (Section 6). The abrupt mid-2025 contract non-prolongations of both CFO Isabelle Adelt (two-year tenure) and CTO Sebastian Heiner (four-year tenure) represent unusually short executive tenures relative to the specialty chemicals

The ongoing SAP S/4 HANA migration carries elevated operational oversight risks, particularly as digital transformation opex continues to weigh on intermediate margins before generating structural efficiencies post- 2030, representing a prolonged ERP integration timeline that exceeds the 18-24 month sector baseline for comparable mid-cap specialty chemical digital transformations (Section 1, Section 2).

Exceptional H1 2026 volume growth benefited heavily from temporary customer pre-buying to hedge against anticipated lubricant inflation, creating material downside volume pressure for H2 2026 as inventories normalize, indicating lower earnings quality than peer organic growth driven by sustainable end-market demand rather than transient ordering patterns (Section 3, Section 10).

Outliers worth noting

The financial and operating data for Fuchs SE over the analyzed period reveals several pronounced statistical anomalies and unexplained divergences that warrant investor scrutiny. The most striking outlier emerged in regional margin performance during H1 2026, where the Americas EBIT margin surged by 779 basis points to 16.2%, while the EMEA region simultaneously contracted by 85 basis points. This 864 basis point regional divergence represents an extraordinary split that far exceeds normal operating variance and suggests fundamentally different underlying business dynamics or temporary distortions across geographies.

A second major outlier appeared in working capital behavior, with raw material inflation driving a EUR 139 million net operating working capital build-up in H1 2026. This was accompanied by inventory expansion of 23% to EUR 130 million, representing an unusually aggressive capital deployment into current assets that significantly exceeded both historical norms and the company's stated 20% NWC-to-sales target. The magnitude and velocity of this working capital inflation suggests either procurement panic-buying, dramatic raw material cost spikes, or deliberate strategic stockpiling that management has not fully explained.

The third anomaly centers on the composition of Q2 2026's exceptional organic growth, where management disclosed that approximately one-third stemmed from customer pre-buying ahead of anticipated price increases, while another third derived from competitors' supply failures. This implies that only one-third of the reported 18.1% organic growth reflected genuine underlying demand expansion–an extraordinarily high proportion of nonrecurring or artificial volume that challenges the sustainability narrative.

Free cash flow performance presented a fourth outlier, collapsing to just EUR 7 million in Q2 2026 versus EUR 64 million in the prior-year period, despite record EBIT generation. This EUR 57 million cash flow deterioration occurred precisely when operating profits reached historical peaks, revealing a severe and unexplained disconnect between earnings quality and cash realization that contradicts the company's stated 80% cash conversion target. Currency translation effects constituted the fifth outlier, with the strengthening Euro generating EUR 26 million in negative translation impacts during H1 2026, concentrated heavily in the Americas where the US Dollar depreciated from 1.175 to 1.139 against the Euro. This 3% currency headwind disproportionately masked underlying operational performance and created a material gap between reported and constant-currency results that complicates like-for-like trend analysis.
