Consequently, any short interest in BlueNord during late 2026 was primarily driven by market participants hedging their exposure to Vår Energi's volatile equity price.

Contrary evidence: Norne Securities reported on 21 October 2026 that the underlying investment case for the combined entity remains robust due to strong European energy security demand.
