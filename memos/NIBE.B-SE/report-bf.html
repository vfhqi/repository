Barriers to Entry and Exit Developing advanced climate technologies requires massive upfront capital investments in manufacturing facilities and specialized testing equipment. Nibe has completed extensive multi-year capacity expansion programs to prepare for future demand growth. Massive capital investments form a primary barrier preventing smaller companies from competing. High upfront capital needs limit new entrants because they cannot match the production efficiency of established players.

1.
New entrants face substantial regulatory approval barriers due to tightening safety standards and environmental mandates. Compliance with the European Union F-gas Regulation requires rigorous certification for handling flammable natural refrigerants. Strict safety and product certifications restrict rapid market entry. 2.
Established heating manufacturers are protected by deep installer networks that are extremely difficult for new players to replicate. Installers typically recommend only a very narrow set of familiar brands to avoid costly installation errors. Deep relationship capital creates a powerful barrier that cheap imported brands struggle to penetrate.
