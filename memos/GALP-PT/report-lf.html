EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 24 findings

Galp's CEO Filipe Silva represented a financially-anchored executive transitioning from a long-term CFO role to chief operator, bringing extensive experience in capital stewardship but lacking early-career field operations. His January 2025 resignation following an internal conduct breach triggered a bicephalous co-CEO structure under Maria João Carioca and João Marques da Silva, creating untested command dynamics during critical offshore appraisal campaigns. The organization delivered consistently strong operational performance driven by high-margin pre-salt assets, yet faces governance constraints from concentrated Amorim Energia ownership and below-peer board independence. Strategic execution over the 18-to-36-month horizon carries medium plausibility, contingent on TotalEnergies' operational delivery in

Namibia and successful downstream platform consolidation.
