B.1 Business Description 7 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Technoprobe SpA designs and manufactures vertical MEMS probe cards, the consumable electrical interface between automated test equipment and individual dies on a silicon wafer, replaced for every new chip design
2.
Primary market. Advanced logic wafer testing for AI GPUs, custom ASICs and (nascent) high-bandwidth memory, sold principally through direct relationships with TSMC and Nvidia and via a Taiwanese OSAT channel partner (WinWay)
3.
Financial character. FY25 consolidated revenue EUR 628.4m (+15.7% year on year); FY25 EBITDA margin 32.1% versus 25.1% in FY24;
3.1
Detail: Q1 2026 revenue EUR 187.0m (+19.0% YoY) at a 37.0% EBITDA margin; net financial position EUR 684.2m at end-2025, virtually zero financial debt
4.
Balance sheet and capital intensity. FY25 capital expenditure EUR 99.0m (16% of revenue, up from an 8-10% historical norm), funding a roughly EUR 200-240m self-funded plan to double manufacturing capacity in Italy and Taiwan by Q1 2027
5.
Sector and geography. Listed on Euronext Milan; founded 1996 (Italy); Asia is the largest revenue geography at 52.7% of FY25 sales, with the Americas at 40.0% and Europe/Italy at 7.3%
6.
Segment mix. Advanced logic vertical MEMS is the core (over 90% of consolidated revenue via the Microfabrica platform), supplemented by final-test interface boards (DIS Tech, acquired 2024) and interface-board manufacturing (Harbor Electronics, acquired 2023)
7.
Investment-style implication. A cyclical, single-cycle-concentrated growth compounder with extreme operating leverage (50-80% EBITDA drop-through), not a defensive holding; the FY23 downturn (revenue -25.4%, EBITDA -50.0%) is the template for how the same leverage runs in reverse
