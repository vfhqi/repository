Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary

Core business and origins

1.
Stable operations: Kitron operates as a leading Scandinavian electronics manufacturing services provider that manufactures both embedded electronics and complete box-built products. Kitron delivers services across the entire value chain from initial design support to assembly and subsequent lifecycle repairs. The company is headquartered in Billingstad Norway and operates modern production sites across three separate continents. This diversified presence helps Kitron mitigate regional demand shifts while maintaining proximity to key multinational customers.

2.
Balanced segments: Kitron divides its operating revenues across five distinct market sectors to maintain a highly diversified business profile. These segments are Connectivity, Electrification, Industry, Medical Devices, and Defence/Aerospace. Defense has recently become the primary growth driver representing 32% of total group revenues in fiscal year 2025. This product mix allows Kitron to absorb cyclical downturns in individual commercial end-markets.
3.
Long history: Kitron trace its origins to several electronics companies established during the 1960s in the region of Arendal Norway. The company unified under the current brand name during the 1980s and listed its shares on the Oslo Stock Exchange in 1997. Systematic geographical expansion and targeted mergers have since transformed the firm from a local subcontractor into an international player. These historical integrations include the strategic acquisition of BB Electronics in 2022 and DeltaNordic in 2026 to strengthen regional market positions across Northern Europe.
4.
Scale benefits: Kitron has successfully expanded its global manufacturing footprint to achieve greater economies of scale over the last three years. The company operates production plants in Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Lithuania, Germany, Poland, the Czech Republic, India, China, Malaysia, and the United States. Manufacturing capacity grew significantly through greenfield investments in Poland and major facility expansions in Norway. This platform helps Kitron capture international outsourcing trends while reducing its relative cost base.
