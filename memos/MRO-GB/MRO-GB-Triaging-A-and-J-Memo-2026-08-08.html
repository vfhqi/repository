B.1 Business Description 10 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Pure-play aerospace supplier. Melrose Industries plc (LSE: MRO) is the parent of GKN Aerospace, a Tier-1 supplier of engine components and aerostructures to civil and military aircraft
1.1
Origin: the pure-play position dates from the April 2023 demerger of GKN Automotive and Powder Metallurgy into Dowlais Group
2.
Two divisions, sharply different economics. Engines (GBP 1,632m FY25 revenue, 31.9% adj. operating margin) designs and manufactures engine structures, fan cases and rotating components
2.1
Airframes contrast: GBP 1,957m FY25 revenue at an 8.0% adj. operating margin, renamed from Structures in February 2026, builds wing structures, fuselages and composite components
3.
Revenue mix. Civil aerospace is 71% of FY25 revenue and defence 29%; group revenue reached GBP 3,589m in FY25, up 8% like-for-like
4.
Engines model is annuity-like. 52% of Engines revenue is recurring aftermarket support under 19 Risk and Revenue Sharing Partnerships (RRSPs) spanning 30-40 year platform lifecycles, entitling GKN to aftermarket cash on roughly 70% of global flying hours
5.
Airframes is structurally lower quality. Aerostructures manufacturing sits in the least-attractive segment of the value chain per sell-side (Rothschild & Co Redburn), with an 8.0% margin versus Engines' 31.9%, and is directly exposed to Airbus narrowbody build-rate delays
6.
Financial character. FY25 adj. operating profit GBP 647m (+23%), adj. diluted EPS 32.10p (+25%), net debt/EBITDA 1.8x, within the 1.5x-2.0x target range
6.1
Cash inflection: free cash flow turned positive at GBP 125m in FY25, from a GBP 74m outflow in FY24
7.
Investment style. This is a self-help margin-recovery and long-term aftermarket cash compounder archetype: a completed restructuring story layered on a structural aftermarket moat, not a pure secular grower
8.
Cash-quality caveat. The FY25 free cash flow beat was partly assisted by a 17% year-on-year expansion in non-recourse factoring to GBP 396m and a GBP 27m capex underspend, per Rothschild & Co Redburn and UBS
9.
Near-term operational event. A May 2026 thermal storage-tank incident at the Garden Grove, California facility (sole-source F-35 canopy manufacturer) triggered a federal emergency declaration, an FBI search warrant and multiple class-action lawsuits
10.
Geographic footprint. GKN Aerospace operates 32 manufacturing sites (rationalised from 52) across 12 countries, with primary operations in the UK, Sweden, the Netherlands, Mexico and the US
