[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. Equinor is a cyclical peak-earnings case dressed as a cash-return case, and it is not compelling. At 402.8 NOK the shares pay a 90th-percentile multiple for one winter of geopolitically inflated oil and European gas prices that every path in our research has reversing from 2027, so the 'Modal return' is negative at both horizons and the case only becomes interesting below about 230 NOK.

  • The change is external, a supply shock, and it has already travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • The Strait of Hormuz is closed, Brent was above 103 USD a barrel at end-September 2026 against prior expectations of 70 to 75 USD, and TTF rose 66.6 percent in Q3 2026.
    • The company is unhedged and about three times as gas-levered as its peers, so it captures the shock in full.
    • Q2 2026 reported net income was 4,848 million USD against 1,312 million USD a year earlier
      • Organic free cash flow was 6.1 billion USD in the quarter against consensus of 4.1 billion USD
      • Gearing fell to 10.4 percent from 22.7 percent at end-2025.
    • The 2026 buyback was doubled to 3 billion USD, and FY2026 consensus EPS has been upgraded 77.2 percent over twelve months (2026-10-02).
      • Realised prices, the financial statements and the sell-side's estimates have all moved: there is no layer left for the change to reach.
  • The internal change is real but second-order to the earnings base.
    • The June 2026 Capital Markets Day replaced the volume target with returns and predictability, halved renewables investment for 2025 to 2027, retired the 15 to 30 percent net-debt target and doubled the buyback.
    • Five of the twelve internal forces are strong, which meets the screen's rule, and none of the false-positive red flags applies.
    • None of it moves the earnings base by more than a few percent a year, against a commodity swing that nearly quadrupled quarterly net income.
      • It is good housekeeping at a 100 billion USD company, not a transformation.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are at a peak, not improving.
    • The near term is very positive on every metric, but the framework's conditional rule asks for plausible logic for it continuing, and the logic in the research runs the other way.
    • Consensus EPS falls from 5.55 USD in FY2026 to 4.15 USD in FY2027 and 3.64 USD in FY2028 (medians of 22 to 24 brokers, 2026-10-05), about minus 19 percent a year
      • No broker has FY2028 above its own FY2026.
      • Consensus revenue falls from 124,672 million USD to 103,503 million USD over the same two years, and the EBITDA margin from 44.2 percent to 36.1 percent.
    • The sell-side is upgrading the years it can see and holding the years the valuation rests on: FY2028 consensus EPS rose 0.2 percent over three months while FY2026 rose 5.8 percent.
      • The Goldilocks test fails at every horizon, and the staircase is mostly known, exogenous or small.
  • The price has already paid for the winter, the clean balance sheet and the doubled buyback.
    • The shares are up 73.7 percent over twelve months and 24.6 percent over three (2026-10-02)
    • The 24-month-forward multiple is 11.03x, the 90th percentile of its own five-year range against a median of 8.19x (2026-10-02)
    • The consensus target of 386 NOK sits below the price and the buy share is 14 percent.
    • At the ten-year median multiple of 9.5x the price implies 24-month-forward EPS of about 42 NOK, 18 percent above consensus's 35.98 NOK, so the price assumes either a sustained gas premium or a sustained 11x multiple.
      • What the price does not carry is the earnings decline consensus itself already holds from FY2027, because the price rose 24.6 percent in three months while FY2028 consensus did not move.
  • The return fails the 20 percent bar at both horizons, on every path in the research.
    • The dividend is the only positive leg, about 4 percent a year
    • Earnings growth is negative on every path
    • The re-rating leg is negative from the 90th percentile of the multiple range.
    • Our modal path puts 9.5x on 24-month-forward EPS of 29.3 NOK at the eighteen-month horizon: 278 NOK of value plus 24 NOK of dividends, 302 NOK, about minus 17 percent a year
      • At three years 326 NOK, about minus 7 percent a year.
      • Even holding today's 11.2x, the eighteen-month return is minus 8.6 percent a year
      • Even on the elevated-gas branch (EPS 38 NOK at 10.5x) it is plus 3.3 percent a year, an F.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Major Corporate Change', and the fit is loose.
    • The inputs are at the screen's threshold and the change is mature on the balance sheet and in distributions, but the major EPS trajectory upgrades the pattern needs are the commodity's, not the company's.
    • 'Demand Inflection', the pattern the share price is trading, fails its mandatory condition: this is a supply disruption without two years' visibility, not a demand explosion.

  • The chain holds all the way to the outputs and breaks at the price.

    • The case is therefore a trade on the winter of 2026 to 2027, not an investment case over eighteen months and three years.
    • It would become a case to test, as 'Trough on Trough' or 'Major CfC Clearing', in the normalisation phase the research itself expects.

      • And the sector's capital discipline has re-rated the majors structurally, so 10x is the new 8x.

      • The fiscal cushion is real and makes the dividend safe at 60 USD Brent
      • The sector has re-rated
      • And if the premium lasts two winters the stock is not expensive on the earnings it would then report.

      • A 10 percent yield on a share whose earnings fall about 19 percent a year on consensus and whose multiple sits at the 90th percentile of its range is a negative total return, not a positive one.
      • The bull branch we modelled, gas elevated through 2028 at 10.5x, returns plus 3 percent a year, an F.
      • The research read as the deliberate case against selects the 'false friend' profile, high short-term cash masking reserve decay (replacement of 48 to 61 percent in 2025) and a transition portfolio that impairs, and concludes the investment should be avoided over its holding period.
      • The sell-side that upgraded FY2026 by 77 percent has not moved FY2028, which is the year the valuation convention prices.
    • Where the case against would win: a second disrupted winter, which would hold the gas premium into 2028 and give the out-years the upgrade cycle they lack.
      • The competing hypothesis is a trade on the winter
      • The framework asks for a case over eighteen months and three years, and over those horizons it fails.

[Robustness of] Foundations upon which the Investment Thesis is built upon (BB 2) Rating = C

The 'Foundations' are robust for what the business is, a low-cost, state-anchored, unhedged producer of Norwegian oil and gas with a locked-in European pipeline customer base, and weak for what the thesis would need, which is a durable improvement in outputs. They make a cyclical thesis plausible and a compounding one implausible, and they date the fragility: the value chain turns against European gas prices from 2027.

  • The value chain supports the thesis for one winter and undermines it from 2027.
    • Europe needs Norwegian pipeline gas and has signed long for it (Uniper fifteen years from January 2027, BASF ten years from July 2025), and the Russian pipeline ban by September 2027 removes a supplier for good.
    • But more than 150 million tonnes a year of new liquefaction arrives from 2026, FSRUs make switching cheap, European gas demand is falling 8 to 10 percent to 2030, and the research's own base case has European gas 40 percent below its 2025 average by 2028.
  • The industry structure is concentrated where Equinor sits and price-taking everywhere that matters.
    • Equinor produces 70 percent of NCS output and supplies about 30 percent of European gas, which confers logistics power, not pricing power: the price is set by OPEC, Qatar, US shale and the weather.
    • The structure is stable
    • The price is not.
  • The business is a strong operator of one basin and a poor allocator outside it.
    • Sverdrup came in 30 percent under budget and unit cost is about 6 USD a barrel
    • Against that sit 21 billion USD of US write-downs over twelve years, a 955 million USD Empire Wind 1 impairment and a 400 million USD Ørsted impairment within a year of purchase.
    • The owner's objectives are not only financial, and the leadership research's own verdict on executing the thesis is 'medium plausibility'.
  • The competitive advantage is real, held and delivered, and it does not translate into the framework's financial quality.
    • The NCS cost and fiscal moat gave an EBITDA margin of 38.12 percent in 2024 against Shell's 23.35 percent, but it is an asset-based advantage in a depleting asset: reserve replacement was 48 to 61 percent in 2025, the reported tax rate is 70 to 80 percent, free cash flow was below 30 percent of net income in each of the last three fiscal years, and post-tax return on capital employed was 14.5 percent in 2025, below the framework's 15 percent even in a strong year.

  • What the 'Foundations' therefore do for the thesis: they make it probable that the company captures the spike in full, and probable that it hands the earnings back when the spike reverses.

    • Neither Element binds the other up or down.
