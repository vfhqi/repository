What is the composition and run-rate of Sdiptech AB's (SDIP.B-SE) net financial items after the September 2026 refinancing (SEK 400m senior secured bonds at STIBOR plus 2% maturing in 2030)? How sensitive is it to Riksbank and Bank of England rate moves through 2028?"

Section 4 – Currency Fluctuations and FX Exposure in the Finance Net

Section summary

  2. The company deploys currency derivatives and local-currency debt allocation to mitigate foreign exchange risks.
  3. Historical exchange rate volatility has previously introduced major non-cash losses into Sdiptech's annual finance net.

1.

Sdiptech's Q3 investor presentation on 23 October 2026 reveals that the United Kingdom accounts for 49 percent of core net sales, while the Nordics contribute 16 percent. This geographic concentration is significantly higher than that of Nordic peer Lifco, which historically maintains a more diversified European footprint. Consequently, sterling fluctuations directly dictate reported earnings per share.
2.

Sdiptech's prospectus published on 29 October 2026 states that a 10 percent change in the exchange rate of the British pound and euro against the Swedish krona would impact operating profit by SEK 83 million based on 2025 results. This sensitivity remains highly consistent with the prior year's level of SEK 85 million as disclosed in the 2024 annual report. Unless currency markets stabilize, these translation impacts will cause unpredictable swings in annual operating income.

Elevated tax sensitivity: Sdiptech's pre-tax profitability is highly exposed to currency shifts even when excluding central derivative valuations. Sdiptech's annual report published on 1 September 2026 notes that a 10 percent change in all exchange rates against the Swedish krona affects profit before tax by SEK 44 million. This is slightly lower than the SEK 51 million sensitivity reported in the previous fiscal year. Addtech's annual report published on 29 June 2026 indicates that its currency risk is highly diversified across multiple European markets, making its consolidated EBIT less dependent on any single foreign currency.
