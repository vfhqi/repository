Financial Contribution of External Fleet

1.
Steady revenue: Research across ten relevant sources, four companies, and one major precedent shows that managing third-party rigs yields valuable strategic options. Odfjell reports External Fleet operating revenue of USD 174 million in FY25 and EBITDA of USD 33 million, representing a robust 19% EBITDA margin. The Deepsea Bollsta Acquisition Case Study

4.
Risk mitigation: Managing a rig prior to acquisition dramatically reduces operational uncertainties. Odfjell acquired the sixth-generation semi-submersible Deepsea Bollsta from Northern Ocean in December 2025 for USD 480 million after managing it since Q1 2022. Odfjell's chief executive officer highlighted that first-hand operational familiarity made the rig accretive from day one. Prior management allowed Odfjell to evaluate the crew and rig performance under real North Sea and Namibia conditions.

Asset integration: The transaction added immediate scale and backlog to Odfjell's own fleet. The acquisition secured USD 355 million in firm backlog under the existing Equinor contract extending until Q1 2028. Because Odfjell already managed the rig, there was minimal operational integration risk or transitional downtime. This seamless transition ensured that the rig's cash generation was not disrupted by ownership changes.
6.
Balance sheet restructuring: The seller used the cash proceeds to prepay bank debt and restructure its liabilities. Northern Ocean utilized the USD 480 million cash settlement to prepay USD 285 million in bank debt and reduce its Sterna affiliate facility to USD 100 million. This transaction allowed the seller to materially improve capital efficiency and prepare for capital returns to its shareholders. Hence, the transaction served the strategic priorities of both the manager and the asset owner.

The remaining managed fleet includes the Deepsea Mira, the Deepsea Yantai, and SFL Corporation's Hercules rig. Contrary evidence: Managing third-party rigs exposes Odfjell's earnings to substantial volatility when managed rigs become idle or change hands.
