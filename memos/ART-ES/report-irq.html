What the event should settle, and why

The case in one line: a top-two maker of high-voltage instrument transformers whose FY2026 is safe and whose next year turns on whether price keeps running ahead of cost, against a sell-side that already sits above guidance and a plan that the company sets to be beaten. Six judgements decide whether to add, hold or cut.

  1. Where FY2026 lands. The top of guidance implies an H2 margin of about 15.8%; consensus EUR 105m needs about 17.3% on consensus revenue; ours, EUR 103.8m at 17.6%; holding H1's 18.4% gives about EUR 108m (our arithmetic). If the company points above the top on 30-Nov, consensus holds and the FY2027 base rises by EUR 2m to EUR 4m; if it stays silent, the beat against consensus is EUR 0m to EUR 2m, as the memo has it.
  2. How FY2027 opens against consensus. FY2026's first range was revenue +9% to +15% at a flat margin; applied to FY2026 that is EUR 112m to EUR 120m of FY2027 EBITDA against consensus EUR 126m (JB Capital EUR 126m, Santander EUR 135m). A plan that puts a FY2027 figure near EUR 126m removes the memo's most probable negative event; silence leaves it at the FY2026 results.
  3. What the plan's 2029 targets imply for the sell-side's FY2027 and FY2028. Consensus FY2028 is EUR 785m and EUR 148m (18.9%); the one FY2029 forecast EUR 871m and EUR 186m (21.3%). A plan at 10% to 12% a year and 18% to 19% implies cuts to FY2028 consensus of about 5% to 10%; a plan at 14% a year and 20% or more holds consensus and moves our model up.
  4. New-order pricing against cost. Each 1% of price is about 0.8 point of margin; the Deep Dive's cautious reading of investor relations' "3% to 4%" lifts our FY2027 EPS 9% and FY2028 17%, still just short of consensus. Rising prices on orders booked since June are the upgrade case; flat prices confirm our plateau.
  5. Orders and capacity: can FY2027 revenue reach EUR 683m? FY2027 revenue is a ramp forecast; each 5% (EUR 33m) is about EUR 0.18 of EPS. A backlog at or above EUR 420m with plants on time supports consensus revenue; book-to-bill below 1.05 is the memo's warning.
  6. The lines below operating profit. Tax (14.2% in FY2025; Santander 16% flat; ours 19%) and net financial items (about EUR 9m) explain about EUR 0.10 of the EUR 0.18 gap between our FY2027 EPS and the consensus median (Deep Dive Memo's correction of the memo's EUR 0.20). A 19% tax steer trims consensus EPS even if EBITDA is right.

Current momentum since June

A1 ★ Orders and backlog now. "Backlog was EUR 417 million in June, book-to-bill 1.22: where are orders and backlog today?"
- Why: there is no company number between June and the FY2026 results. Consensus FY2027 revenue of EUR 683m is +16% on FY2026 consensus; ours EUR 659.6m. The memo's warnings are book-to-bill below 1.05 or backlog below EUR 320m; at or above EUR 420m with orders up 10% at constant currency keeps FY2027 inside our range. Pfisterer's book-to-bill was 1.06 times (18-Nov).
- Tell: a backlog above EUR 420m and double-digit orders raise the odds that FY2027 revenue reaches consensus; "orders normalising" or a backlog flat on June lowers them and makes Santander's EUR 695m the first number to fall.

A2 ★ Pricing on new orders. "Are prices on orders booked since the summer still rising faster than copper and your other costs?"
- Why: each 1% of price is about 0.8 point of margin, EUR 6.6m of FY2027 EBITDA. On 5-Nov investor relations called real price rises of 3% to 4% sustainable (as quoted); our model has price +2.0% in FY2027 and +0.5% in FY2028. The Deep Dive's cautious reading of that claim takes FY2027 EPS to EUR 1.38 before minorities, against consensus EUR 1.40. Ambit Capital sees copper up more than 50% with transformer prices largely flat.
- Tell: "still rising, ahead of cost" supports upgrades to our model and holds consensus; "stable" or "we price for lead time, not margin" ("the lead time is the key, not the pricing power", 5-Nov, as quoted) supports our plateau and a FY2028 consensus margin of 18.9% that is too high.

A3 ★ Where FY2026 lands. "With eleven months done, will FY2026 EBITDA land above the top of your EUR 92.1 to 100.4 million range, and near the EUR 105 million consensus?"
- Why: the top implies an H2 margin of about 15.8% against 18.4% in H1; consensus EUR 105m needs about 17.3%; ours EUR 103.8m at 17.6%; holding 18.4% gives about EUR 108m (our arithmetic). Each point of H2 margin is about EUR 3m. Investor relations was "comfortable" with consensus on 5-Nov (as quoted); JB Capital warned in August that copper and SEG costs could leave FY2026 below it.
- Tell: "above the top, in line with analysts" holds FY2026 and lifts the FY2027 base; "within the range" or a copper caveat means a small miss against consensus in February or March and a lower base for FY2027.

A4 ★ Regions. "In the first half, orders rose about 23% in Europe, the Middle East and Africa and in North America and fell 18% in Latin America: which regions have speeded up or slowed since, and has Latin America turned?"

CFE has MX$161bn for 155 transmission projects and Brazil schedules three 2027 transmission auctions worth over R$50bn (Morgan Stanley, 17-Nov). Each 5% of FY2027 revenue is about EUR 0.18 of EPS.
