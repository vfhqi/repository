This substantial price premium drives utility-scale developers to favor lithium iron phosphate chemistry for long-duration applications. The remaining six percent rebate is scheduled for complete elimination on one January 2027. This regulatory policy shift will inflate base manufacturing costs for exporters reliant on Chinese factory networks.

EVE Energy is negotiating with overseas energy storage system customers who have agreed to absorb some of the incremental tax burdens. Successful cost pass-through will mitigate severe downstream margin compression for leading Chinese cell manufacturers.

CATL experienced sequentially weaker unit profitability in third-quarter results due to the lagging impact of the April export rebate cut. Investors expect sequentially lower unit profits in firstquarter 2027 as the final six percent rebate is eliminated. This persistent tax drag will constrain international gross margins unless cell pricing is adjusted upward.
