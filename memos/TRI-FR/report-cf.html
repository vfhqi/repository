EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Trigano maintains superior profitability and capital discipline relative to severely distressed European recreational vehicle peers, but faces near-term cyclical headwinds from dealer destocking pressures, elevated floor-plan financing costs, and component supply disruptions. The investment case hinges on the company's ability to normalize dealer inventories, materialize BIO Habitat integration synergies, and maintain pricing power during a prolonged downcycle while preserving its defensive net cash position of EUR 279 million.

Leadership and Governance Architecture

Trigano operates under stable executive leadership with Chairman Stéphane Gigou leading since September 2020 and CEO Michel Freiche managing operations since 2016 with nearly four decades of company experience, though Freiche's age of 65 years creates a mid-term succession requirement. The Feuillet family exercises absolute voting control through a 57.87% share capital stake and 66.43% voting rights, creating governance risks of minority shareholder oppression despite refreshed board oversight and intergenerational wealth transfers. The company employed 10,652 full-time equivalent workers with an annual voluntary turnover rate of 17.2%, which sits below standard manufacturing peer averages of 20% to 25% in France.

Strategic Positioning and Structural Evolution

Trigano completed the transformative acquisition of BIO Habitat for EUR 196.7 million net cash in December 2024, adding 960 employees, seven production sites, and EUR 161.6 million in consolidated sales between December 2024 and August 2025. The proprietary Libertium distribution network expanded to 70 French dealerships through the acquisition of 12 sales outlets for EUR 38.8 million in fiscal 2024, contributing approximately EUR 100 million in full-year sales and capturing downstream dealer margins. The company maintains a highly decentralized subsidiary execution model with motorhome production capacity spread across 17 European industrial sites, ensuring no single facility exceeds 20% of output. Management demonstrated financial discipline by completing voluntary early repayment of the EUR 181.6 million BIO Habitat acquisition bank loan in first-half 2026.

External Value-Chain Dynamics

European motorhome registrations have almost doubled over the past decade, with approximately 80% of purchases paid in cash, which shields retail demand from rising interest rates, though near-term cyclical destocking pressures persist. The European RV industry remains highly concentrated with the top five manufacturers holding approximately 85% share, balanced between Trigano and Erwin Hymer Group at around 25% each, with high entry barriers blocking Chinese manufacturers from establishing European retail routes. Chassis supply has normalized from severe post-pandemic shortages to abundant availability, with Trigano distributing sourcing across Stellantis, Ford, and Renault to mitigate single-supplier bottlenecks. Severe dealer destocking driven by floor-plan carry costs of 6% to 8% is expected to normalize within nine to 12 months, with factory order books anticipated to rebound ahead of 2027 if channel inventories clear by late 2026. European private consumption remains persistently cautious with households preferring savings accumulation, compounded by weak Eurozone GDP growth projections of 0.6% and sharp demand contraction across Sweden and Norway due to currency depreciation.

Financial Profile and Margin Trajectory

Trigano's current operating profit margin softened to 9.1% in fiscal year 2025 from 12.8% in fiscal year 2024, with consolidated sales falling 6.8% to EUR 3.7 billion, though Bernstein projects margin recovery to 9.8% in fiscal year 2026 and revenues reaching approximately EUR 4 billion. The company unlocked EUR 314.9 million from working capital through aggressive inventory liquidation in fiscal year 2025, expanding the net cash position to EUR 279.2 million and enabling full voluntary early repayment of the EUR 181.6 million BIO Habitat acquisition loan in H1 2026.

Peer Knaus Tabbert suffered severe distress with adjusted EBITDA margins collapsing to 2.6% in 2024 and 2.7% in 2025, compared to Trigano's superior profitability with historical EBIT margins averaging 9.5% over the long term, creating a massive fundamental outperformance gap.

Most Recent Earnings Delivery

Trigano delivered stable H1 2026 results with consolidated revenue reaching EUR 1,779.7 million (6.2% year-onyear growth), current operating profit margin of 9.0% (up from 8.6% in H1 2025), and consolidated net income of EUR 121.3 million, beating consensus by 3%. Motorhome sales reached EUR 1,373.7 million with 7.1% organic growth, while caravan sales rebounded 15.9% in the first half to EUR 66.0 million. Rapid motorized production expansion of 15% triggered supply chain constraints, forcing Trigano to delay 600 vehicle handovers worth approximately EUR 35 million, shifting revenue recognition to Q3 2026.

High cash inflows enabled voluntary early repayment of the EUR 181.6 million BIO Habitat bank loan and share repurchases of 300,000 shares for EUR 45.9 million in mid-2026, signaling confidence in market fundamentals.

Peer-Relative Momentum and Positioning

Trigano commands the top rank in comparative European leisure vehicle assessment, with H1 operating margin of 9% significantly exceeding the sector median margin of 4.5%. Peer Knaus Tabbert's actual EBITDA margin landed at 2.7% during fiscal year 2025, Winnebago lowered full-year EPS guidance to $1.65 to $2 from $2.10 to $2.80, and LCI Industries reduced consolidated revenue outlook to $3.9 billion to $4.1 billion due to persistent discretionary headwinds.

Trigano maintained its leading European motorcaravans market share of 27.4% for the nine-month period, closely competing with Erwin Hymer Group's 26.3% share. Trigano unlocked EUR 314.9 million of cash through aggressive inventory liquidation, while peer Knaus Tabbert breached its bank covenants in 2025 due to depressed EBITDA and high inventory levels.

Appendix

Outliers worth noting

The most striking anomaly concerns Trigano's cash accumulation profile, with the company maintaining a consolidated net cash position of EUR 279.2 million in fiscal year 2025, achieved despite executing a EUR 196.7 million acquisition of BIO Habitat during the same period. This balance sheet strength stands in sharp contrast to peer Knaus Tabbert, which breached its bank covenants in 2025 due to depressed EBITDA and high inventory levels.

Trigano's financial flexibility was further demonstrated through the voluntary early repayment of the entire EUR 181.6 million BIO Habitat acquisition bank loan in the first half of fiscal year 2026, a deleveraging pace that significantly exceeds industry norms during a cyclical downturn. The company's ability to generate such substantial cash reserves while simultaneously funding transformative acquisitions and maintaining shareholder returns represents a fundamental departure from the highly leveraged capital structures typical of European leisure vehicle manufacturers.

A second material outlier concerns the extreme concentration of sell-side research coverage, which creates unusual information asymmetry risks for public shareholders. The analysis reveals that active broker coverage is dominated by a single institution, Bernstein Research, with analysts Marie Line Fort and Aleksander Peterc providing the primary source of independent equity research. This coverage breadth sits well below the industry median of four to six dedicated analysts for comparably-sized European industrials, leaving consensus estimates vulnerable to single-analyst revisions and reducing the diversity of analytical perspectives available to inform investor decision-making.

The concentrated coverage structure may contribute to valuation inefficiencies, as Bernstein analyst Marie Line Fort points out that Trigano trades at an unjustified 20% discount to its peer multiples despite demonstrating superior profitability and balance sheet strength across all observable financial metrics. The company's margin resilience during the fiscal year 2025 destocking cycle represents a third statistical anomaly relative to peer base rates.

Trigano's current operating profit margin softened to 9.1% in fiscal year 2025 from 12.8% in fiscal year 2024, representing a 370 basis point compression during a severe industry downturn. However, this margin profile dramatically exceeds the performance of direct competitors, as peer Knaus Tabbert suffered severe contraction with adjusted EBITDA margin falling to 2.6% in 2024 and remaining depressed at 2.7% in 2025.

The 640 basis point margin advantage over Knaus Tabbert during a synchronized industry downturn suggests that Trigano possesses structural cost advantages or product positioning superiority that is not fully reflected in current equity valuations. This defensive margin profile appears linked to the company's decentralized subsidiary execution model and entry-level product focus, which insulates profitability from premium segment promotional discounting.
