[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Aalberts is a cyclical recovery with a self-help overlay, priced as if the recovery were already normal. At 41.96 EUR the shares pay about 14 percent a year on our modal path over eighteen months and about 12 percent over three years, short of the 20 percent bar, and the case clears the bar only on a re-rating we call possible rather than probable.

  • The external change is a sector-wide semiconductor-equipment upcycle, and it is in the reported numbers rather than the narrative.
    • Semicon organic growth reached 16.4 percent in the second quarter of 2026 against a company-collected consensus of 3.3 percent, and segment EBITA rose 88 percent to 47.5 EURm on revenue of 335.7 EURm in the first half.
    • The visibility is sector-level: ASML's order backlog is 38.8 billion EUR, ASML plans EUV capacity of 110 systems by 2028, and Huatai expects memory capital expenditure to compound at 36 percent a year to 2028.
    • The force acts on 15 percent of FY2025 revenue, which is why it is a strong force and not a group-wide one.
  • The internal change is broad and executed, not announced.
    • Seven of the twelve internal forces the framework screens are active, three of them strong, which passes the screen's rule.
    • About 400 EURm of low-margin revenue, some 13 percent of the group, was removed in five months (Metalis in November 2025, KAN to 45 percent in December 2025, Broen in March 2026), worth over 100 basis points of FY2026 margin on Jefferies' arithmetic.
    • Two acquisitions in higher-margin niches (Paulo, 169 EURm, aerospace heat treatment in the United States; Grand Venture Technology, SGD 319m, back-end semiconductor) were bought, and Grand Venture Technology is growing 30 percent organically.
    • A 50 EURm restructuring for 25 EURm of savings, a new finance, operations and strategy team, a production system across 127 sites and a 75 EURm buyback a year are running at the same time.
  • The largest segment is not turning, and the chain breaks there.
    • Building is 50 percent of FY2025 revenue and its European residential markets are at the bottom of a trough that management calls severe.
    • Building EBITA fell 3 percent to 100.4 EURm in the first half of 2026 and missed the 104.0 EURm consensus
      • France, the United Kingdom and Eastern Europe are "highly challenging" and only Benelux, the Nordics and Germany are "gradually improving".
    • Our modal path does not build in a European building turn
      • we put it at possible, about 40 percent.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up, but the upgrade cycle the pattern needs is only beginning.
    • The first-half 2026 EBITA margin was 14.4 percent, up 90 basis points, adjusted earnings per share were 1.47 EUR against 1.38 EUR a year earlier, and organic growth went from -2.5 percent in FY2025 to +5.0 percent.
    • About half of the 90 basis points is the arithmetic of deconsolidating KAN and Metalis rather than operating improvement, which is the false friend the risks report warned of.
    • The sell-side has raised estimates 2 to 5 percent over three and six months on FactSet's series to 2026-09-18, after a year of cuts, so the twelve-month change is still -9.6 percent for FY2026.
  • The change has travelled to layers four and five of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain and is entering layer six.
    • It shows in the operating metrics (Semicon volume, a record Industry margin of 18.6 percent, working-capital days, site count), in the financial statements (the first-half margin and earnings per share) and, in the last three months only, in the sell-side's estimates.
  • Our modal earnings path sits at the bottom of the target band, and a plausibly worse drop-through takes it below.
    • Earnings per share on the brokers' basis rise from 2.54 EUR (FY2025) to about 3.54 EUR by FY2028, 11.7 percent a year, carried by revenue growth (3.1 percent a year) and margin (5.7 percent a year) with small help from interest and tax (1.6 percent) and the share count (1.1 percent).
    • If interest stays at 48 EURm, the tax rate on the adjusted base is 27 percent and the buyback stops, FY2028 earnings per share are 3.26 EUR, about 8 percent below the modal path, and the eighteen-month return falls to 6.6 percent a year, an F on the framework's anchors.
  • The price has travelled further than the chain.
    • The shares are up 56.6 percent in twelve months (2026-09-23), the 24-month-forward multiple of 12.4x sits at the 48th percentile of the stock's own ten-year history and the 84th percentile of the last two years, and the entry that a buyer below 25 EUR had in late 2025 is gone.
  • The return at this price misses the bar on the modal path.
    • Holding the multiple at 12.5x, the ten-year median rounded, on the earnings two years beyond each horizon gives 51.1 EUR at eighteen months with the dividend, a return of 21.7 percent, 14.0 percent a year, and 59.6 EUR at three years, 12.4 percent a year
    • on the one-year-forward footing the rates are 12.1 and 13.7 percent a year.
    • The bar is cleared only if the multiple reaches about 14.3x on the mix shift to Semicon, which gives 24.4 percent a year over eighteen months
      • that branch needs Semicon's profit share to become visible at a quarter or more of the group (probable, about 60 percent) and the market then to price the mix rather than the conglomerate (plausible, about 50 percent), and the product is about 30 percent, which is possible.
    • The price below which the modal path clears the bar over eighteen months is about 38.8 EUR
      • today's 41.96 EUR is 8 percent above it.
  • The pattern is 'Major Corporate Change', and the fit is fair rather than crisp.
    • The output layer is dominated by a cyclical Semicon recovery the internal change did not cause, and the pattern's defining output, major upgrades to the earnings trajectory, has appeared only in the last three months.

  • The case therefore is compelling on the fundamentals and not compelling at the price: a recovery we can largely prove, an earnings path inside the band, and a return that needs a re-rating we cannot call probable.

  • Rating = C, because the change forces are material and the outputs are improving at a rate inside the target band, but the third leg of the chain, a 'Modal return' of 20 percent a year, is not met at today's price without a re-rating we cannot call probable

    • a B needs all three legs, and the return leg is a C at eighteen months and at the D boundary at three years.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The strongest case against, in its strongest form, is the one the risks report of 8 June 2026 calls the "false friend": that the first-half beat is a cyclical bounce dressed as a structural turn.
  • Its evidence is real.
  • About half of the 90 basis point margin gain is the arithmetic of deconsolidating KAN and Metalis (50 to 60 basis points on Bernstein's count, 70 to 80 on the risks report's) rather than operating improvement.
  • Building EBITA fell 3 percent to 100.4 EURm and missed consensus of 104.0 EURm.
  • Holding costs rose to 13.6 EURm in the half, 3.6 EURm above consensus, and Jefferies has raised its modelled holding costs to 27 EURm for the year, which is why its FY2026 EBITA estimate did not move on a revenue upgrade.
  • European residential building in Germany, France and the United Kingdom is still in a severe cyclical trough.
  • FY2025 cash conversion of 64 percent was flattered by a 155 EURm inventory release, and FactSet's own free cash flow was 47.7 EURm, 32 percent of reported net income.
  • And the shares have already re-rated from below 25 EUR in late 2025 to 41.96 EUR.
  • What we concede: all of the above on the price, and most of it on the mix of the margin gain.
  • Why we still hold our view of the fundamentals: the Semicon acceleration is in the reported numbers (revenue 335.7 EURm, EBITA up 88 percent to 47.5 EURm in the first half) and is corroborated by ASML's backlog, ASML's stated capacity plan of 110 EUV systems by 2028 and the Dronten plant reaching commissioning in early 2027
    • the Industry margin of 18.6 percent (20.1 percent in the second quarter) is a record and is not a deconsolidation effect
    • and the pricing discipline that held the added-value margin at 63.0 percent through two years of falling volume is proven, not asserted.
  • Where the competing hypothesis wins is on the price, and that is where our rating sits.
Change Forces: are the input change forces sufficiently strong? (E 1, BB 1) Rating = B
  • Rating = B, because the external force in Semicon is sector-wide and evidenced, the internal forces are broad (seven of the twelve screen forces active) and the two act in the same direction
    • not an A because the largest segment, Building, faces an external headwind that is not turning, and because the chief-executive change is three years old.
  • Same direction: the internal change is putting capital where the external force is strongest (Semicon, aerospace) and taking it away from where the headwind sits (low-margin building products), so the two forces compound rather than offset.
  • Timing: the external force is new and intensifying
    • the internal force is mostly executed and now needs to show in organic delivery, which is the part the chief executive himself said must "do better".

Revenue and demand cycle: where is the company's own cycle, and is it turning? (KQ 6, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = B
  • Rating = B, because the company's own cycle has turned: organic growth went from -3.4 percent (FY2024) and -2.5 percent (FY2025) to +2.8 percent in the first quarter of 2026 and +5.0 percent in the first half, and the second quarter was stronger than the first in every segment (Semicon 16.4 percent, Industry 7.0 percent).

Macroeconomic: is a macro force acting on this company more than on the universe? (KQ 7, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = C

Supply side: input costs, availability, concentration? (KQ 5, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = B

  • Inventory days: days inventory outstanding rose to 90 at the half-year from 82 at end-2025, an intentional buffer for the mechatronics ramp

Disruption: is a disruption force strengthening or being disproved? (KQ 3, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = B

Political and regulatory: is a change moving demand, pricing or cost? (KQ 4, RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = C

  • This is a cyclical recovery with a self-help overlay, priced as if the recovery were already normal.
  • Aalberts is a three-segment Dutch engineering group whose Semicon business (15 percent of FY2025 revenue) has inflected hard on the AI-driven lithography cycle (organic growth 16.4 percent in the second quarter of 2026 against a consensus of 3.3 percent; ASML's 38.8 billion EUR backlog), whose Industry business is at a record 18.6 percent margin on aerospace and defence, and whose Building business, half the group, is still at the bottom of a European residential trough.
