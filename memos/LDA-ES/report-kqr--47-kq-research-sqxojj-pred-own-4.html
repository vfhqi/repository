Stanley highlighted that a primary benefit of this overhaul is reducing the risk margin cost of capital from 6% to 4.75% on non-hedgeable liabilities. This reduction directly shrinks the liability side of the solvency balance sheet. For short-tail non-life insurers, this change structurally raises available own funds and reported solvency ratios.
5.
Lower asset charges: The revised Solvency II framework will lower capital charges for specific asset classes to encourage more long-term financing. According to Amundi SA, the reforms will significantly recalibrate capital requirements for senior simple, transparent, and standardized securitisations, aligning them with covered bonds. In addition, Morgan Stanley noted that capital charges for non-STS securitised assets will also decline. These amendments should allow insurers to optimize their investment portfolios without consuming excessive regulatory capital.
6.
Improved equity treatment: The 2027 rules introduce more favourable criteria for long-term equity investments to stimulate capital allocation into European companies. These long-term holdings will benefit from a reduced capital charge of 22% provided they are held for at least five years. Concurrently, standard formula refinements will adjust the symmetric equity parameter to reduce short-term market volatility. This regulatory combination will enable insurers to manage equity exposure with greater stability over multi-year underwriting cycles.
7.
Contrary evidence: Some analysts suggest that the capital releases from the 2027 reforms could trigger aggressive underwriting behaviour rather than boosting shareholder returns. Jefferies reported that mutual insurers might choose to return their solvency gains to customers by lowering premium prices to capture market share. Such pricing pressure could spark a highly competitive price war in the Spanish personal-lines market. Consequently, this development could compress profit margins for commercial players like Linea Directa Aseguradora and dilute the earnings benefits of the regulatory capital release.
