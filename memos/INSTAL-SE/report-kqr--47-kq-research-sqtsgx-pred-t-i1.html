Multi-stage German platform acquisition

1.

This initial transaction required a booked investment of SEK 168 million, which was primarily funded by a directed share issue. The transaction provided a low-risk gateway into Europe's largest installation market.
2.
Majority step: On 16 July 2026, the company entered into an agreement to acquire an additional 27 percent of Fabri AG, elevating its stake to 51 percent. The cash consideration for this majority step amounted to approximately EUR 35.7 million, which was financed using its existing credit facility. Consequently, this transaction triggered majority consolidation of the German platform starting from 1 August 2026. Consolidating these assets will help the group expand beyond its core Nordic borders.
3.
Remaining options: The remaining stages of the acquisition are governed by option structures that stretch out to the next decade. The third step contains an option to acquire another 17 percent of Fabri AG in 2029, conditional on the platform reaching agreed profit thresholds. These structured tranches protect the group against underperformance risks in a foreign market.
