Section 5 – The Takeover Code, Offer Period Dynamics and Consensus Anchorage

Takeover Code Guidance Constraints and Disclosure Capabilities

1.
Formal guidance under Rule 28: Mitie Group PLC's financial targets established in its FY25-FY27 strategic plan are treated as formal profit forecasts under Rule 28.1 of the UK City Code on Takeovers and Mergers. In its Regulatory News Service announcement of 21 July 2026, the company confirmed that its FY27 operating margin floor target of at least 5.0% and its annual free cash flow target of £150m constitute profit forecasts under the Takeover Code. This regulatory classification requires the board to reconfirm that these forecasts remain valid and properly compiled in every subsequent offer-related document. Consequently, management faces strict procedural requirements if it intends to adjust or replace these targets during the active offer period.
2.
Procedural burdens of guidance revisions: Modifying existing guidance or introducing new financial projections while subject to the Takeover Code imposes onerous reporting requirements. Takeover Panel rules mandate that any revised profit forecast issued during an offer period must be reported on by both independent financial advisers and reporting accountants. In regulatory filings regarding similar UK public transactions, offerors and offerees routinely seek dispensations from these formal reporting requirements only when presented on a basis consistent with ordinary-course quarterly guidance. Therefore, Mitie management is highly unlikely to alter its formal FY27 margin floor or cash flow targets at the upcoming H1 FY27 results on 19 November 2026. constraints during offer periods: The active offer period significantly limits Mitie's disclosure capabilities regarding medium-term strategy and post-FY27 operating plans. Under UK Takeover Code provisions, neither the offeree company nor any person acting in concert with it may enter into offer-related arrangements or disclose non-public operational targets without Panel consent. Broker research from Jefferies on 21 July 2026 observed that trading updates during an offer period become largely academic from a fundamental valuation standpoint. As a result, investor communication will remain strictly focused on tracking performance against established baseline targets rather than unveiling new strategic initiatives.
3.
Regulatory clearance progression: The transaction has successfully passed major international regulatory milestones, narrowing the remaining conditions to UK competition review. Mitie announced in an official regulatory update on 21 October 2026 that the European Commission granted unconditional merger control clearance. Furthermore, a news report on 22 October 2026 confirmed that the deal received national security clearance from the UK Investment Security Unit. These approvals satisfy critical closing prerequisites, leaving the UK Competition and Markets Authority Phase 1 decision as the final major regulatory hurdle.
