  2. Strong capacity utilization and margin resilience at major competitors mitigate immediate fears of execution bottlenecks or market demand slowdowns.

1.
Robust pricing: Deutsche Bank Research forecasts that Prysmian's upcoming Q3 2026 results will show solid performance driven by surging data center power cable demand. The broker expects Transmission sales to reach EUR 1,049m with organic growth of 15% and stable margins of 20.9% due to favorable project phasing.
2.
Market saturation: Bernstein Research highlights that Prysmian's Power Grid segment remains fully saturated with pricing acting as the core growth driver. High-voltage execution remains smooth as the company works to pass midwest premium costs to customers.

Backlog security: Jefferies Research notes that Prysmian's record high-voltage backlog of EUR 18bn provides highly visible revenue through 2030. Investors are encouraged by the group's ability to maintain strong structural pricing power and consistent cash generation.

This local gap is unlikely to infect major European projects where multi-year utility commitments remain highly secure.

This strong performance has significantly eased investor anxiety regarding Halden plant fixed-cost underabsorption following the suspension of the Great Sea Interconnector.

7.
Selective growth: Jefferies Research maintains a more cautious Hold rating on Nexans due to slower relative growth rates compared to pure-play peers. The broker notes that while Nexans benefits from improved Transmission visibility, its upside to consensus remains constrained relative to NKT.
