EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Nordnet operates as the only pan-Nordic digital savings platform of scale, serving 2.4 million active savers across Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland with SEK 1,214 billion in savings capital as of March 2026. The platform combines equity brokerage, mutual funds, tax-advantaged pension wrappers, and selective lending into a unified digital offering, generating revenues split between 57% net commission income and 43% net interest income.

Under new CEO Rasmus Järborg (effective April 2026), Nordnet has consolidated fund operations, proprietary asset manager Nordnet Fonder, and private banking into a dedicated Wealth Management business area led by Alexander Frick, shortening the product innovation cycle. The business model delivers exceptional operating leverage, with revenues growing 25% annually since 2019 against only 7% cost growth, supported by Google Cloud migration (56% of applications) and 99.9% platform availability.

Nordnet's financial profile is distinguished by a 42% adjusted return on equity in 2025, 18.4% CET1 ratio, and disciplined capital allocation through a 70% dividend payout ratio and SEK 250 million share buyback program completed in Q1 2026, though the business remains highly exposed to cyclical interest rate sensitivity.
