Section 2 – FY2027 Guidance and FY2027-29 Financial Framework

Section summary
  1. Diageo is highly likely to meet its near-term fiscal 2027 targets due to aggressive self-help cost measures.
  2. Deleveraging to the target level by fiscal 2029 should unlock substantial capital returns through share buybacks.
