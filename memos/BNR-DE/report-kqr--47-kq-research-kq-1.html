Section 2 – Organic growth-rate deltas & volume trajectory 17 findings

Historical Cycle Dynamics and Post-COVID Surges

1.
Exceptional post-pandemic surge: During the 2021 to 2022 supply chain disruption, Azelis recorded 16% and 20% organic revenue growth, while IMCD delivered 19% and 34% organic EBITA expansion
2.
Commodity pricing tailwinds: Brenntag generated record operating gross profit growth during 2021 and 2022, driven by severe price escalation across its Essentials bulk chemical supply chains
3.
Cyclical volume contraction: The subsequent 2023 to 2024 destocking cycle drove organic revenue declines of 6% at IMCD and 1% at Azelis, while Brenntag experienced broader volume-driven revenue headwinds
4.
Peer recovery inflection: Second-half 2024 results marked a return to organic growth, with Jefferies noting ingredients volume growth of 6.5% across specialty markets
5.
Resilient gross margins: Throughout the 2023 downcycle, IMCD and Azelis maintained gross margins above 23- 24%, demonstrating structural pricing power despite falling underlying volumes
6.
Middle East catalyst: Geopolitical supply volatility in 2026 drove a sharp pricing recovery, enabling Brenntag to raise full-year operating EBITDA guidance to between €1.35bn and €1.45bn

Division-Level Volume versus Price/Mix Breakdown

9.

China pricing pressure: Deutsche Bank research highlights that excess chemical production from China created persistent pricing pressure on semi-specialty products across Asia-Pacific and Latin America

Unannounced Mandate Churn and Portfolio Opacity

1.
Opaque principal churn: Chemical distributors do not publicly disclose mid-tier principal mandate gains or losses, masking localized market share shifts between Brenntag Specialties and pure-play peers. Resolution requires targeted expert interviews with regional commercial directors across key chemical producers like BASF and Dow to track mandate rotation
2.
Unreported private label expansion: Financial filings fail to break out revenue contributions from distributor-owned private labels versus third-party principal brands across Life Science end-markets. Resolution requires systematic filing analysis of segment notes alongside field visits to regional customer research laboratories to evaluate brand substitution rates
3.
Unmeasured platform cross-selling: Commercial execution data regarding customer conversion from Brenntag Essentials to Specialties lacks standardized disclosures across regional divisions. Resolution requires expert calls with former divisional sales directors to verify account reactivation rates and cross-selling hit rates
4.
Unquantified disintermediation risk: Direct sales migration to supplier digital portals is unmeasured in quarterly commentary, creating uncertainty around long-tail customer retention. Resolution requires a structured waitand-monitor protocol on producer direct-to-customer digital portal updates combined with principal channel surveys

Segment Profitability and Margin Granularity Gaps

5.
Unreported customer tiering: Segment reporting omits gross margin and conversion metrics split by customer order size or annual spend tier. Resolution requires primary expert interviews with procurement managers and field visits to customer manufacturing facilities to benchmark pricing power
6.
Concealed corporate reallocations: Management disclosures provide insufficient detail on how central digital IT expenses from programs like DiDEX are reallocated between operating divisions. Resolution requires forensic filing analysis of annual report notes and management Q&A tracking during upcoming investor field days
7.
Obscured pricing splits: Quarterly trading updates frequently combine unit price shifts and volume mix changes into single organic growth numbers during volatile pricing periods. Resolution requires wait-and-monitor tracking of chemical price indices alongside annual report volume disclosure reconciliation across peer groups
