Consensus EPS and Direction of Drivers

A validated Bloomberg consensus for Robertet was not available in the research, so the EPS figures below are individual broker estimates rather than a verified consensus. Jefferies (November 2026) projects FY26E EPS of €44.70 and FY27E EPS of €49.40, implying about 10.5% growth. Bernstein (October 2026) forecasts FY27E EPS of €49.54, which is close to the Jefferies figure. Drivers pointing above current estimates are fine fragrance and emerging-market momentum, which led Jefferies to raise consolidated EBITDA forecasts for the next three years by more than 3%.

Drivers pointing below estimates are persistent oil-derived raw material inflation and rising depreciation charges from capital projects, with Bernstein expecting a 2026 EBITDA margin of 19.6%, below the 20.6% recorded in 2025.
