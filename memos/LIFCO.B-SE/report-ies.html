Lifco (LIFCO.B-SE): what the Q3 2026 results do to the existing case

These results weaken the existing case materially: the organic recovery the case treated as cleared has gone back to open, a customer worth about 5% of group sales is leaving, and the share price has fallen to the case's own SEK 285 trigger for reasons that undermine the arithmetic behind that trigger.

1. What changed

Reported on 23 October 2026 for the third quarter of 2026:

  • Organic growth 1.6%, against 4.7% in Q2 2026. Redeye had expected 3.5% and DNB Carnegie 4% to 5%. Year-to-date organic growth is 2.5%. The case's modal path is 3% to 5% through 2027, with an alarm below 2% in a quarter.
  • The organic growth sat in one segment. Systems Solutions grew 8.3% organically. Dental was -0.8%, Environmental Technology -0.8%, Demolition & Tools 1.3%, Transportation Products 3.5%. The case invalidates the organic-recovery driver below 1% in Dental or Systems Solutions; Dental is below that line.
  • A Contract Manufacturing customer of about 5% of group sales is to cut forecasts and move purchasing outside Europe from January 2027. Redeye models SEK 750 million of lost sales and SEK 113 million of lost EBITA in 2027, on a 15% decremental margin. Management declined to give the customer's margin. DNB Carnegie expects no replacement volume before Q3 2027.
  • Profit held. Net sales SEK 7,407 million (+8.3%: acquired 5.6%, currency +1.1%). EBITA SEK 1,708 million (+10.7%), 0.8% above the Bloomberg consensus of SEK 1,694 million and 2.1% below Redeye's SEK 1,745 million. EBITA margin 23.1% (+0.5 points). EPS SEK 2.15 (+9.7%), below the 12% the case's path carries. Operating cash flow SEK 1,662 million (+19.2%). Interest-bearing net debt 1.0x EBITDA at 30 September 2026.
  • Segment margins. Demolition & Tools 24.6% (-0.7 points; the case wants above 25% by Q1 2027, alarm below 24%). Dental 22.5% (the case refutes below 22% by mid-2027). Transportation Products 20.6% (-2.3 points, on UK weakness).
  • Acquisitions. The interim report gives seven companies and about SEK 670 million of annual sales in nine months; management on the call gave eight companies and more than SEK 1 billion. Micronova and Maxlight consolidate in Q4 2026; their sizes are not given. Redeye puts the 2026 paid multiple at about 7x EBITA. The case's H1 2026 figure was about SEK 500 million; it confirms at SEK 2.5 billion or more in H2 2026 and alarms below SEK 1.5 billion.
  • Sell-side. DNB Carnegie cut adjusted EPS by 2% for 2026 (SEK 11.50), 6% for 2027 (SEK 12.40) and 5% for 2028 (SEK 13.40), cut its target to SEK 338 from SEK 373 on 26 October 2026, and kept Buy. Redeye cut fair value to SEK 302 from SEK 325 and its base case to SEK 290 from SEK 340 on 29 October 2026; its reported EPS is SEK 8.76, 10.17 and 11.64 for 2026 to 2028, which it says is 3% below consensus for 2026 and 2027 because consensus does not yet reflect the customer loss. Only these two brokers had published.
  • Index. DNB Carnegie expects Lifco B to leave the OMXS30 on 1 December 2026, with about SEK 18 billion trading across all the index reviews on 30 November 2026.
  • Price. SEK 285.0 on 9 November 2026, against the case's SEK 317.8, 6.8% below the 200-day average.

2. The challenge

Reading one: the organic turn the case banked was one customer's ramp, and the rest of the group has not turned. This is the strongest reading and the case does not hold it. The case calls the demand concern "cleared in hard evidence" on Q2 2026's 4.7%, and its own evidence included Contract Manufacturing at +16.1%. Management now says the departing customer expanded rapidly through 2024 and 2025 and built an outsized concentration inside Systems Solutions. In Q3 that segment supplied the organic growth and four segments out of five were at or near zero. If the customer's ramp was most of Systems Solutions' growth, the group excluding it grew little in 2026, and 2027 organic growth is probably around zero or negative before any recovery elsewhere. The same reading reaches the margin: Systems Solutions' EBITA rose 39% on high capacity utilisation, so the 23.1% group margin may be flattered by volume that is about to go. Redeye's 15% decremental is an assumption; at twice that rate the loss is about SEK 225 million, not SEK 113 million. For this to be right, the customer must account for most of the segment's organic growth and its true drop-through must be well above 15%. Neither is disclosed.

Reading two: the acquisition engine is constrained by capacity, not by price, and the case's invalidation line is live in this half. The case reads slow deployment as discipline. But Redeye says higher rates have held private multiples near 7x, leverage is 1.0x against a 3.0x ceiling, and management calls the pipeline active and its ambition very high. With price, funding and intent all favourable, a nine-month tally of SEK 670 million to a little over SEK 1 billion points at the centre's capacity or the pipeline's quality. The case invalidates on two consecutive half-years below SEK 1 billion of annualised acquired sales. H1 was about SEK 500 million, and Q3 added between about SEK 170 million and about SEK 500 million depending on which count is right, so H2 needs a large Q4. Three new business area heads in one quarter and the Systems Solutions head leaving in spring 2027 add to it. For this to be right, Micronova, Maxlight and the rest of Q4 must be small. It matters more now because, with organic growth at 1.6%, acquisitions carry nearly all the growth.

Reading three, the other way: the case has turned into the oversold setup it said it was not. A dated, forced, non-fundamental seller arrives on 30 November 2026. The price is down about 10% on a customer loss that Redeye sizes at SEK 113 million against 2027 EBITA of SEK 7,758 million. Redeye's reported EPS for 2027 and 2028 (SEK 10.17 and 11.64) still sits above the case's own path (SEK 10.10 and 11.30). On this reading the trigger at SEK 285 is valid and the deletion is the entry. For it to be right, organic growth outside the customer must stabilise, the loss must stay isolated, and the multiple must return to the case's held 28.9x on its reported basis. The weakness is that last condition: the price reached SEK 285 by de-rating, so clearing the bar from here needs a re-rating the case did not assume, and a lower earnings path moves the trigger down with it (a 3% to 6% cut puts SEK 287 at about SEK 270 to SEK 278).

One reading we do not put up: contagion across Contract Manufacturing. The remaining subsidiaries have different customers and end markets, and the case already held the largest customer at 4% to 6% of sales. Nothing in the evidence supports it yet.

  • BB1 Investment Thesis [C]: more pressure. 'Change Forces' rested on the company's own cycle having turned. 'Financial Outputs' and 'Goldilocks growth' rested on about 12% a year; Q3 EPS grew 9.7% and DNB Carnegie expects 2027 growth below Addtech, Indutrade and Lagercrantz. 'Sell-side upgrades' faces cuts instead. 'Modal return' is pulled both ways: a lower price helps, a lower path and a lower multiple hurt.
  • BB2 Foundations [B]: 'Business' the same (margin, cash and balance sheet all held); 'Momentum' more pressure; 'Gravitational pull' more pressure.
  • BB3 Investment Setup [B]: "oversold" less pressure; "clearing" more pressure, because the demand concern is open again; the count of concerns more pressure, because a customer loss is neither cyclical nor sentiment.
  • BB4 Investment Case [C]: more pressure. Part of the mark-to-market risk has been realised and the market leg of congruence is weaker. The Key Risk on the complexity ceiling and acquisition-market competition is under more pressure. The SEK Key Risk is under less this quarter (currency added 1.1%). The short-seller Key Risk is unchanged: no restatement, impairment or qualification, and no answer.
  • The price trigger: reached. The path it was calculated on is the thing these results press on.

4. What would settle it

  • The second review, four working days on. It is likely to carry the remaining brokers' estimates and targets, and so show where consensus settles once the customer loss is in. It carries no new company disclosure and settles none of the three readings.
  • 30 November and 1 December 2026. The index reviews and the deletion. How the price behaves after the forced selling tests reading three.
  • Year-end 2026 and the Q4 2026 report in late January 2027. The H2 acquisition tally against the case's SEK 1 billion, SEK 1.5 billion and SEK 2.5 billion lines, and the sizes of Micronova and Maxlight. This settles reading two.
  • The Q1 2027 report. The first quarter without the customer, so the first sight of the real drop-through and of organic growth without it, and the case's Demolition & Tools margin test. This is the earliest evidence on reading one. DNB Carnegie's replacement date is Q3 2027.
  • Not committed by the company: the customer's margin, organic growth excluding the customer, and any guidance. Lifco gives none. Until Q1 2027 shows it by subtraction, reading one is a question nothing scheduled answers.
