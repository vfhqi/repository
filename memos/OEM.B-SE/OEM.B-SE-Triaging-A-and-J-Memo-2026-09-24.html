[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. OEM International is the right company under a new owner doing the right thing, at a price that has already paid for it. EQT, in control since February 2025, has changed the board, the top team and the use of a net-cash balance sheet, and seven acquisitions in twelve months show the change working; but the shares have risen 53 percent in six months to SEK 187.2, the top of their own range, so the modal return is about 3 percent a year over eighteen months and about 7 percent a year over three years against the framework's 20 percent bar. It is a business to own below about SEK 150, or at today's price only on the day a named successor commits the balance sheet at the old discipline.

  • The change is internal, broad and already acting.
    • A new controlling owner has replaced nearly every layer of leadership above the subsidiaries and has changed what the company does with its cash, which is the one thing the old owners never did.
    • Ownership: EQT acquired 26.19 percent of the capital and 73.68 percent of the votes in February 2025
      • the four founding families sold about 75 percent of their holdings at SEK 110 a share, the largest insider sale in the company's 43 years listed.
    • People: a new chairman in April 2025 (Christopher Norbye, also chief executive of Beijer Ref), six of seven directors first elected in 2025, a Deputy CEO with an acquisition brief in April 2026, a new CFO on 1 August 2026
      • the chief executive of 24 years leaves the role by 31 December 2026 at the latest, and no successor is named.
    • Choices and actions: the acquisition mandate widened to product-owning niches, a dedicated acquisition team set up in 2025, and seven deals in the twelve months to July 2026 adding SEK 476m of annual sales, against about 3 percent a year of acquired growth over 2015 to 2024.
  • A cyclical recovery arrived at the same time, and it is not the change's doing.
    • Organic growth went from 1 percent in Q4 2025 to 5 percent in Q1 2026 and 8 percent in Q2 2026
    • incoming orders rose 23 percent in Q2 2026 to SEK 1,681m and the backlog 34 percent to SEK 1,271m
    • Eurozone manufacturing PMI was 52.7 in August 2026.
    • Construction in Sweden and Finland is still depressed and German industry is soft, so the tailwind is real, partial and cyclical, with about a year of visibility rather than the two-plus the framework wants for a demand-led case.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain for its first step.
    • It runs from the owner (resources) to the mandate and the hires (choices) to the deals (actions) to acquired growth of 9 percent of sales in Q2 2026 (operating metrics) to EBITA up 37 percent and EPS up 31 percent in that quarter (financial statements) to consensus EPS raised about 10 percent over six months (estimates).
    • The next and larger step, leverage toward the SEK 2,000m of capacity the sell-side names under a successor who keeps the cadence, is at the first two layers only.
  • The outputs have stepped up, and the path after the step is 10 to 11 percent a year.
    • Revenue grew 16.6 percent in Q2 2026 after 1.5 percent in FY2025
    • the EBITA margin was 16.2 percent in Q1 2026 and 15.4 percent in Q2 2026 against 15.0 percent in 2025.
    • Our modal EPS on the brokers' basis is SEK 5.40 for FY2026, up about 22 percent, then SEK 6.00, SEK 6.60, SEK 7.25 and SEK 7.95 for FY2027 to FY2030
    • consensus is SEK 5.36, SEK 5.86 and SEK 6.39 for FY2026 to FY2028.
    • The step is largely in the FY2026 base already
    • from here the rate sits at the bottom edge of the framework's 12 to 20 percent band over eighteen months and below it over three years on the 24-month-forward figure, at 9.3 percent a year.
  • The price has already paid for the step and asks the multiple to hold at the top of the stock's own range.
    • At SEK 187.2 the shares trade at 30.4x the dashboard's consensus 24-month-forward EPS of SEK 6.1645, the 100th percentile of the stock's ten-year history
    • at 28.75x on FactSet's five-year series, the 75th percentile
    • and at 29.0x the 24-month-forward figure on our path.
    • The dashboard peers trade at 21.9x (Indutrade), 22.2x (Beijer Ref) and 14.3x (RS Group)
    • on the sell-side's basis OEM's 25.9x 2026e EV/EBITA sits above Addtech's 24.1x and Lagercrantz's 24.2x and a sector median of 18.8x.
    • RedEye calls the level "significantly more demanding than before" and says it "leaves no room for operational hiccups".
  • On the modal path the return is about 3 percent a year over eighteen months and 7 percent a year over three years, an F against the bar.
    • We value the shares at each horizon on a 24-month-forward multiple of 26.2x, a blend of 40 percent on the multiple holding at 29.0x and 60 percent on it falling to the stock's own five-year 10th percentile of 24.3x
    • the blend sits within 0.3x of the five-year median of 26.51x.
    • Earnings growth carries the return at about 10 percent a year on the 24-month figure
    • the cash return is about 1 percent a year
    • the re-rating leg is negative.
    • At 29.0x held the return is 10.5 percent a year, a D on the framework's anchors.
    • Only the leverage branch, SEK 2,000m deployed within eighteen months at historical deal multiples, clears the bar, at about 26 percent a year, and we put that at possible, 20 to 25 percent.
  • The chain holds on the operations and breaks on the price.
    • The business is of the quality the case needs: a 30.7 percent return on capital employed in 2025, a gross margin of 53 to 55 percent held through the cycle, an EBITA margin above the 12 percent floor through three downturns, net cash of SEK 125m at 30 June 2026.
    • The two things the case needs from here, the deal cadence holding through the succession and organic growth of 5 percent or better through 2027, are each plausible at about 60 percent
    • the multiple holding needs both, and we put the joint odds at about 40 percent.

  • The case is therefore a 'Major Corporate Change' in a compounder, at the wrong price.

    • The entry level at which the same path clears the bar is about SEK 150 over eighteen months (about SEK 165 if the Q3 report and the successor announcement together make the multiple's hold probable) and about SEK 132 over three years
    • the 200-day average is SEK 150.4 and the price six months ago was about SEK 122.

  • Rating = D, because the change is material to the company and not, at this price, to us: the inputs (E 1 at B) are broad and acting, the outputs (E 2 at B) have stepped up along the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, and the 'Modal return' (E 3 at F) is 3 to 7 percent a year because the price has already paid for the step and asks a multiple at the top of the stock's own range to hold while EPS compounds at about 10 percent.

    • The parts bind: the return is what this Building Block tests, so the weaker part sets the level.
    • Not C, because a C would say the case ranks near the universe's median for materiality, and a modal return two bands below the bar does not.
    • Not F, because the operating chain is intact and the entry price at which the same case clears the bar is nameable.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The strongest case against our view is the sell-side's, in its strongest form: this is a proven compounder at the start of its second engine.
  • It has historically superior organic growth (7.0 percent a year over 2021 to 2025 against Addtech flat), cash conversion of 66 percent of EBITA over five years, a 30.7 percent return on capital employed, a balance sheet that can fund SEK 2,000m of deals at 2.0x net debt to EBITDA without equity, a sponsor whose playbook at Beijer Ref accelerated both acquisitions and organic growth, and a consensus that by construction leaves unsigned deals out.
  • At 15 percent-plus EBITA growth over the cycle, 27.8x 2026e EV/EBITA is the right multiple (DNB Carnegie's SEK 200 target), RedEye's fair-value range runs to SEK 235, and DNB Carnegie's leverage case gives 30 percent annual EBITA growth
    • a 29x multiple is not a de-rating risk but the level a compounder holds while it compounds.

  • The evidence for it is the Q2 2026 beat of 9 percent on EBITA, the 34 percent backlog rise, the seven deals, and the proven compounders' premium of 22x against 17x EV/EBITA for the unproven holding for five years.

  • We concede most of it.

  • The operating case is right
    • the business is of the quality the counter-case says
    • unsigned deals will enter consensus as they are signed, and each is an upgrade by construction
    • the leverage branch, if taken at the old discipline, justifies the multiple and returns about 26 percent a year.
  • Why we still hold our view: the return at this price rests on the multiple holding
    • the multiple holding rests on two plausible conditions whose joint odds we put at about 40 percent
    • and the branch that would justify the multiple outright, SEK 2,000m deployed, is possible rather than modal, because SEK 275m was deployed in the first half of 2026 against that capacity, the deal sizes are SEK 30m to 250m of sales, the person who would execute it is unnamed, the company's own stated ceiling is a debt-to-equity ratio under 100 percent which it has never approached, and its forty-year habit is caution.
  • A 40 percent branch that returns 10.5 percent a year and a 60 percent branch that returns about -2 percent a year is a modal 3 percent, and the framework's bar is 20.
  • The counter-case is a reason to own the business at about SEK 150, or at SEK 187 on the day the leverage is committed under a named successor
    • it is not a reason to own it at SEK 187 on the hope that it will be.
