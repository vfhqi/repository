The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. Organic growth about -2% (2026), +2% (2027), +2.6% (2028) and +3.5% (2029 and 2030); acquired growth about +11.5% in 2026 (Interket from January, NicEtikett and Tramex from July), about +2% in 2027 (carry-over), then about +2.5% to +3.5% a year from new bolt-ons. Price adds about 1% a year, as cost recovery only.
  • EBITDA margin. The 2027 step (+1.4 points) is self-help and mechanical: S:t Eriks no longer under-absorbing (Q2 2026 EBITA SEK 20m against SEK 47m), full-year cost programmes at S:t Eriks and Tornum, Ettiketto's co-location savings from Q1 2027 and Interket and Clever margins rising from about 6% to 7% towards 9% to 10%, Tramex for a full year. 2028 to 2030 adds cyclical recovery at S:t Eriks and Tornum, Interket and Clever at 11% to 13%, and bolt-ons at about 12%. No head-office savings.
  • Depreciation and amortisation excludes amortisation of acquired intangibles (SEK 100m, 108m, 112m, 117m and 121m), which the model should not include if it builds adjusted EPS. It includes right-of-use depreciation (IFRS 16). About 4.9% to 5.0% of sales.
  • Net financial items: interest at about 4.5% to 4.6% on net debt excluding leases (SEK 1,331m at Q3 2026), about SEK 12m of lease interest and about SEK 5m of other items. H1 2026 was flattered by interest allocated to discontinued operations.
  • Tax: 22.0% for 2026, 22.5% thereafter (more profit in Germany, the UK, the Netherlands and Ireland).
  • Dividend: SEK 0.30 for 2026 (policy 10% to 30% of net profit; the SEK 2.00 for 2025 was paid on the pre-spin group in 2026), rising with reported net profit.
  • Working capital about 19.5% of sales at each year end (continuing Q2 2026 about 20.5% of last-twelve-months sales after S:t Eriks' inventory release; Q4 is seasonally low).
  • Lease payments are the repayment of lease liabilities (principal) only; lease interest is in net financial items.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
