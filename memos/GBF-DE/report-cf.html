Plausible problems to monitor

Bilfinger's lean two-person Executive Board structure concentrates operational control in the CEO, creating a live CFO succession issue at plan midpoint around 2027 that could generate leadership instability precisely when the firm needs execution continuity to deliver its upgraded 2030 margin targets. While Bilfinger ranks second overall in peer momentum behind SPIE SA, it materially lags SPIE's best-in-class negative working capital model of -34 days versus Bilfinger's positive 9% of revenue, translating into structurally inferior cash conversion quality and earnings resilience (see Section 7).
