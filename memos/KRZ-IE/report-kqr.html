1.
Accelerating regulation: Tightening regulatory frameworks in the United States are forcing consumer packaged goods companies to immediately redesign their product formulations. Section 3 – Impact of Weight-Loss Drugs (GLP-1) on Supplier Volumes

Section summary
  1. Glp-1 weight-loss drugs are accelerating a major dietary shift toward high-protein, nutrient-dense, and portion-controlled foods.
  2. Specialized ingredient suppliers like Novonesis and Kerry are experiencing structural volume growth from these companion food innovations.
  3. We have medium confidence because while early volume headwinds exist, the mid-term shift drives higher formulation intensity.

GLP-1 Dietary Shifts and Market Projections

1.
Shifting consumer habits: The rapid uptake of weight-loss drugs is transforming the traditional American grocery shopping basket. Data shows that households using these therapies are increasingly choosing products that support hydration, muscle health, and energy. Consequently, these patients prioritize nutritional value over mindless eating as their overall appetite levels fall.
2.
Evolving consumer trends: Growth is accelerating specifically in wellness-supporting food categories like high- protein dairy and hydration. Purchases of low-calorie and sugar-conscious products are expanding rapidly among active drug users. For ingredient suppliers, this shift creates significant volume expansion opportunities in advanced functional food systems.
3.
Snacking divergence: Modern clinical weight-loss treatments are driving a sharp divergence between indulgent and health-focused snack categories. Consumer survey data highlights that active users report much lower consumption of traditional cookies and chocolate. Conversely, these patients are increasing their intake of protein bars to maintain lean muscle mass during treatment. This shifting behavior suggests that specialized nutritional products will outgrow conventional sweet treats over the next decade.
4.
Oral acceleration: The market expects a massive expansion in oral weight-loss drug prescription volumes starting in 2027. Industry projections suggest oral formulations could capture half of the clinical prescription market by 2028. This transition will broaden user adoption and multiply the consumer demand for portion-controlled and nutrient-dense foods.
5.
Volume pressures: Traditional sweet snacks face material mid-term headwinds as broader healthy eating trends accelerate across developed markets. Analysts forecast that these weight-loss medications could create a consistent annual volume drag of up to 30 basis points on unhealthy snacks through 2035. Consequently, manufacturers must aggressively reformulate their portfolios to prevent long-term volume erosion in mature regions.
