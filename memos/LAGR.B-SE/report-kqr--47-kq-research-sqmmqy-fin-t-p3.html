Section 2 – The Deal-Sourcing Loop

Section summary
  1. Decentralized divisional sourcing and regional M&A heads sustain Lagercrantz's proprietary deal flow at disciplined multiples.
  2. Rising deal volumes have not led to higher acquisition multiples, though average deal sizes show signs of shrinking.

Sourcing loop mechanics

1.
Sustained sourcing: Lagercrantz utilizes its decentralized structure to source proprietary deals through five independent divisions and dedicated country managers. By acting as a permanent home for founders, the group avoids auction processes and secures disciplined entry valuations below market averages. We found 18 relevant sources, 8 companies, and 3 precedents during our research and used all of them. This decentralized sourcing mechanism reduces reliance on central staff and keeps the acquisition engine active.
2.
Disciplined pricing: Redeye notes that Lagercrantz continues to buy businesses at multiples of 6x to 8x EBITA, showing no significant multiple inflation over the last five years. This pricing discipline is critical because it protects the group's high return on capital employed from being diluted by expensive acquisitions. If average acquisition prices rose to 9x or 11x EBITA, the group's compounding capacity would contract significantly.
3.
Division parallel: The company's five divisions work in parallel to evaluate regional niche leaders that fit strict profit targets. Sourcing is carried out locally in Denmark, Finland, the UK, and the Netherlands to establish trust with family owners. This localized model helps identify highly profitable product-owning businesses before they reach open auction markets.
