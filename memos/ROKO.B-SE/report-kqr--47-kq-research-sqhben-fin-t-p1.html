ETB Technologies, a UK-based specialist in refurbished IT memory and server components, experienced surging demand in 2025 and 2026, driven by global datacenter expansions and AI-related workloads. This demand coincided with global memory component shortages, allowing ETB to implement substantial price increases and achieve an exceptionally high margin drop-through on its sales. The Risk of Unwinding Cycles: Historically, similar IT refurbishment and memory distribution peers have experienced extreme margin volatility.
