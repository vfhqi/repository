Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Robertet maintains a leading global niche in natural raw materials, flavors, and fragrances, utilizing a vertically integrated Seed to Scent model that secures high barriers to entry.
  2. Strong historical organic growth and superior fine fragrance exposure have historically driven above-peer growth, but 2026 guidance cuts have pressured short-term stock performance.
  3. Major brokers maintain divergent views on the stock, with bullish views highlighting a deep valuation discount and cautious views focusing on near-term macroeconomic headwinds.
  4. Robertet's unique customer base of local and regional brands insulated the company from the volume stagnation impacting larger multinational competitors prior to the earnings release.
  5. Severe synthetic raw material cost inflation of up to 40% and transport price spikes represented critical near-term headwinds to the group's operating margins.
  6. The sell-side focused intensely on the exceptional growth momentum of the high-margin fine fragrance division while remaining cautious on slower-moving flavor and taste markets.
  7. Market participants highlighted Robertet's vertically integrated natural sourcing network as a highly defensive moat that justified closing the steep valuation discount to peers.
1.
Historically dominant: Robertet operates as a global leader in the natural raw materials, flavors, and fragrances sectors, headquartered in Grasse, France. The company supplies natural ingredients to cosmetics and food manufacturers while also formulating proprietary fragrances and flavors. Sourcing and processing living plant materials form the core of Robertet's business model, positioning the group as a highly specialized player in a commoditizing sector. Sourcing advantages provide a differentiated niche against larger synthetic-focused rivals in the specialty chemicals market.
2.
Structurally integrated: Robertet leverages its proprietary Seed to Scent business model to maintain end-to-end control over its natural supply chains. The company manages plant sourcing, agricultural cultivation, industrial extraction, and creative formulation within a single organization. Vertical integration spans over 30 production sites and partnerships in countries like Madagascar and Brazil. Sourcing agreements with local growers ensure unrivaled access to scarce natural materials that competitors cannot replicate with industrial capital.
3.
Steady expansion: Robertet delivered solid top-line growth over the past three fiscal years, with annual revenues climbing from €721.13 mm in 2023 to €807.61 mm in 2024, and reaching €843.93 mm in 2025. Annual sales grew by 12% in 2024 and expanded by an additional 4.5% in 2025 despite severe foreign exchange headwinds. Constant-currency revenue expanded by 8% in 2025, driven by strong commercial momentum in emerging markets. Sourcing advantages and client wins supported sustained long-term revenue growth for the group.
4.
Highly profitable: Robertet improved its absolute EBITDA and operating margins over the last three fiscal years as premiumization trends supported pricing. Consolidated EBITDA rose from €133.05 mm in 2023 to €157.07 mm in 2024, and reached €174.12 mm in 2025. The recurring EBITDA margin expanded from 18.4% in 2023 to 19.4% in 2024, and peaked at 20.6% in 2025. Lower raw material purchase costs and pricing power drove the exceptional gross margin expansion in recent years.
