Section summary

Section 3 – Changes in Software Pricing Models and Contract Values

Section summary
  1. Embedded software vendors are shifting from upfront perpetual licensing to recurring subscriptions and hybrid capacity-based pricing to monetize AI-driven developer productivity.
  2. Consumption-based and hybrid pricing structures are emerging to mitigate the risk of developer seat compression by linking contract values directly to AI usage or compute capacity.

This commercial pivot ensures that the company can gradually increase long-term recurring income even if upfront sales moderate.

Positive pricing: The transition to cloudbased subscriptions allows IAR Systems Group AB to realize significantly higher contract values over time. In its quarterly report from April 28, 2025, management disclosed that an example subscription customer pays 1.50 times their previous average payments for support, upgrades, and lifetime licenses. Under the old perpetual pricing, the vendor recognized 85% of license revenue upfront, whereas subscriptions distribute income evenly. Predictable cashflows: Software vendors prioritize SaaS transitions because recurring models deliver highly durable and consistent financial results over time. If embedded toolmakers delay implementing similar flexible options, they risk losing market share to agile competitors.
5.
Disintermediating threat: Cloud-hosted AI vendors and foundation model providers pose a direct threat to the traditional systems of record owned by embedded tool vendors. Analysts at Jefferies Research noted on November 11, 2026, that model providers are pushing from foundational models into the application layer, eroding software moats. Autonomous AI agents are increasingly routing around systems of record and disintermediating established engineering workflows. This shift means that the economic value of traditional software interfaces could compress rapidly as clients buy directly from AI labs.
6.
Expanded verification: An explosion in AI-generated code represents a major countervailing force that could offset potential developer seat compression. Analysts at Inderes Commissioned Research reported on October 30, 2026, that the spread of modern digital user interfaces continues to drive solid demand for Qt Group's product portfolio. This shift significantly boosts the demand for automated testing and static-analysis tools to govern synthetic codebases. Consequently, even if coding seats decline, vendors with strong quality assurance offerings can capture incremental compliance spend.

Market Friction and Resistance

7.
Contrary evidence: Some safety-critical device makers continue to show strong resistance to usage-based or credit-based licensing models. Analysts at DNB Markets Sponsored Research reported on February 11, 2025, that perpetual licenses and support agreements made up the vast majority of IAR Systems' sales. Safetycritical engineering firms actively resist switching to consumption frameworks that complicate project budget planning. Therefore, the transition to hybrid and consumption-based pricing may proceed much slower in regulated sectors compared to the broader software market.
8.
Tiered monetization: Embedded simulation and modeling vendors are actively pioneering tiered credit packages to monetize autonomous AI agent usage. Analysts at RedEye reported on November 11, 2026, that Modelon introduced Modelon Credits to turn AI features into concrete revenue streams. This commercial strategy allows the vendor to bill for both human developers and external AI agents that utilize its physics engine. By implementing these hybrid frameworks, vendors can successfully offset traditional seat erosion and capture new recurring revenue streams.
9.
Operational pressure: Traditional industry distributors face severe operational pressure as software procurement shifts away from simple seat-based transactions. A sales executive at a manufacturing organization reported on November 12, 2026, that procurement teams are aggressively reviewing license usage and trimming inactive users. Distributors who historically relied on highvolume, transactional seat renewals are seeing their margins squeezed. To survive, these partners must pivot toward providing specialized services and architectural oversight for credit-based systems.
10.
Other side of the story: Traditional per-seat developer licensing models may remain highly durable and insulated from AI- driven disruption due to extreme switching costs. An embedded software expert noted in December 2025 that safety-critical toolchains rely on deeply integrated, certified ecosystems that are exceptionally difficult to replace mid-cycle. Because replacing these integration architectures can delay qualification by several weeks, device makers will prioritize budget predictability and maintain established seat-based licensing.
