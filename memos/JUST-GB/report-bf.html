EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 35 findings

Just Group plc operates as a specialized UK retirement income provider focused on bulk purchase annuities, individual guaranteed income products, and lifetime mortgages, serving a dominant 31% share of the small-tomedium defined benefit scheme market under £100 million. The business has undergone an exceptional operational turnaround under CEO David Richardson, yet faces a sharp earnings compression in FY25 driven by heightened transaction costs, tightened pricing margins, and a 25-percentage-point drop in Solvency II capital coverage to 179%.

The competitive landscape is transitioning from a golden volume era to a more mature and price-competitive phase, with eight active players now crowding the UK de-risking space and global alternative capital platforms permanently reshaping market ownership. While the group benefits from highly predictable spread-based revenue lines and a differentiated underwriting moat, its foundation remains heavily exposed to UK property valuations and systemic credit risk.
