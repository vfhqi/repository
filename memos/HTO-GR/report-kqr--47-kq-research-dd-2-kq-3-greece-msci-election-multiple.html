Section 2 – Precedents of Index Reclassifications

Section summary
  1. Historical developed market upgrades typically drive absolute equity gains prior to their effective dates but lead to mixed performance and relative consolidation thereafter.
  2. Structural economic improvements and fundamental macroeconomic stability remain the primary long-term drivers of multiple expansion following index reclassification events.
