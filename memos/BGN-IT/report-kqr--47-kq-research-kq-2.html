Executive Summary

Section summary
  1. The key question regarding Alleanza channel net financial assets gathered is partially resolvable today through initial wrapper inflows and account activation trends, though long-term trajectory confirmation requires monitoring second-half deposit ramp-up. The Alleanza partnership gathered over €260 million in Stile Unico insurance investment wrappers from inception in November 2025 through June 2026, including approximately €200 million in the first half of 2026 alone. This initial commercial velocity represents 40% of the full-year 2026 target of €500 million in wrapper assets, establishing early evidence that product market fit exists within Alleanza's distribution network. However, Conto Unico banking account openings reached over 5,000 accounts by June 2026, accelerating sharply from approximately 1,700 accounts at the end of April 2026, tracking toward the full-year 2026 target of 15,000 new current accounts. The sequential acceleration in account openings following full nationwide rollout in April 2026 provides partial confirmation that advisor activation is progressing, though deposit volumes per account remain undisclosed. Reaching the midpoint target of €7.75 billion by 2030 requires an average annual asset gathering pace of approximately €1.45 billion across the 2026-2030 plan period. Against this mathematical benchmark, current runrates demonstrate meaningful initial traction but substantial execution risk over the remaining 54-month period. Achieving the €7.0-8.5 billion 2030 insurbanking target requires shifting the commercial mix from initial wrapper placements to sustained banking deposit conversion across Alleanza's affluent customer base. The structural pivot from insurance-led product distribution to wealth migration represents the critical second-order challenge that determines whether the partnership can scale from early adopter momentum into mainstream deposit gathering.

The primary target for asset migration consists of approximately €130 billion in client wealth currently held with third-party institutions, of which 80% sits inefficiently allocated in cash and bonds. This massive addressable pool provides the fundamental precondition for achieving management's baseline penetration target, yet also exposes execution vulnerability if traditional commercial banks aggressively defend deposit relationships.

Management's strategic roadmap assumes capturing a 5% to 7% share of third-party client wealth by 2030, while Deutsche Bank Research models a more aggressive commercial trajectory assuming a 10% wealth penetration rate, which gathers €13.0 billion in total financial assets by 2030. The wide divergence between base-case and bullish-case penetration scenarios underscores the fundamental uncertainty embedded in long-term trajectory forecasting.

Alleanza operates approximately 10,000 total consultants across the network, with 2,830 licensed private advisors serving as the primary distribution engine, and management plans to expand the private advisor cohort by over 40% to exceed 4,000 advisors by 2030. The completion of full advisor onboarding by mid-2026 removes a major operational bottleneck, positioning the partnership for accelerated second-half 2026 deposit inflows.

However, achieving the baseline target requires the 2,830 private advisors to gather an average of approximately €2.0-2.1 million in net financial assets per advisor by 2030, while initial wrapper productivity stood at approximately €92,000 in wrapper assets per advisor by mid-2026. This 22-fold productivity escalation requirement over the remaining plan period represents the single largest execution hurdle. Stile Unico policies incorporate a programmatic option that automatically transfers assets from traditional life reserves into unit-linked funds over a two-to-five year period, and automatic switching will elevate the unit-linked fund allocation from 46% to approximately 70-80% over 2-5 years.

This embedded revenue acceleration mechanism provides a structural tailwind that distinguishes the Alleanza model from traditional bancassurance distribution, as it systematically compounds fee-generating assets without requiring incremental sales intervention. The automatic switching architecture creates a high-conviction observable that will either validate or invalidate the business case by tracking policyholder opt-out rates over the next 12-18 months. - Earnings Calls Banca Generali projects that the Alleanza partnership will generate €40 to €50 million in incremental net banking income annually by 2030, representing a direct 6.5% to 7.0% contribution relative to Banca Generali's 2024 net profit baseline, while Deutsche Bank Research models a significantly higher revenue trajectory of €79 million by

2030. The near-doubling spread between management guidance and bullish sell-side projections reflects material analytical divergence on achievable penetration rates and fee margin sustainability. Jefferies projects that early commercial rollout will generate low single digit EPS upside for Banca Generali during 2026 and 2027, and as automatic wrapper switching and deposit accumulation mature by 2028, Jefferies projects high single digit EPS accretion of 6.5%. This phased earnings contribution profile implies that definitive resolution of the key question will not emerge until late 2027 or early 2028, when deposit accumulation velocity either confirms or disproves the mathematical feasibility of the 2030 target.

The landscape verdict is that the key question is partially resolvable today through triangulation of initial wrapper inflows, account activation velocity, and advisor network completion milestones. Within Banca Generali's primary network, total client assets per financial advisor reached €48.3 million by mid-2026, and Banca Generali gathered

€4.4 billion in total net inflows during the first half of 2026, delivering a 47% year-over-year expansion that outperformed broader market gathering rates. The core wealth management franchise demonstrates proven organic gathering capability, reducing execution risk around advisor productivity and client acquisition mechanics. However, extending this proven capability into Alleanza's distinct insurance-led distribution channel introduces novel commercial uncertainty around behavioral conversion from protection-oriented policyholders to wealth management clients.

Critical evidence gaps remain around net organic deposit transfers, agent activation velocity across the full 10,000-consultant network, and policyholder opt-out rates on automatic switching. These gaps are material but systematically resolvable through Q3 and Q4 2026 earnings disclosures, expert interviews with former Alleanza regional managers, and field verification visits to agency branches. The absence of granular deposit balance reporting and per-advisor productivity metrics prevents definitive mathematical confirmation of the 2030 trajectory, rendering the question fundamentally indeterminate over the full five-year horizon while remaining incrementally resolvable through quarterly milestone tracking.

The partnership creates meaningful second-order strategic optionality for Assicurazioni Generali, ranging from full minority buyout consolidation to partial stake monetization funding broader European asset management M&A. The capital-light nature of insurbanking allows Banca Generali to maintain its 85% dividend payout ratio without consuming CET1 capital or impairing balance sheet flexibility. This capital-efficient expansion path preserves balance sheet resilience while generating high-margin recurring revenue streams, positioning Banca Generali to sustain peer-leading return on tangible equity through market cycles.

The fundamental investment thesis hinges on whether management can successfully orchestrate behavioral conversion from insurance relationships into integrated wealth management relationships at sufficient velocity to bridge the €1.45 billion annual gathering requirement. Other side of the story: If deposit gathering encounters persistent client resistance or Alleanza advisors prioritize traditional life sales over banking cross-sell, annual asset accumulation may plateau below €1.0 billion, rendering the €7.0-8.5 billion target structurally unachievable and stripping 15-20% of projected EPS growth from consensus models, while triggering valuation multiple compression back toward 11.5-12.5x price-to-earnings from current 14.6-14.9x levels.
