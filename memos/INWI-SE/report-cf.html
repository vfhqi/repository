Outliers worth noting

Inwido maintains a persistently negative net working capital profile that has remained stable over multiple fiscal years, with cash flow from operating activities reaching SEK 515.4 million in Q2 2026 compared to SEK 374.5 million in the prior year, positioning the company in the bottom 5% percentile for working capital intensity among European building materials manufacturers. This stands in stark contrast to peer Svedbergs Group, whose net working capital rose to 26.2% of sales in 2026e primarily driven by Roper Rhodes inventory, and Deceuninck, which suffered a EUR 24 million working capital outflow in FY25 that drove net debt to EUR 98 million.

Inwido's structural cash conversion advantage stems from its decentralized make-to-order production model and superior supply chain orchestration, delivering a capital efficiency outlier that provides exceptional balance sheet flexibility for acquisition financing without requiring dilutive equity raises or covenant-threatening leverage expansion during cyclical downturns.
