Tecan's FY 2025 earnings represented a transitional inflection point where management delivered a surprising H2 profitability beat but guided disappointingly for 2026, fundamentally reframing the investment case from a volume-led biopharma recovery to a complex, self-help cost restructuring under the newly launched "Rewired" transformation program. Tecan reported full-year sales of CHF 882.5 million, down 1.6% in local currencies and 5.5% in Swiss francs, matching the lackluster expectations set by the preliminary trading statement published on January 9, 2026. Adjusted EBITDA for the full year came in at CHF 142.1 million, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of 16.1% of sales, matching consensus and management's guidance while absorbing a combined 200 basis point drag from adverse exchange rates and tariffs.
