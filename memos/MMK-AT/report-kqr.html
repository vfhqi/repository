European Union trade protectionism (cont.): If the European Union expands cold-rolled flat steel anti-dumping models (tariffs of 5.60% to 28.00%) into the packaging sector, import costs for low-cost Asian board will surge. European Union trade protectionism (cont.): This trade friction would structurally restore pricing power to domestic European virgin-biased producers, insulating them from the current Asian capacity additions.

2.
Major global consumer brands: Multi-national brand owners like Procter & Gamble and Danone have the means (massive procurement budgets) and motive (achieving public sustainability goals, including a 50.00% reduction in virgin plastic by 2030) to bypass transitional substrates. Their opportunity arises as nextgeneration barrier coatings reach commercial scale, allowing them to shift directly from rigid plastics to highperformance fiber packaging.
