Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side attention before the third-quarter earnings centered on the structural impact of Germany's recent overhead power line legislation.

1.
Comprehensive capabilities: Friedrich Vorwerk Group SE provides critical energy transportation and transformation infrastructure across German gas, electricity, and clean hydrogen markets. The company executes highly complex engineering projects, including pipeline laying and underground high-voltage direct current cable installation. Decade-long customer relationships with major German transmission system operators secure high revenue visibility for the firm. Insiders maintain a cautious stance on rapid capacity expansion because of the acute shortage of qualified civil engineering personnel.
2.
Steady historical growth: Kleesch & Vorwerk was established in 1962 in Tostedt as a specialized excavating subcontractor. The purchase of Bohlen & Doyen in 2019 expanded industrial execution capabilities in underground cabling and horizontal directional drilling. Financial sponsor MBB SE acquired a majority stake in the business during the summer of 2019. Corporate growth accelerated following the public listing in 2021 to support the clean energy transition across Germany.

Exceptional top line: Friedrich Vorwerk Group SE reported a sharp revenue expansion from €373.36 mm in the fiscal year 2023 to €498.35 mm in the fiscal year 2024. Annual revenues continued their strong upward trajectory to reach €704.33 mm in the fiscal year 2025. Peak contribution from the high- voltage Corridor A-North project drove this massive sales acceleration.

Rapidly rising profitability: Friedrich Vorwerk Group SE recorded an EBITDA increase from €31.99 mm in the fiscal year 2023 to €80.53 mm in the fiscal year 2024. The company achieved an EBITDA of €163.27 mm in the fiscal year 2025, which corresponds to a solid EBITDA margin of 23.18%.
