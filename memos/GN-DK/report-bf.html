This concentration stands in stark contrast to the oligopolistic structure where the top three western players (Jabra at 46%, HP Poly at 29%, and Logitech at 10%) collectively control approximately 85% of enterprise headset revenue, with EPOS capturing just 3% and Chinese/unbranded players controlling the remaining 12%.
