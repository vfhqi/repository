EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Zalando is transitioning from a transactional wholesaler into a multi-layered ecosystem spanning marketplace, logistics, and advertising services, with partner business GMV share increasing to 36.6% in Q1 2026. This structural pivot fundamentally reshapes the group's competitive position within European fashion retail, moving profit pools away from low-margin inventory-led distribution toward high-margin retail media revenues that grew 42% in FY25 to reach 1.8% of group GMV. The B2C segment includes the flagship Zalando app, Lounge by Zalando, and the newly consolidated ABOUT YOU commerce business, with each app targeting distinct customer shopping occasions while sharing common foundational tech and data infrastructure. The B2B operating system offers modular logistics via ZEOS Smart Logistics, which provides partners a 25% cost advantage over traditional drop-shipping, and Tradebyte serves as the integration layer connecting over 1,000 brands to 90+ European marketplaces.

Group revenue grew 16.8% to €12.35bn in FY25, while reported GMV increased 14.7% to €17.56bn, supported by the acquisition of About You. Adjusted EBIT rose to €591m in FY25 yielding a stable 4.8% adjusted EBIT margin, with core gross margins strengthening to 40.0% in FY24 and group gross margin reaching 39.1% in Q1 2026. However, free cash flow dropped to negative €2.9m in FY25, impacted by the €906.8m About You acquisition cash outflow, while capex fell to €222.5m as management focused on maximizing current distribution center automation. The balance sheet remains robust with a net cash position as €1.26bn cash comfortably exceeds convertible bonds of €484.1m, and the group historically maintained an attractive average negative cash conversion cycle of 9 to 20 days. Zalando's FY25 pro-forma GMV growth of 6.8% outperformed ASOS whose 1H26 GMV fell 9.0%, though Shein's 2024 GMV was estimated at $38bn dwarfing Zalando's mature scale of €17.56bn, while Otto Group posted a solid EBITDA margin of 8.20% in FY25/26 exceeding Zalando's margin profile.

The European online apparel market is undergoing significant structural evolution, with online fashion penetration forecasted to rise steadily to approximately 26% by 2028, though Zalando's share of European online apparel fell from 16% in 2022 to 13% in 2025 as secondhand specialist Vinted expanded its market share from 3% to 9% by 2025, while low-cost players Temu and TikTok Shop quickly captured a 2% European market share each.

Major brand partners like Nike report that EMEA marketplaces remain highly promotional as partners aggressively manage inventories, with Nike accelerating returns of aged inventory owned by partners and Puma reporting gross margin contraction of 260 basis points driven by increased promotional activity in wholesale channels. The regulatory environment is shifting favorably, as the proposed European Union €3 flat parcel tax is poised to compress the pricing advantages of cross-border fast-fashion importers, while impending European Union Ecodesign regulations starting July 2026 forbid the destruction of unsold apparel, raising operating costs for unintegrated sellers. - Earnings Calls - Earnings Calls - Earnings Calls Zalando operates across wholesale retail, marketplace platform, retail media advertising, and automated B2B logistics, with marketplace transaction monetization leveraging third-party merchants to capture transaction fees with commissions ranging from 7% to 25%.

Zalando Marketing Services leverages customer data to drive ad monetization expanding revenues 42% in FY25 to approximately €320m, while ZEOS Fulfillment provides costplus third-party logistics expanding B2B gross margins from 11.6% to 15.0% in FY25, and the integration of SCAYLE introduces high-margin software revenues with gross margins of approximately 85%. This multistage positioning creates defensible value capture, as retail media provides exceptionally high operating margins of approximately 40% to offset core retailing pressures, deploying enterprise software via SCAYLE captures a 85% gross margin enabling capital-light scaling, and open logistics via ZEOS secures volume and network utilization establishing a critical operating system bottleneck for continental brand fulfillment. & Co Redburn - Earnings Calls 23 Customer dynamics remain robust despite maturing penetration, with Zalando expanding its active customer base to 62.3 million unique users at the end of Q1 2026, rising 18.8% year-on-year primarily driven by the strategic integration of About You, while stand-alone customer growth remained stable at 5.9% in Q1 2025 reaching 52.4 million.

The upgraded Zalando Plus loyalty program expanded its active subscriber base to 18.5 million members with over 15% of the total customer base enrolled, and Plus members contribute more than a third of B2C GMV. However, Morgan Stanley analysts warn that underlying GMV is declining from existing cohorts due to natural consumer churn, and group-level GMV expansion remains heavily dependent on acquiring new customers raising long-term sustainability concerns.

The B2B segment demonstrates strong switching costs, as brands integrating with the Tradebyte marketplace solution face high switching costs due to complex multichannel data integration, while SCAYLE provides enterprise brand storefronts with a premier SaaS engine and Levi Strauss & Co. selected SCAYLE to power its borderless direct-to-consumer digital channels globally.

Supplier relationships reflect the platform's structural advantages, with Zalando maintaining an unparalleled network of over 7,000 global and European lifestyle brands, standard wholesale retail supplier payment terms requiring a minimum of 90 days net enhancing cash conversion, and the group employing reverse factoring agreements to transfer €559.70m of trade supplier claims to third-party financial providers. Operational restructuring is underway, as logistics infrastructure operates below optimal capacity at approximately 60% to 70% due to overexpanded distribution networks, restructuring efforts involve closing four under-utilized warehouses including the core distribution hub in Erfurt, and warehouse consolidation generated €96.80m in restructuring costs in Q1 2026.

Global brand partners aggressively expand their own directly operated online channels to capture higher retail margins, major brand partners increasingly compete directly against their wholesale channels via owned e-commerce stores, and leading sportswear brands Nike and Adidas target DTC ratios of 60% of total sales bypassing third-party aggregators, though strategic attempts by major global brands to shift entirely to direct-to-consumer channels have largely peaked, as smaller brands require Zalando's established digital reach and localized payments to scale across European borders cost-effectively.

The competitive landscape presents mixed signals, with Zalando's share of European online apparel falling from 16% to 13% while Chinese platform Shein reached a comparable 25% market share in the German online clothing market positioning Shein at a comparable scale level to Zalando's core German presence. Giant aggregator Amazon remains an ineffective channel for more premium branded fashion offerings, and Zalando's dedicated apparel focus protects its premium brand curation against Amazon's generic marketplace.

Platform defenses are strengthening through infrastructure integration, as Zalando's integrated ZEOS B2B logistics platform enhances brand switching costs across the value chain with ZEOS managing end-to-end multichannel fulfillment making it highly unlikely that partner brands shift logistics providers, while ABOUT YOU's SCAYLE provides enterprise shop software securing a lighthouse global contract with Levi's. ZEOS serves as TikTok Shop's preferred logistics partner in Germany, France, and Italy, allowing Zalando to earn logistics fulfillment fees even if social commerce cannibalizes platform sales.

Management quality and strategic execution demonstrate strong alignment, with Robert Gentz managing B2C consumer strategy to enhance digital app engagement through AI-driven personalization, David Schröder overseeing B2B development to scale modular operating capabilities off the platform, and CFO Anna Dimitrova joining on January 1, 2026 bringing 20 years of finance and M&A experience. Standalone mid-term guidance targets a 5-10% GMV and revenue CAGR through 2028, mid-term targets combined with About You target an 8- 13% GMV CAGR by 2028, and capital return has started via a €300m share buyback program representing approximately 5% of outstanding equity. Competitive moats are strengthening, as scalable technology and pan-European fulfillment infrastructure power both B2C and B2B segments, ZEOS logistics platform enhances switching costs for brand partners through deep backend operation integration, and SCAYLE enterprise software secured global DTC contract with Levi Strauss to prove technical capability. Pre-tax ROCE is projected to scale toward 40% on accelerated platform asset-light revenue mix transition, annual capex intensity is scaling down to approximately 2% of revenue until 2028, and free cash flow reached €1,126.30m in 2025 proving excellent underlying operating cash performance.

Long-term structural positioning remains defensible despite near-term disruption forces, as establishing a pan- European automated fulfillment network of 14 logistics centers requires massive capital investments that subscale digital competitors cannot replicate, scaled logistics networks enable Zalando to offer B2B partners up to 25% lower logistics costs than standard drop-shipping models, and the accumulation of over one million body measurements forms a unique data barrier that generalist AI algorithms cannot easily reproduce.

Zalando defends against search disintermediation by acting as one of two European launch partners for Google Gemini's Universal Commerce Protocol, while over 10 million customers interacted with the personalized AI Zalando Assistant in Q1 2026 up from 6 million in all of 2025. Growth adjacencies are expanding, with Zalando's 5-year partnership with the German Football Association until 2030 integrating the platform directly into athletic lifestyle culture, combined lifestyle categories including Sports, Beauty, Kids, and Designer achieving 13% GMV growth on a like-for-like basis in FY25, and the landmark partnership with Vestiaire Collective bringing authenticated designer resale to 14 active European markets.

The investment case centers on Zalando's structural transition from a capital-intensive wholesaler to a highmargin infrastructure platform, capturing value across retail media, marketplace commissions, and enterprise software while defending against disintermediation through deep B2B integration. Near-term profitability faces headwinds from logistics restructuring costs and About You margin dilution, but medium-term targets of 6-8% adjusted EBIT margins by 2028 appear achievable through warehouse consolidation savings, marketplace mix shift, and retail media scaling.

Peer benchmarking reveals Zalando outperforming traditional pure-play retailers like ASOS while trailing specialist competitors on EBITDA margins, yet the platform's unique multi-stage footprint and proprietary sizing data constitute defensible competitive advantages that cross-border fast-fashion disruptors cannot easily replicate. Market share erosion from 16% to 13% signals intensifying competition from recommerce and social commerce, though regulatory tailwinds from EU parcel taxes and textile waste regulations should structurally compress offshore competitor advantages.

Foundation quality is high given proven management execution, robust cash generation, and formidable B2B switching costs, with the primary risk being accelerated brand disintermediation if direct-to-consumer economics improve or social commerce permanently reshapes consumer discovery patterns.

Outliers worth noting

Over 10 million customers interacted with Zalando's personalized AI Assistant in Q1 2026 alone, up from 6 million in all of 2025, representing an extreme statistical outlier in conversational commerce adoption that places Zalando in the top 5% of European digital retailers for AI-enabled customer engagement. This explosive quarterly growth trajectory –effectively 67% quarter-on-quarter acceleration –demonstrates that Zalando is capturing early-mover advantages in agentic e-commerce infrastructure at a scale that peers like ASOS and Boozt have not publicly disclosed achieving.

The platform's selection as one of only two European launch partners for Google Gemini's Universal Commerce Protocol validates this technical leadership position and suggests that Zalando possesses proprietary conversational commerce capabilities that materially exceed peer base rates. As detailed in Section 10 Disruption Forces, this strategic positioning mitigates the existential threat of search disintermediation, yet the qualitative anomaly remains that competitors have not matched this adoption velocity despite similar customer database sizes, raising questions about whether Zalando's AI investment intensity or data infrastructure quality represents an unexplained structural advantage beyond disclosed capital expenditure levels.

Zalando Plus loyalty members contribute more than a third of B2C GMV and represent up to 47% of combined GMV in mature Zalando and Lounge segments, constituting an extreme concentration outlier that places the platform in the bottom 5% globally for customer base diversification risk when benchmarked against traditional multi-brand retailers. This qualitative anomaly reveals that the loyalty program's 18.5 million active subscribers –representing only 30% of the total 62 million active customer base –generate nearly half of all transactional value in core segments, creating a structural dependency on a narrow cohort of high-frequency shoppers.
