The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Grid volume is the main driver. Substations delivered about 3,400 (FY26), 3,650 (FY27), 4,000 (FY28), 4,300 (FY29), 4,600 (FY30) against 3,032 in FY25 (Q1 2026 833, Q2 883), plus price and mix of 0-3% a year (nominal indexation and turnkey stations; no real price). Volume is rationed by the Dutch operators' technician and permit constraints, not by demand or Alfen's capacity (the Almere plant can double output), so growth is set at +8-11% a year, below the operators' +13% plan; permit-free substation expansion from late 2027 is the reason FY28 is the strongest year.
  • Storage revenue follows intake of about €35m a quarter from Q3 2026 (Q1 2026 €54m, Q2 €11m, €30m booked after Q2; backlog €93m at June 2026, of which €43m for H2 2026). H1 2027 is down 33% against milestone-heavy H1 2026. Revenue per MWh falls 5-10% in FY27 on the renewed cell price war; back-to-back pricing with CATL keeps gross margin near 21.5%.
  • Charging volume turns modestly (about 108k points in FY26, 115k in FY27) on the new platform and UK and Southern European entry, against an ASP falling 4% (FY27) and 2% a year after; gross margin drifts from 39.5% (H1 2026) to 38.5%.
  • Opex: €104.1m in FY26 (including a temporary leadership overlap in H2, about €5m in my estimate; Jefferies €8m), €101.2m in FY27 (overlap -€5.0m, simplification -€1.5m, indexation +€2.8m, growth +€0.8m), then +3% indexation plus €1.5m of growth cost a year. Headcount held at about 910-950.
  • D&A (underlying, all-in, excluding impairments): FY25 €18.4m (FactSet's annual line of €13.6m leaves out €4.78m of capitalised-development amortisation and €3.7m of impairment); H1 2026 €10.2m. It rises because about €10m a year of development is capitalised on a five-year life while amortisation is about €5m a year; lease depreciation about €5m; plant depreciation drifts down.
  • Net financial items: lease interest about €1.5m, mortgage and fees, less interest on cash. Lease payments in Part 2 are lease principal only, so lease interest is not counted twice.
  • Tax: a credit at 24% on the FY26 loss; 22% from FY27 (historical profitable-year rates 19.7-22.9%, innovation box at an effective 9% on qualifying income, extended to 2029). Loss carry-forwards and the 2024 carry-back keep cash tax below the book charge to about FY28.
  • Capex: about 3.2-3.3% of revenue, including about €10m a year of capitalised development, within the company's "below 4% of revenue" ceiling.
  • Working capital is on the financials block's definition (receivables plus inventories less payables): 14.0% at FY25, 12.0% at June 2026, 13.0% at FY26 year-end, then 13.5% as growth absorbs inventory (management expects no further inventory reductions). Separately, customer down payments on storage (contract liabilities, not in this definition) unwind by about €12m in H2 2026 and €3m in FY27; this is in my cash-flow cross-check but not in the model's working-capital line.
  • Share count: 21.75m, plus about 0.05m a year from long-term incentive vesting (about 0.22m awards unvested at June 2026). No dividend ("no intention"), no buyback (cumulative free cash flow about nil to FY30), no acquisitions (bolt-ons paused).

How to read these figures
