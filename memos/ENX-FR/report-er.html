Section 16 – Conclusion

Euronext's Q2 2026 earnings delivered a substantial double-digit beat across all major lines compared to conservative sell-side consensus, driven by post-trade diversification and strong equity trading volumes, prompting the sell-side to react with overwhelming optimism and driving price target upgrades as key structural engines began delivering. The company has fundamentally transformed its investment thesis over the reporting period through the successful execution of multiple structural catalysts that significantly de-risk its mid-term organic growth trajectory.

The investment case entering the results period was supported by strong momentum and pan-European diversification, but the successful integration of Athens Exchange Group, imminent September 2026 CSD expansion, and the completed migration of Nordic power futures have fundamentally altered the company's longterm earnings profile. Management confirmed that Euronext is tracking significantly ahead of its Innovate for Growth 2027 financial targets, supported by strong momentum across both transaction-driven and diversified non-volume business lines, although management decided not to revise the 2027 CAGR targets of above 5% for revenue and adjusted EBITDA, reflecting a conservative underpromise and overdeliver posture. - Earnings Calls The most fundamental shift in Euronext's 12-24 month investment thesis centers on the transition from a volume-dependent continental exchange operator to a fully integrated post-trade powerhouse.

Euronext continues to pivot from its historical exchange model to a fully integrated market infrastructure under its Innovate for Growth 2027 strategic plan, completing a group-wide commercial restructuring by creating a single sales team for each asset class to eliminate local silos, structurally integrating its post-trade operations under the unified Euronext Securities umbrella brand to replace localized custody and settlement platforms, with Euronext Securities officially expanding its settlement services for cash equities to Paris, Amsterdam, and Brussels, representing a go-live on September 21, 2026.

This structural evolution is already visible in financial results, with underlying net treasury income through the central counterparty business reaching €22.5 million in Q2 2026, representing a robust +12.5% year-on-year expansion that beat the sell-side consensus forecast of €17.3 million by 30% as higher collateral deposits and wider spreads drove performance.

What would fundamentally change this analysis would be concrete evidence of either accelerated or substantially delayed custody client migration following the September 21, 2026 CSD go-live. Management assured investors that technology and regulatory risks are completely resolved, with several leading custodians participating in testing and confirming they will use the model from day one, although Boujnah described it as building a new market player, which implies a gradual ramp-up in client adoption, with complete custody migration expected to take time as clients gradually transition.

If Euronext Securities successfully captures meaningful custody volumes within the first two quarters–exceeding 10% market share of exchange-active client assets–the organic growth acceleration thesis would be materially validated, potentially justifying aggressive multiple expansion toward the EUR 200 price target established by Morgan Stanley.
