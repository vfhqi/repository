Section 6 – Supplier + Distributor + Channel Analysis 19 findings

Section summary
  1. Pirelli successfully mitigates Middle East logistics and raw material shocks through active surcharges and localized sourcing, while maintaining strict control over premium downstream distribution.

Upstream raw material dependencies and supply security

1.
Critical raw material basket: Raw materials represent 41% of Pirelli's total cost base, with natural rubber and synthetic rubber stably accounting for more than one-third of total procurement spend
2.
Oil derivative exposure: Synthetic rubber (butadiene) accounts for 22% of the raw material basket, natural rubber comprises 26%, carbon black and fillers make up 21%, chemicals represent 15%, and hot rolled steel for steel cord makes up the remaining 9%. Middle East raw material shock: The late-February 2026 war in the Middle East and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz triggered a sharp spike in crude oil, gas, and transport costs
3.
Sizable input cost inflation: Tensions led to a 24% year-on-year increase in Brent crude to $87.5 per barrel in 1H 2026, alongside a 12% rise in natural rubber TSR20 to $2,048 per tonne, directly threatening margins
4.
Mitigation plan execution: Pirelli estimated a gross negative EBIT impact of approximately €100 million for FY 2026 stemming from Middle East raw material, energy, and ocean freight inflation
5.
Substantial pricing recovery: Pirelli offset €80 million of the €100 million gross inflation through €50 million to €55 million in targeted price increases starting April 1, 2026, and parallel cost-containment measures, limiting the net EBIT headwind to €20 million
6.
Securing localized inventory: To bypass Strait of Hormuz bottleneck risks, Pirelli temporarily increased its safety stocks of oil derivatives (including butadiene) in the Asia-Pacific region
7.
Diversified logistics routing: Inbound trade flows were rerouted away from the Strait, moving via the Red Sea and overland through western Saudi Arabia and Oman to preserve factory supply continuity

Circular material partnerships and supplier ecosystem

8.
Strategic European Tyre-to-Tyre consortium: Pirelli coordinates a scalable European circular ecosystem with Pyrum Innovations, BASF, and Synthos to transform end-of-life tires into certified sustainable raw materials
9.
Pyrum Innovations offtake: Under long-term supply agreements signed in July 2026, Pyrum supplies Pirelli with ThermoTireBlack recovered carbon black from German end-of-life tires to replace fossil-based carbon black. BASF and Synthos mass-balance loop: Pyrum delivers pyrolysis oil to BASF to co-feed chemical production (butadiene and styrene), which Synthos then processes into ISCC PLUS-certified synthetic rubber for Pirelli's European plants
10.
Recycled carbon black scaling: In the United States, Pirelli launched a sustainable partnership with Bolder Industries to recover and reuse ISCC PLUS-certified carbon black via pyrolysis
11.
Certified steel cord alignment: In China, Pirelli collaborated with strategic supplier Xingda International to pilot and introduce ISCC PLUS-certified recycled steel into its global tire reinforcement supply network
12.
Xingda production scaling: Xingda expanded its radial tire cord capacity to 1.34 million tonnes in 1H 2026, utilizing its plants in Jiangsu, Shandong, and Thailand to support Pirelli's green sourcing standards

Downstream channels, distributor margins, and e-commerce

13.
Distribution-focused wholesale model: In contrast to Michelin's vertical retail integration, which creates channel tension, Pirelli remains distribution-focused and maintains healthy relations with independent European tire distributors
14.
Spot-deal optimization: Regional distributors interact with Pirelli on a spot-deal basis, enabling Pirelli to strictly manage inventory and prevent dealer overstocking
15.
Retail margin defense: High-value passenger car replacement tires, particularly in the 19-inch and above premium segments, capture the highest margin per tire for independent retail dealers
16.
Price pass-through dynamics: Price increases enacted by Pirelli to offset raw material inflation are being successfully passed through to retailers, though a short margin lag exists as older, cheaper stock cycles out
17.
Digital B2B2C e-commerce transition: Pirelli rolled out a proprietary B2B2C digital "shopping window" platform in Italy, Spain, Germany, Brazil, and Sweden
18.
Driving dealer foot traffic: The digital platform allows final B2C consumers to research tires and buy online while selecting a local partner dealer to book physical fitment appointments
19.
