Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side expectations for Convatec before the earnings update were highly focused on the sustainability of its organic growth acceleration and the transition to its 2027 financial targets.
  2. Market participants closely debated the potential headwind from US reimbursement changes in skin substitutes and the continence competitive bidding program, viewing them as key overhangs on the valuation.
  3. Pre-earnings focus on Convatec's margins centered on the demanding sequential step-up required in the second half of the year to meet the full-year adjusted operating margin guidance.
  4. Unlike its primary European peers Coloplast and Smith & Nephew, which faced guidance downgrades and operational restructurings, Convatec entered the earnings update with robust execution credibility.
1.
Established market leader: Convatec Group PLC operates as a global medical products and technologies company focused on therapies for managing chronic health conditions. The company holds leading positions across four major chronic care categories consisting of Advanced Wound Care, Ostomy Care, Continence Care, and Infusion Care. In 2025, the group manufactured and marketed over 1 billion high-quality consumable products to healthcare providers and patients in approximately 90 countries. This global footprint positions Convatec as a highly defensive business within the medical technology sector.
2.
Diversified revenue profile: Convatec generated total group revenue of over $2 billion during the 2025 fiscal year. Segment revenue was diversified with Advanced Wound Care representing 31% of sales, Ostomy Care 28%, Continence Care 22%, and Infusion Care 19%. Geographically, North America remains the largest regional market for Convatec at 56% of sales, followed by Europe at 30%, and the Rest of World at 14%. This geographic distribution helps insulate the group's EPS from localized economic downturns or specific regional changes in reimbursement policies.
3.
Long corporate history: Convatec was originally founded in 1978 as a specialized division of E.R. Squibb & Sons before merging with Unomedical in 2008 to establish its four core categories. The company subsequently listed on the London Stock Exchange in October 2016 through the largest initial public offering of that year. Convatec was later admitted to the FTSE 100 Index in September 2022 following a successful multi-year turnaround. This stock market history reflects a transition from private equity ownership to a mature public company.
4.
Improved operational execution: Convatec historically suffered from launch delays, manufacturing disruptions, and product portfolio mismanagement under private equity and early public leadership. Former chief executive Karim Bitar initiated the FISBE turnaround strategy in October 2019 to rebuild the company's competitive position. The strategy significantly increased research and development investment to rebuild the company's long-term innovation pipeline. This investment successfully revived Convatec's product portfolio and resolved historical execution weaknesses.
