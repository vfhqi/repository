[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. Beijer Ref is a cause-for-concern-clearing case in a solid-quality serial acquirer, and at SEK 145.3 it is not yet compelling. The private-equity overhang that held the multiple down has cleared, but the two things the re-rating needs, an organic line back above 3 percent in print and a permanent chief executive, are both still outstanding, so the modal return is about 10 percent a year against the 20 percent bar. The case clears the bar only below about SEK 128 on our path, or below about SEK 141 once both concerns have visibly cleared.

  • The change is three identifiable concerns lifting, at three different speeds.
    • The 24-month-forward multiple fell from a five-year median of 26.94x to 21.05x (2026-09-18), the 11th percentile of its own range, because of three things, and each is in a different state.
    • The private-equity overhang cleared on 11 June 2026, when Melker Schörling AB took all the A-shares (14.1% of votes) and EQT fell to a 6.4% economic stake.
      • This is done and partly priced: the shares are up 13.6% over six months.
    • The organic engine printed 2.6% in 2025, -1.4% in Q4 2025, -0.2% in Q1 2026 and +1% in Q2 2026.
      • Management and both covering brokers say it re-accelerates from Q3 2026, with about 5% underlying growth already visible once the Eastern European comparison is stripped out.
      • The first hard print is on 22 October 2026.
      • This is asserted, not shown.
    • The chief executive who built the EQT-era record resigned on 23 July 2026, effective 14 August 2026, with no successor named and no reason beyond "other opportunities".
      • This has just opened and there is no clearing evidence at all.
  • The internal change is a change in who owns and who leads, not yet in what the company does.
    • A new anchor owner, a new chair (Per Bertland, the former chief executive, from 23 April 2026), a lowered leverage ceiling (below 2.5x from below 3x) and a cash-flow target are all real changes at the resources and choices layers, but the strategy and the five-year targets (10 to 15% sales growth, 10 to 12% EBITA margin) were reaffirmed unchanged and there is no guidance to raise.
    • The framework's screen wants three strong forces or a chief-executive change plus a guidance increase
    • this case has a chief-executive departure and no guidance.
  • The external change nets to a slow regulatory tailwind against a present cyclical headwind.
    • The EU F-gas quota halves on 1 January 2027 and the US phases HFCs down 85% by 2036, so older systems must be replaced
    • the green OEM segment grew 25% organically in Q2 2026 with record backlogs into 2027.
    • Against that, US residential replacement is deferred (North American organic sales -2.9% in Q2 2026 after -1.4% in Q1 2026 and -4.1% in Q4 2025; system prices up nearly 100% since 2019), European construction is weak, and 98% of sales sit outside the krona, unhedged.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are improving off a weak 2025, but they have not turned in the metric that matters.
    • Reported EPS rose 4.6% in FY2025 (9.5% on the brokers' basis)
    • first-half 2026 reported EPS rose only 3.2%, so the whole of this year's consensus growth of 10.3% has to come in the second half.
    • The adjusted EBITA margin held at 12.1% in Q2 2026 against 12.2% a year earlier through a 1% organic quarter, which is what an aftermarket distributor is meant to do.
    • Consensus FY2026 EPS is down 6.8% over twelve months
    • FY2027 and FY2028 are up about 3% over three months.
  • The change has travelled three of the six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • It shows in resources (the owner; the departed chief executive), in choices (the leverage ceiling; the cash target; the Sinclair private label) and in actions (Enertrak in Canada; AM Distributors in Florida; the SEK 100m cost programme).
    • It has not yet reached the key operating metric, organic growth, nor the financial statements in a way the market prices, nor the sell-side's estimates for the current year.
    • That is where the chain breaks today, and it is the whole reason the rating is D and the recommendation is a watch.
  • What we think it earns: EPS of SEK 4.95 in FY2025 on the brokers' basis rising to SEK 5.40 in FY2026, SEK 6.05 in FY2027 and SEK 6.75 in FY2028, about 10.9% a year compound, just under the framework's 12 to 20% band.
    • Consensus (six brokers, 2026-09-24) has SEK 5.46, SEK 6.31 and SEK 7.03, about 12.3% a year and just inside the band.
      • We sit 1% to 4% below it because the second half of 2026 must carry the year and because a leaderless company acquires more slowly.
    • The growth is mostly revenue (about 7.0% a year, 4 points organic and 3 acquired), a little margin (about 1.5% a year) and a little below EBITDA (about 2.0% a year), with nothing from the share count.
      • The parts reconcile to the claim.
  • What it costs: SEK 145.3 is 21.9x the dashboard's 24-month-forward consensus EPS of SEK 6.64 (2026-09-23), and 21.5x our modal FY2028 EPS of SEK 6.75.
    • Against the stock's own five-year 24-month-forward range that is the 11th percentile (18.85x to 55.73x; median 26.94x; 25th percentile 23.45x)
      • against the last two years it is the 21st percentile (median 23.67x).
      • Against the dashboard's ten-year bands it sits between 'crash' at 18.50x and 'conservative' at 23.10x.
    • Against peers it is middling: parity with Indutrade at 21.9x, and an 18x EV/EBIT 2027E against the Swedish serial acquirers' five-year average of 24x.
      • Part of that discount is earned: adjusted EBITA margin 10.7% against 14.0 to 22.9% for the peers DNB Carnegie names, return on operating capital 11.4% against a 12% target, working capital 42% of revenue on FactSet's definition.
    • So the price is cheap against the stock's own history and fair against what the business currently earns.
      • What the case costs on the valuation is not the multiple
      • it is the two conditions attached to any re-rating from it.
  • The return does not reach the bar from here.
    • Applying the framework's convention, a multiple of 21.7x to the earnings two years beyond each horizon, the eighteen-month value is SEK 166.7 plus SEK 1.65 of dividends, a total return of +15.8%, or 10.3% a year
    • the three-year value is SEK 188.8 plus SEK 5.55, +33.7%, or 10.2% a year.
    • That is the framework's D anchor.
    • The 21.7x is a blend: 24x if both concerns clear (the two-year median is 23.67x), which we put at about 42% (0.60 on the organic line times 0.70 on the chief executive), and 20x if they do not (the bottom of the range), at about 58%.
      • The re-rating leg on the blend is -0.9%
      • earnings growth carries the whole return.
    • On the cleared branch alone the eighteen-month return is 17.9% a year
      • on the uncleared branch 4.5% a year.
      • Even the cleared branch falls short of the bar from SEK 145.3
      • it reaches it from SEK 141.5.
      • On the modal path the bar is reached below SEK 128 over eighteen months.
    • On a one-year-forward footing beside it, holding today's 24.0x on FY2027 modal EPS of SEK 6.05 flat, the eighteen-month return is 11.4% a year
      • the convention lands about a point lower because it applies a blended forward multiple rather than today's.
  • The earnings path is fragile in one place, the currency.
    • A 10% stronger krona against the euro and the dollar takes about SEK 221m off EBIT, and SEK 2bn of acquisition debt at about 4% adds about SEK 80m of interest
    • together about SEK 300m, or 7% of consensus FY2026 pre-tax profit.
    • Spread over three years that turns the below-EBITDA contribution from +2.0% a year to about -1.0% a year, EPS growth from 10.9% to about 7.5% a year, FY2028 EPS to about SEK 6.15, and the eighteen-month return to 2.8% a year, an F.
    • A currency move the forecasters already expect (EUR/SEK toward 10.40 to 10.50 by end-2026) is enough on its own to take a D case to an F.
  • The pattern it fits is 'OSHQC CfC Clearing', at C, not crisply.
    • Oversold on its own multiple (B), with named and few concerns (B), but the quality gate is met at C rather than the B the pattern asks for, and the clearing evidence is D on the framework's own standard of assuming persistence and requiring hard evidence.
    • The secondary fit, 'Compounder', has a genuinely mis-modelled line (unannounced acquisitions, about 10% a year of EBITA the consensus does not carry) but not the proven-upgrader record.

  • The case is therefore a watch with two dated triggers and a price, not a buy.

    • The chain holds from the inputs to the actions and the margin
    • it breaks at the organic line and at the empty chair
    • the price pays for the overhang clearing but not for the evidence still outstanding.
    • What we are betting on, in one line: that the organic line prints 3%-plus with North America positive on 22 October 2026 and a credible chief executive is named by mid-2027, so that 21x on 11% growth becomes 24x on the same growth
    • and that we can buy it below SEK 128 before the first or below SEK 141 after both.

  • Rating = D, because the modal return of about 10 percent a year does not reach the 20 percent bar, and the input change forces, though real, are half headwind (a chief executive vacancy; a stalled organic engine) and half tailwind (the overhang cleared; the regulated replacement cycle; the green OEM boom), so the change is not yet material enough in the outputs to carry a 20 percent return from SEK 145.3.

    • Not C, though C was live: consensus EPS growth of about 12 percent a year compound to FY2028 sits inside the target band, the multiple is at the bottom of its range, and the staircase of dated events ahead is good
    • but the return on our path, even with a modest re-rating blended in, lands at the framework's D anchor of about 10 to 12 percent a year, and a rating that measures materiality has to answer to the return.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The bull case in its strongest form, which is Jefferies' and DNB Carnegie's of July and August 2026: underlying organic growth is 5 percent, not 1 percent
    • the green OEM segment grew 25 percent with record backlogs into 2027
    • the F-gas quota halves on 1 January 2027
    • the EQT overhang that "historically depressed the valuation" is gone and a long-term industrial owner with a record of capital discipline has replaced it
    • the stock trades at 18x EV/EBIT 2027E, a 20 to 30 percent discount to the Swedish serial acquirers and a five-year average of 24x
    • the targets of SEK 170 to SEK 180 imply about 25 percent upside on top of double-digit EPS growth, so we are being paid to wait.
  • We concede the overhang, the OEM strength, the margin resilience and the cheapness against the stock's own past, and we concede that a patient holder from here probably earns the earnings growth plus the yield, about 12 percent a year.
  • We hold our view for three reasons.
  • First, the discount to the Swedish compounders is in part earned: the adjusted EBITA margin is 10.7 percent against 14.0 to 22.9 percent for the five peers DNB Carnegie names, return on operating capital is 11.4 percent against a 12 percent target and return on equity 12 percent against 25 to 35 percent, and net working capital on FactSet's definition is 42 percent of revenue against Addtech's 18 percent
    • so "a 20 to 30 percent discount" is not "a 20 to 30 percent re-rating".
  • Second, the organic engine is the thing being re-rated, and it has printed below 3 percent for four consecutive reads, each time with a reason.
  • Third, the chief executive who built the EQT-era record left six weeks after the control change, unexplained in any file, and the framework's screen treats an unfilled chair as a red flag until a permanent appointment is made
    • even on the cleared branch the return from SEK 145.3 is short of the bar.
  • The bear case in its strongest form, which is the risks report's of June 2026: the record SEK 4.4bn operating cash flow of 2025 was flattered by destocking, North American bolt-ons dilute margins by about 100 basis points, 98 percent of sales are in non-krona currencies and unhedged (a 10 percent krona move costs about SEK 221m of EBIT), and the joint probability of no failure over the hold is low.
  • We concede the working capital and the currency, which is our drop-through case.
  • We do not accept "park because the thesis fails", because Q2 2026 showed the margin holding at 12.1 percent adjusted EBITA against 12.2 percent through a weak organic quarter, which is what an aftermarket distributor is meant to do.
  • Our response to both is the same: the case is not broken
    • it is not yet compelling at SEK 145.3.

  • Rating = C, because end-market demand is split: the regulated commercial-refrigeration replacement cycle is structural and multi-year (a tailwind), while residential HVAC replacement in the US and Europe is deferred by rates and prices (a headwind), and the group's organic line printed 1% in Q2 2026.

    • North American organic sales were -2.9% in Q2 2026 after -1.4% in Q1 2026 and -4.1% in Q4 2025.

  • European construction: Norway housing starts -24% year on year in June 2026

    • European office vacancy 9.5%

  • Not B, because the tailwind is real but the group-level organic line does not yet show it
