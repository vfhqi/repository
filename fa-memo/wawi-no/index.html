The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Price on the renewal tranche is the main driver. About 40% of shipping volume (about 23m cbm) renews for 2027. I assume a like-for-like uplift of about USD 18 per cbm (from an expiring average of about USD 54–58 to about USD 74), about 80% effective within 2027: about USD 330m of revenue, of which about 70% (USD 230m) reaches EBITDA after cargo-mix dilution (more Chinese cars, less high-and-heavy), a probability-weighted port-fee cost (about USD 30m) and cost inflation. Benchmarks: Höegh signs new contracts above USD 100 per cbm net; spot USD 170 per cbm; one-year charters USD 95–110k a day; management says 2027–2029 contracts carry rates "closely reflecting current strong market conditions".
  • 2028: the 2027 renewals carry over (+USD 60m), a further 20–25% of volume renews at about +USD 5–6 per cbm (+60m), and spot and short-term trades (about 10% of volume) soften by about USD 15 per cbm (-85m) as one-year charter rates fall to about USD 62k a day (DNB Carnegie, 3 November 2026). EBITDA roughly flat.
  • 2029–2030: spot weaker; the three-year 2027 contracts reset about USD 9 per cbm lower for 2030 while the HMG/EUKOR block (about a quarter of volume, fixed at about USD 55 per cbm to end-2029) reprices about USD 7 higher. Reported net freight rate: 63.3 (2026), 68.1, 67.3, 65.0, 62.0 (2030).
  • Volume is fleet-capped: 57.5m cbm (2026), +2% in 2027, about +0.5% a year after. Shapers (14 ships; about 3 delivered by end-2026, about 10 by end-2027, all 14 in 2028) replace chartered and old tonnage more than they add capacity. Contracts set a share of each customer's volume, not minimums.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.

  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
