Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Two-division industrial compounder. Beijer Alma AB is a Nasdaq Stockholm-listed, decentralised industrial group operating through Beijer Components (formerly Lesjöfors, renamed March 2026) and Beijer Tech, spanning roughly 70 subsidiaries across 19 countries
2.
Segment mix. Beijer Components generated 64% of FY2025 group net sales; Beijer Tech generated the remaining 36%, up from 30% in 2023 (Business Foundations)
3.
Product lines. FY2025 subsegment mix: Industrial Springs 52% of group sales, Chassis Springs (automotive aftermarket) 13%, Industrial Products 14%, Fluid Technology 11%, Niche Technologies 10% (Business Foundations)
4.
Geography. Sales outside Sweden reached 75.5-89% of the total depending on segment measure (Other Europe ~40%, Sweden ~25%, North America ~13%, Asia ~6%); local-for-local manufacturing across 19 countries (tearsheet, Business Foundations)
5.
Financial scale. FY2025 net sales SEK 7,750m (+7.6% YoY from SEK 7,203m); net debt/EBITDA 2.4x at FY2025-end, improved to 1.7x by Q1 2026 (Business Foundations, Change Forces)
5.1
Margin conflict: Adjusted EBITA margin is stated as either 15.0% or 13.4% for FY2025 depending on source, see F.2
6.
Balance sheet character. Net debt SEK 3,142m (including leases, FY2025) up from SEK 958m in 2019; gearing 55% (2025) vs 51% (2024); free cash flow SEK 677m (2025) vs SEK 420m (2024) (Business Foundations)
7.
Ownership. The Wall family and associated foundations control 47.8-54.8% of total voting rights (figures vary slightly by source) versus 10.6-12.8% of capital via a dual-class Class A/Class B share structure (Risks, Leadership)
8.
Investment-style implication. A cyclical-industrial serial acquirer / self-help turnaround hybrid: roughly 77% of group sales are exposed to general industrial demand, and the current case rests on a management-driven margin-recovery and M&A-acceleration programme rather than secular end-market growth
