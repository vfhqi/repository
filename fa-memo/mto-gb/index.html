The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Organic revenue growth of 4.2% in FY27 (price about 2.0 points on a 4.1% National Living Wage rise and about 2.2 points of volume), 4.3% in FY28, 3.8% in FY29, 3.5% in FY30 and 3.3% in FY31; acquisitions add 2.6 points in FY27 (Marlowe to August 2026; bolt-ons) and about 0.5 points a year after. How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
