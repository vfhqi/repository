What is publicly known about Novo Nordisk's demand for multi-dose pen injectors of the FlexTouch and FlexPen type in 2027-2029 and about how it treats its pen-component and device suppliers, as this bears on Nolato AB (NOLA.B-SE), which makes pen-injector components for an existing diabetes and obesity customer at Mosonmagyaróvár, Hungary (about SEK 700m of annual sales at full capacity per the company; DNB Carnegie named Novo Nordisk as the most likely customer in April 2024): Novo Nordisk's 2027 outlook and its volume commentary by region and product; the effect on its pen volumes of generic semaglutide in Canada, Brazil, India and China and of its lower United States prices from January 2027; which products and regions use multi-dose pens rather than single-dose autoinjectors; how much of its pen and device component manufacturing is done in-house and which suppliers besides Nolato make pen components for it; its requests to device suppliers for cost reductions (reported by Jefferies in July 2026) and the supply tender it withdrew in early 2026; and any statements by Novo Nordisk, Nolato or brokers since October 2026 about the volumes, timing or pricing of the Hungarian programme?"

Abundant research: Nolato's long-term outlook is evaluated across five unique companies, three historical precedents, and 105 relevant documents retrieved from multiple corporate and regulatory sources.   1. Novo Nordisk is transitioning toward a volume-driven growth strategy in 2027 to offset accelerating pricing pressures.

  3. Supplier volumes remain vulnerable to shifting patient preferences and aggressive price competition from domestic rivals.

Consequently, the company is prioritizing volume expansion over absolute pricing power to sustain its market share. This agreement is designed to scale pen and autoinjector volumes as competition from Eli Lilly intensifies. This external partnership allows the company to optimize its device manufacturing mix while protecting its gross margin.

Section 3 – The Impact of Price Cuts and Generic Semaglutide on Pen Volumes

Section summary
  1. International generic semaglutide entries are driving sharp price erosion and volume expansion in early- launch markets.
  2. The scheduled list price reductions in the United States will shift channel mix and intensify supplier cost-reduction pressures.

Global Generic Approvals and Launches

1.
Rapid deregulation: Generic semaglutide entries are accelerating across international markets as key regulatory approvals expand outside the United States. In Canada, Health Canada approved the first Ozempic generics from Apotex and Dr. Reddy's in May 2026. Consequently, these early launches are establishing a benchmark for global off-patent dynamics. This transition will likely erode Novo Nordisk's historical monopoly over international incretin volumes by introducing multiple low-cost alternatives.
2.
Severe discounts: Brazil is experiencing rapid generic semaglutide market expansion following the core patent expiration in March 2026. For example, EMS launched its generic Ozivy at a 66.1% discount to Ozempic, while Cosmed launched Semavy at a 76.2% price discount. Thus, these aggressive pricing strategies by regional players are driving deep price erosion across Latin America. Ultimately, Sandoz expects these lower price points to significantly expand total market penetration.
3.
Market formalization: Jefferies Research reports that Brazil's semaglutide patent expiration has brought regulated treatment prices down from around BRL 1,000 per month toward BRL 300. This substantial price reduction materially widens affordability, creating a major opportunity for demand to migrate from informal to regulated pharmacies in 2027. Furthermore, the broker expects Hypera's Semavy to achieve approximately BRL 2.0 bn in net revenue over 2027 and 2028. Thus, this rapid market formalization supports massive unit volume growth for regional packaging suppliers.
4.
Fragmented expansion: India represents a heavily genericized semaglutide market with 25 distinct generic brands launched immediately after the March 2026 patent expiry. For example, Sun Pharma has launched Noveltreat and Sematrinity, while Dr. Reddy's has introduced Obeda to capture diabetes volume. Consequently, this rapid influx of competitors broadens affordable peptide access across the region. However, the highly fragmented market structure limits the pricing flexibility of individual generic players.
5.
Strategic step: Jefferies Research expects that the introduction of generic semaglutide in India will expand the addressable market by up to 100 million patients. Branded generic semaglutide is priced at approximately INR 3,000 per month, which is nearly 70% below branded alternatives. Consequently, many price-sensitive patients will enter the GLP-1 class through these affordable generics. Thus, Eli Lilly expects this expansion to ultimately benefit premium offerings as patients seek higher weight loss efficacy.
6.
Delayed entry: China's regulatory framework has delayed broad generic semaglutide competition until April 2027 under the China-Switzerland Free Trade Agreement. Novo Nordisk successfully leveraged this treaty to secure six years of data protection from its initial April 2021 approval. Therefore, Chinese biosimilar developers like Huadong Medicine are blocked from launching generic alternatives before the extension expires. This temporary exclusivity preserves Novo Nordisk's high-margin volumes in the region through 2027.

This decision represents list price reductions of approximately 50% for Wegovy and 35% for Ozempic. Consequently, the massive list price cuts will directly affect Novo's net cash flow starting in fiscal 2027. However, the reduction primarily improves access for cash-pay patients.
8.

The analyst projects a 20% price decline across commercial channels for Wegovy in 2027. This downward pricing trend will likely dampen near-term revenue growth despite expanding overall patient volume. Therefore, the originator faces a challenging margin trajectory as lower-margin channels become more dominant.
9.

Meanwhile, Wegovy pricing is projected to be under even greater pressure, having declined by 30% to 35% year-to-date in late 2026. Consequently, these price adjustments are expected to trigger aggressive cost-reduction efforts from sub-tier suppliers. Thus, component contract manufacturers must prepare for intensified pricing negotiations to protect their operating margins.
10.
Contrary evidence: Morgan Stanley reported on November 9, 2026, that Novo Nordisk's third-quarter 2026 European and Canadian diabetes sales grew 23% at constant exchange rates despite Canadian patent expiries. This resilience was driven by severe supply constraints and quality recalls among generic competitors, which prevented effective volume erosion of the branded product. Consequently, generic competition may have a much smaller near-term impact on pen volumes than initially feared.
11.
Supplier squeeze: Jefferies Research warns that persistent US pricing pressure will continue to weigh heavily on Novo's gross margin through 2027. To defend its long-term target of an 80% gross margin, the drugmaker will likely demand aggressive cost concessions from its manufacturing partners. This pricing pressure represents a meaningful commercial risk for device component suppliers like Nolato. Therefore, supplier margin stability remains highly vulnerable to these top-down price cuts.

Sourcing Splits and External Sourcing

1.
Strategic division: Novo Nordisk maintains a hybrid manufacturing model that balances internal assembly capabilities with a highly specialized external supplier network. An expert interview with the Department Head of Supply Chain at Novo Nordisk on April 8, 2026, revealed that more than 50% of plastic components used in their delivery devices are outsourced to external manufacturing partners. This outsourcing strategy allows the pharmaceutical giant to maintain production flexibility while limiting capital expenditure commitments. Consequently, this reliance on external injection molders directly supports the long-term volume stability of component suppliers like Nolato.
2.
Accelerated assembly: Wegovy single-dose autoinjector devices utilize proprietary technology that requires precise final assembly before commercial distribution. The Department Head of Supply Chain at Novo Nordisk noted on April 8, 2026, that the single-dose pens utilize Ypsomed technology, but assembly is completed internally or via select contract development and manufacturing partners. This division of labor occurs because Ypsomed lacked the required capacity to handle the massive global production volume. Ultimately, this constraint highlights how device manufacturing capacity remains the primary bottleneck in GLP-1 supply chains.
3.
Internal operations: Novo Nordisk preserves vertical integration for its mature and high-volume product lines to protect proprietary processes. A former Vice President at Novo Nordisk stated on July 18, 2024, that the assembly and packaging of Wegovy devices was completed internally using bulk components sourced from external contract manufacturers. In contrast, the high-volume Ozempic drug-device combination was entirely vertically integrated within the same manufacturing facility. Therefore, suppliers of multi-dose pen systems face different demand structures than single-dose partners due to these integrated production lines.

According to company disclosures in April 2026, the contract covers pen-injector components for diabetes and obesity treatments with expected annual sales of SEK 700m once fully operational. Regular serial deliveries of these drug delivery systems commenced during the third quarter of 2026. Consequently, this incremental volume is projected to drive substantial earnings per share accretion as capacity utilization improves.

Encouraging growth: Nolato's Q3 2026 financial performance demonstrated the initial operational positive effects of the Hungarian contract. The company reported on October 28, 2026, that Medical Solutions organic sales grew by 9%, driven by the ramp-up of major drug delivery projects in Hungary and the United States. This represents an acceleration from the 6% organic sales growth recorded for the first nine months of the fiscal year. Therefore, the early stages of this commercial program are unfolding in line with the communicated schedule and volume targets.
