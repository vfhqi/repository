Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The separation of Salix Group leaves Volati with five platforms and high financial leverage, making organic earnings recovery the critical factor for future valuation.
  2. Financial flexibility is highly constrained by Volati retaining all listed preference shares, which creates a fixed annual dividend obligation of SEK 64.15 mm.

1.
Critical investigation: "Volati AB (VOLO-SE), a Swedish decentralised industrial acquirer which, after distributing Salix Group to its shareholders on 15 June 2026, owns five platforms: Ettiketto Group (self-adhesive labels and labelling machines in the Nordics, Germany, the UK and the Netherlands), Communication (telecom masts, towers and mounting hardware through Scanmast and MAFI), Corroventa (water-damage drying equipment and moisture meters, including Tramex), S:t Eriks Group (precast concrete and natural stone for Swedish infrastructure and landscaping) and Tornum Group (grain drying, handling and storage systems, including SIMEZA silos); it also has 1,603,774 listed preference shares with a fixed SEK 40 annual dividend.
