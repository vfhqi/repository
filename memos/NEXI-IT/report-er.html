Section 1 – Brief company description and history 14 findings

1.
Business model definition – entrenched infrastructure: Nexi operates as a leading European PayTech company that provides critical card-based merchant acquiring, card issuing, and digital banking solutions. The company is headquartered in Milan, Italy, and manages approximately 2 million merchants and over 140 million payment cards. Historically focused on bank-led distribution, Nexi is now increasingly moving toward direct merchant acquiring to capture high-growth verticals
2.
Regional footprint – strong domestic concentration: The group possesses a highly diversified geographical footprint spanning over 25 European countries, with exposure to both mature and under-penetrated digital payment markets. However, its operational concentration remains heavily skewed toward its home country, where Italy generates 59% of total revenues as of the first half of 2026. The remaining revenue mix is divided between the Nordics at 17%, DACH at 15%, and Central and South-Eastern Europe at 10%
