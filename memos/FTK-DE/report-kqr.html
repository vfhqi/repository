Section 5 – Regulatory tailwinds and German pension reform

Section summary
  1. Germany's upcoming capital-market-focused pension reforms will unlock a massive retail investment pool starting in 2027, structurally lowering long-term customer acquisition barriers for prepared digital platforms.

Pension reform mechanics and customer TAM

1.
Sweeping regulatory overhaul: The German government has approved the Altersvorsorgedepot to replace Riester starting January 1, 2027, allowing direct, state-subsidized retirement investing in ETFs and equities.
2.
Tiered subsidy design: Under the legislative guidelines, savers receive a 50% match up to €360 and a 25% match up to €1,800, resulting in a €540 maximum basic subsidy.
3.
Substantial market expansion: Deutsche Bank Research forecasts that the pension overhaul could generate more than €8 billion in annual flows and create 10 million new accounts over five years.
4.
Encouraging household participation: Morgan Stanley expects the retirement account to attract approximately 20 million participants who will contribute an average €2,000 annually, yielding up to €40 billion in aggregate yearly inflows.
5.
Early wealth accumulation: The proposed Frühstart-Rente will automatically enroll school-aged children with a €10 monthly federal grant, transferring accrued balances into retirement accounts at age 18.
6.
Long-duration asset growth: Jefferies Research highlights that this structural transition from guarantee-heavy vehicles to funded models will significantly expand the long-term addressable pool of European retail assets.

Competitive positioning and flatex preparation

7.
Proactive platform readiness: To prepare for the launch, flatexDEGIRO has introduced stock savings plans with a 1% commission and fully digitized onboarding for minors in early 2026.

Strategic digital positioning: Management intends to position flatexDEGIRO as a premier provider of the Altersvorsorgedepot by delivering an attractive, cost-efficient, and digital retirement investing platform for clients.

Leveraging local scale: Morgan Stanley highlights that flatexDEGIRO is uniquely positioned to capture these recurring inflows given its existing 590k German accounts and integrated custody banking infrastructure.

Intensifying margin competition: Morgan Stanley notes that while advised pension products face a 1% cost cap, self-directed offers are already seeing price battles with Deka pricing at 10 basis points and Scalable Capital at 15 basis points.
