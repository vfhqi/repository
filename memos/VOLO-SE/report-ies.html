Volati (VOLO-SE): what the Q3 2026 results do to the existing case

These results leave the case broadly where it stood, because the case was written with them in hand, but they admit three readings it does not hold, all of which lean against it: the net pressure is negative and small.

1. What changed

The company reported on 23 October 2026. Against what the case assumed:

  • Group. Net sales SEK 1,176m, up 12% (SEK 1,051m in Q3 2025), 2% below DNB Carnegie's SEK 1,205m. Organic sales fell 3% against the broker's expected rise of 1%. Adjusted EBITA was SEK 87m, up 15% from SEK 76m and exactly on the broker's SEK 87m. Reported EBITA was SEK 58m, down 24%, and 29% below the broker's SEK 81m, because items affecting comparability were SEK 29m against an expected SEK 6m (SEK 19m of restructuring to co-locate label production in Ettiketto, SEK 8m of transaction costs).
  • Platforms, Q3 2026 EBITA against Q3 2025. Ettiketto SEK 64m against SEK 52m, margin 14.3% against about 18%, 5% below the broker. Corroventa SEK 17m against SEK 10m, 28% above the broker, with Tramex 32% of segment sales. Communication SEK 18m against SEK 17m. S:t Eriks SEK 14m against SEK 16m on sales down 10%, 7% below the broker. Tornum minus SEK 9m against minus SEK 4m on sales down 16%.
  • Balance sheet. Net debt to adjusted EBITDA 3.1x at end-September 2026, above the 2.0x to 3.0x target range and below the 3.5x maximum. Operating cash flow SEK 55m (SEK 40m); rolling twelve-month cash conversion 93%. Management said it intends to use seasonally strong year-end cash to reduce gearing before accelerating acquisitions.
  • Sell-side. One active broker. DNB Carnegie kept Hold and cut its target to SEK 25 from SEK 26. Adjusted EPS: 2026e SEK 1.92 from SEK 1.98; 2027e SEK 2.32 from SEK 2.30; 2028e SEK 2.88 from SEK 2.82. But 2027e adjusted EBITA was cut 4% to SEK 393m from SEK 409m, with S:t Eriks cut to SEK 33m from SEK 48m and Tornum to minus SEK 11m from nil; the EPS was held by cost initiatives and lower tax. The broker's leverage estimate including the preference shares rose to 5.0x for 2026e from 4.5x.

The case took all of this as confirmation: underlying EBITA up 15%, the cyclical platforms' margins holding, leverage at its peak.

2. The challenge

Reading one: the 15% was bought, not earned, and the case's dated tests cannot tell the difference. The whole of the year-on-year rise in platform EBITA sits in the two platforms that made acquisitions in 2026: Ettiketto up SEK 12m and Corroventa up SEK 7m. The two platforms where the case places half of 2027's improvement earned SEK 7m less than a year earlier, with the cost programmes the case dates from early 2026 already running. Ettiketto added about SEK 151m of sales and SEK 12m of EBITA, an incremental margin of about 8% (calculated). On this reading Q3 shows a slower decline financed by debt-funded acquisitions, not self-help reaching profits. For it to be right, the programmes must already be largely in the Q3 run-rate, so that what remains for 2027 is comparison arithmetic in the first half and volume thereafter. The uncomfortable consequence is for the tests, not the forecast: the case itself says first-half 2027 growth is almost assured even in its bear. A test that passes on both readings settles neither. The first print that discriminates is Q3 2027, against S:t Eriks' SEK 14m and Tornum's minus SEK 9m.

Reading two: at S:t Eriks the margin test and the cash test pull against each other inside the fragile window. The review records that the first-half production cut generated very strong cash flow and caused the under-absorption, and that management raised production in late 2026 to improve cost coverage, while sales were down 10% and municipal wastewater volumes were slipping. Raising output into falling sales rebuilds inventory: it lifts the margin by absorbing overhead and consumes the cash. The case treats the end of under-absorption as arithmetic and the year-end cash release as seasonal, and asks February for both. For this reading to be right, fourth-quarter demand must fall short of the higher production rate. If it does, the case's 2.7x and its SEK 82m to 85m of fourth-quarter adjusted EBITA are not independent markers, and a good margin print would be weaker evidence than it looks. The same logic says part of the 93% cash conversion is the inventory release now being reversed.

Reading three: the one-offs are the running cost of the model the case needs to restart. Items affecting comparability were a third of adjusted EBITA in the quarter, and management described them as necessary investment to unlock synergies. Both components, transaction costs and integration restructuring, scale with deal-making. The case counts the end of 2026's one-offs as a source of deleveraging that does not depend on the cycle, even in its bear, and separately needs bolt-ons to resume from mid-2027. Those two assumptions sit uneasily together. For this reading to be right, further charges must follow, from later phases in Germany or from new deals. The broker's move to 5.0x from 4.5x on the preference-inclusive measure, made after seeing the quarter, runs in the same direction, though the review does not give its reason and the bases are not reconciled.

Readings we do not count. That Ettiketto's margin shows the quality platform weakening, and that leverage blocks acquisitions, are both already in the case, with thresholds.

3. What it does to the case

  • Internally-driven corporate change (55, C) and Thesis's Change Forces' Strength (56, D): more pressure. Both rest on self-help "already showing in Q3 margins". Reading one says the evidence is thinner than the rating assumes.
  • Riskiness and simplicity of the forecasts (16, F) and Case Riskiness (15, F): more pressure, from reading two, which links two markers the case treats as separate. Both are already at the floor grade.
  • Guidance, sell-side expectations and delivery record (26, D): slightly more pressure. "In line underlying" was a 28% beat at Corroventa offsetting misses at three platforms, and S:t Eriks missed again.
  • Where the stock is in its lifecycle (44, D) and Scope for upgrades (46, D): slightly more pressure. The case's "estimates have stopped moving" holds at EPS and not at EBITA, which the broker cut 4% for 2027.
  • Total shareholder return (90, A) and the fair multiple (82, B): the same on the arithmetic, more pressure on the deleveraging leg the case calls the engine of the return, from reading three.
  • Setups' Fit/Crispness (42, F): the same, with one easing. Management's stated order, cash first and deals second, bears on the case's worry about a deal before the year-end report. It is an intention, not a commitment.
  • Price. SEK 24.2 at 13 November 2026 sits above the 50-day average of SEK 23.4, a marker the case names, crossed but not yet held through February. It is above the case's mild-trough band of SEK 19 to 22 and far below its SEK 40 base. The pinned letters are unchanged at F, F and A.

4. What would settle it

  • The second review, four working days on. It can carry any further broker estimates and targets, and whether a second broker has updated. It is unlikely to add much, since the one active broker has already published. It carries no new company disclosure and settles none of the three readings.
  • The year-end report, which the case dates 11 February 2027. For reading two: operating cash flow and working capital set beside S:t Eriks' margin. A margin gain with cash conversion well below 93% supports the reading. For reading three: whether fourth-quarter items affecting comparability are nil. For the balance sheet: leverage back below 3.0x, the review's marker, against the case's 2.7x.
  • The Q1 2027 report, which the case dates late April. The case's Ettiketto threshold of 14% or above sits below the 14.3% already reported before any co-location saving, so clearing it shows little. A margin clearly above 14.3% with no new restructuring charge would.
  • The Q3 2027 report, probably late October 2027, is the first clean test of reading one.
  • What nothing is committed to answer. The case records that the company gives no organic EBITA, no order intake or backlog at S:t Eriks or Tornum, no platform inventory and no size for the savings. Reading one in particular is likely to stay inferred from segment totals.
