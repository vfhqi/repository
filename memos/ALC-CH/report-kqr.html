Premium Lens Share and Competition

7.
Declining share: Alcon has experienced notable competitive pressure in the United States premium intraocular lens market due to recent competitor product rollouts. Analysts at Bernstein on March 21, 2025, estimated that Alcon's share in the United States presbyopia-correcting lens category fell to approximately 72% as clinicians began trialing rival offerings. This shift directly impacted Alcon's high-margin implantables segment, causing its growth rate to slow to just 2% in the final quarter of 2024. Consequently, investors are closely watching whether Alcon can protect its dominant premium intraocular lens position.
8.
Accelerating competition: Johnson & Johnson has successfully captured market share from Alcon with the rapid adoption of its non-diffractive extended depth of focus lens. An independent California ophthalmologist in a September 25, 2026, survey reported that the newly launched PureSee lens has quickly become their primary goto premium option, representing up to 70% of their premium cases. This rapid conversion has come largely at the expense of Alcon's legacy Vivity lens, which saw its share drop to 25% in that clinical practice. Surgeons favor the rival platform because it delivers better near vision outcomes without increasing nighttime glare.
9.

Analysts at Bernstein on March 9, 2026, reported that the launch of Vivity Pro in early 2027 should replicate the success of the previous PanOptix Pro rollout. They expect this upgraded model to help Alcon regain lost market share by improving patient visual outcomes. This product upgrade cycle is critical because it represents Alcon's main defense against competitor pressure in the premium extended depth of focus segment.

This pressure is expected to persist into early 2027, making a rapid segment recovery unlikely.
