EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Bally's Intralot has completed a transformational business combination merging stable B2B lottery technology with high-growth B2C iGaming platforms under digital-focused CEO Robeson Reeves. The pro forma entity delivered €1.086 billion in FY25 revenue at a sector-leading 39.70% EBITDA margin, positioning the group as a diversified technology and interactive gaming operator across 40 regulated jurisdictions. Revenue predictability remains robust through highly predictable recurring fee streams structured as fixed percentages of wagers, securing over €1.4 billion in contracted backlog.

However, the combined portfolio exhibits significant structural dependency on the UK market, which represents 61% of pro forma revenues, while legacy B2B operations face severe foreign exchange headwinds creating 4.10 percentage point revenue drag.

Business Model and Strategic Foundation

The post-merger model integrates interactive iLottery, sports betting, and lottery technologies, combining digital B2C operational expertise with established B2B regulatory leadership. The B2B division delivers critical transaction processing through central lottery systems, advanced PhotonX terminals, and proprietary iGEM monitoring platforms powering 36 global operations. The B2C segment serves as the major growth engine, offering real-money online casino games distributed via leading brands like Jackpotjoy, maintaining stable 14-15%

UK market share. The legacy B2B business operates across 49 active contracts, with technology service contracts comprising 72% of revenues and management contracts generating 24%. The global footprint spans active contracts inside 40 jurisdictions supported by approximately 2,800 employees, with the UK representing 61% and Americas 16% of revenues.

Financial Profile and Peer Benchmarking

Pro forma combined operations achieved €1.086 billion revenue with 39.70% adjusted EBITDA margin in FY25, targeting full realization of €41 million in post-merger synergies. Legacy B2B revenues declined 8.70% like-forlike with US dollar translation creating 4.10 percentage point drag, while high 45% staff costs create operational deleveraging risk. The asset-light model converts nearly all operating profits into cash, achieving exceptional 88% operating FCF conversion and generating approximately €380 million in pro-forma operating FCF.

Pro-forma returns on invested capital significantly exceed the 10.40% cost of capital, with legacy B2B divisions consistently achieving post-tax RONIC of 25-30%. Against peers, Intralot's 39.70% EBITDA margin leads Everi Holdings (36.80%) but trails digital peers, while 5.86% capex intensity sits above Allwyn (2.80%) and Lottomatica (1.10%) but below Everi's physical-heavy 16.60%. Capital allocation prioritizes aggressive balance sheet optimization targeting conservative 2.5x net debt-to-EBITDA leverage by fiscal year 2028.

Competitive Positioning and Market Structure

The B2B lottery technology segment operates as a highly concentrated oligopoly where Brightstar Lottery dominates with 26 of 46 US state contracts and Scientific Games maintains premier global positioning. The combined group commands the #3 position in the US lottery solutions market and holds the market-leading #2 position in UK online casino operations. Intralot suffered highly material market share loss after losing the Ohio state lottery system contract to Scientific Games, projected to eliminate $20 million in annual EBITDA from mid-2027. Aggressive competitor underbidding during US contract renewals has drastically compressed pricing terms, forcing acceptance of lower 5% fee rates versus 8% previously to secure Montana. Major state-licensed lotteries are increasingly developing proprietary in-house software capabilities, with operators like FDJ designing custom iLottery platforms through internal Core Tech teams.

Value Chain Position and Customer Dynamics

Upstream B2B software stages capture exceptional gross margins of 70-80% via licensing, creating strong competitive moats through highly front-loaded platform development costs. Pro forma BII interactive operations deliver robust 43% EBITDA margins in Q3 2025, accounting for 49.10% of total group pro forma adjusted EBITDA. The stand-alone B2B business remains highly dependent on long-term contracts representing 33% of pro forma sales, with top five contracts representing nearly 50% of legacy revenues.

Long-term contracts secure over €1.40 billion in contracted revenue backlog through fiscal year 2029, with average remaining term to renewal standing at sixteen years. The B2B segment boasts 89% contract renewal rate since 2006 versus peer

Brightstar's 97%, though losing the Ohio contract incurs annual EBITDA loss of approximately $20 million.

Switching Costs and Pricing Dynamics

Retail switching costs are extremely high due to physical terminal replacement and staff retraining, with system migrations typically taking 12-18 months, making competitor displacement highly difficult. Digital iLottery platform switching costs are substantially lower, primarily involving secure database migrations rather than physical infrastructure overhauls. Intense bidding processes are increasingly forcing operators to accept less favorable economic terms, with Montana secured by underbidding at 5% versus 8% previously as peers bid as low as 13%. Physical B2B lottery contracts command exceptional long-term revenue visibility through highly resilient 16-year average terms and robust 89% historical contract renewal rate since 2006.

Supply Chain and Distribution Channel Analysis

The group prioritizes highly localized procurement across a diverse network of 1,190 active suppliers, with Greek operations allocating 51% of procurement to local businesses. Reliance on intensive digital infrastructure exposes the company to severe utility price volatility, with high electricity prices potentially increasing group hosting expenses and requiring substantial greener infrastructure investments. The transformational Bally's Interactive acquisition rebalances the group towards high-growth online gaming channels, with B2C digital operations now generating two-thirds of pro forma group revenues. Physical retail networks face disintermediation risks as wagers migrate rapidly online, while rising UK Remote Gaming Duties compress margins for low-scale distribution partners.

Cross-Value-Chain Signals and Leverage Trajectory

Competitor Lottomatica's financial results highlight rapid consumer channel migration with online sports bets surging 15% year-over-year, supporting online market share expanding 1.90 percentage points and proving omnichannel database integration strength. Semiconductor expert commentary warns that six-fold memory price increases are squeezing traditional hardware manufacturers, elevating cost of goods sold and directly impacting physical retail lottery terminal assembly costs.

Pro forma net debt of €1.492 billion positions the group at 3.46x adjusted net leverage, with management targeting conservative 2.5x long-term leverage achievable by fiscal year 2028. B2C cash generation provides durable liquidity to fund upfront B2B contract capital requirements, with digital operations delivering superior one-to-two year paybacks versus four-tofive years for lotteries.

Operator Quality and Foundation Strength

The board successfully realigned the executive team post-transaction, appointing CEO Robeson Reeves with over 20 years interactive B2C expertise while President Nikolaos Nikolakopoulos retains critical regulator relationships

Management targets uniquely integrated global B2B2C expansion, converging Bally's Vitruvian engine with Intralot's PlayerX to create highly scalable unified gaming stack across 40 jurisdictional launchpads. The board established clear, predictable shareholder return frameworks starting late FY25, instituting 35% dividend payout ratio and recommending initial distribution of approximately €30 million. The pro-forma business model generates exceptionally high operational profitability, achieving sector-leading 39.70% adjusted EBITDA margin in FY25 comfortably above European-listed gambling peer median.

Post-deal improvement remains heavily dependent on successful execution of €41 million annualized cost savings, with restricted geographic and product overlap potentially delaying final synergy realization.

Disruption Forces and Long-Term Growth Runway

High regulatory and capital barriers protect lottery technology platforms, with state-licensed operations requiring strict government examinations and compliance monitoring requiring significant ongoing personnel and systems investment. Securing new lottery contracts demands massive upfront investments in physical networks and terminal deployments, with high initial capital requirements heavily straining group-level free cash flow during bidding cycles.

Online gambling GGR penetration reached 34.20% globally in 2025 and is projected to reach 42% by 2030, with online distribution bypassing high physical retail commissions to drive structurally superior operating margins. The global online gambling market is projected to expand at 9.90% CAGR through 2030, while periodic lottery game price increases mechanically boost EBITDA and overall gaming historically outpaces GDP at 5.40% CAGR.

Legalizing online casino games across US states represents potential $85 billion addressable market, with iGaming estimated to drive $52 billion of GGR if all states legalize online casinos.
