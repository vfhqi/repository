Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. 2G Energy has established robust decentralized energy market leadership through its sustainable combined heat and power systems, but escalating macro pressures pose threat to historical margins.
1.
Established sector positioning: Founded in 1995 and headquartered in Heek, Germany, 2G Energy has scaled to become a nationally dominant manufacturer of combined heat and power systems. This longterm operational footprint represents a major expansion compared to its early days when it was a pure-play regional engine packager.

2.
Diversified technology portfolio: The company develops, produces, and installs high-efficiency combined heat and power solutions, large-scale industrial heat pumps, and peaking power generation systems. This product mix offers more resilient decarbonization options than traditional, less-flexible peer offerings because its engines are engineered to support uniquely wide fuel versatility including hydrogen.
3.
Robust global scale: With more than 10,000 systems sold in over 60 countries, 2G Energy commands approximately 2 GW of total operating capacity. This extensive global footprint exceeds the typical output of new nuclear power plants like Flamanville 3 in France, which delivers approximately 1.6 GW.
4.
Increasing staff footprint: The group's workforce has grown to exceed 1000 employees globally, with core manufacturing centralized in Germany. This staffing level represents a material operational expansion compared to the previous year's payroll of more than 900 employees.
5.
Pleasing historical growth: In fiscal year 2023, consolidated net sales rose by 16.8% to EUR 365.1 million from EUR 312.6 million in the prior year. This double-digit expansion comfortably beat the slower 6.1% growth recorded subsequently in fiscal year 2025, which faced severe post-ERP operational disruptions.
6.
Volatile margin performance: The consolidated EBIT margin reached 7.6% in fiscal year 2023, which was ahead of the 7.0% margin recorded in fiscal year 2022. However, this figure contracted to a lower 6.6% in fiscal year 2025, showing a temporary setback in historical profitability due to elevated ERP conversion costs.
7.
Growing net earnings: Consolidated net profit grew by 9.9% to EUR 18.0 million in fiscal year 2023, compared to EUR 16.4 million in fiscal year 2022. This positive development validated historical efficiency gains before margins were weighed down by a temporarily machinery-heavy revenue mix in later periods.
8.
Subdued sales acceleration: During fiscal year 2024, consolidated sales rose by a modest 2.9% to EUR 375.6 million, up from EUR 365.1 million in fiscal year 2023. This rate of expansion marked a severe slowdown from the 16.8% sales growth achieved in fiscal year 2023 due to intense regulatory and political uncertainties in Germany.
9.
Improving operating margin: The group's EBIT margin rose to 8.9% in fiscal year 2024, up from 7.6% in fiscal year 2023. This profitable expansion contrasted sharply with the subsequent decline to 6.6% in fiscal year 2025, when delayed software transition processes hindered service billing.
10.
Solid equity foundation: The group preserved a solid balance sheet with an equity ratio of 51.0% in fiscal year 2025, down slightly from 52.5% in the prior year. Despite this minor leverage increase, the group's capital structure remained superior to its historical H1 2025 ratio when unallocated prepayments received dropped by EUR 17.0 million.
11.
Favorable share outperformance: During calendar year 2025, the share price surged by 53.5% to close at EUR 35.30, up from EUR 23 at the end of 2024. This impressive appreciation vastly outperformed the benchmark SDAX index, which recorded a lower 25.3% gain over the same fiscal period.
12.
Dynamic stock rally: The share price fluctuated between EUR 32 and EUR 39 in Q1 2026, before skyrocketing to an interim high of EUR 74.10 in early June. This breakout was fueled by robust order inflows across the product portfolio, contrasting with the generally declining mechanical engineering sector across Germany.
13.
Pre-earnings broker initiation: Recommending a Buy, Berenberg's analyst Robert-Jan van der Horst initiated coverage on September 14, 2026, with a price target of EUR 91. This bullish outlook exceeded the existing consensus target of EUR 73 to 80, drawing confidence from significant planned capacity expansions of 750 MW.
14.
Other side of the story: Expressing deep caution, Parmantier & Cie. maintained a Sell rating on September 25, 2026, with a price target of EUR 39. The analyst argued that the current share price discounted a bull case and expressed concern over cash conversion, noting that order intake is not cash flow.
