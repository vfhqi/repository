Section summary

3.

Captive vertical integration strategy versus peer models

2.
Unique pelagic integration: Bakkafrost is the only global salmon farmer integrated all the way upstream into pelagic raw material sourcing and fishmeal production. Subsidiary Havsbrún sources local Faroese landings, retaining raw material super-profits during global forage fish shortages. Mowi is integrated into feed manufacturing but lacks fishmeal or oil plants, sourcing its marine raw materials externally. Feed formulation specialization shift: The feed market is highly concentrated, with BioMar, Cargill, and Skretting holding a combined 69% market share. Feed formulation specialization shift (cont.): Mowi opted to exit independent feed formulation in December 2025 by entering a strategic partnership with Skretting, highlighting the rising R&D complexity of modern aquafeed. Bakkafrost resists this outsourcing trend, retaining full captive formulation control despite Havsbrún's relatively small R&D scale of 165,174 tonnes of feed produced.
3.
Captive R&D versus global scale: DNB Carnegie argues that Havsbrún's niche feed volume of 163,000 tonnes in 2025 makes it challenging to match the R&D efficiencies of global players. Captive R&D versus global scale (cont.): However, Morgan Stanley reports that Bakkafrost's captive feed utilizes an extremely high marine inclusion rate, containing 2.5 to 3 times more marine content than typical industry diets. This premium feed composition directly drives superior fish health and exceptionally high harvest weights in the Faroe Islands.
4.
Large post-smolt marine de-risking: Bakkafrost's vertical integration leverages massive freshwater RAS investments to grow smolt on land to 500 grams. Releasing larger smolt reduces the seawater grow-out phase from the industry standard of 18 months down to just 13 months. This strategy minimizes the time fish are exposed to marine hazards, significantly lowering biological mortality rates.
5.
Downstream processing and logistics control: The group controls its downstream chain via its primary processing plants and its dedicated FarCargo airline cargo division. FarCargo flies fresh, un-frozen premium Faroese salmon directly to high-paying US sushi and Asian retail markets. This downstream logistics control allows Bakkafrost to consistently capture premium price realization over the volatile spot market.
