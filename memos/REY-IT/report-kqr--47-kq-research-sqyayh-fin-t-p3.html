Section summary
  1. IT services providers face a critical transition as AI productivity gains trigger both project volume expansion and client demands for lower contract pricing.
  2. Turnkey, fixed-fee, and outcome-based pricing models allow leading firms to retain efficiency benefits, while traditional time-and-materials models face severe deflationary pressure.

According to the company's annual report published on March 4, 2026, management claims that efficiency gains leading to smaller project sizes will be fully offset by new customer demand because it frees client budget capacity for novel solutions. This means that if clients consistently reinvest their savings, Reply's earnings per share will remain resilient.

Morgan Stanley reported on October 15, 2026, that Accenture's management believes savings on legacy contracts can actively fund new projects, with client relationships helping capture that reinvestment.
