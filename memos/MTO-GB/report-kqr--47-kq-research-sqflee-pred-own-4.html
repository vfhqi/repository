Section 2 – Customer Retention and SME Client Performance

Section summary
  1. Mitie has achieved a strong 91% group retention rate in Q1 FY27, showing that post-acquisition integration has not triggered the severe customer churn initially feared.
  2. Our confidence in this robust customer retention is high, supported by Mitie's official Q1 FY27 trading update and broker reports from Deutsche Bank.
  3. Potential attrition within Marlowe's SME customer base represents a minor risk that is being successfully offset by extensive corporate cross-selling.

Historical Retention Baselines

1.
Exceptional legacy performance: Marlowe historically achieved a remarkable track record of customer retention before being acquired by Mitie. In its annual report published on 2 July 2025, Marlowe reported that its average customer relationship exceeded 10 years. This long-term customer loyalty was supported by consistent compliance rates in the high 90% range, which sat well above national benchmarks. This historical baseline set a very high bar for Mitie to maintain post-acquisition.
2.
High retention levels: Customer retention remained a key strength for Marlowe during its final periods as an independent public company. According to Marlowe's results published on 23 July 2024, the business achieved a 93% customer retention rate in the year. This strong customer retention helped secure a highly visible stream of recurring revenue. Consequently, any post-acquisition decline in this metric would indicate failure in Mitie's integration execution.
3.
Best-in-class operational metrics: High compliance levels underpinned the strong customer retention rates that Marlowe consistently delivered. In its interim results published on 5 December 2024, Marlowe highlighted compliance rates at a best-in-class 98%. Furthermore, first-time fix rates increased to 78% alongside a zero-tolerance focus on customer compliance backlog. These metrics made it difficult for competitors to displace Marlowe from existing contracts.
4.
Low customer concentration: Marlowe's legacy portfolio was built with low customer concentration to minimize systemic contract risks. In its results presentation on 23 July 2024, Marlowe confirmed that no single customer represented over 4% of revenues. This diversified profile protected the business from being crippled by the loss of any single major client. Consequently, Mitie's compliance revenues remain insulated from high client concentration issues.

Fragmented customer profile: The customer profile of Marlowe differs significantly from Mitie's traditional large corporate client base. Mitie must adapt its commercial approach to prevent these smaller clients from slipping away.
