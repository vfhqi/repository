Exact Definition and IFRS Reconciliation

1.
Exact financial definition: Bravida defines its cash conversion metric as cash flow from operating activities adjusted for tax payments, net financial items, and investments in machinery and equipment, divided by adjusted EBITDA over a rolling twelve-month period. This alternative performance measure is not defined under IFRS but is presented to help investors analyze the share of earnings converted to liquid cash. The company argues that this metric shows the underlying cash generation capacity of the operations while excluding interestrelated cash flows. Consequently, this non-GAAP measure provides key insights into the company's dividend and acquisition capacity.
2.
IFRS reconciliation mechanics: To reconcile this indicator to IFRS, Bravida adjusts cash flow from operating activities of SEK 1,453 million in 2025 by adding back taxes paid of SEK 415 million and net interest of SEK 109 million. The company then subtracts capex of SEK -156 million to arrive at an adjusted operating cash flow of SEK 1,821 million. Dividing this adjusted operating cash flow by the reported EBITDA of SEK 2,301 million yields the exact reported cash conversion of 79 percent for fiscal year 2025. This detailed reconciliation proves that the company relies on standard cash flow statements to derive its alternative metric.
