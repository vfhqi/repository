Section 3 – Industry Structure + Concentration + Value Chain Economics 22 findings

Section summary
  1. Sweden's real estate portal market exhibits a steep, highly concentrated winner-take-all structure, though a cyclical expansion in pre-market inventory tests short-term listing capture.

Market Structure and Listing Volume Dynamics

1.
Swedish housing market scale: Total Swedish housing sales reached 170,415 transactions in 2025 according to Statistics Sweden, reflecting a modest 3% recovery year-on-year
2.
Structural market penetration floor: Hemnet retained coverage over 82% of all homes sold in Sweden during 2025, confirming its role as essential transaction infrastructure despite macro volatility
3.
Cyclical listing volume compression: Published listings experienced a severe 30.7% year-on-year drop in Q1 2026, followed by a 14% decline in Q2 2026 as housing turnover slowed
4.
Macro sensitivity to interest rates: High interest rates and cautious consumer sentiment lengthened average listing durations to 64 days in Q2 2026, up from 48 days in 2025
5.
Shift to sell-before-you-buy: Approximately 64% of Swedish property sellers adopted a "sell first" strategy in early 2026, compared to 35% during the 2022 market peak
6.
Pre-market inventory expansion: Weak price development expanded the "Kommande" (coming soon) pre- market segment, where home sellers test pricing without committing upfront capital

Stage-by-Stage Chain Economics and Value Accrual

7.
Asymmetric margin distribution: Real estate portals capture over 50% EBITDA margins, while underlying brokerage chains operate at modest ~10% net margins
8.
Vendor-paid model uniqueness: Sweden operates as a vendor-paid advertising (VPA) market, where sellers directly fund portal listings rather than real estate agents paying subscriptions
9.
Substantial take-rate expansion runway: Hemnet's platform take-rate stands at 15–20 bps of transaction value, significantly trailing REA Group's 30–35 bps benchmark in Australia
10.
Low agent commission share: Online portal listing spend represents only ~1.8% of the total agent commission pool in Sweden, leaving vast headroom for monetization growth
11.
Low transaction cost friction: Total home transaction fees in Sweden remain under 2-3% of property value, creating low consumer resistance to incremental portal fee increases
12.
B2B revenue resilience: High-margin B2B net sales held stable at SEK 54.2m in Q2 2026, as bank mortgage lead demand offset display advertising softness

Concentration Metrics, Competitive Conduct, and Network Effects

13.
Steep market share curve: Hemnet generates approximately 40 million monthly sessions, delivering 2.4x to 4x more traffic per listing than its nearest competitor
14.
Audience reach dominance: Hemnet reaches 22% of the Swedish population weekly, ranking as the fourth largest commercial media platform nationwide
15.
Aggregator vs portal distinction: Secondary player Booli operates as a free data aggregator owned by state bank SBAB, scraping broker websites rather than operating a vendor-paid classifieds model
16.
Traffic engagement imbalance: Identical property listings accumulate 7x to 12x more views on Hemnet than on Booli due to deep-seated consumer habituation
17.
Benign competitor monetization conduct: Alternative portals like Booli and brokerage-backed Boneo avoid charging sellers, leaving Hemnet with a virtual monopoly on paid classified monetization
18.
Minimum scale threshold: Portal monetization dominance requires a 1.5x–2.0x traffic lead over the #2 player, a threshold Hemnet maintains comfortably across mobile and app channels

'Sell First, Pay Later' adoption: Hemnet's SFPL nationwide rollout achieved 40–45% uptake among eligible sellers, removing upfront financial friction
