Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side focus prior to the third quarter of 2026 was heavily concentrated on the stabilization of Scandinavian Tobacco Group's core European machine-rolled cigar margins.
  2. Analysts prioritized the execution of the Focus2030 cost-reduction program and the potential resumption of share buybacks following the lucrative German fine-cut divestment.
  3. Market expectations remained highly skeptical of sustainable growth given persistent structural volume declines across traditional tobacco categories.
1.
Long-standing historical foundations: Scandinavian Tobacco Group possesses roots dating back to the 1750 establishment of Danish tobacco pioneer Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker. Skandinavisk Tobakskompagni combined its cigar and pipe operations with Swedish Match in 2010 to form the modern corporate structure. Global growth has continued since the public listing in 2016, with net sales reaching more than DKK 9 billion for the first time in 2024. Strategic mergers and acquisitions remain a solid foundation of the vision to become the undisputed global leader.
2.
Diversified segment structure: Scandinavian Tobacco Group splits its commercial operations across three primary geographic divisions to optimize regional performance. The Europe Branded division accounts for approximately 33% of net sales, while North America Branded & Rest of World contributes 35% and North America Online & Retail represents 32%. Combustible cigars remain the dominant product category, representing around two-thirds of group sales as of late 2026. Next generation oral nicotine products comprise approximately 5% of net sales, demonstrating a targeted effort to diversify product offerings.
3.
Niche market leadership: Scandinavian Tobacco Group maintains leading global market positions across several traditional niche segments of the tobacco industry. The group operates as the world-leading manufacturer of cigars and pipe tobacco, with cigars accounting for approximately two-thirds of total revenues. Market leadership is established in European machine-made cigars, US handmade cigars, and global traditional pipe tobacco. High entry barriers and very strong cash flow remain the main structural attractions for long-term investors.
4.
Modest top-line growth: Scandinavian Tobacco Group recorded net sales of DKK 9,202.1 mm for the fiscal year 2024, representing an increase from DKK 8,730.9 mm in 2023. The adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 24.1% in 2023 to 22.6% in 2024, reflecting immediate cost pressures. Gross profit before special items grew to DKK 4,278.5 mm in 2024 compared to DKK 4,204.1 mm in 2023. Consequently, net profit for the year fell to DKK 939.7 mm in 2024, showing near-term profitability headwind challenges before Q3 2026.
5.
Severe profitability contraction: Scandinavian Tobacco Group experienced further financial erosion in fiscal year 2025 as net sales fell to DKK 9,036 mm. The adjusted EBITDA collapsed to DKK 1,592 mm in 2025 from DKK 1,591 mm expected, representing a stable but low level of delivery. Diluted earnings per share decreased to DKK 8.35 in 2025 compared to DKK 11.0 in the prior year. Pervasive volume declines in core products were barely mitigated by price hikes or Next Generation Products.
6.
Stellar short-term recovery: Scandinavian Tobacco Group delivered a total shareholder return of 22.5% over the three months preceding the Q3 2026 earnings release. The impressive short-term performance significantly outperformed peer Philip Morris, which grew 20.0% during the same timeframe. Combustible sector rival Altria achieved a modest 5.0% return, while Imperial Brands and British American Tobacco lagged with returns of negative 18.7% and negative 7.0% respectively. Positive momentum was supported by initial evidence that the Focus2030 strategy was successfully stabilising core European market operations after historical volatility.
7.
Robust medium-term outperformance: Scandinavian Tobacco Group posted an absolute share price return of 21.64% over the six months preceding the Q3 2026 earnings update. Relative performance against the domestic market was positive at 11.63% as US handmade cigar retail stores expanded. The valuation discount compared to peers reached an all-time high, prompting selective buying. Investors began pricing in a gradual recovery as margins neared pre-pandemic levels around 22.5% in late 2026.
8.
Depressed long-term performance: Scandinavian Tobacco Group suffered an absolute share price decline of 6.52% over the 12 months preceding the Q3 2026 earnings release. Relative performance against the OMX Copenhagen PI Index was negative at 15.65%, reflecting severe post-Covid mean reversion. Structural volume declines of at least 3% to 5% per year in core products continually depressed investor sentiment. Paused share buybacks and cut dividends further weighed on long-term equity valuation multiples throughout 2026.
