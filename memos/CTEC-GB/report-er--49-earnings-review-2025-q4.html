Section 4 – Financial delivery

Section summary
  1. Management utilized the FY 2025 results to declare the successful completion of the FISBE turnaround and introduce the Accelerate strategy.
  2. Upgraded medium-term growth targets of 6-8% reflect intense operational confidence in a highly differentiated, compounding product pipeline.

  4. Sceptical sell-side analysts monitor near-term margin delivery due to back-end weighting and persistent regulatory headwinds.

Group financial overview

1.
Strong financial delivery: Convatec reported its full-year results for the period ended December 31, 2025, on February 24, 2026, while subsequently releasing its half-year results on August 4, 2026. Convatec actual revenue of $2,439 mm for the full-year 2025 exceeded the company-compiled consensus of $2,429 mm by 0.41%. Operational momentum across core segments helped the group deliver robust financial progress despite regulatory headwinds.
