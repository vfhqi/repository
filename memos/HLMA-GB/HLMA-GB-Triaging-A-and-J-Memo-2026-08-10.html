B Executive Summary 29 findings
Executive Summary -- We are looking for a clear, conviction-carrying statement of the investment thesis, key ratings, and actionable conclusion; avoiding vague or overly hedged language.
Judgement summary
  • Overall verdict. C Halma is an exceptional-quality compounder with 22-23 consecutive years of record adjusted profit and 47 years of dividend growth
  • Detail: But a single hyperscaler customer inside Avo Photonics now represents 20% of group revenue, up from a historic norm of under 3%, and the shares trade at 31.7x forward earnings (76th percentile of the 10-year range)
  • Key risk. The Risks report's own Analysis of Competing Hypotheses names "FALSE FRIEND" the winning failure profile with zero disconfirming items, versus seven against the bull case, built on Avo's ~12-month contract visibility and a ROTIC/cash-conversion squeeze
  • Key support. Structural demand (ageing demographics, safety and environmental regulation, hyperscaler data-centre capex) plus disciplined bolt-on M&A at 10-12x EV/EBIT against a c.30x trading multiple keep the non-photonics core compounding at 6-9% organically
  • Recommendation. D Park at Triaging: the case-simplicity guardrails and the false-friend setup guardrail both fail on the weight of evidence
  • Detail: And the Risks report's own stage-progression verdict ("Park the investment") is adopted rather than overridden, see Section F for the full reconciliation
