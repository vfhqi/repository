EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

His divisional track record demonstrates proven execution, steering France to 3.2% organic recovery in 2017 while managing the group's active M&A screening pipeline. Since taking the helm, Coquart has delivered sales growth from €5,997m in 2018 to €9,481m in 2025, representing a 7.4% CAGR, while orchestrating a transformational datacenter pivot that scaled exposure from 8% to 32% of sales by H1 2026.
