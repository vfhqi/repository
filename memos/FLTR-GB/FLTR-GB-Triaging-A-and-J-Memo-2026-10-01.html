[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = B

Thesis. Flutter is a major cause-for-concern-clearing case at a trough price, not a compounder and not a demand story: the world's best sportsbook pricer has bought back FanDuel's customers at the cost of a year's US profit, the market has priced that loss as permanent, and at USD 76.63 (28 September 2026) the shares sit inside the sell-side's own bear cases. If the customers stay when the generosity is withdrawn, the shares are worth about USD 115 to 130 in eighteen months; if they do not, about USD 60 to 70.

  • The change is a deliberate, self-inflicted profit trough, not a demand trough.
    • After a record 19 percent NFL hold in late 2025 depleted customer wallets and management under-invested in generosity, FanDuel lost handle share to DraftKings, Fanatics and bet365
    • on 5 August 2026 management answered with USD 270m of promotional reinvestment into the 2H26 NFL season, which was the third guidance cut of the year.
    • FY2026 US adjusted EBITDA guidance fell to USD 760m from USD 970m, and from USD 1,050m at the start of the year
      • group adjusted EBITDA guidance fell to USD 2,655m from USD 2,865m, and from USD 2,970m before that.
    • Around the cut, the UK Remote Gaming Duty rose from 21 to 40 percent from April 2026, and the chief executive who built the US franchise announced his departure for 30 September 2026.
  • The internal forces are broad, funded and dated; the external forces are net headwinds.
    • An orderly succession to the integrator of the International business (Dan Taylor from 1 October 2026), a FanDuel leadership reset, a USD 500m Phase Two cost programme to 2029 on top of USD 300m of Phase One savings, USD 65m a year of Boyd market-access savings, a new market-making revenue line guided at USD 50m for FY2026, and the completed Snai and Betnacional integrations are the change inside the company.
    • Against them, competition is intensifying on every front, tax is rising on three continents, prediction exchanges are growing tax-free, and the data suppliers hold pricing power, which is why the inputs are strong but pull in both directions and the 'Change Forces' Element is a C.
  • The change has reached layer four of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain and not yet layers five and six.
    • US average monthly players rose 9 percent in Q2 2026 and 46 percent in July 2026, US handle turned to +2 percent from -9 percent, and structural hold rose to 14.0 percent
    • but adjusted EBITDA fell 45 percent in Q2 2026, and consensus FY2027 EPS was cut 18.7 percent in the three months to 25 September 2026 with every named broker cutting after 5 August 2026.
    • The operating metrics have turned
    • the financial statements and the sell-side's estimates have not.
  • Our earnings path sits 8 to 17 percent below consensus and still roughly doubles earnings per share off the trough.
    • Adjusted EPS falls to USD 4.55 in FY2026 on our path (consensus USD 4.72) and recovers to USD 6.40 in FY2027, USD 8.70 in FY2028 and USD 11.20 in FY2029, a compound rate of 35 percent a year from the trough and 9 percent a year from the FY2025 base of USD 7.94.
    • The 35 percent is the recovery and the 9 percent is the trend, and the case must carry both: this is a return to the long past, not a new trend.
    • The path sits below consensus because we assume US EBITDA of USD 730m against the USD 760m guide and FY2027 US EBITDA of USD 1,020m against consensus USD 1,184m, because we expect promotion to normalise to about 5 percent of handle through 2027, not 4 percent.
  • The price pays for none of the recovery.
    • At USD 76.63 the shares trade on 8.1x consensus 24-month-forward EPS of USD 9.43, the 0th percentile of the stock's own five-year and ten-year ranges (five-year median 19.39x), and on 9.4x our own 24-month-forward EPS of USD 8.13.
    • That is above Entain and Evoke at 5.9x and near Betsson at 7.8x, inside the European peer range, for a business whose US franchise grows where the peers' do not.
    • What is priced is a US margin that never recovers, permanent tax escalation and a fourth guidance cut
    • what is not priced is any re-rating toward the stock's own history, market-making beyond USD 50m, the Boyd and Phase Two savings reaching margin, or a resumed buyback.
  • On our path the 'Modal return' is about 42 percent a year over eighteen months and 33 percent a year over three years, with no cash return, which is an A against the framework's anchors.
    • We apply 11.0x at eighteen months to the earnings of the twelve months ending March 2030 (USD 11.75 on our path), giving USD 129, and 12.0x at three years to the twelve months ending September 2031 (USD 14.90), giving USD 179
    • the one-year-forward footing, 12.9x on our path held at eighteen months, gives USD 120 and 35 percent a year, so the convention adds about 7 percent of value and does not change the verdict.
    • The 11x is a blend of three branches at stated odds, crisp clearing at 14x (42 percent), a muddle at 10x (33 percent) and a fourth cut at 7x (25 percent), and it equals its blend.
    • The return clears the 20 percent bar at any eighteen-month multiple above 8.6x on our earnings and fails it at the peers' 7x, where the eighteen-month return is 4.8 percent a year.
      • None of the return comes from the predictable leg, and about a quarter of the distribution is negative.
    • Under the plausibly worse drop-through from EBITDA to earnings per share (interest staying at USD 640m, a 30 percent tax rate on rising US profit, growing minorities and no buyback, so a net-income-to-EBITDA ratio of 0.40 at FY2029 instead of 0.46), FY2029 EPS is USD 9.40 instead of USD 11.20, the eighteen-month return falls to 25 percent a year, the bottom of the A band, and the multiple needed to clear the bar rises from 8.6x to 10.3x, above the current 9.4x on our path
      • the 'Modal return' would then be a B and this Building Block a C.
  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.
    • It holds from the internal actions to the operating metrics: the players and the handle have turned, and the structural hold that funds generosity rose 40 bps in the worst quarter.
    • It breaks, for now, at the financial statements and the estimates, which are still falling, and it stays broken until the Q4 2026 delivery (68 percent of the year's US EBITDA in one quarter) and the 1H27 promotion level show whether the USD 270m bought re-engagement or rented it.
    • Both are observable weekly on public state data before the Q3 print on 18 November 2026.
  • The pattern is 'Major CfC Clearing', crisp on the entry and not yet crisp on the clearing.
    • The trough multiple and the estimate cuts are complete
    • the guidance rebasing, the abatement of the disruption and the capitulation are partial, because the guide still needs 77 percent of group EBITDA in 2H26 and 13 of 17 rated broker rows are still Buy or Overweight.

  • The case therefore is a material one whose downside is bounded by a multiple at its floor and whose upside needs one condition, that promotion falls back through 2027 with handle intact, in a year when competitors are raising theirs.

    • What it costs on the valuation is nothing on the entry and everything on the exit: the whole return is earnings recovery and the multiple holding, and neither is predictable.

  • Rating = B, because the expected change is material, earnings per share roughly doubling from the FY2026 trough and a modal return far above the bar, but the forces that make it so are as much the absence of further self-harm at a trough price as strong positive change, and the outputs have not yet turned at the financial-statement layer.

    • Not A, because the external forces are net headwinds and the sell-side is still cutting.
    • Not C, though C was live because the internal change began with three cuts and a USD 270m defensive outlay, because on any path where the multiple merely holds at 9x to 10x the return clears the bar with room.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The bear says the reinvestment is defensive and the moat is eroding: DraftKings holds 33 percent of GGR and grew handle 11 percent in Q2 2026, Fanatics took 11.8 percent of New York handle in July 2026, Kalshi's notional sports volume reached USD 12bn, promotional spend stays at 6 percent-plus of handle, US margins flatline near 15 percent, the UK duty and Australian taxes cap International, and the multiple settles at the European peers' 6x to 7x, giving USD 70 to 83 (Jefferies USD 70; Morgan Stanley USD 78; Redburn USD 83).
  • We concede that the promotional step-up was forced, that the share donation to challengers is real and measured, and that three cuts show management misjudged this market twice in a year.
  • We hold our view for four reasons.
  • First, the price already sits inside that bear range, so the bear's success is largely paid for and its failure is not.
  • Second, the demand readings contradict disintermediation: 3.84m US players (+9 percent), a record July, 10.5m World Cup customers, US iGaming +14 percent, and the company's own deposit tracking showing a low-single-digit drag with over 70 percent of prediction-market volume in states where FanDuel has no sportsbook.
  • Third, the structural hold that funds generosity is rising, which is the engine the bear says is broken.
  • Fourth, the cover arithmetic: at the comparison set's lowest enterprise multiple International covers only about half of the enterprise value, so the market is valuing FanDuel at about USD 10.6bn to 12.7bn of enterprise value, roughly 8.9x to 10.7x FY2027 consensus US EBITDA, which is ex-growth but not zero and not the bear's permanent-impairment value
    • the arithmetic is shown at the head of the 'Investment Case' Building Block.
