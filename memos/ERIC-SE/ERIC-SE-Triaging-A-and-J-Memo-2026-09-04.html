Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Core activity. Ericsson supplies mobile network systems, software and enterprise solutions to telecom operators across more than 180 countries, with radio access networks as its primary earnings engine
2.
Segment structure. Three reported segments run the business: Networks, Cloud Software and Services, and Enterprise, with Networks representing 64% of total sales and the group's valuation anchor
3.
Segment scale, Q2-26. Networks sold SEK 33.0bn, Cloud Software and Services SEK 14.7bn, and Enterprise SEK 4.5bn, against group net sales of SEK 52.7bn
4.
Market position. Ericsson holds a 37% radio access network share outside mainland China, carries roughly half of all mobile data traffic outside China, and ranks number one in North America
5.
Group financial character. FY25 net sales were SEK 236.7bn, with Q2-26 adjusted gross margin of 48.4%, adjusted earnings before interest, tax and amortisation of SEK 6.9bn, and net income of SEK 4.1bn
6.
Balance sheet character. Net cash expanded to SEK 68.1bn in Q1-26, up 76% year on year, with net debt to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation held between negative 0.90 and negative 1.1 times
7.
Cash character. Free cash flow before mergers and acquisitions was SEK 0.4bn in Q2-26, against SEK 5.9bn in Q1-26 and SEK 2.6bn in Q2-25, on seasonal working capital build
8.
Licensing annuity. Annual recurring intellectual property licensing revenue ran at approximately SEK 13.0bn at the end of Q1-26, a high-margin stream tied to global smartphone and automotive volumes
9.
Geography and currency. The United States dollar is 58% of 2025 revenues against 53% of costs, the euro 16%, and North American sales returned to near 40% of the group from Q3-2024
10.
Investment style implication. The business reads as a capital-expenditure cyclical with a self-help cost overlay, not a compounder, on organic revenue growth of 0.2% compound between 2019 and 2025
