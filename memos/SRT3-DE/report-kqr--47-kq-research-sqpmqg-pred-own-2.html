Accounting Definitions and Disclosures

Systematic analysis: Financial reporting documentation reveals that this investigation utilized eight relevant sources covering two primary companies to analyze underlying earnings structures. These adjustments remove non-operational volatility to determine underlying performance attributable to parent shareholders. Significant deviation: Sartorius AG distinguishes between reported IFRS net profit and underlying net profit to provide enhanced operational transparency. The reported IFRS net profit includes fluctuating items such as acquisition-related amortization and restructuring costs. Conversely, the underlying basis eliminates these non-recurring elements before applying minority share allocations. Consequently, this separation directly impacts how the 28.5% minority interest in Sartorius Stedim Biotech is deducted.
3.
Structured reconciliation: Sartorius AG defines underlying net profit as consolidated profit after non-controlling interest adjusted for exceptional items. The calculation specifically removes amortization, extraordinary items, and utilizes normalized financial results and tax rates. For the first nine months of 2026, Sartorius AG reported an underlying net profit of €260 million. This adjustment process directly influences the final earnings per share reported to preference shareholders.

Differentiated taxation: Sartorius AG applies a normalized tax rate of 27% to calculate its underlying earnings. In contrast, its listed subsidiary Sartorius Stedim Biotech utilizes a slightly lower normalized tax rate of 26%. This percentage difference means that identical pre-tax adjustments yield varying post-tax impacts at different consolidation levels. These minor tax variations can alter the final profit split between the parent and minority partners.

Consistent exclusion: Sartorius AG excludes all amortization of acquired intangible assets to prevent purchase price allocation distortions. Because these amortization expenses are non-cash, their elimination significantly raises the underlying profit base.

Volatile adjustments: Sartorius AG adjusts its operating results by removing extraordinary items that distort sustainable business profitability. In the first half of 2026, the group identified €13.5 million in efficiency measures and €6.8 million in M&A integration costs. These expenses are added back to EBIT to establish the underlying operating foundation. By removing these transient costs, the company raises the baseline from which minority interest is calculated.

Substantial savings: Sartorius Stedim Biotech recorded a dramatic reduction in extraordinary items during the first nine months of 2026. Extraordinary expenses fell to €19.4 million from €43.9 million in the prior year's corresponding period. Contrary evidence: Sartorius Stedim Biotech reported that some brands like Stedim are never systematically amortized because they carry indefinite useful lives. Adjusted financing: Sartorius AG normalizes its financial result by eliminating volatile fair value hedging and currency translation effects. Since the public holds 28.5% of Sartorius Stedim Biotech, this fraction of subsidiary profit is subtracted.
