Crucial repeat-prescription catalyst: Brokers paid close attention to the structural changes in German healthcare taking effect on July 1, 2026. Jefferies analyst Martin Comtesse explained that repeat prescriptions for chronic patients would transition from a quarterly to a six-month cycle. This regulatory shift was expected to make online pharmacy services much more compelling for stable chronic patients by reducing doctor visits.

CardLink solution expectations: The roll-out of CardLink was viewed as a primary driver for the app-based redemption of prescriptions. Deutsche Bank analyst Jan Koch noted that the CFO Hendrik Krampe had pushed back against expectations of a rapid inflection in e-prescription growth. Positive regulatory amendments: The regulatory backdrop in Germany was highly supportive for digital players in the lead-up to the earnings. Deutsche Bank analyst Jan Koch noted that the German Ministry of Health published regulations that abandoned planned stricter temperature controls for mail-order drug deliveries. This regulatory relief was expected to prevent additional operational logistics costs for online players.
