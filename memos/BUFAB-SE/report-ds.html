Section summary

Q4 earnings bar – conservative targets: The sell-side consensus for adjusted EBITA in Q4 2025 sat at approximately SEK 238 million, underestimating the pricing power of the business

Margin progression – gradual targets: The market expected Bufab to slowly drift toward its 14% operating margin target by late 2026, rather than breaking record margin levels in the first quarter
