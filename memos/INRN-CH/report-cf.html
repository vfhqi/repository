EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus Earnings Per Share and Outlook

The report does not give a consensus EPS figure for this year (2026) or next (2027). The clearest datapoint is that Jefferies cut its 2026 EPS estimate by 3.0% on February 2, 2026, following management changes. A full consensus EPS table is a gap for follow-up work. Jefferies' base case implies a strong recovery: returning e-commerce activity is expected to drive a mid to high-teens earnings compound annual growth rate from 2025 to 2028.

On the top line, base-case forecasts show group revenues rising at a 7.5% CAGR from 2025 to 2028. For 2026, Jefferies models sales of CHF 573 million. On the downside, EPS would decline to approximately CHF 66.50 if two or three major drivers fail together.

Main Earnings Drivers Ranked by Plausibility

Three drivers are rated high plausibility. Capturing the e-commerce and brownfield capex recovery is rated high. The MXVs Small Wheel Vertical Crossbelt Sorter is rated high because it addresses an unserved capacity segment. Procurement efficiencies, including dual-sourced chipsets, are also rated high. Two drivers are rated medium. The Final Destination platform is medium because some major customers still prefer highly customized layouts. APAC and China localization is medium because domestic competitors are expanding with aggressive pricing. The drivers are interlinked and geographically dependent. The combined success rate for all five drivers is estimated at approximately 35%.

Latest Results (First Half 2026)

Net sales were CHF 269.9 million versus CHF 247.7 million in H1 2025. This was up 8.96% and ahead of the Visible Alpha consensus of CHF 258.5 million. EBITDA was CHF 39.9 million versus CHF 38.6 million, but the EBITDA margin settled at 14.80% versus 15.60% a year earlier. EBIT was CHF 27 million versus CHF 27.6 million, in line with the broker consensus of CHF 27.1 million.

Profit was the one miss. Net income fell 6.60% to CHF 19.8 million, missing consensus by 7.91%, because one-off tax expenses arose from the transfer of intellectual property. Order intake rose 3.91%, in line with consensus, and local-currency order growth reached 8.50%. Free cash flow was CHF -19.4 million, reflecting outflows for the Royal Apollo Group acquisition.

In APAC, sales contracted 24.16% to CHF 56.5 million in 2024 against an exceptionally high base from a CHF 12 million South Korean project completed in 2023. This was partly offset by a 10.50% rise in Chinese order intake.

Guidance Reliability and Delivery Record

The company avoids providing official full-year financial guidance, which limits short-term visibility for analysts. It has stated that revenues of approximately CHF 600 million should support an EBIT margin of 15% plus. The delivery pattern is mixed. H1 2025 net profit came in 20.0% below estimates, while H2 2025 order intake beat the Visible Alpha consensus by 2.0%. The beats may be flattering. Consensus beats may simply reflect exceptionally low targets set during cyclical downturns. Sensitivities are meaningful. Operating discipline is the strongest part of the record. The EBITDA margin was stable at 19.1% in both 2023 and 2024 despite consecutive sales contractions.
