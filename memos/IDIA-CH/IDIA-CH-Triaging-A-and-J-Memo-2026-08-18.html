Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Corporate origin: Idorsia Ltd was spun out of Actelion in June 2017 after Johnson & Johnson's USD 30bn acquisition, launching with CHF 1bn of cash and the Actelion discovery engine at Allschwil, Switzerland
2.
Core activity: The company discovers, develops and commercialises small-molecule therapeutics in central nervous system, cardiovascular and immunological disorders, retaining discovery in-house and outsourcing all chemical manufacturing
3.
Lead product: QUVIVIQ (daridorexant) is a dual orexin receptor antagonist for chronic insomnia, approved in the US, Europe, Canada, Japan, Switzerland and China
4.
Second product: TRYVIO, also marketed as JERAYGO, is aprocitentan, the first dual endothelin receptor antagonist approved for resistant systemic hypertension, listed at USD 775 per month in the US
5.
Revenue model: Revenue is hybrid, combining direct product sales in Idorsia-led territories with upfront fees, milestones and tiered royalties from regional licensees including Simcere, Nxera, EMS and Pharmalink
6.
Revenue scale: FY25 total net revenue was CHF 221m, of which product sales were CHF 141.7m, against CHF 113m total net revenue in FY24
7.
Margin character: FY25 US-GAAP EBIT was a loss of CHF 33m, a (14.9%) margin, after FY23 and FY24 operating losses of CHF 802m and CHF 235m on the same US-GAAP basis
8.
Gross margin: Consolidated gross margin on product sales was 80.6% in H1 2026, down from 89.9% in H1 2025 on volume mix and North American gross-to-net rebates
9.
Balance sheet: Total group indebtedness was CHF 1,397m at 30 June 2026, comprising CHF 631m of direct Idorsia debt and CHF 766m of special-purpose-vehicle notes, against cash of CHF 89m
10.
Geography: H1 2026 product sales were CHF 70m in Europe and Canada and CHF 17m in the United States, with a further CHF 6m of sales to regional partners
11.
Headcount posture: Successive restructurings removed roughly 470 Allschwil roles in 2023, up to 270 in 2024 and over 200 in 2025, and cut the US field force from 100 to 20 virtual representatives
12.
Investment style: The name is event-driven and balance-sheet-levered rather than compounding, with value contingent on partnering, regulatory scheduling and refinancing events rather than steady earnings accretion
