Section 3 – Minority Interests and Put/Call Option Liabilities

Section summary
  1. Beijer Ref's put and call option liabilities grew substantially to SEK 3.41 billion in 2025, driven by structural M&A integrations.
  2. Industrial peers like Lifco use similar put and call structures, while US peers like Watsco rely on traditional non-controlling interest deductions.

Balance Sheet Liabilities

1.
Substantial growth: Beijer Ref reported a massive increase in its short-term option liabilities as acquisition activity accelerated. The company's 2025 annual report revealed that the shortterm portion of option liabilities rose to SEK 1,201 million from SEK 439 million in 2024. When combined with the long-term portion of SEK 2,204 million, total option liabilities reached SEK 3,405 million in 2025 compared to SEK 2,677 million in 2024, showing how M&A structures leverage future payouts and impact net debt. This growth reflects the consolidation of multiple high-stakes acquisitions with non-wholly-owned entries.
2.
Consistent accounting: Beijer Ref utilizes the Anticipated Acquisition Method under IFRS to account for transactions involving put and call options. The group consolidates these businesses at 100 percent from the transaction date because it holds options to acquire remaining shares in the future. Any subsequent changes in the fair value of these liabilities, which are classified under Level 3 of the fair value hierarchy, are recognized directly in equity rather than the income statement. This accounting choice prevents option revaluations from causing volatility in reported operating profit.
3.
Rising obligations: Beijer Ref experienced further expansion in its option liabilities during 2026 due to several newly signed transactions. The group's Q1 2026 interim report showed that the acquisition of a 75 percent stake in Idema added a long-term option liability of SEK 107 million. By Q3 2026, the company completed two additional acquisitions where final purchase prices will be paid via options during 2028 to 2031, creating an additional long-term liability of SEK 510 million. These new obligations will continue to expand the total option liability balance as 2026 acquisitions such as FG Europe and Refra are consolidated.
4.
Contrary evidence: Beijer Ref excludes put and call option liabilities from its reported net debt because these payments are estimated rather than contractually definitive. The group's 2022 annual report clarified that these liabilities, despite totaling SEK 1,373 million at the time, were omitted from net debt calculations because they are subject to future earnings developments. This practice contrasts with several Nordic peers that include these liabilities within their core leverage definitions, potentially understating the company's true financial leverage.

This difference in reporting highlights the earnings volatility that Beijer Ref avoids by routing changes through equity.
