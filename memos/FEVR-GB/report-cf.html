EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 1 finding

Materiality of Strategic and Operational Change Fevertree executed a transformational US distribution agreement with Molson Coors in January 2025, licensing its brand exclusively to the partner, which acquired an 8.5% equity stake for £71 million in February 2025 and assumed 100% of US revenue on its P&L. Proceeds from the stake sale funded a £71 million share buyback returning capital to shareholders in 2025, while the partner funds US working capital and guarantees royalty streams from 2026 to 2030.

Group-wide non-tonic revenue reached 47% of total sales in first half of fiscal 2026, with innovation targeting five global flavor platforms to capture premium adult socializing occasions. The original US strategy and commercial team ceased to exist as structured after the early 2025 Molson Coors deal, with US staff expressing shock and confusion leading to unprecedented cultural disruption and loss of organizational togetherness.

Tim Warrillow and CFO Andrew Branchflower have maintained their executive roles since the 2014 initial public offering, while Domenic De Lorenzo succeeded Bill Ronald as Non-Executive Chair in May 2023 after an orderly six-month handover process. 1 (UK)

Ranked Forward EPS Growth Drivers (5-8 Key Drivers) US gross margins fell to 29.6% in fiscal 2025, lagging behind peer Nichols' group gross margin of 45.7%, but Redburn models US partnership gross margins expanding to 50.7% by fiscal 2028 through localized bottling eliminating transatlantic freight costs. The Molson Coors partner reaches 500,000 retail and on-premise accounts nationwide, with Fevertree becoming a top ten brand for major distributors, while wholesalers enjoy 3-4x higher revenue per litre versus beer.

Transatlantic ocean freight cost an estimated £10.8m in 2025, while historical US import tariffs reached an estimated £5.1m in 2025, both of which will be eliminated through local production onshoring. Non-tonic products reached 47% of total group sales in H1 2026, with gingers making up 63% of the non-tonic portfolio, and the non-tonic category achieving a 16% CAGR over three years. Glass energy costs are fully hedged for fiscal 2026, with hedges covering 66% of glass energy for 2027, while aluminum can supplies are materially hedged over the medium term.

Premium draught font systems create highly sticky commercial channels, with UK market share increasing to 13.5% in total mixers and brand share reaching 21.6% of the UK tonic segment.
