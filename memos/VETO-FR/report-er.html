Section 16 – Conclusion

The H1 2026 earnings fundamentally upgraded the Vetoquinol investment case by proving that structural margin expansion is no longer theoretical, but actively materializing through gross margin expansion to 77.1% and operating income margin reaching 17.3% despite persistent foreign exchange headwinds and deliberate complementary product rationalization. The gross margin beat consensus expectations of 75.50% on stronger pricing power, while operating income margin shattered the sell-side whisper margin of 14.20%.

This performance confirms that management's aggressive premiumization strategy is successfully offsetting the temporary top-line friction created by portfolio simplification, which shaved €3.0 million off first-half sales but simultaneously improved the product mix toward high-margin Essentials.

The single most important delta from this earnings print is the double-digit US market recovery, where sales surged 13.1% in constant currency after several quarters of stagnation and supply disruption. This performance far surpassed the mid-single-digit recovery expected by the market. The magnitude of this rebound validates that Vetoquinol has resolved its manufacturing internalization friction and successfully normalized distribution channels, positioning the United States as the premier geographical growth engine going forward.

This US recovery is critical because it demonstrates operational execution capability ahead of the planned 2027 product launches, reducing the execution risk premium that historically weighed on the valuation multiple. Furthermore, the combination of US growth momentum and European resilience provides a diversified geographic profit base that distinguishes Vetoquinol from peers who suffered disproportionate North American volume contraction during the same period.

While the sell-side is optimistic about the margin beat, the underlying volume growth remains weak, and if price increases slow down or face vet resistance, the group's top-line expansion could stall due to stagnant volumes, potentially causing Vetoquinol's mid-teens margins to fail to reach historical high-teens levels.

The corporate consolidation of veterinary clinics is accelerating across mature markets, giving buyers enhanced bargaining power over pharmaceutical suppliers, which is likely to pressure historical pricing power on high-volume standard treatments.
