EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Strategic position and competitive scale

1.
Global market leadership: Sika commands an unrivaled 12% share of the fragmented CHF 100 billion construction chemicals industry, positioning it as the clear global leader with substantially greater scale than any peer.
2.
Localized consultative model: Around 75% of sales are made directly to the project site where Sika's R&D-driven technical specialists insert themselves at the design stage, making their product systems highly indispensable and creating formidable specification lock-in advantages.
3.
Unmatched breadth and reach: Sika's multi-product solutions strategy spans all eight target construction markets while leveraging its massive footprint of 103 national subsidiaries and over 400 factories to secure major global infrastructure projects, whereas most peers compete in only a single vertical.
4.
Defensive market exposure: Climate adaptation and refurbishment account for 55% of revenues, insulating Sika from cyclical new-build housing declines and providing structural resilience through infrastructure-led demand.

Financial profile and capital returns

5.
Subdued near-term organic growth: Sika reported organic sales contraction of 0.4% in FY2025, heavily impacted by residential real estate soft markets, though excluding China construction, organic sales grew by 1.2% in local currency.
6.
Premium gross margin expansion: Sika expanded its FY2025 material margin by 40 basis points to 54.9% through focused pricing actions, while achieving an adjusted EBITDA margin of 19.2% excluding one-off operational restructure costs.
7.
Elevated post-acquisition leverage: Net debt to EBITDA rose to 2.3x at end-FY2025 on an adjusted basis following major acquisition outflows, representing the highest leverage position among specialty chemical peers but with clear deleveraging trajectory.
8.
Returns compression from MBCC: ROIC has rebased from above 20% pre-pandemic to c.11% today due to the highly capital-dilutive MBCC transaction, though this reflects short-term balance sheet expansion rather than deteriorating operational efficiency.
9.
Strong underlying capital efficiency: Capital efficiency remained structurally strong, with ROCE adjusted for acquisitions reaching 22.1% in fiscal year 2024, while operating free cash flow reached CHF 1,356 million in FY2025, representing 12.1% of net sales.
10.
Synergistic margin expansion runway: The finalized MBCC integration is on track to deliver upgraded synergies of CHF 200-220 million by end-2026, supporting structural EBITDA margin expansion toward the upper end of guidance ranges.

Operator quality and strategic execution

11.
Proactive efficiency acceleration: Sika launched Fast Forward to double its historical efficiency savings and build a leaner organization, targeting CHF 80 million in cost savings in the 2026 financial year and CHF 150 million to CHF 200 million at full run rate by 2028.
12.
Dominant R&D investment intensity: Sika targets investing approximately 3% of sales annually on research and development, representing the highest target among building materials peers, while deploying 1,800 R&D specialists across 16 global technology centers and more than 100 local research facilities.
13.
Rapid inflation pass-through capability: Only up to 15% of petrochemical input costs correlate directly with headline crude oil price fluctuations, while Sika passes cost inflation downstream quickly, typically within one to two months of cost changes, protecting absolute margin dollars.
14.
Herculean switching barriers: Onboarding new applicators requires a rigorous 10-to-15-day technical training program to prevent application failures, creating substantial technical qualification hurdles that lock in customer relationships and prevent margin-destructive competitive switching.

Industry structure and long-term growth vectors

15.
Superior structural growth market: The global construction chemicals market is estimated at over CHF 110 billion in size and has expanded at a 7.4% CAGR since 2013, outperforming broader building materials through defensive repair demand and urbanization tailwinds.
16.
Rational competitive conduct: Industry participants maintain a value-over-volume strategy to pass through raw material cost inflation, while inserting proprietary formulations at the design stage locks in high switching costs for customers and sustains premium EBIT margins of 15-25%.
17.
Decarbonization growth opportunity: Sustainability regulations drive demand for low-carbon cement formulations and solar-reflective roofs that lower building energy usage, creating secular growth vectors that structurally outpace GDP expansion rates.

Risk factors and plausible problem to monitor

18.
Drastic China rightsizing requirement: Sika is cutting up to 1,500 employees in China, shifting focus from low- margin volume to high-margin refurbishment, reflecting structural challenges in the world's largest construction market that could persist for multiple years.
19.
Strong currency translation headwinds: Negative foreign exchange effects slashed sales by 5.4% in Swiss francs during the FY2025 period, creating material reporting volatility that obscures underlying operational momentum and margin progression.
20.

Plausible problems to monitor

Working capital management presents a secondary pressure point, as net working capital as a percentage of net sales rose to 19.7% in FY2024 before seasonal adjustments, exceeding peer median levels and suggesting weaker bargaining power in receivables collection or inventory optimization relative to competitors maintaining tighter ratios. Over the next 18 months, the inventory line is most likely to shift against Sika due to Middle East logistics hedging, threatening to widen the gap versus peers with superior supply chain elasticity.

Regional growth dynamics expose a third plausible problem to monitor in Asia-Pacific markets, where segment revenue fell to CHF 1,059 million in H1 2026, reflecting a 2.0% local currency decline, materially underperforming EMEA's 7.7% expansion and falling short of peer Saint-Gobain's 8.5% organic growth in construction chemicals globally during the same period. This geographic imbalance highlights concentrated Chinese residential exposure that Sika has been slower to reposition than competitors.

Organic growth momentum presents a fourth concern, as organic sales contraction of 0.4% in FY2025 placed Sika below the peer median growth rate of approximately 2-3%, with even the adjusted 1.2% figure excluding China Construction remaining at the sector's lower bound. While recovery signs emerged in H1 2026, the trailing twelve-month underperformance suggests delayed volume traction relative to peers capitalizing more aggressively on infrastructure and data center demand.

Leverage positioning constitutes the fifth structural weakness, as net debt to EBITDA rose to 2.3x at end-FY2025 on an adjusted basis following major acquisition outflows, making Sika the most levered peer in its specialty chemicals cohort. Redburn Atlantic notes Sika starts as the most levered peer but projects leverage falling to 0.8x by FY2029, yet the current elevated position constrains capital allocation optionality and exposes earnings to refinancing risks should construction markets deteriorate further.

This leverage overhang differentiates Sika negatively from peers maintaining sub-2.0x ratios while executing comparable M&A strategies. - Earnings Calls & Co Redburn These five plausible problem to monitor collectively indicate that while Sika retains commanding market share and pricing power, its financial efficiency profile has temporarily fallen below peer standards across returns, working capital, geographic diversification, organic momentum, and balance sheet flexibility, requiring sustained execution of Strategy 2028 and Fast Forward to restore top-quartile positioning.
