Section 3 – Peer Group Multiples and Private Equity Competition

Section summary
  1. Valuation multiples among Nordic serial acquirers remain highly disciplined, with most peers paying between 5x and 8x EBITA.
  2. Small-scale peers like Tången and InstallatørGruppen achieve exceptionally low entry multiples below 6x EBITA through proprietary sourcing.
  3. Bilateral negotiations dominate the lower bracket, while competitive auctions are largely avoided by disciplined compounders.

9.
Sourcing process bilateralism: Sourcing deals outside of competitive auctions is the primary method for peers to keep entry multiples low. For example, Tången has completed 24 acquisitions at a weighted average multiple of 4.8x EV/EBITA since 2021. Tången achieved these low valuations because it sourced 70% of transactions outside brokered processes, avoiding auctions entirely.
10.
Tången pipeline screening: Tången employs a highly structured screening framework to identify and secure proprietary deal flow. The company screened approximately 120,000 Nordic small and medium enterprises, shortlisting 380 targets for evaluation. Maintaining this massive pipeline allows Tången to remain highly selective with acquisitions while enforcing strict pricing limits.
11.
Finnish proprietary channels: Auroora focuses almost exclusively on proprietary deals in Finland to avoid competitive bidding wars. Auroora paid average multiples of 5.3x EV/EBITA for platforms and 5.0x for add-on acquisitions in 2025. These low multiples are possible because Auroora targets founder-owned companies that never go to competitive auction processes.
12.
Stable deal structures: Auroora observes that deal pricing and process dynamics have remained highly stable in the Finnish market. However, the use of performance-based earn-outs has become slightly more common in recent transactions. Relying on structured earn-outs helps the company mitigate post-acquisition operational risks while maintaining low initial multiples.
13.
Technical installation pricing: InstallatørGruppen utilizes a highly disciplined bilateral acquisition model in Denmark and Switzerland. The group paid average multiples of approximately 5x EV/EBITA for its acquisitions between 2022 and 2025. Normalizing acquisitions around 5.0x to 5.5x structural EBITA allows the company to protect itself against economic cycles.
14.
Bolt-on multiple advantages: InstallatørGruppen achieves significant pricing advantages by focusing heavily on small bolt-on acquisitions. While the group paid 5.5x EV/EBITA for platform deals, it paid an average of 3.4x for bolt-ons during 2022 to 2025. This suggests that Storskogen may struggle to find high-quality infrastructure assets below 7x EBITA if it competes directly with Sdiptech.
