Section 1 – Key question and brief

  1. The mid-term earnings trajectories of European SMID-cap biotechs are highly resolvable through clinical pipeline milestones and licensing partnerships.

1.
Accelerating pipelines: The primary investment focus resides on how clinical assets transition into commercial royalty engines over the next 18 months to 3 years.
2.
Divergent earnings: Commercial-stage biotechs demonstrate improving earnings visibility and lower interest- rate sensitivity than purely clinical-stage peers.

  2. Strong Phase 2 and Phase 3 trial data support a highly competitive metabolic pipeline despite established United States incumbents.
