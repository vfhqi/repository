Plausible problems to monitor

Lundbeck's adjusted gross margin compressed to 86.3% in Q2 2026 from 88.2% in Q2 2025, with reported gross margin falling to 81.1% due to the structural transition of 27 commercial markets to partner distribution models that impose 25% to 30% commission fees on revenues. This 86.7% adjusted gross margin in H1 2026, while still leading peers, represents a permanent structural dilution toward the guided 87% full-year target, materially below historical performance and directly cross-referenced in Section 4 Financial Profile Change and Section 6 Most Recent Earnings Delivery.

- Earnings Calls Lundbeck's 32% adjusted EBITDA margin significantly underperforms the 40.7% achieved by UCB and 38.6% reported by Ipsen, placing the company in the bottom quartile of its European mid-cap pharmaceutical peer group as detailed in Section 7 Peer-Relative Earnings Momentum. The accelerated R&D investment to DKK 5.6 billion to DKK 5.9 billion in FY2026 represents approximately 22% of revenue, materially exceeding Sobi's target range of 11% to 14%, creating a persistent margin gap that compounds the commission-driven gross profit pressures outlined in Section 4 Financial Profile Change.

Trintellix/Brintellix revenue remained flat at 0% constant exchange rate growth in H1 2026, representing a stalled franchise within the strategic brand portfolio as noted in Section 3 External Change and constituting a material deviation from the double-digit expansion exhibited by Vyepti and Rexulti. International markets grew only 6% at constant exchange rates, substantially lagging the 14% European growth and 20% US expansion documented in Section 6 Most Recent Earnings Delivery, signaling geographic performance fragmentation and mature asset erosion in non-core territories.

Approximately 60% of revenues face patent expirations between 2026 and 2029, creating a looming patent cliff that is not adequately offset by pipeline assets still in clinical development as analyzed in Section 3 External Change and Section 9 Key Drivers Synthesis. DNB Carnegie models project revenue peaking at DKK 25,999 million in 2026e before entering a generic-driven structural decline, representing a mid-term growth reversal that contradicts the current double-digit strategic brand momentum and is cross-referenced in Section 4 Financial Profile Change.

Approximately 79% of sales are generated in non-EUR currencies, with the US market directly representing 55% of global revenue, exposing Lundbeck to extreme geographic concentration risk and heightened currency translation volatility relative to more diversified European peers as detailed in Section 3 External Change and Section 7 Peer- Relative Earnings Momentum. Lundbeckfonden controls 77% of votes and 69% of shares, representing extreme voting concentration that limits minority shareholder influence on strategic capital allocation decisions as documented in Section 1 Leadership and Governance Change.

Fourth-quarter results historically represent a seasonally weaker period for operating margins, with product mix shifts toward costly intravenous biologics creating quarterly margin fluctuations that introduce predictability challenges for full-year EBITDA forecasting as analyzed in Section 5 Historical Earnings Trajectory. Recurring adjustments create a wide gap between GAAP and core operational profitability, reducing earnings quality transparency and complicating comparability with peers who report lower adjustment frequencies as crossreferenced in Section 5 Historical Earnings Trajectory.
