The reasoning behind the inputs

Part 4 is the second set's reasoning as written. Where a later set changed an input, that input's figure in the tables above is the changed one and its reasons are in the changed-inputs file named above; Part 4 is not rewritten.

Four conventions the reader must hold before reading any figure above. 1. Perimeter: the current group without Evoke. Guidance (about EUR 422m FY2026 adjusted EBITDA; EUR 454m FY2027), the FY2027 target and the only visible broker forecasts are all pre-Evoke, Evoke's debt sits in a separate credit silo, and its completion date, share-versus-cash mix and purchase price allocation are unknown. Evoke is given below as a separate overlay with its own figures; it is not in Parts 1 to 3 or in the cross-check. 2. Revenue growth is against FactSet's reported revenue, which in FY2025 (EUR 518m) held only 82 days of Bally's International Interactive (BII). FY2026 revenue of EUR 1,106m is +113.5% reported and +1.8% against the pro-forma FY2025 of EUR 1,086m. Q3 2026 is against a legacy-only Q3 2025 (EUR 74.5m), hence +253%. 3. EBITDA margin is on the company's adjusted EBITDA, not FactSet's operating EBITDA (H1 2026: EUR 184.8m adjusted against EUR 164.4m FactSet operating; FactSet's line runs about EUR 35-45m a year lower). 4. Net financial items include a pre-tax equivalent of minority interests (EUR 9m a year), because the form has no minorities line: interest and other finance costs are EUR 140m, 138m, 134m, 128m and 122m, plus EUR 9m. FY2026 interest includes the EUR 7.3m net FX loss of H1 2026.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • UK online (about 60% of group revenue). Constant-currency growth about +10% in FY2026 (Q1 +10.5%, Q2 +11.6%, H2 about +8-9% as the generosity cut tests volume), about +6% in FY2027, +5% in FY2028, +4% thereafter; euro growth about 1-2 points lower on a weaker pound and the annualisation of the price-led gain. Spain +12-15% in FY2026 on a small base, slowing to about +6%.
  • The UK duty. Remote Gaming Duty 21% to 40% from 1 April 2026: gross cost about EUR 95m in FY2026 (nine months; EUR 34.0m in Q2) and about EUR 127m in FY2027 (the full year; Jefferies' EUR 127m is the annualisation of the casino step, so the April 2027 remote betting duty step from 15% to 25% costs BII little). Post-duty flow-through on incremental BII revenue about 40-45%.
  • Self-help (in-year EBITDA). BII-Intralot synergies (EUR 41m programme: EUR 15m 2026, EUR 25m 2027 stated): EUR 12m, 35m, 40m, 41m. Duty cost mitigation net of overlap with the synergies (EUR 11.3m a quarter delivered from Q2 2026): about EUR 25m in FY2026, EUR 33m from FY2027.
