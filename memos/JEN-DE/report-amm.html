What the company does

Jenoptik is a German photonics group, reorganised on 1 January 2025 into four Strategic Business Units. It is a tier-two supplier of bespoke micro-optical subsystems designed into other people's machines: semiconductor lithography and inspection tools, medical and defence optics, metrology and traffic enforcement.

Sales in 2025 fell 6.30% to 1,046 million euros at an 18.40% margin on earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Semiconductor and Advanced Manufacturing is the engine at 434.4 million euros and a 25.7% margin; Metrology and Production Solutions is the drag at 206.7 million and 7.8%. Market value is 2,161.3 million euros.

A plausible investment thesis

The mechanism to earnings per share is fixed-cost leverage on a capacity step already paid for. The 100 million euro Dresden cleanroom opened in May 2025, doubled micro-optics capacity and was funded from operating cash flow. The second quarter of 2026 is the proof: revenue up 3%, group earnings up 28%, the group margin up 4.0 points to 20.8%, the semiconductor divisional margin 33.4%.

Demand is dated rather than asserted. Global semiconductor sales grew 135% year on year in June 2026, ASML booked 13.2 billion euros of orders in the fourth quarter of 2025, and consensus earnings growth is 18.3% a year as at 8 October 2026. The drivers to track resolve on the November third-quarter print: group order intake above 300 million euros, divisional intake above 120 million and second-half margin above 19.5%, with working capital to revert below 30% of revenue from 32.3% and a Prodomax decision due at the year-end strategy update.
