Outlier 3: Unprecedented AI Project Velocity with Negligible Traffic Impact Rightmove expanded its active AI initiatives from 31 in December 2025 to 43 by May 2026, representing a 39% quarterly growth rate in project pipeline. Outlier 4: Countercyclical GFC Margin Expansion During the 2009 financial crisis, Rightmove's operating EBITDA increased by £1m to £43m despite a 6% revenue decline, lifting margins to 60.4%. This countercyclical profitability expansion places Rightmove in the top 5% of UK consumer internet companies for downturn resilience. However, the 13% year-on-year decline in agent membership in 2008 took seven years until 2015 to fully recover, indicating that while profitability remained robust, the customer base erosion was severe and prolonged.
