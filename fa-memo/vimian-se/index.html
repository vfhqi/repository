The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

Organic growth comes from niche penetration, about 3 to 4 points of price matched by cost inflation, and cross-selling. No market-volume recovery is assumed: US clinic visits have fallen for 18 consecutive quarters. US orthopaedics is flat to low single digit. The bolt-ons are EUR 65m of enterprise value a year at about 9x EBITDA, about EUR 25m of revenue a year, half in the year of purchase.

EBITDA margin basis. EBITDA is company adjusted EBITDA less about EUR 6m to 7m a year of recurring roll-up costs (stay-on bonuses, deal costs) that the company reports in items affecting comparability. FY2025 on this basis: 113.4 (26.7%), equal to FactSet's brokers'-basis actual of EUR 114m (26.7%). The margin path is built from the segment margins above. - Gross margin held at about 69%. - Central costs grow 7% a year. - EBITDA margin rises faster than the EBITA margin because D&A excluding PPA rises: capitalised development and software was EUR 7.7m in the first nine months of 2026, against 3.6m.

  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
