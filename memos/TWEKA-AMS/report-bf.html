EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 21 findings

TKH Group NV is executing a decisive strategic transformation to emerge as a focused, asset-light industrial automation pure-play through the dual-track separation of its capital-intensive Electrification cable business. The company occupies profit-favoured positions in specialized machine vision and automated tire building machinery, protected by over 1,591 active patents and underpinned by market shares exceeding seventy percent in outsourced tire equipment.

Financial performance reflects cyclical earnings volatility driven by Eemshaven subsea cable facility ramp-up delays and Tier-1 tire OEM capital expenditure freezes, but structural margin expansion is emerging through operational recovery and portfolio rationalization. Foundation quality is underpinned by dominant market positions, deep customer qualification barriers spanning twelve to twenty-four months, and ambitious return targets of twenty-five to thirty percent ROCE in the standalone Automation business.

The core investment thesis centers on whether management can sustain disciplined capital allocation and fixed-cost absorption during prolonged cyclical headwinds while executing the complex Electrification carveout without stranded corporate overhead exposure.
