[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = B

Thesis. Demant is a self-help recovery riding a product cycle, and the business change is compelling: a cleaned-up portfolio, a category-leading custom platform, a large retail acquisition and a cost programme have lifted organic growth from 2 percent in FY2025 to 9 percent in Q2 2026 and have already produced a guidance raise and sell-side upgrades. At DKK 321.4 the shares already pay for the part of that recovery the market can see, so what is left to earn is the earnings growth alone, about 16 percent a year over eighteen months, below the 20 percent bar. The case clears the bar at or below about DKK 305, or at today's price only if the crowded second-half launch window is survived and the market then pays the ten-year median multiple.

  • The change is internal, broad and already acting, and none of it depends on the market.
    • Four forces changed the inputs between mid-2025 and August 2026.
    • Oticon Zeal: the custom rechargeable in-the-ear platform reached 23.1 percent of US Veterans Affairs units by July 2026 from 19.2 percent in August 2025, at USD 695, a 42 percent premium to Demant's legacy devices and about 30 percent above Sonova's Virto R at USD 526
      • About two-thirds of its users are first-time wearers.
    • Oticon Reveal: the flagship receiver-in-canal platform launched in August 2026, with management expecting net price increases over Intent.
    • KIND: about 650 German clinics bought for DKK 5.2bn and closed in December 2025
      • Integrating ahead of plan, with the 2026 EBIT contribution raised to DKK 325m from DKK 300m.
    • Cost programme: DKK 500m of structural savings by 2028, DKK 300m landing in 2026 (raised from DKK 250m), DKK 100m delivered in H1 2026 ahead of schedule.
    • Portfolio: EPOS sold in January 2026 and Oticon Medical in March 2026, closing a four-year structural review
      • No further impact from discontinued operations in 2026.
  • The outputs have followed on every line we track, and the breadth is the point.
    • H1 2026 revenue was DKK 12,913m, +15 percent reported and +7 percent organic
    • Q2 was +9 percent organic against consensus of 6.7 percent
    • The gross margin was 77.1 percent, +110bp
    • EBIT before special items DKK 2,134m, +15 percent
    • Adjusted EPS DKK 6.40, +20 percent
    • Gearing fell from 3.4x to 3.0x in six months.
    • Breadth: wholesale +10 percent, retail +8 percent and diagnostics +9 percent organic in Q2 2026, all three beating consensus
      • The pull-through of Zeal into the wider Oticon portfolio is reported as incremental rather than cannibalising.
  • The change has travelled all six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • On 11 August 2026 guidance for FY2026 was raised to 6-7 percent organic growth and DKK 4,400-4,800m of EBIT before special items from 3-6 percent and DKK 4,100-4,500m, and consensus EPS for FY2026 to FY2028 rose 5 to 10 percent over three to six months.
    • So the chain holds through the sell-side's estimates, which is also why the price has moved: the shares are up 42.2 percent over twelve months and 67.6 percent over six, and the market saw the change before we did.
  • The external forces help only modestly, and one of them turns against the case in exactly the quarters that test it.
    • The hearing-aid market grows 3 percent in units and 4 percent in value
    • The over-the-counter and consumer-tech disruption has been disproved by the data
    • Reimbursement is supportive
    • The currency headwind is easing.
    • Against that, four of the five major manufacturers launch premium receiver-in-canal platforms in the second half of 2026 against Demant's Reveal, which sceptics read as a software upgrade on the Intent chip.
    • The mitigation: the Zeal gain sits in a custom in-the-ear form factor the rivals' launches do not compete with head-on, and the company's second-half guidance already incorporates the expected impact of the competitor launches
      • The independent channel, 52 percent of wholesale, is where audiologist mindshare is contested and where the test is run.
  • What we think it earns: adjusted EPS of about DKK 14.2 in FY2026, 16.6 in FY2027 and 19.1 in FY2028 from 11.74 in FY2025, about 17.7 percent a year, 2 to 5 percent above consensus of 13.92, 15.95 and 18.20.
    • Composition: revenue and margin, the lines investors extrapolate, give about 11.7 percent a year together
      • The ratio below EBITDA gives about 4 percent a year as interest falls with debt
      • The share count gives about 1 percent a year from buybacks resuming in H2 2027.
    • Where we differ from consensus: on the interest line, because consensus holds total debt flat at DKK 18,132m through FY2028 while free cash flow of DKK 2,788m, 3,746m and 4,069m has nowhere else to go
      • On the cost run-rate, taken at the company's DKK 500m
      • And on the KIND margin, taken at 20 percent by 2028.
    • Drop-through: on a plausibly worse ratio from EBITDA to EPS (interest held at DKK 700m, tax at 25 percent, heavier amortisation, no buyback before 2028) FY2028 EPS is about DKK 17.6, 8 percent lower, and the three-year rate is 14.4 percent a year, still inside the 12 to 20 percent band.
  • What it costs: DKK 321.4, or 18.1x consensus 24-month-forward earnings of DKK 17.77.
    • That is the 99th percentile of the stock's own two-year range, the 58th of five years and the 37th of ten
      • It sits between the ten-year 'conservative' band of 17.7x and the 'prudent' median of 19.2x, and a little below Sonova at 19.0x.
    • So the re-rating from the 2024-25 trough has been taken.
      • The multiple moved from a two-year median of 15.4x to 18.1x on the Zeal data, the Q1 and Q2 beats and the guidance raise
      • It was earned by whoever bought in late 2025.
  • The return is a C against the 20 percent bar, and the multiple is the swing factor.
    • At an unchanged multiple of 18.0x, no dividend and no buyback until mid-2027, the 'Modal return' is about 16 percent a year over eighteen months (DKK 400) and 13 percent a year over three years (DKK 466).
    • Upside branch: at the ten-year median of 19.2x within eighteen months the return is 20.9 percent a year, a B.
      • It needs the launch window survived and the market then paying the ten-year band
      • We put the odds at about 30 percent, so it is plausible, not modal.
    • Downside branch: at the five-year median of 16.7x the return is 10.2 percent a year, a D
      • On the drop-through case at an unchanged multiple it is about 9.6 percent a year, an F.
      • The case is more exposed below the operating line than the headline growth suggests.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Major Corporate Change', and the fit is crisp.
    • Five of the twelve internal forces are strong, the outputs confirm them on every line, and the change is at full output
    • 'OSHQC CfC Clearing' fitted a year ago and its oversold condition has gone.
  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.
    • It holds from the company's choices, through its actions, its key operating metrics, its financial statements and the sell-side's estimates.
    • It breaks at the price, which already pays for that, and it is tested in the second half of 2026 by a competitive event outside the company's control.

  • The case therefore is a B thesis at a C price: compelling as a business change, not compelling as a return from DKK 321.4.

    • That is a priced watch position, not a park: the price that clears the bar is about DKK 305, and the November data could move the odds before the price does.

  • Rating = B, because the change is material and has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, with 'Change Forces' at B and 'Financial Outputs' at B, and the price pays for it

    • Not A because the 'Modal return' at this price is a C
    • Not C because the forces and the outputs sit clearly above the universe median and the return is not a D.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The hypothesis, at its strongest: the recovery is real but it is in the price, and the second half of 2026 is where it stalls.
      • Price: the shares are up 67.6 percent in six months and sit at the 99th percentile of their two-year multiple range
        • The guidance raise was anticipated and moved consensus by only about 1 percent on the day
        • Bernstein's downgrade of 17 July 2026 and Deutsche Bank's "limited room for re-rating" of 19 August 2026 are the sell-side form of this view, and the Holds are 9 of the 17 named brokers.
      • Earnings quality of the step: the FY2026 EPS step of about 20 percent is flattered by the first-year consolidation of KIND and by a cost programme that is one-off in nature.
      • Product: Reveal is, on Bernstein's reading, a software update on the same Sirius chip as Intent, while Sonova's EON carries a new chip and a smaller housing and GN's Sensia is claimed as the smallest AI hearing aid
        • Four of five manufacturers launch into one condensed window.
      • Zeal's reach: the success is concentrated in a custom category that is about 10 percent of the market and in the Veterans Affairs channel that is 5 percent of sales, where prices are negotiated centrally, and about a third of candidates are excluded by ear-canal size.
      • Channel: the independent audiologists, 52 percent of wholesale, may punish forward integration into retail, and nobody has the data on account losses either way.
      • Positioning: short interest of about 10 percent of free float fuelled the 13 percent rise in a day in May 2026 and can unwind.
      • The operator: guidance was cut four times in two years before it was raised once.
    • What we concede: that the re-rating has happened and we assume none
      • That the launch window is the test and its outcome cannot be seen before the Q3 statement in early November 2026
      • That the FY2026 step is partly inorganic and partly one-off
      • That the operator is a C
      • And that the modal return at DKK 321.4 is a C.
      • Most of the facts in the hypothesis are our facts too.
    • Why we hold our view on the business.
      • The chain is complete and broad: all three divisions beat in Q2 2026.
      • The custom category where Zeal wins is not where the rivals' second-half launches land.
      • The margin levers for 2027-28 (DKK 200m more of cost savings, KIND from about 15 to 20 percent EBIT margin on about DKK 2.0bn of sales, interest falling with debt) are internal and ahead of plan.
      • The balance sheet is repairing faster than the street models.
      • The structural threats the bear case used to carry, over-the-counter devices and consumer tech, have been disproved by the data: 4 percent of surveyed US adults bought an OTC device a year after introduction, and Sony and Lucid have withdrawn.
    • Why we do not hold the view on the price.
      • The return does not clear the bar at an unchanged multiple, and we would not pay for a re-rating whose odds we put at 30 percent.
      • The hypothesis and our response converge on the same action: wait for the price, or for the November data.
    • Where the hypothesis would win: wholesale organic growth below 5 percent in Q3 or Q4 2026 with the gross margin down more than 100bp, or the Veterans Affairs Zeal share below about 20 percent for two months.
      • Those are the readings that would mark the launch window lost, and with it the multiple.
