Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Bravida faces a transition from cyclical recovery to data-centre structural growth, separating its future EPS from past flat performance.

Defining the core question

Core inquiry: "how concentrated or diffuse are the drivers that separate the next 18 months to four years' financials from the last four to six years, and how wide and how skewed is the range of plausible outcomes over the next 18 months?"
2.
Financial significance: Resolving this question determines whether Bravida can achieve its projected adjusted EPS of SEK 7.0 in 2026 and SEK 8.9 in 2027. This would represent significant earnings growth acceleration compared to the flat EPS of SEK 6.01 reported in 2025. Therefore, understanding driver concentration is vital for evaluating the company's multi-year recovery and overall investment thesis.

Significance of key drivers

Data-centre concentration: A critical driver separating future financials from the past is the execution of large data-centre contracts. Projects for Green Mountain, atNorth, and EcoDataCenter increase the company's dependence on few larger projects, which introduces higher inherent complexity. Macroeconomic pressures: Macroeconomic headwinds like the unhedged currency exposure and the upcoming 2027 Swedish wage round also shape the range of outcomes.
