EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 53 findings

Section summary
  1. executive board expansion and deep management continuity secure execution stability through rapid internationalization
  2. upgraded supervisory expertise adds geopolitical depth, but highly centralized decision-making threatens operational scaling

Rheinmetall has undergone significant leadership expansion and governance strengthening over the past 12 to 36 months, marked by CEO Armin Papperger's contract extension through December 31, 2029, an orderly CFO transition from Dagmar Steinert to Klaus Neumann on January 1, 2025, and the creation of a new Chief Operations Officer role filled by René Gansauge. The Supervisory Board added geopolitical depth through the appointment of former German Federal Minister Prof. Sigmar Gabriel and artificial intelligence expert Prof. Dr. Sabina Jeschke in May 2025. However, legacy operational decisions remain heavily concentrated at the CEO level, risking critical execution and program delays, while division infighting and slow internal coordination frequently hinder program execution, leading to unmet contractual delivery obligations.
