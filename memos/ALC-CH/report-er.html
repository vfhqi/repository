Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Alcon established its global eye care leadership through consistent innovation following its spin-off from Novartis.
  2. Strong fundamental performance over the last three years has driven revenue growth but faced near-term competitive challenges in implantables.

1.
Global leader: Alcon operates as the global leader in eye care across the surgical ophthalmology and vision care markets. The company researches, develops, manufactures, distributes, and sells a full suite of ophthalmic products, including advanced equipment, intraocular lenses, contact lenses, and ocular health solutions. Through its extensive commercial footprint in 56 countries, Alcon serves patients and eye care professionals in more than 140 countries.
2.
Long history: Alcon was acquired by Nestlé in 1977 and later became a division within Novartis through a series of transactions completed between 2008 and 2011. On April 9, 2019, Novartis completed the legal and structural separation of Alcon into a stand-alone company through a spin-off transaction. Since this landmark separation, Alcon has focused on launching innovative new products and investing in manufacturing line expansion to secure its independent growth.
3.
Balanced segments: Alcon organizes its operations into two global business segments comprising Surgical and Vision Care. In 2025, the Surgical segment generated $5.8 bn, representing approximately 56.31% (calculated) of Alcon total net sales of $10.3 bn. Meanwhile, the Vision Care segment accounted for $4.6 bn of net sales, illustrating the company's balanced exposure to retail optometry and clinical surgical markets.
