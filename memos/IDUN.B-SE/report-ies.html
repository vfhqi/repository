Idun Industrier (IDUN.B-SE): what the Q3 2026 results do to the existing case

The Q3 2026 results (23 October 2026) strengthen the existing case modestly: they meet the letter of the case's own progress trigger, but they do so through the least dependable route available, and the share price has not acknowledged them.

1. What changed

The case assumed the acquired and refinanced earnings were owned and the organic turn had not reached the company. It set Q3 as the first chance at a positive organic EBITA quarter, against a broker expectation of 3% organic sales growth.

  • Organic line. Organic sales grew 11.1% in Q3 2026 against the broker's 3% and 0.7% in Q2 2026. Group organic EBITA grew 10.9%.
  • Group. Net sales SEK 657 million (+26.1%; broker SEK 629 million). EBITA SEK 101 million (+36.5%; broker SEK 90 million). EBITA margin 15.3% against 14.1% a year earlier and a forecast of 14.3%. Goodwill-adjusted diluted EPS SEK 5.0 (+42%; broker SEK 4.5).
  • Where the beat sits. Service and Maintenance EBITA was SEK 41 million against a forecast of SEK 29 million, at a 15.8% margin against 12.9% expected. Manufacturing EBITA was SEK 66 million at 16.6%, exactly as forecast. The segment's SEK 12 million beat is larger than the group's SEK 11 million beat: the whole surprise is one segment.
  • What drove that segment. Composite water-treatment projects at Ståthöga MA Teknik, with Triton improving. Management said on the call that these project volumes vary between quarters and have low near-term visibility.
  • Cash. Operating cash flow was SEK 38 million against EBITDA of SEK 116 million in Q3 2026, and SEK 170 million against SEK 330 million over January to September 2026 (about 52% on our arithmetic, which may not be the case's definition of cash conversion). Management named SEK 36 million of tax payments and a SEK 31 million rise in accrued, uninvoiced income. Inventory was SEK 506 million at 30 September 2026 against SEK 401 million a year earlier.
  • Balance sheet. Net debt fell SEK 21 million in the quarter to SEK 1,049 million; 2.5x EBITDA on the company's basis, 2.3x pro forma, and about 3.4x on the broker's basis for 2026. SEK 325 million of the SEK 670 million facility was undrawn.
  • Gross margin. 57.8%, down 100 basis points. Management attributes it to acquired mix (Nordbergs Tekniska, Trikåby: lower gross margin, higher EBITA margin); the broker's sector work attributes it to input and transport costs rising 5% to 15%. The review carries both and does not reconcile them.
  • Still weak. Interagro Skog and Culina Products.
  • Sell-side. The one sponsored broker raised adjusted EPS by 2% to 4%: SEK 18.1, SEK 19.5 and SEK 20.4 for 2026 to 2028 (from SEK 17.4, SEK 19.2 and SEK 20.1). Its fair-value range moved to SEK 310 to SEK 435 from SEK 320 to SEK 430: the top rose and the bottom fell, on lower peer multiples.
  • Price. SEK 321.0 on 13 November 2026, against the case's reference price of SEK 320.0. Three-month return -5.6%; relative-strength percentile 27.

2. The challenge

Reading one: the trigger was tripped by something the trigger was not built to measure. The case treats Service and Maintenance as a structural question "until a positive organic quarter answers it", and it made a positive organic EBITA quarter at any price up to about SEK 340 a sufficient condition for progress. It did not consider that such a quarter could arrive as project timing at a single subsidiary. That is what the evidence most plainly shows: the entire beat is one segment, the segment's driver is lumpy project work, management itself declines to extrapolate it, and uninvoiced income rose SEK 31 million in the same quarter, which is what revenue recognised ahead of billing looks like. On this reading Q3 answers nothing about whether the Q2 decline was structural; it adds a second noisy quarter of the opposite sign. For it to be right, Service and Maintenance organic growth excluding Ståthöga would have to be near flat, and the segment margin would have to fall back towards the 12.9% the broker expected once the projects complete. We think this reading is plausible and is the strongest one against taking the trigger at face value.

Reading two: the print the case was waiting for arrived, and the multiple did not move. The case's mechanism is that clearing the external concern lets the multiple return to 18x, at about 50% within eighteen months. It graded the clearing evidence low because it was "a survey and half an order book, not a print". There is now a print, the largest organic beat available, and three weeks later the shares stand SEK 1 above the case's reference price with relative strength lower than before, while the broker cut the floor of its range on peer multiples and the Riksbank raised its policy rate in autumn 2026 with two further rises anticipated in 2027. The strong form: the discount is not waiting on the organic cycle at all. It prices the vehicle (leverage on the broker's basis, minorities, one sponsored voice, thin trading) and the rate regime, so organic clearing is not the gate to the cleared branch. The case holds the rates cap and holds that the cheapness is only partly repaired; it does not hold the possibility that its own named catalyst can fire and be ignored. For this to be right, a second clean organic quarter would also have to leave the multiple near 14x to 15x. The weakness of the reading is that three weeks on about 7,500 shares a day is slow evidence, and that reading one explains the same silence more simply: the market may only be waiting for proof.

The reading that fails for now: a demand inflection arriving early. The case rated 'Demand Inflection' F and said the Swedish turn had not reached the company. An 11.1% organic print with the manufacturing purchasing managers' index at 60.5 in October 2026 invites the opposite view. It fails on where the growth is: Manufacturing, the half the index should lift first, was merely in line, and the review describes industrial customers delaying bulk purchases. An inflection would show there, and it does not yet.

3. What it does to the case

  • The progress trigger. Met in letter: a positive organic EBITA quarter with the price below about SEK 340. The other route (price at or below about SEK 305) is not met. Whether the letter carries the intent is exactly reading one.
  • 'Change Forces', external: demand and own cycle (both C). Less pressure, on one quarter of uncertain quality.
  • 'Financial Outputs': margin and net debt . Less pressure. The case's tests of leverage below 3.0x at year-end 2026 and a full-year margin near 14.0% look likely to be passed on the company's basis.
  • 'Financial Outputs': cash conversion . The same or slightly more pressure. The case's test of above 60% for 2026 is not visibly on track, and organic growth is absorbing working capital, which pulls against the case's assumption that its conditions are positively correlated.
  • Supply side . Slightly less pressure, unresolved, because the review gives two causes for the gross margin.
  • 'OSHQC CfC Clearing': clearing evidence . Pressed upward. The quality gate, which fails on return on capital and cash conversion, is untouched, so the setup's rating is under no new pressure.
  • 'Modal return' and the re-rating odds. The same. The earnings leg is marginally firmer; the price is where it was; reading two presses on the 18x branch.
  • 'Riskiness' . The same. The combination the case names is intact; the forming flag of a near-term cut has eased.
  • The odds on the organic turn led by Service and Maintenance (probable, about 60%). Pressure upward, but not confirmation.

4. What would settle it

  • The year-end report, which the case dates to early March 2027. It settles reading one if Service and Maintenance holds its margin and organic EBITA without a repeat of the project volumes, and if the uninvoiced income has turned into cash. This is the decisive evidence and it is about three and a half months away.
  • The second review, four working days on. It is unlikely to carry much. The single sponsored broker has already revised, and its 26 October and 12 November 2026 notes are in the evidence above. It can carry any drift in consensus and the price; it cannot carry Q4 trading, the invoicing of the accrued income, or a second voice unless one initiates.
  • Monthly and peer evidence in between. The Swedish manufacturing index, and whether Manufacturing-like peers report organic acceleration. A Manufacturing organic pick-up at Idun would revive the inflection reading.
  • What nothing committed will answer. Ståthöga's project backlog and the organic split of the Service and Maintenance beat by subsidiary. The company has not committed to disclose either, and management describes visibility as low. Reading one can therefore only be resolved indirectly, through the next segment print.
  • Reading two. Only time and a second clean quarter resolve it; a deal above the case's 8x line or leverage rising again would confirm the vehicle is what is priced.
