  2. dual-class share mechanics concentrate 50% voting power in CEO Kristo Käärmann, though a 10- year sunset clause and strict voting caps establish formal guardrails against absolute entrenchment.

Dual-class share structure and founder control mechanics

1.
Ten-year dual-class sunset: Wise established a 10-year sunset provision for its Class B voting shares effective from the May 2026 Jersey holdco reorganization and Nasdaq listing, ensuring all high-vote shares automatically expire and convert in May 2036. Disproportionate voting ratio: Class B shares carry nine votes per share with zero economic or dividend rights, compared to one vote per share for publicly traded Class A shares. CEO voting power cap: caps CEO Kristo Käärmann's voting rights at one vote below 50% of total eligible votes while he serves as CEO, which automatically reduces to one vote below 35% if he steps down. Non-CEO voting limits: restricts all non- CEO Class B shareholder groups to an aggregate voting cap of one vote below 35% of total eligible votes on any resolution. Non-transferable vote forfeiture: enforces immediate forfeiture and redemption of Class B voting rights upon any share transfer, death of the holder, or disposition of the underlying Class A share. Jersey holdco reorganization: inserted Jersey-incorporated Wise Group plc as the ultimate holding company via a Part 26 Scheme of Arrangement in May 2026 to facilitate a primary Nasdaq listing. Public co-founder dispute: faced public opposition from co-founder Taavet Hinrikus's vehicle Skaala Investments in July 2025 regarding the bundled voting on US dual-listing approvals and Class B voting extensions. Dissolution of founder concert party: confirmed with the UK Takeover Panel that Taavet Hinrikus and Kristo Käärmann no longer operate as a formal concert party following Hinrikus's 2021 board departure.
