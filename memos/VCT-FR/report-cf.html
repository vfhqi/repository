Section 1 – Leadership and Governance Change

Section summary
  1. family-control structures provide immense strategic stability but shield the group from activist intervention
  2. board refreshment successfully integrates independent ESG expertise while the core management team remains stable

Family control and capital allocation

1.
Dominant family control: the founding Merceron-Vicat family maintains absolute control by holding 63.4% of the group's outstanding share capital.
2.
Concentrated voting power: family voting rights reach 70.68% through holding companies Parfininco and Soparfi, creating a highly insulated governance structure.
3.
Deep executive ties: Chairman and CEO Guy Sidos is the son-in-law of Honorary Chairman Jacques Merceron- Vicat.
4.
Board family density: the 12-member Board of Directors includes five direct members of the founding family, limiting outside minority influence.
5.
Disciplined dividend commitment: the group paid a stable dividend of €2 per share in 2026, matching the 2025 payout.
6.
Conflict protection: independent annual statements confirm zero material conflicts of interest between family duties and public shareholder interests.

Executive transitions and culture shifts

7.
Orderly board refreshment: independent director Kristell Guizouarn joined the board and ESG committee in April 2025, succeeding Xavier Chalandon.
8.
Enhanced ESG focus: Guizouarn provides critical sustainable development expertise to navigate tightening European environmental regulations.
9.
Stable executive core: Chairman and CEO Guy Sidos continues alongside Deputy CEO and CFO Hugues Chomel.
10.
Investor relations alignment: Pierre Pedrosa joined as Head of Investor Relations in early 2024 to lead financial communications.
11.
Workforce retention initiatives: the group continues to execute its 271,497 free share allocation plan first implemented in 2021.
12.
Pension loss compensation: the free share plan is specifically designed to offset employee losses from supplementary pension plan changes.
13.
Compensation policy endorsement: the April 2026 general meeting approved Guy Sidos's executive compensation with an 87.75% favorable vote.
14.
topic-specific reports Other side of the story: extreme family voting concentration shields management; if capital preservation descends into capital stagnation, public shareholders could face structural underperformance.
