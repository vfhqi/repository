EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Earnings Outlook and Core Conclusions

Consensus EPS for this year and next is not stated in the underlying research, so the direction of earnings is inferred from loss-per-share and EBITDA-loss proxies. The best proxy for this year is a FY2026 consensus adjusted EBITDA loss of £62 million, which was materially worse than management's guided loss of £45 million to £55 million. For next year, Deutsche Bank expects the group to reach breakeven in fiscal year 2028, one year behind the company's 2027 target. The overall read is of a loss-making but fast-improving earnings profile, where the path to positive EPS depends on converting instrument placements into recurring consumables.
