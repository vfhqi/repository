Downstream Knock-on Effects

4.
Platform leverage expansion: If major digital service platforms successfully diversify into non-music surfaces like audiobooks, they will capture a larger share of consumer attention. Independent sector growth: Low-cost digital distribution services are allowing independent artists to bypass traditional record deals and retain their master rights. This structural shift has already caused the combined market share of the major labels to decline over the past decade. If this trend accelerates, independent creators will capture a larger margin pool at the expense of Universal's catalog dominance.
6.
Creator disintermediation risks: If digital streaming platforms control both the taste graph and generative models, discovery power will shift away from major labels. This shift would allow platforms to deal directly with independent creators, potentially bypassing traditional label contracts. Consequently, the role of record labels would narrow, turning them into scarce licensed catalog suppliers with significantly reduced long-term pricing power.
