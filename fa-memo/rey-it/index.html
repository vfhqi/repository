The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue growth. Organic growth is about 8.0% in H2 2026 (Q2 2026 was 8.0%; H2 2025 is an easy comparison at about 5%; Bernstein reported no slowdown in July). It is about 7.0% in H1 2027 and about 6.0% from H2 2027 to 2030. The step-down reflects the end of Italy's recovery-plan (PNRR) public spending peak, client AI productivity shared at contract renewal (about -0.5 to -1.0 points a year from 2027), and harder comparisons. Acquisitions add about 2.7 points in H2 2026 and 2.5 points in H1 2027: FedTec, the US federal and defence business bought on 14 July 2026, taken at about €50m to €60m of annual revenue, plus bolt-ons.
