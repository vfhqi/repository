Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Avanza demonstrates strong pricing power in deposit markets by passing through a high proportion of policy-rate cuts during monetary easing.
  2. High-friction transactional dynamics allow international peers like FinecoBank to maintain zero remuneration on core deposits throughout volatile interest rate cycles.
  3. Retained interest models face growing regulatory fair-value pressures in the UK, urging digital wealth platforms to share more cash yield with clients.

Section 2 – Pricing Power in Interest Rate Cycles and Deposit Pricing

Section summary
  1. Avanza's brokerage yield faces structural pressure from a less favorable customer and trading class mix.

Deposit pass-through in cutting cycles

1.
High deposit beta: Avanza demonstrated aggressive pricing power during Riksbank rate cuts starting in May 2024. Morgan Stanley’s report on May 14, 2024, highlights that Avanza fully passed through the initial 25 basis points cut to deposit accounts. This swift execution minimized interest expenses and protected net interest margins. Consequently, rapid rate adjustments allow Avanza to protect interest income during cuts, supporting earnings per share stability unless deposit flight accelerates.
2.
Stronger margin control: Avanza realized higher-than-expected savings in late 2024 by passing through large cuts to customers. DNB Carnegie’s note on November 15, 2024, showed that Avanza achieved a 75% to 80% passthrough rate on subsequent policy rate reductions. These proactive measures exceeded the broker's initial expectations of an 80% pass-through on a 50 basis points cut. This superior pass-through highlights that Avanza can control liability costs effectively during rapid central bank easing cycles.
