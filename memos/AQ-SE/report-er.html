Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. AQ Group enters its Q3 2026 earnings with exceptional operational momentum and a proven decentralized model, though its premium valuation raises the bar for future execution.
1.
Spun-out corporate foundation: AQ Group was established in October 1994 through the merger of Aros Kvalitetsplast and ABB Industrial Systems' transformer business, along with the accessories section of ABB Relays. The company is headquartered in Västerås, Sweden, and lists its shares on the Nasdaq Stockholm main market. Spanning four continents, the manufacturer has grown from its initial Swedish roots into a global contract engineering entity.

Specialized contract manufacturing: Operating within the industrial technology hardware and equipment sector, AQ Group manufactures custom components and complete systems for demanding blue-chip industrial customers. The manufacturer targets high-growth segments such as electrification, data centers, defense, and medical technology. Rather than selling proprietary products, the company focuses entirely on high-quality contract manufacturing according to customer specifications.
3.
Decentralized operating model: AQ Group utilizes a strictly decentralized organizational structure where more than 40 specialized operating companies run as independent entrepreneurial units. This local-forlocal setup places decision-making and customer relations close to the facilities, minimizing transportation and enhancing customer responsiveness. While this model increases operational agility, maintaining the internal corporate culture across global factories remains a constant focus for executive leadership.
4.
Unbroken profitability record: The group has achieved a remarkable 10-year track record of consistent earnings growth, delivering a 14% earnings per share compound annual growth rate. Since its inception in 1994, the company has successfully reported positive net profits every single quarter, spanning over 30 years of continuous operations. In fiscal year 2025, AQ Group surpassed net sales of SEK 9 billion for the first time, reinforcing its long-term financial stability.
5.
Inductive components driver: The high-margin Inductive Components business area has emerged as one of the fastest-growing divisions, expanding its revenue at a 30% compound annual growth rate from 2018 to 2024. This business now accounts for over one-fifth of group revenues, driven by secular demand in industrial drives, power transmission, and renewable energy. DNB Carnegie analysts highlight that this segment sets itself apart by providing high-value design input, transitioning the business from a basic component provider to a sophisticated solutions developer.
6.
Diverse technology segments: Beyond inductive components, AQ Group operates across specialized business areas including Wiring Systems, Sheet Metal Processing, and System Products. Wiring Systems delivers customized cable harnesses and electromechanical modules for demanding commercial vehicles and general engineering applications. Sheet Metal Processing offers complex fabrications, punching, and functional testing, while System Products coordinates the assembly of complete integrated systems.
7.
Resilient recent fundamentals: Over the last three fiscal years, AQ Group's net sales grew from SEK 5.47 billion in 2021 to SEK 8.55 billion in 2024, showing steady organic and acquired momentum. Profit margins before tax remained aligned with historical ambitions, averaging 8.2% from 2021 through 2024, despite severe supply chain disruptions and elevated working capital needs. This fundamental resilience prompted the board of directors in Q2 2026 to elevate its pre-tax profit margin target to above 10% from the historical 8% level.

Superior share outperformance: AQ Group's share price has demonstrated exceptional absolute strength, surging 43.30% over the last 12 months and 16.23% over the last 6 months prior to the Q3 2026 earnings release. On a relative basis, the stock outperformed the OMX Stockholm benchmark index by 31.08% over the past 12 months, 12.58% over 6 months, and 10.60% over the last 3 months. Section 16 – Conclusion

AQ Group's Q3 2026 earnings delivered an outstanding financial performance that significantly exceeded broker and consensus forecasts, with record organic growth of 17.9% driven by rapid expansion in US data center and defense segments, lifting EBT margins to the newly upgraded 10% target. This exceptional quarterly result fundamentally strengthens the company's 18-month to 3-year EPS trajectory, validating management's ambitious goal of doubling earnings per share every five years through a combination of structural secular tailwinds and disciplined capital deployment. The key strategic change underpinning this upgraded outlook is the remarkable acceleration of inductive components demand for data centers and electrification projects, which surged more than 60% year-on-year in Q3 2026 and now represents 6% of total group sales.

The timely acquisition of Norlake assets in Cleveland for USD 13.5 million in September 2026 effectively doubles AQ Group's US manufacturing capacity for these highmargin products, providing critical installed machinery to capture backlogged volumes when the Virginia facility reached its maximum annual sales capacity of USD 50 million. Combined with the board's decision to raise the long-term pre-tax profit margin target to above 10% from the historical 8% level–supported by thirteen consecutive quarters of outperformance–this positions the company to sustain enhanced profitability as technological complexity and design responsibility increase across its divisions.

However, the strongest counter-argument to this optimistic trajectory lies in the precarious balance between structural growth engines and persistent operational execution risks. While AQ Group's decentralized model has historically shielded the company from deep economic downturns, its high exposure to cyclical heavy commercial vehicles and European construction markets remains a significant systematic risk, and if industrial capital expenditure in Europe experiences a severe slowdown, the high-margin inductive components and sheet metal divisions could suffer from rapid destocking and underutilization.

The reported 10.0% EBT margin was partially flattered by temporary geographical utilization gains, as strong demand and high utilization in the US and Chinese inductive businesses helped offset normal holiday shutdowns in Europe, meaning that if the North American industrial segments experience a growth deceleration, the group's margins could contract below the 10% threshold due to persistent utilization deficits in Mexico and New York wire harness facilities.

Furthermore, newly acquired Time 24 brought annual revenues of approximately SEK 124 million but operated with profitability levels below the group average, and chief executive James Ahrgren noted that the target's operating performance fell short of expectations during its initial integration months, requiring experienced operations and finance experts from sister company AQ Rockford to improve purchasing productivity and delivery reliability.

The explicit metrics that would fundamentally alter this investment thesis center on four critical operational and financial indicators. First, sustained quarterly organic growth below 10% for two consecutive periods would signal that the data center and defense momentum has stalled prematurely. Second, EBT margins falling below 9.5% on a rolling four-quarter basis would indicate that the upgraded profitability target is unsustainable and that integration challenges or cyclical headwinds are overwhelming structural margin tailwinds.

Third, inventory turnover remaining below 3.0 turns for more than two quarters would suggest persistent delivery bottlenecks and working capital inefficiency that could constrain cash generation. Fourth, adjusted return on capital employed declining below 14% would demonstrate that the company's capital allocation discipline has deteriorated and that recent acquisitions are destroying rather than creating shareholder value.
