The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

Notes to Part 1 (bases). FY2027 revenue growth (-9.2%) is stated against FactSet's FY2026 revenue of 19,646, which includes the International unit (discontinued from June 2026); it lands on our FY2027 group revenue of 17,830. Like for like against the continuing FY2026 base of about 17,536, FY2027 is +1.7% (core, excluding TalkTalk, +0.3%). Revenue and EBITDA include TalkTalk and PlatformX from 5 October 2026 (consensus and company guidance exclude them). EBITDA is the company's adjusted EBITDA (before specific items). BT's adjusted earnings do not exclude the amortisation of acquired intangibles, so the depreciation and amortisation line is BT's full adjusted charge (FY2027 includes about 25 of TalkTalk depreciation, later years about 50). Net financial items are the adjusted net finance expense, including lease interest and excluding IAS 19 pension interest (a specific item of about 200 a year); cash interest paid runs about 130 below this charge. Tax rate is the adjusted effective rate (22.1% in FY2026; 22.8% in H1 FY2027); cash tax is far lower (about 90 in FY2027, 250 a year in FY2028 to FY2030, 300 in FY2031). Dividend per share is in GBP (8.55p, 8.85p, 9.2p, 9.6p, 10.1p) for the year declared. The share count change is employee share plans (about 30m shares a year).

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
