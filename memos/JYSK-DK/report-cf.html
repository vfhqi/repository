EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 36 findings

Jyske Bank presents a defensive Danish banking franchise executing a multi-year structural cost reduction strategy anchored by the DKK 150 million Bankdata IT migration scheduled for late 2027, while simultaneously pursuing capital-light wealth management expansion through the DKK 40 billion Formuepleje acquisition. The investment case pivots on management's ability to offset chronically weak ordinary lending growth of negative 0.3% annually since 2014 through elevated net interest income from policy rate support and accelerating adoption of the Jyske Frihed flexible mortgage product that captured DKK 6 billion within three months of launch. However, the bank operates from a structurally disadvantaged competitive position relative to Totalkredit-affiliated peers and displays the least ambitious Return on Tangible Book target of 10% among Danish SIFIs, while maintaining exceptional credit quality buffers of DKK 1.83 billion in post-model adjustments that provide four years of normalized loss coverage.
