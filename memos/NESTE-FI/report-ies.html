Neste (NESTE-FI): what the Q3 2026 results do to the existing case

These results weaken the existing case moderately on the leg the three-year return depends on, Rotterdam, while confirming its central claim that 2026 is a peak; the park stands, but one of the case's own invalidation thresholds has been crossed and the price has fallen through its eighteen-month re-open level.

What changed

The quarter beat on everything except the one number the case asked for.

  • Comparable EBITDA was EUR 1,555m for Q3 2026 against a consensus of EUR 1,202m. Comparable EPS was EUR 1.35 against EUR 1.00.
  • Renewable Products sales volume was a record 1.29 Mt against a consensus of 1.10 Mt, on utilisation of 97 percent. Management said plainly that 97 percent is not a baseline and that the sustainable target is in the low 80s.
  • The Renewable Products comparable sales margin was USD 1,068 a ton, below the consensus of USD 1,107 and below Q2 2026's USD 1,223, after a negative hedging effect the review puts at USD 180 a ton. The case's re-open condition was USD 1,100 or more. It was missed; the utilisation condition of 80 percent or more was met.
  • The Oil Products refining margin was USD 48.20 a barrel against a consensus of USD 37.64, with EUR 241m of inventory gains in the reported result.
  • Free cash flow was EUR 877m; leverage fell to 22.6 percent at September 2026 from 29.9 percent at June 2026; the cost programme reached a EUR 697m run-rate against a EUR 350m target; a new dividend policy pays 20 to 40 percent of EPS.
  • Rotterdam: the budget rose to about EUR 3.0 billion from EUR 2.5 billion, technical start-up moved to the end of 2027, and commercial volumes moved to 2028. New major projects beyond Rotterdam are paused.
  • The 2027 term-contract round has started slower than last year, and management calls 50 to 60 percent term cover a working assumption, not a target.

The sell-side raised the near years and cut the far ones. Morgan Stanley lifted 2026 EPS by 20 percent and 2027 by 13 percent and cut 2028 by 11 percent. Jefferies cut its 2027 renewable volume to 4.40 Mt from 5.00 Mt. DNB Carnegie moved Rotterdam's volumes wholly into 2028 and left 2027 to 2028 EPS flat, because higher utilisation of existing assets offsets the delay. Rothschild & Co Redburn downgraded to Neutral with a target of EUR 28.00 from EUR 36.00, projects a renewable margin of about USD 870 a ton in FY2027 and a refining margin of about USD 13 a barrel in 2027, and calls 2026 the peak.

The challenge

We find three readings the case does not hold, and they do not all point the same way.

1. The volume leg was never only Rotterdam. The case treats growth as a one-project plan and the operator as unproven on utilisation. One quarter at 97 percent, a Porvoo turnaround finished on schedule and a cost programme at twice its target say the existing plants hold more earnings than the case counted. DNB Carnegie's flat 2027 to 2028 EPS is this reading in numbers: utilisation replaces the delayed line. If it is right, the internal change explains more of the earnings than the case allowed, and the case's objection to its own primary pattern, that the upgrade came from outside, softens. For it to be right, utilisation after the Rotterdam and Singapore turnarounds has to settle well above the low 80s management itself names, and the record of outages or delayed starts in 10 of the past 20 quarters has to stop. Management's own guidance argues against it, which is why we rate it possible and not probable. It is still a reading the case cannot dismiss, because the case's utilisation alarm sits at 80 percent and the company has just shown what the assets do above it.

2. Rotterdam now arrives into the trough instead of ahead of it, and the eighteen-month case has lost its volume leg. The case weighed Rotterdam as a timing risk: a slip costs about EUR 0.4 of 2028 EPS and takes the multiple from 14.0x to 12.5x. The results admit a harsher reading. Commercial volumes in 2028 fall after the case's eighteen-month horizon is nearly over, so that return now rests on margin and re-rating alone. And the line starts selling in the years the case itself expects competing capacity to land and the margin to settle at USD 800. Capital cost per ton is now put at EUR 2,300, nearly double Singapore's, so the same line earns less on more capital. The case treated margin and Rotterdam as independent; on this reading the delay raises exposure to the margin outcome, because the mix shift toward renewables that justifies 14.0x is later and dearer. For it to be right, the end-2027 start-up has to hold no better than the last date did, and the 2027 to 2028 capacity additions have to arrive as the case expects. Both are plausible.

3. The margin print understates what the assets earned, and the contract round is no longer the clean test the case assumed. Adding back the USD 180 a ton gives about USD 1,248 a ton before hedging, which is our arithmetic and above Q2. If the hedges roll off, the realised margin in 2027 could hold above the case's USD 900 even as spot falls. Against that, management expects a negative hedging effect again in Q4 2026, and the review is not consistent about the USD 180: it attributes it to hedging in two places and to feedstock costs in another. If it is feedstock, it does not roll off. Separately, the case assumed about 60 percent of 2027 volume would be struck in Q4 2026 at a known price. A slow round with buyers waiting means either lower term cover or later pricing, and so more of 2027 exposed to a falling diesel component. That widens the 2027 margin range in both directions.

One obvious alternative fails. The bull reading that the market is mispricing a structural step as a peak is weaker after these results, not stronger: Redburn, which the case cited among its holders, has left it.

What it does to the case

  • 'Investment Thesis' , the return binding: more pressure on the three-year leg, because the modal path assumed Rotterdam within 2027. Partly offset on price: 27.62 EUR at 2026-11-13 is below the case's 30.2 EUR eighteen-month clearing level and above its 25.3 EUR three-year level. The re-open at or below 30 EUR was conditional on the modal path being intact, and it is not.
  • The multiple branches: the case's 42 percent branch at 14.0x needed both conditions. The Rotterdam condition has failed on commercial volumes; pressure runs toward the 12.5x and 11.0x branches.
  • The case's invalidation list: capex above EUR 2,500m is crossed. "No start-up in 2027" is not crossed on technical start-up. The alarm of a dated slip into 2028 is met for commercial volumes. The Q3 margin limb, below USD 1,000, is not met.
  • 'Foundations' , operator: two-sided. More pressure on projects, less on cost, balance sheet and one quarter of utilisation. About the same.
  • 'Investment Setup' , 'Major Corporate Change': slightly less pressure on the internal-change objection; the pattern's own outputs stay open.
  • 'Investment Case' and 'Case Riskiness' : more pressure. The one-project plan is later and dearer, and the price is 2.9 percent below its 200-day average.
  • Top-down readings: Technical Momentum is now pinned C against the A the case carried. Sell-side Momentum stays A, with the two-sided cycle the case predicted now visible in the 2028 cuts.

What would settle it

  • The second review, four working days on, will carry where consensus EPS and targets settle after the remaining broker updates. It will not carry the contract round, post-turnaround utilisation or any Rotterdam milestone. It resolves how far the far-year cuts go, and nothing in the three readings.
  • The 2027 contract round: management expects activity to pick up toward the end of the year. The company has not committed to disclosing a price or a cover level, so the case's USD 1,000 test may go unanswered.
  • Utilisation after the turnarounds, in the first reports of 2027. This settles the first reading.
  • Rotterdam: whether the EUR 3.0 billion and end-2027 hold. The review names no dated interim milestone, so this is likely to stay open through 2027.
  • Q4 2026 hedging and feedstock disclosure settles what the USD 180 a ton was.
