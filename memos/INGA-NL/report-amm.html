What the company does

ING Groep is a large Dutch bank, market value 91,716 million euros at 2 October 2026, earning most of its income from the gap between what it pays on deposits and what it earns on loans and hedging swaps, with net fee income as the second leg. It runs retail banking in the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Spain and other European markets, 15.4 million mobile primary customers at the end of 2025, and a wholesale bank, with the capital mix moving to 45% Wholesale and 55% Retail by 2027.
