Corporate overview – sector agnostic: Sweden-based Röko is an investment company and serial acquirer, or compounder, that acts as a perpetual owner of European small- to medium-sized niche businesses. Founded in April 2019 by Tomas Billing and CEO Fredrik Karlsson, the firm is headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden, and focuses on acquiring businesses with a track record of continuous profit growth. The company's investment philosophy focuses on asset-light, highly profitable entities, typically targeting acquisition multiples of approximately 7x EBITA. This disciplined capital allocation strategy has allowed Röko to expand its footprint rapidly since inception.
