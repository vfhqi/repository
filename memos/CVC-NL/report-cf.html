EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Materiality of Change

CVC Capital Partners is executing a transformational platform diversification from pure-play European private equity into a multi-asset global alternative manager, with non-PE strategies now representing >55% of feepaying AUM (€153bn total) compared to under 50% at IPO. The $1.2bn Marathon Asset Management acquisition (closed July 2026) adds $24bn in US credit AUM spanning asset-backed lending, real estate credit, and structured credit, fundamentally expanding geographic and product reach beyond European direct lending. Inorganic scaling via DIF Capital Partners (€15bn infrastructure FPAUM) and Glendower/CVC Secondary Partners (SOF VI at $9.3bn) establishes top-three European positioning in infrastructure and secondaries alongside core buyout leadership. Private wealth distribution represents a structural business model shift, with aggregate evergreen AUM expanding 4x year-on-year to €6.7bn across PE, Credit, Secondaries, and Infrastructure vehicles, supported by 60+ dedicated personnel versus five at IPO. Leadership transition remains orderly but material, with CFO Fred Watt retiring September 2026 after 20 years, succeeded by former Bank of Cyprus CEO John Hourican bringing 30 years of banking leadership.

Recent Delivery (H1 2026)

H1 2026 demonstrated strong operating leverage with Fee-Paying AUM up 9% YoY to €153bn, FRE up 11% YoY to €442m (57% margin), and adjusted EPS up 11% YoY to €0.40, beating consensus by +5.3%. Exit execution accelerated sharply with LTM signed realizations reaching €23.8bn (+79% YoY) and H1 2026 realizations of €11.5bn (+19% YoY), delivering 2.8x gross MOIC and 21% gross IRR while returning 33% more capital to PE LPs than called over 4.5 years.

Capital formation remained robust with H1 2026 gross inflows of €11bn and LTM deployment of €25.9bn, while private wealth AUM expanded 4x YoY to €6.7bn demonstrating broad-based demand across all four client strategies. Management reaffirmed all forward guidance including aggregate PRE of €600-700m over 2026-2027 and €5bn embedded carry potential, while executing progressive capital allocation via €275m interim dividend (+12% YoY) and €194m of €350m buyback completion.

Forward Credibility

CVC operates as an honest-tracker management archetype with HIGH credibility for fee-related earnings (MFE margins consistently within 55%-60% guided corridor) and MEDIUM credibility for performance fee timing due to external M&A exit dependency, following conservative March 2026 PRE guidance reset. Base case scenario assumes 10% organic FPAUM CAGR to €200bn by 2028, Fund X activation at €27bn in Q2 2028, and PRE recovery to €650m in 2028, yielding FY28 EPS of €1.30 (17% CAGR) with robust downside margin of safety from stable MFE covering 100% of overhead. Historical delivery validates credibility with 100% MFE beat ratio vs guidance over L3Y, FY24 EBITDA of €966m beating consensus €881m, and FY25 EBITDA of €1,091m beating consensus €1,087m, supported by 57% FRE margin profile.

Superior DPI execution returning 33% net positive capital to LPs (€56bn returned vs €42bn deployed) with €46bn cumulative PE realizations at 3.2x gross MOIC and 23% gross IRR establishes exceptional Fund X fundraising credibility.

Sell-Side Positioning

Sell-side coverage comprises four primary brokers and eight named analysts (Deutsche Bank, Morgan Stanley, Rothschild & Co Redburn, ING) with overwhelmingly bullish 83% Buy/Overweight rating mix and zero Sell recommendations, consensus median price target at €17.20 (range €15-€23.70). Valuation methodologies rely on triangulated SOTP frameworks separating annuity-like MFE from volatile carry, with Deutsche Bank applying 22.5x multiple to 2027 FRE (10% peer discount), Morgan Stanley 10x P/E to 2026 PRE, and Redburn 21.8x target P/E to average 2025-2026 EPS, justified by 57% FRE margins and 100bps management fee yield.

Bull thesis centers on multi-asset diversification (Deutsche Bank 18% EPS CAGR through 2029), wealth channel scaling (Morgan Stanley perpetual fee streams), and LP alignment via deal-level negative carry (Redburn 19% EPS compounding 2025-2027 supporting €21 target). Hold/bear arguments focus on delayed PRE realization timing into 2028-2029 (ING downgrade), elevated near-term cost growth from wealth hiring capping FRE margin expansion, flagship Fund X fundraising scale challenges, record industry dry powder inflating asset prices, and three-to-five-year post-IPO lock-up overhang.

Fundamental Peer Position

CVC ranks top quartile for EBITDA margin with H1 2026 adjusted margin of 62.8% (second only to KKR's 70.3%), outperforming European peer EQT's 60% and sector median of 58%, while delivering 9% YoY organic fee revenue growth matching FPAUM expansion and beating Partners Group's -4% contraction. Consensus EPS revisions stabilized with L1M inflecting +1% recovering from L3M cuts of -6% and L12M cuts of -19% (reflecting industrywide realization delays comparable to Partners Group's -21%), with adjusted EPS of €0.40 in H1 2026 (+11% YoY) beating consensus €0.38 and Deutsche Bank raising 2026-2029 FPAUM and FRE CAGRs to 12% and 11% despite CVC trading at 16.1x 2026E P/E (14% discount to peer median).

Pricing power remains unrivaled with 1.0% blended management fee yield significantly outperforming European peer medians near 85bps (PE yield 1.3%, Credit 0.8%, Infrastructure 0.9%), while non-PE platforms expanded 19% YoY to >55% of FPAUM and Marathon acquisition positions CVC as top-three European multi-asset manager alongside EQT and Intermediate Capital Group. Dominated exit distributions with €46bn returned cumulatively to PE LPs over four years generate industry-leading DPI supporting fundraising market share gains.
