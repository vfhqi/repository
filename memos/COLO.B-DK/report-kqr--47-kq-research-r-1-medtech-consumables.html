Part 3: Existential Threats to the Group

While the structural drivers are favorable, stock pickers must remain cautious of several severe sector-wide risks. 1. Systematic US Import Tariffs and Trade Friction: Protectionist policies threaten gross margins across the sector. For instance, Bonesupport pays a 10% tariff on US imports, Vitrolife faces up to 15% on US imports of European medical devices and 25% on Chinese devices, and Surgical Science estimates a 15% tariff rate incurs an annual impact of SEK 15 million. Furthermore, potential Section 232 investigations could impose sweeping tariffs on bionics and prosthetics, severely impacting Embla Medical, which relies on Mexican production and Chinese outsourcing. 2. US Medicare Bidding and Outpatient Reimbursement Cuts: The proposed US Medicare DMEPOS Competitive Bidding Program (CBP) represents a major pricing threat to ostomy, continence, and chronic care, with potential price cuts of 30% at the distributor level and 15% for manufacturers. Outpatient clinical reimbursement revisions (such as those impacting wound care biologics) can also disrupt high-margin clinical portfolios. 3. Hospital Personnel Shortages and Procurement Budgets: Severe hospital nursing and technician staffing shortages remain the primary barrier limiting elective orthopedic surgical volumes. Additionally, pressure on hospital procurement budgets has lengthened sales cycles for capital equipment, creating near-term revenue volatility for simulation and hardware placements. - Earnings Calls 4. Regulatory Bottlenecks, Quality Failures, and Sourcing Yields: Compliance challenges can lead to costly remediation or production caps (e.g., Getinge's Cardiosave Cardiopulmonary drag, Convatec's quality remediation ). Sourcing biological donors is highly variable and can lead to massive inventory writedowns (Tissue Regenix ). 5. Software-Only and Consumer AI Disruptions: For high-end assistive communication (AAC) devices, rapid advances in consumer tablets and AI voice software could allow cheaper, software-only direct-to-consumer alternatives to bypass clinical funding channels (threatens Tobii Dynavox).

In response, there is an accelerating sub-sector rotation toward operating room efficiency solutions that reduce procedure time, nursing labor, and clinical steps:

Medacta's single-use, pre-sterilized joint sets (GMK Efficiency) eliminate clinical reprocessing labor, saving hospitals up to $994 per case. Draegerwerk and B. Braun's Silent ICU and SDC connected devices automate clinical alarm suppression, mitigating intensive care staffing alarm fatigue. Ambu's single-use endoscopes reduce hospital reprocessing delays, optimizing clinical throughput.
