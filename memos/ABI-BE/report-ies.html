AB InBev (ABI-BE): what the third-quarter 2026 results do to the existing case

These results confirm the earnings leg of our case and weaken the case for the shares at this price, materially on the return and not at all on the earnings: the company beat, the estimates barely moved, and the shares now stand about 13 percent above the price at which the case already judged the return insufficient.

1. What changed

  • The print (reported 29-Oct-26, quarter to 30-Sep-26). Organic volume +0.4 percent (consensus +0.3 percent); organic revenue +4.9 percent (consensus 4.1 percent); revenue per hectolitre +4.2 percent; organic EBIT +8.0 percent with 0.9 percent of organic margin expansion on Bernstein's reading; underlying earnings per share 1.21 USD, up 22 percent, against a 1.15 USD consensus. Net finance costs fell to 908 million USD from 1,165 million USD. The interim dividend is 0.40 EUR against 0.15 EUR.
  • Against what the case assumed. Everything the earnings leg needs was delivered: growth inside the guided range, price and mix above the case's 4 percent marker, the second-half margin step the case expected, and the below-EBITDA cascade working through the interest line.
  • Where the profit came from. Middle Americas: revenue +12.1 percent, EBITDA +15.6 percent, a 50.5 percent margin, 41 percent of group EBITDA. South America: EBITDA +13.8 percent. The other three regions shrank in profit: North America EBITDA minus 3.5 percent, EMEA minus 1.4 percent, Asia Pacific minus 22.4 percent.
  • China. Volumes fell 16.8 percent against a consensus of minus 7.0 percent, in a market down 10.5 percent (Bernstein); China EBITDA fell 30.5 percent (Berenberg). The case set its alarm at worse than minus 8 percent. It was breached twice over.
  • The case's two reopening triggers at this print both failed. China was not flat or better, and North American volumes fell 2.5 percent against the case's test of US depletions above +1 percent.
  • Brazil. Beer volume +0.5 percent against industry growth of about 4 percent, with Heineken growing mid-single digits (Jefferies, 12-Nov-26). Morgan Stanley and the company read the same quarter as share gain, with sell-in lagging sell-out. The case had Brazil as the stable driver.
  • Guidance. At the September 2026 capital markets day the company replaced its medium-term 4 to 8 percent organic EBITDA growth with 5 to 9 percent organic EBIT growth; the 2026 year stays on the old range. The case records guidance as unraised in five years and a lift as possible, not probable, at the February 2027 call.
  • The sell-side. Bernstein: target 97 EUR from 96 EUR, FY2026 earnings per share trimmed 0.4 percent to 4.42 USD, FY2027 raised 0.8 percent to 4.88 USD. Deutsche Bank: target 81 EUR from 80 EUR, FY2026 raised 2.1 percent to 4.43 USD, FY2027 4.71 USD, FY2028 5.14 USD, driven by below-the-line items and currency. Jefferies: Buy, 86 EUR. The shares sold off on the day; Bernstein read it as profit-taking after a 28 percent year-to-date rise. Morgan Stanley (3-Nov-26) puts lagged commodity inflation at a 110 basis point margin drag in 2027 against 50 basis points in 2026.
  • The price. 76.94 on the home exchange on 13-Nov-26, against the case's 68.3 EUR and its re-look level of about 60 EUR. The multiple is 16.4x at the 61st percentile of the stock's ten years, against 14.8x at the 28th when the case was written.

Two figures in the evidence do not reconcile and we lean on neither: the quarter's absolute normalised EBITDA (3.02 billion USD beside a 6.00 billion USD consensus in the same table), and end-2026 leverage, given as 2.4x, 2.5x and 2.7x by three brokers.

2. The challenge

Reading one: the re-rating the case said it could not have is already happening, and for a reason the case ruled out. The case put the re-rating branch at about 30 percent and made it conditional on China stabilising. Since then the multiple has moved from 14.8x to 16.4x, out of the case's modal 14 to 16x branch and above the 15.8x the case conceded only if volumes stayed positive, in the same weeks that China fell 16.8 percent. On this reading the market is paying for the EBIT algorithm, the dividend step and the interest line, and has stopped charging for China because a group that grows EBIT 8.0 percent with China's profit down 30.5 percent has shown China does not matter to it. The case was right about the earnings and wrong about what the multiple needed. For this to be right, the 5 to 9 percent must be a genuine lift, the company must deliver in the upper half of it, and the sector must not drag the stock back. If it is right, the case's 60 EUR re-look level is a price we are unlikely to see, and the park has the shape the case itself named: a price that moves against the park.

Reading two: the guidance "lift" is a change of unit, and the multiple has run ahead of nothing. Jefferies' own arithmetic is that EBIT now grows one to two points faster than EBITDA because capital spending has fallen. Five to nine percent on EBIT is then four to eight percent on EBITDA, restated. Sell-side behaviour fits this reading better than the first: a 5 percent earnings beat produced a 0.4 percent cut at one broker, a currency-driven raise at another, and a net one-month revision of minus 1 percent. If this is right, the company still beats and holds, the mis-modelled line is closed, and 16.4x is a premium with less under it than 14.8x had. It needs only that 2027 guidance, when given, sits where the old range would have put it, and that Morgan Stanley's 110 basis points of lagged cost arrives.

Readings one and two cannot both be right, and the evidence in hand does not choose between them.

Reading three: the earnings leg is narrower than the case says. The case holds that the earnings do not need volume, and rests its answer to the "false friend" hypothesis on Latin America. This quarter the group's profit growth came from two regions while three shrank, and one region, at a 50.5 percent margin, is now 41 percent of EBITDA. Inside the other growing region, Brazil's beer volume grew 0.5 percent in a market growing about 4 percent while a funded challenger took the premium end. Read this way, the mechanical compounder is a concentrated position in Mexican and Colombian pricing power, and the Brazilian duopoly the case calls rational and stable is where the false-friend reading would first show. It requires the Jefferies share reading to be the true one and to persist; if the sell-in and sell-out reading is right, it fails.

The reading we do not put up: that China is now clearing because inventories were corrected ahead of the fourth quarter. A destock explains shipments falling faster than the market; it does not explain a market down 10.5 percent or home consumption earning a lower margin than nightlife. The case's structural view stands on this evidence.

3. What it does to the case

  • 'Modal return' and the pinned TSR/Valuation reading: more pressure, and the most in the piece. Our path for FY2027 is 4.88 USD; Bernstein's new number is 4.88 USD, and Deutsche Bank's FY2028 sits below ours. Earnings did not rise; the price did. On the case's own arithmetic a price about 13 percent higher on an unchanged path leaves roughly 3 percent a year if the multiple returns to the case's 15.0x, below the 4.3 percent a year the case itself marked as an F. The ratings store has already re-based this reading from D to F, and the pinned valuation letter has gone from C to D.
  • 'Investment Thesis' : more pressure, because the case states that a 'Modal return' at F takes this Building Block to D.
  • 'Re-rating' and 'Limited multiple risk' : more pressure. The fall to the case's 13.3x trough band is now about 19 percent, not 9 percent.
  • 'Guidance raised' , 'Proven upgrader' , the 'Compounder' fit (C, with B live) and the Setup Building Block : less pressure if reading one holds, the same if reading two holds. The case's second reopening trigger has either fired five months early or not fired at all.
  • 'Staircase' : more pressure. The one step the case called novel has been spent, and the price moved with it.
  • 'Demand' , 'Cockroach' and the 'OSHQC CfC Clearing' fit : more pressure. China's alarm level is crossed and Brazil is a new probable problem beside it.
  • 'Competition' and the 'Latin American duopolies' driver: more pressure, pending the Brazil share dispute.
  • 'No deceleration' : more pressure, short of a grade. Organic revenue growth 4.9 percent against 5.7 percent in the first half; Beyond Beer +19 percent; marketplace value +45 percent.
  • 'Below EBIT' , 'Foundations' and the earnings Key Drivers: the same, confirmed.
  • The pins. Sell-side momentum A to C, which is where our own reading already sat; technical momentum C to B; thematic B to A.

4. What would settle it

  • The second review, four working days on. It probably carries the full set of post-print broker estimates and targets, so it should show whether the minus 1 percent revision is a pre-print trim or the sell-side's considered answer to a beat, and which Brazil share reading the brokers settle on. It should also fix the EBITDA and leverage figures that do not reconcile. It carries no new company disclosure: nothing on China's fourth-quarter shipments, nothing on Brazil share from the company, nothing on 2027.
  • Raise or restatement. The full-year results, which the case dates to February 2027: whether 2026 lands in the upper half of 4 to 8 percent, and whether 2027 is guided on EBIT at a level the old range would not have reached. The company has not committed to giving an EBITDA equivalent.
  • China. Fourth-quarter 2026 and first-quarter 2027 shipments. The review's own test is whether destocking runs beyond two consecutive quarters.
  • Brazil. Monthly industry production data and Ambev's fourth quarter; the strength-linked tax bill is due by 31-May-27 and bears on 2028.
  • The profit contribution of the BEES marketplace. Not disclosed and not committed to. Nothing on the calendar answers it.
