Section 2 – Price, Coverage, and Capacity Feedback Loops

Section summary
  1. Lowering net prices in the United States triggers a reinforcing feedback loop that expands Medicare and commercial insurance coverage, driving massive volume growth.
  2. Novo Nordisk's aggressive manufacturing scaling faces short-term utilization headwinds, while Eli Lilly's massive greenfield expansion secures a compounding capacity advantage.
