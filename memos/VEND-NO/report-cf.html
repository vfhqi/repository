EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 26 findings

Vend Marketplaces ASA emerged in 2024 from the structural separation of Schibsted's digital marketplaces business, culminating in the formal brand transition to "Vend" on 12 May 2025. The spinoff dismantled a historically complex corporate structure where management attention was divided between high-margin classifieds and low-margin news media operations. Revenue growth is projected to accelerate from 4% in 2026 to 13% in 2027 and 2028, while adjusted EBITDA margin is forecast to expand from 39% in 2026 to 49% by 2028.

This pure-play marketplaces entity now operates four core Nordic verticals–Mobility, Real Estate, Jobs, and Recommerce–anchored by dominant horizontal portals including FINN in Norway, Blocket in Sweden, Oikotie in Finland, and DBA in Denmark. The company's forward trajectory depends on executing aggressive costreduction targets, successfully completing the Aurora technology platform consolidation, and navigating persistent dealer pricing resistance in Sweden following a challenging platform migration in late 2025.

The fundamental investment case centers on self-help margin expansion rather than cyclical tailwinds, as listing volumes across key verticals face persistent contraction–residential-for-sale listings in Norway decreased 3% in Q2 2026, Swedish mobility classifieds revenue declined 7% due to private segment volume pressure, and jobs classifieds revenue rose only 5% as a 15% ARPA increase offset a 9% volume decline. Consolidated EBITDA margin of 39% remains well below the peer group median of 59%, creating substantial medium-term expansion potential but also highlighting the company's current operational discount relative to established pure-play competitors. Strong dealer reactions following the Swedish Blocket platform migration forced a postponement of pricing adjustments, introducing near-term execution risk that has prompted sell-side analysts to adopt a cautious stance despite acknowledging the company's dominant market positions and long-term structural advantages.

Materiality of Change

1.
Strategic restructuring has fundamentally altered the operating model through portfolio rationalization, technology consolidation, and capital allocation pivots. Vend completed three major divestments in 2025-2026: the sale of Lendo Group to Clar Global AB on 10 March 2026 for a gain of NOK 503 million, the skilled trades portfolio to Verdane Fund Manager AB on 15 January 2026 for NOK 298 million, and the Prisjakt Group to eEquity on 13 June 2025 for NOK 298 million. The central business initiative is migrating all regional brands onto a single, consolidated tech stack called the Aurora platform, with the company completing the migration of Blocket in Sweden to Aurora in November 2025, leaving only Bilbasen in Denmark scheduled for 2027. Total postseparation shareholder distributions have reached NOK 28.0 billion as of early 2026, with the first NOK 2 billion tranche of a NOK 4 billion buyback program initiated on 30 April 2026. The magnitude of structural change is material, fundamentally resetting the company's margin trajectory, capital intensity, and operational focus from a conglomerate structure to a pure-play classifieds operator
