Facilities management and public service outsourcing models exhibit varying degrees of fixed-cost operating leverage depending on their delivery model:

1.
Subcontracted / Asset-Light Model: High variable costs (subcontractor fees, agency labor) and low fixed costs; highly defensive during volume downturns but exhibits low operating leverage in up-cycles. Self-Delivery / Engineering Field Force Model ("Mitie First"): Higher fixed and semi-fixed cost burden (salaried engineers, fleet, regional branch footprint, technology stack); creates meaningful operational leverage during volume expansions but exposes margins during volume declines or wage spikes.
