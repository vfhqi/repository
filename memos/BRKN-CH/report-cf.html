EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

  1. the poenina merger and ongoing bolt-on M&A have materially transformed Burkhalter into a high-margin, full-service Swiss building technology champion
  2. top forward EPS drivers are decarbonization demand, process digitalization, and systematic M&A execution in highly fragmented local markets
  3. recent delivery remains excellent, highlighted by FY2025 EPS growing 7.23% to CHF 5.78 with a massive gross dividend of CHF 5.20
  4. forward guidance credibility is high, as the management acts as a classic sandbagger with highly predictable and achievable targets
  5. sell-side consensus is increasingly constructive on the compounder model, though J. Safra Sarasin and UBS maintain distinct valuation premiums
  6. Burkhalter holds a premium peer-momentum rank, trading at a valuation premium reflecting its superior cash conversion and lower leverage
  7. there is no active activist presence in the AlphaSense library currently targeting the company's capital allocation or board structure

  3. digital disruption is accelerating via BIM integration and ERP migrations to optimize multi-trade workflows.
  4. regulatory shifts including second-home tax reforms will accelerate renovation activity ahead of the 2028 tax deduction deadline.
  5. acute shortages of technical specialists constrain capacity and remain the primary bottleneck to organic volume growth.
  6. construction cycles show robust structural refurbishment demand offsetting weaker private new-build activity.
  7. favorable macro conditions provide strong currency stability and low Swiss inflation with manageable material costs.
