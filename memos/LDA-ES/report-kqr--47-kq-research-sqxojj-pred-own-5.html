Section summary

Private Exposure

Extraordinary Versus Standard Risks

1.
Significant mitigation: The Spanish Insurance Compensation Consortium protects private property insurers by absorbing peak catastrophic losses from extraordinary natural disasters. This unique state-backed pool is funded through premium surcharges levied on all private Spanish policies. However, private property underwriters remain directly responsible for standard claims that do not reach the strict legal definition of extraordinary events. Therefore, this dual structure creates a sharp divide between catastrophic governmentbacked coverage and standard atmospheric retail exposures.
2.
Restricted boundary: The consortium only activates coverage when wind speeds exceed one hundred twenty kilometers per hour or when tornadoes occur. Ordinary storms with lower wind speeds must be covered entirely by private property policies. Similarly, flooding claims are only covered by the consortium when water enters properties from the ground rather than through leaky roofs. Consequently, these operational boundaries leave local property insurers highly exposed to moderate weather.
