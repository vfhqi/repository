1.
Undervalued public peers: Listed European business services currently trade at historically depressed levels compared to their private counterparts. Morgan Stanley's industry report of 11 May 2026 reveals that listed European business services trade at an average forward EV/EBITDA multiple of just 9.1x. This low valuation means listed companies are highly susceptible to opportunistic takeovers because their trading multiples remain well below historical ranges. This discount could ultimately depress public EPS unless corporate boards execute aggressive defensive share buybacks.

Morgan Stanley's strategy paper of 11 May 2026 notes that unlisted business-services acquisitions have cleared at an average of 12.0x EV/EBITDA since 2020.
