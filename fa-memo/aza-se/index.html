Revenue growth is against the same period a year earlier. This company uses the operating profit form: a financial company (G. Financials; G.5. Digital investment and trading platforms). Operating profit is after depreciation and amortisation, except the amortisation of acquired intangibles, as adjusted earnings are. For banks and insurers, interest is part of the business, so operating profit is normally close to pre-tax profit.

Notes on Part 1. Revenue is the company's operating income (FY2025 base SEK 4,495m, the basis consensus uses), not FactSet's standardised revenue of SEK 5,737m, which includes gross interest and must not be used. Operating profit equals the company's operating profit and its reported pre-tax profit (no credit losses since 2011, no interest-bearing debt). "Net financial items" carries the coupon on SEK 1.3bn of perpetual Additional Tier 1 notes (STIBOR plus 3.25% on SEK 800m and plus 2.85% on SEK 500m), which under IFRS is charged to equity below pre-tax profit and reduces earnings attributable to ordinary shareholders; it is entered here so that the model's EPS is on the shareholder basis (about SEK 60m in 2026 at an average STIBOR near 1.9%, about SEK 70m a year from 2027 at STIBOR 2.25%; the coupon is not tax-deductible, so the model's tax on it overstates net income by about SEK 10m a year, which is immaterial). The tax rate is low because most of Avanza Pension's income bears yield tax charged to policyholders rather than corporate tax; 15% is the outer-year alternative if the bank's share of profit rises. The dividend is 75% of base-case EPS (policy at least 70%; 76% paid for 2025). The share-count change is warrant dilution of about 0.4% a year, capped at 1.6% across live programmes.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
