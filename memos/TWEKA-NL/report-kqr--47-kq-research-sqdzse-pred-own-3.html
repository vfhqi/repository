Tire building cyclicality: The market assumes the Automated Machinery segment will remain a stable cash generator to fund Automation's buy-and-build strategy. In reality, capital expenditure reluctance among Tier 1 tire manufacturers has severely depressed short-term demand. Jefferies reported in August 2026 that Automated Machinery's order book decreased 38% due to delayed order intake. This cyclical downturn will likely place Automated Machinery under pressure and reduce group cash flows.

Competitor pricing pressure: Standalone cable operations are expected to benefit from favorable European demand trends. However, global overcapacity in commodity and fiber optic cables, particularly from Chinese manufacturers, could trigger severe price competition. Management warned in its 2025 annual report that overcapacity has put pressure on selling prices and margins. Such aggressive pricing would erode Electrification's standalone profitability and dilute its margins.

Other side of the story: Despite the clear operational risks and cost overruns, the structural separation is highly likely to unlock significant shareholder value. Re-baselining segment reporting ahead of the separation has already allowed brokers to value the distinct entities on a standalone basis. Jefferies increased its SOTPbased price target by 12% to €65.0 in August 2026, reflecting the standalone value of Electrification. This strategic pivot will narrow the conglomerate discount and drive long-term earnings expansion.
