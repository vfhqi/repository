81.
Analytical Summary:

FMC's computational "population impact model" suggests that the utilization of GLP-1 receptor agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors will have a highly balanced impact on the dialysis market.

82.
Impact Mechanism:

Although these medications delay the progression of chronic kidney disease (CKD), they also significantly reduce cardiovascular mortality (the leading cause of death in late-stage CKD patients), thereby keeping patients alive longer and sustaining the overall dialysis candidate pool over the next decade.

83.
Nordic Care Operator Contrast:

The research findings do not support any material impact from dialysis volumes versus weight-loss drugs on social care operators (Ambea, Attendo, Humana), as they operate strictly as social, disabled, and elderly care providers rather than clinical nephrology or metabolic disease specialists.
