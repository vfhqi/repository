B.1 Business Description 9 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What the company does. Alfa Laval designs and manufactures heat exchangers, separators, fluid handling and marine equipment across three end-market divisions, Energy, Ocean and Food and Pharma, serving industrial, marine and process customers globally
2.
Divisional mix. The Energy division, heat exchangers for HVAC, data centres and clean-tech, captures approximately 31% of group orders; Ocean, marine pumping and fluid handling, approximately 32%; Food and Pharma, sanitary process equipment, approximately 36%, per DNB Carnegie's order-mix breakdown
3.
Financial scale. FY25 net sales reached a record SEK 69,674m, operating income SEK 11,749m, adjusted EBITA SEK 12,334m (17.7% margin)
4.
Net income. Net income SEK 8,322m, all per the group's own reporting cited in the Business Foundation report
5.
Balance sheet. Net debt to EBITDA stood at 0.92x in FY25, rising to 0.96x in Q1 2026 following the Fives Cryogenics acquisition, both comfortably below the group's stated 2.0x ceiling
6.
Capital returns. Post-tax ROCE reached 23.9% in 2025, above the group's own 20% target; adjusted EBITA margin reached 17.7%, ahead of named peer GEA Group's 13-14% adjusted EBITA margin level
7.
Aftermarket mix. The high-margin service and aftermarket business contributes approximately 30% of group sales, serviced through a network of over 600 global distributors, providing a partial buffer against capital-equipment cyclicality
8.
Geography and customers. The customer base is fragmented; the single largest named customer, Tetra Pak, accounts for only 5.4% of net sales, and 76% of sales derive from contracts of under one year
9.
Investment-style implication. The case reads as a quality industrial compounder with a genuine cyclical overlay in the Ocean and HVAC-linked Energy sub-segments, not a pure defensive compounder and not a pure cyclical turnaround
