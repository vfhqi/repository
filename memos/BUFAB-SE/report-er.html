Section 1 – Brief company description and history

  2. Brokers focused heavily on Bufab's ability to exceed its 14% EBITA margin target through superior gross margin execution and sophisticated operational leverage.

1.
Global partner: Bufab operates as a leading global supply chain partner providing sourcing, quality control, logistics, and sustainability services for C-parts. The company simplifies complex supply chains for manufacturing customers by managing over 165,000 individual articles. This B2B distribution model seeks to establish highly resilient and long-term customer relationships by taking end-to-end component responsibility.
2.
Favourable structural: Industrial production and the outsourcing of C-parts management represent the primary structural growth drivers for Bufab. The company's organic sales expansion is further propelled by near-shoring trends and increased customer demand for supply chain resilience. Management actively leverages these drivers to target an average annual net sales growth of 10% and earnings per share growth exceeding 15% over time.
3.
Outstanding historical: Bufab has demonstrated a robust financial track record since its public listing on Nasdaq Stockholm in 2014. Between 2016 and 2025, the company grew its total sales by an average of 13% annually, with acquisitions accounting for nearly two-thirds of this growth. This acquisition-led strategy, combined with rising operating efficiency, propelled an exceptional ten-year adjusted earnings CAGR of 18% from 2015 to 2025.
4.
Lower return: Although Bufab has achieved EPS growth in line with its serial acquirer peers since its initial public offering, its financial profile exhibits key structural differences. Analysts emphasize that Bufab reports a structurally lower return on capital employed and a slightly lower adjusted return on equity than its mature peers. Additionally, the company is exposed to higher cyclicality in its industrial end-markets and maintains a higher net leverage ratio than Addtech, Lagercrantz, and Lifco.
5.
Strong momentum: Bufab's share price exhibited solid positive momentum in the period leading up to the autumn of 2026. The stock recorded an impressive absolute price increase of 27% in the year-to-date period preceding October 2026.
