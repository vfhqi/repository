Executive Summary -- We are looking for a clear, conviction-carrying statement of the investment thesis, key ratings, and actionable conclusion; avoiding vague or overly hedged language.
Judgement summary
  • Overall verdict. C Brembo is a genuine margin and brand leader in premium braking with a coherent mechatronic growth story, but leverage has roughly doubled since the Öhlins acquisition and sell-side sentiment has collapsed from majority-Buy to unanimous Hold.
    • Growth-case caveat. The Sensify software platform that anchors the growth case will not show meaningful revenue before fiscal 2027, versus the automotive-supplier cohort's typically faster monetisation window.
  • Key risk. Sell-side momentum has deteriorated sharply: the active analyst pool moved from 56% Buy / 43% Hold in January 2026 to unanimous Hold by May 2026.
    • Compounding signals. EPS estimates have been cut roughly 21% over the trailing twelve months (factset-ssem.json), and Chinese sales fell 14.7% in Q1-2026.
  • Key support. Brembo earns an EBIT margin roughly double the safety-critical auto-supplier average (9.1%-11% per source, versus ~6% peer average), protected by foundry vertical integration.
    • Moat detail. A 4,150-patent engineering moat and near-exclusive carbon-ceramic supply reinforce the margin advantage against the cohort.
  • Recommendation. Progress to Early-Stage Assessment at low-medium conviction: the business-quality signal is strong enough to justify deeper work.
    • Gating condition. The case is not yet simple (E7) and the entry point cannot be graded until BRE-IT is added to the master-dashboard price feed.
