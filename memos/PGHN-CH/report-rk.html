Outliers worth noting

The second material outlier centers on accounting methodologies, where lowering the infrastructure safety buffer from 50% to 35% boosted H1 2026 performance fee revenue by CHF 50.5 million, while simultaneously H1 2026 EBITDA margins were elevated by catch-up fees as other operating income surged 96% year-on-year. This dual adjustment created a temporary earnings boost that obscured underlying operational deceleration.

The anomaly intensifies when contrasted with perfect alignment between reported and adjusted net income at CHF 1,261 million for fiscal year 2025, while operating cash flow surged to CHF 1,515 million in 2025 from CHF 934 million in 2024. The unexplained divergence between stable earnings quality metrics and aggressive buffer reduction timing suggests methodological changes were deployed to smooth short-term revenue volatility rather than reflect fundamental asset risk reassessment.

The third cross-cutting outlier concerns currency exposure architecture, where the Swiss franc represents the largest cost currency at forty percent of total operational expenses yet generates zero management fee revenues, creating a structural currency mismatch against ninety-one percent EUR and USD revenues. This mismatch manifests in USD revenues at forty-seven percent exceeding USD opex at twenty-five percent, creating unhedged net long dollar exposure, while EUR revenues at forty-four percent exceed EUR opex at fourteen percent, leaving substantial net long euro exposure.

The statistical anomaly emerges when comparing hedging effectiveness, where hedging expenses rose significantly to CHF 48 million in H1 2026 yet remained ineffective against adverse translation of revenues which erased six percent of growth, while the lack of comprehensive translation hedges diluted the operating EBITDA margin by half a percentage point year-overyear. This represents an unexplained treasury policy contradiction where active hedging programs generated elevated costs while failing to protect the dominant revenue translation risk, suggesting either strategic misalignment or structural hedging constraints not fully disclosed to investors.
