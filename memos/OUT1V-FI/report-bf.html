Section 3 – Industry Structure + Concentration + Value Chain Economics

Section summary
  1. The global stainless steel market is massive but highly cyclical, currently experiencing significant policy-driven restructuring over traditional demand-cycle patterns
  2. Global supply is highly consolidated in Asia, while European markets exhibit a highly concentrated tier-1 producer oligopoly
  3. European peers maintain relatively disciplined capacity management, whereas Asian producers continue to drive high volumes via cheap nickel pig iron
  4. European policy shifts like CBAM and July 1, 2026 safeguards fundamentally reshape cost curves, penalizing high-emission imports and protecting domestic margins
