CEO and Chairman leadership assessment

CEO Matteo Laterza represents an asset management specialist turned operator with 20 years of concentrated focus in asset management and corporate finance and an honors degree in Business Economics from Bocconi University in 1989, whose appointment as CEO of Unipol Assicurazioni in November 2024 was designed to lead the structural simplification merging UnipolSai into Unipol Gruppo. Laterza operates alongside Executive Chairman Carlo Cimbri in a dual-leadership structure that preserves Cimbri's strategic dominance over group direction and banking stakes, creating significant key-person dependency concentrated in Cimbri's strategic authority. The internal promotion of a financial specialist who has spent his entire executive career under

Chairman Cimbri leaves his independent strategic pivot untested in a severe market dislocation, representing institutional inertia risk. Operational delivery demonstrates compounding underwriting profitability, with consolidated net profit growing 36.8% in 2025 to €1.53bn and expanding further by 46.8% YoY in 1H26 to €913m, while the Non-Life combined ratio improved from 93.6% in 2024 to 92.9% in 2025 and 91.8% in 1H26, beating plan targets. Solvency II ratio expanded from 212% at YE2024 to 230% at YE2025 and 259% by mid-2026, outperforming sector base rates, yet the transformative banking perimeter expansion via the agreed acquisition of 635 Monte dei Paschi branches for €3-3.5bn proposes creating Italy's second-largest bank, introducing unprecedented execution complexity.

Communication style exhibits conservative guidance posture and strong capital delivery track record, prioritizing granular underwriting metrics like loss ratios and CSM over vague promotional narrative, though management deflects complex technological questions on artificial intelligence and autonomous driving to operational lieutenants during calls, revealing specialist delegation limits. Decision velocity is exceptional, executing the merger by incorporation of UnipolSai into Unipol Gruppo within 10 months while pruning line-of-business overexposures and geographic concentrations, but high decision velocity in banking M&A risks transitioning Unipol into a complex conglomerate, diluting core insurance operational focus and exposing earnings to banking credit cycles.

However, the Strategic Committee comprises 9 members led by Chairman Cimbri with only 1 independent director, and heavy cooperative representation and Cimbri's strategic committee dominance risk marginalizing independent director influence on major banking acquisitions during critical capital deployment decisions.
