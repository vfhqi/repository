EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Overall Assessment of Chief Executive Ömer Tetik

Chief Executive Officer Ömer Tetik demonstrates exceptional standards of strategic execution and a relentless drive for market leadership that has propelled Banca Transilvania to the top position in Romania, delivering double-digit growth over the last five years despite navigating major macroeconomic and geopolitical challenges. The executive team showcases fast and bold strategic decisions when consolidating market share, completing the operational integration of OTP Bank Romania in a record seven months in early 2025 to drive synergies. The bank demonstrated strong adaptability to changing conditions within the regional banking market, maintaining superior returns on equity above 20% while Romanian policy rates shifted between 1.75% and 7% over the decade. Banca Transilvania achieved exceptionally high standards of delivery compared to typical sector averages, generating a return on equity of 23.87% in 2025 which was well above the performance of local competitors. Strong academic foundation in specialized finance, including an undergraduate degree in economics from Middle East Technical University and a Romanian-Canadian MBA in management of financial institutions, prepared the executive well for guiding Banca Transilvania through complex macroeconomic landscapes to support long-term earnings expansion.

Delivery Record and Financial Performance

Banca Transilvania achieved remarkable long-term asset and profit expansion under Chief Executive Ömer Tetik, with BT Group total assets growing from RON 47.6 billion in 2015 to RON 224.4 billion in 2025, representing almost five times scale expansion over the period. BT Group profit attributable to bank shareholders increased from RON 1.27 billion in 2016 to RON 4.48 billion in 2025, with earnings expanding more than three times over the period while sustaining a return on equity above 20% and delivering a 15% compound annual growth rate in earnings per share over a ten-year cycle.

Banca Transilvania completed more than 25 successful integrations in Romania and Moldova under Chief Executive Ömer Tetik, including key transactions such as Volksbank Romania in 2015 and Bancpost in 2018, establishing a specialized internal factory of integration experts to manage these transition projects. The bank outperformed its budgeted targets for the 2023 fiscal year, with net profit for the bank stand-alone reaching RON 2.49 billion and return on equity reaching 34% during the first quarter, reinforcing management's capability to exceed guidance in volatile times.

Strong performance against the 2024 budget saw stand-alone net profit reach RON 3.53 billion, beating budgeted expectations and yielding a 27.02% return on equity, with operating income increasing 27.80% to reach RON 8,224 million.

Governance Structure and Board Oversight

Banca Transilvania maintained a moderate board size of seven directors as of December 2025, including Horia Ciorcilă as Chairman alongside Thomas Gunther Grasse as Vice-Chairman, with shareholders benefiting from having three explicitly independent non-executive directors to monitor management. The bank operates an Audit Committee comprised entirely of independent non-executive directors, including Ivo Gueorguiev as Chairman, Thomas Gunther Grasse, and Mirela Ileana Bordea, with all three independent members attending every scheduled committee meeting in 2025 to keep management honest and protect the integrity of reported earnings.

Management conducts all transactions with subsidiaries and key executives under conditions similar to those applicable to third-party agreements, ensuring that no related-party transactions materially affect the bank's consolidated financial position and protecting minority shareholders from potential economic leakage. Deloitte Audit SRL provides independent external validation of the bank's financial statements, with shareholders reconfirming the auditor's appointment to cover financial years from 2023 through 2027, and the audit firm finding no material weaknesses or independence conflicts in its review of the financial disclosures.

Major committees like Remuneration are dominated by non-independent members, which could allow unchecked executive pay increases despite potential earnings slowdowns, and if the National Bank of Romania raises regulatory requirements, the absence of independent oversight on the pay committee could lead to misaligned incentives that harm future earnings per share.

Insider Share Dealing and Alignment

Banca Transilvania executed direct transfers of deferred shares to several top executive officers on September 16, 2026, with Chief Executive Officer Ömer Tetik receiving 8,325 shares under the bank's 2021 Stock Option Plan as part of his deferred variable compensation, demonstrating that the executive team is actively accumulating equity through performance programs to support future earnings per share expansion. The overall pattern of director and officer dealings over the last year shows strong net buying through deferred option vesting, with insiders acquiring over 12,000 shares through structured corporate incentive plans excluding a single openmarket sale of 1,000 shares, indicating high internal conviction regarding the bank's integrated commercial strategy and long-term earnings potential.

Chief Executive Officer Ömer Tetik demonstrates owner-like thinking by holding a meaningful personal equity stake in Banca Transilvania representing 0.12% of the bank's total shares, the largest stake among the executive management committee, with personal equity ownership aligning executive interests with shareholders and ensuring disciplined capital allocation to maximize long-term return on equity. The high volume of insider share acquisitions may look like a signal of strong conviction, but these transactions represent automatic option exercises under a pre-established 2021 remuneration program where insiders did not commit any new personal cash, and investors should consider that the lack of open-market purchases weakens the evidence of spontaneous executive confidence in future earnings.
