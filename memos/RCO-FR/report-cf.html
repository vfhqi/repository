EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus EPS and Investment Thesis

The research does not document absolute consensus EPS levels for FY27 or FY28, so this summary relies on revisions from broker notes published in late 2026 (Morgan Stanley, Deutsche Bank). Morgan Stanley upgraded its projected EPS by 5% for FY27, citing improved foreign exchange guidance, while Deutsche Bank raised EPS by 4.8% for FY27 and 5.2% for FY28. Consensus expects low-single-digit top-line growth and lower-mid-single-digit bottom-line growth in FY27, which is a modest recovery profile rather than a sharp rebound.

The thesis rests on cyclical volume stabilisation plus the RC Forward plan, which targets about 100 million euros of gross value creation at current operating profit level by FY2028-29.

Earnings expectations stabilised in late 2026 after falling over 80% on a like-for-like basis over four years, which suggests the downgrade cycle may be maturing.

Structural and Cyclical EPS Drivers

Bernstein models an incremental 68 million euros of current operating profit growth under the transformation, a 12% CAGR, below management's implied ambition. Savings arrive slowly, because only 10% of the gross 100 million euros benefit is expected to materialise in the current fiscal year. US cognac value depletions improved sequentially from down mid-single-digits in Q1 to down low-singledigits in Q2, which supports an eventual restocking-led shipment recovery.

China delivered mid-teens sales growth led by online channels, with e-commerce up more than 30%, offering higher-margin direct-to-consumer upside. Foreign exchange now adds about 15 million euros to sales and 3–5 million euros to current operating profit, and this adds roughly four percent to consensus EBIT. The tariff headwind is smaller, yet the 15 million euros reduction will be fully reinvested into commercial marketing, so it is not a margin windfall.

Morgan Stanley estimates the hybrid bond coupon will reduce EPS by 2% to 3% from FY2028 onward, partly offsetting operational gains.

Latest Results

First-half FY27 sales reached 510.9 million euros, an organic increase of 2.9%, and Q2 organic growth of 4.2% beat consensus of 3.6%. Cognac grew 12.4% organically in Q2, but Liqueurs and Spirits fell 7.4%, so the beat was driven by one division. Chinese sell-out through the Mid-Autumn Festival was weaker than the company expected, and wholesalers will likely need to destock in Q3. Americas sales fell 5.5% organically in the first half, with shipments hit by the move from Republic National Distributing Company to Reyes.

Guidance Trustworthiness

Management reiterated FY27 guidance for a return to organic sales growth and a slight organic improvement in operating margin, a deliberately modest target.

Broker Positioning and Peer Comparison

Ratings are split across Underweight (Morgan Stanley), Market-Perform (Bernstein), Buy (Jefferies), and Sell (Deutsche Bank and Redburn), with no clear consensus view. On fundamentals it lags peers: its operating margin fell to 17.7% against Diageo's 28.9%, and inventory reached 203% of net sales.

No active or documented activist investor campaigns were found, and the family's 66.20% voting control discourages aggressive public campaigns.
