EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

1.
LEADERSHIP QUALITY & ARCHETYPE Radical executive pivot: Redcare transitions from Olaf Heinrich's twelve-year pharmaceutical sector specialization to Peter Schmid von Linstow's twenty-year pure digital marketplace expertise drawn from eBay Germany and Visable, marking a strategic shift from regulatory-focused operator to platform-scaling technologist that triggered a 15% immediate share price decline over pharmaceutical experience concerns.
2.
Proven digital transformation credentials: Schmid von Linstow successfully led the transformation of wer liefert was into international marketplace Visable and negotiated Alibaba becoming majority shareholder in 2023, demonstrating elite platform-building and exit execution capabilities across two decades of marketplace leadership at AutoScout24, eBay, and Visable.
3.
Mitigated transition execution risk: The incoming CEO served on Redcare's Supervisory Board since April 2026 ensuring strategic familiarity, while pre-existing executive chemistry with CFO Hendrik Krampe from their eBay tenure promotes immediate senior leadership alignment, substantially reducing the typical disruption profile of external CEO appointments.
4.
Structured pharmaceutical continuity hedge: Management retained Olaf Heinrich in an advisory role specifically addressing German pharmacy regulatory topics through April 2027, providing regulatory navigation insurance during the critical German e-prescription rollout phase while preventing competitive defection risks.
5.
Outlier pharmaceutical experience void: The complete absence of pharmaceutical sector experience in both the CEO and CFO roles combined with the loss of pharmacy-trained commercial leaders like Dirk Brüse leaves the executive suite vulnerable to complex German regulatory shifts, creating an unprecedented structural gap relative to specialized healthcare peers like DocMorris.
6.
DELIVERY RECORD & EXECUTION QUALITY Strong top-line compounding delivered: Redcare expanded revenue to EUR 2,941 million in FY25 representing 49% cumulative growth from EUR 1,721 million in FY23, substantially outpacing traditional pharmacy channel growth and establishing Redcare as the fastest-growing European online pharmacy platform during Heinrich's tenure.
7.
Persistent profitability deficit: Consolidated EBIT margin lingered at negative 0.97% in FY25 and negative 1.65% in FY24, reflecting heavy customer acquisition investments and platform-building costs that position Redcare behind profitable peer Apotea's consistent positive margins despite similar growth trajectories.
8.
Transformative Swiss integration success: The major 51% acquisition of MediService AG in May 2023 contributed EUR 307 million in Rx sales during initial consolidation, demonstrating management's capacity to execute material cross-border transactions while the failed GoPuls logistics integration resulted in goodwill write-down and EUR 13 million contingent consideration release due to commercial unviability, exposing selective M&A execution risks.
9.
Disciplined balance sheet refinancing: Management issued EUR 300 million convertible bonds in April 2025, proactively repurchased EUR 157.9 million in outstanding 2028 debt, and completed EUR 64.5 million convertible redemption in January 2026, substantially strengthening liquidity headroom and extending maturity profiles ahead of peak automation capital expenditure requirements.

11.
GOVERNANCE ROBUSTNESS & STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY Comprehensive board refreshment executed: Redcare completed a major Supervisory Board transition in April 2026 with long-standing Chairman Björn Söder and directors stepping down, replaced by Anja Hendel, Max Müller, and marketplace expert Peter Schmid von Linstow to expand digital platform capabilities, marking the most significant governance overhaul in company history aligned with platform-scaling strategic priorities.
