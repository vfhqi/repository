Executive Summary -- We are looking for a clear, conviction-carrying statement of the investment thesis, key ratings, and actionable conclusion; avoiding vague or overly hedged language.
Judgement summary
  • Overall verdict. B Exosens rides a genuine, well-evidenced European defense-spending change case, but the case is priced richly and leans on one customer for four in every ten euros of revenue
  • Key risk. Theon International is simultaneously Exosens's largest customer, a shareholder seeking a board seat, and a competitor building its own tube supply chain, an unusual coopetition structure the market has not fully priced for governance risk

2.

2.1
Revenue share: Amplification is 68% of FY25 revenue, the highest-margin and most defensible part of the business
2.2

Margin profile: Adjusted EBITDA margin of 32.4% and adjusted EBIT margin of 27.2% sit roughly 15 percentage points above the defense-technology sector median of 18.0% / 11.5%

Dividend policy: A EUR 0.10 per share FY24 dividend initiated a targeted 20-25% payout ratio over 2025-2027

Geography. Europe accounts for 79% of revenue (Germany alone 25%)
5.1

7.2
