EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 6 findings

Marc Llistosella's appointment as CEO in January 2023 marked a decisive inflection point for Knorr-Bremse AG, bringing proven operational leadership to stabilize an organization that had experienced four CEOs in four years. Since taking the helm, Llistosella has fundamentally re-rated sell-side perception from cautious hold to overwhelming buy, with Deutsche Bank Research upgrading to Buy with progressive price target increases to €118, Morgan Stanley maintaining Overweight at €118, and Jefferies Research establishing a sector-high €125 price target.

The core management thesis rests on disciplined execution of operational restructuring, transparent financial stewardship under CFO Frank Markus Weber, and ambitious multi-year margin expansion targets that position Knorr-Bremse for sustained EPS compounding through the remainder of the decade. CEO Quality and Operational Track Record: Llistosella brings deep commercial vehicle expertise grounded in a Diploma in Business Administration and substantial Asian leadership experience. He built the foundational go-tomarket strategy for BharatBenz as Managing Director and CEO of Daimler India Commercial Vehicles from 2008 to 2014, and subsequently led Mitsubishi Fuso Truck & Bus Corporation as President and CEO while heading Daimler Trucks Asia from 2015 to 2018. His track record demonstrates repeatable operator patterns across greenfield building and operational restructuring, including leading structural turnarounds at Fuso that drove early electric commercial vehicle development with the eCanter launch. Deutsche Bank Research noted Llistosella possesses a direct communication style that contrasts with traditional German corporate leaders, bringing authentic operational candor to investor messaging. The Supervisory Board unanimously extended his contract by five years through December 2030 in January 2025, reflecting strong board confidence in his BOOST strategy execution.

BOOST Program Execution and Portfolio Rotation: Management completed the SELL-IT program by divesting non-core assets representing over €750 million in low-margin revenues. The sale of Merak HVAC to OpenGate Capital removes €360 million in low-margin rail sales and expands RVS operating EBIT margin by 40-50 basis points, removing a historical profitability drag. Acquiring duagon for €490 million cash expands highmargin rail electronics and software capabilities, with duagon delivering 15% compound annual growth that enhances technology leadership in control and signaling systems.

FIX-IT initiatives eliminated roughly 2,400 positions while shifting manufacturing and engineering footprints to best-cost countries. Knorr-Bremse reached its greater than 14% operating EBIT margin target early in Q2 2026 at 14.2%, ahead of the FY26 deadline.

Divisional Performance and Cash Conversion: RVS operating EBIT margin expanded to 17.5% in Q2 2026, driven by strong aftermarket demand and pricing power, with the order book expanding to a record €5.9 billion that provides high revenue visibility through 2027. CVS maintained double-digit margins around 10.4-12.0% despite severe cyclical downturns in North American Class 8 truck production, with fixed-cost reductions lowering the break-even threshold by 400 basis points.

Operating free cash flow hit an all-time high of €790 million in 2025, with the cash conversion rate reaching 131% and exceeding the sector median base rate of 85%. Net debt to EBITDA fell to 0.49x, providing substantial capacity for bolt-on M&A, while operating return on capital employed increased to 23.8% in H1 2026, outperforming high-quality industrial peers.
