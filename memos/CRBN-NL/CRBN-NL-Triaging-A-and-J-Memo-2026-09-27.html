[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. Corbion is a self-help margin recovery in a medium-quality fermentation business, with a cyclical pricing trough in its most profitable segment laid on top; it is not a growth case, not a demand inflection and not an oversold compounder. At €22.40 the shares already pay the stock's own five-year median multiple for a recovery whose two decisive prices, wild fish oil and the US dollar, Corbion does not set, so the modal return is about 11% a year against the 20% bar. It becomes a case at about €20, or on the Q3 2026 trading update in late October 2026 showing the Health & Nutrition price reset has landed.

  • The change is internal, real, dated and about half done.
    • Corbion has sold its lowest-margin business, is selling its second-lowest, has cut its cost base and its capital spending, has renewed its top team below the chief executive, and has locked its largest input to early 2028.
    • Emulsifiers was sold for US$362 million (closed 2 April 2024, a €138 million net book profit)
      • about 200 roles were removed for €35 million of recurring savings
      • the 50% TotalEnergies Corbion PLA joint venture has been held for sale since late 2025 with a binding agreement expected within twelve months of 31 July 2026
      • sugar and dextrose (34% of raw materials) are hedged for 100% of 2026 and nearly 100% of 2027 and early 2028.
    • BRIGHT 2030 (20 November 2025) targets 3% to 6% organic growth a year, an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 18% by 2028 from 16.1% in 2025 (11.1% in 2023) and €270 million of cumulative free cash flow over 2026 to 2028.
    • What is missing from the pattern of a major corporate change: there is no new chief executive (Olivier Rigaud since 2019, term to 2027) and the only guidance move in the period was a cut, so the change is a refocus that the sell-side calls "evolution, not revolution".
  • The external forces net to a mild headwind, and the two that set 2027 earnings are not Corbion's to control.
    • Clean-label regulation is a dated tailwind
    • against it stand fish-oil-linked pricing in algae omega-3, Middle East freight and sulfuric acid, a tightening competitive set and a US dollar that is 71% to 80% of revenue and unhedged at translation.
    • A 1% move in the dollar is €2.3 million to €2.4 million of EBITDA
      • the 2025 dollar fall cost €12 million to €13 million of EBITDA
      • the wild-catch fish-oil price, which sets the spot price of AlgaPrime, is read in opposite directions by management (tightening on El Niño) and by the sell-side (a forced price of US$3,500 a ton against a US$3,300 breakeven).
  • 'Financial Outputs' turned up once, stalled, and are turning again in volume but not in price.
    • The adjusted EBITDA margin rose from 11.1% (2023) to 16.1% (2025) and basic EPS rose 63% to €1.29 in 2025
    • then Q1 2026 missed badly (organic sales minus 4.1%, margin 12.9%), and Q2 2026 recovered on volume (organic growth 8.5%, volume and mix plus 10.7%, adjusted EBITDA €51.0 million, a beat) while pricing worsened (minus 2.2%; Health & Nutrition minus 8.2%) and management cut the FY2026 margin guidance from about 17% to above 16% for freight and sulfuric acid.
    • Consensus EPS fell 12% to 17% over six months and has been flat for one to three months
      • the three latest broker updates (September 2026) are Buys, so the momentum has troughed but has not turned.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain once and was reversed at its output end in the first half of 2026.
    • It shows in the company's people (a new chief financial officer, a rebuilt executive committee, a renewed board), its choices (BRIGHT 2030, the joint-venture exit), its actions (the disposal, the restructuring, the hedges, the Rayong plant), its key operating metrics (volume plus 10.7% in Q2 2026; a Functional Ingredients & Solutions margin of 11.5%), its 2025 financial statements (organic EBITDA plus 26.7%) and, in 2025, the sell-side's estimates (ING upgraded from Sell in November 2025).
    • The first half of 2026 cut the last three of those
    • the chain is being travelled a second time.
  • What we think it earns: EPS of €1.27 in 2025 (brokers' basis) rising to €1.30 in 2026, €1.48 in 2027 and €1.70 in 2028, about 10% a year, against consensus of €1.30, €1.51 and €1.78.
    • We sit 2% below consensus in 2027 and 4.5% below in 2028 because we hold the Health & Nutrition margin at 29% rather than 30%, Functional Ingredients & Solutions at 11.8% rather than above 12%, and allow a modest dollar drag.
    • Nearly half of that growth comes from below EBITDA (depreciation, interest and tax), the component investors value least and the one with the least evidence behind it
      • a plausibly worse drop-through cuts EPS about 10% across the path and takes the return to 3.6% a year over eighteen months.
  • What it costs: €22.40, or 13.3x consensus EPS 24 months forward of €1.69 (13.6x on our own path).
    • That is the 53rd percentile of the stock's own five-year range (median 12.96x; 25th to 75th percentiles 11.67x to 16.93x), the 90th percentile of the last two years (median 11.52x) and the 29th percentile of ten years, a history that includes 2020 to 2021 at 20x to 28x when the shares traded as an algae and bioplastics growth story, a business now being sold or defocused.
    • The last twelve months' share-price rise of 42.2% was entirely re-rating, because consensus EPS fell 12% to 17% over the same period
      • the easy part of the re-rating is behind the price.
    • Against the dashboard set Corbion at 13.3x is the cheapest of ten (Kerry 14.2x; Novonesis 22.9x)
      • against ING's food-ingredients set it traded at the peer median of 13.4x, not at a discount.
  • The return does not clear the bar on the modal path.
    • At a blended 13.50x applied to the earnings two years beyond each horizon, plus the dividend, the value is €26.2 at eighteen months and €30.6 at three years: plus 17% and plus 37%, 11.0% a year on both horizons, a D against the framework's anchors.
    • The multiple is the swing factor: each turn is €1.9, 8.5% of the price
      • 14x gives 13.6% a year
      • 15.5x on the cleared branch gives 25% a year
      • 11.5x gives about zero.
    • The cleared branch needs both the Health & Nutrition price reset (about even odds) and the joint-venture sale (about 65%), which together are about one chance in three
      • the bear branch (a fish-oil collapse or a dollar at 1.30) is minus 19% at about one in five.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Major Corporate Change', at C, and no setup crisply.
    • The inputs are broad and half executed, the outputs followed once and stalled once, and the pattern's defining output, major EPS trajectory upgrades, has not appeared.

  • The case is therefore a watch, not a buy: good self-help, a medium business, external price-setters, the re-rating already spent.

    • The chain holds from the inputs to the 2027 margin path in Functional Ingredients & Solutions
    • it breaks at the two prices Corbion does not set and at the multiple, which has already recovered.

  • Rating = D, because materiality is measured by the return, and the change forces (C) drive outputs (C) that, at a multiple which has already recovered to its five-year median, give a modal return of about 11% a year

    • the modal EPS path grows just under 12% a year and the return does not reach the C band without a re-rating we do not expect on the modal branch.
    • C was live, because the staircase of dated steps and the rebased consensus make upgrades plausible
    • not C because both the 12% EPS growth and the 13% return that C needs sit on the one-in-three branch, not the modal one.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The Q2 2026 print marks the turn.
  • Destocking is over and volumes are growing double-digit across every platform but pharma
    • Functional Ingredients & Solutions recovered 250 basis points of margin in one quarter with sugar locked to early 2028 at a five-year low and a second cost programme starting
    • fish oil is tight on El Niño and the 2023 aquaculture contracts rolling off are to be replaced by higher-priced ones from Q3 2026, taking Health & Nutrition back to a 30% margin by year-end
    • the €15 million to €20 million freight and sulfuric acid headwind is a one-year cost that reverses
    • the joint venture sells within twelve months for about €100 million against €69.2 million of book value, TotalEnergies being a willing co-seller, and the proceeds fund a buyback as in 2024
    • the 2028 target of 18% is "not overly ambitious" (ING models 19%).

  • So FY2027 EPS lands at ING's €1.75 or Oddo's €1.84 rather than the €1.51 consensus median, estimate momentum turns from D to B, and a business at the cheapest multiple in its dashboard set with a 7% free-cash-flow yield re-rates to 15x to 16x on 2028 earnings of €1.90 to €2.10, giving €28 to €34 in eighteen months, a 25% to 50% return, with the three Buy targets at €25 to €26 as the sell-side's own floor for that view.

  • What we concede: the volume turn is real and printed

    • the cost base is locked
    • the freight cost is in the guidance and is transient
    • the three latest broker revisions are Buys.
    • If the Q3 2026 update prints a Health & Nutrition margin near 30% with positive pricing, the eighteen-month arithmetic becomes a B at 14x and an A at 15.5x, and we would move the odds on the cleared branch from one in three to about one in two and the 'Modal return' rating from D to C or B.
  • Why we still hold our view: the pricing reset is a promise from a management that has held guidance into three misses in eighteen months
    • the sell-side and management read the fish-oil price in opposite directions and 30% of AlgaPrime volume is on spot with the US$4,000 to US$5,000 contracts expiring in late 2026
    • the multiple has already moved from about 10x to 13.3x on the narrative while the numbers went down, so the market has paid for part of this hypothesis in advance
    • the dollar is unhedged and no part of the bull case addresses it
    • and the framework's own count finds four attributes at B in thirty-seven, which is not the profile of a case to buy ahead of the evidence.
  • Where the hypothesis would win: the Q3 2026 print.
    • It is the observation every research report and this record name as the one that changes the view.
