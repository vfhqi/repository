Shortened lag times: The timeline between earn-out revaluations and goodwill write-downs at Nordic compounders is often virtually instantaneous. For Sdiptech AB, this tight correlation suggests that its recent write-backs could materialize as direct EPS headwinds if the underlying assets do not recover quickly.
