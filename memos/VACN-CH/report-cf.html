EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 43 findings

VAT Group is positioned to outgrow global wafer fab equipment spend by up to two times through an accelerating structural upcycle driven by gate-all-around transistor transitions, extreme ultraviolet lithography adoption, and high-bandwidth memory manufacturing intensity, yet the investment case remains bifurcated between those emphasizing the company's near-monopolistic 70% market share in advanced vacuum valves and skeptics highlighting unprecedented book-to-bill ratios of 1.7x that may mask severe downstream inventory double-ordering risks.

Management pragmatically recalibrated its 2027 sales target downward to CHF 1.6 billion during May 2025, anchoring expectations on more conservative wafer fab equipment assumptions of USD 125 billion versus USD 135 billion previously and a reduced USD/CHF exchange rate of 0.83 versus 0.95 initially, while record Q2 2026 order intake surging to CHF 500 million–up 102% year-over-year–expanded the order backlog by 121% to CHF 648 million.

The thesis hinges critically on whether management's review of 2027 sales guidance amid projected global wafer fab equipment spend reaching USD 145 billion in fiscal 2026 and broker models forecasting over 40% sales growth in fiscal 2027 represents genuine structural demand acceleration or temporary customer panic-ordering that will reverse sharply once semiconductor capital equipment utilization normalizes.
