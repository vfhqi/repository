  3. regulatory tariff frameworks face prolonged approval frictions with the ART, although the recent draft agreement provides medium-term revenue visibility

Dominant market presence: the Air France-KLM group and its global alliance partners command a 56% capacity share of Parisian international travel

Restricted ground services: access to ground handling is strictly capped at three providers at Paris-CDG and four at Paris-Orly
