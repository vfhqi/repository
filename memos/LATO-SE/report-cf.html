Appendix

Outliers worth noting

Latour's wholly-owned industrial operations exhibit significant margin underperformance relative to premium Nordic M&A compounders, with unlisted EBIT margins of 14.0% substantially lagging peer Lifco's 22.4% EBITA margin. This eight percentage point structural margin gap places Latour's unlisted portfolio in the bottom quartile of comparable Nordic industrial holding companies, despite management's stated cycle target of exceeding 15% operating margins.

The margin compression intensifies when examining individual subsidiaries, particularly Swegon's 7.4% operating margin in Q1 2026, which fell dramatically from 9.8% in the prior year, representing an extreme outlier even within Latour's own diversified portfolio. This structural profitability deficit is explored comprehensively in Section 4 Financial Profile Change and Section 7 Peer-Relative Earnings Momentum. Nord-Lock Group demonstrates exceptional margin outperformance within Latour's portfolio, delivering a 29.4% operating margin that places it in the top decile of global industrial fastener manufacturers.

This extreme positive outlier reflects Nord-Lock's proprietary wedge-locking technology and non-discretionary safety-critical positioning across diversified global industrial sectors. The subsidiary's 19% organic sales growth in Q1 2026 further establishes Nord-Lock as a statistical anomaly within Latour's cyclically-exposed industrial portfolio, demonstrating through-cycle resilience that sharply contrasts with the construction-dependent performance of Swegon and Hultafors.

This performance divergence and its implications for portfolio-level earnings quality are analyzed in Section 5 Historical Earnings Trajectory and Section 9 Key Drivers Synthesis.

Latour has delivered a 20-year NAV compound annual growth rate of 14%, outperforming both the peer average of 12% and the SIXRX benchmark return of 10%, establishing the company as a top-quartile compounder over multi-decade horizons despite near-term cyclical margin pressures. This long-term wealth creation record represents a fundamental outlier when contrasted against Latour's current operational challenges, including rolling 12-month growth of only 5% and margins of 14% that significantly undershoot management's stated cycle targets.

The tension between structural long-term outperformance and cyclical operational underperformance creates an unusual bifurcated investment profile rarely observed among Nordic industrial holding companies, as detailed in Section 10 Forward Guidance Credibility and Sensitivity and Section 11 Historical Delivery Track Record. Latour experienced extreme foreign exchange translation headwinds of SEK 900 million on top-line sales during fiscal year 2025, representing an outlier currency impact equivalent to approximately 3.2% of consolidated revenues.

This magnitude of FX-driven top-line erosion places Latour among the most severely affected Nordic industrials during the period, reflecting the company's substantial non-SEK revenue exposure across European and North American operations. Currency fluctuations accounted for more than one-third of the margin decline during the period, establishing FX volatility as an extreme external pressure that materially distorts year-overyear operational comparisons and complicates forward earnings visibility.

The implications of this currency sensitivity for forward financial performance are examined in Section 3 External Change: Value-Chain Forces and Section 6 Most Recent Earnings Delivery.
