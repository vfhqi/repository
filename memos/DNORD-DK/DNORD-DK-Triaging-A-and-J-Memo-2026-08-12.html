B.1 Business Description 7 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Dampskibsselskabet NORDEN A/S is a Danish international shipping company, founded 1871, operating an approximately 400-vessel fleet across two primary segments: Dry Cargo (dry bulk commodities, ores, coal, project cargo) and Tankers (refined products, diesel, jet fuel).
2.
Fleet structure. The core fleet comprises 11 owned vessels, 69 long-term leased vessels and 91 purchase options; NORDEN operates a cycle-adaptive model that shifts between asset ownership and asset-light chartering.
3.
Financial character. FY25 group revenue USD 3,125.7m (down from USD 4,042m in FY24), FY25 net profit USD 120m (down from USD 163m in FY24 and USD 400m in FY23); equity ratio 52.0% at Q1 2026, net interest-bearing debt USD 239.1m.
4.
Sector and geography. Listed on Nasdaq Copenhagen; ~500 employees across 19 offices on six continents; revenue is USD-denominated (approximately 100%), with China (USD 518m in 2025), the United States (USD 223m) and Singapore (USD 172m) as the largest geographic contributors.
5.
Segment mix (post-2025 reorganisation). Six reportable segments: Dry Owner, Dry Operator Large, Dry Operator Small, Tanker Owner, Tanker Operator, Logistics — condensing further to two primary business units (Dry Cargo, Tankers) for external reporting from FY2025.
6.
Investment style implication. A cyclical, asset-trading-levered shipping compounder: the case blends a classical dry bulk/tanker freight cyclical with an embedded, quantifiable options book that behaves more like a real-asset call option than a pure freight-rate bet.
7.
Capital returns. USD 1.2bn distributed to shareholders over the past five years via dividends and buybacks, under a minimum 50% payout policy; a new USD 25m buyback authorised Q1 2026 alongside a DKK 2/share interim dividend.
