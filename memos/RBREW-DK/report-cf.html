EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Royal Unibrew faces a pivotal transformation as highly stable executive leadership prepares for the termination of its PepsiCo Northern Europe bottling contract effective January 1, 2029, which represents approximately 13% of group net revenue and creates a mid-20s percentage hit to group EBIT from negative operational leverage. The company's most recent quarterly results demonstrated strong operational execution, with Q1 2026 adjusted EBIT reaching DKK 273 million, beating consensus by 9.20%, and operating margin expanding 150 basis points to 8.30% through pricing actions and logistics efficiency gains.

Over the last three years, the company has steadily improved its financial profile, with gross profit margins rising from 38.80% in Q1 2024 to 40.30% in Q4 2025, culminating in full-year 2025 EBIT growth of 12% organically to DKK 2,202 million, hitting the top end of management guidance.

Management maintains a conservative honest-tracker communication archetype, consistently guiding under challenging consumer environment assumptions without presuming macro recovery, with analysts observing that management conservatively brackets consensus at early-year stages. The sell-side analyst pool consists of eleven named analysts across five primary European brokerage firms, with a polarized rating mix of 40% Buy, 40% Hold, and 20% Sell, and multiple recent downgrades from Buy to Hold following the PepsiCo announcement, driving the median price target down to DKK 510 from DKK 685. The bull case centers on own- brand soft drinks like Faxe Kondi delivering superior volume growth and expanding EBIT margins by 220 basis points by 2028, while bears caution that structural portfolio changes will keep margins lower for longer and project 2026 organic sales declining by 1.1% on soft consumer demand, with 2029 organic EBIT potentially reducing by 17% due to negative bottling leverage. Within the peer landscape, Royal Unibrew demonstrates top-tier fundamental momentum positioning within the European beverage sector, though the competitive environment is intensifying as Carlsberg captures the reallocated PepsiCo Northern Europe license and significantly strengthens its non-alcoholic presence through the Britvic acquisition.

The company's organic EBIT growth of 12% in 2025 outpaced European peers including Heineken's 3.30% growth, with group EBIT margin expanding 90 basis points to 14%, though total ROIC of 13% remains below the 19% pre-pandemic high due to dilutive acquisitions, while maintaining disciplined leverage at 2.0x net debt to EBITDA, beating Heineken's 2.31x. No active activist presence exists in the shareholder base above the 3% threshold requiring disclosure or public campaign activity.

The forward investment thesis rests on five to eight critical drivers with varying plausibility and interdependency profiles. Ranked by estimated impact and confidence, these include own-brand soft drink acceleration (Faxe Kondi, Jaffa, Lemonsoda) contributing +2% to +4% annual volume with HIGH plausibility, the PepsiCo Northern Europe license termination creating approximately 13% revenue decline and mid-20s% EBIT headwind with HIGH plausibility but high dependency on mitigation execution, International export expansion in West Africa and ACHA regions driving +4% to +12% regional volume growth with MEDIUM plausibility, operational efficiencies from the Faxe automated high-bay warehouse and SAP systems delivering +150 basis points EBIT margin expansion with HIGH plausibility, and price/mix and promotional management through unprofitable promo deselection generating +60 basis points gross margin expansion with HIGH plausibility but medium dependency on competitive intensity.

Management's current guidance framework targets 2026 organic EBIT growth of 6% to 10% corresponding to EBIT of DKK 2,325-2,425 million while projecting reported net revenue to remain broadly on level with 2025 after adjusting for the complete exit from snacks reducing 2026 sales by 3.5%, maintaining a strong historical delivery record with 2025 EBIT reaching DKK 2,202 million meeting the reiterated guidance range and 2024 organic EBIT growth of 15% landing within guided ranges.
