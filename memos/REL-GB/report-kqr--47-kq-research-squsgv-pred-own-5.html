Hedging Strategy and Contract Maturities

1.
Systematic subscription hedging: Under RELX's currency management policy, the company enters into forward foreign exchange contracts to protect its highly predictable recurring sales. Extensive forward portfolio: RELX maintains a substantial portfolio of forward foreign exchange contracts that totals £1,306m in principal. Because these instruments fix exchange rates in advance, the business remains shielded from sudden foreign exchange fluctuations, which supports consistent earnings per share projections. Significant 2026 maturities: RELX has structured its forward contracts so that £563m in principal hedges will mature during 2026. Financial notes published on 23 June 2026 indicate that these 2026 maturities carry a deferred foreign currency hedge reserve gain of £20m before tax.
