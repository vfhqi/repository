This unbroken twodecade streak suggests an extraordinarily stable underlying earnings base and conservative capital allocation philosophy that prioritizes shareholder income over aggressive growth investments, contrasting sharply with peers like Sage Group who maintain higher operational volatility. This adoption rate materially exceeds standard patterns observed across professional information peers deploying generative AI features, where consensus industry benchmarks suggest 12-to-18-month adoption timelines for reaching 50% enterprise penetration. The rapid uptake signals either extraordinary product-market fit, minimal competitive alternatives, or structurally high switching costs that compel immediate adoption, though the underlying drivers remain inadequately explained in management commentary.
