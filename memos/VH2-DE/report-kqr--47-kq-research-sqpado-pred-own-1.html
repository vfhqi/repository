Section 3 – IAS 32 Presentation and Balance Sheet Accounting

Section summary
  1. Friedrich Vorwerk presents its operating partnership non-controlling interests as liabilities under IAS 32 due to contractually mandatory redemption structures.
  2. The profit share of these minority interests reduces consolidated pre-tax profit because the underlying shares are classified as financial debt.
  3. The balance sheet liabilities have grown substantially over recent quarters in tandem with the operating partnership's expanding profitability.

IAS 32 Classification Mechanics

1.
Unusual liability presentation: Friedrich Vorwerk classifies its core minority interest within balance sheet debt rather than equity because of specific partnership structures. According to the Friedrich Vorwerk 2024 annual report on 28 March 2025, the non-controlling interests in Friedrich Vorwerk SE & Co. KG are reported under current and non-current liabilities under IAS 32 rules. This reclassification occurs because German partnership law gives partners cancellation rights that represent a contractual obligation to deliver cash.
2.
Debt treatment confirmed: Friedrich Vorwerk continues this debt presentation in its latest financial reports to comply with international accounting standards. According to the Friedrich Vorwerk 2025 annual report on 31 March 2026, the operating partnership shares of non-controlling shareholders are not recognised in consolidated equity under IAS 32 guidelines. This means that the non-controlling interest is treated as a financial liability on the balance sheet, altering standard leverage metrics.
3.
Finance cost impact: Friedrich Vorwerk's accounting classification has a direct impact on the consolidated statement of comprehensive income by lowering pre-tax profit. According to the Friedrich Vorwerk annual report on 28 March 2025, the earnings share of the 10.1% operating partnership interest is recognized in the net finance costs of the group. This accounting choice reduces consolidated earnings before taxes (EBT), which would otherwise appear as an allocation of net income.
4.
Redemption obligation structure: Friedrich Vorwerk's specific partnership agreements trigger the liability classification under international rules. According to the Friedrich Vorwerk 2025 annual report on 31 March 2026, the legal form of Friedrich Vorwerk SE & Co. KG requires recognizing these non-controlling shares under liabilities. This obligation exists because partnership contracts historically include put options or cancellation rights that must be valued at the present value of the redemption amount.

5.
Surging liability figures: Friedrich Vorwerk's liabilities to non- controlling interests have increased significantly over the recent quarters. According to the Friedrich Vorwerk Q2 interim report on 13 August 2026, non-current liabilities to non-controlling interests rose to €22,339k as of June 2026. This is a substantial increase from €16,614k on 31 December 2025 and reflects the rising profitability of the operating partnership.
6.

According to the Friedrich Vorwerk Q1 interim report on 12 May 2026, the non-current liabilities to non-controlling interests stood at €18,393k during March 2026. This metric demonstrates how the balance sheet liability expands in tandem with the operating partnership's accumulated earnings.
