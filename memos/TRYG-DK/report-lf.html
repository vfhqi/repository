CEO Background, Track Record, and Leadership Quality

1.
Johan Kirstein Brammer brings an elite generalist background combining legal training at Copenhagen, McKinsey strategy consulting in Australia and the UK, and commercial P&L leadership at TDC, distinguishing him from traditional insurance-lifer CEOs who typically spend full careers in underwriting. His divisional track record demonstrates repeatable operator excellence, having driven Danish Private premium growth from 0.8% to over 6% while delivering 28 consecutive quarters of underlying combined ratio improvement and lifting customer retention to an all-time high of 90.8%
2.
Since becoming Group CEO in June 2023, Brammer has delivered exceptionally strong underlying margin expansion, maintaining a Return on Own Funds of 26-39% while driving consistent underlying claims ratio improvements across Private and Commercial lines, cementing Tryg's position as a premier Nordic underwriter. Through H1 2026, he achieved 5% local currency revenue growth in Private segments and delivered a 10-year low combined ratio of 77% in Private Norway, successfully reversing prior margin erosion through disciplined double-digit rate increases

His refusal of growth targets is explicit: "I think there's ample room for us to move on the growth ambitions that we have. Just to be very clear, we don't have a growth target as such...
