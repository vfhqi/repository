The dual-tranche transaction consists of two perpetual notes designed to optimize the group's capital structure and debt maturity profile. This refinancing strategy helps stabilize intermediate obligations but carries coupon payments that impact net income.

Consequently, consensus numbers likely fail to reflect the full interest burden of the new subordinated debt.
