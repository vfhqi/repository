Rent and Concession Structures

1.
Rigid lease costs: Outdoor advertising operators face substantial profit risks due to highly fixed lease obligations. Bernstein Research's note of 12 June 2026 states that street furniture contracts like bus shelters and kiosks are largely fixed-rent in nature compared to transit contracts. This structural rigidity means that cyclical revenue downturns will compress operating margins unless operators negotiate variable fee agreements. Consequently, high fixed rent structures are a key risk that could heavily depress future earnings per share during economic recessions.
