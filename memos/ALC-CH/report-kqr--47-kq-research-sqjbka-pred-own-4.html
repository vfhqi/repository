Section 2 – Tryptyr Prescription Volume and Clinical Adoption

Section summary
  1. Alcon's Tryptyr commercial launch has exceeded initial adoption expectations, capturing approximately 6% market share and securing over 11,000 unique prescribers by late 2026.
  2. Tryptyr's launch trajectory remains significantly behind the historic volume of Bausch + Lomb's Miebo, which has maintained dominant market share and rapid revenue growth.

Rapid Commercial Market Penetration

1.
Strong launch trajectory: Alcon's acoltremon achieved rapid clinical penetration following its commercial release in late 2025. The product achieved approximately 84,000 total written prescriptions in its first five months, demonstrating rapid early uptake among eye care professionals. This momentum has raised management confidence that Tryptyr can achieve its long-term peak sales target of up to $400 million.
2.
Continued share growth: Alcon succeeded in capturing approximately 5% of the US dry eye market by mid- 2026. This market share expanded further to approximately 6% by Q3 2026, driven by growing physician comfort with the drug's unique mechanism. These gains indicate that Alcon is establishing Tryptyr as a leading therapeutic option within Ocular Health.
3.
Expanding prescriber milestones: Alcon expanded its unique provider network to more than 6,600 prescribers within five months of launch. This prescriber base continued to grow rapidly, achieving a milestone of approximately 11,000 unique prescribers by November 2026. This broad physician engagement supports the potential for sustained prescription growth as Alcon ramps up its direct-to-consumer marketing.

This performance was heavily supported by the ongoing commercial launch of Tryptyr alongside the over-the-counter Systane family. The strong division results helped restore investor confidence in Alcon's broader product innovation pipeline.

Sustained quarterly performance: Alcon's Ocular Health division achieved net sales of $500 million in Q3 2026, representing an 8% constant currency increase. This growth was led by both the Systane franchise and the expanding commercial sales of Tryptyr. Bernstein Research forecasts that these dry eye products will remain a primary growth driver throughout FY2026E.

Section 4 – Tryptyr Tolerability, Refill Persistence, and Patient Experience

Section summary
  1. Tryptyr's rapid onset of action is undermined by a common temporary burning sensation that drives immediate patient dropouts.
  2. Bausch + Lomb's Miebo achieves superior patient persistence due to its highly tolerable, water-free lipid formulation.
  3. Confidence in these comparative tolerability trends is high based on consistent feedback from clinical experts and broker surveys.

Clinical Tolerability and Side Effects

1.
Severe localized: Tryptyr's unique neuromodulator mechanism of action causes a distinct and frequent sensory side effect upon instillation. An Alcon sales representative described the drug's common side effect as a strong sixty-second pepperminty burn. This localized burning sensation has raised some safety concerns among prescribing physicians who trial the drug. Clinicians must actively educate dry eye patients to prevent premature treatment discontinuation.
2.
Poorer tolerability: Competitor therapies like Xiidra exhibit a highly burdensome tolerability profile that frequently compromises the overall patient experience. In clinical trials, up to 25% of patients treated with Bausch + Lomb's Xiidra reported experiencing unpleasant taste or dysgeusia. Furthermore, between 5% and 25% of these patients suffered from instillation-site irritation and temporary blurred vision. These persistent side effects explain why Xiidra has historically struggled to achieve rapid and widespread commercial adoption.

Patient Compliance and Refill Persistence

5.
Poor compliance: Real-world dry eye patient compliance remains severely limited by slow therapeutic onset and persistent product discomfort. Historically, up to 80% of dry eye patients discontinue therapy within four months of starting treatment. This high rate of discontinuation is primarily driven by limited product efficacy, instillation discomfort, and delayed onset of action. Consequently, older anti-inflammatory medications like Restasis and Xiidra suffer from poor patient persistence.
6.
Encouraging persistence: Alcon is utilizing specialized fulfillment channels to support early patient persistence and drive consistent product refills. Alcon sales representatives reported that patients utilizing the BlinkRx digital pharmacy program are demonstrating encouraging therapeutic renewal rates. This digital onboarding pathway helps mitigate early dropouts by providing structured patient education on managing the temporary burning sensation. Successful renewal execution remains vital for Alcon to convert free-trial users into longterm revenue contributors.
7.
Rapid adoption: Bausch + Lomb's Miebo achieves superior patient persistence and clinical compliance by delivering rapid symptomatic relief. Miebo acts quickly to stabilize the tear film, allowing patients to experience significant symptom relief within days. This rapid onset of action contrasts sharply with anti- inflammatory drops that take weeks or months to show efficacy. As a result, Miebo's immediate physical feedback loop encourages high patient compliance and satisfaction.
8.
Polarized response: Real-world clinical experience shows a highly polarized patient reaction to Tryptyr during the early phase of treatment. An ophthalmologist noted that while some patients love Tryptyr and report rapid relief, others stop treatment after two days due to intolerability. Patients who experience Sjögren's disease or severe aqueous deficiency are highly receptive to Tryptyr's tear-stimulating mechanism. However, patients with milder symptoms are far less willing to tolerate the drug's instillation burning.
9.
Other side of the story: While Tryptyr's burning sensation limits early adoption, its rapid onset of action could drive better long-term persistence than legacy immunomodulators. Optometrists note that patients frequently abandon Restasis or Xiidra because they require several months to show any benefit. Tryptyr's ability to stimulate natural tear production starting on day one offers immediate positive reinforcement to patients. This rapid physical response could enable Tryptyr to achieve superior long-term compliance once counseling is established.
