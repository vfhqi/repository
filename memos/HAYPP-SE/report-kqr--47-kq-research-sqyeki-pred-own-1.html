Aggressive consolidation: Established global tobacco conglomerates are aggressively entering the modern oral category to capture market share from early online movers. Japan Tobacco announced on November 10, 2026, that it acquired the assets and PMTA of the US nicotine pouch brand CLEW. If major tobacco manufacturers use their immense financial muscle to establish exclusive online-to-offline networks, Haypp could face slower Growth Markets volume expansion and lost market share.

Negative indicators: Financial analysts must monitor early signs of consumer divergence, such as sudden shifts in brand search indexes and declining average order values. Other side of the story: DNB Carnegie reported on May 8, 2026, that the US regulatory environment has eased considerably under the Trump administration, driving a strong inflow of new pouch products. Consequently, total digital displacement of Haypp Group by smaller online peers remains highly unlikely in the intermediate term.
