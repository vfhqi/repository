Appendix

Outliers worth noting

The analysis of Alleima's operations and financials reveals several highly unusual statistical outliers that warrant careful examination. The most prominent anomaly is the SEK 995 million umbilical tubing order secured in Q2 2026, representing the company's largest-ever single order. This contract for advanced SAF 2507 umbilical tubes destined for the Kutei North Hub gas project in Indonesia demonstrates an exceptional concentration of revenue visibility, with 60% of order volume scheduled for delivery in 2027 alone. [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 6, Section 9] This delivery profile creates an unusually front-loaded revenue trajectory that deviates significantly from Alleima's typical order patterns and introduces substantial execution risk if production bottlenecks emerge.

Capital expenditure intensity displays a notable outlier pattern, with peak CapEx guidance reaching approximately SEK 1,100 million for FY2026, representing a substantial elevation above the historical FY2023 baseline of SEK 815 million. This 35% surge reflects simultaneous geographic expansion across multiple manufacturing sites, including the SEK 220 million Mehsana India investment, the completed SEK 255 million Zhenjiang Phase II expansion that doubled local cold-finishing capacity, and the reopening of Tube Mill 2026 in Sandviken which boosted steam generator tubing capacity by approximately 60%. [Cross-ref: Section 2, Section 4] While management allocates over 60% of this capital to high-margin niche segments, the compressed multi-year investment timeline introduces execution complexity rarely observed in specialty steel manufacturing peers.

Operational margin volatility reached extreme levels during 2025, with adjusted EBIT margin bottoming at 4.7% in Q3 2025 due to a prolonged Sandviken mill stoppage for expansion press replacement, followed by a SEK 342 million restructuring charge in Q4 2025 to permanently cut structural costs. [Cross-ref: Section 5, Section 11] This margin trough stands in stark contrast to the subsequent Q2 2026 recovery to 10.6%, representing a 590-basis-point sequential improvement that significantly exceeds typical peer margin variability ranges. [Cross-ref: Section 6] The magnitude and speed of this margin swing suggest unusual operational leverage dynamics tied to fixed-cost absorption during capacity transitions.

Kanthal division organic growth exhibits outlier characteristics, achieving 21% organic revenue expansion in Q2 2026 with margins expanding to 19.6% from 16.7% year-over-year, while the broader Tube division contracted 3% organically. [Cross-ref: Section 6, Section 7] This intra-company growth divergence of 24 percentage points significantly exceeds typical divisional performance dispersion observed in diversified specialty materials peers and raises questions about sustainable addressable market penetration rates in industrial heating applications.
