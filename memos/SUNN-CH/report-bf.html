Extreme vendor concentration on Huawei equipment: Sunrise operates at the negative tail of supplier diversification metrics, relying almost exclusively on Huawei to supply and install its active 5G mobile network equipment and portions of its fixed-line infrastructure. This concentration places Sunrise among the bottom 5% of Western European operators for equipment vendor diversification, contrasting sharply with competitors like Swisscom and Salt who maintain multi-vendor strategies across Nokia and Ericsson. Tax-exempt dividend duration: Sunrise maintains an extraordinary CHF 2.6 billion in qualifying additional paid-in capital reserves, enabling fully withholding tax-free dividend distributions for approximately 9 to 10 years. This capital structure outlier transforms Sunrise into a rare high-yield, tax-optimized equity instrument, significantly enhancing its appeal within yield-seeking defensive equity portfolios during rising interest rate regimes.
