Sustained promotional pressure: Elevated inventory clearance activity and deep discounting across North American retail channels continue to threaten brand-level average unit prices. Retail analysts at Bernstein Research observed on October 30, 2026, that major sportswear brands continue to report inventory gluts in lifestyle footwear franchises. To clear this old stock, retailers are relying on widespread promotional campaigns that train consumers to wait for sales events. Consequently, persistent promotional intensity could severely limit the effectiveness of selective price hikes designed to offset import tariffs.

Other side of the story: Highly premium sportswear brands may maintain double-digit earnings growth even under extreme tariff escalations or severe supplier pricing pressure. Sourcing presentations from Puma SE on November 2, 2026, demonstrate that gross margins can expand through favorable channel mix and rising directto-consumer penetration. By bypassing traditional wholesale partners and expanding online sales channels, brands successfully recover incremental supply chain overheads. Consequently, robust consumer engagement and healthy direct-to-consumer momentum can entirely offset regulatory cost increases and protect bottom-line profitability.
