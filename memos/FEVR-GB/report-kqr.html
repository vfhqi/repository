Commercial Terms and Equity Stake

1.
Transformational partnership: Fevertree transitioned its US commercial operations to Molson Coors on 1 February 2025 under an exclusive long-term licensing agreement. Under this model, Molson Coors assumes full responsibility for sales, distribution, and marketing while consolidating 100% of US revenue for Fevertree products. This asset-light structure allows Fevertree to retain brand identity and product development while leveraging its partner's extensive network.
2.
Profitable structure: Fevertree earns royalty payments representing its share of US partnership profits under the profit-sharing model. This structure ensures that Fevertree benefits from national scale without directly funding the associated working capital requirements. Consequently, the arrangement dramatically improves cash flow visibility and de-risks the transition phase.
3.
Minority investment: Molson Coors acquired an 8.5% equity stake in Fevertree Drinks plc for approximately $88.1 million during the first quarter of 2025. This minority holding was purchased through the issuance of 10.86 million new ordinary shares priced at 654.2 pence per share. The investment aligns both companies' financial incentives and underscores Molson Coors' strategic commitment to the brand.

Strategic alignment: Molson Coors management described the Fevertree relationship as a major move to expand beyond the core beer category.
