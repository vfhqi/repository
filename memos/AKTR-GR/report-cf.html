EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Aktor has undergone a material strategic transformation since 2022, transitioning from a pure construction operator to a diversified infrastructure holding company under new majority shareholders Winex Investments and Castellano Properties, who collectively control over 64% of share capital. The group executed a massive corporate restructuring in 2024-2025, spinning off construction and concessions activities into dedicated subsidiaries while deploying approximately €282 million across five strategic acquisitions to establish five distinct business pillars encompassing construction, concessions, facility management, renewables, and LNG trading.

Revenue scaled rapidly from €1,254.92 million in FY24 to €1,394.99 million in FY25, representing 11.16% year-on-year growth, while Adjusted EBITDA expanded dramatically from €87 million to €169.20 million and total group borrowings surged to €1,293.40 million to fund the transformation program. Management has articulated an ambitious €3 billion five-year capital expenditure program supported by an upcoming €650 million share capital increase to build contracted concessions and renewable assets that will transition the earnings profile toward predictable recurring cash flows.

Net Signal on Materiality of Change

The strategic pivot represents highly material change across leadership, capital structure, and business model. CEO Alexandros Exarchou assumed leadership to drive the transition from Intrakat heritage, orchestrating a corporate transformation launched in 2024 that restructured the parent into a pure holding company. Management deployed approximately €282 million in 2025 across five closed acquisitions including the pivotal €252 million purchase of Aktor Concessions and smaller bolt-on deals targeting facility management, renewables, and network construction capabilities. The shareholder base was fundamentally reset when Winex Investments acquired a 34.78% stake in July 2022, with strategic partners Castellano Properties and Blue Silk combining to hold over 64% of capital and enabling aggressive capital raises to fund the diversification strategy. The organizational structure was radically simplified with the parent operating as a pure holding company providing administrative services while construction activities were hived down into Aktor Constructions subsidiary and concessions spin-off into Aktor Participations Concessions-PPP.

Top Key Drivers Ranked by Impact with Plausibility Verdicts

The forward earnings trajectory depends on five to eight critical drivers with varying plausibility profiles. The €650 million equity capital increase and €3 billion capex program ranks as the highest-impact driver with HIGH plausibility, as underwriting commitments from Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, and UBS make completion highly probable by late 2026, although market volatility or pricing disagreements could delay the offering and stall the growth pipeline.

Scaling of Aktor Concessions and PPP backlog represents another high-impact driver with HIGH plausibility, given the integration of concession assets was already completed as of late 2025 and Greece represents one of the most active European concessions markets with a €40 billion to €50 billion pipeline, though failure to win active tenders or underperforming traffic volumes on motorways could compromise targeted cash generation.

The renewable energy platform expansion targeting 1.30 GW of capacity by 2028 carries MEDIUM plausibility, supported by strategic partnerships like the PPC Renewables joint venture but facing execution risks from persistent grid connection bottlenecks and curtailment of clean power that could depress equity returns below targets. US LNG trading through the Atlantic-SEE venture represents a medium-impact driver with MEDIUM plausibility, as contracted volumes of 1 MTPA from 2030 carry commodity risk passthrough clauses but face uncertainty from potential resumption of Russian pipeline gas flows to Europe that could erode the strategic appeal of the Vertical Corridor.

Margin accretion via AI-integrated cost control systems deploying SAP S/4HANA and Primavera offers HIGH plausibility for improving operational efficiency, with pilot integration already launched and management allocating over €10 million to technology solutions, though system implementation delays or insufficient personnel training could limit effectiveness.

Net Signal on Recent Delivery and L3Y Trajectory

Aktor's L3Y results demonstrate rapid revenue scaling from €412.10 million in FY23 to €1,394.99 million in FY25, driven by the integration of AKTOR S.A., though cash flows exhibit heavy working capital requirements with €82 million outflow in FY25 creating near-term pressure despite positive operating turnaround. Management exhibits a strong sandbagging posture by routinely beating conservative guidance, although non-recurring costs like the €15 million Prodea transaction fee impact headline prints. The most recent H1 2025 earnings delivered a strong top-line beat with revenue of €623 million exceeding €620 million consensus and EBITDA of €65 million beating the €60 million estimate, though cash flows remain constrained by a massive contractual asset buildup of €136 million highlighting persistent working capital intensity. The quarterly earnings trajectory shows consolidated revenue reaching €1,394.99 million in FY25 representing 11.16% year-on-year growth, with consolidated gross profit margin expanding to 13.05% from 9.85% in FY24 and consolidated EBITDA surging €75.80 million year-on-year to €159.70 million.

Forward Credibility and Management Archetype

Management behaves as an honest-tracker adjusting to active portfolio changes rather than a chronic sandbagger or over-promiser, as evidenced by disciplined capital allocation decisions like cancelling the Prodea deal to prevent unnecessary debt accumulation while maintaining flexibility to redirect resources toward higherreturn renewable projects. CEO Exarchou guided to ambitious FY25 revenues of €1.48 billion and EBITDA of €200 million, reiterating a mid-term range of €180 million to €200 million that reflects confidence in operational momentum.

Sell-side analysts consistently note that management routinely exceeded conservative guidance, with Axia Ventures highlighting that the FY25 EBITDA target of €180 million was routinely beaten and H1 2025 EBITDA of €65 million surpassed prior expectations, suggesting a sandbagging-oriented management culture that prefers to under-promise and over-deliver. However, the group exhibits high operational leverage with strong sensitivity to margin shifts due to fixed-price construction contracts, meaning small operational deviations significantly alter pro forma earnings outcomes and create execution risk around ambitious medium-term targets.

Plausible problems to monitor

The confluence of these plausible problem to monitor suggests that Aktor operates with materially higher execution risk, financial leverage sensitivity, and external dependency than the peer median profile. While the strategic vision of transitioning from cyclical construction toward contracted concessions and renewable cash flows carries merit, the path to achieving this transformation remains burdened by governance concentration, working capital intensity, margin gaps, labor market constraints, competitive capital disadvantages, and a single-point-of-failure dependency on the pending equity capital raise.

These structural vulnerabilities collectively position the group in the bottom quartile of regional infrastructure operators across key resilience and execution probability dimensions.

Outliers worth noting

The company's revenue trajectory exhibits a statistical outlier pattern, with fiscal year 2025 revenues reaching €1,394.99 million, representing explosive 238% growth from the fiscal year 2023 baseline of €412.10 million. This three-year compound annual growth rate significantly exceeds the Greek infrastructure sector base rate and places Aktor in the top 5% of regional construction peers for topline expansion velocity.

The magnitude of this inflection is primarily driven by the November 2023 integration of AKTOR S.A., which provided a permanent step-change in base-rate profitability rather than organic market share gains. The inorganic nature of this growth creates comparability distortions that obscure underlying operational momentum and differentiate Aktor from peers executing purely organic expansion strategies. [Cross-ref: Section 5]

Aktor demonstrates an extreme working capital intensity anomaly, with contractual assets surging by €136 million to reach €572 million in the first half of 2025, including €76 million tied to Romanian railway projects and €63 million from domestic Greek infrastructure works. This unbilled contract asset concentration represents approximately 92% of half-year revenues, placing the company in the bottom 5% of infrastructure peers for cash conversion efficiency and significantly exceeding the sector median working capital absorption rate.

Peer AVAX maintains positive net working capital of €192.82 million with substantially lower contractual asset buildup, highlighting Aktor's outlier position. The massive receivables accumulation creates acute vulnerability to project certification delays and payment timing risks that materially differentiate Aktor's liquidity profile from better-capitalized regional competitors. Aktor's capital deployment ambition represents a sector outlier, with management targeting a €3 billion fiveyear capex program supported by an upcoming €650 million share capital increase, including €1.10 billion allocated to renewable energy projects and €1.30 billion to concessions and PPP activities. This investment intensity significantly exceeds the capital deployment rates of regional peers and places Aktor in the top 5% of Greek infrastructure companies for balance sheet transformation velocity.

Competitor AVAX targets a substantially lower medium-term EBITDA of €150 million by 2029, highlighting the relative aggressiveness of Aktor's growth ambitions.
