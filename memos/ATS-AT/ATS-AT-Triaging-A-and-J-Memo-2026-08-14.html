1.
Business: AT&S manufactures high-end integrated circuit substrates and printed circuit boards for micro servers, data centres, 5G base stations and autonomous driving
2.

3.

4.

5.
Margin character: FY25/26 EBITDA of EUR 418m at a 23.3% margin, reported EBIT of EUR 65.6m at 3.7%, and a full-year net loss of EUR 25.6m after EUR 100m finance costs
6.
Balance sheet: Year-end net debt of EUR 1,332m at 3.2x net debt to EBITDA, down from 5.3x, with an equity ratio of 22.6% against a 30.0% target
7.
Footprint: Manufacturing at Chongqing, Shanghai, Kulim and Leoben, with approximately 88% of production in Asia and 12,944 employees at FY25/26 year-end
8.
Customers: Direct sales to Apple, Intel and AMD through six global sales offices, with the five largest customers at 76.5% of revenue in Q1 FY26/27
9.

10.
Investment style: A capital-intensive cyclical in an investment-phase turn, not a compounder; earnings track utilisation, substrate pricing and the Kulim and Chongqing ramps
