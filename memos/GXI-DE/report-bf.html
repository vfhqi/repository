Specialty focus shift: Value is concentrating in complex, highly selective therapies that require advanced drug-device combination platforms.

Energy price hedging: Volatility in energy markets is mitigated by long-term fixed supply agreements and financial derivatives.
11.
Secured supplier relationships: The company's raw material price risks are assessed as low due to stable regional sourcing networks.

Customer-centric cost absorption: Under certain conditions, customers if purchasing in exceptionally high volumes can negotiate volume-based pricing discounts.
