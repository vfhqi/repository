The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • E&S revenue is driven by backlog conversion, not orders. Group backlog was a record CHF 442.3m at 30-Jun-26 (E&S 362.5, service 79.8), 1.22x trailing sales. I convert 55% to 65% of opening E&S backlog within twelve months plus in-year standard isolators, assume H2 2026 order intake of about CHF 200m (H1 247.6), about 470 in 2027, a plateau at about 480 in 2028 as large-pharma capex peaks in 2027 (Deutsche Bank, 15-Oct-26), and recovery from 2029 on US onshoring and Annex 1 upgrades. I lean conversion later than consensus because 2024 and 2025 both saw customer-driven postponements (about 30 projects at end-2024; a CHF 40m GLP-1 project moved to 2027; CHF 15m to 20m of backlog at risk of cancellation and excluded from planning). E&S share drifts down 1 to 2 points a year in standard lines (turnkey rivals), offset by rising project values in complex ADC, eBeam and aseptic-toxic lines.
  • S&C revenue is driven by the installed base (over 1,100 isolators, mandatory requalification, spare parts, retrofits), consumables (Aseptic Technologies closed vials, ABC Transfer transfer bags), Metronik software, and price of +2% to +4% a year. Organic growth 12% (2027), 11% (2028), 10% (2029 to 2030).

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
