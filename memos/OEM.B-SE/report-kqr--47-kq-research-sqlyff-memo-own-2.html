Higher rates continued to limit the headroom for multiple expansion among compounders as central bank policies fluctuated.

3.

Inderes Commissioned Research reported in November 2026 that it raised its cost of equity to 11% to reflect a rising interest rate outlook. This adjustment in discount rates systematically compresses the present value of future cash flows expected from acquisitions. For premium compounders, a higher risk premium is required when sovereign bond yields offer attractive income to institutional investors. This dynamic forces a structural asset reallocation that increases volatility across capital markets.
