EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Medacta Group operates as a specialist designer and manufacturer of innovative orthopedic implants, delivering industry-leading organic growth through surgeon-driven education and personalized digital platforms across joint reconstruction, extremities, and spine segments. The company's vertically integrated Swiss manufacturing model captures peak value chain margins while maintaining deep clinical switching costs through its proprietary M.O.R.E. Institute education network and MySolutions personalized ecosystem.

Financial performance demonstrates exceptional top-line expansion with revenues scaling from €302.50M in FY2020 to €683.80M in FY2025, maintaining adjusted EBITDA margins of 27.90% despite persistent Swiss Franc translational headwinds. The Siccardi family's controlling 69.45% ownership ensures tight alignment with minority shareholders, supporting disciplined capital allocation focused on high-return organic expansion rather than dilutive transformational acquisitions.

Core business model and product portfolio dynamics

Joint reconstruction anchors the financial model, with knees and hips driving 81.2% of FY2025 revenues through differentiated clinical platforms. The GMK SpheriKA system represents the first and only implant specifically optimized for Kinematic Alignment, propelling knee growth to 20.7% in constant currency during FY2025. The AMIS anterior minimally invasive hip approach penetrates over 80% of Medacta's hip implant procedures, offering aesthetic and patient recovery benefits. Extremities revenues surged 46.2% in constant currency to €72.1M in FY2025, accelerated by the strategic acquisition of Parcus Medical for sports medicine integration.

The MySolutions Personalized Ecosystem covers approximately 40% of joint, shoulder, and spine implants through CT-derived 3D planning and patient-matched cutting guides, while NextAR augmented reality provides real-time surgical guidance. NextAR hardware is placed to drive consumable instrument pull-through rather than sold as capital equipment, bypassing hospital budget constraints. Revenue is primarily driven by highmargin implant consumables and single-use instruments, with procedural predictability reducing macroeconomic cyclicality risk. Outpatient Ambulatory Surgical Centers represent a primary growth engine in the US, where surgeon-controlled purchasing decisions are substantially easier to win than hospital tenders.

Financial profile and peer-relative performance benchmarking

Medacta registered a 17.40% constant currency revenue CAGR from FY2021 to FY2025, dramatically outpacing Stryker's 10% organic growth rate and mature peer averages. Gross margin compressed to 67.10% in FY2025 from 68.10% in FY2023 due to Swiss Franc translational appreciation, though constant currency adjusted EBITDA reached a strong 29%. Total net financial debt of €135M represents 1.01x net debt to EBITDA, maintaining a highly conservative leverage profile with total equity expanding to €456.60M in FY2025.

Capital expenditures escalated to €137M in FY2025, with management targeting a €1 of capex for every €1 of sales growth model to support instrument set placement. Cash conversion remains lower than peers at 24% FCF/EBITDA, though free cash flow improved to €15.70M in FY2025 from €8.30M in FY2024. The Board proposed a dividend of CHF 1.10 per share for FY2025, representing a 60% year-on-year increase reflecting disciplined shareholder return expansion.

Value chain positioning and competitive market structure

The serveable global orthopedics market is valued at approximately $53B, with knee reconstruction representing $9.50B and exhibiting consistent mid-single-digit annual expansion. The top three players control more than 80% of global hip sales, while Zimmer Biomet and Stryker alone control over 60% of the global knee market. Reconstructive joint implant average selling prices historically decline by -1% to -2% annually due to persistent commercial and state payer pricing erosion. Specialized design and OEM manufacturing stages capture typical gross margins of 65% to 75% and EBIT margins of 20% to 30%, representing peak value accrual. Medacta manufactures core implants fully inhouse in Switzerland, bypassing contract manufacturing bottlenecks while benefiting from Nairobi protocol tariff protection. A new 10,000 square meter automated warehouse launching in northern Italy in 2026 will streamline European distribution and reduce downstream handling costs.

Customer-side switching costs and structural lock-in mechanisms

Medacta's single largest customer accounts for approximately 1.60% of total group sales, with the top 10 hospital accounts collectively representing approximately 11% of global revenues. Outpatient ASCs account for close to 60% of US revenues, where purchasing decisions are directly controlled by operating surgeons rather than hospital procurement departments. Supplying a new customer with a basic joint instrument kit costs between €30k and €50k, with a 6-to-12-month lag between upfront investment and initial implant sales.

The M.O.R.E. Institute trained more than 3,400 surgeons globally across 675 reference centers in FY2025, with approximately 70% of attendees becoming active purchasing customers within 24 months. Surgeons with more than 3 years of Medacta experience generate 84% of total group sales, supported by 5% of annual sales reinvested into education infrastructure. Approximately 40% of knee, shoulder, and spine procedures utilize MySolutions personalized planning, with single-use GMK Efficiency kits and NextAR AR-guidance creating capital-light surgeon lock-in.

Operator quality and competitive moat sustainability assessment

The Siccardi family maintains a dominant 69.45% controlling stake under a unified shareholders' agreement, with CEO Francesco Siccardi holding a biomedical engineering degree and leading key product development since 2002. The M.O.R.E. Institute's intensive, peer-led medical education converts and locks in surgeons, training more than 3,400 globally across 675 reference centers to build a highly loyal clinical-academic community. MySolutions utilizes the proprietary MyBody database containing over 100,000 anatomical reconstructions, with GMK SpheriKA representing the only implant designed specifically to optimize Kinematic Alignment knees.

Absolute adjusted EBITDA expanded by 19.10% to €190.80M in FY2025, with adjusted EBITDA margins improving to 27.90% from 27.10% in FY2024 through centralized Swiss manufacturing scale. Stringent FDA 510(k) and EU MDR approval pathways demand multi-year clinical trial datasets, while institutional surgeon loyalty makes practitioners highly reluctant to switch primary implant vendors once established. The single largest BB#2 risk remains persistent Swiss Franc translational headwinds eroding reported gross margins, with unfavorable currency appreciation against the EUR and USD serving as the primary early warning signal.
