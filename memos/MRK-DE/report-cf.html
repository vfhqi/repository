EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Merck KGaA has undergone a wholesale generational leadership transition, with Group CEO Kai Beckmann assuming the role in May 2026 alongside new divisional heads across Life Science, Healthcare, and Electronics, positioning the company for a decisive operational inflection following multi-year cyclical headwinds. The group executed deliberate portfolio streamlining through the divestiture of legacy Surface Solutions in July 2025, while simultaneously acquiring SpringWorks Therapeutics for EUR 3.9 billion to establish a dedicated rare disease platform and agreeing to acquire Bio-Techne for USD 11.3 billion to capture advanced multi-omics capabilities.

Organic sales growth reaccelerated to 4.1% in Q2 2026, outpacing flat European pharma peers, while underlying EBITDA pre margin reached 29.4%, exceeding Entegris's 27.7% margin profile. The investment thesis centers on normalized bioprocessing demand patterns driving Process Solutions consumables recovery, accelerating semiconductor materials growth in advanced AI nodes, and the strategic rare disease portfolio expansion, partially offset by severe Mavenclad patent cliffs and elevated leverage from transformative acquisitions.

Materiality of Change and Strategic Positioning

Leadership continuity catalyst: Group CEO Kai Beckmann brings deep internal M&A experience from leading the Electronics division, while new Electronics chief Benjamin Hein previously led Group Strategy and the company's semiconductor transformation, ensuring operational continuity during cyclical recovery.

Pure-play portfolio transition: The group successfully divested legacy Surface Solutions in July 2025 to focus entirely on high-value semiconductor markets, while launching a new three-unit Life Science go-tomarket model in January 2026 organizing operations around Process, Discovery, and Advanced Solutions.

Transformative biological expansion: Merck agreed to acquire US-based Bio-Techne for USD 11.3 billion to capture advanced multi-omics segments and acquired SpringWorks Therapeutics for EUR 3.9 billion to establish a dedicated clinical rare disease platform, dramatically reshaping the therapeutic portfolio. Elevated leverage pressure: Net debt-to-EBITDA pre reached 1.5x after the transformative Bio-Techne multi-omics acquisition, though leverage remains well below the 3.5x maximum tolerated for high-priority strategic assets, temporarily constraining capital flexibility.
