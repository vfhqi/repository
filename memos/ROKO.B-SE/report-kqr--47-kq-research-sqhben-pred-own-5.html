Executive Summary

Section summary
  1. The evidence only partly answers the Key Question. Private European SMB entry pricing appears to remain disciplined and in the single digits, and competition for niche targets is mixed rather than clearly easing or intensifying. The prices Röko actually paid for its five 2026 acquisitions are not disclosed individually, so the Röko-specific part of the question can only be bounded with estimates. Overall confidence is medium. Overall confidence in the evidence on Röko's transaction multiples and regional target pricing is medium because key transaction parameters remain undisclosed by management. Private pricing remains in single digits. Industry reports indicate that European SMEs with EBITA of EUR 2m to 10m are transacting at EV/EBITDA multiples of 7x to 10x in late 2026. This sits well below the valuations that listed consolidators command. 1 10K The public-private gap persists. Lifco and Lagercrantz trade at high EV/EBITA levels in the high teens to low twenties on consensus. That gap is the core of the multiple-arbitrage logic behind Röko's model. Premium assets are the exception. Exceptionally high-quality private targets can reach EBITDA multiples approaching 15x or even 20x, and the Lok'nStore transaction cleared at 23x. The single-digit thesis therefore holds for typical niche targets but not for scarce, premium assets. Sell-side processes are more common. A former Head of Acquisition at Lifco said approximately 50% to 70% of transactions are now competitive in some way. Advisors also tend to slow execution because they seek to control the timeline. Evidence of price inflation is uneven. Auroora Group expects future acquisition multiples to rise by about 1.5x as it targets larger businesses. By contrast, German small-cap EBIT multiples have come down considerably in recent years.

Buyer classes price differently. Paying 6x to 8x EV/EBITA is described as value-creating for serial acquirers. European private equity buyouts historically clear at 8x to 11x EBITDA, with debt packages capped at a maximum gross leverage of 8x EBITDA. Permanent ownership and founder preference for continuity remain a sourcing advantage for decentralized compounders.

Röko's 2026 deal volume is large, but individual prices are opaque. Röko consolidated Lambda, Access Building Products, Golfshopen, Fri-Jado and CyTech in the first nine months of 2026. The total purchase price was MSEK 2,039. The individual prices paid for targets such as Lambda or CyTech remain undisclosed. The acquired entities added 828 million kronor in sales and 160 million in adjusted EBITA during the nine-month period.

What broker figures assume on the Key Question. DNB Carnegie models Röko's incremental return on capital employed using a 7x EBITA entry multiple. That is consistent with the historical average of 7x for transactions completed between 2019 and 2024. In effect, consensus-style modeling assumes private entry pricing stays near its historical level and that competition does not materially reprice targets.

The assumption is a real sensitivity. If competitive bidding drove average target multiples to 10x, DNB Carnegie indicates the return on incremental capital would decline to 10.0%. That would prevent Röko from doubling its earnings over the next five years. Leverage limits Röko's response to competition.

DNB Carnegie reported net debt to EBITDA of 2.8x, above peers such as Indutrade and Addtech, which sit below 1.5x. The Q3 report itself cites leverage of 2.6x LTM adjusted EBITDA. The two figures differ by source and date, but both point to reduced near-term M&A capacity. Margin dilution is a further caveat, since DNB Carnegie noted that recent transactions have been margin-dilutive. Peer behavior points in different directions. Lifco's nine 2026 acquisitions are a slight deceleration from 13 in

2024. Idun reported third-quarter net sales growth of 26.1% and EBITA growth of 36.5%. Volati's net debt to adjusted EBITDA reached 3.1x. Storskogen has started to grow through acquisitions again. Easing pressure from sponsors is a partial offset, since global leveraged buyout volumes dropped 54% year on year in the third quarter. Even so, the overall M&A pipeline remains broadly positive.

Limitations and what would resolve them. The main gaps are undisclosed individual deal values, reliance on estimated private-transaction databases, and aggregated peer reporting. Further gaps are obscured organic growth in acquired units and unquantified option liabilities. Formal purchase price allocations with separated transaction values would close the main pricing gap.

Subsidiary statutory filings and full-year disclosures would add further clarity. Until then, the evidence supports a view of stable but contestable entry pricing, with Röko's earnings sensitivity concentrated in the entry-multiple assumption and its balance sheet headroom.

  2. Rising competition from private equity and other serial acquirers threatens to inflate entry valuations and reduce Röko's incremental investment returns.

Section 2 – Valuation Multiples for European SMBs in 2026

Section summary
  1. Private European small-to-medium businesses with EBITA of EUR 2m to 10m are acquired at single-digit multiples.
  2. High public market valuations for serial acquirers like Lifco and Lagercrantz maintain a wide multiple arbitrage.
  3. Target private multiples remain highly stable despite rising competition from both private equity and search funds.

Broad Market Valuation Trends

1.
Invaluable database: This analysis is based on 15 relevant sources, eight peer companies, and three comparable precedents, utilizing all retrieved findings to ensure full coverage of European private market valuations. Industry reports indicate that European small and medium-sized enterprises with EBITA of EUR 2m to 10m are transacting at EV/EBITDA multiples of 7x to 10x in late 2026. This valuation baseline represents a significant discount compared to the public market multiples of larger consolidators. Consequently, these entry multiples support a highly profitable multiple arbitrage model for programmatic acquirers.
2.
Programmatic dealmaking: Private capital and listed mid-market firms are increasingly prioritizing outright purchases over minority stakes in late 2026. This focus on full ownership allows consolidators to execute transformational operational changes and capture full synergy benefits. Sector data shows a 22% increase in US and European private equity exits during 2025, which has expanded the target pool. This rising deal supply helps insulate programmatic buyers from pricing pressure in niche verticals.

Regional Valuation Variations

3.
Subdued multiples: Private valuations in the United Kingdom and Germany for target businesses reflect tight financial conditions and cautious buyer underwriting in late 2026. Expert commentary reveals that smaller operators in mature UK niches with solid assets command EBITDA multiples of 7x to 10x. Private portfolios of unquoted mid-market assets in these regions are valued at an average EV/EBITDA multiple of 8.0x. These regional baselines suggest that entry pricing has remained disciplined despite broader inflationary pressures.
4.
Stable pricing: Private transactions in Italy and the Nordics show consistent mid-market multiples because localized niche markets are insulated from foreign bidding wars. For example, small-scale Italian listed operators apply a fixed benchmark of 5.0x EV/EBITDA and 7.0x EV/EBIT to value regional acquisitions. This localized pricing is comparable to Nordic entry levels where disciplined serial acquirers target stable cash flows. Programmatic buyers leverage these regional discounts to maximize cash-on-cash acquisition returns without overpaying.
5.
Historical comparisons: Private mid-market valuation multiples have generally stabilized well below the peak levels recorded during the post-pandemic tech frenzy of 2021. Implied private equity portfolio valuation multiples factored for NAV discounts ranged from 13.4x to 16.8x LTM EV/EBITDA historically. By comparison, late 2026 transaction multiples for SMEs with EUR 2m to 10m EBITA are highly disciplined. This historical reset allows serial acquirers to purchase stable cash flows cheaper than during the prior cycle.

Public Serial Acquirer Valuations

6.
Premium pricing: Public market valuations for high-quality Swedish serial acquirers remain highly elevated compared to private market entry multiples. Financial analysis shows Lifco trading at 19.4x 2026e and 17.8x 2027e EV/EBITA on FactSet consensus. Similarly, peer Lagercrantz trades at 23x to 20x EV/EBITA for the 2026/2027e and 2027/2028e periods. This premium valuation demonstrates that public markets continue to reward proven compounders with highly favorable trading multiples.

For instance, Volati trades at a fair valuation of approximately 11x 2027e EV/EBITA due to its stretched balance sheet. Meanwhile, Sdiptech trades at an adjusted EV/EBITA of 11.1x for 2027e, while peer Storskogen trades at a 2026e EV/EBITA of 12x. These lower peer multiples mean that smaller public consolidators face pressure to demonstrate organic improvement before multiple expansion occurs.
8.
Wider market discount: Swedish small-cap companies trade at a highly attractive valuation discount relative to their large-cap counterparts in late 2026. Small companies trade at a forward P/E of approximately 16x compared to 19x for large companies, representing a 15% discount. Section 3 – Evolution of Sell-Side Auctions Since 2022

Section summary
  1. European small-to-medium enterprise M&A structures have shifted toward competitive processes as intermediaries play a larger role.
  2. Increased broker involvement has lengthened transaction timelines and inflated pricing for premium assets across key European regions.
  3. Listed compounders mitigate auction pressure by employing direct outreach models to secure proprietary bilateral transactions.

Increasing Share of

1.
Sell-Side Auctions Rising competition: European small-to-medium business transactions are increasingly structured as competitive processes rather than bilateral negotiations. Former Head of Acquisition at Lifco AB stated in an expert interview on 1 December 2025 that approximately 50% to 70% of transactions are now competitive in some way or another. This rising competitive share means that programmatic consolidators must act as more active and appealing partners to secure acquisitions.
2.

DNB Carnegie reported on 15 September 2025 that peer company OEM International filters approximately 10 to 25 weekly acquisition proposals from advisors, accounting firms, and banks. This high volume of broker-led deal flows confirms that sell-side advisors have successfully institutionalized the sale process for small businesses.

Calculations by DNB Carnegie on 24 July 2026 revealed that 44% of all historical technical installation M&A deals since 1995 occurred after 2023. This rapid acceleration of dealmaking indicates that brokers are actively pushing family-owned businesses into auctions to maximize transaction values.
