ASML (ASML-NL): what the Q3 2026 results do to the case

These results leave the business side of the case where it was, at the top of the universe, and press hard on the one judgement that sets the recommendation, so the net effect is positive and, on our reading, material: the proof the case said it would wait for until January 2027 has started to arrive a quarter early, through the balance sheet and the order commentary, though not yet as a number.

What changed

The quarter delivered what the case assumed on revenue and more than it assumed on margin, orders and timing.

  • Net sales of EUR 11.66 billion for the three months to 27 September 2026, at the high end of the EUR 11.0 to 12.0 billion guided and in line with consensus. Seventeen EUV systems shipped against sixteen in Q2 2026; immersion DUV shipments of 40 units against 23. Net system sales of EUR 8.37 billion, logic 54 percent and memory 46 percent.
  • Gross margin a record 57.5 percent, 230 basis points above the 55.2 percent consensus (147 basis points above the 56 percent Visible Alpha figure), on productivity-upgrade demand from customers short of cleanroom space. Installed Base Management EUR 3.29 billion, almost EUR 400 million above management's own guidance. Operating margin 44.4 percent. Net income EUR 4.96 billion against EUR 2.12 billion a year earlier; basic EPS EUR 12.95, of which a EUR 500 million revaluation gain on the Mistral AI stake is part.
  • Q4 2026 guided at EUR 14.2 to 15.2 billion of sales, a midpoint of EUR 14.7 billion that is 3 percent above the Street, at a 57 percent gross margin. FY2026 narrowed and raised to EUR 44 to 45 billion from EUR 43 to 45 billion; FY2026 gross margin to about 56 percent from 54 to 56 percent.
  • Management said the planned 30 percent capacity increases for 2027 and 2028 will not meet customer demand and that it is investigating further 2028 expansion, which implies more than the 110 low-NA systems previously communicated. It did not name a number, where the market wanted 120 or 125. Firm orders for 2028 are increasingly being received; customers are expressing interest in 2029 and 2030 slots.
  • Non-current contract liabilities, the deposits for deliveries beyond a year, rose to EUR 9,300.6 million at Q3 2026 from EUR 3,570.3 million at Q2 2026, over EUR 5.7 billion in one quarter. Cash and short-term investments rose to EUR 12,735 million from EUR 7,582 million. EUR 4.1 billion of shares were repurchased in the quarter.
  • Value-based pricing negotiations are complete with key customers: no effect in 2026, partial in 2027 as legacy contracts roll off, full from 2028, and "largely accretive to gross margins". The sell-side's range for the uplift is 50 to 200 basis points.
  • The 2030 model was reconfirmed at EUR 44 to 60 billion of sales and 56 to 60 percent gross margin, not raised. China is still guided to about 20 percent of 2026 sales from 33 percent in 2025. The MATCH Act remains an overhang at about 15 percent of revenue on Jefferies' estimate, with no effect on 2026.
  • Samsung has committed to High-NA for DRAM volume production by 2028; TSMC is targeting 2030 insertion. Supply-chain lead times for critical systems sit at 42 to 58 weeks and sub-tier suppliers are struggling to scale.
  • The sell-side response. Huatai raised FY2026, FY2027 and FY2028 net profit by 27.5, 22.0 and 29.2 percent, to EPS of EUR 42.18, 55.00 and 68.35 on sales of EUR 44.49, 59.31 and 74.88 billion. Bernstein raised FY2026 and FY2027 EPS to EUR 43.23 and 57.18 from EUR 38.91 and 53.56, FY2028 to EUR 84 (19 percent above consensus), and models 2030 sales of EUR 98.1 billion at a gross margin well above 60 percent. Jefferies kept Hold and EUR 1,650, raised FY2027 EPS 8 percent to EUR 60.8, but models flattish 2028 margins and 109 low-NA systems and warns that gross margin could fall to 55.2 percent in 2028 if supply loosens. Morgan Stanley kept Overweight and EUR 1,700 but trimmed its mid-cycle multiple to 25x FY2028 earnings from 27x, on doubt that more than 110 low-NA tools can ship in 2028. Rothschild & Co Redburn, Buy and EUR 2,100, calls the discount to the peer group unprecedented. ING said it had rarely seen such a combination of performance and visibility in twenty years. On the other side, Huatai expects cloud capital-expenditure growth to slow to 35.8 percent in 2027 from 91.3 percent in 2026 and Morgan Stanley judges an AI capital-expenditure deceleration highly likely from 2027.

Against the case: the case's modal path had FY2026 EPS at about 39.0 EUR on a 55 percent gross margin, a gross margin of about 57 percent not until FY2028, 110 low-NA systems in 2028 as "probable, about 60 percent", and the upgrade wave as having crested so that further upgrades were "a coin toss". The quarter printed the case's FY2028 margin in FY2026 before any pricing benefit, put the brokers' FY2026 EPS at EUR 42 to 43, and restarted the upgrade wave.

The price: 1,528.2 EUR on 14 October 2026, 11.8 percent above a 200-day average of 1,366.82 EUR, minus 0.5 percent over three months and plus 75.7 percent over twelve, on 23.6 times consensus earnings twenty-four months forward (64.69 EUR), the 50th percentile of the stock's own ten-year range. The case's entry level of about 1,200 EUR (about 1,250 EUR at the ten-year median multiple; about 1,110 EUR under the adverse drop-through) is not crossed, and the case's alarm at the 200-day average is not triggered. The dashboard's revision series of 15 October, which can carry at most a day of the post-results changes, reads EPS plus 5 percent over one month, plus 23 percent over three and plus 39 percent over six, target prices flat over one month, a buy share of 74 percent, and pinned letters of technical momentum A, sell-side earnings momentum A and valuation C.

The challenge

The results admit the case's own reading: an in-line quarter whose beat came from the cyclical component, now being extrapolated by the Street past the company's own frame. We state that reading last, because it is not the strongest. Three readings the case does not hold are stronger.

The first, and the strongest: the proof the case was waiting for has started to arrive, and the case's rule is now being applied against evidence that meets its own test. The case holds the inflection cyclical until proven structural, names the proof as 2028 order coverage observable from January 2027, and says that datum alone would move its FY2029 from 68 EUR toward 85 EUR and the 'Investment Thesis' from C to B. This quarter: firm 2028 orders are being received; the balance of deposits for deliveries beyond a year more than doubled in a single quarter, by over EUR 5.7 billion, which is the hardest form of order evidence this company publishes now that bookings are disclosed annually; management says the capacity it planned for 2028, the 110 systems the case's bull branch required, is not enough; and customers are asking for 2029 and 2030. The case's plateau rests on a mechanism, that "the capacity built through 2028 is absorbed" in 2029. If capacity is still being added into 2029 (the second campus supports capacity from 2029, on Morgan Stanley's reading) and 2029 demand is being pre-ordered, the plateau has lost its mechanism, and a plateau without a mechanism is a base rate, not a forecast. The case concedes this in its own words: "our FY2029 plateau is a base-rate judgement rather than a datum". For this reading to be right, three things must hold: the non-current deposits are for 2028-and-later tools and are non-refundable, which the case says they are; the extra capacity can be built, which turns on Zeiss optics, sub-tier suppliers and 42 to 58 week lead times, and is the one thing Morgan Stanley will not yet credit; and the 2029 and 2030 interest is demand rather than double-ordering under scarcity. The third is the weak point, and nothing in the quarter tests it.

The second: the case's gross-margin driver is mis-sized, not merely early. The case's modal path has gross margin at about 57 percent in FY2028, giving back about 100 basis points in FY2029 as the software-upgrade component, about 200 basis points and cyclical, fades; its bull side is 60 percent by 2030. The company printed 57.5 percent in Q3 2026 and guided 57 percent for Q4, with the pricing reset contributing nothing until 2027 and nothing in full until 2028. So the margin the case puts at the top of its path is the floor before pricing, and the pricing lands in 2028, the year the case has the upgrade margin fading. Bernstein has gross margin well above 60 percent by 2030 and FY2028 EPS of EUR 84; Deutsche Bank sees it trending toward 60 percent by 2028. The size matters and is bounded: on the case's own FY2028 revenue of 63,000 EURm, three points of gross margin is about EUR 1.9 billion of gross profit, about 4 EUR a share after tax on the case's share count, so margin alone does not bridge the case's 62 EUR to Bernstein's 84 EUR. The rest of that gap is units and price, which is the first reading. For the margin reading to be right, the pricing must apply broadly across the NXE and immersion platforms rather than sit in High-NA and upgrades, the contract roll-off must run as described, early High-NA mix dilution must be small, and customers must not claw the price back through the uptime rebates the expert calls describe. None of that is disclosed, and the sell-side's own 50 to 200 basis point range spans the difference between the case being right and wrong on this line.

The third, from the bear side, and one the case half holds without drawing the conclusion: the Street has now extrapolated past the company's own frame two years earlier than the case feared, and management declined the invitation to endorse it. The case's Key Risk is "a 2029 the company has not claimed", with consensus FY2029 revenue of 77,921 EURm against a 2030 ceiling of 60 billion EUR, to be resolved at the 10 June 2027 Capital Markets Day. After this quarter Huatai carries EUR 74.88 billion of sales for 2028, and management, asked in effect to raise the frame, reconfirmed it and would not name a 2028 unit count. The case reads the frame as probably stale. The reading it does not hold is that management, whose record as the case notes includes two guidance cuts in four years, is deliberately not claiming what the Street models, and that the reckoning therefore comes not in June 2027 but in January 2027, when a 2028 capacity number of 115 to 125 systems meets estimates built on more. For this to be right, the capacity investigation must land near its low end, which is what the supply-side evidence supports (42 to 58 week lead times; no fungibility between High-NA and Low-NA optics), and the AI capital-expenditure deceleration Morgan Stanley and Huatai expect from 2027 must reach customers' orders before it reaches ASML's shipments. On this reading the park is right and the entry level is wrong in the other direction, because the downside branch is bigger when the estimates it falls from are higher.

The case's own reading, that the beat is the cyclical component in its purest form (in-line sales; a margin beat on upgrades bought because cleanrooms are full; a tenth of the quarter's net income from a revaluation gain; EUR 4.1 billion of buyback, which one reading takes as capital returned ahead of a downturn rather than invested in supply resilience), is true as far as it goes. It fails as a complete reading of this quarter on one fact. The EUR 5.7 billion rise in long-dated deposits is not upgrade revenue; it is cash for tools in 2028 and beyond, and the "false friend" archetype the case fits itself to has no place for customers paying years ahead for the thing that is supposed to roll over.

What would settle it

  • The second Earnings Review, four working days on, will carry the landed consensus: FY2026 to FY2029 EPS and revenue after Huatai, Bernstein, Jefferies and Morgan Stanley, the one-month revision skew the dashboard cannot yet show, the target-price changes, and whatever the Q3 regional split says about China's share. It will not carry a 2028 capacity number, because management declined to give one; it will not carry the pricing roll-off schedule or a basis-point uplift by quarter, because the company has not committed to disclosing either and the sell-side's 50 to 200 basis point range is a guess; and it will not carry Q4 China system sales, which print in January 2027. It settles the size of the upgrade leg and nothing about the plateau.
  • The January 2027 results settle the first reading: the 2028 capacity decision in units, the annual backlog, FY2027 guidance, and Q4 2026 China system sales against the case's 2.5 billion EUR threshold. A unit number of 120 or more with the backlog up is the case's proof delivered; 110 to 115 with a backlog that has not grown is the third reading.
  • The next two quarters of non-current contract liabilities settle whether the EUR 5.7 billion was deposits for 2028-and-later tools or a timing effect: a sequential fall of more than 20 percent, the threshold the earnings review sets, would say timing; a flat or rising balance through the 2027 delivery year would say orders.
  • Installed Base Management growth below 10 percent year on year for two consecutive quarters, or below 2.8 billion EUR sequentially, would confirm the case's cyclical reading of the margin; a gross margin below 54 percent in any 2027 or 2028 quarter would say the pricing reset failed to replace it.
  • The hyperscalers' 2027 capital-expenditure guidance, due January to February 2027, and TSMC's 2027 capital-expenditure guidance in January 2027 (the case's threshold a cut of more than 10 percent; the earnings review's more than 15 percent) settle whether the deceleration expected from 2027 reaches the order book before it reaches the shipments.
  • The 10 June 2027 Capital Markets Day settles the frame: a 2030 midpoint above 70 billion EUR invalidates the case's plateau; a midpoint at or below it, after this quarter's commentary, would be the strongest evidence yet for the third reading.
  • The pricing question is the one nothing scheduled answers. The company has not committed to quantifying the uplift by platform or by quarter, so the second reading is settled only by the printed gross margin in 2028, which is after every decision the case has to make.
