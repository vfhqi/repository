Section 1 – Business Model + Organisation

Section summary

2.

Experienced leadership team: CEO Dr. Johannes Schmidt and CFO Rudolf Weichert lead a five-member Board of Management.

  2. extreme regional and sectoral fragmentation prevents aggressive consolidation wars, preserving high margin ceilings in industrial and engineering niches
  3. long-term industrial aggregators enjoy distinct cultural and pricing advantages over traditional private equity, which remains constrained by exit horizons and rigid investment mandates

2.

3.
Escalating demographic pressure: Aging owner cohorts face a tightening timeline as 39% of German SME entrepreneurs are currently aged 60 or older.
4.
Chronic transaction failures: Approximately 72% of family-owned companies actively fail to secure a suitable internal or external successor.

Declining generational interest: Young heirs increasingly view taking over traditional family engineering firms as an operational burden rather than an opportunity.

9.

Regionalized operating hubs: Industrial activity is heavily concentrated in Baden-Württemberg and Bavaria, where local federal rules limit national consolidation.
11.
High M&A competition: Technical installation sectors face elevated M&A competition and premium asset pricing within high-population German states.

Moderate growth trajectory: German technical installation and construction markets are forecasted to expand at a modest 2% CAGR through 2030.

Broad industrial footprint: Germany hosts 6,600 machine builders of which nearly 90% are classified as SMEs, presenting a vast target universe.
