BioArctic has transformed from a single-asset research biotech into a financially robust commercial- stage biopharma leader leveraging its validated BrainTransporter technology and growing Leqembi royalties to drive multi-program expansion. Founding lineage – scientific breakthrough: Founded in 2000 by Professor Lars Lannfelt and Pär Gellerfors, Stockholm-based BioArctic AB is a Swedish research-based biopharmaceutical company focused on diseasemodifying treatments for severe central nervous system disorders. The company built its foundation on

Dual-pillar structure – technology platform evolution: BioArctic operates a dual-pillar innovation model combining highly selective monoclonal antibodies targeting misfolded proteins with its proprietary BrainTransporter technology platform. The BrainTransporter platform utilizes transferrin receptormediated active transcytosis across the blood-brain barrier. Preclinical evaluation demonstrates up to a 70-fold increase in brain exposure compared to conventional systemic antibody administration. This enhanced exposure potentially allows for lower systemic dosing, improved therapeutic efficacy, and reduced incidence of adverse effects
