Section 1 – Business Model + Organisation

Section summary
  1. Dometic is transitioning from a cyclical, regionally structured B2B RV equipment supplier to a product- led global outdoor consumer tech player, utilizing a global restructuring program to defend margins.

Operational Activities and Segment Structure

1.
Segment Sales Mix: Dometic operates as a global outdoor technology provider organized into four core segments, with Land Vehicles accounting for 44% of net sales, Mobile Cooling Solutions for 24%, Marine for 23%, and Global Ventures for 9% in FY2025. 14 Jul Dometic Group – Q2 2026 interim_report Dometic net sales and EBITA breakdown by operating segment Land Vehicles Segment: This segment designs and manufactures installed components like air conditioning units, cookers, and windows for recreational vehicles (RVs) and commercial/passenger vehicles (CPVs), alongside standalone products like rooftop tents.
2.
Mobile Cooling Solutions: Formed to unify the Dometic and Igloo brands globally, this segment focuses on passive hardside and soft coolers, active portable coolers, and drinkware products.
3.
Marine Segment Offering: Focusing globally on vessel control, Marine provides steering systems, fuel systems, and marine climate control solutions, mainly targeting leisure boats between 15 and 45 feet.
4.
Global Ventures Segment: This niche vertical comprises Mobile Power Solutions–providing off-grid solar, battery storage, and power electronics–alongside residential and hospitality applications.

Organizational Restructuring and Centralization

5.
Regional Consolidation: To simplify corporate structures and capture cross-border efficiencies, Dometic consolidated its three former regional Land Vehicles divisions (Americas, EMEA, and APAC) into a single global segment in early 2025.
6.
Simplified Reporting: Since segment operations are now managed globally, Dometic disclosures from 2026 onwards provide regional net sales breakdowns but cease reporting regional EBITA.
7.
Operational Execution Drag: While centralization aims to secure group-wide modularity, regional executive exits, such as Land Vehicles Americas Head Todd Seyfert in February 2025, have created interim leadership vacuums.
8.
Centralized IP Management: To institutionalize technical and design leadership, the group centralizes all intellectual property administration under Dometic Sweden AB, managing a portfolio of approximately 1,500 trademark registrations.
9.
Expert Integration Critique: Industry experts argue that aggressive administrative centralization has historically harmed the Marine division's Americas leadership by undermining high-touch local engineering and dealer relationships.

Revenue Model and Macro-Sensitivity

10.
Channels Contribution: The group's primary revenue channels are split between OEM (39% of FY2025 sales), Distribution (32%), and Service & Aftermarket (29%).
11.
OEM Macro-Sensitivity: The OEM channel is highly transactional, highly cyclical, and directly exposed to volatile global production rates of discretionary high-ticket items.
12.
Aftermarket Resilience: The Service & Aftermarket channel provides high-margin, recurring demand for essential repairs and upgrades, leveraging a massive global installed base.
13.
Distribution Classifications: The Distribution model relies on wholesale partners, retail stores, and digital channels, experiencing short-term transactional volatility from distributor inventory destocking cycles.
14.
DTC Strategic Ambition: Dometic is investing in its proprietary digital e-commerce platform with the strategic target of expanding direct-to-consumer sales to represent 20% of non-OEM revenues.

Scale Economies, Cost Programs, and Strategic Actions

15.
Global Restructuring Program: To combat depressed end markets, Dometic launched a global restructuring program in December 2024 to adjust capacities, streamline logistics, and exit non-strategic businesses.
16.
Efficiency Program Extension: In response to persistent RV OEM declines in the US, Dometic expanded the program in Q2 2026, targeting a revised run-rate savings of SEK 900 million by mid-2027.
17.
Footprint Optimization: By Q2 2026, restructuring efforts resulted in the closure of one manufacturing facility and six distribution centers, affecting 405 employees.
18.
Product Innovation Focus: Management continues to protect R&D, lifting its product innovation index–the share of sales from products launched in the last 3 years–to 24% by mid-2026.
19.
Severe Operating Leverage Risks: High capital intensity and fixed overheads leave Dometic exposed to negative operating leverage, which pressured margins during the double-digit RV production downturns of 2024–2026. Other side of the story: Dometic's strategic shift toward lower-ticket consumer and outdoor retail products could fail if its brand lacks consumer pull outside RV circles, leaving the business structurally stuck with highoverhead, low-margin B2B OEM sales.
