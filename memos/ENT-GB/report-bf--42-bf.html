EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Entain operates a diversified multi-brand gambling portfolio across four reportable segments–UK & Ireland, International, Central and Eastern Europe, and the 50/50 BetMGM joint venture–leveraging a unified proprietary technology stack to drive significant operating leverage and omnichannel customer acquisition, though new executive leadership has shifted focus toward capital discipline and portfolio divestments to mitigate severe UK tax hikes.

The segmental revenue distribution shows UK & Ireland representing 34% of 2025 net gaming revenue at £2,185m, International contributing 41% at £2,643m with 60% of online revenue driven by Canada, Spain, and Italy, the BetMGM joint venture delivering £1,065m representing 17% of group sales, and CEE contributing 8% at £522m with 7% year-on-year growth. The group wins and retains customers using a portfolio of over 30 brands including Ladbrokes, Coral, bwin, and SuperSport, with a highly balanced product mix of sports betting generating 49% of NGR and gaming generating 48% in 2025, while online operations represent the primary delivery channel at 73% of 2025 net gaming revenue or £3.84bn, complemented by a retail estate of over 2,400 UK shops generating £1,008m revenue.

Entain's financial profile reveals highly leveraged growth where net gaming revenue expanded from £5,162m in 2024 to £5,325m in 2025 representing 3% reported growth, Group underlying EBITDA reached £1,160m in 2025 representing an 8% constant-currency increase, and online operations achieved an underlying EBITDA of £1,004m translating to a strong 25.70% segment margin. However, the group reported a statutory loss after tax of £681m in 2025 driven by massive non-cash impairment charges reaching £587m including a £488m charge related to UK online gaming tax hikes, alongside a heavy amortisation of acquired intangibles drag at £258m in 2025. The balance sheet carries elevated leverage with reported net leverage at 3.10x in 2025, adjusted look-through leverage improving from 4.30x to 3.60x, a large DPA payment obligation of £291m as of December 2025, capitalized lease liabilities primarily on retail betting shops at £320m, and put option liabilities for CEE minority holdings at £587m.

Adjusted cash flow turned positive to an inflow of £151m in 2025, though cash flows remain constrained by £150m annual DPA payments until late 2027, while management reiterated its long-term target of achieving more than £500m in annual adjusted cash flow by 2028. Entain's core business trades at an implied EV/EBITDA multiple of 4.30x representing a 51% discount to peers, while the 50% stake in BetMGM is valued at £1.45bn which is largely unreflected in the share price.

Outliers worth noting

Entain exhibits an extreme valuation dislocation, trading at an implied EV/EBITDA multiple of 4.30x for its core business–representing a 51% discount to peer medians–while simultaneously holding a 50% stake in BetMGM valued at £1.45bn that remains largely unreflected in the share price. This bifurcated valuation structure creates a statistical anomaly where the sum-of-the-parts appears materially disconnected from the consolidated trading multiple, contrasting sharply with US pure-play operators like DraftKings that command premium multiples despite comparable or inferior margin profiles.
