EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. ArcelorMittal is executing a decisive strategic transformation from volume-centric globalization to disciplined regional capital allocation and downstream premium product mix.
  2. Forward earnings trajectory is driven by $1.8 billion incremental EBITDA pipeline from strategic projects, European trade protections, and aggressive share buyback programs.
  3. Recent earnings delivery consistently exceeded conservative consensus estimates at cyclical inflection points, despite seasonal disruptions and elevated non-operating headwinds.
  4. Management operates as an honest-tracker archetype, aligning capital spending pragmatically to policy visibility and maintaining conservative leverage guardrails.
  5. Sell-side sentiment remains firmly bullish, with major brokers citing structural trade protections and 51% valuation discount to historical averages.
  6. ArcelorMittal holds top-quartile fundamental momentum within European steel peers on margin recovery, leverage discipline, and aggressive capital return velocity.
