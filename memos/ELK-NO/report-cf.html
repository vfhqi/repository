EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Materiality of change

Elkem completed a transformational ownership restructuring in 2026, redeeming 338,338,536 shares from Bluestar through a cashless transaction that left no remaining equity stake, effectively achieving complete listed independence from its former Chinese parent. The Silicones division was divested to Bluestar via a cashless transaction valued at approximately 12.8x 2026e EV/EBITDA, with Bluestar representatives immediately resigning from the board following transaction closing.

Management placed Elkem Iceland under strategic review after prolonged cost inflation, reclassifying it as discontinued operations and assets held for sale. Balance sheet restructuring materially reduced net interest-bearing debt to NOK 6,593 million in Q2 2026 from NOK 9,434 million at year-end 2025, driving the leverage ratio down to 3.2x EBITDA from a proforma peak of 5.0x in Q1 2026. The transition of Dag Teigland to CEO and Helge Aasen to Chairman preserves deep operational continuity, with Aasen having led Elkem for 17 years compared to typical shorter industrial peer tenures.

Top 5-8 ranked key drivers with plausibility

Silicon metal pricing recovery represents the largest driver of forward EPS due to extreme operating leverage in Silicon Products, with DNB Carnegie modeling that every EUR 100/t price increase adds approximately NOK 600 million in EBITDA, and normalizing silicon prices to the 10-year historical average of EUR 2,351/t would yield normalized EPS of NOK 11. The NOK 600 million cost reduction program targets savings starting 3Q26 with high plausibility, supported by expanded headcount targets nearing a reduction of 400 FTEs from an initial 300 target.

Norwegian CO2 compensation scheme stability provided NOK 730 million in government grants in 2025, though the updated regulation requires 40% of compensation to be reinvested into green emissionreduction projects and places a KPI-adjusted cap of NOK 7 billion on total annual industry compensation. Successful divestment of Elkem Iceland carries medium plausibility, with the unprofitable asset generating close to no EBITDA contribution over the last 18 months despite having a carrying value estimated at NOK 1.5 billion on the balance sheet.

Energy hedge arbitrage spreads provide structural cost advantage through long-term contracts securing 80% of Elkem's 3.5 TWh annual electricity consumption in Norway, including a new Statkraft PPA covering 1,534 GWh from 2031 to 2037, though the competitive energy spread has narrowed from historical peak levels as European power prices normalized. The extended Bjølvefossen PPA and flexible furnace capacity enable Elkem to temporarily curtail production and sell power at attractive rates, securing long-term power supply while capitalizing on spot market opportunities.

European silicon metal prices linger near trough levels of EUR 1,635/t versus the historical average of EUR 2,351/t, with global pricing remaining heavily dependent on the cost of Chinese coal for marginal producers, though European protective safeguard measures are expected to gradually support price recovery in domestic metal markets. Regulatory wins include the Norwegian government's approval of Elkem's complaint granting 1.3 million additional free ETS quotas for 2021- 2025, while EU safeguards exempted Norway and Iceland with beneficial country-specific quotas representing 70- 75% of historic sales.

Recent delivery

Q2 2026 headline EBITDA reached NOK 523 million, beating Infront consensus by 29% versus NOK 407 million expected, though DNB Carnegie noted the outperformance was driven by reclassifying Icelandic operations to assets held for sale. Sequential EBITDA improvement in Silicon Products was driven by resumed production at Rana and Salten plants, with divisional EBITDA improving to NOK 319 million in Q2 from NOK 122 million in Q1, while Carbon Solutions EBITDA fell 29% year-over-year to NOK 172 million due to 4% lower sales volumes and depressed average selling prices.

Balance sheet deleveraging was primarily funded via a NOK 1.8 billion equity raise completed during Q2 2026, complemented by a new EUR 1,000 million credit facility comprising a term loan and undrawn revolving credit, with refinanced facilities maturing in June 2031 successfully eliminating NOK 1,000 million of annual maturities over four years.

Forward credibility and management archetype

Elkem explicitly does not provide quantitative guidance, restricting outlooks to qualitative segment commentary, with management classified as an honest-tracker with a cautious bias that typically understates sequential inventory and market rebounds, though outlooks consistently flag cyclical demand drags to mitigate sudden quarterly negative earnings surprises. Historical delivery exhibits frequent operational disruptions including the delayed French Roussillon startup triggering a NOK 185 million EBITDA miss, the Norwegian industry dispute costing NOK 10 million in EBITDA daily, the Salten material fire idling smelting furnaces for several months, and Rana and Salten shutdowns creating a NOK 250 million EBITDA hit. Elkem has suffered repetitive operational setbacks since 2019 limiting through-cycle earnings normalization, with the upcoming NOK 600 million costsaving target carrying high delivery risk based on historical disruptions.

Sell-side positioning

The recommendation mix per Bloomberg consensus shows 63% Overweight and 38% Equal-weight as of August 2026, with price targets spanning from NOK 26 to NOK 40 and the consensus mean target price currently standing at NOK 34.17 versus the last price. Morgan Stanley maintains an Equal-weight rating arguing that silicon price improvements are well-captured in consensus with limited near-term upside, expecting European construction and auto production to remain highly subdued, while noting Elkem trades at approximately 7.7x EBITDA well above its 4.7x historical average.

DNB Carnegie maintains a Buy rating arguing that trough earnings misprice Elkem's assets offering an attractive entry point, highlighting that normalized EPS of NOK 11 implies a P/E of only 3.1x, with the expectation that divestment of the Silicones business will structurally raise throughcycle cash flow.

Fundamental peer positioning

Morgan Stanley notes Elkem trades at approximately 7.7x EBITDA representing a rich valuation versus its 4.7x historic average, with Elkem's valuation historically trading at a 50% discount to specialty peer multiples averaging around 7.4x EBITDA, while historical group EBITDA margins of 11% remained below the 15-20% target achieved by leading downstream chemical peers.

Elkem ranks lower-mid pack in earnings momentum as strategic separations proceed, with high-specialty peers like Shin-Etsu maintaining superior absolute margins of 37% EBITDA driven by high-value specialty functional mix, while Elkem experiences downward consensus revisions of 5% over three months lagging top specialty chemical peers.
