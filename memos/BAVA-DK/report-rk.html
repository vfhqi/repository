1.
Bear Case Credibility: Factual Foundation with Structural Coherence

The bearish investment thesis against Bavarian Nordic rests on a foundation of tangible, filings-backed evidence rather than speculative conjecture. Deferred GSK milestone commitments of DKK 2,566 million represent a concrete cash outflow over the next 12 months, while spiking trade receivables to DKK 1,661 million in late 2024 represent a concrete balance sheet quality deterioration. The quality of bearish allegations surpasses typical short-seller narratives, as unlike peer Emergent BioSolutions which carries a highly distressed CCC1 credit rating, Bavarian Nordic maintains robust solvency, though both companies share a heavy reliance on binary government contracts to sustain high-margin pandemic preparedness stockpiles.

Sell-side skeptics maintain disciplined positions, with Jefferies modeling a downside scenario of DKK 160 per share, representing significant double-digit price downside if core government contracts lapse. The structural coherence of the bear case differentiates it from pattern-based speculation, anchored by high reliance on binary US government contracts to sustain stockpiles of the Jynneos vaccine and strong concerns that free cash flow will fund value-destructive vaccine acquisitions rather than returning capital via buybacks.

The FALSE FRIEND profile has the fewest hard inconsistencies because near-term contract upgrades mask structural erosion, as upgraded full-year 2026 revenue guidance to DKK 5,700 million hides flat underlying travel volume growth models, while public preparedness spikes of DKK 2,500 million create false impressions of stability if driven by phasing.

This profile explains apparent contradictions in the investment narrative where the FALSE FRIEND profile survives with the fewest hard analytical inconsistencies across all commercial categories, yet surging Rabipur sales of DKK 649 million in Q2 are highly inconsistent with outright near-term corporate failure.
