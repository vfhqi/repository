Note on depreciation and amortisation (a deliberate departure from the footnote above). Sonova's adjusted measure, core EBIT, and the consensus built on it include the amortisation of acquired intangibles (about CHF 30m a year by my reconstruction). To keep the model's adjusted EPS on the same basis as the company's core EPS and as consensus, my input includes it, together with right-of-use (lease) depreciation under IFRS 16. Excluding it would lift the model's EPS by about CHF 0.40 a share. Revenue growth is in reported Swiss francs and includes a franc drag of about 1 point a year from FY2028 (local-currency growth 7.4%, 6.3%, 5.6%, 5.8%, 5.8%). The EBITDA margin is core (before restructuring, transaction, litigation and legacy product-liability costs of about CHF 20m to 38m a year) and after IFRS 16. Net financial items include lease interest. The dividend is the dividend declared for each fiscal year (paid the following June), at about 43% of core EPS. Minorities of about CHF 3m to 4m a year are not an input; they are deducted in my cross-check.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
