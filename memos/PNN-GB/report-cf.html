EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 45 findings

Core Investment Thesis and Positioning Pennon Group represents a deeply discounted operational turnaround story following a comprehensive executive leadership reset in April 2026, anchored on a record £3.2 billion AMP8 capital programme but constrained by severe operational underperformance and elevated balance sheet leverage. The stock trades at a 3.6% premium to end-FY27 regulatory capital value (RCV), substantially below United Utilities at 12.6% and representing minimal confidence in operational outperformance delivery.

The new CEO appointment provides a critical fresh start catalyst for operational recovery, positioning Pennon as an exceptional value play relative to richly valued electricity network peers. However, the investment case is severely compromised by persistent outcome delivery incentive (ODI) penalties totaling £42 million net in FY26 and a provisional 1-star environmental performance rating, threatening the 30 basis point outstanding business plan reward.

Balance sheet constraints are acute, with Water Group gearing at 61.8% (upper end of 55-65% target policy) and projected regulated gearing reaching 74% in FY27, eliminating headroom to fund incremental capital programmes.
