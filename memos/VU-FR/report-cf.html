EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 28 findings

Materiality of CHANGE

1.
Strategic pivot acceleration: Vusion is executing a decisive transition from transaction-heavy ESL hardware toward high-margin recurring software and retail media, targeting EUR 2,200 million revenue and 22% EBITDA margin by 2027 under the VUSION-27 strategic plan, while CEO Thierry Gadou maintains extreme concentration of authority through a unified Chairman-CEO structure and the SESIM holding vehicle controlling 10.29% of capital
2.
Severe post-Walmart deceleration risk: Bernstein Research projects new ESL installations will collapse from 262 million units in 2026 to only 145 million in 2027 as the landmark US Walmart rollout completes, driving revenue growth normalization to a 20% CAGR over 2025-2027 compared to the historical 35% CAGR achieved between 2022 and 2025
3.
Margin expansion amid pricing headwinds: Adjusted variable cost margin improved from 25.2% in 2023 to 30.9% in 2025 as adjusted revenue surged from EUR 802 million to EUR 1,527 million, yet Bernstein Research reduced 2027 recurring VAS revenue estimates by 25% to EUR 212.90 million after discovering software pricing compression
4.
Transformative retail media acquisition: The July 2026 agreement to acquire In-Store Media adds EUR 120 million in profitable 2025 revenue and delivers immediate 5.1% accretion to 2027 adjusted EPS estimates, accelerating Vusion's strategic pivot toward high-margin recurring revenue streams
5.
Oligopolistic competitive advantage: Vusion operates within a four-player oligopoly controlling approximately 90% of the global ESL market and achieved an 18.2% adjusted EBITDA margin in 2025, vastly outperforming peer Pricer's 2.4% EBIT margin through superior software integration

Driver 4 - Recurring VAS software scaling (+40% VAS growth, MEDIUM plausibility): Total VAS is guided to grow around 40% in 2026 and reach EUR 650 million representing 30% of sales mix by 2027, though Bernstein Research notes average software pricing per cloud-connected label fell 10.60% year-on-year and retailers have historically shown resistance to high-priced software

Driver 5 - In-Store Media acquisition synergy (+5.1% FY27 EPS, HIGH plausibility): The acquisition of Barcelona-based In-Store Media adds EUR 120 million in highly profitable revenue and is debt-financed with scheduled Q4 2026 closing, providing immediate adjusted EPS accretion of 5.10% for 2027
