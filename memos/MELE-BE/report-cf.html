EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Melexis has navigated a severe cyclical downturn marked by inventory destocking and pricing pressures, yet the company is emerging with strengthened operational oversight, expanded regional capabilities, and renewed momentum in the second quarter of 2026. The executive team, led by CEO Marc Biron and long-tenured CFO Karen van Griensven, has reinforced strategic governance through board expansion that brings crucial nonautomotive and Asian market expertise.

The core investment thesis centers on structural content growth in automotive electrification and ADAS, diversification into high-margin adjacencies like humanoid robotics and AI data center cooling, and disciplined capital allocation within an asset-light fabless model. Revenue growth is returning after a severe cyclical trough, while the gross margin path back to 45% and EBIT margins expanding towards the 25% long-term target begins to materialize.

The business model prioritizes leveraging technology leadership to grow customer business while maintaining reliable customer positioning as "big enough to serve" through volatile industry inventory corrections.
