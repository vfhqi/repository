EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Business model and strategic positioning

1.
Universal banking dominance: PKO-PL operates as Poland's undisputed leader, serving 12.5 million retail customers and nearly 0.7 million businesses through an integrated universal banking model covering deposits, lending, asset management, and bancassurance distribution.
2.
Domestic concentration risk: Operations in Poland represent 98% of the Group's total assets, creating extreme geographic concentration that leaves the bank highly vulnerable to Polish monetary policy shifts and domestic regulatory interventions.
3.
Digital platform leadership: The IKO mobile application has accumulated over 8.7 million active digital users by mid-2026, representing 85% of the bank's active client base and establishing a formidable barrier to retail customer switching.
4.
Rate-sensitive revenue model: Net interest income stagnated year-over-year at PLN 6,074 million in mid-2026, reflecting high sensitivity to terminal rate cuts expected to reach 3.25% by 3Q27 that threaten core profitability.

Financial profile and peer benchmarking

5.
Exceptional operational efficiency: The cost-to-income ratio reached a record-low 29.5% in 2024, demonstrating outstanding scale advantages and delivering an annualized return on equity of 18.4% in the first half of 2026 that significantly outperforms regional banking peers.
6.
Contracting interest margins: Net interest margin reached 4.35% in the first half of 2026, down 59 basis points year-over-year, with Morgan Stanley expecting margins to contract by 56 basis points between 2025 and 2028 as monetary easing accelerates.
7.
Robust capital adequacy: The Common Equity Tier 1 ratio grew to 15.55% in mid-2026, sitting 312 basis points above the minimum required for paying 75% dividend payouts and providing substantial capacity for organic credit expansion.
8.
Resolving legacy litigation: Cumulative FX mortgage provisions reached PLN 24.3 billion since 2019, but provisions decreased to PLN 685 million in the first half of 2026, signaling the tail end of this historical earnings drag.
9.
Superior asset quality: The gross non-performing loan ratio remained low at 3.43% in the second quarter of 2026, supported by machine learning models covering 100% of consumer and mortgage decisions that contain credit risk proactively.

Industry dynamics and competitive position

10.
Structural credit underpenetration: Total loans in Poland represent only 32% of GDP compared to nearly 80% in the Euro Area, providing substantial headroom for credit expansion over the medium term as household leverage normalizes toward European benchmarks.
11.
Moderate market concentration: The market exhibits a Herfindahl-Hirschman Index of approximately 753 as of 2024, with the top five Polish banks comprising 59% of total sector assets as consolidation continues and smaller players face minimum scale pressures.
12.
Disciplined deposit pricing: Current accounts represent about 68% of system deposits, keeping funding costs highly suppressed, while sector-wide excess liquidity has limited competitive pressure on deposit pricing and preserved interest margins across the cycle.
13.
Elevated state control: State-controlled institutions hold a 47% share of total sector assets, introducing governance risks and potential political interference that could disrupt strategic execution and capital allocation decisions.

Value-chain economics and margin sustainability

14.
Dominant funding position: Deposit gathering achieves a 4.35% net interest margin, which remains well above European banking peers, enabling PKO-PL to act as a price setter in the Polish retail deposit market due to its unmatched domestic scale.
15.
High-margin fee streams: PKO TFI holds an 18.20% share of the total Polish investment fund market and controls a 30.8% share of Employee Capital Plans assets, generating resilient non-interest income that buffers against interest rate cyclicality.
16.
Regulatory margin pressures: Credit underwriting margins are periodically compressed by regulatory mortgage holidays and legal costs on foreign currency books, demonstrating how political interventions can materially erode profitability despite operational excellence.

Customer lock-in and retention dynamics

17.
Exceptional digital engagement: PKO's IKO application reaches 8.7 million active digital users, outperforming mBank's 4.2 million, with active digital users representing 85% of total active customers that create substantial behavioral switching costs through platform integration.
18.
Favorable deposit characteristics: Core deposits constitute a resilient 94.3% of total deposits, with retail deposit betas trending at approximately 13% during rate hikes that significantly mitigate funding cost increases during monetary tightening cycles.

20.
Robust economic moat: PKO's PLN 608 billion balance sheet dwarfs the second-largest bank's PLN 368 billion scale, with retail deposits representing every fourth Polish zloty saved and yielding an insurmountable competitive funding advantage through sheer market dominance.

Ambitious profitability targets: The 2025-2027 strategy targets a return on equity above 18% by 2027, with cost-to-income ratio targeted below 35%, both achievable given structural credit headroom and superior operational efficiency demonstrated historically.
