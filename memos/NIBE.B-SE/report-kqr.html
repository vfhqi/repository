This regulatory transition provides an exciting, multi-billion-euro market opportunity that could double the historical European building renovation rate over the next decade. The mechanism drives high-margin retrofits because energy-efficient windows and doors typically generate up to twenty percent energy savings for drafty residential properties. The purest listed plays are Inwido for building envelopes and Lindab for ventilation systems, both of which maintain strong pricing power in premium categories.
