Section 3 – Spot Market Lags and Contract Structures

Section summary

  2. The company maintains high spot exposure compared to d'Amico International Shipping, which uses extensive time charters to secure stable earnings.

Voyage Revenue Recognition and Rate Lags

1.
Deferred earnings: TORM utilizes the load-to-discharge method to recognize freight and demurrage revenue over the estimated duration of each individual voyage. This accounting treatment means that no revenue is recognized during empty positioning voyages before loading cargo. Consequently, TORM's realized TCE rates lag behind spot market indices by several weeks as active voyages must complete before new spot rates are fully captured.
