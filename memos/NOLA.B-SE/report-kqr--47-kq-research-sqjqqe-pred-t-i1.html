Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The trajectory of Nolato's earnings revisions over the next 18 months will determine whether the stock can recover its historical valuation multiple.

Significance of the Key Question

1.
Critical inquiry: "how likely is company guidance to be raised, met or reduced at the earnings events over the next 18 months, and how likely are sell-side consensus forecasts, and the net breadth of revisions, to move higher or lower over the same period?"
2.
Significant impact: Resolving this inquiry determines if Nolato can recover from its current depressed valuation multiple of 12x 2027e EV/EBITA back toward its five-year historical average of 15x. If profit margins normalize quickly, analyst revisions will move higher and re-rate the stock. However, prolonged start-up costs or worsening automotive demand will trigger further consensus cuts and suppress the multiple.
3.
Elevated risks: Persistent start-up costs from new medical programs continue to dilute profitability and pressure the group's near-term consolidated earnings per share. Management reported that investments in Hungary and the United States are temporarily impacting margins due to underutilization. Slower volume ramps will delay the anticipated margin recovery beyond the next few quarters.
4.
Material headwinds: Fluctuations in polymer input costs present a major near-term risk because of pricing lag effects. Nolato faces a three-to-four-month delay when passing volatile raw material price increases onto its customers. If oil-driven raw material headwinds persist through early 2027, operating profit will be severely trimmed across both segments.
5.
Strong demand: Robust volume expansion in the Materials segment driven by data centers and defense applications provides a critical buffer against automotive weakness. Materials grew organically by 27% in the third quarter, demonstrating that high-margin product lines are expanding rapidly. However, severe downturns in European automotive production could still drag down overall Engineered Solutions profit.

Strategic Questions and Consensus Trajectory

6.
Accelerating growth: The highly anticipated SEK 700m GLP-1 obesity and diabetes contract in Hungary represents a major strategic milestone for Medical Solutions. DNB Carnegie expects this project to boost Medical segment sales growth by two to three percentage points annually in 2027 and 2028. Resolving the exact timeline of this contract's contribution is critical for forecasting long-term EPS expansion.
7.
Contrary evidence: Some analysts argue that the negative impacts from raw material costs and start-up expenses are entirely temporary and do not impair Nolato's long-term targets. ABG Sundal Collier maintains that these short-term pressures do not affect the company's ability to reach its financial targets of a twelve percent EBITA margin. The broker expects margin expansion to resume once the short-term headwinds subside.
8.
Other side of the story: While near-term margin pressure from start-up costs and raw material lags could trigger consensus cuts, the strong balance sheet and recent acquisitions of Unitron and M&M Qualtech provide substantial earnings offsets. DNB Carnegie highlights that these strategic transactions immediately lift consolidated sales and earnings. Consequently, any operational weakness in traditional automotive or hygiene components might be mitigated by these new high-margin consolidated acquisitions.

Section 4 – Engineered Solutions Performance and Automotive Industry Tailwinds

Section summary
  1. Nolato's Engineered Solutions performance is defined by a sharp divergence between rapid high-margin Materials expansion and cyclical European automotive weakness.
  2. The structural growth of datacenters and defense applications in the Materials business area provides a critical buffer against localized automotive capacity challenges.

Materials Growth and Data Center Demand

1.
Subdued growth: Nolato's Engineered Solutions segment generated Q3 2026 sales of SEK 1,040 million, representing a currency-adjusted organic decline of three percent compared to the prior year. This revenue reduction reflects intense cyclical pressures across traditional end markets, which offset the expansion of newer technical product lines. Consequently, segment EBITA declined to SEK 112 million with an EBITA margin of 10.8 percent, down from 11.6 percent in Q3 2025.
2.
Nolato Engineered Solutions sales breakdown by market area highlighting Materials and Automotive shares Rapid acceleration: Nolato's Materials business area achieved outstanding growth, with currency-adjusted revenues surging twenty-seven percent year-over-year in the third quarter of 2026. This positive development was fueled by robust global shipment volumes for data centers, satellites, and aerospace applications. While traditional industrial products remain under cyclical pressure, the high-specification Materials division has consolidated its position as a key corporate growth engine.
3.
Highly profitable: DNB Carnegie estimates that the Materials business generates an EBITA margin of at least twenty percent, meaning it contributes roughly half of the Engineered Solutions segment's operating profit. The broker notes that the Chief Financial Officer confirmed Materials' high profit margins remain sustainable despite volatile raw material inputs. This highly lucrative product mix provides a critical buffer against traditional industrial declines, protecting the group's consolidated earnings per share.
4.
Strong demand: Nolato is capitalising on substantial structural demand from data centers and artificial intelligence infrastructure, leveraging its proprietary Trishield technology. The company applies gaskets to optical modules in server cabinets to provide slim and effective shielding solutions against electromagnetic interference. Management expects the rapid development of low Earth orbit satellites to sustain this favorable demand environment throughout 2027.

Automotive Weakness and Restructuring Charges

5.
Severe contraction: Nolato's Automotive market area experienced a sharp volume decline in the third quarter of 2026 due to soft underlying demand and customer model changes. In particular, production challenges at Volvo Cars in Gothenburg weighed heavily on sales volumes during the period. Although other consumer segments showed stability, automotive weakness represents a persistent drag on Engineered Solutions' near-term profitability.
6.
Contrary evidence: Nolato's Materials segment saw its shipment volumes for the automotive market area increase significantly in Q3 2026 despite overall sector weakness. This counter-cyclical growth is driven by the ongoing electrification of vehicle platforms requiring advanced electromagnetic shielding. Consequently, high-value component content can expand even when aggregate light-vehicle assembly rates contract.
7.
Necessary alignment: Nolato has adjusted its automotive cost structure in Gothenburg because production capacity had been designed for higher commercial volumes than those currently ordered. DNB Carnegie expects the weak automotive volumes to persist through Q4 2026, which prevents rapid margin recovery in Gothenburg. While management has implemented initial cost alignment measures, further capacity consolidation could trigger restructuring charges if European automotive assembly fails to rebound in 2027.
8.
Grim outlook: Ambit Capital research highlights that the macroeconomic outlook for European automotive original equipment manufacturers remains exceptionally grim, with no signs of near-term recovery. Several major past car manufacturers in Europe are looking to cut costs dramatically, which pressures component suppliers. This long-drawn European automotive struggle suggests that Nolato's traditional automotive sales will remain severely constrained throughout 2027.
9.
Depressed production: Deutsche Bank Research reports that S&P Global Mobility forecasts European light-vehicle production at 17.2 million units for 2026, representing a nineteen percent drop compared to 2019. This weak backdrop contributes directly to low utilization rates across European factories, forcing suppliers to implement capacity consolidation programmes. Slower automotive manufacturing activity limits the potential for standard component suppliers to achieve positive operating leverage.

Peer Performance and Industrial Conditions

10.
Superior performance: Trelleborg AB reported a strong third quarter in 2026 with an adjusted EBITA margin improving to 19.3 percent, significantly higher than Nolato's Engineered Solutions margin of 10.8 percent. While Trelleborg reported solid six percent organic sales growth, it also noted that European automotive demand was softer during the quarter. This comparison highlights Trelleborg's superior product mix and decentralized model, which shield its earnings from localized industrial downturns.

DNB Carnegie notes that Trelleborg's industrial segment remains highly resilient due to booming aerospace and semiconductor demand. Robust expansion: Danish industrial peer SP Group continues to expand its international footprint and reported strong performance, particularly within its healthcare-related polymer operations. DNB Carnegie uses SP Group as the key peer for Engineered Solutions when determining relative valuation multiples. Unlike Nolato's challenged automotive lines, SP Group has successfully leveraged its diversified industrial exposure to sustain steady growth.

The broker notes that European automakers continue to absorb a portion of tariff costs while offering high incentives to maintain volume. This deflationary pricing environment creates severe headwinds for suppliers like Nolato that rely on traditional combustion engine platforms.

That historical intervention successfully stopped the severe earnings decline of 2022 and 2023, paving the way for sequential margin recovery in subsequent quarters. Applying this same operational playbook to the Gothenburg automotive capacity alignment would help insulate future earnings from European manufacturing weakness.

Other side of the story: While weak European automotive demand and Gothenburg model changes present nearterm headwinds, the structural expansion of high-margin Materials provides a powerful offset. ABG Sundal Collier highlights that the group's long-term twelve percent EBITA margin target remains entirely achievable as mix improves and raw material pricing lags subside. Consequently, the rapid growth of non-automotive applications will support a long-term earnings recovery and multiple expansion.

Section 5 – Strategy, Financial Targets, and Capital Allocation Under New Leadership

Section summary
  1. The strategic direction under Nolato's new leadership prioritizes selective acquisitions and portfolio streamlining to achieve updated financial targets.
  2. Historical transitions among Swedish mid-cap companies show that margin recovery is achievable through active structural and operational adjustments.

Strategic Focus Under New CEO

1.
Seamless leadership: Anders Björklund assumed the role of President and CEO of Nolato immediately following the release of the second-quarter report on July 17, 2026, after serving as the Head of Engineered Solutions. Klas Forsström, Chairman of the Board, noted that Björklund was heavily involved in shaping the group's growth strategy over the preceding four years. The new chief executive inherited an organization focused on expanding its presence in highly regulated sectors such as medical technology. Consequently, this transition represents a continuation of established long-term corporate strategies rather than a major operational shift.
2.
Disciplined expansion: Nolato completed the strategic acquisitions of Unitron and M&M Qualtech in September 2026, adding a combined SEK 600 mm in annual sales with group-level margins. These mechatronics businesses expand the group's capabilities in electronics-intensive medical systems and enhance its position in customers' value chains. To streamline operations, management simultaneously divested the Romanian operations during the third quarter. This active portfolio pruning reflects the CEO's intention to concentrate capital on high-yield areas with strong sustainable growth prospects.
3.
Executive restructuring: Nolato restructured its Group Management in October 2026 by appointing Cecilia Hellner as the new President of Engineered Solutions to fill the vacancy left by the CEO. This corporate management team now comprises eight senior executives, including Deputy CEO and CFO Per-Ola Holmström and medical head Johan Iveberg. This team is tasked with driving profitable growth and integrating the newly acquired mechatronics businesses. Investors expect these leadership changes to improve operational execution and margin performance in the upcoming fiscal years.

Financial Targets Versus Performance

4.
Ambitious objectives: Nolato established new financial targets at its Capital Markets Day in March 2025, which include organic growth exceeding 8%, an EBITA margin above 12%, and return on capital employed exceeding 15% over a business cycle. Christer Wahlquist, the former CEO, stated that these goals represent the next phase of Nolato's strategic journey toward high-margin growth. However, the company's recent operational results have fallen short of these benchmarks. Analysts continue to monitor whether the new CEO can successfully steer the group toward these targets over the current cycle.
5.
Operational gap: Nolato reported an EBITA margin of 10.2% and return on capital employed of 12.4% for the first nine months of 2026. This current profitability sits well below the updated 12% EBITA margin target and the 15% return on capital employed target. Additionally, nine-month organic sales growth was only 3%, reflecting weak industrial demand and automotive sector headwinds. These subdued metrics emphasize that achieving the long-term targets requires a substantial sequential acceleration in production utilization.
6.
Reduced investment: Nolato projected its full-year 2026 capital expenditure to range between SEK 600 mm and SEK 650 mm, representing a planned decrease from the SEK 788 mm spent in 2025. Per- Ola Holmström, the Chief Financial Officer, confirmed that the primary capital investments for the Hungarian facility expansion have been completed. This lower capital intensity should help preserve liquidity and support free cash flow generation. However, any delayed customer ramp-ups could dilute the return on capital employed from these completed assets.
7.
Subdued outlook: DNB Carnegie maintains a Buy recommendation on Nolato but notes that substantial earnings upside depends on approaching the long-term financial targets. The broker's sum-of-the-parts valuation applies a 2026e EV/EBITA multiple of 18x to Medical Solutions and 14x to Engineered Solutions. They expect a gradual margin recovery as new customer projects in Hungary reach full commercial production. However, near-term estimate risks remain high, and achieving the twelve percent margin target is highly dependent on a cyclical volume recovery.

Historical Precedents of Swedish Mid-Cap Transitions

8.
Margins recovery: Instalco represents a relevant Swedish industrial precedent where the founder and main shareholder, Per Sjöstrand, returned as CEO in July 2025 to restore EBITA margins toward 8%. Under Sjöstrand's leadership, the group shifted focus back to profitable growth, pricing discipline, and better project selection. This transition shows that active management intervention can reverse margin dilution. For Nolato, the appointment of an internal leader like Anders Björklund suggests a similar focus on operational execution and cost discipline.
9.
Strategic restructuring: Storskogen Group experienced a leadership transition where Christer Hansson assumed the role of CEO in 2024 to execute critical turnaround initiatives. Under Hansson's leadership, Storskogen divested nine loss-making units and merged entities into smaller clusters to unlock scale synergies. This active portfolio restructuring resulted in higher margins and reduced earnings volatility. This precedent suggests that Nolato's divestment of its Romanian operations under the new CEO could similarly stabilize consolidated profit margins over time.
10.
Structural savings: Alligo AB serves as another Swedish industrial peer that underwent a CEO transition in June 2026 when Samuel Alteborg took the helm. Alteborg's transition was characterized by ongoing restructuring and cost savings that lifted the adjusted EBITA margin by 1.4 percentage points year-over-year to 7.2%. Analysts expected this leadership continuity to support organic growth and drive a substantial earnings recovery. This case highlights how decisive cost-saving programs during transitions can protect operating profit even amidst soft market demand.
11.
Contrary evidence: Contrary evidence: Some historical leadership transitions in the Swedish industrial sector have resulted in prolonged operational setbacks and margin dilution. In the case of Green Landscaping, the sudden departure of the long-standing CEO in 2026 coincided with consecutive quarters of missed profitability targets and rising leverage. This forced analysts to cut margin and cash flow assumptions while widening the company's risk premium. This negative precedent indicates that new leadership cannot guarantee immediate operational turnaround or margin normalization if structural market challenges persist.
