The perimeter convention behind every input. The inputs are on Unilever's reported perimeter as the FactSet history stands (Foods included through FY2025). The share consolidation.   • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
