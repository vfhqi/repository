The evidence shows that Solid State plc possesses moderate pricing power that is structurally constrained by customer concentration, unhedged foreign exchange exposures, and sole-source supplier dependencies, though the company has successfully expanded gross margins to 33.5% in FY26 through strategic mix shifts and operational improvements rather than pure pricing strength.
