Section 2 – Swissmedic GMP Certification and Maeven Facility Status

Section summary
  1. SKAN Group AG expects to secure Swissmedic GMP certification for its Maeven facility in Allschwil during 2026 to support commercial launch in the second half of 2026.

Establishment and Strategic Mission

1.
Strategic milestone: SKAN Group AG established its Swiss subsidiary Maeven AG on February 6, 2025, to run the Pre-Approved Services pay-per-use business in Allschwil. This new legal entity was structured to directly corporate the pre-approved services and provide aseptic filling solutions directly to pharmaceutical clients. By dedicating a separate entity, management seeks to isolate the operational risks of contract manufacturing from its core equipment sales. This strategic move is expected to drive higher recurring service margins once the facility is fully operational.
2.
Accelerated development: Maeven AG helps pharmaceutical and biotechnology customers accelerate the launch cycle of new sterile medicines. The Allschwil facility supports clients by providing dedicated technology transfer, process development, and GMP validation testing. Offering these integrated solutions reduces the setup time for clinical batches compared to traditional contract manufacturers. This specialized support is intended to secure long-term client commitments before commercial manufacturing begins.
3.
Regional integration: SKAN Group AG operates Maeven AG within its primary headquarters in Allschwil, which is located in the Basel region life sciences hub. Out of the group's 1,755 global employees reported in late 2025, over 839 are located at this central Swiss site. This geographical concentration allows Maeven to draw on SKAN's deep internal pool of isolator engineers and regulatory validation experts. Such proximity minimizes technical execution risks during the critical commissioning phase of the aseptic filling lines.

Swissmedic Inspection and Approval Timeline

4.
Planned certification: SKAN Group AG scheduled its Swissmedic GMP validation inspection for the first quarter of 2026. The company remains focused on obtaining the official GMP certification through Swissmedic during the course of 2026. Securing this certification is a strict prerequisite for handling active pharmaceutical ingredients and starting commercial contract manufacturing. Consequently, any delay in this regulatory milestone will directly postpone the facility's commercial launch.
5.
Scheduled launch: SKAN Group AG targets the second half of 2026 to begin commercial operations at the Allschwil facility. Management confirmed in August 2026 that preparations for the market launch of Pre-Approved Services continued successfully. The company's immediate operational goal remains obtaining Swissmedic approval within the 2026 calendar year. Achieving this target will enable Maeven to generate its first commercial payper-use revenues in late 2026.
6.
Managed postponements: SKAN Group AG experienced minor early project delays that pushed the Swissmedic inspection into the first quarter of 2026. Executive management stated in March 2025 that this short delay does not alter their long-term financial targets. The company still expects the facility to eventually generate annual revenues of CHF 50 million with a 50% EBITDA margin. Thus, the minor scheduling shift is viewed by management as a temporary operational adjustment rather than a structural impairment.
7.
Contrary evidence: SKAN Group AG faced earlier project delays during 2024 as internal engineering teams spent extra time optimizing validation protocols before welcoming Swissmedic inspectors. These initial postponements shifted the anticipated Swissmedic inspection from late 2025 into early 2026, illustrating that regulatory preparation timelines are highly fluid and prone to setbacks. If further technical challenges arise during the Swissmedic review, the commercial start could easily slide past the targeted second half of 2026.
8.
Gradual ramp: SKAN Group AG projects that the Allschwil facility will require approximately three years to reach full commercial utilization. Commercial revenues and final profitability numbers are expected to materialize from a fully ramped facility around 2029. This extended timeline reflects the standard validation and onboarding schedules of biotech clients in the sterile filling sector. Investors must therefore expect Maeven to run at lower utilization and carry unabsorbed fixed costs during this ramp-up.

Technical Challenges and Validation Hurdles

9.
Technical validation: SKAN Group AG must execute rigorous media fills to demonstrate the sterility of its aseptic processes prior to Swissmedic inspection. Media fills involve running microbiological growth media through the isolator filling lines to prove no contamination occurs. Any single positive microbial test during these runs necessitates a complete investigation and restart of the validation sequence. Consequently, the complexity of sterile process validation represents a significant operational risk that can delay approval.
10.
Regulatory standards: SKAN Group AG operates in a highly scrutinized environment governed by the latest Annex 1 GMP guidelines for sterile manufacturing. These guidelines impose strict requirements on continuous environmental monitoring, contamination control strategies, and automated disinfection cycles. Expert insights from the industry note that adapting older processes or new facilities to these updated expectations remains challenging. Failing to satisfy Swissmedic's strict interpretation of these guidelines could result in requests for additional technical modifications.
11.
Consumables integration: SKAN Group AG designed its Pre- Approved Services to utilize proprietary consumables like Aseptic Technologies closed vials and ABC Transfer bags. This technical integration requires Maeven to validate the sterile transfer and filling of these specific single-use systems during the Swissmedic qualification phase. Integrating proprietary technology simplifies the validation pathway because the parent company has already compiled extensive safety documentation. Successfully qualifying this integrated setup will promote sales of high-margin consumables alongside pay-per-use services.
12.
System partner integration: SKAN Group AG coordinates with partner filling-machine makers to integrate the custom aseptic equipment operated by Maeven AG. Qualifying these multi-vendor aseptic process lines requires synchronized validation of both mechanical filling components and isolation barriers. Such complex integrations can present unexpected software or mechanical alignment challenges during the media fill stage. However, SKAN's extensive historical experience in building integrated fillfinish lines minimizes the risk of prolonged mechanical integration delays.
13.
Software validation: SKAN Group AG utilizes manufacturing execution software from its recently acquired subsidiary Metronik to manage operations at the Maeven facility. Validating this digital software architecture is a vital component of obtaining Swissmedic GMP certification for the sterile production lines. Under Annex 1 rules, software systems must provide complete data integrity and trace every step of the aseptic filling process. Integrating Metronik's platform provides a standardized digital backbone that facilitates compliance with Swissmedic's strict audit trail requirements.
14.
Other side of the story: SKAN Group AG is the global technology leader in high-quality, process-critical isolator systems, giving Maeven a unique advantage in satisfying Swissmedic requirements. Because the parent company designs and builds the sterile isolators used in the Allschwil facility, the internal team possesses unmatched technical expertise to resolve any validation hurdles during the Swissmedic inspection. This vertical integration suggests that Swissmedic certification is highly likely to be granted, even if minor schedule adjustments are required.

Section 3 – Customers, Contracts, and Pricing Structure

Section summary
  1. SKAN Group AG leverages Maeven AG to offer pay-per-use aseptic filling services aimed at shortening biotech time-to-market.
  2. Detailed commercial terms and signed contracts remain highly confidential during the initial Swissmedic certification phase.

Commercial positioning and targets

1.
Strategic expansion: SKAN Group AG established its subsidiary Maeven AG in early 2025 to directly commercialize pre-approved aseptic filling services to biopharma clients. This novel pay-per-use business model represents a strategic expansion from selling capital-intensive equipment to offering flexible, operationalized services. Management expects that this service offering will help clients shorten their clinical time-to-market while building a highly recurring revenue base. This shift will help support long-term earnings growth by smoothing out the lumpy capital equipment investment cycles.
2.
Ambitious targets: SKAN Group AG targets significant long-term financial contributions from its pre-approved pay- per-use filling lines. The company projects that the pre-approved isolator systems will eventually generate approximately CHF 50 million in annual sales. Once these lines reach full commercial utilization, they are expected to deliver attractive EBITDA margins of up to 50%. These high-margin services will significantly enhance the overall profitability profile of the Services & Consumables segment.
3.
Contrary evidence: Public company disclosures from 2025 and 2026 provide no specific pricing structures or contract details regarding per-batch, per-slot, or minimum commitments for Maeven AG's services. This complete lack of transparency makes it highly difficult for investors to evaluate the commercial viability or competitive leverage of the pay-per-use model compared to standard contract manufacturing organizations. If market adoption of this unproven model is slower than planned, the high fixed depreciation costs of the facility will heavily depress the parent company's short-term earnings.

Customer engagement and pipeline

4.
Broad target: SKAN Group AG is positioning Maeven AG to serve contract manufacturing organizations, biotechnology innovators, and research laboratories worldwide. By supporting early-stage technology transfer, process development, and validation testing, Maeven AG directly assists customers in accelerating their clinical trials. This focus helps SKAN secure early partnerships with pharmaceutical firms before their drug candidates transition to large-scale commercial production. This early pipeline engagement is critical for generating stable, long-term utilization at the Allschwil facility.
5.
Synergistic pipeline: SKAN Group AG intends to leverage its deep existing relationships with major blue-chip biopharma companies to populate Maeven's customer pipeline. Because nearly all leading pharmaceutical companies already utilize SKAN's isolators, transitioning these customers to pay-per-use services represents a natural extension of the partnership. However, the company has not yet disclosed any signed contracts or active commercial negotiations for the Maeven facility. Securing initial reference customers will be the key milestone to validate the commercial appeal of this model.

Pricing models and market reception

6.
Flexible packaging: SKAN Group AG's pay-per-use model is designed to provide "off-the-shelf" delivery of qualified filling capacities. This model eliminates the need for biotech firms to make heavy upfront capital investments in their own sterile filling equipment. By offering a fully pre-approved system, Maeven AG minimizes regulatory compliance hurdles and start-up delays for its clients. This operational flexibility is expected to yield superior pricing power relative to traditional contract manufacturers.
7.
Integrated ecosystem: SKAN Group AG designs its pay-per-use contracts to drive sales of proprietary consumables like closed vials and sterile transfer bags. This integrated service offering bundles the aseptic filling line with Aseptic Technologies closed vial systems to streamline client validation paths. By tying service contracts to proprietary consumables, SKAN aims to secure stable, recurring high-margin product revenues alongside pay-per-use fees. This strategy will help support higher overall operating margins in the Services & Consumables segment.
8.
Other side of the story: Traditional contract manufacturing organizations command well-established, long-term commercial relationships and possess superior operational scale compared to Maeven AG. Biotech customers may resist adopting an unproven pay-per-use model operated by an equipment manufacturer rather than an experienced, full-service CDMO. Furthermore, without minimum volume commitments or slot reservation fees, Maeven's revenue stream could become highly volatile, exposing SKAN to significant utilization risk during market downturns.
