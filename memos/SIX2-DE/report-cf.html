EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 35 findings

Section summary
  1. Sixt SE demonstrates a structural transformation toward high-margin recurring revenue through the global SIXT ONE loyalty platform, capturing over 1.0 million members and driving the direct booking share past 50% in the US, while North American expansion secures sustainable market share gains exceeding USD 1.5 billion in revenue with a 17% market share in Miami. The business delivers a superior premium-driven profitability profile relative to high-leverage US peers, commanding revenue per day approximately 40% higher than US competitors and 70% above Europcar. However, execution credibility remains under repair following consecutive FY24 EBT guidance misses of 27% driven by severe used-car residual value volatility, though Q1 2026 delivered a strong EBT-positive turnaround to EUR 2.1 million that signals the beginning of margin recovery.

Outliers worth noting

The company's 17% market share achievement in Miami represents an extreme regional hub outperformance that significantly exceeds both its 3.5% national US market share and its 18% European ex-Germany positioning. This hub-specific dominance suggests a replicable premium market penetration blueprint, yet the magnitude of the Miami concentration remains statistically anomalous relative to the company's overall North American footprint.

The revenue per day (RPD) premium of approximately 40% versus US peers and 70% versus Europcar further underscores an extreme pricing power outlier that defies typical competitive dynamics in the oligopolistic car rental industry. Such sustained premium pricing suggests either structural brand differentiation or a customer segment mix that differs materially from industry norms. The 82% non-risk fleet ratio achieved through buyback and lease agreements represents another significant operational anomaly, particularly given that this protective structure remains heavily concentrated in European operations while US risk exposure runs materially higher.

This bifurcated risk profile creates an unusual hedge structure where European earnings stability offsets North American residual value volatility, diverging from the more homogeneous risk profiles maintained by global peers like Hertz and Avis.
