Vodafone Metrics and Exclusions

1.
Unstandardized metrics: Vodafone Group Plc defines adjusted EBITDAaL as operating profit after lease depreciation and interest, but excludes restructuring costs and impairments from the metric. This definition allows management to bypass significant recurring cash outlays when presenting profitability. Consequently, the quality of underlying earnings appears higher than statutory operating profit indicates.
2.
Complex adjustments: Vodafone Group Plc calculates its standard free cash flow after lease payments and minority dividends, but excludes certain spectrum additions from the calculation. The measure serves as an internal benchmark for liquidity and dividend coverage. However, the inclusion of joint venture distributions can distort actual cash generation.
3.
Inflated liquidity: Vodafone Group Plc adjusts its free cash flow further by adding back restructuring costs and spectrum payments. This adjustment resulted in a reported adjusted free cash flow of €2.58 bn for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026. By removing restructuring outlays, the metric overstates the organic cash available to pay equity dividends.
4.
Adjusting earnings: Vodafone Group Plc reconciles adjusted basic earnings per share by stripping out amortization of intangibles and restructuring items. The company reported adjusted basic earnings per share of 10.72 eurocents for the fiscal year 2026. This adjustment masks the ongoing cost of business integrations and structural reorganizations.
