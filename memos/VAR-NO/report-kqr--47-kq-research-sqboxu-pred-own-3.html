Danish Hydrocarbon Fiscal Rules

1.
Substantial tax shielding: BlueNord ASA operates under a unique Danish hydrocarbon tax regime that imposes an elevated 64% marginal rate on upstream activities. The Danish Hydrocarbon Tax Act combines a 25% corporate tax with a 52% special hydrocarbon tax where the corporate tax is fully deductible. Vår Energi ASA will inherit this structure to shield its acquired Danish revenues from immediate cash tax liability.
2.
Infinite carry-forward terms: BlueNord ASA has accumulated massive tax losses that are allowed to be carried forward indefinitely under Danish tax regulations. Unlike other regimes, Denmark imposes no annual utilization cap on the offset of these hydrocarbon tax losses against taxable income. This allows Vår Energi ASA to offset 100% of its initial Danish profits until the losses are fully exhausted.
3.
Multi-field tax pooling: BlueNord ASA benefits from the absence of field-by-field ring-fencing under the Danish continental shelf fiscal rules. Hydrocarbon tax losses generated from any single field within the Danish Underground Consortium can immediately offset taxable profits from other fields. This consolidated pooling allows Vår Energi ASA to optimize cash tax across the entire acquired Danish portfolio.
4.
Favorable capital uplift: BlueNord ASA calculates its special hydrocarbon tax with the benefit of a substantial capital expenditure uplift. The Danish regime allows companies to deduct an extra 30% uplift on property, plant, and equipment investments over six years. This additional deduction further shields the taxable income base from the high 64% marginal tax rate.
5.
Progressive claw-back taxation: BlueNord ASA remains subject to a temporary Danish claw-back tax that can impose additional burdens depending on global oil prices. This additional tax applies at a rate of 5% or 10% from 2022 through 2037 if the average Brent crude price exceeds USD 75.0 per barrel. However, the accumulated claw-back tax cannot exceed the historic benefits received from accelerated depreciation, providing some protection for Vår Energi's earnings.

Contractual fiscal protection: BlueNord ASA operates with a degree of protection against adverse tax changes through a dedicated DUC compensation agreement. This agreement secures compensation for the consortium partners if the Danish state introduces unilateral negative adjustments to the fiscal framework. Consequently, Vår Energi's acquired Danish cash flows are insulated from regulatory volatility, supporting stable long-term earnings planning.
