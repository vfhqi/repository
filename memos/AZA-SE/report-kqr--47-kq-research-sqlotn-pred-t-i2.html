Section 4 – Interest Sensitivity, Balance Sheet Constraints, and Swedish Tax Risks

Section summary
  1. Avanza maintains high asymmetric rate sensitivity where Riksbank policy hikes provide significantly greater net interest income upside than equivalent cuts do on the downside.

This asymmetry occurs because Avanza has already lowered historical deposit rates near zero, leaving limited room to pass through further cuts.
