1.
Two stand out: that its own trading valuation stays well above the price it pays for targets, and that organic growth recovers to a sustainable rate. Either could fail without any change in operating quality. The scenarios below are conditional, not forecasts. The consensus baseline. Brokers broadly assume Röko can double its earnings over the next five years without expanding leverage. The engine for this is acquiring small and medium-sized niche firms at an average of about 7x EBITA, far below Röko's own trading multiple of roughly 18x EV to EBITA. 2.
Scenario one: organic growth diverges from expectations. The central uncertainty is the organic engine in the private subsidiaries. DNB Carnegie notes that investors remain uncertain about organic growth because of Röko's shorter track record compared to peers. The evidence so far is uneven. The third quarter of 2025 delivered 5% sales growth but fell short of management's organic growth targets. The premium valuation would then be exposed, because a failure to expand through acquisitions would lead to a notable de-rating of its multiple. The opposite divergence is also possible.
