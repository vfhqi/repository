EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Bjørn Gulden has orchestrated an impressive operational turnaround at adidas AG since assuming the CEO role in January 2023, delivering currency-neutral revenue growth of 12% in 2024 and successfully clearing massive excess inventories while restoring critical wholesale partnerships.

The CEO brings a rare combination of professional athletic credibility and three decades of sports industry operational experience, having previously driven Puma's revenue from EUR 2.985 billion to EUR 8.465 billion during his nine-year tenure.
