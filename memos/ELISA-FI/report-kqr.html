Edge network capabilities: Elisa management highlights proprietary technical differentiation, such as private 5G standalone networks with slicing technology in maritime ports, which local competitors cannot currently match
6.
Incremental network parity: Independent network measurement studies indicate that basic 5G speed and latency metrics across all three Finnish operators have converating into narrow performance corridors rather than wide operational gaps

Telia customer turnaround: Following targeted operational changes and channel investments, Telia achieved the top ranking in the 2025 EPSI Rating customer satisfaction survey across both B2C consumer and B2B enterprise segments in Finland

Spiking postpaid churn: According to company interim reports, Elisa's Finnish postpaid mobile annualised churn surged to 22.3% in Q3 2025 and 23.0% in Q4 2025, representing the highest churn rates recorded since Q3 2017 before easing to 17.2% in Q1 2026 and 16.7% in Q2 2026
2.
Masked voice subscriber contraction: Company disclosures confirm total postpaid subscriber growth was heavily supported by M2M and IoT additions, whereas postpaid voice subscriptions excluding M2M lost 19,000 subscribers in Q4 2025 and 20,000 in Q1 2026 before stabilizing at flat net adds in Q2 2026
3.
Sustained volume leakage: Equity research from DNB Carnegie highlights that Elisa experienced net losses in postpaid voice subscribers excluding M2M in 10 out of 12 quarters through Q1 2026, reflecting persistent organic volume pressure

  3. Sell-side analysts remain sceptical of management's Q4 recovery timeline due to severe re- contracting friction and customer price elasticity.

Section 5 – Peer Market Structure, Competitive Intensity, and Macro Environment 18 findings

Section summary
  1. Finland's three-player mobile oligopoly faces persistent macroeconomic headwinds that amplify price sensitivity across lower-tier mobile subscribers.
  2. Competitors Telia and DNA have strengthened their strategic positioning through customer satisfaction gains and network excellence, challenging Elisa's market leadership.
  3. Sell-side consensus remains split on whether recent promotional intensity represents a temporary cyclical fluctuation or a structural erosion of market pricing power.

Market structure and macroeconomic backdrop

1.
Concentrated market structure: The Finnish mobile telecommunications landscape operates as a stable three- player oligopoly, where market leader Elisa holds approximately 43% subscriber share, followed by Telia at 32% and Telenor's DNA at 25%
2.
Subdued macroeconomic growth: Economic activity in Finland remains subdued, with institutional forecasts projecting modest real Gross Domestic Product growth of 0.7% to 1.0% in 2026 following a prolonged period of economic stagnation
3.
Depressed consumer sentiment: Statistics Finland's Consumer Confidence Indicator remains well below historical averages at negative 10.5, creating an environment where household purchasing power constraints heighten price sensitivity
4.
Polarized consumer behavior: Macroeconomic pressures and elevated unemployment have created a polarized Finnish consumer base, splitting subscriber demand into budget-conscious tier seekers and premium feature adopters
5.
Resilient enterprise activity: Despite weak broader consumer confidence, Finnish corporate and public sector investments in digital infrastructure, data centers, and defense connectivity continue to provide baseline sector support

Competitor turnaround and commercial strategies

6.
Telia's strategic turnaround: Under renewed executive leadership, Telia Finland has executed an operational turnaround strategy focused on capturing high-ARPU small-to-medium enterprise customers and stabilizing consumer mobile market share
7.
Telia's channel investments: Telia has expanded its physical retail store presence and optimized digital customer journeys across Finland, driving its mobile postpaid ARPU up 4% in key consumer segments
8.
Telia's organizational cost control: To fund commercial channels and enhance margins, Telia implemented a group-wide change program that removed 450 net positions in early 2026, with a significant allocation in Finland
9.
DNA's network momentum: Telenor's subsidiary DNA maintains an aggressive commercial posture in Finland, leveraging its award-winning network quality and high data usage per subscriber to capture market momentum
10.
DNA's dual-brand expansion: DNA utilizes a dual-brand retail strategy alongside expanded fiber investments to drive cross-selling and maintain top-tier customer satisfaction ratings across consumer and enterprise segments
11.
Elisa's defensive value posture: In response to peer moves, market leader Elisa is executing a €40 million cost- transformation program while focusing on protecting its ~43% subscriber share through bundled digital security services
