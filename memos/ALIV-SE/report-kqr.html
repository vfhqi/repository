Section 3 – Purchase Shares: European vs. Chinese Localized Suppliers

Section summary
  1. Chinese carmakers in Europe are bifurcating their procurement between heavy-asset localized battery supply chains and traditional European structural components.
  2. Traditional European parts manufacturers retain a high share in heavy physical categories but face severe exclusion in high-value electronic and battery domains.

Uneven distribution: Traditional European suppliers capture the vast majority of physical hardware orders on localized Chinese vehicle platforms but remain marginalized in digital domains. Bernstein Research reported on September 7, 2026, that European suppliers hold a 97% value share in chassis and exterior components. However, this share falls to 60% for electronics and collapses to just 33% for battery systems. This disparity means that European suppliers face structural exclusion from the most valuable electric vehicle systems.

Section 4 – Impact of the 70% European-Content Rule

Section summary
  1. The proposed 70% European-content rule in the Industrial Accelerator Act establishes a strict compliance threshold for public subsidies and incentives.
  2. Chinese carmakers face severe cost and execution barriers in adapting their localized operations to meet these rigorous sourcing standards.
  3. Migrating Chinese suppliers are establishing European factories to follow their clients, threatening to displace traditional European parts manufacturers.
  4. Broad partner country exemptions and cost waiver provisions within the draft regulations could limit the binding power of the content rule.

Technical and regulatory definitions

1.
Stricter regulations: The European Commission proposed the Industrial Accelerator Act in March 2026 to elevate the domestic manufacturing base. Under this framework, supported electric vehicles must achieve a local non-battery component threshold of at least 70% of the ex-works value. This policy represents a structural shift toward regional sourcing to counter rising import volumes.
