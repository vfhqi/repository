EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 1 finding

Business Model + Organisation Bonesupport commands a highly scalable, high-margin model anchored by its patented CERAMENT platform, with US sales accounting for 83% of group total net sales in fiscal year 2025. The company maintains 43 employees in the US at year-end 2025 while utilizing independent distributors who act as commercial sales agents receiving commissions averaging approximately 30-34% of net sales. CEO Torbjörn Sköld brings 15 years of experience from Johnson & Johnson and maintained 2026 constant-currency net sales growth guidance of 33-36%, though highly concentrated geographic exposure creates structural vulnerability to US commercial disruptions.

Financial Profile + Peer Benchmarking Bonesupport showcases best-in-class gross margins of 93% and accelerating EBIT margins toward 25-30%, significantly outperforming slow-growing orthopaedic peers. Net sales expanded from SEK 97 million in 2018 to SEK 1,175 million in fiscal year 2025 while operating result reached SEK 231.7 million in 2025, expanding from SEK 13.9 million in 2023. Unlevered free cash flow reached SEK 65 million in Q2 2026, showing an average conversion rate of 86%. The company carries zero financial bank debt and executed share buybacks of 373,320 ordinary shares for SEK 82.9 million in June 2026. Operating leverage could quickly stall if commercial access or US distributor relationships weaken, since the business model remains highly dependent on premium US pricing to absorb commission costs.

Industry Structure + Concentration The global bone graft material market reached USD 3.0 billion in CY2023 and is projected to expand at a CAGR of 6.6% through CY2030, comfortably exceeding historical global GDP growth rates. Diversified giants like Zimmer Biomet, Smith & Nephew, DePuy Synthes, and Stryker historically hold a ~65% market share, while innovative synthetic biologics command gross margins exceeding 90% by bypassing low-margin commoditized tissue-bank structures. Kuros Biosciences achieved its first annual net profit of USD 2.6 million upon scaling to USD 146.1 million, establishing the minimum scale threshold for pure-play profitability. Capitated pricing and commoditization could compress premium synthetic bone graft margins if clinical differentiation fails to convince cost-conscious hospital committees.

Value Chain Map + Company Position Bonesupport commands a profit-favoured position within the orthobiologics value chain, extracting an elite 91- 93% gross margin at the formulation stage by shielding its proprietary biphasic mode of action with 40 patents. Upstream inputs are anchored on the procurement of raw medical-grade calcium sulfate and hydroxyapatite powders, with advanced drug-eluting products requiring highly purified antibiotic active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), specifically gentamicin for CERAMENT G and vancomycin for CERAMENT V. US commercialization relies on independent distributors who receive commissions averaging 30-34% of net sales to support case-by-case product selection.

Successful clinical application of drug-eluting CERAMENT reduces total hospital treatment costs by an estimated SEK 300 thousand per patient. If hospital value analysis committees enforce strict capitated pricing caps on implant spend, Bonesupport's ability to maintain its >90% gross margin at the formulation stage will compress.

Customer Side Analysis US hospital sales flow through Group Purchasing Organizations, with active accounts at US Level 1 trauma centers expanding to over 140 in 2025 from 15 in 2024. A handful of accounts restricted Q2 2026 CERAMENT G usage due to cost-benefit administrative reviews triggered when total biological spend exceeds initial projections, with restricted accounts typically requiring several weeks to two quarters to resume normal purchasing volumes. Health-economic arguments highlight that treating a secondary infection costs 6.5 times more than preventative void filling, creating surgeon training and clinical re-qualification requirements that create a structural barrier to competitive substitute adoption. Hospital value analysis committees can block usage indefinitely if budget thresholds trigger reviews that local sales representatives fail to resolve.

Supplier + Distributor + Channel Analysis Bonesupport mitigates high-margin supply chain vulnerabilities through strict CMO oversight, though supply chain disruption or failure of contract manufacturers could lead to costly, protracted replacement processes. US tariffs will reduce the US segment gross margin by approximately 0.8 percentage points over time. US commercialization relies on a scalable network of independent commercial distributors who serve as sales agents, with around 200 non-exclusive distributors allowing active replacement of underperforming commercial partners. High gross margins of 95% yield US contribution margins of up to 60% after commissions. Severe single-source contract manufacturing focus exposes Bonesupport to catastrophic production halts if key facilities face sudden regulatory or operational disruptions.
