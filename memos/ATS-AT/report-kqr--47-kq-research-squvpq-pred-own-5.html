Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Resolving the operational outlook of the Chongqing plants is critical for predicting AT&S's future earnings.

Defining the Key Question

1.
Investigative mandate: "For AT & S Austria Technologie & Systemtechnik AG (ATS-AT), what is the outlook for utilisation and profitability of its Chongqing (China) substrate plants given AMD's shift of volumes to Kulim, the Chinese security probe into Intel's domestic business, Chinese directives to replace foreign chips in state- owned telecom firms by 2027, and the customer-financed Chongqing expansion announced in May 2026; and is there any risk of impairment of Chongqing assets or of the Nanjangud (India) plant?"

Consequently, small fluctuations in volume can lead to pronounced shifts in earnings per share. 1.
Triggering event evaluation: Operational and geopolitical headwinds at AT&S's Chongqing substrate plants have escalated potential asset impairment indicators under IAS 36. Under IAS 36 / Ind AS 36, clear indicators of impairment include sustained operating losses, significant changes in the technological or regulatory market, and downward revisions in cash flow projections. Asset carrying values: The carrying value of AT&S's property, plant, and equipment (PPE) remains highly concentrated in its Microelectronics segment. Since Chongqing represents a major Cash Generating Unit (CGU) under this segment, any downward revision in long-term utilization assumptions would severely threaten these balance sheet values. Under these conditions, the Chongqing substrate plants would face permanent structural underutilization, forcing massive asset impairments that would severely erode AT&S's equity value and forward earnings per share.
