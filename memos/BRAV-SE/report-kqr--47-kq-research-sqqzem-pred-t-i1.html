Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Defining the Key Question

1.
Formulated key question: "Bravida Holding AB (BRAV-SE, Nasdaq Stockholm): a Nordic multi-technical installation and service contractor (electrical, heating, plumbing, ventilation, security, building automation) reporting four country segments (Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland), with sales split between project installation (about 52% in FY2025) and recurring service and maintenance (about 48%), now delivering large data-centre contracts for Green Mountain (Norway, through the DC Nordic joint venture with Backe Industri), atNorth (Finland, as principal contractor) and EcoDataCenter (Sweden).
