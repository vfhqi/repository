EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 37 findings

Inwido operates as Europe's largest customized window and door manufacturer, leveraging a decentralized portfolio of 30+ local brands with centralized procurement scale advantages, while benefiting from nondiscretionary energy renovation demand driven by EPBD regulatory mandates. The company defended adjusted EBITA margins at 10.50% in 2025 despite absorbing a SEK 2.0 billion organic sales contraction across 2023-2024, maintaining structural stability well above historical 6-8% pre-pandemic levels.

Inwido generates highly resilient cash flows totaling SEK 4.6 billion from 2021-2025, operating with structural negative working capital at -5.70% of sales that provides valuable interest-free financing from payables. However, aggressive M&A acceleration drove net debt to SEK 2,153 million and leverage to 1.70x operating EBITDA in 2025, though this remains comfortably below the 2.50x covenant threshold.
