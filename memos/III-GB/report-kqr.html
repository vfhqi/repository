Section 1 – Key question and brief

Section summary
  1. The structural mismatch between illiquid underlying assets and rapid retail fund redemptions poses a critical threat to alternative asset managers' EPS trajectories.

Verbatim question and thematic brief

1.
Verbatim mandate: The core query investigates whether the manufacturers of the retail private- markets wave have not moved while their product sells, focusing on Partners Group, EQT, CVC, Bridgepoint, Intermediate Capital Group, 3i Group, Antin, and Tikehau. This thematic driver focuses on how evergreen and semi-liquid funds distributed through wealth platforms threaten the near-term earnings stability of European asset managers.
2.
