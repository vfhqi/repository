[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. Banca Mediolanum is a very good business at a full price. Italy's largest adviser network keeps converting household cash into managed assets at a 70 percent rate while short rates hold, and at 22.9 EUR the shares already pay for that: the modal return is about 9 percent a year against the framework's 20 percent bar, and the case clears the bar only below about 19.9 EUR.

  • The change the thesis rests on is small, mostly external, and already in the numbers.
    • Two forces carry it.
    • The first is the rate path: a 0.9-to-1.1-year government bond book turned the 2022 to 2026 rate cycle into net interest income of 478.5 million EUR in H1 2026, up 30 percent, and the 2026 guide was lifted to +15 to +18 percent in a week when the ECB path has been re-marked toward a December 2026 hike.
    • The second is an organic network step-up: the NEXT pairing programme reached 783 paired consultants in July 2026 from 590 at end-2025, the paired seniors gather 28 percent more managed inflows than unpaired bankers, and July and August 2026 were the two best managed-inflow months in the company's history, at 1.27 billion EUR and 1.09 billion EUR.
    • Neither force is new to the market.
      • The rate force is cyclical and its reversal risk is dated by the research at 12 to 24 months.
      • The network force is a refinement of a forty-year model, not a change to it.
  • The internal change refines the model rather than changing it.
    • There is no chief executive change (Massimo Doris since 30 June 2008), no acquisition, no buyback, and no capital-markets-day reset.
    • What there is: a raised net interest income guide, a first long-term Grandi Patrimoni target (30 billion EUR by 2030 from 21 billion EUR at June 2026), the Flowe disposal, and the NEXT scale-up to over 1,000 consultants by end-2026.
    • The framework's screen rule (three internal forces strong, or chief-executive change plus a guidance increase) is not met
    • Only the guidance increase is strong.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are very positive near term and have reached the sell-side.
    • H1 2026 operating margin rose 20 percent to 683.8 million EUR, net income 16 percent to 555.5 million EUR, and cost-to-income fell to 36.1 percent.
    • Consensus FY2026 EPS has risen 21.0 percent in twelve months to 1.57 EUR.
    • The change has therefore travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain: from the company's choices (3 percent deposit campaigns, junior pairings) through its operating metrics (70 percent conversion; 783 consultants) and its financial statements into the sell-side's estimates.
  • The forward earnings path settles at about 6 percent a year, because the model is converting more volume at a falling unit price and a rising cost of its key input.
    • On our modal path, which sits 3 to 6.5 percent above consensus on the rate line and the inflow run-rate, EPS runs 1.62, 1.70, 1.80 EUR for FY2026 to FY2028 on the brokers' basis
    • Consensus has 1.57, 1.60, 1.69 EUR, which is +2, +2 and +6 percent a year.
    • Four things hold the path below the framework's 12 to 20 percent band.
    • Fee margin: the recurring fee yield has fallen from 214 to 215 basis points in 2023 to 202 in 2025 and 198 in H1 2026, and Morgan Stanley models a further 1.5 basis points a year to 2028.
    • Adviser costs: acquisition costs have risen from 33 percent of gross commissions in 2023 to 36 percent in H1 2026, and the research expects them to stay elevated.
    • Performance fees: 256 million EUR in 2025 and 73.7 million EUR in H1 2026 are a market record, not a run rate.
    • Tax rate: the FY2025 reported base carried a 13.4 percent tax rate against 22.8 percent in FY2024, so FY2025's reported net income growth of 10.5 percent came from the tax line while pre-tax profit fell 1.4 percent.
  • The price has re-rated to meet the case.
    • At 22.9 EUR the shares cost 13.7 times 24-month-forward earnings, the 82nd percentile of the stock's own ten-year history and the 94th percentile of its five-year range, above the ten-year 'mania' band of 13.60x, after a 44.8 percent twelve-month return.
    • Three of the four most recent broker notes are Holds on the stated ground that the case is well understood.
  • The return therefore fails the bar on the modal path, and the multiple is the swing factor.
    • Our modal return is 9.3 percent a year over eighteen months and 9.2 percent a year over three years: a cash yield of about 4 percent, earnings growth of about 6 percent, and a de-rating from 13.7x to a blended 12.7x that costs about 7 points.
    • Hold the multiple at 13.7x and the eighteen-month return is 14.7 percent a year, a C.
    • Let it revert to the ten-year median of 11.4x and it is 2.1 percent a year.
    • Let the cohort converge on Banca Generali's 15.1x and it is 22.0 percent a year, but that convergence is the market's to give, not the company's to earn.
  • The chain holds at every link; it is the quantities that are too small at this price.
    • The forces are real and have propagated fully.
    • The outputs are improving and are of high quality.
    • The arithmetic from them to a 20 percent return does not close, because a 6 percent grower on a 4 percent yield at the top of its own multiple range cannot reach it.
    • The case clears the bar at about 19.9 EUR on the eighteen-month path, about 13 percent below the reference price, or earlier on evidence that the recurring fee margin has stopped falling.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Compounder', at a C fit.
    • A proven upgrader of inflow and net interest income guides, of high quality, whose consensus FY2027 path (+2 percent) sits on a 2.6 percent average Euribor the current rate path does not support.
    • The fit is partial because the mis-modelled line is a macro assumption rather than a mechanical error in the consensus model, and because the market has discounted the company rather than missed it.

  • The case is therefore a high-quality, well-understood compounder at a full price, not a materially mis-priced change.

    • We recommend a neutral stance and a park, with the entry level stated.

  • Rating = D, because the chain holds at every link but the quantities are too small at this price: the change forces are a modest cyclical tailwind and an incremental internal programme ('Change Forces' at C), the outputs are improving but settle at mid-single-digit earnings growth ('Financial Outputs' at C), and the 'Modal return' is about 9 percent a year (at F)

    • The parts combine so that the return binds, since materiality is tested on the return, and a 20 percent bar cannot be met by a 6 percent grower on a 4 percent yield at the 94th percentile of its own multiple range.
    • Not C, because the framework's C anchor for the return is 13 to 16 percent a year and the modal path does not reach it even with the multiple held (14.7 percent a year over eighteen months, and only on that branch).
    • Not F, because the forces and outputs are real, the earnings are of high quality and better than consensus has them, and at about 19.9 EUR the same path clears the bar.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The counter-case, at its strongest: H1 2026 is a rate-and-market peak dressed as structural growth.
      • Net interest income's +30 percent came from a one-off funding relief (promotional deposits repriced from 4 and 5 percent to 3 percent) and the carry on a 0.9-year book at the top of the Euribor cycle, and the cost of funding is already rising again (0.69 percent in Q1 2026 to 0.84 percent in Q2 2026).
      • The recurring fee margin is in structural decline (214 to 198 basis points), with Grandi Patrimoni discounts down to 1 percent and automatic-strategy inflows diluting it further.
      • Adviser costs are at 36 percent of gross commissions and rising.
      • Performance fees of 256 million EUR in 2025 are a market record.
      • FY2025's reported growth was the tax line.
      • So FY2027 EPS is flat at best, consensus at 1.60 EUR is right or high, and the multiple reverts toward its ten-year median.
      • The risks research's own pre-mortem reached this profile, which it labels the "false friend" (asset gathering masks margin compression), as the one with the fewest hard inconsistencies.
    • What we concede.
      • Every number in it is in the research and we have used most of them against the case ourselves.
      • The multiple is at the top of its range.
      • The growth is single-digit.
      • The fee margin is falling and the research gives no reason it stops.
    • Why we do not adopt it as the modal case.
      • First, the bear needs rate cuts, and the rate path this week is up: a December 2026 ECB hike stands in the weekly environment reading of 2026-10-09, and every 20 basis points of Euribor is about 12 million EUR of profit (Jefferies).
      • Second, the volume engine is accelerating, not decelerating: managed inflows set two records after the half-year close, the NEXT productivity gap widened from 4 percent to 28 percent, and the network grew 10 percent year on year
      • That is a volume engine the margin decline has to beat.
      • Third, the capital position (CET1 22.7 percent, nine points over requirement) funds the dividend through the bear's downturn, so the floor on the cash return holds.
    • Our response is therefore to price the bear's half rather than to argue the bull's.
      • A blended multiple of 12.7x (45 percent weight on reversion to 11.4x) and 6 percent growth gives a 9 percent return and a neutral stance, rather than the bear's 11.4x and flat earnings, which would give a negative one.
