Kambi delivered a double-digit EBITA beat in Q2 2026 driven by World Cup momentum and AI automation, prompting a full-year guidance upgrade that exceeded consensus, but the record 14% operator margin creates near-term recycling headwinds while major customer migrations continue to challenge the growth inflection narrative.
