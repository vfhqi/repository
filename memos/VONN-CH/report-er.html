Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary

1.
that provides targeted investment, advisory, and structured solutions for private and institutional clients. The group maintains a pure buy-side advisory focus designed to avoid the credit risks and capital requirements of traditional corporate lending.
2.
Multi-generational ownership structure: Founded originally in 1924 as F.E. Haeberli & Cie, the firm was renamed after Jakob Vontobel acquired it in 1936. The bank went public on the SIX Swiss Exchange in 1986, though the founding family retains majority control to guarantee entrepreneurial independence.
3.
Consolidated operational setup: Vontobel manages its global distribution through two complementary client segments, Private Clients and Institutional Clients, supported by structured Centers of Excellence.
