Business Model and Product Architecture

1.
Product profile – specialized positioning: Melexis NV is a Belgium-based fabless semiconductor manufacturer founded in 1989 that specializes in micro-electronic integrated circuits for automotive and industrial edge applications. The group employs over 2,000 personnel across 12 countries, providing integrated sensing, processing, and actuation chips directly to tier-1 automotive suppliers and original equipment manufacturers globally. TechInsights ranks Melexis as the fourth-largest supplier of automotive sensors worldwide, maintaining leading market share in magnetic position sensors

Operating architecture – asset-light setup: The company operates a fabless manufacturing strategy, outsourcing all front-end silicon wafer fabrication to foundry partners such as sister company X-FAB and TSMC while retaining in-house proprietary design and testing capabilities. This asset-light approach allows Melexis to minimize capital expenditure requirements to approximately 4% to 7% of annual sales while focusing internal investments on research and development
3.
Core exposure – automotive dominance: Automotive applications generate approximately 88% to 90% of group total turnover, creating high structural sensitivity to global light vehicle production cycles and tier-1 inventory adjustments. The remaining 10% to 12% of revenue stems from non-automotive markets, including data center cooling fans, digital health devices, alternative mobility, and industrial robotics
4.
Product portfolio – proprietary sensor leadership: The company's primary product lines comprise Triaxis® 3D Hall-effect magnetic position sensors, inductive position sensors, embedded brushless direct current motor drivers, and Triphibian™ pressure sensors. These integrated circuits address key automotive megatrends, including electric vehicle thermal management, electronic steering and braking, battery monitoring systems, and ambient interior lighting

5.

However, severe tier-1 channel destocking and soft European vehicle production drove sales down 3.3% to €932.8m in 2024 and a further 10.0% to €839.6m in 2025.
