Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Systemair designs and manufactures energy-efficient ventilation equipment: air handling units, circular duct fans, air distribution devices and air curtains
2.
Brands and heritage. Four brands, Systemair, Frico, Fantech and Menerga, built from the circular duct fan invented in 1974 by founder Gerald Engström
3.
Scale. FY25/26 net sales of SEK 12,503m, roughly 7,400 employees and own sales companies in 51 countries, exporting to over 135
4.
End-market mix. Commercial and industrial applications are 85% to 90% of sales, with commercial construction alone at roughly 30%
5.
Split of new build and renovation. Sales split roughly evenly between new construction and renovation, with equipment upgrade and retrofit revenue up 33% in FY25/26
6.
Geographic mix. Western Europe is 44.7% of FY25/26 sales, Germany the largest single market at 11%, Nordics 16.4% with Sweden at 5%
7.
Revenue model. Standard stock products are 40% to 45% of group turnover, sold day by day; the remainder is engineered systems such as Geniox air handling units
8.
Financial character. FY25/26 adjusted operating margin 9.4%, gross margin 36.7%, return on capital employed 15.8%, up from 14.8%
9.
Balance sheet. Net debt to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation of 0.54x, equity to assets 61.8% against a 30% policy floor
10.
Manufacturing footprint. Twenty-seven production facilities across 18 to 19 countries and approximately 90 legal operating entities, run on a decentralised model
11.
Ownership. Founder Gerald Engström holds 43.5% of capital and votes through Färna Invest, having bought 1.5m shares in March 2026
12.
Investment style implication. A change-driven corporate-transformation case bolted onto a solid-quality construction cyclical, not a compounder and not a deep-value cyclical
