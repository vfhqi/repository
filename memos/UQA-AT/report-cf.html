EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 1 finding

UNIQA Insurance Group AG operates under stable executive leadership with CEO Andreas Brandstetter in place since 2011 and all seven Management Board mandates synchronized until June 2028, executing an upgraded UNIQA 3.0 strategy that targets 6% annual premium growth and 7% annual EPS growth. The firm delivered 12% gross written premium growth to EUR 4,924 million in H1 2026, substantially outperforming the 6% strategic target and demonstrating accelerating revenue momentum.

The investment thesis confronts a critical structural risk as synchronized Management Board mandate expiries in June 2028 create potential strategic and operational discontinuity, while the absence of activist presence is anchored by core shareholders controlling 63.7% of votes through a pooled voting trust.

Forward earnings per share trajectory is governed by five load-bearing drivers ranked by estimated impact: Austria Health pricing indexation and new business value contributing an estimated 35% EPS impact with high plausibility; CEE net combined ratio reduction below 93% delivering 25% EPS impact at medium plausibility; Austria admin cost ratio reduction to 13% providing 15% EPS impact with high plausibility; UNIQA Re Zurich external expansion contributing 15% EPS impact at medium plausibility; and real estate platform external capital fees generating 10% EPS impact with high confidence. The Austria Health segment operates as the defensive earnings anchor, commanding a 44% market share and delivering 11.7% premium growth in H1 2026 through a balanced split of 5.5% pricing adjustments and 2.5% volume expansion. CEE underwriting improvement remains highly volume-dependent as the strategy targets 8% annual premium growth by 2028, though Polish growth demonstrates vulnerability with 4.6% volume expansion offset by only 0.7% pricing power. 8 / CSR reports - Earnings Calls - Earnings Calls Management demonstrates a highly conservative forecasting archetype with consistent guidance outperformance across the last three fiscal years, beating the guided EUR 490-510 million EBT range with actual FY25 results of EUR 516 million, exceeding FY24 guidance with EUR 442 million versus EUR 426 million baseline, and systematically delivering above prudent targets.

Recent H1 2026 earnings delivery showcased strong double-digit profit growth with net consolidated profit climbing 11% to EUR 258 million and earnings before taxes expanding 10.5% to EUR 327 million, though this performance was heavily supported by net investment income surging 46.7% to EUR 587 million while the technical underwriting result fell 5% to EUR 360 million as management strengthened reserving buffers. Capital adequacy remains exceptionally robust with a Solvency II ratio of 271% at June 30, 2026, expanding from 170% in FY20 to 275% in FY25, providing a 41 percentage point cushion above the upper bound of the 180-230% strategic target range.

Sell-side positioning reflects cautious neutral consensus with limited analyst coverage creating concentrated research risk. Deutsche Bank Research maintains a Hold rating with a EUR 10 price target citing limited near-term valuation upside, while Morgan Stanley assigns an Equal-weight rating emphasizing CEE execution dependencies and yield curve sensitivities.

Peer-relative positioning demonstrates superior revenue momentum as UNIQA's 12% H1 2026 gross written premium growth substantially outpaced Vienna Insurance Group's 5.4% expansion, Generali's 5.8% growth, and Allianz's nominal 0.1% business volume increase, though the net combined ratio of 91.6% in H1 2026 deteriorated from 90.5% in H1 2025 with the Austrian segment rising to 97.4% from 94.7% while International operations improved to 91.7% from 92.4%.
