Forbo Holding faces material operational and governance transformation as new CEO Johannes Huber ends a period of executive instability while reported net sales contracted 3.3% to CHF 1,085.4 million in FY 2025 and operating profit plummeted 27.2% to CHF 87.8 million, driven by low building activity in core European markets. The company operates from a position of structural weakness characterized by severe margin erosion with EBIT margins contracting to 8.1%, yet maintains fundamental resilience through a completely debt-free balance sheet and natural raw material cost insulation via linseed oil decoupling from petroleum volatility.

Active shareholder SEO Management AG exceeded the 5% threshold and secured board representation, catalyzing accelerated strategic repositioning under a new functional operating model for Movement Systems with direct CEO control and localized capacity expansions in the United States and Japan to bypass trade barriers and restore divisional profitability.

Materiality of Change Assessment

The magnitude of internal transformation at Forbo represents a high-materiality inflection point driven by the appointment of Johannes Huber as CEO bringing global construction industry experience from Hilti and hiring Heinz Hössli as permanent CFO providing deep listed industrial technology financial expertise. Bernhard Merki's election as Chairman marks a governance transition away from long-tenured executive leadership to independent oversight, while the departure of Marc Deimling triggers a division-wide organizational shift placing Movement Systems under direct CEO control. The strategic recalibration includes localized manufacturing expansions at the Flotex plant in Hazleton, USA and modernizing the Fukuroi site in Japan, representing the largest regional capital investment in 50 years. SEO Management AG's activist presence proposing its Managing Partner Dr. Ilias Läber to the Board of Directors establishes a direct channel for shareholder-led operational development and increased capital discipline potentially optimizing Forbo's debt-free balance sheet. However, leadership is actively addressing Forbo's historically hierarchical and inward-oriented culture, indicating deep-rooted structural challenges that extend beyond financial metrics into organizational capability and adaptability.
