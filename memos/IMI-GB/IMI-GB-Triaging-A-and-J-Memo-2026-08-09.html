2.

3.
Scale: FY25 revenue was GBP2,304m, up 4% reported and 5% organic; market capitalisation GBP6.90bn per the tearsheet snapshot; the group employed approximately 10,000 people as of May 2026
4.
Geography: Revenue is geographically balanced across Europe 44%, Americas 29%, Asia Pacific 19% and Middle East and Africa 8% of FY25 sales
5.
Manufacturing footprint: IMI manufactures across 18 countries with low-cost hubs in Brno, Queretaro, China and India, and a global commercial footprint of over 50 countries
6.
Revenue mix: Approximately 45% of group revenue is aftermarket, parts, upgrades and servicing; aftermarket represents around 60% of Process Automation and around 75% of Climate Control revenue
7.
Financial character: FY25 adjusted operating margin reached 20.0%, return on invested capital 14.0%, adjusted cash conversion 96%, and net debt to EBITDA 1.0x at the low end of a 1.0x-2.0x target range
8.
Capital returns: IMI announced a GBP500m share buyback in March 2026, following a GBP200m buyback completed July 2025, alongside a 10% dividend increase to 34.20p for FY25
9.
Recent portfolio action: The group agreed the sale of its Truflo Marine naval business in November 2025 for GBP225m, approximately 15x adjusted operating profit, and acquired smart-sensing specialist TWTG for EUR25m in October 2024

Ownership and listing context: IMI plc has traded on the London Stock Exchange as a FTSE 100 constituent, with a market capitalisation of GBP6.90bn per the tearsheet snapshot, dated within the source vintage used in this memo
