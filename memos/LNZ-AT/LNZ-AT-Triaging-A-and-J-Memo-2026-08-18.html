Business Description (B.1) 10 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it makes. Lenzing produces wood-based cellulosic fibres under the TENCEL, LENZING ECOVERO and VEOCEL brands, plus dissolving wood pulp, from nine production sites globally
2.
Market position. Lenzing, Sateri and Birla Cellulose together control over 80% of global specialty cellulosic capacity; Lenzing holds 75% of modal and 99% of TENCEL-branded lyocell
3.
Integration. The group is approximately 95% backward integrated into dissolving wood pulp, with biorefineries of 320,000 tonnes in Austria, 300,000 tonnes in Czechia and a 600,000-tonne Brazilian joint venture
4.
Revenue scale. FY25 group revenue was EUR 2,602m, down 2.3% year on year, and revenues have been virtually flat at EUR 2.60bn since 2022
5.
Segment split. Division Pulp earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation rose 3.0% to EUR 452m in FY25, while Division Fiber earnings collapsed to EUR 0.10m on external fibre revenues of EUR 1.90bn
6.
Balance sheet. Net financial debt fell 11.9% to EUR 1.35bn and net leverage improved to 3.30 times from 3.90 times, against a 2.50-times strategic target
7.
Capital structure. A EUR 500m perpetual hybrid bond carries a 9.00% coupon, a EUR 545m syndicated facility was arranged in May 2025, and a EUR 300m rights issue is planned for 2026
8.
End markets. Two-thirds of fibre sales go to the cyclical apparel market and one-third to nonwovens; Asia was 61% of FY25 external revenue, Europe including Turkey 29%
9.
Ownership. B&C Privatstiftung is the ultimate parent via B&C Group, and pulp producer Suzano holds a 15% stake under a long-term syndicate agreement
10.
Investment style. The financial shape is a highly cyclical, capital-intensive price taker in a restructuring phase, not a compounder, with return on capital employed of 0.70% in FY25
