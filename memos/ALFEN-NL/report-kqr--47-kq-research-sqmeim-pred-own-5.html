This methodological discrepancy means that Alfen’s self-reported figures can appear significantly more volatile than independent broker calculations depending on the timing of debt repayments or share transactions.
2.

In its comprehensive financial model published on 21 May 2026, Jefferies calculates free cash flow by subtracting capital expenditures and the repayment of lease liabilities directly from operating cash flow. This approach ensures that lease commitments, which are substantial under IFRS 16, are fully captured as an ongoing cash drain before assessing the yield available to equity investors.

According to a Deutsche Bank Research report published on 21 August 2025, Alfen's company-defined free cash flow stood at only €300 thousand for the first half of 2025.
