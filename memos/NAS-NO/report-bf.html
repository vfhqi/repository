EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Norwegian Air Shuttle operates a focused short-haul network anchored by a 57% domestic Norwegian market share and 95-aircraft Boeing narrow-body fleet, now integrating tour operator Nordic Leisure Travel Group to capture high-margin hotel stages, yet faces structural headwinds from volatile fuel exposure, currency sensitivity, severe Boeing delivery delays, elevated pilot wage inflation, and materially lagging returns versus low-cost leader Ryanair's 19% ROIC benchmark.
