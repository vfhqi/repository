EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Section summary
  1. Sanofi operates as a global biopharmaceutical leader focused on specialty care, vaccines, and general medicines.
  2. Historical financial metrics show steady core growth but lag the high-margin expansion of peers like Eli Lilly and Regeneron.
  3. Broker sentiment ahead of the third-quarter results remained cautious due to pipeline setbacks and Dupixent concentration risks.

Headline conclusion. Sanofi's Q3 2026 print reset near-term earnings expectations sharply upward. Net sales of €14,444 million exceeded the compiled consensus of €13,254 million, a beat of about 9%. The profit lines beat by far more.

Business operating income was €5,430 million, beating consensus by 26%. Business EPS of €3.55 beat the consensus projection of €2.81, also by about 26%. Business net income was €4,255 million, up 20.0% year on year. Before the release, the debate was cautious.

Analysts had described the equity story as catalyst-light, with key pipeline readouts pushed into 2027. Retail prescription trackers had nonetheless pointed to double-digit Dupixent upside versus consensus. That helps explain why the revenue and earnings upside was largely unpublished-expectation-driven rather than a consensus-model surprise.

What drove the beat and the guidance upgrade. The primary engine was Dupixent. Dupixent net sales were €5,687 million, up 34.8% at constant exchange rates. Launch products across specialty care grew 31%.

ALTUVIIIO reached €408 million, up 37.8%. Margin was the second driver. Gross margin was 81.0%, up 2.1 percentage points year on year. Favorable product mix and US gross-to-net adjustments supported the expansion.

Cost discipline was the third. R&D of €1,849 million was 7% below consensus. SG&A of €2,309 million was 6% below consensus. Offsetting this, other operating expenses rose to €2,168 million on higher profit sharing with partners tied to Dupixent.

Management raised 2026 guidance to low-teens sales growth at constant exchange rates, from around 10%. It also guided business EPS growth in the mid-teens.

Quality of the beat. The upgrade is real but not entirely repeatable. Jefferies estimated that one-off US pricing adjustments contributed approximately €300 million. Deutsche Bank put this at roughly one-third of the revenue outperformance.

Vaccines sales of €3,185 million fell 6.6% at constant exchange rates on anticipated phasing in respiratory prevention. The currency headwind is estimated at about 1% on both sales and business EPS. Management expects the favorable pricing benefits to normalize into 2027, with slightly negative pricing characterizing global biopharma sales. The upgrade is therefore best read as a higher 2026 base with a more modest 2027 growth rate. - Earnings Calls Post-Dupixent trajectory and the pipeline.

Protecting earnings beyond 2031 is the central strategic task. Management expects only a moderate revenue impact when Dupixent loses exclusivity and aims to preserve core earnings and cash flow through the transition. The expanded Regeneron alliance adds four next-generation long-acting immunology candidates, with $1 billion upfront, up to $7 billion in milestones and a 50:50 profit split. Management reaffirmed 2030 targets of about €10 billion for new launches and about €25 billion for Dupixent.

It trimmed its 2030 vaccines ambition to about €9 billion from more than €10 billion. The near-term pipeline has been narrowed. Sanofi discontinued amlitelimab in atopic dermatitis and itepekimab in respiratory indications. The frexalimab Phase 3 readout moved to 2028, and US submissions for efdoralprin alfa moved into 2027.

Management acknowledged that existing assets are insufficient to cover post-2031 Dupixent losses, with the R&D head spending about half his time sourcing late-stage assets. The CEO has signaled larger, later-stage M&A, a departure from smaller bolt-on deals. - Earnings Calls milestones. Two approvals landed after the quarter. The FDA approved Infilbray on November 18, 2026 for adults and pediatric patients with type 3 Gaucher disease, the first oral therapy for its neurological manifestations.

The label carries clear caveats. It includes warnings for suicidal ideation and behavior, and depression occurred in 10% of the treated group versus 0% in the imiglucerase arm. Such safety profiles may restrict early adoption. The European Commission approved VaxRabeo on November 19, 2026 for all age groups, with a 60-month shelf life and improved thermostability.

Both approvals are positive for diversification, though neither is sized to offset the Dupixent concentration. 12 simplification and leadership reset. Belén Garijo became CEO on May 1, 2026, replacing Paul Hudson. Paulo Fontoura became Global Head of R&D on September 1, 2026. The Executive Committee was streamlined to eight members, and CFO François Roger's mandate now includes corporate business development and M&A. Portfolio streamlining continues. Cheplapharm will take over twenty mature medicines and three manufacturing sites in exchange for a 26.4% equity stake. Medley in Brazil is being sold to EMS Group, with closing no earlier than the fourth quarter of 2026. Together these divestments are modeled to reduce 2027 sales by €1.3 billion. The revenue reduction is designed to elevate the gross margin profile.

Sell-side response. Estimates moved up for 2026, but views on durability diverged. Morgan Stanley raised 2026 business EPS to €8.98 from €8.70 and 2027 to €9.91 from €9.18. Bernstein moved to €9.17 for 2026 and €9.96 for 2027.

Rothschild & Co Redburn cut its 2027 estimate by 4.1% to €9.33. Redburn argued that higher Dupixent forecasts widen the post-exclusivity revenue hole. Deutsche Bank highlighted management messaging that operating margins should improve every year through 2030. The post-results debate is therefore less about the quarter than about whether 2026 momentum carries into the post-2031 transition.

Competitive, macro and supply-side context. Competition for Dupixent is building. Experts indicated first-line share among biologic-naïve patients fell from about 80% to 30% as Ebglyss and Nemluvio gained adoption. AbbVie targets a 2029 launch for its half-life-extended IL-13 antibody.

Head-to-head trials of Novartis's Rhapsido against Dupixent are scheduled to read out in 2027. In vaccines, Beyfortus sales fell 15.4%, with US sales down 39.3% on lower CDC central orders. The macro backdrop is mixed. The US dollar represented 60.4% of net sales.

The German Health Finance Commission has recommended €42.3 billion in savings for 2027. Sanofi's US pricing agreement brings a three-year import tariff exemption. The same agreement is expected to cut Medicaid prices by an average of 61% for certain therapies. On the supply side, Sanofi plans at least $20 billion of US investment through 2030.

Management estimates that about 60% of pharmaceutical active ingredients globally originate from China. The Frankfurt insulin API facility targets full production by 2032.

Outlook, limitations and open questions. Taken together, the evidence supports a higher 2026 earnings base and an improving margin profile. Digital initiatives target a reduction in development cycle times of more than 40%. However, experts caution that true end-to-end AI transformation remains several years away.

The post- Dupixent bridge still depends on external business development and on early-stage programs that have yet to read out. Pipeline pruning has left the transition story light on near-term clinical catalysts. This summary rests on company disclosures, broker notes and expert-network commentary. It cannot observe unpublished buyside positioning directly, and some broker figures differ across sources.

Further work should focus on four items: the 2027 pricing step-down, the pace of vaccine recovery after the quarter's phasing, the scale and timing of the next acquisitions, and early commercial uptake of Infilbray under its safety monitoring requirements.
