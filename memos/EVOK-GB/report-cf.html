EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Evoke plc faces a forced strategic exit through a recommended all-share acquisition by Bally's Intralot S.A. at £243.1 million equity value and a 138% premium, triggered by draconian UK Remote Gaming Duty increases to 40% that fundamentally disrupted standalone profitability and forced management to withdraw medium-term targets. The C-suite overhaul featuring CEO Per Widerström and CFO Sean Wilkins materially upgraded execution capability, with 9 of 11 Executive Committee roles entirely new since October 2023, though the pending acquisition introduces leadership friction as the entire executive team faces redundancy or displacement.

Materiality of Change

UK Remote Gaming Duty hike to 40% represents a £125m-135m pre-mitigation annualized cost, fundamentally disrupting online economics. £180 million gross synergy target by Year 2 post-close, with 75% realizable within first 12 months, anchors transaction rationale. Aggressive retail contraction through 230 shop closures following initial 68 closures in Q4 2025, targeting £11 million EBITDA benefit. Net leverage of 5.2x and £769 million maturity wall in July 2028 create severe refinancing pressures and going concern uncertainties.

Top Key Drivers (Ranked by Impact and Plausibility)

Driver 1 - Bally's Cost Synergies (HIGH impact, HIGH plausibility): £180m annual savings by Year 2 through marketing optimization, operational efficiencies, and IT consolidation. Driver 2 - UK Tax Mitigation (HIGH impact, MEDIUM plausibility): Target 50% offset of £125m-135m tax headwind via supplier renegotiation and lower bonusing. Driver 3 - Sports Turnaround (MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM plausibility): Address product gaps in Spain and Italy following 12% staking decline. Driver 4 - Retail Margin Protection (HIGH impact, HIGH plausibility): £11m EBITDA benefit from 230 shop closures and 5,000 Vantage cabinet upgrades. Driver 5 - International Expansion (HIGH impact, MEDIUM plausibility): 19% Core market growth in Italy, Denmark, and Romania reduces UK concentration.

Recent Delivery and L3Y Trajectory

FY25 Adjusted EBITDA of £356.2m hit 20.0% margin target, expanding 220 bps despite 2% revenue growth trailing consensus. Historical trajectory marked by severe H1 2024 profit warning with £35m-40m EBITDA miss, followed by reassuring FY24 recovery. International division delivered 19% growth and £175.2m EBITDA, representing over half of group profitability and demonstrating significant performance delta. Underlying free cash flow of £188m offset by £34.1m net cash outflow due to working capital timing and £181.4m debt interest burden.

Forward Credibility and Management Posture

Guidance formally withdrawn following strategic review, with management transitioning from ambitious turnaround planner to orderly asset seller under regulatory distress. Historical posture characterized as overpromiser, with ambitious medium-term targets proving unsustainable and mixed guidance accuracy across reporting periods. Management delivered solid FY24 recovery after H1 2024 miss, achieving in-line FY25 margin and beating H1 2025 EBITDA guidance.

Sell-Side Positioning

Eight active analysts from seven brokerages cover evoke, with price target dispersion ranging from 35p (Deutsche Bank) to 110p (Jefferies). Deutsche Bank's Richard Stuber downgraded from Buy to Hold in January 2026, citing disproportionate 40% duty impact given 70% UK revenue exposure. Jefferies analysts James Wheatcroft and Matthew Copeland transitioned to restricted coverage in early 2026 following transaction announcement. Hold-rated analysts highlight £125m-135m unmitigated EBITDA hit and warn mid-tier brands risk consuming 100% of cash flows in taxes.

Fundamental Peer Position

Bottom quartile momentum rank among peers, trading at 5.0x EV/EBITDA versus Lottomatica's 8.9x, reflecting depressed valuation multiples. Disadvantaged 9.0% UK online market share ranks third behind Flutter and Entain, with subscale operators under £100m revenue expected to be squeezed out. Implied online margins could compress to just 6% pre-mitigation under 40% tax, though 50% mitigation target aims to partially offset. Weak cash conversion relative to peers, with net cash outflow of £34.1m and £238.6m finance costs dragging consolidated performance.

Activist Presence

No active hostile proxy activity identified in library, with major shareholder representation serving as sole structural governance anchor. Founding family shareholder unanimously agreed to support the recommended Bally's Intralot transaction, securing critical shareholder backing.

Most Material Risks

1.
Risk 1 - Integration Execution Failure: Complex legacy technology stack integration presents severe execution risks that could delay or derail £180m synergies, with supplier concession failures potentially blocking 50% tax mitigation and rendering standalone cash generation entirely negative. Risk 2 - Black Market Migration: Crushing tax hikes accelerate customer leakage to unlicensed offshore operators that escape heavy UK tax burden entirely while actively advertising, potentially eroding online profitability before synergies realized. Risk 3 - Refinancing Maturity Wall: £769 million maturity in July 2028 and springing RCF clauses in January 2028 create going concern uncertainties, with credit rating downgrades below B- triggering forced CLO selling and collapsed £112m market cap limiting standalone capital-raising alternatives.
