Section 2 – TOUCH US Selling Price and Revenue Assumptions

Section summary
  1. Medartis anticipates robust US revenue growth for its TOUCH prosthesis driven by premium pricing and raised volume forecasts.

Official Device Cost Structures

1.
High-value reimbursement pricing: The Medicare pass-through application submitted for KeriMedical's TOUCH prosthesis outlines a premium device cost structure. According to a regulatory report by Dinsmore & Shohl on 6 July 2026, the application lists the total cost of the three required components at USD 8,985. This consists of the cementless cup at USD 3,095, the neck and liner at USD 2,995, and the cementless stem at USD 2,895. This high baseline price is expected to significantly enhance Group core gross margins if approved.
2.
Conservative internal calculations: Medartis's executive management uses a more cautious pricing model for its immediate business planning. During the Q2 2025 earnings call on 19 August 2025, Chief Executive Officer Matthias Schupp explained that his US calculations are based on a rough estimate of USD 4,000 to USD 4,500 per device. This conservative range represents the expected commercial selling price before securing final administrative codes. This commercial price will ultimately determine the rate of US EPS growth as unit volumes scale.
3.
Contrary evidence: The company has not yet published any official, binding updates on its realized US selling price in its August or November 2026 statements. In its ad hoc announcement on 2 November 2026, Medartis reported rapid adoption and raised volume projections but omitted specific pricing figures. This suggests that actual contract prices with ambulatory centers may fluctuate below the initial USD 4,000 baseline.

4.

This rapid growth in the United States contributed to a record core first-half revenue of CHF 30.3 million. Achieving these targets is vital for improving regional operating leverage.
5.
Stronger year-end guidance: The company has further increased its volume projections following extraordinary autumn demand in the North American region. On 2 November 2026, Medartis issued an ad hoc release projecting that US TOUCH procedures will reach approximately 3,000 units by the end of 2026. This represents an increase of 1,200 units over the elevated August forecast, confirming that demand is accelerating dynamically. This volume growth is expected to push Group core sales growth beyond the guided 17% to 19% range.

This listing provides public reimbursement that helps stabilize global average selling prices.
