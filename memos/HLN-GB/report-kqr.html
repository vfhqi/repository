Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Specialized consumer health categories remain highly insulated from private label penetration due to intense brand equity and clinical recommendations.
  2. In contrast, commoditized personal care and household cleaning segments experience significant market share erosion to premium own-label offerings.

Defining the core question

1.
Core question: "In European and United States pharmacies and supermarkets since 2024, how fast have store brands gained share in dermocosmetic skin care, sensitive-teeth toothpaste and over-the-counter digestive and pain remedies, compared with mass skin care and household cleaning? Are the premium storebrand ranges that make clinical claims winning shelf space from Sensodyne, Eucerin, CeraVe and Gaviscon, and are the branded leaders still raising prices without losing volume?"
2.
Analyzed dataset: This analysis evaluates four focal consumer healthcare companies alongside more than twelve distinct broker and regulatory sources.
3.
Inquiring durability: Resolving whether pricing power remains durable for three to five years determines the long-term margin trajectory for brand leaders like Haleon.
4.
Varying penetration: Another related issue is how private label penetration varies across channels in different European pharmacy markets.
5.
Monitoring volumes: Investors must watch if pricing fatigue will eventually contract volumes for leading consumer brands.

Investor relevance and financial impact

6.
Predicting margins: Over the next three years, the duration of the pricing power window directly dictates gross margin stability and EPS growth.
7.
Elevated multiples: Brands that sustain clinical premiumisation command higher valuation multiples than commoditized household goods peers.
8.
Squeezed segments: Intense store-brand competition in commoditized areas squeezes mid-tier household products and limits price increases.
9.
Other side of the story: Some consumer health categories remain insulated from private label gains due to decades of professional recommendations and high loyalty.
