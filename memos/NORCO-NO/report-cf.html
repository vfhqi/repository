EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Norconsult ASA demonstrates highly experienced executive leadership with orderly transitions in regional divisions preserving operational continuity, while unmatched employee co-ownership drives record retention and talent attraction, significantly outperforming listed peer turnover rates. The company operates without any active activist presence currently documented in the shareholder registry, indicating a highly supportive and stable core investor base.

Strategic priorities target a top three position in the Nordics, underpinned by a 10% adjusted EBITA margin goal and disciplined cash conversion, while transformative 2025 acquisitions of Metier and Aas-Jakobsen accelerate infrastructure and project management depth with the Aas-Jakobsen transaction representing an enterprise value of NOK 1.43 billion.

Public infrastructure demand remains highly resilient balancing persistent weakness in the private residential sector, while competition remains highly intense as peers like Sweco and AFRY scale and restructure to protect margins, and technological disruption from AI and BIM is slowly automating drawing production while face-toface judgment remains essential. Norconsult delivers resilient net revenue growth and superior operating margins that consistently outperform peer medians, with balance sheet expansion via bank debt funds strategic platform acquisitions while maintaining leverage below peer ceilings, achieving net revenue of NOK 10,103 million in 2025, growing 10% year on year.

Historical performance illustrates a consistent organic growth trajectory, with net revenues growing to NOK 10,103 million in 2025 compared to NOK 9,186 million in 2024, and organic growth reaching 6% adjusted for calendar effects in 2025. Net revenue successfully crossed the NOK 10 billion milestone in 2025 driven by robust public sector infrastructure demand and stable organic growth of 6%, though reported operating profits faced headwinds from ERP upgrades and M&A integration costs with EBIT missing broker forecasts by 18%, while excellent full-year cash conversion of 130% supported a high dividend proposal of NOK 1.80 per share representing an 86% payout ratio, and analysts cut 2027e adjusted EPS forecasts by 2% to reflect higher longterm financing expenses and persistent wage inflation.

Norconsult commands superior organic growth and margin stability within the Pan-Nordic technical consulting peer group, while Sweco remains the scale and profitability benchmark and Sitowise and AFRY struggle with restructuring drag and weak buildings markets, with robust pricing power and contract indexation defending Norconsult from severe margin compression experienced by smaller competitors, and exceptional cash flow quality secured by Norconsult's historically negative net working capital profile and fast billing cycles.

Outliers worth noting

Norconsult's employee co-ownership structure represents a fundamental organizational outlier, with workers owning approximately half the company and generating a 66% participation rate in share purchase programs– this figure stands more than three times higher than the 20% peer average participation rate, creating an unprecedented alignment mechanism that differentiates the firm from listed competitors like Sweco, Multiconsult, and AFRY who operate under conventional equity structures.

The company's adjusted EBITA margin of 9.5% in 2025 (calendar-adjusted) significantly outperformed the calculated peer median of 7.5%, yet this premium exists despite Norconsult maintaining materially higher overhead commitments through its coownership programs, suggesting an unexplained operational efficiency advantage or superior contract mix that warrants deeper investigation into whether margin sustainability can persist as the firm scales internationally beyond its core Norwegian fortress.

The group exhibited an exceptionally high cash conversion rate of 130% in FY2025, maintaining close to 100% operating cash conversion consistently over multiple years, which deviates dramatically from the typical 80-90% base rate observed across capital-light professional services firms and raises questions about the structural sustainability of working capital benefits as the business mix shifts toward larger platform acquisitions with potentially longer payment cycles.

The Aas-Jakobsen acquisition at 13.9x 2024 adjusted EBITA marked a striking departure from Norconsult's historical mid-single-digit bolt-on acquisition multiples, representing both the largest transaction in company history and a valuation outlier that suggests either unprecedented strategic value in bridge engineering capabilities or potential execution risk if integration synergies fail to materialize according to the projected NOK 25 million pre-tax target. - Earnings Calls The 2023 employee gift share program created a NOK 285 million drag on 2024 operating profit, representing an extraordinarily large non-recurring compensation expense that distorted year-over-year comparisons and created unusual volatility in reported earnings metrics, yet this one-time event paradoxically reinforced the company's structural co-ownership differentiation while simultaneously complicating investor understanding of underlying operational momentum.

Norconsult achieves 1.5x the gross revenue of secondplace domestic competitor Multiconsult while simultaneously holding a strong second-place momentum ranking in the Pan-Nordic peer universe, creating an apparent paradox where absolute Norwegian market dominance coexists with relative underperformance against Sweco's superior scale and geographic diversification, highlighting an unexplained strategic tension between defending domestic leadership and pursuing aggressive international expansion that could fracture resource allocation priorities.
