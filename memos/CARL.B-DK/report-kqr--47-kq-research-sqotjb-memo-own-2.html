Section 2 – Carlsberg Balance Sheet Cycles and Multiple Re-ratings

The historical 2015 to 2019 deleveraging cycle

The group's net debt to EBITDA leverage ratio fell from a peak of 2.34x in 2015 to 1.25x by the end of 2019. This disciplined balance sheet management allowed the company to restart share buybacks in 2017, culminating in a substantial DKK 4.1bn repurchase program executed during 2019.
