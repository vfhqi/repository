Outliers worth noting

The third outlier concerns the extreme geographic revenue concentration in a market experiencing structural contraction. North America contributes 38% of total group revenues, yet UK footwear spending has contracted since 2019, impacting fashion retailers exposed to lower-income youth. This positions JD Sports with disproportionate exposure to two mature markets simultaneously facing weak like-for-like trends, while organic revenue grew just 2.1% in FY26 with retail LFL sales contracting by 2.1%.

The company's lack of meaningful exposure to high-growth Asian markets places it as an outlier compared to peer sportswear retailers who have deliberately diversified into regions with stronger demographic tailwinds and rising middle-class spending power. Management commentary emphasizes store conversion opportunities but does not address why the group has systematically avoided entering faster-growing emerging markets where barriers to securing brand allocations may be materially lower than in saturated Western geographies.
