EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus Earnings and Drivers

Analysts project adjusted earnings per share will climb to 3.6 Swedish Kronor for the 2026 fiscal year and 4.5 Swedish Kronor for the 2027 fiscal year, according to DNB Carnegie.

The primary upward driver points to a massive backlog of signed contracts delivering future annual recurring revenue estimated at over 2.5 billion Swedish Kronor, which will accelerate as large North American hospital networks transition to active cloud deployments.

The primary downward risk centers on hospital resource constraints and implementation bottlenecks that could delay cloud contract deployments and depress near-term EBIT margins, particularly as high-volume US contracts create substantial start-up costs that weigh on near-term margins.

Operating margin compression from upfront cloud infrastructure investments and heavy customer onboarding costs temporarily obscures the exceptional long-term earnings trajectory embedded in recurring software subscriptions.

Magnitude of Change

Sectra is aggressively transitioning its core software offerings to cloud-based subscription services under the Sectra One program, migrating customers from traditional perpetual software licenses to modern cloud deliveries.

Cloud recurring revenues surged 55.0% to 915,810 thousand Swedish Kronor in fiscal 2025/2026 from 591,058 thousand Swedish Kronor, while cloud recurring revenues surged seventy-five percent to 315 million Swedish Kronor in Q1 2026/2027, with rolling twelve-month cloud recurring revenues surpassing 1,000 million Swedish Kronor for the first time.

The structural business model shift creates delayed revenue recognition that will depress near-term earnings per share before yielding substantial operating leverage as cloud contracts deliver stable subscription cash flows over multi-year horizons.

This transition represents a fundamental strategic pivot that will reshape Sectra's financial profile and valuation trajectory over the next three years.

Latest Results

Sectra achieved net sales of 963 million Swedish Kronor in Q1 2026/2027, representing a 25.8% increase, surpassing the twelve percent organic sales growth forecasted by DNB Carnegie, while operating profit rose 60.9% to 191 million Swedish Kronor, beating the broker forecast of 40% growth.

Cloud recurring revenue surged seventy-five point three percent to 315 million Swedish Kronor, helping Sectra exceed 1 billion Swedish Kronor in rolling twelve-month cloud recurring revenue for the first time and exceeding the 70% growth expected by analysts.

Historical delivery record shows management has proven its financial credibility by routinely exceeding all three group-wide long-term financial goals, with the equity to assets ratio reaching 47.8% in 2025/2026 comfortably beating the thirty percent stability floor, while operating profit per share over five years grew 103.0%, vastly outperforming the fifty percent baseline target.

Management typically beats consensus by triggering positive consensus earnings revisions after delivering operating profit beats, with DNB Carnegie raising adjusted earnings per share estimates by three percent following moderating external costs and slower headcount growth, though this unbroken record may reflect systematically conservative guidance rather than operational outperformance, as management routinely talks down near-term margin potential to establish a low bar for earnings beats.
