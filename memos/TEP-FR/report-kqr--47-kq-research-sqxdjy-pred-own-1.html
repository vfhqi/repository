Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Understanding restructuring costs is critical to assessing Teleperformance SE's true underlying earnings per share and valuation. Evaluating these charges reveals how effectively the company is navigating its transition to artificial intelligence and offshore delivery.

Defining the Key Investigative Focus

Management has historically treated these severance and consolidation expenses as one-off items to present a stronger recurring margin profile. This classification strategy can obscure the ongoing structural expenses required to downsize onshore operations and migrate delivery offshore. Consequently, analyzing these cash outflows is crucial for investors to calculate true normalized cash flows.
3.

For instance, the company currently trades at a depressed forward price-to-earnings multiple of just 5.8x as investors price in structural disruption. If restructuring charges remain elevated beyond FY2026, it will continue to limit capital available for share buybacks and de-leveraging. This valuation disconnect implies that even stable cash generation could drive a significant re-rating if execution succeeds. Thus, restructuring costs might not prevent multiple expansion if core operational metrics stabilize.
