1.

This indicates that the core measurement business is not suffering from severe structural erosion, although the ongoing hardware weighting continues to cap gross margin expansion.

Divisional margin trends: Hexagon AB experienced varying margin pressures throughout 2026 as its corporate structure was reshaped by key divestments and acquisitions. These consecutive quarterly declines highlight how the removal of high-margin software revenues from the divested Octave segment has fundamentally reset Hexagon's baseline profitability.
