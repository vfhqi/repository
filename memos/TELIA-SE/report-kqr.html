Section 2 – Market Repair and Tariff Pricing Dynamics

Section summary
  1. European converged telecom operators are successfully transitioning from aggressive price-cutting to rational tariff indexation, driving ARPU recovery across the Nordic and Benelux regions.
  2. However, structural pricing gaps between promotional front-book offers and indexed back-book bases continue to elevate churn risks, keeping top-line momentum fragile in Finland and Switzerland.

Nordic Mobile Market Repair

1.
Selective repricing mechanisms: Telenor is executing targeted portfolio optimizations across its Nordic footprint, introducing a NOK 50 back-book increase in Norway in November 2026 to defend mature retail margins.
2.
Competitive market constraints: Morgan Stanley notes that despite Nordic operators pursuing selective repricing actions, intense competition in core markets continues to challenge historical safe-haven valuations.
3.
Finnish tariff stagnation: Elisa has upgraded its customer base using value-added services but expects that flat postpaid ARPU trends will delay service revenue growth acceleration until late 2026.
4.
Finnish campaign recovery: Elisa initiated a campaign price increase of approximately EUR 10 in early 2026, encouraging DNA and Telia to follow, which indicates constructive domestic market repair.
5.
Discounted contract overhang: Bernstein research warns that Finnish mobile operators face extended margin pressure because customers locked into highly promotional 12-month contracts significantly delay the positive pricing recovery.
6.
Sweden portfolio upgrades: Telia's strategy to migrate Swedish postpaid subscribers onto unlimited plans through family tariffs supports modest domestic ARPU expansion.

Benelux Indexation and Front-Book Steps

7.
Contracted inflation protection: Bernstein reports that CPI-linked price adjustments are widely embedded in Dutch consumer contracts, supporting predictable inflation pass-through and protecting core retail connectivity margins.
8.
Dutch promotional offsets: Deutsche Bank highlights that KPN's implementation of front-book price increases successfully offsets retail promotions, maintaining highly covered cash returns that provide durable sector yield support.
9.
Broadband speed restructuring: KPN successfully adjusted its broadband portfolio by removing its cheapest 100Mbps tier for new customers, which helps back-book price increases stick.
