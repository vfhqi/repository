This matches its projected 2027 share, reflecting strong surgeon loyalty relative to key competitors. Consequently, Alcon's high-margin surgical revenue remains insulated from severe erosion.

Competitor share dynamics: Rival product launches are driving subtle shifts in competitor market shares. Jefferies Research reports that Johnson and Johnson's share is expected to decline from 18% in 2026 to 17% in 2027. These dynamics show that RxSight represents a more immediate share threat than traditional players.
3.
Transitory competitive pressures: Market observers believe Alcon can withstand the current wave of competitive product introductions. Bernstein Research argues that the market pressure on Alcon's implantable business is transitory rather than structural. Although Johnson and Johnson is launching competitive trifocal and extended depth of focus options, Alcon's scale allows it to outspend peers on research. Therefore, Alcon is well-positioned to defend its market share over the next year.

An academic ophthalmologist revealed that their practice saw premium lens volumes decline slightly by two to three percent year-over-year.
