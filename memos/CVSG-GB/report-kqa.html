This investigation was officially launched by the CMA in May 2024 following an initial review. The final orders, which were published on 22 September 2026, establish legally binding rules designed to improve pricing transparency and competition across the veterinary market.
2.
Potential risk: Regulatory interventions represent a major structural challenge for CVS Group because any contraction in clinical fees directly degrades the company's operating profit. Historically, medicine sales have generated a substantial portion of veterinary practice profits. If medicine margins are compressed by the new rules, the company must raise professional service fees to protect its overall operating margin and support its earnings per share.
3.
Stable consensus: Broker and company-compiled consensus forecasts for FY27 currently assume that CVS Group will deliver adjusted EBITDA between £149.0m and £151.7m. 5.
