Growing competitive threats: Private competitors such as Grimaldi Group are positioned to capture regional market share during capacity normalization phases. These agile, non-listed operators can aggressively discount freight rates on major Atlantic and Mediterranean routes to secure baseline cargo volumes. Consequently, intense competition from private operators limits the pricing ceiling that listed carriers can command during upcoming multi-year contract renewals.
12.
Containerized transport alternatives: The persistent use of standard container ships and flat rack systems to transport finished vehicles sets a firm limit on specialized car carrier rates. Shippers are projected to move up to 3 million vehicles via container vessels in 2026 due to the specialized vessel shortage. If container shipping rates decline, containerized transport options will cap the premium rates that specialized rollon/roll-off operators can charge.

Geopolitical Shifts and Regional Risks

13.
Localized trade restrictions: The United Kingdom represents another emerging risk corridor where additional trade barriers may soon be introduced. Policymakers are contemplating higher import tariffs on Chinesemanufactured pure electric vehicles to match the European Union's protectionist duties. If the UK aligns its trade defense package, redirected Chinese auto exports will create severe logistical imbalances and depress spot freight rates.
14.
Restructured dealer inventory: Shippers face further volume pressure as domestic dealer inventory changes swing from restocking to significant destocking. Analysts project a massive destocking of roughly 770,000 units in 2027 compared to an estimated restocking of 340,000 units in 2026. This sharp downward inventory correction will likely reduce wholesale volumes and weigh on long-term vessel utilization.
