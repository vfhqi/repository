Executive Summary

Section summary
  1. The European base chemical and fertilizer sub-sector's eighteen-month to three-year EPS recovery is partially resolvable, with high confidence in fertilizer margin protection through CBAM implementation and coatings pricing retention, medium confidence in selective portfolio restructuring benefits, but low confidence in upstream base chemical margin normalization due to persistent structural energy cost disadvantages that threaten to prevent meaningful commodity chemical recovery relative to low-cost international competitors. The European chemical industry is undergoing deep structural capacity rationalization, with over nine percent of upstream capacity projected to permanently close by twenty-thirty and announced ethylene closures already reaching nearly three million tonnes, as major players including Dow, LANXESS, and Solvay accelerate plant shutdowns to counter uncompetitive local costs.

Despite severe margin compression, European ethylenenaphtha contract margins surged fifty percent year-on-year in October, signaling potential reversal of headwinds, while producers aggressively pursue portfolio separations including LANXESS's Envalior stake monetization and BASF's planned divestments. Fertilizer markets exhibit structural resilience anchored by the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism's definitive phase implementation in January twenty-twenty-six, which introduces comprehensive carbon certificate obligations for importers and creates a fairer competitive playing field for low-carbon European manufacturers, though high European gas prices are projected to increase Yara's third-quarter gas bill by seventy-five million dollars year-on-year.

Yara's strategic acquisition of the Gulf Coast Ammonia plant enables partial decoupling from European gas prices, shifting geographical exposure to thirty-eight percent Henry Hub-linked production versus forty-two percent European TTF, while Tessenderlo expands its sustainable agriculture portfolio through Swedish potassium sulfate plant acquisitions.
