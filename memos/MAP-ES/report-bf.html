EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Business Foundation and Strategic Positioning

Geographically diversified insurance operator leveraging dominant Iberian core, profitable Latin American partnerships, and specialized reinsurance capabilities across 38 countries. Iberia serves as leading profit contributor, representing over 32% of total group premiums through comprehensive life and non-life solutions. - Earnings Calls Geographic premium split: Iberia 28%, Brazil 15%, and Mapfre Re 26% of total consolidated premiums. Dense bancassurance joint ventures provide critical distribution networks, notably the exclusive Banco do Brasil alliance running until 2031. BB Mapfre segment delivers exceptional profitability, offsetting less profitable direct channels in Brazil. Revenue heavily weighted toward Non-Life lines, which consistently generate approximately 80% of premiums totaling 22.40 billion euros. - Analyst/Investor Underwriting focus transitioning from defensive portfolio cleansing in Auto to profitable growth, targeting over 13% ROE and 93-94% combined ratio by 2026.

Financial Performance and Technical Profitability

Record-low combined ratio of 92.20% in 2025 represents structural turnaround in technical profitability versus historical peer averages. Technical margin recovery: Non-life combined ratio improved from 94.40% in 2024 to 92.20% in 2025.

Net attributable profit expanded 19.60% to 1.08 billion euros in 2025, crossing the 1 billion euro threshold. Group ROE reached 12.40% (13.30% excluding extraordinary items), bridging gap with retail direct competitors though lagging Admiral's exceptional 53%. Balance sheet resilience strong with Solvency II ratio of 210.40% in late 2025, comfortably exceeding strategic target. Brazil primary profitability driver with combined ratio of 82% in late 2025, down 2 percentage points year-on-year.

Competitive Strengths and Structural Moats

1.
Dominant market positions: Number one multi-risk position in Spain (18.10% share) and second in Spanish Motor (17.10% share).
2.
Massachusetts auto concentration: Leading private auto with 18% market share representing recurring premiums of 200 million dollars annually. Highly integrated value chain position controlling superior margins in primary underwriting and reinsurance, with Mapfre Re sustaining 91.20% combined ratio. Multi-policy bundling creates substantial switching costs, with customers typically holding multiple distinct policies enabling relationship pricing and strengthening retention. - Earnings Calls Strong execution and underwriting discipline under Chairman Antonio Huertas, targeting sustainable return on equity exceeding 13%.

Primary Risks and Structural Vulnerabilities

Revenue model geographic diversification remains highly dependent on currency translation stability, with prolonged macroeconomic volatility derailing premium growth. Structural profitability gains heavily exposed to inflation and severe secondary natural peril events, risking combined ratio reversal above 95%. Net earnings hit by 85 million euro claim from California wildfires in 2025.

Third-party brokers and bancassurance distributors wield significant commission power, with partners capturing up to 60% of first-year premiums. High-margin bancassurance structurally exposed to renewal friction if banking partners demand higher commission splits after 2031. Vertical integration threats from automotive OEMs gaining long-term credibility, challenging traditional primary insurance distribution models with embedded telematics offerings. Investment portfolio heavily exposed to peripheral European sovereign debt risk, with widening Spanish credit spreads compressing regulatory capital headrooms.
