Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Bufab faces significant operating leverage as organic growth recovers because opex-to-sales remains at the high end of its ten-year historical range.
  2. Understanding this fixed-cost sensitivity is critical because minor shifts in organic volume can drive disproportionate changes in earnings per share.

Central Query and Core Scope

1.
Strategic question: "how much fixed-cost operating leverage does this business model carry, how much scope is there for it to work over the next 18 months to four years, and, where it is relevant to the company's strategy, how feasible is cost elimination or reduction, both incremental and in material large cuts?"
