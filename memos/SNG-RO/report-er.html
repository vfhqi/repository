The H1 2026 earnings released on August 14, 2026, fundamentally altered the market's understanding of Romgaz by demonstrating that the company can sustain record profitability even as traditional upstream revenues contract sharply. Consolidated revenues declined 8.59% year-on-year to RON 3,882.98 million, driven primarily by a 10.04% decrease in gas sales revenues due to lower physical delivery volumes, yet net profit grew 3.37% to RON 1,735.90 million, achieving a record net profit margin of 43.7% in Q2 2026. The earnings release exposed a material positive deviation from the bearish consensus expectations established prior to the results. The company's ability to deliver record bottom-line margins despite tightened regulated gas selling prices falling from 120 RON/MWh to 110 RON/MWh under GEO 12/2026 demonstrates management's capacity to navigate punitive regulatory constraints through aggressive cost discipline and favorable fiscal engineering. Nevertheless, the earnings simultaneously highlighted severe structural vulnerabilities that will intensify over the next eighteen months.

Shareholders approved contracting a EUR 100 million revolving credit facility in October 2026, adding to existing EUR 325 million facilities and multiple EUR 500 million bond issuances. This structural shift from a high-dividend, cash-generative model to a capital-constrained, leveraged growth phase represents the most profound strategic transformation in the company's modern history.

Romgaz completed the acquisition of Azomures fertilizer production assets on September 2, 2026, designed to establish a partial natural hedge against volatility by consuming natural gas in higher value-added industrial applications, yet the business transfer agreement requires up to RON 68.78 million in additional funding to maintain activity during the transition, compounded by RON 49.80 million in consumables and raw materials.
