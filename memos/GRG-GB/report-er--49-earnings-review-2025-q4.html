Humble beginnings: Greggs began in 1939 when John Gregg started delivering fresh eggs and yeast by bicycle to families in Newcastle. The company has since transformed into the leading seller of food-on-the-go in the United Kingdom. Greggs now operates a massive retail network of over 2,700 shops across the country. The company's extensive scale helps Greggs maintain its market-leading position in takeaway food and savouries.
2.
Efficient integration: Greggs differentiates itself by operating a highly efficient vertically integrated business model. The company manufactures most of its products centrally, transports them frozen, and bakes them fresh in shops. An insourced supply chain is highly unusual in the food-to-go sector and delivers exceptional end-to-end efficiency advantages. Controlling the entire process from start to finish allows Greggs to offer unbeatable value to customers.

Unbeatable value: The core appeal of Greggs is built upon a strong value perception that undercuts high-street competitors. Greggs prices its menu well below high-street coffee and sandwich peers such as Pret A Manger and Costa Coffee. Focused value leadership protects its reputation for value and keeps the brand highly resilient.
