Executive Summary

Section summary
  1. The Key Question, how plausible the change now expected at Convatec is given its record of delivery and the strengths it already has, can be answered only in part today. Overall confidence in the plausibility of the expected margin and organic growth progression is medium. The strengths are demonstrable in the evidence. The proof points that would settle the answer are not yet observable: the H2 2026 margin step-up, validation of the automated Infusion lines, and conversion of the US GPO wins.
  2. The feasibility of Convatec's transition from a turnaround play to a high-margin compounding business hinges on leveraging its historical automation and established product pipeline rather than acquiring new commercial capabilities. Net read: Delivering on the anticipated H2 2026 margin step-up and the 24 percent operating margin target by 2027 will materially de-risk consensus EPS growth forecasts and potentially narrow the valuation discount to peers.

Finding 1: What consensus assumes. Morgan Stanley estimated that adjusted earnings per share will rise from 17.7 cents in FY2025 to 26.3 cents by FY2028, supported by sequential margin expansion and organic revenue growth. The wider framing assumes delivery of the anticipated H2 2026 margin step-up and the 24 percent operating margin target by 2027. Organic growth is expected to accelerate from the 5% recorded in the first half of 2026 to 6% to 8% from 2027.
