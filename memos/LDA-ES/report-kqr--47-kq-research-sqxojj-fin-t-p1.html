Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Operating leverage dictates whether direct insurers can sustain rapid earnings growth during competitive Spanish motor pricing recoveries.

1.
Core focus: "Linea Directa Aseguradora (LDA-ES): a Spanish pure direct-to-consumer personal-lines insurer selling motor (about 81% of premiums), home (about 15%) and health under the Vivaz brand (about 3%) insurance, plus small pet and SME lines, through telephone, web and app from a single site, with its own repair centres (Centro Avanzado de Reparaciones) and no branches or brokers. 1.

Mapfre reported consolidated acquisition expenses of €6,110.10 million in 2025, representing a massive portion of its operating cost base compared to direct-response competitors. These high intermediary costs limit Mapfre's ability to lower prices aggressively, which means direct carriers like Linea Directa can maintain a sustainable pricing discount during soft market periods.
2.
Structural efficiency gap: Pure direct-to-consumer insurers operate with a fundamentally lower administrative overhead than branch-heavy peers. DNB Carnegie notes that Nordic and European P&C direct models achieve a 10 percentage point combined ratio advantage primarily due to structurally lower customer acquisition costs and higher operational automation. This cost advantage means direct platforms can sustain profitable growth unless rising marketing expenditures on search engines erode their competitive edge.
3.
Divergent customer churn: Traditional agent-led distribution networks provide established insurers with a strong defense against price-driven policyholder shopping. DNB Carnegie reports that traditional carriers benefit from low customer churn and strong brand loyalty compared to direct channels where consumers are highly pricesensitive. Consequently, agent-led networks can pass higher claims inflation costs to clients because price is rarely a deal-breaker for loyal customers.
