Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Global category leader: Straumann is the world's largest dental-implant company by share, holding a 35% global implant market share across a premium flagship brand and a portfolio of value challenger brands
2.
Revenue scale and growth: The group generated CHF 2.61bn of revenue in FY25, up 8.9% organically (4.1% reported after Swiss Franc translation), from dental implants, biomaterials, CAD/CAM prosthetics, digital equipment and clear aligners
3.
Margin profile: Core EBIT margin was 25.2% in FY25 (26.5% at constant currency), against a 70.1% core gross margin, both among the highest in listed medtech
4.
Segment architecture: The dual-brand model spans a premium Straumann brand (Roxolid, SLActive, iEXCEL) at 50-55% of the premium segment, and challenger value brands (Neodent, Anthogyr, Medentika, NUVO) at 18-20% of the value segment
5.
Third franchise, structurally weaker: Orthodontics (ClearCorrect) is the acknowledged weak link, with an estimated CHF 600-700m spent without achieving profitability and a 15% share among surveyed general practitioners against Align's 82%
6.
Geography and channel: Revenue splits EMEA 42.9%, North America 26.0%, Asia-Pacific 21.9% and Latin America 9.2% (Q1 2026), sold roughly 95% direct across 70 countries
7.

8.
Currency structure: A structural mismatch of 20% of costs in Swiss Francs against only 1% of revenue, the single most quantified risk across the research corpus
9.
Recent capital allocation: Full ownership taken of maxon dental and Promaton in 2025, DrSmile divested to Impress in 2024, and clear-aligner manufacturing transferred to Smartee with the Markkleeberg, Germany plant closed
10.
