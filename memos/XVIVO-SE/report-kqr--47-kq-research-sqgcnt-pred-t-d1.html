Record of Commercial Timeline Slippages

1.
Delayed heart timeline: Xvivo's commercial launch timelines have repeatedly experienced slippage due to regulatory and operational hurdles. Xvivo's press release on July 28, 2025 announced that the European CE mark for its heart perfusion solution would be delayed by six to twelve months due to a prolonged consultation process regarding pharmaceutical ingredients. This component delay pushed back the initial commercial rollout into late 2026. This demonstrates that regulatory requirements can disrupt planned product launch timing and delay near-term revenue generation.
2.
Kidney launch postponement: The commercial introduction of Xvivo's Kidney Assist Transport in the United States was also deferred. Christoffer Rosenblad, CEO of Xvivo, stated during the April 24, 2025 earnings call that launching the kidney technology without US-specific clinical data would not succeed. Consequently, the company had to adjust its strategy to generate clinical data over four to six quarters, effectively delaying the full launch from 2025 to 2027. This highlights how a lack of localized clinical studies can stall commercial expansion timelines in key markets.
3.
Liver strategy shift: Xvivo changed its regulatory approach for the United States liver market to accelerate its timeline. Xvivo's Q3 2026 presentation on October 22, 2026 indicated that the company paused its DeLIVER PMA trial to pursue a 510(k) pathway. This change was enabled by the FDA approval of a competitor's hypothermic oxygenated perfusion (HOPE) device in 2026. While the 510(k) pathway is expected to expedite US market access by early 2028, the prior PMA pause represents a significant pivot in regulatory execution.
