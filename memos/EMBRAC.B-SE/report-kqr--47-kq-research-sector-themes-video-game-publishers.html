Story 1: The Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Web Shop Disintermediation Flywheel

2.
Statement: Mobile video game publishers are actively bypassing the standard 30% Apple and Google app store commissions by migrating legacy player bases to proprietary web shops and desktop launchers, instantly driving massive margin expansion.
3.
Excitement & Scale: This represents a pure margin-capture story with near-zero execution risk. It applies directly to stable, long-tail back-catalogues, and could realistically scale to cover over 50% of total mobile bookings, adding hundreds of basis points straight to EBITDA.
4.
Mechanism: Revenue collected via external web shops avoids platform commissions, reducing distribution fees from 30% to standard payment-processing rates of 3-5%, instantly expanding gross and operating margins, boosting cash flow, and driving valuation multiples higher.
5.
Repricing & Window: Publishers can reprice and incentivize immediately by offering exclusive web-shop currency discounts (e.g., 10% bonus items), and the window remains open permanently, supported by regulatory pushes like the EU’s Digital Markets Act.
6.
Purest Listed Plays: Modern Times Group (MTG) and Stillfront Group.
7.
First Signs: In Q2 2026, MTG's DTC revenues reached 38% of group bookings (and 51% in its Midcore segment), lowering average platform fees from 17% to 12% YoY. Stillfront reached 41% DTC bookings in 2025 and expanded this to 46% in Q2 2026.
8.
Next 6-12 Months Catalysts: Further sequential expansion of DTC booking shares in quarterly reports and new web-store rollouts for legacy mobile games.
9.
How Widely Told: Moderately discussed by specialist mobile analysts, but largely ignored by generalists who look only at flat consolidated organic growth.
