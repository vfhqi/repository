EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 18 findings

Paul Chaplin represents a highly specialized scientific operator who joined Bavarian Nordic in 1999 as Director of Immunology, progressed through Head of Research and Division President roles, and was appointed CEO in May 2014 following a proven record managing critical US government contracts. His 12-year CEO tenure delivered a transformational shift from risky biotech to commercial-stage vaccine leader, anchored by strategic acquisitions of GSK travel vaccines (Rabipur, RabAvert, Encepur) and Emergent BioSolutions assets (Vivotif, Vaxchora), alongside successful manufacturing technology transfers completed on schedule and within budget. The company capitalized on unprecedented mpox vaccine demand in 2022, supplying over 15 million doses and driving Public Preparedness sales to a record DKK 5,027m in 2023. Chaplin executed aggressive share buybacks totaling DKK 500m completed in July 2026, followed by an upgraded DKK 750m program announced in August 2026. However, his communication style reveals plain-spoken transparency when acknowledging US commercial difficulties and regulatory setbacks such as the delayed ACIP MMWR publication for Vimkunya, but many investors dislike the too bullish communication style regarding pipeline and outbreak-driven sales opportunities according to DNB Carnegie.
