EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Sampo has completed its transformation into a pure-play P&C insurer following the October 2023 demerger of Mandatum and the divestment of life insurance operations, representing a material structural simplification that has fundamentally reshaped the investment thesis. The acquisition and integration of Topdanmark, completed in September 2024, has driven management to raise ultimate run-rate synergy targets to EUR 140 million by 2028, nearly doubling the 2026 interim target to EUR 105 million. This structural consolidation positions Sampo as a regional Nordic and UK P&C champion with enhanced scale, pricing power, and capital efficiency across its core underwriting franchises. No active activist presence has been identified in available public sources, suggesting the strategic transformation has proceeded without external shareholder pressure.

- Earnings Calls The forward EPS trajectory is governed by six to eight interdependent drivers, each carrying distinct impact profiles and execution dependencies. First, Topdanmark cost synergies remain the highest-impact catalyst, with all incremental synergy gains deriving from IT transformation cost savings in Denmark, though a EUR 118 million IT impairment loss recognized in Q3 2026 signals meaningful execution complexity. Second, UK motor pricing recovery shows clear cyclical inflection, with ONS CPI motor prices accelerating to positive 5.7% in May 2026 as Hastings added 180,000 customers in Q2 2026, supporting both volume and margin expansion. Third, digital claims conversion has reached the target threshold of over 70% claims reported digitally in Q2 2026, enabling sustained productivity improvements across Nordic retail channels. Fourth, share buyback execution remains highly predictable, with the EUR 350 million programme completed in September 2026 at an average price of EUR 9.24, and broker base-case assumptions pointing to EUR 450 million annual buybacks going forward.

Fifth, elevated investment yields are expected to boost pre-tax underwriting profits through sustained positive real interest rates, though high yields could tempt weaker competitors to discount premiums aggressively. Sixth, Partial Internal Model extension to former Topdanmark books provides EUR 60 million to EUR 90 million in capital relief, supporting the 90% operating result distribution framework introduced in early 2026.
