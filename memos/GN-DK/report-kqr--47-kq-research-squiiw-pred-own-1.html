Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The restructuring of GN Store Nord through the DKK 17 billion Hearing division divestment will transform the company from a heavily indebted group into an overcapitalized pure-play audio and gaming player.
  2. Balance sheet deleveraging will dramatically lower financial expenses and fund at least DKK 4 billion in shareholder distributions, which remains the primary catalyst for the investment thesis.

Strategic Financial Shift

1.
Essential query: "GN Store Nord A/S (GN-DK), a Danish maker of audio and gaming hardware which, after selling its Hearing division to Amplifon, consists of the Enterprise division (Jabra office headsets, video collaboration devices, BlueParrott and FalCom tactical communication headsets) and the Gaming division (SteelSeries gaming headsets, keyboards, mice and software). What is the composition of GN Store Nord's (GN-DK) interest-bearing debt before the closing of the Hearing sale to Amplifon (bank facilities, any bonds or Schuldschein loans, maturities in 2028 and 2030, interest rates and prepayment terms), how much of it can and will be repaid at closing, what lease liabilities remain with the continuing Enterprise and Gaming business, how will the 56 million Amplifon shares be accounted for (fair value through profit and loss or through other comprehensive income) and will Amplifon dividends on them be recognised as financial income, and what do the company and brokers (DNB Carnegie, Bernstein, Jefferies, Jyske Bank, Oddo BHF) assume for net financial items in 2027 and 2028?""
2.
Strategic pivot: GN Store Nord will transition into a pure-play audio and gaming company by divesting its Hearing division to Amplifon for a total consideration of DKK 17 billion. This transaction will allow the company to focus on its continuing Enterprise and Gaming businesses consisting of Jabra, FalCom, and SteelSeries. Consequently, the future growth trajectory and profitability of the continuing business will define the longterm investment thesis of the remaining entity.
3.
Massive deleveraging: GN Store Nord aims to drastically reduce its net interest-bearing debt of DKK 9.6 billion using the cash proceeds of DKK 12.6 billion. This capital reallocation will strengthen the capital structure and lower the company's financial leverage target to 1.0x to 1.5x. This restructuring is expected to significantly reduce net interest expenses and lower the cost of capital over the next three years.
4.
Organizational restructuring: GN Store Nord is executing a rightsizing program to eliminate DKK 200 million of stranded administrative costs with full effect from 2027. Management has already implemented these cost initiatives to protect operating margins of the remaining business. Unless these structural cost savings are achieved, stranded overheads will pressure operating margins of the continuing segments.
5.
Contrary evidence: Niels Granholm-Leth of DNB Carnegie noted in April 2026 that selling the highly profitable Hearing division leaves GN Store Nord as an overcapitalized, highly cyclical small-cap company. This view highlights that the remaining headset and gaming peripheral businesses are highly exposed to macroeconomic sentiment. Thus, the strategic focus increases cyclical risks for shareholders compared to the diversified model.

Valuation and Earnings Impact

6.
Capital return: GN Store Nord plans to return at least DKK 4 billion to shareholders through share buybacks and dividends between transaction closing and the end of 2027. Management expects to launch an initial Safe Harbor share buyback program of up to DKK 1 billion immediately after closing. This substantial return of excess capital is anticipated to drive EPS accretion and revive investor sentiment.
7.
Valuation mismatch: Bernstein analyst Susannah Ludwig maintained an Underperform rating on GN Store Nord in November 2026, citing structural risks and intense competition in the Enterprise headset market. This raises the related question of whether continuing operations can achieve the necessary margin expansion without the stable earnings of the Hearing division. Consequently, the valuation of continuing operations remains highly dependent on a market recovery.
8.
Other side of the story: While critics argue that divestment strips GN Store Nord of its most reliable cash generator, the transaction crystallizes massive value and eliminates the severe debt overhang from the SteelSeries acquisition. The resulting debt-free balance sheet and the DKK 4 billion capital return provide an immediate catalyst for EPS. Therefore, financial de-risking outweighs the near-term operational volatility of the continuing divisions.

Section 2 – Pre-Closing Capital Structure and Debt Composition

Section summary
  1. GN Store Nord maintained a total of EUR 1.5 billion in committed credit facilities maturing in 2028 with potential extensions to 2030 immediately prior to its Hearing division sale.
  2. The pre-closing debt structure consists of a EUR 937 million drawn term loan, private placements maturing in 2036, and short-term uncommitted facilities.

Pre-Closing Credit Facilities

1.
Substantial refinancing: GN Store Nord successfully established a EUR 1,000 million committed term loan facility and a EUR 500 million revolving credit facility in late 2025. This major refinancing package pushed the key debt maturities of the company to 2028 with options to extend until 2030. These bank facilities replaced previous near-term maturities and significantly improved overall borrowing terms and conditions for the group.
2.
High utilization: GN Store Nord had fully utilized EUR 937 million of its committed term loan facility by the end of 2025 while cancelling the remaining undrawn EUR 63 million. The EUR 500 million committed revolving credit facility remained completely undrawn as a liquidity buffer. This drawn term loan represents the largest interest-bearing facility on the balance sheet prior to the divestment closing.

Long-term headquarter financing: GN Store Nord utilizes structured leasing arrangements to finance its corporate office facilities. The specialized headquarter financing will be repaid gradually through lease payments until 2037. The contract grants the company an option to buy back the headquarter facility at maturity which prevents long-term lease exposure from becoming a permanent drag on earnings.

Section 5 – Accounting Treatment of Amplifon Shareholding and Dividends

Section summary
  1. GN Store Nord's board seat and shareholder agreement with Amplifon's parent company establish a strong case for significant influence and equity-method accounting.
  2. Classification under IFRS 9 would force the company to choose between fair value through profit and loss or fair value through other comprehensive income.
  3. Precedent corporate transactions reveal that board representation is the primary determinant of whether minority shareholdings trigger equity-method accounting or fair-value measurements.

Valuation and Lock-Up Structure

1.
Significant holding: GN Store Nord will hold a substantial 16% stake in Amplifon upon the closing of the Hearing division transaction at the end of 2026. This equity position consists of 56 million newly issued ordinary shares of Amplifon that are valued at €11.27 per share at the time of agreement. Consequently, this multi-billion Danish Krone stake allows the continuing enterprise to retain valuable exposure to industry upside following the divestment of its primary business division.
2.
Staggered release: GN Store Nord agreed to a strict 15-month staggered lock-up agreement for its newly acquired Amplifon ordinary shares. Under these contractual terms, about one-third of the 56 million shares will be released after nine months, while the remaining portion remains restricted until the lock-up expires. This gradual release schedule prevents the company from immediately liquidating its entire equity position and protects the market price from sudden downward selling pressure.
3.
Planned divestment: GN Store Nord management intends to reduce its newly acquired equity stake in Amplifon in a responsible and structured manner over the medium term. The company's chief executive officer explained that holding this minority position is not a permanent strategic decision for the continuing headset and gaming business. Unless market conditions deteriorate, the gradual sale of these liquid shares will provide substantial extra cash flows to fund future capital allocation priorities and buybacks.
4.
Valued contribution: DNB Carnegie analyst Niels Granholm-Leth valued the 56 million shares of Amplifon at DKK 5.4 billion in his October 2026 sum-of-the-parts model. This valuation is based on an assumed share price of €12.0 per share for the Italian audiology retail leader, compared to the transaction issuance price of €11.27 per share. This financial asset represents a major portion of GN's non-core assets which will bolster the group's overall net asset value post-closing.

Technical Accounting Designation Criteria

5.
Significant influence: GN Store Nord will obtain valuable board representation and enter into a detailed shareholder agreement with Ampliter at closing. Under IAS 28, having a representative on the board of directors and participating in policy-making decisions establishes significant influence despite holding a sub-20% stake. Consequently, this structural governance right suggests that the company must account for its investment as an associate using the equity method rather than standard fair value rules.
