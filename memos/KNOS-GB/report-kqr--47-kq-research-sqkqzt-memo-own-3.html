Seasonal dilution: Kainos Group plc planned to onboard 200 early-careers colleagues to support long-term capacity requirements and replace high-cost contract staff. Calendar headwinds: Kainos Group plc experiences lower billable working days in the second half of its fiscal year due to winter holidays and office shutdowns. For example, the business previously experienced a 2% decline in gross margins during H1 FY23 specifically due to two additional UK bank holidays.
