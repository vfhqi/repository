This turn-key service model eliminates hospital resource constraints, which means procedural adoption is accelerating rapidly.

The company plans to complete these installations on its 22 Embraer Phenom aircraft by mid-2027. This continuous real-time transmission of organ status data during transit reinforces TransMedics' premium service moat.
3.
Platform hardware upgrades: TransMedics is completing the development of its next-generation Gen 3.0 technology platform. This new platform features a completely redesigned, smaller form factor with a lower part count designed for automated assembly. Management expects these upgrades to drive significant future operating leverage, which will directly improve long-term earnings per share.

Market penetration metrics: TransMedics participated in approximately 26% of all United States heart, lung, and liver transplants in 2025. This represents about 5,100 of the 20,100 total targeted cases in the country. This substantial market share dwarfs peers like XVIVO, indicating that TransMedics dominates premium highcomplexity cases.

OrganOx and Asset-Light Perfusion

6.
Asset-light commercial model: OrganOx leverages a highly profitable, scalable business model that leases devices to medical institutions. Unlike TransMedics, which operates its own expensive transport network, OrganOx partners with third-party logistics firms. This strategy keeps SG&A costs exceptionally low, which means operating margins can expand quickly as consumable sales rise.
7.
Pioneering warm perfusion: The OrganOx metra system is a transportable normothermic liver perfusion platform utilized in over 10,000 transplants. The device automatically perfuses donor livers with oxygenated blood-based solutions at normal body temperature. This automated control reduces the clinical burden on transplant staff, which improves local hospital economics.
8.
Clinical performance comparisons: In the COPE European clinical trial, OrganOx metra demonstrated a 50% reduction in liver discards. In comparison, TransMedics OCS doubled liver utilization for circulatory death donors in its PROTECT trial. Both technologies show excellent patient survival outcomes, which suggests the expanding warm perfusion market can accommodate multiple players.
9.
Parent company synergies: Terumo Corporation is aggressively expanding the OrganOx business after acquiring the company in 2025. Terumo long supplied critical components like centrifugal pumps under the CAPIOX brand. These manufacturing synergies are expected to lower production costs, which will boost future segment profitability.
