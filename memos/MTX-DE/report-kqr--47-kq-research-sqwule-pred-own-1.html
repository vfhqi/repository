This quality issue, first announced in July 2023, has required accelerated inspections of the A320neo fleet and pressured free cash flow. The company was forced to cut its dividend over the 2023 to 2026 period to cope with these cash obligations. MTU's investment thesis remains highly sensitive to this program because prolonged financial pain undercuts share valuation and suppresses earnings growth.
