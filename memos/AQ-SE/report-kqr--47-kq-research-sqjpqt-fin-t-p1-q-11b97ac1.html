AQ Group AB (AQ-SE): Deep Dive & Micro-Operational Testing

1.1 Cost Architecture & Operational Leverage Split AQ Group operates a highly decentralized organizational model that prioritizes entrepreneurship, rapid decisionmaking, and structural cost efficiency across its 17 operating countries.

1.
Cost Structure: In full-year 2025, net sales reached SEK 9,071 million. For the first nine months of 2026 (9M 2026), raw materials and consumables totaled SEK 3,679 million, personnel expenses stood at SEK 2,108 million, and other external expenses (including facility costs, energy, and logistics) were SEK 695 million. Pass-Through Material Mechanics: AQ Group does not purchase basic bulk raw materials directly; it purchases semi-finished industrial inputs, such as sheet metal, insulated wiring, and copper/aluminum cabling. Pass-through mechanisms are embedded in customer contracts via indexation clauses, but a structural lag of 3 to 6 months exists before price changes in raw inputs are fully reflected in customer billings. Direct pass-through raw materials constitute approximately 40% to 45% of total revenue. Labor Cost Flexibility: In low-cost Central and Eastern European (CEE) manufacturing hubs (Bulgaria, Poland, Lithuania, and Estonia), personnel expenses exhibit high variable flexibility. Labor flexibility is maintained through temporary labor contracts, shift adjustments, and government-supported temporary layoff schemes. During volume contractions, internal cost reductions in CEE plants historically achieved more than twice the value of direct state subsidies. 6 7 - Earnings Calls Head Office & Site Overheads: AQ Group maintains a minimal central head-office overhead structure (~0.5% of sales), allocating nearly all operational overhead directly to local operating units. Site overheads (depreciation, plant leases, utility baselines) represent a fixed commitment of approximately 7% to 9% of group sales.

1.3 Analysis of Q3 2026 Results & Holiday Utilization Dynamics AQ Group reported net sales growth of 21.4% year-on-year, an EBIT increase of 36%, and an EBT margin of 10.0%.

Driver 1: US and China Facility Utilization During European Summer Shutdowns: Historically, Q3 is AQ

Group’s seasonally weakest quarter due to extended European factory holiday closures in July and August. In Q3 2026, management sustained record operational throughput by loading high-capacity utilization rates across plants in North America (US) and China. These regions supplied surging local demand in power grid infrastructure, data-center power equipment, and defense, directly offsetting the European seasonal lull.

Driver 2: Structural Product-Mix Shift: A greater proportion of total net sales originated from custom

medium-voltage inductive components, power enclosures, and defense assemblies, which carry higher gross margins than legacy commercial vehicle wiring harnesses.

Driver 3: Turnaround of Underperforming Acquired Assets: The mdexx business achieved positive earnings

contributions following restructuring, eliminating the operational drag experienced in 2025. 1.4 Facility Underutilization & Capacity Resizing Plans Plattsburgh (New York, USA) & Mexico Wiring Plants: Expanded to capture North American electrification and commercial vehicle business.
