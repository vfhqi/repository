1.
Positive momentum: Swedish housing transactions are displaying a clear turnaround that serves as a key leading indicator for regional renovation activity. This turnaround points to a gradual recovery in Nordic home improvement volumes by early 2027.

This sentiment sits well below the Swedish historical average of negative 21.1, indicating that immediate volume growth will be driven by urgent repairs. Consequently, retail volume recovery is expected to remain modest until broader consumer confidence normalizes.

This restrictive backdrop delays the retail volume recovery as homeowners defer big-ticket discretionary projects.

Corporate disclosures from Wickes Group PLC show that the local housing transactions rate remains stable although below historical norms as of September 2026. Sector research from Morgan Stanley forecasts that the British residential construction and repair market will experience ongoing organic softness in the near term. This base case assumes that macroeconomic weakness will continue to weigh on volume demand before recovering in 2027. Consequently, Travis Perkins' earnings projections have been lowered to reflect a slower-than-anticipated retail and professional volume turnaround.
