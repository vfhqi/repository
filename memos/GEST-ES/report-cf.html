Outliers worth noting

Gestamp's first-half EBITDA margin of 11.2% trails peer CIE Automotive's exceptional margin of 19.1% by nearly 800 basis points, representing one of the widest profitability gaps within the European automotive components sector and suggesting either structural product mix disadvantages or operational inefficiencies that persist despite the Phoenix Plan restructuring efforts. Acek's extensive related-party steel supply relationship with affiliate Gonvarri totaling €567.77 million annually represents an outsized supplier concentration risk that appears statistically anomalous compared to peer diversification practices and raises questions about arm's-length pricing discipline.

North American EBITDA margins excluding Phoenix costs reached 8.8% in Q2 2026 yet management targets double-digit margins, implying a required 120+ basis point sequential improvement that would represent an unprecedented single- quarter margin expansion velocity for a mature regional manufacturing footprint. Foreign exchange fluctuations reduced reported first-half revenues by €157 million, representing approximately 2.7% of total group sales and materially exceeding the typical 1-1.5% FX translation impact observed across European automotive suppliers with comparable emerging market exposure. Gescrap's EBIT margin contracted to 5.8% in late 2025, falling well below the 8-12% range typically sustained by established metals recycling operations and suggesting either operational execution issues or structural margin pressure unique to Gestamp's scrap consolidation model.
