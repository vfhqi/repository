Section 3 – Revenue dynamics: winners, losers and repricing power

Section summary
  1. Zero-fee instant rails threaten legacy acquirers' merchant margins but increase transaction routing complexity that modern orchestrators successfully monetize.
  2. Large enterprise contracts on IC++ structures pass cost reductions immediately to merchants, limiting any acquirer margin capture.
  3. Payment companies defend overall take rates by bundling premium compliance and fraud solutions on top of commoditized movement pipelines.

Card economics and contract structures

1.
Squeezed margins: Legacy merchant service charges face direct pressure as zero-fee alternative bank rails cannibalize high-margin card transactions.
2.
Pass-through dynamics: Under standard IC++ contract models, which account for 77% of transaction value, acquirers automatically pass fee decreases to merchants.
3.
Acquirer margin compression: Enterprise merchants on IC++ contracts can negotiate processing margins as low as 0.01% on high-volume transactions.
4.
Blended contract opportunity: Acquirers serving smaller merchants on blended contracts can temporarily retain interchange reductions for a period of 12 to 18 months.
5.
Network fee inflation: Rising scheme charges have offset interchange caps, with network fees now representing 20% of European merchant acceptance costs.
6.
Stable take rates: Despite competitive pressures in Europe, Adyen reported a stable take rate of 16.2 basis points in the first half of 2026.
