Section 6 – Raw Material Pass-Through and Margin Volatility Loops

Section summary
  1. Hexpol utilizes index-based price adjustments that pass through absolute raw material costs to stabilize absolute earnings while inducing reported margin percentage volatility.
  2. Rapid commodity price fluctuations trigger significant working capital expansions and contractions that temporarily strain operating cash flows before normalizing.
  3. Non-acquisitive specialized comparators like EMS-Chemie and formulation consolidators like Avient avoid index pricing lags to achieve superior margin stability.

Mechanics of Pricing Lag

1.
Contractual adjustments: Hexpol manages pricing volatility by utilizing index-regulated contracts with approximately 50% of its customer base. These contracts adjust prices on a monthly or quarterly basis, which creates a contractual lag of ninety days during rapid raw material price shifts. This index pricing lag means that sudden raw material spikes are not immediately reflected in sales prices, temporarily squeezing cash flow.
2.
Immediate price changes: Hexpol implements instant adjustments for customers without pricing contracts to protect absolute margins. For larger contractual clients, the company must wait for the next scheduled review period to pass through volatile cost shifts up or down. While this transparent mechanism ensures customer acceptance, it introduces short-term operating cash flow volatility depending on commodity direction.
