EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 26 findings

The fundamental picture facing Altareit presents a complex duality: the company has executed a profound institutional leadership transition with the appointment of Edward Arkwright as CEO in January 2025, bringing professional management pedigree from Aéroports de Paris, yet ultimate control remains firmly anchored through founder Alain Taravella's family-owned holding structure that commands 99.85% ownership through parent Altarea, effectively insulating the entity from any activist shareholder pressure or minority governance influence. This defensive corporate architecture provides strategic latitude for management to pursue strategic diversification into photovoltaics, data centers, and real estate debt funds–generating 7,960 total residential bookings in 2025 with a 17% surge in institutional block sales offsetting a catastrophic 34% collapse in retail investor volumes following the termination of the Pinel tax incentive scheme.

The financial profile reflects severe cyclical revenue compression with consolidated revenues plunging 25.4% to EUR 1,845 million in 2025, yet operating income FFO rose a robust 11.1% to EUR 76.5 million driven by highmargin new-generation residential project deliveries, while shareholders' equity stood at a solid EUR 716.7 million in H1 2026, loan-to-value improved to 29.5%, and S&P Global affirmed the BBB- investment-grade rating with stable outlook in March 2026. Crucially, available cash reserves totaled EUR 1,047 million in H1 2026, enabling the group to repay EUR 343 million of maturing bonds entirely from internal cash in April 2025, limiting net debt to just EUR 302 million and providing a substantial liquidity cushion against prolonged market weakness.
