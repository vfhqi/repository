The operating drivers behind the inputs

Revenue. H2 2026 at £252m (+3.3% reported on H2 2025's £244m): organic growth returns as guided, Kuantan ships the transferred volumes, Power's deferred contracts land, Components grows about 6% on its ex-Plano base, EMS excluding the transferred customer grows about 4%; currency assumed neutral. H1 2027 at £243m (+6.5% on H1 2026's £228.1m): the Kuantan customer's £14m H1 2026 shortfall laps (I assume about £10m recovers), plus about 3% underlying growth; the £550m order book (13 months of revenue, book-to-bill 1.12x, 1.14x in Power) and discoverIE's six-month order-to-revenue lag mean this half is largely written. H2 2027 at £261m (+3.6%). FY2028 +4.0%: defence continues, restocking plateaus, the 4% to 6% medium-term target's low end. FY2029 and FY2030 +3.6% and +3.5%: below the market's 5% to 7% because TT holds no share-gain or new-market engine; the first set's judgement that standalone growth sits below the industry's. Price contributes 1 to 1.5 points a year throughout; volume the rest.

Margin and EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA is adjusted operating profit plus depreciation and amortisation excluding acquired-intangible amortisation (FY2025: £37.2m plus £12.6m equals £49.8m, 10.3%, the brokers' basis). The operating-margin bridge from 7.7% (FY2025) to 9.8% (FY2028): volume absorption at a 25% to 30% drop-through on about £43m of revenue growth, plus 180bp to 220bp; the administrative cost programme (£3m net in FY2026, over £6m annualised from FY2027, nothing further), plus 120bp; Power mix, plus 30bp to 40bp; central cost leverage, plus 20bp; the Plano last-time-buy comparator, minus 70bp; wage, compliance (EO 14415, NIST) and energy inflation on a fixed base of about £110m to £120m at about 3% a year, minus 150bp cumulatively. How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
