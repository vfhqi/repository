Section 4 – Competitive Dynamics and Gigawatt Campus Bid Awards

  1. Gigawatt-scale data center campus construction remains highly concentrated among established incumbents like Turner and Skanska due to the unprecedented technical scale, execution complexity, and severe bonding capacity constraints.
