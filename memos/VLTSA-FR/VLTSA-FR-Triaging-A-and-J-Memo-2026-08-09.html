B.1 Business Description 8 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Integrated renewable operator. Voltalia both owns and operates generation assets as an independent power producer and sells the full project lifecycle as a service, development, EPC construction, operations and maintenance, with 2025 turnover split 54% Energy Sales and 46% Services
2.
Scale. Turnover grew from EUR 57.4m in 2015 to EUR 587.8m in 2025, a ~26% ten-year compound annual growth rate, on a 12 GW development pipeline split Europe 31%, Africa and International 34%, Latin America 34%
3.
Technology mix. Solar is 67% of installed capacity; the SPRING plan has just narrowed technology scope to solar, onshore wind and battery storage, abandoning biomass, hydrogen and small hydro
4.
Geographic concentration. Latin America is a disproportionate 54% of installed capacity, and Brazil specifically is the source of the year's dominant operating problem, grid curtailment
5.
Balance sheet character. Gross financial liabilities of EUR 2.49bn against EUR 954m of equity, net debt EUR 2.18bn, net debt to EBITDA of 10.3x at constant exchange rates, an all-in debt cost of 6.14%
6.
Segment economics. Energy Sales earned a 59% EBITDA margin in 2025 and generates 82% of consolidated EBITDA on 54% of turnover; the Renvolt services carve-out earned 9%; the blended group EBITDA margin is 36%, down from 42% in 2018
7.
Ownership. The Mulliez family holds 71.3%, with Proparco (2.5%) and the EBRD (1.6%) as the next-largest holders; no dividend has ever been paid, with a first payment targeted for 2028
8.
Investment style. This is an event-driven restructuring case, not a compounder or a classic cyclical: a new CEO (Robert Klein, January 2025) is executing a self-funded turnaround (SPRING) against a severely impaired 2025 base (a EUR 128.1m net loss)
