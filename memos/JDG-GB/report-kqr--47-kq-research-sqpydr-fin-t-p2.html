1.

To protect its earnings from rapid sterling fluctuations, the company typically hedges all expected exposure using forward contracts and currency options. 2.
Sensitive translation thresholds: The translation of foreign earnings back into sterling introduces material volatility to reported pre-tax profits for Judges Scientific. In its annual report on 31 March 2026, the company disclosed that a 5% variation in exchange rates on year-end residual exposure would impact pre-tax profits by £0.1m. To mitigate this, the company maintained currency options to sell €9.8m and $41.3m at pre-agreed rates throughout the fiscal period. These hedging arrangements help stabilize reported earnings per share during periods of high currency volatility but cannot prevent long-term translational headwinds.
