EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Zoran Bogdanovic is an archetypal operator-track CEO with a 30-year internal climb across diverse geographic territories, offering deep execution-level credibility though his exclusive life-career focus inside Coca-Cola HBC risks creating a career-administrator bias. His operational delivery at Coca-Cola HBC since December 2017 has been outstanding, achieving record 11.7% comparable EBIT margin in 2025, expanding margins by 40 basis points organically despite severe inflation, and nearly doubling comparable basic EPS to €2.72 from €1.44 in 2019. Return on invested capital expanded 100 basis points to 19.4% in 2025, while the group captured 110 basis points of NARTD value share in Q1 2026, marking six years of continuous gains, with energy volumes growing 27.0% representing the tenth consecutive year of double-digit segment expansion.

Bogdanovic exhibits an exceptionally strong animal-CEO archetype through restless drive to continually push stronger execution, prioritizes rapid local decision-making and digitisation over corporate deliberation, displays an owner mindset by cancelling share buybacks to fund transformational Africa expansion, and under his leadership CCH's 2024 actual sales exceeded initial 2022 forecasts by 30% while actual operating profits outperformed by 40%. His 2,022% of basic salary shareholding far exceeds the 450% guideline, demonstrating superior equity alignment, though discretionary sales of 30,000 shares in February 2026 and 8,393 shares in March 2026 to cover tax liabilities warrant monitoring.

Governance robustness is characterized by a 13-member Board where 46% qualify as independent, but concentrated 44% co-control by Kar-Tess Holding and The Coca-Cola Company structurally limits voting contestability. Average board tenure was reduced to 5.5 years, minimizing entrenchment risks, and external auditors reported zero material weaknesses or restatements over the last three years. The senior leadership team demonstrates exceptional depth with an 82% internal appointment rate in 2025 and 96 successors identified for function head roles, while CFO Anastasis Stamoulis brings 18 years of continuous service and world-class operational financial discipline. Expert network insights overwhelmingly validate management's public narrative, confirming that RGM tools and ways of working are highly standardized across geographies with specialized Kantar XTEL integrated with SAP for trade promotion optimization, procurement experts rate CCH's 90% to 95% System Line Efficiency Delivery as best-in-class globally, and the CCH and KO partnership is materially more synergistic and collaborative than rival PepsiCo structures. However, former CCBA head of commercial excellence warns that realizing African synergies will be a slow burn, with 70% of the cooler float nearly 30 years old in Kenya and physical distribution infrastructure in Tanzania highly inefficient requiring significant replacement capex.

The single most important credibility risk is that Bogdanovic's entire career inside CCH creates a potential career-administrator blind spot regarding non-carbonated M&A, while the pivot to high-growth markets exposes CCH to unpredictable geopolitical curfews and energy shortages with operating in emerging territories leaving group margins highly vulnerable to sudden currency collapses, management cancelled the share buyback program frustrating equity investors, fails to disclose clear long-term ROIC targets for CCBA beyond low-singledigit EPS accretion, and cannot repatriate €850.0m of cash trapped in Russian operations. The CCBA acquisition shifts CCH's risk profile by transferring cash flows from established markets into structurally volatile

African currencies, risking severe multi-year ROIC dilution if fund repatriation blocks occur.

The definitive operator quality verdict confirms and reinforces the high operator rating, with CCH standing out as one of the most consistent earnings compounders in European staples, though the market prices CCH at a 10% discount to European staples despite superior delivery. The African expansion could dilute group ROIC if severe FX devaluations and infrastructure deficits overwhelm CCH's playbooks, yet Bogdanovic's track record of navigating extreme volatility in Switzerland, Greece, and Nigeria demonstrates repeatable crisismanagement capabilities that justify high plausibility in continued execution excellence.
