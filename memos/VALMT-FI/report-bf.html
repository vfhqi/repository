Section summary
  1. Structural entry barriers remain exceptionally high in premium markets due to deep-seated trust and reference requirements, positioning Valmet as a bottleneck.
  2. Technological evolution acts as a process efficiency multiplier rather than a threat, as advanced digitization drives higher capacity without diluting aftermarket service volumes.
  3. Environmental regulations accelerate long-term demand for circular, low-carbon process lines and plastic substitution technologies, aligning with Valmet's net-zero transition roadmap.
