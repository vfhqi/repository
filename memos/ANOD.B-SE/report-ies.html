Addnode (ANOD.B-SE): what the Q3 2026 results do to the existing case

These results strengthen the operating half of our case by a full step, because the evidence trigger the case set for re-opening was met with room to spare, while the share price has moved the other way against the case's own return thresholds, so the net is positive on the evidence and unresolved on the return.

What changed

  • Design Management, the test the case set. The case re-opens on Design Management EBITA at or above SEK 100m on 23 October 2026 with the savings run-rate confirmed, and moves the full-clearing odds toward probable above SEK 109m with positive Design organic growth in Q1 2027. Design Management reported EBITA of SEK 122m in Q3 2026 (SEK 43m in Q3 2025) on net sales of SEK 595m, up 21 percent, a 20.5 percent margin. Consensus was SEK 101m and Redeye SEK 89m.
  • Savings. Management says the restructuring programme is complete and lowers the annual cost base by about SEK 100m, half realised in the second half of 2026 and half in the first half of 2027. That is confirmation of the plan, not a disclosed realised figure.
  • Group. Q3 2026 net sales SEK 1,438m, up 10 percent, organic 3 percent; EBITA SEK 250m against SEK 149m, a 17.4 percent margin against 11.4 percent, 19 percent above the Modular Finance consensus of SEK 210m; diluted EPS SEK 0.80 against SEK 0.40.
  • The comparison. Q3 2025 was depressed by SEK 80m of Autodesk renewals pulled into Q2 2025, which took SEK 70m off that quarter's EBITA. Redeye puts underlying group EBITA growth at about 14 percent.
  • The vendor. Autodesk's partner compensation levels are confirmed unchanged for 2027, and more three-year agreements come up for renewal in 2027 than in 2026.
  • Process Management. Net sales SEK 417m, up 6 percent; EBITA SEK 100m; a record 24.0 percent margin, the ninth consecutive quarter of improvement; a ten-year contract with the City of Stockholm worth about SEK 100m.
  • Against the case. Management expects roughly flat Design organic growth in Q4 2026. Product Lifecycle Management organic growth was minus 3 percent. Contract-duration disclosure was thinner than in the prior quarter, per DNB Carnegie. Net debt was SEK 2,642m at 30 September 2026; the review gives no leverage ratio for that date. Operating cash flow was SEK 9m. Capitalised own work was SEK 52m against SEK 45m a year earlier and DNB Carnegie's expected SEK 47m.
  • Sell-side. DNB Carnegie kept its buy rating, raised its target to SEK 75 from SEK 70 (26 October 2026) and raised adjusted EPS by 10 percent for 2026 to SEK 4.0, 4 percent for 2027 to SEK 4.4 and 2 percent for 2028 to SEK 4.6. Redeye raised EBITA estimates by 8 percent for 2026 and 4 percent for 2027 to 2028, and trimmed its base case to SEK 99 from SEK 102 (27 October 2026) on a higher risk-free rate.
  • Price. SEK 62.3 on 13 November 2026, 16.1 percent above the 200-day average, with relative strength at the 74th percentile. The case states SEK 55.5 as the price below which the eighteen-month modal return clears the bar and SEK 48.5 for both horizons. The price is above both.

The challenge

Reading one: the quarter restored a level and the market is paying for a trend. Design Management EBITA rose SEK 79m year on year, and SEK 70m of that is the prior-year timing effect. On our arithmetic the underlying improvement is about SEK 9m, earned while absorbing a commission headwind the brokers put at SEK 25m a quarter. That is a real absorption of the cut, and it is also exactly what the case said a clean quarter would show: the 2025 level back, not a material growth rate. What the case does not hold is the consequence. Redeye says the savings flowed through faster than expected, and DNB Carnegie cites seasonal support in costs and higher capitalised work. If part of 2027's saving was booked in Q3 2026, the brokers' upgrades are a pull-forward, and their own pattern says so: plus 10 percent for 2026, plus 4 for 2027, plus 2 for 2028. For this reading to be right, the Q4 2026 Design margin has to fall back materially from 20.5 percent on flat organic sales, and realised savings by early 2027 have to add little beyond what Q3 already contained. It is plausible, and it is the reading that turns a met trigger into a one-quarter event.

Reading two: the case is watching the wrong division. The case treats Process Management as the strong third where the change does not happen. In Q3 2026 it earned SEK 100m against Design's SEK 122m, at a margin Redeye now carries at 23.6 to 24.0 percent for 2027 and 2028, with the largest own-software contract in the group's history signed. If proprietary public-sector software is becoming the largest and steadiest profit line, then foundations judged on the reselling half describe a shrinking share of what an owner buys, and the multiple the case caps at its partial-clearing branch is too low for the mix. For this to be right, the 24.0 percent margin has to hold without help from capitalised development, which rose in the quarter and which the case names as its weakest earnings-quality point, and the division has to grow organically above the 2 percent the case flags as its alarm level. The review gives no Process Management organic figure, so this reading is possible, not yet probable.

Reading three: the confirmation from Autodesk is a yearly reprieve, not a settlement. The case puts the failure branch, a further cut or an extension of direct billing within eighteen months, at about one in four. Unchanged compensation for 2027 covers most of that window and lowers those odds. The strongest opposite reading is that terms now confirmed one year at a time make the dependency explicit, that the confirmation is silent on direct billing and on contract length, and that a quarter with good news to give came with less contract-mix disclosure, not more. Management is asking to be trusted on a 2027 renewal cohort while withholding the one figure that would size it. For this to be right, the one-year share has to be rising beyond the one-third the case assumes, so the 2027 cohort is thinner than the schedule implies. The review also weakens one support the case leaned on: Mensch und Maschine's gross profit grew only 1 percent in Q1 2026 under the same compensation changes, so the peer did not simply absorb them.

We do not put up macro deterioration as a fourth. The brokers raise it, the case already holds it, and nothing in this quarter changes it.

What it does to the case

  • The park verdict. One of its own re-opening conditions is met on the evidence. The price condition has moved further away. The case never said which governs when they disagree, and that is now the question.
  • 'Financial Outputs' (E 2, BB 1), D. Less pressure. Outputs improved in the current year's third quarter and 'Sell-side upgrades' (RA 1, E 2, BB 1) now has upgrades behind it. DNB Carnegie's SEK 4.0 for 2026 still sits below the case's SEK 4.18 for FY2025, so the Building Block's "not C" reasoning is only partly answered.
  • 'Change Forces' (E 1, BB 1), D. Same. No new internal force; the supply-side force is paused for a year, not reversed.
  • 'Modal return', C. More pressure. On the case's unchanged modal values of SEK 72.9 and SEK 83.8, a price of SEK 62.3 gives about 12 percent a year to April 2028 and about 11 percent a year to October 2029 on our arithmetic, both below the bar. Higher estimates offset part of this, not all.
  • 'Foundations' (BB 2), D. Slightly less pressure through the supplier line; reading two would add more if it held.
  • 'Investment Setup' (BB 3), C. Pressure both ways. The cut was shown absorbed, which the case required. No broker gave up; they raised. The trough multiple and the estimate cuts, the two measurable conditions that passed, are both receding.
  • 'Investment Case' (BB 4), D. Slightly less pressure on 'Riskiness' through the failure odds; the same on 'Simplicity', because the transmission is no more trackable and disclosure thinned.
  • Pinned letters, as context. Sell-side momentum F to D and technical momentum F to D since the case was written; valuation still pinned A, while the store's re-based TSR/Valuation reads D.

What would settle it

  • The second Earnings Review, four working days on. It should carry the remaining brokers' estimates and targets and a settled consensus, which tells us whether the upgrades are front-loaded across the coverage or only at two houses. It is unlikely to carry anything on contract duration, realised savings or leverage, because the company has not disclosed them.
  • The Q4 2026 report, February 2027. The first test of reading one: the Design margin on flat organic sales against what the case calls a hard comparison, the leverage ratio at year-end, and the cash acceleration management has pointed to.
  • Q1 2027. Design organic growth, the case's remaining condition for moving the full-clearing odds, and the first sight of the 2027 renewal cohort.
  • Process Management organic growth and capitalised development by division. These would test reading two. Neither is in the review.
  • Contract-duration mix. The company has made no commitment to disclose it and gave less this quarter. Reading three may stay open until Autodesk's 2028 terms are announced, which has no date.
