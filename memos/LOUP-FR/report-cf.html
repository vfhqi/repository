Materiality of Change Assessment LDC SA has undergone material structural transformation over the trailing thirty-six months characterized by orderly executive leadership transition with Philippe Gelin appointed Chairman of the Executive Board on 24 August 2023, exceptional revenue reaching €7,283.2M in fiscal year 2025-2026 representing 15.2% year-on-year growth and surpassing the €7.0B long-term revenue target one year ahead of schedule, and Group EBITDA reaching €719.7M against the strategic plan target of €560M, exceeding the long-term five-year plan target by 40%.

The operational transformation encompasses NIAGARA program targeting additional 1.2M chickens capacity by 2028 and upstream integration securing 35.5% of breeder Yer Breizh to control the upstream chain, representing a fundamental shift toward vertical integration and capacity expansion. Strategic ambition elevated materially with the strategic plan targeting surpassing €10 billion in revenue with operating income of €550M by 2031 following early completion of prior targets.

Outliers worth noting

LDC SA's fiscal year 2025-2026 EBITDA performance represents a true statistical outlier, reaching €719.7M against a long-term strategic plan target of €560M, constituting a 40% overperformance relative to the baseline five-year projection established at plan inception. This extraordinary margin expansion places LDC in the top decile of European food processors for year-on-year EBITDA growth acceleration, particularly when benchmarked against peer-group median performance where most competitors struggled to exceed single-digit EBITDA progression during the same period. [Cross-ref: Section 6] The Group's revenue trajectory achieved another material outlier, delivering €7,283.2M in fiscal 2025-2026, representing 15.2% reported growth and completing the €7.0 billion strategic revenue target one full year ahead of schedule.

When decomposed to organic growth rates, LDC recorded 7.5% organic expansion at constant exchange rates, substantially outpacing European poultry peer Scandi Standard's low-single-digit organic performance and placing LDC within the top quartile of regional protein processors for underlying momentum. This acceleration occurred despite industry-wide headwinds from avian influenza disruptions and volatile input costs, suggesting either exceptional operational execution or potentially unsustainable pricing power that may face reversion pressure.
