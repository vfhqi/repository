Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The severe contraction of SKAN's high-margin service segment threatens overall mid-term profitability targets.
  2. Determining the structural versus temporary drivers is critical for evaluating future earnings growth and cash flows.
