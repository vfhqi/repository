EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Qt Group has consolidated executive authority around its chief executive officer during a crucial operational restructuring phase, with executive team reorganization and stable board composition providing strategic continuity despite potential organizational risks from highly centralized decision-making. The company is executing an aggressive strategic shift toward subscription-based SaaS models following the EUR 204 million acquisition of IAR Systems in late 2025, which is designed to maximize long-term recurring revenue despite creating near-term top-line pressure.

Organic revenue growth decelerated to 2.6% in 2025 from 10.5% in prior periods, and Inderes expects the rapid software-as-a-service licensing model shift of IAR Systems will drag organic revenue growth down to negative 4% in 2026 before driving accelerated recurring growth in 2027.

Current consensus earnings estimates reflect this transitional phase, with substantial dispersion between covering brokers. Inderes expects adjusted earnings per share of EUR 1.52 for 2026e, rising to EUR 2.47 in 2027e and EUR 2.85 in 2028e. DNB Carnegie projects adjusted earnings per share of EUR 1.09 for 2026e, EUR 1.96 for 2027e, and EUR 2.79 for 2028e. The wide variance between these forecasts highlights execution uncertainty surrounding the EUR 20 million cost-savings program and the speed of customer conversion to subscription models. Both brokers anticipate a dramatic earnings inflection point in 2027 as restructuring benefits materialize and IAR Systems transitions complete, though this recovery thesis remains vulnerable to persistent macroeconomic headwinds. The magnitude of internal strategic changes has been profound and multifaceted. Qt Group launched a comprehensive operational reorganization on 20 April 2026 to optimize its cost structure and capitalize on acquisition synergies, with executive plans aiming to simplify decision-making and eliminate organizational redundancies across the unified group.
