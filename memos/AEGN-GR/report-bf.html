Section 3 – Industry Structure + Concentration + Value Chain Economics 21 findings

Section summary
  1. the European aviation industry is projected to grow passenger trips at a 2.1% CAGR through 2045, outstripping demographic headwinds
  2. capacity constraints from supply disruptions have replaced historical overcapacity, supporting structurally higher fare yields
  3. the market remains dominated by six large airline groups controlling 70% of capacity, yet country-level fragmentation persists
  4. low- cost carriers continue to take share from legacy networks, leveraging point-to-point routes and structural cost advantages
  5. legacy flag carriers maintain a double-digit margin advantage in premium transatlantic corridors, while short-haul yields face cyclical pressure
  6. competitor conduct is bifurcated between disciplined capacity cuts by flag carriers and aggressive market-share expansion by ultra-low-cost operators

Historical traffic baseline: Global air traffic expanded by 5.3% for full-year 2025, while Europe reported a 7.6% increase

GDP elasticity correlation: Air traffic demand historically moves with economic output, with Spain's airport passenger growth typically double its real GDP expansion
