EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 5 findings

1.
Fortum operates as a Nordic-focused utility with earnings heavily dominated by low-carbon power generation, where the Generation segment alone captures 88% of group comparable EBITDA, while the pending NOK 5.1 billion acquisition of Norwegian retailer Elmera Group represents a strategic doubling-down on fragmented retail markets that remain structurally low-margin. The operational foundation rests on a nearly fully decarbonized 47 TWh generation portfolio split between nuclear power at 52% and hydro power at 44%, and CEO Markus Rauramo's strategy targets a €330 million increase in comparable operating profit by 2030 from a €930 million baseline, though the retail customer-solutions business remains a low-margin, highly fragmented commodity market where integration synergies may fail to deliver if price competition intensifies . Fortum reported IFRS sales of EUR 4,989 million in 2025, representing an underlying drop of 14% compared to EUR 5,800 million in 2024, while financial net debt reached EUR 1,479 million translating into a prudent net debt to comparable EBITDA ratio of 1.2x that sits well below the board's maximum ceiling of 2.5x. Liquid funds stood at EUR 2,903 million at the end of 2025, supplemented by EUR 3,806 million in undrawn credit lines, and the board proposed a dividend of EUR 0.74 per share for 2025, translating to a 90% payout of comparable EPS, demonstrating robust balance-sheet strength and cash return capacity that benchmarks favorably against highly volatile European peers

. However, achieving the targeted 14% comparable RONA remains highly exposed to power price drops, and if Nordic wholesale electricity prices fall below €40/MWh the group's highly capital-intensive hydro and nuclear assets will face severe return compression that self-help cost efficiencies cannot offset . The Nordic electricity generation market produces approximately 442 TWh of power annually with demand projected to expand at a 3-4% CAGR through 2030, driven by growing demand from data centers and electrification, yet the retail market serves approximately 16.6 million customers across approximately 350 competing companies, resulting in structurally low profit margins. Generation remains highly fragmented with more than 350 active operators, though developers maintain strict capacity discipline refusing wind additions that fail to meet hurdle rates , while intense price competition in Sweden and Finland limits organic growth potential and retail operators face potential legislative changes including proposed national caps on customer mark-ups that could erase any scale efficiencies gained from consolidation . Fortum sits in a generation stage with a 28.1% EBIT margin in 2025, avoiding low-return regulated distribution networks since divesting its Swedish distribution business in 2015, and the operational foundation focuses on low-carbon Nordic power generation with 98% of output being CO2-free in 2024. Pricing power is limited near-term with 80% of 2026 output hedged at €40/MWh, though the group captures an additional €8-10/MWh optimization premium leveraging its flexible hydro assets , but prolonged wholesale power price drops below the €40/MWh hedged rate would compress generation margins without regulated grid revenues to offset the decline .

Nordic industrial customers show low appetite for signing ten-year power purchase agreements, actively preferring shorter contract tenors ranging between three and seven years to maintain flexibility , while Consumer Solutions serves 2.29 million total paying customers including 2.23 million electricity accounts, though retail electricity remains highly commoditised resulting in constant customer churn pressures. Fortum has hedged 80% of its remaining 2026 generation at 40 EUR/MWh and increased its 2027 hedged ratio to 65% at 41 EUR/MWh , yet a shift toward short-term industrial contracting and retail price-cap legislation would limit the ability to lock in favorable power prices, exposing generation margins directly to spot market volatility .
