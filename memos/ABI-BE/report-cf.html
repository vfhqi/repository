EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 25 findings

AB InBev has executed a clean strategic pivot from M&A-driven inorganic growth to organic volume expansion, anchored by three high-margin levers: premiumization through global megabrands (Corona, Stella Artois, Michelob Ultra), Balanced Choices portfolio expansion (no-alcohol beer growing 27% in Q2 2026), and Beyond Beer RTD scaling (Cutwater delivering triple-digit growth). The Board transition to Dirk Van de Put as Chairman in June 2026 brings deep consumer packaged goods expertise, while CEO Michel Doukeris has pivoted the executive committee toward sales and marketing backgrounds rather than transactional finance expertise.

The sell-side consensus shows a strong buy bias as most covering houses maintain positive ratings on consistent operational delivery. However, China revenue collapsed 8.8% in Q2 2026 due to persistent on-premise weakness, and Brazil faces intense competitive pressure from Heineken and Petropolis, creating material downside risk to the consolidated margin expansion thesis. The balance sheet has been repaired to 2.87x Net Debt/EBITDA (below 3x for the first time), unlocking a massive 6 billion USD share buyback program alongside a 22% dividend increase to EUR 1 per share.

Revenue growth has been highly predictable with positive organic sales every quarter since 1Q22, and the company met or exceeded consensus in 15 of the last 17 quarters. Forward EPS trajectory hinges on sustained premiumization momentum (estimated +9% EPS impact), BEES B2B platform monetization (estimated +3% EPS impact), and transactional FX hedge relief turning positive in H2 2026 against the structural headwinds of unhedged aluminum spot price spikes and prolonged China on-premise depression.
