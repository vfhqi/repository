EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Materiality of CHANGE

1.
Structural margin expansion: Plejd has driven EBIT margins from 11.6% in 2023 to a half-year representative 28.9% in 2026 through production insourcing.
2.
Category diversification: Transitioned from a pure lighting-control play to a four-category platform including thermostats (TRM-01) and commercial LED panels (LPN-01).
3.
Geographic pivot: Shifted focus from Nordic dominance (96% of 2023 sales) toward European replicability, with Netherlands installation growth recently reaching 58.4%.
4.
Leadership continuity: Maintained a stable management team with 10+ year tenure, recently strengthened by nominating Axis AB founder Martin Gren to the board.

Top Key Drivers

5.
LED panel ramp: Dominant driver LPN-01 aims for the Swedish small-commercial market, with early wholesaler sell-through hitting all-time highs in July 2026.
6.
Netherlands penetration: Key proof point for European expansion; achieving a Norway-like Norway-like adoption curve is critical for sustaining 30%+ growth.
7.
Production optimization: Internalizing manufacturing for luminaires like DWN-01 is expected to yield structural gross margin improvements towards mid-50% levels.
8.
Commercial sensor launch: The upcoming CMS-01 presence sensor marks a formal entry into enterprise- scale installations, expanding the addressable market.
9.
Norway market recovery: Recalibrating growth after a difficult comparison against the 2025 TRM-01 launch which saw 67% installation growth.

Recent Delivery and Credibility

10.
Q2 profitability miss: Reported EBIT of SEK 61.8 million was 19% below Redeye estimates despite a 30.3% year-over-year revenue increase.
11.
Margin volatility: Management flagged temporary Q2 margin pressure from LPN-01 ramp-up costs and a SEK 5.2 million negative FX impact.
12.
Guidance archetype: Management acts as an honest tracker, emphasizing H1 28.9% EBIT margins as representative over volatile individual quarters.
13.
Predictable compounding: EPS has compounded by more than 50% per annum since reaching profitability in 2020, supported by net cash.

Positioning and Risks

14.
Crowded bull case: Sell-side sentiment remains overwhelmingly positive, with Redeye maintaining a SEK 1,210 base case despite the Q2 margin miss.
15.
Peer-leading penetration: Generates SEK 51.4 in sales per capita in mature markets, vs. SEK 6.7 for peer Shelly in its strongest region.
16.
Geographic concentration: A material risk remains as Sweden and Norway still represent the vast majority of Group revenues.
17.
No activist presence: No active activist presence in AS library for Plejd as of August 2026.

Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro)

Section summary
  1. demand remains structurally robust driven by energy efficiency, though Plejd faces intensifying competition in the commercial segment and cyclical FX volatility.

Demand

1.
Energy efficiency tailwinds: Growth is increasingly driven by energy savings via scheduling and presence control in both homes and businesses.
2.
Residential demand resilience: Installation figures in Norway grew 50% organically in early 2026, supported by the TRM-01 thermostat launch.
3.
Search interest deceleration: Google Trends-based demand tracking in Sweden decelerated to 12% in Q2 2026, down from 34% in Q4 2025.
4.
Installation growth volatility: Norway's installation growth slowed to 33.5% in Q2 2026 vs. 67% in Q2 2025 due to high comparison bases.

Competition

5.
Incumbent segment overlap: Larger commercial lighting players like Fagerhult Group acknowledge Plejd is taking market share in smaller commercial buildings.
6.
Feature-set differentiation: Redeye notes that standard commercial LED panels are plug-and-play, narrowing Plejd's historical installation advantage.
7.
Consumer vs. Pro-led: Competitors like Shelly focus more on consumer-led markets, whereas Plejd dominates the professional electrician channel.
8.
Pricing comparisons: Wholesaler checks suggest a SEK 300 price premium for Plejd panels versus cheaper alternatives, though electricians highlight configuration savings.

Disruption

9.
App-based configuration: Plejd disrupts traditional DALI systems by allowing full-spectrum light-tone control via app, removing manual switch-flipping.
10.
Installation flexibility: Wireless technology removes the need for special cabling and complex programming required by legacy smart home systems.
11.
Software-led loyalty: High app-store rankings in the Netherlands (ranking ~135) suggest rising organic demand for Plejd's software ecosystem.

Regulation

12.
Energy Performance Directive: EU directives like the EPBD are educating the market on smart lighting benefits, acting as a demand catalyst.
13.
Environmental certification: Rumors regarding China-sourced housing were refuted; Plejd’s in-house manufacturing supports certification for large projects.

Supply

14.
Production insourcing: Transitioned to optimized production of downlights (DWN-01) to drive long-term gross margin expansion.
15.
Inventory stabilization: Shifted from high safety-stock levels during the component crisis to normalized inventory levels in mid-2026.

Cycle

16.
Wholesaler destocking: Revenue was impacted in Q2 2026 as wholesalers normalized inventories after a heavy build-up in Q1.
17.
Seasonality patterns: Q1 is typically a stronger installation quarter in mature markets, leading to improved app rankings in subsequent periods.

Macro

18.
Significant FX headwinds: Plejd faced a SEK 5.2 million negative currency impact in Q2 2026, double Redeye's forecast.
19.
US Dollar exposure: Company benefits from a weaker USD as a significant portion of component purchases are denominated in dollars.
20.
Other side of the story: Cyclical wholesaler swings can mask underlying demand; if electrician loyalty breaks during a construction downturn, the asset-light model won't prevent a revenue collapse.

Section 4 – Financial Profile Change

Section summary
  1. financial profile is characterized by accelerating operating leverage and high ROIC, though Q2 2026 showed temporary margin compression from investment timing.

Growth and Margin Trajectory

1.
Robust revenue CAGR: Organic sales have maintained a CAGR of ~30% since 2021, reaching SEK 934.7 million in FY2025.
2.
Dramatic margin expansion: EBIT margins inflected from 11.6% in 2023 to 24.6% in 2025 as sales outpaced a relatively fixed cost base.
3.
Temporary margin stall: Q2 2026 EBIT margin was flat at 22.5% year-over-year as gross margin gains were offset by personnel expansion.
4.
Gross margin resilience: Group gross margin improved 290 bps year-over-year to 54.9% in Q2 2026, despite ramp-up costs for the LPN-01.

Capital Intensity and Cash Flow

5.
Asset-light efficiency: Plejd maintains a high ROIC >30% by focusing on software and final assembly rather than heavy industrial manufacturing.
6.
Net cash position: The company ended Q2 2026 with SEK 235.9 million in cash, up from SEK 78.5 million a year earlier.
7.
Self-funded expansion: Operating cash flow after working capital reached SEK 82.1 million in Q2 2026, up 55.1% year-over-year.
8.
R&D capitalization: Capitalized work on own behalf grew 25.4% to TSEK 51,948 in H1 2026, reflecting increased investment in new products.

Shareholder Returns and Benchmarking

9.
No dividend policy: Despite strong profitability, Plejd pays no dividends, prioritizing reinvestment into European expansion and product development.
10.
Peer-leading penetration: Per-capita sales of SEK 51.4 in mature markets vastly exceeds peer Shelly (SEK 6.7), indicating deeper market wallet-share.
11.
Negative EPS revision: Analysts trimmed 2026-2028e EPS estimates by c.3-6% following the Q2 profitability miss and higher OPEX base.
12.
Other side of the story: Operating leverage cuts both ways; the high fixed-cost base means even a 3% revenue miss resulted in a 19% EBIT disappointment.
