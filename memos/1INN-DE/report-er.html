Skeptics argue that keeping these loss-making entities unconsolidated masks the true profitability profile of the international SaaS business model, and as these subsidiaries scale, the consolidated Group margin could face material pressure. Additionally, if international pilots in France and the UK fail to achieve rapid customer traction, or if the transition to Clusterix's subscription-based pricing model encounters delays, the company's aggressive EBIT compound annual growth targets may prove unsustainable.
