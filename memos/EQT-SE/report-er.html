Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. EQT AB entered its Q3 2026 earnings from a position of scaled global leadership in active ownership strategies, though its near-term outlook remained highly dependent on resolving the industry-wide exit drought and accelerating capital realizations.
1.
Established industry champion: EQT is a Sweden-based global private markets investment firm that was founded in 1994 and listed on the Nasdaq Stockholm in September 2019. The company operates as a thematic active investor across four primary segments, which include Private Capital, Infrastructure, Real Estate, and its newly established Secondaries division. According to EQT's own corporate history, the firm has scaled from a single Nordic fund to managing over EUR 270 billion in assets.

Robust global positioning: EQT has secured its position as the second largest private equity firm globally and ranks fifth in the global infrastructure market. The group manages a diversified portfolio of over 330 companies, which collectively employ more than 550,000 people. This scale represents a massive expansion from its initial public offering in 2019, when the firm employed only 665 full-time employees compared to over 1,800 today.

Consistent investment performance: EQT's core active ownership model has delivered competitive returns, generating a 2.5x to 2.6x realized gross MOIC since inception across all major investment verticals. This structural focus has left the company highly exposed to equity valuations, whereas peers like Blackstone and KKR have historically achieved faster AUM growth by leveraging large private credit franchises. Consequently, EQT's performance remains highly geared toward a cyclical recovery in corporate mergers and public equity listings.
