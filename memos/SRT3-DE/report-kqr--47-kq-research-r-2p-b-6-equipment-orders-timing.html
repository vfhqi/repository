Section 3 – Capex Budgets and CDMO Spare Capacity Hurdles

Section summary
  1. Global biopharmaceutical and contract manufacturing capital expenditure is stabilizing as investments shift from capacity additions to productivity.

  3. Strategic mergers and acquisitions like the Novo-Catalent transaction are altering procurement schedules but do not indicate systemic capacity overhangs.

This capital spending is expected to peak at $58.87 billion in 2027 before declining slightly by 1.30% in 2028. Consequently, these robust spending plans suggest that large drug makers will maintain steady equipment procurement through 2027, thereby supporting the mid-term earnings recovery of European bioprocess suppliers.
2.
Disciplined capital strategies: Top-tier pharmaceutical companies are maintaining capital allocation discipline by focusing on geographic optimization rather than overbuilding capacity. Deutsche Bank's report on 28 July 2026 showed that the average capital expenditure of the top 20 global pharmaceutical firms is projected to hover around 5% of sales through 2030. This flat capital intensity indicates that recent investments represent a geographic shift toward the United States rather than a move away from contract manufacturing. Unless these firms drastically cut outsourcing, this disciplined approach ensures a steady, non-cyclical stream of equipment and consumables orders.
