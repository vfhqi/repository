A third operational anomaly manifests in extreme geographic revenue concentration, where over 85% of consolidated sales originate in France alone. While European diversification is supported by leisure sites across five non-French continental nations, the extreme French concentration exposes CDA to idiosyncratic regulatory risks, as demonstrated by the impending loss of the Tignes ski area concession to local public operator SPL ALTTA in June 2026, which generated around €66 million in revenue.

This geographic concentration outlier amplifies the group's vulnerability to localized climate volatility, regulatory interventions, and municipal insourcing threats that could disproportionately impact consolidated performance relative to more geographically diversified peers.
