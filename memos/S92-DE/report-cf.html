CHANGE MATERIALITY

SMA Solar Technology AG initiated a comprehensive restructuring program in September 2024 targeting €150 million to €200 million in annual cost savings, expanded it in September 2025 to capture an additional €100 million, and now aims for a combined €250 million run-rate EBIT improvement by end-2027. The structural pivot shifts hardware development and manufacturing for the Home & Business Solutions segment to external contract partners while preserving premium specifications, with final assembly localized in Krakow, Poland, lowering the sustainable breakeven revenue level to €300 million to €400 million.

Internal R&D now focuses exclusively on secure software, cybersecurity, and smart energy management platforms like ennexOS, with the Global Competence Center in Bangalore designated as the central hub for software development. Deutsche Bank expects FY26 revenue of EUR 1,654 million reflecting 9% year-on-year growth, segment EBIT margins for HBS narrowed to -26% in Q2 2026 from -152% in FY25, Group EBITDA margin forecast to reach 12% in FY26 yielding EUR 196 million, with restructuring expected to deliver EUR 250 million in run-rate EBIT improvements by end-FY27.

This transformation represents a material strategic discontinuity redefining SMA from a cyclical inverter manufacturer into an asset-light, software-led energy management provider.
