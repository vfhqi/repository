B.1 Business Description 7 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Dynavox Group AB designs, manufactures and distributes augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) devices for people with severe speech and motor impairments, spanning eye-gaze hardware, symbol-based touch software and clinical funding-navigation services
2.
Primary market. The addressable population is roughly 50 million people globally with severe verbal impairments, of whom only 2% receive assistive support annually, inside a total addressable market estimated at USD 4.70 billion by the Business Foundation report
3.
Financial character. FY2025 revenue of SEK 2,467 million grew 25% reported and 34% currency-adjusted; gross margin 68.4%; reported EBIT margin 10.3% (adjusted 14.4%); net debt/EBITDA 1.7x at Q1 2026, improving from 1.9x
4.
Balance sheet and capital return. Consolidated net financial debt SEK 909 million at FY2025 year-end including SEK 207 million of IFRS 16 lease liabilities
4.1
Capital return: Board proposed its first-ever dividend of SEK 0.50 per share for FY2025, targeting a 40% payout policy
5.
Segment mix. Reports as a single operating segment; product revenue (goods) is 95.25% of FY2025 sales, services 4.5%, licensing royalties the remainder; Upper Extremities-equivalent eye-gaze and touch hardware dominate the portfolio alongside TD Snap/Boardmaker software
6.
Geography. North America generated 75-80% of FY2025 sales (the Business Foundation and Change Forces reports state both 75% and 76%, not reconciled in source); Europe roughly 19% and rising to 23% in Q1 2026; rest of world approximately 5%
7.
Investment style implication. A medically-prescribed, insurance-funded compounder with event-driven margin optionality: the growth engine is structural and secular, but the near-term case is an event-driven "margin-inflection-from-transformation-cost-tapering" story layered on top
