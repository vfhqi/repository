Section 2 – EBITDA and EBITA Definitions and Capitalised Development Amortisation

Section summary
  1. GN Store Nord's EBITDA definitions under IFRS 5 and IFRS 18 do not add back capitalized development project amortization, leaving these expenses inside operating metrics.
  2. Historical R&D capitalization at GN Store Nord contrasts sharply with the conservative full-expensing policies of key industry peers like Logitech and Demant.
  3. The continuing capitalization of product development costs creates reporting discrepancies with peers but remains a key driver of GN Store Nord's EBITDA margins.

Q3 2026 Reconciliation

1.
Substantial reporting differences: GN Store Nord reported continuing operations EBITDA of DKK 457 million under IFRS 5 and DKK 459 million under IFRS 18 in Q3 2026. Across four companies and twenty sources analyzed, this compares to adjusted EBITA of DKK 413 million. These differences stem from specific reclassifications under new accounting standards. This reconciliation is crucial because it affects how future earnings are evaluated by analysts.
2.
Clear bridge: Under IFRS 5, the transition from adjusted EBITA of DKK 413 million to EBITDA of DKK 457 million is bridged by adding back DKK 44 million of depreciation and software amortizations. The company reported operating profit of DKK 358 million, which bridges to adjusted EBITA by adding back DKK 55 million of amortization of acquired intangible assets. This clear math demonstrates that EBITDA under IFRS 5 excludes acquired intangible amortization but retains regular depreciation.
3.
Reclassified adjustments: Under IFRS 18, GN Store Nord calculates its EBITDA of DKK 459 million by adding back DKK 39 million of foreign exchange operating income or expense to the previous total. The new standard reclassifies these operating currency effects, which are added to the operating profit of DKK 321 million along with DKK 44 million of depreciation. This minor adjustment of DKK 2 million explains the discrepancy between the two EBITDA definitions presented.
4.
Structured reconciliation: GN Store Nord presents a structured view of its continuing operations in its Q3 2026 interim report, which forms the basis for these calculations. The company reported a revenue of DKK 2267 million and a gross profit of DKK 1324 million, leading to an adjusted EBITA margin of 18.2 percent. This strong margin performance is the foundation for future targets that management will present.

Development Cost Amortisation

5.
Internalized costs: The amortization of capitalized development projects is left inside EBITDA rather than being added back, which significantly impacts the reported profitability. In Q3 2026, GN Store Nord recorded DKK 100 million of amortization, impairment, and depreciation of development projects. Because this amount is not added back to EBITDA, the metric is substantially lower than if development amortization were treated like acquired intangible amortization.
6.
Distinct definitions: GN Store Nord explicitly defines its EBITA to include the amortization of development projects and software developed in-house. This definition excludes only the amortization and impairment of acquired intangible assets and any gains or losses on divestments. Since development amortization is left inside EBITA and EBITDA, the underlying operational cost of product development is fully reflected in these metrics.
7.
Sizable capitalization impact: GN Store Nord capitalized DKK 150 million of development costs in Q3 2026, while expensing DKK 207 million directly in the income statement. The net balance of capitalization and amortization of development projects remains a critical driver of profitability. This accounting choice means that EBITDA remains sensitive to the volume of ongoing development work and the speed of amortization.
8.
Strong cash alignment: The cash flow statement of GN Store Nord highlights how development projects are treated, with capitalized development costs listed under investing activities. During Q3 2026, the company generated an operational free cash flow of DKK 773 million including continuing and discontinued operations. This strong cash performance helps mitigate concerns that capitalization policies obscure true earnings or cash generation capability.

Section 3 – Impact of IFRS 18 Adoption on Financial Reporting and Guidance

Section summary
  1. The transition to IFRS 18 in FY2027 will restructure GN Store Nord's income statement and shift its primary guidance metric toward EBITDA.
  2. Under IFRS 18, GN Store Nord's operating profit will include foreign exchange operating items and exclude acquired intangible amortization.
  3. Financial analysts expect GN Store Nord to utilize EBITDA as a management-defined performance measure to maintain reporting consistency.
  4. Confidence in these structural accounting changes is high because they are based on official Q3 2026 transition reports and broker projections.

New Income Statement Categories

1.
Streamlined presentation: GN Store Nord will adopt the IFRS 18 accounting standard starting on 1 January 2027 to align with global reporting requirements. This regulation replaces the old IAS 1 standard to provide a more standardized income statement structure across industries. The new format divides all income and expenses into operating, investing, financing, income taxes, and discontinued operations. This systematic categorization will change how continuing operations are evaluated by the market.
2.
Modified operating definition: The operating category under IFRS 18 is no longer restricted to core business activities and acts as a default category. Any income or expense that does not fit into investing, financing, tax, or discontinued operations is placed here. For GN Store Nord, this means that historical non-operating items will now fall directly within operating profit calculations. This change will structurally alter the historical baseline of the company's operating performance.
3.
Investing and financing separation: GN Store Nord will separate its investment returns and financing costs into distinct financial statement categories under the new standard. Income from cash and cash equivalents is now segregated from operating results, totaling DKK 6 million in the Q3 2026 restatement. This clear division prevents financial and treasury activities from obscuring the true operational performance of the core Enterprise and Gaming divisions.
4.
Enhanced peer comparability: The standardization under IFRS 18 will allow investors to compare GN Store Nord directly with global peers who adopt the standard at the same time. NH Investment and Securities notes on 14 October 2026 that the new standard will improve the comparability of operating profit across different sectors. This means that GN Store Nord's financial performance can be evaluated more transparently against European and global technology companies.

Reclassifications and Operating Profit Impact

5.
Reduced operating profit: GN Store Nord's Q3 2026 operating profit decreased from DKK 358 million under IFRS 5 to DKK 321 million under IFRS 18. This significant reduction of DKK 37 million is driven by new operating reclassification rules under the standard. The restated operating profit is lower because foreign exchange operating losses are now fully absorbed within operating expenses rather than financial items. This compression highlights how accounting shifts can lower reported performance without operational changes.
6.
Reclassified foreign exchange: Under IFRS 18, GN Store Nord reclassifies its net foreign exchange effects from financial items into operating expenses. In the Q3 2026 financial statement, this foreign exchange operating impact amounted to a negative DKK 39 million. This treatment aligns with peer companies in export-driven sectors where operating currency movements must be recognized within operating margins rather than below the line.
7.
Intangible asset amortization: The amortization and impairment of acquired intangible assets is reclassified below the operating profit line under IFRS 18. For GN Store Nord, this resulted in adding back DKK 55 million of acquired intangible amortization in Q3 2026. This reclassification helps offset some of the negative currency impacts, partially restoring the reported operating profit under the new standard.
8.
Reclassified financial items: Under IFRS 18, financial expenses at GN Store Nord are adjusted by reclassifying currency effects on intercompany balances. In Q3 2026, these reclassifications reduced financial expenses from DKK 154 million to DKK 109 million. This adjustment means that interest expenses are isolated from foreign exchange volatility under the new financing category.

Management Performance Measures and EBITDA

9.
Defined performance measures: GN Store Nord will introduce Management-Defined Performance Measures to explain its non-IFRS metrics to the market. These measures require strict reconciliation to the closest IFRS metric under the new guidelines. In Q3 2026, GN Store Nord presented a new EBITDA measure under IFRS 18 as an official management-defined measure to bridge the reporting gap.
10.
Reconciled EBITDA differences: The restated EBITDA under IFRS 18 is DKK 459 million compared to DKK 457 million under IFRS 5. This difference of DKK 2 million is because the new standard adds back the foreign exchange operating impact of DKK 39 million to the lower operating profit of DKK 321 million. This calculation confirms that GN Store Nord's new EBITDA definition under IFRS 18 will actually remain higher than its traditional continuing-operations EBITDA.
11.
Contrary evidence: The company's presentation on 5 November 2026 indicates that the implementation of IFRS 18 will have a very limited financial impact on GN Store Nord. While operating profit falls from DKK 358 million to DKK 321 million in the restated Q3 2026 results, the adjusted EBITDA only changes by DKK 2 million. This suggests that the reporting transition is largely cosmetic and does not alter the underlying cash flows or the long-term profitability of the business.

Operating margin distortions: The inclusion of foreign exchange gains and losses in operating profit under IFRS 18 can introduce significant volatility into quarterly margins. NH Investment and Securities reports on 14 October 2026 that early adoption of the standard led to a 2 percentage point decline in operating margins for some consumer companies due to adverse currency movements. This demonstrates that GN Store Nord's operating profit may become more volatile under the new standard than under historical reporting.
