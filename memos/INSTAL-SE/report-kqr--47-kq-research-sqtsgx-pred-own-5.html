Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Monitoring short-term drivers reduces valuation risks and clarifies EPS path during Instalco's turnaround under its margin programme.
1.
Key Question: "For Instalco AB (INSTAL-SE), which company disclosures and which external data series allow an investor to monitor the drivers of its earnings over the next six months (October 2026 to April 2027): what the company reports each quarter (organic, acquired and currency growth by segment; backlog with organic and currency splits; segment EBITA; cash conversion; leverage) and what it does not (backlog duration and fixed-price share, award margins, subsidiary-level write-downs, revenue per employee, the effect of the ABC customer prioritisation, Fabri's standalone results); which external series track the drivers with a lead (Byggstartsindikatorn building starts, Konjunkturinstitutet specialised-construction price and order expectations, Swedish construction bankruptcies, the Ahlsell, Rexel, Solar and Onninen distributor reports, Bravida's and Assemblin Caverion's quarterly commentary on Swedish pricing, the Swedish wage-round timetable for the electrical and plumbing agreements in spring 2027, Riksbank decisions); and how experienced Nordic installer analysts and investors say they monitor utilisation, pricing and cash at a decentralised group between reports?"

Determining the path of earnings per share

2.

According to DNB Carnegie's report of 29 September 2026, the company's adjusted EPS is projected to expand significantly to SEK 2.30 in 2026e and further rise to SEK 2.99 in 2027e. This acceleration rests on the successful execution of the Instalco 2.0 turnaround initiative introduced by founder Per Sjöstrand.

No relevant precedents were excluded from this analysis because all available Nordic technical installation data was integrated.

Optimising internal efficiency: Investors must monitor whether internal operational measures can successfully offset contract inflation and project selection errors. DNB Carnegie's report of 23 January 2026 highlights that improving revenue per full-time employee and normalising annual bad debt provisions represent the critical levers. Resolving these operational indicators determines whether the Sweden segment can recover from its recent meagre margin trajectory.

Residential starts grew by 7.5 percent year-on-year in July 2026, while nonresidential starts surged by 13.2 percent. This robust non-residential performance is highly favorable for Instalco because its Sweden segment focuses heavily on commercial and public facilities. Consequently, strong commercial activity should offset residential weakness and stabilize segment earnings per share over the next six months.

Resilient Danish indicators: Danish technical installation markets exhibit strong structural growth driven by energy efficiency and green transition investments.
