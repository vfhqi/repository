Section 3 – Services and Consumables Margins and Pre-Approved Services

Section summary

  2. The commercial launch of Pre- Approved Services through Maeven AG in late 2026 is poised to act as a major catalyst for segment revenue and profitability.

Margin Analysis and Segment Performance

1.
Temporary contraction: The Services and Consumables (S&C) segment of SKAN Group AG experienced a decline in its EBITDA margin from 29.50% in fiscal year 2024 to 25% in fiscal year 2025, followed by a further contraction to 20.10% in the first half of 2026. S&C segment EBITDA reached CHF 13 mm in the first half of 2026, up 29.70% from CHF 10 mm in the first half of 2025 despite the lower margin percentage. This margin compression is characterized by management as temporary rather than structural because the prior periods included an extraordinary high-margin consumables order at Aseptic Technologies. S&C net sales rose 47.20% to CHF 64.60 mm in the first half of 2026, benefiting from the strategic integration of Metronik and ABC Transfer.
2.
Broader benchmarks: Compared to high-margin life sciences peers, SKAN Group AG's S&C segment occupies a mid-tier profitability profile that has room for expansion. For comparison, the Danish cell-counting peer ChemoMetec AS reported best-in-class EBITDA margins exceeding 50% and gross margins above 90% driven by its highly automated "razor-and-blade" consumables model., the Swiss lab automation peer Tecan Group AG reported an adjusted EBITDA margin of 15.90% in the first half of 2026, with a medium-term strategic target to reach a 20% adjusted EBITDA margin by fiscal year 2028 under its "Rewired" cost-efficiency program. The expansion of SKAN's proprietary consumables, such as closed vials and sterile transfer bags, is designed to elevate its S&C margin closer to pure-play consumables peers.
3.
Contrary evidence: The decline in the S&C EBITDA margin shows some structural headwinds from integration costs and a dilutive business mix from newly acquired entities. SKAN Group AG's H1 2026 segment margins were weighed down by a 3.50% reduction in overall workforce headcount and the consolidation of lower-margin service structures. Most of SKAN's capital investments in fiscal year 2024, totaling CHF 53.80 mm, were directed toward developing this facility. The commercialization of Pre-Approved Services is expected to significantly reduce time-to-market for pharmaceutical clients conducting stability tests.

The capital expenditure phase from fiscal year 2021 through fiscal year 2025 peaked with the construction of the Maeven cleanrooms and the installation of two advanced filling lines. Subsidiary Contributions and Synergies

High-margin consumables and associated filling sets scale directly with the commercial progression of these molecules.

Metronik provides web-based Manufacturing Execution System (MES) software that serves as the digital backbone for GMP-compliant pharmaceutical production. Other side of the story: While management emphasizes the strategic benefit of its pay-per-use Maeven AG model, the facility could face underutilization risks if client onboarding is slower than expected. Furthermore, scaling a service business requires a different operational capability than selling high-end equipment, which could create short-term cost inefficiencies during the initial ramp-up phase.

Consequently, the average capital base is artificially deflated, driving a distorted return on capital employed (ROCE) of 10.30% in fiscal year 2025. This direct write-off mechanism inflates reported asset efficiency metrics compared to international standards.

Peer return benchmarks: Peer return on capital employed calculations are more conservative because they capitalise and test goodwill under IFRS guidelines. Meanwhile, peer Getinge AB reported a return on capital employed of 11% in fiscal year 2025 under IFRS standards.
