EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Section summary
  1. The Key Question can be partly answered now, with high confidence on unscripted budget shifts and synergy tracking, but medium confidence on margin compression dynamics and standalone valuation multiples due to incomplete integration metrics and regulatory execution risks.

Global streaming platforms and European broadcasters are aggressively reallocating commissioning budgets away from high-cost scripted drama toward cost-effective unscripted formats, with scripted originals commissioned by the top six global streamers falling by twenty-four percent year-on-year and overall scripted production declining by eight percent, reflecting a structural pivot to protect operational margins under financial discipline.

This shift is economically rational because unscripted programming operates on pre-financed cost-plus pricing models that eliminate the deficit financing risks plaguing high-end scripted dramas, allowing independent producers to maintain stable operating margins without capital exposure during production phases. production businesses typically generate steady mid-teen operating margins due to the prevalence of cost-plus commissions, yet ITV Studios recorded a temporarily compressed adjusted EBITA margin of 10.6 percent in the first half of 2026, down from 12.0 percent in the prior year, demonstrating seasonal volatility in high-margin licensing deliveries.

Buyer consolidation across Europe–including RTL's acquisition of Sky Deutschland, Sky's proposed acquisition of ITV's broadcast channels, and MFE's consolidation of ProSiebenSat.1 –creates fewer, larger commissioning entities that threaten to squeeze independent production margins through unified purchasing power, particularly for smaller standalone producers lacking negotiating scale.

Endemol Shine integration.

Historical public market pricing valued ITV Studios at only 5.6-times to 6.8-times 2026E EBITDA, representing a persistent 15 percent to 30 percent discount to broker target multiples, suggesting meaningful valuation upside if the standalone structure successfully closes. Global streaming platforms have become more disciplined spenders, which has balanced out their historical negotiating advantage over major production firms and allows independent studios to negotiate more favorable terms on intellectual property retention, protecting long-term library monetization opportunities.

Conversely, commissioners typically acquire perpetual worldwide exploitation rights under cost-plus structures, which means that while producers face low immediate financial risk, their long-tail earnings are strictly capped unless they cofinance projects, limiting margin expansion potential under fully funded commission models.

Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy announced potential intervention in the Sky-ITV transaction on public interest grounds related to news plurality and on-demand programming diversity, which risks blocking the entire deal or delaying completion past late 2027, introducing severe regulatory execution risk to ITV's standalone demerger thesis and prolonging its exposure to cyclical linear advertising declines.

The financial trajectory of both Banijay and ITV Studios ultimately depends on whether unscripted format demand sustains structural growth amid consolidated buyer power, whether cost synergies offset joint venture dilution, and whether regulatory approvals unlock standalone valuation re-ratings –factors that remain partially resolved pending future quarterly disclosures and transaction completions.

Section 2 – Commissioning Budgets: Unscripted vs Scripted

Section summary
  1. Broadcasters and global streaming platforms are shifting budgets from expensive scripted dramas to cost-effective unscripted formats.
  2. Industry data confirms that global streaming platforms cut scripted orders by twenty-four percent to protect profit margins.
  3. Independent producers with scaled unscripted catalogs are best positioned to gain market share during this spending transition.

Streamer demand shifting

1.
Shifting streamer priorities: Global streaming platforms are actively reallocating their programming budgets to prioritize profitability over volume expansion. According to Bernstein Research on 15 September 2026, streaming companies are projected to make up 40% of global content spending in 2026, growing 6% year-on-year. However, this budget growth is becoming highly selective as platforms emphasize financial discipline. This transition means platforms favor unscripted formats to sustain high viewer engagement without incurring debt.
2.
Contracting scripted commissions: High-budget scripted dramas are facing substantial commissioning cuts as streaming buyers pull back from peak production levels. According to Ampere Analysis cited in RTL Group's MIPCOM presentation on 7 October 2025, scripted originals commissioned by the top six global streamers fell by 24% year-on-year. This industry-wide scripted pullback, which overall fell by 8%, has forced independent studios to adapt. Consequently, major buyers are slashing expensive scripted hours to protect their operational margins under severe pressure.
3.
Strategic unscripted integration: Streaming platforms have upgraded reality and lifestyle programming from occasional experiments into core strategic categories. According to industry reports on 1 September 2026, pure-play streamers utilize unscripted formats to build culturally relevant viewing habits. Because unscripted formats drive consistent viewing time, platforms use them to support their newer advertising tiers. This structural shift ensures that unscripted content attracts sustained investment even as total scripted spending contracts.
