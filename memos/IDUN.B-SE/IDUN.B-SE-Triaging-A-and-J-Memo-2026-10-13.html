[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Idun Industrier is an oversold compounder with a transient cause for concern, not a recovery case and not a demand inflection: a Swedish perpetual owner of twenty-two niche business-to-business leaders whose adjusted earnings per share grow about 22% in 2026 and about 10% a year after that from companies it has already bought and debt it has already refinanced, priced at the floor of its own ten-year multiple range because its organic line has shrunk for six quarters and its leverage sits at the ceiling. At SEK 320 the arithmetic pays about 16% a year over eighteen months and 14% a year over three; the case clears the 20% bar only below about SEK 305, or after the first positive organic quarter re-rates the shares.

  • The change that is moving the numbers is internal and financial, and it is already in the accounts.
    • Seven acquisitions since November 2025, after a pause of more than a year to deleverage, add about SEK 50m a year of EBITDA from three of them (MEAB Stainless, Trikåby, Mouldex) at margins above the group's, with AGB and Nordbergs on top
    • The SEK 220m bond redeemed on 30 June 2026 saves SEK 8m a year on top of the SEK 20m a year the 2025 refinancing saved.
    • None of this is a forecast.
    • Adjusted earnings per share rise from SEK 13.90 in FY2025 to about SEK 17.0 in FY2026 and SEK 19.1 in FY2027 on our modal path, against the sole broker's SEK 17.40 and SEK 19.20, whatever organic demand does.
    • Consensus FY2026 earnings per share rose 24.8% in twelve months
      • That rise is the roll-in of owned earnings, not organic beats.
  • The change that would make the case compelling is external, and it has not reached the company.
    • The Swedish industrial cycle is turning in the surveys (manufacturing purchasing managers' index 56 in July and August 2026, 58.1 in September) and order books are improving at about half the subsidiaries, but organic sales grew only 0.7% in Q2 2026 and organic EBITA fell 7.7%
    • Management itself does not expect a clearer turnaround until 2027.
    • The weakness is named and local: LMI's storm-delayed root-rot products, Stegaföretagen against an exceptional 2025 car-wash comparison, P&L Nordic's training budgets, ILEMA's pulp-and-paper customers.
      • Heavy-industry maintenance (Triton, Ståthöga) and Manufacturing are growing.
    • Peers in the cohort are already growing organically (Indutrade +5.4%, Lagercrantz +6%, Lifco 4.7% in Q2 2026), so the divergence is partly mix and partly company.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are improving on the headline and deteriorating underneath.
    • Revenue rose 14.1% in H1 2026 and the earnings path is inside or above the 12% to 20% band over all three horizons from today's trailing base
    • But cash conversion fell from 77% in 2024 to 59% in 2025, working capital rose to 29.3% of trailing revenue at June 2026, and net debt to EBITDA rose from 1.8x in Q1 2025 to 2.8x at June 2026 on the company's definition (3.4x with minority put and call options), against a 3.5x ceiling.
    • So the engine that produced the 2026 growth is throttled for the next twelve months by management's own "somewhat slower" guidance, and the continuation the framework asks for depends on an organic turn dated 2027.
  • Along the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain the acquisition and refinancing change has travelled all the way to the sell-side's estimates; the organic change has not left the resources-and-choices layers.
    • That split is the case in one picture: the earnings are owned, the re-rating is conditional.
  • What it costs: nothing in absolute terms, and much in relative terms, for reasons the market can name.
    • At SEK 320 the shares are 15.0x our modal FY2028 adjusted earnings of SEK 21.3 (14.3x the dashboard's consensus of SEK 22.30), the 2nd percentile of the stock's own ten-year range against a five-year median of 21.25x
    • Against the premium Swedish compounders at 22.1x EV/EBITA the stock's 13.7x is a 38% discount.
    • The discount has identifiable causes: 85% ownership of consolidated EBITA, leverage near its ceiling, an 8.4% reported return on capital, one sponsored broker, and 7,274 shares a day of trading.
      • The modal path repairs these only in part.
    • The price has moved against the contracted catalysts, not with them: the multiple fell as the earnings rose, and the shares are -2.4% over twelve months while the dashboard sector rose 19.4%.
  • The return is fair, not compelling.
    • On the 24-month-forward footing, with the multiple a blend of a cleared branch at 18x and an uncleared branch at 14.5x at even odds over eighteen months, the value including cash returns is SEK 401.1 at 2028-04-12 (16.2% a year) and SEK 474.0 at 2029-10-12 (14.0% a year)
    • Both are inside the framework's C band and short of the 20% bar.
    • The earnings leg (about 10% a year on the forward run-rate) is the predictable part
      • The re-rating is the swing: on the cleared branch alone the eighteen-month return is 24.4% a year, on the uncleared branch 7.8% a year.
      • The cash return is 0.4% a year by policy.
    • A plausibly worse drop-through from EBITDA to earnings per share (the minority share drifting to 25% and SEK 10m more interest) takes the eighteen-month return to 10.2% a year and the 'Modal return' from C to D
      • The return is as exposed to the ownership structure as to the cycle.
  • The pattern it fits is 'OSHQC CfC Clearing', at C for crispness, with 'Compounder' secondary.
    • A perpetual owner at the bottom of its range on a transient external concern (Swedish short-cycle demand) and a self-inflicted internal one (leverage taken to the ceiling to buy seven companies)
    • The fit is not crisp because the quality gate asks for B and the business's financial outputs are C, and because the clearing evidence is a survey and half an order book rather than a print.
  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.
    • It holds from the internal forces through the financial statements to the sell-side's near-year numbers.
    • It breaks at the point where the return needs the market to pay 18x rather than 15x, which needs two things first: organic EBITA positive and leverage falling with the engine relit, which we put at even odds within eighteen months.

  • The case therefore is: good operator, real niches, bought well, earnings growing whatever happens

    • But levered to the ceiling, illiquid, one sponsored broker, organic line still shrinking
    • At this price the arithmetic pays 14% to 16% a year unless the Swedish cycle re-rates it.
    • Buy below about SEK 305 or after the first positive organic quarter, not before.

  • Rating = C, because the change is material in the owned earnings (a rise of about 22% in adjusted earnings per share in 2026, already consolidated or contractual) and immaterial in the return at this price (about 16% a year over eighteen months and 14% a year over three, short of the 20% bar), with the external force that would make it compelling still in the surveys rather than the company's organic line.

    • B was not live once the return was built: the earnings leg alone gives about 10% a year on the forward run-rate, and the re-rating that would lift it to the bar carries even odds and a 2027 date.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • In its strongest form: Idun is a "false friend", a serial acquirer whose reported quality (60% gross margins, over-50% niche shares, co-owning managers, a 30%-a-year EBITA record from 2020 to 2025) masks an organic engine that has shrunk for six quarters, a parent that owns 85% of the EBITA and converts 59% of it to cash, a balance sheet at 2.8x to 3.4x against a 3.5x covenant with about 1.0x of minority options outside it, an eight-person centre that would see a subsidiary failure after it has happened, and a crowded Swedish deal market at 8x to 10x EBITDA that forces the group either to overpay or to stop.
    • The engine stalls at the ceiling just as the organic weakness proves structural
      • The sole broker's fair-value range keeps falling (four cuts this year)
      • And on 7,274 shares a day the price goes to the bear path's SEK 313 (4% acquired growth, a 14x exit multiple) or the stress case's SEK 269, with no buyer of size on the other side.
    • The evidence for it is real and we carry it: organic EBITA -3.7% in FY2025 and -3.6% in H1 2026 against a 2020-2025 organic record of +9% a year
      • The gross margin down 170 basis points in H1 2026
      • Cash conversion from 77% to 59%
      • Working capital at 29.3% of revenue
      • Leverage from 1.8x to 2.8x in six quarters
      • The sector evidence of late-surfacing failures at small-centred peers.
    • What we concede: the engine is throttled for the next twelve months and the broker's path, not ours, is the one the market will read
      • Service & Maintenance is a structural question until a positive organic quarter answers it
      • The combination of weaknesses is the reason the 'Investment Case' is rated D
      • A concentrated holder cannot leave this stock quickly.
    • Why we still hold our view: the 2026 earnings growth is contractual or already consolidated, not forecast
      • The niche structure held its gross margin through the whole 2023-2025 slowdown, which a false friend's would not
      • The prices paid (6x to 8x) are the cohort's good half, and the sector evidence is that the discount tracks what was paid
      • The operator's response to the problem (pause, deleverage, refinance, cluster, slow again) is what a disciplined owner does
      • And the competing hypothesis's own price, SEK 313, is 2% below where the shares already are, so the market has priced the false friend and not the compounder.
    • Where the case against would win: a Q3 2026 or Q4 2026 report with Service & Maintenance organic EBITA still falling while the purchasing managers' index stays above 55.
      • That would say execution, not cycle, and the clearing would be false.
      • The bound on the downside is the multiple at its floor, 14x on three earnings bases, SEK 274 to SEK 312.

Congruence: The congruence judgement: does the whole hang together across past, business, value chain and market environment; where ratings disagree, why (Part 4, E 3, BB 4)
  • Does the whole hang together?
