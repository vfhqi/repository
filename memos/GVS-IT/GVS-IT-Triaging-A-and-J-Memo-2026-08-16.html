Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Core activity: GVS S.p.A. designs and manufactures microporous filtration membranes and filter assemblies, casting its own membrane upstream and assembling sterile devices downstream
2.
Primary market: Healthcare and Life Sciences supplied 67.3% of H1 2026 group revenue, with healthcare applications representing over 70% of total group turnover
3.
Divisional split: Safety contributed 19.4% of H1 2026 sales and Energy & Mobility 13.4%, with Healthcare subdivided into MedTech, Transfusion Medicine and Life Sciences
4.
Revenue scale: FY25 reported revenue was EUR 424.7m, down 0.9% on FY24, against EUR 428.5m in FY24 and EUR 387.6m in FY22
5.
Half-year delivery: H1 2026 revenue was EUR 215.0m, down 0.3% reported and up 3.3% at constant currency, versus EUR 215.6m in H1 2025
6.
Margin character: H1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was EUR 55.4m at a 25.7% margin, up 62 basis points, with Q2 2026 at 26.8%
7.
Balance sheet: Net financial debt was EUR 239.3m at 30-Jun-26, a leverage ratio of 2.2x, against EUR 240.1m at end-2025 and EUR 268.0m at June 2025
8.
Customer structure: Business-to-business sales were EUR 327.9m, or 77.2% of FY25 revenue, with no single customer above 10% of group revenue in FY25
9.
Manufacturing footprint: The group runs 18 plants across seven countries and 29 commercial offices, employing 3,973 staff on a local-for-local production model
10.
Ownership and listing: GVS listed on Borsa Italiana in June 2020; GVS Group S.r.l. holds a 63% stake, with ultimate control via Lighthouse 11 SpA
11.
Investment style: The financial shape is a self-help margin recovery inside a flat revenue base, so the style label is event-driven restructuring rather than compounder
12.
Product range: The catalogue spans blood bags, pooling sets and bedside filters in transfusion, breathing, anaesthesia and drug-delivery filters in MedTech, and Elipse respirators in Safety
13.
Vertical position: GVS buys plastic resins and polymers from large specialised chemical suppliers, then casts its own membrane in Italy and the United States and assembles devices in class 10,000 and 100,000 cleanrooms
14.
Geographic revenue mix: Reported currency exposure over the trailing year was 44.7% United States dollar, 28.3% euro and 18.3% Chinese yuan, with sterling, real and yen making up the balance
15.
Recent transactions: GVS acquired the Haemonetics whole-blood business in January 2025, closed the Puerto Rico plant in October 2025 and repurchased 4.35 million treasury shares for EUR 18.7m in July 2026
16.
Capital position: Net capital expenditure ran EUR 16.6m in the first half of 2026 against EUR 44.6m a year earlier, and management has set a normalised rate near 6% of revenue

Certification base: Thirteen manufacturing sites hold ISO 13485 medical-device certification and eight hold IATF 16949 for mobility supply
