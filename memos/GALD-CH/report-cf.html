Appendix

Plausible problems to monitor

Gross-to-net pricing headwinds from the Nemluvio launch continue to create persistent margin pressure, with expense efficiencies required to offset the gross-to-net evolution and higher launch royalty rate structures, representing a structural disadvantage versus traditional dermatology products with more stable pricing dynamics. The company faces currency translation sensitivity as reported absolute margin percentages remain vulnerable to US dollar depreciation against Swiss franc denominated headquarter costs, creating a mismatch exposure not faced by US-domiciled peers like AbbVie or regionally diversified competitors.

Executive compensation governance reveals friction with 38% of shareholders voting against the Compensation Report at the 2026 Annual General Meeting, signaling meaningful investor dissatisfaction that exceeds typical Swiss-listed company opposition levels and suggests potential alignment concerns between management incentives and shareholder value creation.

Commercial execution faces significant talent attrition risk, with high attrition in commercial functions due to intense sales pressure, recent leadership shifts in Canada diluting aesthetic business focus, and Middle East operations suffering after one-third of UAE sales staff were let go, creating organizational instability during critical product launch windows that could disrupt physician relationships and market share capture.

Fillers and biostimulators growth decelerated to just 7.6% constant currency in standalone Q2 2026 due to a highly competitive aesthetics market environment, materially lagging the 24.6% group organic growth rate and signaling potential category maturation or share loss in this historically core franchise versus faster-growing biologics. Competitive pressure intensifies as HA-filler demand has weakened with US quarterly spend down 18%, representing a structural market headwind that threatens Galderma's traditional injectable aesthetics revenue base and could accelerate if Korean low-cost entrants continue aggressive pricing strategies.

Pipeline concentration risk emerges as Nemluvio patents expire in 2036, with limited visibility on replacement blockbuster candidates creating long-term revenue sustainability concerns that differentiate Galderma negatively from diversified pharmaceutical peers with deeper clinical pipelines across multiple therapeutic areas and staggered patent expiration profiles.
