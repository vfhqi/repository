EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 27 findings

Assicurazioni Generali presents a balanced investment case anchored by exceptionally stable senior leadership under CEO Philippe Donnet's reconfirmed 2025-2027 triennium, systematic business simplification through legacy back-book disposals, and the newly created General Manager role consolidating insurance operations under Deputy CEO Giulio Terzariol. However, persistent boardroom divisions over executive appointments–with three Caltagirone-backed minority directors and Mediobanca representative Rebecchini abstaining from the critical Terzariol vote–expose deep governance fractures beneath the executive stability facade. The 1H 2026 results delivered a clean across-the-board operating beat, with Operating Result of EUR 4,505 million growing 11.2% year-on-year, Adjusted Net Result reaching EUR 2,543 million up 13.7%, and Solvency II ratio at 216% fully absorbing the newly launched EUR 500 million share buyback. Life net inflows surged 33.9% to EUR 8.3 billion, while Asset & Wealth Management emerged as the fastest-growing segment with Operating Result up 31.3% to EUR 735 million, propelled by Banca Generali's 45.8% contribution growth to EUR 401 million boosted by performance fees. Resilient retail pricing with increases reaching 4.1% in retail and SME non-life segments successfully defended underwriting margins against claims inflation, though the undiscounted combined ratio deteriorated 0.7 percentage points to 93.8% due to severe natural catastrophe events impacting the P&C segment despite balanced premium growth.

The forward trajectory hinges on management's ability to simultaneously execute structural underwriting margin recovery through retail tariff discipline, scale capital-light wealth management fee income via the Alleanza insurbanking partnership and record Banca Generali client asset gathering, realize Conning and alternative credit acquisition synergies, capture the anticipated nine percentage point Solvency II regulatory capital relief from the impending Amending Directive, and optimize reinsurance treaty structures to dampen catastrophe volatility–while navigating concentrated Italian sovereign exposure totaling EUR 39.9 billion in BTP holdings sensitive to six percentage point solvency drops per 100 basis point spread widening.

The covering broker pool of sixteen analysts remains balanced but cautious with seven buy and seven hold ratings alongside only two sell recommendations, while the average target price expanded to EUR 42.5 from EUR 40.5 following resilient operating results. Morgan Stanley's multiples-based sum-of-the-parts valuation framework applies 13.6 times earnings to the property casualty segment, eleven times to Life utilizing nine percent capital costs, and thirteen times to asset management, yielding a base case target of EUR 41 reflecting higher Banca Generali estimates and structural retail business shifts.

Contrarian bear arguments focus on looming catalyst deficits as Morgan Stanley downgraded to equal-weight judging the equity is running out of near-term catalysts, cautioning that consensus already assumes twelve percent annualized growth leaving minimal scope for upside.
