Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Fluctuations in the USD/EUR exchange rate structurally dictate MTU Aero Engines' profitability because its commercial aerospace revenues are dollar-denominated while its production costs are heavily eurodenominated.

Currency Exposure and Segments

1.
Critical inquiry: "MTU Aero Engines AG (MTX-DE, Xetra): German aero-engine module maker and risk-and-revenue- sharing partner on Pratt & Whitney's PW1100G geared turbofan (GTF), the IAE V2500 and GE engines (GEnx, GE9X), and the world's largest independent commercial engine maintenance provider; two segments, the Commercial and Military Engine Business (OEM: series engines, spare engines, spare parts, military engines such as the EJ200) and the Commercial Maintenance Business (MRO: engine overhaul, leasing and asset management). Consequently, active FX portfolio management remains a critical determinant of group earnings and cash conversion.
