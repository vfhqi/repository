EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 28 findings

Materiality of Change 1&1 AG is undergoing a transformative structural shift from an asset-light MVNO model to a fully-fledged network operator, marked by the December 2025 acquisition of 100% of 1&1 Versatel from United Internet and the rollout of Europe's first fully virtualized 5G Open RAN architecture. This transition introduces material financial volatility as Vodafone roaming costs are fully expensed within EBITDA rather than partially capitalized, while reported FY26 EBITDA guidance was downgraded to EUR 740 million from EUR 800 million to account for restructuring charges.

The strategic repositioning prioritizes value-over-volume tariff restructuring, deliberately sacrificing 150,000 postpaid contracts in H1 2026 to improve long-term margin quality. However, the business model faces severe execution headwinds from systemic tower provider delays delivering only 5 active sites in 2022 against a 1,000 site target and a catastrophic network outage in May 2024 that paused customer migration for five months.

The fundamental peer positioning remains challenged, with 1&1 ranking in the bottom quintile of regional operators as high startup network costs compress margins versus incumbents achieving superior pricing power and stable cash generation. Despite regulatory progress reaching over 34% household coverage by mid-2026, the company confronts regulatory and competitive barriers including highly contested low-band spectrum negotiations that remain blocked by Germany's incumbents, creating persistent roaming dependency that undermines the economic rationale for infrastructure ownership.
