Section 1 – Brief company description and history 15 findings

Section summary

1.

2.
Strategic holding structure – global footprint: The group has built a complex global holding structure, owning a 46.1% stake in Turkish airport operator TAV Airports and a 43% stake in India's GMR Airports

This regulatory breakthrough transforms the investment narrative from a story dominated by regulatory paralysis and political friction to one anchored by multi-year tariff visibility and attractive risk-sharing mechanisms.

The framework provides the foundation for an €8.2 billion capital program and eliminates the existential threat of a "no-ERA" deadlock that had plagued investor sentiment throughout the first half of 2026.
