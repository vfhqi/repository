Section 3 – Calculation and Floor of the Guaranteed Royalty

Section summary

  2. This contractually guaranteed profit floor underpins a substantial portion of near-term consensus group EBITDA growth through fiscal year 2028.

Royalty Mechanics and Profit Sharing

1.
Structural shift: The licensing agreement effective February 1, 2025 transfers the US operating P&L to Molson Coors' financial reporting while Fevertree retains core brand control.
2.
Balanced economics: Profits from the US operations are shared based on a contractually agreed formula where both partners receive a broadly equal share of profits at the EBITDA level.
3.
Guaranteed cashflow: Molson Coors has committed to guarantee an absolute level of these annual royalty fees to Fevertree over the period spanning 2026 to 2030.
4.
De-risked growth: Deutsche Bank's report on October 6, 2026 highlights that the profit guarantee provides greater visibility and protection against any weaker-than-expected US performance.
5.

6.
