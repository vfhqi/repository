Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What the company sells. AIXTRON designs metal-organic chemical vapour deposition systems that grow ultra-thin compound semiconductor layers on wafers, sold to chipmakers as capital equipment
2.
Product family. The G10 platform splits three ways: G10-SiC for silicon carbide power, G10-GaN for gallium nitride power, and G10-AsP for arsenide-phosphide optoelectronics
3.
Revenue split by type. FY25 equipment sales were EUR 444.6m, or 80% of revenue, with spares and services at EUR 112.0m, the remaining 20%
4.
Revenue split by application. Q1 2026 equipment revenue ran optoelectronics 52%, light-emitting diodes and micro-LEDs 31%, and gallium nitride plus silicon carbide power 17%
5.
Financial scale. FY25 revenue was EUR 556.6m, down 12% from EUR 633.2m in FY24, with FY25 operating profit of EUR 100.3m at an 18.0% margin
6.
Balance sheet character. Q2 2026 total available liquidity was EUR 816.2m including the EUR 450m convertible bond, and the equity ratio moved from 88% to 61% across the half
7.
Manufacturing model. Roughly 90% of physical manufacturing is outsourced, with final assembly, integration and configuration retained at Herzogenrath in Germany and Cambridge in the United Kingdom
8.
Geographic mix. Asia took 64% of Q1 2026 revenues, Europe excluding the United Kingdom 27%, and the Americas the remaining 9%
9.
Market position. AIXTRON held a 77% global metal-organic chemical vapour deposition share in 2024, Veeco 8% and Advanced Micro-Fabrication Equipment 4%
10.
Investment style. The business is a high-fixed-cost capital-equipment cyclical with monopoly niche shares, not a compounder: revenue compounded at 5% between 2008 and 2024
11.
Headcount and sites. Year-end 2025 headcount was 1,117, down 7.46% from 1,207, with a new Penang assembly plant under construction in Malaysia
