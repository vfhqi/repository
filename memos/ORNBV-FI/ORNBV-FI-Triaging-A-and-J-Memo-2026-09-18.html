[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Orion is a superb near-term earnings story that the market has already identified correctly, at a price that leaves the return to an unproven pipeline. At EUR 81.65 the shares carry the Nubeqa royalty outputs in full and a multiple with a dated fuse, so the case clears the framework's 20 percent bar only at an entry of about EUR 71 to 73, or if the company's own pipeline proves a second leg before the market pays for it.

  • The change is external, structural and already three quarters of the way along the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • Bayer commercialises Nubeqa and guides about 50 percent sales growth in 2026
    • the royalty tiers reset each calendar year and rise with cumulative sales, so the top tier is reached earlier every year and Orion's average royalty rate rises mechanically toward its 30 percent maximum from about 25 percent.
    • The top tier was reached in Q2 2026.
    • Royalties rose 76.6 percent to EUR 94.7 million in Q1 2026 and Innovative Medicines sales rose over 60 percent in Q2 2026.
    • Weekly US prescription data show the fastest-growing branded challenger in its class, and the European chemotherapy-free label adds 30,000 to 40,000 candidates a year.
      • The evidence is external data and a partner's guidance, not management's word.
  • The outputs have already moved, on the coveted lines.
    • Revenue rose from EUR 1,191 million in FY2023 to EUR 1,888 million in FY2025, the adjusted EBIT margin from 19.6 percent to 33.1 percent, and EPS from EUR 1.54 to EUR 3.56
    • Q2 2026 revenue was EUR 522 million, up 25.1 percent, at an operating margin of 33.86 percent.
    • The sell-side has followed: consensus FY2028 EPS is up 36.7 percent in twelve months, every visible broker raised after Q2 2026, and the earnings-momentum pin is A.
    • Management has beaten every initial guidance range for two years and specified the 2026 range upward twice, to EUR 2,000 to 2,100 million of net sales and EUR 650 to 750 million of operating profit.
  • The change inside the company is continuity, not change.
    • The chief executive has been in post since November 2022 after 23 years inside
    • the strategy pivot toward in-house oncology and biologics is articulated and funded but its first products are years away, and its record outside oncology is three failures in two years.
    • The internal forces are those of a well-run company doing ordinary things
    • the external force alone carries the materiality.
  • What we think it earns.
    • On our modal path EPS grows from EUR 3.56 in FY2025 to EUR 4.09, EUR 4.81 and EUR 5.40 in FY2026 to FY2028, 14.9 percent a year, inside the framework's 12 to 20 percent band, from revenue (11.5 percent a year) and margin (EBITDA margin 36.5 to about 38.5 percent), with the below-EBIT items adding about one point and no buybacks.
    • The path then slows to 6 to 7 percent a year in FY2029 and FY2030 (EUR 5.78 and EUR 6.00) as the US price negotiation shows in the numbers.
      • Consensus is EUR 4.03, 4.68, 5.08 and 5.73 for FY2026 to FY2029
      • our path is 6.3 percent above it at FY2028.
  • What it costs.
    • EUR 81.65 is about 15.8x 24-month-forward earnings, the 8th percentile of the stock's own ten-year range and the 12th of five years, against a five-year median of 18.51x and a ten-year median of 21.00x.
    • The multiple fell because the earnings mix changed: about four fifths of operating profit now sits in one royalty stream with a 2033 expiry, a 2027 generic entrant in its class and a US price cut booked for 2029.
      • The two-year median of 15.84x, not the ten-year median, is the anchor.
    • About half the discount is the sector's: eighteen of the thirty-seven peers with data sit at or below the 12th percentile of their own ten-year ranges, on the same US-policy reasons.
  • The chain breaks at the last link, from price to return.
    • On the framework's convention, which values the shares at each horizon on the earnings two years further out, the modal return is about 9 to 11 percent a year over eighteen months and about 4 to 8 percent a year over three years, because the convention brings the slower years into the window and the modal multiple blend (15.3x at eighteen months, 13.9x at three years) is slightly below today's.
    • A re-rating to the five-year median needs class pricing to hold after generic enzalutamide (about 0.6 odds) and the pipeline to validate (about 0.35), together about 0.2: possible, not probable.
      • At the re-rating branch's 18.0x the eighteen-month return would be 21.2 percent a year
      • at the de-rating branch's 13.5x it would be 0.5 percent a year.
    • The case clears the bar over eighteen months at an entry of EUR 70.7 to 73.0, about 11 to 13 percent below today, on the same earnings path and the same multiples.
  • The pattern is 'Compounder', with a payoff that is three quarters spent.
    • The sell-side has modelled the royalty step-up too slowly at eight consecutive guidance events and the company has raised each time
    • but the top tier has now been reached, the one-month revision is nil, and what remains of the consensus gap is a judgement about class pricing rather than a model error.

  • The case therefore is a neutral park with a price at which it turns: the earnings are not in doubt, the market has priced them and the fuse together, and the return from here is left to a pipeline the market values at zero.

  • Rating = C, because the parts bind at the last link: the change force is strong ('Change Forces' B), the outputs are improving on the coveted lines ('Financial Outputs' B), and the return does not clear the bar ('Modal return' D) because the price already carries the outputs and the multiple carries a fuse.

    • Materiality is what this Building Block measures, and a change that is material to the outputs but not to the return from this price is a C.
    • Not B, which was live, because no leg of the modal case reaches 13 percent a year on the convention at either horizon
    • not D because the case clears the bar at an entry about 11 to 13 percent lower, so the thesis is a near miss on price rather than a failure of substance.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The strongest case against, assembled from the two brokers at Hold and Reduce and the expert calls, because the risks digest in the folder does not address this company: Orion is a single-royalty vehicle whose growth is fully priced.
  • The royalty tier mechanics that produced three years of beats are exhausted, so the upgrade engine stops with H2 2026
    • the class gets a cheap generic enzalutamide from August 2027 with payers already reported to be rejecting Nubeqa's premium
    • a twenty-five percent US price cut follows from 2029
    • and the 2033 loss of exclusivity cannot be offset by peak opevesostat royalties of EUR 160 million.
  • The rest of the business is roughly break-even at the operating line after group R&D (Q1 2026 EBIT of EUR 114.8 million less royalties of EUR 94.7 million leaves about EUR 20 million for everything else), so there is no floor under the equity from the rest of the company.
  • What we concede: the tier mechanics are largely spent
    • the multiple's fuse is real and dated
    • the ex-Nubeqa business does not cover the enterprise value at any multiple (the arithmetic is at the head of Building Block 4)
    • and three non-oncology failures in two years (ODM-111, ODM-105, Tenax LEVEL) mean the second leg is unproven.
  • Why we still hold a C rather than a D: the near-term outputs are not in dispute and grow about 15 percent a year on the coveted lines
    • consensus already carries the US price cut and models Nubeqa deceleration to 7.1 percent group revenue growth in FY2028, so the bear's numbers are the market's numbers
    • the multiple is already at the ten-year "crash" band of 15.80x
    • and the case clears the bar at about EUR 71 to 73, inside a normal quarter's range for this stock (52-week range EUR 55.7 to 82.8).
  • Where the competing hypothesis wins: on the recommendation, which is a park at this price.
    • It loses on the rating, because this is not a broken case
    • it is an expensive entry to a sound one.
