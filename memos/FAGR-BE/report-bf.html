EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Conclusions Fagron's financial profile is defensive and high quality. It combines a historical organic revenue CAGR of 7.8% (2017 to 2021), an average adjusted EBITDA margin of 21.6% over the same period and average operating cash conversion of 73.9%. Management quality looks solid on execution, incentives and communication. Evidence includes a 100% performance-based long-term incentive plan split between financial and sustainability targets and EPS growth of 9% annually over eight years.

The business case is strongest where scale, vertical integration and regulatory barriers combine. Strict Good Manufacturing Practices create high entry barriers, and the model links bulk ingredient sourcing to downstream compounding.

Earnings durability depends on factors outside management's control. The main ones are FDA tolerance of high-margin API compounding and the fading of shortage-driven demand, as regulators are encouraging a shift toward lower-margin sterile-to-sterile repackaging.

Section 1 – Business Model and Organisation

Section summary
  1. Fagron maintains a resilient business model supported by three integrated segments that personalize medication globally.
  2. Fagron operates across three key geographic regions with its sterile compounding services anchored by U.S. facilities.
  3. Fagron exhibits strong revenue predictability backed by defensive healthcare demand and clear multi- year financial targets.
  4. Fagron leadership changes and active broker coverage support its expansion despite regulatory challenges and operational headwinds.

Segmented business model

1.
Integrated operations: Fagron operates as a leading global pharmaceutical compounding company through three distinct operational segments. The company personalizes treatments by supplying raw materials, developed brands, and ready-to-use compounded preparations. Fagron segments consist of Essentials, Brands, and Compounding Services, which allow Fagron to occupy the leading global market position across the entire value chain.
2.
Synergistic bundling: Fagron simplifies pharmaceutical compounding for pharmacists through its specialized Brands segment. The Brands segment represents proprietary intellectual property, innovative vehicle platforms, and scientific formulations. Fagron frequently bundles Brands with Essentials to differentiate its commercial offering, strengthen customer loyalty, and drive cross-selling opportunities in daily practice.
3.
Global distribution: Fagron Essentials supplies raw materials, equipment, and packaging for compounding to pharmacies and healthcare industries across three major regions. Pharmacists receive high-quality materials to prepare safe and effective treatments. Sourcing activities provide an extensive assortment of more than twenty-five thousand stock keeping units to minimize product concentration risks globally.
4.
Dual compounding: Fagron Compounding Services prepares sterile and non-sterile compounds tailored to patient prescriptions for pharmacies and healthcare facilities. The segment operates under two distinct models in the United States. Pharmacists perform patient-specific compounding on a prescription-by-prescription basis regulated through five hundred and three A pharmacies. Large facilities perform bulk compounding in batches regulated through five hundred and three B outsourcing pharmacies to achieve economies of scale.

Geographic footprint and facilities

5.
Segmented regions: Fagron manages its operational activities through regional divisions in Europe, Middle East, and Africa, Latin America, and North America-Pacific. The European region delivered solid organic growth of 4.2% at constant exchange rates in fiscal year 2025. Latin American operations delivered exceptional organic revenue growth of 14.1% at constant exchange rates during the same period.
6.
Flagship sterilization: Fagron Sterile Services operates a flagship 503B outsourcing facility in Wichita, Kansas, to serve healthcare providers. The facility complies with Section 503B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to compound sterile medicine in large batches. Wichita operations primarily utilize active pharmaceutical ingredient to sterile manufacturing processes.
7.
Strategic expansion: Fagron expanded its sterile compounding footprint in North America by acquiring a 503B outsourcing facility in Boston, Massachusetts, from Fresenius Kabi in July 2022. The transaction added a fifty thousand square foot state-of-the-art facility to serve customers on the East Coast. Integration of this facility allowed Fagron to consolidate its product offering and implement a unified regional sales team.
8.
Licensing milestone: Fagron obtained a critical license from the California State Board of Pharmacy for its new Anazao Health facility in Tampa, Florida, in late 2025. The license allows Fagron to transition from its old site and ship patient-specific compounded medications directly into California. Obtaining licenses for tightly regulated states is a key milestone to increase capacity utilization rates and improve regional profitability.

Revenue dynamics and targets

9.
Defensive demand: Fagron operates a highly defensive business model with predictable revenues and strong cash conversion. Demographics such as an aging population and personalized medicine trends support compounding volumes. Outsourcing trends continue to grow because of tightening regulatory requirements that drive hospitals to outsource.
10.
Target reiteration: Fagron reiterated its full-year 2026 outlook for mid-to-high single-digit organic sales growth at constant exchange rates. Management expects the adjusted EBITDA margin to reach circa 20% for the full year. Solid performance in Europe and Latin America has supported profitable growth and offset shortterm headwinds in North America.
11.
Longterm plan: Fagron outlined its Compounding for Growth plan for the 2025 to 2030 period during its Capital Markets Day in April 2025. The plan targets high-single-digit to lowdouble-digit organic revenue compound annual growth rates at constant exchange rates. Strategic objectives aim to generate thirty percent of total revenue from Fagron Brands by 2030 to drive overall profitability.
12.
Operational leverage: Fagron achieves margin improvements through operational leverage, automation, and procurement synergies from acquisitions. The company expects to raise the EBITDA margin of acquired businesses up to the group average within eighteen to twenty-four months. Sourcing raw materials through its FDA-registered facility in Brazil helps unlock global procurement efficiencies and reduce manufacturing costs.
