Section 5 – Organic Growth Definition and M&A Policy

Section summary
  1. Renew operates a dual organic growth methodology that includes post-acquisition expansion from day one in its presentations.
  2. This practice contrasts with more conservative annual report metrics and complicates standard peer comparisons across the UK sector.
  3. Understanding these calculations is essential to determine whether underlying growth is truly organic or M&A-driven.

Dual Definitions of Organic Growth

1.
Controversial presentation footnote: Renew applies an unusual methodology in its investor materials to calculate organic expansion. According to the company's preliminary results presentation on 21 November 2025, the group asserts that all growth from acquisitions that occurs after the date of acquisition is treated as organic. This means that if an acquired business expands under Renew's ownership, that incremental revenue is immediately classified as organic rather than being isolated for the first twelve months. Consequently, this definition can significantly inflate presented organic performance unless closely scrutinized.
2.
Formal statutory definition: The company's annual report presents a more conventional and restricted accounting treatment for statutory reporting. In the annual report published on 17 December 2025, Note 30 states that organic growth reflects the revenue movement year on year excluding the impact of any acquisitions made in the current or comparative financial period. Under this formal definition, the group reported a negative organic growth rate of 2.5% for FY25 because the contributions from recent transactions were stripped out. Therefore, statutory earnings per share calculations are insulated from early acquisition distortions.
3.
Presentation impact on reported growth: The presentation methodology can result in massive discrepancies compared to standard organic reporting. In its annual results for the year ended 30 September 2023, Renew presented a forty-three percent organic revenue growth since 2010 based on its presentation definition. However, this figure includes substantial post-acquisition growth that standard accounting would classify as inorganic for the first year. This means that presented metrics may give investors an overly optimistic view of baseline performance.
4.
Historical presentation averages: The presentation methodology has historically allowed Renew to showcase highly stable long-term growth averages. In its interim results presentation on 12 May 2026, management highlighted a stable track record of compounding where the company has averaged nine percent organic growth over the last five years. However, this calculation is based on the presentation footnote that counts all post-acquisition growth as organic, which differs from standard accounting. This means that the reported historical average may overstate pure organic performance.

Post-Acquisition Organic Inclusions

5.
Day-one organic classification: Newly acquired businesses contribute directly to Renew's reported organic performance from the moment the transaction closes. In the preliminary results presentation on 25 November 2024, the group confirmed that organic growth includes newly acquired business performance immediately following acquisition if it exceeds their purchase base. This policy was applied to the integration of Browne in March 2021 and Enisca in November 2022, allowing their post-deal expansion to boost organic results. This operational practice means that successful crossbrand collaboration immediately improves organic metrics.
6.
Acquisition timing distortions: The timing of acquisitions can temporarily distort both ROCE and organic calculations. In the preliminary results for the year ended 30 September 2024, Renew noted that acquisition timing reduced return on capital employed by 200 basis points to 25%. This distortion occurs because newly acquired businesses like Full Circle are integrated near the year-end, affecting average capital calculations. This means that short-term earnings metrics can be highly volatile following large M&A transactions.
7.
Contrary evidence: In the statutory annual report published on 17 December 2025, Note 30 explicitly excluded the entire revenue contributions of Full Circle, Excalon, and Route One from the FY25 organic growth calculation to prevent distortion. This direct exclusion demonstrates that statutory disclosures do not permit the day-one presentation methodology. This proves that the company's marketing materials and statutory accounts present conflicting metrics, which means that analysts must rely strictly on statutory notes to evaluate true underlying operational performance.
8.
M&A compounding model: The group's compounding model relies on a symmetrical balance between transactional expansion and organic growth. During the Capital Markets Day on 6 June 2024, management stated that the historical profile of organic to inorganic growth has been remarkably symmetrical over the long term. This balanced approach is supported by strong cash conversion of over 60%, which is reinvested into acquisitions of typical sizes between £10m and £40m. Ultimately, this dual-engine strategy supports long-term earnings per share expansion.

Sector and Peer Methodology Comparisons
