EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

The company recently updated its 2026-2028 strategic plan to enforce capital discipline, cutting organic capex guidance to €8.5-10.0 billion and concentrating 89% of investments in highreturn Iberia and United States geographies.

[Cross-ref: Section 6] This downstream-led outperformance was amplified by Repsol's highest middle-distillate yield positioning among European refiners, with a product slate skewed 55% toward middle distillates [Cross-ref: Section 7], enabling the company to capture exceptional margin premiums during supply chain disruptions that peers with lighter product slates could not replicate.
