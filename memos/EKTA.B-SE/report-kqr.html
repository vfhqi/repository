4.

CMS finalized a 2.31 percent conversion factor cut for calendar year 2027 in its November 4 rulemaking. Clinicians face an 11 percent reduction in fees with another 4 percent cut proposed to further restrict capital equipment budgets.
5.
Shifting treatment economics: Medicare policies for stereotactic body radiation therapy mandate strict fractional limits for reimbursement. CMS guidelines specify that any SBRT course exceeding five fractions must be coded as standard intensity-modulated radiotherapy instead. This regulatory threshold forces cancer clinics to optimize their treatment plans for rapid, high-dose delivery systems.
6.
Contrary evidence: Proton beam treatment delivery payments continue to experience wide geographical disparities under localized contractor pricing. CMS proposed national pricing for these services because charges ranged from $122 to $1,374 for identical CPT codes in 2024. This pricing proposal contradicts the view that all radiation oncology modalities are shifting smoothly toward unified, predictable national bundled rates.

This centralized bidding system intensifies pricing pressure on Western capital equipment manufacturers selling into emerging regional hospital networks.
