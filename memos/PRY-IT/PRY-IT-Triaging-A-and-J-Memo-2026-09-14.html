[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Prysmian is the best-run cable company in the world riding two real demand inflections, grid transmission and AI data-centre fibre, and at €123.6 the shares have already paid for them. The change has travelled every layer of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain and the earnings path we believe in compounds at about 20% a year to FY2028; the price stands at 17.8x 24-month-forward earnings, the 88th percentile of five years, so the modal return from here is about 7% a year against the framework's 20% bar.

  • The change is external, sector-wide and already two years old.
    • High-voltage awards exceeded €15bn in the first nine months of 2024 against a €2bn to €3bn annual market in the late 2010s, and data-centre fibre demand doubled in 2024 and nearly doubled again in 2025.
    • The Transmission backlog is about €18bn at 30-Jul-26
      • spot fibre rose from about $3/km in late 2025 to over $20/km by July 2026
      • the Molex agreement is €5.5bn over ten years with €550m upfront (20-Jul-26).
    • Both forces are tailwinds with two-plus years of visibility.
      • The headwinds (FX cut H1 2026 revenue by €302m; a €15m aluminium premium; Specialties organic sales -2.4% in Q2 2026) are smaller and cyclical.
  • The company rebuilt itself ahead of the demand, so the demand converts to margin.
    • A new chief executive from 18-Apr-24, the Accelerating Growth plan, Encore Wire (July 2024), Channell (2025) and Atkore (announced 3-Aug-26) are a dense internal change that the screen's false-positive checks do not catch.
    • Guidance was raised twice in FY2025 and again on 30-Jul-26, to €2.80bn to €2.90bn of EBITDA and €1.65bn to €1.75bn of free cash flow for FY2026.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up on every line the framework names.
    • Adjusted EBITDA went from €1,628m (FY2023) to €2,398m (FY2025) and a record €730m in Q2 2026
    • Transmission margin is 21.2% and Digital Solutions 22.6% in Q2 2026.
    • Consensus FY2027 EPS rose 25.9% and FY2028 38.7% in twelve months (2026-09-11).
      • The change is in the sell-side's estimates, the sixth layer, which is why the upgrade runway near term is short.
  • The chain holds from forces to estimates, and our earnings path clears the growth band.
    • EPS of €4.30 in FY2025 on the brokers' basis rises to about €7.40 in FY2028 on our modal path, 19.8% a year, with revenue and margin carrying most of it.
    • The FY2027 step (about +24% consensus, +27% our modal) is above the band and carries over-extrapolation risk
      • growth then slows to 8% to 9% a year as competitor capacity arrives.
  • The chain breaks at the price.
    • €123.6 is 17.7x the €6.99 of 24-month-forward earnings, against a five-year median of 15.08x and a 25th to 75th percentile band of 14.02x to 16.58x.
    • The May 2026 move (€94.96 on 30-Mar to €156.90 on 11-May, +65%) priced the hyperscaler negotiations and the repricing
      • the July fall of 20.8% removed part of the froth, not the premium.
  • So the 'Modal return' fails the bar.
    • Applying 16.0x at eighteen months to the earnings two years beyond (our €8.45 for the twelve months to March 2030) gives €135.2 plus €1.00 of dividend: +10.2%, about 6.7% a year
    • at three years, 15.5x on our €9.5 gives €147.3 plus €3.37: +21.9%, 6.8% a year.
    • Only a multiple held at today's 17.7x reaches 14% a year, a C on the framework's anchors
      • nothing plausible reaches B unless the multiple rises, which we rate unlikely.
    • On a plausibly worse drop-through (Atkore interest and amortisation, tax drifting to 25%, 2% dilution) FY2028 EPS is about €6.55 and the eighteen-month return is -1.5%, so this Building Block would fall to D.
  • The pattern is a 'Demand Inflection' that has already inflected, fit B, with 'Major Corporate Change' a B secondary
    • the fit is not crisp because cable manufacturing has no one-to-many scalability and thin fixed-cost leverage.

  • The case therefore is a material change with an immaterial return at this price: compelling as a business, not compelling as an investment above about €104.

  • Rating = C, because the change forces and the outputs rank in the top quarter of the universe, but the total shareholder return they leave at €123.6 is about 7% a year in the modal case, below the framework's bar.

    • Not B, because the framework's 'Modal return' anchors put 7% a year at F and even a held multiple gives only about 14% a year
    • not D, because both the external and the internal change forces are among the strongest we see and are already in the financial statements.

Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The bull's case, at its strongest: consensus for 2027 and 2028 is systematically too low, so the multiple is not what it looks.
    • Two-thirds of Digital Solutions has yet to reprice off legacy frame agreements
    • Molex and a €10bn hyperscaler pipeline to 2035 are locked
    • Transmission is booked to 2030 at doubled pricing per km
    • tariffs are enforced at the border
    • Atkore at 7.1x post-synergy repeats a playbook that delivered 80% of Encore's synergies in two years.
    • On that view FY2028 EPS is €8.1 to €8.5 (Jefferies, UBS, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley), and 15x that in eighteen months is €150 to €160 plus dividends, about 15% to 18% a year, close to the bar.
  • We concede two things.
    • The earnings upside is real and skewed up in Digital Solutions
    • and the multiple's floor is higher than history because about half the profit now carries multi-year contracts.
  • We hold our view for three reasons.
    • Even the bull path needs the multiple to stay at 17x to 18x to reach 20% a year
    • the largest segment, Electrification at 39% of EBITDA, has margins at a cyclical high (I&C 9.5% against 11.1% a year earlier) and grows with Atkore
    • and the price has already risen 54.5% in twelve months and stands 25% to 35% above the bear targets of €79 to €95.
    • The modal case does not clear the bar
      • the bull case only just does.
      • That is a park, not a progression.
