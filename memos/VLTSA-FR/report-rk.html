Outliers worth noting

Three extreme statistical and operational outliers emerge across the comprehensive analysis of Voltalia that demand immediate investor attention and ongoing scrutiny. The first and most severe outlier centers on Voltalia's extreme leverage position, where net financial debt-to- EBITDA reached a staggering 10x in 2025, representing a dramatic departure from both historical norms and peer benchmarks. This metric stands in stark contrast to comparable peer Neoen, which utilizes project leverage of 8x to 10x net debt-to-EBITDA, yet Neoen benefits from significantly stronger institutional backing through Brookfield and Temasek ownership.

The 10x leverage multiple is particularly alarming because it occurred during a period when the asset existing WACC escalated to 9.3% in 2026, squeezing project IRR-WACC yield spreads. The leverage outlier directly feeds into multiple pre-mortem failure narratives, particularly the falsefriend scenario where headline capacity growth masks deteriorating cash generation and forces reliance on continuous asset sales to maintain financial stability.

The second critical outlier manifests in the unprecedented severity of Brazilian grid curtailment, where the local grid operator ONS capped output leading to 23.5% curtailment of local output in 2025, representing an extraordinary disruption to core operational cash flows.
