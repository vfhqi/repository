The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Organic growth: FY2026 10.4% (Q4 about 15%, supported by a backlog of SEK 1,386m, +57%, and the Irish order of about SEK 117m); FY2027 4.6% (Q1 +10%, Q2 +6%, Q3 +2%, Q4 +1%) as 2026 comparisons, the end of distributor pre-buying and the non-recurrence of the Irish order bite; 4.5% in FY2028 and 5.0% in FY2029 to FY2030 (the through-cycle rate). Price, all pass-through of cost inflation, is about 2 to 2.5 points of this in FY2027 and about 1.5 to 2 points thereafter; there is no real price.
  • Acquired growth: carry-over of the 2026 deals (about SEK 113m in FY2027, mostly Q1) plus new deals bought evenly through each year: about SEK 400m of annual sales in FY2027 (a slower year through the chief-executive change), SEK 450m in FY2028, SEK 500m in FY2029 and SEK 550m in FY2030; acquired growth 5.0%, 6.2%, 6.3% and 6.2%. These deals are not announced; consensus excludes them.
  • Currency: about -0.3% in FY2026; zero thereafter.
  • EBITA margin: 16.0% in FY2026 and FY2027, 16.25%, 16.45% and 16.65% in FY2028 to FY2030. Organic sales drop through at 27% (25% from FY2029); acquired sales at 14% EBITA; central costs and the Jönköping warehouse add about SEK 26m in FY2027 and about SEK 10m a year after; small integration gains of about SEK 5m a year. Gross margin held at about 37% (nine months of 2026: about 37.2%); a give-back of 0.3 to 0.5 points is the main downside risk for 2027.
  • EBITDA margin: EBITA margin plus depreciation excluding acquired amortisation (about 1.6% to 1.7% of sales, rising with owned property and IFRS 16 leases). Quarterly margins follow OEM's seasonality: Q2 and Q4 lower, Q1 and Q3 higher.
  • Depreciation (excluding acquired amortisation): SEK 69m in 2025, about SEK 100m in FY2026 (nine months SEK 72m), rising with property, Jönköping (from February 2027), leases of acquired companies and the IT platform.
  • Amortisation of acquired intangibles (not in the D&A input; reported EPS only): about SEK 82m, 108m, 155m, 205m and 250m.
  • Net financial items: the FY2026 gain (nine months +SEK 28m) is mostly currency and does not recur; from FY2027 small net costs (lease interest, unwinding of deferred considerations, lower interest income as cash funds deals). No gearing in the base case.
  • Tax: 20.5% in FY2026 (nine months about 20.3%), 21.0% thereafter.

How to read these figures
