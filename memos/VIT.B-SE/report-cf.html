High transaction revenue volatility: transaction-based recurring revenue grew 15% organically YoY in Q2 2026, driven by real estate agency activity after contracting -14% YoY in FY25. Stable recurring mix profile: recurring software revenues represented 86% of net sales in H1 2026, remaining aligned with vertical software peer medians near 85% to 90%. Low structural customer churn: group customer churn remained consistently below 1.0% to 1.5% annually, proving deep software embeddedness across 27,500 vertical customer bases.

Margin trajectory and accounting restatements

2.
Expanding operating profit margin: underlying EBITA margin reached 29.0% in Q2 2026, up 10 bps YoY, comparing favorably to European VMS aggregator medians near 25%. Accounting restatement margin lift: ESMA agent accounting implementation reduced reported FY25 net sales by SEK 307 million (-8%) while mechanically expanding EBITA margin by +240 bps to 28.8%. Cash EBIT performance tracking: Cash EBIT reached SEK 235 million in Q2 2026 (25% margin), representing an 18% YoY cash profit increase. Flat headcount efficiency lever: management maintained topline expansion with zero net organic headcount growth in 2026, leveraging internal AI tools across 600+ developers. Operating leverage recovery trajectory: full-year EBITA margin expanded to 28.4% in H1 2026, rebounding from 26.4% pre-restatement in FY25 as cost efficiency programmes materialized.

Cash flow generation and capital conversion

3.
Strong cash flow seasonality: operating cash flow reached SEK 876 million in H1 2026, driven by upfront annual subscription billing cycles in Q1. Elevated capital development intensity: capitalized R&D software development costs absorbed SEK 232 million in H1 2026 (12.8% of net sales), exceeding peer capitalization rates. Lumpy FCF conversion profile: rolling 12- month free cash flow reached SEK 600+ million, yielding an EBITA to FCF conversion ratio near 50% to 60%.

Persistent working capital efficiency: asset-light operational model maintains low net working capital requirements, enabling rapid conversion of operating profits to liquid reserves. Reliable dividend growth delivery: proposed FY25 dividend of SEK 3.68 per share represented 34% payout, extending 24 consecutive years of annual dividend increases.

Leverage, balance sheet and M&A capital deployment

5.
Disciplined net leverage positioning: interest- bearing net debt stood at SEK 3,013 million (1.8x Net Debt/EBITDA) as of June 2026, within the ~2.0x target threshold. Continuous M&A capital deployment: completed four acquisitions across 2025 and H1 2026 (Intergrip, NMG, Autonet, Infometric), adding SEK 315 million in annual sales. Substantial acquisition firepower: unused credit facilities of SEK 1.2 billion plus cash reserves provide over SEK 1.5 billion in M&A capacity without equity dilution.
