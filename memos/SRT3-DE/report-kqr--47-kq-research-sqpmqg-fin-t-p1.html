Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. High-leverage bioprocess business models convert volume recovery into substantial margin expansion due to a cost structure dominated by fixed manufacturing overheads.
  2. Historical precedents show that during the 2009-2011 recovery and the 2016-2019 cycle, suppliers consistently realized overproportionate earnings growth as capacity utilization improved.

Corporate Parameters and

1.
Key Question Key Question: "How much fixed-cost operating leverage does this business model carry, how much scope is there for it to work over the next 18 months to four years, and, where it is relevant to the company's strategy, how feasible is cost elimination or reduction, both incremental and in material large cuts?"
2.
Strategic alignment: Sartorius AG operates as a strategic holding company controlling a 71.5% interest in its publicly listed subsidiary Sartorius Stedim Biotech. This structural relationship means the parent company consolidated results are heavily dominated by the bioprocess business, which makes up about four-fifths of group revenues. Understanding this consolidated structure is vital for investors because the earnings per share of Sartorius AG preference shares is directly tied to the performance of its underlying bioprocess operations.
3.
Capital dynamics: Sartorius AG share capital is divided equally into 37,440,000 ordinary shares and 37,440,000 non-voting preference shares. The preference shares historically trade at a 20% to 30% premium over the ordinary shares, reflecting distinct liquidity and investor preferences in German markets. This capital structure means that diluted earnings per share metrics are sensitive to minority interest allocations, especially since the French-listed subsidiary is consolidated with a 28.5% free float.

Relevance of

4.
Operating Leverage Segment evolution: Sartorius is transitioning its reporting structure starting 1 January 2027 to align its two primary business segments directly with its corporate legal structure. Under this new framework, the Bioprocessing Solutions segment will perfectly mirror Sartorius Stedim Biotech, while Research Solutions will handle all lab-related business. This realignment is designed to eliminate previous product allocation complexities, allowing investors to evaluate the direct operational leverage of each business model separately.
5.
Operating leverage: Sartorius future earnings per share progression is highly sensitive to fixed-cost operating leverage because its extensive manufacturing investments have established a high base of fixed costs. Management guides for group underlying EBITDA margin expansion of 50 to 75 basis points annually starting in 2027. This expansion depends heavily on volume recovery, since ramped-up capacity utilization at factories added during 2020 to 2023 will allow revenues to grow faster than operating expenses.
6.
Contrary evidence: none found, based on a comprehensive search of company filings, broker research, and expert call transcripts from October 2024 to November 2026.

Industry Context and

Strategic Feasibility Sector dynamics: Life science tools companies face intense investor pressure to optimize cost structures and protect margins as the industry normalizes. Large peers such as Danaher are leveraging operational scale to protect margins while navigating cyclical fluctuations. Because bioprocess equipment demand has been highly cyclical, the ability to capture incremental margins during upturns represents the primary differentiator for sector earnings growth.

Section 2 – Operating Leverage and Historical Cycle Precedents

Section summary
  1. Operating margins across the bioprocess sector contracted sharply during the 2023-2024 destocking downturn but began expanding sequentially in late 2025 as consumables demand normalized.

Cost Structure and Operating Leverage

1.
High operational leverage: Sartorius and its bioprocessing peers operate with a high proportion of fixed costs that typically represent 50% to 60% of their total operational cost base. This high fixed-cost structure means that any meaningful recovery in market-wide sales volume directly accelerates operating profitability because factory overheads are distributed over a much larger volume of product units. Consequently, incremental margins during periods of volume expansion tend to be exceptionally high, driving rapid earnings per share growth that can surprise the market during cyclical upturns.
2.
Strong profitability profile: Sartorius Stedim Biotech benefit from a highly favorable, high-margin product mix where validated single-use consumables such as bags and filters represent approximately 75% of total sales. These single-use products generate stable, recurring revenues and carry superior gross margins compared to heavy hardware equipment. This favorable mix protects underlying EBITDA during periods of stable manufacturing run-rates and drives rapid margin expansion as utilization rises.
3.
Elevated capital intensity: Lonza Group demonstrates the high capital intensity and utilization dynamics that characterize major contract manufacturers and key customers of bioprocess suppliers. The company's biologic revenues depend heavily on bioreactor capacity, utilization rates, and commercial contract structures. Because long-term commercial contracts offer much higher operating margins than short-term clinical trials, Lonza's profitability remains highly sensitive to channel mix and asset utilization.
4.
Specialized industry niches: Repligen Corporation occupies a highly profitable niche by focusing on pure-play bioprocess filtration, perfusion, and chromatography consumables. The company's commercial strategy helps entrench long-term customer relationships and drives steady, premium-valued organic growth. This pure-play consumable exposure allows Repligen to capture elevated operating margins during market upturns as monoclonal antibody production volumes expand globally.

Historical Post-GFC Recovery 2009-2011

5.
Overproportionate profit recovery: Sartorius Group experienced rapid operating EBITA expansion during the post-GFC recovery in fiscal year 2010. Group sales revenue climbed 9.5% to €659.3 million, while operating EBITA surged by a good 40% to €85.5 million. This remarkable performance lifted the group operating EBITA margin by nearly three percentage points to 13.0%, demonstrating the tremendous power of operating leverage.
6.
Scaled margin expansion: The Biotechnology Division of Sartorius drove group profitability in 2010 by growing sales 8.0% to €432.6 million. In the Asia-Pacific region, the division recorded an underlying EBITA margin of 14.3% in 2009 as regional sales recovered. This strong momentum continued into 2010, prompting management to raise margin guidance to approximately 16.0% for the full year.
7.
Escalating scale economies: Sartorius Stedim Biotech reported an increase in operating EBITA of 16.1% to €70.0 million in fiscal year 2010. This performance raised the subsidiary's underlying EBITA margin from 15.0% to 16.2% during the recovery. Management attributed this margin expansion primarily to economies of scale from rising sales volumes rather than price increases.
8.
Dynamic demand tailwinds: Sartorius Group continued to see rapid margin expansion in the first half of 2011 with consolidated operating earnings jumping 40.7% to €51.1 million. This operational performance improved the group operating margin from 11.5% to 14.4% in just one year. The Biotechnology Division reached an operating margin of 16.8% in the first half of 2011, driven by strong single-use demand.

Pre-Pandemic Expansion Cycle 2016-2019

9.

The company's underlying EBITDA margin expanded from 26.2% in 2015 to 29.3% in 2019. This expansion was supported by double-digit annual sales growth which culminated in total sales of €1,440.6 million in 2019.
10.
Improved operational productivity: Danaher Corporation achieved a 100 basis point expansion in its operating profit margin during fiscal year 2019. This margin expansion was driven by higher core sales volumes and continued productivity initiatives that provided 115 basis points of positive impact. These benefits were partially offset by a 15 basis point dilutive drag from newly acquired businesses during the same period.
11.
Highly profitable scale: Thermo Fisher Scientific registered a significant increase in total operating margin from 15.5% in 2018 to 18.0% in fiscal year 2019. This operating income growth was primarily driven by profit on higher sales and productivity gains. Unfavorable sales mix and strategic investments only partially offset these structural operating leverage benefits.
12.
Expanding core margins: Tecan Group reported a 70 basis point increase in its reported EBITDA margin to 19.3% of sales in fiscal year 2019. This improvement was supported by a strong margin trend in its traditional core business, which offset 10 million Swiss francs in acquisition-related costs. Sales growth and operational efficiencies allowed the laboratory automation peer to expand operating profit margins to 15.1%.

Section 3 – Downturn Decrementals and Recovery Incrementals

Section summary
  1. Sartorius and its listed subsidiary stabilized their margins through cost control and volume recovery, while peers experienced severe decremental pressures from under-utilization.
  2. Extensive capacity additions between 2020 and 2023 have created a temporary gross margin drag through elevated depreciation and under-utilization, leaving substantial revenue headroom.
  3. The consolidation of Polyplus and BIA Separations introduces structural amortization charges, while recovery in highmargin consumables remains critical to restore historical gross profitability levels.

Sartorius Decrementals in the

1.
Downturn Severe compression: Sartorius Group suffered severe margin pressure during the destocking phase of fiscal years 2023 and 2024. Consolidated group revenues fell slightly to €3,381 million in 2024, while underlying EBITDA declined to €945 million. This resulted in an underlying EBITDA margin contraction to 28.0% from 28.3% in the prior year. The decremental margin was mitigated only by the rapid execution of a comprehensive cost-saving efficiency program that took €100 million out of the P&L.
2.
Segmented downturn: The Bioprocess Solutions division of Sartorius managed to stabilize its fullyear 2024 performance with sales reaching €2,690 million. This segment recorded an underlying EBITDA margin of 29.3% in 2024 compared to 29.2% in 2023. Conversely, the Lab Products & Services division experienced a sharp demand contraction with sales falling 3.0% in constant currencies and its margin contracting to 22.9%. Unfavorable product mix and low capacity utilization heavily depressed the laboratory segment's profitability.
3.
Contrary evidence: Sartorius Stedim Biotech reported that its consolidated underlying EBITDA margin actually improved sequentially during the seasonally weak third quarter of 2024. Standalone underlying EBITDA margin reached 27.1% in the third quarter of 2024, which exceeded the 26.7% recorded in the third quarter of 2023. This performance contradicts the expectation that low volume necessarily drives margin erosion in every sequential quarter of a severe industry downturn. Efficiency measures helped offset the severe operational deleverage from internal inventory reduction.
4.
Steady improvement: Sartorius Group delivered strong margin expansion in the first nine months of fiscal year 2025 and 2026 as bioprocess customer volumes returned. Group underlying EBITDA margin expanded by 1.70 percentage points to 29.7% for the full year 2025. Profitability further improved in the first nine months of 2026, with the underlying EBITDA margin reaching 30.2% on sales of €2,737 million. Positive volume effects and manufacturing economies of scale successfully counteracted dampening product mix effects from rising equipment sales.
5.
Quarterly acceleration: Sartorius Stedim Biotech reported sequential margin improvements in the third quarter of fiscal year 2026. Revenues for the quarter rose 8.0% to €762.10 million, while underlying EBITDA reached €237.10 million. This yielded a high underlying EBITDA margin of 31.1% compared to 31.3% in the prior-year quarter. Incremental revenue gains were primarily driven by consumables recovery, which has structurally higher gross margins than equipment.

Asset Expansion and Greenfield Infrastructure Substantial

1.
APAC investment: Sartorius committed a major share of its 2025 and 2026 capital deployment to the greenfield Songdo site in South Korea. Once completed, this plant will serve as a cornerstone manufacturing hub to produce sterile consumables for the fastgrowing Asia-Pacific market. Placing the facility in close proximity to the largest installed cell culture capacity in the world helps increase regional value creation and resilience against geopolitical shocks. This localized presence will allow Sartorius to capture long-term demand without incurring high shipping costs.
2.
Accelerated domestic capacity: Sartorius pursued substantial domestic expansions at its corporate headquarters in Göttingen, Germany, and Aubagne, France, during fiscal year 2025. In Aubagne, the company nearly doubled its production cleanroom space to enhance automated lines for sterile single-use systems. Simultaneously, Göttingen received significant capital investments to advance membrane and filter production capacity to support upcoming commercial bioprocess campaigns. These coordinated upgrades prepare the group’s infrastructure for future volume recovery once customer destocking fully resolves.
3.
Elevated capital: Sartorius AG executed a significant multi-year capacity expansion program from 2020 to 2023 to build a highly resilient global manufacturing footprint. These capital investments focused on doubling the cleanroom space at the Aubagne site in France, expanding membrane and filter capacities in Göttingen, and starting greenfield construction in South Korea. The extensive construction program drove the company's capital expenditure ratio to a peak of 17.1% of sales revenue in fiscal year 2023. This massive expansion has established a substantial, front-loaded fixed-cost base that continues to cycle through the consolidated income statement.
4.
Rising depreciation: Sartorius Stedim Biotech reported that total amortization and depreciation expenses climbed to €318.10 million in fiscal year 2025 compared to €301.70 million in 2024. The expansion of the global asset footprint contributed to a structural rise in the cost of sales, which reached €1,622.20 million in 2025. Depreciation of these newly added manufacturing assets represented an increasing share of total operating costs as factories came online. This high depreciation drag represents a temporary headwind that can only be diluted through significant volume increases.
5.
Substantial headroom: Sartorius is finalizing its large capital expenditure plan with no current designs to open any additional manufacturing sites. Bernstein Research expects future capacity expansions to be much less capital intensive, reducing annual capital expenditures down to the €300 million range in 2027 and 2028. This investment freeze indicates that the current asset base provides substantial revenue headroom to support future growth without requiring fresh capital. Consequently, the group can significantly expand production across its existing footprint as customer demand returns.
6.
Normalized capital discipline: Sartorius intends to transition its capital allocation strategy toward normalized spending after years of aggressive manufacturing footprint build-out. Management reiterated that fiscal year 2026 serves as a transition period, with capital expenditures on a clear path to stabilize in the medium term. This shift toward capital discipline will help improve future free cash flow generation as the group leverages its completed plant investments. Consequently, the reduced investment intensity will allow operating leverage to flow directly to the bottom line as capacity utilization recovers.

  2. Emerging Chinese competitors and capacity decisions by major contract manufacturers could disrupt the anticipated recovery path.
  3. Customer compensation from tariff surcharges and competitive share loss to larger peers represent key early risks to midterm consensus expectations.

The preceding analysis establishes that Sartorius's future earnings per share progression is highly sensitive to fixed-cost operating leverage, with management guiding for group underlying EBITDA margin expansion of 50 to 75 basis points annually starting in 2027, depending heavily on volume recovery as ramped-up capacity utilization at factories added during 2020 to 2023 will allow revenues to grow faster than operating expenses. The evidence base supporting this conclusion is robust in certain dimensions but notably incomplete in others, creating meaningful uncertainty around the precise magnitude and timing of the anticipated margin expansion.

1.

Similarly, Sartorius Stedim Biotech achieved steady profitability gains throughout the pre-pandemic cycle between fiscal years 2015 and 2019, with underlying EBITDA margin expanding from 26.2% in 2015 to 29.3% in 2019, supported by double-digit annual sales growth which culminated in total sales of €1,440.6 million in 2019. These historical cycles provide a reliable template for understanding how volume expansions translate into margin gains across multiple market environments. The evidence regarding recent cost restructuring initiatives is also strong. Sartorius implemented a comprehensive efficiency program in late 2024 with Executive Florian Funck stating in the third-quarter earnings call on 17 October 2024 that the initiative delivered over 100 million euros in cost savings during 2024, with approximately 70% of these savings coming from cost of goods sold via raw materials procurement.
