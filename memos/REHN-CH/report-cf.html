The first plausible problem to monitor emerges from elevated staff turnover rates, which deteriorated to 9% in 2023 from 6.8% in 2022, signaling mounting cultural integration friction during the strategic reorganization phase. This personnel churn rate substantially exceeds the typical Swiss utility sector baseline of approximately 5-6% annual turnover, suggesting deeper organizational instability during the transition from Christian Petit's departure through Patrick Bertschy's interim tenure and François Fellay's eventual appointment.

The elevated attrition coincides with multiple simultaneous executive departures across the Energy, Human Resources, and Property business units, amplifying execution risk during a critical strategic pivot.
