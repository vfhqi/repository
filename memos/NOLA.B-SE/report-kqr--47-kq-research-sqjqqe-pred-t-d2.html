ABG Sundal Collier also expects central costs to return to normal levels, giving a direct boost to 2027 EBITA. Currency translation works against these drivers rather than for them. Section 2 – Growth Drivers and Broker Forecasts for 2027-2028

Section summary
  1. The projected recovery in Nolato's financials for 2027-2028 is underpinned by key drivers that aim to reverse the revenue declines of 2021-2025.
  2. Inorganic mechatronics acquisitions and the high-margin Materials expansion are expected to distribute growth alongside the primary Hungarian medical contract.
