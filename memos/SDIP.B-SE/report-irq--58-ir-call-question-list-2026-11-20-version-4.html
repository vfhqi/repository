SEK 125m for 2027, against our SEK 90m, is worth up to 2.5% more in a full year. A total multiple near 9 times is our alarm. A ceiling on what 2027 can carry without new shares caps the upgrade.

2 ★ Fourth-quarter cash and year-end debt. "Cash conversion was 62% in the third quarter against a target of 70% to 90%; last year's fourth quarter was 134%. What should we expect this fourth quarter?"
- Pick from: "Where does leverage end the year, from 2.9 times in September?" / "How many million kronor of earn-outs are paid in the fourth quarter, and in 2027?" / "DNB Carnegie has leverage at 2.0 times by the end of 2027. Is that with or without SEK 100m of acquisitions a year?"
- Reads: above 100%, with leverage at 2.7 times or lower, is the memo's proof for adding. Below 90% stops the stream of deals when the narrative needs it; our upgrade odds fall from 60% to 40%.

3 ★ The margin. "The margin has missed consensus four quarters running; the third was 20.0% against 20.9%. DNB Carnegie has 21.4% for the fourth. How many million kronor of drag from Hilltip and Mecno drop out, and when?"
