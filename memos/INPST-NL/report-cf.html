EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 33 findings

InPost S.A. faces a critical inflection point as a recommended all-cash takeover at €15.60 per share consolidates ownership among FedEx, Advent, A&R Investments, and PPF Group, removing public market pressures while simultaneously loading the balance sheet with €4.9 billion in debt. The consortium cleared its minimum acceptance condition securing 89.81% of shares, bringing together FedEx at 37%, Advent at 37%, A&R at 16%, and PPF at 10%, while private ownership removes short-term capital market pressure but increases leverage to €4.9 billion in debt.

The most recent Q2 2026 results revealed headline revenue expansion reaching PLN 4,177.6 million with an 18.2% year-on-year increase, but Group Adjusted EBITDA margin declined by 330 basis points to 25.0% due to ongoing product mix shifts and investments. Management has downgraded fullyear FY26E Adjusted EBITDA guidance from flat growth to a mid-single-digit decline, citing extended UK logistics transformation timelines and continued domestic competitive investments, while simultaneously reducing capital expenditure targets from PLN 2.4 billion to PLN 2.1 billion.

InPost lowered its full-year FY26E Adjusted EBITDA guidance to a mid-single-digit decline from previously flat, while capital expenditure guidance was reduced to PLN 2.1 billion from the previously guided PLN 2.4 billion target.
