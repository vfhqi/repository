Tube Supply Ceilings and OCCAR Allocations

1.

High contract utilization: The landmark OCCAR procurement contract consumes a massive portion of the group's long-term supplier commitments. Bernstein Research indicates in a flash note from December 10, 2025, that the contract for 100,000 binoculars requires 200,000 Exosens tubes, which represents exactly 50% of the 400,000 tubes contracted under Theon's five-year framework agreement. Interdependent supply alignment: Theon seeks to mitigate procurement bottlenecks through a deep, highly symbiotic relationship with its primary tube supplier. Bernstein Research notes in a flash note from December 4, 2025, that Theon's 400,000-tube framework through 2030 will consume approximately 52% of output over that same period. This mutual reliance secures critical components for Theon's massive backlog but also leaves its operations vulnerable to any production disruptions at Exosens.
