This represents an exceptionally rare backward vertical integration strategy in the European heparin market, where upstream raw pig intestinal mucosa sourcing is dominated by Chinese slaughterhouses, placing ROVI among the top ~5% of European pharmaceutical companies pursuing upstream biological raw material self-sufficiency rather than relying on established Asian supply chains.
