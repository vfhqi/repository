Aggressive repricing in 2023: The platform shifted to broad deposit remuneration on January 1, 2023, paying interest across investment accounts and raising its average customer deposit rate to 0.66 percent in Q1 2023, representing a direct quarterly pass-through beta of approximately 44 percent against the cumulative 1.50 percentage point policy rate hike.

Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Avanza's interest rate transmission directly dictates its earnings outlook as Net Interest Income accounts for approximately one-third of the group's total income.
  2. Rising customer sensitivity to deposit rates represents a core headwind to platform margins during rate adjustments.

Defining the central inquiry

1.
Investigating rate transmission: "Avanza Bank Holding AB (AZA-SE), Sweden's largest digital savings and investment platform: 2.39 million retail, Private Banking and Pro customers and SEK 1,277bn of savings capital in one reported segment (Savings and Investments) across three regulated entities (Avanza Bank, Avanza Pension, Avanza Fonder); FY2025 operating income of SEK 4,495m split net interest income 33%, net brokerage 28%, fund commissions 17%, currency-related income 16%, other 6%; organic entry into Denmark planned for the second half of 2027. For Avanza Bank Holding AB (AZA-SE), how have customer deposit volumes, the interest-bearing share of deposits and the average deposit rate responded to Riksbank rate increases in the past (the hiking cycle from May 2022 to September 2023, from 0% to 4.0%), quarter by quarter: how quickly and how fully did Avanza raise savings-account and investment-account rates, what share was passed through, how did deposits move relative to savings capital, and how did net interest income respond against the company's published sensitivity at the time; and what do the company's statements and Nordnet's experience in the same cycle imply for the pass-through and deposit behaviour to expect after a Riksbank hike from 1.75% on 4 November 2026?"

Importance of net interest income

2.
Core revenue driver: Avanza's business model relies heavily on net interest income to offset cyclical transaction revenues because NII historically constituted 33% of the platform's total operating income. According to Deutsche Bank's report on 30 September 2026, NII rose from SEK 321 million in 2021 to around SEK 1.6 billion in 2023 due to the Riksbank's sharp policy rate increases. g Q Avanza quarterly interest income and expense breakdown from Q1 2023 to Q3 2026 This demonstrates that net interest income acts as a crucial earnings cushion when brokerage and fund commissions face macro headwinds.
3.
Elevated sensitivity parameters: Avanza's earnings remain highly sensitive to the Riksbank's policy choices, which means small rate changes yield massive bottom-line swings. In its interim report of 15 October 2026, management disclosed that a 1 percentage point policy rate hike would increase full-year net interest income by SEK 1,100 million. Understanding how closely historical results tracked this sensitivity is vital because it determines how accurately analysts can forecast future earnings per share.

Risk profile and deposit migration

4.
Accelerating cash migration: Customer self-allocation into higher-yielding savings accounts compresses Avanza's net interest margin and directly threatens profitability. Morgan Stanley's note of 29 January 2024 observed that rising competition on deposit rates and cash migration to interest-paying accounts exacerbated net interest margin pressures during the peak hiking cycle. Consequently, any failure to anticipate customer deposit behavior could lead to material downside revisions for projected earnings.
5.
Peer platform dynamics: Evaluating the historical behavior of Nordnet alongside Avanza provides essential insights into Swedish digital platform competitiveness. Jefferies reported on 8 July 2026 that both Avanza and Nordnet are able to pass rate moves quickly to depositors, but Nordnet has a higher sensitivity to interest rates overall. Thus, comparing deposit pass-through rates during the previous hiking cycle helps investors determine if Avanza possesses superior pricing power over its closest regional peer.
6.
Other side of the story: Some analysts argue that brokerage activity and fund inflows will eventually eclipse interest rate sensitivity as the primary driver of Avanza's long-term earnings compounding. Deutsche Bank projected on 30 September 2026 that continued customer growth will expand Avanza's recurring earnings base, enabling a 14% EPS compound annual growth rate through 2028 regardless of rate normalization. Therefore, focusing solely on interest rate cycles might overlook the platform's robust structural growth tailwinds.

Section 2 – Quarter-by-Quarter Deposit Rates and Pass-Through Betas

Section summary
  1. High confidence evidence shows Avanza maintained zero deposit pass-through during 2022 before shifting to aggressive pricing adjustments in 2023 that raised cumulative household deposit betas toward 50 percent.
  2. Commercial peers including Swedbank and SEB experienced similar pass-through acceleration as rising interest rate sensitivity compressed net interest margins across the Swedish banking sector.

Initial rate suppression in 2022

1.
Zero interest pass-through policy: Avanza maintained zero interest payments on retail investment and transaction accounts throughout 2022 despite four consecutive Riksbank policy rate hikes. According to Avanza's preliminary financial statement published on 20 January 2023, deposit interest was restricted exclusively to corporate savings accounts yielding 1.50 percent at year-end 2022. This initial total rate suppression allowed the platform to absorb early central bank tightening without incurring interest expenses. Consequently, early policy rate increases translated directly into unprecedented net interest margin expansion for the group.
2.
Sector deposit margin discipline: Major Swedish commercial banks similarly limited retail deposit rate adjustments during the first half of the Riksbank hiking cycle. A research note from DNB Carnegie dated 19 September 2022 highlighted that Swedish household deposit rates remained near zero percent through July 2022 despite short-term STIBOR rising significantly. The report observed that commercial lenders passed on virtually all rate hikes to mortgage borrowers while keeping deposit costs flat. Therefore, Avanza's initial decision to hold deposit rates at zero was fully aligned with broader market pricing.
3.
Quarterly earnings benefit: Lagging deposit transmission created substantial earnings tailwinds across Swedish financial institutions during late 2022. DNB Carnegie reported on 15 July 2022 that Avanza's published interest rate sensitivity guided for a SEK 550 million annual benefit per 100 basis points rate increase. The broker emphasized that rising deposit margins successfully offset depressed trading and commission revenues across the platform. As a result, Avanza expanded its interest earnings base without inflating customer funding costs during the initial tightening phase.

Strategic deposit repricing in 2023

4.
Broad account remuneration shift: Avanza instituted a structural shift in deposit pricing on 1 January 2023 by paying interest across retail investment savings accounts (ISK), endowment insurance, and equity accounts. As disclosed in Avanza's first quarter interim report on 24 April 2023, total interest expenses reached SEK 159 million in Q1 2023 compared to zero in Q1 2022. The company reported that its average customer deposit rate jumped to 0.66 percent during the quarter. This policy shift represented a direct quarterly pass-through beta of approximately 44 percent against the 1.50 percentage point policy rate hike delivered between November and February.
5.
Proprietary savings account expansion: Avanza accelerated deposit pass-through further during the second quarter of 2023 by aggressively promoting its proprietary savings product. In its second quarter interim report published on 14 July 2023, management confirmed the average deposit rate climbed to 1.05 percent as interest expenses reached SEK 150 million. The platform raised its savings account yield to 3.25 percent by 30 June 2023, causing savings account balances to double to SEK 14.8 billion. Consequently, total interest-bearing customer deposits reached 91 percent of platform liquidity by mid-year.
6.
Targeted rate hike pass-through: Morgan Stanley observed on 13 July 2023 that Avanza increased customer deposit remuneration by roughly 95 basis points between April and July 2023 against a 75 basis point policy rate increase. The note stated that this tactical adjustment reflected a marginal pass-through beta exceeding 100 percent for that specific period. Morgan Stanley emphasized that aggressive pricing was necessary to retain liquid cash on the platform as competition intensified. Thus, Avanza prioritized asset retention over short-term net interest margin expansion during mid-2023.
7.
Sequential deposit yield escalation: Avanza reported in its third quarter report on 19 October 2023 that its average customer deposit yield expanded further to 1.44 percent. Total quarterly interest expenses rose to SEK 217 million as policy rates reached 4 percent. Management noted that the average interest yield on internally financed lending reached 3.94 percent, helping to mitigate expanding deposit costs. This demonstrated that Avanza's cumulative deposit beta reached approximately 36 percent by Q3 2023.

Jefferies reported on 17 October 2023 that Swedish banks passed on nearly 100 percent of rate increases to savings accounts during 2023, driving cumulative domestic deposit betas to 50 percent. The analysis highlighted that Eurozone household deposit betas remained lower at 20 to 25 percent over the same timeline. This regional discrepancy meant Swedish institutions faced sharper interest margin compression than Eurozone peers.

Section 3 – Deposit Volume Dynamics and Asset Allocation Shifts

Section summary
  1. Total deposits at Avanza stabilized near 13 percent of savings capital during the 2022-2023 hiking cycle as escalating retail rate sensitivity drove rapid asset migration.
  2. Customer self-allocation into higher-yielding savings accounts and fixed income products successfully arrested cash outflows but altered the corporate deposit mix.

Aggregated balance sheet compression: Avanza's customer deposits experienced notable pressure in late 2022 as rising macroeconomic uncertainty and Riksbank rate hikes altered consumer behavior. According to Avanza's interim report published on 18 October 2022, private and corporate customers began transferring liquid assets from the platform to higher-yielding external deposit accounts. This trend was particularly visible among corporate and Private Banking customers who sought immediate rate relief. This showed that paying interest across retail accounts successfully stabilized core customer funding. Furthermore, deposits in accounts paying interest rapidly grew to represent 93 percent of total deposits on Avanza's balance sheet by 31 March 2023.

Progressive deposit mix transformation: Deposits as a percentage of total savings capital continued to contract throughout 2023 as customers converted cash into securities. As a result, while customer liquidity was successfully retained on the platform, the average revenue margin on mutual funds deteriorated due to asset mix shifts.

This proved that liquid assets were becoming less sticky as retail investors actively optimized their portfolios.

Subdued trading activity: Subdued retail engagement capped brokerage revenues even as customer cash balances were redeployed. Morgan Stanley observed on 5 July 2023 that Avanza's total trades fell by 16 percent quarter-on-quarter in Q2 2023. The note explained that while headline cash balances rose 2 percent month-on-month in June to SEK 95.9 billion, the deposit mix skewed heavily toward the higher interest-bearing savings account which reached SEK 14.8 billion. Section 4 – NII Performance Against Published Sensitivity

Section summary
  1. Medium confidence evidence shows Avanza's actual net interest income growth underperformed its initial theoretical sensitivity guidance due to rapid deposit pricing adjustments and cash migration.
  2. Discrepancies were primarily driven by a three-month lag on the liquidity portfolio, delayed repricing on the lending book, and the tactical choice to remunerate tax-wrapped investment accounts.

Theoretical sensitivity vs actual results

1.
Theoretical rate sensitivity: Avanza historically guided that a 1 percentage point increase in the Riksbank policy rate would generate SEK 550 million in additional annual net interest income. This baseline sensitivity model assumed that customer deposit behavior and interest rate durations would remain entirely static during the interest rate cycle. However, actual transmission was highly complex because interest rates on deposits had to be adjusted. Thus, the company's early guidance proved to be an imperfect predictor of actual earnings because it ignored competitive pricing pressure.
2.
Drastic guidance downgrade: Avanza fundamentally altered its NII sensitivity profile in late 2022 by introducing interest payments across retail investment accounts. This strategic pivot meant that a subsequent 75 basis point rate hike would only boost annual NII by SEK 50 million. This represented a massive reduction from the SEK 400 million benefit implied by previous management guidelines. Consequently, this structural change showed that deposit remuneration severely compressed sensitivity as the platform prioritized customer retention over margin optimization.
3.
Initial quarterly underperformance: Avanza reported a disappointing 4 percent operating profit miss in the first quarter of 2023 due to underperforming NII expectations. The platform suffered a 5 percent sequential decline in net interest income during the quarter. This drop occurred because the platform began paying interest on 93 percent of its deposit base. As a result, early rate hikes failed to translate fully into NII growth because re-pricing customer liquidity inflated expenses much faster than consensus models anticipated.
4.
Escalating sensitivity targets: Avanza's NII sensitivity guidance remained highly dynamic, with later reports indicating a SEK 290 million to SEK 700 million impact from a 1 percentage point interest rate decline. This wide guided range reflected the inherent difficulty in forecasting customer deposit behavior as the interest rate environment peaked. Broker models suggested that consensus NII expectations were exposed to material downside risk because they did not fully account for these sensitivity guidelines. Consequently, actual NII results continued to highly depend on competitive pricing rather than stable management targets.

Explaining the actual transmission discrepancies

5.
Delayed rate transmission: Portfolio lags and delayed asset repricing further explained why actual NII did not match the immediate parallel shift sensitivity. Avanza experienced a three-month interest rate lag on its liquidity portfolio investments. Furthermore, management chose to delay the pass-through of policy rate increases to its mortgage borrowers. This tactical delay meant that NII growth was deferred sequentially rather than realized immediately, creating short-term underperformance against static sensitivity guidelines.
6.
Exploding interest expenses: The rapid expansion of high-yield savings accounts created a severe margin mismatch that undermined the platform's sensitivity models. Avanza's average deposit rate escalated quickly, which dramatically increased interest expenses run-rates. For instance, the rapid influx of capital into the proprietary savings account drove up deposit costs. This trend proved that deposit beta expansion compressed margins far more severely than the platform's baseline parallel shift calculations had allowed.
7.
Treasury reinvestment offsets: Spreads on the treasury portfolio provided a partial offset to escalating deposit costs, creating localized outperformance. In late 2024, expanding spreads on treasury portfolio investments helped push NII 5 percent above consensus. This outperformance occurred because higher yields in the treasury portfolio backbook cushioned the platform from immediate rate cuts. This demonstrated that treasury duration management mitigated headwinds even as the core retail deposit base became highly sensitive.

Comparable peer sensitivity management

8.
Platform sensitivity management: Regional peer Nordnet managed its net interest margin much more aggressively during the cycle by limiting deposit interest. Nordnet maintained its decision not to offer interest on investment accounts in Norway, Denmark, and Finland. This geographic insulation meant Nordnet paid interest on only 26 percent of deposits compared to Avanza's 93 percent. Consequently, Nordnet's experience suggests that geographic diversification preserves NII sensitivity when a single domestic market faces intense pricing pressure.
9.
Commercial bank benchmarks: Traditional Swedish commercial banks also presented a contrasting model of sensitivity management due to their structural funding advantages. Swedbank maintained extremely high rate sensitivity, guiding that a 100 basis point rate increase would yield SEK 6,025 million in additional NII. This high sensitivity was preserved because a large portion of household transaction deposits remained at zero percent interest. This proves that traditional banking models retained superior pricing power over digital savings platforms which are forced to pay high rates to retain cash.
10.
Geographical margin protection: Diversified Nordic platforms demonstrated that geographic focus directly determined NII sensitivity preservation. Nordnet utilized its multi-country footprint to offset Swedish margin pressures through more supportive rate environments in other regions. This structural advantage allowed the competitor to maintain overall NII resilience even as its Swedish deposit margins faced intense local competition. This highlights that geographic concentration increases NII vulnerability during aggressive domestic hiking cycles.

148 149 150 Mortgage repricing dynamics: Swedish mortgage pricing dynamics also played a critical role in the timing of NII sensitivity realization. Incumbent commercial banks implemented mortgage repricing with significant legal notice periods, creating short-term margin compression. However, as new rates took full effect, these lenders captured substantial NII expansion. This delayed transmission confirms that lending book repricing

Contrary evidence and long-term outlook

12.
Contrary evidence: Avanza's absolute net interest income expanded from SEK 321 million in 2021 to around SEK 1.6 billion in 2023 despite the significant drag from deposit repricing. This massive increase indicates that the negative impact of deposit mix shifts was largely offset by the sheer scale of central bank tightening. Thus, the absolute NII expansion still provided an extremely effective earnings cushion for the platform even if it fell short of the theoretical sensitivity models.
13.
Other side of the story: Some broker analyses suggest that NII sensitivity will rapidly improve as soon as interest rates begin to decline. The platform's flexible deposit account structures allow Avanza to reduce customer interest rates swiftly, which helps protect net interest margins. Furthermore, any decline in deposit yields will likely encourage customers to shift idle cash back into high-margin equities and mutual funds. Therefore, temporary underperformance against NII sensitivity models does not permanently impair long-term profitability because easing cycles trigger a powerful offset in transaction-based revenues.

Section 5 – Nordnet's Experience and Implications for the November 2026 Hike

Section summary
  1. Nordnet's pan-Nordic deposit structure mitigates its Swedish interest rate sensitivity because only one- fifth of its total funding is exposed to intense domestic pricing pressure.
  2. The impending Riksbank policy rate decision on November 4, 2026 will test platform pricing power as both companies prepare to adjust customer remuneration.

Nordnet's pan-Nordic structural insulation

1.
Geographic funding diversification: Nordnet maintains a highly diversified geographic deposit base that shields its aggregate interest margins from intense Swedish price competition. According to DNB Carnegie's report on 10 January 2023, Swedish customer deposits represent only about 20 percent of Nordnet's total deposit volume. This multi-country distribution allows the platform to suppress deposit rates in Norway, Denmark, and Finland where Avanza does not operate. As a result, Nordnet retains superior pricing flexibility compared to its purely domestic peer.
2.
Remuneration exposure disparities: Nordnet managed to sustain lower overall funding costs because a smaller portion of its total customer liquidity was interest-bearing. DNB Carnegie's research update on 7 July 2023 indicated that only 27 percent of Nordnet's customer cash was held in interest-bearing accounts. In contrast, Avanza was forced to pay interest on 93 percent of its platform deposits during the same period. This structural difference means that Nordnet preserved higher net interest margins than Avanza throughout the historical tightening cycle.
3.
Lending portfolio composition: Nordnet's loan book configuration offers additional margin resilience due to a heavy concentration in higher-yielding products. Jefferies reported on 8 July 2026 that Nordnet's lending portfolio is skewed 60 percent to margin loans and 40 percent to mortgages. This contrasts with Avanza's loan book which is weighted 60 percent toward mortgages. Consequently, Nordnet's aggregate interest income is less sensitive to direct policy rates because margin lending yields typically carry higher risk premia.

Historical pricing pass-through experience

4.
Remuneration escalation triggers: Historical evidence shows that Swedish deposit repricing on Nordnet's platform was directly triggered by Avanza's aggressive tactical moves. DNB Carnegie reported on 21 February 2023 that Nordnet implemented interest payments on Swedish tax-wrapper accounts only after Avanza announced its own policy. This competitive reaction capped Nordnet's interest rate sensitivity across Swedish accounts. However, the platform maintained zero deposit interest in other Nordic regions where local competitive pressure remained benign.
5.
Lending book transmission speeds: Nordnet transmitted historical rate adjustments rapidly to its borrowers while simultaneously delaying pass-through to depositors. DNB Carnegie observed on 7 July 2023 that Nordnet expanded its lending yields by 20 to 80 basis points during the second quarter of 2023. The broker noted that deposit rate increases remained strictly isolated to Swedish savings products. Therefore, this asymmetric pricing strategy allowed Nordnet to generate substantial earnings compounding during the peak hiking phase.
6.
Easing cycle margin compression: The subsequent Riksbank rate cuts from 4 percent to 1.75 percent compressed net interest margins for both digital investment platforms. Jefferies documented on 8 July 2026 that the Swedish central bank delivered rapid rate cuts between May 2025 and September 2025. During this down-cycle, Nordnet expected a 50 percent pass-through beta on margin lending rate reductions. This pricing discipline helped the platform stabilize absolute net interest income despite significant macroeconomic headwinds across the Nordic region.
7.
Cash migration velocity difference: Competitive pressure on digital platforms differed because customers in Sweden exhibited far higher interest rate sensitivity. DNB Carnegie observed on 11 January 2023 that Swedish savers actively migrated cash balances to higher-yielding alternatives far faster than savers in neighboring countries. The broker highlighted that Nordnet did not face similar cash outflow risks in its Finnish and Norwegian segments where savers remained less mobile. This regional discrepancy meant that Avanza was structurally more vulnerable to rapid cash migration than its diversified competitor.

November 2026 rate hike implications

8.
