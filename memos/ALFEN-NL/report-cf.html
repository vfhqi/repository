EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Alfen NV faces highly disruptive executive turnover during a critical strategic pivot from high-volume hardware expansion to high-margin bundled software and services, while European EV charging demand experiences a sharp cyclical contraction offset by robust structural expansion in grid infrastructure and energy storage. The company delivered group revenue of €261.5 million in H1 2026, surpassing compiled consensus of €258.1 million, with adjusted EBITDA of €16.4 million representing a minor beat against the €16.1 million consensus estimate.

However, revenue growth remains highly front-loaded in the first half, leaving 2H margins exposed to high operational hurdles, while group operating margins are severely diluted by structural transformation costs and defensive charging campaigns.

Executive board turnover has been highly disruptive and sudden, introducing execution risks during the strategic transformation. CEO Marco Roeleveld retired early on October 1, 2025 due to an unforeseen medical condition, succeeded by Michael Colijn who brought extensive smart charging expertise as former Chief Executive of Heliox since 2020. CFO Onno Krap stepped down on July 1, 2026 after serving since May 2024, with Jefferies analyst David Kerstens noting investors will worry about his abrupt short-notice departure. The commercial role was eliminated when CCO Michelle Lesh stepped down on March 31, 2025, leaving the statutory board focused on only two directors. This concentration of leadership change coincides with strategic focus shifts from high-volume hardware expansion to high-margin bundled software and services, with management narrowing its geographic focus to five core North-Western European markets during the late 2024 strategic revalidation.

The materiality of strategic and operational change is profound across all three business segments. Alfen delivered 53,700 charge points in H1 2026, representing a 12.3% volume drop from H1 2025, with charging sales declining by 17.4% to €51.1 million, while Smart Grid Solutions revenue grew by 14.9% in H1 2026 to €111.6 million. Storage revenue surged by 87.8% to €98.8 million, driven by two mega projects. Management completely discontinued its DC charger portfolio to fully commit to AC destination charging and signed a transformational 5 GWh supply partnership with CATL to deploy sodium-ion storage in July 2026.

Alfen transitioned from a functional to a business unit structure on May 1, 2026 to accelerate execution, initiating a €4.5 million restructuring provision and recognizing €3.5 million of charges during the second quarter of 2026.

Restructuring opex right-sizing is estimated to deliver a 15% impact on core operating profit expansion, sodiumion battery deployment targets a 10% gross margin contribution in utility storage, inventory liquidation has released substantial working capital cash flow with a 20% impact, while portfolio simplification will trigger a temporary 15% revenue decline as DC hardware is discontinued. Dutch distribution system operators plan record-high grid expansion investments from 2026 to 2028, but Smart Grid expansion is held back by severe grid operator labor shortages in mature markets.
