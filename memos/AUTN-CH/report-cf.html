Appendix

Plausible problems to monitor

Autoneum's operational and governance profile reveals several metrics and structural developments that lag peer medians or sector base rates, warranting closer monitoring despite the company's overall progress toward medium-term profitability targets. Board member Yanni von Roy-Jiang did not stand for re-election in April 2026 due to potential Mercedes-Benz conflicts, serving only one year after being elected in 2025 and reducing the Board's direct Chinese industry oversight, a particularly concerning development given the group's strategic pivot toward Asian markets where governance expertise is critical.

Employee training averaged 2.77 days in 2024, falling below the 3.1-day industry benchmark because of operational restructuring measures, suggesting human capital development may be underfunded relative to sector norms during a period requiring rapid BEV product innovation. Appraisal coverage reached 89% in 2024, missing the group's 95% target, indicating gaps in internal governance execution that could impair talent retention and performance management systems. Organic revenue in BG Asia declined by 9.8% in H1 2026, revealing subdued local demand before acquisitions masked underlying weakness in a market critical to the Level Up strategy's success. BG Asia EBIT margin fell to 5.1% in H1 2026 from 7.7% in H1 2025, reflecting integration costs and start-up expenses at Wuhu and Anqing, placing regional profitability materially below North American and SAMEA segment performance.

The broader sell-side landscape presents structural visibility challenges, as global coverage remains highly restricted with key international brokers maintaining no active coverage, limiting institutional investor engagement and potentially constraining multiple expansion regardless of operational execution. Finally, the revenue contraction was driven by continued appreciation of the Swiss franc and resulting negative FX effects, with foreign exchange devaluations creating a massive CHF 102.7 million translation drag in 2025, an exposure magnitude that exceeds typical automotive supplier currency risk profiles and persistently undermines consolidated financial reporting despite strong local currency performance across most segments.

Outliers worth noting

Autoneum's Business Group SAMEA delivered an exceptional 32.5% organic local currency revenue growth in H1 2026, placing it in the top 5% of automotive supplier regional performance globally during a period when most markets experienced contraction or single-digit expansion. This outlier performance was achieved through aggressive inflation-linked pricing mechanisms in hyperinflationary South American economies, sustaining a remarkably high 14.4% EBIT margin that significantly exceeds the typical automotive supplier regional margin range of 3-7% and suggests either exceptional local market power or unsustainable pricing gains vulnerable to competitive erosion.
