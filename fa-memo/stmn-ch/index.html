The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Organic growth: 8.6 percent (FY2026; Q4 about 9.5 to 10 percent after 7.1, 8.5 and 9.2 percent in Q1 to Q3), 8.25 percent (FY2027), 8.0 percent (FY2028 and FY2029), 7.5 percent (FY2030). Built as market growth of about 2 to 4 points (US near zero through 2027, Europe 3 to 5 percent, China negative on price in 2027) plus share gain of 4 to 6 points (iEXCEL at close to 40 percent of premium volume, Neodent, digital pull-through, ex-China APAC and Latin America), fading as the iEXCEL conversion wave annualises in 2027 to 2028.
  • Price and mix: about +0.5 to +1.0 percent (FY2026), -0.5 to -1.0 percent (FY2027, VBP 2.0), +0.3 to +0.5 percent a year after.

How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
