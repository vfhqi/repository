The Central Key Question

1.
Key Question: "which reinforcing or balancing feedback loops are at work in this business model, such that dynamics at play today create additional scope for further positive or negative dynamics in the near and medium term (reinforcing feedback loops), or the opposite (balancing loops)? Ultimately, this labor ceiling means that Bravida's growth will depend on its recruitment capacity rather than customer demand or capital availability alone.

In the Q1 2026 earnings call, Bravida's management highlighted that executing on these built-up advances drives cash conversion lower.
