Executive Summary -- We are looking for a clear, conviction-carrying statement of the investment thesis, key ratings, and actionable conclusion; avoiding vague or overly hedged language.
Judgement summary
  • BLUF. Park TT Electronics. The business is genuinely improving and the share price has improved faster, leaving no margin of safety at GBp 137.6
  • Overall verdict. D TT Electronics is the weakest fundamental profile in its six-name peer set, and the share price has already re-rated ahead of the operational proof
  • Bad news first, the earnings base is flattered. The FY25 recovery leaned on £3.5m of non-repeat Plano last-time-buy profit and a £14.8m inventory liquidation, neither of which repeats
  • Organic revenue is still shrinking. FY25 organic revenue fell 2.7%, and the May-26 trading update showed sales down 4.8% organically over four months
  • Earnings quality is poor. Statutory operating loss reached £28.2m on £41.4m of impairments, and the adjusted effective tax rate hit 57.1%, cutting adjusted earnings per share to 6.9p
  • Governance carries a real scar. Deloitte cited material internal control weaknesses, and 2023 operating profit was restated down by £5.7m after a forensic investigation at Cleveland
  • The good news is genuine but narrow. European aerospace and defence book-to-bill reached 135% in 2025 on 15.3% European operating margins, the best regional number in the group
  • The balance sheet is fixed. Net debt excluding leases fell £29.8m to £50.3m, covenant leverage 1.1x against a 3.0x ceiling, removing solvency from the argument
  • Self-help is real and measurable. Cleveland factory productivity rose from 57% in Q3-24 to 81% by January 2026, and rework fell from 12% to 5%
  • Corporate change is the strongest setup fit. A new chief executive, chief financial officer and chair arrived within twelve months, alongside a divisional reorganisation and a Components disposal review
  • The entry point is the binding objection. The stock trades at GBp 137.6 against a unanimous 110p sell-side target, roughly 25% above where the analysts who follow it think it belongs
