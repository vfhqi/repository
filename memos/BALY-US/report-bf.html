EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bally's Corporation operates as a globally diversified omni-channel gaming operator managing 20 retail casinos across 11 US states, one casino in the UK, international lottery technology services, and emerging North America Interactive digital platforms, yet faces severe financial distress characterized by elevated leverage exceeding 7.3 times lease-adjusted net debt to EBITDAR and substantial going-concern warnings issued in August 2026. The company's aggressive expansion strategy centers on completing a $1.34 billion permanent Chicago casino while simultaneously integrating the Intralot B2B business and restructuring its sub-scale interactive operations, but execution remains constrained by massive lease liabilities totaling $4.12 billion in minimum operating lease payments and restrictive debt covenants requiring temporary lender waivers through March 2027. The core business model exhibits medium structural strength anchored by a protected 20-year Rhode Island monopoly for online slots and table games extending through 2043, yet this durable asset is overshadowed by depressed returns on invested capital hovering in negative to low single digits and operating margins consistently trailing regional peers like Red Rock Resorts, which maintains casino margins exceeding 70% compared to Bally's mid to high 20% range.
