Section 10 – Pre-Mortem: Executive Summary

The Analysis of Competing Hypotheses framework identifies the FALSE FRIEND profile as the winning hypothesis, presenting only one hard inconsistency against the weight of available evidence. This profile assumes that surface-level operational metrics mask underlying structural decay, particularly regarding volume erosion and channel quality deterioration across Vitrolife's integrated portfolio. The competing YES profile, which assumes sustainable double-digit organic growth, faces multiple hard inconsistencies driven by prolonged China demographic softness, the massive SEK 5.36 billion Igenomix goodwill impairment, and escalating private equity procurement centralization.

The outright failure NO profile is similarly refuted by expanding gross margins to 60.3% and the substantial SEK 500 million share buyback program, which provide defensive operational buffers inconsistent with imminent business collapse. The single hard inconsistency confronting the FALSE FRIEND scenario stems from strong Americas consumables organic growth of 15% in Q2 2026, which directly contradicts the hypothesis of pervasive volume decay.

However, this regional strength appears insufficient to invalidate the broader pattern of structural deterioration evident across fifteen diagnostic evidence items evaluated in the matrix. The framework assessed critical markers including negative 1% organic sales growth, severe genetic services pricing erosion, clinic network insourcing of testing services, and aggressive competitor bundling campaigns. These disconfirming data points collectively reveal that headline profitability improvements derive primarily from portfolio rationalization and cost restructuring rather than genuine underlying demand strength.

The joint failure probability across the ten-plus detailed failure narratives is estimated at 45% over the investment horizon, representing a medium-to-high risk threshold that justifies extreme analytical caution.
