EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. the strategic spin-off of Amrize successfully isolates Holcim's rest-of-world operations, creating a highly focused regional champion in European and emerging markets.
  2. the structural division between core Building Materials and Building Solutions allows Holcim to capture high-margin downstream specification value.
  3. the aggressive portfolio rotation away from hyper-competitive markets toward highly consolidated zones is structurally enhancing consolidated returns.
  4. recent executive shake-ups establish a performance-driven leadership core capable of driving integration and capturing synergies from newly acquired assets.

Holcim has emerged as a highly focused regional champion in European and emerging markets following the strategic Amrize spin-off, structurally enhancing consolidated returns through aggressive portfolio rotation toward highly consolidated zones while capturing high-margin downstream specification value via its Building Materials and Building Solutions division. The company's post-spin financial profile demonstrates industry-leading operating margins, with recurring EBITDA reaching CHF 3,992 million at a 25.4% margin and recurring EBIT of CHF 2,876 million at an 18.3% margin in FY2025, comfortably outperforming Heidelberg Materials' 15.6% operating margin.

Holcim's Return on Invested Capital improved to 11.2% in FY2025, rising steadily from 8.9% in 2021, though trailing CRH's 12.1% adjusted ROIC, reflecting ongoing downstream integration investments.

1.
Business Model and Geographic Concentration: Holcim completed the spin-off of its North American business Amrize on June 23, 2025, successfully isolating the company from US macro risks while retaining Europe, Latin America, and AMEA operations. Europe remains the largest segment generating 56% of post-spin net sales, having expanded its recurring EBIT margin by 430 basis points from 2020 to 2025. Latin America represents 20% of sales but delivers the highest operating margins in the group, benefiting from highly consolidated markets like Mexico and Ecuador that mitigate cyclical construction demand. The reorganized product segmentation into Building Materials and Building Solutions drives stable pricing power, with Building Materials covering cement and aggregates through resource scarcity advantages.
2.
Financial Profile and Peer Benchmarking: Holcim's restated FY2025 net sales declined 2.9% to CHF 15,724 million, primarily driven by the North American spin-off capital market separation. Organic sales rose 3.0% in local currency during FY2025, with recurring EBITDA margin expanding 90 basis points supported by alternative fuel usage reaching 39% globally. Holcim's 18.3% EBIT margin exceeds Heidelberg Materials' 15.6% operating margin, though Buzzi Unicem achieved a sector-leading 27.4% EBITDA margin driven by profitable US cement exposure. Net financial debt was reduced 55.2% to CHF 3,785 million, improving the net debt-to-recurring EBITDA leverage ratio to 0.9x from 1.2x, comparing favorably to Heidelberg's 1.2x and CRH's 1.4x. Free cash flow of CHF 2,154 million with a 54% conversion ratio exceeded Heidelberg Materials' 45.1% cash conversion rate in FY2025.
3.
Value Chain Positioning and Margin Distribution: Holcim operates across aggregates extraction, cement manufacturing, ready-mix concrete, and building solutions, with cement remaining the primary profit engine generating approximately two-thirds of heavy-side EBITDA globally. Aggregates gross margins average around 45%, outperforming downstream concrete businesses, while highly consolidated cement markets like Latin America generate EBIT margins exceeding 40%. Ready-mix concrete margins remain structurally thin with typical EBIT margins of 5% to 10%, acting mainly as a downstream distribution channel. Building Solutions delivers attractive EBIT margins between 10% and 20% with higher return on capital due to significantly lower capex requirements. The strategic €1.85 billion acquisition of Xella expanded walling solutions with a platform providing projected EUR 1 billion net sales in 2026 and run-rate synergies of EUR 60 million reducing the transaction multiple to 6.9x EBITDA. Industry Structure and Competitive Positioning: Switzerland exhibits extreme market concentration with a Herfindahl-Hirschman Index surpassing 4,000, where Holcim commands approximately 57% dominant market share in a landlocked oligopoly. France displays moderate consolidation with an HHI of approximately 2,076, where Holcim maintains industry-leading 32% market share among three firms controlling 71% concentration. Mexico features an HHI of 2,561 with CR3 of 77%, where Holcim holds 18.6% share as a disciplined challenger to rational leader Cemex. Holcim ranks as the third-largest global cement producer with 203.40 million tons annual capacity, compared to Heidelberg Materials' 186.90 million tons formidable capacity. Holcim's deliberate portfolio optimization has materially increased its sales-weighted HHI since 2018, systematically exiting fragmented markets like India and Brazil to consolidate high-margin, import-insulated territories. Customer Dynamics and Channel Power: Holcim's highly diversified customer base across retail self-builders and large contractors keeps individual concentration low, while dynamic surcharge mechanisms enable rapid passthrough of energy and CO2 inflation to customers. Direct energy comprising fuel and electricity accounts for approximately 9% of total net sales, with Holcim securing price stability by hedging approximately 75% to 80% of European energy exposure for 2026. Group alternative fuel usage reached 39% of thermal energy in 2025, targeting 50% global thermal substitution by 2030 compared to Heidelberg's 43% target, with Europe reaching 70% in Q1 2026. The 2,365-store Disensa franchise network operates as the largest regional construction materials network spanning eight Latin American countries, capturing high margins via franchisee royalties, supplier rebates, and intermediation margins.

Operator Quality and Foundation Strength: CEO Miljan Gutovic succeeded Jan Jenisch in early 2026, historically accelerating downstream solutions and operational performance while executing the NextGen Growth 2030 program focusing on high-margin sustainable construction. Management systematically divested low-growth, hyper-competitive heavy assets generating CHF 6.4 billion cash proceeds from the Indian divestment, directing capital rotation toward highly consolidated, high-HHI markets with stable pricing.

Management consistently achieved or exceeded mid-term targets, with FY2025 organic sales rising 3.0% and recurring EBIT increasing 10.3% organically, attaining the upper-bound of mid-term expectations. Scarce limestone quarry concessions create immense, insurmountable localized physical barriers, with greenfield plant development highly restricted by prohibitive capital requirements and acquiring permits taking up to a decade in mature markets.

Holcim delivers premium Return on Invested Capital rising from 8.9% in 2021 to 11.2% in FY2025, comparing favorably to Heidelberg Materials' 10.4% return.

Disruption Forces and Structural Evolution: The European Commission's July 2026 ETS review proposed benign structural reforms for cement producers, delaying free carbon allowance phase-outs from 2034 to 2038 while making free allocations conditional on verified local decarbonisation investments from 2031. Holcim is aggressively lowering clinker factors through innovative product formulations, targeting reduction from 70% in 2025 to 65% by 2030 utilizing calcined clay and limestone to reduce processing CO2 emissions by up to 50%. Low-carbon ECOPact concrete reached 31% of ready-mix sales and ECOPlanet hit 39% of cement sales in Q1 2026, with ECOPlanet calcined clay cement delivering up to 30% lower production costs while commanding green premiums. Holcim is strategically expanding Building Solutions to diversify away from cyclical, heavy-side assets, projecting 50% of group net sales by 2030 from 37% in 2024 with attractive EBIT margins of 10% to 20% and low capital intensity.

Plausible problems to monitor

Holcim's restated FY2025 net sales declined 2.9% to CHF 15,724 million compared to CHF 16,201 million in FY2024, primarily driven by the North American spin-off, representing a notable revenue contraction that positions the company below the peer median growth trajectory. While Holcim's Return on Invested Capital improved to 11.2% in FY2025, this remains below CRH's superior Adjusted ROIC of 12.1%, highlighting a persistent capital efficiency gap versus best-in-class global peers despite the company's strategic portfolio rotation toward higher-return markets (Section 2 – Financial Profile + Peer Benchmarking).

Holcim delivered CHF 2,154 million in free cash flow with a cash conversion ratio of 54% in FY2025, targeting maintenance above 50% annually, yet this conversion rate lags Heidelberg Materials' reported 45.1% in FY2025, though the absolute conversion performance suggests room for working capital optimization relative to sector leaders who consistently achieve conversion rates approaching 60% in consolidated markets (Section 2 – Financial Profile + Peer Benchmarking).

Outliers worth noting

Holcim's Latin American operations represent a striking positive outlier, delivering the highest operating margins within the group at 20% of sales while simultaneously commanding exceptional pricing power through highly consolidated market structures. This anomaly is most pronounced in Mexico, where the market exhibits an exceptionally high Herfindahl-Hirschman Index of 2,561 with Holcim holding an 18.6% market share as a disciplined challenger to rational leader Cemex.

The extreme outlier emerges in Ecuador, where Holcim controls 59% of capacity within an extraordinary HHI of 4,219, representing one of the most concentrated cement markets globally and dramatically exceeding typical sector concentration norms while being further amplified by the 2,365-store Disensa retail franchise network that locks in distribution channel access. When benchmarked against direct peers, Holcim's 18.3% EBIT margin sits comfortably above Heidelberg Materials' 15.6% operating margin, yet notably trails Buzzi Unicem's margin outlier of 27.4% EBITDA margin due to Buzzi's highly profitable US cement exposure.

This Latin American structural anomaly, discussed comprehensively in Section 5, demonstrates how geographic portfolio concentration toward oligopolistic markets generates returns substantially above sector base rates.

A second critical outlier manifests in the mechanical regulatory consolidation dynamic within European cement markets, where the phase-out of free EU ETS allowances is projected to force 25% to 30% of European clinker capacity to shut down, representing an unprecedented structural dislocation that will allow low-carbon leaders to capture significant market share as smaller, high-emission competitors exit. This regulatory-driven consolidation creates a powerful cost curve steepening effect, with efficient producers gaining a projected 30% cost advantage by 2030.

Holcim's positioning represents a statistical anomaly relative to sector peers, having achieved a 70% alternative fuel substitution rate in Europe during Q1 2026 with 14 plants exceeding 80% substitution, which translates into projected annual cost savings of CHF 180 million by 2030. When compared to Heidelberg Materials' more modest 43% thermal substitution target, Holcim's aggressive 50% global target by 2030 positions the company as a clear front-runner.

This decarbonization leadership outlier, analyzed in Section 10, operates as a gravitational pull mechanism that systematically advantages Holcim over capital-constrained regional competitors who cannot afford similar decarbonization investments, fundamentally reshaping competitive dynamics in a manner atypical of traditional commodity building materials markets. A third notable outlier emerges in capital efficiency metrics, where CRH's Adjusted ROIC of 12.1% in FY2025 surpasses Holcim's 11.2% return on invested capital, despite Holcim demonstrating consistent ROIC progression from 8.9% in 2021. More strikingly, emerging market peer Cementos Argos achieved a 17.0% Return on

Capital Employed, representing a significant positive performance outlier driven by Colombian cement demand recovery. Holcim's 54% cash conversion ratio in FY2025, while meeting its mid-term target above 50%, trails when compared to peer benchmarks, as Heidelberg Materials reported a cash conversion rate of only 45.1% in FY2025, suggesting Holcim maintains a moderate positive differential in converting balance sheet profits into tangible free cash flow.

However, Holcim's net capital expenditure discipline at CHF 973 million, representing approximately 6.19% capex intensity with a stated target of up to 4.0% of net sales on growth capex, positions the company as moderately capital-intensive relative to pure-play downstream solutions peers but lighter than fully integrated global majors. This return profile outlier, detailed in Section 2 and Section 9, reveals that while Holcim delivers industry-leading operating margins, its aggressive downstream Building Solutions expansion may temporarily dilute consolidated ROIC before projected synergies from high-multiple acquisitions like Xella and Cementos Pacasmayo fully materialize, creating a transitional performance anomaly relative to more disciplined capital allocators in the peer set.
