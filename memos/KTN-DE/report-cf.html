EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Materiality of Change

1.
Strategic portfolio transformation: Kontron is executing a decisive shift toward high-margin Software & Solutions, targeting 75% of total EBITDA from software by 2030 while aggressively restructuring unprofitable clean-energy operations through 500 job cuts and EUR 30 million in annual savings starting in 2027.
2.
Anchor shareholder realignment: Ennoconn's equity stake increased to 48.36% in August 2026 through a mandatory tender offer, fundamentally altering governance dynamics and unlocking EUR 40 million in annual procurement and supply chain synergies backed by Foxconn's global manufacturing scale.
3.
Structural margin expansion: The company terminated EUR 200 million of low-margin electronic manufacturing services, which lifted the gross margin to 40% and is divesting another EUR 75 million of lower-margin revenues during 2026, fundamentally resetting the profitability baseline above peer-median benchmarks.
4.
Regulatory-driven replacement cycle: The European railway sector faces a mandatory transition from GSM-R to FRMCS 5G standards starting in 2027, where Kontron holds greater than 50% market share in European railway communications, presenting a multi-year replacement-driven growth wave.

Key Forward Drivers (Ranked by Impact and Plausibility)

5.
Driver 1 – Software Segment Mix Shift (HIGH impact, HIGH plausibility): Software & Solutions already represents 54% of current group profits, up from 4% four years ago, with management targeting 75% of total EBITDA by 2030, leveraging gross margins of approximately 60% and EBITDA margins approaching 20% to structurally expand group profitability.
6.
Driver 2 – GreenTec Structural Turnaround (HIGH impact, HIGH plausibility): The CSP 2026 program eliminated 424 FTEs in H1 out of a 500 FTE target with management expecting annual cost savings of over EUR 30 million starting in 2027, which will eliminate the division that dragged reported group earnings down in first-half results.
7.
Driver 3 – Ennoconn and Foxconn Partnership Synergies (HIGH impact, MEDIUM plausibility): The partnership with Ennoconn increasing its shareholding to 49.4% in August 2026 is expected to generate EUR 40 million annually in medium-term synergies as parent company Foxconn leverages its procurement scale to resolve Kontron's component shortages.
8.
Driver 4 – Defense & Aerospace Expansion (HIGH impact, HIGH plausibility): The Aerospace & Defense division expanded strongly by 31.8% in H1 2026 with defense sales expected to exceed EUR 200 million in fiscal year 2026, capitalizing on NATO encrypted data communication and armored vehicle computing platforms amid rising geopolitical tensions.
9.
Driver 5 – CRA Security Software Monetization (HIGH impact, MEDIUM plausibility): The EU Cyber Resilience Act mandates strict secure-by-design updates starting in late 2027, where Kontron's CRA-compliant KontronOS embedded on standard hardware targets 80 million licenses by 2030 after securing its first major Cyber Resilience contract of EUR 20 million in H1 2026.
10.
Driver 6 – FRMCS 5G Railway Roll-out (MEDIUM impact, MEDIUM plausibility): The EU will mandate transitioning from GSM-R to FRMCS 5G standards starting in 2027 where Kontron holds a greater than 50% market share in European railway communications, though expert timelines suggest the latest standard publication is end of 2027 or 2028 with active FRMCS equipment only available around 2029.
