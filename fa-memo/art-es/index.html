Margin by segment, qualitatively: Automation (relays; SEG at an adjusted EBITDA margin reported above 20% under Aurelius, which I take as about 17% to 18% in FY2027 given the company's pro-forma H1 2026 disclosure) is above the group margin; Measurement and Monitoring is near or slightly above it; Network Reliability (reclosers, capacitor banks, tendered Latin American frame contracts) is below it. The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. Supply-constrained, not demand-constrained, through FY2027: backlog grew from an implied €273m (end-2024) to €355m (end-2025) and €417.2m (30-Jun-2026), cover rising from about 7.4 to 9.4 months of revenue; orders +15.7% in H1 2026 (book-to-bill about 1.22). Growth therefore follows the capacity programme (Measurement and Monitoring +20%, Tepeji del Río +25%, Mungia, Indonesia doubling, a Chinese plant from 2027). Build: volume +8%, +10.5%, +10%, +9%, +8.5%; price +3.5%, +2.0%, +0.5%, 0%, −0.5%; acquisitions +4.3 points (FY2026: SEG and the RTR annualisation) and +2.2 points (FY2027: SEG's full year); currency −2.5 points (FY2026) and −0.5 point (FY2027). H2 2026 +16% (organic about +11%, SEG +5.6 points, currency −1 point); H1 2027 +16% (SEG +4.7 points); H2 2027 +13%.
  • EBITDA margin. The step happened in FY2026 through the direct margin (37.3% in FY2025; 40.9% in H1 2026: price above cost, mix toward SF6-free, higher-voltage and automation products, efficiency). Because the backlog turns in about nine months, there is no long tail of old low-price contracts to roll off; from FY2027 the margin rises only through overhead re-absorption (structural costs from about 23.5% of income in FY2026 to about 22.4% in FY2028) and mix, then eases as price-cost turns negative when Tier-1 and Asian capacity arrives (2027 to 2029). H2 2026 at 17.6% (copper lag of three to nine months after copper +40% year on year in Q2 2026, new-plant ramp costs, SEG integration) against 18.4% in H1 2026 and 15.9% in H2 2025.
  • Depreciation and amortisation. Rises with capital spending of €43m to €45m in FY2026 and FY2027 (management guides about €40m for FY2026 on its own basis; FactSet's basis, including intangibles, ran about 13% to 19% higher in FY2025 and H1 2026) and with 5-year amortisation of capitalised development (about €7m capitalised in FY2025); H1 2026 was €8.7m. Excludes SEG's purchase-price-allocation amortisation (assumed €1m to €2m a year from FY2027).
  • Net financial items. About −€9m in each of FY2023 to FY2025 and about −€4.5m in H1 2026 (derived). Interest on gross debt (€143.8m at 3.6%, 79% fixed or hedged) is only about €5m; lease interest, factoring and guarantee costs, exchange differences and hyperinflation results in Turkey and Argentina make up the rest and do not fall with net debt, so the line eases only slowly as net debt falls.
  • Tax. 16% in H1 2026, 14.2% in FY2025; Bizkaia nominal rate 24%, reduced by research and innovation deductions and deferred tax assets whose recovery the company has pushed to 2028 and later (€24.7m now expected in 2030 and after). Rising toward 22% as profits shift to Mexico (30%), Brazil (34%), the US and Germany (SEG). The global minimum tax applies only once revenue exceeds €750m in two of four prior years (FY2031 on my path).
  • Dividend. 45% payout of my adjusted earnings per share (50% for FY2025; covenant ladder of 25%, 30% or 50% of net profit by leverage; the Strategic Plan 2030 will set policy).
  • Share count. 58,104,114 shares issued after the 1,010,101 new shares admitted on 26-Jun-2026 (issued for cash, so captured in Part 2, not in the share-count row); 51,677 treasury shares; 58.05m outstanding; weighted average about 57.57m in FY2026 and 58.05m thereafter. No buybacks; further float expansion is expected to be secondary.
  • Working capital. FactSet net working capital (receivables plus inventories less payables) was 13.0% (H2 2023), 17.0% (H2 2024), 17.8% (H2 2025) and 20.9% (H1 2026, seasonally high) of last-twelve-month revenue; inventories €108.1m at June 2026 against €84.4m at end-2025. I assume partial normalisation only (19% to FY2027) while growth exceeds 10%; reverse factoring (€33.8m) is assumed to stay in place.
  • Leases. Not disclosed in our files; leases implied at about €33m to €36m (FactSet total debt €180m against company gross financial debt €143.8m); payments estimated at €6m to €8m a year.
  • Acquisitions. FY2026: SEG Electronics €50.55m and Uptech Sensing €4.2m (35%), less a little cash acquired. Nothing thereafter in the base case, although the balance sheet (net financial debt about €50m, falling to net cash by FY2030) makes a further bolt-on likely; each €100m at SEG's multiple would add about 5% to 7% to earnings per share.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
