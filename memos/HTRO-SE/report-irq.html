What the call should settle, and why

The case in one line: a cyclical niche specialist in the second year of an upswing, with a lower cost base and a better mix than in its last cycle, whose next two reports are bounded and likely beats, and whose second half of 2027 either proves a Fiber Solutions margin floor near 10% through hard comparisons or reverts to its own 2023 template. Six judgements decide that.

  • Judgement 1, the North American refill. North American sales rose 80% in the third quarter to SEK 1,190m, 48% of the group; the chief executive put North American Fiber Solutions' organic growth at 13% (New from the reports), so much of the 80% is Data Center and the Superior acquisition. The memo's base is +12% in 2027, front-loaded. If management reads the surge as end use (new builds, middle-mile links to data centres, BEAD from 2027), the base holds; if any part was customers restocking, a 20% fall from the second quarter of 2027 costs SEK 0.58 to 0.65 of 2027 EPS (2027 EPS about SEK 2.1 to 2.2, down 22% to 24%). If the 80% is mostly Data Center, the refill risk moves from the carrier channel to the hyperscaler channel (judgement 2).
  • Judgement 2, the Data Center level. SEK 692m in the third quarter (+44% organic, 16.9% margin) against SEK 538m in the second; management expects the fourth quarter to normalise toward the first-half pattern. Our base is SEK 550m in the fourth quarter and SEK 2,640m in 2027 (+15% organic). Each 10 points of organic growth is SEK 0.15 to 0.20 of EPS; a fourth quarter below SEK 500m would signal a hyperscaler pause, above SEK 600m would lift 2027 toward the upside scenario (EPS about SEK 3.2).
  • Judgement 3, the Fiber Solutions margin and price. 9.4% in the third quarter, from 5.4% a year earlier, on volume. The memo reads the recovery as half structural (the cost base, SEK 120m of savings at full run rate since the first quarter of 2026) and half cyclical (absorption against a price headwind in United States HDPE duct). Each 100 basis points is SEK 52m of EBITA, SEK 0.17 of EPS; a stall at 8.7% costs SEK 0.12, a slip to 7% costs SEK 0.42.
  • Judgement 4, supply as the binding constraint. Glass fibre and selected connectors "were beginning to restrict growth", backlogs rising, the order book flat at four months through 17% organic growth, working capital out SEK 205m in nine months, cash conversion 72%. If the cap means revenue deferred and relief comes in the first half of 2027 (Redeye), working capital and conversion are the cost; if it means revenue lost to integrated rivals with locked-in glass (Corning, Belden) and relief only from 2028 to 2030 (Corning, AFL), it is a volume risk the memo's base case does not carry. New from the reports.
  • Judgement 5, the open seats. The chief financial officer moved to run Fiber Solutions on 1-Nov-2026 with an external search; Data Center has an acting head; the deputy chief executive who built the acquisition programme resigned in June. No announcement on either seat since 1-Nov-2026 (New from the reports). Both permanent appointments by the fourth-quarter report (5-Feb-2027) remove the largest internal risk; no names by then would, alongside Harsh Environment and supply, make a third problem at once.
  • Judgement 6, the margin ceiling. LTIP 2026 vests on an average adjusted EBITA margin of 9.8% to 11.1% over 2026 to 2028, against the 12% target; our path averages 10.8%. If management stands by 12% with Harsh Environment at 15%, our 2028 margin (11.3%) is low; if the incentive range is the real plan, the 2028 targets will be met only with acquisitions, and FY2029 consensus (SEK 4.18, one broker) comes down toward SEK 3.6.

Range A: scope for guidance and sell-side upgrades or cuts over the next four quarters (the fourth quarter of 2026 to the third quarter of 2027)

A1 ★ Data Center in the fourth quarter. New from the reports. "You said Data Center activity should normalise toward the first-half pattern in the fourth quarter, after project timing and seasonality in the third, including school work at Superior over the summer. Is our reading right that this means sales nearer SEK 540 to 580 million than the SEK 692 million of the third quarter, and does the margin fall back toward the first-half 15.5% as well?"
- Why: our base is SEK 550m; the one broker, Redeye, has SEK 582m. Each 10 points of Data Center organic growth is SEK 220 to 265m of revenue, SEK 45 to 65m of EBITA and SEK 0.15 to 0.20 of EPS. The memo's tripwires are SEK 500m and SEK 600m. Redeye ties part of the normalisation to schools reopening, which would make the third quarter partly a recurring summer peak rather than a one-off.
- Tell: "back to the first-half run-rate, underlying momentum unchanged" = our base and a likely beat on 5-Feb-2027; "lower, as some projects completed" = the lumpiness reading of the earnings summary and the first test of the hyperscaler channel; "the third quarter is closer to the new level" = the upside scenario; "the summer school work recurs each year" = a seasonal pattern to build into the 2027 quarters.

A2 ★ What made the third quarter's Data Center beat. New from the reports. "In July you explained the fall in the Data Center margin from 21% to 15% by high-margin projects that flattered the prior year by 2 to 3 points. How much of the third quarter's 16.9% and its sales came from one or two large projects, for example at Communication Zone, and how much was product timing that reverses?"
- Why: about three quarters of the third quarter's EBITA beat against the broker came from Data Center; the memo notes that one Communication Zone project drove much of it and that project concentration is not disclosed. Services are 39% of segment sales, so the incremental margin is only 15% to 25%. Management's own July answer shows the segment margin swings 2 to 3 points on project mix.
- Tell: "spread across many customers" = the SEK 550m to 650m quarterly range is a base, not a peak; "one or two projects, as in 2025" = the third quarter was a peak and the market's fourth-quarter bar should sit nearer SEK 550m, with the margin nearer 15%.

A3 ★ North America: Fiber Solutions or Data Center, end use or refill. New from the reports. "North American sales rose 80%, while North American Fiber Solutions grew 13% organically. How much of the 80% was Data Center and the Superior acquisition, and within Fiber Solutions, how much was customers building and how much customers or distributors restocking? How should we think about growth as the comparisons reach +44% and +51% in the middle of 2027?"
- Why: judgement 1, the largest in the memo: base +12% in 2027 on about SEK 2.5bn of North American Fiber Solutions; a 20% fall from the second quarter of 2027 is SEK 175 to 195m of EBITA and SEK 0.58 to 0.65 of EPS, and Clinton would be under-absorbed again. The memo puts that adverse case at 20% to 25% over eighteen months. A 13% organic Fiber Solutions rise is far less of a refill signal than 80%.
- Tell: "the 80% is mostly Data Center and the acquisition; Fiber Solutions direct under frame agreements" = the carrier-channel refill risk is smaller than the memo carries, and the North American risk is the hyperscaler one (A1, B1.3); any mention of customers building inventory ahead of price rises or shortages = the refill reading, and the cut risk from mid-2027 rises.

A4 ★ What the supply constraint has cost. New from the reports. "In the third quarter you said glass fibre and selected connectors were beginning to restrict growth. Without a figure, how should we think about the sales the constraint cost you: was it lost or only deferred, how long are your lead times now, and which products are tightest?"
- Why: the memo puts a glass or connector delivery failure at 10% to 15% over eighteen months, worth a quarter's SEK 150 to 200m of revenue; the earnings summary says the case has no reading in which demand is right and the revenue does not arrive. The shortage binds hardest in bend-insensitive data-centre fibre and multi-fibre connectors. Management has refused to quantify sales lost to supply, so the question asks for the direction, not the number; Jefferies says leading suppliers are booked into the first quarter of 2027.
- Tell: "deferred, the order book is building" = working capital and conversion take the strain, earnings hold; "some orders went elsewhere" = the integrated rivals take the allocation, a volume risk to 2027; "connectors, not glass" = Sumitomo Electric's 13 to 21 times connector expansion by 2028 is the relief to watch.

A5 ★ The Fiber Solutions margin in the fourth quarter. New from the reports. "Fiber Solutions earned 9.4% in the third quarter, with the SEK 120 million savings programme at full run rate since the first quarter. With higher fibre and transport costs now coming through and the usual winter seasonality, how should we think about the fourth-quarter margin, and is our reading right that the improvement this year came from volume and the savings rather than from price?"
- Why: our fourth-quarter base has Fiber Solutions above 9% against Redeye's 8.0%; the memo's fourth-quarter test is above or below 9% (the earnings summary sets an alarm at a group adjusted gross margin below 38%). Each 100 basis points is SEK 52m of EBITA, SEK 0.17 of EPS. The savings are now fully in the base, so any further gain in 2027 has to come from volume or price.
- Tell: "seasonally lower but well above last year's" = the cost-base floor of 8% to 9% holds and the 5-Feb-2027 beat is likely; "cost increases arrive before our price increases" = the earnings summary's first reading: the third quarter was the quarter before the pressure.

A6 ★ Price pass-through. New from the reports. "In April you said you aim to pass on cost increases in full so that gross profit stays stable in kronor, and in July that some contracts reprice with a delay. Is our reading right that this protects gross profit but not the gross margin in percent? Roughly what share of Fiber Solutions sales reprices with a lag, how long is the lag, and have customers accepted the increases you announced?"
- Why: the memo models pass-through with a one-quarter lag and calls pricing power nominal: real pricing exists only in defence connectors, subsea and, while the shortage lasts, data-centre hardware (15% to 20% of sales), worth at most 50 to 100 basis points of group gross margin in 2026 to 2027. The adjusted gross margin was 39.8% in the third quarter, from 37.5% in the second half of 2025. A rule of stable gross profit in kronor means that if fibre costs rise 10% the gross margin in percent falls, even with full pass-through. Management has refused to size the increases, so ask for the mechanism.
- Tell: "yes, the rule is kronor, not percent" = expect the gross margin to dip while fibre costs rise, with no change to the case; "most contracts index within a quarter" = the margin is protected; "we raise prices case by case" = the gross margin falls before it recovers; any sign that scarcity lets the company price data-centre, subsea and defence orders above cost = the positive form of the supply reading.

A7 The adjusted gross margin and submarine. New from the reports. "The adjusted gross margin of 39.8% in the third quarter: how much came from the Data Center mix and from the submarine shipments, which you said in April would add at least SEK 50 million against the first two quarters, and which continue only through the fourth quarter?"
- Why: the earnings summary argues the margin test was passed on mix and timing; the Q4 2024 level was 41.4%. Each point of gross margin on about SEK 2.2bn of quarterly sales is about SEK 22m of EBITA a quarter. The extra SEK 50m or more of submarine sales is about 2% of the quarter's sales at a margin above the group's.
- Tell: "mostly mix" = expect it to fall back toward 38% in the first half of 2027 without a change to the case; "mostly price and cost" = the margin has a structural element the memo does not credit.

A8 The order book. New from the reports. "The order book is about four months of sales, unchanged from the second quarter. Without the 2027 submarine figure, roughly how much of it is long-dated submarine work, and has the Fiber Solutions part lengthened or shortened since the summer?"

- Tell: "Fiber Solutions cover up" = demand still ahead of supply into 2027; "flat, mostly submarine" = less visibility than the four months suggests.

A9 ★ The lines below EBITA. "What should we model for the tax rate in 2027, after 29.6% in the third quarter and 26.7% for nine months, and for net interest? And how do you expect contingent consideration on JOWO and Superior to move, after the SEK 28 million revaluation in the third quarter?"

Earn-out revaluations and items affecting comparability add SEK 0.10 to 0.20 a year of reported-EPS noise with a negative skew, enough to turn an in-line quarter into a reported miss. The earnings summary flags "no cushion below EBITA" as live inside the beat.

A10 Working capital and cash at the year-end. "Working capital took SEK 205 million in nine months and conversion was 72% in the third quarter. How much is inventory built against supply shortages, and how much should reverse at the year-end?"
