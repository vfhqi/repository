Capitalization Balances and Composition

Under IAS 38 accounting rules, these active development projects are not subject to standard annual amortization until they are fully completed and brought into active commercial service. Consequently, a sudden termination or failure of these pending R&D initiatives would trigger immediate and severe write-downs directly hitting net income since they carry no prior amortization cushion.
