Outliers worth noting

1.
Subcutaneous immunoglobulin (Xembify) surge against a stalled incumbent. Subcutaneous immunoglobulins surged by 40.3% at constant currency in Q3 2026, far above the 11.3% growth of the wider immunoglobulin franchise. The share data are equally unusual: CSL's US subcutaneous volume share eroded from 82% to 70% while Grifols rose to 14% by mid-2025. A challenger taking this much share from a market leader with the highest gross profit per liter is rare in an oligopoly. [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 6, Section 7, Section 9]
2.
China albumin collapse to decade-low pricing. Albumin revenue fell 13.2% at constant currency in Q3 2026, after a 13.9% decline over nine months. Chinese albumin prices have plunged to decade-low levels, and regional hospital usage has halved in mid-west China hospitals since the start of 2025. This is a bottomtail result for a plasma franchise, and the body offers no firm date for recovery. [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 6, Section 9]

Leverage swing from extreme peak to still-above-peer levels. The Shanghai RAAS transaction reduced leverage from a previous 9.0x peak, and net leverage reached 3.98x at Q3 2026, versus 4.2x a year earlier and 5.1x in September 2024. The oddity is that, despite this pace, Grifols still carries a heavier debt load than its closest global pharmaceutical peers. The starting point was an outlier, and the level is still an outlier.
