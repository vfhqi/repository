EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 27 findings

Standard Chartered is executing a structural pivot toward capital-light wealth management and network-driven fee income, emerging from a decade of restructuring into a compounding growth phase that has delivered its 2026 return targets a full year early in fiscal 2025. The transformation agenda carries material execution risk from aggressive AI-driven workforce reductions, geopolitical overlays across Middle East exposures, and binding leverage ratio constraints, yet the bank has demonstrated consistent delivery against conservative guidance while maintaining robust capital buffers that support over 15% annual shareholder distributions relative to market capitalization.
