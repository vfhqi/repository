EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. Amadeus is a resilient market compounder whose near-term volume headwinds are offset by robust pricing power and high switching costs
  2. the structural bear case on AI disintermediation is fundamentally overdone, while the YES profile dominates on low inconsistencies
  3. structural failure modes are focused on migration complexity and geopolitical drag, rather than AI disintermediation
  4. Amadeus is structurally long the US Dollar, introducing margin volatility when the Euro strengthens
  5. competitive and regulatory friction from antitrust allegations represents a tail risk to market dominance
