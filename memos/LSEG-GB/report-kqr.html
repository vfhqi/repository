  1. Evaluating the concentration of financial drivers determines whether London Stock Exchange Group can sustain its double-digit earnings compounding over the next four years.
  2. The range of near-term earnings outcomes is driven by policy rates, unhedged currency translation, and the funding of large debt-financed acquisitions.

Management notes that the massive share repurchases of the past have allowed earnings growth to consistently outpace operating profit growth. With buybacks guided to slow down, LSEG must now rely more heavily on core subscription revenue acceleration and operational leverage to drive its financial targets.

This subscription strength could effectively offset interest rate headwinds, suggesting that the range of plausible earnings outcomes is less skewed to the downside than transaction-based models imply.

These defensive characteristics, combined with targeted pricing actions and margin expansion, support a highly stable double-digit earnings compounding profile over the medium term.
