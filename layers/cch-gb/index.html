Written last by the Investment Analyst for its group. The rating above stands for it, so it carries no number of its own.

The external forces support the earnings model and oppose the multiple. Demand is accelerating on a structural emerging-market base, the categories CCH is building (energy, zero sugar, single-serve, out-of-home coffee) are the fastest-growing in the sector, and competition has not dented a six-year share-gain record. For the Consumer: build a legacy base case on the algorithm (6% to 7% organic revenue, 20 to 40bp margin) with explicit 2027 phasing, and carry Russia and Egypt as separately quantified scenarios rather than averaging them into the base.
