Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Competitor pricing decisions in Spanish motor insurance directly dictate the underwriting margins and earnings recovery of Linea Directa.
1.
Primary inquiry: "Linea Directa Aseguradora (LDA-ES): a Spanish pure direct-to-consumer personal-lines insurer selling through telephone, web and app from a single site in Tres Cantos, Madrid, with no branches or brokers and its own repair centres (CAR); segments are motor (about 81% of gross written premiums), home (about 15%), health (about 3%, formerly the Vivaz brand, 50% ceded to Munich Re under a quota share to December 2029) and small pet, SME and mobility lines; Spain is over 99.9% of premiums. What is Mutua Madrileña's likely motor tariff policy for 2027 (it froze tariffs for 30% of auto customers in 2025 and 50% in 2026 and raised its motor combined-ratio ceiling from 95% to 97%), what were its motor combined ratio and claims trends in 2026, has it said what would end the freeze, and how have Mapfre, Allianz Seguros, AXA Seguros, Generali and Grupo Catalana Occidente said they will price Spanish motor in 2027, including any stated intention to return to volume growth once margins normalise?"

Strategic Significance and Earnings Transmission

2.
Critical exposure: Linea Directa relies on motor insurance for 81.4% of its premium revenue, making its profitability highly sensitive to competitor pricing. Because the Spanish motor insurance market is dominated by Mutua Madrileña and Mapfre, pricing freezes by these giants limit Linea Directa's ability to raise average premiums without losing customers. This directly affects Linea Directa's combined ratio, which must remain in the mid-90s to support earnings per share growth.
3.
Price transmission: Changes in competitor tariffs flow directly to direct-to-consumer insurers like Linea Directa through customer acquisition costs and retention rates. When competitors freeze tariffs, price-sensitive consumers are less likely to switch, which forces Linea Directa to increase marketing spend to sustain its premium growth targets. 4.

This growth occurred while the company reduced its combined ratio by 1.2 percentage points to 91.1%, proving that internal underwriting discipline can offset external competitive headwinds.

5.

These CAR workshops enable the group to bypass intermediary markups, reduce average repair costs, and maintain stable supply chains for parts. Strong operational performance in these centers directly protects the underwriting margin and insulates earnings per share from severe physical claims severity trends.
