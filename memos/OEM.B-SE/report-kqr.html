Core Question and Investment Landscape

1.

2.
Strategic transition: The entry of financial sponsor EQT as the controlling owner represents a pivotal shift for OEM International as it moves from family-based control to an aggressive private equity model. EQT acquired approximately 26% of capital and 73% of votes in late Q1 2025e to accelerate the company's acquisition pace. This transition directly impacts the 18-month to 3-year earnings per share outlook by driving a higher volume of M&A deals under the new ownership. Investors must understand how this aggressive playbook changes the underlying valuation and historical capital allocation strategies.
3.
Growth dynamics: Balancing strong organic growth with accelerated M&A is the primary operational challenge facing OEM International over the next 18 months. The company recorded an impressive 14% organic growth rate and a 40% surge in order intake in Q3 2026. However, sustaining this momentum against tough historical comparisons remains a critical variable for future earnings. A failure to successfully integrate new businesses could degrade historical return on capital metrics.
4.
Accounting discrepancies: Clarifying how different research houses report earnings per share to FactSet is essential because amortisation definitions distort consensus estimates. Redeye reduced its 2027 estimated earnings per share to SEK 5.87 on 30 October 2026 to incorporate the impact of intangible amortisation. If other analytical platforms omit these acquired intangible amortisation expenses, the reported consensus will remain artificially inflated. Understanding these reporting bases prevents investors from misinterpreting the stock's actual premium relative to peers.

Related Risks and Strategic Gaps

5.

A new board of directors has been appointed following EQT's entry to guide this expansion. Investors must monitor whether these governance shifts impact the decentralised operational business model of the company.

Ultimately, these strategic risks might outweigh the near-term benefits of accelerated cash reinvestment.
