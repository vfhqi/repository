EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Investment Thesis Cairn Homes represents a structurally advantaged Irish homebuilder executing a capital-light growth strategy targeting circa 6,000 unit completions over FY26-FY27, supported by a record EUR 1.89 billion forward order book and upgraded return on equity guidance of circa 17.0% anchored to conservative year-end debt to GAV of circa 20%. The company operates within a persistently undersupplied market where Ireland's population expanded by 66,900 to 5.5 million in April 2026, yet national completions of 16,679 units in H1 2026 track materially below the 50,000 units required annually to address chronic housing deficits.

Historical revenue growth of 29% in FY24 and 10% in FY25, coupled with operating profit expansion to EUR 168.6 million and return on equity reaching 16.6% in fiscal year 2025, demonstrates platform scaling momentum and double-digit profitability growth. The most recent H1 2026 results validated this trajectory with revenue of EUR 455.5 million representing a 60% increase year-on-year, 1,139 unit completions marking 61% output expansion, operating margin rising 140 basis points to 16.4%, and earnings per share reaching 9.3 cents achieving an 82% year-on-year increase.

Strategic execution encompasses Passivhaus apartment development at scale, EUR 50 million share buyback programs, and progressive interim dividends hiked 10% to 4.5 cents per ordinary share.

Change Materiality Assessment Leadership transitions remain orderly and performance-aligned with CEO Michael Stanley maintaining base salary constant unchanged since the 2015 IPO, CFO Richard Ball appointed in February 2024 with base salary of EUR 375,000 aligning with restrained fixed pay strategy, and planned hand-over of the finance area completed in 2024 by former CFO Shane Doherty. Board refreshment introduced experienced oversight through Alan Ralph joining as independent Non-Executive Director on 1 July 2026 and Pat Farrell appointed effective 1 October 2026, following departures of Non-Executive Directors Alan McIntosh and Gary Britton who served since the IPO in 2015. Strategic pivots towards capital-light land sourcing including options and joint ventures to reduce overall balance sheet capital intensity enabled the 6,000 homes delivery target across fiscal years 2026 and 2027 supported by over 6,000 social and affordable homes delivered or contracted to state partners since 2022. However, reliance on state partnerships exposes Cairn to policy shifts where margins and capital recycling will degrade if government funding cools. External market dynamics feature acute supply deficits with only 2,500 active listings in Q1 2025 driving asking price inflation back up to 13% year-on-year in March 2025, yet low market consolidation where Cairn and Glenveagh contribute only approximately 12% of total national housing output while developers completing fewer than 150 units accounted for 70% of 2023 completions.

Ranked Key Drivers (Forward EPS Trajectory 18M-3Y Horizon) Driver 1 – Seven Mills Development Scaling (35% impact, HIGH plausibility, HIGH confidence): The Clonburris landmark site holds circa 5,500 units representing one-third of the total landbank with volume projected to expand from 100 to 1,000 units underpinning medium-term group delivery targets. Driver 2 – State- Backed Buyer Demand Support (25% impact, HIGH plausibility, HIGH confidence): Concentrated suburban sales on first-time buyers utilizing durable state incentive programs including extended Help to Buy initiative underpinning deposit funding, First Home Scheme where approximately 55% of build projects qualify for funding, and coverage where almost 60% of new homes meet Help to Buy criteria.

Driver 3 – Croí Cónaithe Apartment Viability (15% impact, MEDIUM plausibility, MEDIUM confidence): The Croí Cónaithe Cities Scheme provides up to EUR 144,000 per apartment restoring urban construction economics, evidenced by over 150 apartment units rapidly sold following the Exchange Square launch and over 4,000 apartments in active pipeline including around 2,000 apartments to be delivered to state agencies via six ongoing forward-fund developments.

Driver 4 – Build Cost Inflation Control (15% impact, HIGH plausibility, MEDIUM confidence): Management expects build cost inflation of approximately 2.5% outperforming wider industry cost pressures with 95% active site costs secured for the year and forward pricing 50% locked for the next fiscal year. Driver 5 – Capital Return EPS Accretion (10% impact, HIGH plausibility, HIGH confidence): The new EUR 50 million buyback directly supports EPS accretion goals funded by stronger cash generation enabling attractive capital distribution yields of approximately 7% while net debt is managed to approximately 20% of gross assets.

Cross- driver risk: Excessive concentration on state schemes creates significant policy risks where completions and operating profit will collapse if subsidies are removed.

Validation H1 2026 results delivered outstanding financial performance with revenue of EUR 455.5 million representing a 60% increase year-on-year compared to EUR 284.5 million, total unit completions reaching 1,139 units representing a 61% expansion over 708 units, operating margin rising 140 basis points to 16.4% driven by operating cost growth capped at 7%, earnings per share reaching 9.3 cents achieving an 82% year-on-year increase over 5.1 cents, operating cash flow inflecting to a EUR 22.4 million inflow representing a EUR 141 million improvement, and management announcing a new EUR 50 million share buyback and hiked interim dividend 10% to 4.5 cents.

Performance by product showed net average selling price stood at EUR 393,000 representing a modest 1.6% increase, average suburban house prices reached EUR 434,000 up more than 4% year-on-year, H1 2026 completions dominated by apartment structures accounting for 63% of the total unit mix, and houses and duplexes comprised the remaining 28% and 9% of sales. Analyst reactions featured Deutsche Bank analyst Jonathan Coubrough maintaining a buy rating with a price target of 245p, upgraded FY26 EPS forecast by 2% to reflect the strong operational delivery, and management upgraded FY26 return on equity guidance to circa 17.0% from 16.5% previously.

Historical delivery track record demonstrates 2,241 unit completions in FY24 exceeding initial guidance of circa 2,200 units, operating profit reached EUR 150.0 million in FY24 beating the original guidance target of EUR 145 million, fiscal year 2025 operating profit reached EUR 168.5 million outperforming the upgraded guidance midpoint of EUR 162.5 million, and return on equity reached 15.1% in FY24 and 16.6% in FY25 surpassing original targets of 15% and 15.5%.
