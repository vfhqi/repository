EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Mips AB exhibits executive stability under CEO Max Strandwitz through an orderly CFO transition, though the upcoming October 2026 departure of CFO Karin Rosenthal demands close monitoring as the company expands its safety footprint beyond traditional helmet categories.

The company extended its SEK 2 billion sales target timeline from 2027 to 2029, reflecting prolonged post-pandemic retail bicycle inventory normalization, while the SEK 682 million Koroyd acquisition diversifies the portfolio into body armor, protective gloves, and industrial footwear.

Retail bike inventory has normalized to a decade low, paving the way for premium brand wins and volume recovery, though significant USD foreign exchange exposure drives EBIT volatility.

The July 2026 patent litigation settlement with BrainGuard Technologies eliminates a costly quarterly legal drag, unlocking substantial forward earnings power and structurally accelerating the path toward management's 50 percent EBIT margin target by 2029.

The adjusted EBIT margin expanded by 4.4 percentage points to 33.2 percent, with adjusted EBIT rising 50 percent year-over-year, demonstrating scalable operating leverage inherent to the ingredient brand licensing model.

The definitive patent litigation settlement reached on July 2, 2026 removes a key overhang, requiring a one-off USD 3.25 million charge in Q3 2026.
