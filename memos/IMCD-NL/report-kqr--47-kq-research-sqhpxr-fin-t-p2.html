EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

IMCD N.V.'s structural pricing power is critical for predicting its long-term valuation and earnings development under volatile macroeconomic conditions, with the company's lateral value chain and technical formulation capabilities providing high margin stability while remaining exposed to cyclical volume fluctuations. This comprehensive analysis examines how IMCD's gross profit growth is driven by the interplay between passthrough pricing spreads and true specialty pricing power across multiple operating environments, including supply shocks, deflationary normalization, volume downturns, Chinese oversupply, currency volatility, and regional demand divergence.

IMCD's replacement-cost pricing model proved highly effective during the post-pandemic supply shock of 2021- 2022, with the company achieving gross profit margin expansion of 0.60 percentage points to 24.9% in 2022, demonstrating that supply-chain constraints structurally shift negotiation power toward active specialty distributors. However, during the 2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis, IMCD deliberately avoided excessive markups to prevent downstream demand destruction, prioritizing customer retention over short-term profit maximization by collaborating closely with key principals to protect market share and preserve healthy customer relationships.

This strategic restraint reveals that true specialty pricing power is moderated by long-term commercial considerations rather than representing unlimited margin extraction capability during supply disruptions. deflationary normalization period of 2023-2024 validated IMCD's specialty pricing defense, with gross profit margin increasing slightly to 25.4% from 25.3% despite organic volume declines, achieved through localized margin improvement initiatives and favorable product mix shifts that successfully retained post-pandemic pricing gains rather than surrendering them to customer bargaining.

During customer destocking cycles, volume declines rather than price cuts drove negative organic trajectory, meaning gross margins remained structurally protected even as absolute gross profit growth slowed. This pattern indicates that IMCD's specialty focus generates defensive unit economics that insulate conversion margins from deflationary pressures, distinguishing it fundamentally from commodity-oriented distribution peers.

Technical approval barriers ensure that specialty distributors retain robust pricing power during volume downturns because alternative ingredients require extensive testing, making customers reluctant to switch suppliers simply to capture short-term cost savings. Specialty sales representatives negotiate pricing on an individual customer basis rather than utilizing automated indices, enabling sales teams to hold prices steady even when underlying bulk chemical feedstocks deflate, shielding the distributor's gross margin from rapid downward normalization.

However, semi-specialty products represent approximately 20% of IMCD's total group revenues, and severe pricing pressure in standard chemical lines can drag down the company's consolidated earnings per share expansion. This portfolio mix reveals that roughly 80% of IMCD's business demonstrates true specialty pricing power while the remaining semi-specialty exposure remains vulnerable to commodity-like pricing cycles.

Chinese oversupply severely disrupted regional distribution channels during 2024-2025, with excess Chinese supply entering Latin America because of trade barriers in Europe and the United States, directly undercutting domestic prices in Brazil and forcing local distributors to face immediate margin compression. The removal of Chinese export VAT rebates on nitrochlorobenzene-chain products in 2026 provided immediate pricing relief, raising the cost floor for Chinese exporters and allowing Western distributors to restore healthy pricing margins.

China's anti-involution campaign targets loss-making sales across ten major industrial sectors, successfully driving a recovery in domestic spot prices and structurally raising the pricing floor for global chemical exports. These policy shifts suggest that the acute phase of Chinese dumping pressure has substantially moderated, providing a more favorable pricing environment for IMCD's semi-specialty exposure going forward.

IMCD routinely quotes product prices in US dollars but invoices customers in local currencies in emerging markets, meaning local-currency-invoiced sales fluctuate directly with underlying exchange rate shifts, introducing severe transaction volatility that influences local top-line performance regardless of volume stability. During 2025, IMCD experienced significant pricing pressure in Brazil because it quotes in dollars and invoices in reais, with the group losing 10% of local sales on transaction differences while local warehouse and administrative costs remained fixed in local currencies, directly squeezing regional operating margins.

Second quarter 2026 organic revenue growth reached 10.3% in EMEA and 9.8% in APAC but lagged severely at 1.6% in the Americas because Americas pricing was delayed and relatively muted compared to Europe, directly restricting the Americas' contribution to consolidated EPS growth. This regional divergence demonstrates that operational currency mismatches and weak local demand can temporarily override IMCD's inherent specialty pricing power in specific geographies. - Earnings Calls IMCD's US operations have high exposure to cyclical coatings and construction, and because elevated borrowing costs froze domestic housing and construction activity during 2024-2026, demand for coatings, adhesives, and sealants remained deeply depressed, preventing regional volumes from recovering and forcing sales teams to prioritize margin protection over volume expansion.

Azelis grew 5.6% organically in the Americas in 2Q26 whereas IMCD only grew 1.6% organically because IMCD has heavier exposure to cyclical US industrial coatings while Azelis is more heavily weighted toward life sciences, demonstrating that regional product mix remains the primary driver of earnings divergence during industrial downturns. This contrast reveals that segment composition materially influences pricing power realization, with life sciences portfolios demonstrating superior resilience compared to industrial coatings during high interest rate environments. Azelis focuses on high-margin specialties where gross margins can reach 40% for highly specialized formulations, with pricing determined by product specialization and customer segmentation in a customized pricing structure that ensures margins on advanced molecules remain disconnected from initial input chemical costs, directly supporting long-term earnings per share compounding during down cycles.

Brenntag manages its business through strict operational split between Essentials and Specialties divisions, with Brenntag Essentials focusing on commodity distribution while Brenntag Specialties targets price and margin management, differentiating low-margin high-volume last-mile logistics from technically guided formula-driven specialty distribution. Brenntag Essentials passes through price changes quickly compared to specialty peers, whereas the manual negotiation process in Specialties delays price transmission but makes margins more defensible during deflationary periods, meaning Brenntag's commodity division is highly vulnerable to rapid feedstock price drops.

This peer comparison confirms that IMCD's specialty-focused model provides structural margin stability that commodity-oriented competitors cannot replicate.

Market consensus for FY26 expects organic sales growth of 4.8% and organic gross profit growth of 3.5%, with a stable FY26 gross margin of 24.7% and an operating EBITA of €549 million, reflecting widespread belief that IMCD will successfully defend its specialty pricing as demand normalizes. A downside scenario outlined by Jefferies envisions flat organic revenues and persistent margin erosion to pre-pandemic levels of 8.5% driven by rising competition and weaker regional demand, which would drop fair value to €70 representing a material 21% downside risk for investors.

An upside scenario where organic revenue growth accelerates to post-pandemic levels of 15% to 20% would unlock substantial operational leverage and drive dramatic EBITA margin recovery, increasing IMCD's fair value to €145. These divergent scenarios underscore that IMCD's valuation remains highly sensitive to the resolution of its true specialty pricing power across different macroeconomic environments. The research reveals several critical evidence gaps that constrain confidence in the pricing power assessment.

IMCD's management does not provide transparent breakdown of organic gross profit growth into price, volume, and mix components, only giving qualitative commentary during quarterly earnings broadcasts, preventing analysts from determining whether true pricing power or volume recovery is driving the group's compounding performance. Precise customer churn rates during destocking cycles remain undisclosed in public filings, with client defection highly concentrated in price-sensitive semi-specialty product lines, and without exact churn data investors cannot calculate the lifetime value of customers or assess the long-term impact on earnings per share.

Local regional pricing indexes for specialty chemical formulations are extremely difficult to track because specialty chemical sales are manually negotiated on an individual customer basis, causing analysts to struggle assessing real-time gross margin trends across diverse geographies. Future quarterly disclosures with enhanced price-volume-mix transparency and targeted expert interviews with former business directors would materially improve the precision of this assessment.

The analysis identifies emerging competitive threats that consensus forecasts may underestimate. Principal risks to the specialty distribution model stem from potential supplier shifts to direct sales or massive industry consolidation, and while currently considered unlikely, any shift in this direction would severely impact IMCD's volume development and trigger immediate downward revisions to EBITA targets. Digital platforms reduce information asymmetry and accelerate structural disintermediation risks particularly among smaller customer accounts, threatening the traditional high-margin opaque pricing model that specialty distributors rely on, and if customers gain direct price transparency through online channels IMCD's ability to maintain high pricing spreads will be permanently compromised.

However, Specialty distributors face limited long-term threats from commoditization or Chinese imports due to high technical barriers to entry and the criticality of supply reliability, and if these formulation barriers remain insurmountable IMCD will continue to sustain stable unit margins and deliver highly predictable earnings per share compounding regardless of macro fluctuations. The balance between these defensive technical capabilities and emerging digital disintermediation risks will determine whether IMCD's structural pricing power remains intact over the next economic cycle.

In conclusion, the evidence supports a medium-to-high confidence assessment that IMCD possesses genuine specialty pricing power across approximately 75-80% of its portfolio, driven by technical formulation lock-in, regulatory switching barriers, and opaque value-based pricing mechanisms that successfully defended gross margins through the 2023-2024 deflation cycle. The remaining 20-25% semi-specialty exposure faced material margin pressure from Chinese oversupply during 2024-2025, though Beijing's anti-involution policies and VAT rebate eliminations have structurally improved the pricing floor.

Regional currency transmission mechanisms and cyclical US industrial exposure create temporary headwinds that can mask underlying pricing power, but these represent operational friction rather than structural impairment. The key forward-looking uncertainty centers on whether digital procurement platforms and direct-to-customer sales initiatives by Chinese manufacturers will gradually erode the information asymmetry that underpins specialty distribution margins, or whether IMCD's extensive laboratory network and technical formulation capabilities will prove sufficiently defensible to sustain long-term earnings compounding at mid-single-digit organic growth rates.
