[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. Addnode is a cause-for-concern clearing case in a Swedish serial acquirer, not a growth case: its largest division resells and implements Autodesk software, Autodesk cut the channel's renewal commissions and multi-year terms in 2026, and a SEK 100m cost programme plus a renewal cohort that turns in 2027 can restore the profit that was lost but cannot make the growth rate material. At SEK 54.1, 75 percent above the July 2026 low, the shares already pay for a partial clearing and for no re-rating, so the only part of the return that clears the framework's 20 percent bar is a re-rating from about 12x to the sector's 14.5x, and that re-rating depends on the supplier not changing the terms again.

  • The change acting on the company is external and adverse, and the company's answer is defensive.
    • Autodesk is about 33 percent of group sales and, with Dassault, about 70 percent
    • In 2026 it cut renewal commissions (about SEK 20m of net sales a quarter, about SEK 80m a year), withdrew the multi-year discounts, and about one-third of three-year contracts are moving permanently to one-year terms, which is why Design Management's EBITA fell from SEK 162m to SEK 33m in Q2 2026.
    • The internal answer is a SEK 100m Symetri cost programme (half in 2026, all by 2027) for a SEK 28m charge, a pause in acquisitions with net debt to EBITDA at 2.4x against a 2.5x ceiling, and a slow tilt to proprietary software.
      • It is sized to offset the cut, not to add to earnings.
    • The only clear tailwinds (Swedish infrastructure and defence budgets, EU cyber and reporting rules) act on Process Management, about a quarter of sales and the highest-margin third of profit
      • Construction and German automotive, the larger end-markets, are contracting.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are still falling, and the recovery we model restores 2025 rather than extends it.
    • Q2 2026 organic growth was -11 percent reported (-5 percent adjusted), the EBITA margin 10.2 percent against 16.3 percent, reported EPS down 64 percent, and sell-side EPS has been cut 17 percent in six months.
    • On our path EPS on the brokers' basis goes from SEK 4.18 in FY2025 to about SEK 3.80 in FY2026, SEK 4.15 in FY2027 and SEK 4.50 in FY2028, about 9 percent a year from the trough and below the framework's 12 to 20 percent band on every horizon.
    • The one output clearly improving is cash: operating cash flow of SEK 425m in H1 2026 against SEK 170m, the end of the Autodesk working-capital trough.
  • The change has travelled only part of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • It shows in the company's choices and actions (the programme, the charge, headcount down to 2,988 from 3,064) and in the sell-side's estimates on the downside
    • It has not yet reached the key operating metrics or the financial statements on the upside, because no quarter has yet shown the cost-out or the organic turn.
    • The first test is the Q3 2026 report on 23 October 2026, the next the Q1 and Q2 2027 comparisons.
  • The price already assumes the partial clearing and no re-rating.
    • At 12.9x consensus 24-month-forward EPS (12.2x on our path) the multiple is at the 3rd percentile of its ten-year history, below the ten-year 'crash' band of 13.5x, and about 12 percent below the sector's current median of about 14.6x
    • The consensus already carries the 2027 turn.
    • The "two divisions cover the enterprise value" argument of July 2026 was made at SEK 31.70
      • At SEK 54.1 the enterprise value is about SEK 9.85bn and Process Management and PLM cover 43 to 70 percent of it depending on the multiple, so the bounded-downside argument is no longer available.
    • On reported EPS (SEK 2.87 in FY2025) the same price is 16 to 19x, which is not a trough
      • The cheap reading rests on the brokers' adjusted basis, and the add-back is only partly explained.
  • The return clears the bar over eighteen months only through the re-rating leg, and not over three years.
    • The modal case (the partial clearing, a 14.5x multiple on the earnings two years beyond each horizon) gives SEK 72.9 including the dividend by April 2028, 22.0 percent a year, and SEK 83.8 by October 2029, 15.7 percent a year
    • The re-rating is 19 of the 35 points of the eighteen-month return.
    • Without any re-rating the earnings and cash legs give about 10 percent a year
      • If the market prices earnings after capitalised-development amortisation, the return falls to about 10 percent a year on both horizons and the 'Modal return' Element would be a D.
    • The price below which both horizons clear the bar on the modal path is SEK 48.5.
  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.
    • It holds on the cost half, which is in management's control and has a delivered precedent (PLM's SEK 45m): we put at least SEK 80m realised by early 2027 at about 0.7, probable.
    • It breaks on the vendor half: positive Design organic growth in 2027 with Autodesk's terms unchanged we put at about 0.5, possible, so the full clearing is about 0.35, and a further unilateral move by the vendor within eighteen months about 0.25, with three precedents in two years.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Major CfC Clearing', and the fit is C.
    • The measurable conditions pass (the multiple at A, the estimate cuts at B)
    • The behavioural ones fail (the company reiterated a 17 percent margin target in the quarter the margin was 10.2 percent; the sell-side is 100 percent buy).

  • The case is therefore a fair, not compelling, bet on a supplier's restraint: a recovery of lost ground in a half of the business whose economics the supplier sets, priced for the partial clearing, with the return above the bar sitting in a re-rating that company evidence can earn only if the vendor stays quiet.

  • Rating = D, because the expected change is not material enough: the forces are net adverse with one defensive internal force, the outputs fall in 2026 and recover to the 2025 level only in 2027, EPS grows about 9 percent a year from the trough, and the modal return clears 20 percent only over eighteen months and only through a re-rating that depends on a supplier's policy

    • Over three years it is 15.7 percent.
    • The parts combine by binding: a 'Modal return' of C cannot lift 'Change Forces' at D and 'Financial Outputs' at D, because the return's only above-bar leg is the one the company's own actions cannot deliver.
    • Not C, because a C would need the outputs to be improving in the current year or the three-year return to reach the bar, and neither holds
    • Not F, because the trough is dated and the recovery path is mechanical in part.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The hypothesis: the market has mistaken a reporting artefact for a structural break.
    • Design's customers are still there (customer counts up year on year; Autodesk's own design revenue grew 18 percent)
      • The Q2 2026 collapse was SEK 70m of prior-year pull-forward plus a SEK 28m one-off charge
      • Underlying Q2 2026 EBITA of SEK 176m beat the comparable SEK 168m
      • The cost-out is SEK 100m against a SEK 80m-a-year headwind
      • So 2027 EBITA runs above SEK 1bn and EPS above SEK 4.5, and at 12.9x the shares re-rate to 18x on the first clean quarter.
    • The evidence for it is real: we concede the base effect, the customer counts and the cost-out.
    • We hold our view because the structural part of the change is not a base effect: one-third of three-year contracts are moving permanently to one-year terms (DNB Carnegie), the renewal commission cut recurs every quarter, Autodesk's direct share keeps rising, the Q1 2026 figures were restated, and management has said it cannot expect organic growth in Design.
    • A clean quarter restores the 2025 level
      • It does not make the growth rate material.

The case in 8 to 10 sentences (the thesis as tested), kept in full at both ends of the record (Part 1, E 3, BB 4)
  • This is a cause-for-concern clearing case in a serial acquirer, not a compounder case and not a demand case.
  • Addnode's largest division resells and implements Autodesk software, and in 2026 Autodesk cut the renewal commissions, withdrew the multi-year discounts that made customers sign for three years, and the three-year cohort came up thin, so Design Management's EBITA fell from SEK 162m to SEK 33m in a quarter and the shares fell 69 percent in a year to SEK 31.70.
  • The company's answer is a SEK 100m cost programme sized to the SEK 80m-a-year commission cut, a pause in acquisitions to bring leverage back from 2.4x, and a slow tilt to proprietary software
    • The public-sector third (Process Management, margins above 20 percent, every Swedish municipality) and the PLM division (recovering to a 9.4 percent margin on defence demand) carry the group meanwhile.

    • No quarter has yet shown the cost-out or the organic turn, which arrive on 23 October 2026 and in the first half of 2027.
