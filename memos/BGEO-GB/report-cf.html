Strategic Change Materiality The landmark acquisition of Ameriabank in March 2024 for $303.6 million at a highly attractive 0.65x NAV multiple represents the most material strategic change in the group's post-demerger history, establishing a dualmarket Caucasus banking hub spanning Georgia and Armenia. Executive transitions enhanced regional expertise with Giorgi Shagidze appointed Group CFO to focus on international growth opportunities, bringing deep regional banking experience as former CEO of Moldova's largest commercial bank maib.

The group underwent complete operational reorganization into three focused reporting divisions while executive management refreshed entirely since 2018, creating integration risks from rapid leadership and board turnover that could result in lost institutional knowledge and cultural friction during Armenian operations integration. Ownership structure remains highly stable with Georgia Capital holding 14.86%, institutional backing from Dimensional Fund Advisors at 4.67% and JP Morgan Asset Management at 3.90%, with zero activist presence recorded and unanimous buy sentiment from sell-side analysts including Jefferies and Deutsche Bank maintaining positive stances throughout multiple credit cycles.
