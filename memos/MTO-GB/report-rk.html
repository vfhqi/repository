The critical strategic verdict is that MITIE's operational fundamentals exhibit persistent structural tensions between headline growth metrics and underlying cash generation quality, with the OCS takeover offer functioning as a definitive valuation backstop that materially de-risks the standalone investment thesis but does not eliminate the risk that current operational momentum masks future margin erosion should the transaction fail to complete or face regulatory prohibition.
