The reasoning behind the inputs

Part 4 is the second set's reasoning as written. Where a later set changed an input, that input's figure in the tables above is the changed one and its reasons are in the changed-inputs file named above; Part 4 is not rewritten.

Four conventions behind every figure in this form. - The EBITDA margin is on FactSet's annual operating basis, which equals the brokers'-basis actual: FY2026 SEK 4,028m, 17.7%. It equals underlying EBITA (company EBITA excluding earn-out revaluation gains and restructuring one-offs) plus non-acquisition depreciation and amortisation (FY2026 SEK 469m, 2.07% of revenue, mostly right-of-use depreciation of leases). - FactSet's quarterly "EBITDA" line is really underlying EBITA. The four FY2026 quarters sum to SEK 3,566m against the annual SEK 4,028m. So the Part 3 quarterly margins sit about 2.1 points above the quarterly figures in the financials block, by construction. - The "Depreciation and amortisation" input excludes amortisation of acquired intangibles. That amortisation was about SEK 546m in FY2026. I forecast it at SEK 640m, 690m, 735m, 780m and 825m for FY2027 to FY2031. - Earn-out revaluation gains are excluded from every margin. They were SEK 86m gross in FY2026 and about SEK 79m net of restructuring in H1 FY2027. I assume none after H1 FY2027.
