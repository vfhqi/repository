1) Household & Personal Care, Beauty & Consumer Health This sub-sector displayed some of the most extreme divergences of the earnings season:

1.
Upgrades and Beats: L'Oréal delivered a blowout quarter with like-for-like (LFL) sales accelerating to 7.7% (beating consensus of 5.4%), driven by a luxury fragrance boom and skincare momentum. Upgrades and Beats (cont.): Unilever upgraded its expected volume growth to "at least 3%" following a solid 5.20% underlying sales growth (USG) beat.
2.
Cuts and Misses: In stark contrast, Reckitt and Haleon both cut their full-year top-line guidance. Reckitt missed consensus due to manufacturing constraints in North America Cuts and Misses (cont.):, while Haleon’s 2.60% organic growth missed the 3.20% consensus, dragged down by OTC reclassification issues in China and weak cold and flu consumption in North America. Beiersdorf continued to face structural headwinds, with its tesa adhesives division heavily impacted by semiconductor shortages in its Electronics business. 2) Food Producers Nestlé managed a 4% organic growth rate, driven by a strong Real Internal Growth (RIG/volume) beat of 2.80% (vs. 1.50% consensus) as pricing moderated.
