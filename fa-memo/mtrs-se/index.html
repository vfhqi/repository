The reasoning behind the inputs

Two notes for the model before anything else. 1. Working capital basis. The working-capital line is the company's operating working capital (OWC), which nets off customer advances: 6.5%, 5.2% and 4.2% of rolling twelve-month sales at Q1, Q2 and Q3 2026, against a 10% to 13% target. I estimate end-2025 at about 6.0% (about SEK 880m). FactSet's net working capital (receivables plus inventories less payables: SEK 2,335m, or 15.9%, at end-2025; 27.0% at Q3 2026) excludes about SEK 3.5bn of customer advances. If the model's 2025 base is FactSet's 15.9%, it will show a false 2026 inflow of about SEK 1.7bn (SEK 2,335m falling to about SEK 650m). Rebase 2025 to about 6.0% on the OWC basis. If the model must stay on FactSet's basis, use 25.2%, 22.0%, 21.0%, 20.5% and 20.5%, and add the advance float separately: about +SEK 2.1bn in 2026, then -SEK 0.15bn, -0.3bn, -0.2bn and 0bn. The OWC inputs carry the key 2027 to 2030 dynamic: the advance float stops growing and then runs off. 2. Perimeter. FoodTech (Speria) is kept consolidated in every year, as consensus keeps it, so the forecasts read like for like. The divestment is an overlay, given at the end of the guidance section below.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

Demand and revenue. - Group order backlog: about SEK 28bn at Q3 2026 (+178% year on year); DCT about SEK 22bn (calc.), about 3.7 times DCT's 2025 sales. - Group orders: about SEK 27.5bn in 2026, falling to about SEK 22bn in 2027 as capacity-securing orders normalise. The backlog still edges up to about SEK 32bn at end-2027. - DCT revenue is limited by capacity and components, not demand: Q3 2026 SEK 1,794m (+32% on Q2), Q4 2026 SEK 2,350m, then about +4% a quarter through 2027. DCT growth slows to +14%, +13% and +9% in 2028 to 2030 as rivals' capacity lands and warm-water designs reduce chiller demand (chillers are 37% of DCT's mix). - AirTech is +1% in 2027 (the 2026 evaporative-pad pre-buy does not repeat; battery demand at a floor), then +5% a year. - FoodTech grows about +4% to +5% a year. - Currency translation: about -6% in 2026, about -1% in 2027, then flat. How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
