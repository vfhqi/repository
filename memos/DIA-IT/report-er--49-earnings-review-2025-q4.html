The second is the adjusted EBITDA margin holding within the 32% to 33% range after the €8 million of deferred NES launch costs flow through, which would show operating leverage rather than cost timing.

The third is evidence from PLEX placements and flex-assay utilization that onboarding is shortening. An expert reported hospital onboarding times of six to 15 months, so a visible compression would matter. The fourth is China revenue staying within management's guided decline of €5 million to €7 million as the Shanghai and Beijing procurement changes take hold. The fifth is tuberculosis contract renewals that retain volumes without material price concessions.
