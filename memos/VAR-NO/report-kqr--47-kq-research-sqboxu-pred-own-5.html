Pricing Formulas and Indexation Structures

4.

These marketing agreements were established with Eni SpA as the sole off-taker following the exit of other partners in late 2022. The contracts operate under strict arm's-length commercial principles to ensure fair market value pricing. These structures are set for an initial five-year term with automatic ten-year extensions unless terminated.
5.
Flexible marketing: Var Energi capitalizes on Eni's extensive European trading platform to optimize its physical natural gas marketing. Approximately 50% of the subsidiary's natural gas production is routed directly through Eni's commercial infrastructure. The pricing formula for these volumes allows Var Energi to influence the underlying indexation based on regional market spreads. This commercial flexibility helps maximize realized gas prices during periods of high price volatility.
6.
Spot exposure: Var Energi maintains a high level of direct exposure to short-term European natural gas price movements. Historical assessments show that approximately 85% of the company's gas production is exposed to short-term spot and month-ahead indexation. This spot exposure allows the group to benefit from favorable pricing spreads between the Dutch Title Transfer Facility and the UK National Balancing Point. This pricing framework exposes near-term earnings to commodity market swings unless hedging is active.
7.
Contrary evidence: Var Energi reported that its third quarter 2026 gas sales did not fully capture peak spot prices due to contract lags. This pricing lag demonstrates that fixed contracts can limit near-term EPS upside during sudden price spikes.

Credit Terms and Receivables Factoring

8.

Outstanding trade receivables from Eni Trade and Biofuels SpA ballooned to USD 988.2 million as of September 30, 2026, up from USD 578.8 million at mid-year. This rising balance restricts the subsidiary's immediate cash conversion until payments are settled.
9.
Active factoring: Var Energi mitigates its credit exposure and working capital pressure through structured financial arrangements with external commercial banks. Under these credit discount agreements, the company sells and transfers the ownership and credit risk of its oil and gas invoices directly to banks. The corresponding receivables are immediately derecognized from Var's balance sheet. This factoring program represents a crucial liquidity management tool to support regular operations.
10.
Fluctuating balances: Var Energi's utilized factoring capacity varies each quarter based on the group's cash flow requirements and outstanding invoice volumes. Total related-party receivables stood at USD 1,135.2 million before factoring offsets. This credit framework allows Var to defend its dividend capacity during working capital bottlenecks.
