EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Vår Energi has undergone a fundamental leadership transition with Nick Walker joining as CEO in September 2023 after leading Lundin Energy through its successful sale to Aker BP, while former CEO Torger Rød transitioned to COO and Carlo Santopadre became CFO in December 2024 after 15 years at majority shareholder Eni. This transformation occurs as Eni retains 63.04% controlling ownership, creating a governance structure that provides deep technical capabilities while raising questions about alignment between majority and minority shareholder interests. The company has executed a transformational offshore expansion by agreeing to combine with BlueNord in July 2026, marking its first step outside of Norway, alongside a strategic asset swap with Equinor in June 2026 that acquired a 32.5% interest in the massive Peon gas discovery and extends Gjøa's economic lifetime from the early 2030s to around 2045.

The financial profile demonstrates significant momentum through record quarterly revenue of USD 3.7 billion in Q2 2026, more than doubling year-on-year driven by both higher production and higher realized commodity pricing, though average production costs reached USD 11.2 per boe, up from USD 10.4 in Q1 primarily due to foreign exchange effects and seasonal planned maintenance. The company has achieved remarkable deleveraging to a leverage ratio of 0.4x net debt to EBITDAX in Q2 2026, down from 0.9x in Q2 2025, while generating exceptional post-tax cash flow from operations of USD 2.1 billion and free cash flow of USD 1.4 billion during the second quarter. Production volumes have expanded through major project startups, with 31% yearon-year production growth reaching 376 kboepd in Q2 2026, though this achievement comes after the companyoperated Balder X project faced multiple postponements before the Jotun FPSO finally achieved first production in June 2025. 6 - Earnings Calls The investment case centers on five to eight ranked key drivers that shape forward earnings trajectory.

The BlueNord combination adds a +10% long-term production target with high plausibility contingent on regulatory approval, expected to deliver postmerger synergies of USD 250-300 million with medium plausibility dependent on refinancing timelines.

Gjøa optimization through the Equinor swap extends asset life to 2045 with high plausibility contingent on transaction approval. Additional structural drivers include unhedged winter gas exposure providing direct earnings leverage to European pricing spikes, yielding a 10.5% consensus dividend yield, and elevated quarterly distributions of USD 350 million targeting returns of 25-30% of CFFO after tax over commodity cycles.
