EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus EPS

Like-for-like consensus EPS was upgraded by 4.6% for the current fiscal year and 5.2% for the following year, as recorded by Bloomberg on 30 September 2026, although the report does not state absolute consensus levels.

1.
Individual forecasts diverge: Bernstein forecasts 10% EPS growth in fiscal 2027, while Morgan Stanley's base case uses proforma fiscal 2027 EPS of 163 pence.

Drivers Above or Below Consensus

Upside to consensus could come from cost savings of $540 million in fiscal 2026 against guidance of $300 million and from India, where Jefferies expects a 10% sales CAGR against company guidance of 4% to 6%.

Downside pressure comes from cash, where guided three-year cumulative free cash flow of $8 billion falls well below consensus closer to $10 billion, and from the view that consensus forward earnings estimates are still too optimistic.

Level of Internal Change

2.
Internal change is very high: Debra Crew stepped down as CEO on 16 July 2025 and Sir Dave Lewis took over permanently on 1 January 2026, CFO Nik Jhangiani will step down in 2027 to be replaced by WPP CFO Joanne Wilson, and North America leadership changed in March 2026. Structure and portfolio are also being rewired, with five regions and 23 country or cluster organizations, the sale of the 65% stake in East African Breweries to Asahi for $2.3 billion in net proceeds, and terminated distribution agreements with Moët Hennessy.

Main Drivers of Future Earnings

The most critical driver is the North American turnaround, where Diageo expects to reverse sales declines and return to market share growth by fiscal 2029. Framework cost savings are the second driver, with a new $1 billion target for fiscal 2027 to 2029, made up of $850 million from overheads and $150 million from supply chain.

Supply chain agility is a distinct driver, with $150 million in cumulative savings expected from targeted supply chain initiatives over the next three fiscal years. India and emerging markets are a driver, as Standalone India sales grew 3.4% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, led by a 7.1% expansion in the Prestige & Above segment. Premium beer and ready-to-drink formats are the fifth driver, with Guinness delivering strong double-digit organic growth in Great Britain and Ireland in the first quarter, though ready-to-drink products typically carry lower gross margins than traditional bottled spirits.

Driver Interaction and Plausibility

Plausibility is rated medium for the North American recovery and high for cost savings, India and premium beer, yet the cost-savings and North American turnaround must succeed together to expand operating margins, so the combined chance of delivering all five drivers is low.

Latest Financial Results

First-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales were $4,931 million, up 1.2% reported, and group organic net sales grew 1.4% against a consensus forecast of negative 0.4%.

3.
Regional surprises were mixed: North America declined 3.6% against a consensus of negative 6.7% and Asia Pacific grew 4.1% against 0.8%, while Standalone India grew 3.4% against a consensus of 6.4%. Diageo did not disclose quarterly operating profit, EBIT, or EPS in the trading statement, so the profit readthrough rests on the full-year backdrop, where pre-exceptional basic EPS was 165.3 cents against 164.2 cents.

Reliability of Management Guidance

Management reiterated guidance of broadly flat organic net sales and low-to-mid-single-digit organic operating profit growth, and it has a credible savings record, having over-delivered on Accelerate with $540 million against $300 million. Reliability on revenue is weaker because previous profit warnings in late fiscal 2024 show management frequently lacks visibility over distributor inventories, and some analysts suspect deliberate low-balling of near-term targets.

Broker Positioning

Five brokers and 16 named analysts cover Diageo, and Refinitiv data on 10 November 2026 shows a rating mix of 63% Overweight, 29% Equal-weight, and 8% Underweight. The constructive camp argues that top-line growth plus framework cost savings will drive 11% earnings compound growth through fiscal 2030. The dissenting camp argues that wealthier demographics are driving a structural decline in premium spirits spending due to health, wellness, and GLP-1 trends, and warns that retail depletions remain soft, with a US distributor stock unwind expected in the second quarter of fiscal 2027.

Fundamental Position Against Peers

Diageo's profitability is strong, with a 29.4% organic operating margin against Heineken's 14.6% and Pernod Ricard's 25.76%.

Outside Forces

Inflation and destocking remain the key external drags, as developed-market income growth has lagged cumulative inflation and destocking in Latin America and North America has depressed reported net sales growth. Fiscal 2027 also carries a pre-mitigation US tariff impact of $100 million, a $25 million foreign exchange hit to operating profit, and an effective interest rate rising to about 4%.

Activist Activity

No activist campaign is documented in the report, only speculation fuelled by a 20% valuation discount to the ten-year average, and a stretched balance sheet limits the traditional playbook.

Top Risks

The first risk is that North American volumes fail to stabilize, since Diageo's financial profile faces severe downward pressure if North American volume declines do not stabilize as guided.
