EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. Eutelsat is executing a high-stakes structural pivot from legacy GEO video to next-generation LEO connectivity.
  2. Top forward EPS drivers are highly capital-intensive and dependent on geopolitical sovereign funding.
  3. Margin guidance has been underwhelming despite inline FY26 operational vertical revenue delivery.
  4. Sell-side analysts maintain persistent Hold and Sell ratings due to massive capital expenditures.
  5. Compounding structural risks are threatening near-term free cash flow and long-term equity returns.

Materiality of strategic change

1.
Rapidly expanding LEO: Legacy video is declining while LEO connectivity grew c. 70% YoY to €297 million in FY26.
2.
Sovereign connectivity mandate: The SpaceRISE consortium signed the implementation agreement for the €10.6bn IRIS² programme on August 7, 2026.
3.
Critical fleet expansion: Eutelsat announced the procurement of 229 additional LEO satellites from Airbus Defence and Space for €1 billion.
4.
Balance sheet recapitalization: A completed €1.5bn capital raise reduced year-end net debt/EBITDA leverage down to 2.32x in FY26.
5.
Other side of the story: LEO services carry structurally lower EBITDA margins, meaning that revenue growth dilutes consolidated group profitability.

Key forward EPS drivers

6.
Primary volume driver: LEO commercial contracts are projected to drive c. 30% revenue growth in the operating verticals for FY27.
7.
Highly plausible volume: Commercial LEO scaling remains highly plausible due to robust backlog conversion into enterprise and maritime sales.
8.
Severe structural drag: Structural video decline remains the primary multi-year margin headwind, contracting by 14.4% in the fourth quarter.
9.
Highly certain drag: Direct-to-home video decline is structural and highly certain to continue due to OTT platform substitution.
10.
Growing sovereign demand: Major government contracts provide high-visibility cash flow support, highlighted by the €350 million NEXUS agreement.
11.
Moderately plausible support: Geopolitical demand is high, but dependent on fluid national defence budgets over the mid-term.
12.
Non-dilutive cash incentive: Expected US C-band clearing incentive payments will deliver $504 million in 2031 to aid long-term refinancing.
13.
Highly predictable inflow: Regulatory approval has been finalized by the FCC task order, making cash inflows highly predictable.
14.
Other side of the story: If government funding budgets tighten, the combined business model faces catastrophic capex overruns across IRIS².

Delivery and management credibility

15.
Stable revenue delivery: FY26 operating verticals revenue met guidance at €1,197 million, up 1.8% on a like-for-like basis.
16.
Clear margin compression: Consolidated adjusted EBITDA margin fell by 3.2 percentage points to 51.2% due to LEO product mix.
17.
Lowered mid-term targets: Management represents an honest-tracker archetype after lowering FY29 margin targets from 65% to above 60%.
18.
Divergent forward capex: Forward capex credibility is strained as guided FY27 spending jumped to €1.2 billion vs consensus €825 million.
19.
Other side of the story: Frequent shifts in capex guidance indicate that long-term capital requirements remain highly fluid and unpredictable.

Market positioning and activism

20.
Cautious sell-side consensus: Major brokers hold neutral ratings, maintaining price targets around €2.40 to €2.90 on high capex concerns.
21.
Differentiated peer execution: Eutelsat is a LEO first-mover, unlike peer SES which is prioritizing its Intelsat integration.
22.
Absent activist pressure: No active activist presence was detected in Eutelsat's AS library due to heavy state-backed ownership.
23.
Dominant sovereign block: The French state increased its core stake to 30% during the late 2025 equity recapitalization.
24.
Other side of the story: Without activist pressure to return capital, state owners will prioritize sovereign strategic capabilities over minority equity returns.

Major investment risks

25.
Legacy business contraction: Rapidly declining video broadcast revenue directly cannibalizes the highly profitable legacy GEO cash generation pool.
26.
Dilutive mix dilution: Growing LEO connectivity verticals depress consolidated margins below legacy levels of over 70%.
27.
Formidable low-earth competition: Disruption from SpaceX's Starlink threatens to erode commercial connectivity pricing and market share.
28.
Other side of the story: A simultaneous acceleration in video declines and Starlink pricing pressure would trigger a severe structural liquidity crisis.
