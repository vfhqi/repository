Critical warning signs: A failure to invoice and collect payments from the Irish project order represents a key indicator of prolonged capital tie-up. The massive data center order is valued at approximately SEK 117m and is scheduled for concentrated delivery in late 2026. If the Rest of Europe segment continues to show elevated receivables in upcoming quarters, it would confirm that milestone payment terms are delayed. This delay would signal that cash conversion will remain depressed well into the next fiscal year.
8.
Localized friction: Continued accounts receivable expansion at the larger operating units would indicate persistent collection challenges. Trade receivables rose by SEK 126m in Q3 2026, primarily concentrated in a few key distribution hubs. If CFO Daniel Warnholtz fails to implement centralized inventory and credit controls, local manager autonomy could delay balance sheet normalization. This internal friction would prolong the cash cycle and restrict group-wide liquidity.
9.
Other side of the story: While a prolonged working capital cycle would pressure short-term liquidity, OEM's net cash cushion provides sufficient runway to execute M&A without immediate stress. The group's disciplined pricing of acquisitions at approximately 0.95x sales ensures that capital allocation remains highly accretive. Therefore, even a delayed cash reversal is unlikely to permanently impair the long-term compounding power of the EQT-led expansion model.
