EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. materiality: Rémy Cointreau has undergone a significant structural shift with new CEO Franck Marilly and the launch of the RC Forward transformation plan, targeting €100m of gross Current Operating Profit value creation by FY29 through a newly created five-member Steering Committee aimed at accelerating execution. However, this transformation unfolds against severe cyclical headwinds, with group sales stabilizing at only 0.20% organic growth in FY26 while gross margins contracted by 370 bps organically due to provisional Chinese tariffs, pushing reported current operating profit margins to 17.7%, leverage to 3.22x ND/EBITDA, and forcing a 50% ordinary dividend cut. The strategic reorientation pivots from dogmatic premiumisation toward pragmatic volume recovery and affordability, though the transformation plan's financial delivery is heavily back-loaded with only 10% to 15% of savings expected in fiscal year 2026-27.

Recent Delivery and Execution Track Record

Group sales reached €935.3m, up 0.20% organically in FY26, while gross margin contracted by 370 bps organically to 65.8% driven by provisional customs tariffs and price-mix deterioration, causing reported Current Operating Profit margin to fall by 440 bps to 17.7%. Free cash flow rebounded to €53.8m in FY26, yielding an improved cash conversion rate of 27% helped by a €77.8m positive variance in trade payables phasing, though ordinary dividend was reduced to €0.75 per share from €1.50 in FY25 to protect the balance sheet.

Historical execution reveals severe pricing power erosion, with price-mix for the Cognac division dropping by -8.30% in FY26 as Nielsen data reveals that prices decreased year-on-year for 92% of Rémy Cointreau's SKUs. Net Debt to EBITDA ratio rose to 3.22x in FY26 from 2.40x in FY25, with Group "A-ratio" leverage guided to remain below the 3.50x covenant threshold for fiscal year 2027, though Morgan Stanley warns that a high-20s organic EBIT decline would risk breaching 4x debt covenants.

Forward Guidance Credibility and Management Archetype

Management targets a return to organic sales growth and slight EBIT margin improvement in FY27, anchored to the RC Forward transformation plan's €100m gross value creation by FY28-29, representing an impressive 17% Current Operating Profit CAGR at constant foreign exchange rates. Historical posture classification shows a transition from over-promiser archetype to pragmatic honest-tracker, as management acknowledged that performance fell below high ambitions during the past three years.

Executives emphasized that the turnaround will require time to implement properly across global offices, signaling measured expectations for near-term results. Sell-side consensus expectations have undergone massive downward resets of approximately 49% over the trailing 12 months, with cautious brokers trimming forward projections following FY26 results citing substantial technical foreign exchange translation drags.

Sell-Side Positioning and Rating Distribution

The analyst pool remains deeply divided, with rating mix per Bloomberg consensus showing 51.10% buy/outperform, 36.30% hold/neutral, and 12.60% sell/underperform ratings as of June 9, 2026, while the consensus forward P/E of 24.90x represents a 44% premium to European consumer staples averages, highlighting growth pricing risks. Bear analysts led by Mitch Collett of Deutsche Bank maintain a Sell rating with a €33 price target, arguing the stock trades on a generous CY26E P/E of 29x, a 55% premium to European staples, while warning that consensus expectations of 50 bps margin expansion in FY27 are optimistic compared to its guided -240 bps drop.

Chris Pitcher of Rothschild & Co Redburn maintains a Sell rating and a €37 price target, estimating that the Cognac region's aging stocks have reached an unprecedented ten years of annual demand, threatening price stability, with long-term operating margins forecasted at only 22% by FY30, falling eight percentage points short of company targets. Bull analysts led by Trevor Stirling of Bernstein Research maintain an Outperform rating and a €54 price target, arguing the stock is highly attractive trading at only 1.30x enterprise value to book value of inventory, while modeling a robust 14% adjusted EPS CAGR from FY27 to FY30 driven by RC Forward self-help measures.

Fundamental Peer Momentum Ranking

Rémy Cointreau sits in the lower tier of its peer group on the four-dimensional momentum matrix, with group organic revenue growth of 0.20% in FY26 trailing behind Campari's resilient FY25 expansion of +2.40%, while reported Current Operating Profit margins fell to 17.7% in FY26, tracking below Diageo's estimated profit margins, whereas Davide Campari expanded its adjusted operating margins by +60 bps organically to reach 20.9% in FY25.

Massive aging eaux-de-vie inventory holdings drag net working capital efficiency to sector-trough levels, with the group carrying €2,168.40m of inventory on its balance sheet, mostly maturing eaux-de-vie, as maturing inventory cycles in the cognac region have expanded to ten years of annual demand, though free cash flow rebounded to €53.8m, representing a 27% conversion rate, while Net Debt to EBITDA expanded to 3.22x in FY26, close to Pernod Ricard's leverage.
