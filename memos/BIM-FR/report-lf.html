EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This summary draws only on the findings in Section 1–Section 16. Under the formatting rules for this report it is written as plain paragraphs rather than bullets. Chief executive. Pierre Boulud has a strategy and commercial background, not a long P&L-owner one. He was educated at ESSEC, spent seven years in management consulting and held extensive commercial leadership roles at Ipsen and bioMérieux. bioMérieux appointed him Chief Executive Officer effective July 1, 2023. Founding family leader Alexandre Mérieux moved to Executive Chairman. The report flags that his experience running independent profit centers was relatively brief before he assumed the top role. Earlier-role financial comparisons could not be completed. The findings do not support the hypothesised Novartis or Alcon tenure.

His record at bioMérieux is mixed. Organic sales growth was 10.3% in 2024, above the 8% to 10% target range. Growth slowed to 6% in 2025. 2026 organic growth guidance was cut from 5%–7% to 3%–5% in April 2026. The 2027–2028 target was lowered to a 3% to 6% range, replacing the earlier 7% compound annual growth target.

On the positive side, Deutsche Bank reported a willingness to disclose more granularity on growth and margin drivers. Brokers are divided. Morgan Stanley's Aisyah Noor is the most prominent sceptic. Deutsche Bank's Jan Koch remains a supporter.

The audit committee chair, Harold Boël, is classified as nonindependent in the board table, although Section 16 describes independent directors as chairing both key committees. Takeover defences include statutory threshold-crossing restrictions, buyback authorizations and change-ofcontrol clauses in key debt contracts.
