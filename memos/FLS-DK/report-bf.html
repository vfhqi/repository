Appendix

Plausible problems to monitor

FLSmidth's operational profile reveals several areas where the company lags established peer medians and sector base rates, though none represent existential threats to the core business model. The most material underperformance centers on historical cash conversion, where the group averaged 76% over the long run, falling meaningfully short of peer quality standards and creating persistent investor concern about working capital discipline.

This conversion drag was particularly acute in Q2 2026, when operating cash flow turned negative DKK 84 million due to near-term net working capital build-up, highlighting the structural inventory challenges embedded in the service-expansion strategy. The scale gap versus category leader Metso represents a second persistent weakness, with Metso's Minerals segment generating 80% higher revenue than Weir and FLSmidth within identical verticals, limiting FLSmidth's ability to capture operational cost efficiencies and negotiate volume-based supplier terms.

Revenue momentum presents a third area of relative underperformance, as FLSmidth recorded a negative 10% revenue CAGR over the last three years compared to peer group medians in the mid-single-digit positive range, reflecting the deliberate strategic pruning of low-margin legacy backlogs but nonetheless signaling structural volume pressure during the transition period. Earnings quality emerges as a fourth technical weakness, with DNB Carnegie noting that 22% of historical adjusted EBITA was amortized versus just 8% at Metso, indicating heavier historical acquisition-related intangible loads that reduce through-cycle earnings durability. Finally, sustainability procurement momentum has decelerated, as spend with science-based target validated suppliers decreased by 1.6 percentage points in H1 2026, moving the company away from rather than toward its ambitious 30% procurement target and creating potential reputational risk with ESG-focused mining customers who increasingly audit their equipment suppliers' climate credentials.
