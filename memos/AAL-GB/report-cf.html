EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 36 findings

Anglo American's executive renewal consolidates operational expertise ahead of the Anglo Teck combination, though board diversity temporarily eroded and senior ranks thinned during transition. The proposed all-share merger with Teck Resources creates a global copper-focused powerhouse targeting USD 800 million in pre-tax annual synergies, though China SAMR antitrust clearance remains the sole outstanding regulatory milestone.

Structural electrification trends support demand, yet Chinese real estate drag and severe South American water scarcity constrain supply dynamics. Net debt declined to USD 8.2 billion at H1 2026, supported by 85% cash conversion and tightly rationed capital expenditure guided to USD 3.2 billion for 2026. Historical earnings transitioned from cyclical peak in FY21 to depressed trough levels in FY25, severely impacted by USD 6.8 billion in cumulative De Beers and Woodsmith impairments.

H1 2026 results beat consensus, with underlying EBITDA reaching USD 4,002 million (+35% year-on-year) and copper unit costs falling 21% to 136 c/lb, though by-product credits remain highly volatile. Management exhibits conservative sandbagging, consistently enabling operational outperformance, yet statutory delivery remains marred by severe non-core asset write-downs and prolonged project execution delays.
