EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 45 findings

Section summary
  1. HELLENiQ Energy delivered exceptional Q2/1H 2026 results driven by geopolitical disruption in Middle East crude flows, successfully navigating Hormuz supply shocks while capturing record middle distillate crack spreads and executing the largest scheduled maintenance turnaround in company history. The late-February 2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis disrupted Middle East crude and product transits while total group investments surged to €407 million in 1H26, driven by planned heavy maintenance cycles, with nearly €250 million consumed by the crucial Aspropyrgos refinery turnaround. The strategic pivot to Norwegian and American crude grades combined with flexible maintenance deferral at Thessaloniki positions the company to extend cyclical margin capture through 2027. However, structural renewable capacity delays and persistent Greek grid curtailments continue to constrain the energy transition timeline, while concentrated ownership under Paneuropean Oil (40.4%) and GROWTHFUND (31.2%) eliminates any meaningful activist catalyst. The equity story represents a cyclical cash-cow funding a protracted green utility transformation under an honest-tracker management archetype that consistently delivers financial beats during refining upturns but faces execution headwinds on clean energy milestones.
