EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 28 findings

Argenx SE has undergone a transformational commercial inflection over the last twelve months, driven by the rapid global scaling of Vyvgart (efgartigimod) across multiple neuromuscular indications. The company transitioned from a founder-led organization to a commercially focused biopharma under CEO Karen Massey, while simultaneously expanding its pipeline through the $2.2 billion acquisition of Forte Biosciences and advancing five registrational-stage clinical programs.

Revenue growth reached 60% year-over-year in Q2 2026, with product sales of $1.5 billion and operating margins inflecting to 32% –approaching large-cap benchmarks despite being at half the revenue scale. The company holds $5.2 billion in cash, enabling aggressive reinvestment into clinical development while maintaining zero debt. However, this hyper-growth trajectory faces material headwinds including severe payer step-therapy mandates that restrict market access, one-time distributor benefits masking flat secondary volumes in Japan, and uncontrolled clinical trial expense inflation that could compress margins if CIDP uptake slows.
