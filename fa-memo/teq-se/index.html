The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Basis: EBITDA and EBITA are on FactSet's operating basis, which excludes contingent-consideration (earn-out) revaluations; Teqnion books those inside its own EBITA (18.8 MSEK of gains in January to September 2026, 34.5 MSEK in 2025). Net financial items exclude currency revaluation of sterling liabilities (about -30 MSEK in January to September 2026) and include discount unwinding on earn-outs (about 9 to 10 MSEK a year). Both excluded items are modelled at zero.
  • FY2026: nine months actual (revenue 1,484.4; FactSet EBITDA 245.9, 16.6%) plus Q4 2026 revenue of about 522 (+12.5%: about 69 MSEK of acquired revenue, organic about -2%, currency about -1%) at an EBITDA margin of 14.5% (Q4 is the weakest quarter; the clean Q4 of the restructured portfolio is untested).
  • Revenue: carry-over of 2026 deals (about 144 MSEK in 2027); new deals of 250, 270, 280 and 290 MSEK of annual revenue in 2027 to 2030, about 45% recognised in the year of the deal; organic growth about +0.5% (2027, price about +2%, volume and last exits about -1.5%), +2% (2028), +2.5% (2029 to 2030); currency about -1% in 2027 (a modestly stronger krona), zero after.
  • Margin: Väst mix about +0.3 points a year; organic drop-through of about 35% on volume; price offsets wage and input inflation with a three- to six-month lag (no net margin from price); central costs rise to about 2.5% of sales for controls, internal audit and sustainability reporting; a haircut of 0.2 to 0.4 points for unit failures, which have occurred every year since 2024.
  • D&A: about 3.6% of sales (mostly right-of-use and plant depreciation), excluding acquired-intangible amortisation (about 8 MSEK in 2026 rising to about 16 MSEK in 2030).
  • Net financial items: 4.0% (2027) and 4.25% (2028 onwards) on gross bank debt, which rises from about 810 MSEK to about 1,150 MSEK; cash of about 180 MSEK earning about 1.5%; lease interest about 4.5%; discount unwinding about 9 MSEK a year. A 1 point rate rise costs 8 to 10 MSEK before tax.
  • Tax: Sweden 20.6% and UK 25% weighted by profit, plus non-deductible parent costs: 23.0% rising to 23.5% as Väst grows.
  • Cash flow: capital spending about 1% of sales; working capital about 23% to 24% of sales (year-end balances are seasonally low; 24.8% at Q3 2026); lease payments (principal) about 2.9% of sales (53.2 MSEK in 2025); acquisitions less disposals include upfront payments for new deals (paid multiple rising from about 5.5x to 6.25x EV/EBITA, about 80% upfront) plus about 55 to 65 MSEK a year of earn-out and deferred payments on earlier deals (55.5 MSEK paid in January to September 2026). FY2026 uses FactSet's nine-month figure of -230 MSEK plus about -60 MSEK for Q4; whether FactSet's line includes the earn-out payments is unverified.
  • Model caution on working capital: because the working-capital input is a percentage of total revenue, the model will charge the working capital of acquired companies as an operating outflow even though it is paid for inside the acquisition price. My free cash flow excludes that acquired working capital; the model's free cash flow will therefore be about 45 to 65 MSEK a year lower than mine unless the Financial Analyst nets it out.
  • Leverage check: net debt (company definition, excluding leases and earn-outs) about 600, 700, 800, 885 and 965 MSEK at the end of 2026 to 2030, about 1.7 to 1.9 times EBITDA; gross bank debt approaches the 1,000 MSEK facility (agreed early 2026 for three years) around its 2029 renewal.
  • Share count: 17.17 million throughout; no dividends; no buybacks; warrants (55,500 exercisable in 2028 at 255.68 SEK, up to 85,000 in 2029) are issued for cash and would add at most about 0.8% and about 14 MSEK, not modelled.

The company's guidance, and how your inputs compare with it

Teqnion gives no annual guidance and FactSet holds no consensus (zero brokers). Its targets are a pyramid: (1) EPS doubled every five years; (2) net debt below 2.5 times EBITDA; (3) EBITA margin above 9%. - Margin floor: my adjusted EBITA margin of 12.4% to 13.8% clears the 9% floor in every year (company-reported EBITA, including earn-out gains, would be higher still). - Leverage ceiling: my net debt runs at about 1.7 to 1.9 times EBITDA (company definition), inside the 2.5 times ceiling; including the roughly 200 MSEK of contingent considerations it is about 2.5 times in 2026, falling to about 2.1 times. - EPS doubling: on the company's rolling arithmetic the markers are 9.86 SEK (FY2026), 13.68 (FY2027), 15.10 (FY2028), 11.14 (FY2029) and 11.46 (FY2030). My reported EPS of about 8.7, 10.4, 12.1, 14.1 and 16.4 misses the first three markers and beats the last two comfortably, because the comparison years (2022 to 2023 peaks, 2024 to 2025 troughs) swing. - Informal commentary: "no clear trend shift" in order books, margins now "sufficient", more unprofitable units still to exit, working capital lagging the income statement while acquisitions continue. My inputs are consistent with this: flat organic growth in H1 2027, no further margin step beyond mix. - The only past numerical suggestion: rolling EPS "might" reach about 10.13 SEK; it reached 9.45, and on my figures falls to about 8.7 at the 13 February 2027 report, rises to about 9.8 by Q3 2027, and first exceeds 10.13 at the FY2027 report in February 2028 (about 10.4).

The bridge from the company's own reported figures to the basis consensus uses

There is no consensus, so the bridge that matters is from Teqnion's reported figures to the adjusted basis of these inputs (which matches FactSet's operating EBITDA and adjusted EBIT lines). - EBITA: company EBITA, less contingent-consideration revaluation gains booked as other operating income, equals adjusted EBITA. January to September 2026: 214.2 less 18.8 equals about 195.4 (13.2% against a reported 14.4%). FY2025: 203.1 less 34.5 equals 168.6 (FactSet's line implies about 160, removing a further few MSEK of items it treats as unusual). - EBITDA: company EBITDA (271.0 MSEK in 2025) less the same gains is close to FactSet's operating EBITDA (228 MSEK in 2025; 245.9 MSEK in January to September 2026). - PBT: reported PBT, plus currency revaluation losses on sterling liabilities (or less gains), less earn-out revaluation gains, plus amortisation of acquired intangibles, equals adjusted PBT. Q3 2026: the company's own bridge is reported EBT of 47 MSEK plus 16 MSEK of currency headwind less 5 MSEK of earn-out reversals, giving 57 MSEK underlying (before adding back about 2 MSEK of amortisation). - EPS for FY2026: adjusted 9.56 SEK against reported about 8.6 to 8.8 SEK. The bridge in after-tax SEK per share: earn-out gains about +1.1; currency revaluation about -1.5 to -1.7; acquired-intangible amortisation about -0.35; tax effects of these items about -0.1 to +0.1. - EPS from FY2027: reported equals adjusted less about 0.45 to 0.7 SEK of amortisation, plus any currency revaluation (about ±0.2 to 0.25 SEK per 1% move in GBP/SEK) and any earn-out gains. - Free cash flow: the company's "free cash flow excluding acquisitions" (221 MSEK rolling at Q3 2026; 131.1 MSEK January to September 2026) appears to be before lease principal; my figure deducts lease payments, so it is about 55 to 60 MSEK a year lower on the same underlying cash. How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
