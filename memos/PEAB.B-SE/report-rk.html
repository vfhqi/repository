Section 1 – Published Short-Seller Reports

Section summary
  1. Peab AB has avoided targeted activist short-seller campaigns, though it remains a primary vehicle for broad Nordic residential short-covering cycles and faces regulatory risk in Finland

Macro short interest dynamics

1.
Nordic housing squeeze: Peab was heavily targeted by short sellers during the 2023-2024 Nordic residential downturn as a proxy for Sweden's weak housing market. Short covering rallies: the stock experienced substantial upward volatility driven by short covering following interest rate pivots. Consensus expectations gap: recovery expectations in residential development remain overly optimistic relative to slow-moving underlying market metrics. Delayed earnings flow-through: long project lead times suggest that any stabilization in residential sales will take multiple quarters to lift earnings.

Finnish competition compliance audit

2.
Regulatory probe initiated: the Competition and Consumer Authority in Finland launched an audit of Peab Industri Oy in May 2025. Anticompetitive market focus: the investigation targets potential prohibited cooperation among competitors in the Finnish asphalt paving market. No intermediate findings: the Finnish regulator's compliance audit remains active as of mid-2026 without any formal ruling or fine. Severe downside penalties: compliance failures in this probe carry risks of material fines, damaged customer trust, and exclusion from public procurements. Competitor cooperation: direct rivals such as GRK Infra Oyj have also confirmed they are fully cooperating with the Finnish authority's ongoing asphalt inspections.

Absence of activist reports

3.
No dedicated campaigns: no named activist short-seller report has been published specifically targeting Peab's accounting or corporate governance. Clean compliance record: the group maintains a zero-tolerance policy for anticompetitive conduct and has fully cooperated with all regulatory inquiries. Bull rebuttal: the absence of dedicated short reports indicates that bears are trading Peab purely on macro cyclicality rather than structural or accounting fraud.
