Unmapped currency hedging: Granular details regarding the combined entity's centralized hedging ratios for highly volatile African currencies like the Nigerian naira are excluded from public reports, meaning analysts cannot accurately model near-term translation risks without monitoring the next formal financial statements in late 2026, according to Bernstein's 27 March 2026 report.
