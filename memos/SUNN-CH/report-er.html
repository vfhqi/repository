Section summary
  1. Sunrise Communications AG operates as Switzerland's leading challenger telecommunications

2.
Corporate profile and operational footprint: Sunrise Communications AG is the second-largest full-service telecommunications provider in Switzerland, delivering mobile, broadband, fixed-line telephony, and IPTV services to approximately 5.4 million customer connections. The company operates a dual-infrastructure network consisting of a national mobile network covering 4G and 5G/5G Standalone technologies, alongside a hybrid fixed footprint combining owned HFC cable with wholesale FTTH access
3.
Market positioning and segment structure: Sunrise operates as the primary market challenger to statecontrolled incumbent Swisscom, holding approximately 31% market share in TV, 28% in broadband, and 26% in mobile services. The business is managed across three primary reportable segments: Residential Customers (B2C), Business Customers & Wholesale (B2B), and Infrastructure & Support Functions
4.
Multi-brand consumer strategy: The consumer segment is driven by a multi-brand commercial framework where the main brand, Sunrise, targets quality-sensitive premium customers. To capture value across all market tiers, Sunrise deploys dedicated flanker brands including yallo (smart-shopper segment), swype (appbased digital), Lebara, and newly launched CHmobile (budget segment)
5.
Corporate history and 2024 spin-off: Originally formed in 2001 through the merger of diAx and NewTelco, Sunrise was acquired in November 2020 by Liberty Global through its Swiss subsidiary UPC Schweiz for CHF 6.8 billion. On November 8, 2024, Liberty Global completed a tax-free spin-off of the combined entity to its shareholders, re-listing Sunrise on the SIX Swiss Exchange under the ticker symbol SUNN
6.
Three-year fundamental revenue trajectory: Group revenue showed gradual top-line stabilization across the 3-year historical period, printing CHF 3,035.2 million in FY23, CHF 3,018.0 million (-0.6% YoY) in FY24, and CHF 2,983.4 million (-1.1% YoY) in FY25. B2C subscription revenue headwinds from legacy UPC customer migrations were partially offset by sustained expansion in B2B service revenues

Earnings and cash flow trends: Adjusted EBITDAaL remained stable near CHF 1.0 billion, reading CHF 1,043.6 million in FY23, CHF 1,023.0 million in FY24, and CHF 1,008.7 million in FY25.
