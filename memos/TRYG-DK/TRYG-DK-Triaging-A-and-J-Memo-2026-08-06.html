B.1 Business Description 9 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Scale and footprint. Tryg is the largest non-life insurer in Scandinavia, handling 2.1 million claims annually for 6 million clients across Denmark, Norway and Sweden
2.
Segment mix. Private lines, motor, contents, house, accident, travel, pet and health, are 68% of insurance revenue; Commercial is 32%, with Private Denmark alone roughly 30% of group revenue
3.
Geography. Denmark contributes approximately 50% of group revenue, Sweden 30% and Norway the remaining 20%, spreading localised weather and claims volatility
4.
Market position. Tryg is number one in Denmark at 24.4% share, number three in Sweden at 16.5% (via Trygg-Hansa) and fourth in Norway at 12.9%, a post-RSA Scandinavian non-life share of approximately 18%
5.
Earnings model. The model is underwriting-centric, not investment-led, technical results have historically contributed around 90% of total pre-tax profits, with the free portfolio de-risked to 90% covered bonds and cash
6.
Financial character. FY2025 combined ratio was 80.3%, insurance service result DKK 7.9bn, and return on own funds 40.3-40.6% (two source figures, see F.2), against a Solvency II ratio of 192% in Q1 2026
7.
Ownership structure. TryghedsGruppen smba owns 49.8% of outstanding shares excluding treasury stock, recycling dividend income into a member premium rebate for Danish policyholders
8.
Capital returns. Total dividend of DKK 8.20 per share for 2025 (82% payout), a DKK 1bn extraordinary buyback and DKK 17-18bn of distributions targeted over 2025-2027
9.
Investment style implication. The profile reads as a defensive, low-beta capital-return compounder rather than a growth or investment-income story, anchored on cost leadership and an oligopoly market structure
