Section summary

Morgan Stanley expects these efficiencies, alongside organic growth, to help Croda rebound to 20.9% in 2028 and restore normalised operating leverage.

Strategic execution: Specialty ingredient companies can successfully deliver post-cycle margin targets when they combine cost discipline with aggressive portfolio rationalization rather than waiting for volume recoveries. The historical precedents of Elementis and Croda show that reviving profitable volume drop-through is most achievable when management actively prunes low-margin volumes. Consequently, Croda's ability to hit its 20% margin target by fiscal year 2028 depends on shifting its product mix back toward premium beauty actives and pharma ingredients.
12.
Other side of the story: Although several peers have successfully met margin targets through self-help, achieving a margin above 20% remains highly vulnerable to prolonged volume weakness. If underutilized shared facilities continue to suffer from fixed-cost dilution, cost-saving tranches alone will be insufficient to protect margins against persistent input inflation.
