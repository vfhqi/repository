EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. the direct-to-consumer digital model avoids high branch costs and broker commissions, driving superior operational efficiency compared to traditional intermediaries.

Linea Directa Aseguradora operates a pure direct-to-consumer insurance model without any physical branch network or traditional broker intermediaries, centralizing all customer contact points at its Tres Cantos headquarters and thereby avoiding high distribution costs that burden branch-heavy Spanish peers. This structural cost advantage enables the company to pass internal savings directly to customers while maintaining superior operational efficiency. The firm's strategic focus remains anchored on motor insurance as the dominant revenue pillar, supplemented by targeted diversification into home and health segments, all distributed through highly automated digital channels that handle over ninety percent of policyholder interactions.

Business Model and Revenue Architecture

1.
Extreme motor concentration risk: motor insurance represents 81.4% of total written premiums in 2025, exposing the group to auto-specific cost inflation and leaving it structurally vulnerable compared to diversified multi-line competitors.
2.
Strong premium momentum within constraints: motor gross written premiums reached €924.0M in 2025 with robust 11.8% year-on-year growth, positioning the company as Spain's sixth-largest motor insurer though trailing massive market leaders like Mapfre.
3.
Profitable diversification lever deployed: the home insurance segment contributes 14.8% of total written premiums and delivered an outstanding combined ratio of 87.1% in 2025, covering over 776,000 properties with premiums growing 6.3% to €167.8M.
4.
Nascent health growth channel: health insurance under the Vivaz brand represents only 3.4% of premium share, but premiums accelerated 14.7% to €38.8M in 2025, outperforming the Spanish market average.
5.
Spain-centric operations constrain scale: the company operates almost exclusively in Spanish territory with negligible Portuguese presence, serving 3.73 million policyholders through highly predictable recurring annual premium contracts.
