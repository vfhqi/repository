EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Materiality of change assessment

1.
K+S AG completed an orderly internal succession of both CEO and CFO roles in June 2025, with Dr. Christian H. Meyer ascending from CFO to CEO and Dr. Jens Christian Keuthen assuming the CFO position, preserving execution continuity on major capital projects without disruptive external interventions.
2.
The company announced a transformative €350 million acquisition of Qemetica's specialty salt business in June 2026, funded through €320 million of convertible bonds, which will add high-margin evaporated salt capacity with approximately 40% EBITDA margins and expand K+S's European market share to roughly 20%.
3.
K+S committed approximately €600 million to the Werra 2060 dry processing transformation, targeting 20% operating cost savings and a 50% reduction in saline wastewater by summer 2028, while continuing to ramp Bethune capacity toward 4 million tonnes utilizing low-cost secondary solution mining caverns.
4.
The company faces a structurally disadvantaged cost position with mid-cycle EBITDA margins of 25% to 35% compared to peer baselines of 40% to 50%, while net debt including mining provisions reached €1,588.5 million at Q1 2026, representing 2.33x the guided EBITDA midpoint and masking true long-term leverage exposure.
5.
K+S delivered volatile earnings over the last three years as EBITDA collapsed from a peak of €2,423 million in fiscal 2022 to a trough of €558 million in fiscal 2024, while adjusted free cash flow dwindled to only €29 million in fiscal 2025, constrained by capital-intensive German and Canadian assets.
