Section summary
  1. Nibe Industrier AB has navigated a challenging multi-year market downturn across its core residential heating divisions while successfully maintaining its leadership position.
  2. A recent shift in market sentiment and broker upgrades has improved expectations for earnings growth over the next eighteen months.
1.
Headline read: Nibe's third-quarter 2026 interim report, published on November 16, 2026, was a clean beat that beat consensus expectations on both organic growth and operating profit. It came after a pre-earnings debate dominated by the slow normalization of European heat pump inventories. It did not remove the structural questions over premium pricing power, which sit behind a clearly better cyclical print.
2.
Top line: Net sales were SEK 11,232 million, up 11.40% from SEK 10,086 million in Q3 2025. This was about 1.16% above the Visible Alpha consensus of SEK 11,103 million. Group organic growth of 10% was a 100 basis point beat against Morgan Stanley's estimate. Management stressed that this was seven consecutive quarters of organic growth.
3.
Profitability: Operating profit reached SEK 1,524 million, a 33.80% increase. The operating margin expanded to 13.60% from 11.30%. DNB Carnegie noted operating profit was 6% higher than estimated, driven by favorable product mix. This is the largest deviation from broker expectations at group level.
4.
Climate Solutions: The largest division delivered net sales of SEK 7,356 million, up 9.43%. Its operating margin was 15.70%, against 14% a year earlier. Morgan Stanley judged the 9% organic growth to be in line with forecasts. The margin was the material surprise, because it stood out against consensus expectations of 14%.
5.
Element: This was the biggest deviation from consensus. Organic growth reached 14.90%, beating the consensus expectation of 8.70%. Net sales rose 19.89% to SEK 3,357 million. The margin expanded to 10.10% from 7.50%. Morgan Stanley noted that divisional operating profit of SEK 341 million was 15% above consensus. The driver was semiconductor tailwinds and data center electrification spending, which gives Nibe a nonresidential earnings buffer.
6.
Stoves: Operating profit rose to SEK 50 million from SEK 24 million, a 6% margin. Organic growth of 3.10% beat the 1.40% consensus. The beat sits alongside a negative outlook change. Nibe cut the short-term margin target to 6% to 8% from the historical 10% to 13%. Management expects new trade tariffs to create an annual operating profit drag of about SEK 150 million.
7.
Balance sheet: Net debt to EBITDA fell to 2.4x from 2.7x in the previous quarter. DNB Carnegie highlighted that net debt declined by more than SEK 1 billion quarter-on-quarter. The deleveraging was helped by working capital reductions. That reduction was limited by a temporary build-up, as the transition to propane-based R290 heat pumps required dual component pipelines.
8.
Cash flow caveat: The cash story is weaker than the leverage story suggests. DNB Carnegie's full-year forecast still shows free cash flow slightly negative at SEK -376 million for 2026. It also forecasts capital expenditure of SEK 1,244 million. Management's target is to reduce working capital to twenty percent of net sales over the next several quarters.
9.
Operating leverage: Utilization is the central earnings swing factor. DNB Carnegie had estimated utilization of only 40% to 45% before the print. It now estimates it improved to approximately 65% during the third quarter. DNB Carnegie also holds that fixed cost leverage is the single largest earnings swing factor for Nibe. Because the company can accommodate a major increase in sales without significant capital expenditures, incremental volumes should convert at high margins.
10.
Estimate revisions: Brokers raised outer-year numbers and trimmed the current year. DNB Carnegie cut 2026e adjusted EPS to SEK 1.69 from 1.72 on a temporarily high tax rate, but raised 2027e to SEK 2.08 from 2.04. Morgan Stanley cut 2026 EPS by 1% on higher financial expenses and tax, and raised 2027 and 2028 EPS by 4% on stronger top-line growth. The pattern shows that the beat is read as volume-led and durable for forward years.

Morgan Stanley took a neutral line, stating that the share price trades close to fair value. It also lowered its target valuation multiple because long-term growth expectations remain constrained by escalating competition in Europe. Going in, the two houses already differed. Ambitions unchanged, timetable stretched: Management kept its long-term framework of average annual sales growth of 20% over a business cycle. It also kept a group operating margin of at least 10% and SEK 80 billion in net sales by 2029. The sales goal requires a rapid restart of acquisition activity once leverage normalizes. The only explicit downgrade is Stoves, so the outlook change is narrow in targets but broad in what it requires (volumes, utilization and deleveraging).
16.
Temporary or structural: The bull case is that higher operating rates will continue driving margin expansion as fixed costs are absorbed. The counter-case is that the third quarter could be a seasonal peak, and margins could quickly contract if re-stocking slows. A fair reading is that the quarter confirms a cyclical recovery with real operating leverage. It does not yet prove that margins will be protected from structural pricing pressure.

Limitations and next steps: This summary relies on broker commentary, company disclosures and a small number of expert checks, and some figures are broker estimates rather than reported data (utilization, free cash flow, capital expenditure). Only one post-earnings period is observed, and no subsequent results have been reported. The key variables to track in coming disclosures are utilization progress, price-to-cost spread against Chinese competition, free cash flow conversion, and the Stoves tariff offset.

Stable leadership: Nibe Industrier AB operates as a leading international provider of energy-efficient indoor climate comfort solutions and components, headquartered in Markaryd, Sweden. The company was founded more than 70 years ago and has successfully grown through an aggressive decentralized acquisition model since its initial stock listing in 1997. This structured approach has allowed Nibe to expand its global footprint across Europe, North America, and Asia.
2.
Divergent operations: Nibe manages its extensive global business through three dedicated operating segments which focus on climate control, industrial components, and consumer heating. The largest division is Nibe Climate Solutions, which accounts for 65% of net sales and 76% of operating profit. The other segments, Nibe Element and Nibe Stoves, contribute 28% and 7% of net sales, respectively. These diverse business units provide Nibe with a highly resilient operational structure during cyclical downturns.
3.
Volatile growth: Nibe achieved net sales of SEK 40.84 bn in fiscal year 2025, representing a minor total growth of 1.7% compared to the previous year. This performance followed a challenging period in fiscal year 2024, when total group sales declined by 6.8% due to severe European heat pump destocking. Despite these external headwinds, Nibe stabilized its group EBIT margin at 10.1% in 2025 through comprehensive cost-cutting actions. This financial trajectory demonstrates Nibe's capacity to defend margins amid severe cyclical demand downturns.

Unfavourable benchmarks: Nibe experienced more volatile financial performance over the last three years compared to high-performing Nordic industrial peers. While Nibe was impacted by regional residential construction declines, peers benefited from more diversified industrial exposures. Consequently, Nibe has traded at a discount to historical valuation averages as it recovers.

This upward price momentum reflected rising investor optimism regarding an end to distributor inventory corrections.

Subdued performance: Nibe's share price relative performance over a sixmonth horizon was slightly negative despite the longer twelve-month recovery. The company recorded a negative relative return of 4.11% over six months as investors remained cautious about high leverage. This temporary share underperformance reflected ongoing market concerns about capacity utilization and slow new-build housing permit recoveries in Germany.

Analyst Deijenberg upgraded Nibe to Buy in August 2026 and subsequent raised the target price to SEK 54 on October 7, 2026.
