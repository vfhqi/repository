B.1 Business Description 19 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Core product. Melexis designs and tests micro-electronic sensor and driver integrated circuits, chiefly magnetic position sensors, current sensors, pressure sensors and motor drivers
2.
Segment mix. Sensor integrated circuits are 77% of sales across eight product lines, drivers and actuators 23% across three, and microcontrollers are deliberately excluded
3.
End-market concentration. Automotive applications are 88% to 90% of group turnover, and were 89% of second-quarter 2026 sales, with the balance in data-centre cooling, digital health, alternative mobility and robotics
4.
Geography. Asia-Pacific is 60% of destination sales and Mainland China alone 28% to 29%, up from under 20% five years earlier
5.
Manufacturing model. Front-end wafer fabrication is outsourced, principally to sister company X-FAB, while Melexis retains probing and final test in its own facilities
6.
Financial character, revenue. FY25 net sales were EUR 839.6m, down 10.0%, after EUR 964.3m in FY23 and EUR 932.8m in FY24
7.
Financial character, margin. FY25 gross margin was 38.6% and operating margin 16.0%, against management's through-cycle targets of 45% and 25%
8.
Financial character, earnings. Diluted earnings per share fell from EUR 5.18 in FY23 to EUR 4.24 in FY24 and EUR 2.78 in FY25
9.
Financial character, returns. Return on invested capital averaged 33.0% across 2017 to 2024 and compressed to roughly 18.0% in 2025
10.
Financial character, balance sheet. Net debt was EUR 255.8m at end-June 2026 with net debt to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation comfortably below 1.2 times
11.
Capital intensity. FY25 capital expenditure was EUR 32.44m, a 3.86% capital intensity, against roughly 15% for European integrated device manufacturers
12.
Latest reported period. Second-quarter 2026 revenue reached EUR 217.1m with gross margin 40.5%, operating profit EUR 38.6m and diluted earnings per share EUR 0.76
13.
Customer structure. The largest customer was 13% of 2024 sales and 14% in the first half of 2025, and the top ten were 42% and 44% on the same basis
14.
Route to market. Revenue routes 62% direct to customer and 38% through distributors, with cash prepayments frequently required in China
15.
Market position. Melexis holds 8.0% of the global automotive sensor market, fourth behind Bosch 18.6%, Infineon 15.2% and NXP 15.1%, plus 5.3% of automotive driver integrated circuits
16.
Research base. Research and development runs at 12% to 14% of sales, supporting over 400 active patents, 40 new patent families a year and 19 new products launched in 2025
17.
Ownership. Two founder-family vehicles hold 50% plus one share, Elex NV at 25% and Sensinnovat BV at 25% plus one share, across 40,400,000 ordinary shares
18.
Workforce. Headcount reached 2,010 in 2024 across twelve countries, with manufacturing and test sites in Belgium, Bulgaria, Malaysia and the Philippines
19.
Investment style. A high-return, asset-light cyclical whose earnings track automotive semiconductor content per vehicle rather than vehicle volumes
