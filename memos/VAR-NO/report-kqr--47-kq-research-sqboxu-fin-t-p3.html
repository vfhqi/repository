Section 2 – The Norwegian Petroleum Tax Lag and Cash Flow Cycles

Section summary
  1. The lag in Norwegian petroleum tax settlements creates a volatile cash flow cycle where high- commodity-price years generate massive cash outflows in subsequent years.
  2. This tax-lag effect suppresses cash flows as commodity prices normalize, squeezing free cash flow and challenging the dividend sustainability of unhedged operators.

Mechanics of the Tax Lag

1.
Lagged settlements: The Norwegian petroleum tax system operates on a lagging schedule of ten annual instalments. Under this regime, operators pay half of their estimated tax liability during the actual income year and settle the remaining half in the first half of the subsequent year. This payment cycle inevitably creates a one-year delay in cash settlements. Consequently, cash flows weaken rapidly if commodity prices drop while past liabilities fall due. Heavy outlays: Var Energi experienced massive cash tax payments of approximately USD 1.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026.
