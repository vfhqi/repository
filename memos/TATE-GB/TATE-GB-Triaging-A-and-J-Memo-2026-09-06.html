B Executive Summary 31 findings
Executive Summary -- We are looking for a clear, conviction-carrying statement of the investment thesis, key ratings, and actionable conclusion; avoiding vague or overly hedged language.
Judgement summary
  • BLUF, overall case verdict. C Tate & Lyle is a genuinely repositioned specialty ingredients business whose equity has stopped being an ownable change-driven investment case and become a capped cash-takeover instrument
  • Worst reading, case simplicity. D The case fails four of five simplicity guardrails, carries far more than two fulcrum drivers, and runs a value chain thick with active headwinds
  • Sell-side earnings momentum. F Dashboard revisions show earnings estimates down 29% over twelve months, target prices down 9% over six, and buy ratings down 19% over three
  • Standalone delivery has been poor. FY26 pro forma revenue fell 3% to £2,006m, adjusted earnings per share fell 16% to 40.4p, and return on capital employed collapsed from 12.8% to 8.0%
  • Cash generation missed its own target. Adjusted free cash flow fell £26m to £164m and cash conversion dropped to 70% against a stated better-than-75% target, on a £26m working-capital build
  • The risks report recommends PARK. Its researcher verdict is to park the standalone case until CP Kelco volume stabilisation is proven, while monitoring the arbitrage discount to the Ingredion offer
  • This memo adopts that PARK and hardens it. The arbitrage leg does not clear the hurdle either, so the recommendation is to park the whole name, not merely the standalone leg
  • The arithmetic is the crux. At 553.5p the 11.1% gross spread to the 615p offer is worth roughly 8% a year to an H2-2027 close, against 32.3% downside to the 375p undisturbed price (calculated)
  • Break-even completion probability is 74.4% (calculated). The market is already paying for a high-probability close, so the residual return does not compensate eleven-jurisdiction antitrust risk
  • Technical momentum is strong but deal-pegged. B The shares sit 29.4% above the 200-day average on a 75th-percentile relative-strength reading, a re-rating bought entirely by the bid, not by trading
  • Main support is asset quality. A 46% global pectin share, the number-one gellan gum position and two-thirds of the top thirty customers retained beyond five years are real and mechanically defensible
  • Main risk is deal failure. Antitrust review across eleven jurisdictions runs into the second half of 2027, and a block returns the shares to approximately 375p, or 288p to 300p on bear multiples
  • The best setup fit has already fired. Tate met all five tests of a huge change-from-current clearing event in April 2026, and the bid monetised that setup at 375p before this memo could act on it
  • Recommendation, medium conviction. Park with a defined re-entry trigger, and revisit only on deal lapse, because the ownable version of this case exists only at the undisturbed price
