The Azelis Turkish Currency Precedent

1.
Strategic restructuring: Azelis Group NV successfully mitigated its hyperinflation exposure by changing its Turkish subsidiary's functional currency from the Turkish Lira to the Euro effective 1 April 2025. This operational shift reflected a gradual transition in the subsidiary's economic environment, where underlying transactions and sales became increasingly Euro-denominated. By implementing this change, Azelis established a major corporate precedent that other specialty chemical distributors can replicate to stabilize highly volatile non-operating finance results.
2.
Regulatory amendments: The functional currency change at Azelis was enabled by recent amendments to Turkish Decree No. 32. These legal modifications allowed Turkish entities to execute contracts and collect payments directly in foreign currencies. Without these government-backed regulatory reforms, Azelis would have struggled to justify the Euro as its primary economic currency under IFRS rules, showing that external legal structures dictate operational accounting viability.
3.
Accounting discontinuance: Following this functional currency change on 1 April 2025, Azelis immediately discontinued the application of IAS 29 hyperinflation accounting for its Turkish subsidiary. The subsidiary adopted the hyperinflation-adjusted book values of assets and liabilities as of 31 March 2025 as their historical cost basis. This clean break meant that Azelis successfully eliminated ongoing non-cash monetary P&L losses from its Turkish operations.
4.
Positive financial impact: The financial reporting of Azelis experienced an immediate reduction in non-operating volatility during the 2025 fiscal year. Management confirmed that the monetary loss on hyperinflation decreased compared to 2024 primarily due to this functional currency change. This reduction in monetary losses directly protected the distributor's net profit margin and helped improve core cash earnings per share.
