EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

The Analysis of Competing Hypotheses reveals that the FALSE FRIEND profile wins on having the fewest hard inconsistencies across operational data, as a record backlog of €45.4 billion and net customer advances of €14.866 billion mask severe cash consumption and physically capped production rates. We assess the bear case probability as moderate given structural supply constraints offset by the deep defense backlog. The joint probability of concurrent failures is elevated, as materials shortages and environmental disruptions are operationally linked, though unmatched defense backlog visibility of over six years provides a substantial operational buffer against temporary concurrent disruptions.

Credibility of the bear case

Sell-side bear cases project a severe price floor of €241 representing a 21% contraction based on a 2028 sum-ofthe-parts valuation, assuming Falcon deliveries are capped at 35 units annually while no new Rafale export contracts are secured by 2027, with an 8x EV/EBIT multiple applied to the core business. An alternative bear valuation targets €259 if Rafale deliveries slow and business jet deliveries remain flat through 2028, with group operating margins contracting 170bp to 6.9% in fiscal year 2028 and a 7x EV/EBIT multiple reflecting a 50% discount to business jet peers.

The official Falcon 10X entry-into-service delay to 2029 disconfirms the nearterm civil growth pillar, while Safran engine bottlenecks require at least 18 months to align with elevated airframe assembly targets and early deliveries to Indonesia carry significant margin dilution due to high zero-profit supplier content.

Winning ACH profile and hard inconsistencies

The YES profile assumes successful thesis execution where supply chain headwinds resolve entirely, the NO profile defines outright thesis failure triggered by persistent Falcon delivery delays, and the FALSE FRIEND profile captures backlogs masking flat delivery rates and dilutive initial export margins. The YES profile suffers six hard inconsistencies which makes predictable EPS trajectory highly unlikely. The deferred French Rafale deliveries free up near-term export slots but highlight local budget limits, while the termination of SCAF removes long-term European R&D funding but drives local French development. Severe skilled labor shortages prevent rapid manufacturing ramp-up while inflating temporary workforce spending, regional wildfires west of Bordeaux threatened critical manufacturing sites but did not halt long-term operations, the company's internal MRO network faces declining competitiveness from high labor and structural cost structures, and negotiations for the 114 Rafale jets face complex local-sourcing workshare disputes before final contract signing.

Highest-plausibility failure modes

Low initial margins on Indonesian deliveries carry zero-profit supplier pass-through content, triggering heavy contract provisions and margin dilution that compress operating profits near-term, with escalating contract liabilities and rising inventory write-downs during initial deliveries, representing high plausibility supported by H1 2026 results showing a 220bp provisioning headwind. Severe skilled aerospace labor shortages in France requiring expensive reliance on temporary workers drive higher personnel and training costs that erode structural margins across all sharing programs, with prolonged unfilled job positions exceeding historical industry averages at GIFAS, representing high plausibility as 85,000 jobs must be filled. The unprecedented €46.6 billion order backlog masks unresolved upstream supplier delivery bottlenecks, as cash flow advances are consumed while actual delivery rates remain structurally capped, with flat year-over-year delivery counts despite multi-year booked orders rising, representing high plausibility as upstream capacity constraints limit Mérignac final assembly rates. Temporary spike in Asian business jet orders masks structural market share losses to Gulfstream, as Dassault loses ultra-long-range customers due to the Falcon 10X delay, with increasing sales momentum of Gulfstream G800 securing customer reference commitments first, representing high plausibility as Gulfstream holds dominant product scale.
