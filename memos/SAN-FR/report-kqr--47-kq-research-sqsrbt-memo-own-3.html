Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The post-Opella Biopharma focus makes Sanofi highly dependent on the commercial durability of Dupixent and the execution of its external growth strategy.
  2. Understanding buy-side sentiment on Sanofi's capital allocation and patent cliff is essential to evaluating its intermediate business earnings growth and risk profile.

Strategic context and research scope
