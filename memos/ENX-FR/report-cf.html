EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Euronext has undergone material transformation through strategic acquisitions and operational integration, transitioning from a traditional cash equity exchange into a vertically integrated pan-European capital markets infrastructure. The group successfully diversified its revenue mix to approximately 60% non-transactional activities, achieving €121 million in run-rate EBITDA synergies from the Borsa Italiana acquisition–more than doubling its initial €60 million target.

Leadership succession planning proceeds in a highly structured manner, though the pending departure of CEO Stéphane Boujnah in May 2027 introduces execution risk if successors alter strategic direction, while aggressive post-trade and technology integration could trigger operational outages if migration deadlines slip.

The strategic architecture emphasizes geographic federalism with decentralized national exchange governance, enabling sustained market share defense at 63% to 64% in cash equities, while the group has delivered €260 million in cumulative run-rate synergies across all acquisitions, consistently exceeding initial operational targets. Technology migrations to the proprietary Optiq platform have been completed for Borsa Italiana cash and derivatives markets, with Athens Exchange scheduled for June 2027, while the launch of Nord Pool Power Futures in March 2026 migrated 100% of open interest and the Euronext Securities expansion to France, Belgium, and Netherlands equity settlement began in September 2026. Governance concentration risks persist as reference shareholders holding 23.56% of shares occupy three board seats, deviating from Dutch corporate code guidelines, and shareholders rejected the remuneration report in May 2025, reflecting discontent with board compensation policies.

Clearing internalization emerges as the dominant growth driver with high plausibility and high confidence, delivering strong risk-sensitive portfolio netting that boosts margins toward structural ceilings, while CSD European expansion targets €50 million in annualized revenues addressing European settlement fragmentation with medium plausibility due to operational complexities and entrenched incumbent practices. FICC internationalization gains momentum in Spain and Portugal, enhancing high-margin non-Italian transaction capture with medium plausibility, while power futures migration creates unique hedging advantages repatriating Nordic power clearing volumes with high plausibility, and retail data monetization drives low-latency feed demand growing the advanced data franchise with high confidence.

The primary bear mechanism involves unexpected interest rate cuts combined with prolonged low-volatility trading cycles severely compressing posttrade treasury yields and equity transaction fees, while aggressive retaliatory pricing from Clearstream or Euroclear could completely neutralize projected CSD revenue growth and coordinated defensive pricing actions could entirely offset post-trade expansion. Euronext delivered exceptional Q2 2026 results with revenue of €544 million beating consensus by 4% on broadbased asset class strength, adjusted EBITDA reaching €360 million beating consensus by 8% on superior operating leverage, the adjusted EBITDA margin reaching 66.1% expanding by 230 basis points year-on-year to outperform competitive peer averages, and adjusted EPS reaching €2.42 beating market consensus by 9% on achieving enhanced operational efficiency.

Segment performance showed equity market revenue growth of 24.9% to €132.7 million driven by persistent cash trading volatility, Capital Markets and Data Solutions growth of 17.2% to €193.8 million boosted by strong primary admissions, FICC markets revenue rising 12.3% to €98.4 million supported by Nord Pool integration, and Securities Services revenue rising 12.5% to €96.9 million supported by growing custody assets.
