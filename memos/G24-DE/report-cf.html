EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 1 finding

Materiality of Change

Scout24 executed an orderly internal CEO succession in March 2025, appointing Ralf Weitz with 17 years of company experience, ensuring high strategic continuity while strengthening execution credibility across core digital products. The company completed a dual C-suite transition within 12 months, bringing in external CFO Martin Mildner with 25 years of digital experience from ProSiebenSat.1 and United Internet, adding capital markets pedigree to the executive team.

The strategic paradigm fundamentally shifted toward an Agentic OS model for real estate, moving beyond traditional listing volume to building an interconnected ecosystem, supported by over EUR 400 million invested in technology infrastructure and strategic acquisitions since 2021. Scout24 executed its largest international expansion by acquiring Spanish portals Fotocasa and Habitaclia for EUR 153 million, contributing approximately EUR 60 million in annual revenue at an initial 18% EBITDA margin.

Capital allocation priorities shifted materially, with the company upsizing its FY26 share buyback program to EUR 350 million as part of an authorized EUR 500 million framework, reflecting confidence in organic cash generation.

Recent Delivery and Execution Quality

Scout24 delivered Q2 2026 revenue of EUR 193 million, representing 20% reported growth and exceeding consensus by 0.8%, while group ordinary operating EBITDA reached EUR 116 million, missing sell-side consensus by only 0.6%. Core organic revenue expansion reached 10.4% year-over-year in Q2 2026, with German Professional monthly ARPU increasing 10.4% to EUR 1,225, driven by agent migration to higher tier membership packages.

The company achieved industry-leading performance with 10.5% organic revenue growth in H1 2026 reaching EUR 372 million, while organic ordinary operating EBITDA margin expanded by 160 basis points year-over-year to 63.0%, hitting a record 65.4% in Professional during Q2 2026. Scout24 established a consistent operational beat pattern, delivering ten consecutive quarters of EBITDA beats against sell-side consensus from Q3 2023 through Q4 2025, though beat magnitude moderated from 5.4% in late 2023 to 1% throughout FY25 as sell-side models aligned with management guidance.

The company maintained a flawless multi-year track record, delivering double-digit revenue growth for five consecutive years from FY21 through FY25 while expanding group ordinary operating EBITDA from EUR 304 million in FY23 to EUR 406 million in FY25, systematically raising guidance mid-year in FY22, FY23, and FY25.

Forward Credibility and Guidance Confidence

Management reconfirmed full-year FY26 guidance targeting 16-18% reported revenue growth and an ordinary operating EBITDA margin of up to 61%, with the inorganic consolidation of Spanish assets accounting for 6-7 percentage points of reported top-line growth. At the May 2026 Capital Markets Day, management raised mid-term targets, guiding to annual revenue growth at the top end of high-single to low-double digits through 2028, with consolidated ordinary operating EBITDA margin expanding to approximately 64% by 2028 and an upgraded operational target to reach 700 thousand Private Seeker+ subscribers.

Scout24 fits the honest tracker with sandbagging tendencies archetype, consistently issuing conservative initial targets before upgrading at H1 prints, establishing HIGH guidance credibility. Forward guidance credibility is underpinned by strong operational assumptions relying on 10-11% organic B2B subscription growth and 3-4% Private ARPU growth, with high incremental gross margins above 85% on subscription upgrades ensuring guidance could absorb a 10% top-line shortfall in Private without breaking full-year EPS targets due to ongoing share repurchases. Over 80% of top-line delivery stems from internal pricing power and membership tier upgrades rather than macro volume, with B2B subscription pricing expanding by 10.4% year-over-year in H1 2026 despite persistent German building permit contractions.

Sell-Side Positioning and Broker Consensus

Sell-side coverage remains overwhelmingly bullish with an 80%+ Buy-equivalent rating mix across five active broker houses, with Morgan Stanley maintaining Overweight, Bernstein Research maintaining Outperform, Jefferies Research maintaining Buy, Deutsche Bank Research maintaining Buy, while Rothschild & Co Redburn launched with a Neutral rating. Broker price targets range from EUR 80 to EUR 126 against a closing share price near EUR 76, with targets standing at EUR 80 for Rothschild, EUR 90 for Bernstein, EUR 103.50 for Jefferies, EUR 114 for Morgan Stanley, and EUR 126 for Deutsche Bank.

The dominant bull narrative frames Scout24 as strongly insulated from AI disintermediation, with Morgan Stanley arguing that market fears around AI terminal value disruption are mispriced and Deutsche Bank highlighting that Scout24 managed AI disruption risk better than European peers by building its proprietary Immo.AI intelligence layer on 30 years of German property data. The primary contrarian bear thesis from Rothschild & Co Redburn argues that faster growth is already fully priced in and that acquiring number-two asset Fotocasa in Spain complicates the equity story, with AI lowering software production barriers making it easier to unlock upside at UK peer Rightmove than at Scout24.

Recent post-Q2 2026 earnings notes reconfirmed full-year forecasts across the broker pool, maintaining stable consensus EPS estimates, with all major analysts reiterating their ratings and price targets following the quarterly print.

Peer-Relative Position and Competitive Standing

Scout24 ranks second overall in fundamental momentum across European digital classifieds peers, outpacing competitors in organic growth and margin expansion despite macro headwinds, with double-digit B2B Professional subscription growth insulating the company from broader European transaction volume slowdowns. The company achieved 10.4% organic revenue expansion in Q2 2026, outperforming Rightmove's 7% growth rate, while German Professional ARPU expanded 10.4% year-over-year to EUR 1,225, and core organic ordinary operating EBITDA margin reached a record 64.2% benefiting from a 3.5% decline in organic personnel costs.

ImmoScout24 maintains an unrivaled market position with approximately 70% market share in German real estate classifieds through deep network effects and brand awareness. Scout24 demonstrates rapid AI search scaling with HeyImmo and AI search generating 3.3 million monthly interactions in June 2026, up 47-fold year-over-year, while referral traffic from external large language models accounted for less than 1% of total traffic and Propstack AI users generate 50% higher ARPU.

Over 80% of revenue consists of recurring B2B and B2C subscriptions, ensuring earnings predictability through property cycles, while the company maintains conservative net leverage at 1.5x EBITDA while executing substantial share repurchases.

Activist Presence and Governance Catalysts

Historical activist engagement by Elliott Advisors in May 2019 accumulated a 7% economic stake following a failed private equity takeover bid, publicly demanding operational separation of AutoScout24, a EUR 500 million share buyback, and board representation. Management executed a compliance response, initiating a strategic review in August 2019 that culminated in the sale of AutoScout24 to Hellman & Friedman for EUR 2.9 billion, establishing the current pure-play real estate model.

No active activist presence currently exists in the shareholder base for Scout24 SE, with equity ownership transitioning fully to institutional asset managers and private equity firms completely exiting the shareholder base. The capital return framework instituted during activist engagement remains embedded in current capital allocation discipline, providing ongoing structural benefits to shareholder returns.

Material Risks and Downside Scenarios

Cultural execution risk emerges if second-level management turnover accelerates, as organizational changes could disrupt historical agent sales momentum and operational continuity. Acquiring challenger platforms in Spain dilutes group margins near-term, exposing Scout24 to cross-border execution risk and potential AI disintermediation across B2C subscriber flows. Prolonged macroeconomic stagnation combined with severe building permit contraction could trigger widespread real estate agent insolvencies, eroding core subscription ARPU and Private Seeker+ conversion rates.

Near-term free cash flow conversion remains constrained by integration costs and long-term incentive plan cash settlements, exposing the bottom line to margin compression if Spanish turnaround timelines slip or macro softness depresses transaction enablement volumes. Subdued rental search traffic combined with marketing cost inflation in Private could permanently depress B2C conversion rates, preventing Scout24 from achieving its 700 thousand subscriber milestone by 2028.

Acquiring subscale number-two assets in Spain temporarily dilutes reported group margins, exposing Scout24 to integration drag if cross-border ARPU expansion slows relative to pure-play domestic peers. The bullish sellside consensus reflects potential groupthink around AI monetization, with material downside risk to consensus EPS estimates if generative AI tools disintermediate B2C Seeker+ traffic. Management's ambitious Capital Markets Day targets rely heavily on expanding Private subscriptions to 700 thousand by 2028, with consumer pushback against new pricing tiers potentially accelerating churn and falling short of the guided 64% margin level, triggering sell-side earnings downgrades.
