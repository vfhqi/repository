What the call should settle, and why

The case in one line: one of Europe's most predictable earnings streams, priced near its ten-year median multiple because the market fears artificial intelligence will reprice Legal; the next twelve months' estimates rest on Legal at 9% to 10%, a margin step of about 50bp to 60bp, Exhibitions' recovery and a February buyback that is the one number RELX gives. Six judgements decide whether and when to buy.

  1. Judgement 1, Fourth-quarter momentum and where FY2026 lands. Nine months: group 7%, Legal 10%, STM 6%, Risk 8%, Exhibitions 4%. FY2026 EPS: consensus 140.6p (Morgan Stanley's compilation), Bernstein 142p, Deutsche Bank 138.5p, ours 143p. If the fourth quarter runs at the nine-month rate with H2 margin progress near H1's +70bp, FY2026 prints at or above Bernstein's 142p and the 151p to 154p FY2027 figures at the low end must rise; if Exhibitions and Risk's transactions soften further, FY2026 lands near 140p and the low end holds.
  2. Judgement 2, Legal's rate into 2027. Consensus 9.8% for FY2026 and FY2027, Bernstein 10% and 10%, ours 10% and 9%. Each 2 points is about £39m of revenue and about 1% of FY2027 EPS; Legal at 8% at the AGM update would start a cut cycle; still 10% in February puts Bernstein's 160p in play.
  3. Judgement 3, the margin step in 2027 against an investment step. Consensus 35.8%, Bernstein 36.1%, ours 36.1%. Each 50bp is £53m of profit and 1.5% of EPS: consensus has room to rise if no step comes; a step of a point of revenue (Thomson Reuters' third-quarter margin miss shows such steps happen) costs about 100bp in the year and a 3% cut.
  4. Judgement 4, the 2027 buyback. Bernstein £1.5bn, ours £1.75bn, consensus-implied about £2bn, Rothschild & Co Redburn £2.07bn. Small for EPS (each £0.25bn is about 0.15%), large for the day: £1.5bn reads as a cut and lowers the implied consensus, £2bn or more as a raise.
  5. Judgement 5, below the operating line. Ours: tax 23.0%, finance cost £375m in FY2027; the sell-side implies 21% to 22% tax and £324m to £354m of finance cost (or £405m implied by consensus). Each tax point is about 1.3% of EPS and each £25m of finance cost about 0.7%: if the company steers to our tax rate, consensus net income is 1% to 2% too high even with the margin delivered.
  6. Judgement 6, Exhibitions in an odd year. Bernstein 7% for FY2027, ours 4% (£57m of revenue below consensus). If rebooking outside the Gulf is normal, Bernstein is right and ours is cautious; if the Middle East stays weak into the May Arabian Travel Market, consensus has to come down.
