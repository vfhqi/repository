Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

  1. The trajectory of Lagercrantz's earnings per share over the next 18 months depends heavily on its ability to maintain high capital efficiency and execute acquisitions amid shifting macroeconomic conditions.

Significant uncertainty: "Key question: how likely is company guidance to be raised, met or reduced at the earnings events over the next 18 months, and how likely are sell-side consensus forecasts, and the net breadth of revisions, to move higher or lower over the same period?"

Redeye noted on 28 October 2026 that Lagercrantz traded at 29.7x LTM EV/EBIT, which is 5% below its five-year median of 31.4x. The broker expects earnings growth, rather than further multiple expansion, to drive the share price going forward. Therefore, meeting or raising guidance is critical to sustaining the current premium valuation compared to broader market indices.

Structured valuation: Lagercrantz's valuation is highly sensitive to policy-rate expectations because higher discount rates directly elevate its implied cost of capital. DNB Carnegie estimated on 5 October 2026 that the company's cost of equity stands at 8% based on a 4% risk-free rate and a 4% market risk premium. Valuation stability: Lagercrantz's fair value remains highly dependent on capital efficiency and cash flow conversion to justify its premium rating. Redeye applied a weighted average cost of capital of 8% in its basecase valuation on 28 October 2026.
