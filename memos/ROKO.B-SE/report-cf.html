M&A execution rebounded strongly from Q2 2026 onwards following a relatively slow 2025 pacing, with acquisitions averaging a disciplined 7.0x EBITA multiple over the 2019-2025 period, including the major Fri-Jado platform acquisition representing 11% of sales.
