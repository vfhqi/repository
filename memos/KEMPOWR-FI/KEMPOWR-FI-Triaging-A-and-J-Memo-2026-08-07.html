B.1 Business Description 6 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Kempower designs, manufactures and services modular DC fast-charging hardware for electric vehicles, separating the power cabinet from satellite dispensers to cut site footprint and enable dynamic power-sharing.
2.
Primary market. Public passenger-car charge-point operators (75% of the installed base) and commercial-vehicle fleets (25%), sold direct plus via resellers across more than 60 countries from Finnish and North Carolina assembly plants.
3.
Financial character. FY2025 revenue EUR 251.3m (+12% year-on-year), gross margin 47.6%, operative EBIT margin (4.9%), net cash position with EUR 126.3m total liquidity and no dividend.
4.
Segment mix. 95.3% of 2025 sales were point-in-time hardware; recurring software (ChargEye) and lifecycle services were only 4.7%, though services grew 45% year-on-year in Q1 2026 to 7% of revenue.
5.
Geography. Nordic revenue share fell to 27% of Q1 2026 sales from 44% a year earlier as North America (+230% year-on-year) and Rest-of-Europe (+87%) scale.
6.
Investment style. A self-help margin-repair story layered on a secular volume story: event-driven cost programme and geographic pivot riding a structural EV-infrastructure growth wave, not a steady-state compounder.
