EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

The complete removal of the founding management team under Gilles Grapinet has ended a decade-long legacy of unintegrated acquisitions, while a deeply renewed executive committee has been rapidly assembled to drive the operational turnaround under the North Star 2030 program, though rapid multi-layered management turnover risks execution disruption during platform convergence. The company has shifted corporate tone to executionfocused pragmatism and cash discipline, executing a highly dilutive €500 million capital increase in early 2026 to resolve leverage issues.

Worldline suffered a severe structural organic revenue deceleration over the last three years, falling from 6% in 2023 to a decline of 2.70% in 2025, underperforming sector benchmarks as Adyen achieved double-digit expansion, while adjusted EBITDA margin collapsed from 24.10% in 2023 to 18.30% in 2025, far below Nexi's stable profitability.

Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro)

Section summary
  1. merchant demand remains highly price sensitive driving a commoditized race to the bottom that dilutes pricing power
  2. competitive intensity is squeezing market share, leaving legacy players exposed to agile challengers and enterprise giants
  3. modern single-architecture platforms and account-toaccount payments threaten to disintermediate fragmented legacy stacks
  4. regulatory compliance burdens, especially AML and DORA, impose heavy structural compliance costs and force revenue de-risking
  5. technology supply chains face geopolitical tensions, inflating data center infrastructure and hardware procurement costs
  6. the merchant acquiring cycle is highly vulnerable to discretionary spending contraction and shifting software budgets
  7. macroeconomic headwinds in core European markets depress retail transaction volumes and inflate debt refinancing costs
