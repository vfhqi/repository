1.
What it does. IntegraFin Holdings is the listed parent of Transact, the UK's leading adviser "wrap" investment platform, run on wholly proprietary in-house software built by its own Australia-and-London development unit
2.
Primary market. The group serves financial advisers and their clients through Transact's custodial wrap, IntegraLife's onshore and offshore tax wrappers, and Time4Advice's CURO adviser CRM, with revenue and profit before tax wholly attributable to the UK and Isle of Man
3.
Revenue engine. Ninety-nine percent of platform revenue is recurring ad-valorem administration fees levied monthly in arrears on GBP 77.8bn of closing funds under direction, with Transact at 97% of group revenue and CURO the remaining 3%
4.
Financial character. posH1 FY26 group revenue was GBP 85.8m, up 11% year on year, on an underlying profit-before-tax margin of 51%

5.

6.
Segment mix. mixedEffectively monoline: Transact platform administration is over 95% of revenue, with the CURO adviser-CRM business (Time4Advice) a small, currently loss-making non-ad-valorem adjacency at 3% of group revenue
