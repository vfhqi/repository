EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

AQ Group AB presents a compelling transformation narrative centered on structural growth in defense and data center markets, though execution remains anchored to cyclical European industrial demand. The board upgraded the EBT margin target to above 10% from 8% previously in July 2026, reflecting management's confidence in sustainable profitability expansion driven by greater design complexity. However, total sales growth reached only 6% in fiscal year 2025, which fell short of the 15% goal, exposing persistent challenges in achieving aggressive top-line ambitions during cyclical downturns.

Change materiality across the organization registers as moderately high with significant strategic repositioning underway. The board maintains strong industrial continuity despite co-founder board departures, with board transitions showing a shift toward independent technology leadership with new appointments. Structurally, AQ Group reorganized its decentralized structure to encompass 24 autonomous divisions currently, supporting localfor-local manufacturing proximity to customers.

The financial profile demonstrates robust margin resilience with Q2 2026 operating margin improving to 9.9% versus 9.3% in the prior year period, though organic growth momentum remains uneven across business cycles.

The top six ranked key drivers shaping forward earnings trajectory comprise structural electrification tailwinds, serial acquisition integration, cyclical exposure mitigation, and operational efficiency gains. First, inductive components for data centers scaled to represent 6% of AQ Group's total net sales in the quarter, establishing a high-plausibility structural growth platform with doubling production capacity.

Second, AQ is investing SEK 60 million in northern Sweden to meet strong defense programs pipeline, creating predictable multi-year volume visibility insulated from general capital goods cycles. Third, serial M&A consolidation leverages a net cash position of SEK 427 million, supporting future acquisitions through cycles, targeting 5% annual inorganic growth contribution through fragmented market roll-up. Fourth, cyclical commercial vehicle exposure presents moderate downside risk as cyclical demand from heavy truck and bus OEMs has contracted significantly compared to previous high volumes.

Fifth, management believes the upgraded 10% target is structurally sustainable due to greater design responsibility globally, reflecting margin quality improvement through valuechain repositioning. Sixth, contractual price adjustment clauses pass raw material price changes to customers with 3-6 month lag, providing margin protection mechanisms against commodity volatility with temporary friction.

Recent earnings delivery demonstrates exceptional beat rates on profitability metrics while exposing persistent top-line growth challenges. AQ Group achieved SEK 2,577 million in net sales during Q2 2026, marking a new historical peak for the company, with sales expansion driven by 9.1% organic growth, which was propelled primarily by volume increases. Critically, operating EBIT margin reached 9.9% in the quarter, beating the 9.4% consensus estimate on strong operating leverage, prompting immediate target upgrades.

However, cash flow from operating activities declined to SEK 23 million from SEK 232 million in Q2 2025, impacted by increased inventories and growing trade receivables due to volume ramp-ups, revealing working capital absorption pressures during rapid expansion phases. The quality of earnings remains highly credible with AQ Group consistently exceeding EBT targets for thirteen consecutive reporting periods historically, establishing a track record of conservative guidance and regular consensus beats.

Forward guidance credibility rates as moderately strong on margin targets but structurally weak on growth ambitions. The annual sales growth target remains unchanged at above 15% through business cycles, yet this demanding threshold has proven consistently unachievable during recent cyclical pressure periods. The upgraded margin target appears more realistic and defensible given the board upgrading EBT margin targets following 13 consecutive quarters above 8% before raising it to 10%.

Sensitivity analysis reveals sales in non- SEK currencies comprise approximately 74% of revenue, creating transaction and translation exposures, with a 10% change in exchange rates impacting annual sales by SEK 660 million if currency movements are sustained, representing material FX risk exposure that could distort reported performance. Forward tracking mechanisms focus on monthly transformer production capacity which currently stands at 70 systems, with the operational goal of 98% on-time delivery precision compared to a 94% actual outcome, providing transparent operational leading indicators for investors monitoring execution quality. - Earnings Calls Sell-side positioning reflects concentrated coverage with constructive bias but limited independent verification across multiple research houses.

The available research database reveals AQ Group ranks second among closest peers on our four-dimensional matrix currently, supported by DNB Carnegie raising AQ's 2027-2028 adjusted EBIT estimates by 3-5% following strong Q2 performance. The valuation framework employs AQ Group raising its EBT margin target to over 10% reflecting increased design complexity globally as justification for premium multiples relative to contract manufacturing peers. However, the bull-bear debate centers on whether prolonged downturns in commercial vehicles could compound with lag-based pricing pressure; if copper and steel prices spike rapidly while Volvo and Scania simultaneously slash production volumes, the 3-6 month pass-through lag will severely squeeze operating margins before cost-cutting measures can take effect, representing the primary downside scenario articulated by covering analysts.

Fundamental peer positioning establishes AQ Group as a second-tier outperformer within Nordic contract manufacturing, trailing only defense-driven Kitron but significantly ahead of margin-challenged peers. Kitron ASA claims the overall top position propelled by defense-related sales acceleration in Q2 2026, while Incap Oyj sits at the bottom of the group due to severe margin pressure in India. AQ Group's competitive differentiation stems from retaining above-par economies of scale supporting higher underlying operating margins, while avoiding standard commoditized assembly by taking greater responsibility for design of complex inductive components.

The peer ranking table reveals AQ Group delivers 9.9% actual EBIT margin, surpassing Note's 7.7% margin in Q2 2026, demonstrating sustained margin superiority versus volume-focused competitors. Balance sheet positioning provides further differentiation as AQ Group maintains a net cash position of SEK 427 million, supporting future acquisitions through cycles, contrasting with more leveraged peer capital structures.

Activism status registers as non-existent with no active activist presence in the AS library for AQ Group, though governance structure reveals persistent founder influence through the nomination committee continuing to deviate from the Code due to founder dependency, with major shareholders defending having two dependent founders on the committee due to their joint 38% stake. This concentrated ownership structure creates alignment benefits through substantial insider equity holdings but introduces governance friction around independent oversight mechanisms.

Key risks stratify across cyclical demand volatility, working capital absorption pressures, integration execution challenges, and commodity price pass-through timing lags. The most material near-term risk involves severe cyclical volume slowdowns depressing wiring system factory utilization in Mexico and New York sites, which could rapidly compress group-level margins if defensive and data center growth fail to offset automotive weakness.

Integration risks manifest through mdexx integration progressing slower than ambitious goals, diluting Group margins in 2025, exposing the execution complexity of serial acquisition strategies during operational turnarounds. Working capital management presents escalating concerns as management targets 3.5 inventory turns annually but currently operates at 3.2 turns, suggesting liquidity absorption during rapid volume expansion phases.

Structurally, the combination of exposure to sudden price spikes in purchased steel, aluminum, and copper coupled with contractual lag mechanisms creates short-term margin compression risk if input cost inflation accelerates beyond customer pricing adjustment cycles. The ultimate stress scenario involves simultaneous commercial vehicle production cuts, raw material price spikes, and data center demand plateau, which would overwhelm the company's defensive structural growth buffers and expose the inherent cyclicality of the underlying contract manufacturing business model.
