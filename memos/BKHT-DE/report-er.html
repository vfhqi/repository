Section 16 – Conclusion 1 finding

The Q1 2026 earnings and subsequent completion of the Bikeleasing divestment fundamentally transformed Brockhaus Technologies from a diversified technology holding with a high-growth digital platform engine into a cash-rich vehicle with a single, cyclical, and currently unprofitable core asset. The single most important thing that changed is that the investment thesis shifted entirely away from organic earnings growth and toward management's ability to rapidly redeploy approximately €240 million of net proceeds into accretive new acquisitions.

With continuing operations generating negative adjusted EBITDA margins of -19.9% in Q1 2026 and full-year 2026 guidance targeting only break-even profitability, the standalone equity story now depends entirely on capital allocation execution rather than operational delivery. The massive capital return proposal of approximately €180 million, including a €90 million share buyback tender at €24 per share, represents management's clearest signal that it recognizes the holding's structural discount and is willing to return value directly to shareholders.

This decision validates the Bikeleasing sale as a successful realization of embedded value at an enterprise valuation of €525 million, more than double the original €102 million equity investment in 2021.
