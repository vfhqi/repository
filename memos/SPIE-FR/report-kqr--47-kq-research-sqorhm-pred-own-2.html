Section 3 – Surtax Thresholds under the 2026 and 2027 Finance Laws

Section summary

The 2026 Surtax Framework

1.
Escalating fiscal burdens: The French Finance Act for 2026 extended the exceptional corporate tax contribution on large enterprises for another year. Under this law, companies generating French revenues above EUR 3bn are subject to a surcharge rate of 41.2% on their corporate income tax. This temporary measure targets the largest corporate operators to help address the national budget deficit.
2.
Broadened tier structures: The French government adjusted the lower threshold for the corporate surcharge while preserving the top tier rate. For the 2026 fiscal year, the threshold for the lower 20.6% surcharge rate was raised to EUR 1.5bn in revenue from the previous EUR 1bn limit. Large companies exceeding the EUR 3bn threshold face the full 41.2% surcharge, representing a combined statutory rate of 36.13%.
3.
Clarified regulatory parameters: The French Tax Administration updated its official administrative guidance to resolve calculation ambiguities for multi-technical service providers. On 12 August 2026, authorities updated guidance BOI-IS-AUT-60 to confirm the EUR 1.5bn revenue threshold for the second year. This administrative update allows companies to use qualifying treaty-based foreign tax credits to offset their final surtax liabilities.
