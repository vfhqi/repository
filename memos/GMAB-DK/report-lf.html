EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Chief Executive Leadership Assessment

Jan van de Winkel demonstrates strong relentless drive by transforming Genmab from a royalty-centric platform into a commercial-ready biotechnology powerhouse, delivering twenty-two percent revenue growth to $3,238 million in the first nine months of 2026. He exhibits bold decision-making speed by initiating four Phase 3 trials for petosemtamab within eleven months of the Merus acquisition and rapidly integrating ProfoundBio to advance Rina-S. His outstanding strategic adaptability is evidenced by the successful pivot from royalty dependence to independent commercialization ahead of impending Darzalex patent expirations, mitigating terminal value

Governance Robustness and Board Oversight

Genmab maintains robust corporate governance with five of nine board members satisfying strict Nasdaq independence requirements and substantial equity ownership guidelines ensuring director alignment with shareholder interests. The Audit Committee maintains deep financial expertise with all four members (Erenbjerg, Hoffmann, O'Farrell, Connelly) meeting Nasdaq independence standards to ensure rigorous accounting oversight.

However, twenty percent of shareholders voted against the 2025 remuneration report due to uncapped executive equity awards, reflecting persistent tension between Nordic governance norms and U.S.-style compensation practices. Genmab remediated a material weakness in internal controls over royalty revenue accounting identified in 2023, successfully restoring effective financial reporting systems by the end of

2024.

Insider Share Dealing Signals

Chief Financial Officer Anthony Pagano executed an open-market sale of 1,100 shares for DKK 1.99 million in February 2026, representing routine diversification following tax obligations rather than strategic concern. The absence of high-conviction open-market purchases by directors or officers over the past twelve months–with insiders acquiring shares only through automatic RSU vestings–may signal limited executive confidence in the post-Darzalex commercial transition. The board received 5,300 restricted stock units worth DKK 13.18 million in October 2026 with three-year vesting periods, establishing long-term equity alignment with shareholder value creation.

Expert Testimony Against Management's Story

A former Genmab director of strategic operations warned that building independent sales infrastructure remains highly resource-intensive and risky compared to the company's previous licensing agreements with pharmaceutical partners. A former forecasting director stressed that current immunology candidates like acasunlimab are unlikely to become major clinical players, leaving the company critically dependent on successful launches of Rina-S and petosemtamab to offset the Darzalex cliff. Former operations directors noted that employee engagement scores dropped from eighty-four percent to seventy-seven percent between 2022 and 2025, suggesting that rapid organizational growth creates commercial bottlenecks and morale challenges that could disrupt clinical timelines.

Largest Credibility Risk to Management

The single largest threat to management credibility is the untested assumption that Genmab can replicate blockbuster commercial success independently–critics argue the company's past profitability stemmed from partnership luck with Johnson & Johnson rather than proprietary marketing capabilities, while operating expenses have surged to $2.88 billion at the 2026 midpoint. Compounding this execution risk, Genmab reversed decades of debt-free operations by carrying $4.992 billion in borrowings as of September 2026, leaving financial flexibility highly sensitive to Darzalex royalty concentration ahead of looming patent expirations. If the newly acquired wholly-owned assets–particularly petosemtamab (with Deutsche Bank peak sales estimates of over $2 billion) and Rina-S (with projected peak sales exceeding $2 billion)–fail to achieve blockbuster commercial status, Genmab's earnings power will decline significantly as Darzalex royalties erode post-2029.
