Section 3 – Industry Structure + Concentration + Value Chain Economics

Section summary
  1. the high-pressure water jetting and hydraulics industries present structurally distinct margin profiles, where niche water-jetting fragmentation yields exceptional premium pricing while hydraulics remains cyclical and OEM-concentrated.

Industry Scale, Structural Growth, and Macro Drivers

Growth trajectory – macro correlation: Historical expansion in the fluid power sector tracks long-term global gross domestic product (GDP) growth. Growth trajectory – regional dynamics: High-end hydraulics markets, specifically within the Chinese industrial landscape, reached a total addressable market (TAM) of RMB43 billion in 2023 with a 5% historical compound annual growth rate (CAGR) from 2017 to 2023. Structural driver – technology upgrades: Regulatory shifts, global infrastructure investment, and advanced manufacturing initiatives underpin steady structural demand across mobile applications.

Structural driver – end-market tailwinds: Liquid cooling expansion within artificial intelligence (AI) data centers, chemical processing, and liquefied natural gas (LNG) terminal infrastructure provide robust structural tailwinds for high-pressure fluid transfer components.

Value Chain Economics and Margin Pools

Value chain stage – margin distribution: Capital and margin accrual within the fluid power value chain varies significantly between standard mechanical elements and engineered component niches. Most-profitable stage – specialized pumps: High-pressure water-jetting plunger pumps generate premium, cycle-resistant margin profiles, enabling leading manufacturers to consistently sustain operating EBITDA margins exceeding 25%.

Most-profitable stage – specialized hydraulics: Within the hydraulics sector, specialized high-precision components like pumps, valves, and motors achieve high profitability, with Jiangsu Hengli's pump, valve, and motor segment reaching a gross profit margin (GPM) of 48.80% in 2025. Margin-thin stage – standard cylinders: Standard hydraulic cylinders and lower-tier mobile components exhibit lower margin ceilings and are highly exposed to agricultural and construction equipment downcycles.

Pricing leverage – water-jetting dominance: Water-jetting plunger pumps enjoy high pricing leverage because high pressure (rated from 1 to 2,000 horsepower) represents a mission-critical, low-cost component in complex industrial systems.
