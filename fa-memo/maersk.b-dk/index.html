The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

The split is not shown where a two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple or price is missing, zero or negative (losses ahead): a multiple's change has no meaning there.

The two horizons are the model's fixed columns after the last reported period, so they end on the dates shown, not 18 months and three years from today.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Ocean is built as volume times (average loaded rate less a cash cost per FFE). The cash cost per FFE (bunker plus network, handling and overhead, net of Ocean's other revenue) is calibrated on reported quarters: about 1,770-1,810 in FY2025 and Q2 2025, about 1,800 in Q1 2026, 2,139 in Q2 2026 and 2,193 in Q3 2026 (bunker +25-44%, congestion, Hormuz contingencies). It falls to about 2,100 (Q4 2026), 1,950 (Q1 2027), 1,850, 1,800, 1,780 (Q4 2027) and 1,770 (FY2028) as congestion clears, the Suez return cuts fuel per FFE by about 8-10%, oil eases (Brent about 85 in 2027, 75 from 2028) and self-help arrives (about 0.6bn by FY2027 and 1.0bn by FY2028), partly offset by about 3% a year of handling, labour, EU ETS and canal-toll inflation; then 1,795 (FY2029) and 1,800 (FY2030).

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
