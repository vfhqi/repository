EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Gerresheimer AG confronts a multidimensional operational and governance crisis that has fundamentally reset its earnings trajectory, margin structure, and capital market credibility. The forced resignation of CEO Dietmar Siemssen in October 2025, followed by the early departure of interim CEO Uwe Röhrhoff in August 2026, passed operational control to CFO Wolf Lehmann and Board Member Achim Schalk under a joint emergency leadership structure.

This leadership vacuum emerged amid active BaFin investigations into premature revenue recognition practices, internal violations of accounting standards, and mandatory IAS 8 restatements that corrected historical financial errors. Net financial debt reached EUR 1,920 million at fiscal year-end 2025, expanding leverage to 4.95x adjusted EBITDA and forcing distressed covenant waivers that suspend leverage testing through Q3 2026.

The materiality of change stems from the EUR 1.5 billion strategic divestment of Centor and the global Primary Packaging Plastics business to Apax Funds, which represents the primary deleveraging mechanism, yet Deutsche Bank's analysis highlights that these divestitures will dilute remaining group adjusted EBITDA margins to approximately 14.5%, widening the performance gap versus clean peer balance sheets.

Free cash flow finished at negative EUR 86 million in FY25, with management lowering FY26 guidance to between negative EUR 50 million and negative EUR 100 million, placing Gerresheimer at the bottom quartile of peer cash conversion and necessitating severe factoring credit volume reductions that created major operating cash flow headwinds.

The forward earnings trajectory is anchored by five to eight critical drivers ranked by estimated EPS impact. The Centor and PPP divestment deleveraging is projected to reduce outstanding debt by approximately 70%, materially lowering annual interest expenses, while accelerating syringe demand driven by robust GLP-1 and biotech customer growth represents the primary organic revenue engine. The newly launched 'gto' transformation program targets initial EBITDA savings of EUR 10 million to EUR 20 million in FY26, yet standalone margins are expected to take at least three years to recover to historic 20% EBITDA margin levels.

Expanded production networks in Zhenjiang Dagang double primary packaging capacity in China, positioning the company to capture regional biologics demand, though persistently weak cosmetics demand and significantly declining oral liquid container volumes continue to pressure the glass division. - Earnings Calls Recent earnings delivery confirms severe operational stress across multiple segments. FY25 revenues reached EUR 2,321 million but retroactive corrections forced by German regulator investigations reduced reported FY24 revenues by EUR 44.6 million, while adjusted EBITDA erosion of EUR 31 million slashed reported margins by 110 basis points.

Q3 2025 actual adjusted EBITDA fell 11.2% short of consensus expectations due to weak demand, and persistent destocking headwinds led analysts to slash FY26 adjusted EPS estimates by 37%. The board suspended the FY25 dividend due to a reported separate-entity net loss of EUR 688 million, marking a complete halt to shareholder returns amid balance sheet restructuring.

Management credibility and archetype assessment reveals a consistent over-promiser pattern. Gerresheimer issued successive profit warnings throughout FY25, severely damaging capital market credibility with institutional equity investors, while industry experts note a consistent history of revising and resetting mid-term guidelines. The company has reset midterm targets downwards over the last 18 months, with FY25 adjusted EBITDA margin finishing at 16.8% and missing the initial target by over 500 basis points.

The adjusted EBITDA leverage covenant is reset to not exceed 4.75x as of November 2026, leaving minimal margin for operational error and tying strategic flexibility to successful divestment execution.

Sell-side positioning has coalesced into a cautious unanimous Hold consensus following repeated execution disappointments. Deutsche Bank maintains a Hold rating citing persistent operational uncertainty, Jefferies transitioned to Hold under lead analyst Christopher Richardson highlighting high execution and integration risks, and Bernstein upgraded to Market-Perform only after bankruptcy risks subsided post divestment announcement.

Deutsche Bank drastically cut its price target from EUR 34 to EUR 22 in February 2026 before raising it to EUR 26, while Jefferies reduced its target from EUR 34.10 to EUR 26.80 in May 2026. Deutsche Bank's Falko Friedrichs maintains a cautious Hold stance due to the unresolved BaFin accounting investigation, and Bernstein's Delphine Le Louet projects it will take at least 3 years to recover to historic 20% EBITDA margins.

Fundamental peer positioning places Gerresheimer at a structural disadvantage across revenue growth, margin profile, and earnings quality dimensions. Gerresheimer standalone organic revenue grew flat at 0.3% in FY25, placing it last among peers, while West Pharmaceutical grew organic sales by 13% in 2Q26, leading the primary drug packaging sector. Gerresheimer adjusted EBITDA margins compressed to 16.5% in FY25, lagging peer averages by over 1,000 basis points, whereas West Pharmaceutical expanded its adjusted operating margins by 230 basis points to 22.6% during 2Q26. Gerresheimer net financial debt reached EUR 1,920 million at end- FY25 with leverage at 4.95x adjusted EBITDA, while Stevanato leverage sits around 1x EBITDA and West Pharma maintains a supportive net cash position.

Gerresheimer earnings quality remains deeply impaired by active BaFin investigations into bill-and-hold accounting practices, creating a persistent credibility discount relative to unblemished peers. Activist presence in the available research library shows no evidence of active institutional activism targeting governance reforms or strategic alternatives, though shareholders rejected the proposed executive pay system at the 2025 AGM, forcing structural governance concessions.

Second, operational stabilization remains highly fragile, and if customer GLP-1 project launches face further delays, unutilized glass capacity will trigger ongoing margin dilution and covenant breach risks. Third, the EUR 390 million Bormioli integration failed to protect group profitability, resulting in 260 basis points of margin erosion, with slower synergy realization and high financing costs rendering the acquisition dilutive to multiples, establishing a pattern of M&A value destruction that undermines confidence in future capital allocation decisions.

The convergence of leadership instability, regulatory investigations, covenant pressure, and structural margin dilution creates a uniquely challenging setup where even successful divestment execution may not sufficiently restore peer-competitive returns on capital or margin profiles within the medium-term forecast horizon.
