Section 1 – Brief company description and history 14 findings

Section summary
  1. Asmodee enters earnings with exceptional top-line momentum, rapid balance sheet deleveraging, and unanimous sell-side upgrades following its successful spinoff from Embracer Group. Corporate origins – established foundation: Founded in 1995 in France and operationally headquartered
1.
in Guyancourt with corporate registration in Karlstad, Sweden, Asmodee has grown into the premier global publisher and distributor of tabletop games. The group holds the #1 market position in Europe and the #3 position in North America within the €13bn global tabletop market.
2.
Integrated ecosystem – competitive moat: Asmodee operates an integrated model spanning game creation across 22 in-house studios, a catalogue of over 400 owned intellectual properties, and a direct distribution network across 27 countries with reach into 100+ worldwide. This dual publishingdistribution model connects over 7,000 hobby stores and mass-market retailers, creating an unmatched routeto-market moat that third-party IP owners actively seek.
3.
Product mix – dual growth engines: Revenue is generated across resilient Board Games (~39% of sales, including proprietary blockbusters CATAN, Ticket to Ride, Dobble/Spot It!, and Exploding Kittens) and highgrowth Trading Card Games (~50-60% of sales, including Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, and Star Wars: Unlimited). Board games provide evergreen high-margin stability, while trading card game distribution injects substantial volume expansion across retail channels.
4.
Embracer ownership – historical interlude: Acquired by Embracer Group in March 2022 for ~€2.75bn, Asmodee provided stable cash flow generation during group-wide volatility but saw its historical bolt-on M&A engine temporarily paused. During this period, Asmodee represented 37% of Embracer Group's net sales and 29% of its adjusted EBIT.
5.
Spinoff execution – balance sheet reset: On February 7, 2025, Asmodee was listed on Nasdaq Stockholm (ASMDEE B) via a Lex ASEA tax-free dividend distribution to Embracer shareholders. Ahead of the listing, Embracer injected €400m of equity proceeds from its Easybrain divestment, reducing Asmodee's pro forma net debt from €893m (4.1x leverage) to €393m (2.2x leverage) and ring-fencing its independent capital structure.
6.
Financial trajectory – revenue expansion: Asmodee's net sales expanded from €1,215.2m in FY 2022/23 to €1,368.8m in FY 2024/25, before accelerating to €1,683.6m in FY 2025/26 representing 25.9% organic revenue growth. Top-line expansion significantly outpaced published sell-side consensus estimates of mid-single-digit organic growth going into public listing.
7.
Profitability trajectory – margin scaling: Group operating profit recovered from -€710.3m in FY 2022/23 (impacted by PPA impairment allocations under prior ownership) to +€116.7m in FY 2024/25 and +€167.6m in FY 2025/26. Full-year FY 2025/26 Adjusted EBITDA reached €285.4m with a 17.0% margin, tracking closely toward management's >18% medium-term target.
