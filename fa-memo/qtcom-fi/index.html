The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. 2026: nine months reported (EUR 165.7 million) plus Q4 of EUR 78.0 million (+1.2% on a Q4 2025 that already held 2.5 months of IAR and large distribution deals; about +2% organic). 2027-2030: developer, maintenance and consulting excluding IAR +8.0% / +7.5% / +8.0% / +7.6% (realised price about +4.5% a year on 5-10% list increases; net seats 0 to +1%; new customers, testing at about 15% a year and Cyber Resilience Act conversions +2-3%); royalties +2% then +4% a year (fixed unit prices, design wins of 2022-2025 reaching production against light-vehicle production of +1% a year per S&P Global); IAR +15% / +15% / +10% / +8% as the subscription base builds. Organic growth at constant currency about +4% (2026), then about +6.5-7% a year. EUR/USD assumed flat at about 1.15-1.16; no hedging.
  • EBITDA margin (underlying, before restructuring). 2026 underlying EBITDA EUR 61.5 million: nine months EUR 33.8 million on one basis (Q1 6.4, Q2 15.0, Q3 12.4) plus Q4 EUR 27.7 million (35.5%). 2027: operating costs +0.7% (EUR 182.2 million to EUR 183.5 million): about EUR 11 million of programme savings not yet in the 2026 run-rate and about EUR 1 million of residual security-unit saving, offset by about EUR 3.6 million of wage inflation, about EUR 4 million of reinvestment (APAC sales, AI research), about EUR 1.5 million of bonus normalisation and about EUR 1-2 million of commissions and IAR growth costs. 2028-2030: incremental EBITDA margin of 55% (QT's own 2022-2024 range is 49-59%; consensus implies 67% in 2028).
  • Quarterly phasing (Part 3). The margin step is largest in Q1 2027 (22.2% against 12.2%, the first quarter with full savings against none) and smallest in Q3 2027 (27.1% against 24.0%, because Q3 2026 already held most of the savings). Q4 carries about 32% of revenue and about 39% of EBITDA.
  • Depreciation and amortisation excludes the amortisation of acquired intangibles (about EUR 12.5 million a year in 2026-2027, my assumption of 12.0 / 11.0 / 10.0 thereafter); it includes depreciation of fixed assets and right-of-use assets, about EUR 1.4 million a quarter.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
