EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus Earnings and Valuation Context

Sartorius AG is projected to deliver adjusted earnings per share of 5.37 euros in fiscal year 2026 and 6.38 euros in fiscal year 2027 according to Deutsche Bank Research in October 2026, positioning the company for steady double-digit earnings growth as bioprocessing markets normalize. The company trades at a forward price-toearnings multiple of 40.8 times 2027 estimated earnings, which Bernstein Research notes already reflects the anticipated business recovery, suggesting that further stock appreciation requires organic growth acceleration of two to three percentage points above current market expectations.

Sartorius AG historically trades at a thirty-seven percent premium on 2025 EV/EBITDA compared to peer Lonza Group AG, with analyst Delphine Le Louet expecting an eighteen percent earnings compound annual growth rate through 2030, validating the valuation premium through structural single-use bioprocessing market leadership. Rothschild & Co Redburn forecasts a 16.5% earnings per share compound annual growth rate from fiscal year 2026 through fiscal year 2029, with group revenue projected to reach 4,107 million euros in fiscal year 2027 representing a 9.8% constantcurrency growth, underpinning robust mid-term expansion potential supported by recovering biopharma demand.

Ranked Drivers of Future Earnings with Plausibility Assessment

The primary driver of future earnings per share growth is consumables volume recovery across both operating segments, with management confirming that recurring consumables remain the backbone of company growth, primarily impacting group sales revenue and gross profit margin, with high plausibility because customer destocking has fully normalized, establishing high-margin recurring revenue streams as the foundation for operational leverage.

Single-use technology adoption represents the second major driver, with Sartorius AG holding a leading position of approximately 30% in the single-use bioprocessing market, impacting bioprocess solutions sales and underlying EBITDA margin with high plausibility because single-use penetration is expanding due to superior cost and flexibility profiles, supporting sustained competitive moats and margin expansion. Equipment and instrument recovery serves as the third driver with medium plausibility, as management noted encouraging signs of recovery in bioprocess equipment and laboratory instruments demand, though customers remain cautious regarding capital expenditures under a high-interest rate environment, creating cyclical upside potential contingent on macroeconomic stabilization.

Bioanalytics and AI-driven drug discovery workflows constitute the fourth driver with high plausibility, as management reported substantial lab division growth led by bioanalytics, primarily affecting Lab Products and Services sales and Research Solutions margins because AIdriven workflows require massive high-quality wet-lab datasets driving instrument placements, positioning Sartorius to capture digital transformation tailwinds in pharmaceutical research.

Polyplus transfection reagents integration represents the fifth driver with medium plausibility, allowing the company to establish a strong presence in advanced therapy space and move beyond traditional hardware into viral vector and cell therapy manufacturing, though integration challenges remain, creating optionality in high-growth advanced therapeutics markets despite execution risks.

Sartorius AG reported robust top-line growth during the third quarter of fiscal year 2026, with group sales revenue reaching 926.3 million euros representing a 9.9 percent reported increase, exceeding market expectations due to recovering bioprocess volumes according to Jefferies Research analyst James Vane-Tempest, demonstrating accelerating commercial momentum across core business lines. Group underlying EBITDA increased by 13.4 percent year-on-year to reach 279.9 million euros, beating consensus estimates, with the resulting underlying EBITDA margin of 30.2 percent expanding by ninety basis points year-on-year due to scale effects according to Bernstein Research analyst Delphine Le Louet, translating into quarterly earnings per ordinary share of 1.27 euros, reflecting powerful operating leverage from consumables recovery.
