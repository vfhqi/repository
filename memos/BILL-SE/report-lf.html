EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Section summary
  1. Ivar Vatne is a disciplined, cost-focused leader whose credibility rests on delivering a critical SEK 800 mm restructuring program amidst European overcapacity.
  2. Governance is robust and independent, though the upcoming departure of the EVP Legal creates a temporary gap in strategic project oversight.

Chief Executive and Delivery Record

1.
Vatne Quality: Ivar Vatne shows strong relentless drive and cost discipline, evidenced by his immediate implementation of an 800 million SEK savings program upon taking office.
2.
Strategic Adaptability: The chief executive demonstrates high adaptability to market conditions by shifting the North American strategy toward packaging materials when graphic paper demand plummeted.
3.
Standards of Excellence: Billerud results show mixed performance against peer benchmarks, as European margins of 2% in early 2026 trailed top-quartile competitors like Mondi.
4.
Delivery Record: Ivar Vatne has a strong record of cost execution, having reached a previous 1.5 billion SEK efficiency target ahead of its 2024 schedule.
5.
Strategic Risk: The single largest risk is European overcapacity suppressing price increases, which could prevent the 800 million SEK cost program from translating to EPS.

Governance and Credibility

6.

7.

Section 1 – Chief Executive's Career and Background

Section summary
  1. Ivar Vatne is a finance-heavy executive whose career at Arla and P&G prepared him for Billerud’s current need for rigorous cost optimization.
1.
Vatne Background: Ivar Vatne was born in 1978 and attained an MSc in Economics from the Norwegian School of Economics and Business Administration before entering the consumer goods sector. His early career was spent in various financial positions at Procter & Gamble over ten years, followed by a role as Vice President Finance for the Europe Region at Fiskars. This background provided deep functional experience in finance and operations within highly competitive, low-margin industries, which matches the current cost-out requirements at Billerud.
2.
Sector Experience: Ivar Vatne transitioned to the food industry as Chief Financial Officer for Arla Foods in both Sweden and the United Kingdom before joining Billerud in May 2019. His career has crossed three major industries–consumer goods, design-driven home products, and dairy–suggesting a versatile leadership style focused on financial discipline rather than deep industry-specific specialization. The appointment was an internal promotion, as Vatne served as Billerud's CFO and Deputy CEO before being named acting CEO in July 2023.
3.
Appointment Context: The Board of Directors appointed Ivar Vatne permanently in December 2023 following a thorough recruitment process that evaluated both internal and external candidates. Chairman Jan Svensson stated that Vatne was the best candidate due to his deep knowledge of the company’s strategic direction and his ability to drive continuous improvements to secure competitiveness. This internal hire provided continuity during a period of market volatility, though it also signaled a commitment to the existing strategic path.
4.
Other side of the story: Ivar Vatne's background is almost exclusively in financial functions, which may limit his perspective on the complex operational engineering required for large-scale mill conversions.

Section 4 – Chief Executive's Leadership Style

Section summary
  1. Vatne is a plain-spoken, cost-focused manager who prioritizes value over volume and rigorous financial discipline.
1.
Leadership Style: Ivar Vatne utilizes a plain-spoken and direct communication style, often emphasizing "items and areas we can control" such as fixed-cost discipline. He demonstrated fast decision-making by implementing a new regional organization in August 2023, just weeks after becoming acting CEO. This approach suggests he thinks more like a hired manager tasked with efficiency than an owner-operator focused on long-term transformative risk.
2.
Strategic Focus: The chief executive explicitly states a strategy of value over volume, choosing to walk away from positions that do not generate sufficient margins. His appetite for risk is low to moderate, as shown by the withdrawal from the Viken Skog BCTMP project in Norway to preserve capital. He sets guidance based on "efficiency enhancement" targets, having reached the 1.5 billion SEK goal in 2024 through 190 different work streams.
3.
Direct Voice: Ivar Vatne’s voice is captured in his commitment to releasing Billerud’s full potential through continuous improvements. He stated, "We remain focused on the items and areas we can control and will execute plans to realize efficiencies." Another quote highlights his pragmatic view: "I'm encouraged by the steady progress to strengthen cost competitiveness." These quotes reinforce a leadership style centered on operational discipline and realistic incremental gains.
4.
Other side of the story: Ivar Vatne's focus on cost-cutting is sometimes presented as broader strategy, which may mask a lack of innovative ideas for top-line revenue growth in a stagnant market.

Section 9 – Governance Structure and Warning Signs

Section summary
  1. Governance is clean with no material weaknesses reported, supported by formal ethics systems and high-level risk management.
1.
Control Systems: Billerud operates a Responsible Business Compliance Program focusing on anti- corruption, human rights, and international sanctions. The company maintains a "Speak-Up Line" for anonymous whistleblowing, with cases investigated by a dedicated Legal & Strategic Projects function. This system, reported quarterly to the Audit Committee, provides a formal structure to prevent failures rather than just reacting to them.
2.
Risk Management: The Board of Directors annually approves eight top-level policies, including Governance and Finance policies, to steer the Group. An Enterprise Risk Management framework evaluates risks based on likelihood and impact, with risk ownership identified for each strategic objective. These systems suggest that Billerud’s governance is designed to provide active oversight of capital allocation and operational execution.
3.
Warning Signs: Research surfaced no material weaknesses or restatements in Billerud's audit history over the last three years. Related-party transactions, such as the purchase of steam from Bomhus Energi AB, are disclosed and have not significantly affected the Group's position. The absence of dual-class shares ensures that voting rights remain proportional to economic ownership, protecting minority shareholders from founder control.
4.
Other side of the story: Billerud operates in high-risk markets and regions, which the company admits increases its exposure to potential corrupt practices and human rights violations.

Section 10 – Signs of Company Culture

Section summary
  1. Culture is a blend of engineering excellence and crisis resilience, though recent job cuts may be impacting internal morale.
1.
Employee Satisfaction: Billerud’s culture is described by CEO Ivar Vatne as having a 150-year history of engineering excellence and a customer-focused approach. The company tracks culture through an annual employee survey, which specifically monitors awareness and effectiveness of the "Speak-Up Line" whistleblowing system. This suggests a culture that values formal compliance and structured feedback rather than a purely entrepreneurial environment.
2.
Restructuring Impact: The company announced a headcount reduction of 650 positions in late 2025 to strengthen competitiveness. This significant downsizing, representing a 15% reduction in permanent employees over three years, was implemented through a "leaner organizational model" across production units. While necessary for cost discipline, such widespread cuts often indicate a shift toward a bureaucratic "costout" culture rather than a growth-oriented high-performance one.
3.
Performance Metrics: Billerud struggles with safety culture, as the Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR) for 2024 was 5.5, missing the target of 2.5. Management has responded by increasing safety walks and risk observations across European operations to improve proactive safety behaviors. This data point indicates that while the company excels at financial execution, it has room to improve in operational safety standards.
4.
Other side of the story: The resilience of employees during the Karlsborg fire and weather-related disruptions suggests a strong "boots-on-the-ground" culture that performs well under pressure.

Section 16 – Management Quality: Evidence For and Against

  3. Governance is a clear strength that supports management credibility, ensuring that capital is allocated with rigorous independent oversight.

The formal "Speak-Up Line" and risk management frameworks ensure that management quality is unlikely to decline unchecked. This structure provides a credible safety net for investors focused on the next three years of EPS development.

The strongest evidence against him is the unbalanced European industry situation, where pricing power remains soft despite internal cost gains. These factors leave the company's EPS development highly dependent on external market timing rather than just management's internal efforts.
