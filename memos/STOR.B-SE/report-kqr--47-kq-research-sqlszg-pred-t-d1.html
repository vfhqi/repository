Structural gaps in segment leadership

Johan Ekström, the Head of M&A, took over as interim Head of Services in May 2026 after Jesper Kronstrand's brief tenure, leaving the division without a permanent executive. A formal executive search and the announcement of a permanent hire would close this gap by clarifying the operational strategy. Achieving a stable management structure is crucial, as sustained leadership is necessary to restore the Services division's margins to historical double-digit levels.
2.
Unquantified construction recovery: Storskogen's infrastructure services businesses remain heavily impacted by the prolonged Nordic construction downturn, leaving the timing of an earnings recovery highly uncertain. While the company's Q3 2026 report on October 23, 2026, highlighted signs of improvement in Sweden, demand within construction-related units remains generally subdued. Monitoring upcoming housing starts data and regional building permits will help determine if this recovery is sustainable. Without a robust demand rebound, the Services segment may struggle to sustain its progress toward the target margin of over 10 percent.

Financial and valuation unknowns

Goodwill impairment risks: Storskogen's history of balance sheet resets creates uncertainty regarding the long- term performance and impairment risks of its legacy acquisitions. DNB Carnegie's note of October 20, 2026, highlights that investors continue to question the underlying quality of the 2021 to 2022 acquisition cohort. Reviewing the Q4 2026 and FY 2026 year-end reports will reveal if this positive turn is sustainable. Sustaining organic profitability is vital because prolonged negative organic growth would undermine the self-funding acquisition engine.
