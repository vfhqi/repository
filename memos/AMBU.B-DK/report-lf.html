EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

CEO Britt Meelby Jensen represents an exceptionally credentialed commercial operator with 25+ years in healthcare leadership, bringing rigorous strategic consulting foundations from McKinsey and proven life-science CEO experience across Atos Medical, Zealand Pharma, and Dako A/S. Appointed in May 2022 under a critical turnaround mandate following repeated guidance cuts and cash burn, she delivered a rapid operational transformation that tripled organic revenue growth from 4% to 13.1% and expanded EBIT margins fivefold from 2.7% to 13% within three financial years.

However, the executive profile carries inherent credibility tensions: historical CEO tenures averaged only three to four years per company, specializing in business model repositioning and strategic exits rather than multi-decade organic compounding, while expert network insights reveal a sharp operational disconnect in gastroenterology where inferior optical resolution, prohibitive per-unit costs of $1,800-$2,000, and entrenched reusable scope economics constrain single-use adoption well below management's ambitious TAM expansion targets.

The governance scaffolding combines robust oversight mechanisms with concentrated ownership dynamics that warrant careful scrutiny. Ambu maintains a two-tier governance structure with a nine-member board featuring 100% independence among shareholder-elected directors and strong medtech domain expertise across global strategy, commercialization, and R&D transformation, supported by clean unqualified audit opinions from EY and zero material accounting restatements or control weaknesses across the last three financial years.

Yet the dual-class share structure grants founding family descendants 61.5% voting control through 10-to-1 voting Class A shares despite holding only 17.3% of combined share capital, creating a 3.5x voting control differential over economic stake. Executive compensation exhibits strong pay-for-performance alignment, with total executive remuneration falling 21% in FY 2024/25 to DKK 25.5 million as variable payouts normalized from maximum levels, and the CEO-to-employee pay ratio standing at 18x well below the Danish listed peer average of 35x.

Longterm incentive plans enforce stretching organic revenue growth and EBIT margin performance conditions over a 3-year period, with FY 2024/25 PSU targets split across group organic growth (35%), Endoscopy Solutions growth (35%), and EBIT margin (30%), following governance adjustments that extended executive LTIP performance periods from 1 year to 3 years after investor consultation.
