Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The valuation multiples of veterinary consolidators respond dynamically to CEO strategy updates, organic growth inflection points, and the resolution of regulatory investigations.
  2. Understanding these multiple behaviors is critical because they determine the cost of capital and direct the trajectory of acquisitions and earnings growth.

Defining the Key Question

1.
Examining veterinary consolidators: "For CVS Group plc (CVSG-GB), how have the forward price-to-earnings and EV/EBITDA multiples of UK and European listed veterinary and healthcareservices consolidators behaved in the 6 to 24 months after three kinds of event, and what multiple did each trade at while its organic growth ran at low single digits and its earnings growth came mainly from acquisitions and share buybacks: (a) a new chief executive's first strategy update that reset or replaced medium-term targets; (b) a reacceleration of like-for-like or organic growth from below 2 percent to above 3 percent (CVS Group itself in 2019 to 2021, Pets at Home's vet group, Medicover, Terveystalo in 2023); and (c) the closing of a regulatory investigation or overhang?
