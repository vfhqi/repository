EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. the corporate restructuring has accelerated significantly through the partial IPO of Titan America on the NYSE and targeted portfolio actions.
  2. five key strategic drivers underpin the long-term investment thesis, led by US geographic expansion and digital plant optimization.
  3. recent financial delivery has been exceptionally strong, with EBITDA reaching a record €606 million in FY2025.
  4. management exhibits an honest-tracker posture, providing credible and achievable guidance under the new Titan Forward 2029 strategy.
  5. sell-side sentiment remains highly constructive as analysts expect the valuation gap to US building peers to close.
  6. the company occupies a topquartile position in regional peer momentum, showing industry-leading ROACE of 18.20%.
  7. there is no active activist presence due to a defensive 48.89% voting block held in concert by the founders.
  8. near-term headwinds center on sustained softness in the residential construction end-market and energy cost volatility.
  9. the overall investment case strengthens as valuation unlocks through US list re-rating and synergy realization.
