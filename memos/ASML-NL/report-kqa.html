Section 1 – Key question, brief, and competing hypotheses 10 findings

The Key Question driving this analysis is: ASML (ASML-NL): test competing hypotheses for ASML's earnings after the 2026 to 2027 supply-limited period: (1) a structural step-up (rising lithography intensity in logic and memory, AI capex, value-based pricing of 5% to 8% a year, installed-base growth) taking FY2029 EPS to about EUR 90 as consensus implies; (2) a cyclical plateau after pull-forward, Chinese stockpiling and capacity catch-up, with FY2029 EPS near EUR 68, as in past digestion years (FY2019, FY2023); (3) a downturn (memory price break, AI capex pause, export controls) with FY2029 EPS near EUR 49.

In the current 2026–2027 stage, ASML remains primarily supply-constrained, with low-NA EUV capacity targeted at 65 systems in 2026 and rising to at least 85 systems in 2027. Customers are actively securing these shipments through long-term agreements with volume and price commitments, supported by upfront downpayments.

Post-2027 Structural Growth Dynamics

1.

2.
Robust visibility: Long-term agreements and upfront customer commitments provide unprecedented revenue visibility that largely covers EUV capacity through 2027 and into 2028
3.

Cyclical Headwinds and Downside Risks

Segmental Divergence in Lithography Tech

1.
Segmental divergence: While advanced logic and DRAM experience unprecedented AI-driven demand, mainstream DUV system sales face cyclical digestion as Chinese mature-node capacity additions normalize
2.
Memory leadership: Strong contract-backed demand for high-bandwidth memory accelerates advanced DRAM capex, stepping up memory lithography intensity faster than logic-foundry nodes in the near term
3.
Capacity optionality: ASML plans low-NA EUV capacity of 110 systems by 2028 to support structural advanced-node ramping even as near-term tool shipments remain supply-constrained
4.
Adoption disparities: Intel leads High-NA deployment at 18A today, whereas foundry peers delay volume adoption to A10 nodes around 2030, allowing low-NA EUV single exposures to persist

Regional Dynamics and Geopolitical Cleavages

5.
Geopolitical rebalancing: China's system sales contribution is guided to normalize toward 20% as stockpiling of immersion tools moderates from the record peaks seen in prior fiscal years
6.
Regulatory overhang: The proposed MATCH Act represents a potential long-term sales threat that could block all DUV immersion shipments and servicing to Chinese mainland fabs
7.
Domestic substitution: Emerging Chinese toolmakers like Shanghai Yuliangsheng threaten mature-node DUV dominance by trialing domestic immersion lithography systems with local contract manufacturers
8.
Holistic matching: Despite structural and cyclical divergence across tech nodes, ASML maintains a stable DUV attach ratio because advanced EUV expansion mechanically drives matching DUV layers
