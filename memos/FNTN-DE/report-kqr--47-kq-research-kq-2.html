Section summary
  1. The key question is fully resolvable from available evidence because freenet AG's adjusted free cash flow guidance of €270m to €300m provides €34m to €64m of headroom above the €236m dividend minimum. Under the combined stress of a persistent €50m annual MNO wholesale headwind and elevated Euribor borrowing costs on €449.5m of unhedged floating-rate debt, freenet AG's €2 minimum dividend floor for FY2026 through FY2028 remains fundamentally secure, supported by disciplined cash conversion efficiency, structural IPTV margin expansion, and substantial covenant headroom within a conservative 1.5x net leverage framework. The MNO wholesale dispute originated from conservative accounting treatment on a single mobile network operator contract recognized as a €12.9m gross profit reduction in Q4 2025, which management fully modeled into future planning as a €50.0m annual EBITDA headwind running from FY2026 through FY2028. This drag is already incorporated into freenet's FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance range of €500m to €530m, eliminating surprise downside risk to baseline earnings targets. Freenet operates a regulatory-protected service provider model maintaining direct customer ownership across approximately eight million postpaid subscribers representing 20% consumer market share, with exclusive marketing rights for Deutsche Telekom and Vodafone mobile tariffs across roughly 400 MediaMarktSaturn electronics stores in Germany. This channel dominance provides asymmetric commercial leverage during ongoing near-weekly C-level renegotiations with network partners, reducing the probability of permanent wholesale margin erosion.

At year-end 2025 freenet carried €575.9m in gross financial liabilities with €449.5m or 78% exposed to unhedged variable rates tied directly to Euribor benchmark moves, yet executive management projected total annual group interest expense of €15.5m for FY2026 based on a variable borrowing corridor between 2.6% and 3.6%. The absolute interest burden remains modest relative to group EBITDA scale, and successful refinancing in April 2026 fully repaid the €250.0m short-term acquisition bridge loan through an over-subscribed €350.0m Schuldschein promissory note loan priced at the bottom of target marketing ranges, with floating Schuldschein tranches including €68.0m at Euribor plus 1% due 2029, €100.0m at Euribor plus 1.20% due 2031, and €25.0m at Euribor plus 1.40% due 2033, extending gross debt maturity out to 2033 while rebalancing the group debt profile to 58% variable and 42% fixed debt by mid-2026. Natural cash balance hedges and extended maturity structures further dampen rate sensitivity, with financial models indicating that a 50 basis point parallel Euribor shift impacts annual pre-tax earnings by only €1.3m net.

Freenet AG maintains a resilient cash conversion efficiency exceeding 60%, generating €270m to €300m in guided FY2026 adjusted free cash flow that provides €30m to €64m in surplus headroom above the €2 minimum dividend floor. The disciplined EBITDA-to-FCF bridge strictly caps net capital expenditure at €45m, lease obligations at €60m, net interest at €25m, and tax payments at €60m, while absorbing a temporary €40m working capital outflow that includes the full settlement of the €26m mobilezone advance payment liability.

Conservative working capital stress-testing demonstrates that fully absorbing the €26m mobilezone liability repayment alongside peak tax and interest obligations cannot breach the guaranteed dividend payout baseline. At the absolute lower bound of guidance, the €2 floor consumes 87.4% of free cash flow, leaving substantial unencumbered liquidity to absorb unforeseen operational friction without triggering covenant pressure or forced capital reallocation.

Rapid EBITDA expansion in waipu.tv will generate over €85m in incremental annual profit by FY2028, more than neutralizing the €50m MNO wholesale headwind, as Deutsche Bank Research forecasts that waipu.tv will increase its annual adjusted EBITDA contribution from €36.0m in FY2025 to at least €120.0m in FY2028, delivering an incremental €85.0m profit uplift that completely offsets the €50.0m MNO agreement drag. Executive management reported that waipu.tv expanded its paying customer base to 1.832 million subscribers in H1 2026, registering 77 thousand net new additions across the six-month period, with freenet's updated 2028 strategic ambition committing to a 15% to 20% annual subscriber compound growth rate for waipu.tv, aiming to capture up to 3.0 million total IPTV households by year-end FY2028.

High-margin subscription scaling and targeted advertising monetization drive IPTV gross margins above 41%, establishing TV and Media as freenet's primary incremental earnings engine and structurally offsetting mobile division headwinds well before debt refinancing deadlines in 2029 through 2033. Management reduced revolving credit facility drawings from €100.0m at year-end 2025 down to €60.0m as of June 2026 to conserve liquidity, while group leverage rose from 1.2x to 1.5x net debt to adjusted EBITDA following acquisition consolidation, remaining far below the 3.0x maximum covenant ceiling.

This 1.5x leverage buffer eliminates any near-term refinancing risk or forced debt paydown pressure that could compromise dividend commitments. The combination of structural operational momentum in IPTV, disciplined working capital management, extended debt maturity profiles, and conservative leverage positioning establishes a multi-layered defensive moat protecting the €2 dividend floor even under sustained adverse wholesale and interest rate conditions through FY2028.

While the company faces legitimate tactical headwinds in mobile wholesale pricing and floating-rate interest exposure, the aggregate financial cushion and operational growth trajectory render the dividend floor materially de-risked for concentrated long-only investors requiring predictable 18-to-36 month cash flow visibility and 8.5% yield support.
