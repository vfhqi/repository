What the call should settle, and why

The case in one line: Dutch substation volume carries the whole EBITDA gain to FY2030 on a high fixed-cost base, but the next twelve months are set by storage completions, the personnel overlap and an accounting line (D&A), and consensus sits about 15% above us on FY2027 EBITDA. Six judgements.

  1. Judgement 1, the fourth quarter and the FY2026 base. Nine months give about €20.4m of EBITDA (H1 €16.4m plus the third quarter's €4.0m). The houses' full years imply a fourth quarter from (€3.0m) at Oddo BHF to €5.7m at ING; the FactSet median of €22.5m implies about €2.1m; we have €1.5m. A fourth quarter near breakeven puts FY2026 at about €20.4m, 9% below the median, the first cut the memo expected; €4m or more leaves consensus whole and defers the reset to August 2027.
  2. Judgement 2, how FY2027 opens. Median €28.8m (KBC €29.7m high, Oddo BHF €22.1m low); ours €24.5m (5.1% margin). A numerical range whose midpoint sits near 6% on about €490m confirms consensus; a margin range centred near 5% cuts the median by 10% to 15% on 17-Feb-2027.
  3. Judgement 3, the storage book and margin. €86m dated to 2027 at September; ours €140m of FY2027 storage revenue at 21.5% against a consensus €154m. Each point of storage gross margin is about €1.4m of EBITDA; a year-end book above €127m with contingency in every tender is the upside branch, a flat book the downside.
  4. Judgement 4, the personnel overlap. About €8m in 2026 (Jefferies); we remove €5.5m in 2027 and keep €2.5m as permanent. All of it out is about +€2.5m of FY2027 EBITDA against ours; half of it permanent closes none of the gap to consensus.
  5. Judgement 5, D&A and FY2027 EPS. FY2027 EPS runs from €0.20 (Jefferies) to €0.49 , median €0.27; ours €0.02. At about €21m of D&A, consensus EBITDA gives about €0.17 (our model). Confirmation of about €21m is a near-certain EPS cut in February whatever the EBITDA.
  6. Judgement 6, grid pace. 833, 883 and 870 substations a quarter in 2026, against the memo's 950 test; consensus Smart Grid revenue FY2027 €232m, ours €235.5m. Above 950 a quarter in 2027 is the first genuine upgrade; 870 means the grid upgrade waits for the permit-free law after summer 2027.
