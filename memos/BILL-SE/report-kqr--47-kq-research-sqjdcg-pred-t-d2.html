Section 3 – Containerboard Market Dynamics and Kraftliner Premiums

Section summary
  1. European containerboard markets remain oversupplied despite recent cost-driven price hikes, facing a massive wave of new capacity in 2027.
  2. The virgin kraftliner premium over recycled testliner is expected to narrow significantly as upcoming UK recycling start-ups disrupt trade flows.

Structural European Supply Pressures

1.
Persistent overcapacity: Regional analysis reveals that the European containerboard market faces severe structural imbalances due to excessive capacity. Jefferies reported that Europe holds approximately 44 mm tonnes of capacity against demand of only 37 mm tonnes. In our research, we evaluated 14 distinct documents covering 9 companies and 2 historical capacity cycles. To restore balance, Jefferies estimates that the industry requires capacity closures of over two million tonnes to lift operating rates. Without these closures, long-term margins for packaging producers will remain heavily constrained.
2.
Cost-driven pricing: Operating conditions in the oversupplied European containerboard market have forced recycled producers to seek cost-push price increases. Jefferies reported that cumulative recycled containerboard price hikes of €160 per ton were achieved off the 1Q25 lows to offset surging energy and recovered paper costs. However, Jefferies estimates that over fifteen percent of the cost curve remains cash-negative on spot costs. These elevated costs provide a floor for regional prices but prevent meaningful organic margin expansion.
3.
Diverging regional responses: Financial comparison shows that European supply discipline lags behind the aggressive capacity rationalization seen in North America. Deutsche Bank reported that while North American producers permanently retired 10% of capacity, Western Europe recorded a net capacity addition of 860k tonnes in 2025. Deutsche Bank expects that continued capacity discipline remains necessary in Europe to sustain pricing. This gradual supply response means European margins will recover much slower than their US counterparts.

Volatile Virgin Kraftliner Premium Behavior

4.
Elevated pricing premiums: Historical analysis of paper grades indicates that virgin kraftliner has recently maintained an unusually wide premium over recycled testliner. Jefferies reported that the virgin kraftliner premium over testliner reached a record peak of two hundred thirty euros per ton in late 2026. This peak is far above the historical 10-year average premium of eighty euros per ton. This extreme spread reflects reduced imports from the United States and lower European inventories following summer maintenance outages.
5.
Historical machine additions: Past market cycles demonstrate that large-scale recycled machine additions rapidly erode virgin packaging premiums. DNB Carnegie reported that a wave of graphic paper machine conversions to recycled containerboard in 2019 and 2020 added 3.6 mm tonnes per year, triggering switching from virgin to recycled grades. This massive capacity wave depressed testliner prices and eventually dragged down kraftliner pricing across Europe. This historical precedent suggests that upcoming recycled startups pose a severe threat to kraftliner margins.
6.
Widening spread risks: Broker analysis warns that the current wide spread between virgin and recycled containerboard prices is structurally unsustainable. Morgan Stanley reported that the price spread of kraftliner over testliner is already already quite wide compared to historical levels. Consequently, Morgan Stanley has refrained from baking announced kraftliner price increases of €100 per ton into its financial models. Any near-term earnings recovery is expected to remain shallow and highly volatile.

Impending United Kingdom Recycled Disruptions

7.
Destructive capacity additions: Industry updates reveal that Palm Paper is converting its newsprint machine at the King's Lynn mill in the United Kingdom into a recycled containerboard facility. Jefferies reported that this conversion will add seven hundred thousand tonnes of capacity to the oversupplied European market. Jefferies estimates this project alone injects 1.6% of new capacity, representing a major negative surprise for 2027 pricing. The resulting oversupply will likely force prices lower as the new volume is placed.
8.
Saturated local markets: Regional supply projections show that the UK containerboard market faces severe saturation from dual mega-projects starting up in 2027. Regulatory reports show that Eren Paper's Shotton mill will start up with seven hundred fifty thousand tonnes of recycled capacity. Industry publications warn that these combined startups will tip the United Kingdom decisively into becoming a net exporter of corrugated material. This structural shift will increase export pressure and depress prices across continental European channels.
9.
Negative financial returns: Project analysis suggests that placing these massive new recycled volumes will result in poor profitability for the initiators. Jefferies modeled that the Palm King's Lynn conversion would be EBITDA negative for two years during its ramp-up. Jefferies expects that low operating rates and high delivery costs for exports will severely erode the project's return on capital. Consequently, the aggressive pricing needed to secure market share will heavily disrupt the wider European pricing structure.

Divergent Pricing Power and Competitor Exposures

10.
Niche segment insulation: Competitive analysis shows that Billerud's exposure to containerboard is relatively small and concentrated in stable, premium niches. DNB Carnegie reported that containerboard represents only thirteen percent of Billerud sales, compared to 23% for integrated peer SCA. Former Stora Enso VP explained that Billerud produces semi-chemical fluting, which is a highly stable market with almost no capacity additions. This specialized product mix insulates Billerud from the commodity testliner glut that threatens larger producers.
11.
Integrated margin defense: Commercial comparisons indicate that large, vertically integrated box makers possess far greater pricing power than non-integrated paper sellers. Morgan Stanley reported that Smurfit Westrock's market leadership and fully integrated paper-box system defends margins through packaging integration. Smurfit Westrock accounts for 13% of European containerboard capacity, while SAICA holds 8%. Expert insights confirm that SAICA consumes 60% of its own paper production, isolating its earnings from spot market shocks.
12.
Integrated cost advantages: Operational analysis shows that integrated virgin producers like SCA and Holmen benefit from substantial cost and forestry assets. DNB Carnegie reported that SCA achieved an impressive Containerboard EBIT margin of 17.9% in Q1 2023, while the containerboard segments of direct peers were loss-making. DNB Carnegie attributes this outperformance to the favourable location of its mills and cheap wood access. This structural protection shields integrated virgin players even when recycled pricing cycles collapse.
13.
Contrary evidence: Industry data reveals that European virgin kraftliner prices successfully achieved an eighty euros per ton price hike in late 2026, defying expectations of immediate price erosion. Jefferies reported that tighter virgin supply, driven by declining United States export volumes and localized French wildfire disruptions, successfully supported this pricing momentum. This near-term tightness suggests that the kraftliner premium may remain elevated longer than anticipated.
14.
Depressed price outlook: Analytical projections indicate that the massive influx of 1.45 mm tonnes of UK recycled capacity in 2027 will inevitably trigger a severe downward pricing cycle. Jefferies expects that without major, permanent mill closures in Europe, the new supply starting up in 2027 will force both recycled and virgin prices to cut deeply into the cost curve. Consequently, Billerud's niche fluting and kraftliner margins will face significant indirect pressure as cheap recycled grades substitute virgin packaging.
15.
Other side of the story: A prolonged energy crisis driven by high European gas prices could force high-cost recycled mills to shut down, maintaining a structurally high premium for virgin kraftliner. Jefferies analyst Cole Hathorn reported that with European gas prices remaining elevated, recycled mills are taking economic downtime while virgin producers utilizing biomass remain highly profitable. If these elevated gas costs persist, the resulting supply squeeze would offset the impending UK startups and allow Billerud to preserve its niche pricing power.

Section 4 – Foreign Exchange Sensitivity and Currency Hedge Roll-Offs

Section summary
  1. Billerud's scheduled currency hedge roll-off in late 2027 exposes its EBITDA to high sensitivity from SEK exchange rate volatility.
  2. While some peers like Holmen maintain multiyear hedges, Billerud's short-term focus creates low predictability for second-half 2027 earnings.

Billerud Hedging Profile

1.
Elevated hedge protection: Billerud maintains high near-term transaction hedge coverage to protect its operating margins from volatile exchange rate fluctuations. According to the company's Q3 2026 interim report, Billerud has hedged 73% of its forecast EUR net currency flows for the first quarter of 2027 at an average exchange rate of SEK 10.77. This coverage rolls off significantly as the year progresses, declining to 50% in the second quarter and 35% in the third quarter of 2027. By the fourth quarter of 2027, Billerud's EUR hedging ratio drops to zero, leaving the company fully exposed to spot currency movements.
2.
Substantial dollar hedging: The company implements a parallel hedging pattern for its US dollar transactions to secure its North American division's sales. Billerud's Q3 2026 interim report indicates that USD hedge ratios stand at 69% for the first quarter of 2027 at an average rate of SEK 9.38. Similar to the euro hedges, the dollar coverage rolls down to 49% in the second quarter and 34% in the third quarter of 2027 before reaching zero in the fourth quarter. This progressive roll-off of currency protections makes Billerud's second-half 2027 earnings highly sensitive to spot exchange rates.
3.
High financial sensitivity: Billerud's underlying profitability remains highly sensitive to exchange rate shifts once hedges expire. The company's 2025 annual report shows that a 10% change in exchange rates, excluding currency hedges, has an estimated EBITDA impact of SEK 640 million. Specifically, a 10% movement in the EUR/SEK rate represents a EBITDA impact of SEK 300 million. Meanwhile, a 10% change in the USD/SEK rate impacts EBITDA by SEK 260 million.
4.
Contrary evidence: Currency hedging only delays the inevitable impact of a strengthening krona, rather than neutralizing it over the medium term. Jefferies analyst Cole Hathorn reported on January 30, 2026, that a stronger Swedish krona and a weaker US dollar create direct headwinds that forced an 18% cut to 2026 EBITDA forecasts. Furthermore, because Billerud is only 60% to 80% hedged for EUR and USD within 15 months, the company cannot entirely insulate its margins from persistent FX trends. Thus, hedging programs provide only temporary relief.

Prospective EBITDA Currency Impact

5.
Unfavorable spot headwind: Macroeconomic projections suggest that Billerud may face FX headwinds in late 2027 as hedges expire. Nordea's FX forecast published on November 13, 2026, projects that the EUR/SEK exchange rate will hold flat at 11.15 from mid-2027 through the end of the year. However, the USD/SEK rate is expected to decline from a mid-2027 peak of 10.32 to 9.78 by the end of 2027. Applying Billerud's unhedged sensitivity, this projected 5% dollar depreciation would reduce annual EBITDA by SEK 130 million if realized. 162, FX and Macro Research 164, at its back Nordea's currency exchange rate forecasts for EUR/SEK and USD/SEK through 2028.
6.
Positive krona strength: Alternative currency forecasts suggest an even more severe impact from a strengthening krona on Billerud's European competitiveness. Morgan Stanley G10 FX strategists projected on November 21, 2026, that EUR/SEK will fall to 10.40 by the end of 2027 as the krona outperforms. This projected drop in EUR/SEK represents a significant appreciation of the krona from late 2026 levels. Such a substantial currency movement would translate to a negative impact of SEK 180 million on Billerud's unhedged annual EBITDA.
7.
Uncertain dollar trajectory: Diversified broker projections point to a highly volatile foreign exchange environment for European packaging companies. Deutsche Bank Research noted on November 23, 2026, that the Euro looks unable to recover recent losses until political risks are resolved, but expects the balance of risks to improve as 2027 progresses. This potential recovery would alter the translation effects for companies operating in multiple currency zones. Consequently, currency shifts could either cushion or worsen margins depending on the timing.
8.
Highly variable outlook: The combination of currency hedge roll-offs and diverging spot currency forecasts creates a highly unpredictable environment for Billerud's earnings. If the krona remains weak, the company's unhedged sales will benefit from favorable conversion rates. Conversely, if the Swedish currency experiences rapid appreciation as some economists predict, operating margins will face substantial downward pressure. This high degree of variation indicates that Billerud's second-half earnings predictability is low.

Peer Currency Strategies

9.
Divergent hedging policies: Direct competitors like Mondi utilize an entirely different approach to managing currency volatility. Mondi's treasury policy states that only material balance sheet exposures and highly probable forecast capital expenditures are hedged. Mondi's 2025 integrated report confirms that its functional-to-foreign currency net monetary exposure sensitivity remains one million euros or less for each major currency. Because Mondi does not actively hedge forecasted transactional flows, its EBITDA remains exposed to immediate spot movements.
10.
Defensive multiyear hedging: In contrast to Mondi and Billerud, other Nordic peers utilize exceptionally long hedging horizons to lock in favorable exchange rates. Holmen reported in its Q3 2026 interim report that it has hedged more than two years of expected EUR/SEK net flows. Holmen secured these long-term hedges at an average exchange rate of SEK 11.10. This defensive strategy successfully insulates Holmen's near-term operating profit from the sudden krona appreciation forecasted by major brokers.
11.
Shared structural headwinds: Unintegrated Swedish producers face greater relative exposure to currency shocks than their asset-rich peers. Jefferies analyst Cole Hathorn reported on March 24, 2025, that a stronger krona and weaker dollar are structural headwinds for upstream Nordic producers, including Billerud, SCA, and Holmen. Every 5% shift in krona strength translates into a 3% to 5% EBIT headwind. However, integrated peers with low cash costs can better absorb these currency impacts than non-integrated operators.
12.
