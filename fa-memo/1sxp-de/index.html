The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. Underlying organic growth (constant currency, without the compensation) of about 4.5% in FY2026, 7.0% in FY2027, 6.5% in FY2028 and FY2029, and 6.0% in FY2030. FY2027 reported growth (5.5%) is about 1.5 points lower because the FY2026 base contains about EUR 15m of compensation. Drivers: the injectables packaging market grows about 9% a year. The drag from vaccines and mRNA polymer fades: mRNA fell from about 8% to about 5% of sales in FY2026, about -3 points of growth, and from FY2027 it plateaus. GLP-1 revenue (about EUR 125m to 135m in FY2026) grows 12% to 15% a year. The high-value share rises from 57% to about 60% by FY2029. Like-for-like price is about +0.5% in FY2027 and 0% thereafter. Currency is assumed neutral (spot euro-dollar about 1.12 would add about 0.5 to 1 point in the first half of FY2027). North Carolina adds no revenue inside the window.
  • EBITDA margin (company basis, including joint-venture income). The FY2026 underlying base is about 27.7% (reported 27.5%, with the EUR 12.25m write-down and the EUR 15m compensation netting to about EUR +2m). Since FY2022 the realised incremental margin on extra revenue has been about 32%; marginal contribution through plants already built is 45% to 55%. I use about 30% in FY2027 (the third-quarter FY2026 exit rate of 26.8%, start-up costs at the new Hungarian ready-to-use cartridge plant, syringe tender pressure after Stevanato's November 2026 customer cut), then 36% to 37% in FY2028 to FY2030 as Hungarian syringes, St. Gallen cartridges and Lebanon vials fill. The efficiency programme adds about EUR 10m to 13m net by FY2029. Joint-venture income grows from about EUR 15m to about EUR 21m. Wage inflation is half recovered through February labour indexation.
  • Quarterly phasing (Part 3). The fourth quarter of FY2026 is revenue of about EUR 272m (+10%; about +4% without the compensation) and EBITDA of about EUR 81.5m (30.0%; about 26% without it). In FY2027, the first quarter laps a quarter flattered by extra shifts. The second laps the trough (DDS -9.8% organic) and about EUR 7m of write-down. The third laps the 8.3% organic beat but also the 26.8% margin. The fourth laps the compensation. Reported growth and margin will therefore look strong in the second and third quarters of FY2027 and weak in the fourth, with no change in the underlying trend.
  • Depreciation. Nine months of FY2026 EUR 65.5m (+14%); the third quarter about EUR 22.2m. It rises with plants commissioned through FY2027 and then slows as capital spending tapers. It is 8.5% to 8.8% of sales.
  • Net financial items. These are on the company basis: interest on parent cash-pool debt, pension and lease interest, facility fees and currency-derivative results. Joint-venture income is excluded, because it sits in EBITDA. Nine months of FY2026 EUR -6.2m; FY2025 about EUR -13m (derived). The charge falls as free cash flow turns net debt (about EUR 104m at September 2026) into net cash in FY2029. The parent facilities (EUR 412m, ending 31-Dec-2027) are assumed rolled on similar terms.
  • Tax. The rate applies to pre-tax profit including joint-venture income: about 21.8% in FY2025 and about 22.9% in the nine months of FY2026 (derived). Pillar Two sets a floor. The 23.5% the company quotes for FY2025 is measured on pre-tax profit excluding the joint ventures, and must not be used with an EBITDA that includes them.
