Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro) 40 findings

Section summary
  1. parcel volumes are growing robustly, driven by Finnish recommerce, while B2B logistics remains cyclical and subdued
  2. competitive intensity is rising as DHL acquires Venipak in the Baltics, and Swedish warehousing faces persistent overcapacity
  3. structural digitalization is replacing paper mail as digital substitutes siphoning high-margin print volumes accelerated by OmaPosti adoption
  4. regulatory pressure intensifies with the Digital First Programme triggering steep letter declines despite universal service relief
  5. cost bases are under pressure from high subcontractor inflation and network operational integration requirements
  6. cyclical recovery is moderately emerging in the Finnish economy but contract logistics demand remains slow to rebound
  7. macroeconomic headwinds persist with geopolitical uncertainties and trade wars alongside volatile Swedish Krona FX movements
