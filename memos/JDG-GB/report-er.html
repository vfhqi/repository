The group focuses on acquiring small, successful, and cash-generative businesses that are strong exporters in niche markets. Since its re-admission in 2005, the group has completed 25 acquisitions using disciplined capital allocation. Rather than integrating these companies, Judges preserves their operational autonomy and relies on local management to drive growth. This disciplined strategy has historically kept Return on Invested Capital above 15% on a long-term basis
