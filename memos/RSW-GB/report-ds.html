The rapid revenue acceleration in Position Measurement, which grew 36% year-on-year in the third quarter of 2026, provided a highly favorable mix effect given its status as the highest-margin segment in the portfolio. This mix improvement helped narrow the valuation discount relative to historical levels and peers.

Section 3 – What happened and the consensus deviation

Section summary
  1. Renishaw's performance deviated sharply from expectations due to a massive acceleration in high-margin semiconductor and defense orders.

The pivotal development in the six-month window was the unscheduled trading statement published on April 20, 2026, which announced a material upgrade to full-year guidance. Since the H1 results on February 11, the company had experienced an unprecedented acceleration in demand from semiconductor manufacturing equipment builders and the aerospace and defense sectors, causing a rapid expansion of the order book. Full-year revenue guidance was upgraded to £775m-£805m, while adjusted profit before tax was raised to £145m-£165m.

Jefferies analysts noted that this guidance upgrade represented a 4% revenue increase and a 7% adjusted profit before tax increase at the mid-point. More importantly, the implied margins for the second half of fiscal year 2026 surged to 21.40%, indicating that the group's structural cost savings and high operational gearing were flowing directly to the bottom line. This margin level was exceptionally close to management's long-term target of above 20%, which many analysts had modeled as highly elusive. The Position Measurement segment was the standout, delivering 36% year-on-year revenue growth in Q3.

Deutsche Bank's Richard Paige highlighted that the upgraded guidance implied an exceptionally high operational gearing of approximately 67% in the second half. Historically, when Renishaw has upgraded its H1 guidance range, the final full-year outturn has consistently landed toward the upper end or exceeded the revised range. This pattern of conservatism suggested that the company was highly likely to beat the mid-point of its revised guidance, further boosting buy-side sentiment.
