Section summary

  2. upmarket bakeries and supermarket meal deals represent growing market-share threats to traditional value offerings
  3. multi-aggregator delivery expansion drives volume reach but dilutes core operating margins through elevated commission fees
  4. state-regulated payroll changes and packaging taxes impose material overhead burdens on low- margin food retail models
  5. major centralized logistics projects expand peak capacity but increase short-term operating depreciation before utilization ramps up
