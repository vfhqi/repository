Section 4 – Financial delivery

Section summary
  1. Melrose delivered an H1 2026 print that beat top-line and operating profit consensus, but paused its share buyback due to the Garden Grove incident.

H1 2026 Financial Results Summary and Consensus Comparisons

Reporting period baseline – H1 2026 print date and scope: Melrose Industries PLC reported its unaudited interim results for the six months ended 30 June 2026 on 31 July 2026.

Group revenue delivery – solid beat driven by Engines acceleration: Group revenue reached £1,873m (+10% constant currency year-on-year), outperforming Visible Alpha sell-side consensus of £1,833m by 2.2%.

Group adjusted operating profit – solid beat vs published expectations: Adjusted operating profit rose 16% year-on-year to £347m, beating published sell-side consensus of £342m by 1.5% and reaching an operating margin of 18.5% (up 50 bps year-on-year).

Adjusted diluted EPS delivery – significant beat over prior year: Adjusted diluted EPS expanded 22% year-onyear to 17.7p (compared to 15.1p in H1 2025), significantly outperforming the 15.1p print from the prior period.

Ex-Garden Grove operational delivery – clean underlying print strength: Excluding the £16m top-line and £9m operating profit impact from the Garden Grove thermal incident, underlying Group revenue was £1,889m (+11%) and adjusted operating profit was £356m (+19%), representing a 4% beat against consensus.

Full-year guidance framework – confirmed on an ex-Garden Grove basis: Management reiterated full-year 2026 guidance of £3,750m-£3,950m revenue, £700m-£750m adjusted operating profit, and £150m-£200m free cash flow, but explicitly noted that this framework excludes the unquantified Garden Grove operational disruption and legal liabilities.

Divisional Segment Performance: Engines Beat vs Airframes Drag

Engines revenue performance – strong beat across OE and MRO: Engines revenue reached £896m (+19% constant currency), beating sell-side consensus of £862m by 4%, driven by strong broad-based growth across original equipment (+23%) and aftermarket MRO (+15%).

Engines operating profit – margin expansion ahead of targets: Engines adjusted operating profit rose 21% to £303m, beating consensus of £289m by 5%, with operating margin reaching 33.8% (+40 bps year-on-year) vs 33.5% expected by the market.

RRSP variable consideration – normal seasonal cadence confirmed: Variable consideration within Engines reached £206m (up from £182m in H1 2025), representing 57% of full-year mid-point guidance (£340m- £380m) and 68% of divisional profit, matching historical seasonal patterns.
