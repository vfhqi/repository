Institutional Shareholder Services operates as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Deutsche Börse following the February 2026 completion of the remaining 19.69% minority stake acquisition for €1.15 billion at 20x adjusted EBITDA, a transaction that eliminated joint-venture friction but exposed the parent to renewed activist pressure from TCI's 5.15% stake disclosure. The consolidated ESG and Index business line delivered €585 million in net revenues during FY25, representing stagnant reported growth of just 1% despite underlying constant-currency expansion of 4%, underscoring the material structural headwinds facing the proxy advisory and sustainability data franchise.

The investment case confronts severe disintermediation risk as tier-one asset managers including J.P. Morgan deployed proprietary Proxy IQ platforms, actively bypassing third-party advisory services through AI-powered internal voting tools, a trend that threatens to render external proxy advisors obsolete if adoption accelerates across the institutional client base. Regulatory pressure intensifies through active SEC subpoena enforcement litigation filed in September 2026 demanding internal client voting records, while the DOJ withdrew its historical antitrust business review letter, exposing the duopoly to heightened federal scrutiny and First Amendment retaliation risks from compelled data disclosure.
