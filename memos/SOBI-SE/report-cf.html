Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro) 44 findings

Section summary
  1. Rare-disease demand is inflecting through next-generation factor switches, though oral competitors threaten established therapeutic franchises
  2. Competitive threats are intensifying across cardiometabolic and hematology markets, led by quarterly siRNAs and potent bispecific antibodies
  3. Technological disruption from gene therapies is proving commercial underperformers, cementing the long-term relevance of advanced factors and mimetics
  4. Regulatory friction is delaying major launch timelines while US pricing reforms compress specialty gross-to-net margins
