[Cross-ref: Section 4, Section 7]
3.
Operating Margin Compression: The group's EBIT margin fell to 4.4% in FY25/26 from 4.6% in the prior year, remaining well below historical levels above 5% and underperforming the European peer median EBIT margin of approximately 3.85%, constrained by automatic Belgian wage indexation of EUR 70 million and aggressive promotional price matching under the Lowest Price Guarantee. [Cross-ref: Section 4, Section 6, Section 7]

Automatic Wage Indexation Exposure: Employee benefit expenses represent 16.6% of group revenue, with Belgium's automatic wage indexation system forcing EUR 75 million in net operating expense increases in 1H25/26, creating severe structural margin headwinds versus competitors in neighboring countries with more flexible labor cost structures and franchised business models. [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 6]
5.
Negative Working Capital Disadvantage: Colruyt's working capital profile trails leading negative-working- capital peers with elevated inventory days, constraining cash conversion efficiency relative to scale leaders like Tesco and Sainsbury who benefit from superior supplier payment terms and faster inventory turnover. [Cross-ref: Section 7]
6.
Consensus Revision Momentum Weakness: Colruyt experienced consensus EPS revisions of -1.0% (L1M), -3.4% (L3M), and -0.4% (L12M), ranking fifth out of six European grocery peers on overall earnings momentum, reflecting persistent sell-side skepticism about structural margin recovery amid intensifying Sunday trading competition and sticky labor cost inflation. [Cross-ref: Section 7, Section 8]
7.
Pricing Power Constraint: The Lowest Price Guarantee mechanism forces Colruyt to match all competitor promotions, preventing full pass-through of purchase cost inflation and compressing gross margins during periods when the CPI/PPI differential turns negative, creating a structural disadvantage versus hard discounters Aldi and Lidl who maintain joint sourcing efficiencies without guarantee obligations.
