This structural disadvantage stems from SCE funding historical wildfire settlement claims with debt excluded from its regulatory capital structure, a financing architecture that avoids immediate rate base dilution but imposes a permanent earnings tax at the parent level.

Management has not articulated a concrete timeline or capital structure optimization pathway to eliminate this drag, particularly problematic given Edison International Parent and Other's deepened core loss due to higher interest expense on holding company debt, though partially offset by lower preferred dividends in recent quarters.
