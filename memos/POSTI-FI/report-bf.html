Section 1 – Business Model + Organisation

Section summary
  1. Posti operates a hybrid postal and logistics model across six countries, driving transformation via its Astra program and integrated operations unit to offset structural mail declines.

Operational structure and business unit overview

1.
Unit-level operations: Posti Group Corp operates as a parcel, freight, postal, and contract logistics provider across Finland, Sweden, and the Baltics. Divisional structure: Operations are organized into three primary reportable segments: eCommerce and Delivery Services, Fulfillment and Logistics Services, and Postal Services. eCommerce and Delivery Services: The unit delivers parcel services across Finland and the Baltics, alongside groupage freight and value-added logistics in Finland. Fulfillment and Logistics Services: The segment provides contract logistics and in-house logistics operating across 620,000 square meters of warehouse space in Finland and Sweden. Postal Services unit: The division delivers corporate and consumer letters, direct marketing, publications, and digital messaging nationwide in Finland. Geographic footprint: Posti operates across six countries, generating 90.2% of net sales in Finland, 4.6% in Sweden, and 2.5% in the Baltics.

Revenue mix and macro-predictability

2.
Divisional revenue mix: In 2025, eCommerce and Delivery generated EUR 640.9 million (43%), Postal Services EUR 529.6 million (37%), and Fulfillment EUR 301.7 million (20%). Growth portfolio transition: Strategic growth segments reached over 67% of Group net sales in Q2 2026, up from 65% in Q2 2025. Contractual vs spot mix: Fulfillment and B2B freight rely on multi-year contracts, while B2C parcel and postal revenues are transactional and volume-sensitive. Macro-sensitivity dynamics: Parcel and freight flows closely track Nordic consumer confidence and GDP growth, exhibiting cyclical sensitivity in B2B freight. Universal service obligation exposure: Universal service obligations accounted for just 3.6% of Group net sales and 5.1% of delivery volumes in Q2 2026. Seasonal revenue concentration: Financial performance is heavily back-loaded, with the fourth-quarter peak season driving annual operating profit.

Customer acquisition and channel distribution

3.
Commercial win model: Posti wins enterprise customers through integrated cross-segment selling, offering combined warehousing, freight, and last-mile parcel delivery. Physical delivery infrastructure: Distribution relies on an extensive network of over 3,300 service points and expanded automated outdoor parcel lockers. Digital post integration: If customer migration to OmaPosti continues, digital lock-in deepens following the April 2026 acquisition of Kivra’s Finnish digital post business. Self-service first mile: First-mile B2C efficiency is driven by expanding consumer self-service locker drop-offs for growing recommerce traffic. Government digital transition: Public sector demand is accelerating digital channel adoption following the April 2026 'Digital First' legislative reform.

Volume transition and operating leverage

4.
Structural mail decline: Addressed letter volumes fell 24% in Q2 2026, accelerating a decade-long paper volume contraction of approximately 75%. Parcel growth trajectory: Total parcel volumes delivered in Finland and the Baltics grew 12% in Q2 2026 to 19.0 million units, driven by C2C recommerce. Astra renewal program: Posti launched the Astra program to merge Finnish delivery networks, targeting EUR 40 million in cost efficiency by 2029. Posti Operations launch: On June 1, 2026, Posti launched the Posti Operations unit, transferring 9,000 operational employees into a single synergistic structure. Sorting automation leverage: Operational efficiency in declining mail is protected by machine vision sorting automation reaching over 90% accuracy.

Strategic delivery and management change

5.
L3Y operational execution: Posti completed its initial public offering on Nasdaq Helsinki in October 2025 while scaling modern mega-warehouses like Järvenpää.
6.
Executive leadership structure: President and CEO Antti Jääskeläinen leads the executive team alongside CFO Timo Karppinen. Operational leadership realignment: Yrjö Eskola was appointed Chief Operations Officer on April 1, 2026, to directly execute the Astra renewal program. Commercial leadership updates: Jani Koivu assumed leadership of eCommerce and Delivery Services in December 2025, and Kaj Kulp took over Strategy in March 2026. Legal team transition: Anniina Bergström joined as Senior Vice President, General Counsel, in June 2026, succeeding interim counsel Minna Jokinen. Mid-term financial targets: Management targets organic growth >=2% at Group level (>=5% outside Postal) and adjusted EBIT growth >5% through 2029.
7.
Other side of the story: Accelerating postal volume decline outpaces delivery network cost rationalization; if Astra savings lag, fixed-cost deleverage will compress group margins.
