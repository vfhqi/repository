Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Shifting market narratives and technological anxieties have driven the European telecom sector to lag the broader equity market since June 2026.
  2. Rising interest rates and elevated capital expenditure requirements continue to press on industry return profiles and corporate earnings expectations.

Defining the Underperformance Framework

1.

After a strong run from 2023 to May 2026 (the median share rose about 60 percent in three years), European telecoms shares have lagged the Stoxx Europe 600 since June 2026: the median share is about 8 percentage points behind over the six months to mid-November 2026.
