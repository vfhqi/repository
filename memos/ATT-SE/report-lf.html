EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

This Management and Leadership report evaluates Attendo AB's executive team, governance structures, and organizational capacity under CEO Martin Tivéus, who has led a significant turnaround since 2018 but will depart by late 2026. The analysis examines a highly agile consumer-digital executive whose cross-industry pivots diverge significantly from traditional Nordic healthcare operator career tracks, with Tivéus having announced his resignation in June 2026 to lead an international corporation headquartered outside of Sweden.

The report synthesizes financial performance, strategic execution, governance quality, insider alignment, and third-party commentary to assess management's capacity to deliver the ambitious SEK 9.00 adjusted EPS target by 2028.

Executive Quality and Track Record Tivéus has delivered outstanding financial turnaround and strategic repositioning since his 2018 appointment, with net sales increasing from SEK 11.1bn in 2018 to SEK 18.99bn in 2025 and operating profit EBITA margins recovering to 9.9% in 2025. His leadership style demonstrates highly transparent, decision-forceful characteristics with an owner-aligned mindset, evidenced by his direct holding of 150,000 shares and 713,730 warrants compared to a zero-share holding average among local care provider peers.

The CEO archetype evaluation reveals strong ratings across drive, decision-velocity, capital discipline, and transparency, with Tivéus successfully executing rapid strategic portfolio pruning by exiting low-margin outsourcing segments in Norway and Denmark. His conservative but highly achievable guidance posture has consistently over-delivered, with the company targeting SEK 5.50 adjusted EPS by 2026 but reaching SEK 6.00 in 2025, beating the goal early.

However, his lack of traditional healthcare public-sector roots presents a potential limitation in municipal negotiation leverage compared to peers with deeper welfare-sector tenure. Strategic Execution and Capital Allocation Management has executed disciplined M&A and capital return programs that materially strengthened the business platform and shareholder alignment. The 2024 acquisition of Team Olivia Sweden added annual sales of approximately SEK 1.35bn at higher margins than Attendo's legacy Swedish business, while the 2026 acquisition of A-klinikka Oy expanded Finnish services in substance abuse treatment.

Capital discipline remains exemplary, with Attendo buying back over 10% of outstanding shares between 2024 and 2026 while maintaining net debt to EBITDA leverage at 1.1x in 2025, below the 1.5x-2.5x target ceiling. The March 2026 Capital Markets Day targets were well-received with strong credibility, as DNB Carnegie expressed high confidence in the SEK 9.00 target, citing strong underlying demographic tailwinds. Nevertheless, the aggressive retreat from municipal outsourcing may limit long-term organic revenue scaling potential if demographic growth slows or greenfield occupancy targets lag expectations.

Governance Structure and Board Oversight Attendo maintains a clean single-class share structure and COSO-aligned internal controls that protect shareholders, with a streamlined board of six directors where five out of six ordinary board members are fully independent of the company and its major shareholders. The board was recently refreshed following Directors Suvi-Anne Siimes and Tobias Lönnevall declining re-election at the 2026 assembly, while Ulf Mattsson was reelected as Chairman of the Board of Directors.

Compensation structures demonstrate strong owner alignment through equity-heavy incentives, with the Board abolishing all short-term variable cash remuneration in 2021 to address public debate around private welfare sector pay structures. However, governance exhibits two notable weaknesses: the Board assesses that no independent internal audit function is required, relying instead on its Group internal control team, and former anchor sponsor Nordstjernan completely divested its equity stake in the company during late 2025, potentially weakening institutional lobbying capacity during municipal contract disputes.

Senior Leadership and Organizational Bench The CFO transition from Fredrik Lagercrantz to Mikael Malmgren was executed through a highly structured corporate finance handover that preserved capital discipline. Current CFO Mikael Malmgren assumed the role in June 2023, bringing extensive strategy experience from McKinsey & Co and prior industrial CFO roles, having previously served as CFO of Eltel and led the Workplace Safety division of BB Tools. Divisional leadership demonstrates strong bench depth in Finland, where Managing Director Virpi Holmqvist provides deep stability with over 14 combined years of company service, having previously served as Touhola CEO and Pihlajalinna CFO. In contrast, the Scandinavian division experienced significant executive churn, with Business Area Manager Ulrika Eriksson leaving in June 2023, her replacement Patrik Högberg departing by January 2025, before 23-year company veteran Malin Fredgardh Huber was promoted to lead Scandinavia. The frequent turnover within the Scandinavian division may limit the Group's ability to replicate Finland's margin recovery in its Swedish home care portfolio if integration of acquired assets falters.

Culture, Employee Alignment, and Operational Resilience Attendo sustains a resilient, decentralized, value-led culture backed by stable employee satisfaction metrics, though strained by public funding cutbacks and localized staffing crises. The group-weighted Employee Net Promoter Score registered at 21 in 2025, compared to 22 in 2024 and 20 in 2023, maintaining solid levels above the long-term target of 20, while organization-wide employee turnover was stable at 24% in both 2025 and 2024.

The operating model emphasizes a flat organizational structure characterized by strong unit-level leadership where local managers carry individual operational accountability, introducing dedicated group managers at the department level in nursing homes. However, operational resilience faces persistent headwinds, as employee absenteeism remained high at 24.0% in mid-2026, showing no improvement from prior interim periods.

The company addressed acute labor shortages through international recruitment and workforce restructuring, but Attendo terminated 150 employee contracts in December 2024 as a direct consequence of reduced social welfare and healthcare funding in Finland, raising concerns about sustainable staffing models under continued fiscal pressure. - Other topic-specific reports (Finland) Expert Network Insights Versus Public Statements Expert commentary reveals a highly structured turnaround in Finland driven by price renegotiations, confirming management's public narrative while highlighting long-term risks around nurse retention and pricing ceilings.

Expert sources validated that lowering the mandatory Finnish staffing ratio from 0.65 to 0.60 eased labor market pressure and that consolidating 300 municipalities into 20 welfare regions created standardized operating models across Finland. Pricing power materialized as management projected, with experts confirming an average 10% price increase in framework contracts compared favorably to pre-crisis low-single-digit historical indexation rates.

However, expert views diverged from public optimism on several fronts: contractual price adjustments lag wage inflation because most agreements only allow annual adjustments based on predetermined labor indices, and negotiations for disabled care and social psychiatry lagged elderly care, leaving those segments undercompensated for historical cost inflation. The proprietary recruitment channel through Silkkitie Oy was confirmed as strategically important, though experts cautioned that over-reliance on international recruitment may fail if immigration rules tighten or integration rates drop, potentially restricting occupancy and capping profitability. Insider Trading Signals and Capital Market Alignment Insider equity holdings demonstrate robust executive alignment through substantial warrant positions, though active open-market transaction data remains seasonally thin. Martin Tivéus holds 220,000 shares and 555,000 warrants, CFO Mikael Malmgren maintains 7,000 shares and 93,500 warrants, and Finland MD Virpi Holmqvist holds 21,695 shares and 20,607 warrants, demonstrating substantial long-term personal commitment across the leadership team.

The compensation framework shifted decisively toward equity grants rather than cash bonuses, with the AGM adopting the Employee Stock Option Program 2026 to award up to 450,000 options and senior staff participating in the Performance Share Program 2026, granting up to 315,000 shares. Importantly, no major active board members executed open-market share liquidations during the last 12 months, signaling continued confidence in the turnaround trajectory.

Corporate buyback activity has been aggressive and strategic, with substantial open-market share buybacks throughout 2026, including the repurchase of 125,000 shares in late July 2026, and the AGM approving the cancellation of 6,393,639 treasury shares to optimize the capital structure, aligning with the stated SEK 9.00 EPS accretion model.

Single Largest Credibility Risk The single largest credibility risk to Attendo's 2028 financial targets is the imminent CEO succession during a critical execution phase, compounded by structural margin pressure from lagged pricing indexation in Scandinavia. Martin Tivéus announced his resignation in June 2026 to lead an international corporation headquartered outside of Sweden, with the Board initiating a formal recruitment process while Tivéus remains until late 2026 or early 2027.

While DNB Carnegie viewed the transition timing as reasonable given the completed business turnaround and maintained a BUY rating, assessing that if a successor is secured before late 2026 the transition will not affect Attendo's financial targets, execution risk remains elevated because the new CEO must simultaneously navigate Finnish welfare region budget constraints, accelerate Scandinavian margin recovery, and maintain aggressive capital return commitments.

The structural risk is amplified by the fact that contractual price adjustments lag wage inflation because most agreements only allow annual adjustments based on pre-determined labor indices, meaning any delay in municipal indexation negotiations directly compresses margins before the leadership transition is complete. Furthermore, the lack of traditional healthcare publicsector roots may limit Attendo's regional political negotiation leverage if local municipality relations sour, creating asymmetric downside risk if the incoming CEO lacks Tivéus's specific commercial agility and cross-industry adaptability during a period of heightened fiscal pressure across Nordic welfare systems.
