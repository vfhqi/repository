Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side focus prior to the H1 2026 earnings centered on EXOR's persistent holding company discount and its substantial cash generation.
  2. Severe operational challenges at Stellantis and CNH Industrial contrasted sharply with the luxury resilience of Ferrari, dominating the investment debate.

Controlling ownership: EXOR is a prominent European diversified investment holding company controlled by Giovanni Agnelli B.V., which represents the descendants of Senator Giovanni Agnelli. The company was formed in 2009 by merging the historic holding entities IFI and IFIL. Giovanni Agnelli B.V. holds approximately 55.93% of the outstanding ordinary shares and 84.69% of the voting rights.

3.
Solid track record: EXOR has delivered a highly competitive long-term fundamental return with a net asset value per share compound annual growth rate of 16.3% since inception through December 2025. Concentrated investment structure: Listed companies represent the vast majority of EXOR's asset base, accounting for €24.5 billion or 68.4% of its gross asset value at 30 June 2026. Ferrari remains the single most dominant holding at €12.3 billion, representing 34.2% of the entire gross asset value. Stellantis, Philips, and CNH Industrial comprise the other cornerstone public assets in the long-term portfolio.

Management intends to concentrate future capital allocation on much larger listed platforms to maximize long-term shareholder value.
