Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Línea Directa entered its Q1 2026 earnings with substantial operational momentum following its successful post-inflation turnaround.
  2. Broker sentiment had turned modestly constructive in the six months leading to April 2026 as underwriting margins recovered.
  3. The direct-to-consumer business model positioned the insurer with a structural efficiency advantage over traditional branch-based peers.
1.
Direct business model: Línea Directa operates a unique direct-to-consumer insurance model in Spain that completely bypasses branch networks to sell policies via telephone and digital channels. This centralized operational structure in Tres Cantos allows management to maintain superior control over efficiency compared to traditional competitors.
2.
Corporate history: The company was originally founded in 1995 as a 50/50 joint venture between Bankinter and Direct Line Group. Bankinter acquired full control of the entity in 2009 by buying the remaining 50% stake before executing a highly debated public spin-off in April 2021.
3.
Current ownership structure: Following its listing on the Madrid Stock Exchange on April 29, 2021, the company operates as an independent listed entity. Bankinter still maintains a 17.4% minority ownership stake in the insurer.
4.
Market position and sector scale: By premium turnover, the group ranks 13th among Spanish non-life insurance groups. The company holds a c.7% market share in Spanish motor insurance, ranking fifth, and a 2.5% market share in home insurance, where it ranks thirteenth by premium volume.
5.
Fundamental performance turnaround: After suffering severe underwriting losses in FY2023 with a combined ratio of 104.1%, the company consolidated its recovery in FY2024 with a 94.7% combined ratio. By FY2025, Net income increased by 33.5% to €85.7 million, proving the success of anti-inflation price adjustments.
