Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side focus prior to the FY 2024 results centered on SKAN's ability to maintain double-digit revenue growth amid broader life science capital expenditure normalization.

Pioneering market position: SKAN Group AG operates as the global market and technology leader in high-quality, process-critical isolator systems designed for aseptic and aseptic-toxic drug filling. The Allschwil-based pioneer has established itself as an indispensable partner for the international biopharmaceutical industry since its founding in 1968. System installations now protect product integrity and safety in more than 35 countries globally. Strong technology capabilities allow the company to capture highly complex pharmaceutical projects worldwide.

Dual segment structure: SKAN Group AG generates revenues through two distinct operating segments named Equipment & Solutions alongside Services & Consumables. The equipment segment provides mission-critical solutions like custom isolators and integrated aseptic filling lines to pharmaceutical producers. Meanwhile, the service segment delivers aftermarket maintenance, requalification, and digital software solution systems. Balancing these segments helps the group reduce historical project business volatility effectively.

Revised Annex 1 guidelines require pharmaceutical manufacturers to implement comprehensive contamination control strategies and strongly favor automated barrier technologies over human-dependent cleanrooms. Consequently, the structural shift from traditional cleanrooms to advanced isolators continues to drive robust long-term backlog growth for the group's Equipment and Solutions segment.

Rigorous enforcement environments help SKAN Group AG maintain its dominant market position because clients prioritize proven quality standards over lower-cost equipment alternatives.

Favorable shipping terms: SKAN Group AG manages potential United States import tariff risks by contractually utilizing Delivery at Place shipping terms for its equipment deliveries. Bilateral trade agreements and local onshoring investments will de-risk the mid-term capital plans of SKAN's primary biopharmaceutical customers.
