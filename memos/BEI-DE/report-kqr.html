Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Beiersdorf's valuation is depressed due to fears of Nivea's structural decline, making the assessment of its high-margin Derma division critical for investors.
  2. Understanding the sum-of-the-parts value of Eucerin and Aquaphor tests whether the stock offers an attractive entry into dermocosmetics. The Key Question this report investigates is: "What share of Beiersdorf's operating profit comes from its Derma business (Eucerin and Aquaphor), compared with Nivea and La Prairie? What margin and sales growth do brokers model for Derma to 2028? What value would a sum-of-the-parts put on Derma at the multiples paid for dermocosmetics businesses, such as L'Oréal's Dermatological Beauty division and Galderma, and how does that compare with Beiersdorf's whole market value today?"

Expanding segment: Experts highlight that the dermocosmetics sector continues growing ahead of total skincare, offering a highly resilient premium demand profile.
4.
Divergent trajectories: While Nivea faces retail destocking pressure, the Derma division continues to outperform as a structural winner.

Why the answer matters

Favourable exposure: An analyst argues that Derma's high-margin Eucerin and Aquaphor portfolio can offset mass-market competitive threats.
