For the second quarter of 2025, IAR Systems' adjusted operating margin contracted sharply to just 11.7% from 24.2% in the prior-year period. Consequently, short-term net sales and profitability decline before recovering in subsequent years.

Section 5 – European Software Peer Cost Programs and Labor Market Constraints

  3. Stringent labor regulations and statutory change negotiation rules in Germany, Sweden, and Finland significantly prolong restructuring timelines and inflate severance outlays for technology firms.

The company reduced its global workforce by approximately 45 employees, representing nearly 10% of its total personnel, to improve overall execution. Yubico fully recognized SEK 21.0 million of restructuring expenses in the first quarter of 2026 but projected annualized gross savings of SEK 95 million starting in the second quarter. This precedent confirms that large European cost programs require patience because operational benefits take several years to materialize.
