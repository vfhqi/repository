[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Addtech is a proven Swedish decentralised compounder whose adjusted earnings should grow about 12% a year as it scales its acquisition engine and lifts its margin structurally through proprietary mix and real pricing, while its organic volume, flat for eleven quarters, is only now turning in the order book. At SEK 343.8 the shares trade at 28.6x 24-month-forward earnings, the highest multiple in the 59-stock comparison set, so they already pay for that compounding: the 'Modal return' is 12.8% a year over eighteen months and 12.0% a year over three years against the 20% bar. Great business, priced for it; watch the organic line and the price.

  • The change inside the company is structural, and it is the spine of the case.
    • On 1 October 2025 five business areas became six and twelve business units became fifteen
    • The chief financial officer says the structure supports 15 to 20 acquisitions a year, up from 10 to 15
    • The acquisition net has moved into continental Europe and the United Kingdom, where 42% of FY2026 sales now sit outside the Nordics
    • And pricing is applied within days of supplier moves across customised products that are two-thirds of sales.
    • These are the screen's three-plus strong internal forces, without the two it weights most: there is no chief executive change (Niklas Stenberg since 2018) and no guidance to raise (the company gives none).
    • So 'Change Forces' (E 1, BB 1) is B: a continuity management scaling a proven model, which is a strength for 'Foundations' and a modest change force here.
  • The external demand force has turned from headwind to early tailwind, and it is the swing factor.
    • The weighted Swedish and Eurozone manufacturing PMI crossed 53 in June 2026, and Addtech's organic growth has historically accelerated two to five quarters after that crossing
    • The Q1 FY2027 book-to-bill was slightly above 100% after softer quarters
    • Automation's orders improved sequentially for five consecutive quarters
    • Grid orders recovered after the transmission operators' delays.
    • The cohort's organic lines have already turned (Indutrade +5%, Lagercrantz +6%, Sdiptech +11% in their latest quarters), which says the sector cycle has moved and Addtech lags on Energy comparisons and sawmills rather than losing share.
    • Against it: eleven consecutive quarters of flat or negative organic growth to Q4 FY2026, sawmills about 5% of EBITA and still falling, Process customers still postponing, and management's own timing language hedged.
  • 'Financial Outputs' (E 2, BB 1) have turned up in margin, capital efficiency and estimates, but not yet in organic volume.
    • The EBITA margin reached a record 16.6% in Q1 FY2027 against 15.8% a year earlier, with gross margin up 100 basis points year on year in all six business areas
    • Return on working capital rose to 81% from 77%
    • FactSet consensus FY2027 EPS is up about 5% over three and six months and all six visible brokers rate Buy.
    • Organic growth was 0% in Q1 FY2027, against the sell-side's expected minus 2%
      • Revenue growth was 5.7%, all of it acquired.
  • The change has travelled five of the six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • It shows in the company's resources and choices (the reorganisation, two internal promotions into Group Management), its actions (nine acquisitions in FY2026, pricing in all six areas, Automation restructured), its financial statements (margin, return on working capital) and the sell-side's estimates.
    • The layer it has not reached is the key operating metric, organic volume, and that layer decides which branch of the valuation the market lands on.
  • What we think it earns: adjusted earnings per share of about SEK 9.7 in FY2026 (our inferred base on the brokers' basis; reported EPS was SEK 7.95) rising to about SEK 13.6 in FY2029, about 12% a year, with 14% in the first year.
    • The path is revenue growth of 9% a year (organic 3% on average, acquired 6% to 7%, currency about minus 0.5%), an EBITA margin moving from 16.0% to about 17.0%, half a point a year from the interest line, and a flat share count.
    • Our FY2027 is 1% above the consensus median of SEK 10.95 and 4% to 5% below DNB Carnegie
      • Our FY2029 is 8% above the median, because the median fades acquired growth it has not seen announced and holds the margin flat.
  • What it costs: SEK 343.8, or 28.6x 24-month-forward consensus earnings of SEK 12.02 on 2026-10-03, the highest P/E of the 59, level with Lagercrantz (28.4x) and above Lifco (26.9x) and Diploma (27.5x).
    • Against the stock's own history that is the 32nd percentile of five years (median 29.74x) and the 61st of ten (median 25.60x)
      • The multiple has drifted down from a two-year median of 31.46x while margins rose, which tells us the market is paying less for the same quality as the organic stagnation has lengthened.
    • On our own adjusted path the 24-month-forward figure now is SEK 12.95 and the multiple 26.5x
      • The under-modelled out-years are the 'Compounder' mechanism, not a reason to call the shares cheap.
  • The return does not clear the bar on the modal path, and it clears it on the cleared branch only at eighteen months.
    • At our blended multiple of 27.2x (60% odds of the cleared branch at 29.0x, 40% of the uncleared at 24.5x) the 'Modal return' is 12.8% a year over eighteen months and 12.0% over three years, including a 1.2% dividend yield.
    • Hold the multiple at 28.6x and it is 16.6% and 13.9%
      • Confirm the organic turn and take the five-year median of 29.0x and it is 17.7% and 14.4%
      • Lose the turn and take 24.5x and it is 5.3% and 8.3%.
    • The case clears 20% a year only at about SEK 313 or below on the eighteen-month horizon, or at today's price with the multiple at about 30x on our above-consensus earnings.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Compounder', reasonably crisply (B).
    • The sell-side models one year of acquired growth and then fades it, holds the margin flat and mixes two earnings bases in its mean, so the out-year consensus sits below the company's demonstrated rate
    • The record of beating consensus in 12 of 18 years is the evidence that the fade is the error.
    • The error is a fade of a few percent a year, not a step in a named line, which is why the fit is B and why the mis-modelling does not rescue the return.
  • What breaks it: the risks report's "false friend": acquired earnings and a mix-flattered margin masking a volume stall that does not end, so the multiple compresses toward 20x EV/EBITA and the shares toward SEK 295.
    • Behind that sits the drop-through: if the stepped-up deal count is debt-funded at today's variable rates and the tax rate drifts to 24.5%, horizon earnings fall 5.6% and the 'Modal return' falls to 8.8% and 9.9% a year, an F, which would take this Building Block from C to D.

  • The case is therefore a business we rate highly and a share we do not need to own at this price.

    • The actionable output is a level: about SEK 313 on the modal path, last offered in April 2026, or the organic confirmation that moves the modal multiple to the cleared branch and the eighteen-month return to about 18% a year.

  • Rating = C, because the change is material in margin and in acquisition capacity ('Change Forces' B, 'Financial Outputs' B) but is already paid for, so the third link of the chain, a 20 percent return, does not hold (the 'Modal return' D), and a Building Block whose question is whether the change is sufficient to drive that return cannot rate above the middle when the return leg is the one that fails.

    • Not B: B would need a modal path that clears the bar on at least one horizon without a held or higher multiple, and ours does not.
    • Not D: the earnings leg is probable and the sell-side is raising, so the materiality is real
    • What is missing is the price.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • In its strongest form: Addtech is a "false friend".
    • Headline EBITA grows 11% to 12% on bought earnings and on a margin flattered by project mix and earn-out revaluations (SEK 13m of revaluations in Q3 FY2026; a reported 17.3% Q4 FY2026 margin that was 16.4% underlying), while the underlying business has not grown its volume for eleven quarters and will not, because its decentralised portfolio is over-exposed to short-cycle Nordic industrial capital spending (60% of the top subsidiaries), because its organic recovery depends on a PMI it does not control, and because in the one area where it should have grown, Energy, with Svenska Kraftnät's budget behind it, sales fell 7% in the latest quarter.
    • On this reading the April 2026 downgrade to Hold was right and the June upgrade premature
      • The margin step reverses when the mix normalises
      • The acquisition engine faces rising competition in a saturated Nordic market and unfamiliar regulation abroad
      • And the multiple, at the top of the comparison set, compresses to 20x EV/EBITA and the shares to about SEK 295, a 14% fall, or to the ten-year "conservative" band of 19.4x on earnings, SEK 233 on today's consensus, a 32% fall.

    • The evidence for it is real: eleven flat quarters against a 6% to 6.5% organic norm

      • Two years of EBITA growth below the 15% target (14%, then 12%)
      • The earn-out and underlying-margin adjustments
      • The Hold-rated view that multiple expansion is capped without organic acceleration
      • And the risks report's own analysis of competing hypotheses, in which this profile carries the fewest inconsistencies.

    • What we concede.

      • The eleven quarters
      • That the organic half of the model is external in its timing
      • That part of the margin gain is mix that can reverse
      • That the Energy decline in Q1 FY2027, while explained by comparisons, is not yet reversed in revenue
      • That coverage is thin enough that the Q1 "beat" rested on one house's estimate
      • And that the mark-to-market downside on a de-rating alone is 16% to 23% at a prudent trough and 32% at the ten-year "conservative" band.
    • Why we still hold our view on the business.
      • The order evidence has turned where the hypothesis says it should not have: a book-to-bill above 100%, five quarters of sequential order improvement in Automation, grid orders recovering.
      • The cohort's organic lines are already positive, which is what a sector cycle looks like, not what share loss looks like.
      • The pricing is applied within days by subsidiaries selling customised products in all six areas, which is not how mix accidents behave.
      • The acquisition multiple has not risen (a 6.8x EV/EBITA median) and the engine is self-funded at 1.3x net debt to EBITDA.
      • And the group has recovered profitability within two quarters of every industrial drawdown in the record.
    • Where the hypothesis wins, we have already given it the result.
      • It is why our modal multiple is 5% below today's, why the uncleared branch carries 40% of the odds, why the earnings odds carry a downside skew, and why the recommendation is neutral and the actionable output is a price, not a buy.
      • If organic growth is at or below 0% in Q2 and Q3 FY2027 with a book-to-bill below 100%, or the PMI falls back below 50, the hypothesis is the mode and the case is invalidated at this price.
