Structured power protection: Helios Towers utilizes dual-track power price escalators to protect against fuel price volatility across its footprint. Helios Towers acts as a critical power provider, securing grid power for an average of 16 to 17 hours daily and using diesel backups only for remaining hours. Because power costs are passed through directly, the operator avoids fuel price spikes and captures incremental efficiency gains.
