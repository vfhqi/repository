The reasoning behind the inputs

Bases used throughout (read first). - EBITDA is after transaction costs and before items affecting comparability (restructuring). It equals the company's EBITA, with acquisition costs charged as a recurring cost (about SEK 70m in 2026 and SEK 90m a year thereafter), plus depreciation and amortisation other than amortisation of acquired intangibles. On this basis FY2025 EBITDA was about SEK 4,772m, 12.9% of revenue (company underlying EBITA SEK 3,962m plus about SEK 810m of such depreciation and amortisation), against the brokers'-basis actual of SEK 4,749m (12.8%). The model's history line, FactSet operating EBITDA of SEK 4,514m (12.2%) for FY2025, is after the SEK 150m Q4 2025 restructuring charge and other items and is not comparable: FY2026 at 13.0% is a rise of about 10 basis points on a like-for-like basis, not 80. - Depreciation and amortisation excludes amortisation of acquired intangibles (SEK 218m in 2025; about SEK 250m, 290m, 320m, 350m and 380m for 2026 to 2030). It includes right-of-use (lease) depreciation, so the model's EBITDA is on an IFRS 16 basis. - Net financial items include interest on lease liabilities. Lease payments in Part 2 are therefore lease principal only. - Acquisitions less disposals are the full cash consideration paid for acquired stakes plus settlements of put and call option liabilities and earn-outs. Working capital in Part 2 is on FactSet's definition (receivables plus inventories less payables: 36.0% at end-2025, 40.1% at Q3 2026). Caution for the model: if the model derives the working-capital cash flow from the change in working capital as a percentage of revenue, it will count the working capital of acquired businesses (about 37% of their sales: about SEK 960m in 2026, 1,260m in 2027 and 925m a year from 2028) a second time, on top of the acquisition price. Our own free cash flow below counts it once, inside the acquisition price. - Dividend per share is the dividend declared for the fiscal year (paid the following spring). - Share count is 507.0m throughout. The LTI 2026 programme (at most 600,000 B-shares) is hedged with treasury shares bought in the market; the purchase (up to about SEK 95m) and later delivery leave the count unchanged and are too small to enter as a buyback. No new shares are assumed.
