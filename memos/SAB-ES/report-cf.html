EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 2 findings

MATERIALITY OF CHANGE

Banco Sabadell has completed a comprehensive executive transition establishing a highly unified management structure under new CEO Marc Armengol, though personnel departures and union resistance trigger operational execution delays across the retail branch network. The bank successfully defeated BBVA's hostile voluntary tender offer, which secured only 25.33% acceptance in October 2025, through a standalone capital commitment strategy that included upwardly revised dividend targets to deter shareholder tenders.

The completed sale of TSB to Santander for GBP 2.9 billion on April 30, 2026 unlocked over 400 bps of capital, funding a €2.5 billion extraordinary payout and creating a pure-play Spanish banking franchise. Standalone revenue is expected to decline 14.10% in 2026 as TSB is fully deconsolidated from operations, with standalone net interest income projected to contract 18.20% in 2026 before rebounding 4.20% by fiscal 2028 on rate volume dynamics.

Spanish credit demand is recovering strongly with on-balance sheet customer funds growing 4.7% YoY and performing loans increasing 3% QoQ during the recent quarter, demonstrating robust commercial momentum across corporate and consumer segments.

TOP KEY DRIVERS (RANKED BY IMPACT AND PLAUSIBILITY)

1.
Driver 1: Euribor Interest Rates – Domestic net interest income remains highly sensitive to Euribor repricing, with standalone NII projected to decline 18.20% in fiscal 2026, representing the single largest structural driver with high plausibility and high confidence as rate cuts materialize across the Eurozone cycle. Driver 2: SME Loan Growth – Spanish performing loan expansion targeting mid-single-digit loan growth to offset the margin headwind from falling interest rates, with 3% estimated impact, high plausibility supported by recovering domestic economic activity, and medium confidence dependent on macroeconomic growth trajectories. Driver 3: Cost Rationalization – The early retirement programme delivering €40 million annual savings and capping recurrent operating cost growth below 3% annually, with negative 3% estimated impact, high plausibility backed by executed workforce plans, and high confidence on management's demonstrated cost control capabilities. Driver 4: Shareholder Buybacks – Management launched a €331 million share buyback driving

forward EPS accretion as outstanding shares are reduced, with 3% estimated impact, high plausibility given excess capital post-TSB divestment, and high confidence dependent on sustained excess capital levels above the 13% threshold. Driver 5: Provision Stability – The bank expects cost of risk stable at approximately 40 basis points for the second half of fiscal 2026, with 40% estimated impact reflecting the magnitude of provisioning to earnings, high plausibility backed by benign Spanish credit quality, and high confidence tied to underlying asset quality trends in the domestic SME portfolio.

Driver 6: Reserve Regulation – A potential ECB mandate to increase reserve requirements creates a margin dilutive overhang estimated to hit earnings by 1.40%, with medium plausibility dependent on regulatory discretion, and medium confidence tied to uncertain ECB policy decisions on non-remunerated minimum reserves.

RECENT DELIVERY ANALYSIS

Banco Sabadell reported net profit of EUR 624 million in 2Q26 representing 28% year-on-year growth, though reported results remain heavily distorted by EUR 249 million of net one-offs including the TSB sale capital gain and restructuring charges. Spain's business units demonstrated robust commercial expansion with performing loans increasing 3.6% year-on-year to EUR 107.1 billion and new business lending to SMEs and corporates growing 38% quarter-on-quarter, indicating extremely strong domestic demand recovery across all core loan segments.

Standalone NII reached EUR 896 million in 2Q26 increasing 3% quarter-on-quarter to mark a sequential inflection from the seasonal trough, while loan-loss provisions came in 3% below consensus expectations reflecting a solid credit risk profile and benign Spanish asset quality environment.

FORWARD CREDIBILITY AND MANAGEMENT ARCHETYPE Management delivered a consistent track record of strategic execution across historical plan horizons, fully accomplishing all financial objectives in 2021 while reorganising core domestic operations and recovering the TSB division from a €220 million loss in 2020 to a €118 million profit in 2021. The executive team exhibits a highly conservative guidance archetype acting as a classic sandbagger by setting highly achievable initial interest income hurdles to manage sell-side expectations, with initial 2026 net interest income guidance reiterated at over 1% growth now viewed as highly achievable by analysts. Execution volatility remains highly dependent on transactional one-offs and macro factors rather than pure operational improvements, with Sabadell facing significant execution hurdles due to the prolonged takeover offer from rival BBVA creating eighteen months of takeover distraction and collateral organizational damage.

SELL-SIDE POSITIONING AND RATING DISTRIBUTION

Refinitiv consensus tracks seventeen active brokers covering Sabadell with a highly balanced but cautious stance, where the rating mix sourced from Bloomberg consensus represents 35% Overweight, 48% Equal-weight, and 17% Underweight reflecting execution uncertainty on the post-bid equity story. Consensus price targets show wide dispersion from €2.95 to €4 with a mean price target of €3.40 via Refinitiv consensus suggesting modest upside from current levels, while consensus EPS forecasts stand at €0.23 for fiscal year 2026 reflecting initial revenue pressure ex-TSB before recovering to €0.30 in 2027 and €0.33 by 2028.

Deutsche Bank argues that accelerating domestic loan volume growth offsets interest rate margin pressure post-BBVA while Morgan Stanley cautions that in low-rate cycles Sabadell's profitability will remain below efficient Spanish peers, and Redburn suggests current multiples already reflect expected earnings and return improvements limiting further upside potential.

FUNDAMENTAL PEER POSITION

Banco Sabadell achieves a strong technical score securing the second-highest momentum rank of 95.5 among Spanish peers behind CaixaBank which maintains the highest overall momentum score and ranks first in Redburn's systematic factor framework, while Sabadell experienced a positive 2.9% EPS upgrade leading domestic Spanish consensus revisions post-2Q26 results.

Sabadell remains the least efficient Spanish bank operating with a 48% cost-to-income ratio versus peers, significantly lagging Bankinter's peer-leading 34.30% efficiency ratio that matches global digital benchmarks and CaixaBank's improved 39.60% ratio, exposing structural scale disadvantages in operational efficiency that constrain relative profitability and return on tangible equity versus the domestic peer set.

ACTIVIST PRESENCE OR ABSENCE

BBVA launched a voluntary tender offer to acquire 100% of Sabadell creating significant strategic uncertainty, with the adjusted exchange ratio set at one share for each 5.0196 Sabadell shares plus cash, though Sabadell's board repeatedly rejected the offer citing a significant undervaluation on both book value and price-to-earnings metrics.

The tender offer secured only 25.33% acceptance in October 2025 rendering BBVA's hostile transaction completely ineffective, as Sabadell's strategy of standalone value generation through increased planned 2025 dividends of approximately EUR 1,450 million clearly prevailed over BBVA's narrative of scale and synergies in defending shareholder support. 2.

decline rapidly the bank faces severe and structural profit misses that management cannot offset through costcutting alone (detailed in Section 3 External Change and Section 10 Forward Guidance Credibility). Risk 3: Deposit Repricing Acceleration – Unexpected deposit repricing acceleration could if rate cuts deepen completely erode standalone customer spread gains and trigger severe margin pressure, particularly given Sabadell's elevated sensitivity to liability pricing dynamics relative to peers with superior structural hedging positions and more diversified funding bases (detailed in Section 6 Most Recent Earnings Delivery and Section 9 Key Drivers Synthesis).
