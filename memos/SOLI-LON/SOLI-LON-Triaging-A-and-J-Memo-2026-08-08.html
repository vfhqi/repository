Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Hybrid design-in and distribution model. Solid State plc is a UK small-cap specialist electronics group combining design-in manufacturing with value-added component distribution, listed on AIM
2.
Three operating divisions. Systems (Steatite secure computing and radio, Active Silicon imaging, Q-Par RF antennas), Power (Custom Power battery packs, UK and California) and Components (the Solsta brand: Pacer, Willow Technologies, Gateway)
3.
Seven UK sites plus North America. Roughly 425 to 433 employees (476 average monthly in FY26 versus 429 in FY25), anchored by the newly expanded Ashchurch systems facility in Tewkesbury and Custom Power's California site
4.
Revenue scale and mix. FY26 (year to 31-Mar-26) revenue of GBP 154.1m, up 23.2% from GBP 125.1m in FY25; defence and security represent 47% of group revenue; components distribution still 38.8% of the mix
5.
Margin character. FY26 adjusted operating margin 6.2%, recovering from 4.8% in FY25 but still well below the FY24 peak of 10.5%; group gross margin 33.5%, up from 31.4%
6.
Balance sheet. Net debt cut to GBP 4.2m from GBP 7.4m, leverage under 0.5x EBITDA, cash conversion 141% in FY26 (173% in FY25); a committed GBP 15.0m revolving credit facility with a GBP 10.0m accordion
7.
Investment style. A defence-levered cyclical recovery story riding a genuinely defensible niche, not a smooth compounder
7.1
Earnings are lumpy and contract-timing dependent: FY24 GBP 163.3m revenue at 10.5% margin, FY25 GBP 125.1m at 4.8%, FY26 GBP 154.1m at 6.2%
8.
FY2030 ambition. Management's "2030 strategy" targets GBP 250m revenue, 20p adjusted EPS and 12% operating margins (versus 6.2% today), plus a circa 20% total-shareholder-return compound annual growth rate to 2030
