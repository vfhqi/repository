Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Understanding the operating leverage of Sdiptech and its serial acquirer peers is critical for predicting EPS growth across economic cycles.

The core investment inquiry

1.
Key question: "how much fixed-cost operating leverage does this business model carry, how much scope is there for it to work over the next 18 months to four years, and, where it is relevant to the company's strategy, how feasible is cost elimination or reduction, both incremental and in material large cuts?"

Impact on earnings and multiples

2.

If Sdiptech possesses a highly flexible cost structure, it can sustain solid earnings and protect its operating margins during economic slowdowns. This structural resilience allows compounders to maintain profitability across cycles without operational disruptions. Thus, resolving this leverage question is the key to unlocking significant re-rating potential for Sdiptech.
3.
Valuation discount risk: High fixed costs can lead to severe margin compression during economic downturns, which typically justifies a lower trading multiple. RedEye highlights that market confusion over portfolio restructurings and mixed organic performance has historically depressed returns. If operating leverage is structurally poor, the gap between Sdiptech and its peers is likely to expand. Resolving this uncertainty is critical for evaluating whether the stock can narrow its valuation discount over time.

Sdiptech's Q2 2026 interim report showed that organic sales increased by 11% while organic adjusted EBITA grew by only 5%. This divergence indicates that operational drop-through was constrained despite the healthy top-line demand. Unless these cost-base issues are resolved, investors will continue to see muted profit expansion across the group's main business lines.
