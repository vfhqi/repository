EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 37 findings

Digi Communications NV demonstrates highly stable founder-led governance under long-tenured CEO Serghei Bulgac and controlling shareholder Teszari Zoltan, executing an aggressive disruptive volume growth strategy across Southern European telecommunications markets through proprietary fiber-to-the-home infrastructure and value-for-money pricing that drives industry-leading 11% to 15% annual revenue compounding. The company completed a transformational MVNO-to-MNO transition in Spain during July 2025, fundamentally improving operating economics by replacing variable wholesale fees with predictable fixed network costs, while simultaneously monetizing mature network assets through EUR 750 million in FTTH sales to Macquarie consortium to fund international expansion.

Group consolidated revenue reached EUR 1,190.7 million in H1 2026 with 10.6% year-on-year growth, while adjusted EBITDA excluding operating leases expanded 19% to EUR 330 million with margins reaching 27.7%. However, high infrastructure capital intensity of 29% of sales and elevated network depreciation of EUR 612.6 million annually suppress reported net earnings to negative EUR 0.16 per share despite strong operational cash generation, creating extreme bottom-line sensitivity to modest shifts in revenue growth, operating margins, and foreign exchange rates.
