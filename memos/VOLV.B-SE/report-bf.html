EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. Volvo operates a highly profitable divisional structure anchored by its dominant Trucks business and complemented by industrial machinery segments
  2. services and technology scale provide crucial margin insulation against the high macro-cyclicality of heavy capital equipment
  3. comprehensive portfolio pruning and manufacturing investments structurally upgrade margins and regional assembly cost structures

2.

3.

4.
Powertrain transformation: Strategic zero-emission adjustments and the Cespira hydrogen engine joint venture targeting commercial launch before 2030 position the group for regulatory compliance, though slower vehicle transition necessitated a SEK 1.6 billion asset writedown in 2025.

Financial profile and peer standing
