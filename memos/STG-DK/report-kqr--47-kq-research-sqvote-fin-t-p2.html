Section 2 – Europe Branded: Machine-Rolled Cigars, Downtrading, and German

Section summary
  1. Scandinavian Tobacco Group has managed to expand gross margins in its Europe Branded division despite enduring structural volume declines.
  2. Consumer downtrading and uncoordinated competitor pricing have historically led to market share erosion during periods of unilateral price hikes.
  3. Impending German excise tax increases between 2027 and 2030 will test industry volume elasticities and accelerate illicit trade risks.
