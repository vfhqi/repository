Raw Material Sourcing and Price Mitigations

1.
Sourcing complexity – diversified raw material matrix: Convatec purchases a wide variety of inputs for its cost of goods sold, including polymers, resins, adhesives, silicone, chemical feedstocks, metals, packaging, utilities, and freight. To protect the business from supply-side shocks, no single material exceeds 5% of the overall cost of goods sold. This raw material diversity helps mitigate cost impacts because several inputs are uncorrelated to global oil prices
2.
Hedging protection – contract terms locked: The group's near-term margin profile is strongly insulated by contractual arrangements with suppliers. Management has forward-purchased raw materials, hedging approximately 80% of its exposure for the remainder of the fiscal year. This forward procurement strategy typically locks in terms for 6 to 12 months to absorb localized cost spikes
3.
Inventory cushions – finished and raw buffers: The supply chain is further secured by substantial safety stock holding patterns. Convatec carries an average of five months of finished goods and five months of key raw materials. This significant buffer prevents raw material price volatility from flowing directly into the profit and loss account
4.
Cost of goods breakdown – cost components identified: Raw materials constitute approximately 45% of total cost of goods sold, which represents 40% of group revenues. The remaining cost components include overheads at 30%, which is comprised of freight at 5% and utilities at 2%, and labor at 25%.
