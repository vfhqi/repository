Tracking Historical Guidance Beats

This immense double-digit outperformance demonstrated that the company's initial targets were set at highly conservative baselines relative to actual operational capacity.
2.
Elevated performance: The company continued its conservative forecasting philosophy into the fiscal year 2024 reporting period. Coca-Cola HBC initially guided for organic operating profit growth of 3% to 9% on a group level. Final financial results revealed that the company surpassed the upper limit of its guided range to deliver 12.2% organic growth. This outcome verified that management's initial projections consistently underestimated price realization capabilities across diverse geographies.
3.
Narrowing outperformance: Coca-Cola HBC sustained its beat record in fiscal year 2025 but by a significantly smaller margin than in prior years. Initial guidance for the year targeted organic revenue growth of 6% to 7% and organic operating profit growth of 7% to 10%. The company ultimately reported organic revenue growth of 8.1% and organic operating profit growth of 11.5% for the twelve-month period. While the company exceeded its guided ranges, the operational outperformance margin revealed a clear contracting trend over the multiyear sequence.
