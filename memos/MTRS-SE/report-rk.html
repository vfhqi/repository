Outliers worth noting

Across the comprehensive bear case, pre-mortem, and currency risk analysis, three fundamental anomalies stand out that warrant heightened scrutiny. These outliers represent statistically significant departures from peer norms or internal historical patterns, each carrying material implications for the investment thesis. The first major outlier concerns Munters' extraordinarily wide non-GAAP earnings adjustment gap, which has expanded dramatically from historical levels.

Adjusted EBITA of SEK 1,862 million exceeded operating profit by SEK 634 million in 2025, compared to a gap of SEK 358 million in 2024, representing a 77% year-over-year increase in the adjustment spread. This widening divergence between reported and adjusted metrics substantially exceeds typical peer adjustment patterns in industrial capital goods, raising fundamental questions about underlying earnings quality. The company capitalized SEK 193 million of internally generated intangible assets during 2024, with historical capitalization reaching SEK 118 million out of SEK 162 million total R&D in 2021, indicating that over 70% of historical research expenditure has been capitalized rather than expensed.

When combined with SEK 77 million in restructuring charges and SEK 207 million from contingent acquisition considerations adjusted out of EBITA during 2025, the cumulative adjustment magnitude suggests that reported profitability may significantly overstate the sustainable cash-generating capacity of the underlying business.
