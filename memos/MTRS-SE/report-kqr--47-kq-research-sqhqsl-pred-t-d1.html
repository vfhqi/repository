Consequently, manufacturers importing complete cooling assemblies into the United States face materially higher import cost barriers, unless they rapidly relocate their supply chains.

Therefore, prolonged price-lag episodes will continue to compress operating margins across the customized thermal management sector.
3.

This lag implies that manufacturers must absorb near-term tariff shocks, so near-term profitability will remain pressured until contracts are renegotiated.

Immediate margin contraction: Munters Group AB's interim report of 22 October 2026 confirms that persistent Section 232 tariff headwinds had a materially negative impact of approximately 2 percentage points on its Data Center Technologies operating margins during the third quarter. Because Munters still imports significant cooling equipment from its European facilities to support its Virginia factory scale-up, these strict trade duties directly penalize near-term financial results.
