EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 21 findings

EQT Corporation is led by Toby Z. Rice, an operator-track founder-turned-CEO with 12 years of hands-on Appalachian E&P experience who co-founded Rice Energy, scaled it to 1.3 Bcfe/d production, and sold the company for $8.2 billion in 2017. Rice mounted a successful activist proxy campaign in 2019, winning over 80% shareholder approval to replace legacy EQT management after targeting the company's $1,250/ft well costs versus Rice Energy's proven $700–$735/ft standard. Rice executed $14 billion of strategic M&A to vertically integrate EQT, acquiring Equitrans Midstream for $5.2 billion, Olympus Energy for $1.8 billion, and establishing the first large-scale integrated U.S. natural gas producer. The analysis evaluates whether Rice's operator excellence, capital discipline, and strategic boldness can sustain EQT's thesis execution through commodity volatility and inherited midstream liabilities.
