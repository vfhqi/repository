EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Hacksaw AB has navigated a period of significant structural repositioning marked by the permanent appointment of Ana Vrabic Verdir as Group CEO in August 2026 and the full consolidation of its Malta-based Hacksaw Gaming Ltd subsidiary to 100% ownership in June 2025, establishing a governance foundation that has enabled the company to maintain its exceptional financial profile while scaling its proprietary OpenRGS platform across 40+ licensed markets.

The company delivered EUR 197 million in net sales during 2025, representing 44% organic growth, while sustaining an industry-leading adjusted EBIT margin of 82% in Q2 2026, a profitability level that dramatically surpasses the 23% Nordic software peer median and positions Hacksaw as a structural outlier in the iGaming B2B supplier landscape. However, operational execution remains heavily dependent on key personnel such as Operational CEO Marcus Cordes, while the company's ten largest B2C customers accounted for 44% of total group revenue in 2025, creating meaningful concentration vulnerabilities that warrant continuous monitoring as the business scales internationally.

The core investment thesis centers on Hacksaw's ability to compound revenue growth at rates exceeding 30% annually through a dual-engine strategy: accelerating in-house game release cadence from four to five titles monthly and scaling third-party content through its OpenRGS platform, which now hosts 11 partner studios. Direct operator integrations generate 65% to 70% of high-margin revenues, while aggregator partnerships contribute 30% to 35% of total sales, providing distribution flexibility that supports geographic expansion into newly regulated North American jurisdictions including Alberta, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut. The addressable market opportunity remains structurally attractive, with global iGaming gross gaming revenue projected to grow at approximately 12% CAGR from 2024-29 as online penetration rises from 27% in 2025 to 33% in 2029, creating a favorable demand backdrop that supports Hacksaw's hyper-growth trajectory despite intensifying competition from scaled incumbents such as Evolution, Playtech, and Light & Wonder.

Strategic changes implemented over the past 18 months have positioned Hacksaw for accelerated international scaling, though execution risks remain elevated. The company shifted its parent reporting currency from Swedish kronor to Euro effective January 1, 2026, aligning functional currency with its primary European subsidiary operations, a technical recalibration that has introduced near-term foreign exchange translation headwinds but improves long-term operational transparency.

The launch of Hacksaw Ventures in March 2026 as a dedicated investment and acceleration arm, with early investments in Kitsune Gaming Studios and Jinx Gaming, underscores management's ambition to leverage platform distribution advantages to support promising developers while increasing content output, a strategic initiative that diversifies the portfolio while maintaining capital discipline. Geographic expansion into regulated markets such as Alberta and Pennsylvania has been partially offset by proactive exits from grey US sweepstakes markets in Louisiana, Montana, and California during 2025, demonstrating prudent regulatory risk management that prioritizes long-term license sustainability over short-term revenue maximization.

Key EPS drivers for the forward period are concentrated in five high-materiality variables: in-house game release tempo (estimated 15% impact), OpenRGS studio scaling (estimated 25% impact), North American market access tempo (estimated 12% impact), constant currency growth dynamics (estimated -4% impact), and sweepstakes regulatory drag (estimated -3% impact). Group CEO Ana Vrabic Verdir outlined in July 2026 that the group increased its release cadence to five games monthly, with CFO Mikael Rahm confirming that five in-house games per month is the new normalized level, a product velocity enhancement that directly supports volume expansion while requiring elevated personnel cost assumptions.

OpenRGS onboarded Good Times Studios and Aloha Gaming in Q2 2026, bringing total partner studios to eleven, though Jefferies notes that OpenRGS expansion dilutes gross margins due to a conservative 63% revenue-share paid to partner studios, creating a margin mix headwind that must be carefully balanced against the benefits of accelerated content output. Revenue grew 31% reported in Q2 2026, but constant currency growth reached 33% due to FX headwinds, while first half 2026 constant currency revenue grew by 35% compared to 29% reported growth, illustrating the persistent currency translation drag that obscures underlying operational momentum in reported Euro figures.

Recent delivery performance has been characterized by consistent margin execution but modest revenue volatility driven by foreign exchange movements and seasonal patterns. Reported revenues reached EUR 59.29 million in Q2 2026, representing 31% growth YoY and missing consensus by approximately 2%, while the adjusted EBIT margin remained stable at 82%, a mixed quarterly print that triggered short-term share price weakness despite underlying operational strength. In-house game releases stepped up from four to five games per month, representing a new normalized level, while commercial execution remained strong with 106 deals closed, including 63 new client agreements, demonstrating robust pipeline progression that supports forward growth expectations. Hacksaw delivered exceptional sales expansion from EUR 32 million in FY22 to EUR 197 million in FY25 while maintaining an adjusted EBIT margin above 80% throughout its three-year expansion, establishing a credible track record of hyper-growth execution that validates management's long-term financial targets despite near-term currency noise.

Plausible problems to monitor

Extreme customer concentration remains a structural weakness, with the ten largest B2C customers accounting for 44% of total group revenue in 2025, despite modest improvement from the largest single customer declining from 20% to 15% of revenues. This concentration profile tracks materially worse than diversified software peers and creates asymmetric downside exposure should any Tier 1 operator relationship deteriorate.

The ultra-competitive slot market landscape features over 100 providers releasing more than 500 new monthly titles industrywide, creating extreme lobby saturation that threatens individual game lifecycle economics and player attention retention. Proactive sweepstakes market exits in Louisiana, Montana, and California during 2025 reduced sweepstakes exposure to low single-digit percentages, yet California litigation alleging supplier liability for sweepstakes platforms triggered short-term share volatility and exposes ongoing regulatory fines and reputational contagion risks.

Rising intangible asset capitalization through R&D game development costs increasing at approximately 36% CAGR, albeit from a EUR 3.0 million base and representing a stable 2% of revenues, introduces balance sheet risk if game lifecycles compress faster than the four-year amortization schedule assumes.

OpenRGS margin dilution mechanics are increasingly material, with third-party partner content commanding 50% to 63% revenue-share splits that structurally compress gross margins relative to pure in-house development. The most recent quarter delivered a 2% revenue miss versus consensus expectations at EUR 59.29 million despite 33% constant currency growth, highlighting persistent foreign exchange translation headwinds as GBP invoicing exposure reached 23% in 2024.

Sequential growth deceleration to 26% reported in Q4 2025, despite 31% constant currency performance, underscores vulnerability to currency volatility. Seasonal summer activity deceleration creates predictable Q2 and Q3 soft periods across the wider online gambling industry due to lower player engagement, while rising operating cost inflation and elevated global interest rates compress consumer discretionary purchasing power and increase player acquisition costs for B2C operators.

US onboarding friction remains elevated, with time-consuming operator-by-operator regulatory certification processes delaying game launches and potentially yielding low capital returns if state reregulation stalls. A Swedish Gambling Authority regulatory penalty in 2024, though reduced on appeal from SEK 2.6 million to SEK 20 thousand, signals compliance execution gaps that could recur under intensified regulatory scrutiny across multiple jurisdictions.

Outliers worth noting

Hacksaw AB exhibits a margin profile that constitutes a statistical outlier within the iGaming B2B sector, maintaining an adjusted EBIT margin of 82% in Q2 2026, which materially exceeds the peer-leading Evolution's 57.3% and the sector median of approximately 37%. This represents a 59 percentage point premium over the Nordic software supplier peer median EBIT margin of just 23%, raising questions about the sustainability of such extraordinary profitability in a competitive market saturated with over 100 content suppliers.

The company's structural advantage stems from its asset-light slot-focused business model, where depreciation and amortization represent only 2% of total revenues compared to Evolution's 7%, reflecting the absence of laborintensive live dealer studio infrastructure.

306 The OpenRGS platform architecture represents a structural anomaly in traditional B2B game distribution models, functioning as a proprietary remote server that enables rapid third-party development while consolidating regulatory compliance centrally, with partner studios generating 50% of Hacksaw's total game output in 2025. This inverted platform economics model stands in sharp contrast to conventional aggregators and platform providers, who typically charge distribution fees rather than share royalty commissions.

The financial mechanics of this arrangement create unusual margin dynamics, with Hacksaw retaining between 37% and 50% of OpenRGS partner revenues after paying revenue-share to third-party studios, materially diluting gross margins relative to inhouse content. Hacksaw operates with zero interest-bearing debt excluding minor lease liabilities of EUR 3 million, positioning it as a balance sheet outlier when compared to the Nordic software peer median leverage ratio of 1.0x.
