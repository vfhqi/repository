Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Colruyt Group NV is Belgium's largest food retailer, running Colruyt Lowest Prices, OKay, OKay City, Bio-Planet, Cru and the affiliated Spar network across 678 stores
2.
How it earns. A dedicated pricing department of 100 people records 150,000 competitor prices daily across 250 catchment areas, runs 2 million calculations and cuts 500 to 600 item prices every day
3.
Scale and concentration. FY25/26 revenue was EUR 10,568m, of which food operations were 94.2% (EUR 9,960m) and Belgium 96.1%, with Luxembourg under 1%
4.
Segment mix. Non-food and health are 5.5% of revenue, energy interests 0.2%, and wholesale plus foodservice 12.8%, with direct-to-consumer store sales above 80% of turnover
5.
Financial character. FY25/26 operating profit was EUR 465m on a 4.4% margin, gross margin 30.5%, EBITDA EUR 879m, and free cash flow to the firm EUR 188m
6.
Balance sheet. Net financial debt including leases fell to EUR 271m from EUR 297m, with a net cash position of EUR 108m excluding lease liabilities at March 2026
7.
Vertical integration. Colruyt Group Fine Food runs nine production sites and over 1,200 full-time employees, annually handling 42,500 tonnes of meat, 17,950 tonnes of cheese and 7,300 tonnes of coffee
8.
Upstream footprint. The group buys from over 6,000 Belgian agricultural undertakings with 600 intensive structural partnerships, plus 300 dairy farmers on a guaranteed five-year milk price
9.
Business-to-business leg. Solucious generated EUR 366.3m in FY25/26, growing 26% year on year, serving more than 20,000 professional customers including hospitals, hospitality and school canteens
10.
Digital leg. Online is 9% of group retail revenue, up from nearly 8%, with Collect&Go home delivery reaching 85% of Belgian households and 250 collection points
11.
Ownership. The founding family and the Korys investment vehicle act in concert, with the combined stake stated at 73.50% in one source and 77.86% in another (see F.2)
12.
Investment style. A domestic defensive with a self-help cost programme, not a compounder and not a cyclical, sitting closest to a value-plus-yield situation with a structural cost problem
