According to a Snf UK report on 4 June 2026, French Tax Code Article 39-1-1 permits interest deductions if expenses are actual, justified, and incurred in direct corporate interest. This deductibility lowers the net cash cost of the EUR 250m hybrid coupon, directly protecting future earnings per share from fiscal drag.
2.
Restricted terms: Rémy Cointreau must navigate complex transfer pricing and related-party limitation rules to secure full tax deductibility. A Snf UK document published on 4 June 2026 reports that French Tax Code Article 212 restricts interest deductibility on related-party loans to market rates. If tax authorities challenge the interest rate and re-characterize non-deductible payments as dividends under Article 109, the group's tax shield is lost, reducing retained earnings and profit available for EPS.
