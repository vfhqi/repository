B.1 Business Description 8 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. Alzchem is a vertically integrated German specialty chemicals producer running a closed-loop "Verbundsystem" across four Bavarian sites plus one Swedish plant, converting coal, lime and electricity into nitrogen-carbon-nitrogen chemistry
2.
Primary markets. Two segments: Specialty Chemicals (human nutrition creatine, animal nutrition Creamino, defense nitroguanidine, ~67% of FY25 sales, 94% of group EBITDA) and Basics & Intermediates (commoditized precursors, ~28-33% of sales, thin margin)
3.
Scale. FY25 revenue EUR 562.1m, FY25 EBITDA EUR 116.5m (20.7 % margin), FY25 EBIT margin 16.0%, market cap approximately EUR 1.69bn (tearsheet-dated), 1,765 employees
4.
Balance sheet character. Net debt/EBITDA 0.50x at FY25, net cash position of approximately EUR 30m at Q1 2026; over 70% of the current EUR 400m capex program is non-repayable-grant or prepayment funded
5.
Geography. Germany-concentrated: 59 % of FY24 sales from Germany, 76% from Europe overall; North America approximately 18%; single-site operational risk in the Bavarian "chemical triangle" is a repeatedly flagged structural weakness
6.
Segment mix shift. Specialty Chemicals margin ( 27.7%-29.9% depending on period) is decoupling from Basics & Intermediates (3.6%-0.8% margin), which is loss-adjacent and shrinking on Chinese dumping and European steel/agri weakness
7.
Investment style implication. A capacity-expansion / structural change-driven growth story (self-help capacity build funded by external grants) rather than a classic steady compounder
7.1
Visibility caveat: Earnings visibility is high but concentrated in two lumpy capacity ramps (German nitroguanidine H2 2026, creatine plant H2 2027)
8.
Ownership. Free float 74.27% (Jan-26) to 64% institutional (Jun-26); the Czechoslovak Group (CSG) built an aggregated 20.10 % stake by June 2026, partly via cash-settled swaps, alongside long-tenured Chairman-linked holder "four two na GmbH" at 10.14%
