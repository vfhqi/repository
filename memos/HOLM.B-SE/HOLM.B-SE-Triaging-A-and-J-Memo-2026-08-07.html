1.
What it does. Holmen is a Swedish forest-products group operating four segments: Forest, Renewable Energy, Wood Products and Board and Paper, built on 1.3 million hectares of owned Swedish forest land
2.
How it earns. The company harvests roughly 2.8 million cubic metres of wood annually from owned land, achieves 50 to 60% wood self-sufficiency, and sells paperboard, sawn timber, market pulp substitutes and renewable electricity
3.
