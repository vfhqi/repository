2.
Corporate background and market leadership: Founded by royal decree in 1731, Tryg AS is Scandinavia's largest non-life insurer, serving approximately 6 million customers with a workforce of over 6,800 employees. The company maintains a market-leading #1 position in Denmark (~23% market share), while ranking #3 in Sweden (~16% market share) and #4 in Norway (~13% market share). Tryg operates primarily across Private (69% of group insurance revenue) and Commercial (31% of group insurance revenue) segments
3.
Geographic breakdown: Tryg maintains a balanced revenue footprint across Scandinavia, with Denmark generating 46% of group insurance revenue, Sweden contributing 32%, and Norway representing 22%. This geographic diversification provides Tryg with a broader underwriting base than it possessed prior to the acquisition of RSA Scandinavia
4.
Strategic transformation via M&A: A pivotal transformation in Tryg's modern history occurred in June 2021

when it completed the acquisition of RSA Scandinavia's Nordic operations in a joint transaction, acquiring Trygg-Hansa in Sweden and Codan Norway, while subsequently selling Codan Denmark to Alm. Brand. This transaction significantly expanded Tryg's scale in Sweden and Norway, reinforcing its top-three market status across all three main Scandinavian countries. Historical 3-year fundamental performance: Over the past three years, Tryg has demonstrated high underwriting profitability, delivering a combined ratio around 80-81% and an operating Return on Own Funds (ROOF) between 35% and 40%.

Insurance revenue grew by 3.4% in local currencies during H1 2026, reaching DKK 20,972m compared to DKK 19,888m in H1 2025. However, reported H1 2026 insurance service result declined to DKK 2,845m from DKK 3,846m in H1 2025 due to a pre-announced DKK 1.2bn pretax charge related to a Danish Supreme Court workers' compensation ruling.

Relative performance against Nordic peers: Measured by Return on Own Funds (ROOF) of ~40%, Tryg generates higher profitability than key Nordic peers such as Sampo (~26% ROOF) and Gjensidige (~36% ROOF). Relative performance against Nordic peers (cont.): However, Tryg's underlying earnings per share (EPS) growth is weaker, with projected 2025-2028e operating EPS CAGR of ~3.7% to 5.5%, lagging peers like Sampo and Gjensidige which average >10% EPS growth.

5.
Nordic competitor positioning: Among primary Nordic non-life insurance competitors, Sampo holds the largest Nordic and Baltic scale following its full acquisition of Topdanmark, while Gjensidige leads the Norwegian market with a 26% market share. Nordic competitor positioning (cont.): Smaller domestic peer Alm. Brand holds a ~14-15% market share in Denmark following its Codan Denmark acquisition, targeting an 82% combined ratio by 2028
6.
Asset portfolio de-risking: Tryg maintains one of the most conservative investment portfolios in the European insurance sector, having actively de-risked out of equities and higher-risk assets into Nordic AAArated covered bonds (~95% of asset allocation) and government bonds. Asset portfolio de-risking (cont.): This low-risk allocation produces stable, bond-like investment yields while reducing overall P&L volatility
7.
Share price performance metrics: Over the 12 months leading into mid-2026, Tryg's share price

underperformed the broader market, delivering an absolute performance of -4.57% and a relative performance of -0.25% versus the OMX Copenhagen PI Index. Over 3-month and 6-month horizons, absolute stock performance stood at +0.46% and -2.95%, respectively. Morgan Stanley sell-side rating downgrade: In July 2026, Morgan Stanley analyst Daniel Wilson-Omordia downgraded Tryg from Equal-weight to Underweight, lowering the price target from DKK 165 to DKK 145.
