B.1 Business Description 8 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Three-segment hardware group. Norbit ASA is a Trondheim-headquartered Norwegian technology group operating three segments: Oceans (subsea sonar and acoustic sensing), Connectivity (satellite/GNSS tolling and fleet tracking hardware) and PIR - Product Innovation and Realisation (contract electronics manufacturing, increasingly defence-weighted).
2.
Revenue scale and mix. Group revenue reached NOK 2,502.5 million in FY2025 (up 43% year on year from NOK 1,751.4 million), split PIR NOK 1,085.7m (43%), Oceans NOK 877.9m (35%) and Connectivity NOK 613.3m (25%) as measured by segment sales.
3.
Margin architecture. Oceans and Connectivity are the proprietary, high-margin core (adjusted EBIT margins of roughly 30% and 27% respectively in FY2025); PIR is the dilutive contract-manufacturing anchor at roughly 19% EBIT margin (elevated to 20% in Q1 2026 on defence mix).
4.
Balance sheet character. Net debt/EBITDA fell to 0.78x in FY2025 (0.20x guided for 2026, net cash by 2027); the equity ratio stood at 46% in FY2025; operating cash conversion reached 105% of EBITDA in Q1 2026.
5.
Geography. Approximately 79.67% of FY2025 revenue was EMEA-sourced; Americas and APAC each contributed the balance in single-digit-to-low-teens percentages; roughly 80% of group sales are international.
6.
Ownership and leadership. Founder-CEO Per Jorgen Weisethaunet has led the company since 2001 and holds 10.9% of shares; founder Steffen Kirknes holds a further 9.64%; the top three shareholders control 30.07% of equity with no activist presence recorded.
7.
Investment-style character. The business reads as a hybrid growth compounder with a cyclical, contract-manufacturing-driven overlay: durable proprietary-technology moats in Oceans and Connectivity sit alongside a fast-scaling, concentration-exposed, lower-margin PIR segment whose near-term growth is the dominant driver of group numbers.
8.
Recent strategic step-up. Norbit raised its 2026 revenue roadmap target to above NOK 3.0 billion (from a prior February-2024 target of above NOK 2.75 billion for 2027) and agreed to acquire Water Linked AS for NOK 330 million in June 2026, funded by a new term loan.
