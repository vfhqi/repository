EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

INFICON Holding demonstrates high executive transition quality with stable CEO leadership from Oliver Wyrsch and an orderly CFO succession planned for mid-2026, preserving long-term financial discipline and minimizing executive disruption risks through 2026. The company maintains strong board governance through substantial direct equity ownership of 217,737 shares among directors and annual re-election mechanisms that enforce strict accountability to shareholders. Strategic priorities remain highly consistent with no active activist intervention, focusing on high-margin organic capacity expansion and market-share gains in semiconductor technology through customized process control software.

External forces present a highly favorable outlook as accelerating advanced semiconductor architectures and liquid cooling demand drive core volumes, while niche specialization and high-end technological barriers protect INFICON from intense low-end competition, achieving market shares up to 60% in specialized technologies. Swiss neutrality provides a critical competitive advantage, allowing INFICON to remain unrestricted with Chinese chipmakers unlike heavily regulated US peers, while completed manufacturing relocations to Malaysia and China successfully bypass heavy US import tariffs. Global supply chain restructuring strengthens component availability across over 1,000 suppliers, though significant currency exposure with 72% of revenues generated outside Europe exposes profits to adverse dollar movements against the Swiss franc, partially offset by resilient pricing power demonstrating sustained margins near 50% through cycles.

Quarterly sales reached record highs in mid-2026 with Q2 2026 revenues of USD 198.10 million driven by accelerating semiconductor demand globally, representing 18.30% organic growth, though revenue volatility remains linked to cyclical Chinese chipmaker investments and geopolitical trade disruptions that delayed equipment spending, while working capital efficiency improved significantly with elevated cash flow generation and accelerated inventory turns to 2.50 times in Q2 2026.

INFICON demonstrates a consistent pattern of beating conservative market expectations with operating income of USD 42.30 million in Q2 2026 exceeding Visible Alpha consensus by 18%, triggering Jefferies to raise mid-term EPS forecasts by 11% to 21%, while gross profit margin improved to 46.60% benefitting from completed efficiency measures and positioning for operating margins above 20% driven by rising sensor intensity and sustained long-term pricing power.
