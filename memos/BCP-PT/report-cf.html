EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 27 findings

Banco Comercial Português (BCP-PT) represents a compelling structural recovery story anchored by exceptional Portuguese domestic profitability, subsiding Polish legacy litigation costs, and best-in-class operational efficiency. The bank successfully re-elected its stable leadership team for the 2026-2029 mandate, providing strategic continuity under CEO Miguel Maya, who has served since 2018. BCP maintains robust capital buffers with a phased-in CET1 ratio of 15.3% as of June 2026, an exceptional liquidity coverage ratio of 326%, and a conservative loan-to-deposit ratio of 68.5%, positioning the bank well above regulatory minimums and peer benchmarks. The group's "Deliver more value 28" strategic plan targets compelling shareholder returns through distributions of up to 75% of profits while sustaining returns on equity above 13.5%, underpinned by Portuguese operations that generate 78% of consolidated profitability. However, the investment case faces material headwinds from persistent reference shareholder overhang as Fosun evaluates divestment of its 20.45% stake, alongside heightened sensitivity to accelerated Eurozone monetary easing that could compress net interest margins across both Portuguese and Polish franchises.
