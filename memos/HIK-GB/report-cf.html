This margin performance significantly outpaces the sector median EBIT margin of 21.2% and reflects exceptional pricing power in Middle Eastern and North

African chronic disease markets where local manufacturing footprint and regulatory relationships create defensible moats. The company's net working capital days of 264 days in H1 2026 represents a substantial outlier, rising from 245 days in late 2025, positioning Hikma in the bottom quintile of working capital efficiency among diversified generic pharmaceutical peers. This metric compares unfavorably to Teva's target of 80% cash conversion and suggests structural challenges in regional inventory management and receivables collection cycles, particularly across MENA government tender channels where extended payment terms are common.

The elevated working capital absorption directly constrains free cash flow generation despite strong reported profitability.
