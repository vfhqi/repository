Corporate History and Concession Framework

1.
Concession Agreement extended to 2046: Established in 1996 as a public-private partnership under the Airport Development Agreement (ADA), Athens International Airport S.A. ("Eleftherios Venizelos") secured a 20-year concession extension ratified by Law 4594/2019, extending its operating rights through June 11, 2046. The extension established an intangible asset model under IFRIC 12 with full usufruct rights reverted to the Greek State at expiration without compensation
2.
Legal protection and competitive moat: The ADA acts as a lex specialis, granting AIA exclusive commercial rights and establishing strict legislative limits that prevent the development or upgrade of competing international airports within a 50 km radius of Athens city centre. AIA also holds a statutory right-to-match on any new concession tender launched six years prior to the 2046 expiry under Law 5045/2023
3.
Ownership structure post-IPO: Following the 30% stake divestment by the Hellenic Republic Asset Development Fund (HRADF) in February 2024 at €8.20 per share, AIA listed on the Athens Exchange. The strategic shareholding is anchored by AviAlliance GmbH at 50.19%, Growthfund (state entity) at 25.60%, and a free float of 24.21%
