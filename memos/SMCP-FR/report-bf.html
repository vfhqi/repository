Plausible problems to monitor

SMCP's operational and financial profile reveals several critical underperformance areas when benchmarked against industry peers and sector base rates. These Plausible problems to monitor highlight structural vulnerabilities that constrain the company's competitive positioning and expose inherent risks in its business model. The most pronounced weakness manifests in capital efficiency metrics, where SMCP's NOPAT ROCE of 5.6% in 2025 substantially trails Ralph Lauren's historical ROIC of 16.0%, signaling inferior asset utilization and earnings quality across the operational base.

This return deficit becomes particularly concerning when contextualized against the peer set, as even mid-tier competitors like Hugo Boss achieve 10.0% ROIC, suggesting SMCP operates with structurally higher capital intensity or margin compression that prevents competitive cash-on-cash returns.

259 263 260 264 Operating margin volatility represents another significant structural weakness, with SMCP's EBIT margins contracting sharply from 9.2% in 2022 to 4.4% in 2024 before recovering to 7.8% in 2025. This extreme cyclical sensitivity reflects the company's high fixed-cost retail network and limited operational flexibility during demand downturns. The leverage profile compounds financial fragility, as SMCP's net debt including IFRS 16 liabilities reached EUR 531.3 million at the end of 2025, with lease obligations accounting for EUR 383.8 million of total debt.

This lease-adjusted leverage burden substantially exceeds that of competitors like G-III Apparel, which maintains minimal net debt, exposing SMCP to elevated refinancing and covenant risks in stressed scenarios.
