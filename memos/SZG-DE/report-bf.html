  1. the European steel sector is a highly fragmented, mature market facing structurally depressed demand but undergoing consolidation through major corporate reorganisations
  2. newly implemented trade barriers like CBAM and the July 2026 TRQs create a structurally higher price floor and restrict global oversupply
  3. the industry profit pool is migrating toward mills with lower carbon footprints and high-value product portfolios, leaving high-cost operators exposed

Underperforming absolute output: Domestic output has fallen even faster than demand, decreasing 39% since 2007 as imports seized market share
3.
Deep consumption deficit: This demand level is 17% below the 2018 pre-pandemic volume and 32% below the 2007 peak

Low capacity utilisation: Low regional demand has compressed average European steel mill capacity utilisation to just 61% to 62%

Concentrated stainless market: Only four major players operate in the highly consolidated European stainless steel market

Fragmented carbon steel: The carbon steel sector remains highly fragmented compared to both the US market and regional stainless

Major carbon competitors: Six primary players compete in the European flat carbon market, led by ArcelorMittal and thyssenkrupp

Projected price tailwind: CBAM carbon costs should add an estimated €30 to €70 per ton to domestic European steel prices
