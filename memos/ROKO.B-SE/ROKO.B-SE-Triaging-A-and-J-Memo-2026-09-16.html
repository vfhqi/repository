[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Röko is a young Swedish serial acquirer of Lifco lineage whose stall in 2025 was cyclical and whose acquisition engine is running again. At SEK 1,808 the shares pay about 17% a year for that, below the framework's 20% bar, because the price has already recovered 30% from its spring low and the balance sheet that funded the recovery now rations the next leg.

  • The change is internal, and it is the model running harder rather than a new direction.
    • Röko buys European niche businesses at about 7x EBITA and holds them for ever
    • the change we test is the re-acceleration of that engine after a slow 2025.
    • Three platform deals in 2025, EBITA growth of 9% against a 15% ambition, and management "not satisfied"
      • four platform deals consolidated in H1 2026 adding SEKm 1,249 of combined annualised sales, and CyTech in August 2026 as the fifth.
    • A planned succession on 22 April 2026: Johan Bladh, party to 32 of 33 transactions, became chief executive, and co-founder Fredrik Karlsson moved to Deputy CEO to source deals full-time.
    • Financial net debt to EBITDA rose from 2.0x at year-end 2025 to 2.6x at 30 June 2026 against a 3.0x ceiling
      • the step-up was funded with the balance sheet, by choice.
  • The external forces are a mild headwind that is easing, not a tailwind.
    • No external force meets the framework's 'Demand Inflection' test
    • the company's own cycle is the one force turning up.
    • Organic sales growth 0% in Q4 2025 and 2% for 2025
      • 6% in Q1 2026 and 3% in Q2 2026.
      • Peers recovered faster, at 5 to 6%, so Röko lags a sector recovery by 150 to 300 basis points.
    • The currency drag on reported sales and EBITA halved from 6% in Q1 2026 to 3% in H1 2026
      • the US tariffs of 2025 are washing out of the comparisons.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up on every line the framework reads except cash returns.
    • Q2 2026 sales rose 31% to SEKm 2,045 and adjusted EBITA rose 37% to SEKm 427, a 9% beat of consensus.
    • The adjusted EBITA margin was held at 20.9%, up 90 basis points year on year, while the acquired units diluted it
      • pricing did the offsetting.
    • Cash conversion rose to 72% of adjusted EBITA in H1 2026 from 67%.
    • Consensus FY2027 EPS is up 18.1% in twelve months
      • the buy share rose 25 points to 50%
      • the dashboard's revision-momentum score is 10.
  • The change has travelled all six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain.
    • It shows in the new chief executive and the decision to lever up, in the five deals, in organic growth and margin, in the reported quarter and in the sell-side's numbers.
    • The question is whether the next leg arrives, further deals inside the ceiling and organic growth at about 4%, before the multiple is tested.
  • What we think it earns: EPS on the brokers' basis of SEK 61.50 for FY2025 rising to about SEK 92 for FY2028, 14.4% a year, nearly all of it from acquired revenue, none from margin.
    • On the framework's valuation convention, 21.7x the 24-month-forward EPS of SEK 106 at March 2028 gives SEK 2,300, +27.2%, 17.4% a year over eighteen months
      • 22.75x the SEK 124 at September 2029 plus SEK 17 of dividends gives SEK 2,838, +57.0%, 16.2% a year over three years.
      • On a one-year-forward footing the same path gives 15.6% a year.
    • Cash returns are nil by policy
      • earnings growth carries the return
      • the re-rating is the swing factor.
  • What it costs: SEK 1,808, or 21.0x 24-month-forward earnings of SEK 85.93, the 8th percentile of a seventeen-month listed history whose median is 24.40x.
    • Against peers the multiple is in the middle: a 20% discount to the proven Swedish compounders (Lifco 25.8x, Addtech 26.9x, Lagercrantz 26.1x) and a 60% premium to Sdiptech at 13.1x.
      • On reported FY2025 EPS of SEK 51.63 the trailing multiple is 35x.
    • The buyer also takes 2.6x financial net debt to EBITDA, put and call option liabilities of SEKm 2,776 to minority holders, and a consensus set by one house.
  • The price already carries most of the change, which is where the chain breaks as a return.
    • The shares rose 30% from the 52-week low of SEK 1,394 through the H1 2026 deals and the Q2 beat
    • the modal return of about 17% a year sits inside the framework's C band and below its 20% bar.
  • The re-rating is available on one condition, and the sell-side's own convention makes that condition plausible.
    • Consensus FY2028 revenue growth is 4.2%, which is organic growth alone: the sell-side models no acquisitions beyond those announced, while the company has done three to seven platform deals a year since 2019.
    • If the FY2028 consensus EPS is revised up by 10% or more by March 2028, the multiple returns to its own median of 24.4x and the eighteen-month return is about 31% a year, an A.
      • If it is not, the fade is proved right, the multiple settles at about 19.5x and the return is about 6% a year, an F.
      • We put the cleared branch at plausible, 45%
      • the modal case is the odds-weighted blend.
  • The earnings line is the case's soft spot, and the framework's drop-through sensitivity shows its size.
    • A plausibly worse ratio from EBITDA to EPS, with rates 1.5 points higher, the minority share two points higher and tax at 27%, takes about SEKm 115 off FY2028 net income, 8.6%, and the eighteen-month return to 10.7% a year, a D.
    • That is the size of the balance-sheet risk in the earnings line.
  • The pattern it fits is 'OSHQC CfC Clearing', at a C.
    • A high-quality young compounder whose organic engine stalled in 2025 while its sector was attacked, whose multiple fell from a median of 24x to 21x, and whose 2026 shows the stall was cyclical.
    • The fit is half-crisp because the price moved before the concern cleared and a second concern, leverage, has taken its place.
    • 'Compounder' is the secondary fit: the mis-modelled line is named, the upgrader record is seventeen months long.

  • The case is therefore a good business at a fair price with a levered engine.

    • We park it at SEK 1,808 and progress it below about SEK 1,700, where the eighteen-month path pays 22% a year, or earlier if the cleared branch's evidence arrives.

  • Rating = C, because the change is real and has reached the sell-side's numbers, but the price already carries most of it: the modal return at SEK 1,808 is 16 to 17% a year, inside the framework's C band, and the 20% bar is cleared only on the branch where the FY2028 consensus is upgraded by 10% or more and the multiple returns to its own median.

  • Not B, because B would need a modal return of about 18% a year or more
    • that needs an entry near SEK 1,750 or the cleared branch to be more likely than not, and we put that branch at plausible, 45%, for the reasons given under 'Modal return'.

Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The hypothesis at its strongest: Röko is a false friend, a levered roll-up whose adjusted EBITA grows while its reported earnings, its cash after minorities and its balance sheet decay, so that 21x is not cheap but full for what it is.
    • The gap between adjusted EBITA (SEKm 1,339) and operating profit (SEKm 1,051) for 2025 is 27.4%, with SEKm 242 of acquisition amortisation excluded from the adjusted line
      • the amortisation-to-sales ratio is 4% against 2.5% at Addtech, Indutrade, Lagercrantz and Lifco.
    • The put and call option liabilities of SEKm 2,776 rise SEKm 191 for every SEKm 100 of subsidiary EBITA and are charged to equity, SEKm 150 in 2025 and SEKm 239 in 2024
      • average ownership is 75% and SEKm 148 of dividends went to minorities in 2025.
    • Leverage rose from 2.0x to 2.6x in six months
      • six of the last eight deals came through auctions with Nordic private multiples at 8 to 10x
      • long-term cash conversion is 60%
      • currency is unhedged
      • 80% of revenue is cyclical
      • the one covering broker is at Hold with a bear case of SEK 1,400.
  • What we concede: the earnings line the multiple is struck on is 19% above reported (SEK 61.50 against SEK 51.63)
    • the option liabilities are debt-like and grow with success
    • leverage now rations the engine
    • cash after minorities is about 60% of EBITA, not the 72% headline
    • entry multiples are rising.
  • Why we still hold our view: the false friend's defining failure, adjusted EBITA up while reported profit contracts, is not observed.
    • Reported EPS rose 7.5% in H1 2026, SEK 29.71 on SEK 27.63, and FactSet reported net income rose 16% in Q2 2026, SEKm 207 on SEKm 179.
    • The amortisation is non-cash and its cash cost sits in the acquisition line, which our return arithmetic carries through the debt and a flat share count.
    • Interest-bearing leverage alone is 0.6x adjusted EBITDA
      • the options are exercisable over five to ten years and are near cash-neutral for net debt on exercise.
    • FactSet free cash flow was 135% of reported net income in FY2025 and conversion rose in H1 2026.
  • What the hypothesis wins: its slower version, that the model works but the price already pays for it, is exactly what our modal return of about 17% says.
    • We hold a C, not a D, because the engine is running and the earnings are real
    • and not a B, because the hypothesis is right about the price.
    • We make no claim that any segment or asset covers the enterprise value
    • the 18x trough we use under 'Limited multiple risk' is a multiple argument, not a cover.
