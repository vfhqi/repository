Section 5 – Regulatory and Policy Shifts: US Pricing, Tariffs, and Local Manufacturing

Section summary
  1. Geopolitical tariffs and U.S. pricing mandates represent a material risk to gross margins for European pharmaceutical exporters.
  2. Structural onshoring and strategic pricing agreements with the U.S. government are emerging as essential supply chain defense mechanisms for specialty biopharma. Net read: Imposing import tariffs and most-favored-nation pricing tethers threatens to compress operating profits for innovators lacking domestic U.S. manufacturing infrastructure.

U.S. drug pricing reforms and MFN mandates

1.
Compressing net margins: Former market access executives note that the Inflation Reduction Act removes five years of economic life from innovative medicines, forcing manufacturers to launch at materially higher initial prices to offset back-end erosion.
2.
Eroding commercial pricing: Industry experts project that the Medicare Maximum Fair Price of $274 per month for semaglutide, commencing on 1 January 2027, will leak into commercial pricing contracts and force down private insurer net prices globally.
3.
Tethering global benchmarks: A consultant noted on 13 November 2025 that Donald Trump's executive ultimatum demanding most-favored-nation drug pricing parity by September 2025 has forced European exporters to delay or skip clinical launches in lower-priced European markets.
4.
Squeezing metabolic earnings: Morgan Stanley's 11 September 2026 report stated that Medicare negotiations will compress Ozempic's pricing into its historical 10-15% annual decline band starting in 2027, accelerating diabetes franchise volume erosion.
5.
Accelerating discount trends: DNB Carnegie's 4 February 2026 analysis highlighted that the $274 Medicare price benchmark represents a steep drop from the estimated $383 Medicare net price in 2025, which will severely compress Medicaid margins.
6.
Reorganizing patient access: Novartis has adjusted its patient access programs because the Inflation Reduction Act's $2,000 Medicare out-of-pocket cap, implemented in 2025, significantly reduced the need for charitable foundation aid.

Tariffs and supply chain onshoring dynamics

7.
Imposing structural costs: The baseline 15% tariff agreed between the United States and the European Union on 28 July 2025 represents an estimated $13-19 billion annual penalty that directly compresses innovator gross margins.
8.
Diversifying manufacturing footprints: AstraZeneca's 29 April 2025 earnings call highlighted its mitigation strategies against import duties, relying on 11 domestic U.S. manufacturing sites to produce the vast majority of its domestic medicine volume.
9.
Securing regulatory exemptions: On 31 August 2026, UCB announced a strategic agreement with the U.S. government to exempt its portfolio from tariffs and price mandates in exchange for onshoring a $2 billion biologics facility in Georgia and donating essential medicines.
10.
Mitigating supply shocks: Camurus disclosed in its 17 August 2026 questionnaire that it has heightened vendor diversification and contract manufacturing strategies to protect against material supply chain disruptions.
11.
Investing in localization: Roche's 28 September 2026 presentations outlined a massive onshoring program committing $50 billion to U.S. operations by 2029 to shield its high-margin diagnostics and oncology franchises.
12.
Disrupting historical investment: Sanofi's board of directors joined other European majors on 22 September 2026 in warning that European life sciences are losing the global investment race as over $600 billion is redirected to the United States and China.
13.
Benefiting allied hubs: Indian active pharmaceutical ingredient suppliers like Natco Pharma noted in their 29 May 2026 annual reports that the U.S. Biosecure Act is driving a structural diversification away from Chinese CDMOs toward globally compliant contract manufacturers.
14.
Other side of the story: Although companies claim that localized manufacturing and political agreements protect earnings, a health economist at Johns Hopkins University cautioned on 23 June 2023 that the Inflation Reduction Act's revenue impact remains an unavoidable 5% structural drag on global biopharma margins.
