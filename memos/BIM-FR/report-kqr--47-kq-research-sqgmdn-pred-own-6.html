Target Codes and Fee Structure

This payment ladder means bioMérieux's premium BIOFIRE panels, which frequently target over twelve organisms, rely heavily on maintaining the highest tiered rate of $416.78 to support their premium hardware placements.

Dedicated coding: Point-of-care testing on bioMérieux's SPOTFIRE platform relies on proprietary program codes that are subject to strict clinical necessity reviews. Specifically, the SPOTFIRE Respiratory/Sore Throat panel uses dedicated Proprietary Laboratory Analyses code 0564U to report ten viral and four bacterial targets. Providence Health Plan guidelines specify that code 0564U is potentially medically necessary only when accompanied by qualifying diagnostic ICD-10 codes or billed in an approved place of service.
