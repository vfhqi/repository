The reasoning behind the inputs

Segments: revenue and margin by segment, where the company reports them

The company reports two product segments and three business areas. Product segments (FY2025, FactSet): Medical Supplies, Devices and Equipment SEK 15,223m (91% of sales); Services SEK 1,564m (9%). No margin is reported by product segment. Business areas (H1 2026 sales share and adjusted EBITA margin): North (Sweden, Norway, Finland, the Baltics) about 31% of sales at 13.6%; West (Benelux, United Kingdom, Ireland, Denmark) about 51% at 9.6%; Central (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Poland, Czech Republic, Slovakia) about 18% at 9.2%. Group adjusted EBITA margin 9.9% in H1 2026 and 9.5% in FY2025. North's share was 39% in 2023; every point of mix shift from North to West or Central costs about 4 basis points of group margin.

My business-area path. North: sales growth about 12% in 2026 (10% organic in Q2 2026 on the lapsed defence comparison, acquisitions Med Kjemi and Ambercell), 7 to 8% in 2027, 6 to 7% after; margin 13.2% in H2 2026 and 13.0% in H1 2027 (Gothenburg double running), 13.8% in H2 2027 and 14.3% in 2028 (SEK 50 to 60m of savings land here); share of group falling to about 29% by 2028. West: sales growth about 17% in 2026 (Van Heek, RMS, Murray Surgical, homecare), 11% in 2027, 10% after; margin 9.7% in 2026, 9.9% in 2027, 10.1% in 2028; share rising to about 53%. Central: sales growth about 15% in 2026 (GHC/MPF, Labo Clinic), 10% in 2027, 9% after; margin 9.4% in 2026, 9.7% in 2027, 10.0% in 2028 (product pruning); share about 18 to 19%. Group adjusted EBITA margin 9.8%, 10.0%, 10.4% for 2026 to 2028; the mix drag from North's falling share (about 10 basis points a year) is inside those figures.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

Revenue growth. Organic sales growth 5% in H2 2026 (Q3 2026 against a normal comparison of 5% in Q3 2025; Q4 2026 against -1% in Q4 2025), 4.5 to 5% in 2027, 4.5% a year to 2030: about 3 to 3.5% volume and mix plus 1 to 2% nominal price pass-through; no preparedness orders. Acquired growth 10.5% of sales in 2026 (the 2025 cohort annualising plus the seven 2026 acquisitions of about SEK 1.3bn of annual sales: Van Heek about 350, GHC/MPF about 300, Ambercell about 250, RMS 170, Labo Clinic about 100, Murray Surgical 73, Med Kjemi about 50), 7% in 2027, 6% in 2028, 5% in 2029, 4.5% in 2030, from a new-deal budget of about SEK 0.95bn in 2027 rising to SEK 1.05bn in 2030 at 6 to 8x EBITA, which is an acquired EBITA contribution of about 7% in 2027 and 6 to 7% thereafter against the company's 10 to 12% target. Currency -0.5% in 2026, -1.5% in 2027 (the house view of the krona strengthening toward EUR/SEK 10.40 to 10.60), -0.5% in 2028, nil after. Quarterly revenue (SEK m): Q3 2026 about 4,750; Q4 2026 about 5,420; Q1 2027 about 5,060; Q2 2027 about 5,160; Q3 2027 about 5,220; Q4 2027 about 5,910.

EBITDA margin. The EBITDA margin is on the brokers' adjusted basis (FY2025 actual 12.0%, SEK 2,015m), which is the company's adjusted EBITA (before items affecting comparability and before amortisation of acquired intangibles) plus depreciation and amortisation other than acquired intangibles (SEK 421m in 2025: lease depreciation about SEK 280m, tangible depreciation and capitalised-software amortisation the rest). Adjusted EBITA margin path: 9.5% (2025), 9.8% (2026), 10.0% (2027), 10.4% (2028), 10.6% (2029), 10.7% (2030). Drivers: acquired-company and organic mix plus pruning +25 to 30 basis points a year; Gothenburg -SEK 20 to 25m in H2 2026 and -SEK 15m in H1 2027, +SEK 25 to 30m in H2 2027, +SEK 55 to 60m a year from 2028; provisioning normalisation -SEK 15m in Q4 2026 and -SEK 25m a year from 2027; no purchasing synergies; no central cost cut; no price above inflation. Quarterly adjusted EBITA margin: Q3 2026 9.6% (about SEK 456m, +19%), Q4 2026 9.8% (about SEK 530m, +13%), Q1 2027 9.7%, Q2 2027 9.9%, Q3 2027 10.0%, Q4 2027 10.3%; the Part 3 EBITDA margins add about 2.4 to 2.5 points of other D&A to these. The rolling twelve-month adjusted EBITA margin crosses 10% at Q2 or Q3 2027.

Depreciation and amortisation. About 2.5% of sales throughout: lease depreciation rising from about SEK 280m (2025) toward SEK 320m (2028) and beyond with the estate; tangible depreciation including SEK 10 to 18m a year for the Gothenburg distribution centre from 2027; amortisation of capitalised development and distribution rights on a 10-year software life. Excludes amortisation of acquired intangibles (about SEK 341m in 2025, rising to about SEK 400m in 2026 and SEK 490m by 2030), which the model should treat as the bridge from adjusted to reported earnings.

Net financial items. The company's reported line (SEK 350m in 2025; SEK 407m in 2024), which includes lease interest of roughly SEK 50m, the unwinding of discount on contingent consideration and unhedged currency effects, on top of interest on net debt of about SEK 4.5 to 4.9bn at an average 4.5 to 5% with about 30% hedged by swaps. Flat because net debt stays flat in absolute terms while acquisitions continue; no rate relief assumed; a one-point rate move is SEK 35m. Because the lease interest sits in this line, the lease payments in Part 2 are the principal portion only.

Tax. 24% (22.6% in 2025 after the IPO removed non-deductible shareholder-loan interest; 33% in 2023 to 2024; Carnegie's normalised 25%; peers 22 to 24%; mix drifting toward higher-tax Germany and France).

Dividend. Above 30% of net profit; SEK 0.39 for FY2025 (30% of reported EPS); my figures are 32 to 33% of reported EPS of SEK 2.19, 2.55, 3.12, 3.63 and 4.12 for 2026 to 2030, declared for each fiscal year and paid in the following year (cash paid: about SEK 150m in 2026, 270m in 2027, 330m in 2028, 385m in 2029, 445m in 2030).

Share count. 383.7m diluted; incentive-plan dilution capped at about 0.81% across the 2025 and 2026 plans; no acquisition-funded issuance; no buybacks (private targets at 7x against the public share at about 16x EV/EBITA, and leverage at the ceiling).

Capital spending. Tangible capital spending at about 1% of sales (the 2022 to 2024 run-rate) plus the Gothenburg tail (about SEK 150m in 2026, SEK 30m in 2027) plus capitalised development and distribution rights of SEK 180 to 220m a year (SEK 94m of development in 2025 plus trademarks, patents and rights; DNB Carnegie assumes SEK 160 to 170m). 2025 actual SEK 590m on FactSet's combined line.

Working capital.
