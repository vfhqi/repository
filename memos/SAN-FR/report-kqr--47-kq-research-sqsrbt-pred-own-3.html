Immunology and Specialty Care Growth

This impressive performance was heavily propelled by strong underlying demand and an extended benefit from operational improvements that yielded highly favorable gross-to-net price adjustments in the US. Management stated that these operational actions allowed them to maximize net pricing, thereby providing a significant temporary boost to biopharma operating income.

These statutory changes could force Sanofi to lower its list prices to maintain formulary access, compressing gross revenues.
7.
Squeezed channels: Consolidated group purchasing organizations hold the ultimate power to exclude Sanofi's high-margin brands if net pricing concessions fall short. Sanofi disclosed in its March 3, 2025 prospectus supplement that GPOs like Ascent, Zinc, and Emisar control eightyfive percent of claims. This extreme channel consolidation enables payers to demand steep discount expansions, increasing gross-to-net volatility.

Regulatory Shifts and Tariffs

8.
Unprecedented benchmarks: The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services could introduce severe pricing pressure through newly finalized international reference models. TScan Therapeutics reported that the mandatory GLOBE model will require manufacturers to pay incremental inflation rebates beginning on April 1, 2027. Tying US drug reimbursement directly to lower international benchmarks represents a major risk for Sanofi's high-priced immunology portfolio.
9.
Voluntary frameworks: Sanofi's participation in state Medicaid discount programs could face substantial margin erosion under expanding federal pricing initiatives. Rigel Pharmaceuticals reported that all fifty US states have applied to join the GENEROUS model. This voluntary framework encourages manufacturers to offer supplemental rebates to achieve most-favorednation pricing, further deflating net realizations.
10.
Tariff exemptions: Sanofi's multi-billion dollar domestic investment strategy remains a critical tool to mitigate geopolitical trade risks. Bernstein Research reported on December 5, 2025, that Sanofi committed to spending twenty billion euros on US research and manufacturing. While this commitment secures a temporary tariff exemption, any escalation in trade disputes could disrupt active pharmaceutical ingredient imports.
11.
Other side of the story: Sanofi's recent gross-to-net outperformance might represent a sustainable optimization of its distribution channel rather than a temporary accounting benefit. Morgan Stanley reported on October 30, 2026, that Dupixent's recent US growth was supported by improved gross-to-net provisioning and tighter controls. If these operational enhancements permanently reduce unauthorized 340B rebate utilization, Sanofi could maintain structurally higher net yields.
