EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 18 findings

Bodycote faces a transformative inflection point following the recommended £1.65 billion cash acquisition by Veritas in September 2026, which crystallizes underlying asset value at a 24.3% premium to the undisturbed share price and validates the strategic repositioning underway under new CEO Jim Fairbairn. The group's operational thesis centers on capturing a multi-year civil aerospace super-cycle while executing aggressive site consolidations that shift the portfolio mix toward higher-margin Specialist Technologies (S3P and HIP), which management targets to reach 35% to 40% of group revenue by 2028 from approximately 30% historically.

This structural realignment underpins management's confident target of operating margins exceeding 20% by fiscal year 2028, supported by at least £15 million of run-rate savings from the Optimise program.

Recent financial delivery demonstrates strong operational momentum with H1 2026 core organic revenue surging 9.6% driven by commercial aerospace growth of 24.6%, though this was partially offset by automotive revenues declining 4.4% organically amid persistent Western European production weakness. Core adjusted operating margins expanded 30 basis points to 16.2% in H1 2026, though this understates the underlying progress as variable pay normalization and new program startup costs absorbed approximately 100 basis points of potential margin expansion. The group's balance sheet remains exceptionally strong with net debt of just 0.7 times EBITDA and over £120 million returned to shareholders via completed buyback programs since March 2024, demonstrating disciplined capital allocation alongside organic growth investments.
