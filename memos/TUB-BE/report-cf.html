EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 2 findings

Materiality of Change

Financière de Tubize has undergone a fundamental balance sheet transformation, fully eliminating its EUR 41.3 million bank debt in May 2025 and achieving an unprecedented 99.98% solvency ratio by June 2026, up from 97.49% in December 2025. This debt-free profile enables direct transmission of rising UCB dividend flows, which grew 4.3% year-on-year to EUR 102.3 million in H1 2026. At the operational level, the board has undergone strategic refreshment with Bruno Holthof assuming the chairmanship in April 2025, Cyril Janssen appointed Vice-Chairman in August 2025, and Stef Heylen joining in April 2026, collectively strengthening scientific oversight and pharmaceutical expertise.

At UCB, the core underlying asset, transformative inorganic expansion occurred through the acquisitions of Candid Therapeutics for up to USD 2.2 billion and Neurona Therapeutics for up to USD 1.15 billion in June 2026, adding potentially best-in-class BCMA/CD3 bispecific antibody cizutamig and off-the-shelf disease-modifying cell therapy rezanecel. Commercial execution momentum accelerated dramatically, with Bimzelx revenue surging to EUR 1,528 million in H1 2026, up 91% yearon-year, supporting an upgraded global peak sales target of at least EUR 7 billion. UCB's adjusted EBITDA margin expanded structurally to 40.7% in H1 2026 from 29.6% in H1 2025, although operational cash flow dropped sharply to EUR 369 million from EUR 711 million due to working capital build and acquisition outlays.

Recent Delivery Performance

UCB delivered an impressive headline beat in H1 2026, reporting an adjusted EBITDA margin of 40.7%, expanding by 11.1 percentage points year-on-year, with core EPS reaching EUR 6.84, almost doubling from EUR 3.53 in H1 2025, and group sales growing to EUR 4,270 million, registering a 22% reported increase. Bimzelx generated global sales of EUR 1,528 million, growing 91% year-on-year and accounting for 37% of product sales, while legacy brand assets Cimzia, Briviact, and Keppra beat consensus estimates and drove headline revenue outperformance.

Management responded by upgrading full-year operational targets, raising 2026 sales guidance to expect low-to-mid teens percentage growth at constant exchange rates, elevating adjusted EBITDA guidance to midteens to low-twenties percentage growth, and increasing Bimzelx's global peak sales target to at least EUR 7 billion. However, operational cash flow from continuing activities dropped sharply to EUR 369 million from EUR 711 million in H1 2025, driven by elevated inventory levels and trade receivable expansions, while net financial debt surged to EUR 2,821 million by June 30, 2026. UCB has demonstrated a highly reliable pattern of operational outperformance over the last three years, exceeding initial financial targets in each completed fiscal year through a conservative forecasting model, with the rapid ramp-up of Bimzelx confirming the company's ability to execute complex, multi-indication global launches.

Forward Guidance Credibility

Management maintains a highly conservative sandbagging archetype that consistently yields significant guidance beats, with UCB's management team exhibiting a clear conservative tracking style and historically beating its initial annual adjusted EPS guidance by an average of more than 10% over the last decade, demonstrated by multiple intra-year guidance upgrades during both the FY24 and FY25 reporting cycles. Financière de Tubize acts as a pure pass-through entity, meaning UCB's guidance upgrades translate directly into higher holding company dividend income, with management's updated operational targets calculated based on current rules and regulations, intentionally excluding speculative healthcare policy shifts, and the revised Bimzelx peak sales guidance of at least EUR 7 billion remaining highly conservative compared to sell-side consensus.

Morgan Stanley projects UCB's core group EBITDA margin will expand to 38.5% in FY27, supporting core EPS of EUR 13.47 in the base case, with an upside scenario where rapid immunology market expansion drives core EPS to EUR 14.10 in FY26, and a downside scenario where slower Bimzelx script growth and competitive pressure restrict FY26 core EPS to EUR 12.08. ING estimates that UCB's core EPS will grow at a +19% CAGR over 2025- 2028, outperforming the 5% peer average.

Sell-Side Positioning and Consensus

The overall rating mix per Bloomberg consensus as of August 24, 2026, shows 77% Overweight and 23% Equalweight, with 0% Underweight ratings on UCB. The price target dispersion exhibits a range spanning from EUR 220 to EUR 340, with consensus estimates placing UCB's valuation at 16x 2027 estimated core price-to-earnings, representing a 25% sector premium. However, deep polarization exists regarding Bimzelx's long-term competitive positioning, with Jefferies modeling Bimzelx peak sales at EUR 6.7 billion, which is 16% below consensus expectations, and Morgan Stanley reducing post-2027 sales forecasts by 1% to 4% due to anticipated first-line net pricing pressure.

Morgan Stanley maintains an Equal-weight rating and EUR 260 price target on balanced near-term risk-reward, Jefferies upgraded UCB to Hold with a EUR 220 price target following the post-H1 share de-rating, while ING reiterates a Buy rating with a EUR 304 price target on superior multi-year growth potential.

Fundamental Peer Position

UCB leads its large-cap biopharma peers in fundamental organic growth momentum, recording H1 2026 revenue growth of 22%, outperforming the major biopharma peer median of 6%, with adjusted EBITDA margins expanding to 40.7% in H1 2026 versus H1 2025 margins of 29.6%. At the holding company level, Financière de Tubize's market capitalization of EUR 6.2 billion reflects a structural holding discount to UCB's market value, with diversified Belgian holding companies like GBL trading at narrower discounts of roughly 21% compared to Tubize's persistent 50% discount.

However, two concerning plausible problem to monitor emerged in H1 2026: Bimzelx sales missed Visible Alpha consensus by -0.52%, showing vulnerability to step-therapy pricing pressure, and net financial debt surged to EUR 2,821 million from EUR 7 million in December 2025, driven by rapid clinical pipeline acquisitions.

Outliers worth noting

The investigation uncovered two genuine statistical outliers that place Financière de Tubize and its core asset UCB at the extreme ends of their respective peer distributions. The first outlier concerns the persistent and severe holding company discount at which Tubize trades relative to its net asset value. The company's shares trade at approximately a 50% discount to NAV, which positions it in the bottom 5% of European holding companies and represents more than double the 21% average discount observed among diversified Belgian holding peers such as GBL, Sofina, and Ackermans & van Haaren.

This extraordinary valuation gap cannot be fully explained by the mono-holding structure alone and suggests either profound market inefficiency or unquantified governance and liquidity risks embedded in the concentrated family shareholding arrangement. The discount magnitude has proven structurally persistent across multiple market cycles despite Tubize's complete debt elimination and rising dividend transmission from UCB.

The second major outlier identified relates to UCB's unprecedented clinical-stage acquisition spending intensity during the first half of 2026. The company deployed EUR 2.3 billion in upfront cash payments to acquire Candid Therapeutics and Neurona Therapeutics within a compressed timeframe, representing one of the largest committed capital outlays for early-stage pipeline assets relative to UCB's mid-cap biopharma peer group.

When normalized against UCB's market capitalization and operating cash flow generation, this transaction volume positions the company in the top 5% of aggressive acquirers among European specialty pharmaceutical companies over comparable six-month periods. The outlier nature of this capital deployment becomes even more pronounced when considering that both acquired assets remain in Phase 1 or Phase 2 development with

A third statistical anomaly emerges from the extreme divergence between UCB's reported profitability expansion and operational cash generation during H1 2026. While the company achieved an adjusted EBITDA margin of 40.7%, representing an 1,110 basis point year-on-year expansion that places it in the top quartile of biopharma margin performance, its operating cash flow from continuing operations simultaneously collapsed to EUR 369 million from EUR 711 million in the prior year period.

This represents a 48% decline in cash conversion that stands as a bottom-decile outcome when benchmarked against peer companies experiencing comparable EBITDA growth. The magnitude of this profitability-cash flow disconnect signals either aggressive working capital expansion, significant non-cash accounting benefits inflating reported earnings, or a fundamental shift in the business model's cash generation characteristics that has not been adequately explained in management commentary.
