Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The timing and volume of customer production ramps are the primary determinants of Qt Group's high- margin royalty revenue, which is critical for achieving sustainable long-term EBIT scalability.
1.
The Key Question: "Which named Qt Group (QTCOM-FI) customer programmes that use the Qt framework (for example Chinese electric-vehicle makers' new models, the Suzuki e Vitara digital cockpit, Hyundai and Kia platforms, Mercedes-Benz, medical-device and consumer-electronics design wins announced in 2022-2026) are scheduled to enter or ramp production in 2027-2028 , what production volumes do S&P Global or other forecasters expect for those vehicles and devices, and what have Qt Group, its customers or analysts said about the timing of the royalties these design wins generate?"
2.
Understanding the production schedule for these programs is vital because Qt Group's business model transitions from upfront developer license fees to recurring per-device distribution licenses once products reach the market. For an investor, the development of earnings per share (EPS) is highly sensitive to this transition because distribution licenses carry significantly higher margins than consulting or initial software sales, as noted by DNB Carnegie on August 5, 2026. A delay in the ramp of a major automotive platform, such as the Suzuki e Vitara or the Mercedes-Benz electric E-Class, would directly impact the company's ability to meet its long-term financial target of 20-30% annual revenue growth. The answer to this question also reveals the company's exposure to cyclical end-markets and geopolitical risks, particularly in the US and China. Qt Group management stated during the Q4 2024 earnings call that their automotive segment represents 15% to 20% of revenue and is subject to slowing factors such as potential tariffs. If Western automakers lose market share in China or if production volumes are cut due to trade disturbances, the distribution license revenue–which DNB Carnegie estimated would grow only modestly in 2025–could face further downward revisions. Conversely, the successful ramp of programs in the 2027-2028 window would validate the company's "design-to-royalty" strategy and support a structural improvement in EBITA margins toward the company's 30% target. Furthermore, this question is closely related to the integration and license conversion of IAR Systems, which was acquired in October 2025. As IAR Systems moves from perpetual to subscription-based licenses, the overall group earnings will be pressured in the short term, making the "old Qt" distribution licenses an even more critical buffer for EPS. An expert and former CIO at Qt Company explained on April 7, 2021, that the runtime licensing model allows business to increase rapidly without the constant need for new license sales, providing a baseline for yearover-year growth. Any shortfall in the projected production volumes for 2027-2028 would therefore challenge the valuation multiples that currently assume a significant profit leap in 2027.

Section 2 – Automotive Customer Programs and Production Ramps

Section summary
  1. Major automotive programs from Suzuki, Mercedes-Benz, and the Hyundai/Kia group are set to drive volume-based royalties starting in early 2027, despite broader market headwinds.

Suzuki e Vitara and Urban Crossover Programs

1.
Suzuki's dedicated HEARTECT-e platform, which underpins the new e Vitara, is scheduled to enter the increasingly crowded electric crossover segment with production starting in early 2027. News reports from November 6, 2026, indicate the vehicle is positioned as a credible starting point for urban electric mobility, utilizing a familiar nameplate to compete with Chinese entrants. Because the e Vitara uses the Qt framework for its digital cockpit, this program represents a significant volume driver for distribution licenses in the 2027-2028 window. The success of this launch is critical as Suzuki banks on its proven engineering philosophy to maintain its trusted brand status in the electric era.
2.
Visteon, a key Tier 1 supplier, secured a cross-car platform display business with a Japanese OEM that includes a hybrid crossover launch in 2028. According to Visteon's Q3 2026 earnings call, about 25% of their new business awards from that quarter are scheduled to launch in 2028, following early ramps in 2027. This timeline aligns with Qt Group's own observations that automotive design wins typically lag production by several years. For investors, this confirms that the royalty stream from these 2026 design wins will not meaningfully impact EPS until the 2028 fiscal year.

Mercedes-Benz and the China Market Strategy

3.
Mercedes-Benz has announced plans to move toward almost fully localized sourcing in China, starting with the new electric E-Class in H2 2027. Deutsche Bank Research noted on October 28, 2026, that this strategic shift aims to make the company less dependent on global trade profit pools and mitigate the impact of intensified price competition. Since Mercedes-Benz uses Qt for its digital displays, this localization effort is a critical anchor for Qt's APAC revenue through 2028. The company is prioritizing its technological frontier in China to "take the bull by the horns" rather than standing still against local competition.
4.
Contrary evidence: Mercedes-Benz has completely halted new investment in its German operations as of November 2026, judging that domestic labor costs are five times higher than in Eastern Europe. This shift in manufacturing footprint could disrupt established supply chain relationships and delay the integration of new software features if R&D funding is similarly adjusted. While China remains a growth focus, the deterioration of the European industrial base poses a risk to the long-term volume stability that Qt Group relies on for its distribution licenses.

Hyundai and Kia Global EV Expansion

5.
Kia Corporation is aggressively expanding its EV lineup, targeting 580k units in 2026 with a long-term goal of reaching a 33% EV mix. According to Kia's Q3 2026 presentation, the company is focusing on the EV2, EV4, and PV5 models to diversify its growth engines across commercial and passenger segments. These models are expected to ramp production through 2027 and 2028 , supported by a new $2.2 bn EV plant in Gwangju. As a major user of the Qt framework, Kia's ability to maintain its sales momentum is a primary indicator for Qt's royalty growth.
6.
Hyundai Motor Company is also scaling its domestic and international production, with plans to increase U.S. localized production to over 80% by 2029. News from November 13, 2026, suggests that Hyundai is reviewing a plan to expand its Georgia metaplant to 800k units to avoid American tariffs. This massive scale-up provides a broad platform for Qt framework deployment across the Genesis and Hyundai brands. The expert perspective from a Hyundai VP on May 11, 2022, emphasizes that Qt's value justified the commercial version for such largescale, high-end applications despite licensing changes.

Comparable Past Cases and Market Precedents

7.
The transition of the automotive industry toward software-defined vehicles (SDVs) mirrors the earlier smartphone revolution, where software began to define the value of the hardware. Bernstein Research observed on October 8, 2025, that source code in a single car is projected to increase from 100 mm lines in 2020 to 600 mm lines by 2030. This expansion suggests that per-vehicle software content and royalties will naturally rise as vehicles incorporate more displays and ADAS features. Qt Group's dominance in the instrument cluster market positions it to capture a larger share of this growing value pool than in previous ICE cycles.
8.
Other side of the story: While the automotive pipeline is robust, intense competition from Android Automotive OS and real-time 3D engines like Unity and Unreal could limit Qt's future market share. An expert and former product manager at Qt Group warned on April 6, 2021, that OEMs might eventually bring more software development inhouse to reduce reliance on third-party frameworks. If large OEMs like Volkswagen or Toyota standardize on their own internal operating systems, the long-term distribution license revenue for Qt could peak earlier than investors currently expect.

Section 3 – Production Volume Forecasts and Economic Drivers

Section summary
  1. Global production forecasts for 2027-2028 remain cautiously positive, though recent downgrades for the China market signal potential volatility for Qt's short-term royalty outlook.

S&P Global and Industry Production Targets

1.
S&P Global's November 2026 forecast indicates that global light vehicle production will turn negative in 2027, driven primarily by weak domestic demand in China. Jefferies Research reported on November 12, 2026, that global production for 2027 was cut by 1.22 mm units, representing a 0.2% year-over-year decline. For Qt Group, which has a strong representation in Chinese EV manufacturing , these downgrades suggest that distribution license revenue could be more volatile than management's initial 10-20% growth guidance suggested. The forecast reflects a material slowdown from the rapid pace of growth seen in recent years.
2.
In North America, S&P Global expects a slight 0.6% increase in production for 2027, reaching approximately 15.04 mm units. This modest growth is tempered by fuel-price inflation and supply-chain pressures, which may dampen consumer demand for high-end vehicles featuring advanced digital cockpits. DNB Carnegie notes that Qt Group's 2026 EBITA margin is partly dependent on these volumes, and any further reduction in North American production could trigger downside risks to the share price. The ability of OEMs to manage inventory levels will be a key indicator for the 2027-2028 period.

Regional Market Divergence

3.
The European passenger car market is expected to show more meaningful improvement in 2027, with volume gains anticipated to reach 12.6 mm units. Deutsche Bank Research reported on November 10, 2026, that while the macro outlook remains murky, electrification uptake and competitive offerings from Chinese OEMs are driving a sustained upward trajectory in EV sales . For Qt Group, this European recovery offers a hedge against the softness in the China market, provided that its traditional OEM partners can maintain their market share. The region has seen ten consecutive months of double-digit growth in electrified vehicle registrations.
4.
Contrary evidence: Domestic wholesale volumes for Hyundai fell 8.9% in October 2026 despite more working days, highlighting an uneven recovery in the Korean market . Morgan Stanley noted on November 2, 2026, that upcoming launches for the Tucson and GV90 will be required to offer relief from this downward trend. If these domestic launches fail to gain traction, the total volume of Qt-enabled devices shipped from Korean factories could fall below analyst expectations. This regional weakness could offset the gains made by Kia in the US and European markets.

Consumer Electronics and Industrial Outlook

5.
Qt Group's exposure to consumer electronics, which typically follows different cycles than automotive, saw growth even in the soft year of 2023. DNB Carnegie analyst Matti Riikonen noted on August 9, 2024, that as consumer spending power recovers, the replacement cycle for smart devices should lift distribution license volumes. The company's focus on mission-critical verticals like medical devices and industrial automation provides a buffer against the more volatile consumer segments. Expert insights from a former sales director at Qt on April 2, 2021, suggest that the medical device market remains a huge and stable growth opportunity.
6.
Industrial automation demand continues to grow, with Componenta raising its 2027 revenue forecast based on strong order books in the energy and defense sectors. Inderes Commissioned Research reported on November 2, 2026, that the majority of this growth is already covered by orders received, providing high visibility into 2027 revenues . Since industrial devices often have longer lifecycles than cars, the royalties generated from these design wins provide a very profitable and stable long-term base. This stability is a key differentiator for Qt Group compared to more purely consumer-focused software peers.
7.
Other side of the story: The "physical AI" revolution could shift the demand from traditional UI frameworks to more agentic, voice-controlled systems. NXP Semiconductors noted on October 27, 2026, that AI-enabled processors will more than double their share of industrial and IoT revenue by 2027. If the industry moves away from graphical touchscreens in favor of automated, headless systems, the addressable market for Qt's UI framework could shrink significantly over the next decade. Investors should monitor whether Qt can successfully adapt its tools for these new, less visual interfaces.

Section 4 – Timing and Triggers of Royalty Payments

Section summary
  1. Distribution royalties typically trigger upon the shipment of end-devices, creating a highly scalable but cyclical revenue stream that lags design wins by several years.

The 3-5 Year Design-to-Production Lag

Analyst reports consistently highlight that Qt's distribution license revenue follows a significant time lag from the initial software sale. DNB Carnegie explained on July 25, 2024, that many car model designs began featuring Qt software in 2021-2022, and with a 3-5 year time lag, more models are expected to reach production in 2026-

1.
2028. This means that the record design wins announced in 2023 will only begin to generate meaningful distribution royalties during the 2027 fiscal year. For EPS growth, this creates a "back-heavy" profile for the company's long-term targets.
2.
Management confirmed this cyclicality during the Q3 2024 earnings call, noting that runtime revenue growth would increase again in 2025 as the global economy recovers. Juha Pekka Varelius stated on October 31, 2024, that while royalty growth was lower in 2024 due to the absence of new program launches, the trend over the cycle remains positive . The company does not offer discounts on distribution licenses, ensuring that increased production volume translates directly into higher gross margins. This lack of price pressure is a significant advantage in a competitive market.

Per-Unit Royalty Structures

3.
The distribution license model is typically based on a per-unit fee, which can range from a few dollars to higher amounts depending on the complexity of the device. An expert and former CIO at Qt Company noted on April 7, 2021, that for high-volume automotive customers, Qt receives euro or dollar commissions per car sold . This model allows revenue to increase rapidly as customers scale their own sales, creating an annuity-like revenue stream once a device is in the market. The stickiness of the product, due to high switching costs, ensures these royalties continue throughout the product's life.
4.
Contrary evidence: In the consumer electronics sector, royalty revenue can be more volatile due to shorter product lifecycles and higher price sensitivity. DNB Carnegie noted on February 6, 2025, that while unit prices have not changed, the volume of shipped end-devices in consumer sectors fell below trend growth in 2024. If consumer electronics manufacturers shift to lower-cost open-source alternatives for their next-generation devices, the recurring royalty stream could be shorter-lived than in the automotive or medical sectors. This variability makes it harder for analysts to forecast quarterly runtime revenue accurately.

IAR Systems and Subscription Triggers

5.
The acquisition of IAR Systems in 2025 adds a new dimension to Qt's royalty timing, as the company transitions IAR from perpetual to subscription licenses. Inderes Commissioned Research reported on February 27, 2026, that IAR's revenue is expected to decrease at a "double-digit rate" in 2026 before returning to clear growth in 2027. This transition mirrors Qt's own successful shift to subscriptions, but it delays the earnings contribution from the acquisition for at least 18 months. Investors are watching for the "trough" in IAR's revenue before expecting a significant EPS contribution.
6.
Broker DNB Carnegie expects IAR's EBITA contribution to be negative in 2026, turning positive only in 2027 as the license model turnaround completes. The analyst notes that the quicker the transition is executed, the sooner IAR can start growing its top line again. This deliberate short-term earnings hit is intended to build a more predictable and scalable long-term business, similar to the "old Qt" ecosystem. The success of this strategy will be a major factor in determining if Qt Group can reach its 2027 profitability targets.
7.
Other side of the story: The complexity of modern software-defined vehicles may lead to more frequent OTA updates, potentially creating new triggers for royalty payments. If OEMs shift toward a "software-as-a-service" model for their vehicles, Qt could potentially charge for major software version updates throughout the car's lifecycle rather than just a one-time shipment fee. While this is not currently the standard model, the industry shift toward ongoing monetization of in-car experiences could provide a significant tailwind for Qt's distribution license revenue in the 2030s.

Section 5 – Competitive Positioning and Platform Strategy

Section summary
  1. Qt's cross-platform efficiency and recent expansions into quality assurance tools strengthen its competitive moat, though the rise of Android Automotive OS remains a long-term threat.

The Multi-Industry Moat

1.
Qt Group's ability to serve 70 different industries from a single technology platform is a key differentiator against more niche competitors. DNB Carnegie analyst Matti Riikonen noted on August 5, 2026, that the most important sectors–automotive, medical, and industrial automation–each represent roughly 10-20% of revenue , providing a diversified base. This horizontal approach allows the company to reuse R&D across sectors, such as applying automotive-grade safety features to medical devices. A former head at Qt Group explained on December 18, 2023, that this strategy was a deliberate choice to focus on embedded systems where Qt has a significant advantage.
2.
The company's recent entry into the Software Quality Solutions (SQS) market through acquisitions like froglogic and Axivion has further expanded its addressable market. Inderes Commissioned Research noted on February 27, 2026, that the TQA business has tripled its sales since being acquired, growing much faster than the core ecosystem. By offering both development and testing tools, Qt creates a comprehensive lifecycle for device makers , making the platform even stickier and harder for competitors to displace. This "cross-sell" strategy is a natural evolution as software complexity increases across all target industries.

Competition from Android and Web Technologies

3.
Despite its strong position, Qt faces growing competition from Android Automotive OS (AAOS), which is gaining significant market share for In-Vehicle Infotainment (IVI) systems. Bernstein Research reported on October 8, 2025, that AAOS is expanding especially among European and American automakers due to its integration with the Google ecosystem. An expert and former CIO at Qt warned that while Android is suitable for basic UI needs, it lacks the customization and brand differentiation required for high-end applications. Qt remains the dominant choice for safety-critical systems like instrument clusters, where real-time reliability is mandatory.
4.
Contrary evidence: Volvo Cars has entered into a deep partnership with Google, migrating its global AAOS software development environment to the Horizon platform. Press releases from September 22, 2026, indicate that this partnership allows Volvo to solve its "toughest engineering challenges" and deliver new experiences faster. If other major OEMs follow Volvo's lead and fully commit to the Google ecosystem , the long-term growth potential for Qt's automotive business could be capped. The rise of open-source software development platforms represents a direct challenge to Qt's paid commercial model.

IAR Systems and Low-Level Development

5.
The acquisition of IAR Systems gives Qt Group a strategic entry point into the earliest stages of microprocessor development. DNB Carnegie noted on February 27, 2026, that IAR's tools are at the "heart of microprocessor development," providing a differentiator compared to Qt's traditional focus on UX design. By owning the tools used to program the chips themselves, Qt can influence architecture decisions much earlier in the design cycle. This expanded portfolio makes Qt a more comprehensive partner for semiconductor companies like Renesas and Qualcomm.
6.
Comparable past case: Rightware, a Finnish competitor that was acquired by ThunderSoft in 2016, specialized in HMI development for the automotive sector. Jefferies Research noted on August 24, 2022, that Rightware's Kanzi product was used by over 50 auto brands, showing the high value placed on specialized UI tools . Qt Group's strategy of acquiring similar "best-in-class" tools like froglogic and Axivion suggests they are following a proven path to consolidate the embedded development market. The success of Rightware demonstrates that there is a significant, long-term market for specialized, high-performance UI frameworks.
7.
Other side of the story: The rapid advancement of AI in software development could democratize the creation of user interfaces, potentially lowering the value of specialized frameworks like Qt. If AI tools can generate highquality, cross-platform code without the need for a comprehensive paid library, the premium pricing of Qt's developer licenses could come under pressure. Qt Group management acknowledged in their Q3 2026 report that the growing integration of AI into workflows represents both an opportunity and a source of uncertainty for the industry.

Gaps in the Evidence

1.
One major gap in the evidence is the exact per-unit royalty fee Qt Group receives for specific high-volume models like the Suzuki e Vitara. While we know the revenue model is based on per-device distribution licenses, the company does not disclose its revenue split by customer segment or model on a quarterly basis, as noted by DNB Carnegie on August 5, 2026. This lack of transparency makes it difficult for investors to calculate the precise EPS impact of a single production ramp. Accessing a detailed expert call with a former Qt sales executive specifically focusing on recent automotive contracts would be necessary to close this gap.
2.
There is also limited data on the actual production volumes of medical devices using the Qt framework. While the medical sector is described as a "defensive" and "prominent" growth driver, specific named programs and their associated production ramps in 2027-2028 are not as publicly documented as automotive programs. Understanding the scale of these medical wins is important for judging the company's resilience to automotive cycles. Waiting for the company's 2026 Annual Report, which may provide more qualitative color on nonautomotive sectors, or conducting a site visit to a major medical OEM partner could help resolve this.
3.
The evidence regarding the impact of US tariffs on Chinese EV manufacturers' production volumes is still evolving. While management mentioned this as a "slowing factor" in 2025, the long-term effect on Qt's distribution licenses from Chinese customers in 2027-2028 is uncertain. DNB Carnegie notes that US automotive companies could face "unexpected tariffs" that would invalidate current royalty estimates. A regulatory filing or trade report from H1 2027 regarding finalized tariff structures between the US, EU, and China would be required to update these projections with higher confidence.
4.
Finally, the success of IAR Systems' transition to subscriptions is still in its early stages. While management targets a three-year conversion, the actual churn rate of legacy IAR customers during this period is not yet clear. If a significant number of developers refuse to switch to the new model, the projected 2027 earnings recovery could be at risk. Monitoring IAR's quarterly ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) growth throughout 2026 will be the most effective way to judge if the acquisition is meeting its strategic goals.

Section 7 – If the Answer Differs from the Common View

Section summary
  1. A failure of Western OEMs to compete with Chinese EV makers, or a faster-than-expected shift to open-
1.
source AI tools, would significantly undermine Qt's long-term royalty-based earnings. If the Key Question is resolved differently than most sources expect–specifically, if the global automotive recovery stalls through 2028–Qt Group's earnings would face a sharp downward revision . Most brokers, including DNB Carnegie and Inderes, currently model a "trough" in 2025 followed by a "profit leap" in 2027-2028. However, if the cyclical pressure in consumer electronics and automotive persists due to permanent shifts in consumer behavior or trade barriers, the high-margin distribution licenses will not provide the expected margin scalability. This would force the company to rely more heavily on its lower-margin consulting and one-time software sales, potentially lowering its EBITA margin below the 25% target.
2.
A major threat to the common view is the potential for Chinese EV makers to prioritize domestic, open-source software stacks over Western commercial frameworks like Qt. While Qt currently enjoys a strong position in China, as noted by management in the Q3 2025 call, any regulatory mandate for "self-reliant" software in the Chinese automotive sector would devastate Qt's distribution license pipeline. This would have a knock-on effect on suppliers like Renesas and Qualcomm, who have optimized their hardware for the Qt ecosystem. If the China market becomes a "niche" for Western software companies, the total addressable market for Qt would be significantly smaller than investors currently assume.
3.
Early signs of this shift can be seen in the rise of the Horizon platform by Google and Accenture, which aims to provide an open-source alternative for the Android Automotive ecosystem. If leading OEMs like Volvo Cars successfully demonstrate that they can build high-quality experiences without expensive third-party frameworks , it could trigger a wider industry defection. This would empower OEMs to take full control of their software stacks, as feared by some experts, and reduce Qt's role to a secondary, non-exclusive tool. Investors should watch for any new "open automotive alliances" that exclude commercial framework providers.
4.
Furthermore, the impact of AI-assisted development is often viewed as a positive productivity gain for Qt users, but it could also lower the barrier to entry for new competitors. If an AI can generate safety-certified C++ code for embedded systems, the unique value of Qt's pre-written, certified libraries would diminish. This would be a structural shift that most sources are currently ignoring in favor of short-term productivity stories. If "design becomes the new code," as Figma suggests, the gravity of the industry may shift away from traditional development frameworks entirely, leaving Qt with a legacy user base rather than a growing one.
5.
Regulators in the US and EU have the power to push the outcome away from current expectations through finalized tariff decisions scheduled for late 2026 and 2027. If these tariffs result in a full-blown trade war, the global supply chains for the very devices Qt powers would be permanently disrupted. This would lead to higher costs for OEMs, potentially causing them to cut features–including the advanced digital cockpits that generate the highest royalties for Qt. The "tariff truce" mentioned by analysts in mid-2025 is fragile and remains the most significant short-term variable for Qt's EPS development.
