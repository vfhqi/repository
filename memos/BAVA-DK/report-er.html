Section 16 – Conclusion

Bavarian Nordic's H1 2026 earnings, reported on August 21, 2026, delivered a substantial top- and bottom-line beat against consensus expectations, with total revenue reaching DKK 2,034 million in Q2 2026, representing 34.5% ahead of the company-compiled consensus of DKK 1,512 million. The single most important change to the investment thesis is the upgraded structural sustainability of the Public Preparedness division. Management raised full-year Public Preparedness division revenue guidance to approximately DKK 2,500 million, up from the prior range of DKK 2,300 million to DKK 2,500 million, reflecting incremental mpox orders.
