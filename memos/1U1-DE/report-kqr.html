Section 2 – German market dynamics and Telefónica / 1&1 negotiations

Section summary
  1. German mobile consolidation remains speculative as Telefónica Deutschland pursues an organic turnaround while United Internet restructures its stake in 1&1.

United Internet capital restructuring and equity stakes

1.
Strengthened capital base: On 5 October 2026, 1&1 AG announced a 20% capital increase where its parent company United Internet AG swapped an €850.24 million loan receivable from 1&1 Versatel for 35,352,929 newly issued shares.
2.
Creeping corporate control: This debt-for-equity transaction increased United Internet AG's total shareholding in 1&1 AG from 87.19% to approximately 89.32% as of October 2026.
3.
Simplified group funding: This restructuring of intercompany debt was advised by law firm Hengeler Mueller on 8 October 2026 to simplify the group's internal financing structure while avoiding any cash outflow.
4.
Substantial deleveraging effect: Deutsche Bank Research calculated on 6 October 2026 that this balance sheet transaction will reduce 1&1's net leverage from 3.9x to 2.8x net debt to EBITDAaL for 2027.
5.
Imminent minority buyout: Deutsche Bank Research also noted on 6 October 2026 that pushing United Internet AG's stake above 90% enables a merger squeeze-out of remaining minority shareholders.

Operational restructuring and labor-law constraints

6.
Severe personnel downsizing: In response to the wholesale migration, Telefónica Deutschland CEO Santiago Argelich announced on 28 September 2026 that the company is reducing its workforce by 1,000 employees, representing a 15% labor cut.
7.
Aggressive corporate restructuring: On 10 September 2026, 1&1 AG launched a transformation program at 1&1 Versatel to cut headcount from 1,350 down to approximately 1,000 full-time positions.
8.
Depressed near-term earnings: Deutsche Bank Research reported on 11 September 2026 that 1&1 AG will incur one-off restructuring costs of €60m that lower its 2026 EBITDA guidance to €740 million.
9.
Delayed structural synergy: Deutsche Bank Research noted on 11 September 2026 that 1&1 AG must negotiate with labor unions before completing these job cuts, creating operational execution risks.

Consolidation hurdles and bilateral relationship dynamics

10.
Sustained operator skepticism: On 28 September 2026, Telefónica Germany CEO Santiago Argelich stated that while three network operators would be healthier, they have no active local consolidation projects under discussion with 1&1.
11.
Organic corporate strategy: Santiago Argelich emphasized on 28 September 2026 that Telefónica Deutschland's current business plan relies entirely on organic commercial strategy without factoring in mergers or acquisitions.
12.
Stabilizing commercial outlook: Telefónica Germany reported on 28 September 2026 that it has nearly digested the wholesale loss of 1&1 to Vodafone, setting up fiscal year 2027 as its first clean operational period.
13.
Other side of the story: Bernstein Research argued on 24 September 2026 that unless regional regulators explicitly ease merger restrictions, operators will be trapped in capital-intensive standalone infrastructure rollouts that limit investment returns.
