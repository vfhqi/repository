EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 26 findings

Section summary
  1. transformational partnerships with Roche and Boehringer Ingelheim have fundamentally reshaped Zealand Pharma from a pure cash-burning biotech into a milestone-rich developer with unprecedented liquidity reserves, but the investment case now faces material headwinds from disappointing Phase II efficacy data for petrelintide, extreme competitive replication pressures in the obesity market, and prolonged regulatory setbacks across rare disease franchises that collectively create a catalyst vacuum extending into late 2027.
