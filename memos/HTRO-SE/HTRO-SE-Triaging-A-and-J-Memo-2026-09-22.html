[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Hexatronic is a real recovery with a mix change underneath it, run by a new management team, and it is compelling as a business story and not compelling as an investment at SEK 39.74. The change reached the outputs in Q2 2026 and the market had already paid for it: the shares have risen 94.7 percent in twelve months and sit at the stock's own ten-year median multiple, so what remains is earnings growth of about 18.6 percent a year with no dividend and no re-rating we can call probable, about 15 percent a year over eighteen months against the 20 percent bar. The case clears the bar about SEK 5 lower, or on a Q3 2026 print that shows the margin surviving raw-material inflation.

  • The change is the company's own doing, with a cyclical turn beside it.
    • A chief executive appointed in March 2025 cut SEK 120m of annual cost against a SEK 110m plan and ahead of schedule, re-cut the group into three reported segments with 2028 targets, raised SEK 600m of equity in May 2026 and bought three service and connector businesses at 4.7x to 6.0x EBITDA, so four of the framework's twelve internal forces are strong and none of its red flags is present.
    • The external forces net to a tailwind: US fibre demand restarting as rates fall and BEAD money begins to flow, hyperscale and enterprise data-centre demand, and defence budgets
      • against them stand a European fibre-to-the-home market still falling 10 percent, fibre and resin prices rising with a lagging pass-through, and a Deputy Chief Executive leaving mid-pivot.
    • The largest single number in the quarter, North American Fiber Solutions +44 percent, belongs to the cycle rather than to the change
      • the margin floor beneath it belongs to the change.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up, for one quarter.
    • Q2 2026 brought organic growth of +11 percent from -2 percent in Q1, adjusted EBITA +33 percent to SEK 224m at a 10.0 percent margin, an 11 percent sales beat and a 12 percent EBITA beat, and the sell-side raised its numbers and its targets.
    • The Fiber Solutions margin rose from 5.2 percent in Q4 2025 to 9.1 percent in Q2 2026 with the cost programme complete, so the floor is structurally higher whatever volumes do.
    • Cash conversion and working capital have not followed: 84 percent conversion in Q2 2026 after 26 percent in Q1, and net working capital at 26 percent of sales.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, once.
    • It shows in the chief executive's appointment, his choices, his actions, the operating metrics, the financial statements and the sell-side's estimates
    • the July upgrade round priced the Q2 beat and the estimates have been flat for a month since, so the next layer of proof is the Q3 2026 print on 2026-10-22.
  • What we think it earns.
    • Earnings per share of about SEK 2.10 in FY2026 rising to about SEK 3.50 in FY2029, 18.6 percent a year, at consensus for FY2027 to FY2028 and below the single visible broker for FY2029
    • revenue growth and margin expansion supply about 14 percent a year of EBITA growth together, the coveted components, and falling interest supplies the rest.
  • What it costs.
    • SEK 39.74, or 13.6x 24-month-forward earnings of SEK 2.93, which is the stock's ten-year median exactly, above its two-year and five-year medians, and below eight of the ten grid peers only because that set sits at the top of its own ranges.
    • The 'Trough on Trough' entry that existed at SEK 24.33 in February 2026 has been paid for
      • the price moved against the park.
  • The return does not clear the bar on the modal path, and the re-rating that would clear it is possible rather than probable.
    • With no cash return, earnings growth on the 24-month-forward figure of 22 percent over eighteen months and 40 percent over three years, and the multiple held at 13.6x, the modal return is 14.7 percent a year over eighteen months and 12.0 percent a year over three years, a C on the framework's anchors.
    • A re-rating to 16x needs Data Center to hold organic growth and margin at or above 15 percent and the group margin to hold through raw-material inflation
      • the joint odds are about 39 percent, rounded to 35 percent, and at 16x the eighteen-month return would be 27.8 percent a year.
    • The blend of the two branches, 14.5x, gives 19.7 percent a year over eighteen months
      • we state the return on the modal branch and show the blend beside it.
  • The earnings path has no cushion below EBITA.
    • A plausibly worse drop-through (tax 27 percent, net interest SEK 110m, amortisation SEK 130m, 226m shares) takes FY2028 earnings per share from SEK 3.05 to SEK 2.74, 10 percent lower, and the returns to 6.9 percent and 8.2 percent a year, F on the framework's anchors.
  • The pattern is 'Major Corporate Change', fit B.
    • The mechanism runs from the chief executive's appointment through the segment structure and the acquisition framework to the closures, the deals and the equity raise, and then into the Q2 2026 numbers
    • the fit is not crisp on one point, the cycle's share of the quarter.
  • Where the chain holds and where it breaks.
    • It holds from the inputs to the outputs and into the estimates.
    • It breaks at the price: a recovery that was worth buying at the February trough is now valued as an earnings-growth case at a median multiple, so the last link, the return, is the weak one, and a chain is as strong as its last link.

  • The case therefore is a good company change and a real cyclical turn, already in the price, worth buying about SEK 5 lower or on a Q3 print that shows the margin surviving raw-material inflation.

  • Rating = C, because the change is material at the input and output layers, both B, but the third leg binds: at SEK 39.74 the modal return is about 15 percent a year over eighteen months and 12 percent over three years, below the 20 percent bar, with no cash return and no re-rating we can call probable.

    • Not B, because B would need either the re-rating branch to be probable (we rate it possible) or the single broker's FY2029 earnings per share of SEK 4.17 to be our path (we take SEK 3.50)
    • either would lift the eighteen-month return above 20 percent.
    • The B was live and the price is what closed it.

  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The Q2 2026 surge in the United States was inventory restocking by carriers after an eighteen-to-twenty-four-month destocking
    • Europe stays depressed
    • the newly built US duct capacity at Ogden, Utah and Clinton, South Carolina under-absorbs again
    • the acquisitions of labour-intensive installers dilute the manufacturing margin
    • and the research's "false friend" reading, acquisition-driven growth masking core decay, then holds.
  • We concede that the restocking reading cannot be excluded from one quarter, that the earnings review's own author names it the most credible bear case, and that Data Center grew 56 percent in total but 27 percent organically, so acquisitions carry about half of that segment's growth.
  • We hold our view on the business because the Fiber Solutions margin rise from 6.4 percent to 9.1 percent came with the cost programme completed, so the floor is structurally higher whatever volumes do
    • because Dura-Line's +23 percent and Prysmian's +18 percent Digital Solutions growth show the US demand is industry-wide rather than a Hexatronic inventory effect
    • and because the sell-side's bar for Q3 2026, Fiber Solutions organic growth above 5 percent and a group margin above 8 percent, is a low one to clear.
  • Where we do not hold it is the return: the restocking risk is exactly why the multiple should not be assumed higher than its median, and at the median the return is a C.
