What the company does

thyssenkrupp is a German industrial group of five divisions: Automotive Technology, Decarbon Technologies, Steel Europe, Marine Systems and tk accelis. In the year to 30 September 2025 adjusted operating profit was 83 million euros on revenue of 32,837 million euros, a 0.3 percent margin. Steel Europe is the European Union's number two flat-steel producer but bottom of its group on unit margin; Marine Systems leads the world in conventional submarines, backlog above 20 billion euros; tk accelis is Europe's number one materials distributor.

The chief executive, mandated to May 2031, is converting the group into a holding of listed segments: Marine Systems listed on 20 October 2025 with 51 percent retained, 49 percent of tk accelis is approved for distribution with a late-October listing, and the HKM joint venture half share went to Salzgitter in July 2026. The Steel Europe sale stalled with EP Group in late 2025 and Jindal in May 2026. The Krupp Foundation holds about 21 percent of the votes, Cevian Capital 8.2 percent.

Each listing converts a conglomerate discount into separately priced parts; the December 2025 agreement with IG Metall cuts 11,000 positions by September 2030; the HKM exit saves more than 300 million euros a year from 2028; and European Union safeguards from 1 July 2026 have pushed hot-rolled coil toward 800 euros per tonne. The Investment Analyst's memo of 10 October 2026 has adjusted earnings per share at about minus 0.80 euros in fiscal 2026, a charge year, 1.05 euros in fiscal 2027, 1.40 euros in fiscal 2028 and about 1.85 euros by fiscal 2031, after the tk accelis minority leakage.
