EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. strategic transformation is highly material as Prosus shifts from a holding company to an active AI-driven platform operator.
  2. portfolio value drivers are highly diversified, spanning Tencent's dominant cash flows and high- growth food delivery and fintech ecosystems.
  3. recent financial delivery exceeded expectations with robust double-digit organic revenue growth and record unlisted free cash flow.
  4. forward execution credibility faces near-term investment headwinds as increased spending on iFood and JET limits immediate EBITDA expansion.
  5. sell-side positioning is overwhelmingly bullish, supported by wide discounts to net asset value and Tencent lookup optionality.
  6. fundamental peer positioning is highly competitive, with Prosus delivering peer-leading organic growth across its core digital assets.
  7. activist structural barriers remain high due to Naspers' cross-holding voting control, preventing direct minority investor intervention.
  8. critical risk signals are intensifying in core food delivery markets, driven by aggressive local competition and regulatory mandates.
  9. the investment case remains strong due to structural self-funding, a massive valuation discount, and ongoing capital return momentum.
