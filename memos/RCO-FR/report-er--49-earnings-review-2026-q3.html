Section 16 – Conclusion

What the FY25-26 reporting event changed. The June 4, 2026 release confirmed a year in which the top line held and profit did not. Full-year sales of €935.3 million were flat organically at +0.2% but fell 5.0% on a reported basis. Current operating profit fell 11.5% organically to €165.4 million, taking the margin to 17.7%.

The numbers were not the main change, because the Cognac profit decline was slightly ahead of consensus expectations. The change was in the mid-term frame. The RC Forward programme targets €100 million of gross value creation by fiscal 2028-29, which implies a 60% EBIT uplift against FY25-26. In parallel, the tariff threat had forced management to withdraw its fiscal 2029-30 targets.

The outlook has therefore shifted from a cyclicalrecovery debate to a debate about self-help delivery, and the self-help case is still largely unproven.

Weighing structural margin optimisation against structural headwinds. The case for RC Forward is credible in design. It rests on five strategic pillars, including procurement optimisation and distribution efficiency, and on an organic overhead reduction of 1.3% already achieved. Its weakness is flow-through.

Management intends to use transformation savings to fund commercial execution rather than allow full profit flow-through, and pricing power remains limited, so the business must reinvest to support volume recovery. The tariff relief shows the same pattern. The expected tariff hit was cut to €5 million from €20 million, yet EBIT margin guidance was maintained because management planned to reinvest the savings. On the other side of the ledger, the China outcome is a managed one, not a resolved one.

The definitive anti-dumping duties will not be applied, but leading brands still face a de facto price increase from the imposed price floors. In the US, cognac depletions improved from an 8% decline to a 4% decline between the third and fourth quarters and inventory was reported at roughly four months of coverage. Even so, cognac value depletions still declined by low-single digits in the second quarter of fiscal 2026-27.

Net debt to EBITDA expanded from 2.40x to 3.22x, and the proposed dividend of €0.75 is a 50% cut from €1.50.

The organic gross margin contracted by 3.7 percentage points to 65.8%, while aging eau-de-vie inventories reached a historic high of 203% of annual sales and offbalance sheet eaux-de-vie purchase commitments rose to €443.6 million. Consensus blind spots. Four points appear under-weighted in the debate. First, volume and value are decoupled. Cognac volumes rose 7.8% in FY25-26 but were offset by a negative price-mix of 8.3%, so headline volume recovery can coexist with weak profit growth.

Second, the mix of the recovery matters. Bernstein observed that the volume recovery was largely driven by Cointreau while core Rémy Martin cognac remained weak, and the division that supplies most profits is not the one carrying the improvement. Third, shipment data may be distorted by transitions. Benelux and France shipments rose by a double-digit percentage as wholesalers bought stock ahead of the distributor handover, which suggests some reported strength is timing.

Fourth, many sellside analysts had historically projected a rapid cyclical bounce, yet persistent inflation and high borrowing costs point to a prolonged trough. The sell-side is also divided on its own conclusions, with Jefferies reiterating a Buy rating while Morgan Stanley kept an Underweight rating.

Limitations. This synthesis relies on company disclosures, broker commentary and expert-call material that the report itself shows to be inconsistent in places, including mixed fiscal-period labelling and differing hedging-rate figures. It also leans on sell-side modelling that has repeatedly proved too optimistic about the cycle. Independent depletion and price-mix data would strengthen the conclusions.

The framework above is analytical and is not a forecast or a recommendation. Two key questions for upcoming disclosures. First, how much of the €100 million RC Forward value creation will reach reported current operating profit after reinvestment, and in what annual phasing? Second, can management show US and China shipments aligning with depletions, with the upcoming sub-VSOP launch and the Reyes transition completed, while price-mix turns positive instead of absorbing the volume gains?
