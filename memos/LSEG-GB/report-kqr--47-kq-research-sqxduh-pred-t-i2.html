Section 2 – Organic Growth and Clearing Margin Normalisation

Section summary
  1. The sell-side expects LSEG's 2027 organic growth to slow as capital markets volume and net treasury income normalise from their high 2026 levels.
  2. The H1 2026 EBITDA margin expansion of 260 basis points included a 140 basis point SwapClear partner-share benefit, which will create a tough base of comparison for H1 2027.
  3. LSEG's collateral-driven clearing treasury income and subscription model offer greater cyclical resilience than the rate-sensitive and transaction-heavy models of Deutsche Börse, Euronext, and CME Group.
  4. LSEG's acquisitions of Octus and Trumid will significantly increase net finance expenses and temporarily dilute consolidated operating margins through 2028.
  5. The full consolidation of Tradeweb boosts reported group growth but increases minority interest allocations, limiting the net earnings that accrue to parent shareholders.

Organic Growth Normalisation

1.

Deutsche Bank Research reported on 13 November 2026 that the transactional tailwinds of 2026 would gradually subside next year. This cyclical normalization will cool down the exceptional growth seen in capital markets, thereby limiting near-term earnings expansion unless subscription business acceleration offsets the drag.
2.
Softening volumes: LSEG's Markets division experienced an exceptional performance in 2026 but faces a high base of comparison that sell-side analysts are actively factoring into their 2027 models. Morgan Stanley reported on 3 September 2026 that a sharp normalization in volatility and lower collateral balances could slow group growth. Consequently, investors are focusing on the sustainability of transactional revenues into 2027 because any rapid deceleration would directly dilute the company's operating leverage program.
3.
High baseline: LSEG reported in its third-quarter 2026 update that total organic income excluding recoveries grew by 8.8%, driven by a 12.5% expansion in Markets. This strong transactional performance was supported by a 21.3% organic increase in net treasury income. These elevated cyclical figures create an exceptionally high hurdle rate for the group's 2027 organic growth comparisons.
4.
Moderating momentum: LSEG's forward growth narrative is heavily debated among institutional investors who question how much trading activity will persist. Morgan Stanley reported on 13 November 2026 that the central debate for European exchanges entering 2027 is the persistence of exceptional trading activity. If transactional volumes normalise too rapidly, LSEG will rely entirely on its subscription data assets to sustain its double-digit earnings growth trajectory.

This extreme exposure means that declining interest rates pose a much larger threat to Deutsche Börse's earnings than to LSEG's collateral-driven model.

Transactional exposure: Euronext and CME Group are highly geared to transaction-driven volume fluctuations compared to LSEG's diversified subscription-heavy model. Deutsche Bank Research noted on 13 November 2026 that Euronext and Deutsche Börse possess significantly higher exposure to traditional capital markets activity. While this exposure allowed peers to deliver faster growth during the 2026 trading boom, LSEG's subscription business will provide superior revenue stability during the 2027 cyclical slowdown.
