Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What the business is. IMCD is a Rotterdam-headquartered distributor and formulator of specialty chemicals and food ingredients, acting as the outsourced sales and technical arm for chemical producers
2.
How it earns. It buys specialty ingredients from producers, adds laboratory formulation advice, local stock and small-batch logistics, then resells to industrial customers on a gross-profit spread
3.
Listing and scale. The shares trade on Euronext Amsterdam, with a market capitalisation of approximately EUR 5.34bn and FY2025 reported revenue of EUR 4,778.9m
4.
Headcount and footprint. The group employs 5,246 people across 118 offices in more than 60 countries, and reports its results in euros
5.
Product and counterparty breadth. It distributes roughly 52,000 specialty products sourced from over 3,000 principal suppliers to more than 71,000 active customers
6.
Two-segment mix. Life Sciences generated EUR 1,426.8m in H1 2026, 54% of sales, and Industrial EUR 1,204.8m, 46%, with a 53% and 55% Life Sciences split cited elsewhere
7.
What sits inside each segment. Life Sciences covers pharmaceuticals, beauty and personal care, and food and nutrition; Industrial covers coatings and construction, advanced materials and lubricants
8.
Geographic mix. Europe and the rest of Europe, the Middle East and Africa took 43% of FY2025 revenue, Asia-Pacific 26%, North America 20% and Latin America 10%
9.
Segment revenue in absolute terms. FY2025 gave Europe, the Middle East and Africa EUR 2,078.1m, Asia-Pacific EUR 1,252.3m, North America EUR 947.1m and Latin America EUR 501.4m
10.
Formulation-led model. Specialty ingredients are engineered into customer recipes, so switching requires laboratory re-testing and regulatory re-registration, which is what makes the customer relationship sticky
11.
Laboratory network. The group operates 86 market-focused application laboratories, a count also stated as 82 in earlier broker material, with six opened or upgraded during 2025
12.
Supplier contracting. More than 90% of supplier agreements carry exclusivity clauses, typically running three to five years or on an evergreen basis
13.
Supplier tenure. All ten of the largest principal relationships exceed ten years, and the top ten suppliers account for approximately 5% of cost of goods sold
14.
Customer fragmentation. The ten largest customers represent roughly 4% of revenue and the single largest below 1%, with IMCD Germany's largest account below 1% of local gross profit
15.
Technology backbone. A single global enterprise resource-planning system implemented in 2001 runs the group, supporting immediate group-wide price pass-through and rapid onboarding of acquired businesses
16.
Digital tools. The MyIMCD customer portal has over 28,000 activated users across 35 countries, and the SalesAssistant artificial-intelligence tool was deployed globally during 2025
17.
Asset-light economics. Transport, storage and logistics are outsourced entirely, with maintenance capital expenditure of EUR 11.5m in FY2025, 0.24% of sales
18.
Financial character, FY2025. Revenue grew 1% reported, gross margin was 25.0% against 25.4%, operating EBITA fell 6% to EUR 498.0m and the EBITA margin was 10.4%
19.
Financial character, H1 2026. Revenue reached EUR 2,638.4m, up 7% reported and 11% at constant currency, with operating EBITA of EUR 284.9m and gross margin of 24.9%

Cash and balance sheet. FY2025 free cash flow was EUR 465.2m at 91.4% cash conversion, and net debt stood at EUR 1,559.6m at 30 June 2026 on 2.8x leverage

Covenant position. Bank facilities carry a maximum leverage covenant of 4.25x EBITDA, and a further EUR 100m revolving facility was added in January 2026, taking the total to EUR 700m

Guidance policy. Management provides no quantitative forward guidance, citing an order book of six to eight weeks and macroeconomic and trade-policy uncertainty
