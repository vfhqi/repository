EPS Impact and Strategic Value

Conversely, a successful joint bid would significantly enhance long-term EPS visibility.

Collaborative bidding advantage: Renew's strategy of bidding jointly with four group subsidiaries represents a collaborative push to significantly increase its footprint with National Highways. This collaborative execution could push operating margins above the typical 5% to 7% range. Consequently, joint wins are highly accretive to group EPS over the long term.

Deutsche Bank's research on 12 May 2026 notes that RIS3 renewals spend will increase 75% compared to RIS2. This structural expansion highlights why the SDF2 outcome dictates Renew's organic growth.

SDF2 Framework Structure and

1.
Scale Massive program expansion: National Highways is launching its next-generation Scheme Delivery Framework 2 to manage key strategic road renewals. Renew's annual report of 17 December 2025 notes that SDF2 is an eight-year framework commencing in April 2027 with an estimated total spend of £14.5bn. This massive framework is designed to serve as the primary delivery vehicle across both the RIS3 and RIS4 investment cycles. Securing places on this program is critical for the group to maintain its long-term organic growth.
2.
Expanding addressable market: The scale of the £14.5bn SDF2 procurement highlights a significant growth opportunity when compared to historical programs. Under the original six-year SDF1 framework starting in January 2022, Carnell secured five lots worth more than £147m over six years. The exponential jump to a £14.5bn framework reflects a national aggregation of procurement lots. Consequently, this expanded program will dictate Carnell's long-term highways maintenance and renewals revenue.

Structured funding growth: The transition to SDF2 aligns with the UK Government's strategic shifts in transportation funding. Renew's annual report of 17 December 2025 reveals that the draft RIS3 determination outlines a total funding envelope worth £24.5bn. Benchmark framework comparisons: The scale of SDF2 stands out when compared to other major UK public sector construction frameworks. Bidding Timetable and Revenue Continuity

5.

Renew's interim report of 12 May 2026 confirms that its successful SDF1 frameworks have been extended by five months through to February 2028. This extension guarantees that Carnell and its sister subsidiaries will continue executing their active workbanks without interruption. This interim extension is highly favorable for near-term EPS visibility as it prevents any gaps in cash generation.
6.
Procurement timeline targets: The formal procurement timetable for the next-generation framework has already commenced its multi-stage rollout. Renew's interim report of 12 May 2026 states that bidding preparations were underway ahead of the procurement process commencing in June 2026. This schedule gives National Highways over 18 months to evaluate bids before the framework's official April 2027 start. This extended evaluation window ensures thorough compliance checking but delays ultimate award certainty for bidding contractors.
7.
Revenue protection mechanisms: The alignment between the SDF1 extension and the SDF2 bidding process acts as a buffer for Renew's order book. Renew's trading update of 1 April 2026 highlights that trading in Infrastructure has remained resilient with solid momentum. Contrary evidence: Delays in governmental determinations can create short-term uncertainty for highway framework timelines. If National Highways delays the SDF2 award past February 2028, Carnell could face a temporary revenue gap.

Route One brings specialized expertise in bridge deck maintenance and holds several long-term frameworks under SDF1. This acquisition directly strengthens the joint bidding group's ability to target highly specialized structures and concrete maintenance lots.
