EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Securitas is executing a strategic pivot from stability to profitable growth, anchoring its investment case on achieving a 10% average annual adjusted EPS growth target through a combination of Technology & Solutions mix expansion, systematic portfolio pruning of low-margin guarding contracts, finalized Stanley Security integration delivering over MUSD 50 in synergies, and accelerated divestment of non-core assets including French aviation and Global Elite Group, while the government SCIS business wind-down creates near-term revenue contraction but delivers structural margin accretion through cash-neutral working capital release.

Materiality of change is substantial and multidimensional. The orderly CFO transition to Matteo Dall'Ora on April 1, 2026 injected tech-and-service-forward leadership while preserving continuity, with Dall'Ora bringing extensive finance experience from ASSA ABLOY and 12 years at Atlas Copco. Strategically, the June 2026 Capital Markets Day marked a decisive shift in ambition, replacing the previous operating margin condition with an earnings per share performance target of 10% adjusted EPS CAGR over a business cycle.

Operationally, the group achieved its 8.0% operating margin target in the second half of 2025, with Technology & Solutions margins expanding to 11.5% and Security Services margins improving to 6.0%, representing sustained trajectory inflection after 20 consecutive quarters of margin improvement.
