Section 1 – Brief company description and history

The company conducts operations in 62 countries and employs more than 32,000 people globally. Under a diversified business model, the group pairs traditional oil and gas production with transition-related satellites. Favorable structural features position Eni S.p.A. as a highly diversified energy competitor internationally.
