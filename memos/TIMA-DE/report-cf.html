EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

ZEAL Network operates as the dominant private online lottery broker in Germany, commanding 44% online market share against fragmented state-owned operators who collectively hold 58%, positioning the company as the clear leader in a structurally attractive digital migration story. Management guides FY26 revenue to €250-260 million with EBITDA of €70-75 million, reflecting a conservative sandbagging archetype demonstrated through consistent historical outperformance, including FY25 revenue of €218.5 million that exceeded upgraded guidance of €205-215 million by 9.25%.

The investment case centers on sustainable structural growth from German online lottery penetration, aggressive proprietary product diversification through high-margin charity raffles, rapid virtual slot machine scaling, and geographic expansion via the recently completed SevenCanyon UK acquisition.

Recent strategic changes represent material inflection points that fundamentally expand the addressable market and reduce traditional jackpot dependency. The SevenCanyon acquisition, financed through a disciplined €40 million seven-year Deutsche Bank term loan, targets the UK prize draw market with annual billings of £1.3 billion and is expected to contribute high-single-digit million euros in positive EBITDA during the first full year, establishing a second home market outside regulatory-concentrated Germany.

Proprietary charity lottery Traumhausverlosung achieved €38.9 million in billings during FY25 with management targeting over 50% growth in FY26, while virtual slot machine games expanded to over 740 titles with monthly active users surging 34% year-on-year to 35,000 in Q1 2026. These diversification initiatives collectively reduce exposure to volatile state lottery jackpot cycles and create sustainable high-margin revenue streams with structural competitive advantages.

Payment processing expenses surged 24% to €457 thousand in Q1 2026, driven primarily by increased PayPal usage among customers. This represents a material margin headwind that compounds with customer verification costs that jumped 79% to €208 thousand due to more stringent regulatory requirements. Commission expenses for third-party game developers also rose 25% in Q1 2026 to €132 thousand, indicating that the Games segment expansion is driving triple-layered cost inflation across payment rails, identity verification, and content licensing. Total direct operating costs increased 19% to €5.80 million, far outpacing the 6.2% revenue growth achieved in the same period.

Consolidated EBITDA margin compressed significantly to 28.5% in Q1 2026 from 34.7% in Q1 2025, representing a 620 basis point deterioration that reverses the multi-year margin expansion trajectory. While management attributes this to strategic investments in new products, the magnitude of compression raises concerns about permanent structural margin reset rather than temporary investment cycles. Full-year FY2025 EBITDA margin of 31.5% already represented a decline from 32.9% in FY2024, establishing a two-year margin contraction pattern.

Jefferies expects no further billing margin improvements following the successful optimization to 17.8% in Q1 2026, indicating that the single most powerful organic margin driver of the last three years has been fully exhausted. Outliers worth noting

ZEAL Network exhibits several statistical anomalies that position the company in the extreme tail of performance distributions when benchmarked against broader digital platform operators and online gambling peers. The most striking outlier is the company's EBITDA margin of 31.50% in FY2025, which materially exceeds the broader online gambling peer median, while simultaneously maintaining a free cash flow to sales ratio exceeding 20% that reflects an exceptionally asset-light brokerage model.

This cash generation efficiency reached 143% conversion of net profit into free cash flow during FY2025, a metric that places ZEAL in the top 5% of digital consumer platforms when measured against capital-intensive gaming operators that typically require substantial infrastructure investment for proprietary content development and regulatory compliance infrastructure.

The rapid scaling trajectory of proprietary charity lottery Traumhausverlosung presents a third outlier, achieving €38.90 million in billings during FY2025 with €15.90 million in revenues implying a 40% billing margin that substantially exceeds the core lottery gross margin of 17.80% in Q1 2026. The company's post-M&A leverage profile represents a fourth statistical anomaly, with an estimated net debt to EBITDA ratio of approximately 0.60x that remains well below the mandated 2.50x covenant ceiling despite executing a transformational international acquisition. The financing structure utilizing a €40 million seven-year term loan from Deutsche Bank to fund the £33.80 million cash purchase of SevenCanyon suggests access to unusually favorable credit terms when benchmarked against typical mid-cap technology M&A transactions, potentially reflecting either unobserved credit enhancement mechanisms or exceptional lender confidence in the cash generation profile.

The historical guidance accuracy pattern constitutes a fifth outlier, with management consistently exceeding initial forecasts over the last four consecutive fiscal years including actual FY25 revenue of €218.50 million exceeding the upgraded target of €205 million to €215 million. This conservative sandbagger archetype that under-promises and over-delivers diverges sharply from the overpromise-underdeliver pattern observed in approximately 60-70% of European small and mid-cap growth companies, suggesting either exceptional forecasting discipline or systematic lowballing of achievable targets to manage investor expectations.
