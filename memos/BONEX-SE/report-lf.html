EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Chief Executive Credentials and Commercial Pedigree

Torbjörn Sköld brings exceptional technical and commercial credentials to Bonesupport, holding an MSc in Industrial Engineering from the Royal Institute of Technology and an MBA from INSEAD, combined with sixteen years of leadership experience at DePuy Synthes within Johnson & Johnson's orthopaedic franchise from 2007 to 2023.

His career includes extensive international operating experience across the United States, United Kingdom, Denmark, and Germany, accumulated over more than twenty years dedicated to commercializing medical device businesses in global markets.

Sköld demonstrated corporate P&L leadership as CEO of Swedish medtech manufacturer Stille AB from March 2023, executing the pivotal Fehling Instruments acquisition before transitioning seamlessly to Bonesupport on September 1, 2025, following a planned resignation announced in December 2024.

Bonesupport appointed him specifically to serve as an external commercial accelerator for the CERAMENT platform, leveraging his orthopaedic pedigree to convert clinical milestones into robust earnings per share growth.

Operational Delivery Record at Bonesupport

Under Sköld's leadership during the third quarter of 2026, Bonesupport delivered exceptional financial performance with net sales increasing 39% to SEK 408.8 million and the reported operating margin reaching 30% compared to 22% in the prior year, unlocking substantial operational leverage from a 92% gross margin.

The company's adjusted operating result surged to SEK 139.3 million from SEK 78.6 million year-over-year, while earnings per share before dilution rose dramatically to SEK 3.47 compared to SEK 1.49 in the prior-year ninemonth period, proving highly profitable commercial execution.

Critical US market expansion drove sales growth of 40% to SEK 344.4 million in Q3 2026, supplemented by a pivotal distribution partnership with Stryker Australia Pty Ltd covering Australia and New Zealand that contributed SEK 3.8 million through initial start-up orders.

Favorable US reimbursement changes effective October 1, 2026, assigning qualifying CERAMENT G cases to highest-paying Diagnosis-Related Groups, combined with publication of the SOLARIO study in The New England Journal of Medicine proving local antibiotic efficacy, provide powerful commercial catalysts to accelerate future earnings per share.

Sköld's commercial success has been supported by favorable tailwinds and regulatory groundwork inherited from previous leadership, as the pivotal FDA approval and initial US commercial launch of CERAMENT G were executed in late 2022 under former CEO Emil Billbäck, meaning near-term earnings per share growth cannot be entirely attributed to current executive decisions.

At Stille AB, Sköld demonstrated exceptional commercial scaling with organic growth reaching 16.50% in Q3 2026, while net sales in Q3 2024 surged to MSEK 127.90 from MSEK 70.10 in the prior-year period, representing an 82.45% increase driven by strong organic demand and strategic M&A consolidation.

Expert Clinical Validation and Competitive Positioning

Orthopedic surgeons express high clinical confidence in CERAMENT G as an exceptionally valuable tool backed by robust clinical data, while industry experts note that CERAMENT and competitor Stimulan represent the two largest synthetic products in the drug-delivery segment, with CERAMENT's pre-mixed formulation offering significant clinical advantages over Stimulan's manual mixing requirement.

The strategic termination of the Zimmer Biomet partnership allowed Bonesupport to transition to direct US sales and substantially improve operational margins, while the company benefited significantly from a specialized New Technology Add-on Payment reimbursement code that helped drive significant sales growth by mitigating hospital purchase hesitation.

Clinical adoption faces persistent barriers including very low physician awareness of local antibiotic therapy products even in academic settings, limited procedure applicability with synthetics covering less than 30% of extremity procedures, though meaningful new competition remains unlikely in the near term due to high regulatory and clinical data requirements protecting existing market players.

RedEye analysts estimate Bonesupport maintains an uncontested monopoly in the niche drug-eluting bone void filler market with no direct competitive products expected to challenge CERAMENT G and V for at least five to seven years, while DNB Carnegie believes extensive clinical data demonstrating bone remodeling and infection prevention makes it extremely difficult for new competitors to gain traction.

Hospital customers occasionally introduce temporary purchasing restrictions on CERAMENT G usage due to localized cost-benefit concerns in a go-stop-go pattern, and procurement committees can impose severe restrictions on premium-priced products during budget pressure periods, creating near-term volume headwinds despite underlying clinical validation.
