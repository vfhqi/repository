Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The transition from CardLink to Proof of Patient Presence threatens to disrupt online pharmacies by altering the digital prescription channel.
  2. This regulatory shift directly impacts earnings per share and DACH profitability, making valuation multiples highly sensitive to transition success.

Defining the Key Question

1.
Pivotal question: "How likely is the German transition from CardLink to Proof of Patient Presence with Health ID (Phase 2 from Q4 2027) to disrupt app-based e-prescription redemptions at Redcare Pharmacy N.V. and DocMorris, both for new and for existing patients: gematik's timetable and readiness, the options to extend CardLink again, Health ID and EU Digital Identity Wallet adoption among patients over 55, what share of Redcare's German Rx redemptions currently run through CardLink, and what precedents (the German e-prescription rollout of 2024, Sweden's BankID-based e-prescription access, other digital identity switches) show about the depth and length of redemption dips?"
2.
Critical vulnerability: The proposed digital migration introduces regulatory risks that compress online pharmacy earnings per share. Redcare Pharmacy warned on 4 March 2026 that regulatory changes could unfavorably alter its business model. Because prescription sales carry high basket values, technical friction during the PoPP rollout would depress the highly lucrative repeat-prescription cohort growth.
3.
Broad exploration: This investigation assesses related concerns, such as how the statutory 50% co-payment increase in 2027 will affect digital shift incentives. Across the research, 15 relevant sources, three companies, and two comparable past cases were analyzed. According to a Jefferies Research note dated 26 October 2026, the upcoming statutory co-payment hike is driving heightened customer price sensitivity.

Financial Trajectory and Valuation Sensitivity

4.
Rapid expansion: Redcare Pharmacy continues to post exceptional DACH momentum, driven by German prescription sales that surged 56.3% year-over-year in the third quarter of 2026. According to Redcare's interim report published on 29 October 2026, DACH adjusted EBITDA margins reached 4.2% compared to consolidated adjusted margins of 2.9% for the nine-month period. Any redemption dip would jeopardize consensus EBITDA targets of 95 million euros.
5.
Imminent turnaround: DocMorris raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to 12.5% to 15% growth, supported by a 78% surge in German prescription sales. In its earnings release dated 15 October 2026, DocMorris announced achieving adjusted EBITDA breakeven in the third quarter with a positive 0.6 million Swiss francs. Unless CardLink transitions smoothly, the company risks achieving free cash flow breakeven by 2027.
6.
Contrary evidence: Redcare Pharmacy announced on 7 September 2026 that gematik preliminary specifications lay the groundwork for incorporating electronic health cards into the remote care infrastructure. This official regulatory progress suggests that the transition is less likely to cause terminal disruption than feared.
7.
Other side of the story: The transition from CardLink to PoPP is structured to optimize patient security, but it may cause short-term transaction dips if patient registration stumbles. A Jefferies Research report dated 8 October 2026 indicates that Redcare trades at 0.45 times EV-to-Sales, which represents a 21% discount to DocMorris at 0.57 times. If digital identity activation lags, these low-multiple valuations show that investors already discount structural regulatory headwinds.

These developments are expected to protect repeat prescription volumes without causing transaction friction.

This feedback loop ensured that cross-border pharmacies could influence the future remote-care framework directly. The consultative approach has fostered collaboration between regulatory agencies and the European Association of E-Pharmacies.
3.
Slower transition: German healthcare digitization is experiencing delays because Gematik shifted the official rollout of Proof of Patient Presence Stufe 2 Online Check-in from late 2026 to late 2027. This timeline extension resolves immediate operational pressures for cross-border digital pharmacies. The delays have prompted Gematik to structure a temporary parallel run of both technologies to maintain patient access.

Many elderly patients do not possess digital contact methods such as email or mobile phone numbers. This persistent technical resistance among older demographics threatens to disrupt remote digital prescription redemptions when CardLink ceases.
