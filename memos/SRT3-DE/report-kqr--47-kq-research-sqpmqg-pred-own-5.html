Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The transition to IFRS 18 is a critical event for Sartorius AG because it reconfigures profit presentation, increases audit oversight of management-defined measures, and modifies key metrics that drive valuation multiples and earnings per share.

Corporate structure and relationship

1.
Key Question: "Sartorius AG (SRT3-DE, preference shares), a German life-science tools group with two segments: Bioprocess Solutions (about four-fifths of sales: validated single-use bags, filters, fluid management and bioreactors plus bioprocess equipment, run through its 71.5%- owned listed subsidiary Sartorius Stedim Biotech) and Lab Products & Services (about one-fifth: bioanalytics instruments such as Incucyte and Octet, laboratory balances, pipettes, consumables and MatTek cell models); from 1 January 2027 these are reported as Bioprocessing Solutions (identical to Sartorius Stedim Biotech) and Research Solutions. How will IFRS 18 (mandatory from 2027) change Sartorius AG's (SRT3-DE) presentation of operating profit, the finance result and its "underlying" EBITDA, EBIT and EPS measures as management-defined performance measures, has the company or Sartorius Stedim Biotech said anything about restating 2026 comparatives, and how have other German and French life-science or medtech companies handled the transition?"
2.
Significant control: Sartorius AG maintains deep financial and operational control over its French subsidiary, Sartorius Stedim Biotech, holding 71.5% of its share capital and 83.0% of the voting rights. This control is supported by shared infrastructure, central service invoicing via Sartorius Corporate Administration GmbH, and €2.4 bn in long-term intercompany loans. Consequently, any reporting changes under IFRS 18 will directly affect both entities' reported earnings and their joint consolidated performance metrics. This deep integration means that changes in interest or administrative expense presentation at the subsidiary level will immediately impact the parent company's consolidated EPS.
3.
Strategic realignment: Sartorius AG plans to adjust its external segment reporting starting January 1, 2027, to reduce reporting complexity. The new Bioprocessing Solutions division will exactly mirror Sartorius Stedim Biotech, while Research Solutions will contain all remaining lab businesses. This alignment matches the timing of the IFRS 18 implementation, simplifying how analysts model the subsidiary's contribution to the parent's EPS.
4.
Contrary evidence: none found in company filings, broker research, or regulatory announcements published between February 2025 and November 2026 regarding any corporate opposition to these reporting structures.

Investor significance and analytical metrics

5.
Critical transition: Equity investors view the mandatory transition to IFRS 18 as a critical milestone because it reconfigures the income statement into operating, investing, and financing categories. The standard introduces a newly defined operating profit subtotal and requires management performance measures to be audited. Because these rules change how underlying profitability is displayed, they can shift perceived valuation multiples and EPS figures.
6.
Redefined metrics: Sartorius AG's financial presentation will change as IFRS 18 requires the operating profit to be the starting point for the consolidated cash flow statement. Additionally, certain net finance costs and foreign exchange differences must be allocated to their related operating or investing categories. This redistribution will reshape key historical metrics, requiring brokers to update their long-term valuation models.
7.
Standardized reporting: Sartorius AG's historical reliance on alternative performance measures like Underlying EBITDA will be subjected to much stricter regulatory and audit oversight under the new management performance measure framework. Companies must now disclose these metrics in a dedicated note and reconcile them directly to IFRS-defined subtotals. This change reduces management's ability to selectively adjust earnings, providing investors with a more transparent and standardized view of true operating performance.
8.
Other side of the story: Sartorius AG's underlying operational cash flows and business economics remain completely unchanged by IFRS 18. While the reclassification of financing items, lease expenses, or foreign exchange differences will alter intermediate subtotals like operating profit, the net income and overall cash generation of the group will not experience any actual structural variation.

This operational shift ensures that the central margin indicator aligns with the standardized statutory presentation.

Aligned measures: Sartorius Stedim Biotech confirmed on October 22, 2026, that it will mirror the parent group by calculating Underlying EBITDA from operating profit.
