Plausible problems to monitor

Outliers worth noting

The company's extreme seasonal profit concentration represents an unexplained operational oddity, with virtually 100% of group operating profits generated in H1, while Q2 2026's adjusted EBIT beat was highly reliant on nonoperational items, specifically SEK 240 million in IEEPA-related tariff refunds, following consecutive profit warnings in Q3 2024 and Q4 2024. This H1-only profitability pattern places Husqvarna in the bottom 5% of industrial durables companies for earnings distribution stability, creating severe forward visibility constraints that distinguish it from more diversified seasonal peers like Toro and Makita. [Cross-ref: Section 5, Section 6]

Husqvarna's operating margin excluding items affecting comparability fell to 6.2% in 2025, compared to peer Toro's 12.0% adjusted margin and Makita's trailing margins near 14%. This 580-basis-point margin gap versus Toro places Husqvarna in the bottom 5% of the outdoor equipment peer set, representing an unexplained operational oddity given overlapping product portfolios, comparable end-market exposures, and Husqvarna's stated premium brand positioning in professional segments.
