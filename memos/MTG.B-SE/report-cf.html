Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro)

Section summary
  1. global mobile gaming spending exhibits flat to low-single-digit recovery as direct-to-consumer channels increasingly bypass traditional app store metrics
  2. publisher profit pools expand structurally through platform commission cuts and direct-to-consumer payment platforms
  3. generative AI drastically alters content production cost structures, compressing development timelines while lowering developer headcount requirements
  4. tightening platform privacy rules impair marketing attribution, forcing a structural shift toward first-party AI targeting models
  5. competitive intensity is skewing toward consolidation and multi-platform distribution to combat escalating user acquisition costs
  6. post-pandemic cyclical digestion is concluding, with player engagement concentrating in high-LTV, evergreen live-service titles
  7. macroeconomic pressures and rising user acquisition costs compress margins for publishers lacking proprietary ad-tech infrastructure
