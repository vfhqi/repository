Section 2 – Public Preparedness and Biodefense Contract Cycles

Section 3 – Travel Health Growth and Competitive Landscape

Section summary
  1. Bavarian Nordic's Travel Health segment demonstrates strong commercial progression driven by robust rabies and tick-borne encephalitis sales.
  2. Commercial launch acceleration for new vaccines like Vimkunya remains highly gated by official advisory committee publications and recommendations.
  3. Competitive threats from next-generation platforms and peer travel portfolios require defensive commercial execution and targeted pricing strategies.

Commercial Segment Performance

1.
Robust progression: Bavarian Nordic's Travel Health segment achieved record performance in the first nine months of 2026. Segment revenue reached DKK 2,886m, representing 24% reported growth compared to DKK 2,327m in the prior year. This performance was heavily driven by Rabipur/RabAvert sales which climbed to DKK 1,975m from DKK 1,424m. Management raised the full-year Travel Health revenue guidance to DKK 3,400m to reflect strong rabies vaccine demand in the United States.
2.
Steady growth: Bavarian Nordic's tick-borne encephalitis vaccine Encepur recorded moderate commercial progress. Revenue for Encepur increased by 9% to DKK 562m in the first nine months of 2026 compared to DKK 516m in the same period of 2025. Wholesaler stocking dynamics in Germany fluctuated due to German market supply constraints during the third quarter. Analysts note that Encepur maintains a stable market share of approximately 31% in Germany.
3.
Rapid launch: Bavarian Nordic's newly launched chikungunya vaccine Vimkunya showed exceptional growth trajectory. Vimkunya sales surged by 258% to DKK 151m in the first nine months of 2026 compared to DKK 42m in the prior year. Conversely, oral cholera vaccine Vaxchora declined by 29% to DKK 21m due to niche outbreak-driven demand patterns globally. Meanwhile, oral typhoid vaccine Vivotif grew by 25% to DKK 168m following reduced return rates.

To mitigate this barrier, Bavarian Nordic is successfully encouraging wholesalers to stockpile inventory ahead of official publication.
