This represents an increase compared to the €35.89 million reported on December 31, 2025. The growth was driven by other temporary differences, which rose from €11.71 million to €15.24 million over the six months. Higher deferred tax assets support future earnings per share by reducing cash tax payments in upcoming fiscal years.

Tax deduction holdings: Tax deduction assets remained completely flat at €15.08 million during the first half of 2026. The interim report shows that this asset class did not change from the level recorded on December 31, 2025. Recovery Probability and IAS 12 Guidelines

Companies must evaluate both the expected reversal of liabilities and projected taxable income before recognizing these assets. Arteche management performs detailed estimates of future tax benefits based on approved multi-year corporate budgets. If actual regional profits fall below these budgets, the group would face mandatory asset write-downs that directly reduce net income.
7.
Historical credit generation: Historical records show that the group had unused tax credits of €31.23 million as of June 30, 2025. The group recognized a deferred tax asset of €15.30 million against those credits, leaving the remainder unrecognized due to recovery limits. These tax credits are subject to a thirty-year offsetting limit under Bizkaia regional tax guidelines. This long carryforward period reduces immediate write-down risks, which provides stability for long-term earnings per share.
8.
Audit and risk evaluation: Regulatory guidelines require continuous monitoring of tax treatment uncertainties that might affect deferred tax asset values. The group complies with international standards to ensure that all recognized assets reflect probable tax outcomes. This process incorporates estimates of tax rates expected to apply when the assets are realized. Any change in local tax laws would immediately alter the asset valuations, which creates volatility in deferred tax expenses and reported profits.
9.
Contrary evidence: Some financial experts warn that legacy deferred tax assets can present significant capital challenges during periods of regulatory transition. A former bank director noted in April 2021 that resolving these assets requires strict management oversight to clean up balance sheets. While industrial firms face different dynamics than financial institutions, large unrecognized tax assets can still indicate underlying profitability struggles in key regions. This means that over-reliance on paper tax assets may mask operational cash flow weaknesses if regional earnings fail to meet expectations.

Tax Credit Interactions and GloBE Rules

9.
Credit accounting classifications: Under the OECD GloBE guidelines, the classification of R&D tax incentives is highly critical. Qualified Refundable Tax Credits must be paid in cash or cash equivalents within four years of being claimed, and they are treated as taxable income rather than a reduction in covered taxes. In contrast, non-refundable tax credits directly reduce covered taxes, which can significantly lower the GloBE effective tax rate below the 15% minimum. This distinction means that non-refundable credits create top-up risks if they compress the local ETR too far.
