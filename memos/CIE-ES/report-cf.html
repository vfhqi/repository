EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 9 findings

Materiality of Change

CIE Automotive exhibits exceptional leadership stability with CEO Jesús María Herrera leading since 2013 after a 22-year internal career progression, supporting a disciplined multi-technology expansion strategy through the EUR 270.6 million Aludec acquisition that created a new aesthetic components division in March 2026, while maintaining strict financial discipline with a mandatory 20% Return on Investment hurdle for all capital projects.

The company operates in a challenging, regionally divergent external environment characterized by structurally flat mature markets in Europe and North America offset by high-growth markets in India and Brazil, intense Chinese OEM pricing pressure in domestic markets, and USMCA trade renegotiation risks creating medium-term visibility constraints for Mexican manufacturing operations. The financial profile demonstrates exceptional profitability and cash conversion compared to European peers, with consolidated EBITDA margins reaching 19.1% in H1 2026 and operating cash conversion achieving 71.2% of EBITDA, supporting rapid deleveraging from 1.19x adjusted NFD/EBITDA in June 2026 toward a target below 0.7x by year-end 2027 while simultaneously raising dividend payout ratios to 42% in 2026 and 50% in 2027.

Recent Delivery and Forward Credibility

CIE Automotive delivered historic revenue and margin resilience in H1 2026 with consolidated revenue reaching EUR 2,103.8 million marking the best six-month period in company history, while EBITDA margin reached 19.1% and EBIT margin consolidated at 14.3% reflecting excellent manufacturing cost controls, and attributable net income grew to EUR 191.3 million up 2.9% year-on-year despite a nearly EUR 80 million negative foreign exchange headwind.

The company demonstrated exceptional margin resilience and structural cash conversion over the L3Y period, with EBITDA margin expanding from 18.0% in FY 2023 to 18.9% in FY 2025 despite flat volume cycles, operating cash conversion reaching 71% of EBITDA in Q3 2025, and consolidated revenues stabilizing near EUR 3.96 billion in FY 2025 while overperforming global production trends by 10.3 percentage points in H1 2026.

Management exhibits an exemplary strategic plan execution archetype, successfully completing its 2021-2025 Strategic Plan by delivering on all core financial and operational targets including organic revenue growth reaching 25 percentage points above the global market, average EBITDA-to-operating cash conversion reaching 66.9% exceeding the 65% target, and cumulative capital expenditure reaching EUR 1,058 million despite the global vehicle market falling 22 million units short of initial expectations.

CIE Automotive leads its peer group with industry-leading operating margins and superior earnings momentum, ranking first among European auto suppliers with a 14.3% EBIT margin in H1 2026 vastly outperforming the sector median of 5.0%, achieving 9.3% organic growth significantly exceeding peers like Forvia at negative 1.9%, Valeo at 0%, and Continental at negative 0.3%, and demonstrating elite cash conversion at 71.2% of EBITDA compared to peer conversion rates averaging below 45%.
