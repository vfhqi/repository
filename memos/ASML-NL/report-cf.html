EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 1 findings

ASML Holding NV occupies an unprecedented monopoly position in extreme ultraviolet lithography systems, commanding 100% market share in EUV technology that enables all advanced semiconductor manufacturing below 7nm process nodes. The company retains a 100% monopoly in EUV systems, with zero direct commercial competitors, creating structural pricing power and customer lock-in that insulates the business from traditional competitive dynamics.

The investment case centers on ASML's ability to scale Low-NA EUV capacity from 44 units in fiscal year 2025 to at least 60 units in 2026 and 80 units in 2027, driven by insatiable artificial intelligence infrastructure demand that is fundamentally rewriting memory architecture and logic foundry capacity requirements. However, the bull case confronts three material headwinds: delayed High-NA EUV adoption by leading foundry customer TSMC through 2029, which removes a significant revenue catalyst; structural normalization of China sales from 33% in 2025 toward 20% in 2026 as export controls tighten and domestic tool substitution accelerates; and potential legislative destruction of DUV immersion sales through the proposed MATCH Act that could eliminate low-teens percentage points of total revenue overnight.
