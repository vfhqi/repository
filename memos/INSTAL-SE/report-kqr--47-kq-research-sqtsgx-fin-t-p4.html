Debt structure and facility terms Instalco’s SEK 3.4 billion facility consists of a term loan and a revolving credit facility, both originally carrying twoyear maturities with extension options, and the company exercised a one-year extension in June 2026 so that the facility now runs to 2028 with a further one-year extension option. The extension was made on the same conditions as the 2025 agreement, according to Instalco’s 29 June 2026 announcement, but the supplied documents do not disclose how much of the drawn balance is fixed-rate or floating-rate.
