Section 2 – US Regional Casino Cost Structure and Fixed-Cost Leverage

Section summary
  1. High contractual obligations at Bally's Corporation create severe operating leverage compared to peers with more real estate ownership.
  2. Rigid lease structures and extensive labor agreements limit cost flexibility across US regional casino portfolios.
  3. High promotional reinvestment rates and varied state gaming taxes drive the main differences in operator margins.

1.
Rents Severe burden: Bally's Corporation faces extreme fixed rent expenses that significantly elevate its operational risk and limit margin flexibility. SEC filings from August 2026 revealed that the company has minimum annual rent payments under operating leases of $142.6 million due within the next year. These heavy outflows represent a highly rigid fixed cost base that cannot be easily reduced in an economic downturn, thereby pressuring earnings per share.
2.
Rigid terms: Bally's Corporation is tied to escalating long-term leases with Gaming and Leisure Properties that guarantee rising cash expenses. The company reported in March 2026 that its primary master lease requires initial payments of $101.5 million, while its second master lease demands $32.2 million. Because these leases carry a minimum one percent annual escalation regardless of performance, they impose severe downward pressure on operating cash flows during revenue declines.

This operational deleveraging resulted in a severe twenty-one percent decremental margin because cost-saving initiatives could not keep pace with declining customer volumes.

Century Casinos reported that net operating revenues for the first quarter of 2020 were severely impacted by temporary property closures starting in mid-March. Because Century was forced to pay full master lease rents even during complete shutdowns, its cash burn reached eight million dollars per month, demonstrating how rigid leases amplify downside risk.
3.

This massive volume contraction drove property-level Adjusted EBITDA down, particularly in the distributed gaming segment which fell to a 9% margin. The operator experienced a forty-two percent decremental margin on a consolidated basis because its fixed casino overhead could not be removed.

Century Casinos reported that its June 2020 Adjusted EBITDA grew 51% year-over-year in Colorado despite operating with fewer slot machines.
