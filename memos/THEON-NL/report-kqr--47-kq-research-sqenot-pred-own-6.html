Section 3 – The 2027 Federal Defence Budget and Allocation Timelines 13 findings

Section summary
  1. The proposed 2027 German core defense budget is set to increase substantially to support military modernization.
  2. Structural shifts will move equipment procurement funding into the permanent recurring core budget.
  3. Lower commitment authorizations reflect a transition from contract signing toward physical program execution.

Overall defense budget expansion

1.
Growing core budget: Germany plans to expand its core defense budget significantly to fund long-term rearmament. Morgan Stanley reported on 12 October 2026 that the 2027 draft core budget under Einzelplan 14 will rise by 29% to €109.7 billion. This core expansion is supplemented by €30.0 billion from the Bundeswehr Special Fund, which means total resources reach €139.7 billion. This massive funding lift will directly accelerate contract allocations, supporting strong nearterm earnings growth for Theon

Accelerating equipment cash: German military planners are boosting direct cash allocations for equipment purchases. Morgan Stanley reported on 12 October 2026 that the gross military equipment cash envelope is expected to rise by 21% year-on-year to €63.4 billion. Since infantry equipment and night-vision systems are funded through this cash pool, this accelerated spending should directly boost production volume and secure steady cash flow generation for Theon

Structural funding shift: Defense procurement funding is shifting permanently into the recurring federal budget. Morgan Stanley noted on 12 October 2026 that Chapter 1405 procurement cash is set to rise by 55% to €30.0 billion in 2027. This structural migration ensures that equipment financing is moving away from the temporary Special Fund, which is projected to be fully depleted by 2030. This funding stability provides long-term planning certainty for suppliers, which protects Theon from the risk of a sharp fiscal cliff
