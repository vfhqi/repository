The reasoning behind the inputs

Segments: revenue and margin by segment, where the company reports them

The company reports three business areas (net sales and adjusted EBITA). 2025 actuals: Industry SEK 14,319m (43%), Trade SEK 9,588m (29%, adjusted EBITA SEK 831m, 8.7%), Services SEK 9,232m (28%); group adjusted EBITA SEK 3,117m (9.4%). The 2025 Industry and Services margins are not in the files read; my estimates are Industry about 10.6% and Services about 10.0%, with central costs of about SEK 155m to SEK 165m, which reconcile to the group figure. Latest quarter (Q2 2026): Industry SEK 3,967m at 11.0%; Services about SEK 2,579m at 10.3% (7.7% in Q1 2026); Trade SEK 2,315m at 9.2% (7.1% in Q1 2026).

My base case by segment (sales, adjusted EBITA margin): - Industry: FY2026 SEK 14.8bn, 10.9%; FY2027 SEK 15.7bn, 11.2%; FY2028 SEK 17.0bn, 11.4%; FY2029 SEK 18.4bn, 11.4%; FY2030 SEK 19.8bn, 11.4%.
