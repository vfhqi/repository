Plausible problems to monitor

flatexDEGIRO exhibits several operational and financial metrics that fall materially below peer medians or sector base rates, despite its strong overall execution trajectory. These plausible problem to monitor warrant attention as they represent potential drag factors on the company's otherwise robust momentum and may constrain future performance relative to best-in-class competitors. The firm's projected 51% operating margin sits well above European wealth peers but materially below Avanza's 70% level, establishing a persistent efficiency gap against the top Nordic platform operators.

This structural margin disadvantage of approximately 1,900 basis points reflects higher operational complexity and suggests flatexDEGIRO has not yet achieved the asset-light business model efficiency of the most streamlined competitors. Avanza's dual IT platform presents suboptimal execution risks compared to Nordnet's unified infrastructure model, yet even with this handicap, both Nordic peers sustain margins far exceeding flatexDEGIRO's current trajectory, as detailed in Section 7.

Average customer acquisition costs surged 77.7% to EUR 139 in the first half of 2026, representing a dramatic deterioration in marketing efficiency that substantially exceeds peer norms. Customer acquisition costs rose to EUR 96 in H1 on a reported basis, but the quarterly progression reveals accelerating pressure, with rising customer acquisition costs to EUR 171 in Q1 2026 offsetting near-term marketing efficiency gains.

This volatile and rising CAC trajectory contrasts unfavorably with peers who maintain more stable unit economics, as discussed in Section 3 and Section 7, and signals intensifying competitive pressures in core DACH markets.

Gross additions of 212,300 represented a 12.3% gross additions decline during the first half of 2026, marking a material deceleration in the customer growth engine that underpins long-term earnings compounding. While the 11.1% client base expansion outgrows the European peer average of 10%, the negative inflection in gross additions suggests mounting headwinds from intensified competition in the German market and commission-free offerings from peers like Trade Republic and Scalable Capital that have eliminated standard commission structures entirely, as explored in Section 3.

The combination of declining gross additions with surging acquisition costs creates a troubling dual headwind to profitable expansion. Net interest income accounted for 33% of 2025 revenues, representing a lower NII reliance compared to Nordnet's 41% level, which initially appears favorable but actually reflects underdeveloped interest-earning capabilities relative to Nordic peers who have optimized treasury operations and margin lending penetration.

This gap suggests flatexDEGIRO has not fully monetized its customer cash deposits and securities portfolios to the extent achieved by best-practice competitors, leaving revenue on the table in the current rate environment. Hargreaves Lansdown defends premium 45 bps fees, risking further asset drag to cheaper rivals, yet flatexDEGIRO faces the inverse challenge of operating in an increasingly commoditized zero-commission environment where Trade Republic designed a real-time best-price algorithm to bypass traditional third-party order venues.

The company's pricing power remains constrained by aggressive neobroker competition, limiting its ability to expand commission yields even as transaction volumes grow, as detailed in Section 3.

Outliers worth noting

flatexDEGIRO's EBITDA margin expansion from 35.9% to 48% between FY23 and FY25 constitutes an extreme profitability inflection rarely observed among established financial platforms without accompanying revenue deterioration. This anomalous operating leverage delivery, explored comprehensively in Section 5, reflects the unique benefits of completing regulatory remediation while simultaneously scaling proprietary technology infrastructure.

The 19% upward revision to FY26 EPS estimates by Jefferies following Q2 results represents an analyst revision outlier that places flatexDEGIRO at the extreme positive end of European wealth platform estimate momentum, as documented in Section 8. - Earnings Calls An unexplained anomaly emerges in the substantial increase in average customer transaction size to EUR 6,400 in H1 2026 from EUR 5,600 in FY25, partially attributed to high-margin US trades recovering to roughly 20% of trading volume.

This trade-size outlier, analyzed in Section 6, lacks complete explanation in company disclosures and may reflect either favorable customer mix shifts toward higher-net-worth clients or temporary concentration in specific high-value securities like the SpaceX IPO. The sustainability of this anomalous transaction size remains uncertain and warrants ongoing monitoring as a potential forward earnings sensitivity.
