The reasoning behind the inputs

Part 4 is the second set's reasoning as written. Where a later set changed an input, that input's figure in the tables above is the changed one and its reasons are in the changed-inputs file named above; Part 4 is not rewritten.

Bases used throughout. Revenue is the company's reported net sales (FY2025 SEK 3,849m, matching FactSet fundamentals; the brokers'-basis actual is SEK 3,899m). The EBITDA margin is the company's adjusted EBITDA margin: before items affecting comparability, after IFRS 16, so it is before lease costs, and lease principal is entered separately in Part 2. Depreciation and amortisation excludes acquisition (purchase price allocation) amortisation of about SEK 35 to 42m a year, so EBITDA less D&A equals the company's adjusted EBIT. Net financial items include lease interest and assume no currency revaluation (the ±SEK 15m a year noise band is not modelled). The tax rate applies to adjusted pre-tax profit. Working capital is on FactSet's definition (receivables plus inventories less payables: SEK 216m, 5.6% of revenue, at end-2025; SEK 242m, 5.3%, at Q3 2026). Lease payments are lease principal; current lease liabilities were SEK 33.4m at 30 September 2026. The share-count change of 0.50m in 2026 is the March 2026 warrant tender (502,402 new shares issued in exchange for warrants, no cash). If FactSet's 31.8m already includes them, set FY2026 to 0. FY2029 includes conversion of LTIP 2026/2029 hurdle shares.

How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
