The exceptional cost discipline enabled adjusted EBITDA margin expansion of 320bps on a reported basis and 260bps on a constant currency basis to 52.7%, beating Visible Alpha consensus of 51.8% by 90bps, with management subsequently upgrading full-year margin expansion guidance to approximately 100 basis points.
