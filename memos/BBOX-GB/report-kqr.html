Significance of the logistics M&A wave

Rapid consolidation: Tritax Big Box and its European logistics peers face a rapidly consolidating landscape where benchmark acquisitions such as Prologis's recommended bid for Segro set high pricing references. Understanding next targets determines whether re-rating momentum can persist over the next 18 months amidst macroeconomic pressures.

For instance, CTP trades at a 39% discount to its net asset value, which is significantly wider than its historical peer average. These discrepancies decide which entities are most vulnerable to opportunistic take-private transactions by global institutional capital.
