EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Technoprobe commands a dominant position in logic testing through vertical MEMS probe cards, operating as device-specific consumables tied to wafer volumes that isolate the model from near-term capital expenditure volatility while strategic equity partnerships with Teradyne and Advantest cement its position as the preferred test interface developer. The company achieves superior average gross margins near 45.0% through highvolume vertical integration, with significant volume expansion driving rapid profitability improvements demonstrated by the 37.0% EBITDA margin achieved in Q1 2026. Proprietary MEMS platform technology acquired through Microfabrica in 2019 now drives over 90% of revenues, establishing an unreplicable manufacturing monopoly. Consolidated revenues reached a record €628.4 million in FY2025, representing strong 15.7% year-on-year growth driven by artificial intelligence demand.

Competitive Position and Market Structure

1.
The global probe card market reached $2.75 billion in 2025, with advanced probe cards representing $2.25 billion of total market size.
2.
The global advanced probe card market is expected to expand at an annualized rate of 9% from 2024 through 2029, exceeding historical broader wafer fab equipment growth.
3.
The top three players commanded approximately 50% of the wafer probe card market in 2025, with the remainder highly fragmented across lower-end segments.
4.
Advanced logic testing behaves as a highly profitable duopoly between Technoprobe and FormFactor, with Technoprobe commanding a dominant 60% market share in advanced logic vertical MEMS.
5.
Competitive relationships have deteriorated into fierce global patent disputes, with MPI initiating patent infringement action against Technoprobe in US federal court on April 17, 2026.
6.
Volume-output of advanced logic testing requires proprietary vertical MEMS process technology, with developing MEMS probe cards requiring massive capital investments and lengthy customer qualification cycles.

Section summary

Core Business Description and Technology

1.
Specialized testing interfaces: Technoprobe designs and manufactures customized electro-mechanical interfaces, known as probe cards, to test semiconductor functionality at the wafer level.
2.
Logic testing focus: The group focuses on the non-memory or system-on-chip segment, achieving global leadership through its proprietary vertical MEMS probe cards.
3.
Proprietary MEMS platform: The acquisition of Microfabrica in 2019 provided a unique MEMS process technology that now drives over 90% of revenues.
4.
Diverse TPEG family: The patent-protected TPEG probe technology consists of multiple customizable variants, including Phantom and Merlion, optimized for specific customer applications.
5.
High-power architecture: The patented HiP High Power probes minimize needle burning during testing, which is critical for mobile and data center applications.
6.
Advanced packaging enabler: The probe cards are tailored to handle high pin counts and narrow pad pitches, supporting complex chiplet architectures.
7.
Silicon photonics solution: The Eclipse optoelectronic probe card enables parallel optical-electrical testing, addressing critical yield bottlenecks in co-packaged optics.

Revenue Model and Generation Mechanics

8.
Consumable revenue model: Probe cards are highly predictable consumables because they are completely custom-made for specific chip designs and cannot be reused.
9.
Wafer volume linkage: Revenue is directly tied to underlying wafer shipment volumes and the rapid cadence of new chip design tape-outs.
10.
High design exposure: Shorter design cycles and custom ASIC proliferation protect the revenue stream even during broader semiconductor capital expenditure downturns.
11.
Pricing power indicators: The advanced vertical MEMS duopoly with FormFactor allows Technoprobe to maintain superior pricing power and gross margins.
12.
Back-end test expansion: Through the acquisitions of DIS Tech and Harbor Electronics, the company designs specific motherboards and interface boards for final testing.
13.
Motherboard revenue stream: The final testing motherboard segment opens up a complementary, highdensity PCB market that expands the total addressable market.

16.
Platform agnostic hardware: Technoprobe probe cards are completely agnostic to the automated test equipment platform, allowing flexible customer deployment.

18.
Strategic WinWay alliance: The partnership with WinWay in Taiwan allows Technoprobe to supply vertical MEMS probe heads to Taiwan's OSAT clusters.
19.
