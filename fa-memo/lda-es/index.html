The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue (FactSet basis: earned premium plus investment and other income). Earned premium lags written by about six months (triangular weighting: an earned half reflects about a quarter of the written growth two halves earlier, half of the previous half and a quarter of the current half). Written growth slows from 9.2% (H1 26) to about 7.5% (H2 26), 7.0% (FY27), 6.6% (FY28), 5.8% (FY29) and 5.4% (FY30), so earned premium grows 10.3%, 7.4%, 6.8%, 6.0% and 5.5%. Policies: 3.99m (FY26), 4.17m, 4.32m, 4.47m, 4.61m (net additions fall from about 278,000 a year to about 180,000 as motor prices rise); average premium +1.0%, +2.1%, +2.9%, +2.3%, +2.1%.
  • Operating profit (= pre-tax profit) = earned premium times (1 minus CR) + investment result + other items. Technical result 102.6, 102.0, 113.0, 116.9, 118.8; investment result 43.5, 46.5, 50.0, 52.5, 55.0 (portfolio of about EUR 1.26bn growing 5% to 6% a year at a flat return; trailing return fell to 2.75% in June 2026; rental income from the renovated Madrid building resumes by mid-2027, about EUR 1.5m in FY27 and EUR 3m to 3.5m a year after); other non-technical items -7.0, -7.0, -7.5, -7.5, -8.0. Pre-tax profit 139.1, 141.5, 155.5, 161.9, 165.8. The model reproduces H1 26 (pre-tax about 69.5, net 52.1).
  • CR (the swing driver: 1 point is about EUR 12.8m pre-tax, EUR 9.6m net, in FY27). FY26 91.4% (H1 91.1%, H2 91.6%). FY27 92.0% (H1 91.6%, H2 92.4%): motor +0.5 point (2027 earned price of about +2% set by 2026's 1% to 2% written price, against claims cost per policy of +3.5% to +4.5% with a 2027 Baremo update of about 3.5% against 2.7%, net of new-business seasoning), home +1.7 points (weather normalising), health -8 points (HNA network from Q1 2027). The company's guidance, and how your inputs compare with it

  • Guidance is verbal and outcome-only: a CR in the "low 90s" (Q1 26 call, 27-Apr-26, after "comfortable with 92% to 93%" in February 2026); an expense ratio below 20% over the medium term; more than 4 million customers; net profit above EUR 100m; solvency not below 150% with about 180% as the dividend reference; no premium, EPS or payout guidance ("we aim to outgrow the market").
  • My inputs meet the CR guidance in every year (91.4% to 92.2%), pass EUR 100m of net profit in FY26 and 4 million customers in Q1 27, and keep solvency near 196%. They do not reach an expense ratio below 20% before FY30 (20.1%), because I assume the operating leverage is reinvested in price, marketing and technology, as Q2 26 (flat year on year at about 21.4%) suggests.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.
  • Historical figures use FactSet's standardised lines, which can differ from the company's own presentation.
  • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
