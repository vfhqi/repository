[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Orange Polska is a self-help cash case in a well-run Polish incumbent, and it is a good case that the market has already paid for. At PLN 13.545 the shares trade at 12.8x consensus 24-month-forward earnings after a 57 percent rise in twelve months, so the re-rating that this kind of case earns has been taken, and the 'Modal return' from here is about 2 percent a year over eighteen months and 9 percent over three years against the framework's 20 percent bar. The change is real, broad and fully propagated; its materiality to a return from this price is what fails.

  • The change is internal, broad and three years into its course.
    • Six of the twelve internal change forces the framework screens are strong, and none of them is a promise.
    • A labour transformation of up to 1,000 departures (about 12 percent of staff) over 2026 and 2027 is 71 percent executed for its first year by June 2026.
    • Indirect costs were held flat in 1H 2026 against a 5.5 percent contractual wage rise
      • the capital envelope is capped at about PLN 1.8 billion a year through 2028.
    • Retail prices rose by PLN 5 across core consumer services in Q1 2026 with a 3.6 percent CPI indexation on open-ended contracts
      • the perimeter was cut (Orange Energia sold, copper sales ceased, subsidiaries merged)
      • the dividend floor was raised 15 percent to PLN 0.61.
    • What is absent is the pattern's sharpest markers: the chief executive has been in post since 2023 and was reappointed to 2029, and the balance sheet at 1.0x EBITDAaL is being left alone.
  • The external forces are mild tailwinds, not a demand inflection.
    • A four-player oligopoly holding 97 percent of mobile revenue has spent two years indexing prices instead of cutting them
    • the regulator removed twenty years of wholesale price control in January 2026
    • energy is contracted to 2035.
    • Core telecom service revenue grows 4.9 percent a year and the mobile market 2.3 percent a year at 138 percent penetration, so nothing external is sufficiently strong on its own
      • the case rests on what the company does.
  • The 'Financial Outputs' have arrived on every line and have reached the sell-side. 1H 2026 revenue +7.6 percent, EBITDAaL +6.2 percent, organic cash flow +43 percent
    • full-year 2026 guidance raised on all three lines on 28 July 2026, and the 2028 organic cash flow ambition raised from at least PLN 1.2 billion to at least PLN 1.4 billion.
    • FY2028 consensus earnings per share rose 21 percent in the six months to 2026-09-18
      • every one of the eleven covering institutions raised its target after the Q2 print.
    • So the change has travelled every layer of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain: from the 2023 appointment and the 2025 strategy, through the actions, into the operating metrics, the financial statements and the estimates.
      • There is no layer left for a new holder to be paid for discovering.
  • What we think it earns is about 11 percent a year, and more than half of it is arithmetic below EBITDAaL.
    • Our path is earnings per share of about PLN 0.80 in FY2026, PLN 0.89 in FY2027, PLN 0.99 in FY2028 and PLN 1.09 in FY2029, 10.7 percent a year from a clean 2026 base and about 8 percent below the consensus PLN 1.08 for FY2028.
    • Revenue contributes about 3.0 percent a year and margin about 1.3 percent
      • flat depreciation against a growing EBITDAaL, fading disposal gains and falling interest contribute about 6 percent.
      • Investors extrapolate the first two components
      • they do not extrapolate the third, and after FY2029 earnings growth reverts towards the EBITDAaL rate of about 4 percent.
    • The dividend rises from PLN 0.61 towards PLN 0.85 by 2029 on our path, about 5 percent a year of yield at today's price, floored and well covered.
  • What it costs is the whole case.
    • PLN 13.545 is 12.9x consensus 24-month-forward earnings and 14.0x ours, the 74th percentile of the stock's own five-year range (median 11.35x) and above the peer median of 11.7x.
    • The quality names in the set (Elisa, Telia, Tele2, Swisscom) trade at 14x to 20x on lower growth
      • the incumbents most like it (Orange, Deutsche Telekom, Telekom Austria, Proximus) trade at 8x to 12x.
    • Since the Q2 print the shares have fallen 7.4 percent over three months and 18 percent from their June high while every broker upgraded and the buy share fell 16 points: the market has already asked the valuation question.
  • The return fails the bar at every multiple we can defend, so the chain holds through five layers and breaks at the price.
    • We value the shares at 12.0x the earnings two years beyond each horizon, a multiple between the stock's own five-year mean (12.13x) and two-year median (11.47x) and a modest premium to the peer median.
    • At 12.0x the return is +3.6 percent over eighteen months (2.4 percent a year) and +27.9 percent over three years (8.6 percent a year): an F on the framework's anchors.
    • Hold the multiple at today's 14.0x on our earnings and it is 12.9 percent a year over eighteen months and 13.6 percent over three years, a D or a C
      • on consensus earnings with the multiple held at 12.9x it is 12.1 percent a year over eighteen months, still a D.
      • To reach 20 percent a year the shares need 15.4x on our earnings or 14.4x on consensus, above the five-year 75th percentile of 12.81x.
    • The earnings path itself is fragile in the half that flatters it: depreciation rising 2 percent a year, disposal gains at zero from FY2027 and finance costs flat cut earnings growth from 10.7 percent to 5.2 percent a year with EBITDAaL unchanged, and the eighteen-month return to -7.1 percent a year.
  • The pattern it fits is 'Major Corporate Change', crisply, and at its endpoint.
    • The fit is a B: broad internal inputs, every output improving, full maturity along the chain.
    • The pattern's payoff is the upgrade cycle, and this one has happened
    • a crisp fit to a pattern at its endpoint is the same fact as the F on 'Modal return'.
  • What would make it compelling is the price, not the business.
    • Below about PLN 10.5 to PLN 11, a 24-month-forward multiple of about 11x on our earnings and a dividend yield near 6 percent, a settling multiple no longer costs the return and the case clears 20 percent a year over eighteen months.
    • A Q3 2026 print on 26 October 2026 with IT and integration revenue below PLN 400 million or indirect costs rising is the likeliest route to that level, and it would not by itself break the case.

  • The case therefore is a sound business bought late: a park with a level, not a rejection.

  • Rating = C, because the change is material in its inputs and its outputs but not in the return it leaves at this price: 'Change Forces' at B and 'Financial Outputs' at B carry a change that has travelled the whole chain, and 'Modal return' at F says the market re-rated the shares by 57 percent on those same outputs.

    • The parts bind: a change that is fully priced is not a material change for a new holder, so the Building Block sits below its first two Elements and above its third, which measures the price as much as the change.
    • Not B, because B would need the return near the bar on a multiple we can defend, and it is not on any multiple inside the stock's own five-year interquartile range
    • not D, because the change itself is not in doubt and a price 20 percent lower would make it material.

Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The case against, at its strongest: the market is right to pay 12.8x, and is still under-paying.
    • A telecom operator with a 1.0x balance sheet, 100 percent local-currency debt, a dividend floor covering two-thirds of guided cash and growing 15 percent a year, an oligopoly that indexes prices, a footprint reaching two-thirds of Polish homes at 13 percent capital intensity, flat depreciation for three years and a management that has beaten every annual target since 2021 is a compounder in a sector of ex-growth incumbents.
    • On that view the twelve-month re-rating is the market catching up with a business that changed between 2021 and 2025, not overshooting it
      • the multiple deserves the Nordic and Swiss quality names' 14x to 20x, not the incumbents' 12x.
    • Consensus at PLN 1.08 for FY2028 is if anything low, because the sell-side has booked 2H 2026 as a revenue decline
      • the June to September fall of 18 percent is profit-taking after a 100 percent rise, not a verdict.
    • At 14x consensus 24-month-forward earnings of PLN 1.19 in March 2028 the shares are worth PLN 16.7 plus PLN 0.72 of dividend, a return of 28 percent over eighteen months, 18 percent a year: a C nearly a B.
  • What we concede.
    • The delivery, the balance sheet, the dividend's sustainability, the oligopoly's discipline, and that the sell-side's FY2026 revenue line looks too low.
    • We also concede that the framework's mediocrity calibration reads a defensive telecom's natural Cs as weakness, and that for a yield investor this is a sound hold.
  • Why we still hold our view: the earnings growth the quality multiple would be paid for is the wrong kind.
    • About 6 of the 11 points a year come from flat depreciation, fading disposal gains and lower interest, which investors do not extrapolate and which our sensitivity shows can halve without any operating miss
    • after FY2029 earnings revert to the EBITDAaL rate of about 4 percent, and a March 2028 investor looking at FY2030 earnings will see that.
  • Second, the price already sits above the peer median on a re-rating the market has just tested and declined to extend.
    • A stock that falls 18 percent from its high on unanimous upgrades and a 16-point drop in the buy share has met its marginal seller at 14x.
  • Third, the parent.
    • Management's incentives vest in Orange S.A. shares, the brand fee takes about 6 percent of EBITDAaL, four of thirteen directors are independent and no board member has bought a share in twelve months
    • a quality multiple is paid for a business whose owners and managers share the minority's interest, and this one's do not, for reasons that are structural rather than culpable.
  • Where the case against would win.
    • If FY2027 earnings land at or above the consensus PLN 0.90 and the European telecom sector holds its high percentiles, the multiple holds or rises to 14x
    • we put that branch at about 30 percent, possible rather than plausible, and it is why the Building Block is C rather than D.

[Robustness of] Foundations upon which the Investment Thesis is built upon (BB 2) Rating = B
