This marginal volume contraction contrasts with self-pay procedures like LASIK or plastic surgery, which plummeted by 50% to 60% during the same recession. In its February 2010 report on operations, Vitrolife reported that fourth-quarter sales increased by 7% in local currency. Contrary evidence: Broader macroeconomic weakness can occasionally cause short-term slowdowns in local in- vitro fertilisation cycle growth that challenge corporate volume targets. However, once clinics reopened, stimulated cycles immediately grew by 25% compared to the prior-year period as pent-up demand returned rapidly.
