Cranswick operates a highly diversified, vertically integrated food production portfolio spanning genetics, feed milling, farming, and primary and secondary processing across fresh pork, poultry, convenience foods, gourmet products, and pet products. The company's core competitive advantage stems from deep vertical integration across the entire agricultural value chain, producing 55% of its internal pig requirements and achieving 100% self-sufficiency in poultry processing.

This farm-to-fork model captures margins at every stage while providing unmatched biosecurity control and supply chain resilience, creating structural cost advantages that isolated competitors cannot easily replicate without comparable multi-hundred-million-pound capital commitments. - Other topic-specific reports Revenue generation relies fundamentally on long-term supply contracts with major UK grocery multiples, structured through transparent open-book costing models that automatically incorporate direct and indirect cost movements.

Cranswick has successfully transitioned retail customer agreements away from transactional spot pricing to structured cost-of-production contracts that reset quarterly to reflect pig price movements representing 70% of cost of goods sold, with annual resets for inflationary overhead factors such as wages and energy. These open-book cost models ensure highly consistent margin delivery through volatile agricultural cycles, with standard cost-plus matrix agreements automatically passing volatile pig price movements directly to major retail partners.

This pricing architecture successfully protected profitability during the severe post-Ukraine inflationary surge, enabling operating margins to expand to a record 7.9% in fiscal year 2026.

Cranswick delivered record revenues of £2,982.5 million in fiscal year 2026, representing 9.5% year-on-year growth driven by an outstanding 8.3% volume expansion in UK food. Adjusted operating profit increased by 14.5% to £237.0 million, while adjusted operating margin rose 35 basis points to a company record of 7.9%. The company relies fundamentally on volume growth rather than price inflation to drive sales, with its recent 8.3% volume expansion easily beating the flattish peer rates observed at competitors.

The business has achieved an unbroken 36-year record of consecutive dividend growth, with medium-term targets consistently beaten while maintaining return on capital employed at an exceptional 18.5%. This sustained operational excellence reflects both superior execution and the structural benefits of vertical integration in capturing value across multiple stages.
