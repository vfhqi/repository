Track Record of Strategic and Operational Delivery

1.
Excellent credibility: Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA has established high credibility by meeting or exceeding its guidance targets across consecutive fiscal years. During the 2023 financial period, the group reported revenues of 401 million euros, landing within its guided range of 385 million to 405 million euros. Operational efficiency drove EBITDA to 40.7 million euros, surpassing the guidance limit of 40.5 million euros. This execution pattern proves management's strong financial discipline, supporting positive future earnings growth.
2.
Consistent execution: Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA maintained its flawless delivery record in the 2024 fiscal year. Consolidated revenues reached 447.4 million euros, which met the company's projection of 440 million to 460 million euros. Gross operating profit reached 51.6 million euros, comfortably beating the high end of its 45.8 million to 50.6 million euros guidance. These consecutive successes confirm that management sets achievable targets, making future projections highly reliable.
3.
Superior profitability: Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA translated strong demand into exceptional profitability as its EBITDA margin reached 11.5% in 2024. This performance exceeded the guided margin range of 10.4% to 11.0% due to positive pricing power. In comparison, peer Siemens AG expanded margins in its Electrification division by 770 basis points since fiscal 2020. Delivering margins above guidance enhances the quality of group earnings and strengthens long-term earnings per share.
4.
Strategic alignment: Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA has successfully executed its 'Energizing Futures Together' strategic goals with zero major operational misses. The company integrated RTR Energía in 2025 and launched its SIBE continuous market transition in February 2026 without corporate disruption. This smooth strategic expansion underpins the feasibility of long-term targets, showing that inorganic growth does not derail baseline operational execution.
5.
Operational efficiency: Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA achieved significant cash conversion under its strategic roadmap to support corporate growth. The group's free cash flow of 24.89 million euros in the 2024 financial period reflected disciplined working capital management. Securing this capital protects the group's dividend payout of fifty percent without relying on expensive debt financing.
6.
Successful expansion: Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA has proven its ability to deliver on strategic operational goals in international markets. The company reinforced its presence in the critical United States market by successfully completing over 30 power quality projects in 2024. Delivering on these complex overseas projects demonstrates strong global execution capabilities, reducing dependence on Southern European markets and protecting future EPS.
7.
Other side of the story: The company's habit of beating guidance may stem from an intentional strategy of setting low targets rather than genuine operational outperformance. If management underpromises to guarantee comfortable results, the group's true organic growth potential remains obscured. Relying on conservative forecasting creates an artificial execution record that could quickly unravel if global utility investments slow down.

Outliers worth noting

Arteche Lantegi Elekartea SA achieved an extraordinarily high EBITDA margin of 18.4% in the first half of 2026, substantially exceeding both its own multi-year strategic plan targets and representing a remarkable 260 basis point expansion from the 15.8% margin recorded in the same period of 2025. This exceptional profitability performance places the company at the upper extreme of the electrical equipment manufacturing sector, where typical EBITDA margins for instrument transformer specialists range between 10% and 14%.

The magnitude of this margin expansion is particularly striking given that it occurred during a period of simultaneous capacity expansion, integration of newly acquired businesses, and persistent global inflationary pressures on raw materials. The operating margin improvement to 12.7% in fiscal year 2025 from 10.0% in 2024 demonstrates that this profitability surge represents a structural shift rather than a temporary accounting anomaly.

While management attributes this performance to disciplined pricing strategies and improved product mix optimization, the speed and magnitude of the margin expansion significantly outpaces comparable peers in the power distribution equipment sector and raises questions about the sustainability of these exceptional economics as competitive pressures intensify.

The completion of a 100 million euro accelerated bookbuild placement in June 2026 following the company's transition to the Spanish continuous market SIBE represents an exceptionally aggressive capital markets maneuver for a mid-cap electrical equipment manufacturer. This substantial equity issuance occurred despite the company maintaining a remarkably conservative leverage ratio of only 0.5 times net debt to EBITDA and generating robust free cash flow exceeding 21 million euros in the first half of 2026 alone.

The timing and scale of this dilutive transaction appears unusual given that the group had already reduced its net financial debt from 26.2 million euros at end-2024 to 22.8 million euros at end-2025, demonstrating strong organic deleveraging capacity without external capital.
