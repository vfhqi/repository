EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 45 findings

Capgemini is executing a high-stakes portfolio pivot anchored by the €3.3 billion WNS acquisition and a €700 million Fit-for-Growth restructuring program, targeting mid-single-digit organic growth and 130 to 150 basis points of margin expansion by 2028. The company demonstrates top-tier organic revenue momentum at 5.1% in Q2 2026, outpacing peers like Accenture and Infosys, while upgrading full-year constant currency growth guidance to 8.5% to 9.0%.

However, heavy restructuring charges drove H1 2026 operating profit down 10.1% year-on-year and pushed the effective tax rate to an unusually elevated 37.5%, masking underlying operational resilience. The strategic thesis rests on agentic AI orchestration demand, European digital sovereignty tailwinds, and supply chain modernization, offset by persistent continental European utilization pressure, AI-driven pricing deflation risks, and elevated attrition at 18.6%.

Sell-side analysts remain constructively divided, with 63% Overweight consensus but price targets spanning €95 to €208, reflecting divergent views on margin restoration credibility and long-term AI disruption impacts. No activist presence exists in the AlphaSense library.
