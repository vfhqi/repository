Section 3 – Hedging Strategy and 2027 EUR/USD Hedging Coverage

Section summary
  1. Sartorius AG manages transaction risks using a rolling foreign currency hedging strategy designed to cover approximately 60% of its near-term U.S. dollar exposure.
  2. The group designates only the spot component of forward contracts under IFRS 9 to build reserves that mitigate transaction-driven volatility.

Hedging Strategy and Derivative Instruments

1.
Systematic risk reduction: Sartorius AG manages currency risks using a systematic rolling hedging approach that spans up to one year. This rolling strategy is monitored regularly to adjust to shifting macroeconomic conditions and baseline business projections. Financial derivatives are strictly deployed for risk reduction and never for speculative trading purposes. This programmatic approach is designed to reduce operating profit margin volatility from transaction mismatches.
2.
Designated spot element: Sartorius AG designates only the spot element of foreign exchange forward contracts in cash flow hedges. Under IFRS 9, the effective portions of changes in fair value are recorded in other comprehensive income. These accumulated balances are then reclassified into profit or loss when the underlying transactions occur. This formal hedge accounting preserves net result stability over time by aligning impacts.
3.
Apparent margin pressure: Sartorius AG experiences a structural presentation mismatch because hedging gains and losses are recognized in other operating income. The underlying physical transaction is recorded at the prevailing spot rate inside cost of sales, creating apparent pressure on the gross margin. Deutsche Bank Research highlighted that this presentation anomaly does not reflect underlying operational performance because it is compensated elsewhere. Analysts must evaluate consolidated margins rather than gross margins to understand performance.
4.
Contrary evidence: Sartorius AG might suffer from derivative valuation risks if cash flow hedge accounting cannot be fully applied. Management warned that future derivative availability depends entirely on maintaining adequate credit lines with banks. If these credit lines are restricted, mark-to-market valuation swings could adversely affect consolidated operating profit directly.
5.
Subsidiary concentration effects: Sartorius Stedim Biotech, the primary operating subsidiary of Sartorius AG, holds the vast majority of the group's forward contracts. At the end of 2025, the subsidiary held €297.7 million in EUR/USD forward contracts. Meanwhile, the total consolidated group EUR/USD forward nominal volume was €342.1 million. This distribution confirms that bioprocessing segment cash flows dictate the group's hedging activities.
