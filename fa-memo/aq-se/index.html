The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. FY2026: nine months SEK 7,501m plus Q4 2,730m (+17.5 percent: organic about 15 percent with delayed data-centre and Finnish defence deliveries recovered, acquired about 2.8 percent from Time 24, Gumboda and Norlake, currency about zero). FY2027 +10.0 percent = organic about 7.5 percent (data centres +50 percent, capacity-bound by Norlake fill and 2027 transformer lines; defence +25 percent; base business +3 percent as restocking plateaus and comparisons harden from Q2 2027) + acquired about 3.0 percent (carry-over of 2026 deals plus new bolt-ons at the evidenced 2 to 4 percent cadence) - currency about 0.5 percent (stronger krona against the dollar). FY2028 +8.0 percent (organic about 5.5 percent as data-centre growth halves on competitor capacity and the 800V re-award window; acquired 2.8 percent). FY2029-30 +7.5 percent (organic 4.5 percent through the cycle, acquired 3 percent). Q3 2026's 17.9 percent organic growth was mostly the base business recovering from a weak Q3 2025, so it is not extrapolated.
  • Quarterly path. Q1 2027 +16.2 percent is against a Q1 2026 depressed by minus 4.8 percent currency; growth drops to single digits from Q2 2027. Q3 2027 assumes a mild seasonal dip (Q3 2026 was flat on Q2 because of US and China utilisation during European holidays, which management called partly temporary).
  • EBITDA margin. Company basis (EBIT plus total D&A), matching the brokers' basis (FY2025 12.9 to 13.0 percent). FY2026 13.4 percent includes a SEK 22m Gävle property gain (about 0.2 points). The EBIT margin rises from about 9.65 percent underlying (2026) to 10.0, 10.2, 10.25 and 10.3 percent through mix toward the structural slice and the Time 24, Norlake, India and delivery-precision fixes, partly offset by about 3 percent a year of acquired revenue at below-group margins, OEM productivity price cuts and Eastern European wage inflation. Incremental EBIT margins about 11 to 13 percent, against 15 percent in nine months 2026. Real price contributes nothing in the base case.
  • D&A. Total D&A about 3.55 percent of sales (SEK 362m, 400m, 431m, 464m, 499m), rising with capex at 3.0 percent of sales in 2027 (northern Sweden defence capacity SEK 60m, Hungary, 2027 transformer lines, Norlake property) and with right-of-use depreciation. The inputs exclude my estimate of amortisation of acquired intangibles (SEK 10m in FY2026 rising to 18m in FY2030; not disclosed in the files), as the form requires.
  • Net financial items. About SEK minus 1 to minus 2m a quarter today (company EBT less EBIT); turning positive as net cash including leases compounds from about SEK 0.6bn (end-2026) to about 2.4bn (end-2030) at about 2 percent interest income, against lease interest on about SEK 400m of lease liabilities.
  • Tax. The P&L rate already rose to about 19.8 percent in nine months 2026 (calculated from company EBT and reported net income), consistent with Pillar II top-up being accrued since 2025 in Bulgaria, Estonia and Lithuania; further creep of about 0.5 points a year in 2027-28 as substance carve-outs decline and Bulgarian profits grow, then slower. Cash tax steps up more than P&L tax in 2027 (my estimate SEK 255m cash against 233m P&L) as accrued top-up is paid.
  • Dividend. About 24 percent of EPS, the realised payout under the policy of about 25 percent of cycle-average profit; declared for each year and paid the following April.
  • Share count. No buybacks, no scrip, no shares issued for acquisitions; warrant exercises (2024/27 at SEK 152.10, about SEK 10m in 2027; 2026/29 at SEK 273, about SEK 25m in 2029 if in the money) are entered as shares issued for cash. Basic shares 91.7m rising to about 91.95m.
  • Capex. SEK 280m in FY2026 (201m in nine months plus Q4 projects), then 3.0, 2.8, 2.7 and 2.7 percent of sales.
  • Working capital. 31.9 percent of trailing sales at Q3 2026; 31.0 percent at end-2026 after Q4 deliveries; easing slowly to 30 percent as growth slows. The 3.5 inventory-turn target is not assumed met (2.9 now; missed since it was set). How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
