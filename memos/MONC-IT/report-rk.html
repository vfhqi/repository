EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 6 findings

1.
Bear Case Credibility Assessment: The bearish thesis against Moncler SpA demonstrates moderate empirical

support grounded primarily in seasonal revenue concentration and near-term volume headwinds, though structural decay allegations remain largely speculative. Active short-seller reports are entirely absent for Moncler, contrasting with active activist campaigns targeting its European luxury peers, while major brokers maintain widespread neutral stances driven by slowing tourist traffic and flat volume growth across Europe, with rating momentum skewed heavily away from buy recommendations as hold ratings dominate active coverage.

The factual evidence base centers on gross inventory expansion of 11.8% year-on-year reaching EUR 872 million in FY2025, with obsolescence provisions reaching EUR 333 million representing 38% of gross inventory, alongside organic growth of 3% driven entirely by price while volumes turned slightly negative. However, adjusted profit of EUR 627 million aligned tightly with reported net profit, indicating absence of aggressive adjustments, which undermines allegations of accounting manipulation.

The bear case carries low-tomoderate probability of long-term correctness given Moncler's exceptional full-price retail execution history and rapid recoveries from past seasonal lulls, though near-term operational headwinds from tourist deceleration and pricing resistance warrant vigilant monitoring.

2.
Winning Pre-Mortem Profile: The FALSE FRIEND hypothesis emerges as the dominant structural risk framework

with zero hard inconsistencies against current operational data. The FALSE FRIEND profile emerges dominant as price-driven growth masks structural volume decay across core segments, carrying zero hard inconsistencies with current operational data showing flattish underlying volumes. This profile reconciles regional performance polarization including EMEA retail revenues declining 8%, negative organic volumes in Q2, and retail space expansion driving growth while underlying store-level sales density fails to hit the historical target of €40,000 per square meter.

In contrast, the outright NO hypothesis carries five hard inconsistencies with Moncler's sustained high profitability and positive comparable store sales growth, while the YES hypothesis shows two hard inconsistencies with empirical European volume deceleration trends. The FALSE FRIEND profile uniquely explains how H1 operating margin rose 70bps to reach an impressive 19% margin due to tight retail cost control while masking underlying volume decay through successive retail pricing actions and physical store network expansion.

3.
Highest-Plausibility Failure Modes: Three compounding failure mechanisms present elevated joint probability of

structural impairment. First, the highest-plausibility failure mode is severe seasonal demand concentration coupled with warmer winter temperature anomalies, as outerwear concentration remains highly acute with fourthquarter sales accounting for half of annual brand revenue. Second, the least-monitored structural risk is space productivity dilution from massive new flagship retail footprint expansions, as the upcoming Fifth Avenue flagship requires a prolonged ramp-up period and may fail to match historical density.

Third, intensified tax enforcement on offshore wealth deters Chinese high-net-worth spend in core European flagship locations, with wider France-to-China retail price gaps of 30% leaving Moncler highly exposed to sudden travel disruptions. These failures cluster around seasonal volatility and tourist dependency, creating correlated downside exposure where warmer winters, weakening tourist flows, and retail expansion inefficiency compound simultaneously during peak revenue quarters.

4.
Key False-Friend Indicators: Four diagnostic warning signals indicate metrics appearing supportive while

concealing terminal risks. First, robust mid-single-digit sales growth masks multi-year negative volume trends across core physical retail channels, as successive price increases of 5-10% ultimately stall organic volume growth in maturing European regions, indicating structural brand maturation if pricing tailwinds fade entirely. Second, stable short-term operating margins conceal structural underinvestment in necessary non-outerwear product category diversification, as peer Salvatore Ferragamo experienced severe market share loss after spending only 5.4% on marketing historically, with flatlining marketing spend vitiating long-term brand equity if consumer re-engagement requires larger support.

Third, declining store-level sales density to approximately €35.6k per square meter flags systematic retail execution issues, as flat-to-negative organic sales volumes represent a highly diagnostic joint monitorable KPI indicating underlying brand maturation. Fourth, short-term outlet margin accretion masks long-term brand equity dilution and pricing power erosion, as expanding retail footprints create a false-friend illusion of growth despite flat comparable store productivity. These indicators demonstrate how surface-level financial strength paradoxically signals future structural weakness.

5.
Net Currency Verdict: Moncler faces a persistent structural currency headwind that materially erodes reported

earnings and amplifies seasonal vulnerability. Management expects a 4% headwind to impact reported revenues during fiscal year 2026, with quantitative sensitivity models showing a 10% depreciation in the CNY shaving EUR 0.10 off reported EPS, while a 10% drop in JPY and USD collectively reduces reported EPS by EUR 0.09. The core structural mismatch stems from about 90% of group costs remaining denominated in euros while eurodenominated revenues represent less than 40% of total sales, creating structural transactional exposure.

While Moncler limits its currency hedging strategy exclusively to transaction risks with minimum currency hedging limits of 75% at campaign starts increasing to 90% at campaign ends, translation exposures remain completely unhedged and drive persistent reported dilution. The most critical risk concentration centers on the Chinese Yuan remaining the largest risk representing over 20% of revenues, where if the Chinese Yuan depreciates by 20%, reported revenues contract by EUR 129 million diluting global growth benchmarks.

Currency headwinds directly threaten the investment thesis by forcing reliance on aggressive retail price increases to defend margins, which exacerbates consumer volume resistance and heightens tourist flow sensitivity.

6.
Cross-Part Risk Synthesis: The three analytical lenses–bear case validation, pre-mortem failure narratives, and

currency resilience assessment– demonstrate severe compounding effects rather than offsetting diversification. The bearish volume decay concerns align precisely with pre-mortem FALSE FRIEND indicators showing pricedriven growth masking underlying demand weakness, while currency headwinds intensify margin pressure requiring further pricing actions that accelerate volume deterioration. Specifically, weaker tourist flows in EMEA exacerbate the 30% price gap between France and mainland China retail boutiques, as June traffic decelerated significantly across regions exposing the brand to extreme seasonality, with warmer winters and currency-driven tourist lulls threatening to trigger rapid revenue declines during critical seasonal periods.

The joint failure probability is assessed as medium-high given that seasonal demand concentration, unhedged translation exposure, and space productivity dilution create correlated downside scenarios where adverse weather patterns, euro strength, and Chinese tourist repatriation compound simultaneously during fourth-quarter peak sales windows. The investment case faces triple vulnerability as volume headwinds limit pricing pass-through capacity precisely when currency mismatches demand margin defense, while massive retail expansion dilutes productivity metrics during the transition period. Strategic mitigation requires monitoring fourth-quarter winter performance, flagship space productivity trends at the Fifth Avenue location, and stabilization of organic volume growth independent of pricing tailwinds to validate whether operational execution can overcome structural currency and seasonal handicaps.
