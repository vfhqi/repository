EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Business Model + Strategic Positioning

1.
Integrated value chain architecture: Molins operates a highly vertically integrated business model spanning quarries, cement manufacturing, ready-mix concrete, precast solutions, and circular economy divisions across 18 countries, capturing value at multiple stages of the construction lifecycle.
2.
Transformational scale expansion: The €1,400 million acquisition of Secil in March 2026 delivers pro forma revenues of €1,638 million and EBITDA of €534 million, elevating Molins to a scaled Iberian platform while adding 10 million tonnes of cement capacity in Portugal and Brazil.
3.
Geographic revenue diversification: Post-Secil, the group exhibits balanced geographic exposure with approximately 70% euro-denominated revenues, materially reducing emerging market currency volatility compared to the pre-acquisition profile.
4.
Downstream precast acceleration: Precast solutions represent 26.7% of proportional consolidated sales, driven by aggressive M&A execution including Concremat, Zenet, and the Baupartner joint venture with Titan Cement, positioning Molins to capture industrialized housing demand.
5.
Superior margin delivery: Molins achieved a proportional EBITDA margin of 26.02% in FY2025, exceeding both Titan Cement at 20.30% and Vicat at 11.50%, demonstrating exceptional operational efficiency across diversified markets.
6.
Elite return profile: Calculated post-tax ROCE exceeded 15% in FY2025, outperforming Titan's 12.60% and reflecting high-margin contributions from the protected Mexican joint venture and disciplined Argentine operations.
7.
Exceptional cash conversion: Free cash flow reached €176 million in FY2025 with a normalized 54% conversion rate, supporting both strategic acquisitions and a stable 40% dividend payout ratio despite significant growth capital deployment.
8.
Leverage transition: Net leverage is estimated to rise to 2.40x EBITDA on a pro forma basis following the Secil acquisition, funded through a €500 million senior unsecured bond at 5.50% with 2033 maturity, transitioning from a net cash position of €94 million.
9.
Mexican JV profitability dominance: The jointly-controlled Corporación Moctezuma operation generated €942 million in sales and €428 million in EBITDA in FY2025, representing the most profitable regional segment and benefiting from elite oligopolistic market structures.

Outliers worth noting

Cementos Molins exhibits an extreme positive margin outlier, with a proportional EBITDA margin reaching 26.02% in FY2025 that significantly exceeds the European heavyside peer average and surpasses Buzzi SpA's already-strong 28.80%, while dramatically outperforming Vicat S.A.'s outlier-low 11.50% margin. This exceptional profitability positioning reflects the company's highly advantaged exposure to disciplined oligopolistic markets in Mexico and South America, where protective inland logistics barriers and rational competitor behavior enable structurally superior pricing power compared to fragmented European basins.

The Mexican joint venture generated €428 million in EBITDA on €942 million in sales in FY2025, delivering industry-leading operating margins across the region. The margin superiority is mechanically reinforced by captive quarry integration, aggressive alternative fuel substitution reaching 25% globally, and downstream precast solutions that bypass commodity concrete competition. Cross-referencing Section 2 Financial Profile and Section 3 Industry Structure confirms this outlier status derives from deliberate geographic portfolio construction toward consolidated, import-protected inland basins rather than heroic operational execution alone.

Molins presents an extreme negative leverage outlier following the transformational Secil acquisition, with net debt estimated to increase to 2.40x EBITDA on a pro forma basis, representing a dramatic reversal from the company's historic position as a net cash operator. Prior to the transaction, Molins maintained a net cash balance of €94 million in FY2025, compared with net cash of €91 million in FY2024, placing it in the top decile of peer financial conservatism alongside other family-controlled building materials operators.

The €1,400 million Secil acquisition closed in the first quarter of FY2026 and was partially funded by a €500 million senior unsecured bond carrying a 5.50% fixed coupon with a 2033 maturity. This leverage spike exposes Molins to heightened interest expense sensitivity and constrains future capital allocation flexibility for dividend expansion or opportunistic bolt-on acquisitions during the critical 24-to-36 month integration window.

Cross-referencing Section 2 Financial Profile and Section 9 Operator Quality reveals this outlier positioning creates substantial execution risk if synergy realization or deleveraging timelines extend beyond management's base case assumptions, particularly given the concurrent family governance fracture documented in Section 9.
