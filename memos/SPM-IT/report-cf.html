EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Saipem has executed a decisive strategic pivot toward high-margin offshore engineering and construction, abandoning volatile legacy exposure following a 2022 capital raise that restored balance sheet stability under CEO Alessandro Puliti, a former Eni operational veteran who assumed leadership in August 2022. The company has systematically de-risked its portfolio by selling its Saudi shallow-water drilling fleet to ADES for USD 285 million and pursuing strict value-over-volume bidding that drops speculative low-margin onshore work entirely.
