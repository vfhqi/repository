[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Inter Cars is a cleared-concern case in a scale compounder, bought after the clearing has been priced. Four flat years of earnings ended in 2026 because parts prices turned from deflation to inflation and two automated hubs started paying back, and the whole of that change has already travelled into the sell-side's estimates and into a share price that has risen 71.4% in twelve months to 941.0 PLN. So the modal return from here is about 13% a year, a C against the framework's 20% bar, and the case clears the bar only below about 800 PLN or if the structural-margin claim turns out larger than consensus already holds.

  • The change is external first and internal second, and both have turned.
    • Spare-parts prices rose 1.5% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026 after falling 3.7% in 2025, which for a distributor earning a percentage margin on 134 days of stock is the single most important external change of the year.
    • The demand base is aging and shifting toward the independent channel: a European parc of 294 million vehicles averaging 12.9 years in 2025, and an independent-aftermarket share of 68% in 2025 from 50% in 2000.
    • The largest European rival, LKQ Europe, is shrinking organically (−5.1% in Q2 2026, −12.6% in Germany), which hands share to the scale leader without a fight.
    • The internal programme (two robotised hubs done, a third due about the end of the first quarter of 2027; 28 foreign branches a year) is real and resourced at PLN 1.6bn over five years, but it is in its fourth year, so it is a continuation rather than a new force.
      • That is why 'Change Forces' is a B and not an A.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have moved by about a third in one half-year.
    • H1 2026 EBITDA rose 31.0% and net profit 42.1% on revenue growth of 12.8%, because the gross margin stepped to 30.1% from 29.0% and the EBITDA margin to 7.5% from 6.5%.
    • Operating cash flow rose 40.5% to PLN 792.7m, the first strong cash reading after five years of negative free cash flow on the standardised definition, and net debt to EBITDA fell to 1.76x from 1.98x.
    • July and August 2026 sales grew 15%, with the foreign subsidiaries at 18 to 19%.
    • Cash conversion is the weak line: consensus free cash flow is only 28% of net income in FY2026, rising to 61% by FY2028, so the earnings recovery is ahead of the cash recovery.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain and into the price.
    • It shows in the team (stable), the choices (organic and automated), the actions (branches, hubs), the key operating metrics (gross margin, SG&A at 14.4% of sales), the financial statements (H1 2026) and the sell-side's estimates (FY2027 EPS consensus +13.9% in six months).
    • This is a mature change, not an early one.
  • What we think it earns: reported EPS of about 75.0 PLN in FY2026 (+31% on 57.08 PLN), 87.6 PLN in FY2027 and 102.1 PLN in FY2028, which is 16 to 17% a year after the recovery year, about 4% above the consensus medians of 72.00 PLN and 84.19 PLN.
    • The decomposition reconciles: revenue growth of 10.7% a year and margin expansion of 6.9% a year give EBITDA growth of 18.3% a year over FY2025 to FY2028, the below-EBITDA lines add 2.4% a year, the share count adds nothing.
    • The rate a reader should carry is 15.6% a year from FY2026 to FY2029, because the 21% three-year figure has the recovery year compounded in.
  • What it costs: 941.0 PLN is 9.885x consensus 24-month-forward EPS of 95.195 PLN, the 84th percentile of the stock's five-year range (median 7.85x) and a premium to the dashboard sector's median of 8.5x
    • Only on the ten-year window, which includes the 2016 to 2020 years of faster compounding and positive free cash flow, does it sit low, at the 26th percentile.
    • The contracted catalysts (Stęszew, the automation savings, returning parts inflation, the 30% gross margin) are in consensus (EBITDA margin 8.0% by FY2028) and in the price
      • The mean target of 1,000.8 PLN sits 6% above the price.
      • This is the price-moved-against-the-park pattern.
  • The return is a C, carried entirely by earnings growth.
    • At 9.5x the earnings two years beyond each horizon, the modal value is 1,136.7 PLN at eighteen months and 1,348.4 PLN at three years including dividends, 13.4% and 12.7% a year.
    • The dividend is immaterial (1.42 PLN a share, 0.15% of the price), and the re-rating leg is a small negative because the multiple we hold is a little below today's.
    • The chain holds at the forces and the outputs and breaks at the price: the same path clears 20% a year over three years only from about 780 PLN, and we take 800 PLN as the entry level.
    • The case has no margin for the currency: a plausibly worse drop-through from EBITDA to EPS (an unhedged 4 to 5% zloty move, 200 basis points on the facility, Stęszew depreciation above plan) takes about 9% off FY2028 EPS and the return to 6.6% and 9.3% a year.
  • The pattern is 'OSHQC CfC Clearing', and the fit is C, lost on timing rather than substance.
    • The concern (parts deflation, a strong zloty, an investment phase) was transient and has cleared with hard evidence
    • What the pattern lacks today is the oversold condition, because the setup fitted at 550 to 650 PLN a year ago and has been paid for.
  • What breaks it: a zloty fall during a resumed Polish price war while Stęszew is being stocked, on a cost base of which an estimated 60% is in euros against 24% of revenue, with no hedge at all.

  • The case is therefore material as a change and ordinary as a return at this price: a good business whose recovery the market has already paid for, which is why we park it with a price trigger and a results trigger rather than progress it.

  • Rating = C, because the Building Block measures materiality in three links (forces, outputs, return) and the third binds: the forces (B) and the outputs (B) are real and have moved earnings by a third, but at 941.0 PLN the 'Modal return' is about 13% a year (C), so the expected change is no longer material to the shareholder from here.

    • The parts combine as a chain, so the weakest link sets the level.
    • B was live and would be right below about 800 PLN, where the same path clears 20% a year over three years, or on evidence that the structural margin runs beyond the 8.2% we hold for FY2028.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The strongest case against is the risk research's false-friend reading: strong headline growth masking a working-capital-heavy, unhedged, thin-margin distributor whose H1 2026 margins were flattered by the zloty and by a PLN 23m insurance gain, and whose export arbitrage (a 25% Polish price advantage) is eroding as suppliers harmonise prices across Europe by about 2030.
    • The evidence for it is real.
      • Free cash flow was negative on the standardised definition in every year from FY2021 to FY2025
      • Inventories are about PLN 6.0bn and 134 days
      • There were zero currency hedges across 2023 to 2025 with an estimated 60% of cost of goods in euros against 24% of revenue
      • A 5% euro move is worth PLN 79.3m of the parent's net profit
      • The parent's direct exports already fell 10%
      • Related-party guarantees stand at PLN 1,054.7m
      • Inventory provisioning is 0.58% of stock.
    • What we concede: the cash conversion history is poor, the hedging policy is a bare bet on pass-through, and the H1 2026 margin had 0.2 percentage points of currency help and 0.4 percentage points of one-off in Q2.
    • Why we still hold the view on the business: the working-capital build is the inventory for two new hubs and is slowing (net working capital 27.5% of revenue at Q2 2026 from 32.4% at Q3 2025; operating cash flow +40.5% in H1 2026)
      • Consensus free cash flow turns positive in FY2026
      • The gross-margin expansion is 1.1 percentage points of which currency is 0.1 to 0.2, the rest purchasing scale and inflation pass-through
      • And the export arbitrage is a parent-level business being replaced by local subsidiaries by design.
    • Where the counter-case bites is the price, not the business.
      • It is the reason our modal multiple is 9.5x and not the ten-year median of 10.6x, the reason the return is a C, and the reason the recommendation is to wait for 800 PLN or for the Q3 print.

  • Rating = B, because the turn from parts deflation to inflation is the single most important external change of 2026 for a distributor earning a percentage margin on 134 days of stock, and supplier concentration is low
    • The risks are pan-European price harmonisation and a base-oil shortage.
    • Tailwind now.

A value chain that favours scaled independent distributors, a demand base that is counter-cyclical and aging, a largest rival in retreat, and a business with real and widening operating advantages run by a proven operator team together support the thesis; the fragilities are a three-year-old Polish price war that is calming rather than ended, supplier price harmonisation that erodes an export arbitrage by about 2030, and the financial shape of a distributor.

  • The other actors support the thesis, and the support is not fragile on the horizon.
    • Thousands of independent workshops buy on availability, catalogue and delivery speed rather than price, place half their orders as regular customers, and are a safe harbour in downturns
    • Regulators have just re-confirmed the independent channel's access to 2028
    • 2,100 suppliers with none above 10% of revenue sell on annual contracts with volume rebates and no minimums.
    • The fragile actor is the competitor set in Poland, where smaller distributors started a price war in late 2022 and where the company must sell at market price
      • The calm of 2026 rests on returning parts inflation.
  • The industry structure is concentrating toward the advantaged, which is the company.
    • The independent channel's share rose from 50% to 68% in twenty-five years
    • Scaled distributors win rebates and automation that sub-scale players cannot fund
    • LKQ Europe, the largest in Europe, is losing share and has delisted 71,000 SKUs.
    • Density, availability and delivery frequency decide garage purchasing, which favours the two Polish players over the Western consolidators in CEE and Germany.
    • At the European level the structure is still a long tail (the top seven hold about 25% of a market with 50,000 distributors), so the structural rating is a C, not because the pull is weak but because the concentration is regional.
  • The business has advantages it can prove and delivers the outputs garages value.
    • Central hubs controlling over two million SKUs, 660,000 square metres of logistics space, two robotised hubs, an e-Catalogue with 250,000-plus users and about 3 million order lines a month, and a franchise model that turns managers into owners on a 50/50 margin split
    • Growth of 12.8% against Auto Partner's 11.0% in H1 2026 and 12% in euro terms against a sector median of about 4% says the net utility is at least equal to the best rival's.
    • The advantage compounds mechanically: more volume buys better rebates and denser routes, which buy more volume.
      • What has not compounded is earnings per share, which interest and working capital absorbed from FY2021 to FY2025.
  • The financial outputs are a distributor's, and that is the reason the Building Block is not an A.
    • An EBIT margin of 5.3% in FY2025, a gross margin of 30.1%, 134 days of inventory, free cash flow negative on the standardised definition in each of FY2021 to FY2025, a return on new capital implied at about 11% pre-tax from the Zakroczym savings: none of these meets the framework's quality thresholds, and none of them is a fragility of the thesis either, because the thesis relies on scale, density, automation and pass-through rather than on pricing power or cash yield.
  • Management is a better operator than capital allocator.
    • A chief executive who built the logistics systems and has run the company since May 2017, a five-member board unchanged since January 2024, two hubs delivered and 28 foreign branches a year on the operating side
    • On the capital side a zero-hedge currency policy, a 2.5% payout, one buyback at 575 PLN, founder adviser fees and three consecutive revenue-target misses.
    • The operating record over twelve years (sales and EBITDA both compounding at about 16% a year, 2013 to 2025) is what the thesis rests on, and it carries the operator rating to a B.

  • Where the foundations connect to the price: the thematic cross-reference cannot be made from this folder, and the business-level themes (an aging parc, channel share gain, CEE consumer growth, the return of parts inflation) are slow themes rather than accelerating ones, so nothing here argues for a multiple above the range the five-year history supports.

    • The foundations are the reason to want the business
    • They are not the reason to pay 941.0 PLN for it.

Summary: Written Summary of the Investment Case (not rated) (E 3, BB 4)
The case in 8 to 10 sentences (the thesis as tested), kept in full at both ends of the record (Part 1, E 3, BB 4)
  • This is a cleared-concern case in a scale compounder, bought after the clearing has been priced.
  • Inter Cars is Europe's largest listed independent parts distributor, growing 12 to 15% against a market growing 2% by taking share from a faltering largest rival and from sub-scale players across 21 countries, and its earnings were flat from FY2021 to FY2025 (49.38 PLN to 57.08 PLN of EPS) because parts prices deflated, the zloty strengthened and the company stocked two new automated hubs.
  • In 2026 all three turned: parts prices rose 1.5% in Q2, the gross margin stepped to 30.1%, the EBITDA margin to 7.5%, EBITDA grew 31% and net profit 42% in H1, operating cash flow rose 40%, and the sell-side raised FY2027 EPS by 14% in six months.
  • We expect EPS of about 75 PLN in FY2026 (+31%), 87.6 PLN in FY2027 and 102.1 PLN in FY2028 (16 to 17% a year), a little above consensus, with the margin returning to its FY2021 to FY2022 level plus the first contribution from Stęszew.

  • The case is compelling as a business and ordinary as an investment at this price.
  • It clears the bar below about 800 PLN, or if the structural-margin claim proves larger than the 8.2% EBITDA margin we hold for FY2028.
