Business Description (B.1) 13 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Core activity: Fuchs SE formulates and blends lubricants and specialty fluids, holding over 10,000 customised product formulations and more than 2,800 active original-equipment-manufacturer specifications
2.
Market position: The company is the world's largest independent lubricant manufacturer, with a global lubricants share below 3.00% and a 60.0% share of the global shock-absorber fluid market
3.
Value-chain seat: Fuchs procures 100% of its base oils externally and runs no refining, occupying the specialty formulation stage rather than the upstream refining stage
4.
Segment mix: The portfolio splits roughly 55.0% industrial lubricants and 45.0% automotive lubricants, with over 4.00% share in specialised industrial segments against 2.00% in commoditised automotive fluids
5.
End-market mix: Aftermarket and Trading is the largest end market at 26.0% of group sales, followed by Primary Industry and Energy at 19.0%
6.
End-market mix, remainder: Vehicle Components Manufacturers account for 17.0%, On-Highway Vehicle Manufacturers for 15.0% and Industrial Equipment and Machinery for 9.00%
7.
Geographic footprint: Europe, the Middle East and Africa generated 51.0% of FY25 sales by customer location, Asia-Pacific 30.0% and the Americas 19.0%, with EUR 2,048m, EUR 1,002m and EUR 695m respectively by company location
8.
Operating model: The group runs 67 operating subsidiaries and 38 production sites on a local-for-local principle, sourcing roughly 95.0% of raw materials locally and selling roughly 75.0% direct
9.
Financial character: FY25 revenue was EUR 3,563m with a 12.2% EBIT margin, on record EBIT of EUR 435m and a gross margin of 34.9%
10.
Returns and cash: Pre-tax return on capital employed was 22.2% and free cash flow before acquisitions EUR 316m in FY25
11.
Balance sheet: The group carried net liquidity of EUR 151m at FY25 year end, rising to EUR 203m at the end of the first quarter of 2026, with capital expenditure at 2.00% to 2.50% of sales
12.
Ownership: The Fuchs family holds 59.0% of the voting ordinary shares against 29.5% of total economic capital, with equity split equally into 65.5 million ordinary and 65.5 million non-voting preference shares
13.
Investment style: The financial signature is that of a low-capital-intensity compounder with a cyclical industrial demand overlay, not a growth story and not a distressed turnaround
