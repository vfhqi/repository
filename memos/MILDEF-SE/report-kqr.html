Section 2 – Budgetary Reallocation and Platform-to-Soldier Shifts

Section summary
  1. European procurement is undergoing a structural shift as capital migrates from heavy land platforms toward tactical electronics, dismounted soldier systems, and unmanned capabilities.
  2. The redirection of platform budgets into short-cycle software and sensors provides unprecedented visibility for specialized non-prime contractors.

platform budget reallocation trends

1.
Declining land commitments: Germany's 2027 draft budget shows long-term commitment authorisations (VE) falling by 25% to €232 billion, indicating a transition from contract authorization toward execution.
2.
Reallocated capability mix: German land vehicle commitments are dropping from 45% of VE in 2026 to 29% in the 2027 draft.
3.
Rising electronic priority: German air defence and electronic warfare authorisations will increase from 1% to 11% of total commitments in 2027.
