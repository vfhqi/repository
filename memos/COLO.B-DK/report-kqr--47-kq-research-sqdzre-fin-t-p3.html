Hospital Placement Dynamics

1.
Critical gateway: Coloplast relies heavily on clinical selection during initial hospital stays to secure long-term patient relationships. Ostomy patients typically use their products for around ten years, while continence patients can remain on a single brand for up to 30 years. This extremely high retention rate means that hospital clinical selection dictates market share for decades.
