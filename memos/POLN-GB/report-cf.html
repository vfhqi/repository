This payout corresponds precisely to the board's stretch EBITDA target achievement of £64.6 million in FY25, following a similar pattern in FY24 where EBITDA of £57.5 million outperformed the stretch target of £55 million by 5%, suggesting a highly calibrated remuneration framework that consistently rewards at the upper bound. Equalisation adjustment mechanism creating a £2.4 million dilution to Net Investment Assets in FY25 represents an unusual structural feature whereby newer fund investors paid interest to older investors to compensate for capital drawn down prior to late entry.
