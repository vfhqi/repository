Contrary evidence: Morgan Stanley reported on October 23, 2026, that emerging competitive biologics are actively targeting improved efficacy and dosing convenience. This analysis suggests that Dupixent's long-term commercial dominance is highly vulnerable to disruption from assets with less frequent dosing. Consequently, these commercial dynamics could accelerate Sanofi's volume deceleration faster than consensus models currently project.
