Section 2 – The Acquisition Flywheel and Compounder Pace

Section summary
  1. Buying profitable C-parts distributors at disciplined multiples accelerates Bufab's cash flow reinvestment flywheel.
  2. High leverage and lower capital returns compared with Nordic serial acquirers limit the sustainable pace of Bufab's acquisition strategy.
  3. Confidence in the acquisition flywheel's strength is medium, based on historical transaction multiples balanced against rising leverage constraints.

Acquisition Flywheel Mechanics

1.
Profitable compounding: Bufab reinvests its generated cash flow into acquiring regional distributors to expand its geographic reach and product portfolio. This transaction model relies on buying niche businesses that quickly benefit from the group's global purchasing resources. Executing these deals is designed to generate incremental cash flow for further reinvestment over the next three years.
2.
Strategic expansion: Bufab completed the acquisition of German sourcing specialist Novia Group in October 2025 to strengthen its presence in Western Europe. The group financed this platform deal using its existing credit facilities with a purchase price based on an enterprise value of EUR 60 million. This addition is highly marginaccretive because the target historically operated with margins significantly above Bufab's profitability targets of 14 percent.
3.
High-margin acquisition: Bufab acquired Vital S.p.A. in late 2024 to capture a leading distribution position in the Italian C-parts market. The business was purchased at a highly disciplined entry valuation of six times EV/EBITA including earn-outs. This disciplined purchase price demonstrates that the group can secure highly profitable niche distributors without overpaying.
4.
Niche integration: Bufab expanded its UK operations in July 2026 through the acquisition of DC Iron for a total consideration of SEK 420 million. The acquired British distributor of ironwork parts generated sales of GBP 14.8 million during the previous fiscal year. This transaction represents the group's strategy of targeting profitable companies in attractive niche segments to sustain long-term sales growth.
