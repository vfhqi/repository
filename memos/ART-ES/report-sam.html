Where the sell-side's view is heading

The Investment Analyst's judgement

The upgrade cycle has stopped: the Investment Analyst judges financial year 2027 and 2028 consensus earnings are more likely cut than raised from March 2027.

  • Twelve and six months: financial year 2027 earnings per share rose 34% over 12 months and 18% over six; EBITDA 27% and 14%; sales only 7% and 5%; target price 58% in six months.
  • Three months and one month: earnings per share rose 5%, EBITDA 7% and target price 14%, and the Buy share rose 33 points to 100%; over one month every measure is 0%.
  • What drove it: the Investment Analyst judges the upgrades were margin-led rather than revenue-led, so any cut is more likely to come from margin too; the shares fell about 17% while estimates did not move.
  • Where next: the Investment Analyst expects 2027 and 2028 consensus to be cut (about 60% and 65% odds of a 5% cut) after the 24-Mar-2027 results, as first 2027 guidance lands below consensus.
  • Cuts without downgrades: new initiations near management's plan could pull the 2027 mean to €1.25 to €1.30 while the Buy share stays near 100%; by April 2027 the +18% six-month revision also drops out.
  • What would prove it wrong: a Strategic Plan 2030 at or above €950m and 21% (about 25% likely), a second-half 2026 margin at or above 18.4%, or 2027 guidance that brackets consensus.
