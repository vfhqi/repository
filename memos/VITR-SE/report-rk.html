The outright failure NO profile is similarly refuted by expanding gross margins to 60.3% and the substantial SEK 500 million share buyback program, which provide defensive operational buffers inconsistent with imminent business collapse. The single hard inconsistency confronting the FALSE FRIEND scenario stems from strong Americas consumables organic growth of 15% in Q2 2026, which directly contradicts the hypothesis of pervasive volume decay.

However, this regional strength appears insufficient to invalidate the broader pattern of structural deterioration evident across fifteen diagnostic evidence items evaluated in the matrix. These disconfirming data points collectively reveal that headline profitability improvements derive primarily from portfolio rationalization and cost restructuring rather than genuine underlying demand strength.

This probability calculation reflects the compounding nature of identified risks, particularly private equity clinic consolidation driving centralized procurement and ongoing pricing pressure, and regional macroeconomic drag depressing cycle volumes while forcing capital purchase delays.

The meta-analysis clustering reveals that these root causes do not operate independently but rather arrive simultaneously during broader economic contractions, materially elevating the probability that multiple failure modes manifest concurrently.
