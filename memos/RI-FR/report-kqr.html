Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The development of distributor inventory levels and pricing power over the next 18 months will dictate whether European spirits houses can recover their earnings per share or remain dependent on aggressive cost cutting.

  2. Pernod Ricard and Rémy Cointreau face continued wholesaler destocking headwinds, while Diageo suffers market share losses and Campari achieves relative volume outperformance.
