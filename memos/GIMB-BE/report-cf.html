Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro) 36 findings

Section summary
  1. underlying deal flow faces cyclical headwinds from sluggish European consumer spending and a broad-based industrial slowdown across core manufacturing sectors
  2. mid-market private equity multiples remain highly competitive, forcing Gimv to pay premium valuations compared to historical sector medians
  3. digital transition in food safety is accelerating rapidly, driving automated compliance software adoption and consolidated market share gains
  4. tightening European environmental and food standards heighten compliance costs, forcing portfolio companies to accelerate supply chain reform
  5. banking syndicates maintain restrictive credit terms, although deposit rate cuts provide marginal cost relief for leveraged mid-market capital structures
  6. private equity exit markets show uneven normalisation, with liquidity concentrated in mega-cap assets while mid-market inventory remains largely illiquid
  7. persistent geopolitical tensions and elevated interest rates restrict portfolio capital deployment and squeeze net asset value expansion

Cautious manufacturing outlook: Businesses are becoming increasingly hesitant regarding future capital commitments due to persistent global supply chain friction

Horticulture service growth: Acquired assets such as Groupe Cap Vert benefit from rising municipal demand for climate-adaptive urban forestry services
