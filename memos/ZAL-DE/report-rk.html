EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 8 findings

This comprehensive three-part analytical review examines Zalando SE through the lens of bear-case evidence quality, pre-mortem failure scenario planning, and currency exposure resilience. The analysis concludes that Zalando exhibits a deceptive "false friend" investment profile where superficial B2B logistics revenue acceleration and marketplace GMV expansion obscure persistent structural B2C retail volume stagnation, cohort decay, and margin dilution.

The company faces medium-high aggregate failure probability estimated at 35-40%, driven primarily by active customer GMV erosion requiring unsustainable acquisition spend, compounded by social commerce traffic disintermediation and stubborn 60% German return rates. Currency exposure remains well-managed through disciplined 15-month forward hedging, presenting only a neutral to minor net headwind to medium-term earnings trajectories.

The investment thesis remains structurally challenged until management demonstrates sustained organic B2C revenue re-acceleration above 5% YoY and proves that high-margin B2B software contracts can offset low-margin fulfillment economics. A strict hold-and-watch posture is warranted, with capital deployment gated on two consecutive quarters of positive organic B2C GMV momentum and verified delivery of €100 million ABOUT YOU integration synergies by 2028.
