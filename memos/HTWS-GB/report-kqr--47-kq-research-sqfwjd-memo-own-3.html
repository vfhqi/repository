Strict accounting: Helios Towers applies the five-step framework of IFRS 15 to recognize revenue only when customer collections are deemed probable. This accounting policy ensures that reported sales represent only realizable customer economic inflows.
2.
Cumulative balances: The unrecognized revenue figures of USD 46.8 million and USD 50.4 million represent cumulative balances of services provided rather than single-period amounts. Helios Towers explicitly states in its annual report that these balances reflect services provided to customers that had yet to meet the probability criteria. Helios Towers discloses the gross value of services provided but not recognized under its receivables notes as USD 46.8 million.
