EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Greencore has executed a transformative strategic pivot through its £1.50bn acquisition of Bakkavor UK, completed on 16 January 2026, creating a £4bn pro-forma revenue UK convenience food champion with substantially enhanced negotiating leverage across the consolidated UK grocery ecosystem. The transaction delivered 43.0% reported revenue growth to £1,318.0m in H1 2026, though pro-forma organic growth moderated to 3.2%, reflecting subdued legacy Bakkavor volumes and challenging weather comparatives.

Management under CEO Dalton Philips and CFO Catherine Gubbins has assembled a highly credible and cohesive leadership team with prior collaborative experience at daa plc, positioning the group to execute a complex multi-year integration program while targeting at least £80m in annual cost synergies by FY29.

The underlying margin trajectory demonstrates solid structural progress, with pro-forma adjusted operating margins reaching 5.6% in H1 2026, up 60 basis points year-on-year, continuing a multi-year expansion from 4% in FY23 to 6.50% in FY25. Balance sheet leverage closed H1 2026 at 2.3x net debt to adjusted EBITDA, outperforming initial 2.5x guidance and remaining comfortably within the 3.50:1 maximum lender covenant. However, free cash flow recorded a temporary £76.0m outflow driven by £66.8m of exceptional Bakkavor transaction costs and £86.0m of seasonal working capital timing, compressing trailing cash conversion to 3.1% from a robust 66.5% in FY25.

The forward earnings pathway is underpinned by five high-plausibility structural drivers: Bakkavor cost synergies targeting at least £80m by FY29 with 50% realization by January 2027; strategic footprint rationalization including US asset divestment of a business with $500m revenue capacity; factory-level automation deployment through 871 operational excellence projects, with each 1% staff reduction saving £8m in wage costs; automatic inflation pass-through models protecting 75% of raw material costs and full hedging of gas and electricity in FY26; and new business onboarding including 308 new products launched in H1 2026 and strategic brand licensing with Myprotein targeting 100 basis points of annualized growth.

Recent H1 2026 delivery exceeded broker expectations, with pro-forma adjusted operating profit of £73.30m beating the Deutsche Bank estimate of £67.50m by 8.59%, while pro-forma adjusted operating margin expanded 60 basis points to 5.60% versus legacy Bakkavor's 5.30% FY24 baseline. Historical earnings trajectory confirms consistent outperformance, with adjusted EBIT growing from £76.30m in FY23 to £125.70m in FY25 and management repeatedly upgrading guidance through multiple in-year consensus upgrades, demonstrating a reliable sandbagger signaling posture that under-promises and over-delivers.

Sell-side positioning exhibits strong polarization, with Deutsche Bank maintaining a Buy rating and 325p price target citing robust synergies, Shore Capital providing warm House Stock coverage highlighting outstanding operational execution, while Jefferies holds at Hold with 185p target, cautioning on relatively muted 3.2% pro-forma growth and unprecedented National Living Wage pressures. The target price dispersion spans 75.68% from low to high, reflecting deep disagreements on labor cost recovery and synergy execution.

Fundamental peer positioning places Greencore first in momentum rank with 43.0% reported revenue growth and 5.60% operating margin, outpacing Bakkavor's 1.20% organic growth and 5.70% margin, though trailing Premier Foods' 17.02% trading margin in the branded segment. No active activist presence exists in the AlphaSense library, with management retaining full strategic control over the integration roadmap and US divestment timeline.

Critical risks center on three structural threats: first, escalating exceptional charges totaling £73.70m in H1 2026 driven by Bakkavor transaction costs could exceed budgeted integration expenses; second, free cash flow conversion collapsed to 3.1% due to working capital timing, with recovery dependent on year-end reversal; third, apparent margin outperformance may stall if Bakkavor integration costs exceed synergy estimates and inflationary food-to-go volume drops persist.

Additional macro headwinds include highly inflationary UK National Living Wage increases and £7.5m annualized cost from Employer National Insurance hikes implemented in April 2025.

1.
Investment verdict: Greencore represents a high-conviction structural consolidation play with credible management execution, visible £80m synergy pathway, and defensive pass-through pricing models, though near-term cash conversion recovery and Bakkavor volume stabilization remain critical gating factors for sustained medium-term return on invested capital expansion toward the 15% North Star target.
