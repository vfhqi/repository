Baseline Currency Exposures

Significant translational exposure: Röko AB reports that about 89.6% of its consolidated sales are generated in foreign currencies, primarily the British Pound, Euro, Danish Krone, and Norwegian Krone. The remaining 10.4% (calculated) is generated in Swedish Krona, indicating that the group lacks a natural domestic currency hedge. This heavy concentration in foreign currencies means that any broad-based appreciation of the Swedish Krona will directly compress reported group-level sales and EBITA metrics.
2.
Dominated segment profile: Segment B2B accounts for approximately two-thirds of Röko AB's sales, with heavy exposure to the British Pound and Euro. For the full year of 2025, B2B net sales in the United Kingdom reached MSEK 1,695, representing about 40% of the segment's total sales of MSEK 4,237. Because major B2B holdings like Fri-Jado in the Netherlands and ETB Technologies in the UK operate entirely in foreign currencies, their contribution is highly sensitive to exchange rate shifts.

Regional consumer footprint: Segment B2C comprises about one-third of Röko AB's business and shows distinct exposure to the Danish Krone and Euro.
