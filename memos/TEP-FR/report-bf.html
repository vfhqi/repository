Outliers worth noting

1.
Extreme workforce attrition rates: The corporate agent workforce experienced an elevated 68% attrition rate in fiscal year 2024, positioning Teleperformance at the bottom quintile of workforce stability metrics across the global BPO sector. While elevated turnover is endemic to offshore customer care operations, Teleperformance's attrition rate materially exceeds sustainable base rates observed at best-in-class competitors, where annual turnover typically ranges between 45% and 55% in comparable geographies. This structural attrition pressure creates chronic recruitment costs of $80 to $100 per replacement agent while simultaneously complicating the transition toward outcome-based commercial models, where transaction and outcome-based deals reached mid-single digit growth representing over 15% of total group revenue in H1 2026. The persistent labor instability represents an unexplained structural anomaly given management's emphasis on employee satisfaction initiatives and suggests underlying cultural or compensation misalignment across offshore delivery centers.
