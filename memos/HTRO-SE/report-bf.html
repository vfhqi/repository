EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Hexatronic is successfully pivoting from commoditized FTTH hardware to highly differentiated connectivity niches, driving a structural margin expansion across key regions. The group operates through three divisions: Fiber Solutions (microducts, cables, submarine systems), Data Center (high-density cabling and installation services), and Harsh Environment (subsea and military connectivity). Data Center revenues expanded to 25% of net sales in Q1 2026 versus 19% in Q1 2025, while Fiber Solutions decreased to 58% from 66%, reflecting the deliberate portfolio rebalancing.

The business is geographically diversified with Europe accounting for 50% of Q1 2026 revenues and North America rising to 38%. No single customer represents over 10% of total group sales, materially reducing concentration risk relative to smaller niche peers.

Financial Profile and Peer Performance

The global data center capacity has risen from 60 GW in 2020 to 97 GW in 2024 and is projected to expand a further 133% to reach 226 GW by 2030, underpinning structural demand for Hexatronic's high-margin Data Center segment. Group net sales fell 10% in Q1 2026, driven by a 2% organic decline and 9% FX headwinds, but Data Center delivered 20% organic sales growth in Q1 2026, beating most hardware peers. Adjusted EBITA margin improved sequentially to 8.6% in Q1 2026, ending five quarters of decline.

The peer benchmarking reveals Hexatronic's 10.72% EBITDA margin and 11% ROIC trail Prysmian's 12.20% margin and 12% ROIC, while substantially lagging Corning's structural outlier 24.5% EBITDA margin and 14.2% ROIC. Interest-bearing net debt excluding IFRS 16 increased to SEK 1,672 million in Q1 2026, with leverage at 2.2x EBITDA, elevated versus Prysmian's lower-levered balance sheet.

Competitive Positioning and Value Chain Economics

The top five fiber manufacturers control approximately 67% of global preform production capacity, with Corning leading at 20% global capacity, followed by YOFC at 15% and ZTT at 11%. Hexatronic deliberately bypasses asset-heavy glass preform manufacturing to focus on downstream cabling, passive connectivity, and projectbased system integration. The group is present at the Cabling & Conduit Extrusion stage with typical 25-35% gross margins and 5-10% EBIT margins, Passive Connectivity & Assemblies with 35-50% gross margins and 20- 30% EBIT margins, and Network Deployment & Integration with 15-25% gross margins and 10-15% EBIT margins.

The largest single customer accounts for less than 4% of total group sales, the three largest customers combined generate 9.68% of sales, and the top ten customers account for 30% of Fiber Solutions sales compared to 50% in Data Centers. The patented air-blown fiber technology requires seamless interoperation of Stingray cables and ducts, the Stingray cable sheath has zero shrinkage preventing costly fragile splice points, and Viper Ease micro cables reduce installer preparation times by 50% to 70%, establishing formidable switching costs.

Operator Quality and Strategic Execution

CEO Rikard Fröberg assumed office in March 2025, and the performance improvement program completed in Q1 2026 delivered annualized cost savings of SEK 120 million, exceeding the initial SEK 110 million target. Hexatronic prioritizes acquisitions over dividends, targeting 3-6 service-oriented companies annually at 5x to 7x EBITDA multiples. JOWO Systemtechnik was acquired in April 2026 to expand Harsh Environment capabilities in Europe, and Superior Fiber & Data was acquired in June 2026 for USD 29 million to broaden US installations.

The patented air-blown fiber technology enables fast deployment and lower total cost of ownership for installers, Stingray fibers feature a smooth, low-friction sheath and zero jacket shrinkage to protect fragile splice points, and Viper Ease reduces installer cable preparation times by 50% to 70% to lower deployment costs. Adjusted EBITA margin recovered sequentially to 8.6% in Q1 2026 ending five quarters of contraction, Data Center solutions delivered a 16.8% adjusted EBITA margin in Q1 2026 beating targets, but factory under-utilization in Europe compressed the Fiber Solutions adjusted EBITA margin to 6.4% in 2025.

Risk Factors and Structural Headwinds

The European FTTH market remains weak as builders shift focus from passings to connecting subscribers, lower volumes have caused severe factory under-utilization and weak fixed-cost coverage, and overcapacity has put severe downward pressure on European microduct and duct pricing. CommScope and Hexatronic do not manufacture bare glass, exposing them to market-driven raw fiber price spikes, and Hexatronic remains highly dependent on third-party glass suppliers including direct competitors like Corning for bare fiber.

Working capital changes consumed SEK 83 million of operating cash for Hexatronic in Q1 2026, highlighting persistent cash conversion challenges during seasonal inventory builds. The aggressive pivot to service-heavy acquisitions could permanently dilute return on capital employed if integrating low-margin installers fails to offset asset-heavy fiber plant under-utilization, while the lack of upstream glass preform manufacturing leaves the group highly exposed to severe supply squeezes or pricing discrimination from vertically integrated competitors.
