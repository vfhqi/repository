Section 6 – The 800V DC Counter-Case and Technical Debates

Section summary
  1. Transitioning facilities to 800V DC and integrating optical interconnects presents a compelling counter-case that slashes copper volume and bypasses high-frequency physical reach limitations.

Facility-Level 800V DC Architecture

1.
Radical copper reduction: Upgrading to 800V DC distribution reduces critical copper cabling mass by approximately 45% to 65% versus standard low-voltage setups.
2.
Grey space compression: Direct medium-voltage solid-state transformers collapse the grey-space footprint from twelve meters to under three meters.
3.
Power efficiency acceleration: Skipping multiple intermediate power conversion stages improves end-to- end facility efficiency by approximately 5%
4.
Severe arcing risks: Operating at 800V DC raises critical safety concerns because direct current lacks a natural zero-crossing point.
5.
Phased deployment path: Near-term implementation focuses on row-level sidecar power racks to bridge legacy facilities.
