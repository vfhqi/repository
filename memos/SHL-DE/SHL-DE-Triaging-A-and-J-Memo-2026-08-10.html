B.1 Business Description 7 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What the company does. Siemens Healthineers designs, manufactures and services medical imaging, radiotherapy and in-vitro diagnostics equipment, organised since fiscal year 2026 into three reportable segments: Imaging, Precision Therapy and Diagnostics
2.
Primary markets. Imaging (magnetic resonance, computed tomography, X-ray, molecular imaging) is the largest segment; Precision Therapy combines Varian radiotherapy, Advanced Therapies and ultrasound; Diagnostics covers centralised-lab, specialty-lab and point-of-care in-vitro testing
3.
Financial character. Group revenue of EUR 23.38bn in FY25 (Morgan Stanley, Business Foundations digest) with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 20.5%, the highest of its three-way peer set against GE HealthCare's 15.3% and Philips' 11.0%
4.
Balance sheet. Net debt/EBITDA stood at 3.1x at Q2 FY26, elevated by the 2021 Varian acquisition, which stepped leverage from 0.9x to 3.4x
4.1
Rating and path: A Moody's A3 investment-grade rating and a stated deleveraging path toward 2.5x by FY27
5.
Segment mix. Imaging and Precision Therapy carry recurring-revenue shares near 50% of sales; Diagnostics runs a razor-and-blade model with 90% recurring revenue from reagents and consumables, though equipment sales are only 10% of that segment's revenue
6.
Geography. An installed base exceeding 700,000 systems across 180 countries; Americas comparable revenue grew 7.8% in H1 FY26 while China Diagnostics revenue fell as much as 9% in Q2 FY26 on volume-based procurement pricing cuts
7.
Investment style implication. A quality-compounder franchise (photon-counting CT monopoly, helium-free DryCool MRI, Value Partnership contract backlog of EUR 6bn) currently overlaid with an event-driven catalyst set: the Diagnostics carve-out and the Siemens AG parent spin-off vote due early 2027
