EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 29 findings

Materiality of CHANGE

1.
Structural pivot toward asset-light expansion: Jumbo executed a landmark franchise agreement with BALFIN Group in May 2026 to expand into six Eurasian markets (Ukraine, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan) with BALFIN independently managing logistics from a China-based hub, fundamentally shifting the capital allocation model away from owned-store expansion
2.
Aggressive asset-backing of core network: management invested approximately EUR 75 million since 2021 to acquire ownership of nine previously leased hyper-stores, raising the owned property ratio to 70% of the physical network and insulating operating margins from inflation-linked lease escalations that pressure European discount retail peers
3.
Board restructuring and remuneration realignment: the July 2025 annual general meeting elected a refreshed thirteen-member board for a two-year term, while proposed 2026 remuneration changes fully align executive pay through profit distribution mechanisms, though 30.38% shareholder dissent at the July 2026 AGM signals growing institutional scrutiny of executive compensation policies
4.
Romanian market headwinds intensify: Romania's VAT increase to 21% in August 2025 compressed regional gross margins by nearly 10% as Jumbo initially absorbed the tax burden, while local currency depreciation against the Euro and a technical recession in early 2026 drove H1 2026 Romanian sales down 7% year-on-year versus stable growth across Bulgarian and Greek operations
5.
Supply chain structural disruption: prolonged Suez Canal shipping disruptions force cargo rerouting around Africa, doubling transit times to 60 days and inflating freight costs, while management's 70% Asian sourcing concentration exposes operations to severe shipping bottleneck disruptions absent among domestically sourced European discount peers
