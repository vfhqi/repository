EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Addtech reported its interim results for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026/27 on 22 October 2026, covering the three months ended 30 September 2026. The earnings release demonstrated remarkable operational resilience despite persistent macroeconomic headwinds across European manufacturing sectors. The company delivered net sales of SEK 5,907 million in the second quarter, matching consensus expectations and representing an increase of 8.39 percent year-over-year.

This performance confirmed that Addtech's decentralized technical solutions model continues to generate sustainable compounding returns through a balanced combination of organic expansion, strategic acquisitions, and structural margin improvement.

Addtech generated organic sales growth of 2% during the second quarter, recovering from flat performance in the first quarter despite tough comparative figures in both Energy and Sawmill businesses, meeting analyst expectations for soft short-term organic volumes. While this modest organic progression initially appears underwhelming, it represents a critical inflection point following three consecutive years of below-trend performance. Prior to the Q2 release, DNB Carnegie anticipated organic revenue growth acceleration in the second half supported by the 33/66 weighted Manufacturing PMI index crossing the 53 threshold, which historically signals a cyclical uptick in organic volume. The sequential improvement from zero to positive organic growth validates management's thesis that the prolonged industrial destocking cycle has exhausted itself. The most significant positive surprise emerged in operating profitability rather than top-line sales.

Addtech recorded EBITA of SEK 987 million in the second quarter, representing a 16.94 percent increase over the prior year, with the reported EBITA margin expanding by 120 basis points to 16.7% compared to 15.5%, indicating resilient operating profitability across core segments like Automation and Electrification. This exceptional margin expansion occurred despite flat organic volume growth, demonstrating the structural pricing power embedded within the group's niche technical trading franchises.

Operating profit was boosted by a net SEK 55 million positive effect in the Safety segment arising from earn-out revaluations and restructuring, and excluding this nonrecurring item, EBITA grew approximately 10 percent year-over-year during the quarter. Management successfully leveraged strategic price increases and favorable product mix shifts to offset localized cost inflation, validating their ability to protect shareholder returns through cyclical downturns.

Divisional performance revealed stark divergence between public infrastructure-exposed businesses and private industrial segments. Highly robust demand within power transmission and distribution segments continues to drive growth for Addtech's Energy segment as Nordic grid operators increase capital expenditures to support the transition to renewable energy sources, providing a highly resilient public customer base largely unaffected by broader manufacturing downturns.

Robust spending momentum is also evident in defense applications and specialized data center installations, with Addtech's Safety segment subsidiaries collaborating closely to capture the unprecedented defense spending uptick across the Nordic region, helping offset structural volume declines in weaker industrial niches. Conversely, sluggish demand conditions persist within construction, building, and installation sectors, with high interest rates discouraging private developers from initiating new projects, leading to subdued volumes for safety subsidiaries exposed to these markets and remaining a major drag on group organic growth.

Capital allocation execution remained highly disciplined throughout the reporting period. Addtech sustained excellent capital efficiency as its return on working capital rose to 82% for the rolling twelve months, representing a step-up of 500 basis points from the 77% achieved in the prior year's period, supported by strategic pricing and inventory reductions. Addtech maintained net interest-bearing debt of SEK 5,044 million at the end of the second quarter with leverage ratio at 1.4x net debt to EBITDA, well below the company's internal target of net debt to EBITDA below 2.0x, ensuring substantial headroom for ongoing acquisition activities.

This conservative financial positioning provides exceptional flexibility to accelerate the European M&A pipeline without requiring equity dilution or excessive leverage.

The international acquisition strategy delivered tangible progress during the period under review. Acquired businesses contributed 5 percent growth to net sales during the second quarter, with active execution of the continental European M&A pipeline supporting the acquired sales growth and matching management's strategic focus on expanding the business footprint internationally. Management highlighted a favorable acquisition market and well-filled transaction pipeline across Europe, successfully completing four acquisitions during the fiscal year adding SEK 500 million in combined turnover, with acquired companies continuing to broaden the international business footprint and enhance structural margins. The strategic shift toward larger, higher-quality continental targets represents a material positive mix change that should sustain returns on capital above historical averages.
