Business overview – global leader: Headquartered in Reggiolo, Italy, Comer Industries is a global mechatronics leader specializing in power transmission systems. The company designs and manufactures planetary drives, gearboxes, and e-mobility solutions. Its key products serve agricultural, construction, wind power, and electric vehicle sectors. Listing on Euronext Milan has elevated its corporate profile. Currently, the group operates 15 production sites globally with approximately 4,000 employees
