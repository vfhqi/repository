Assa Abloy (ASSA.B-SE) — what the third-quarter 2026 results do to the existing case

These results strengthen the existing case moderately on its central tension, organic volume, while the share-price move since has removed the small valuation support the case had.

1. What changed

The case assumed organic volume near zero (the case's figures: 0% in Q1 2026, +3% cumulative since 2019), a 10% growth algorithm running on price and acquisitions, and a margin held inside a 16-17% corridor by savings racing acquisition dilution.

What the company reported on 27 October 2026, for the three months to 30 September 2026:

  • Organic growth 4%, split 2% price and 2% volume, as management stated on the call. Deutsche Bank had expected 3.5%, with 1% volume and 2.5% price. Bernstein dates the first volume growth above 1% to Q2 2026. That is two consecutive quarters of positive volume.
  • Adjusted operating margin 17.4%, a record, up 60 basis points year on year and above the corridor; 17.3% excluding a one-off US tariff refund of about USD 5 M. Operating income SEK 7,102 M, 2% ahead of the Visible Alpha consensus of SEK 6,944 M. Drop-through 47%.
  • Electromechanical organic growth 9%; direct-material price-to-cost a positive 60 basis points. Neither of the case's two early-warning signals fired.
  • EPS SEK 4.24, up 14%; cash conversion 117%; net debt to EBITDA 2.0 times.
  • The weak spots: Entrance Systems margin 17.0% against a 17.3% consensus, with 70 basis points of dilution from Sentinel and Gunnebo; Asia Pacific organic growth of minus 1%; US residential starts with no sustained recovery.
  • Ahead: a restructuring charge of SEK 1.3 bn in Q4 2026 for the eleventh footprint programme, with savings Redburn puts at SEK 800 M to SEK 850 M a year within two years. The case carried this programme as already launched and worth SEK 1bn annualised; the review's figure is smaller and later.
  • Sell-side response, split. Redburn kept Buy and SEK 500, raised FY26 adjusted EBIT 1%, but cut FY27 sales growth 3.5%. DNB Carnegie raised its target to SEK 440 on 28 October, then cut to Hold and SEK 435 on 2 November on currency, with FY27 and FY28 adjusted EBIT down 1.9%. The review's source list also shows a Morgan Stanley move to Underweight dated 12 November 2026, but carries none of its argument.

One conflict inside the evidence we cannot resolve: management's 2% volume sits beside DNB Carnegie's statement, a day later, that organic volume growth "remains absent".

2. The challenge

Reading one: the false-friend pattern is ending, and the case is too cautious. The case treats a volume inflection as something that nudges one guardrail. The stronger reading is that it dissolves the central tension. If 2% volume is real, the 47% drop-through is operating leverage on units, not two offsetting forces in a race, and the margin is earned rather than defended. It also reopens a setup the case ruled out early: the sell-side under-modelling an upgrade. Deutsche Bank expected half the volume that arrived, and consensus EPS has been revised up over one, three and six months. For this to be right, three things must hold: the 2% is unit volume and not electromechanical mix counted as volume (premium products carry prices 40% to 70% higher, and the company has not said where mix sits); it persists into Q4; and it is not confined to one division. Redburn's note that the residential hardware business has returned to volume growth supports breadth. DNB Carnegie's "absent" contradicts it.

Reading two: the trigger fired for the wrong reason. The case's best-fitting setup is a quality compounder clearing a friction patch, and the friction is residential. But the volume came from the segment the case already called robust: Americas organic growth of 6% and EMEIA 5%, both led by non-residential work. Residential has not turned. Meanwhile architecture billings are in contraction, and channel contacts reported in late September that customers were deferring non-essential hardware upgrades. On this reading the two positive quarters are commercial demand running late-cycle hot, not the depressed third recovering, and the case would be upgrading its central risk just as the strong leg peaks. It is right if non-residential growth fades through 2027 while residential stays flat. Against it: specification-led orders grew at a high-single-digit rate, and management reported the same demand pattern in October.

Reading three: the margin is at a ceiling the company itself sets, and the price has capitalised it. The case credits margin congruency as the dependable half of delivery. At 17.4% the margin is above a corridor management has declined to raise, and its stated preference is to reinvest excess into growth rather than run above it; the tariff refund went into research and development. Entrance Systems shows dilution still arriving. So further margin beats are unlikely by design, and the review's own conclusion calls 17.4% a probable cyclical peak. The case never had to weigh this, because it was written with the multiple moderately low. That has reversed (see below). It is right if Q4 adjusted margin falls back toward 16.8% or lower.

A fourth, smaller point: the algorithm is shrinking as it rebalances. Organic is up, but acquisitions added only about 2% in Q3 and are guided to 2% in Q4, against the case's 5% acquired component. Redburn pushed unannounced deals out twelve months. The group's ambition moved from over SEK 220 bn by 2028 to over SEK 250 bn by 2030; from FY25 sales of SEK 152,409 M that is arithmetic for roughly 10% a year instead of roughly 13%. A healthier mix, a lower total.

3. What it does to the case

  • E8.RA8, fit with false-friend setups : less pressure. The case's stated trigger, two consecutive quarters of positive organic volume even at 1-2%, is met on the review's evidence, subject to the conflict above. E8.RA4, the primary setup, is under upward pressure for the same reason, unless reading two is right.
  • E8.RA1, mis-modelled EPS upgrader : less pressure. The sell-side was low on volume.
  • E1.RA2, robust base : mixed, net more pressure. Growth congruency improves on volume. The valuation leg worsens: the case had the multiple at 18.6x and the 32nd percentile of its own history; the price is now SEK 395.7 (13 November 2026), 12.7% above the 200-day average, within about 1% of the 52-week high, on 20.5x at the 77th percentile. The price has crossed above the SEK 365 median target the case measured against. The consensus target is now SEK 416, about 5% above the price, so the case's thin-mispricing finding stands on a dearer stock.
  • E1.RA1, change forces present : same to slightly stronger on electromechanical growth; the smaller, later footprint savings pull the other way.
  • E7, case simplicity : same. Another restructuring programme and more acquisitions to integrate.
  • E11.RA3, disruption forces : same. Nothing in the quarter tests the channel threat.
  • MR2, sell-side earnings momentum and MR1, technical momentum : the pinned letters now read A and D respectively. The memo's C on MR2 was set qualitatively, so part of that gap is not these results.

4. What would settle it

  • The second review, four working days on. It is likely to carry the completed round of broker estimate and target changes, and it should carry the substance of the Morgan Stanley Underweight move, which matters because it is the one broker arguing structural deterioration after a good quarter. It will not carry any new company fact: nothing has been reported since 27 October. It cannot settle readings one to three.
  • The Q4 2026 report, expected early in 2027 (the review gives no date). Volume for a third quarter settles the case's trigger. Adjusted margin excluding the SEK 1.3 bn charge, against 16.8%, settles reading three. The review's own tripwire is 16.5%.
  • Residential volume in the Americas would separate reading one from reading two. The company gives this in commentary, not as a committed disclosure.
  • Where mix sits in the price and volume split. The company has not committed to disclose this, and the quarterly split itself is given verbally. This is a question that nothing scheduled answers.
