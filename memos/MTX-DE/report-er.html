Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. MTU Aero Engines occupies a critical niche as a Tier 1 engine module specialist and the world’s leading independent engine maintenance provider for commercial and military aviation.
  2. The company’s share price underperformed the broader aerospace sector in the months leading up to the Q1 results as investors weighed the long-term liability of the GTF recall. MTU Aero Engines operates as a globally recognized expert in commercial and military aircraft engines, specializing in the development, production, and maintenance of hightech modules. The company maintains a balanced product portfolio that covers all thrust categories, with technology present in one in three commercial aircraft flying globally. MTU's history includes founding as BMW Flugmotorenbau in 1934 and going public in 2005 after being an affiliate of DaimlerChrysler Aerospace. Adjusted revenues for the full year 2025 reached €8.7 bn, demonstrating the scale of the company’s operations

across its two primary segments. The maintenance segment accounts for roughly 67% of adjusted revenue, servicing over 30 engine types and maintaining around 1,500 engines annually. Original Equipment operations typically involve risk and revenue sharing partnerships with major manufacturers like Pratt & Whitney and GE Aerospace.
