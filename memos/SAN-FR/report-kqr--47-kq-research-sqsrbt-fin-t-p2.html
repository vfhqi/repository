Medicare Part D and Formulations Grouping

This regulatory shift means that secondary formulation launches can no longer delay price negotiations by resetting the clinical exclusivity clock. Consequently, biopharma developers face accelerated timelines for government-mandated discounting.

Sanofi's regulatory filings indicate that Dupixent is not expected to be impacted by the Inflation Reduction Act negotiations until 2031.
