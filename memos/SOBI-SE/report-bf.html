Plausible problems to monitor

1.
Below-peer reported returns: Sobi's reported ROCE fell to 0.7% in 2025, placing it in the bottom quartile versus specialty pharma peers like Novartis (12% ROIC) and Sanofi (10% ROIC), driven by heavy intangible amortization expense of SEK 3,338 million from historical purchase price allocations [Cross-ref: Section 2].
2.
Outsourced manufacturing vulnerability: Sobi's reliance on single API suppliers takes several years to replace due to strict regulatory requirements, and the FDA issued a Complete Response Letter in June 2026 for NASP due to manufacturing deficiencies triggered by manufacturing control strategy deficiencies at CMO facilities, exposing severe regulatory execution risk versus vertically integrated peers that maintain direct manufacturing control [Cross-ref: Section 6].
3.
Concentrated customer dependency: Sobi's largest customer accounts for approximately 17 per cent of revenue across all three core therapeutic segments, while the second-largest customer generates 16 per cent of group sales, creating structural revenue volatility risk where top-two buyers command 33% of sales versus the specialty pharma median of ~20% customer concentration [Cross-ref: Section 5].
4.
Rising gross-to-net pressure: American commercial payers have become more restrictive in rare disease coverage, enforcing stringent patient diagnostic criteria to manage costs, and US commercial contracts require Sobi to offer significant gross-to-net discount adjustments to secure positive formulary positions, indicating accelerating rebate headwinds that threaten Vonjo's commercial profitability in a highly competitive myelofibrosis market [Cross-ref: Section 5].
5.
Oral competitor administration advantage: Subcutaneous pegcetacoplan infusion requires extensive patient and provider training, creating significant behavioral switching inertia versus oral options, and Novartis' Fabhalta presents a convenient oral alternative for C3G which some patients may clinically prefer, with Fabhalta offering convenient daily dosing that presents a major compliance headwind, positioning Aspaveli with structural administration friction despite superior 68% proteinuria reduction versus Fabhalta's 35%, as daily oral convenience may override efficacy benefits in real-world nephrologist prescribing [Cross-ref: Section 5, Section 7, Section 8].

Outliers worth noting

1.
Extreme vendor financing leverage: Sobi operates with 644.0 days payable outstanding in 2025, representing a top-5% working capital position versus large pharma peers like Novartis (80 NWC days) and Sanofi (90 NWC days), yet management provides minimal disclosure on partnership-specific payment terms that enable this structural advantage [Cross-ref: Section 8]. Exceptional Altuvoct market penetration velocity: Sobi captured 6 percentage points of German hemophilia A market share within 2.5 months of late-2024 launch, expanding to 57% by early 2025, representing top-5% launch acceleration versus typical 12-18 month specialty therapy adoption curves, while management attributes success solely to clinical superiority without quantifying promotional spend intensity or physician incentive structures [Cross-ref: Section 7].
