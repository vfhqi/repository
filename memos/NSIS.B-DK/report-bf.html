EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Business Model Strength & Post-Merger Execution Novonesis operates a highly diversified biosolutions model across Food & Health and Planetary Health divisions, shielding the business from lumpy single-market cycles through value-based pricing and high-intimacy cocreation that drives industry-leading customer retention above commodity-grade peers, while scale advantages stem from a centralized, highly flexible global fermentation footprint that decouples incremental volume growth from operational cost inflation.

Post-merger execution has exceeded targets with cost synergies realized ahead of schedule, validating the ambitious 2030 GROW strategy targets, while leadership under CEO Ester Baiget and CFO Rainer Lehmann exhibits strong execution capabilities backed by rigid, performance-linked incentive frameworks. The January 2024 merger created a biological powerhouse with zero execution hiccups, achieving the post-merger cost synergy target of €80 million to €90 million ahead of schedule by late 2025, while sales synergies contributed 1 percentage point to organic growth in 2025, tracking toward €200 million by 2027.

The €1.5 billion acquisition of DSM-Firmenich's Feed Enzyme Alliance closed in June 2025, adding direct distribution, while the 2030 GROW strategy targets 6% to 9% organic sales CAGR with approximately 39% adjusted EBITDA margin and approximately 16% adjusted ROIC excluding goodwill.

Adjusted gross profit margins reached an exceptional 59.1% in 2025, representing a 240 basis point expansion driven by economies of scale, productivity improvements, synergy capture, and falling energy and agricultural raw material costs, while adjusted EBITDA margins improved to 37.1% for the full year 2025.

Capital expenditures scaled to €470.8 million, or 11.3% of sales in 2025, and are guided to rise to 12-14% of sales in 2026, though management targets reducing capex back to high-single-digit of sales by 2030.
