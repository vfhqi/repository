Section summary

Historical Swiss Franc Translation Volatility

1.
Severe headwinds: Nestlé has historically faced substantial revenue translation drags due to the multi-year appreciation of the Swiss franc against major global currencies. The company reported negative currency impacts on sales of -7.8% in 2023, -3.7% in 2024, and -5.7% in 2025, which continuously depressed reported growth below organic performance. Under these volatile foreign exchange conditions, underlying earnings per share experienced significant translation pressure. This structural relationship varies depending on the specific country mix and currency basket behavior during the fiscal period. Consequently, currency-driven margin dilution directly impacts the net earnings drop-through for investors.

Persistent transaction exposure: Nestlé manages its foreign exchange transaction risks through systematic and centralized derivative hedging programs across its global operating units. The group reported realized exchange differences in the consolidated income statement representing a loss of Swiss franc 250 million in 2025 compared to a loss of Swiss franc 198 million in 2024. Unhedged translation exposures are consolidated into the cumulative translation reserve within equity.

The company experienced a negative currency translation effect on sales of -6.2% in the first half of 2026, which subsequently eased to a negative -3.7% impact for the nine-month period. This shifting trend highlights how short-term currency fluctuations dictate reported revenue trajectories.
