EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

The bear case against Sunrise Communications exhibits moderate-to-high credibility, anchored in hard filingbased evidence of persistent revenue contraction rather than speculative narratives. Activist opposition to the 2019 UPC acquisition exposed fundamental asset overvaluation concerns, though Liberty Global's subsequent takeover at a premium validated the strategic value of the standalone business. Current operational data reveals B2C fixed subscription revenues declined by 8.7% in Q2 2026 and underlying mobile ARPU contracted by 3.1%, establishing clear patterns of structural segment decline that cannot be dismissed as transient. & Co Redburn The Analysis of Competing Hypotheses framework identifies a Hold-and-Watch posture as the optimal investment progression stance, with the aggregated pre-mortem failure probability assessed as high due to persistent structural ARPU dilution.

The winning pre-mortem hypothesis profile is the FALSE FRIEND scenario, where short-term positive volume signals and an attractive untaxed dividend yield mask terminal revenue degradation and leverage expansion. The major uncertainty remains consumer ARPU response following tariff increases implemented on August 1, 2026, representing the critical data gap that must close before advancing to a Proceed verdict. & Co Redburn High-plausibility failure modes cluster around three systemic transmission channels.

Revenue failure modes center on sustained B2C fixed subscription contraction of 8.7% and wireless ARPU erosion, which narrow the path to EBITDAaL growth and risk eventual debt leverage expansion. Margin failure modes involve customer migration from owned HFC networks to wholesaled fiber, introducing systemic long-term margin pressure as rental costs exceed owned cable infrastructure economics. The most dangerous false-friend trap is the untaxed 8.3% dividend yield masking elevated 4.7x net leverage, where tax-free capital reserves of CHF 2.6 billion permit sustained distributions but hide structural cash flow deterioration beneath accounting-driven dividend coverage metrics. & Co Redburn Risk vectors exhibit high correlation and compounding effects rather than natural offsets, elevating joint failure probability beyond what independent scenario analysis would suggest.

The HFC-to-fiber subscriber migration directly connects network rental costs to declining owned cable utility margins, while high balance sheet leverage of 4.7x couples with upcoming spectrum auction liabilities to threaten medium-term dividend sustainability. Commercial price clustering sees aggressive competitor promotional campaigns correlate with structural discount brand growth that dilutes high-margin subscription ARPU.

The joint probability of operational failure is compounded by high leverage if top-line contraction continues to jeopardize untaxed dividend payouts, with underlying wireless subscription revenue representing the most critical monitorable metric that excludes favorable convergence adjustments to highlight true operational health. & Co Redburn Currency exposure represents a net neutral factor over the 18-to-36-month investment horizon due to comprehensive balance sheet hedging overlays that fully insulate earnings from G10 exchange volatility.

All operational revenue is generated in domestic Swiss francs, eliminating passive top-line translation risks, while post-hedged currency sensitivity remains effectively 0% under normal market swap environments. However, the most severe currency-related pre-mortem trigger is the risk of hedging counterparty default during extreme credit freezes, where swap termination costs would deplete capital reserves and expose the unhedged foreign debt portfolio to massive profit volatility. Plausible problems to monitor and statistical outliers reveal structural underperformance relative to regional competitive benchmarks across multiple operational dimensions. Stabilizing customer inflows and implemented price hikes may successfully offset segment revenue declines before refinancing hurdles occur, while capital reserves of CHF 2.58 billion fully secure untaxed dividends for Swiss private investors until 2033. However, these positive factors remain contingent on flawless execution in a highly promotional competitive environment where Swisscom and Salt maintain aggressive pricing umbrellas that constrain Sunrise's pass-through pricing power and structural ability to recapture lost ARPU momentum.
