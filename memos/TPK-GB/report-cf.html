EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Travis Perkins has experienced prolonged governance instability through three CEO transitions in under 18 months, culminating in the appointment of Gavin Slark in January 2026, who brings proven distribution sector expertise from prior leadership roles at SIG plc, Grafton Group, and BSS Group. Operationally, the business suffered severe branch management churn, including 100% depot manager turnover in one northern region over 12 months, driven by ERP implementation friction and flawed incentive structures.

The financial trajectory reveals severe operating margin compression from 7.7% in 2019 to 3.0% in H1 2026, though recent performance shows encouraging signs with 100 basis points of gross margin expansion year-on-year to 27.4% in H1 2026.
