Group judgement

External change forces

Layer 1 · External change forces

Written last by the Investment Analyst for its group. The rating above stands for it, so it carries no number of its own.

The external environment gives Coloplast an unusually predictable top line and an unusually hostile margin line. Demand is structural and visible for years; the only demand problems are company-specific attachments (China, Kerecis outpatient, European dressings), and those are now small or annualising. Against that, every external force acting on price and cost is negative or neutral: payers in Europe and the US, input and wage inflation, and a re-energised competitor whose effect is to raise Coloplast's required commercial spend. Currency, the largest macro swing factor, turns from headwind to tailwind for FY27, which flatters reported FY27 growth and hides weak constant-currency profit growth. For the model: revenue drivers carry low uncertainty; margin drivers carry moderate, mostly one-sided (downward) uncertainty, with dated regulatory steps.
