Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Understanding Teqnion's fourth-quarter margin weakness is vital for evaluating its earnings stability and long-term valuation relative to peers.
  2. This report analyzes seasonality, historical quarterly performance, and structural drivers across five Nordic serial acquirers using over one hundred sources.

Defining the

1.
Key Question Investigating performance: "Teqnion AB (TEQ-SE), a Swedish decentralised serial acquirer and perpetual owner of about 40 niche industrial, product and service businesses, listed on Nasdaq First North, reporting two business areas from 2026: Teqnion Nord (Sweden and the Nordics: industrial components, hydraulics, compressors, contract manufacturing, construction-linked products, headstones, NFC wristbands) and Teqnion Väst (United Kingdom and Ireland: vending, healthcare decontamination validation, compressed-air systems and service, composite doors, fasteners). What drove the weak fourth-quarter margins at Teqnion AB (TEQ-SE) in 2024 and 2025 (including year-end inventory adjustments, write-downs, losses at Hem1 and Reward Catering, bonus accruals and project losses), how did Teqnion's quarterly margins and cash flow behave by quarter from 2019 to 2023, and what do the company's statements and those of Nordic peers with similar portfolios say about the seasonality of fourth-quarter profit and cash flow?"
2.
Severe pressure: Decreased margins in the fourth quarter directly depress Teqnion's earnings per share and disrupt its core compounding strategy. The company reported a weak 6.3% EBITA margin in the fourth quarter of 2024, which led to a 26% drop in annual earnings per share. Resolving whether these dips are temporary seasonal effects or chronic structural failures determines if Teqnion can meet its long-term financial target to double earnings per share every five years.
3.
Depressed multiples: Persistent weak profitability during fourth quarters raises the risk premium that investors apply to Teqnion's stock and lowers its valuation. If margin volatility is structural, investors may discount the stock relative to proven peers such as Addtech or Lifco. Understanding the drivers of these write-downs, such as the goodwill impairments of 64.3 million Swedish kronor at Reward and Hem1, helps size the risk of future capital allocation mistakes.
4.
Stable performance: Historically, Teqnion's financial performance showed solid margins and stable profitability metrics prior to the macro headwinds of 2024. From 2019 to 2023, Teqnion maintained an EBITA margin above its nine percent target each year, peaking at 12.2% in 2019. This historical stability indicates that the recent extreme margin drops represent an operational anomaly rather than a permanent structural feature.
