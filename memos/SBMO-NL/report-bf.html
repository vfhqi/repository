The highly specialized asset-light strategy avoids full shipyard integration risk, partnering instead with Chinese and Singaporean yards for hull construction while retaining high-margin long-term operations and maintenance contracts that generate 54% EBITDA margins in the Lease & Operate segment versus 20% in Turnkey engineering.
