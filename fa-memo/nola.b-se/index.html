The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. 2026: nine months actual (7,311) plus Q4 of 2,545 (three months of acquisitions, about SEK 160m; no Romania; seasonal weakness). 2027 (+8.5%): acquisitions' extra eight months +4.3 points; Romania -0.5; organic +5.8 (Hungarian GLP-1 programme +2.3, other Medical about +2.2, Materials +1.5, rest of Engineered Solutions 0, price -0.3); currency -1.0. 2028 (+5.6%), 2029 (+4.6%), 2030 (+3.8%): all organic; the Hungarian programme reaches about SEK 680-700m by 2029 (company: about SEK 700m at full capacity; ABG: full capacity Q4 2027; former CEO: gradual to 2029), Materials growth slows from 15% to 10%, Medical price-downs of about 0.5-0.7% a year from 2028. No unannounced acquisitions; no currency effect after 2027.
  • EBITDA margin. Adjusted EBITA plus depreciation excluding acquired amortisation (the brokers' basis: 17.0% in 2025). The 2026 margin (16.0%) carries about SEK 60m of transient drags (raw-material lag about SEK 30m in Q2-Q3, start-up costs in Hungary and the US) and a structural rise in central costs (about SEK 25m to 60m). 2027 (+0.7 points): about half from reversing the lag and acquisitions, half from Hungarian and Materials volume at 25-30% incremental margins, less price-downs, wage inflation and currency. 2028-2030: Medical margin to 12.5-12.8%, Materials mix lifting Engineered Solutions; gains slow to about 0.1-0.2 points a year as price-downs at renewals and new-site start-ups offset volume leverage.
  • Quarterly phasing (Part 3). Growth is high to Q2 2027 because the acquisitions were consolidated from 1 September 2026; it falls to organic rates (about 5-6%) from Q4 2027. EBITDA margin follows Nolato's seasonality (Q4 weakest because of holiday shutdowns; Q2-Q3 strongest) with an underlying rise of about 0.4 points a year.
  • Depreciation and amortisation excludes acquired amortisation (about SEK 45m in 2026, 56m in 2027-2028, 50-55m later). It rises with the Hungarian lines and the acquisitions (DNB: tangible depreciation SEK 470m in 2026, 501m in 2027) but more slowly than revenue as capital spending settles.
  • Net financial items. Q1-Q2 2026 were small (currency gains), Q3 was -18; about -22 a quarter from Q4 2026 on net financial debt of about SEK 1.3-1.5bn including leases and pensions; falling as debt is repaid. No new acquisitions assumed.
  • Tax rate. 22.8% for nine months 2026 (19.0% a year earlier); 22.5% from 2027. Pillar Two is immaterial (SEK 1m).
  • Dividend. About 60% of reported profit after tax (policy at least 50%; 59-63% in 2024-2025).
  • Capital spending. Guidance SEK 600-650m for 2026 (from 788 in 2025); about 5.6% of revenue thereafter, rising in money terms as Poland and Malaysia are filled and new programmes are won (consensus 603, 663, 709 for 2027-2029).
  • Working capital.
