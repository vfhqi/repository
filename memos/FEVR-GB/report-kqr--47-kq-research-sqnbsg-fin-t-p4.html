Section summary
  1. The transition of Fevertree's US business to a licensing model with Molson Coors is a key structural shift that fundamentally reshapes the company's profit and risk profile.

Section 2 – US Royalty Agreement and Profit-Share Calculation

Section summary
  1. The long-term licensing agreement with Molson Coors transforms Fevertree's US business into a highly protected, capital-light model.
  2. Financial guarantees from the licensee insulate Fevertree from operational downside while driving the majority of mid-term group profit expansion.

Partnership Structure and Profit Sharing

1.
Transitioning operations: Fevertree Drinks PLC restructured its US operations by disposing of Fevertree USA Inc. to Molson Coors on 30 January 2025. Under the exclusive license effective from 1 February 2025, Molson Coors assumed full commercial responsibility including sales, distribution, and local production. This shift transfers the US partnership P&L onto the licensee's books, meaning Fevertree now extracts its share of profits via royalty fees.
2.
Sharing economics: Fevertree Drinks PLC and Molson Coors share US profits based on a contractually agreed formula. Under this structural arrangement, both parties receive a broadly equal share of profits generated by the US partnership. The brand owner's statutory reported US revenue now reflects royalty income plus UK-produced goods sold at cost, rather than direct consumer sales.
3.
Protecting downside: Molson Coors agreed to guarantee an absolute level of royalty fees to Fevertree Drinks PLC from 2026 to 2030. This contractual guarantee reduces operational volatility and materially de-risks the US profit trajectory over the medium term. Consequently, Fevertree is insulated from near-term execution failures or weak volume growth in the highly competitive premium mixer segment.
