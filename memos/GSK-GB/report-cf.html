EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

MATERIALITY OF CHANGE

GSK has transitioned to a pure-play biopharma model under new Chief Executive Officer Luke Miels, consolidating commercial operations into five core therapeutic areas while accelerating margin expansion toward 31% through structural portfolio optimization and the elimination of consumer health operations. Management upgraded its 2031 long-term group revenue target to exceed £40 billion, with Specialty Medicines projected to represent more than 50% of total group sales.

The ViiV Healthcare shareholding restructuring eliminated Pfizer's put-option liability, freeing £1 billion of balance sheet capacity and streamlining minority interest obligations after Shionogi acquired Pfizer's 11.7% stake for $2.125 billion. The newly announced Accelerate Growth cost-savings program targets £1.9 billion in annual savings by 2029, requiring £2.4 billion in total restructuring costs including £2.1 billion in cash to fund late-stage pipeline acceleration and protect operating margins during the approaching dolutegravir loss of exclusivity.

1.
KEY DRIVERS (RANKED) #1 HIV Long-Acting Transition (35% Impact | HIGH Plausibility): GSK transitions from daily oral therapies to long-acting injectables including Cabenuva and Apretude, which demonstrate highly resilient and sticky patient demand with bi-monthly dosing offering superior adherence and margin economics.
2.
#2 Dolutegravir Exclusivity Cliff (-50% Impact | HIGH Plausibility): Legacy oral HIV tablets face severe generic competition as dolutegravir's primary US composition patents expire in April 2028, threatening approximately 50% of ViiV's total sales and requiring rapid lifecycle migration to next-generation therapies.
3.
#3 Accelerate Growth Cost Program (15% Impact | HIGH Plausibility): The three-year restructuring initiative delivers £1.9 billion in annualized cost savings by 2029 to safeguard core operating margins near 31% during patent cliffs, with Jefferies modeling substantial drop-through to profitability to offset royalty payaway and tax headwind drags.
4.
#4 Depemokimab Respiratory Launch (10% Impact | HIGH Plausibility): Investigational twice-yearly biologic depemokimab targets severe asthma to disrupt the traditional monthly treatment paradigm, with management projecting the combined IL-5 portfolio will deliver over £4 billion in peak year sales after demonstrating a 58% reduction in annualized asthma exacerbations in SWIFT trials.
5.
#5 Precision Oncology Pipeline (15% Impact | MEDIUM Plausibility): Strategic acquisitions of IDRx and Nuvalent add high-duration, highly profitable assets to the oncology franchise, with precision KIT inhibitor velzatinib targeting Exon 11 and Exon 9 mutations to replace standard first-line therapies, representing over £6 billion in peak sales opportunity according to Jefferies.
6.
#6 Bepirovirsen Hepatology Launch (5% Impact | MEDIUM Plausibility): Antisense oligonucleotide bepirovirsen targets a potential functional cure in chronic hepatitis B patients, though expert physicians highlight that weekly liver monitoring requirements represent a logistical adoption challenge for clinic networks, with Mount Sinai specialists projecting modest 5% penetration within two years of launch.
