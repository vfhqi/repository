Section 3 – External Change: Value-Chain Forces (Demand, Competition, Disruption, Regulation, Supply, Cycle, Macro) 30 findings

Section summary
  1. value-chain forces present severe pricing and competitive headwinds as the US obesity market transitions toward low-cost oral formats and government-reimbursed channels

Demand

1.
Surging global TAM: peak global obesity addressable market is projected to reach USD 190 billion in the base case vs USD 100 billion sector forecasts
2.
Unprecedented Medicare enrollment: the new federal USD 50 monthly program enrolled 600,000 seniors in its first two months, validating massive latent demand
3.
Lagging volume capture: branded semaglutide weekly prescriptions have remained relatively flat following the Medicare Bridge launch, underperforming initial bull projections
4.
Medicaid budget pushback: reduced Medicaid coverage across multiple US states represents growing headwind for low-income patient access, capping near-term volume expansion

Competition

5.
Losing market leadership: Eli Lilly holds a dominant 61.5% US obesity weekly prescription market share, compared to Novo Nordisk's lower 38.5% share
6.
Ramping oral competition: Lilly launched Foundayo (orforglipron) in April 2026, generating USD 98 million in sales in its first partial quarter
7.
Pervasive compounding supply: mass compounding continues, with an estimated 1.5 million US patients utilizing non-FDA-approved semaglutide copies, impacting branded revenues
8.
Stricter active ingredient limits: the FDA announced intent to restrict compounded active pharmaceutical ingredients, which could force patients back to branded alternatives

Disruption

9.
Rapid consumerization pivot: direct-to-patient digital health platforms are resetting customer acquisition models by lowering the traditional physician barriers to entry
10.
Accelerating oral transition: long-term forecasts suggest non-peptide oral formats could capture up to 70% of the obesity market due to patient convenience
11.
Disruptive gene alternatives: one-and-done pancreatic gene therapies like Fractyl's Rejuva show strong preclinical weight loss, posing a long-term threat to chronic daily regimens
12.
Clinical execution doubts: experts remain highly skeptical of gene therapies, citing irreversible long-term toxicities and a lack of robust human safety data

Regulation

13.
Aggressive pricing pressure: Maryland became the first US state to cap government employee health plan spending on Ozempic to control budgets
14.
Enforced list-price cuts: Novo will lower US list prices for Wegovy by approximately 50% to USD 675 starting January 2027 to expand access
15.
Government discount mandates: the federal Medicare Part D program negotiated a 71% list-price discount for semaglutide, establishing a USD 274 price ceiling
16.
Successful patent enforcement: Novo won a preliminary injunction against Ceban Ziekenhuisfarmacie to halt unauthorized semaglutide nasal spray compounding in the Netherlands

Supply

17.
Enlarged production footprint: Novo expanded its fill-finish network to 14 sites following the closing of the USD 11.7 billion Catalent acquisition
18.
Regulatory site hurdles: quality-related FDA inspections at acquired sites like Bloomington pose sustained operational risks to pipeline asset approvals
19.
Substantial clinical displacement: external companies are exiting Catalent facilities as Novo converts those sites entirely for its own proprietary biological needs
20.
Elevated capital intensity: ramping up global capacity requires DKK 55 billion in 2026 capex, compared to Eli Lilly's USD 18 billion expansion program
21.
Dedicated tabletting investments: Novo is investing €432 million in its Athlone facility to upgrade and retrofit capacity for the oral portfolio

Cycle

22.
Steep treatment discontinuation: real-world studies indicate over 50% of Wegovy patients stop therapy within one year due to gastrointestinal intolerance

Inevitable weight regain: discontinuing therapy leads patients to regain two-thirds of lost weight, creating a critical long-term commercial reliance on chronic compliance
24.
Early adopter plateau: branded weekly script data shows near-term flattening trends, implying the initial early-adopter wave is fully saturated

Ecosystem engagement focus: customer acquisition is shifting from clinical weight-loss data toward digital health ecosystems, including smart scales and continuous monitors

Macro

Eroding financial margins: Novo expects a net financial items loss of DKK 1.1 billion in FY26, driven by heavy interest expenses
