Business Profile and Sector Positioning

1.
Strategic overview: Systemair AB is a Sweden-based HVAC specialist founded in 1974 by Gerald Engström, operating across 51 countries with approximately 7,400 employees and SEK 12.5bn in FY2025/26 sales
2.
Product portfolio breadth: The business delivers ventilation products–including circular duct fans, air handling units (AHUs), air distribution devices, and air curtains–primarily under the Systemair, Frico, Fantech, and Menerga brands
3.
Decentralized operational setup: Operating through a highly decentralized network of ~90 legal entities and 26 production facilities in 18 countries, decision-making sits close to local contractors
4.
Regulatory and sustainability tailwinds: Sector demand is structurally anchored in EU energy efficiency mandates (e.g., EPBD) and growing indoor air quality (IAQ) requirements across commercial and public buildings
5.
End-market tilt: The group maintains a resilient commercial and industrial revenue focus (85-90% of sales) relative to cyclical residential exposure

Historical 3-Year Financial Performance vs Peers

Top-line revenue trajectory – stable expansion: Net sales grew from SEK 9,634m in FY2021/22 to SEK 12,503m in FY2025/26, reflecting a 10-year CAGR of 7.7% and FY2025/26 organic growth of 6.1%.
