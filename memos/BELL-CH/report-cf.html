EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bell Food Group maintains a stable leadership structure and delivers resilient earnings momentum through its concentrated four-pillar strategy focused on Swiss market leadership, European charcuterie expansion, sustainable DACH poultry growth, and convenience food development. The company achieved a record half-year profit of CHF 55.4 million in H1 2026, representing a 21.3 percent increase compared to the prior-year period, while expanding its consolidated EBITDA margin by 0.6 percentage points to 7.2 percent.

Management guides full-year 2026 operating profit to land between CHF 180 million and CHF 195 million, supported by disciplined capital expenditure reductions to CHF 300 million and the completion of major Swiss infrastructure modernization programs in Oensingen. However, the group faces structural vulnerabilities from elevated raw material cost sensitivity, persistent European pork market overcapacity, ongoing livestock supply constraints, and heavy reliance on Swiss agricultural protectionism that insulates domestic margins from low-cost European imports.

Bell Switzerland grew net revenue by 2.2 percent to CHF 1,202.9 million as domestic sales volumes expanded by 2.1 percent, while Hubers/Sütag poultry segment expanded net revenue by 5.8 percent to CHF 381.4 million with organic sales growth of 8.3 percent driven by strong turkey demand.

Unadjusted EBIT contracted 29.1 percent to CHF 118.3 million during the 2025 financial year due to severe restructuring charges at the Austrian Eisberg convenience plant and goodwill impairments, though adjusted EBIT actually rose by 2.8 percent to CHF 172 million.
