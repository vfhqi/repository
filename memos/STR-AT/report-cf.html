Section summary

STRABAG SE stands at an inflection point defined by exceptional operational momentum, robust infrastructure demand tailwinds, and strategic international diversification, yet faces persistent valuation headwinds from low share liquidity and concentrated shareholder restrictions. The company has delivered record financial performance across FY2025 and H1 2026, driven by a historic €36 billion order backlog that provides 1.6 years of revenue visibility and enables a highly selective bidding strategy focused on high-margin public infrastructure megaprojects.

Management's conservative sandbagging archetype consistently delivers substantial margin beats against initial guidance, with FY2025 EBIT margin reaching 6.7% versus the 4.5% guide. However, the operational excellence narrative is tempered by structural seasonal volatility, goodwill impairment risks from international acquisitions, and potential public infrastructure disbursement delays in core German markets.
