Section 16 – Conclusion

The single most important change from the latest earnings is the step-up in 2026 guidance, which was backed by delivery. LSEG raised its full-year 2026 organic constant currency revenue growth guidance to at least 8.0%, from 7.0% to 7.5% at the half-year. It also lifted its EBITDA margin expansion target to 100 to 120 basis points, from around 100. The quarter supported both moves.

Third-quarter revenue of £2,501m was 1.01% ahead of the £2,476m consensus. Organic constant currency total income growth was 8.80%. The equity free cash flow target rose to at least £2.8bn from £2.7bn, but remained slightly below consensus. The upgrade is real, but its composition matters more than its size.

The beat came mainly from cyclical and transaction-linked lines rather than from the desktop franchise the market worries about. Markets grew 12.5% organically. Net treasury income beat consensus by 9%, on higher cash collateral balances. Data and Analytics, meanwhile, grew 5.2%, flat on the second quarter and in line with expectations.
