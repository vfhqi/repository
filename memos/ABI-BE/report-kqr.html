Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

The Core Question and EPS Relevance

1.
Explicit formulation: This research directly addresses the following word-for-word Key Question: "What share of volume and of gross profit does alcohol-free beer (0.5% alcohol or less) make at AB InBev, Heineken and Carlsberg in their main European markets, what gross profit per hectolitre does it earn against the fullstrength version of the same brand and at what price gap, how fast is that share rising, and is any European Union or national tax change proposed that would remove its exemption from beer duty?"
2.
Critical EPS driver: Over the next 18 months to 3 years, the capacity of zero-alcohol offerings to deliver superior margins directly shapes group earnings per share projections for major European brewers.
3.
Profitable mix dynamics: The volume expansion of this segment serves as a vital tool to stabilize long-term category volumes while optimizing overall profitability.

Market Maturity and Regulatory Windows

4.
Early commercial window: The United Kingdom represents an early-stage volume growth runway where penetration has currently reached only about 3%
5.
Proven mature scale: In contrast, Spain's mature beer market demonstrates that zero-alcohol products can achieve a double-digit volume share of total beer consumption.
6.
Tax change risk: National or European tax adjustments pose a threat to current duty exemptions that would significantly compress the segment's high-margin investment window.
7.
Premiumization substitution dynamics: Premiumization continues to lead the portfolio, but zero-alcohol offerings provide differentiated product-mix substitution opportunities that defend consumer franchise participation.
8.
Other side of the story: Broad macro pressures and rising commercial promo rates in Europe could offset high unit profitability gains expected from the mix shift.
