EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 29 findings

Section summary
  1. Tesco demonstrates strong defensive characteristics through sector-leading margins, substantial cash generation, and progressive capital returns despite modest near-term volume headwinds and intensifying competitive pressure from discounters. Executive leadership transitions under Ken Murphy and the promotion of Ashwin Prasad to UK CEO position the company for structural margin expansion via high-margin adjacencies including Tesco Media and Finest premiumisation, while the banking divestment successfully de-risked the balance sheet and released £700 million for shareholder returns. Forward earnings visibility remains credible anchored by conservative guidance patterns, £500 million annual cost savings, and upgraded free cash flow targets of £1.5 billion to £2.0 billion, though regulatory headwinds including £235 million in National Insurance costs and market share erosion of 30 basis points pose material near-term risks to the 18-month to 3-year EPS trajectory.
