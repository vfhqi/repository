Executive Overview: European Food, Household, and Personal Care Sector De- Rating and Turnaround Dynamics (2021–Late 2026) European food, household, and personal care (HPC), and consumer health companies experienced a broad, multiyear de-rating from 2021 through mid-2026. This sector-wide contraction in valuation multiples was driven by severe commodity, energy, packaging, and petrochemical inflation, sluggish organic volume growth following aggressive pricing actions, rising interest rates that pressured high P/E premiums, and unique operational execution issues across individual portfolios. 1 By late 2026, several strategic, operational, and structural worries had begun to clear. Many leading companies have initiated significant cost programs, portfolio reshaping (including high-profile spin-offs and divestments), and leadership changes–most notably the chief executives appointed at Nestlé and Unilever–to pivot back to volume-driven growth and enhance capital returns. While certain headwinds such as persistent input cost volatility and competitive private label pressures remain, brokers are identifying distinct paths to re-rating for select high-quality compounders, while maintaining a cautious outlook on those facing margin resets or structural execution challenges.
