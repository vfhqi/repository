Monte-Carlo SBM delivered record financial results for fiscal year 2025/2026 with consolidated revenue expanding 12% to €861.6 million and fully diluted earnings per share growing to €4.60 per share, fundamentally validating the company's transition from a post-pandemic recovery story into a sustainable luxury asset compounder.
