Plausible problems to monitor

High inventory builds absorbed EUR 48 million of operating cash flow during H1 2026, representing a concerning working capital absorption pattern that diverges from efficient asset deployment. While the concentrated analyst pool of only two major brokers and five named analysts provides limited independent coverage diversity, the dispersed price target range from EUR 28 to EUR 50 underscores substantial valuation uncertainty and limited consensus formation.

This shallow coverage universe constrains institutional visibility and reduces the quality of independent fundamental checks relative to more broadly followed semiconductor equipment peers. Severe customer concentration risks manifest through approximately 70% of sales tied to a single foundry partner, while Technoprobe's H1 2026 EBITDA margin of 44.4%, despite exceeding Besi's historical operating margin of 29.3%, reflects an operational profile heavily dependent on maintaining sole-supplier status at key hyperscaler accounts.

Bears highlight the material threat of FormFactor qualification potentially triggering up to 20% share loss in AI GPU probe cards, compounded by High Bandwidth Memory revenue timing remaining highly uncertain due to yield issues. This dual exposure to customer concentration and qualification timing delays represents a structural vulnerability absent in more diversified testing peers. Management's cautious fourth quarter 2026 guidance implies a 300 basis point sequential EBITDA margin reduction to approximately 49%, suggesting either embedded conservatism consistent with the sandbagger archetype or genuine concerns about capacity utilization sustainability as newly expanded facilities come online.

The maintenance of 12-week commercial lead times limits forward order backlog visibility, potentially increasing revenue volatility and constraining management's ability to provide credible medium-term forecasts during periods of demand inflection. The abrupt resignation of independent director Paolo Enrico Dellachà on March 24, 2025, triggering a sudden board-level committee vacancy, followed the earlier irrevocable resignation of non-independent director Nicola Dell'Oro on May 27, 2024, raising immediate board vacancy concerns.

This pattern of board instability, while subsequently addressed through strategic appointments, introduces governance continuity questions during a period when the company requires stable oversight to navigate aggressive capacity expansion and complex international partnership structures including the Golden Power-conditioned China joint venture.

FY24 EBITDA of EUR 136.5 million, with a 25.1% margin, reflected severe dilution versus the DIS Tech business branch integration, trailing the peer group and demonstrating that earlier acquisitions of Harbor and DIS are only now inflecting from highly dilutive levels as integration finishes. This prolonged margin suppression window, extending across multiple fiscal years, reveals material execution friction in post-merger integration relative to management's original synergy timelines and raises questions about the company's ability to efficiently absorb future acquisitions.

Since most revenues are denominated in US dollars, unfavourable exchange rate movements pose a primary sensitivity risk to margins, with growth in H1 2026 partially offset by a EUR 30 million unfavorable FX impact from exchange rate movements. This structural currency mismatch, where revenues price exclusively in dollars while costs accumulate in euros, creates an unhedged macro exposure that differentiates Technoprobe's risk profile from peers with more balanced currency matching and represents a persistent headwind to reported margin stability absent active hedging programs.
