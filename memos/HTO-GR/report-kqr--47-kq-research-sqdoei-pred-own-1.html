Section 2 – Terms of OTE's CPI-Linked Price-Adjustment Clause

Section summary
  1. OTE's inflation-linked mechanism establishes a structured framework to regularly adjust postpaid tariff rates based on macroeconomic indices.
  2. The pricing clause enables OTE to defend its operating margins and support its long-term cash flow metrics amidst a highly competitive landscape.

Core Contractual Parameters

1.
Postpaid contract coverage: Hellenic Telecom structured its CPI-linked price-adjustment clause to target approximately two million postpaid contract connections across Greece. The pricing mechanism applies systematically to both consumer and business segments, bridging mobile and fixed line services. This broad contract coverage ensures that OTE can defend its consolidated average revenue per user against domestic inflationary pressures.
2.
Indexation and reference terms: The contract terms stipulate that price adjustments are calculated using the official Greek consumer price index (CPI) over a defined twelve-month reference period. This clause is explicitly written into the terms and conditions of postpaid agreements at the time of contract signing, establishing a clear legal framework. By embedding these terms early, OTE minimizes contractual disputes and secures a predictable pathway for annual tariff optimization.
3.
Annual recurrence and caps: The contract language establishes that the CPI-linked tariff adjustment is designed to recur annually, allowing OTE to protect its margins on an ongoing basis. Under the active pricing framework, OTE applied a 2.6% upward adjustment in December 2025, which reflects a structured approach to matching inflation. While specific regulatory floors are monitored closely, the presence of an annual cap prevents excessive consumer pushback, which sustains long-term customer retention across retail segments.
