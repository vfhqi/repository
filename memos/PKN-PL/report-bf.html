EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

2.

5.
Challenged petrochemical integration: Steam crackers extend value chains but generated PLN 6.3 billion EBIT losses due to Chinese oversupply and high energy costs.

Financial Performance and Capital Efficiency

6.
Merger-driven revenue expansion: Revenue reached PLN 372,767 million through M&A integration; organic growth remains essentially flat across the period.
7.

9.
Negative free cash outlook: Heavy transition capex of PLN 31.9 billion will keep group FCF negative through 2026 despite PLN 47.2 billion operating inflows.
10.
Comfortable leverage metrics: Net debt-to-EBITDA stood at 0.14x with PLN 26.4 billion cash, providing robust short-term liquidity despite capital intensity.
11.
Peer valuation discount: Analysts apply lower multiples versus MOL due to unclear capital allocation and FCF generation concerns through the transition period.

Unmatched regional refining scale: Processing capacity of 42.6 million tonnes dwarfs all CEE peers, supporting dominant wholesale market positions across Poland and Baltics.
14.
Deep infrastructure lock-in: Ownership of Gdansk/Butinge marine terminals and Baltic Pipe bookings extending to 2037 create substantial entry barriers.

Unpassable carbon cost burden: Czech refinery tests revealed ETS 1 costs cannot be passed through, forcing PLN 2.7 billion asset impairments downstream.

Mechanical versus heroic expansion: Competitive advantage stems from structural state-backed mergers rather than operational dynamism or innovation excellence.

Resilient CEE fuel demand: Regional transport fuel consumption exhibits structural resilience versus Western European decline, supporting refining utilization rates.
