EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

SKAN Group AG has undergone planned leadership succession with a highly experienced new CEO taking the helm to drive scaling, while the Board of Directors refreshed with digital and governance expertise. The corporate strategy is shifting from a hardware focus to high-margin recurring solutions via recent software and transfer consumable acquisitions and standardized platforms, though the financial profile experienced temporary projectled top-line compression with group net sales falling 7.7% to CHF 333.3 million in FY25 due to customer project postponements.

CHANGE MATERIALITY

The fundamental transformation underway at SKAN represents material structural evolution rather than mere cyclical adjustment. The core Strategy 2030 centers on consolidating market leadership, expanding the addressable market, growing aftermarket services, and accelerating digital transformation, with the Group aiming to drive more than 50% of EBITDA from scalable recurring revenues (Services & Consumables) over the mid-term. Strategic acquisitions of a 76% controlling interest in Metronik for EUR 25 million to internalize MES software capabilities and an 84% controlling interest in ABC Transfer to add rapid transfer beta bags to its consumables portfolio underscore management's commitment to portfolio diversification beyond lumpy equipment sales. However, structural demand is shifting towards high-containment isolators driven by next-generation drug pipelines, though vaccine line demands have significantly collapsed, creating near-term execution headwinds that obscure the underlying strategic repositioning.

TOP DRIVERS (Ranked by Impact and Plausibility) The forward earnings trajectory hinges on five primary value drivers with differentiated risk profiles. Antibodydrug conjugate (ADC) isolator demand represents the highest-impact driver at 15%, supported by modern oncology drugs requiring complex, aseptic-toxic large-scale systems, with SKAN receiving several orders in the double-digit million range for complex ADC filling lines.

Services & Consumables scaling delivered 28.9% growth to CHF 116.5 million in FY25 with exceptional segment EBITDA reaching CHF 29.2 million at a 25.0% margin, representing the most credible near-term driver given demonstrated execution momentum. The preapproved services launch planned for the second half of 2026 targets CHF 50 million in revenues once fully operational with projected EBITDA margins up to 50%, though software validation difficulties postponed regulatory approval from late 2025 to the first quarter of 2026, introducing execution uncertainty.

Backlog conversion represents an 18% impact driver, with the order backlog rising 8.7% to CHF 346.1 million in FY25 as management targets upper teens net sales growth in FY26 as postponed projects resume, yet approximately CHF 15 million to CHF 20 million of the order backlog remains exposed to cancellation risks. Metronik and ABC Transfer integration contributed CHF 16.2 million in FY25, representing a 5% incremental driver contingent on successful commercial leverage of SKAN's installed base.

RECENT DELIVERY ASSESSMENT

Historical execution reveals bifurcated performance between strategic initiatives and near-term financial targets. SKAN historically achieved or exceeded its financial targets between FY2022 and FY2024, with profit margins continuously overachieving guided corridors, reaching 15.8% in FY24 versus the 13% to 15% target. However, FY25 results revealed a significant project-led downturn with net sales falling 7.7% to CHF 333.3 million versus management's initial guidance of mid-teens growth, and group EBITDA margin reaching 11.6%, failing to meet the initial guided target range of 14% to 16%.

Critically, execution misses are highly clustered in customer project delays rather than internal operational failures, with the post-pandemic decline in vaccine demand triggering widespread customer deprioritization of sterile lines. High-margin Service & Consumables sales expanded by 28.9% in FY25, confirming strategic execution remains intact despite cyclical equipment headwinds. Cash flows remained highly resilient with operating cash flow growing 37.3% to CHF 64.1 million in FY25, supported by high volumes of customer advance payments reflecting stable order intake, validating the underlying quality of the project backlog despite revenue recognition delays.

FORWARD CREDIBILITY ASSESSMENT

Management guidance credibility faces moderate impairment following the severe FY25 miss, though structural indicators support medium-term recovery plausibility. Forward guidance credibility is moderately impaired after the severe FY25 project-led sales and margin miss, with SKAN missing its initial FY25 net sales target of mid- teens growth, printing a 7.7% contraction due to project postponements, while the FY25 EBITDA margin of 11.6% fell substantially below the initial guided corridor of 14% to 16%. However, the order backlog reached CHF 346.1 million in FY25 supporting revenue conversion visibility for 2026, though approximately CHF 15 million to CHF 20 million of the current backlog is exposed to potential cancellation risk, as management guides for high-teens sales growth and an EBITDA margin of 13% to 15% in FY26.

Sensitivity to FX and project execution remains materially elevated due to Swiss Franc presentation constraints, as SKAN reports in Swiss Francs but operates globally, making financial results highly sensitive to EUR and USD exchange rate fluctuations. SKAN targets net sales growth in the mid-to-upper teens and gradually increasing EBITDA margins to upper teens, with the base case contingent on normalized customer decision cycles converting the elevated backlog into accelerated revenue recognition throughout FY26 and FY27.

SELL-SIDE POSITIONING Sell-side coverage landscape remains structurally thin with limited actionable insights available in the AlphaSense research pool. Source evidence is thin or absent for sell-side commentary in the AlphaSense pool with no substantive findings generated. The absence of comprehensive broker coverage introduces information asymmetry risks for external investors seeking independent validation of management projections, particularly regarding the commercial viability of pre-approved services and the sustainability of elevated service margins. This coverage gap likely reflects SKAN's mid-cap positioning and limited free float following the founding family's controlling ownership structure, constraining institutional engagement and reducing external accountability mechanisms that typically discipline forward guidance trajectories. PEER POSITIONING

Relative competitive assessment reveals temporary underperformance across top-line and margin metrics versus key equipment and consumables peers. SKAN's -7.7% sales contraction in FY2025 lags the positive medians of its closest peers, with SKAN's 11.6% EBITDA margin representing significant profitability compression compared to Sartorius at 30.3%. Stevanato secured a 26.0% adjusted EBITDA margin in Q2 2026, leading the primary packaging peer group, highlighting SKAN's relative margin deficit during the equipment cycle trough.

However, SKAN's Services & Consumables segment expanded 28.9% in FY25, mirroring robust consumables demand across key peers, demonstrating competitive parity in the highest-growth, highest-margin segment that represents the strategic future. SKAN maintains structurally negative working capital of CHF -25.3 million to fund growth internally, providing a structural cash flow advantage versus capital-intensive peers like Stevanato.

The competitive gap is transitory and segment-specific rather than structural, with peer convergence expected as equipment demand normalizes and SKAN's service mix expands toward the 40% to 50% of sales targeted under Strategy 2030.

ACTIVISM EXPOSURE

No active activist investor holds a position greater than 3% or has submitted public demands, proxy filings, or board nominations in the AlphaSense library. The founding family's controlling ownership structure and the company's strong long-term strategic focus on recurring revenue transformation reduce near-term activism vulnerability. However, prolonged underperformance versus guidance could attract operational activists focused on accelerating the equipment-to-services transition, streamlining the manufacturing footprint, or challenging capital allocation toward pre-approved services infrastructure before commercial validation. The absence of vocal external shareholders also creates limited countervailing pressure against potentially over-optimistic mediumterm margin targets, particularly if competitive intensity from German turnkey suppliers intensifies. KEY RISKS (Asymmetric Downside Scenarios) The investment case confronts four primary risk vectors with material downside asymmetry.

Long-term global process capex digestion could severely suppress new isolator orders if major CDMOs freeze capacity additions, representing the most severe structural threat given SKAN's 65% equipment revenue concentration. Acquisitiondriven debt expansion could restrict financial flexibility if severe project delivery execution delays prolong the highmargin service ramp-up, particularly given non-current financial liabilities surging from CHF 9.5 million to CHF 129.7 million in FY25 to fund acquisitions, though net debt to EBITDA remains comfortable at 0.97x.

The aggressive push into proprietary filling machinery and software integration risks alienating long-standing fill-finish partners, leading to a breakdown in historical co-referral networks and increased development costs, threatening SKAN's neutral Switzerland positioning that historically enabled commercial partnerships with German filling equipment specialists. Protracted customer capital digestion could erode SKAN's negative working capital advantage if advance downpayments dry up during order delays, eliminating the structural cash flow benefit that currently funds growth without dilutive equity raises.

The confluence of cyclical headwinds and strategic execution risks creates meaningful near-term downside if FY26 backlog conversion disappoints or pre-approved services experience further regulatory delays.

INVESTMENT VERDICT

SKAN Group AG presents a strategically compelling but tactically challenged investment opportunity characterized by structural portfolio transformation masked by severe cyclical equipment headwinds. The company is executing a credible strategic pivot toward high-margin recurring revenues through organic service scaling and targeted acquisitions of complementary software and consumables capabilities, with demonstrated execution success evidenced by 28.9% Services & Consumables growth and 25.0% segment margins in FY25.

However, the severe near-term delivery failure with 7.7% sales contraction and 420 basis points of margin compression versus guidance materially impairs forward credibility, particularly given the CHF 15 million to CHF 20 million backlog cancellation exposure. The investment case hinges on three sequential proof points over the next 12 to 18 months: successful conversion of the CHF 346.1 million backlog into upper-teens FY26 revenue growth, commercial launch and margin validation of pre-approved services in H2 2026, and sustained Services & Consumables organic growth above 10% demonstrating installed base monetization.

The risk-reward profile favors patient capital willing to tolerate near-term volatility for exposure to a structurally advantaged niche leader transitioning toward a higher-quality, more predictable earnings stream, contingent on normalized pharmaceutical capital spending cycles and absence of further strategic execution setbacks. Current positioning represents a show-me story requiring tangible FY26 delivery before fundamental re-rating, with the founding family's long-term orientation providing strategic stability but limiting near-term catalysts absent external validation of the recurring revenue transformation thesis.

[Cross-ref: Section 4, Section 7]

Forward guidance credibility is moderately impaired after the severe FY25 project-led sales and margin miss, with net sales contracting 7.7% versus guided mid-teens growth and EBITDA margin reaching 11.6% substantially below the 14% to 16% corridor, establishing a persistent pattern of conservative-to-optimistic guidance failures that extends to FY24's 12.9% sales growth falling short of mid-to-upper teens targets, undermining management's credibility on forward commitments despite operational explanations. [Cross-ref: Section 10]

Non-current financial liabilities surged from CHF 9.5 million to CHF 129.7 million in FY25 to fund acquisitions, while the equity ratio dropped to 26.5% from 52.7% as goodwill from acquisitions was offset directly against equity, establishing compressed balance sheet flexibility that contrasts with peer Getinge's stable 1.0x net debt to EBITDA leverage and creates restricted financial maneuverability if severe project delivery delays prolong the high-margin service ramp-up beyond management's H2 2026 commercial launch timeline. Outliers worth noting

SKAN Group AG's equity ratio plummeted from 52.7% to 26.5% in FY25, driven by a highly unusual accounting treatment where goodwill from acquisitions was offset directly against equity rather than capitalized on the balance sheet, contrasting sharply with peer Getinge's stable 1.0x net debt to EBITDA leverage ratio and representing a dramatic structural divergence from conventional balance sheet management practices in the capital equipment sector.

SKAN maintains structurally negative net working capital through non-linear project billing structures that enable customer advance downpayments to fund operations, a capital cycle configuration that stands in stark opposition to peer Stevanato's negative €32 million free cash flow in Q2 2026 due to intensive plant investments and Gerresheimer's €-50 million to €-100 million free cash flow headwinds from reduced factoring volumes, marking SKAN as a statistical anomaly within its capital-intensive peer cohort.

SKAN's EBITDA generation displays extreme back-half weighting, with FY25 H2 generating CHF 37.7 million versus just CHF 0.9 million in H1, representing a 42x semi-annual profitability swing that far exceeds typical project-based seasonality patterns observed across comparable cleanroom and pharmaceutical equipment manufacturers, suggesting either highly concentrated milestone billing structures or exceptional operational volatility that warrants deeper scrutiny. [Cross-ref: Section 5] SKAN's Services & Consumables segment delivered a 25.0% EBITDA margin in FY25 while the Equipment & Solutions segment compressed to just 4.3%, creating a 21 percentage point margin disparity between business units that significantly exceeds peer Sartorius's 30.3% consolidated EBITDA margin and Stevanato's 26.0% adjusted EBITDA margin, revealing an unusually bifurcated profitability profile where high-margin recurring revenues are masking severe structural pressure in the core equipment business.
