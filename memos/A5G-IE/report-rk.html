Outliers worth noting

The comprehensive analysis of AIB Group reveals three statistically significant anomalies that materially deviate from peer medians and sector base rates, warranting close investor scrutiny. First, AIB's structural hedge execution timing stands out as an extreme outlier, with the bank locking in an average yield of 2.3% on its new €10 billion structural hedge significantly below the available 5-year EUR swap rate of 2.8% in early 2026.

This 50 basis point execution gap represents a profound opportunity cost that compounds annually across a multi-year duration, creating a structural drag on net interest income relative to peers who timed their hedge additions more opportunistically or who benefit from maturing low-yield swaps repricing at current elevated market rates. Deutsche Bank explicitly highlights that peer Bank of Ireland is positioned to capture larger interest income pickups due to upcoming low-yield maturities, placing AIB at a marked competitive disadvantage in the critical net interest margin progression cycle referenced throughout Section 9 and Section 20.
