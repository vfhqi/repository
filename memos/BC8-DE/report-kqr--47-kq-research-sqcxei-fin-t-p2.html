Executive Summary

1.
Bottom line: Bechtle's pricing power is real but mostly takes the form of cost pass-through, not independent price-setting. Without genuine price-setting abilities beyond cost pass-through, reversing hardware price inflation will squeeze margins. The evidence therefore supports conditional, not unconditional, pricing power. It is strong when input costs rise in open-market and catalogue business, and weaker in fixed-price frameworks and standard software. Component-price shock (2026): Deutsche Bank reported that Bechtle passed most input-cost increases to customers while maintaining healthy volume demand. Gross margin on business volume fell to 13.3% in Q3 2026 from 14.3% a year earlier. Absolute gross profit still grew 10.9% to EUR 324.1 million. The pre-tax margin on business volume stayed at 3.8%. Deutsche Bank's claim is therefore verified on absolute gross profit. What brokers and consensus assume on the Key Question: The market widely assumes Bechtle will easily preserve absolute profits by passing through upstream hardware price inflation. Jefferies adds that scale gives Bechtle superior backend rebate terms with OEMs. Deutsche Bank points to a 33% rise in order backlog to a record EUR 3.6 billion, which supported an upgraded earnings outlook. Consensus thus assumes passthrough holds and absolute profit stays protected. Where the report's evidence disagrees, and which side it favors: On the 13.3% margin, the company attributes the decline to selected, transaction-specific large-volume orders with below-average margins. A mix explanation fits the stable pre-tax margin better than structural erosion. On Jefferies' view that Bechtle is insulated, Deutsche Bank noted that more than half of recent revenue growth was price-driven. The evidence favors pass-through proven on the way up but untested on the way down. The gross margin erosion once memory shortages ease is still unquantified.

On wage inflation, the Section 4 net read says wage inflation is passed through multi-year terms. The body instead finds no automatic monthly indexation, so short-term wage inflation lags remain, and the body evidence is favored. The Section 6 table rates Section 3 as High confidence, but the exact lag times and official triggers for price escalations are not publicly detailed. That rating is better read as Medium. 2023. As hardware became available, OEMs intensified their fight for market share. That forces Bechtle to lean on back-end margin components. In a normal year, 100 to 150 basis points of gross margin are tied to supplier rebates. Bechtle's cushion in a price collapse is vendor rebates and mix, not frontend pricing.
