EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

MATERIALITY OF CHANGE

Kuros Biosciences has systematically professionalized its executive suite to support commercial scale, bringing in deep extremities and trauma expertise, while Board governance has transitioned towards greater independence and structural oversight despite the abrupt resignation of a former member. The commercial focus is pivoting beyond spine into extremities and trauma to target a combined $2.9B market by 2032, while dual production capabilities and the Medtronic alliance are designed to drive massive operational leverage toward 2028 profitability targets.

Medical device sales reached USD 146.10 million in 2025, growing 72% year over year, marking a fundamental inflection from clinical-stage biotech to first-time profitable commercial entity. The company reported its first net profit of USD 2.60 million in 2025, representing a dramatic reversal from historical losses and positioning the firm on a credible trajectory toward sustained profitability and cash generation.

The establishment of domestic U.S. manufacturing in Georgia (operational H2 2026) eliminates structural tariff headwinds while the MagnetOs MIS Delivery System launch addresses the high-growth minimally invasive segment. Strategic leadership transitions, including the appointment of I.V. Hall as COO with trauma expertise, signal deliberate preparation for multi-segment expansion beyond the core spine franchise.

Minimally invasive procedures represent half of the 1.7 million U.S. fusions annually, providing substantial addressable runway. A second-generation system is planned for 1H 2027, extending the innovation cycle and defending against competitive encroachment.

Extremities Segment Expansion via ASTRA [HIGH Impact | HIGH Plausibility]: Segment expansion aims to capture the $1.3 billion global extremities market, with management aiming to establish long-term clinical superiority directly against patient autograft. Kuros successfully tripled its active foot and ankle surgeon base during 2025, demonstrating early commercial traction. The Level I ASTRA clinical trial provides differentiated evidence generation in a segment historically dominated by less rigorous clinical data, potentially creating a durable competitive moat.

U.S. Manufacturing Footprint Optimization [HIGH Impact | HIGH Plausibility]: Kuros is constructing a new manufacturing facility in Alpharetta, Georgia, on track to begin production in H2 2026. This facility completely eliminates import tariff exposure that cost the company USD 2.0 million in 2025, directly flowing through to EBITDA margin expansion. Domestic production also reduces supply chain complexity and improves responsiveness to U.S. hospital demand fluctuations.
