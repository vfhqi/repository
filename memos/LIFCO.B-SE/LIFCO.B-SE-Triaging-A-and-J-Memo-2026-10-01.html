[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Lifco is a twenty-year compounder whose ordinary compounding was interrupted by a 2025 industrial trough and by a short-seller report nobody in the research chain has read, and at SEK 317.8 the shares have already paid for the part of the resumption we can see. The Q2 2026 report of 14 July 2026 showed the interruption ending; on our modal path earnings compound at about 12 percent a year, the multiple holds at the stock's ten-year median, and the return is about 12 percent a year over eighteen months against a 20 percent bar. The case becomes compelling at about SEK 285, or if the M&A cadence and the demolition-robot cycle both return and earn a re-rating we do not now assume.

  • The change is external and cyclical, and it is ordinary for this company.
    • The industrial half of the group had its worst year since the 2014 listing in 2025 (organic EBITA growth 0.1 percent), and the Q2 2026 report showed the turn: organic growth 4.7 percent against 3.4 percent consensus, after 1.2 percent in Q1 2026 and -2.0 percent in Q2 2025.
    • Management calls 2025 the trough and H1 2026 "a clear return to normality"
      • the comparatives were easy.
    • Two external headwinds run against the turn: SEK strength (3 percent off H1 2026 sales) and a construction cycle that has not turned for demolition robots (Eurozone construction PMI 42.8 in June 2026).
  • Nothing inside the company has changed, and that is the strength of the business and the weakness of the thesis as a change story.
    • The chief executive has been in post since 2019, the strategy is explicitly unchanged, there is no guidance to raise, and the acquisition programme ran slower in H1 2026 (about SEK 500 million of annualised sales against SEK 2.2 billion for all of 2025).
    • The one internal step, a reorganisation into five segments, is a capacity choice made to source more deals, not a new strategy.
    • Pricing is real and applied (3 to 7 percent in the first year after a deal; 3 to 6 percent a year in the niches) but it is a standing practice, not a new lever.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up at a pace inside the framework's 12 to 20 percent band.
    • Q2 2026 revenue +10.8 percent, EBITA +14 percent to SEK 1,771 million, margin +60 basis points to 23.1 percent, H1 2026 reported EPS +12.8 percent to SEK 4.23.
    • On our path reported EPS goes from SEK 8.00 (FY2025) to SEK 9.00, SEK 10.10 and SEK 11.30 (FY2026 to FY2028), about 12 percent a year, from revenue growth of about 10 percent (organic 4, acquired 7.5, currency -1.5) and a little margin
      • the parts reconcile to the claim.
    • The growth is the company's ordinary compounding, not an inflection, so there is no over-extrapolation risk
      • but 12 percent is the floor of the band and the three-year figure slips toward 11 percent as the base grows.
  • The change has travelled four of the six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain and reached the sell-side's estimates only faintly.
    • It shows in the company's actions, its key operating metrics and its financial statements
    • next-year consensus EPS is up 3.8 percent over six months, the buy share fell 16 points to 44 percent, and sell-side earnings momentum is pinned D.
    • Upgrades ahead are plausible but small, 2 to 4 percent a year from unannounced deals, because the median consensus omits them and fades revenue growth to 4.8 percent by FY2028.
  • What it costs is a ten-year-median multiple, and the company-specific discount has mostly closed.
    • SEK 317.8 is 26.9x the blended 24-month-forward consensus (53rd percentile of the stock's own ten years; 27th percentile of five years) and 28.9x on our reported-basis path.
    • Addtech is at 28.6x and Lagercrantz at 27.7x, so the 21 percent EV/EBITA discount of May 2026 is now a 3 to 6 percent P/E gap
      • the shares rose 14.2 percent in the six months to 25 September 2026.
    • The remaining premium to the sector's median of about 16x belongs to the compounder complex, which is rate-sensitive and has no tailwind.
  • The chain holds from the actions to the financial statements and breaks at the price.
    • Earnings growth carries the return (×1.177 over eighteen months)
    • the re-rating leg is zero on the modal path and negative by year three (27.0x)
    • the cash leg is a 0.9 percent yield.
    • 'Modal return': 12.1 percent a year over eighteen months (total +18.7 percent), 9.6 percent a year over three years (total +31.6 percent), against the 20 percent bar.
    • The plausibly worse drop-through from EBITA to reported EPS (currency at -3 percent a year, interest and amortisation growing with EBITA, tax at 24 percent) takes EPS growth to 9 to 10 percent and the return to 3.6 percent a year at eighteen months: an F.

Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The case against, at its strongest: Lifco's reported compounding is an acquisition-driven headline that masks flat organic earnings, and the ten-year-median multiple is not fair but generous.
    • Organic EBITA growth was 0.1 percent in 2025
      • Dental's organic EBITA grows 1 percent a year through a cycle.
    • Margins are held by low-margin onward invoicing and by adding back about SEK 1.1 billion a year of acquired-intangible amortisation
      • SEK 3,060 million of put/call liabilities sit off the headline
      • there is no internal audit function over 275 companies.
    • A three-person centre is already the bottleneck (H1 2026 deals at a quarter of the 2025 pace) and the acquisition market is turning against the model as private equity and rival acquirers bid up targets.
    • 83 percent of EBITA is earned outside Sweden and the krona is strengthening
      • the short-seller report of 14 January 2026 has not been rebutted because, on this reading, it cannot be.
    • On that reading the trough multiple is 18.6x to 22.7x, the downside is 15 to 31 percent with earnings unchanged, and the bear models go further (DNB Carnegie's SEK 270 bear case; Redeye's SEK 136 to 180).
  • What we concede: the organic engine is slow and cyclical, and the decade's growth was two-thirds acquired.
    • The amortisation gap and the put/call liabilities are real claims that the adjusted-basis brokers ignore and that our reported-basis metric does not.
    • The H1 2026 cadence is the case's live weakness
      • the translation exposure is larger than peers' and the sell-side's FX houses are against it
      • the mark-to-market risk at a median multiple is large, and we rate it D.
    • The report is unread and the company's silence is a choice we would not have made.
  • Why we still hold our view, which is a neutral one: the outputs are what they are on the strictest basis available, and cash does not mask decay.
    • Reported EPS, after amortisation and after the put/call accruals, grew 10 percent in 2025 and 12.8 percent in H1 2026

    • The goodwill (SEK 16.2 billion) has never been impaired

      • the acquisitions were paid for at 5.8x to 6.8x EBITA, not the 8x-plus the bear case needs.
