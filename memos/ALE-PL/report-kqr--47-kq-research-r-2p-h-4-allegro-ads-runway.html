Section 4 – Impact of Artificial Intelligence Shopping Agents by 2028

Section summary
  1. Consumer AI agents are projected to capture substantial e-commerce volume by 2028, introducing structural risks to search-based marketplace advertising.
  2. Large marketplaces are actively defending their high-margin ad streams by blocking third-party web crawlers and deploying proprietary, in-app shopping assistants.

Projected Adoption Timelines

1.
Rapid expansion: US broadline retail is experiencing a significant shift as agentic commerce starts to scale from a low base. Bernstein Research reports in October 2026 that US agentic e-commerce sales are projected to grow from five billion dollars in 2025 to over one hundred and forty billion dollars by 2029. This rapid expansion represents approximately nine percent of total online retail. Consequently, these agents will increasingly intermediate the core consumer discovery layer by 2028.
2.
Gradual transition: Morgan Stanley anticipates that the long-term adoption curve for consumer shopping agents will resemble the early adoption of e-commerce. The broker notes in October 2026 that e-commerce reached only four percent of retail sales by its fifth year. Because compute capacity is heavily constrained and models must become more efficient, agentic commerce will take longer to scale than hyped expectations. This slower curve provides a temporary window for marketplaces to adapt.
3.
Limited traction: Current merchant platforms report that live agentic transactions represent a negligible portion of total sales volumes. Emkay Research observes in October 2026 that consumer traffic initiated by AI agents remains extremely small on major networks. Customers still heavily prefer traditional browsing interfaces over early conversational search applications. Therefore, agent-driven transactions will not impact marketplace earnings targets over the next eighteen months.
4.
Integrated operations: Merchants are increasingly adopting full-chain AI tools to automate business analysis and marketing workflows. Huatai Research reports in October 2026 that platforms are moving from single-point assistant tools toward intelligent hosting systems. These applications help sellers manage product listings, ad placements, and fulfillment without manual oversight on daily operations. Consequently, merchantside AI adoption will accelerate platform transaction efficiency.
5.
Payment readiness: Financial networks are rapidly deploying dedicated transaction protocols to support secure machine-to-machine checkout. Bernstein Research reports in October 2026 that Visa is currently live with approximately fifty AI checkout partners. These early integrations aim to establish verifiable consumer identities behind autonomous shopping agents. This payment readiness ensures that infrastructure is prepared for agentic transactions before volume scales.

Disruption to Search Advertising

6.
Disintermediated search: Sponsored product listings face severe structural headwinds as autonomous agents bypass traditional marketplace result pages. Deutsche Bank Research warns in October 2026 that agents completing purchases directly will reduce consumer exposure to high-margin sponsored ads. This shift creates a severe long-term monetization risk for core marketplaces that rely on search ad impressions. Consequently, future ad growth must depend on off-platform channels like DSP and video.
7.
Advertiser caution: Marketers are showing heightened concern regarding the impact of agentic commerce on their traditional search budgets. Jefferies Research reports in October 2026 that forty-five percent of surveyed partners expect to reallocate budget away from paid search if agentic commerce scales. However, sixty percent of respondents believe the transition will shift ad formats rather than reduce overall digital ad spend. This sentiment suggests that total ad budgets remain highly resilient despite technological shifts.
8.
Shrunken funnel: The marketing budget allocation for digital retail media is expected to contract as AI agents automate product comparison. Bernstein Research notes in October 2026 that agents shortening the discovery phase will shrink the overall ad profit pool. Brands may reallocate their promotional spending from retail media directly to dominant consumer AI platforms instead. This budget shift represents a direct threat to marketplace operating profit.
9.
Contrary evidence: Morgan Stanley suggests that established e-commerce platforms can successfully retain their advertising economics through a mutual handshake protocol. The broker notes in October 2026 that marketplaces can connect their inventory directly to external LLM agents while preserving the transaction and relationship on-platform. Under this framework, absolute advertising revenues are modeled to grow with gross merchandise value rather than collapse. This suggests that agentic discovery may not destroy on-site retail media.
10.
Regional vulnerability: European e-commerce aggregators exhibit varying levels of risk exposure to agentic search based on their product uniqueness. Morgan Stanley observes in October 2026 that platforms with non-exclusive inventory face greater pricing pressure from agentic comparison. While Zalando screens as highly exposed, Allegro remains well-positioned due to its domestic trust and integrated logistics. However, both platforms face uncertainty regarding where ad dollars sit by 2028.

Strategic Marketplace Responses

11.
Blocked access: Dominant e-commerce operators are actively restricting third-party AI crawlers to defend their customer data and ad revenue. Deutsche Bank Research reports in October 2026 that Amazon began blocking Meta's Muse agent from its shopping interface. This aggressive maneuver protects Amazon's proprietary search pages from disintermediation by external shopping assistants like Muse. However, this strategy risks directing agent-led demand toward participating competitors.
12.
Defensive portals: Regional classifieds platforms are integrating proprietary generative tools to maintain their direct customer relationships. Bernstein Research notes in October 2026 that major European portals like Scout24 and Rightmove have launched conversational assistants in partnership with ChatGPT. These integrations ensure the customer lead remains with the marketplace rather than migrating to external agents. Consequently, the platforms successfully defend their historical lead monetization models.
13.
Secure gateways: Omnichannel retailers are developing custom infrastructure to facilitate external agent transactions on their own terms. Morgan Stanley reports in October 2026 that Walmart has introduced its Agent Gateway to preserve first-party data. This infrastructure allows external agents to shop while protecting Walmart's retail media revenue and pricing control. Thus, larger scale platforms can neutralize the disintermediation threat.
14.
Other side of the story: While agentic discovery threatens to bypass sponsored marketplace listings, the rise of AI shopping agents may actually stimulate overall transaction volumes. Morgan Stanley argues in October 2026 that AI agents could add up to six percent to industry gross merchandise value by simplifying product discovery and checkout. Marketplaces with unmatched regional assortments and trusted logistics will capture this incremental demand. Therefore, the overall expansion of the e-commerce transaction pool may offset any near-term decline in core search advertising margins.
