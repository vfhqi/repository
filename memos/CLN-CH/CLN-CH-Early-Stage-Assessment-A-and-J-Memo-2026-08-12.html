B.1 Business Description 20 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Swiss specialty chemicals producer. Clariant AG makes high-value formulated chemicals in three divisions, employs 10,281 staff and operates more than 70 production sites from Muttenz, Switzerland
2.
Three-division operating model. The group restructured from five business units to three in January 2023, capping management layers at six between the chief executive and frontline operators
3.
Care Chemicals is the largest division. It generated CHF 2,112m of FY25 sales at an 18.3% EBITDA margin+, serving personal care, home care, crop solutions, mining and oil services customers
4.
Catalysts is the highest-margin division. FY25 sales were CHF 816m at 20.1% EBITDA margin, supplying process catalysts to petrochemical refineries across propylene, ethylene, syngas, fuels and biofuels
5.
Adsorbents and Additives is the third division. FY25 sales reached CHF 987m at a 16.0% EBITDA margin, covering bentonite purification media, halogen-free flame retardants and polymer additives
6.
Revenue has contracted for three consecutive years. Reported sales fell from CHF 5,198m in FY22 to CHF 3,915m in FY25, reflecting divestments, portfolio pruning and Swiss franc translation
7.
Margin has expanded across the same period. EBITDA margin before exceptional items rose from 15.6% in FY23 to 17.8% in FY25+, and reached 18.2% in the second quarter of 2026
8.
Geographic mix is balanced. FY25 sales split 42% Europe, Middle East and Africa, 29% Americas and 29% Asia-Pacific, with European assets accounting for 30% of group sales
9.
Customer base is highly fragmented. More than 15,000 active credit accounts+ are on the books, down from 17,000 the prior year, with no single customer above 10% of consolidated sales
10.
Pricing runs on two contract types. Within Care Chemicals, 40% of contracts are formula-indexed to feedstocks and 60% carry value-based price management set by commercial teams
11.
The group is not backward integrated. Clariant owns no steam crackers and buys primary olefins externally, under minimum ethylene purchase commitments of CHF 720m that cap sourcing flexibility
12.
Balance sheet carries moderate leverage. Net debt rose to CHF 1,493.1m at 30 June 2026 from CHF 1,413.9m at end-FY25, a ratio of 2.2 times net debt to EBITDA
13.
Cash conversion has improved sharply. Free cash flow conversion reached 52% on a trailing-twelve-month basis+ in the second quarter of 2026, against a stated annual target of well over 40%
14.
Returns on capital sit in single digits. Return on invested capital was 6.9% in FY25, down from 9.2% in FY24, against a stated weighted average cost of capital of 7.8%
15.
Research spending is modest. Research and development cost CHF 125m in 2025, close to 3% of group sales, with an innovation sales ratio of 19.4% in the second quarter of 2026
16.
Capital returns run through par value reduction. Shareholders approved a CHF 0.42 per share distribution paid in June 2026, cutting total share capital by CHF 139.4m
17.
Ownership is concentrated. Saudi Basic Industries Corporation holds a static 31.5% stake- acquired in 2020 at an average entry cost of CHF 22.00 to CHF 27.00 per share
18.
Two board seats sit with the largest holder. Following the April 2026 rightsizing from eleven to eight directors, Saudi Basic Industries holds two of eight seats
19.
Litigation is disclosed but not provisioned. Follow-on civil ethylene cartel claims are carried as contingent liabilities with zero financial provisions- recorded in the accounts
20.
Investment style classification is event-driven cyclical. The earnings profile combines high fixed-cost operating leverage in Catalysts with a consumer-facing Care Chemicals base and a binary legal outcome
