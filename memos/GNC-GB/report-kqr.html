Section summary
  1. Long-term agreements provide multi-year revenue stability but subject convenience food manufacturers to varied raw material and labor cost pass-through lags.
  2. Automated pricing models protect profit margins from sudden cost inflation, though some manufacturers remain exposed to manual retail customer negotiations.

1.
Extended visibility: Cranswick confirmed it successfully onboarded major pigmeat volumes under its ten- year strategic agreement with Sainsbury's.
2.
Strategic partnership: Cranswick secured another long-term commercial commitment through its ten-year agreement with Morrisons.
3.
Multi-year venture: Hilton Food agreed to a ten-year collaboration with NADEC for its new processing facility in Saudi Arabia.

Successful renewals: Deutsche Bank reported on 29 January 2026 that Hilton Food secured successful core retail meat renewals in Denmark and the Netherlands.
