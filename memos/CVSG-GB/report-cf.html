EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CVS Group delivered adjusted earnings per share of 85.6p for the 2026 financial year, marking a 6.9% increase, while consensus forecasts for fiscal year 2027 target adjusted EPS between 89.3p and 99.6p with adjusted EBITDA expectations of 149.0 million to 151.7 million pounds. The company reported consolidated revenues of 712.8 million pounds representing a 5.9% increase, supported by a gradual recovery in like-for-like sales growth to 2.1% compared with 0.2% in the prior year, though this performance remains below the long-term organic target of four to eight percent growth.

CVS Group maintained its adjusted operating margin at 19.9% in the 2026 financial year aligning with the lower end of its long-term target range of 19% to 23%, though stripping out research and development tax credits reveals an underlying operating margin of 17.6% which declined slightly from 17.8% in the previous period. The group's leverage ratio rose to 1.63 times net debt to bank test EBITDA in 2026 compared to 1.18 times post-divestment, reflecting cash outflows for Australian practice acquisitions and share buyback executions, though leverage remains comfortably below the stated balance sheet guardrail of two times net debt to EBITDA.

Leadership, Strategy, and Corporate Ambitions

CVS Group maintained stable executive leadership under Richard Fairman as Chief Executive Officer, Robin Alfonso as Chief Financial Officer, and Paul Higgs as Chief Veterinary Officer during the 2026 financial period, ensuring that the company's long-term five-year growth plan launched in November 2022 continues without disruption to reduce execution risks and support a gradual recovery in adjusted earnings per share.

The company finalized the divestment of its UK crematoria operations in May 2025 to Anima Care UK Limited for an initial cash consideration of 42.4 million pounds at an attractive ten times multiple of adjusted EBITDA, allowing the group to record a massive one-off exceptional profit on disposal of approximately 32.0 million pounds. The group successfully increased its Australian footprint to 57 operating practice sites by the end of the 2026 financial year, lifting Australian revenues to 79.1 million pounds and generating 11% of total group revenue and sixteen percent of adjusted EBITDA before corporate costs. CVS Group restarted its domestic consolidation strategy following the final conclusion of the Competition and Markets Authority investigation in March 2026 after previously freezing all domestic practice acquisitions for over two years, allowing the board to return to accretive domestic acquisitions at highly disciplined multiples.
