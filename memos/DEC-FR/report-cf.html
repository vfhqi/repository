Appendix

Outliers worth noting

Programmatic digital trading yields a substantial 25% to 30% CPM uplift over traditional bookings, establishing a pricing premium that sits at the extreme high end of digital advertising monetization benchmarks. While peers report single-digit to mid-teens programmatic uplifts, JCDecaux's premium positioning suggests either superior inventory quality or advanced data capabilities that warrant ongoing validation. This outlier pricing power creates significant margin expansion potential but also introduces execution risk if competitive dynamics shift or if premium inventory supply increases across the sector.

Almost 50% of adjusted revenue aligns with the strict criteria of European Green Taxonomy, placing JCDecaux in the top quintile of European media companies for ESG alignment. This level of taxonomy compliance substantially exceeds the typical 15-25% range observed among European advertising peers and reflects either genuine operational leadership or aggressive classification approaches. The company's environmental positioning creates both regulatory advantages in municipal tenders and potential reputational capital that could translate into contract renewals.

JCDecaux won 80% of competitive street furniture tenders worldwide in 2024, establishing a win rate that significantly exceeds the sector base rate of approximately 50-60% for incumbent operators. This exceptional tender success suggests either structural competitive advantages in municipal relationships or pricing strategies that sacrifice near-term margins for long-term contract security. The outlier win rate raises questions about the sustainability of contract economics if aggressive bidding strategies are driving success.
