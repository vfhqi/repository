Section 1 – Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Nestlé's valuation and investor sentiment hinge on whether the company can successfully transition to a volume-driven growth model amid portfolio changes and margin pressures.

Defining the Key Question

1.
Central question: "For Nestlé S.A. (NESN-CH), what does each covering broker assume for FY2026, FY2027 and FY2028 on the lines between organic growth and underlying EPS, and what does each expect the company to say at its full-year results on 18 February 2027?"
2.
Significant uncertainty: The resolution of these broker assumptions directly dictates Nestlé's valuation because the company faces a critical transition period under new leadership. Investors are highly focused on whether the company can maintain its mid-term targets, including organic sales growth of over 4% and real internal growth of at least 2%. Any deviation from these targets at the February 2027 announcement will likely reset the longterm earnings growth algorithm and shift investor valuation frameworks downward.
