3. Strategy

The company gives no numerical guidance, so he will not offer numbers unprompted. Put Redeye's figures to him one at a time and ask "nearer this, or nearer that?". Ask what the company's own words ("normalise") mean in numbers. Its recent cues have understated results: the July warning on third-quarter margin came before a margin of 11.3%. So "cautious" is itself an answer.

1 ★ Data Center in the fourth quarter. "Data Center is to 'normalise' towards the first half, after 692 in the third quarter. Redeye has SEK 582m for the fourth. Is 'normalise' nearer 540 or 580?"

- Reads: 580 is Redeye's number: in line. 600 or more is a beat on this line and leans 2027 towards the upside case (EPS about SEK 3.2 against Redeye's 2.85). Nearer 540 is below Redeye but inside the memo's bar for a group beat. Below 500 points to a pause; each 10 points of Data Center growth is SEK 0.15 to 0.20 of EPS.

2 ★ The fourth-quarter margin. "The group margin was 11.3% in the third quarter. Redeye has 9.6% for the fourth, with Fiber Solutions at 8.0% after 9.4%. What is the normal seasonal drop, in points?"
- Pick from: "Is Fiber Solutions nearer 8.0% or 9.4% in the fourth quarter?" / "Are submarine shipments the same size as in the third quarter?" / "In July you warned on margin and then reported 11.3%. Is 'normalise' the same kind of caution?"
- Reads: a seasonal drop of about a point, or Fiber Solutions nearer 9.4%, supports a beat of Redeye's 9.6%. A drop of nearly two points means a report in line, which the memo says would end the run of beats early.

3 ★ The first quarter of 2027. "Redeye has SEK 2,038m of sales in the first quarter of 2027, 20% up on this year's cold first quarter, at a margin near 10.6%. Should analysts be nearer 20% growth or 25%?"
- Pick from: "The order book was four months of sales. Still four?"
- Reads: "nearer 25%" supports the second beat (our 65%) and a further nudge to 2027 (55%). "About 20%" leaves the second beat resting on margin alone.

4 ★ North America. "Fiber Solutions grew 13% organically in the third quarter, with Europe down 9%. What did North American Fiber Solutions grow, and is 2027 nearer 5% or 15%?"

5 ★ The supply shortage, in kronor. "How many million kronor of third-quarter sales slipped into the backlog because of glass and connectors?"
