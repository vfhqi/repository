Executive Summary

1.
Headline verdict: Siemens Healthineers delivered a mixed Q3 FY2026, with top-line growth of 2.8% missing expectations while underlying profitability and EPS outpaced consensus estimates. Comparable growth missed the consensus of 4.5% by 1.7 percentage points. Every later finding follows from this split between a weak top line and a flattered bottom line.
2.
Revenue deceleration: Group revenue reached €5,764 million, with comparable growth slowing from 7.6% in the prior-year quarter to 2.8%. The shortfall came from Imaging phasing and Diagnostics weakness. Precision Therapy was the only segment to beat.
3.
Tariff-inflated margin: The reported adjusted EBIT margin of 19.1% included a 370 basis point contribution from one-off tariff refunds. Excluding this benefit, the underlying margin was 15.4%. That was in line with the 15.3% consensus. The profitability "beat" is therefore mostly an accounting artefact, and operations met expectations rather than exceeded them.
4.
EPS quality: Adjusted basic EPS of €0.70 rose 10% year over year, but one-off tariff refunds supported it by €0.15. Underlying EPS of €0.55 fell slightly short of prior-year comparables. The headline beat does not signal an improving earnings run-rate.
5.
Imaging shortfall: Imaging grew 2.3% organically against a consensus of 5.4%. Management attributed the miss to customer site-readiness delays that pushed CT and MR shipments into the next quarter. The underlying margin of 21.6% also declined from 23.7% a year earlier on unfavorable mix and share-based payment provisions.
6.
Precision Therapy strength: Comparable growth of 9.2% beat the 7.7% consensus, driven by the Advanced Therapies launch and strong radiation oncology shipments. The underlying margin of 14.1% was marginally below the prior year's 14.4%. Growth quality is high, but conversion into margin is not yet improving.
7.
Diagnostics deterioration: Diagnostics comparable revenue fell 5.5%, missing the consensus decline of 2.9% on volume-based procurement pressure in China. The underlying margin of 3.0% compares with 9.2% in the prior-year quarter, which pension release benefits had inflated. Management expects another mid-single-digit decline in the final quarter. The Imaging segment focuses on advanced diagnostic imaging equipment, while Diagnostics delivers laboratory testing solutions, Varian provides cancer care technologies, and Advanced Therapies offers minimally invasive clinical solutions. The company maintains direct representation in more than 70 countries and services major medical providers globally.

Varian holds more than 60% of the global radiation oncology treatment and software market. Guided therapeutic interventions: Siemens Healthineers supplies highly integrated angiography systems and mobile C-arms under its Advanced Therapies business segment. Advanced Therapies products support interventional radiologists and cardiologists during image-guided minimally invasive surgeries. The segment leverages strategic partnerships with major medical device manufacturers to accelerate therapeutic workflow integration.

Imaging organic revenues achieved a 7.5% compound annual growth rate between fiscal year 2021 and fiscal year 2024.
