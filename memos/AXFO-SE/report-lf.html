EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CEO Quality and Background Margulies combines a KTH engineering degree, ABB supply chain work, ICA Sweden retail roles and Dagab and Hemköp leadership, giving her a profile closely matched to Axfood's logistics-led strategy.

Her appointment was an internal succession, named on April 17, 2024 and effective August 15, 2024, so continuity rather than reinvention defines the leadership profile.

Over 18 years in grocery, with divisional roles supported by group infrastructure, leave unproven standalone capital allocation experience as a central open question about her profile. Delivery Record At Hemköp she lifted net sales from SEK 6,354 mm to SEK 7,878 mm and operating margin from 3.60% to 4.40%, a clearly positive divisional track record. City Gross reached adjusted rolling twelve-month breakeven in the third quarter of 2026, meeting her secondhalf 2026 ambition and delivering on a public commitment. Bålsta productivity rose 25% between early 2025 and the third quarter of 2026, but the project was planned before her tenure, so part of the credit is inherited.

The adjusted operating margin held at 4.10% in 2025, matching the prior year, while 2026 capex guidance was trimmed to SEK 2.0–2.1 billion, indicating steady but unspectacular financial delivery. Leadership Style She speaks directly on calls but states that "we don't do any guidance for the future," which reduces near-term visibility for investors. - Earnings Calls Her pilot-and-evaluate approach, seen in the Linköping City Gross pilot, limits execution risk, though DNB Carnegie warns it may slow defensive rollouts against faster rivals.

Insider activity over twelve months was one non-cash allotment of 504 shares, with no open-market buying or selling, signalling neutral rather than convicted sentiment. The concurrent exits of CFO Lexmon, Hemköp's Etemadi and Snabbgross's Pettersson create a stacked leadership transition, with CFO successor Jon Johnsson arriving by August 2027.
