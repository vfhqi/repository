Belron Price-Mix Performance and Targets

1.
Moderating gains: D'Ieteren reported that Belron achieved a price-mix contribution of 5.1% in FY2025 and 3.4% in H1 2026. 2.
Sustained complexity: Bernstein Research projected on 25 September 2026 that Belron's medium-term revenue algorithm of 5-6% relies on 3-4% from value, mix, and price. This contribution is expected to outpace underlying market volume growth of approximately 2% due to rising windscreen complexity. Consequently, Belron's top-line development remains highly insulated from broader economic downturns.
3.
Technological leverage: Jefferies Research reported on 16 March 2026 that Belron is targeting a further 2 percentage point operating margin improvement to exceed 25% by FY2028. This margin trajectory is supported by the high-margin nature of calibration services, which carry almost no direct parts cost. However, some broker notes question if Belron can sustain these gains as the calibration penetration rate approaches a plateau in North America.

Volume trends: D'Ieteren reported that Belron delivered organic sales growth of 7.3% in H1 2026, which was supported by a 3.9% increase in total job volume. Bernstein Research noted on 25 September 2026 that this organic growth was split between volume and 3.4% price/mix. Consequently, these liability concerns protect a baseline reimbursement floor for certified national providers like Safelite.
