EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 25 findings

Avanza Bank emerges from this comprehensive assessment as a Swedish digital wealth platform operating with exceptional domestic market positioning but facing strategic inflection risks as it attempts organic international expansion while navigating structural net interest income compression and elevated platform reinvestment cycles. The investment case pivots on whether management can execute a complex dual-platform build in Denmark while preserving industry-leading operating margins of approximately 70% against a backdrop of Riksbank monetary normalization and intensifying Nordic competition from pan-regional rival Nordnet.
