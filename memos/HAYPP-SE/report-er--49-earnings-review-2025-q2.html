Executive Summary

Section summary
  1. Haypp Group AB delivered disappointing Q2 2025 financial results on August 7, 2025, with reported net sales declining 2.3% to SEK 921.5 million versus SEK 942.8 million in the prior year period, missing consensus expectations of SEK 953.3 million as the severe Zyn product shortage in the United States and calendar effects from Easter constrained top-line growth.
  2. Haypp Group entered the Q2 2025 earnings period backed by three years of robust sales expansion and solid margin improvement. Net read: Strong stock performance and major price target upgrades reflected optimistic growth expectations, though brokers maintained neutral ratings on regulatory risks.

Despite the revenue shortfall, Haypp Group achieved a record-high gross margin of 19.2% in Q2 2025 compared with 14.3% in the prior year, exceeding consensus expectations of 18.3% by 90 basis points, primarily driven by high-margin service revenues from the expanding Media and Insights division.

However, adjusted EBIT of SEK 38.3 million missed consensus expectations of SEK 45.3 million, representing an operating margin of 4.2% that was restricted by increased investments in growth operations and overhead costs in the United States and United Kingdom.

1.
EPS outlook compression: Carnegie analysts reacted to the Q2 2025 miss by cutting their near-term earnings assumptions, lowering the 2025 adjusted EPS forecast by 9% to SEK 4.43 from SEK 4.80, and trimming the 2026 adjusted EPS forecast by 3% to SEK 6.50 from SEK 6.70, reflecting the impact of stepped-up investments in the United States organization.

Crucially, analysts kept their long-term earnings assumptions virtually unchanged, leaving the 2027 adjusted EPS forecast at SEK 8.50 compared to the previous SEK 8.53, signaling confidence that short-term supply headwinds will dissipate over the next three years.

The company maintained its ambitious financial targets announced at the April 2025 Capital Markets Day, aiming for net sales compound annual growth of 18% to 25% through 2028 alongside an adjusted EBIT margin target of 5.5% with flexibility of 150 basis points, expecting nicotine pouches to drive up to 85% of total sales by the target period end.

2.
Customer acquisition deterioration: The active consumer base contracted to 536,000 in Q2 2025 from 591,000 in the prior year period, heavily impacted by US supply bottlenecks and state-level market closures, representing a near-term headwind to top-line growth that does not reflect structural demand erosion.

Total order volumes mirrored this trajectory, decreasing to 1,170,000 in Q2 2025 from 1,252,000 in Q2 2024, exacerbated by Easter timing and severe product shortages in America that limited operating leverage and posed a threat to short-term earnings per share development.

Growth segment active consumers declined by 34% to 115,000 during Q2 2025 as the company was forced to close sales in certain states and discontinue all US tobacco sales in late 2024 due to regulatory adjustments and flavor bans.

3.
Resilient transaction economics: Despite volume pressures, the average order value remained highly stable at SEK 701 in Q2 2025 compared with SEK 699 in Q2 2024, as consumers maintained stable order sizes despite facing limited brand availability in growth markets, protecting the profitability of each transaction and keeping fulfillment costs optimized.

Customer cohort data reveals robust lifetime value expansion, with active consumers increasing their average order value by 12% and order frequency by 42% from their first to their fifth purchase, providing a highly predictable revenue stream for the company.

Haypp Group acquires the vast majority of new customers organically through non-paid search engine results, dominating organic search to generate virtually free consumer traffic and maintain minimal marketing costs, shielding margins from escalating paid advertising costs in growth markets.

4.
Prudent marketing discipline: Management adopted a highly cautious spending posture in the United States during Q2 2025, remaining prudent on customer acquisition spending until Zyn brand inventory restrictions are resolved, with postponed large-scale marketing campaigns helping protect the underlying operating margin during temporary supply disruptions.

The company detailed plans to reinvest a portion of its expanding gross profit back into enhancing customer retention and acquisition once inventory normalizes, with management acknowledging that adjusted EBIT margin will face pressure over the 2025 to 2027 investment period as a direct result of stepped-up investments in United States and United Kingdom personnel.

The group warned investors that its path to the 2028 profitability target will be highly non-linear, with adjusted operating margin over the 2025 to 2027 period restricted by heavy expansion investments, as management emphasized that reinvesting record high gross profits into organizational scale will maximize long-term shareholder value.

5.
Margin-neutral promotional intensity: Manufacturer-funded discounts in the United States nicotine pouch category created a negative price mix during the period, driven primarily by promotional activity in the US business that represented a marginal drag on net sales growth, but these promotions were fully offset in the cost of goods sold, leaving product margins unharmed.

Underlying category momentum remained robust, with the company recording 23% like-for-like volume growth in nicotine pouches during Q2 2025, proving that long-term modern oral fundamentals remain strong across core and growth regions as the secular transition toward tobacco-free alternatives accelerates, supporting long-term earnings per share expansion through a shift toward higher-margin pouch categories.

6.
Supply-driven volume volatility: On a like-for-like basis adjusted for temporary supply bottlenecks and product rationalizations, Haypp Group achieved an impressive 40% volume growth in US nicotine pouches during Q2 2025, indicating that long-term consumer demand remains intact and will reaccelerate once inventory normalizes.
