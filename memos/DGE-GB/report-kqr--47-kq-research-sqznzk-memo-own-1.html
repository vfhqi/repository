According to Diageo's Q1 trading statement on November 5, 2026, this performance included a 1.1% volume increase and a 0.3% price/mix benefit. Excluding the impact of East African Breweries, organic sales rose by 0.6% in the quarter. This initial performance represents a small step in the right direction but leaves the top-line recovery fragile.

Section 4 – Disposal Status of EABL and Royal Challengers Bengaluru

Section summary

East African Breweries Disposal Details

1.
Successful progress: Diageo has successfully advanced the divestment of its majority stake in East African Breweries to Asahi Group Holdings for a total equity value of three billion dollars. The transaction is strictly structured between shareholders and has received initial unconditional regulatory approvals in Uganda and Tanzania. This strategic exit will allow Diageo to concentrate on its core international spirits portfolio while reducing its debt profile.
2.
Financial parameters: Asahi Group Holdings entered into a commitment agreement for up to six hundred and fifty billion yen to fund the acquisition of Diageo Kenya Limited. The transaction reflects an implied enterprise value to EBITDA multiple of seventeen times based on Diageo Kenya's fiscal 2025 performance. This high valuation multiple highlights Asahi's willingness to pay a premium for regional scale.

EABL's extensive digital distribution network in Kenya represents an immediate growth platform for Asahi's global premium beer portfolio. This integration is expected to offset weak domestic volume trends in mature Asian and European markets.

This operational adjustment is designed to encourage local market competition while protecting third-party distributors.

Royal Challengers Bengaluru Franchise

This monetization allows United Spirits to focus on its premium beverage alcohol assets while unlocking substantial non-core capital for redeployment. The transaction was approved by the Competition Commission of India in June 2026 but awaits final clearance from the local cricket board.

Unlocking this capital enables direct reinvestment into higher-growth premium alcohol segments within India.

This payment significantly supported the subsidiary's standalone pre-exceptional earnings growth during the first quarter of fiscal 2027. The cash generation underscores the financial health of the franchise as the sale process nears completion.

Management automatically extended the long-stop date to mid-December 2026 to allow sufficient time for the final governing body approvals. This strategic delay ensures that United Spirits will not face material financial outflows from premature execution.

This substantial capital return would significantly support parent company balance sheet deleveraging.
