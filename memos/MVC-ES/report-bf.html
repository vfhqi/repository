EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 53 findings

Metrovacesa operates a low-risk residential development model anchored by an exceptional 8-year land bank that insulates margins from open-market inflation, though the massive inventory duration structurally dilutes capital returns. The business concentrates on residential homebuilding across five high-demand Spanish provinces, holding a massive landbank of approximately 37,500 units representing roughly 8 years of delivery visibility.

Financial performance demonstrates superior EBITDA margins and exceptionally low leverage, with a remarkable 17% annual dividend yield in FY25, though returns remain capped with analysts projecting a longterm ROCE of 12%. The structural housing deficit of nearly one million homes and concentrated top-tier consolidation create favorable industry dynamics, while selling prices rose 12.9% year-on-year compared to only 3.9% construction cost inflation, demonstrating robust pricing power.

- Earnings Calls Management led by CEO Jorge Pérez de Leza exhibits strong execution discipline and conservative guidance, consistently beating targets while maintaining an exceptionally low net loan-to-value ratio of 14.4%. The company benefits from majority ownership by Banco Santander and BBVA, providing stable capital access and strategic support. However, the business model faces risks from regulatory intervention on housing affordability, prolonged municipal zoning delays, and concentrated shareholder structure with only 4.3% free float. Alternative living segments and industrialized construction present long-term growth optionality, though steep equity requirements, restrictive land financing of only EUR 500 million systemwide, and zoning timelines as long as 15 years create formidable barriers protecting established operators.
