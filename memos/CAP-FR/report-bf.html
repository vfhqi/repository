Revenue productivity per employee stands at approximately USD 66,000, which positions Capgemini materially below Accenture's premium level of over USD 90,000.

This USD 24,000 gap represents a fundamental efficiency disadvantage that directly impacts gross margin generation capacity and limits the firm's ability to extract maximum value from its massive 417,600-person workforce. The productivity shortfall becomes particularly concerning when viewed alongside gross margin contraction of 30 basis points in the first half of 2026, suggesting that labor arbitrage advantages are eroding faster than Capgemini can transition toward higher-value automated delivery models.

This efficiency gap is further compounded by the reality that Operations and Engineering managed services generate gross margins of only 20% to 25%, materially below the 35% to 40% gross margins captured by pure-play consulting competitors in upstream strategy work.
