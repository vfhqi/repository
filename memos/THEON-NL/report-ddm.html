1. The deciding issues

Issue 1: the margin step-up

  • Judgement: a margin of 27% or more in FY2027 is plausible, close to probable (Richard: "plausible/probable"). A margin of 28%, and growth above the guided 20%, are possible (Watson's words, here and below).
  • For: the fourth quarter of 2025 printed 28.4%, and a repeat gives FY2026 about 27.0% (research 1). Revenue and margin have met or beaten guidance every year.
  • Against: the margin was exactly 26.2% in FY2025 and in the first nine months of 2026. No company release shows the thermal sensor order the upgrade "depends mainly" on. About 0.8 of a point of the FY2025 margin came from an accounting change (research 1). Notion's notes of the call record that the first thermal product "could initially be slightly below the group average".
  • The expert's 20% to 24%: rejected; every quarter reported since 1-Apr-2026 is above it.
  • The basis of the margin is unsettled, and it is worth about 5% of earnings per share. From FY2027 the model's margin leaves out about EUR 10 to 11 million a year of amortisation of intangibles acquired with HGH and Merio, about 1.4 points. The 5-Nov-2026 memo read the company's own adjusted margin as keeping that cost in. On that reading the new input of 27.2% prints as about 25.8%, below the company's own 26% baseline, and investor relations' 27% would mean about 28.4% in the model: FY2027 earnings per share of about EUR 1.85, not EUR 1.76 (Watson's arithmetic). The inputs take the cautious reading. It is the first question for the company.
  • FY2028 growth of 17.0% is the least secure input. It is below the guided 20%. With the higher FY2027 base it puts FY2028 revenue EUR 50 million above the model's build by product, which the 5-Nov-2026 memo capped on tube supply. About 6% of FY2028 earnings per share rests on the two growth changes together, and about 3.5% on the 17.0% alone (my arithmetic).

Issue 2: interest and tax

  • Judgement: interest and tax take back little or nothing net (probable). In FY2027 lower tax adds EUR 3.6 million to net income and heavier interest takes EUR 3.8 million (my arithmetic).
  • Tax at 23%, from 25%, is fair: the charge was 23.1% of profit before tax in FY2025 and 22.2% in FY2024 (Watson's arithmetic from the 2025 annual report). The first half of 2026 implied about 30%: one half-year against two full years. Brokers already use 23% to 24%.
  • Interest, EUR 5 million a year heavier from FY2027, may be too low: Note 31 of that report prices the revolving credit facility at one-month Euribor (2.47% on 6-Nov-2026, Bank of Finland) plus 1.5%. The company said on the call plus 200 basis points. On all debt that takes a further (0.6)% off FY2027 earnings per share at one-month Euribor, and up to (1.8)% at three-month Euribor of 2.79% (my arithmetic).

Issue 3: orders and leverage

  • Judgement: leverage above 3 times at year-end is probable, and it could stop the upgrade being paid for.
  • End-2026 leverage, largely explained: the sources differ mainly on when HGH closes, and that date is not settled. December 2026 (management, 18-Nov-2026) gives about EUR 543 million of net debt, 3.2 times (my arithmetic), fitting "more than 3x at year end" and Deutsche Bank's 3.4 times, on net debt EUR 30 million higher. January 2027 gives the 5-Nov-2026 memo's 1.5 times.
  • The covenant, unsettled: the sources give 3.0, 3.5 and 4.25 times; the company confirms none.
  • Orders: nine-month orders were 0.74 times revenue; about EUR 105 million is still to be signed by 31-Dec-2026 for the guided "about 1.0x" (research 1).
  • The Exosens stake: investor relations told Richard "Strategic plan is to keep the stake". The chief executive indicated on 18-Nov-2026 that the company "could monetize their financial assets" to speed deleveraging (the AlphaSense research of 19-Nov-2026). A sale inside 18 months, 30% likely in the 5-Nov-2026 memo, stays possible.

3. Guidance and consensus

  • A margin guidance upgrade by 30-Jun-2027 is plausible, close to probable; a growth upgrade is possible. "Base case" and "a conditional management view" are one statement: management's base case, dependent on thermal sensor orders, not a commitment.
  • The dated events: the year-end release, late December 2026, where the orders wording could be cut; preliminary FY2026 results, mid-February 2027 on the company's pattern; the first-quarter update, late April or early May 2027, the likeliest occasion.
  • Consensus: it has a 26.0% margin for FY2027 and FY2028, so guidance of 27% adds about 4% to its operating profit (my arithmetic).

4. The strongest counter-case

The margin has been 26.2% for three reporting periods. Nine-month gross profit beat consensus by 5.1% and operating profit by 0.8%, so the higher gross profit has not reached operating profit. For this to win: a fourth-quarter 2026 margin below 27%, and FY2027 guidance at "more than 26%".

5. What would transform the case again, and what to watch

  • Monitoring item 1, the fourth-quarter 2026 margin, due mid-February 2027: the changed inputs need 27.8%. Below 27% reverses the verdict on the margin.
  • Monitoring item 2, a named thermal sensor or drone camera order: investor relations said launch "in the next 1–2 months", so by about 19-Jan-2027.
  • What transforms the case again: the covenant confirmed at 3.0 times (negative) or 4.25 times (positive); an Exosens stake sale (positive).

The four Bottom-up ratings. Upward pressure: Thesis's Change Forces' Strength (the margin starts to amplify growth). Downward pressure: Foundations' Robustness (leverage passes management's own 3 times ceiling; the covenant is unconfirmed) and Setups' Fit/Crispness (the earnings upgrade overlay grows; the liquid lever weakens). Toward more risk: Case Riskiness (forecasts above consensus rest on unannounced orders).

Verdicts

  • Forecasts and return: transforms, positive. Earnings per share rise 6.0% and 12.0%.
  • "The margin is a flat line, not a lever": transforms, positive. 27% or more is plausible, close to probable.
  • Interest and tax: adjusts, mixed. They cancel.
  • Leverage and the Exosens stake: adjusts, negative. The print above 3 times comes sooner; a sale of the stake is less certain, not ruled out.
  • Fair multiple: no change. The evidence cuts both ways.
