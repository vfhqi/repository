Overview

Valuation and Operational Recovery Analysis: Straumann Holding AG and Global Medtech Peers This report synthesizes professional broker research to analyze the historical valuation phases, current broker target cases, and strategic growth drivers of Straumann Holding AG (STMN-CH), alongside a comparative analysis of 12 global medtech and dental peers. Data Source and Precedent Inventory Companies Researched and Used: 13 (Straumann Holding AG, Align Technology, Sonova, Coloplast, Cochlear, Alcon, Envista, Dentsply Sirona, Demant, Convatec, Smith & Nephew, Medacta, and Siemens Healthineers). No named entities were omitted.
2.
Precedents Identified: 5 successful re-rating/recovery precedents from valuation bottoms (Demant, Siemens Healthineers, Sonova, Envista, and Convatec) and 4 prominent cases of prolonged non-recovery or delayed turnaround (Align Technology, Dentsply Sirona, Smith & Nephew, and Alcon).
