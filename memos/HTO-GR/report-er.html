Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side focus prior to Hellenic Telecom's Q3 2026 earnings centered on the threat of aggressive fiber overbuild from Public Power Corporation.

1.
Strategic positioning: Hellenic Telecom operates as the leading telecommunications provider in Greece, delivering mobile, fixed-line, TV, and ICT services. The company has successfully transitioned into a focused pure-play Greek operator after fully divestment of its international operations. This simplified structure allows management to focus resources on domestic network superiority.
2.

Deutsche Telekom currently controls the company with a major equity stake of approximately 55%. This strong backing provides the group with substantial technological and global brand leverage.

This efficiency compares favorably with European peers such as Proximus, which reported a domestic EBITDA margin of 35.5% in 2025. High operating efficiency helps Hellenic Telecom generate superior free cash flow relative to its peer group.
