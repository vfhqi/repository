EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

2.

3.
Strategic pivot to full RF subsystems through the Synertia platform entry into RF generators doubles the addressable market and positions Comet to capture an ambitious 25% market share by 2030, representing a complete business model transformation from component supplier to integrated systems provider.
