EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 27 findings

1.
Novo Nordisk operates a dominant metabolic disease platform controlling the global GLP-1 landscape, yet faces intensifying competitive pressure from Eli Lilly's superior clinical efficacy and looming patent cliffs that threaten to compress returns on invested capital from historic peaks of 65% to structural lows near 33%. The company has executed aggressive forward integration through an USD 11 billion Catalent acquisition to resolve aseptic filling bottlenecks, while deploying record capital expenditure of DKK 55 billion in 2026 to scale API and device manufacturing. Despite these investments, severe US pricing headwinds erode 70% of gross sales through rebates, and critical formulary losses at CVS Caremark threaten to transition 100,000 patients to competitor therapies. Long-term value creation hinges on successfully transitioning patients to next-generation combinations like CagriSema before the 2031-2032 semaglutide patent expiration, while expanding into highvalue comorbidities including MASH, cardiovascular disease, and chronic kidney disease. The foundation quality remains adequate but deteriorating, constrained by unprecedented capital intensity, compressed cash conversion, and execution risk on unproven pipeline assets that must offset imminent generic erosion.
