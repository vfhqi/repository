Section 3 – Capital structure, debt capacity, and funding availability 2 findings

Section summary
  1. nexfibre possesses robust sponsor equity backing and debt liquidity that isolates it from the capital scarcity crippling smaller alt-nets.

nexfibre Capital Structure and Sponsor Injection Mechanics

nexfibre was originally established as a joint venture funded with 4.5 billion pounds of combined equity and debt commitments to construct a wholesale full-fibre network across the UK. The joint venture equity structure comprises InfraVia Capital Partners holding a 50 percent controlling stake alongside Liberty Global and Telefónica holding 25 percent equity interests each.

To fund the 2.0 billion pound acquisition of Substantial Group, sponsors committed 1.0 billion pounds of net new capital into nexfibre, comprising an 850 million pound equity injection from InfraVia Capital Partners and 150 million pounds jointly from Liberty Global and Telefónica. Liberty Global and Telefónica's 150 million pound equity contribution is efficiently funded via a dividend distribution from Virgin Media O2, avoiding direct HoldCo cash drain.

As part of the acquisition agreement, Virgin Media O2 receives 1.1 billion pounds in cash proceeds for its wholesale traffic commitment, utilizing 150 million pounds to acquire Netomnia's 500,000 retail customers and allocating the remaining 950 million pounds toward net balance sheet deleveraging. The Netomnia transaction structure unlocks a total planned investment program of 3.5 billion pounds in nexfibre capital expenditure spanning through 2040.

Debt Capacity, Undrawn Liquidity, and Operating Cash Flow Support

Prior to inorganic transactions, nexfibre maintained approximately 2.0 billion pounds of committed but undrawn debt facilities out of its initial 4.5 billion pound capital package to fund network consolidation.
