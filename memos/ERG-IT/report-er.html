Additionally, ERG historically secured highly favorable wind turbine pricing through long-term framework agreements signed in 2018 and 2020, but following expiration experienced a rebound in unitary capital expenditure per megawatt, forcing active adjustment to procurement processes.

Conversely, if auction clearing prices fall below €65 per MWh or if grid connection delays push commercial operations dates beyond 2028, the investment thesis would face severe structural impairment, as the combination of compressed merchant pricing and elevated financing costs would eliminate the return premium that justifies ERG's pure-play valuation multiple. In conclusion, ERG's Q2 2026 results demonstrate operational resilience and strategic discipline in the face of challenging weather conditions and macroeconomic pressures.

The successful integration of UK assets, confirmation of full-year guidance, and progress on strategic asset rotation all support near-term earnings stability. However, the long-term investment case faces meaningful pressure from rising capital costs that compress project economics and threaten the sustainability of the 25%+ IRR thesis. The company's future performance will depend critically on its ability to execute its authorized pipeline at attractive auction economics, manage grid connection bottlenecks, and navigate supply-chain inflation without sacrificing return quality.

Until these execution milestones are achieved and the upcoming Industrial Plan provides concrete visibility on capital allocation beyond 2027, the investment thesis remains constructive but capped, justifying a cautious wait-andsee stance aligned with the broader sell-side consensus.
