The Key Question asks how concentrated or diffuse the drivers are that separate STG's next 18 months to four years from the last four to six years, and how wide and how skewed the range of plausible outcomes is over the next 18 months. The outcome range, however, looks wide and skewed to the downside, mainly because of execution history and US demand and tariff uncertainty. The size of the 2027 base reset, the contract-manufacturing margin and the buyback timing cannot yet be confirmed. The full-year results due 3 March 2027 are the first hard test.

Stripping out these total exceptional refunds will lower the baseline profit margins for 2027.
