EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Overall Conclusions

1.
Verdict on the chief executive: Eric Hémar is a strong founder-operator with a long delivery record, but his authority is largely unchecked and his claims are only partly verified by outsiders. He has delivered on long-term targets by doubling group revenues every five years since 2009. He also invested his own and family savings to launch ID Logistics in 2001. How far he shows the Section 16 qualities: Section 16 rates him strong on five of six. These are relentless drive, fast decisions, adaptability, direct communication and high standards. The sixth, capital allocation, is only partial, because he balances high acquisition leverage with rapid subsequent deleveraging.
2.
Single largest credibility risk: the claim most likely to prove wrong is that the start-up drag from record contract launches is "fully manageable". Management described the start-up effects of launching 27 new operations during 2025 as fully manageable, with losses following a predictable eighteen-month curve before reaching breakeven. The group launched 27 new contracts in 2025 compared to the original guidance of 20. Skeptics note that margin dilution from the J-curve effect could persist if contract launches continually exceed historical averages, while voluntary staff departures rose to 15.1% in 2024 from 12.3% in 2023. A compliance claim has already been contradicted: a zero-tolerance policy versus the August 2026 False Claims Act settlement.
