Direct Margin Drivers and Price Dynamics

1.
Expanding direct margins: Arteche reported a significant expansion in its direct margin from 29.7% in fiscal year 2022 to 37.3% in fiscal year 2025. This positive performance continued into the first half of 2026, with the direct margin reaching 40.9% as the absolute direct margin grew to 120.1 million euros. Management attributes this milestone to a combination of higher sales volumes and product mix optimization. This trajectory suggests that the company is successfully executing its profitability goals
2.
Pricing strategy implementation: Arteche has implemented a targeted and individualized pricing strategy by market and specific product category to capitalize on tight supply conditions. This pricing power is particularly strong in high-voltage segments where global competitive intensity is lower. Elevated market pricing has allowed the company to comfortably cover rising input costs. Consequently, pricing actions have served as a critical driver of the recent direct margin step-up

Capacity scarcity support: Industry experts indicate that grid equipment price increases are heavily supported by manufacturing capacity not expanding as fast as demand. This dynamic suggests that current elevated margins are partially reliant on temporary supply constraints
