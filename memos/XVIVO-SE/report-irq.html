What the call should settle, and why

The Investment Analyst's case in one line: right business, wrong earnings base. Over two to three years the skew of outcomes is favourable; over the next two to three reports the skew of guidance and sell-side revisions is unfavourable. The call cannot test the share price, but it can test the six judgements that decide how large and how fast the cuts are.

  • Judgement 1, the largest lung customer's step-down. Lung Bioengineering (United Therapeutics) bought 132 in 2025 and about 150 this year (it built about 10 of inventory in Q3 2026); it now runs its own approved platform. The Investment Analyst's base is a fall of 65 in 2027 (range 40 to 100); both brokers carry 5% to 6% of 2027 sales. Each 35 of revenue either way is about 0.65 of FY2027 earnings per share. This is the widest operating uncertainty of 2027 and nothing about the contract is disclosed.
  • Judgement 2, the European heart ramp. The base is 155 of heart revenue in 2027 (quarters of 30, 33, 42 and 50), with the first CE-marked commercial invoices in December 2026. Each 35 either way is about 0.63 of FY2027 earnings per share; heart at the sell-side's 180 to 194 is also what lets management hold its 2027 targets for another quarter.
  • Judgement 3, the lines below EBITDA (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation). Disclosed CE-mark amortisation of 39 a year, a 20% tax rate (DNB Carnegie models 15% through 2028) and the end of 2026's currency gains take 8% to 10% off FY2027 consensus earnings per share before any operating number changes. This is most of the gap between the Investment Analyst's FY2027 earnings per share of 3.82 and the pre-results consensus median of 6.08 (DNB Carnegie now 5.70).
  • Judgement 4, the 2027 targets. EBITDA margin above 30%, operating profit (earnings before interest and tax) margin above 20%, abdominal gross margin 70%; the brokers are at 23% to 25% EBITDA margin. The Investment Analyst puts a miss above 90% and a re-dating at about 55% by the Q4 2026 report or the March annual report, 85% by July 2027. A re-dating to a year the company can beat is a negative headline and a positive set-up; a reaffirmation in January is a neutral headline and a negative set-up.
  • Judgement 5, operating-cost growth. The base is 13% growth in costs excluding depreciation in 2027 (to about 562); the risk is one-sided toward 15% to 17%. 17% costs about 0.5 of FY2027 earnings per share; management has said investment continues.
  • Judgement 6, the United States Food and Drug Administration on the heart file. This moves FY2028, not FY2027. The Investment Analyst assumes approval in late 2028; consensus FY2028 earnings per share (9.5 to 10.5) rests on a launch in late 2027 or early 2028. The same event is upside to our numbers and downside to consensus, and to the price.

Order of play. Ask the customer, European heart and below-EBITDA questions first: that is where our numbers and consensus diverge most and where investor relations can actually answer (definitions, policies, schedules). Then the 2027 targets and costs. Leave the Food and Drug Administration, competition and governance to the end, because the answers there are mostly "subject to review".

Range A: scope for guidance and sell-side upgrades or cuts over the next four quarters (Q4 2026 to Q3 2027)

A1 ★ The 2027 targets. "You kept the 2027 targets (EBITDA margin above 30%, operating profit margin above 20%, abdominal gross margin 70%) in July and again in October, while both brokers who publish models are at 23% to 25% for 2027 and RedEye says you will fall short. The Q3 2026 report calls 2027 'a financial springboard' to 2028. Are the targets still for calendar 2027? When will the board formally review them: with the Q4 2026 report on 28 January, the annual report in March, or a capital markets day?"
- Why: the targets were set by 2024, before the services losses, the customer's platform and the CE-mark amortisation were known. The re-dating is a small estimate event (brokers already discount it) and a large narrative event (retail holds the targets as a promise).
- Tell: "springboard" language plus a review date = the reset comes in Q1 2027 and can be set where it is beaten; "we stand by them" with no review date = the negative set-up for January 2028.

A2 ★ Numerical guidance or an EBITDA-to-earnings bridge. "You have never given numerical annual guidance, in a year with three launches. Would you consider giving 2027 depreciation and amortisation, the tax rate and capitalised development, or an EBITDA-to-earnings-per-share bridge, at the Q4 2026 report or a capital markets day?"
- Why: DNB Carnegie and RedEye differ by about 80% on 2027 net income on sales that differ by about 10%, because of the four numbers the company does not disclose (the cost of each launch, the amortisation each approval triggers, the tax rate, the currency). The Investment Analyst says a bridge or a first numerical guidance would move its guidance score by 15 to 20 points and halve the below-EBITDA uncertainty.
- Tell: any yes brings consensus to our level in one step rather than four; a no leaves the alignment to each report.

A3 ★ Amortisation, approval by approval.
