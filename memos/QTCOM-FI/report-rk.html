The comprehensive analysis of QT Group reveals a medium-to-high investment failure probability of 35-40% across three interconnected risk domains spanning bearish structural headwinds, latent pre-mortem failure mechanisms, and acute unhedged foreign currency exposure. Recommendation centers on maintaining a disciplined hold-and-watch position until operational restructuring delivers confirmed organic growth re-acceleration above 15% alongside transparent quarterly disclosure of contract maturity splits and standalone product segment performance.
