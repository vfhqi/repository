Section 2 – The Medtech Life-Cycle Pattern and Multiple Evolution

Section summary
  1. Small medtech multiples expand rapidly during regulatory approval and launch phases as speculative clinical value drives sentiment.
  2. Peak hypergrowth is characterized by elevated EV/Sales multiples, which are highly sensitive to interest rate hikes and macro shocks.
  3. Deceleration triggers immediate de-rating and multiple compression, forcing a strategic shift from top- line expansion to gross margin protection.
  4. Takeover premiums and strategic interest from diversified conglomerates provide a robust valuation floor during operational transition setbacks.
