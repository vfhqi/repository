Business Description (B.1) 10 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does: Flow Traders is a global electronic market maker founded in 2004, quoting continuous two-way prices in over 25,000 exchange-traded products across more than 150 venues
2.
How it earns: revenue is net trading income, being gross trading income less exchange, clearing and prime-brokerage fees; gross trading income was EUR 798.2m in FY25 against EUR 480.5m of total income
3.
Trading model: positions are market-neutral and hedged close to instantaneously, so profit comes from bid-ask spreads and price differences between correlated instruments, not from market direction
4.
Scale and mix: FY25 total income EUR 480.5m, equity market-making 59% of net trading income and fixed income, currencies, commodities and crypto the remaining 41%; digital assets alone are 24%
5.
Geography: Europe is 64% of group revenues, with the Americas and Asia-Pacific 36% combined; European operations carried 55% of 1H26 operating expenses at EUR 121.9m
6.
Financial character: FY25 EBITDA margin 41.4% (FY24 45.3%, FY23 23.0%), FY25 EBIT margin 35.4%, and a three-year net-trading-income compound growth rate of 1.8% to end-2025
7.
Balance sheet: trading capital reached a record EUR 1,150m at 30 June 2026 and shareholders' equity EUR 970m, against EUR 197.5m of drawn borrowings and EUR 13,062m of uncommitted prime-broker facilities
8.
Capital returns: the regular dividend was suspended in July 2024 under the Trading Capital Expansion Plan, and management confirmed at the June 2026 Capital Markets Day that all profits are retained through 2030
9.
People: period-end headcount was 641 full-time equivalents at 30 June 2026, down from 656 at 31 March 2026, with over 40% of the workforce in technology
10.
Investment style: the shape is a capital-intensive, volatility-geared cyclical with an embedded long-volatility payoff, not a compounder; earnings are event-driven and management cannot issue net-trading-income guidance
