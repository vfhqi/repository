Second, capital-intensive infrastructure buildouts with multi-year municipal commitments exhibit high confidence even when facing extended delivery timelines. Conversely, merchant optimization, technology breakthroughs, and cross-border policy alignment remain structurally uncertain and require defensive positioning or extended observation periods before capital deployment. The tiered confidence approach therefore risks anchoring to backward-looking regulatory certainty while missing inflection points in technology adoption curves or sudden shifts in municipal privatization politics that could rapidly upgrade currently low-confidence dimensions into highconviction investment opportunities.
