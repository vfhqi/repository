The split is on the return's own basis: the two-year-forward price-to-earnings multiple and two-year-forward earnings per share, today against each horizon. The multiple and earnings parts multiply (1 + one, times 1 + the other, less 1, is the share price's change); dividends add to that.

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Revenue. Organic growth about 2.0% in FY2026 (H1 actual 1.2%; H2 about 2.7%), 3.0% in FY2027, 3.3% in FY2028 and FY2029, 3.0% in FY2030, of which price and indexation about 1.5 to 2 pp a year (wage inflation normalising, so volume must supply the rest, mainly German grid work). Acquired growth about 5.3% in FY2026 (ROFA from June, SGS from about September, BLOCK, Worley, Artemys, Invizo, nimeg), about 5.0% in FY2027 (carry-over about EUR 360m plus half a year of new bolt-ons, about EUR 240m), then about 3.5% to 3.7% a year. Currency -0.2% in FY2026, nil after. Consensus models no acquisitions after 2026; I model EUR 350m a year because SPIE has bought every year since 2013 and its 7% to 9% total-growth target assumes it. How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
