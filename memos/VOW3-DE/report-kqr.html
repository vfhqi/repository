Section 6 – Confidence classification

The systematic confidence classification across the four analytical dimensions reveals a tiered evidence structure ranging from high-conviction, data-rich market dynamics to emerging technological pathways constrained by incomplete regulatory and commercial frameworks. The premium and volume OEM competitive landscape benefits from granular market share data, disclosed transaction pricing, and concrete localization investments that support high-conviction investment theses.

In contrast, the auto supplier and tyre dimension shows strong evidence on battery chemistry transitions and tariff impacts but faces material uncertainty around Umicore's cathode utilization recovery and the sustained pass-through of raw material inflation. The downstream channels analysis presents a medium confidence profile due to observable dealer margin compression and charging network expansion metrics, yet critical residual value assumptions on electric vehicles remain subject to severe volatility and balance sheet risk.

The logistics and specialized niches dimension demonstrates the widest confidence dispersion, with car-carrier capacity dynamics supported by transparent charter rate data and fleet expansion plans, while small modular reactor commercialization remains anchored primarily in preliminary trade studies and aspirational regulatory frameworks rather than proven economic or operational validation.
