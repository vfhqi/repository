The group owns critical global infrastructure, maintaining capacity on over 70 subsea cable systems and operating one of the largest Internet of Things platforms with 240 million connections. Under the leadership of chief executive Margherita Della Valle, the company is undergoing a period of significant portfolio transformation to create a simpler business.
