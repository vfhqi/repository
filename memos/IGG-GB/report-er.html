Section 16 – Conclusion

IG Group's H1 2026 results delivered a robust 18% revenue expansion to £642.8 million against elevated sell-side expectations, yet the announcement of the transformational $1.3 billion Underdog acquisition and the strategic Jersey redomiciliation proposal have fundamentally reset the investment thesis from a stable UK-listed fintech broker into a high-growth, US-centric prediction market platform play. The earnings print itself met consensus estimates with minimal deviation, but the strategic pivot announced alongside the financial results represents the most significant structural change in the company's modern history, forcing investors to re-underwrite the equity on entirely different risk-return parameters.

The single most important change emerging from this update is the proposed acquisition of Underdog, which establishes IG Group as an immediate leader in the fast-growing US daily fantasy sports and prediction markets sector. This transaction is projected to double the group's US revenue contribution from 22% to 40% of the combined group and shift the average customer age from 42 down to 34, fundamentally altering the demographic and geographic profile of the consolidated business.

This pivot represents a deliberate strategic bet that US prediction markets will emerge as a dominant speculative asset class over the next three to five years, allowing the combined entity to capture exponential growth in a structurally under-penetrated market. Management guided that the acquisition will be broadly neutral to adjusted earnings per share in year one and double-digit percent accretive by year three, with Deutsche Bank scenario modeling suggesting up to 39% EPS accretion by FY29 under maximum incentive scenarios.

The investment case now rests heavily on whether Underdog can successfully scale its prediction market infrastructure and convert its massive registered user base into high-value, active traders capable of delivering the ambitious EBITDA targets required to vest the employee incentive plans.
