Section summary

1.
Corporate description – unique dual model: Glencore operates as a leading diversified producer and marketer of natural resources, with globally integrated activities spanning physical production, refinement, processing, storage, and transport. This dual model allows the company to capture value throughout the commodity supply chain, producing over 60 physical commodities across more than 30 countries. The group is structured into two core segments: Industrial activities (mining and smelting assets) and Marketing activities (physical trading and arbitrage). Under this hybrid structure, the company leverages ownsourced industrial production to feed its highly profitable global trading division

Section 8 – Change in strategy and structure 15 findings

Section summary
  1. Glencore has structurally pivoted toward coal retention and portfolio simplification, abandoning its planned coal demerger in favor of high-margin cash generation.

Coal Strategy and EVR Integration

1.
Strategy retention – post-consultation: Following the acquisition of Elk Valley Resources (EVR) in July 2024, Glencore undertook an extensive consultation process to assess shareholder views regarding a potential demerger of its coal and carbon steel materials business.
