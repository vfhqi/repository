1.
Core activity: ChemoMetec develops, manufactures and markets automated fluorescence-based cell counting instruments and disposable consumables for cell therapy manufacturing and bioprocess control
2.
Revenue model: A razor-and-blade structure in which consumables and services represented over 75% of total sales in recent periods, with the LCB market (life science research, cell and gene therapy, bioprocessing) at 95% of H1 FY2025/26 revenue
3.

4.
Financial character: FY24/25 revenue DKK 495.60 million (+22% YoY), gross margin 95% in H1 FY25/26, EBITDA margin 55% in H1 FY25/26, zero interest-bearing bank debt and net cash of DKK 341.85 million at end-2024/25
5.
Balance sheet: Cash equivalents of DKK 274.7 million at 31 December 2025, net debt/EBITDA of -2.0x, free cash flow conversion regularly exceeding 90% of net income
6.

7.
Geography: North America historically 59% of group revenue (the single largest structural exposure), Europe 32%-34%, Rest of World 9%-10%
