Section 1 – Business Model + Organisation

Section summary
  1. OMV has transformed its operational profile by transitioning Borealis into the at-equity consolidated Borouge Group International (BGI) joint venture with ADNOC.
  2. OMV's revenue streams exhibit high cyclicality and volatility driven by Dated Brent oil, European gas hub prices, and chemical indicator margins.
  3. The group's Strategy 2030 pivots toward gas expansion and specialty circular chemicals, supported by the upcoming transition to new leadership in late 2026.

Operational structure and asset base

1.
Three-pillar operational setup: OMV operates through three primary segments consisting of Energy, Fuels, and Chemicals.
2.
Transformed chemical structure: The group closed the Borouge Group International (BGI) transaction on March 25, 2026, combining Borealis and Borouge.
3.
Deconsolidated chemical reporting: Following BGI's closing, OMV lost control of Borealis and now accounts for the JV using the equity method.
4.
Consolidated upstream assets: The Energy segment focuses on exploration and production across core regions in Europe, Norway, Libya, and the UAE.
5.
Downstream fuel integration: Fuels operates three European refineries located in Austria, Germany, and Romania with over 90% utilization targets.
6.
Joint venture ownership: BGI is owned equally at 50% by OMV and ADNOC, functioning as their global polyolefin growth platform.
7.
Regional fuel marketing: Retail operations span 1,708 filling stations across eight European countries to distribute refined products.

Segment revenue and macro sensitivities

8.
Volatile transactional model: Segment revenues are highly transactional, driven by spot sales of crude oil, gas, and refined fuels.
9.
Contractual supply pricing: Gas storage and delivery contracts have multi-year tenors, utilizing market-indexed pricing mechanisms at delivery.
10.
Extreme Brent sensitivity: OMV's annual Clean CCS Operating Result shifts by EUR 50 million for every USD 1 change in Brent.
11.
Gas pricing impact: Clean operating results fluctuate by EUR 45 million per EUR 1/MWh change in the realized gas price.
12.
Downstream margin sensitivity: A USD 1/bbl shift in the European refining margin impacts operating results by EUR 110 million.
13.
Cyclical polyolefin pricing: Chemicals revenues are highly exposed to European olefin indicator margins, which fluctuate with global capacity additions.
14.
Regional regulatory caps: Fuel retail margins are regularly compressed by government-imposed price caps in Austria and Romania.

Strategic transformation and management changes

15.
Leadership CEO transition: Emma Delaney will assume the Chief Executive Officer role in September 2026, succeeding Alfred Stern.
16.
Strategy 2030 roadmap: OMV's core strategy centers on transforming from a fossil fuel producer to a circular chemicals leader.
17.
Reduced organic capex: The updated Strategy 2030 targets average yearly organic capex of EUR 2.8 billion between 2026 and 2030.
18.
Lower carbon focus: Derisked capital allocation directs 30% of the EUR 14 billion cumulative 2026–2030 capex to sustainable projects.
19.
Gas as growth engine: if Neptun Deep delivers on time in 2027, it will add 70 kboe/d of low-cost production.
20.
Mid-term efficiency target: OMV targets over EUR 500 million in CFFO savings by 2027 through its group-wide efficiency program.

BGI synergy goals: The group targets over USD 500 million in annual EBITDA synergies from the BGI combination by 2030.
