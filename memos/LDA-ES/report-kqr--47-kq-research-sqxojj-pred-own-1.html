Section 2 – Legislative Mechanics of the Baremo Update

Section summary
  1. The Spanish Baremo scale is statutorily tied to the public pension revaluation, which is indexed to the twelve-month average of consumer price inflation.
  2. We have high confidence in these legislative mechanics because the 2021 pension reform establishes a clear, non-discretionary mathematical framework based on official inflation indexes.

Pension Revaluation and Statutory Links

1.
Direct statutory alignment: The Spanish Baremo compensation scale is legally bound to the annual revaluation of public pensions to ensure victims do not lose purchasing power. This legislative integration means that any change in retirement benefits automatically dictates the rate of bodily injury inflation. Consequently, motor insurers face systematic regulatory costs that are directly linked to public pension updates rather than industry-specific trends.
2.
Automatic indexing rules: Spanish public pensions are adjusted annually under the 2021 pension reform framework to protect retirees from consumer price inflation. This statutory framework mandates an automatic adjustment that removes political discretion from the baseline calculation. As a result, insurers must model their long-term bodily injury liabilities based on macroeconomic consumer price index developments across Spain.
3.
Broad societal impact: The annual pension revaluation in Spain directly affects more than 10 million people across the country. Because the Baremo scale is tied to this massive fiscal mechanism, its adjustment has broad political and social significance. This political sensitivity makes it unlikely that the government overrides the CPI indexing rules to favor insurers.
4.
Guaranteed minimum adjustments: The Spanish government ensures that contributory pensions and minimum benefits are legally protected from depreciation. While minimum benefits can receive extra statutory increments, the baseline Baremo scale reflects the standard contributory pension update. This means motor insurers must prepare for a guaranteed floor of claims inflation during periods of high consumer demand.
5.
Persistent CPI pressures: Spain faces persistent inflationary pressures in services and energy products that keep the pension indexation rate elevated. This persistent inflation directly increases the expected cost of future bodily injury settlements through the Baremo link. Consequently, insurers face prolonged underwriting headwinds from CPI unless domestic price pressures cool.

Mathematical Inflation Formula and Timing

6.
Precise indexing formula: The Spanish indexing formula calculates pension revaluations using the average year- on-year inflation over a specific twelve-month cycle. This mathematical average spans from December of the previous year to November of the current year. Actuaries utilize this formula to estimate how bodily injury scales will evolve prior to official ministerial decrees being finalized.
7.
Projected indexation estimates: Spanish research institutions like Funcas estimate the average reference inflation for pension revaluations to guide market expectations. For the 2027 cycle, these preliminary projections help insurers anticipate the rate of claims inflation. This forecasting capability allows motor insurers to adjust their commercial pricing strategies before the final ministerial rate is published.
8.
Specific calendar months: The calculation for the 2027 Baremo update utilizes inflation data specifically from December 2025 to November 2026. The National Institute of Statistics publishes the preliminary November CPI figure in late November to allow early planning. This timeline allows insurers to project future liability severity adjustments before the final rates are formalized.
9.
Preliminary indicator timing: The National Institute of Statistics releases the preliminary CPI figure for November in late November to provide early inflation indicators. This preliminary figure allows analysts to calculate the average twelve-month CPI with high accuracy before the official confirmation. This timing ensures that insurers obtain early visibility into Baremo adjustments before the year-end closing.

Formal publication timeline: The National Institute of Statistics is scheduled to confirm the definitive November inflation data on December 15. Motor insurers rely on this official mid-December date to finalize their yearend reserving calculations for outstanding claims.
