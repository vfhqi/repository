[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = D

Thesis. ORLEN is a cyclical peak dressed as a corporate change. The disciplined management installed in April 2024 has stopped the capital waste, restored governance and raised the dividend to PLN 8.00, and the European refining "Golden Age" has lifted earnings to a high that the market has already paid for at 8.86 times the earnings two years out. From PLN 144.32 the dividend is the only positive leg of the return, because the refining margin is normalising, the state signed a windfall tax on 1-Oct-26 and resumed the retail price cap on 3-Oct-26, and our modal total return is -8.8 percent a year to April 2028 and +0.6 percent a year to October 2029 against a bar of 20 percent a year.

  • The internal change is real, strong and already in the outputs.
    • A new chief executive (April 2024) and a returning finance chief (September 2025) halted Olefins III at a projected PLN 41 to 54 billion and recast it at PLN 35.8 billion, brought capex in under budget two years running, took the company off the Norges Bank exclusion list a year early (July 2025), pursued the legacy managers, and raised the dividend from PLN 6.00 to PLN 8.00 against a PLN 7.20 consensus.
    • These are the inputs of a 'Major Corporate Change' case, and five of the twelve internal forces are at B.
    • The change is two and a half years old.
      • It propagated into the financial statements in 2025 and the first half of 2026, and the next internal step, the 2035 strategy update, has been delayed to Q4 2026.
  • The external forces have turned against the case, and two of them became law in the first week of October 2026.
    • The model refining margin fell from USD 17.0 per barrel in Q1 2026 to USD 14.5 per barrel in Q2 2026, the crude differential halved from about USD 10 per barrel to about USD 5 per barrel into Q3 2026, and the lead broker's path is USD 7.8 per barrel by late 2026 and normalisation by 2027 or 2028.
    • A USD 1.0 per barrel change moves EBITDA by about PLN 0.6 billion, 12 percent of EBITDA.
    • The windfall tax was signed on 1-Oct-26 (a 60 percent levy; a PLN 4 billion budget target; up to PLN 6 billion a year on the state assets minister's estimate).
      • The CPN retail price cap resumed on 3-Oct-26.
      • The Czech Republic announced a retail margin cap and a refinery windfall tax from October 2026.
    • The latest estimate in the consensus series is dated 2026-09-30, so neither October event is in the numbers the price rests on.
  • 'Financial Outputs' are at a cyclical peak, and every forward reading has them falling.
    • Consensus EPS on the brokers' basis goes from PLN 23.44 in FY2026 to PLN 19.36 in FY2027 and PLN 14.93 in FY2028 (medians of 8 brokers)
    • Consensus EBITDA from PLN 53,842 million to PLN 48,193 million to PLN 44,024 million.
    • Our own modal path is lower: PLN 23.0, PLN 17.0, PLN 14.5, then PLN 15.5 in FY2029, a compound rate of about -12 percent a year from FY2026.
    • The sell-side's upgrade cycle has run its course: FY2027 consensus EPS rose 88.9 percent in twelve months and fell 1.8 percent in the last month.
  • The change has travelled all six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, which for this pattern means it is behind the share price, not ahead of it.
    • People, choices and actions moved in 2024
    • The operating metrics, the financial statements and the sell-side's estimates recorded it in 2025 and 2026
    • The shares rose from PLN 101.88 in late 2025 to PLN 150.20 in August 2026, +77.0 percent over twelve months.
    • The contracted catalysts (Baltic Power first power in July 2026, Grudziądz, the PLN 8.00 dividend, the Equinor contract) are delivered or announced.
  • What the price assumes is a margin that stays above mid-cycle into 2028, or a market that keeps paying 8.7 times for a falling number.
    • At 8.86 times the 24-month-forward consensus EPS of PLN 16.29 the shares sit at the 82nd percentile of their own five-year range (3.98x to 10.73x, median 6.07x) and between the ten-year 'prudent' 7.80x and 'defendable' 8.70x bands.
    • At the ten-year median of 7.80x the price implies 24-month-forward EPS of about PLN 18.5, above consensus.
    • What is not priced: the windfall tax, the price cap, the 2027 capex peak of PLN 45 to 48 billion, and a normal multiple on normalised earnings.
  • The modal return does not approach the bar, and neither does the bull branch.
    • On our path the 24-month-forward EPS is PLN 15.7 at April 2028 and PLN 16.4 at October 2029, the multiple de-rates to 7.5x, and the dividend of PLN 8.00 a year is the whole of the positive return: a value of PLN 125.75 including cash returns at eighteen months (-8.8 percent a year) and PLN 147.00 at three years (+0.6 percent a year).
    • Hold the multiple at 8.86x and the return is +1.1 percent and +5.5 percent a year, still an F.
    • Grant the bull the margin and a held 8.7x and the eighteen-month return is +9.2 percent a year, a D.
    • A plausibly worse drop-through from EBITDA to EPS (the windfall tax at PLN 6 billion a year and interest on PLN 60.9 billion of net debt) takes the eighteen-month return to -18.2 percent a year.
      • The downside has no floor from the earnings, only from the dividend.
  • The pattern is 'Major Corporate Change', fitted at a D, because the pattern's defining output, major EPS trajectory upgrades, was delivered in 2025 and the first half of 2026 and the next trajectory move is down.
    • The chain holds from the inputs to the outputs
    • It breaks at the link between the outputs and the return, because the outputs are about to fall and the multiple already pays for them.

  • The case is therefore a park on the return alone, before any of its risks is weighed.

    • The dividend and the balance sheet make it a tolerable holding
    • The framework's bar makes it not a case.

  • Rating = D, because the change that is strong is internal and already in the outputs, the change that is coming is external and adverse, and the 'Modal return' from this price is an F: the three Elements combine as a chain in which the first link ('Change Forces', C) no longer drives the second ('Financial Outputs', D) in the direction the third ('Modal return', F) needs.

    • Not a C, because materiality here means a 20 percent a year return and no branch of the case we can evidence reaches it.
    • Not an F, because the internal change forces are genuine, the dividend is real and the business is not in distress.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The strongest form is the prolonged downstream "Golden Age": European refining closures of 2.5 million barrels a day, the loss of Russian product and crude, Hormuz and Red Sea disruption, OMV and MOL printing indicator margins of USD 20.30 and USD 20.80 per barrel in Q2 2026, Erste's bonanza running into 2027, BOS's USD 27, 21 and 16 per barrel path for 2026 to 2028, and ORLEN's hedging of only 20 percent of its cracks, which leaves the upside open.
    • On that view the margin stays above USD 13 per barrel through 2027, EPS holds near PLN 20 through FY2028, and the stock at 8.9x with a 5.5 percent yield is cheap.
    • Concession: the margin may stay above mid-cycle through 2027.
      • We rate that possible, about one in three.
    • Our answer is arithmetic.
      • On the bull's own path (FY2027 EPS about PLN 21.0, FY2028 about PLN 18.5, FY2029 and FY2030 PLN 18.0, FY2031 PLN 18.5; our construction of the bull's case), the 24-month-forward EPS at April 2028 is about PLN 18.0, and at a held 8.7x the value is PLN 156.6 plus PLN 8.00 of dividend, PLN 164.6, +14.1 percent over eighteen months, +9.2 percent a year.
      • At three years it is PLN 183.9 including PLN 24.00 of dividends, +8.4 percent a year.
      • The competing hypothesis, granted in full, is a D on the framework's anchors.
    • A B needs two conditions: the margin held above USD 13 per barrel through 2027 (possible, about one in three) and a 'mania' multiple of 10.0x for a state-taxed refiner (unlikely, about one in seven; 10.0x is above the five-year 90th percentile of 9.47x).
      • The conditions are not independent, so the joint odds sit above their product
      • We put them at about one in ten, which is unlikely.
      • The bull case is a D at best
      • The modal case is an F.
Change Forces: are the input change forces sufficiently strong? (E 1, BB 1) Rating = C

External forces: are there sufficiently strong external forces acting on the company, new or intensifying, and in which direction? (RA 1, E 1, BB 1) Rating = D
  • Rating = D, because the external forces are strong and intensifying but the net direction is a headwind: the refining cycle is turning down from its peak, the state has moved from mooted to enacted extraction in the last week, and the one clear tailwind, tight European product balances, is cyclical with less than two years' visibility.
  • Refining margin: the single largest driver of group earnings has turned.
    • The model refining margin was USD 17.0 per barrel in Q1 2026 and USD 14.5 per barrel in Q2 2026, against Morgan Stanley's USD 18.80 per barrel estimate for the quarter.
    • The crude differential fell from about USD 10 per barrel in Q2 2026 to about USD 5 per barrel in Q3 2026.
    • Morgan Stanley expects USD 7.8 per barrel by late 2026 and normalisation by 2027 or 2028
    • The historical normalised level cited is USD 4 per barrel and the projected mid-cycle USD 8 to 9 per barrel.
    • A USD 1.0 per barrel change moves EBITDA by about PLN 0.6 billion, 12 percent of EBITDA.
  • State extraction: it has become law.
    • The windfall tax bill was signed on 1-Oct-26 with a 60 percent levy and a PLN 4 billion budget revenue target
    • The state assets minister estimates the impact on ORLEN at about PLN 6 billion a year
    • Erste's reading of it as repackaged corporate income tax is PLN 4.1 billion cumulative over 2027 to 2029.
    • The CPN retail price cap resumed on 3-Oct-26, forcing petrol prices down by up to 32 groszy a litre.
    • The Czech Republic announced a retail margin cap of 2.5 crowns a litre and a planned 50 percent refinery windfall tax from October 2026, a possible EUR 226 million burden.
  • Tailwinds: real but cyclical.
    • European refining closures of 2.5 million barrels a day support tight Central and Eastern European balances
    • Strait of Hormuz tensions pushed Brent past USD 120 per barrel in early 2026 and it averaged USD 103.80 in Q2 2026
    • OMV's indicator margin was USD 20.30 per barrel and MOL's USD 20.80 in Q2 2026.
    • Erste (13-Aug-26) argues the refining margin bonanza could run into 2027.
  • Structural supports: Norwegian gas and Baltic Pipe capacity booked to 2037
    • EU recovery funds of PLN 3.5 billion for Baltic Power and PLN 1.7 billion for hydrogen
    • RED III mandates that favour integrated players.
  • Not a C, because the two decisive forces for the next eighteen months, margin normalisation and enacted extraction, are both adverse and both evidenced, and the tailwind is the one the research itself calls unsustainable.

[Robustness of] Foundations upon which the Investment Thesis is built upon (BB 2) Rating = C

The 'Foundations' support the business's survival and its dividend well, and support a positive earnings change poorly, because the actor that matters most in this value chain is the Polish state, and the state takes the margin at every peak.

  • The industry structure is as supportive as a refining and fuels market gets.
    • ORLEN holds 60 percent of Polish refined products, 54 percent of Czech wholesale and 87 percent of the Baltic market
    • No competitor has built greenfield refining in Central and Eastern Europe in fifteen years
    • European capacity is shrinking by 2.5 million barrels a day
    • Competitors are calm, margin-prioritising and weakening.
    • This protects the volumes and the dividend.
    • It does not protect the margin.
  • The sovereign overrides the structure at the point the thesis needs it to stand aside.
    • The State Treasury at 49.9 percent is owner, customer, regulator, tax collector and price-setter.
    • In two years it took PLN 15,417 million through the 2024 compensation fund, capped retail prices twice in 2026 (March to June, and from 3-Oct-26), signed a windfall tax on 1-Oct-26, and appointed two board members directly.
    • A duopoly whose price is set by the government is not an oligopoly in behaviour.
    • The fragility is political and dated: an election in autumn 2027, a sovereign with a deficit above 6 percent of GDP, a Moody's downgrade to A3 in September 2026.
  • The operator has improved materially and provably since April 2024, and operates inside a structure it does not control.
    • Capex came in under budget two years running, Olefins III was halted, the Norges Bank exclusion ended a year early, legacy managers were pursued, and the dividend was paid above consensus.
    • The same management cannot set its own board, its own pay (the chief executive earned PLN 2.97 million in 2025, below the tenth percentile of peers) or its own retail price, holds 1,743 shares, and has postponed its own strategy update to Q4 2026.
  • The business's financial outputs are those of a low-return, politically taxed conglomerate.
    • ROACE was 2.0 percent in 2025, the net margin 1.0 percent in 2024 and 2025, the reported tax rate 75.0 percent in FY2025, petrochemicals lost PLN 6,302 million of EBIT in FY2025 and write-downs were PLN 16.3 billion.
    • The hard moats (scale, infrastructure, the energy-security mandate) are real and cannot be monetised through the state's price and tax regime
    • The same ownership that guarantees the moat guarantees the extraction.

  • So the 'Foundations' make the dividend and the balance sheet robust and the earnings improvement fragile.

    • The thesis needs sustained earnings from a margin the company does not control and a return on new capital the business has not shown (green projects at 8 to 9 percent IRR against a 10 to 12 percent hurdle).
    • The foundations hold under the part of the thesis that is already delivered and give way under the part that is still to come.

  • ORLEN is a cyclical peak dressed as a corporate change.

  • The case is not compelling.
  • It is a park.
