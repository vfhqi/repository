Business Model and Sector Positioning

Market position – core banking software leader: Founded in 1993 and headquartered in Geneva, Temenos AG is a global banking technology vendor operating across 38 countries with over 2,000 installations in 140+ countries. Core deployment flexibility – choice across models: The company provides core banking software across three deployment models, including on-premise, public/hybrid cloud, and Software-as-a-Service (SaaS), serving banks from Tier 1 global institutions down to Tier 5 regional banks and neobanks.

Addressable market footprint – $25bn SAM: Temenos operates within a core $25bn Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) that is projected to grow at a 7% CAGR to $30bn by FY28, with 40% of absolute growth driven by outsourcing of in-house bank IT. Sector market share – top-ranked industry vendor: Industry data from IBS Intelligence places Temenos as the #1 global core banking software provider for 21 consecutive years, holding a stable global market share.

Product Stack and Modernization Strategy

Core platform – Transact core banking: The flagship Transact platform serves as the central system-of-record engine, providing real-time, API-first processing for retail, corporate, and private banking. Front-office experience – Temenos Infinity: The digital front-office platform handles onboarding, origination, and multi-channel engagement, though expert commentary notes front-end market penetration has lagged core system sales. Progressive modernization – composable core solutions: Launched at the 2026 Temenos Community Forum (TCF), new composable deposit and lending modules allow Tier 1 and Tier 2 banks to modernize core capability incrementally without risky "big bang" migrations. SaaS and cloud infrastructure – cloud-native transition: Temenos SaaS operations run on Microsoft Azure and AWS, providing auto-scaling and lower total cost of ownership for regional and digital banks. Wealth management expansion – acquisition of additiv: Completed on July 17, 2026, the acquisition of Swiss fintech additiv expands Temenos' footprint into mass-affluent wealth management and adds an AI-driven journey orchestration layer.

Historical 3-Year Fundamental Trajectory

Revenue and ARR evolution – transition to recurring revenue: Proforma total non-IFRS revenues grew from $1,044m in FY24 to $1,071m in FY25, while Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) expanded 15% in FY25 to reach $860m, representing over 90% of product revenue.

Profitability and margins – operational leverage expansion: Non-IFRS EBIT expanded from $305m (31.6% margin) in FY24 to $372m (34.7% margin) in FY25, driven by cost efficiency programs initiated in H2 2024.

Free cash flow generation – highly cash-generative profile: Free cash flow grew 15% in FY25 to $256m, supported by a shift toward subscription and SaaS licensing, ending FY25 with leverage at 1.2x net debt to EBITDA.

Medium-term financial targets – raised FY28 guidance: At the February 2026 CMD, management raised its organic FY28 targets to ARR >$1.23bn, non-IFRS EBIT of ~$480m (36% margin), and FCF of >$410m.
