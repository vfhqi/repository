EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 2 findings

MATERIALITY OF CHANGE Hiab completed the transformative USD 1,035 million Labrie Environmental Group acquisition on July 1, 2026, increasing Americas share of consolidated sales from 43% in 2025 to 54% in 2027 and establishing Environmental Vehicle Solutions as a fourth business area focused on highly predictable waste and recycling markets. Hiab restructured from six divisions into three business areas on April 1, 2026, implementing a decentralized operating model that targets sales and R&D actions to eliminate regional operational bottlenecks and accelerate time-to-market following the standalone demerger.

Hiab is closing its ZEPRO tail-lift factory in Bispgården, Sweden, and relocating manufacturing to Stargard, Poland, with the supply chain program targeting EUR 20 million in annualized savings to structurally expand margins during the 2026 period.

Scott Phillips became President and CEO on April 1, 2025, while the Board size was reduced to seven members and Ilkka Herlin declined re-election in 2026, ending decades of direct board-level family oversight, signaling a shift toward professional governance structures. The Labrie acquisition represents a multiple of 9.2x March 2026 LTM comparable EBITDA but financing via EUR 900 million debt pushes pro forma gearing to 70%, exceeding the 50% target threshold, creating nearterm deleveraging pressure through H2 2026.

KEY DRIVERS (RANKED) Driver 1 – Labrie margin expansion and integration (Impact: 36% EBITA lift, Plausibility: HIGH): Labrie generated USD 83 million in comparable operating profit on USD 491 million in sales for the LTM period ended March 2026, with management expecting a 17% comparable EBIT margin during H2 2026 and DNB Carnegie projecting Labrie to lift Hiab's 2027 adjusted EBITA by 36%. Driver 2 – Zepro manufacturing relocation (Impact: EUR 20 million savings, Plausibility: HIGH): The consolidation program targets EUR 20 million in annualized structural P&L savings starting in 2026, though total restructuring expenses will reach approximately EUR 30 million with EUR 18 million already booked through H1 2026.

Driver 3 – Services aftermarket expansion (Impact: EUR 700 million target, Plausibility: HIGH): Hiab targets EUR 700 million in service sales by 2028, supported by 56,000 connected fleet units in 2025 tracking toward 90,000 by 2028 and Services segment recording a 26.1% comparable operating margin in Q2 2026.

Driver 4 – Eco-portfolio expansion (Impact: 50% sales share, Plausibility: HIGH-MEDIUM): Eco-portfolio sales reached 45% of total sales in Q2 2026, totaling EUR 181 million, with Hiab targeting a 50% eco-portfolio share by 2028 to satisfy strict carbon reduction regulations and commanding premium pricing for sustainable load-handling solutions.

Driver 5 – US industrial and Class 8 recovery (Impact: 13% volume lift, Plausibility: MEDIUM): North American Class 8 truck orders grew 8% YoY in early 2026, while Hiab secured a EUR 37 million Moffett forklift order in Q2 2026 from a US home improvement retail customer, signaling a cyclical turning point in delivery equipment demand. RECENT DELIVERY

Orders received surged to EUR 437 million, up 16% YoY and beating peer-median expectations, with organic orders increasing by 13% in constant currencies, while comparable operating profit reached EUR 61 million, up 1% YoY, representing a solid 15.1% margin in Q2 2026. Hiab delivered Q2 2026 comparable operating margin of 15.1% vs 14.1% consensus, beating expectations by 100 bps, and management raised FY 2026 margin floor to above 14.5% from 13.5%, confirming systematic conservative guidance and strong operational execution.

Services sales increased by 4% to EUR 123 million driven primarily by recurring contracts, with services comparable operating margin improving to 26.1%, offsetting Equipment sales falling by 1% to EUR 281 million dragged down by lower US deliveries. FORWARD CREDIBILITY

Hiab's management aligns with the classic sandbagger archetype, consistently establishing conservative floor-level profitability targets, as evidenced by 2024 comparable EBIT margin floor of above 12% being exceeded with a final print of 14.9%, establishing high credibility through systematic beats. Book-to-bill turned positive in all geographic areas for two consecutive quarters, confirming a demand recovery, while the Labrie acquisition brings a substantial USD 435 million order backlog to sustain Environmental Vehicle growth, providing multi-quarter revenue visibility.

SELL-SIDE POSITIONING

The active sell-side coverage pool is highly concentrated among specialized Nordic brokers led primarily by DNB Carnegie, with Morgan Stanley officially discontinuing active coverage of the standalone entity in July 2022, limiting global institutional research penetration. DNB Carnegie analyst Tom Skogman maintains a Buy rating and raised his target price to EUR 75, arguing that margins could soon reach record highs due to EUR 20 million in cost savings, while rating mix shows 57% Buy and 39% Hold.

Hiab trades at 13.4x 2026e EV/EBITA, which is justified by its asset-light business model, representing a substantial premium to Palfinger's multiple of 9.3x based on superior capital efficiency.

FUNDAMENTAL PEER POSITION

Hiab's comparable operating margin of 15.1% outperforms Palfinger's actual EBIT margin of 7.4% by 770 bps, while Hiab's operative return on capital employed reached 25.0% LTM, significantly outperforming Palfinger's actual ROCE of 15% Strategy 2030+ target, confirming top-quartile fundamental positioning.

Services represent 30% of sales and 51% of comparable EBIT at Hiab, compared to Palfinger's service share of sales declining to 17.4% in early 2026, providing Hiab with a superior defensive buffer against equipment cyclicality.
