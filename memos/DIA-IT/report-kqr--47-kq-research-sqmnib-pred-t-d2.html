Section summary
  1. DiaSorin's financial performance faces a critical transition as concentrated macro and competitive drivers threaten to widen the company's future earnings range.

Core Investigation

1.
Primary inquiry: "DiaSorin S.p.A. (DIA-IT), an Italian in-vitro diagnostics maker of closed analyzers and the reagents run on them, reporting three segments: Immunodiagnostics (LIAISON chemiluminescence analyzers and a specialty assay menu, including the automated latent tuberculosis QuantiFERON workflow partnered with QIAGEN), Molecular Diagnostics (LIAISON MDX, LIAISON PLEX syndromic multiplexing and the LIAISON NES point-of-care system) and Licensed Technologies (Luminex xMAP microspheres, instruments and royalties). Section 2 – Molecular Segment Growth and LIAISON Platform Placements

Section summary
  1. DiaSorin molecular growth relies on LIAISON PLEX and NES commercial execution.
  2. Peer performance at bioMérieux and Cepheid indicates high volatility from respiratory seasonal testing.
  3. Instrument manufacturer Stratec provides critical pipeline reads on DiaSorin analyzer placement trajectories.

Multiplex Platform Acceleration

1.
Promising acceleration: DiaSorin reported that its automated multiplexing segment grew by 36% at constant exchange rates in Q3-2026 compared to Q3-2025. This notable performance was primarily driven by the commercial expansion of the LIAISON PLEX platform and the rollout of its gastrointestinal panel. Consequently, this growth has helped offset a weak respiratory testing market before the upcoming winter season. Management expects PLEX to be a key driver of molecular growth going forward.
2.
High customization: DiaSorin reached over 170 PLEX customers by the end of Q3-2026, representing an expansion from 150 customers earlier in the year. Customer contract dynamics show that roughly 65% of clients opted for the flexible pricing panel. This high adoption of customizable mini-panels shows that hospitals favor clinical diagnostic stewardship over rigid fixed menus. Thus, custom panel selection represents a major differentiator against competitors.
3.
Intense competition: Comparable point-of-care peer bioMérieux installed over 900 SPOTFIRE systems during Q3- 2026 alone. This rapid expansion took bioMérieux's total point-of-care installed base to nearly 8,200 systems, growing 50% year-over-year. In comparison, DiaSorin has built a smaller initial footprint for LIAISON NES since its April 2026 launch. Therefore, DiaSorin faces a highly crowded point-of-care environment where firstmovers dominate commercial clinics.
4.
Contrary evidence: Deutsche Bank reported that DiaSorin's multiplexing acceleration in Q3-2026 was largely independent of the summer US Cyclospora outbreak. This implies that the 36% growth rate was driven by genuine platform placements rather than temporary epidemiology. However, some analysts remain skeptical that DiaSorin can achieve its targeted molecular growth of double digits without significant winter flu seasons.

Point of Care Launch Trajectory

5.
Strategic distribution: DiaSorin initiated the US launch of LIAISON NES in April 2026 through partnership agreements with McKesson and Fisher Scientific. These distribution networks are designed to expand nearpatient molecular testing across outpatient networks. Approximately 40% of early NES placements successfully converted customers from manual antigen testing to rapid PCR. However, the platform's commercial pullthrough remains heavily dependent on upcoming winter respiratory panels.
6.
Progressive clearance: DiaSorin received FDA clearance and CLIA waiver for its LIAISON NES Group A Strep assay in 2026. This second clearance completes the critical immediate-care menu alongside the initial four-plex respiratory panel launched in early 2026. The company aims to leverage this expanded menu to increase system utilization rates. Nevertheless, menu breadth remains narrow compared to established competitor platforms.

Favorable feedback: An expert call with an integrated delivery network director confirmed that LIAISON NES offers an attractive workflow and pricing. The system performs highly automated four-plex molecular testing in approximately fifteen minutes at room temperature storage. However, the expert noted that long-term clinical adoption depends on launching wider non-respiratory panels. Hence, menu execution is more critical than initial hardware cost.

Section 3 – Currency Fluctuations and Tariff Refund Dynamics

Section summary
  1. DiaSorin maintains a highly predictable sensitivity to EUR/USD fluctuations, where a one-cent movement impacts revenues by €6 million to €8 million.
  2. Non-recurring US tariff refunds provided a significant EBITDA margin tailwind in 2026 but will not recur as a core driver in 2027.
  3. Unlike DiaSorin's transactionfocused hedging, peer Roche operates with entirely unhedged translation exposure, exposing its reported results to Swiss franc strength.

Currency Sensitivity and Rules of Thumb

1.
Favorable tailwinds: DiaSorin experienced a significant shift in currency dynamics during the third quarter of 2026, recording a €5 million foreign exchange tailwind at current exchange rates. This positive movement contrasted sharply with the severe currency headwinds of €17 million in the first quarter and €3 million in the second quarter of 2026. Deutsche Bank adjusted its outer-year estimates upward to incorporate this more favorable foreign exchange environment, illustrating how exchange rate volatility directly shifts the baseline for future earnings calculations. Established sensitivity: DiaSorin management reiterates a formal corporate rule of thumb to help analysts model the financial impact of EUR/USD currency fluctuations. Under this rule, every $0.01 movement of the US dollar against the euro over a 12-month period impacts annual revenues by €6 million to €8 million. Furthermore, this same one-cent fluctuation carries a corresponding impact on adjusted EBITDA of approximately €2 million to €3 million. - Earnings Calls 100 Guidance parameters: DiaSorin based its initial 2026 financial guidance on an average expected EUR/USD exchange rate of 1.18, which was sourced from Bloomberg consensus in early 2026. However, during the nine months ended September 2026, management observed that if the exchange rate stabilized at approximately 1.11 to 1.12, the full-year negative FX impact would drop to €15 million. Alberto Donati noted that if the dollar strengthened further to a rate of 1.10, the total estimated foreign exchange headwind for the year would decrease to just €10 million.
2.
Hedging implementation: DiaSorin manages its translation and transaction risks through targeted financial contracts rather than speculative trading. To mitigate foreign exchange risks from currency fluctuations, the company executes currency forward sales. As of June 30, 2026, these currency forward sales were recognized in the financial statements at a net negative fair value of €1.75 million, compared to a negative fair value of €0.33 million recorded at the end of December 2025.

Peer Currency Comparison

3.
Unhedged translation: Peer Roche Diagnostics operates under a contrasting foreign exchange management strategy that leaves translation exposure unhedged. While Roche utilizes forward contracts and options to hedge transaction exposures arising from foreign currency cash flows, it does not hedge translation risk. Consequently, the steady appreciation of the Swiss franc against major currencies, particularly the US dollar, has historically created a massive gap between Roche's constant-currency and reported results.
4.
Quantifiable impacts: Roche reported that its core operating profit is highly sensitive to currency translation, with a 1% change in the US dollar exchange rate against the Swiss franc causing a CHF 52 million impact. This unhedged translation risk was evident in the first half of 2026, where Roche's core operating profit grew by 10% at constant exchange rates but declined by 1% when reported in Swiss francs. Similarly, core earnings per share rose by 9% at constant exchange rates but fell by 2% in reported Swiss franc terms.
5.
Broker assessment: Morgan Stanley noted that updated foreign exchange rates in early 2026 created a 6% headwind on Roche's reported sales and an 8% headwind on its reported core EPS. This translation drag was higher than the initial headwinds of 4% on sales and 6% on core EPS projected at the end of 2025. 113 By the third quarter of 2026, currency translation headwinds stabilized slightly, prompting Roche to guide toward a full-year negative sales impact of 3% and a core EPS impact of 5%. US Tariff Refund Dynamics

6.
Non-recurring windfalls: DiaSorin's profitability in 2026 was significantly bolstered by cash refunds relating to duties previously paid under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA). Following a March 5, 2026, nationwide injunction from the US Court of International Trade, US Customs was ordered to refund these tariffs. DiaSorin received a partial tariff refund of $2.50 million in the second quarter of 2026, which was part of the approximately $9 million in total cash paid under the historical IEEPA tariffs.
7.
Margin enhancement: DiaSorin received an additional tariff refund of approximately €5 million during the third quarter of 2026. This Q3-2026 refund contributed approximately 1.50 percentage points to the quarterly adjusted EBITDA margin, elevating it to 34% at constant exchange rates. Alberto Donati confirmed that excluding this non-recurring refund, the underlying Q3-2026 adjusted EBITDA margin would have been between 32% and 33%, aligning with the company's full-year guidance.
8.
Sustained inflation: Morgan Stanley adjusted its financial models to reflect the non-repeat of these tariff refunds in 2027. While the €5 million refund provided a clear bridge for 2026 margins, its absence in 2027, combined with rising inflation in plastics, raw materials, and logistics, is expected to compress margins next year. Although DiaSorin continues to pursue any remaining eligible tariff refunds, the timing and approval of future disbursements remain highly uncertain. 124 - Earnings Calls Contrary evidence: Deutsche Bank raised its outer-year adjusted EBITDA and EPS forecasts for DiaSorin by 3% and 4%, respectively, to account for a more favorable currency environment and the recognized tariff reimbursements. This adjustment suggests that if the US dollar remains structurally stronger than the EUR/USD 1.18 baseline, the positive currency transaction tailwind could fully offset the margin drag from the nonrepeat of the IEEPA tariff refunds.

Collaborative monetization: DiaSorin and QIAGEN share a highly integrated partnership that underpins the automated QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus workflow on LIAISON systems. This high-margin immunodiagnostics testing solution serves as a critical revenue engine for both diagnostics firms. The automated platform allows high-throughput labs to replace traditional skin tests, capturing premium pricing in a growing market. This collaborative setup secures predictable reagent volumes that directly enhance longterm earnings per share.
2.
Robust cash generation: QIAGEN maintains a leading global market position in tuberculosis testing where its QuantiFERON tests represent the gold standard. This strong competitive position helps secure high recurring diagnostic revenues across joint LIAISON installations. The partnership continues to capture steady volume expansion as laboratories convert from historical manual skin testing. As a result, the franchise remains the primary driver of immunodiagnostics segment profitability for DiaSorin.

Slowing expansion: QIAGEN experienced a temporary growth slowdown in early 2026 due to the rebasing of US immigration testing.
