EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

AB Sagax operates under exceptional founder-led governance with David Mindus maintaining continuous CEO tenure since 2004 and massive personal shareholding alignment, yet the company officially slashed its mediumterm return on equity target from over 15% to at least 12% and reduced profit growth ambitions to 5-10% annually in April 2026, signaling strategic recalibration as the portfolio matures beyond SEK 68 billion and capital allocation increasingly pivots toward defensive retail parks while funding relies heavily on non-voting Class D shares to protect founder voting control.

The external operating environment presents mounting headwinds as economic occupancy softens regionally with Sweden and Finland deteriorating, aggressive competitor consolidation persists while Sagax's low implied yields cap near-term re-rating potential, tightening environmental and tax regulations elevate compliance costs, yet persistent supply constraints protect landlord pricing power and the European logistics cycle is stabilizing for gradual rental recovery from late 2027, though simultaneous occupancy declines and rising funding costs could trigger severe valuation write-downs.

Financial profile dynamics reveal structurally decelerating rental revenue and net operating income expansion as organic growth slows and portfolio scale limits transaction velocity, with rental revenue growing only 2.8% in Q1 2026 versus 7.7% in FY25, while headline leverage metrics tighten as net debt to EBITDA rises to 6.0x and interest coverage deteriorates to 3.9x, though robust operating cash flow generation protects the balance sheet despite understated economic LTV calculated at 57% far exceeding company-reported 44%, creating potential credit rating pressure if occupancy drops further.

Historical earnings trajectory over the last three years demonstrates steady expansion through indexation and roll-ups but reveals clear deceleration as like-for-like rental growth collapsed to 0.5% in Q1 2026 from 3.9% in FY24, profit from property management per share fell 2.3% year-on-year, economic occupancy slipped to 94.7% with Sweden dropping to 91.4%, while earnings quality remains highly dependent on indexation mechanisms that are increasingly vulnerable to roll-offs as 29% of leases expire in 2026-2027, exposing tight interest coverage ratios if rental growth stalls.

Most recent Q1 2026 earnings delivery revealed deteriorating underlying operational metrics marked by surging vacancies and stalling organic growth, with profit from property management of SEK 2.85 per share representing a 2.3% year-on-year decline and falling below consensus estimates, while recurring cash flow per share collapsed 13.0% due to higher taxes and the group vacancy rate spiked 110 basis points quarter-on-quarter to 5.3%, even as management reiterated full-year guidance of SEK 4.5 billion representing minimal 1.3% growth constrained by strengthening Swedish Krona and rising debt costs.

Peer-relative earnings momentum analysis reveals Sagax underperforms its logistics peer group in organic rental momentum as local structural bottlenecks drag like-for-like growth to sector lows of 0.5% versus CTP's 4.6% and SEGRO's 6.0%, while CTP and SLP dominate top momentum rankings through rapid high-yield acquisition-led expansion with revenue growth of 12.3% and 26.0% respectively, though leverage limits tighten across the Nordic sector and apparent peer outperformance remains highly dependent on capital market access that would face severe pressure if equity pricing drops.
