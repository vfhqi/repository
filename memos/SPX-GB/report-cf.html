EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

The materiality of change at Spirax Group PLC centers on executive transitions that delivered stable corporate continuity while introducing leaders with substantially less ground-up steam-sector experience than historic predecessors, including CEO Nimesh Patel who assumed the role in January 2024 after serving as CFO since September 2020, succeeding ten-year CEO Nicholas Anderson. Board refreshes improved oversight through the addition of Tim Cobbold as Chair in January 2025, bringing extensive experience as CEO of three listed groups including Chloride, De La Rue, and UBM, yet highlighted governance complexity and cultural friction during the ongoing restructuring phase. The forty-two percentage point gap substantially exceeds typical seasonal working capital swings observed across diversified industrial peers, particularly given that management targets operating cash conversion above 80% and delivered 89% cash conversion in 2025.

While peer Weir Group reported working capital climbing to 26.7% of sales on H1 production transfers, the magnitude of Spirax's deviation suggests either aggressive inventory positioning beyond disclosed geopolitical hedging or unacknowledged collection difficulties in key regional markets. This outlier warrants heightened scrutiny given the absence of proportional inventory disclosure detail in interim statements.
