Section 4 – Re-Rated vs. Non-Re-Rated Peer Case Studies

Section summary
  1. Valuation re-ratings for serial acquirers are driven primarily by low balance sheet leverage and durable organic growth.
  2. Structural factors like high financial leverage and cyclical segment exposure can severely compress public trading multiples.

Drivers of multiple expansion

1.

This organic turnaround combined with nine year-to-date acquisitions supported a c45% share price rally and multiple expansion because it proved the durability of the compounder model.
2.
Moderate balance sheet leverage: Low leverage and strong cash generation allow premier consolidators to maintain active transaction pipelines across economic cycles. Redeye Research's note of 24 July 2026 highlighted Indutrade's interest-bearing net debt to EBITDA of 1.7x and strong cash conversion. This comfortable debt profile enabled the group to complete nine acquisitions in the first half of 2026, which means investor confidence remained highly resilient and sustained its premium public trading multiple.
3.
Judges Scientific historical inflection: Historical inflections at Judges Scientific illustrate that multiple expansion occurs when M&A returns after a period of operational stabilization. Jefferies Research's note of 3 September 2024 reported that the group's disciplined buy-and-build model delivered a 23% EBIT CAGR from 2005 to 2025. This exceptional track record of buying niche businesses at 3x to 7x EBIT allowed the group to trade at premium multiples because consistent capital allocation drove massive accretion.

Drivers of flat or compressed multiples

4.
AddLife leverage constraint: Stretched balance sheets directly restrict a serial acquirer's transaction capacity and compress its trading multiple. DNB Carnegie's report of 28 June 2024 noted that AddLife's net debt to EBITDA reached 3.5x at the end of 2023, which severely hampered its acquisition pace. SDI Group multiple compression: SDI Group highlights that disciplined deal pricing cannot expand public multiples if the group lacks the scale of larger peers. Deutsche Bank Research's note of 20 February 2026 reported that SDI Group's shares traded at a modest forward P/E multiple of 11.6x despite acquiring PRP Optoelectronics. This low valuation persisted because the market remained highly focused on short-term contract delays and smaller company size, which suggests that transactional execution alone cannot trigger a valuation re-rating.

DiscoverIE cyclicality exposure: Exposure to cyclical segments can trap a serial acquirer's multiple even when transactional execution is strong. Jefferies Research's report of 30 October 2024 downgraded its price target for discoverIE because organic revenue growth slowed significantly. Structural differences and segment analysis

8.

Judges Scientific's interim results of 23 September 2026 reported that Materials Sciences operating profit declined to £5.2 million while the Vacuum segment remained more resilient at £8.1 million. This divergence shows that vacuum-based technologies often benefit from higher aftermarket demand, whereas Materials Sciences is more vulnerable to volatile public and university funding cycles.
9.
Deal size dynamics: Executing larger acquisitions introduces integration risks that can depress a compounder's multiple compared to lower-risk bolt-on strategies. Jefferies Research's report of 4 March 2025 noted that Judges' larger acquisitions, such as Scientifica and Armfield, delivered lackluster operating margins postacquisition. This highlights that outsized transactions often require significant restructuring, which means highly disciplined small bolt-on deals are far more supportive of steady multiple expansion.
10.
Other side of the story: Although low leverage and strong organic growth are the primary drivers of multiple expansion, macroeconomic factors like high global interest rates can cap valuations across the entire consolidator sector. Jefferies Research's note of 2 November 2023 warned that the unhelpful interest rate environment tested even high-quality compounder models. Consequently, peer multiples will compress regardless of individual operational excellence if elevated debt costs restrict the availability of cheap capital for buy-and-build execution.
