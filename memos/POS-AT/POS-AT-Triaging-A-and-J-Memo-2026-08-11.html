B.1 Business Description 8 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does. PORR AG is an Austrian-headquartered European construction group delivering design, engineering and civil-engineering, transport-infrastructure and specialised building construction across seven home markets
2.
Segment architecture. The group runs a decentralised five-segment model, AT/CH, Germany, Poland, CEE (Czech Republic, Slovakia, Romania) and Infrastructure International, with central corporate functions housed in parent PORR AG
3.
Scale. FY25 revenue reached EUR 6,295.9m (+1.7% year on year from EUR 6,190.50m), production output EUR 6,818.00m (+1.0%), and the group employed an average of 20,829 staff in 2025
4.
Margin character. FY25 EBIT margin was 3.1%, up from 2.6% in FY24, against a stated 2030 target of 3.5% to 4.0%; this still trails the pure-contracting peer median of 3.8%
5.
Balance sheet. PORR ended FY25 with a net cash position of EUR 93.1m (from net debt of EUR 1.7m in FY24) and free cash flow of EUR 232.9m (+68.6%)
6.
Geography. AT/CH generates approximately 49% of group sales but 68% of EBIT; Germany is 16% of sales at under 2.0% market share; Poland is a fast-growing 14% of output
7.
Order book. The order backlog broke EUR 10bn for the first time (EUR 10,004m at Q1 2026), securing multi-year revenue visibility, with the mix 60.6% civil engineering, 33.6% building, 7.5% residential
8.
Investment style implication. The business-foundations read frames PORR as a cyclical public-infrastructure beneficiary rather than a compounder, an event/cyclical-driven case, not a steady structural-growth story
