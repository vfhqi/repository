Cover each of these sub-sectors and name the European listed companies in each: regulated electricity and gas networks, water utilities, integrated utilities, nuclear operators and their suppliers, hydro, renewable developers, power storage, district heating, waste,

airports, toll roads, ports and rail infrastructure. Do not answer with broad macro forces (interest rates, the oil price, economic growth, artificial intelligence in general); go one to three levels below them. This is a brainstorm for a stock picker: find the six to ten most exciting, plausible stories specific to a sub-sector or group of similar companies that specialists (experts on expert calls, specialist sell-side analysts, managements, regulators, the trade press) are starting to talk about, where things are improving now and should keep improving over the next 6 to 12 months, and where the story is about the next 1 to 4 years or longer being much better than today. Proof is not required; the story must be plausible, intelligent and able to capture investors' imagination.

Look for: a change in regulation or in a regulator's stance; a market being opened or consolidated; a new product, technology or business model creating demand; a multi-year spending programme on one kind of asset; a shortage of capacity in one niche; a specific use of artificial intelligence that lifts margins or growth for one business model; a national policy or political change that favours one country's companies.

For each story give: one sentence stating it; why it is exciting and how big it could become; the mechanism to revenue, margins and valuation; the sub-sectors and named European listed companies most exposed, especially the purest plays; the first signs so far (orders, prices, volumes, rule texts, management comments, dates); what news over the next 6 to 12 months would feed it; and how widely it is talked about yet.

Rank by how large and how early they are, add three very early stories only a few specialists mention, and note separately any new development that seriously threatens a group."
