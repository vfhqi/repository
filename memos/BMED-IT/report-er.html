Banca Mediolanum delivered record-breaking results for Full Year 2025, driven by an exceptional expansion in net commission income and a substantial bottom-line beat against consensus expectations. The earnings print fundamentally reinforced the core investment thesis that BMED operates a structurally superior asset-gathering franchise anchored in its highly productive Family Banker network, automated client conversion mechanisms, and vertically integrated product manufacturing.

The group delivered a record net income of €1.24 billion for the full year, representing an 11% increase compared to €1.12 billion in fiscal year 2024, while net inflows into managed assets soared 18% to €9.06 billion, comfortably exceeding company guidance of €8.0 billion to €8.5 billion. These exceptional results demonstrate that BMED has successfully transitioned from a rate-driven earnings model to a fee-based growth engine powered by systematic deposit conversion and automated equity reallocation strategies. - Earnings Calls The key change that emerged from the FY2025 results centers on management's demonstrated ability to sustain double-digit net interest income growth while simultaneously accelerating managed asset inflows to record levels.

Net interest income stood flat year-on-year at €812.12 million compared to €811.15 million in 2024, comfortably beating the previous guidance of a 3% decline. More critically, the Intelligent Investment Strategy (IIS) accumulated €5.05 billion in money market assets by the end of 2025, up 76% year-on-year, with new money invested in the strategy reaching €2.2 billion to drive structural asset-gathering momentum. This automated conversion pipeline represents a multi-billion-euro deferred margin expansion opportunity that was previously underappreciated by the sell-side.

The combination of rising advisor productivity through the Next program, exceptional capital strength with a 23.0% CET1 ratio, and a structurally elevated dividend floor of €0.80 per share for 2026 establishes a visible path toward sustained mid-teens earnings growth over the next 18 to 36 months. - Earnings Calls The strongest counter-hypothesis to this optimistic thesis rests on BMED's acute sensitivity to prolonged equity market corrections.

BMED's business model is highly geared to financial markets because commission income represents approximately 70% of total revenues. Banca Mediolanum's elevated sensitivity to broader equity markets represents a substantial risk to sell-side commission forecasts if market volatility increases. If equity markets undergo a major correction, BMED's high share of client money in equities will lead to sharp downward revisions in AUM balances and performance fees, completely offsetting any rate-driven interest income benefits.

Furthermore, management's highly optimistic automated conversion narrative remains heavily vulnerable to severe global equity sell-offs. If equity markets experience a prolonged bear market, client satisfaction and conversion rates will suffer as the automatic equity step-in mechanisms expose retail investors to rapid capital losses, potentially prompting clients to disable the automatic transfer plans and seek safe-haven yields.

A 20% equity market correction would not only compress existing asset valuations but would also disrupt the €5 billion IIS conversion pipeline, causing clients to pause automated equity purchases and potentially withdraw capital entirely.
