MATERIALITY OF CHANGE The leadership transition at SkiStar AB represents a structured operational handover with Sara Jinnerot Uggelberg assuming the Chief Financial Officer role in February 2025, bringing extensive listed market experience, though secondary management churn and declining seasonal employee return rates from over 60% to 55% in 2024/25 flag underlying organizational friction that could escalate into execution risk if talent retention deteriorates further. Strategic priorities have shifted decisively toward Scandinavian operations and a year-round activity model targeting 7 million activity days by 2030, while the restructuring into two streamlined operating segments and the integration of the highly profitable EQPE private label with 70% gross margins position the group to optimize capital allocation and capture premium-valued operational mix benefits, though excessive summer overhead expansion could dilute returns if off-season demand fails to offset high year-round employee and maintenance costs.
