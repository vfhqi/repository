Outliers worth noting

OMV exhibits extreme capital intensity outlier status among European integrated energy peers, recording capex intensity of 9.1% versus a peer median of approximately 6.5%, placing the company in the bottom ~5% of capital efficiency. The outlier positioning becomes more pronounced when benchmarked against European refiners Repsol at 6.2% and the broader European integrated peer set, highlighting OMV's structurally higher reinvestment requirements during its strategic pivot toward gas expansion and Middle East chemical platforms. While this represents an accounting-driven structural shift rather than operational deterioration, the magnitude of the negative CAGR positions OMV in the bottom ~5% of European energy revenue growth trajectories and complicates like-for-like peer financial comparisons across the historical analysis window.
