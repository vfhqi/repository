EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 10 findings

Halma plc exhibits highly material positive change driven by accelerating organic growth in advanced photonics connectivity, record capital deployment in accretive bolt-on acquisitions, and premium margin expansion approaching the 22.0% Adjusted EBIT target. The group successfully upgraded full-year FY26 guidance to mid-teens organic constant currency growth from initial upper-single digit expectations, reflecting exceptional execution across Environmental & Analysis and Safety sectors.

However, the investment case carries elevated structural risk due to unprecedented customer concentration where a single hyperscaler represents 19% of group revenues, fundamentally altering the historical risk profile of the diversified niche compounder model.

RECENT EARNINGS DELIVERY (H1 2025/26 AND FY25)

Halma delivered outstanding double-digit beats across all key financial metrics in its most recent H1 2025/26 results. Reported revenue reached £1,237.4m, representing a 5.76% beat against consensus expectations of £1,170m. Adjusted EBIT of £282.0m exceeded consensus by 14.12%, driven by 210 basis points of margin expansion to 22.8% from 20.7% in the prior year period. Organic constant currency sales growth accelerated to 16.7%, far outpacing the long-term historical average of approximately 7.0%.

Environmental & Analysis led sector performance with 35.3% reported revenue growth, while Safety achieved all-time record margins of 26.9% and Healthcare returned to growth with 7.4% organic expansion. Adjusted EPS grew 28.6% to 55.32p, significantly exceeding consensus estimates of 48.0p. The only material disappointment centered on cash conversion dropping to 79.0% from 108.0% in H1 2025 due to strategic £75m working capital outflows to support inventory builds, though management reaffirmed full-year recovery to the 90% KPI target.

FORWARD GUIDANCE CREDIBILITY AND DELIVERY TRACK RECORD

Management exhibits a classic sandbagging posture, systematically issuing conservative early-year guidance that enables sequential intra-year upgrades. Halma met or exceeded organic growth targets in nine of the last eleven years, validating high forward credibility. The group upgraded FY26 guidance twice during the period, first from upper-single digits to low-double-digits, then to mid-teens organic constant currency growth.

Adjusted EBIT margin guidance was raised by approximately 50 basis points to around 22.0%. Historical delivery shows 22 consecutive years of record Adjusted profit, with FY25 delivering 112% cash conversion against the 90% KPI and margins of 21.6% beating initial guidance of around 21.0%. Forward tracking metrics remain constructive with year-to-date order books tracking comfortably ahead of revenue and book-to-bill ratios above 1.03x supporting second half delivery.

Sterling appreciation creates an estimated £14m profit headwind representing approximately 3-4% translation drag, though this is fully incorporated into upgraded guidance.
