Section 16 – Conclusion

The Q2 2026 earnings print fundamentally upgraded the Demant investment case by delivering a broad-based beat-and-raise that validated the commercial traction of the Oticon Zeal platform and demonstrated accelerated deleveraging ahead of expectations. The single most important thing that changed is that Demant has successfully transitioned from a period of margin compression and strategic uncertainty into a credible multi-year earnings upgrade cycle, driven by management raising full-year 2026 guidance to 6-7% organic growth and EBIT before special items of DKK 4,400-4,800 million, significantly exceeding the previous 3-6% growth and DKK 4,100-4,500 million EBIT range.

330 - Sales Revenue The integration of KIND Group is progressing ahead of schedule, driving an impressive 19% organic growth in internal product wholesale revenue in Q1 2026, and the group's net interest-bearing debt fell by DKK 792 million in H1 2026, meaning the group could reach its gearing target range as early as H1 2027 instead of the original 18-24 month timeline.

Gross profit reached DKK 9,958 million in H1 2026, translating to a gross margin of 77.1%, which increased from 76.0% in H1 2025 and beat consensus of 76.2% by 90 basis points, driven by positive product, geography, and channel mix from KIND. This rapid balance sheet repair and margin expansion fundamentally de-risks the near-term investment case and creates optionality for capital returns.

Management's consistency in maintaining its long-term financial aspiration of delivering 6-8% annual organic revenue growth confirms that the company continues to expect structural market share gains over the next three years, accelerated by the launch of the premium flagship platform Oticon Reveal in August 2026, which incorporates the world's first Dual AI sound processing system and proves that Demant's product development is moving faster than the slower hardware platform cadences of peer Sonova.

Demant upgraded its costsaving initiatives in H1 2026 to deliver DKK 300 million in savings for the full year compared to DKK 250 million originally projected, while continuing to target total annual structural savings of DKK 500 million from 2028 onwards, which supports management's commitment to improving the group's long-term operating margin.

Sell-side analysts reacted with polarized stances to the beat-and-raise print, with Jefferies raising its target price to DKK 345, Morgan Stanley to DKK 296, and DNB Carnegie to DKK 320, while Bernstein maintained a Market- Perform rating arguing that the strong H1 results and guidance upgrade were already anticipated by buy-side investors, and the appointment of Bassel Rifai as President of the Hearing Aids business area on June 1, 2026, allows CEO Søren Nielsen to focus fully on overall Group strategy, reducing key-person execution risk as the group has nearly doubled in size since 2017.

Structural disruptive threats from over-the-counter devices remain exceptionally low because the highly anticipated OTC market has experienced extremely muted customer adoption with only 4% of surveyed adults purchasing an OTC device, app downloads for tracked consumer OTC hearing aids plummeting 60% year-on-year in May 2026, and Apple's FDA-cleared AirPods Pro 2 update acting as a massive lead generator that will ultimately push younger patients toward traditional clinical channels. The collapse of the OTC disruptive narrative materially upgrades the long-term durability of Demant's vertically integrated dispensing networks.

Foreign exchange tailwinds provided substantial near-term relief, with Demant upgrading its full-year outlook to reflect a reduced exchange rate headwind of DKK -125 million on adjusted EBIT compared to the DKK -200 million previously expected, driven primarily by a stronger US dollar relative to the Danish krone in the first half.

Demant completely eliminated its previous forecast of a DKK -25 million tariff hit in its Diagnostics business, anticipating a full refund of previously paid tariffs in the second half of 2026, which surprised the market as Bernstein had previously modeled the tariff expense as a persistent drag.

Gross margin expansion came in ahead of plan at 77.1% in H1 2026, comfortably beating the sell-side consensus expectation of 76.2%, driven by positive product and regional mix alongside initial contributions from the KIND retail integration. The combination of favorable currency movements, tariff reversals, and manufacturing leverage creates a highly supportive margin trajectory through the remainder of fiscal 2026.

The strongest counter-hypothesis to this bullish conclusion is that competitive intensity has accelerated dramatically in the second half of 2026 with four of the five major manufacturers launching new premium platforms within a highly condensed window, and sceptics argue that Oticon Reveal is an incremental software upgrade running on the same Sirius processor chip as Oticon Intent whereas Sonova's competing EON platform features a major hardware and chip refresh, GN Hearing launched its premium ReSound Sensia claiming it as the world's smallest AI hearing aid with a Deep Neural Network processor that is 17x more energy efficient, and WS Audiology launched its Signia MaX platform.

Bernstein's Ludwig raised concerns that Demant may struggle to defend its average selling prices in H2 2026 as peers like Sonova and Signia launch major hardware-driven competing platforms, which could limit Demant's market share expansion and gross margin recovery. If the simultaneous rollout of competing AI-enabled platforms dilutes Demant's technological edge, the wholesale average selling prices could face severe downward pressure, undermining the gross margin expansion achieved in the first half.
