EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 21 findings

Nexi has undergone profound leadership and strategic transformation over the past twelve months, with Bernardo Mingrone's elevation to CEO in March 2026 signaling a pivot from growth-oriented expansion to execution-led stability and cash preservation. The company operates without active activist pressure, leaving management unconstrained, though the March 2026 Capital Markets Day marked a defensive strategic pivot from growth compounding to income-driven cash generation, backed by a proposed €350 million dividend for FY26.

This strategic reset triggered a severe 20% share price collapse as management lowered medium-term targets from an initial 14% EBITDA CAGR to low-single digit revenue growth through FY28. The company's earnings trajectory reflects mounting structural headwinds from Italian banking consolidation, platform fragmentation across more than forty disparate systems, and intensifying competitive pressure from agile neovendors, offset partially by robust free cash flow generation and disciplined deleveraging.
