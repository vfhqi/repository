Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. Evaluating Vimian Group AB capitalization practices is crucial because capitalizing development costs artificially inflates reported segment adjusted EBITA margins and EPS while masking true organic research and development reinvestment needs.

Core Investigation

1.
Critical inquiry: "What is Vimian Group AB capitalising as R&D and software (EUR 6.0m in 2025; EUR 7.7m in the first nine months of 2026 against EUR 3.6m a year earlier)? - Which projects, by segment (for example Clarity XR dental imaging, OvaCyte, the Pet Allergy Explorer, Nextmune Bio, systems)? - Over what useful lives is it amortised? - How does the amount compare with the capitalisation practice of Swedencare, Vetoquinol, Virbac and IDEXX? - How would expensing it change segment adjusted EBITA margins in 2026-2027?"

Why Capitalization Practices Matter

2.

This capitalization directly reduces the opex recorded on the income statement, meaning reported adjusted EBITA margins appear higher. If these costs were expensed as incurred, Vimian's reported EBITA and EPS would decrease over the 18-month to 3-year horizon.

Section 3 – Amortisation Policies and Useful Lives

Section summary
  1. Vimian Group AB applies systematic straight-line amortization of five to fifteen years for internally generated intangibles once available for use, while keeping assets under development subject to annual impairment tests.

General Amortisation Framework

1.
Consistent amortization: Vimian Group AB systematically amortizes intangible assets with finite useful lives on a straight-line basis over their estimated useful periods. The Group begins recognizing this amortization expense only from the specific date the developed asset is available for active clinical or operational use. This systematic method ensures that capitalized development outlays are gradually charged against earnings as the underlying veterinary technologies generate commercial revenues.
2.
Regular adjustments: Vimian Group AB reviews the useful lives and residual values of its material intangible assets at the end of each reporting period. Management modifies amortization schedules prospectively if expectations differ significantly from previous baseline estimates. This continuous oversight ensures that the carrying value of internal software and R&D platforms accurately reflects their actual economic utility over time.
3.
Rigorous criteria: The Group separates its internal projects into distinct research and development phases under IFRS guidelines. Costs from the research phase are immediately expensed as incurred, whereas development expenses are capitalized if future economic benefits are highly probable. Capitalized costs generally comprise dedicated R&D personnel salaries, raw materials, and specialized third-party consulting services.
4.
Mandatory testing: Vimian Group AB conducts mandatory annual impairment tests for internally generated intangible assets that are not yet available for use. These assets under development are evaluated using value-in-use cash flow calculations to ensure their carrying amounts do not exceed recoverable values. This proactive testing helps prevent the overstatement of assets on the balance sheet before commercialization begins.
5.
Prudent estimations: Vimian Group AB's management relies on critical accounting estimates to differentiate between immediately expensed research costs and capitalized development assets. These internal calculations directly impact the timing of operating expense recognition and subsequent amortization charges. Financial analysts scrutinize these subjective decisions because they heavily influence reported profitability and asset valuation across quarters.
6.
Capitalization cost elements: Vimian Group AB's capitalized costs during the development phase include materials, direct employee salaries, and directly attributable consultant fees. Any administrative, indirect, or research-phase expenditures that do not satisfy IFRS guidelines are expensed directly within the profit and loss statement. This delineation determines how much investment cost is deferred to future period balance sheets rather than hitting current margins.

7.
Extended useful lives: Vimian Group AB amortizes its internally generated intangible assets, which primarily represent developed IT systems, over five to ten years. However, the 2024 annual report disclosed a wider range of five to fifteen years for these specific assets. These longer amortization periods help reduce annual operating expenses by spreading development costs over a decade or more.

This range is substantially broader than the seven to ten years applied in earlier fiscal periods. These expanding parameters allow the Group to manage depreciation and amortization headwinds more flexibly following large acquisitions.

Section 5 – Financial Impact of Expensing on Segment Adjusted EBITA Margins

Section summary
  1. Fully expensing Vimian Group AB capitalized R&D and software would significantly reduce reported group adjusted EBITA margins by over one hundred and forty basis points in 2025 and more than two hundred basis points in 2026.
  2. The transition to a full expensing policy would compress consensus earnings per share estimates for 2026 and 2027, creating notable valuation headwinds and driving multiple expansion unless multiples adjust downward.

Group Baseline Margin Model

1.
Elevated baseline: Vimian Group AB reported a baseline group adjusted EBITA margin of 24.8% for the full year of 2025, which was supported by capitalization practices. The group capitalized EUR 6.0m of intangible assets during 2025, representing approximately 1.41% of its total reported sales. If these development costs had been immediately expensed, the group adjusted EBITA margin would have fallen to 23.39% in 2025. This highlights how capitalization practices directly alter the historical baseline before analyzing future years.
2.
Expanding investments: Vimian Group AB capitalized R&D and software spending rose rapidly to EUR 7.7m during the first nine months of 2026. This year-to-date capitalization reflects an annualized full-year 2026 projection of EUR 10.27m if the run-rate continues. Jefferies expects full-year 2026 revenues to reach EUR 481.3m under current operating assumptions. Under this model, the annualized 2026 capitalization would represent approximately 2.13% of total sales, indicating a much larger margin benefit than in previous periods.
3.
Softening margins: Jefferies estimates that Vimian Group AB will report an adjusted EBITA margin of 24.5% in 2026 and 24.9% in 2027. This expensing model shows that true operating margin progression remains lower than reported numbers suggest.
