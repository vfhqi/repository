Section 3 – Haypp Group AB's Valuation and Milestone Performance

Section summary
  1. Haypp Group's valuation and share price behavior are highly sensitive to regulatory milestones and the execution of its United States ZYN strategy.
  2. Operational margin shortfalls during heavy investment phases have temporarily pressured multiples, but robust top-line growth and core Nordic profitability support long-term re-rating potential.

Swedish Regulatory Headwinds and Licensing Resolution

1.
Regulatory setback: Haypp Group faced a major operational threat when Stockholm authorities announced plans to withdraw Snusbolaget Norden's tobacco sales permit on September 25, 2024. The city regulators alleged that the Snusbolaget platform had failed to maintain proper age verification protocols upon delivery. While operations continued normally during the subsequent appeal process, the dispute initialed a period of severe share price volatility. This sudden regulatory headwind caused the company's valuation to compress significantly as investors feared disruption to core Nordic revenues over the medium term.
2.
Upholding decisions: The Swedish licensing dispute culminated when the Supreme Administrative Court denied Snusbolaget's final leave to appeal in October 2026. Following this decision, the subsidiary immediately discontinued all sales on its Snusnetto storefront on October 19, 2026. Management reassured shareholders that this closure only affected five hundred active customers and would not materially impact financial performance going forward. Consequently, the resolution of this long-standing dispute removed a major overhang, allowing the market to refocus on growth.
3.
Compliance adaptation: Haypp Group proactively mitigated the parcel locker ruling by implementing advanced electronic age verification across its Swedish shipping network. The technical team developed biometric age verification solutions for parcel lockers in early 2026 and completed the full rollout by the end of October 2026. DNB Carnegie highlighted on April 7, 2026, that these rigorous compliance capabilities serve as a powerful competitive advantage. Thus, the group successfully turned elevated regulatory barriers into a stronger competitive moat.
4.
Contrary evidence: Some analysts argue that persistent local regulatory disputes in Sweden and the United States will permanently restrict Haypp's valuation multiples. Carnegie warned on November 7, 2024, that the company would require clear evidence that regulatory threats are fully resolved before its shares can sustain new historical highs. Under this cautious view, the recurring costs of legal defense and localized shipping compliance will weigh on earnings. Therefore, regulatory friction is a structural drag on margins rather than a temporary milestone.
