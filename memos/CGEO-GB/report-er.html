EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Georgia Capital PLC delivered outstanding second quarter and first half 2026 results reported on August 4, 2026, which fundamentally strengthened the investment case for long-only equity investors. Net Asset Value per share in Georgian Lari reached a record high of GEL 175.12 as of June 30, 2026, representing a robust 13.1% quarteron-quarter increase that exceeded historical expectations of moderate mid-single-digit expansion. In sterling terms, NAV per share expanded even more impressively by 15.6% quarter-on-quarter to GBP 50.10, which Deutsche Bank characterized as outstanding relative to the prior flat period.

The earnings delivery substantially beat conservative sell-side expectations across multiple dimensions. The Net Capital Commitment ratio improved by 6.8 percentage points quarter-on-quarter to a record-low -2.9% as of June 30, 2026, shattering the long-term 10% target and proving that Holdco liquid funds comfortably exceed outstanding capital commitments. Medical insurance revenue expanded by an exceptional 49.3% year-on-year to GEL 78.7 million in 2Q26, driven by newly awarded tenders and double-digit policy repricing, substantially beating the sell-side's mid-teens expectations. Aggregated net operating cash flow for large private companies grew by 41.4% year-on-year to GEL 76.2 million in 2Q26, significantly outpacing prior-quarter growth rates of 29.8% in 1Q26.

The most transformative development was the launch of a massive GEL 1 billion capital allocation programme running through the end of 2029. This programme represents a massive expansion in shareholder returns, funded through existing liquid assets and robust free cash flow generation from private business dividends, with at least half dedicated to share buybacks and potential dividends. The company successfully finalized its prior GEL 700 million capital return programme well over a year ahead of its original December 2027 timeline through full redemption of local sustainability-linked bonds and persistent share buybacks.

Management announced the full redemption of its remaining US$ 50 million sustainability-linked local bonds, settled on August 19, 2026, officially eliminating all outstanding holding company-level debt.

The strategic divestment of the housing development business, m2, marked another pivotal milestone in the group's capital-light investment model. This divestment represents a major milestone in capital-light execution by completely eliminating direct residential real estate exposure, substantially reducing the group's overall risk profile and net debt. The m2 disposal significantly de-risked the balance sheet by reducing aggregate net debt of the emerging portfolio by approximately US$ 39 million, cutting the segment's net debt-to-EBITDA ratio from 3.8x down to 2.0x.

Underlying private portfolio performance demonstrated extraordinary operating resilience despite regional geopolitical tensions. The retail pharmacy business achieved 22.3% year-over-year EBITDA growth in 2Q26, supported by consistent gross profit margin improvement to 34.1% driven by favorable trading terms with key suppliers, and maintained an exceptionally high cash conversion rate of 100.9% during the first half.

Private K- 12 education experienced exceptional organic demand as the total number of learners expanded by 8.8% yearon-year in 1H26, with academic year revenue delivering a highly robust 10.4% year-on-year expansion after adjusting for calendar differences. - Earnings Calls The dominant listed asset, Lion Finance Group, representing 47% of total portfolio value, provided substantial NAV support. Deutsche Bank noted that Lion Finance Group's stellar Q2 performance, with profits up 20.6% yearon-year, heavily supported Georgia Capital's NAV appreciation.

Deutsche Bank's Trodd noted that Lion Finance Group's shares rallied 36.5% in sterling year-to-date, boosting Georgia Capital's NAV and narrowing the discount to NAV to approximately 16%. However, several headwinds warrant careful monitoring. P&C pre-tax profit fell by 11.4% year-on-year to GEL 7.9 million in 2Q26, dragged down by a GEL 3.0 million Tbilisi hailstorm motor claim, though excluding this one-off weather impact, pre-tax profit rose by 23% year-on-year.

The K-12 education segment delivered GEL 23.0 million revenue in 2Q26, but operating expenses rose by 11.9% due to expansion-related salaries, causing raw EBITDA to decline by 6.8% year-on-year. Tourism revenues from Iran declined sharply by 64.7% in the first half of 2026 due to the US-Iran conflict, representing a moderation in service export inflows.

The sell-side response was overwhelmingly constructive. Deutsche Bank's Trodd reiterated a Positive investment rating on Georgia Capital following the dual catalysts of strong underlying bank results and the robust private portfolio update. Analyst Gavin Trodd strongly endorsed the new GEL 1 billion capital allocation programme, noting it represents a major upgrade to shareholder return expectations. Analyst Ewan Lovett-Turner argued that the private portfolio's prospects remain positive, fuelled by dominant market positions and a highly resilient domestic economy. Investment case transformation judgment: The 1H26 earnings fundamentally strengthened the investment case by delivering a rare triple catalyst of record-breaking NAV growth, unprecedented balance sheet deleveraging to zero Holdco debt, and a massive capital return programme that exceeds all historical precedents. The successful monetization of m2 validates management's capital-light strategy and removes a major risk overhang, while the -2.9% NCC ratio provides exceptional strategic flexibility for selective reinvestment or further shareholder distributions.

The persistent dominant market positions across insurance, healthcare, pharmacy, and education, combined with Georgia's structural GDP per capita expansion beyond USD 10,000, create a highly compelling compounding platform. However, the investment case remains heavily dependent on the 47% portfolio concentration in Lion Finance Group and the sustainability of Georgia's exceptional macroeconomic expansion, which skeptical analysts warn could normalize rapidly if geopolitical tailwinds reverse.

Long-only investors should view this print as a clear re-rating catalyst that transforms Georgia Capital from a highly discounted holding structure into a credible permanent capital vehicle with visible multi-year NAV compounding potential.
