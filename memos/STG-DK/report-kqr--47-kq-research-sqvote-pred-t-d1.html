Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. The feasibility of Scandinavian Tobacco Group's strategic transition hinges on whether cost synergies and buybacks can sustain earnings growth without requiring unproven operational execution.

Resolving the Key Question will show if stable free cash flows can safely support restarted capital returns.

  1. Scandinavian Tobacco Group faces steep challenges in recovering its lost European volume market share due to recurring supply disruptions and intense value-brand competition.

Management attributed this contraction primarily to severe raw tobacco quality issues that disrupted the availability of its flagship Signature brand in France and the Netherlands. Consequently, the company has struggled to maintain its core volume base as consumers migrated to competing brands.
2.
Delayed stabilization: Deutsche Bank Research reports that Scandinavian Tobacco Group has experienced successive years of underperformance in its European machine-rolled cigar business. Although the Signature raw tobacco supply issue was resolved by late 2026, recovering lost retail shelf space is projected to take several quarters. Ambitious targets: Scandinavian Tobacco Group has established a Focus2030 strategic target to increase its volume market share in key European markets to above 29% by 2030.
