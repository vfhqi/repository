These tranches carry coupons of 0.6848% maturing in 2028 and 0.9823% maturing in 2031, stabilizing long-term funding costs. This fixed-rate structure successfully shelters the group's financing expenses from interest rate hikes, helping support future earnings per share.

A 100-basis-point change in interest rates as of December 31, 2025, would impact pre-tax profit by CHF 3 million, down from CHF 11 million in 2024. The matching interest on lease liabilities, which reached CHF 38 million in 2025, adds a steady financing drag.
