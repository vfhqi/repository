[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. AstraZeneca is a de-rated compounder waiting on a clearing event, not a change case. At 12,400p (164.87 USD) the shares pay us for the approved portfolio's Core EPS path of about 11 percent a year and a 2 percent dividend, and they pay for the re-rating only if the AVANZAR readout in November or December 2026 clears. At the even odds we give that readout the modal return is about 15 percent a year over eighteen months, a C against the 20 percent bar; the case becomes compelling either about 6 percent lower or on the far side of the readout.

  • The change in 2026 is in the price of the business, not in the business.
    • Core EPS has compounded at 12 to 13 percent a year for two years (7.26 to 9.16 USD, FY2023 to FY2025) and the Core operating margin has risen from 31.5 percent (FY2025) to 34 percent (H1 2026), while revenue growth has slowed from 18 percent (FY2024) to 9 percent (FY2025) to 5 percent CER (Q2 2026).
    • None of that is new.
    • What is new is a half-year of late-stage failures (Wainua CARDIO-TTRansform on 9-Jul-26, SERENA-4 on 14-Sep-26, the eVOLVE-Lung02 discontinuation, Ultomiris in HSCT-TMA) and China's eleventh VBP round, which together took the shares 21 percent off their 52-week high and the 24-month-forward multiple to 13.43x (2026-09-18), the 17th percentile of its own five years and below its ten-year "crash" band of 13.50x.
  • The input change forces are a continuation, not a change, and they net to a mild headwind.
    • The external forces pull against each other: structural demand for specialty biologics (oncology +15 percent to 14.1bn USD in H1 2026, 46 percent of revenue; Tezspire +45 percent CER) against government price-setting in the US, China and Japan (a 68 percent Farxiga maximum fair price cut from 2026; failed VBP bids; China growth 2 percent in Q1 2026).
    • The internal forces are the fourteenth year of one chief executive's strategy: no chief-executive change, guidance reiterated rather than raised, a launch calendar that is the one strong force (Etcamah, Baxfendy, tozorakimab, Enhertu's new indications) and a formal cap on deals at 10 to 20bn USD that closes off the mega-merger route.
    • The screen's decision rule is not met.
  • The 'Financial Outputs' are improving where the case needs them least and standing still where it needs them most.
    • The margin is rising a point a year and Core EPS compounds at 11 to 12 percent, but the top line has halved its growth rate, H1 2026 operating cash flow fell 875 USDm to 6,224 USDm on working capital and cash tax, and the sell-side has trimmed FY2026 to FY2028 EPS by 1.2 to 2.5 percent over three months rather than raising it.
    • Consensus sits at the top of guidance (FY2026 Core EPS 10.27 USD, +12.1 percent, against "low double-digit"), so there is no gap for guidance to close
    • the upgrade room is in one line, tozorakimab's peak sales, from 2028.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain for the approved portfolio and has not begun for the thing the return needs.
    • The launches are in the company's actions, its product sales, its financial statements and the sell-side's numbers.
    • The multiple has moved the other way, and it moves back only on a clinical result.
  • What we think it earns and what it costs.
    • Core EPS of 10.25 USD in FY2026 rising to about 14.0 USD in FY2029 on our path, about 11 percent a year (consensus 12.3 percent), from 7 points of revenue growth, 3 of margin and about half a point below the line
    • no buyback contribution.
    • At 164.87 USD the shares are 13.2x the 24-month-forward earnings of 12.49 USD on our construction, and 14.7x one-year-forward.
  • The return clears the bar in one branch only.
    • Applying the framework's convention (the multiple on the earnings two years beyond the horizon, 14.4 USD at March 2028) and a multiple blended at even odds between a cleared branch at 15.0x and an uncleared one at 12.5x, the eighteen-month total return is about 23 percent, 14.9 percent a year, a C
    • the cleared branch alone gives 21.6 percent a year, a B, and the uncleared 8.0 percent, an F.
    • Earnings growth carries about 10 points a year, cash 2 with no odds attached, and the re-rating about 3 in expectation
    • the re-rating is the swing factor, worth about plus 7 points a year cleared and minus 7 uncleared.
  • The chain breaks at the margin if the drop-through is plausibly worse.
    • With no margin expansion in FY2027, half a point a year thereafter, the Core tax rate back to 18 percent and net interest 100m USD higher, the 24-month-forward EPS at March 2028 is about 12.9 USD, 10 percent below our path, and the blended return falls to 7.0 percent a year, an F
    • the 'Modal return' would fall from C to F and this Building Block from C to D.
    • The C therefore rests on the margin point a year, and the FY2027 guide in February 2027 is the tell.
  • The pattern it fits is 'OSHQC CfC Clearing', at C.
    • A high-quality compounder has de-rated on a concern (R&D attrition and what it implies for growth after 2030, when 63 percent of today's revenue loses exclusivity) that the next three years' earnings are largely insulated from
    • the fit is not crisp because the clearing evidence is a dated readout, not a trend in hand, and the concern's sub-type (mis-execution) is the one the framework trusts least to clear.

  • The case therefore is a good business at a fair price with a favourable skew and a sub-bar expectation.

    • The skew at eighteen months is about -11 percent (the bears' own destination) against about +34 percent (the cleared branch).
    • Below about 11,600p (154.4 USD, 6.3 percent lower) the modal path clears the bar without the readout
    • above it, the case clears only in the cleared branch.

  • Rating = C, because on materiality the parts combine to a modest whole: the input forces are a continuation rather than a change, the outputs are steady at 11 to 12 percent Core EPS growth on a slowing top line, and the return reaches the bar only in the branch of a clinical readout we put at even odds.

    • The Elements do not bind at the weaker
    • all three are C, and the whole is a C because nothing in the chain is strong enough to lift the others: the launches are real but replace lost exclusivity, the margin gains are real but paid for by R&D held at 23 percent of sales, and the multiple is low but half of that discount is the industry's.
    • Not B, because B would need either a cleared AVANZAR as the modal case or an EPS path inside the 12 to 20 percent band with a re-rating on top, and we have neither.
    • Not D, because the earnings path is robust to the event (the finance director and Morgan Stanley both say the 2030 target stands without AVANZAR), so the downside branch still returns about 8 percent a year, not zero.

Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The case against, at its strongest: the multiple is not low, it is right, and it will go lower.
    • The R&D engine has failed four Phase III trials in a half-year (Wainua CARDIO-TTRansform, SERENA-4, eVOLVE-Lung02, Ultomiris HSCT-TMA)
    • the "75 percent success rate" was a run that has ended, and the Risks report's pre-mortem finds the pipeline-failure narrative the most internally consistent of its three.
    • Sixty-three percent of revenue faces post-2030 loss of exclusivity (Morgan Stanley)
    • R&D at 24 percent of sales caps the margin (DNB Carnegie) and Bernstein's downside case has it at 33 percent.
    • Core earnings exceed reported by 39 percent on over 4bn USD of amortisation and 1.1bn USD of capitalised CSPC upfront, executive bonuses are 20 percent weighted to Core EPS, receivables factoring of 2.6bn USD flatters operating cash flow and inventory days have stretched to 315.
    • Management's willingness to entertain a Bristol Myers Squibb merger in August 2026 shows it knows the organic path to 80bn USD is in doubt (Deutsche Bank).
    • On this reading AVANZAR is more likely than not to miss on overall survival (Deutsche Bank), the market then prices the cliff, and Morgan Stanley's bear case of 11,000p on 16x cut earnings is the destination: -11 percent, with earnings growth fading to low single digits after 2029.
  • What we concede.
    • The cliff is real and undated relief from it depends on assets not yet in the profit-and-loss account.
    • R&D intensity will not fall while twelve assets are being pushed through Phase III, so margin expansion is a point a year, not two.
    • The Core-to-reported gap is the sector's largest and the cash-flow presentation (factoring, working capital) deserves the monitoring line we give it.
    • The readout is a coin toss
    • we do not claim to know it.
  • Why we still hold our view.
    • First, the earnings that pay for the next three years are not the pipeline's: the company's 20bn USD bridge from end-2026 to 2030 comes mostly from approved products in new indications, the risk-adjusted target assumes a 60 percent success rate that the H2 2026 run has not broken over a full year (four readouts delivered in H1 2026; tozorakimab, CLARITY-Gastric01, DESTINY-Lung04 and the Etcamah approval since), and even Morgan Stanley's bear model, built on pipeline failure, keeps five-year growth positive at 5 percent.
    • Second, the bears' own numbers bound the downside: Deutsche Bank's target after upgrading is 11,700p (-6 percent), Morgan Stanley's bear is 11,000p (-11 percent), and both are built on a higher multiple than today's (14x and 16x on lower earnings).
    • Third, at 13.4x 24-month-forward the market is paying a sector multiple (the broader large-cap pharma sector trades on about fourteen times) for above-sector growth (Jefferies: a 13 percent Core EPS CAGR to 2030 against a sector 8 percent).
  • Where our view stops.
    • None of this makes the modal return clear the bar.
    • It makes the skew favourable (about -11 percent against about +34 percent over eighteen months) and the expectation a C.
    • The counter-case is right that the case is about post-2030, and we are not paid enough at today's odds to underwrite post-2030 before the event
    • we would be at a price about 6 percent lower, or after the event at the price it leaves.
