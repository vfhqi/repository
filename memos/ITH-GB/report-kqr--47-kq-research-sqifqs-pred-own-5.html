Section 4 – Ithaca Energy Leverage Ceiling and Covenants

Technical Definitions and Exclusions

1.
Optimized profile: Adjusted Net Debt at Ithaca Energy is defined as senior unsecured notes and bank drawings less cash and cash equivalents. This specific metric is defined to exclude accrued interest on borrowings, lease liabilities, and unamortised fees. Excluding these non-debt obligations helps the company present a highly optimized leverage ratio to credit markets.
2.
Operating metrics: Ithaca's Adjusted EBITDAX metric is defined as earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization, and non-cash items. This measure also excludes exploration and evaluation expenditure, revaluation gains or losses on financial instruments, and restructuring costs. This adjusted metric provides a clearer view of underlying operational performance but can mask cash flow volatility during down cycles.
3.
Leverage boundaries: Ithaca's board operates with a self-imposed corporate ceiling of 1.25x net debt to EBITDAX under its capital allocation framework. In contrast, the company's formal Reserves Based Lending facility contains a much higher maximum covenant ratio of 3.5x. This dual-track structure means the company possesses substantial technical headroom before triggering formal bank defaults.
