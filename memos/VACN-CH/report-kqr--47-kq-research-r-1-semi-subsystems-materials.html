  1. The acceleration of advanced packaging and high-power logic architecture translates directly into high-margin substrate and subsystem content gains across European suppliers.
  2. However, structural capacity constraints and local Chinese procurement initiatives threaten to disrupt traditional market share allocations over the next three years.
