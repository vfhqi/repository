This partner-led strategy allows SMA Solar to expand internationally without heavy capital investments.

Peer comparisons show that companies like Array Technologies negotiate explicit vendor rebates with suppliers to share Section 45X benefits. Credit Calculation and Financial Impact

5.
Lucrative credit math: Utility-scale solar inverters qualify for a substantial tax credit of 1.5 cents per watt under Section 45X. Jefferies estimates that because SMA Solar supplies several gigawatts of capacity to the United States, these credits can yield massive bottom-line benefits. For instance, every gigawatt of qualifying localized inverter volume translates to EUR 15 million in gross manufacturing credits. This potential cash injection remains a key positive driver that is completely excluded from official guidance.
6.
Volume eligibility hurdles: Qualifying for the full credit requires satisfying strict domestic content and manufacturing requirements. A former First Solar executive explained that simple final assembly is insufficient because the Internal Revenue Service requires detailed tracing of bills of materials. Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, components must also meet escalating non-foreign entity of concern thresholds starting in 2026. This means SMA Solar's qualified volume will depend on eliminating prohibited foreign materials from its supply chain.
7.
Regulatory tariff protection: Securing Section 45X compliance provides critical operational safety under updated federal guidelines. Crowell and Moring reported in August 2026 that power inverters complying with Section 45X are considered domestically produced. This classification exempts complying equipment from restrictive federal national security covered lists and import bans. Consequently, successful localization in Arkansas protects core revenue bases from being locked out of the US market.
