EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 25 findings

1.
Legrand operates as a pure-play building electrical infrastructure specialist with 90% of revenues flowing through distributor channels and 32% of group sales now sourced from high-growth datacenter solutions. The geographic revenue mix is balanced across North America at 42%, Europe at 38%, and Rest of World at 20%, while datacenter revenues surged to 32% of total group sales in first-half 2026 from 26% in fiscal 2025. The group maintains a highly decentralized front-office architecture that preserves local customer relationships while centralizing back-office operations to secure operational efficiency and disciplined cost management. Systematic bolt-on M&A completed 31 transactions from 2020 to 2025, deploying €4.3 billion for €1.7 billion in acquired sales. Strategic 2030 targets aim for €12 billion to €15 billion in group sales while maintaining a stable 20% adjusted operating margin floor and allocating €5 billion for further acquisitions
