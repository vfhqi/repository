EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Consensus Earnings Per Share

DNB Carnegie's 2027e base case is adjusted earnings per share of SEK 3.45 on net sales of SEK 10,969 million, and its bull case yields roughly SEK 3.60. Analysts cut their 2027e and 2028e adjusted EPS estimates by 2% to 2.5% after the Q3 2026 report, so the direction of consensus revisions is downward for next year. The research contains no full-year 2026 consensus EPS figure, so the current-year number cannot be stated and the 2027e base case is the only EPS anchor available.

Ranked Drivers of Future Earnings

Driver 1 is the Hungarian obesity and diabetes contract, which is projected to add roughly 3% to 4% (calculated) to annual group EPS at peak utilization and has high plausibility because the customer contract is secured and production lines are operational.

Driver 2 is the Materials segment, where sales rose 27% adjusted for currency in Q3 2026 and plausibility of sustained growth is high because the division serves diversified accounts. Driver 3 is the raw material price adjustment, which should add approximately 2% (calculated) to future EPS once the drag is eliminated and has high plausibility because pricing clauses are contractual. Driver 4 is the integration of Unitron and Qualtech, which DNB Carnegie projects will add 1% and 2% to EPS respectively on a full-year basis, although plausibility is only medium because integrating electronics and polymer supply chains carries execution risk.

Driver 5 is the automotive recovery, which would add roughly 2% to 3% (calculated) to consolidated EPS through operating leverage but is rated low plausibility because customer model transitions are expected to persist. Because several drivers must work at once, the full higher-earnings case carries a combined probability of roughly 55% (calculated).

Latest Results

Q3 2026 net sales were SEK 2,500 million, held to 3% organic growth as industrial weakness offset strong medical expansion. Adjusted EBITA of SEK 262 million was 3% below consensus, and the adjusted EBITA margin fell to 10.5% from 11.7% a year earlier. Earnings per share of SEK 0.64 (down from SEK 0.80) missed consensus of SEK 0.72 by 11%. Medical Solutions organic growth of 9% beat the 7% expected by ABG Sundal Collier, while Engineered Solutions declined 3% organically. A SEK 10 million raw material headwind weighed on the quarter, and the acquisitions added SEK 56 million of sales in their first month of consolidation.

Guidance Trustworthiness

Management maintains a strict policy of giving no formal earnings or revenue forecasts because of high customer dependence, which leaves little to audit beyond capital spending and long-term targets. The one specific guide is 2026 capital expenditure of SEK 600 million to SEK 650 million, down from SEK 788 million in 2025, and the Hungarian expansion has already concluded, which supports the guide's credibility. Brokers trust the long-term medical transition but discount short-term targets because of cyclical industrial challenges.

Broker Positioning

DNB Carnegie maintains an official buy recommendation, while ABG Sundal Collier provides commissioned research without an active rating. Analysts expect the adjusted EBITA margin to stabilize at 10.7% in 2027, which remains below the 12% corporate target. The sell side may be overestimating the speed of the Hungarian ramp-up, with DNB Carnegie's Laséen offering the strongest dissenting evidence on automotive underutilization.

Peer Position

Nolato ranks in the lower percentiles of its peer group on organic growth and margin trajectory, with SP Group at 23% organic growth in Q3 2026 against Nolato's 3%. Nolato's balance sheet is the stronger of the larger peers: its leverage is 1.0x net debt to adjusted EBITDA, whereas Gerresheimer's adjusted EBITDA leverage rose to approximately 5x.

Outside Forces

The global GLP-1 market is projected to expand to USD 254 bn by 2034 from USD 62.80 bn in 2025, which underpins the Medical Solutions growth case. Polymer materials account for approximately 36% of sales, and contractual price adjustment lags of 3 to 6 months expose the group to temporary margin compression.

Currency translation reduced sales growth by 7% in early 2026, and DNB Carnegie lowered its 2027-2028 operating profit estimates by 3%, mainly on foreign exchange.

Manufacturing close to customers' regional assembly plants minimizes shipping costs and tariff exposure.

Major Risks

A rapid shift toward oral GLP-1 formulations could severely disrupt Nolato's high-volume pen injector assembly lines. Underutilization at the expanded Mosonmagyaróvár plant would trigger high fixed depreciation costs and depress medical margins below targets. If Hungarian validation delays coincide with continued automotive contraction, annual EPS could fall 5% to 8% (calculated) below consensus.

Return on capital employed fell to 12.4% in Q3 2026, short of the long-term target of more than 15%, so integration and ramp-up slippage would strain capital efficiency further.
