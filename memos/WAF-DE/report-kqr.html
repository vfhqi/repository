Section 2 – Wafer supply-demand dynamics and utilization by region

Section summary
  1. The global 300 millimetre raw wafer market outside China is projected to enter a structural shortage from late 2027 as massive artificial intelligence demand outpaces prudent capital expenditure by established silicon suppliers.
  2. Regional utilization is bifurcating as European and Taiwanese wafer manufacturers run near full capacity, while Chinese suppliers operate at lower utilization but rapidly absorb domestic mature-node logic and memory volumes.
  3. High-bandwidth memory and advanced packaging are absorbing an unprecedented volume of leading-edge silicon, directly crowding out raw wafer supply for downstream analog, industrial, and automotive chipmakers.

1.

2.
Prudent ex-China supply: Bernstein's 24 September 2026 report models that global ex-China silicon wafer supply will grow at a mere 5% compound annual rate through 2028 as suppliers actively avoid greenfield capital investments.
3.
Imminent market shortages: Bernstein's 24 September 2026 report estimates that ex-China raw wafer utilization will reach 97% in 2027 and 102% in 2028, leading to severe supply deficits that result in a raw wafer shortage outside China.
