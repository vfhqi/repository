1.
Business. Frequentis designs and integrates safety-critical control-centre communication systems, hardware plus software, for civil and military air traffic control and for public-safety agencies
2.
Segments. Air Traffic Management delivered EUR 400.6m of FY25 revenue, 69% of group; Public Safety and Transport delivered EUR 179.5m, 31% of group
3.
Customer base. Over 90% of revenue comes from state-run authorities and public agencies, across more than 550 organisations in 150 countries, with roughly 400 projects run each year
4.
Revenue scale. FY25 revenue was EUR 580.1m, up 20.8% organically, from EUR 480.3m in FY24 and EUR 427.5m in FY23; H1 2026 revenue was EUR 342.9m
5.
Reported margin. FY25 EBIT was EUR 46.8m at an 8.1% margin, including an EUR 8.0m claim settlement; FY24 was 6.7% and FY23 6.2%

Segment margins. FY25 Air Traffic Management EBIT was EUR 20.0m on a 4.9% margin; Public Safety and Transport EBIT was EUR 27.0m on a 15.0% margin

Gross margin path. Reported gross margin ran 79.8% in FY24, 76.1% in FY25 and 68.0% in H1 2026, as materials and purchased services rose 38.0% then 88.0%
9.
Balance sheet. Net cash was EUR 107.4m at 30 June 2026 against EUR 6.6m of bank and other financial liabilities; the equity ratio stood at 38.1%
10.
Geography. Europe was 58% of FY25 revenue at EUR 339.3m and the Americas 27% at EUR 158.9m; H1 2026 Americas revenue was EUR 132m, 39% of group
11.
Order book. Order intake was EUR 680.2m in FY25 and EUR 361.7m in H1 2026; orders on hand reached a record EUR 835.0m at June 2026

Ownership and listing. Listed in Vienna and Frankfurt since May 2019; Hannes Bardach holds approximately 68% of the shares, B&C Holding Österreich 8%, free float near 24%
