[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. secunet Security Networks is a crisp 'Demand Inflection' in a certified near-monopoly, and at EUR 267.5 (2026-10-09) the shares have already paid for it. Legislated German and European spending on defence cryptography, sovereign cloud and border security is reaching the one company whose BSI approvals to GEHEIM let it serve the classified part of that demand, and the order book, the revenue line and the sell-side's estimates all show the wave arriving; what the price leaves to a buyer today is a modal return of 6.7 percent a year over eighteen months and 8.6 percent over three years, against a 20 percent bar.

  • The change is external, legislated and sector-wide, and it has already reached the order book.
    • The forces are the strongest and most visible this company has faced, and they have more than two years of visibility.
    • The German special fund of EUR 100bn, the proposed EUR 500bn infrastructure fund and NATO's trajectory to up to 5 percent of GDP on defence and security by 2035 designate cyber defence a sovereign investment
      • Germany's 2027 draft raises the core procurement budget by 55 percent to EUR 30bn.
    • H1 2026 order intake was EUR 287.4m, up 78.7 percent on EUR 160.8m
      • Book-to-bill 1.4x against a history of 1.0x to 1.1x
      • The backlog reached EUR 360.8m at 2026-06-30, up 29.4 percent from EUR 278.9m at 2025-12-31.
    • Mandatory EU rules move in the same direction on dated fronts: the Entry/Exit System live across 29 Schengen states from April 2026, NIS2, the Cyber Resilience Act, eIDAS 2.0, and a quantum-safe cryptography mandate from 2030.
  • The company captures a disproportionate share of that demand because certification excludes almost everyone else from the classified core.
    • It holds the only BSI approvals to GEHEIM for SINA workstations, gateways and, since May 2025, cloud
    • Public authorities accepted FY2025 list-price rises without buying less.
    • The Public Sector was 94.2 percent of H1 2026 revenue and grew 29.4 percent
      • Group order intake grew about four times as fast as Bechtle's.
    • The moat is national, not international, and it is granted by a regulator that could also loosen it
      • That is where the 'Foundations' are fragile.
  • The internal change is continuity under a parent-promoted chief executive, not transformation, and it is secondary to the external force.
    • The strategy (sovereign cloud and AI platform by 2030) is articulated and resourced, the new products are certified and dated, and guidance was specified to the upper end
    • But acquisitions and shareholder returns point the wrong way, with a EUR 17.4m write-down on SysEleven in 2025 and the payout corridor cut to 30 to 50 percent in March 2026.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up together for the first time since 2021.
    • Revenue grew 19.2 percent in H1 2026, Q2 EBIT rose 134.1 percent on revenue up 31.6 percent, FY2026 revenue guidance was specified to the upper end of EUR 460m to 500m on 2026-08-13, and consensus earnings per share were revised up over every window with no cuts.
    • The cash leg went the other way: operating cash flow was EUR -60.1m in H1 2026 on a deliberate inventory build to EUR 144.7m, cash fell to EUR 6.9m and net debt was EUR 22.8m at 2026-06-30.
      • FY2025 turned an H1 outflow of EUR -9.0m into EUR 62.5m for the year, so the swing is reversible
      • It is also the near-term binary.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, which means the market has had every layer to read.
    • From the chief executive's sovereignty strategy and the regulator's certifications, through the actions (cloud, partnerships, the inventory pre-buy), into orders and backlog, revenue and EBIT, and the sell-side's estimates.
    • There is no layer left for a buyer today to discover first.
  • What we think it earns: earnings per share of EUR 5.15 in FY2025 rising to EUR 8.42 in FY2028, 17.8 percent a year, from revenue growth of 11.1 percent a year and an EBIT margin rising from 11.3 percent to 13.2 percent.
    • Consensus expects 20.2 percent a year to FY2028 and sits above our path from FY2027, because the two brokers model 20 percent revenue growth and a 13.0 percent margin in FY2027 while management's own long-term EBIT margin target is 11.0 to 11.5 percent and the growth is arriving as hardware.
  • What it costs on the valuation is where the chain breaks.
    • The shares rose from EUR 191.50 on 2026-08-12 to EUR 267.5 on 2026-10-09, 40 percent, against consensus earnings revisions of 4 to 6 percent, so about four-fifths of the move was multiple.
    • The 24-month-forward multiple went from about 22x to 30.59x on consensus earnings, 32.5x on our path: the 67th percentile of its own five years, the 75th of the last two, above the ten-year median of 29.80x, and the second-highest in the 17-stock set.
    • The consensus target of EUR 253.33 is below the price
      • The sell-side has been chasing the price, not leading it.
  • The return therefore does not clear the bar on the modal path.
    • At the ten-year median multiple of 29.8x on the earnings two years beyond each horizon, the value is EUR 294.7 at April 2028 and EUR 343.0 at October 2029 including dividends, 6.7 and 8.6 percent a year.
    • Held at today's 32.5x it is 12.9 and 11.7 percent
    • Only at the 'defendable' 35.0x does the eighteen-month return approach the bar, at 18.6 percent.
    • The case clears 20 percent a year on the modal path below about EUR 224 at eighteen months and about EUR 199 at three years.
  • The pattern is 'Demand Inflection', and the fit is B.
    • Crisp on the demand and the position, loose on the conversion, because growth here costs inventory, people and gross margin rather than arriving one-to-many.
  • What breaks it: a Bundestag budget slip in Q4 2026, the quarter that makes the year, landing on EUR 6.9m of cash and EUR 144.7m of inventory
    • A company-driven multiple reverting toward the two-year median of 25.98x
    • Or an EBIT margin that stays at management's own 11.5 percent rather than reaching our 13.2 percent.

  • The case is therefore a compelling business case and not a compelling investment at EUR 267.5.

    • We park it on price, not on quality, and progress it if the price returns to the entry zone or the earnings path moves toward consensus without the price moving.

      • The healthcare-connector cycle and peak SINA workstation shipments create temporary growth while the Business Sector loses share and the recurring revenue never scales.

    • Where the hypothesis bites is exactly where our rating lands: the Business Sector (6 percent of revenue) and the cloud transition are not in our path's margin assumption, and the price pays for a path above ours.
