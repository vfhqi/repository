EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Section summary
  1. Fresnillo plc exhibits highly stable executive leadership yet faces severe regulatory and macroeconomic headwinds in its core Mexican operations, offset by historic precious metals pricing tailwinds driving exceptional current revenue and a significant cash-flow inflection. The company is executing a strategic geographic pivot through its first major international acquisition in Canada, while streamlining its portfolio by terminating underperforming Mexican contracts. However, rapid Canadian diversification masks declining grades at core Mexican assets, and exceptional earnings beats hide structural cost increases across all core assets. The cash-flow inflection is heavily dependent on cyclical spot metal prices, and a sudden collapse in metal prices paired with a sticky, strong Mexican peso would expose the company's inflated structural cost base and trigger severe margin compression.
  2. Historical earnings show a strong recovery inflection driven by pricing tailwinds, despite severe margin pressure from unprecedented local currency appreciation and one-off adjustments. The most recent 1H26 results showcased record revenue and EBITDA beats on historic metal prices, paired with a 31% capital expenditure rationalization that boosted net cash.
  3. Fresnillo commands a premium sector ranking due to unparalleled silver asset scarcity, yet its forward momentum remains highly leveraged to spot metal prices. Guidance credibility is moderate given frequent production downgrades, while earnings remain highly sensitive to silver, gold, and peso fluctuations.
  4. The company's L3Y operational track record is marked by chronic volume and grade downgrades due to recurring underground bottlenecks, while financial delivery has been buoyed by gold outperformance.
