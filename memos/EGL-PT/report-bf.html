Outliers worth noting

Mota-Engil's revenue compound annual growth rate of 20% from 2021 through 2025 substantially exceeds typical infrastructure construction industry baselines and positions the group in the top decile of global multi-segment builders, creating questions about the sustainability of this exceptional expansion trajectory given the capitalintensive nature of the underlying businesses.
