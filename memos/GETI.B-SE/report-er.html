Corporate origins – established foundation: Founded in 1904 in Sweden as an agricultural equipment maker, Getinge entered medtech in 1932 producing sterilisers, expanded globally under Electrolux ownership in the 1960s, and was acquired in 1989 by entrepreneurs Carl Bennet and Rune Andersson before listing on Nasdaq Stockholm Large Cap in 1993. Carl Bennet AB remains the principal shareholder with 20% of capital and 50% of voting rights, providing longterm structural anchor ownership. Recurring revenues from consumables, spare parts, and services account for 67% of total turnover, providing substantial revenue visibility.
