[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = C

Thesis. Sonova is a self-help case on top of a product cycle in a quality oligopoly leader, and it is compelling as a business and not compelling as an investment at CHF 264.60. The shares re-rated from about 16x to 20.2x two-year-forward earnings between May and October 2026, so the price now pays in advance for a crowded launch window that has not yet been won, and the modal return from here is 8.5 percent a year over eighteen months and 9.4 percent a year over three, against the 20 percent bar.

  • The change inside the company is real, broad and already acted on.
    • A complete change of leadership between June and December 2025 (chair, chief executive, chief financial officer, chief operations officer, chief research officer) chose to narrow the perimeter, regionalise the company, take cost out and set a growth algorithm, and did all four within a year.
    • Consumer Hearing, about 5 percent of revenue at a CHF 35.8m normalised EBITA loss in FY2026, is in discontinued operations
      • Its removal is a permanent gain of about 200 basis points of group margin.
    • The four-region model went live on 1-Apr-2026
      • The productivity programme targets CHF 90m of run-rate savings by year four with net savings from year two
      • The mid-term frame is 5 to 10 percent local-currency sales growth and 7 to 12 percent core EBIT growth a year, with CHF 6bn of revenue by FY2030/31.
    • The screen's decision rule is met twice over: a chief-executive change together with a guidance-framework increase, and at least five of the twelve internal forces strong, with none of the seven red flags present.
  • The outputs so far are the product cycle's, not the corporate change's.
    • The Infinio and Sphere platforms launched in August 2024 under the previous team are what lifted the operating metrics
    • The corporate change's own outputs are still ahead.
    • Wholesale grew 10.9 percent in local currency in H2 FY2026 against a market growing 2 to 4 percent
      • Total US VA unit share reached 54.0 percent in July 2026, up 450 basis points year on year
      • Virto R reached a run-rate of about CHF 120m within months.
    • Year-two net savings, the exit's accretion in the continuing accounts, the regional model's commercial effect and the Asian build are forecasts, not reported figures
      • This is why the fit to 'Major Corporate Change' is crisp on inputs and less crisp on maturity.
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up and the sell-side is upgrading.
    • Three of the four key outputs are improving against the last three years, and estimates have risen on every horizon over six months with no cuts on any line.
    • The normalised EBITA margin rose to 22.5 percent in FY2026 from 21.6 percent, up 240 basis points in local currency, with H2 at 25.4 percent
      • Normalised EPS from continuing operations rose 16.0 percent in local currency to CHF 10.42.
    • FY2027 consensus EPS has risen 4.7 percent over six months and FY2028 4.2 percent
      • The named sources of further upgrades are specific: the smaller Sphere, year-two savings, component deflation and procurement.
    • Cash conversion is the one output that fell: operating free cash flow CHF 519.1m in FY2026 from CHF 581.7m, on tax, currency and a sharp fall in days payable
      • We read it as timing.
  • The engine runs inside the framework's band in local currency and below it in francs, which is the fact the whole return turns on.
    • Normalised EPS grew 16.0 percent in local currency in FY2026 and was flat in francs
    • Our modal path compounds EPS at about 9 percent a year in francs from CHF 10.34 (FY2026, brokers' basis) to CHF 13.40 in FY2029.
    • Translation cut FY2026 reported sales by CHF 221.0m and normalised EBITA by CHF 106.5m (150 basis points)
      • FY2027 guidance carries a 2 to 3 point core EBIT headwind at early-May-2026 rates
      • Hedges run one to six months only
      • The environment is a European stress episode in which the franc is bought.
    • Revenue growth contributes about 6.5 percent a year of the EPS growth and margin about 2.2 percent, the two components investors extrapolate
      • Buybacks contribute nothing because the CHF 1.5bn programme is paused.
  • The price already assumes the launch window is won.
    • At CHF 264.60 the shares are 20.20x two-year-forward consensus earnings, which implies 24-month-forward EPS of CHF 13.10
    • Consensus revenue of +8.3 percent in francs for FY2027 sits above the top of the 5 to 8 percent local-currency guidance once the guided franc drag is applied.
    • The multiple is at the 51st percentile of the stock's own five-year range (median 19.84x) and just below the ten-year median of 20.70x
      • The price is 47.9 percent higher over six months and CHF 0.80 below the 52-week high
      • The consensus target of CHF 245.43 is below the price and the buy share has fallen to 24 percent.
    • The research was written at CHF 193 to 209 and at 16x to 18x
      • Its re-rating argument has been consumed since it was written.
  • The return fails the bar in every branch but one, and that branch is one in five.
    • On the framework's convention (a 20.0x multiple on the earnings two years beyond each horizon, plus dividends) the value is CHF 299.0 at eighteen months (8.5 percent a year) and CHF 346.5 at three years (9.4 percent a year).
    • The bull branch needs the launch window won (odds about even) and the market then paying the ten-year 75th percentile of 23.10x (about two in five given the first): about 26 percent a year at odds of about one in five.
    • The plausibly worse drop-through (restructuring treated as recurring, tax drifting to 20 percent, a further 2 points a year of franc drag) cuts EPS growth to about 6 percent a year and the return to 1.5 percent a year over eighteen months
      • The probability-weighted expectation across the three branches is about 6.6 percent a year.
    • The case clears the bar on the modal path below about CHF 227 on the eighteen-month horizon and below about CHF 200 on both, which is where the shares traded between May and August 2026.
  • The chain holds the whole way and breaks only at the price.
    • The change has travelled all six layers of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, from resources to choices to actions, into the operating metrics, the financial statements and the sell-side's estimates
    • What the market has done is pay for the layers that are not yet reported.

  • The case therefore is a good business and a good corporate change bought by the market in advance: the pattern is 'Major Corporate Change' at B, the foundations are B, and the return at this price is F, which makes the thesis a C for a capital decision.

  • Rating = C, because materiality for a capital decision is the product of the three Elements and the third fails at this price: the input change forces are strong, broad and acted (B), the outputs are improving with named sources of upgrades (B), but the modal return from CHF 264.60 is 8.5 to 9.4 percent a year (F).

    • Not B because the return fails outright rather than narrowly
    • Not D because the change is real, dated and already in the financial statements.
  • Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.
    • The strongest case against is that the growth is real but deceptive: a "false friend" whose first derivative is good and whose second is already turning.
    • Store-network growth in Germany and Canada masks margin dilution from lead-generation costs the market cannot see
      • Costco volumes at $300 to $400 and managed care at $300 to $500 lift units while the group average price decays
      • The Costco re-entry, the VA contract gains and Virto R all annualise in H2 FY2027 at the moment Demant's Reveal, GN's Sensia and WS Audiology's platform launch into the same channels
      • Demant's Zeal has already cut Sonova's combined rechargeable and multi-processor custom share in the VA from 61 to 45 percent
      • Amplifon phases out about CHF 60m of wholesale revenue from late FY2027 as it internalises procurement from GN Hearing
      • The franc takes 2 to 3 points a year off core EBIT
      • And at 20x, with the consensus target below the price, the downside is Morgan Stanley's bear case of CHF 135 at 14x on CHF 9.62 of EPS, or Jefferies' CHF 128.

    • The research's own analysis of competing hypotheses scored this profile with zero hard inconsistencies against four for the bull case.

    • We concede the annualisation, which is arithmetic

      • The custom-category loss, which the VA data shows
      • The Amplifon phase-out, which is the buyer's stated plan
      • The franc, which is structural until the cost base moves
      • And the opacity of retail unit economics, which the chief executive keeps so by declining to discuss Costco or margin by customer.
    • We hold that this is a hypothesis about the second derivative of a business whose first derivative is good on every public series: total VA share rose 450 basis points year on year to 54.0 percent in July 2026 after Zeal's entry
      • Wholesale grew 10.9 percent against a market of 2 to 4 percent
      • Gross margin held at 73.7 percent through the Mexicali ramp and the dearer dual-chip bill of materials
      • Leverage is 1.1x
      • The exit is a permanent 200 basis point gain.
    • The hypothesis has two dated public tests (two consecutive months of total VA share below 48 percent; an H1 FY2027 wholesale print below 5.0 percent local currency), and a zero-inconsistency score is a property of a hypothesis that predicts a deterioration which has not yet appeared, not evidence that it will.
    • Where the competing hypothesis and we agree is the conclusion that matters for capital: at CHF 264.60 the modal return does not clear the bar, so the "false friend" need not be true for the shares to be the wrong price
      • It need only be possible.

    • Four of the five majors launch premium platforms in the second half of 2026
    • And Amplifon's purchase of GN Hearing (DKK 17.0 billion) will internalise its procurement from 40 to about 90 percent by 2029, a phase-out of about CHF 60m of Sonova revenue from late FY2027.
  • Total share: against that, Sonova's total VA unit share rose to 54.0 percent in July 2026, up 450 basis points year on year and 48 basis points month on month, and wholesale prices have remained highly stable across the key manufacturers.
  • German retail: Demant also bought KIND (about 650 to 900 stores; the figures differ by source) and is now the top German retailer ahead of Sonova's GEERS and AudioNova network, Germany being 25 percent of AudioNova's footprint.
  • Launch window: Oticon Reveal and ReSound Sensia were announced in August 2026, and Demant raised guidance on Reveal on 9-Oct-2026
    • The window is the single test every source names, and it is unresolved.
  • Not F because Sonova is gaining total share (its largest year-on-year wholesale share gain in six years in FY2026) and the Zeal loss is in one sub-category of one channel
    • Not C because the launch window is unresolved and it is the test that decides the return.

  • Rating = D, because one macro force acts on this company far more than on the universe, the Swiss franc: translation cut FY2026 reported sales by CHF 221.0m (6.1 points) and normalised EBITA by CHF 106.5m (150 basis points), the cost base is about 15 percent in francs against 1 percent of sales, FY2027 guidance carries a 1 to 2 point sales and 2 to 3 point core EBIT headwind at early-May-2026 rates, and hedges run one to six months only (CHF 277.7m of forwards at March 2026, down from CHF 391.3m).
  • Regime: the environment this week (2026-10-09) is a European credit-and-sovereign stress episode, the regime in which the franc is bought as a safe haven
    • The research names safe-haven franc appreciation cycles as the profit-shock mechanism

  • Sensitivities: a 5 percent USD/CHF move is CHF 65m of sales and CHF 17m of EBITDA
    • A 5 percent EUR/CHF move is CHF 70m of sales and CHF 25m of operating profit.
  • Not F because the rest of the macro picture is benign for a defensive healthcare name: fixed-rate debt (a 100 basis point rise in rates would add CHF 1.7m of pre-tax income on CHF 521.3m of cash) and a defensive turn in the environment.
