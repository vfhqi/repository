The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Price (the main driver): contractual CPI-linked steps each December on about two million postpaid contracts: 2.6% (December 2025), about 3.7% (December 2026, set by 2026 Greek inflation of 3.8% for January to September), then about 2.8%, 2.3%, 2.2%. Revenue effect about +22 / +30 / +22 / +18 / +16 EURm (FY2026 to FY2030) at about 90% to 95% margin. Without price, core EBITDA falls about 0.6% to 0.9% a year in FY2027 and FY2028 and is flat after.
  • Volume and mix: FTTH homes passed 2.4m (end-2026) to 3.5m (2030); retail FTTH subscribers about 800k to about 1.48m; postpaid net additions 55k to 60k a quarter falling to about 35k; prepaid lines -5% to -6% a year. Net volume and mix adds only about +5 EURm of EBITDA a year, because prepaid erosion offsets postpaid gains (Q2 2026 mobile service growth of +2.3% was almost all the December 2025 price step).
  • Transit exit: -170 EURm of revenue in FY2026 and about -125 EURm in FY2027 (company: -130 EURm), zero EBITDA effect; it is most of the FY2027 revenue fall and about 3 points of the margin rise.
  • System Solutions: +30% in FY2026 (H1 2026 299.8 against 182.5 EURm), -15% in FY2027 as the RRF ends in December 2026 (staged, not a cliff: spill-over and maintenance), -5% in FY2028, then +2% to +3%; about -12 EURm of EBITDA in FY2027.
  • National wholesale: -3% (FY2026), -6% (FY2027: EETT's 10% to 13% FTTH wholesale cut plus early migration), -8%, -8%, -7% as the Vodafone and PPC joint venture (completion targeted H1 2027) takes Vodafone volume; about -51 EURm of EBITDA cumulatively by FY2030. Bear case -15% to -20% a year from FY2028 if Nova follows.
  • Costs: gross efficiency about 25 EURm a year (voluntary exits, indirect costs 28% to about 26.5% of service revenue, digital, copper switch-off from FY2028) against about 17 EURm a year of cost inflation (wages, services, energy hedge cover falling from 80% to about 66% in FY2027, the 2027 UEFA renewal); net about +8 EURm a year.
  • D&A: rises slowly (capex above D&A; the 78 EURm spectrum renewal amortised from FY2028; rural fibre concession). Slightly above consensus (597 / 601 / 607 / 621).
  • Net financial items: the 500 EURm 0.875% bond (matured September 2026) replaced at about 3%; about +10 EURm a year from FY2027; includes about 8 EURm of lease interest.
