The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Lung (the cash engine). United States EVLP disposables grew 183 percent in Q3 2026 and 92 percent year to date against a destocked 2025 base; three OPO hubs are activated against a target of four to five. I model lung revenue excluding the largest customer and excluding the Q3 2026 timing items (8.5 of finance-lease recognition; about 10 of customer inventory build) growing 25 to 30 percent in 2027 and about 20 to 25 percent in 2028, driven by the DCD donor mix, the CMS OPO rule upheld on 28 September 2026 and hub activations. The largest customer (Lung Bioengineering, 132 of 2025 revenue, about 150 in 2026) moves to its own approved platform while continuing to buy STEEN Solution and components: I model its revenue at about 85 in 2027 and about 80 thereafter, a step-down of about 65 (DNB Carnegie's 5 to 6 percent of 2027 revenue). Net lung growth: +22 percent in 2026, +8 percent in 2027, +18 percent in 2028, +14 percent in 2029, +13 percent in 2030.
  • Heart. CE mark 11 August 2026; French national reimbursement 18 May 2026; derogation sales of 22 to 24 a quarter in Q2 and Q3 2026 convert to commercial pricing from Q4 2026; Tier 2 markets (the Netherlands, Austria, the United Kingdom, Belgium) obtain reimbursement during 2027; about 25 commercial centres by end-2027 and about 40 by end-2028. United States: PMA received by the FDA on 28 September 2026; I assume approval in late 2028 and launch in Q1 2029, one year after the company's "targeted end-2027", with Continued Access Protocol sales only before then. Heart revenue: 90, 155, 275, 420 (United States about 90), 580 (United States about 190).
  • Liver and kidney.   • Earnings per share appears on four bases, which must not be mixed: reported basic; FactSet's adjusted diluted, before unusual items; the Viewforth definition, which is the model's standing convention; and the brokers' adjusted basis, on which consensus is based.
  • Consensus is FactSet's median of the broker estimates it holds. Where FactSet's line-by-line consensus differs from the broker-by-broker detail, the line-by-line table takes precedence.
  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
