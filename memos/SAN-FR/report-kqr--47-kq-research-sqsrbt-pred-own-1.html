Section 2 – Dupixent US Prescription Trajectory: Atopic Dermatitis versus COPD and

  3. Emerging competition from long-acting biologics and alternative therapeutic mechanisms presents a mid-term market share risk to Sanofi's long-term franchise dominance.

Pulmonology

Mature Volume Trajectory in Atopic Dermatitis

1.
Dominant expansion: Jefferies Research reported on October 30, 2026, that Dupixent's US total and new prescriptions continue to increase in atopic dermatitis despite new competitive entrants. Underpenetrated potential: Morgan Stanley reported on September 15, 2026, that US biologic penetration in atopic dermatitis stands at only high-teens to 20%. Clinical Profiles and

1.
Dosing Schedules Targeted blockade: Eli Lilly's Ebglyss selectively neutralizes the IL-13 cytokine to resolve inflammatory lesions, whereas Galderma's Nemluvio blocks the IL-31 receptor alpha to target the chronic itch-scratch cycle. This distinct mechanism allows Nemluvio to excel in pruritic symptoms, while Ebglyss matches Dupixent's skin lesion efficacy but offers a lower incidence of conjunctivitis. Consequently, these agents segment the market, allowing physicians to choose therapies based on specific patient symptoms. This therapeutic division is expected to slowly erode Dupixent's dominance.
2.
Convenient scheduling: Eli Lilly's Ebglyss offers a significant maintenance advantage with its FDA-approved once-monthly or once-every-eight-weeks dosing regimens. This extended maintenance interval is highly preferred by patients over Dupixent's standard every-two-weeks injection schedule. Galderma's Nemluvio similarly supports once-monthly or once-every-eight-weeks dosing for patients who achieve clear skin. These extended schedules are expected to improve long-term patient compliance and challenge Dupixent's market retention.
3.
