Intangible Capitalization Policies

DNB Carnegie's research on 10 September 2026 highlights this extension as an aggressive policy that defers depreciation into future periods, artificially lifting current margins. Provisioning and Reserve Reductions

4.

DNB Carnegie's report on 10 September 2026 shows Asker's reserve fell to 6.5% of gross inventory from 14.9% in 2021.
