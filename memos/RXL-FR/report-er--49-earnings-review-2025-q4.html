  1. Rexel's medium-term outlook is increasingly shaped by structural electrification tailwinds and active portfolio optimization.
  2. The strategic shift toward North American data centers has structurally elevated the company's margin floor.
  3. Despite macroeconomic volatility in Europe, management remains confident in achieving its medium-term profitability targets.

Secured leadership continuity: Under Guillaume Texier, who assumed the chief executive officer role in September 2021, Rexel has successfully pivoted toward a high-growth electrification and digital services business model. Ambitious profitability objectives: Under the Axelerate 2028 strategic plan, Rexel aims to achieve a current adjusted EBITA margin of above 7% in the medium term. This self-help agenda represents a structural stepup from historical trough margin levels.
