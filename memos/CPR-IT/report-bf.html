EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Core Business Model and Portfolio Architecture

1.
Global aperitif leadership: Campari operates as the undisputed leader in premium aperitifs, with Aperol and Campari anchoring 44% of net sales and driving high-margin organic growth across four strategic Houses of Brands.
2.
Premium spirits diversification: The portfolio spans whiskey (14% of sales), tequila (10%), and cognac (5%), targeting premiumisation and occasion expansion across developed and emerging markets.

Financial Profile Versus Peers

3.
Industry-leading organic growth: Campari delivered an 8.40% organic sales CAGR from 2019 to 2025, outpacing Diageo by 3.50 percentage points and Pernod Ricard by 5 percentage points.
4.
Expanding operating margins: Adjusted EBIT margin reached 20.90% in FY25, below Diageo's 30% but supported by 60 basis points of organic expansion and structural cost containment delivering 200 basis points by FY27.
5.
Superior capital efficiency: Post-tax ROCE of 10% in FY25 exceeded category specialist Rémy Cointreau's 7.70%, reflecting disciplined capital allocation and faster deleveraging to 2.50x net debt/EBITDA.

Value Chain Position and Integration

6.
Downstream distribution control: Owned route-to-market footprint expanded from 5 countries in 2004 to 27 nations representing 93% of sales, reclaiming third-party margins and improving working capital efficiency.
7.
Manufacturing footprint scale: Production capacity doubled from 8 plants to 25 sites globally, securing vertical integration across distilling, aging, and packaging stages to capture premium profit pools.

Supply Chain Dynamics and Input Exposure

8.
Favorable agave tailwinds: Collapsing Mexican agave spot prices from MXN 30/kg to under MXN 4/kg drove Espolòn gross margins above 55% in H1 2025, providing material COGS relief.
9.
Elevated packaging vulnerability: Packaging materials represent 31% of COGS, exceeding Diageo's 28%, exposing Campari to persistent glass bottle inflation and energy-intensive upstream cost pressures.
10.
Concentrated US wholesaler reliance: Heavy dependence on two dominant national distributors under the three-tier system limits direct retail control and exposes non-priority brands to aggressive destocking pressures.

Operator Quality and Foundation Strength

11.
Proven leadership stability: CEO Simon Hunt's 30 years of spirits expertise and prior William Grant CEO tenure (2016-2020) ended prolonged management uncertainty and re-energized commercial execution.
12.
Robust organizational health: Exceptional 84% employee survey participation and Fair Pay recertification confirm strong talent infrastructure and equitable reward structures supporting long-term capability building.
13.
Disciplined capital allocation: Faster-than-planned deleveraging to 2.50x, over €210 million in asset divestments, and a 54% dividend increase demonstrate strict shareholders-first mindset and balanced capital returns.
14.
Unrivalled brand equity moats: Aperol's 17% 20-year CAGR, 93% owned distribution reach, and 300,000 bartender academy engagements create defensible customer pull and downstream margin capture.
15.
Sustained volume outperformance: Organic sales CAGR of 8.40% (2019-2025) and 4% US volume growth through January 2026 significantly outpaced Diageo (-3.10%) and Pernod Ricard (-4.10%), validating portfolio resilience.
16.
Visible margin expansion pathway: 60 basis points of organic EBIT margin improvement in FY25 and 72.70% recurring free cash flow conversion support medium-term operating leverage and structural efficiency gains.

Structural Disruption Threats

17.
Generational sobriety headwinds: Gen-Z moderation trends and health consciousness threaten long-term traditional spirits volumes, while ready-to-drink prepared cocktails achieved 22.60% volume growth in FY25, diluting gross margins.
18.
Fickle consumer loyalty risk: Low switching barriers for alternative beverages and aggressive micro-distiller competition challenge Aperol's spritz occasion dominance, particularly among younger demographic cohorts.

Exceptional topline outperformance: Campari delivered an 8.40% organic sales CAGR from 2019 to 2025, outpacing Diageo by 3.50 percentage points and Pernod Ricard by 5 percentage points–placing it in the top 5% of global spirits peers for sustained revenue compounding. Unprecedented agave cost deflation: Spot agave prices collapsed 80% from MXN 30/kg to MXN 2- 4/kg, delivering a structural gross margin tailwind far exceeding typical commodity cycles and positioning Campari's tequila portfolio in the bottom 5% of global input cost exposure. [Cross-ref: Section 2 + Section 6]
3.
Best-in-class employee engagement: The 2025 Camparista Survey achieved an exceptional 84% global participation rate using Microsoft Viva Glint–significantly exceeding typical Fortune 500 benchmarks of 60- 70% and placing Campari in the top 5% for organizational listening and talent activation.
