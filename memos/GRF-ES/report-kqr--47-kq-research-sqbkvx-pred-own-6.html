Section 2 – China's Imported Human Albumin Market in 2026

Section summary

Hospital Consumption Under DRG Rules

1.
Restrictive policies: Chinese regulatory authorities implemented stringent diagnostic-related-group payment regulations starting in early 2025 to control rising public medical expenses. This comprehensive analysis of the Chinese albumin market evaluates 18 relevant sources, 5 peer companies, and 2 comparable past cases. These fiscal limits restrict government reimbursement of imported human albumin to patients with severe illnesses and specific biochemical thresholds. Consequently, hospitals are forced to reduce inappropriate clinical utilization, resulting in severely constrained clinical volumes across key healthcare facilities.
2.
Controlled prescribing: The Chinese healthcare insurance fund faces post-pandemic deficits, prompting provincial administrators to expand clinical audits to pharmacies and private healthcare centers. Independent channel checks indicate that these policy changes have caused hospital usage of human albumin in some regions to fall by half. Although overall national volumes grew slightly by 0% to 3% in early 2026, the strict oversight of claims continues to depress traditional hospital prescription rates.

Supply Chains and Pricing Pressures

5.
Elevated inventories: Chinese plasma distributors currently navigate a prolonged supply-driven market correction characterized by high levels of manufacturer channel stock. Because public hospitals have shown no brand favoritism since 2025, this premium pricing structure creates heightened market share risks for foreign players.
