Section 1 – Business Model and Organisation

Section summary
  1. Koninklijke Philips N.V. operates three primary business segments with concentrated geographic exposure in mature economies led by North America and Europe.
  2. Royal Philips combines spot capital equipment sales with stable, recurring service agreements to capture long-term clinical and consumer demand.
  3. Royal Philips demonstrates operating leverage driven by cost productivity programs, consistently meeting or exceeding its financial performance targets.
  4. Leadership focus shifts to strategic portfolio expansions and active margin recovery while resolving lingering legal and regulatory obligations.

Segment Structure and Geographic Footprint

No other active broker research or named analysts with relevant reports were identified in the available research findings. The analysis of Koninklijke Philips N.V. is therefore heavily informed by this single institutional perspective alongside official company disclosures.

Diverse business portfolio: Koninklijke Philips N.V. generates its revenue through three principal segments, consisting of Diagnosis & Treatment, Connected Care, and Personal Health. The Diagnosis & Treatment segment represents the largest division, accounting for 48% of total group sales in the fiscal year 2025. Connected Care contributed 28% of group sales in fiscal year 2025, while Personal Health made up 21% of the total.

Specialized medical technologies: The professional healthcare portfolio of Koninklijke Philips N.V. includes leading market positions in Image Guided Therapy, Precision Diagnosis, and hospital Monitoring. Within Precision Diagnosis, the company manufactures advanced diagnostic imaging systems, cardiovascular ultrasound equipment, and informatics platforms. The Connected Care division specializes in hospital and ambulatory patient monitoring solutions, clinical analytics, and home Sleep and Respiratory Care.
4.
Concentration in mature geographies: Koninklijke Philips N.V. is organized into North America, Greater China, and International Regions, with mature geographies delivering EUR 12,915 million in sales in the fiscal year 2025. Growth geographies generated EUR 4,917 million in sales in fiscal year 2025 compared to EUR 4,863 million in fiscal year 2024.

The rising demand for clinical informatics and automated workflow optimization drives sales of high-margin enterprise software inside the Connected Care segment. The consumer-facing Personal Health division targets premium product categories such as advanced power toothbrushes and shavers to capture growing wellness trends.
