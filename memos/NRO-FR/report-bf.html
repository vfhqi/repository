Appendix

Outliers worth noting 3 findings

1.
Neurones generated an outstanding 39% return on capital employed in the 2025 financial year, representing a statistical outlier that places the group in the top 5% of European IT services providers on capital efficiency metrics. By contrast, peer Sopra Steria recorded a return on capital employed of 20.40% in 2025, while Capgemini's operating profitability stood at 13.30% in 2025, supported by its massive global offshore delivery model and scale. This exceptional return performance stems from Neurones' massive net cash position of €337 million at the end of December 2025 and almost zero financial debt, with total liabilities dominated by trade payables of €235 million. However, management has not provided a comprehensive strategic justification for maintaining such an enormous under-leveraged cash cushion rather than deploying it into higherreturn M&A or accelerated organic expansion. The extreme capital conservatism appears misaligned with the group's articulated growth ambitions and structurally limits absolute shareholder value creation despite delivering impressive percentage returns
2.
Neurones reduced its receivables outstanding period to 74 days in 2025, the lowest level in fifteen years, while consolidated supplier work-in-progress stood at 36.1 days in 2025, down from 38.9 days in 2024. This working capital performance represents a corporate oddity within the European IT services landscape, as peer Capgemini saw its days sales outstanding rise to 67 days in 1H26 compared to 65 days in 1H25, and factored €130 million of receivables in 1H26 to bolster cash flow, while Sopra Steria targeted 50 days of DSO in FY2025, up from 45 days in FY2024 due to collection tensions. The dramatic fifteen-year low in DSO suggests Neurones has achieved an unusual balance of power with its enterprise clients, yet management has not articulated the specific commercial practices, contract structuring innovations, or client relationship dynamics that enable this outlying collection efficiency. The sustained improvement during a period of industry-wide payment pressure raises questions about whether the group is offering implicit pricing concessions or accepting lower-margin contracts to accelerate cash conversion
3.
[Cross-ref: Section 8]. Neurones achieved 5.10% organic revenue expansion in 2025, significantly outperforming a declining domestic IT services market, while this organic performance compared to a decline of 2.20% in total reported revenues at peer Sopra Steria. This divergence places Neurones in the top 5% of French mid-market IT providers on organic momentum during a cyclical downturn. The domestic digital consulting and services revenue experienced a cyclical contraction of 1.8% in 2025, with industry association Numeum projecting a modest recovery of 1% growth for French IT services in 2026. Management attributes this outperformance to completing 60 AI implementation projects in 2025, scaling capabilities under a newly appointed Chief AI Officer and French enterprise cloud planning increasingly prioritizing data sovereignty and localized control of regulated data. However, the company has not disclosed granular win-rate data, specific client defections from larger competitors, or quantified market share gains by vertical to substantiate this structural share capture versus temporary project timing benefits. The absence of detailed competitive displacement evidence leaves open whether this outperformance reflects sustainable positioning or cyclical fortune
