Outliers worth noting

The second material outlier is KPN's adjusted-to-reported EBITDA gap, where reported EBITDA of EUR 2,716 million diverges from adjusted EBITDA AL of EUR 2,636 million, with lease adjustments totaling EUR 155 million. This adjustment magnitude places KPN in the top 5% of European telecom operators for non-GAAP metric reconciliation spreads. While peer European operators exclude lease expenses from EBITDA, making adjusted EBITDA AL the standard comparable metric, the absolute quantum of adjustments significantly exceeds peer medians including Proximus and BT Group.

This pattern raises questions about the sustainability of reported cash generation and whether management incentive structures create bias toward optimistic adjusted metrics. [Cross-ref: Section 4 + Section 8]
