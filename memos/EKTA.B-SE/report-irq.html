  1. The case, and what the holding means for the questions. The second quarter gave the revenue half its first evidence (organic +5%, the first sales beat under this chief executive) but from converting old orders, not new demand (book-to-bill 1.00), and the gross margin without the refund (about 38.2%, our arithmetic) sat below the memo's 39.5% test. What the call should settle, and why

The case in one line: a number two in a two-player industry whose new management has fixed cost, price and order quality, so FY27 profit is covered and the margin range looks conservative, while FY28 rests on a gross margin and an order intake that the second quarter did not yet prove. Six judgements decide whether to add, hold or cut.

  1. Where FY27 lands. The first half was about 12.7% (derived in our scoring); the top of the range needs about 14.2% in the second half, against 15.6% reported a year earlier, which was flattered by capitalised development. Viewforth has 13.7% (EBIT SEK 2,363m); DNB Carnegie 13.9%. A steer that the second half holds last year's underlying margin puts FY27 near 13.9% and supports Jefferies' 3% to 5% consensus EPS rise; caution on fourth-quarter mix leaves it at 13.3% to 13.5%, in consensus already.
  2. The gross margin without refunds. Second quarter about 38.2% (our arithmetic) against 37.9% a year earlier and 41.1% in the first quarter. One point of gross margin moves FY28 EPS about 8% (SEK 0.36). Hardware mix that takes back all the price gain is the memo's central fragility; mix that eases as Evo pricing reaches revenue retires it.
  3. Orders. Book-to-bill 1.00 after 1.11; Jefferies: orders down 1% a year on average over 18 months. Two points of FY28 growth are SEK 0.29 of EPS (6%). Orders growing ahead of sales in the third quarter support FY28 at 4%; another quarter near 1.00 makes consensus FY28 revenue (+4.5%) look high.
  4. How FY28 will be guided in May. First year without refunds, hedged dollar rate down from about 10.12 to about 9.14 kronor, Accela launched. The memo puts a FY28 EPS cut of 2% or more between May 2027 and March 2028 at about 35%, a raise at 25%. A first range that opens at or above consensus (13.7% margin) is the memo's third sign for adding; a buffer below it is the likeliest path.
  5. The regions that carry the revenue half. Consensus mean FY27 China SEK 2,610m against our SEK 2,350m; Americas SEK 4,756m against our SEK 4,660m. China converting faster than we model is the plausible upside to our revenue; an Accela-led pause in United States orders is the plausible downside.
  6. Cash. Free cash flow SEK 48m in the quarter. Our FY27 is SEK 1,524m; DNB Carnegie SEK 1,647m. A second half that reverses receivables keeps the dividend story intact; slipping customer advances would be the first sign orders are hurting cash.
