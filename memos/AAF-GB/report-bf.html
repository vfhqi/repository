Airtel Africa achieved a five-year constant-currency revenue CAGR of 21%, representing a top-decile growth trajectory within global telecommunications operators and significantly exceeding MTN Group's 16% constant-currency CAGR over the identical measurement period. This revenue velocity translated into operating free cash flow growth of 14% on a reported-currency basis, massively outpacing MTN's 3% OpFCF CAGR despite both operators facing similar macro-currency headwinds across overlapping Sub-Saharan African markets.

The growth anomaly stems from Airtel's aggressive smartphone-led data migration strategy and deeper mobile money penetration, which collectively drove data revenue to surpass voice as the primary revenue stream while maintaining strict cost discipline. [Cross-ref: Section 1, Section 2, Section 9]
