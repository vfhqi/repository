Lenders are not passing through the rate hikes on the liability side, which is in line with expectations of low deposit betas.
18.

Standalone data from August 2026 shows Romanian private sector deposits grew by 8.58% year-on-year, driven by an 8.09% increase in local currency deposits. Strong deposit inflows provide Banca Transilvania with a low-cost, stable liability base to fund its aggressive loan growth targets. Healthy deposit dynamics support loan-to-deposit ratios well below statutory limits across the major regional banks.

Domestic banks must closely monitor foreign currency lending to prevent systemic credit risk from unhedged corporate borrowers.

Sovereign exposure remains a core component of the balance sheet for the top three domestic banks.
