Peer mills such as Outokumpu similarly struggle with sub-optimal utilization, but Aperam's 65-75% range places it at the lower end of European stainless operating rates (see Section 3 and Section 7). Excessive executive and board continuity risks insular strategic planning, potentially blinding management to aggressive competitive moves by European peers.
