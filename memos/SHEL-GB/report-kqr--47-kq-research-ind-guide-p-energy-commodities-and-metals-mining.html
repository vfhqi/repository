Documented capital overruns at Teck Resources QB2 and BHP Jansen Stage 2 alongside completed turnarounds at Saipem and Copper 360 provide unambiguous historical templates for risk assessment and opportunity identification that require minimal forward-looking assumptions.

The medium-confidence tier encompasses cyclical timing indicators and cross-asset analogues where structural relationships hold historically but exhibit variable lead times across different commodity cycles. Memory cycle analogues showing resource equities de-rating three to six months before earnings peaks offer valuable frameworks, yet the precise timing windows remain inherently probabilistic given macroeconomic regime shifts and geopolitical disruptions can compress or extend historical patterns.

Similarly, sovereign spread widening as a valuation variable for French infrastructure represents a theoretically sound but empirically noisy signal given multiple confounding political and monetary policy variables. The low-confidence dimension centers on emerging structural themes lacking sufficient historical precedent for robust pattern recognition. Behind-the-meter data center power plays developed by Orron Energy represent novel energy infrastructure business models where monetization pathways and competitive dynamics remain largely untested at commercial scale.
