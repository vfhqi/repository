The Innovation Box Incentive Scheme

5.

The company originally concluded this agreement with the Dutch tax authorities in 2021 to apply retrospectively for the fiscal years 2019 through 2024. This regime historically lowered the effective rate to 20.6% in 2022 by providing a substantial tax deduction on eligible profits.
6.
Extended benefit timeline: Alfen successfully extended its existing innovation box agreement with the Dutch tax authorities in December 2024, securing the favorable treatment for another five years. This new ruling applies from 2025 through 2029, assuming that the underlying facts and operational circumstances do not change. The prolonged agreement provides long-term tax visibility for investors, ensuring that returning profitability will immediately benefit from a significantly lower tax burden.
7.
Zero current tax benefits: Alfen recorded a 0% tax benefit from the innovation box scheme in the fiscal year 2024 because of the net taxable loss. This represents a sharp decline from 2023, when the innovation box reduced Alfen's effective tax rate by 3.4%. This volatility shows that tax incentives provide no relief when operational performance deteriorates, meaning that the positive earnings per share impact is entirely linked to achieving operating profitability.
