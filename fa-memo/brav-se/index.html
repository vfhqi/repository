The reasoning behind the inputs

Bases used in Parts 1 to 3. - EBITDA is adjusted: EBITA before items affecting comparability, plus depreciation and amortisation excluding acquired intangibles (including right-of-use depreciation under IFRS 16). Unrounded margins: 8.53%, 8.45%, 8.55%, 8.66%, 8.64%. Excluded items: - the SEK 158m tax-free ABEKA gain (Q2 2026); - Swedish restructuring of about SEK 75m in FY2026 (SEK 20m Q1, SEK 40m Q2, about SEK 15m Q3); - any booking in H2 2026 of the about SEK 145m interest and cost element of the Stavanger award. - Amortisation of acquired intangibles is close to nil for Bravida on the FY2025 profit and loss arithmetic, so the model's EBIT equals adjusted EBITA. - Net financial items include lease interest (about SEK 50 to 60m). Lease payments are principal repayments only. - Working capital is on FactSet's basis (receivables plus inventories less payables): 20.8% at end-FY2025 and 22.5% at Q2 2026. I use it as the proxy for total operating working capital, including customer advances. - Acquisitions less disposals in FY2026 is SEK +208m from ABEKA less about SEK 190m of bolt-on spend (SEK 113m in H1 per FactSet, plus Åslunds El and others). - Buybacks in FY2026: the two completed SEK 100m programmes plus about SEK 50m of an assumed third programme in Q4 2026. FY2027 includes the redistribution of the Stavanger cash. - Change in share count is conversions of incentive C shares into ordinary shares (178,138 in May 2026).

The operating drivers behind the inputs

Revenue. - Core organic growth: about +5% (FY2026), +4.5%, +3.0%, +2.5%, +3.0%. - FY2026 is the lagged Swedish and Nordic recovery on easy comparisons (Q3 2025 organic −2.5%, Q4 2025 −2.2%). - From 2027 roughly half is price that passes through wage and material inflation. - FY2028 to FY2029 slow as the Riksbank and Norges Bank tightening from late 2026 reaches installation revenue. - Acquired growth: +1.0%, +1.5%, +2.0%, +2.0%, +2.0%. Private-equity consolidators bid for the same targets. - ABEKA: −1.25 points (FY2026) and −0.4 points (FY2027). - Currency: about −0.3% and −0.5% in FY2026 to FY2027. - Data-centre contracts, by year (FY2026 / FY2027 / FY2028 / FY2029): - Green Mountain (Bravida's share NOK 4.5bn, cost-plus, end-2026 to early 2029): 100 / 1,700 / 1,750 / 650. - atNorth (about SEK 2.2bn, principal contractor including civil works, about two years; about SEK 300m allowed for slippage): 300 / 1,050 / 550. - EcoDataCenter (SEK 650m to Q1 2028): 230 / 350 / 50. - A possible further SEK 850m Swedish award (press release 25 August 2026, unverified, half weight): 50 / 200 / 100. - Refill limited until about mid-2028: management can run about two large projects at once.

Margin. - Recovery-phase drop-through (the share of each extra krona of revenue reaching EBITA) of about 13% in H2 2026, fading toward the average margin as idle capacity is used in 2027. About 90% of the cost base is variable. - Swedish savings: SEK 40m inside FY2026, the full SEK 65m from FY2027. - Finland from about 1.5% (FY2025) to 3.3%, 4.0% and 4.5%. - 2027 Swedish wage round: a −SEK 25m lag effect, recovered by SEK 15m in FY2028. How to read these figures

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
