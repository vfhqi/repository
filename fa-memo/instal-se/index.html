The reasoning behind the inputs

Part 4 is the second set's reasoning as written. Where a later set changed an input, that input's figure in the tables above is the changed one and its reasons are in the changed-inputs file named above; Part 4 is not rewritten.

Basis of the inputs. EBITDA is on the brokers' basis (before amortisation of acquired intangibles and before items affecting comparability), which is the company's adjusted EBITA plus depreciation of property, equipment and right-of-use assets; FY2025 actual on this basis is SEK 1,229m (9.0 percent) against adjusted EBITA of SEK 875m (6.4 percent), so the depreciation line runs at about 2.6 percent of revenue. EBITDA less the Part 1 depreciation line therefore equals adjusted EBITA (SEK 1,039m, 1,248m, 1,386m, 1,516m, 1,640m for FY2026 to FY2030; margins 6.9, 7.4, 7.7, 7.9, 8.0 percent). Revenue for FY2025 is SEK 13,598m (FactSet; SEK 13,599m on the brokers' basis), so the growth inputs give revenue of SEK 15,053m, 16,860m, 17,995m, 19,195m, 20,496m.

Two things the Financial Analyst must know about the inputs. 1. The form has no line for minority interests, and minorities are material here (SEK 32m in FY2025, SEK 36m in H1 2026 alone before Fabri; Fabri at 51 percent from 1 August 2026 adds about SEK 55m a year). I have therefore folded the minority charge into "Net financial items", grossed up at the tax rate so that the model's post-tax profit is attributable profit. The two components are: net financial items proper SEK -225m, -220m, -210m, -210m, -215m; minorities SEK -70m, -125m, -135m, -115m, -100m, grossed up at the year's tax rate to SEK -91m, -163m, -178m, -151m, -132m. If the model has its own minorities line, enter the financial items at the first set of figures and the minorities at the second (ungrossed) set instead. The model's interest cover and any cash-interest derivation should use the first set. 2. Working capital is on the FactSet gross definition the block uses (receivables plus inventories less payables; 11.7 percent of revenue at end-FY2025 and at Q2 2026; 13.8 percent at end-FY2024; 13.1 percent at end-FY2023), not the company's net definition (1.6 percent at end-2025, 2.3 percent at Q2 2026). Holding FY2026 at 11.7 percent charges the model about SEK 170m of build, which blends the operating build I expect (about SEK 100m, after a Q4 release smaller than Q4 2025's SEK 451m) with Fabri's acquired balance (about SEK 160m, which in the accounts arrives through the acquisition line). From FY2027 the small annual improvement reflects the Instalco 2.0 working-capital controls; I do not assume SPIE-style negative working capital.
