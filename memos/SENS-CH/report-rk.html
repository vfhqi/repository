EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sensirion Holding faces material medium-term earnings risk from high operating leverage and concentration in regulatory HVAC markets if core industrial demand normalizes rapidly. The quantitative downside case projects revenue contraction to CHF 290 million through unwinding regulatory HVAC frontloading growth, collapsing EBITDA margins to 10% mirroring historical cyclical underutilization patterns, severe earnings disappointment with downside EPS falling to CHF 0.50 as operating leverage amplifies demand shocks, and depressed valuation multiples contracting the EV/EBITDA multiple to 12x due to protracted R&D monetization delays.

The overall likelihood of the bear case is medium, tempered by strong liquidity reserves, with stalling A2L refrigerant sensor demand representing the single largest near-term revenue risk, though accelerating EV battery-monitoring series production would disprove the core bear thesis.

Section 10 – Pre-Mortem: Conclusion

Sensirion faces multiple operational, competitive, and macroeconomic downside risks that could trigger severe margin compression and revenue declines over the next three years. Looking back from a hypothetical vantage point in 2029 where earnings have badly disappointed, the most likely pathway to failure emerges from the intersection of regulatory demand normalization and structural cost rigidity. A severe cyclical downturn and HVAC normalization could contract revenues to CHF 290 million by unwinding regulatory HVAC frontloading growth, while high fixed costs compress the EBITDA margin to 10%, mirroring historical cyclical underutilization patterns.

- Earnings Calls The overall likelihood of this failure pathway is assessed as medium. The overall likelihood of the bear case is medium, tempered by strong liquidity reserves. This reflects the tension between Sensirion's robust balance sheet position and its vulnerability to demand shocks amplified through high operating leverage. Sensirion's high operating leverage and concentration in regulatory HVAC markets create a material medium-term earnings risk if core industrial demand normalized rapidly.

Several co-occurring failures would likely manifest together during this disappointing scenario. Regulatory demand reversals in the US and Chinese subsidy rollbacks would likely occur together during global downturns, creating a synchronized global demand shock. Ongoing Swiss Franc strength and rising modular assembly mix jointly depress aggregate gross profitability, compounding revenue weakness with margin deterioration.

Sensirion's net currency position represents a persistent structural headwind, as unhedged revenue streams face continuous devaluation against a rigid Swiss Franc cost base. The combination of these failures would create a particularly damaging scenario where high Swiss labor overheads prevent rapid cost downscaling during unfavorable transaction rate movements. The least discussed risk by brokers relates to execution delays and capacity constraints rather than end-market dynamics. Investors routinely discount the consequences of prolonged cleanroom delays coinciding with sudden industrial demand normalizations. While sell-side analysts focus heavily on tracking A2L regulatory adoption and automotive project milestones, they systematically underweight the operational fragility embedded in Sensirion's capacity expansion timeline.

A delay in the second Stäfa cleanroom beyond 2028 would leave the company unable to capitalize on any demand recovery, creating a structural bottleneck precisely when growth is needed to absorb fixed costs. Critical early warning signs would manifest across multiple dimensions well before the earnings collapse became apparent. Weekly customer order trends are the best resolving data to determine underlying post-launch consumption rates, providing the earliest indication of weakening end-market demand.

Reviewing quarterly Stäfa project milestones helps resolve if further execution delays are emerging, while tracking international assembly revenues will reveal margin dilution if modular sales outpace component growth. On the currency front, recent annual FX penalties of over 3% represent a severe structural shift from historically calm periods, as Swiss National Bank monetary actions kept the EUR/CHF exchange rate depressed near 0.93 in late 2026.

Additionally, customer supply-chain de-risking artificially inflates medical revenues, creating a temporary distortion in structural demand metrics, making inventory days at major CPAP customers a critical forwardlooking indicator. Other side of the story: Accelerating EV battery-monitoring series production would disprove the core bear thesis, while rapid expansion of EV battery-monitoring series production can completely offset domestic HVAC normalization if European automotive demand accelerates.

The company's proven ability to quickly cut underperforming divisions and focus on high-margin core products demonstrates management's capacity for strategic discipline when needed.
