Section summary

Strategic demerger: Unilever completed the landmark separation of its historically margin-dilutive Ice Cream division on December 6, 2025. The separation transaction established The Magnum Ice Cream Company as an independent listed entity trading on Euronext Amsterdam, the London Stock Exchange, and the New York Stock Exchange. Unilever retains a minority equity investment of 19.85% in the newly independent company. The structural demerger aims to sharpen corporate focus and enhance valuation multiples.
2.
Transformative transaction: Unilever announced a major agreement in March 2026 to combine its Foods business with US seasoning giant McCormick & Co. The transaction is structured as a tax-efficient Reverse Morris Trust valued at up to €29 billion. The Foods transaction will effectively mark the end of over a century of food operations for the company. Completing the separation will shape Unilever into a highly focused beauty and personal care enterprise.
3.
Complex control: McCormick will assume full operational oversight of the combined food entity and retain eight out of twelve board seats upon completion by mid-2027. Unilever and its shareholders will hold a combined 65% stake in the joint business, with Unilever directly holding 9.9% and its shareholders owning 55.1%. Several shareholders criticized corporate leadership at the annual general meeting for bypassing an investor vote on this massive transaction. The joint venture structure exposes European investors to highly leveraged, US-listed equity volatility.
