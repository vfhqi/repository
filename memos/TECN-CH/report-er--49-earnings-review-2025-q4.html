Under her stewardship, the company initiated the comprehensive "Rewired" program to future-proof operations and address historical commercial execution gaps. Analysts at Deutsche Bank expressed confidence that her executive experience will help unlock the company's full operational potential over the next three years.

Shareholders also voted on the election of three new independent members to ensure optimal Board composition and oversight during the turnaround.

Analysts at Bernstein highlighted that closing the subscale genomics business acts as the first step in margin leverage for the group.

Software expansion: Tecan acquired FlowPilot software to enhance its robotic workcell orchestration capabilities and expand its custom automation offering. Acquired software technologies complement the existing Labwerx custom automation division to serve broader lab workflows. Management expects the integrated software suite to unlock an incremental addressable market exceeding 200 million dollars.

Targeted profit expansion represents a steep acceleration compared to the 16.10% adjusted EBITDA margin recorded in 2025. Analysts at Deutsche Bank anticipated that the market would remain skeptical of this target given inconsistent operational execution in recent years.

According to Bernstein estimates, roughly two-thirds of these savings will come from aggressive cost-containment measures rather than volume growth. Key efficiency initiatives include localizing consumables manufacturing and transferring product assembly to Malaysia and Vietnam.
