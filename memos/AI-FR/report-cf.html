EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Air Liquide has delivered a materially positive transformation across governance, strategy, and financial execution, anchored by the successful completion of its ADVANCE strategic plan and the transformative 3 billion euro DIG Airgas acquisition in South Korea that immediately unlocks semiconductor carrier gas contracts with SK hynix.

Governance stability is locked in with four-year renewals of CEO François Jackow and Chairman Benoît Potier, while strategic operational expertise is reinforced through executive committee updates.

The DIG Airgas integration accelerates Asian expansion, cutting-edge technology investments in Quobly and Cryocap secure structural niches in quantum computing and heavy industrial decarbonization platforms, and executive ambition has inflected upward with extended margin expansion targets of +100 bps p.a. through 2027, backed by aggressive structural efficiencies.

The financial profile demonstrates recovering organic growth toward mid-single digits as comparable sales grew +2% in 2025, recovering from cyclical European slowdowns and matching Air Products' low-single-digit trend while lagging Linde's 3% underlying expansion.

Sequential margin expansion targets of +100 bps p.a. are consistently met, with underlying operating margins expanding +100 bps in 2025 excluding volatile energy pass- through impacts, though Air Liquide's recurring operating margin of 20.70% still stands approximately 800 bps behind peer leader Linde's 30% margin.

Management raised the 2022-2027 cumulative operating margin expansion target by an additional 100 bps to a total cumulative objective of +560 bps.

Strong operating cash flow conversion of over 80% of EBITDA supports rapid deleveraging, with cash flow from operating activities reaching 6.86 billion euros in 2025 representing an 8% increase, and the group converted 84% of EBITDA to cash flow from operations over 2022-2025, demonstrating much better performance than peer Air Products which suffered negative FCF due to massive mega-project capex.

Prudent cash management is driving net debt/EBITDA approaching 1.0x versus peer Linde at 1.6x.

The five most critical forward earnings drivers ranked by impact include DIG Airgas HBM Integration with an estimated +1.50% sales impact rated HIGH plausibility and HIGH confidence, Japanese AI Chip Gas Units with +0.74% sales impact rated HIGH plausibility and MEDIUM confidence, Industrial Merchant Pricing with +3.40% price effect rated HIGH plausibility and HIGH confidence, Decarbonization and CCS Projects with +0.60% sales p.a. rated MEDIUM plausibility and MEDIUM confidence dependent on public subsidies, and US Gulf Coast Reshoring ASUs with +1.30% sales impact rated HIGH plausibility and HIGH confidence.

The group backlog reached a record 5.50 billion euros in Q1 2026, anchoring future revenue growth, with approximately 40% of the active project backlog dedicated to Electronics projects.

The DIG Airgas driver focuses on South Korean carrier gas sales expanding rapidly through high-bandwidth memory chip packaging contracts, with the 3 billion euro acquisition immediately adding 900 million euros of combined sales, securing a long-term nitrogen supply contract worth nearly 200 million euros with SK hynix that will deliver over 50 million euros in additional EBITDA by 2030, and management expects the acquisition to be net profit accretive within 12 months of integration.
