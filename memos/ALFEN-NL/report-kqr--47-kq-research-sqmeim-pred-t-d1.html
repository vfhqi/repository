Section 6 – Precedents of Margin Recovery in Multi-Segment Hardware Makers

Section summary
  1. Historical precedents show that multi-segment industrial hardware manufacturers can successfully recover margins through aggressive portfolio pruning, SKU rationalization, and structural simplification.
  2. Successful corporate turnarounds typically require three to five years and rely on optimizing existing core franchises rather than acquiring entirely new digital or technological capabilities.

Industrial Turnaround Timelines and Methods

1.
Aggressive portfolio pruning: Mitsubishi Electric demonstrates that structural margin expansion requires identifying and winding down low-margin divisions to remove legacy drag. To reach this conclusion, this research evaluated 22 relevant sources, analyzed 8 distinct hardware companies, and reviewed 4 comparable past cases. This portfolio optimization is expected to help raise its adjusted operating profit margin from 8.5% to 14.2% by 2031E. Consequently, Alfen's margin path depends on similarly decisive action within underperforming divisions.
2.
Decades-long structural shifts: Hitachi provides a strong precedent of a highly diversified conglomerate successfully recovering its profitability by systematically exiting commoditized hardware segments. From 2010 onwards, the company divested low-margin operations like consumer electronics, TVs, and air conditioning to refocus on high-value digital power control and edge data systems. These structural divestments lifted operating margins from break-even levels to mid-single digits. This long-term transition suggests that multi-segment hardware turnarounds are multi-year processes that require exiting non-core markets.
3.
Rigorous operational right-sizing: Woodward successfully drove a structural margin improvement in its industrial segment over five years by focusing on SKU rationalization and cost alignment. The manufacturer reduced its legacy SKU count by over 40%, eliminating 25,000 of its 60,000 SKUs to free up factory capacity for higher-margin products. This portfolio cleanup combined with value-reflective pricing pushed industrial operating margins up by 720 basis points year-on-year to 22.1% in its third quarter of 2026. Thus, eliminating product complexity represents a proven operational lever for margin recovery.
4.
Decentralized accountability execution: ABB restructured its operations and improved its business mix by systematically exiting low-margin engineering, procurement, and construction contracts.
