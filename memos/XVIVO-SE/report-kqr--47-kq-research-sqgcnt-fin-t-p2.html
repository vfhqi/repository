Section 1 – The Key Question and Why It Matters

Section summary
  1. This report evaluates Xvivo Perfusion's long-term pricing power across key thoracic and abdominal markets to assess its EPS trajectory.

The Key Question

1.
Investigating pricing power: "how much pricing power does this company have, and how does it hold up across different operating conditions, specifically: A United States hospital-budget squeeze with flat organacquisition reimbursement and widespread staffing shortages; the transition of European heart sales from compassionate-use and derogation pricing (7 percent of H1 2026 revenue per DNB Carnegie, August 2026) to commercial pricing after the 11 August 2026 CE mark and French national reimbursement of 18 May 2026; the entry of a cheaper hypothermic liver device (Bridge to Life) into Xvivo's European liver franchise, where Xvivo reports over 50 percent share; the largest customer (Lung Bioengineering, a United Therapeutics subsidiary, 132 million Swedish kronor of 2025 revenue per the 2025 annual report) moving to its own approved EVLP platform while continuing to buy STEEN Solution and components; a strengthening Swedish krona against the dollar and euro with United States tariffs on European-made disposables; and the United States heart launch targeted for the end of 2027 against TransMedics' installed base?"

Defining the Investment Case

In the 2024 annual report, Xvivo reported that its disposable gross margins reached 82 percent, demonstrating strong pricing power in established lung products. If Xvivo can maintain these margins while launching its heart technology in Europe and the United States, its earnings per share will grow rapidly. This makes the thoracic segment's pricing power the most critical variable for long-term investors.

Margin dilution risks: Abdominal gross margins are under abdominal gross margins are under temporary pressure because of competitive pricing conditions and geographic sales mix changes. This dilution was primarily driven by lower-priced kidney sales in emerging markets, which highlights the risk of entering cost-sensitive segments. Unless Xvivo stabilizes abdominal pricing, overall operating profitability will remain constrained.
4.
External macro headwinds: Currency fluctuations and trade barriers present immediate risks to Xvivo's commercial execution because the company manufactures many products in Europe. In its third-quarter 2025 earnings call, management noted that a weakened US dollar and potential tariffs could impact short-term sales.
