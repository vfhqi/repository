Company profile and segment positioning: Gestamp Automoción is a Spanish multinational specialized in the design, development, and manufacture of highly engineered metal components for the global automotive industry. Since its foundation in 1997, the company has grown from a local Spanish metal stamping supplier into a global technological leader operating across body-in-white, chassis, and mechanisms segments. It focuses on manufacturing safety-critical structural elements of vehicles designed to absorb kinetic energy during collisions. Gestamp's product portfolio remains largely propulsion-agnostic, allowing the company to successfully capture outsourcing trends from global OEMs regardless of their transition to electric powertrains
