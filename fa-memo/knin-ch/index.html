Read the turnover margins with care: they rise while EBIT falls because freight-rate deflation shrinks turnover. Conversion is the measure that tracks profitability. Sea and Air are about 42% of group gross profit but about 78% of EBIT, so their unit gross profit is the model's swing variable (about CHF 38m of EBIT per 1%).

The operating drivers behind the inputs

  • Volumes: Sea +1.5% (2026), +3.0% (2027), +3.5% a year after; Air +6.5%, +5%, then +4% a year. The 2026 share gains from DSV's Schenker integration (6 to 7 points of the 10% Air growth in Q3 2026; Road turnover +14%) fade as DSV's service recovers (on-time delivery back to 94% by early October 2026) and it recaptures volume over three to four quarters.
  • Unit gross profit (constant currency): Sea gross profit per TEU about CHF 452 in 2026, then -4% (2027), -5% (2028), flat (2029), +1% (2030); Air gross profit per 100kg about CHF 84, then -3%, -2%, flat, +1%. Reasons: container deliveries of 3.1m TEU (2027) and 4.4m (2028), fleet capacity growth of 9.5% and 12.7% against demand of about 4%, Suez transits recovering (54% of pre-disruption levels in early October 2026), air capacity returning, shorter shipper contracts, and partial pass-through of automation savings. The buy-sell lag supports unit gross profit in Q4 2026 and H1 2027, which is why the decline is placed in H2 2027 and 2028.
  • Operating costs: about +1.4% (2027) and +1.2% (2028) in Swiss francs: cost inflation of about 2.5% and volume-driven costs, less the residual CHF 50m of the CHF 200m programme (mostly H1 2027), gross artificial-intelligence savings of about CHF 65m (2027) and 125m (2028) against deployment costs of about CHF 20m and 10m, and Road and Contract Logistics improvements.
  • Currency: translation of about -2% (2027) and -1.5% a year after, on both gross profit and costs (net about CHF -35m of EBIT a year).
  • Depreciation: the Q3 2026 underlying run-rate (about CHF 242m a quarter once the CHF 4m Sea credit is replaced by the normal CHF 18m to 19m) and continued Contract Logistics lease additions give about CHF 975m in 2027, above consensus (928).
  • Revenue and EBITDA margin: revenue falls in 2027-2028 because ocean and air rates and fuel surcharges fall faster than volumes grow; the EBITDA margin on turnover rises mechanically for the same reason. The margin inputs are set so that EBITDA equals recurring EBIT plus D&A: CHF 2,460m (2026), 2,485m, 2,490m, 2,595m and 2,740m.

How to read these figures

  • Amounts are in millions of the reporting currency; per-share figures are in units of that currency. Negative figures are shown in brackets.

  • Viewforth's forecasts are the Excel model's base case. There are no bull or bear cases.
