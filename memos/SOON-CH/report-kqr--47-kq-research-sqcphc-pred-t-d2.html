Sonova Historical FX Translation Dynamics

1.
Severe headwind: In analyzing these currency dynamics, this research identified four key companies, three historical appreciation cycles, and evaluated twenty-seven distinct sources. Sonova has historically faced massive currency headwinds due to its Swiss cost base and global revenues, particularly in 2011/12 when CHF appreciation reduced reported sales by 16.5%. During this H1 2011/12 period, currency translation slashed reported consolidated EBITA by CHF 53.2 million, dragging down financial performance.
2.
Sudden shock: The unpegging of the Swiss franc in January 2015 caused another massive currency translation drag that severely compressed Sonova's reported revenues. For FY2015/16, the strong franc eroded reported EBITA by CHF 31.5 million, dragging down the core operating profit margin to 20.8%. This impact occurred despite the company achieving a new record sales level of CHF 2,072 million.
3.
Persistent drag: During the 2022 to 2025 appreciation cycle, Sonova's currency headwinds intensified significantly due to persistent franc strength. For the full year 2025/26, unfavorable exchange rates reduced reported sales by 5.8 percentage points or CHF 107 million. This headwind ultimately reduced normalized EBITA by CHF 106.5 million and compressed the margin by 1.5 percentage points.
4.
Hedging limitations: Sonova manages its currency transaction exposure through short-term forward contracts that typically have a duration of between one and six months. However, the group does not apply hedge accounting, which leads to visible gains and losses in financial expenses during sudden currency shifts. This leaves the group's transactional margins highly exposed to Swiss franc volatility compared to peers with longer hedging horizons.
5.
Contrary evidence: Neutral translation effects have occasionally occurred during brief periods of currency stabilization, such as in the first half of fiscal year 2026/27. During this period, Sonova reported that exchange rate movements reduced sales by only CHF 13.7 million or 0.8 percentage points. Management currently expects currency translation to remain broadly neutral for the full fiscal year.
