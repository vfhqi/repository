Outliers worth noting

The first extreme statistical outlier for Gjensidige Forsikring concerns the unprecedented scale of capitalized IT project accumulation, where uncompleted IT projects have represented over 50% of the intangible software balance since 2017, a pattern that would have pushed the FY 2021 expense ratio to an all-time high of over 16% had these costs been expensed upfront rather than capitalized. This aggressive capitalization practice stands in stark contrast to peer Sampo, which limits capitalization strictly to major system developments and amortizes IT software over a conservative 3 to 10 years, and peer Tryg, which has addressed legacy IT costs by expanding its Tata Consultancy Services collaboration and transferring ownership of older systems.
