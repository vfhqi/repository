EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Bear Case Overview The bear case against Carl Zeiss Meditec reflects a confluence of structural headwinds that extend beyond cyclical market volatility.

Morgan Stanley initiated coverage with an Underweight rating, citing unresolved headwinds and extremely low earnings visibility, while establishing a price target of €25.9, representing a 20% implied downside.

The company confronts a valuation premium of 10% to European peers despite a 30% multiyear de-rating, yet this premium appears unjustified given deteriorating fundamentals.

The forward P/E compressed from 37x in 2023 to only 16x in 2026, reflecting a lower growth profile and a collapsing ROIC profile to a weak 4.5% estimate.

Most critically, Morgan Stanley models a bearish target of €16.21 implying a 46% downside from the current market price, assuming EBIT margin remains flat at a depressed 8% through fiscal year 2028, with multiple compression reaching a floor of 12x P/E on a weak €1.35 EPS, driven by sluggish 1.3% revenue growth over the next two years. Accounting and Governance Red Flags Material accounting irregularities and escalating parent-company charges represent significant governance concerns that directly erode operational profitability.

Refractive consumables inventory destocking in China reduced revenue by a mid-double-digit million euro figure, while the withdrawal of old bifocal IOLs in China triggered a €7.4 million scrap charge to reduce gross profit in 2025/26.

More disturbingly, underperforming acquired technology forced a €31.5 million write-down on CTI intangible assets in 2023/24, and the company booked an extraordinary €13.1 million impairment to discontinue the InfiniteVision Optics project.

The widening non-GAAP adjustment gap raises serious earnings quality questions, as adjusted nine-month EBITA of €124.5 million exceeded reported EBITA of €108.4 million, creating a substantial adjustment gap.

Perhaps most alarming, total lease expenses to Carl Zeiss AG will surge from €2 million to €10 million per year starting from fiscal year 2027/28, while corporate charges for IT, HR, and shared services will increase by €30 million in three steps through fiscal year 2028/29, severely squeezing future margin targets. Winning Pre-Mortem Hypothesis The Analysis of Competing Hypotheses conclusively identifies the FALSE FRIEND profile as the winning scenario, presenting the most insidious risk to investors.

The FALSE FRIEND profile wins the ACH analysis because it exhibits zero hard operational inconsistencies across all core datasets, and if temporary positive signals are discounted, deceptive metrics align perfectly with long-term structural turnaround failure.

This contrasts sharply with the bullish YES profile which is highly inconsistent with reality, carrying thirteen separate hard inconsistencies across critical financial categories, as rising overhead and asset impairments directly contradict the core thesis of rapid operational margin recovery.

The FALSE FRIEND profile proves particularly dangerous because strong Microsurgery growth of 21% in constant currency during Q3 2025/26 is a highly diagnostic positive driver, yet this segment recovery is inconsistent with immediate outright failure but highly consistent with the FALSE FRIEND profile, masking deeper structural deterioration beneath surface-level recovery metrics. Highest-Plausibility Failure Modes Multiple high-probability failure vectors cluster around execution risk, Chinese market dynamics, and structural cost inflation.

The core threat is unmitigated pricing erosion from China VBP and intensifying local competition, with a delay in successor registration posing a €15 million EBITA risk for the upcoming tenders.

The ambitious restructuring program faces severe implementation challenges, as the targeted EUR 160 million net savings are heavily back-end weighted through 2029, and if labor negotiations in Germany stall, fiscal year 2027 will remain a low-margin transition period.

Manufacturing relocation introduces substantial execution uncertainty, with the planned shift to India presenting operational friction that remains poorly monitored by consensus analysts.

Customer service deterioration compounds these risks, as private equity-backed clinics find Zeiss service contracts excessively expensive pressuring customer operating margins severely, clinical directors report repair times taking over a month for essential German-serviced ophthalmic hardware, and Zeiss does not provide temporary equipment replacements forcing clinics to lose diagnostic capacity. False-Friend Scenarios Deceptive positive signals create multiple traps for unwary investors who mistake temporary recovery for sustainable turnaround.

Digital platform adoption presents a particularly insidious false-friend dynamic, as high subscription-based adoption of ZEISS Clinic 360 appears to signal digital strength but potentially cannibalizes high-margin legacy hardware sales by enabling clinics to optimize existing equipment usage and delay new microscope purchases.

Similarly, rapid SMILE Pro laser installations may appear highly favorable yet disguise soft underlying procedure volumes, while private clinical networks demanding aggressive consumable pack price discounts mitigate their own operational pressure at the expense of company margins.

The most dangerous false-friend scenario emerges in China, where successful re-registration of the bifocal IOL signals cataract recovery but triggers severe volume-based pricing discounts, and if nationwide VBP implementation proceeds, the replacement bifocal volume remains structurally margin-dilutive despite superficially positive volume metrics. Competitive Deterioration Carl Zeiss Meditec confronts intensifying competitive pressures that directly challenge core bull-case pillars around technology leadership and market position.

Independent experts note Zeiss fails to penetrate the cataract segment leaving its surgical division unable to compete against Alcon, while the launch of the QUATERA phaco system failed to secure clinical adoption, leaving Alcon with an 80% market share.

The competitive threat extends beyond market share losses to ecosystem lock-out, as clinical platforms like Alcon Unity VCS create superior dual-segment surgical workflows, widening the competitive gap with Zeiss.

Product quality gaps compound positioning weaknesses, with specialist survey data revealing Zeiss presbyopic lenses are inferior and priced higher than primary competitors.

This deteriorating competitive position directly invalidates management's technology leadership narrative and threatens long-term pricing power across key product categories. Net Currency Impact Foreign exchange dynamics represent a material structural headwind rather than a transitory cyclical challenge, directly pressuring reported earnings conversion.

Unfavourable foreign exchange movements will act as a net headwind for near-term EPS due to unhedged export billing, though Deutsche Bank notes that FX pressures should ease slightly in the next fiscal year as exchange rates stabilize, yet reported revenues will continue to lag constant-currency growth if the euro holds above 1.15 against the US dollar.

The magnitude of currency exposure creates significant earnings volatility, as a 5% USD fluctuation moves revenues by an estimated €28.50 million based on historical currency shares, a 10% USD depreciation triggers an adverse €17.10 million impact on consolidated operating EBITDA results, and a 20% negative dollar shift dilutes consolidated EPS by €0.26, straining reported earnings growth.

The reverse-engineered currency bear case reveals severe downside potential, where a 10% depreciation of the US dollar and renminbi would reduce consolidated EPS by a combined €0.29, the company's rolling 12-month forward contracts will fail to protect margins if devaluations persist, and this currency threat directly compounds the pre-mortem failure narrative of Chinese volume-based procurement pricing erosion. Largest Cross-Part Risks The most dangerous cross-cutting risks emerge where multiple failure modes reinforce each other, creating compounding negative scenarios that exceed the sum of individual threats.

The intersection of Chinese pricing erosion with unhedged currency transaction exposure presents the single most acute compound risk, as domestic competitors lowering prices while the euro appreciates renders German export costs completely uncompetitive regardless of operational improvements.

Parent-company overhead inflation directly undermines restructuring savings, with the €40 million combined increase in rent and shared services charges consuming substantial portions of the targeted €160 million ProfitUp net savings before any operational benefits reach shareholders.

The back-end loaded nature of restructuring savings intersects dangerously with near-term earnings visibility requirements, as capital markets may lose patience and re-rate the multiple downward before fiscal year 2028 benefits materialize.

Customer service deterioration compounds competitive ecosystem lock-out risks, as poor technical support and exorbitant contract pricing actively drive clinical defections toward Alcon's integrated platform at precisely the moment when Zeiss needs to defend existing accounts against bundled phaco-lens offerings. Peer Benchmarking Context Relative performance metrics versus comparable medtech peers reveal Carl Zeiss Meditec's severe operational underperformance across multiple dimensions.

The company's forward P/E compressed from 37x in 2023 to only 16x in 2026, reflecting a lower growth profile and a collapsing ROIC profile to a weak 4.5% estimate, representing bottom-quintile capital efficiency within the medtech sector.

Margin disparity versus peers proves particularly striking, as the projected 2026 operating margin of approximately 8% remains highly depressed compared to Alcon's reported core operating margin of 20.6% in second-quarter 2026, underlining strong operational execution.

Currency hedging sophistication lags global peers, with Carl Zeiss Meditec mitigating exchange risk exclusively using simple currency forwards completely avoiding options, while Alcon hedges its balance sheet exposures with limited exception using forwards, swaps, and options.

The relative positioning disadvantage extends to disclosure quality and strategic flexibility, where rigid hedging mandates and limited instrument optionality constrain the company's ability to dynamically respond to volatile currency environments that peers navigate more effectively. Stage Progression Recommendation The aggregate evidence mandates a strict hold-and-watch posture until demonstrable restructuring execution offsets Chinese pricing pressures and currency headwinds.

The joint probability of operational failure is high because macro and pricing headwinds remain active across key geographic segments, Deutsche Bank maintains a Hold rating because meaningful near-term margin expansion is unlikely during the 2027 transition period, and we recommend a hold-and-watch stance because limited operational visibility prevents underwriting the mid-term adjusted operating targets.

Critical monitoring thresholds include the US clinical adoption rate for the newly approved Mel 90 excimer laser, as if US FDA approval is delayed the MEL-90 combined LASIK offering will fail to drive the expected 2027 SBU Ophthalmology turnaround, potential pricing pressure in China IOL tenders representing the primary structural pre-mortem re-run trigger for gross margins, and if the new bifocal IOL registration stalls the resulting tender exclusion will trigger an immediate pre-mortem re-run.

Investors should avoid adding capital until quarterly evidence confirms that ProfitUp savings materially offset parent-company overhead inflation, Chinese VBP pricing stabilizes above catastrophic levels, and clinical adoption metrics for new laser platforms demonstrate sustainable procedure volume recovery rather than temporary equipment replacement cycles.
