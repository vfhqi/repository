Executive Summary -- We are looking for a clear, conviction-carrying statement of the investment thesis, key ratings, and actionable conclusion; avoiding vague or overly hedged language.
Judgement summary
  • Bottom line up front, overall case verdict. C Croda is a genuine cyclical-trough plus self-help case whose change forces are real and dense, but whose operating gearing has not yet dropped through to profit
  • Key risk, case simplicity. D Six or seven live drivers spread across nine business units and 36 countries breach the focused-bet and narrow-perimeter guardrails, and headwinds are far from zero
  • Key risk carried from the risks report. Extreme operating deleverage at the 11 shared manufacturing sites is the single most consequential failure mode, and first-half divisional operating profit landed 14% below consensus
  • Key support. A bottom-decile multiple on its own ten-year history, a £100m efficiency programme already 46% banked, and one-quarter input-cost pass-through give the case an unusual floor

Business Description (B.1) 13 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
What it does: Croda International makes high-performance specialty ingredients for consumer care, pharmaceutical and agricultural formulators, and sells direct rather than through chemical distributors
2.
Segment mix: FY25 sales split Consumer Care £972.7m (57%), Life Sciences £532.2m (31%) and Industrial Specialties £194.5m (12%)
3.
Life Sciences composition: the division divides into Pharma at about 55%, Crop Protection at about 30% and Seed Enhancement at about 15% of divisional sales
4.
Geographic mix: FY25 revenue by region was EMEA £685.1m (40%), Asia £426.5m (25%), North America £401.1m and Latin America £186.7m
5.
Manufacturing footprint: the group operates across 36 countries with 44 principal manufacturing sites, of which 11 are shared multi-purpose facilities serving several business units
6.
Customer base: about 6,000 specialty ingredients go to more than 17,000 customers, with the top 20 historically below 17% of group turnover
7.
Customer type: local and regional brands are 82% of Consumer Care sales and 56% of Crop Protection sales, up from 73% of Consumer Care in 2019
8.
Financial scale: FY25 revenue was £1,699.4m, adjusted operating profit £295.3m, adjusted operating margin 17.4% and adjusted earnings per share 146.1p
9.
Half-year position: H1 2026 sales were £880.5m, adjusted operating profit £155.8m at a 17.7% margin, and adjusted earnings per share 78.6p
10.
Balance sheet: net debt closed FY25 at £523.8m and 1.3x EBITDA, rising to £577.9m and 1.4x at 30 June 2026, inside the 1.0x to 2.0x policy range
11.
Cash and returns: FY25 free cash flow was £161.6m, capital expenditure £108.2m at 6% of sales, and the dividend 111p at a 76% payout ratio
12.
Input structure: 58% of raw-material volumes are bio-based, palm derivatives 35% of bio-based volume and corn 27%; raw materials are about 34% of 2025 sales
13.
Investment style: the shape is a self-help and cyclical-recovery profile geared to shared-site utilisation, not a mechanically compounding growth business
