Section 3 – Industry Structure + Concentration + Value Chain Economics

Section summary
  1. the global transition to alternative payment methods is accelerating, driven by mobile-first adoption in underbanked emerging regions.
  2. extreme regional fragmentation persists across global local payment methods, preventing any single sovereign scheme from dominating.
  3. local payments bypass high card interchange fees, structurally shifting the profit pool away from traditional network rails.
  4. Boku's specialized network aggregates complexity for global e-commerce titans,
  5. generalist processors face intense margin pressure in card-present markets, driving merchants to adopt multi-processor orchestration strategies.
