Defining the Key Question

Key question: "how likely is company guidance to be raised, met or reduced at the earnings events over the next 18 months, and how likely are sell-side consensus forecasts, and the net breadth of revisions, to move higher or lower over the same period?"

Since dismounted night-vision systems historically accounted for 93% of revenue, the transition to diversified platform-based systems represents a fundamental shift. Testing Theon's FY2026 Targets

1.
Upgraded revenue trajectory: Theon's management expressed high confidence in meeting the upgraded FY2026 revenue guidance of c. EUR 600m. 2.
Operating margin resilience: Theon achieved an adjusted EBIT margin of 26.2% in the first half of 2026, aligning perfectly with its upgraded full-year guidance of above 26%. This performance compares favorably to the first half of 2025, where the adjusted EBIT margin was slightly lower at 25.8%. Sustaining this profitability level into the second half of 2026 depends heavily on maintaining a favorable product mix toward higher-margin thermal systems. If margins remain above 26%, the positive operating leverage will protect the group's EPS from dilution
