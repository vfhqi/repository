Inventory Cycle Mechanics and Current Position

1.
Escalating price signals: Standard distributor loops accelerate when initial price increases prompt distributors to buy heavily before further hikes occur. Morgan Stanley reports that distributors historically build excess supply during rising markets to secure inventory against growing demand. This buying behavior artificially inflates apparent demand and supports further price increases, but it inevitably leads to severe overstocking and subsequent price drops. Managing these cycles is crucial because earnings decline sharply during the destocking phase.
2.
Depleted channel inventories: The US tubular market currently exhibits tight supply conditions with channel inventories falling well below historical levels. Morgan Stanley notes that lower import volumes and recovering demand have depressed inventories and boosted OCTG pricing ten percent year-to-date. This inventory drain has pushed months of supply below the long-term median, creating a highly supportive backdrop. Consequently, these supportive dynamics suggest that distributor restocking will continue to fuel price momentum.
