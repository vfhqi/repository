EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Patrick Pouyanné is an elite operator-track chief executive with extensive public sector foundations and nearly three decades of deep operational tenure, though his crisis-driven internal appointment in 2014 preserved corporate continuity while potentially preventing radical outside-industry strategic pivots.
