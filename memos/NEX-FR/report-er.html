Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sell-side focus prior to the Q3 2026 earnings centered on Nexans' subsea backlog visibility and the mitigation of the Great Sea Interconnector project suspension.
  2. Market participants prioritized the commercial integration of Republic Wire and the structural expansion of power grids and data center demand.
1.
Steady transformation: Nexans operates as a global pure player in sustainable electrification, designing and manufacturing advanced cable systems across its three core business segments of PWR-Transmission, PWR- Grid, and PWR-Connect. The company has successfully executed a multi-year portfolio rotation strategy to focus entirely on electrification by divesting non-core industrial and telecom assets. Strategic shifts help position Nexans at the intersection of structural energy transition trends.
