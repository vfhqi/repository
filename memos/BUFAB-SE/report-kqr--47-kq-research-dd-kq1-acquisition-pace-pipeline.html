Section 4 – Comparative Deal Track Record and Competitor Benchmarking 15 findings

Section summary
  1. Bufab's historical M&A pace has lagged behind highly active peers due to its distinct integration- heavy model and historical leverage constraints.
  2. Highly decentralized peers such as Lagercrantz and Addtech execute up to twelve deals annually by delegating deal-sourcing to divisional levels.
  3. Embracing hands-off ownership allows peers to scale rapidly, while Bufab's pull-integration strategy inherently limits execution speed.

Decentralized Sourcing and Organizational Designs

1.
Sustained deal velocity: Lagercrantz targets eight to twelve acquisitions per year, representing a systematic deployment of capital across five highly focused divisions
2.
Strict decentralized operations: Addtech operates a highly decentralized model with over 150 subsidiaries, minimizing central headquarters staff to maintain agility
3.
Proactive divisional sourcing: Sourcing at Addtech is run entirely by individual business area managers, enabling parallel acquisition processes to run simultaneously across Europe
4.
Continuous programmatic volume: Carnegie research highlights that Lifco completed 13 acquisitions in 2024 alone, adding substantial annual sales volume of approximately SEK 2 billion
5.
Permanent holding structures: Indutrade preserves the entrepreneurial spirit of its 200 plus companies by maintaining completely independent operations instead of forcing integration or restructuring

Integration Styles and Execution Speeds

6.
Strategic capability acquisition: Bossard views M&A as a tool to fill geographical white spots and acquire new technological capabilities like lightweight ultrasonic welding
7.
Prolonged integration processes: Bossard focus on operational readiness and culture means integration takes several years to execute fully across its global C-parts supply network
8.
Perpetual hands-off ownership: Lifco provides secure, perpetual ownership and explicitly avoids seeking operational synergies or moving physical facilities of acquired entities
9.
Systematic margin targets: Lagercrantz demands that all acquired companies meet a high profit to working capital target exceeding sixty percent
10.
Synergy-free business models: Indutrade operates as a long-term home for small and medium businesses, refusing to centralize operational functions or create corporate silos

Bottlenecks Limiting Bufab's M&A Pace

11.
Leverage-induced deal pauses: High debt levels historically halted Bufab's acquisition agenda, as seen when net debt surged to 3.2x EBITDA in late 2022
12.
Complex integration requirements: Bufab utilizes a pull-integration model which focuses on growth synergies, requiring active supply chain integration that slows transaction velocity
13.
Organizational complexity risks: Carnegie warns that as Bufab grows, its corporate structure risks becoming too complex to handle if it attempts to integrate every target
14.
Capital deployment constraints: Unlike peers that fund deals solely through cash flow, Bufab's weaker relative balance sheet historically restricted its capital allocation flexibility
15.
Other side of the story: Highly integrated platforms like Bufab can achieve superior purchasing power and cross-selling, but risk complete operational bottlenecks if management cannot scale its centralized integration capabilities
