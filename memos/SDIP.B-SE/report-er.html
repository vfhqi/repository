Section 1 – Brief company description and history

Section summary
  1. Sdiptech's completed portfolio streamlining and robust organic performance position it well against premium serial acquirers, although historical valuation discounts relative to its peers persist.
1.
Decentralised infrastructure focus: Sdiptech is a Swedish technology group that acquires and develops market-leading niche businesses focused on products and services for safer, more sustainable, and efficient societies within the growing European infrastructure sector.

2.
Four core segments: Sdiptech operates a highly decentralised governance model across four primary business areas consisting of Supply Chain & Transportation, Water & Bioeconomy, Energy & Electrification, and Safety & Security.
3.
Completed structural pivot: Sdiptech finalized a prolonged and complex restructuring phase in the second quarter of 2026, which streamlined the business by divesting 11 non-core entities and its remaining elevator operations to focus purely on high-margin niches.
4.
Geographical market concentration: Sdiptech's primary revenue driver is the United Kingdom, which accounted for 49% of group sales in 2025, followed by the Nordics at 16%, the rest of Europe at 15%, the United States at 6%, and Italy at 6%
5.
Robust historical growth: Over the three-year period spanning 2022 to 2025, Sdiptech achieved a strong compounded annual growth rate of 14% in sales and 15% in EBITA, outperforming several smaller serial acquirers in the Swedish market.
6.
Significant valuation discount: Sdiptech has historically traded at a depressed forward multiple of 11.7x EV/EBITA, representing a sharp valuation discount of 22% compared to proven compounder peers such as Addtech, Indutrade, Lagercrantz, and Lifco.
7.
Partially justified discount: Sdiptech's lower return on capital employed of 7% from 2025 to 2027e and historically inconsistent acquisition track record justify a minor valuation discount to its premium peers, although the current market discount is excessively harsh.
8.
Undervalued core operations: Sdiptech continues to trade at a substantial 35% discount to peer multiples despite having successfully strengthened its capital structure and stabilized its core operating margins.
9.
Strong absolute performance: Sdiptech's share price demonstrated solid momentum leading up to its Q3 earnings, with absolute gains of 13.30% over the last three months, 11.77% over six months, and 42.24% over the last 12 months.
10.
Consistent market outperformance: Sdiptech has consistently outperformed the broader OMX Stockholm index on a relative basis, posting relative gains of 34.03% over the past 12 months and 10.60% over the past six months.
11.
Steadily rising targets: DNB Carnegie analyst Carl Korsheden maintained a consistent Buy rating on Sdiptech while steadily raising the target price from SEK310 in April 2026 to SEK330 in June 2026 and to SEK340 in July 2026.
12.
Upward valuation drift: Redeye analysts progressively upgraded Sdiptech's fundamental base case valuation from SEK280 in April 2026 to SEK290 in May 2026, ultimately lifting it to SEK300 in July 2026 following the restart of the M&A engine.

  3. Severe geographical concentration in the United Kingdom remains a critical macro vulnerability that could disrupt long-term organic growth projections.
  4. Sdiptech's Q3 2026 results demonstrated remarkable organic sales acceleration that comfortably exceeded sell-side estimates.
  5. Solid volumes were partially offset by temporary margin contraction in its supply chain segment and lower seasonal cash conversion.
  6. Structural refinancing moves will lower ongoing interest expenses and enhance earnings per share starting in 2027.

Completed leadership transition: Sdiptech executed a critical executive transition in June 2025 when former business area manager Anders Mattson took the helm as Chief Executive Officer, while the previous chief executive Bengt Lejdström transitioned back into his former role as Chief Financial Officer to streamline the group's financial operations.

Optimized product focus: Sdiptech has systematically shifted its acquisition focus over the last several years away from lower-margin service businesses and toward high-margin firms owning proprietary products, which has successfully elevated the overall portfolio quality and strengthened defensive characteristics.
