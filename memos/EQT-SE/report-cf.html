EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

EQT AB is executing a transformative structural pivot through a highly structured generational leadership handoff and the integration of Coller Capital to establish Secondaries as a core fourth segment, while significantly elevating global and Asian board representation to cement the firm's ambition to scale private markets globally. The combined platform has delivered adjusted total revenue of EUR 2,732 million, up 16% year-on-year, with fee-paying assets under management growing by 10% in H1 2026 to reach EUR 155.4 billion, though rapid leadership and M&A expansion could potentially dilute EQT's distinct corporate culture and drive key partner departures.

Despite heavy front-loaded investments into retail wealth channels, underlying EBITDA margins are holding stable at 60% in 2025, matching the 2024 print and outperforming the 47% average for global alternative asset managers.
