EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 15 findings

This executive summary synthesizes the comprehensive management quality assessment of argenx SE under its co-founder Tim Van Hauwermeiren and successor CEO Karen Massey. The analysis reveals a highly capable founder-operator who successfully scaled Vyvgart from clinical concept to a multi-billion-dollar commercial franchise, achieving exceptional revenue growth and operating margin expansion while maintaining strong clinical execution across multiple indications.

However, the assessment uncovers significant operational friction beneath the polished corporate narrative, including severe commercial territory overexpansion, deteriorating key opinion leader relationships, and governance deviations that compromise independent oversight during a critical leadership transition.
