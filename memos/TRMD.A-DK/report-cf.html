EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

TORM's investment thesis rests on exceptional cyclical profitability driven by geopolitical disruptions that have pushed spot freight rates to historic peaks, though this extraordinary performance remains fundamentally dependent on unsustainable external conditions rather than organic operational improvements. The company achieved record quarterly net profit of USD 338 million in Q2 2026 with time charter equivalent earnings surging 146% year-on-year, yet this performance is entirely attributable to maritime chokepoint closures and vessel diversions that artificially tighten global capacity.

While TORM's integrated One TORM platform delivers consistent commercial premiums and management maintains a conservative guidance track record of sequential upgrades, the company's spot-heavy exposure, aging fleet profile, and rising technical operating expenses create severe downside vulnerability if Middle East tensions resolve and trade routes normalize.
