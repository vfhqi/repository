EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

YouGov delivered 16% reported revenue growth to £388.9 million in FY25, driven primarily by the Shopper acquisition, while underlying organic growth decelerated to only 1% and adjusted operating margins contracted to 16% from historical peaks. Cash conversion deteriorated sharply to 71% in FY25 from 94% in FY23, pressured by £11.6 million in net working capital outflows driven by expanding trade receivables. The group operates under elevated financial leverage, with net debt at £144 million and a leverage ratio reaching 2.1x in early FY26 following the debt-funded Shopper acquisition.

Leadership transitions place Wayne Levings as CEO elect to execute commercial scaling, while founder Stephan Shakespeare refocuses on AI innovation and CFO James Davies strengthens financial discipline. The multi-wave Value Delivery Plan targets over 350 basis points of margin expansion by FY27, with Wave 1 completed and expected to contribute £2.5 million to profitability.
