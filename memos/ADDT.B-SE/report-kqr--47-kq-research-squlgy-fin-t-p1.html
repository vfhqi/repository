This structure means that subsidiary-level cost discipline determines the group's overall margin trajectory during cyclical demand swings. Consequently, the degree of fixed-cost rigidity across these units represents the core determinant of earnings volatility for shareholders.
3.
Pronounced impact: Changes in organic volume can trigger substantial swings in earnings per share due to built-in operating capacity. Therefore, mapping this leverage is essential to assess the downside valuation risk in a high-multiple sector.
