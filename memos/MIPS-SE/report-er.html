1.
Business model – asset-light ingredient technology brand: Founded on over 30 years of scientific research from the Royal Institute of Technology and Karolinska Institutet, Mips operates an asset-light ingredient brand business model. Rather than manufacturing complete helmets, Mips supplies component kits and licenses intellectual property, enabling gross margins to remain consistently above 70%

Category diversification – tri-pillar market strategy: Mips organizes its commercial operations across three primary helmet categories: Sports, Motorcycle, and Safety.
