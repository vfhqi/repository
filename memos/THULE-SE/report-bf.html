Outliers worth noting

Thule's 10-year average free cash flow conversion reached 81%, positioned in the top decile of consumer durables peers despite operating in capital-intensive hard-goods manufacturing. Post-tax ROIC reached 10.3% in FY 2025, exceeding Dometic's single-digit capital returns, while capex intensity remained exceptionally low at 3.2% of sales versus typical hard-goods manufacturing benchmarks of 4-6% [Cross-ref: Section 2, Section 4].

This cash generation outlier stems from Thule's asset-light assembly footprint and strategic outsourcing to over 760 external suppliers for raw material components, concentrating capital deployment in high-return IP development and automated logistics rather than primary fabrication infrastructure [Cross-ref: Section 6]. The persistent gap between Thule's cash conversion and peer medians suggests either structural manufacturing efficiency embedded in its European footprint or understated reinvestment requirements that could emerge if supply chain insourcing accelerates to mitigate raw material volatility [Cross-ref: Section 3, Section 4].
