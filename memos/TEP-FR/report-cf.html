EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF) 33 findings

Teleperformance SE faces a pivotal strategic inflection characterized by profound leadership transition, accelerating AI-driven disruption, and persistent structural margin pressures despite embedded specialized services valuation upside. The appointment of Jorge Amar as CEO in March 2026 follows nearly five decades of founder leadership and initiates a comprehensive portfolio review targeting non-core asset disposals that could generate up to EUR 2.5 billion in capital returns.

However, the company confronts severe organic growth deceleration driven by generative AI seat cannibalization that threatens up to fifty percent of core revenues while competitors maintain superior topline momentum. Operating margins remain resilient at 13.2 percent versus a peer median of 12.9 percent, sustained by Majorel integration synergies and upgraded restructuring programs targeting EUR 150 million to EUR 170 million in annual savings, yet elevated non-recurring cash outlays of EUR 120 million to EUR 140 million substantially dilute near-term free cash flow generation.

The sell-side remains deeply divided between valuation bulls highlighting twenty-five percent FCF yields and structural bears warning of terminal value erosion, while management's track record reveals a consistent pattern of over-promising with chronic organic growth misses and frequent mid-year guidance resets. Forward execution credibility hinges on successfully navigating the compound risks of rapid technological disintermediation, wage inflation in offshore
