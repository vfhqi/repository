[Materiality of] Investment Thesis (BB 1) Rating = B

Thesis. Sdiptech is a repaired serial acquirer, not yet a compounder: a new chief executive has sold what hid the core's 21% margins and 11% organic growth, and at SEK 247.8 the shares pay for the repair but not for the 15% return on capital that the re-rating needs. On our modal path of about 15% a year earnings growth and no dividend the return clears the 20% bar over eighteen months at a blended multiple, but only just, and only because we give even odds or a little better to the market moving the stock out of its low-quality cohort.

  • The change is internal, multi-dimensional and dated, and it was made by the company rather than done for it.
    • Anders Mattson became chief executive on 1 June 2025 with Bengt Lejdström returning as chief financial officer the same day
    • a strategic review in July 2025 became eleven completed divestments of service-heavy units plus the Metus elevator business by Q2 2026, at an average 6.7x EBITA and raising SEK 295m in H1 2026
    • SEK 184m of preference shares were redeemed in March 2026 and SEK 250m of bonds repurchased in April 2026
    • a return-on-capital target above 15% was set at the November 2025 capital markets day and put into the 2026 bonus plans
    • acquisitions restarted in April 2026.
    • Five of the twelve internal forces are strong (chief executive, other leaders, strategy, standards, perimeter), against the screen's rule of three, and none of the seven red flags fires.
    • The external forces net to a small tailwind in the two growth segments (37% of 2025 EBITA) and a headwind in the largest currency: 49% of 2025 core sales are in the UK and are hedged for no more than twelve months.
  • The change has travelled the whole 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, which is unusual for a case at Triaging and is the reason the rating is not lower.
    • It shows in the people (June 2025), the choices (the product-only strategy and the November 2025 targets), the actions (divestments, redemption, acquisitions), the key operating metrics (organic sales growth from -4% in Q2 2025 to +11% in Q2 2026; cash conversion 90% on a trailing basis; ROCE 13.2%), the financial statements (core adjusted EBITA of SEK 988m LTM at a 21.2% margin) and, since the 17 July 2026 report, the sell-side's estimates (FY2027 mean EPS +7.8% over six months to 18 Sep 2026).
  • 'Financial Outputs' have turned up in the core before they show in the group's reported lines, which is why the improvement is real and still partly hidden.
    • All four key metrics moved the right way in the last two quarters: organic growth, margin (group adjusted EBITA margin 20.3% in Q2 2026 from 18.8%), cash conversion (70% in the quarter, 90% LTM, from 54% and 47%) and leverage (2.80x from 3.39x).
    • Consensus group revenue for FY2026 still falls 3.6% because the divested units leave the base, so the reported group lines lag the core.
    • The margin rise is mix from divestment rather than underlying gain: the like-for-like core margin fell 0.6 points in Q2 2026 and gross margin slipped to 59.3% from 61.1%.
      • The improvement is in what was cut, not yet in what was kept.
  • The market has repriced the earnings and not the multiple, so the price pays for what is proven and not for what is promised.
    • The shares are +37.1% over twelve months and +33.8% over six, but the 24-month-forward multiple is 13.5x, below the stock's own five-year median of 14.31x, at the 53rd percentile of its ten-year history, about half the multiple of the proven Swedish compounders (Addtech 28.9x, Lagercrantz 28.0x, Lifco 26.8x) and above the cohort it is filed in (Addnode 11.5x, Storskogen 9.1x).
    • What is priced: the earnings recovery to about SEK 18 by FY2028, the completed restructuring, leverage inside target and organic growth at about 5%.
      • What is not priced: a return on capital at 15%, an acquisition engine at SEK 100m of EBITA a year, and the multiple that goes with either.
  • Our modal earnings path is about 15% a year, revenue-led, inside the framework's 12% to 20% band, and it reconciles.
    • Adjusted EPS of SEK 14.3 (FY2026), SEK 16.5 (FY2027), SEK 19.0 (FY2028) and SEK 21.8 (FY2029): 5% organic plus 7% acquired less 1% currency gives 11% revenue growth, the margin adds about 0.6% a year, a falling finance line adds about 3 points and the share count takes away 0.5, for a product of 14.5% a year.
  • The 'Modal return' clears the bar at eighteen months with almost no margin, and it clears it only with the re-rating leg.
    • With no cash return, earnings growth of about 15% a year and a re-rating from 13.5x to a blended 14.7x, the return is about 21.6% a year over eighteen months and about 18.5% a year over three years, the bottom of the framework's B band.
    • On the cleared branch alone (16.5x, the stock's own 75th percentile) the eighteen-month return is 31% a year
    • on the uncleared branch alone (12.5x, the two-year median) it is 9% a year.
    • The bar clears on the modal path below SEK 252
    • the price is SEK 247.8.
  • The chain holds from the change to the outputs to the estimates and breaks at the return on capital, which is where the re-rating is paid for and where the past argues against the thesis.
    • The group has never earned 15% on its capital
    • it reports 13.2%, up from 11.9%, against an average of about 11%
    • in Q2 2026 the two growth segments' ROCE rose 10 and 9 points while the two largest areas' fell 4 and 18 points.
    • New deals at 7x with a 20% IRR hurdle raise the marginal return
    • every point of goodwill on the next deal dilutes the ratio.
  • The earnings path is fragile below EBITA, and that fragility is what keeps the rating at B rather than A.
    • The plausibly worse drop-through (interest not falling, SEK 50m a year of currency losses in the finance net, tax at 29% rather than 26%) takes EPS growth to about 10% a year and the eighteen-month return to 10.3% a year at the blended multiple, a D
    • and because that path fails the cleared branch's own condition, the honest multiple is 12.5x and the return is -1.5%, an F.
    • A 10% weaker pound costs about SEK 1.6 of EPS, 10% of FY2027.
  • The pattern is 'Major Corporate Change', and the fit is crisp.
    • Resources, choices and actions have all changed with dated evidence, the outputs have translated, and the multiple has not yet moved.
    • No other 'Target Setup' fits: the stock is not oversold, nobody has capitulated, the upgrade record is two quarters old and the demand tailwind is in a third of the group.

  • The case is therefore a self-help case in a serial acquirer with a compounder's engine restarting behind it: compelling on the change and the growth, conditionally compelling on the price, and a bet on the odds rather than a bet with a floor.

    • The downside is the cohort multiple, SEK 165 to SEK 211 on 24-month-forward EPS of SEK 18.35.
    • The preferred entry is SEK 230 or below, where the modal path returns about 28% a year over eighteen months and the adverse drop-through about 15%.

  • Rating = B, because the change is material on every layer of the 'Six Layer Inputs-outputs' chain, the four key outputs and the sell-side's estimates have already moved, our modal earnings growth of about 15% a year sits inside the target band, and the 'Modal return' of about 21.6% a year over eighteen months clears the 20% bar at our blended multiple

    • not A, because the return clears only with the re-rating leg, with almost no margin at SEK 247.8, and the adverse drop-through takes it below the bar
    • not C, because the outputs have moved and the estimates are rising, which a C thesis cannot show.

Most plausible competing hypothesis, and our response.

  • The hypothesis at its strongest: Sdiptech is a Tier 6 serial acquirer that has cleaned its portfolio but not its model.
    • It buys small businesses at 7x to 9x EBITA for initial yields of 11% to 14%, funds them with variable-rate debt and earn-outs, and earns a spread of a few points over its cost of capital that goodwill and amortisation turn into a 13% return on capital.
    • It has never earned 15%, and the two largest areas' returns fell in the last quarter.
    • Half its sales are in a currency it hedges twelve months at a time without a disclosed ratio.
    • The Q2 2026 organic growth of 11% was backlog clearance in transport
      • the cash conversion of 70% followed a quarter at 54% and a quarter at 134% and reflects inventory timing.
    • The earn-out liabilities of about SEK 900m are deferred debt that the accounting lets the company write back through profit when acquired units miss their targets, so the reported recovery can be a false friend.
    • The sell-side is two houses at 100% buy, which is crowding, not confirmation.
    • The market has shown in Storskogen and Volati what it pays for this model when the growth stops: 9x.
      • On that view fair value is the cohort's 9x to 11.5x on 24-month-forward EPS of about SEK 18, or SEK 165 to SEK 211, and the shares at SEK 247.8 are 15% to 33% above fair.
  • What we concede.
    • The return on capital is unproven, and the last quarter's divergence by area (Supply Chain & Transportation -4 points, Water & Bioeconomy -18 points) is the bear case's best recent fact.
    • The Q2 transport growth had backlog in it, and the organic run-rate is nearer 6% than 11%.
    • The gross margin fell to 59.3% and the operating-cost base is higher after the divestments, so operating leverage runs both ways.
    • The hedge policy is inferior to peers' and undisclosed as a ratio.
    • The multiple is company-driven, and the cohort's 9x is a real floor if the engine stalls.
    • Coverage is thin.
  • Why we hold our view, first: the divested units fetched 6.7x EBITA in cash, and units in decay do not fetch that.
    • SEK 295m was received in H1 2026.
    • This is the strongest evidence against the false-friend reading, and the risks report itself names it.
  • Second: the cash recovery is on a trailing measure, not a single quarter's working-capital release.
    • Cash conversion is 90% LTM, and free cash flow was SEK 792m in FY2025.
  • Third: the estimates rose after the Q2 report at every horizon to six months, and a false friend produces beats that are cut later.
    • The next test is dated: the Q3 2026 report on 23 October 2026.
  • Fourth: the earn-out revaluation gain was SEK 45m in 2025 against SEK 968m of core adjusted EBITA.
    • That is a 5% flattering we watch
    • it does not carry the earnings.
  • Fifth, and decisive for the recommendation: our return does not need the compounder multiple.
    • It needs a 55% chance of the stock's own 75th percentile, and even the uncleared branch returns 9% a year on the modal earnings.
  • Where the hypothesis would win: if the engine stalls, or if the return on capital does not move by the FY2027 report.
    • The risks report's own aggregate failure odds (45% to 65% over 24 months against a 30% to 40% base rate for Nordic serial acquirers) are a pre-Q2 view of a company that has since passed its two named tests.
    • The hypothesis is why the thesis is B and not A, why the 'Foundations' and the 'Investment Case' are C, and why the preferred entry is SEK 230 rather than the price now.
    • It is not a reason to reject the case.
