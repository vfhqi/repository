To what extent, and in which direction, is competitive intensity changing?

F3031st of 35

Layer 1 · External change forces

Judgement: competitive intensity is rising, and it is rising fastest in the line that carried the last year's growth.

  • The share Volex took in FY2026 is returning to competitors. The company itself said in October that its prior data-centre outperformance included meeting near-term needs while other suppliers were supply-constrained. As component supply normalised, the capacity premium and part of the share go back. This is the Wisdom Library's 'Tactics Are Level One' and 'The Early-Mover Window Is Not a Moat' in a single disclosure: a window, not a moat.
  • Tier one is consolidating hyperscale copper. Huatai Research expects Amphenol to become the sole supplier of copper connections for Nvidia AI systems; Amphenol bought CommScope's connectivity business; BizLink bought Interplex Datacom (392m) and Xin Fu Sheng Optoelectronics.   • Encroachment elsewhere. BizLink bought PAS Appliance Systems in Mexico (June 2026), a direct entry into North American appliance harnesses, Volex's chosen route out of commodity power cords. Chinese appliance-harness makers are taking share at Volex's European OEM customers. Dhoot Transmission has opened UK and Slovak sites.
