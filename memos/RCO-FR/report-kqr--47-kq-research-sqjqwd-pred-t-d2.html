Section 4 – Currency Hedges and FX Transmission Mechanisms

Section summary
  1. Rémy Cointreau’s rolling hedging program delays transaction currency moves by 12 to 18 months, converting short-term exchange volatility into gradual margin adjustments.
  2. The company's substantial fiscal year 2026-2027 dollar and renminbi cover options provide a strong buffer against immediate downside risk while enabling the group to capture some upside.
  3. Contrasting peer treasury policies show that while Rémy Cointreau seeks systematic coverage, rivals like Pernod Ricard rely primarily on unhedged structures and natural financing offsets.

Gradual transmission: Rémy Cointreau utilizes exchange rate derivatives to smooth transactional volatility by delaying immediate spot market impacts, drawing from an analysis of 12 relevant sources across 5 companies. The company implements currency hedging over rolling 18-month to 36-month horizons to manage its commercial exposure. This long-term strategy prevents sudden currency movements from disrupting short-term current operating profit margins.

Deferred impact: The company's rolling hedging policy means that currency fluctuations typically take several quarters to fully flow through to earnings. For instance, the significant exchange rate swings in fiscal year 2025- 2026 resulted in delayed profit impacts due to existing derivative cover. Consequently, the true economic effects of euro-dollar shifts are transmitted to operating profit with a lag of 12 to 18 months.
