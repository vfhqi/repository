EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (BLUF)

Legrand stands at an inflection point driven by structural datacenter expansion offsetting cyclical building market weakness, with management executing a disciplined portfolio transformation toward digital and energy transition segments while maintaining best-in-class cash conversion and operating margins above peer medians. The company has successfully pivoted its revenue mix with datacenter sales reaching 32% of pro-forma revenues by first-half 2026 and delivering above 30% organic growth, while adjusted operating margins remained resilient at 20.8% despite near-term acquisition dilution.

The September 2026 Singapore Capital Markets Day marked a material strategic upgrade, raising organic growth targets to 6% to 8% through 2030, adjusted operating margin targets to 21% to 22%, and total annual sales growth excluding foreign exchange to 11% to 13% toward EUR 16- 18 billion.

- Analyst/Investor Leadership maintains highly stable executive continuity with CEO Benoît Coquart securing term renewal in May 2026 after leading since 2018, while three new Executive Committee members joined in January 2026 to align with Ambition 2030 targets. Capital allocation priorities shifted materially toward growth, with management raising free cash flow dedicated to M&A to approximately 60% from 50% previously and targeting 5% annual sales contribution from acquisitions through 2030. The datacenter infrastructure build-out represents the dominant organic growth engine, supported by global IT load expansion to 180 gigawatts by 2030 and addressable content value rising from $2.5 million to $3.3 million per megawatt, with datacenter organic sales delivering above 30% expansion in the first half of fiscal 2026.

However, peer-relative organic momentum reveals material challenges, with Legrand's 10.4% organic growth trailing all five primary peers including ABB and Eaton which achieved up to 58% organic order growth in Q2 2026, though aggressive M&A execution helps bridge this organic deficit. Sell-side sentiment remains overwhelmingly bullish with 70% Overweight, 20% Equal-weight, and 10% Underweight ratings across fifteen analysts, price targets ranging tightly from EUR 139 to EUR 200 with a median near EUR 170, and consensus projecting FY27 adjusted earnings per share of EUR 6.70 on group revenues of EUR 12,250 million.

Management operates as a consistent conservative honest-tracker archetype, routinely exceeding initial annual targets with FY25 sales growth reaching 13.1% against the original 6-10% target and FY26 organic guidance upgraded to 8- 10% from 4-7% initially following strong first-half performance. & Co Redburn The European residential construction turnaround represents critical secondary recovery optionality, with nondatacenter segments comprising 60% of revenues currently at cyclical lows, recovery benefits lagging building permits by typical 12 to 18 month transmission periods, and French residential permits exhibiting encouraging 23.9% year-on-year expansion though sluggish construction presents persistent near-term volume headwinds.

Architectural disruption risk from 800VDC systems remains contained with the shift negatively impacting only 20% of datacenter sales, management expecting less than 5% of racks to be full LVDC by 2030, coexisting hybrid architectures benefiting the rest of portfolio, and slow native adoption extending the traditional alternating current runway. Pricing power resilience underpins margin defense with Legrand achieving average positive pricing of 1.5% annually historically, management expecting 1% annually through 2030, adjusted operating margin targets raised to 21% to 22%, and Americas division driving margin expansion through strong operational gearing.

Material downside risks center on three vectors. First, technical disruption concerns focus on 800VDC architectures threatening Legrand's dominant 17% datacenter PDU market share with narrower product portfolios diluting modular design upside relative to integrated peers like Schneider. Second, working capital inefficiencies emerged with free cash flow falling 17% below consensus in Q2 2026 due to larger outflows, broader project delivery increasing working capital demands, trade net working capital growing to 19.5% of sales from datacenter inventory builds, and increased custom project complexity placing higher short-term cash conversion pressure.

Third, compound cyclical failure scenarios pose existential margin threats, with unexpected hyperscaler capex retrenchment combined with prolonged European residential construction freeze potentially breaking the 21% margin floor through severe operational deleverage. No active activist presence emerged in the AlphaSense library over the last 36 months.
